The PMWatch Story Score Sheet
Do you want to contribute in a significant and meaningful way to our drive to build a compelling case against the media? Easy. Take ten minutes of your time and analyze a story on the Palestinian-Israeli conflict using the PWMatch Story Score Sheet. (1) Copy and past the page blow to your favorite word processor (notepad would do). (2) Read your story carefully, keeping track of the score sheet. (3) Fill the score sheet. (4) Send the score sheet to the publication in question and bcc us at pmw-letters@yahoogroups.com. Your analysis will be stored in our database of letters and used for our reports.
