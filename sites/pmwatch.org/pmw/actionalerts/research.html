Join The PMW Media Research Group!

PMWatch is starting a research division to help gadflies and opinion piece writers in their task of writing fact-based, solid pieces.

The research division basically will serve PMW members by finding facts and figures, articles, references, etc., when people ask for such information. This will help speed up the writing process of opinion pieces, essays, letters, etc.

Researchers already have at their disposal quite a collection of essays (our features section) , letters, and links to help them in their task.

I've started a mailing list just for pmw researchers called pmw-research@egroups.com

If you would like to join the research group, please send an email to:

pmw-research-subscribe@egroups.com
