Let PMWatch help you publish your opinion piece

In our communications with various national newspapers, our complaint over the absurd disproportion of column and opinion space given to pro-Israeli voices vs. the meager space given to pro-Palestinian voices (the ratio according to our data is 8-to-1) has been met time and again with the same response: we would LOVE to publish more pro-Palestinian columns, except that we are not getting enough quality submissions. Since none of us who is reading this was born yesterday, we all know this is pure hogwash. BUT: we now have them on-record giving us the lack of material as a reason for not publishing.

So, step two is to provide them with the submissions and to keep a close tab on how many we have submitted and how many they have published, and how many of the other side they have published in the meantime. Hard facts, whenever we talk to an editor or a board member, always put put them on the defensive and they do take such challenges to their professionalism seriously.

Therefore: PMWatch is soliciting help me with providing us with a constant supply of unpublished, original pro-Palestinian opinion columns and essays that the authors would like us to submit on their behalf. The submission will be simply delivered by PMWATCH on behalf of the author, and the author retains full rights, etc., as if they had sent it off themselves.

Please send all submissions to: pmwatch@zworg.com

Please make sure you send us:

  • Full Name
  • Street Address
  • Home and work phone numbers
  • email address
  • Professional title
  • Any relevant affliliations, etc.,
  • One sentence description of what you do (e.g., is a Palestinian-American living in San Jose)

Each time an essay is sent out, the author will be cc-ed on the communication.

We will be sending out to all the major newspapers, with all necessary provisions made for newspapers such as The Washington Post that publishe only exclusive columns.

The essays should be around 700 words long, well written and to the point.

  • - Right of Return (compare Kosovo)
  • - Dismantling ALL settlements, in line with international law
  • - Full unconditional Israeli withdrawal from all 1967 occupied territories
  • - Racism in Israel (apartheid is not just in the WB & GZ)
  • - Reducing US Aid to Israel (even symbolically)
  • - On why the US supports Israel unconditionally
  • - On the Myth of Baraks concessions
  • - The non-viability of the Oslo accords Please suggest topics.

  • - The myth of Rabin the dove
