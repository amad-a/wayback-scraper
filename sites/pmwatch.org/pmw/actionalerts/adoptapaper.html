Adopt a paper today!

In our grass-roots mobilization effort to counter the outrageous bias in favor of Israel in the US media, Palestine Media Watch is kicking off its "Adopt-a- paper" program.

Interested parties would select one local newspaper they have access to on a daily basis and monitor its opinion pages for blatantly pro-Israeli columns and opinion pieces. When they find such columns, they would send a message alerting PMWatch and its members of that piece and hopefully motivate people to write a response.

If you are interested in adopting a newspaper, please send a note that includes you full name, your email address, you city and state, and the name of the newspaper you would like to adopt, to:

  pmwatch@zworg.com

Upon receipt of your note, PMWatch will follow up with guidelines on the logistics of monitoring and submitting to PMWatch.

Our goal is to mobilize as many people as possible so that newspaper editorial boards -- the shapers of public opinion in the United States -- are aware that a second opinion does exit and must be taken into account.
