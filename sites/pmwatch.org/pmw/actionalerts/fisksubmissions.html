[SUBMITTED - The Independent - October 21, 2000]

We in the West live in a world where there is enormous pressure to conform to a certain line about the Arab-Israeli conflict. Therefore it is not surprising if, as the Palestinian Media Watch has reported, The Independent is under attack for publishing articles by Robert Fisk. I ask you to resist this pressure. Fisk is under attack because he is truthful, not becaused he is biased. The honor of the Western news media on the topic of the Middle East is so frayed that the firing of Fisk would practically be a death blow. Please be brave.

[SUBMITTED - The Independent - October 20, 2000]

Robert Fisk is one of the very few who dare to speak. The reason he does not work for Israel is simple: He does not support genocide. Now that the Palestinians are, once again, dying every day on the hands of the Israeli soldiers, it is only natural that the pro-Israeli movements in Great Britain and elsewhere will do everything to try to silence him. My worry is that the Independent itself might feel under pressure and be the first to do the job. This should never happen.

Here are three main reasons. First, Robert Fisk has the right to speak. After all, he is only telling the truth: Israel's policy is based on violence and torture and this is why the Peace Process will never work.

Second, the rest of the world needs to know the facts. The story that we hear everyday is that the Palestinians are violence lovers and, therefore, deserve to die. Robert Fisk says this is wrong: Palestinians are being tortured in their homeland and it is Israel that has to back off.

Finally, Robert Fisk is good for both Israel and its close ally, the US: (1) if the world has to believe and understand where Adolf Hitler went wrong, Israel's policy of mass murder must change; and (2) if the US is to win against 'international terrorism', the most effective way is to give up its hypocritical foreign policy. The vast majority of Muslims do not support violence, but, unfortunately, there are times when terrorists become loved ones for many.

Dr. Aziz Testas -- testas@canada.com
