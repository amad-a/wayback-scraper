Become a Media Gadfly

Are you tired of media distortions about the Palestinian struggle for independence? Are you tired of the pro-Israeli bias that mindlessly portrays the Israelis as peace-loving victims and the Palestinians as irrational, greedy killers?

Well, DO something about it.  Join Palestine Media Watch and its army of media gadflies and pressure the press into covering the crisis objectively.

How? Simple:

1.    Go to http://www.pmwatch.org and read about the effort

2.    Register to the Palestine Media Watch mailing list by sending an email to: pmwatch-subscribe@egroups.com

The purpose of Palestine Media Watch is to fight anti-Palestinian bias in the US media and to call for giving Palestinian views greater room for expression. Palestine Media Watch is a grass-roots media watch group established on October 5, 2000, to help fight US media bias against Palestinians via direct action: writing letters and making phone calls. Our strength is in the commitment of our dedicated members - our gadflies - who day after day, through their informed letters to newspapers, work for a more balanced, more humane, and more informed portrayal of the Middle East conflict.
