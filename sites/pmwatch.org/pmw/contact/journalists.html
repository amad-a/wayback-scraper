Palestine Media Watch contact information -- Journalists
 

The following are the email addresses of reporters. When you complain about a reporter's piece in a particular newspaper, be sure to share your grievance with the other reporters in that paper. In that way, your displeasure is communicated to all reporters at once rather than one reporter at a time.

If you know the email address of a reporter, please share it with us.
