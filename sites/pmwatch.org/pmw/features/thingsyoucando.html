Little things you can do!
  
   

  • Fax in your letter to your newspaper editors once in a while -- faxes catch their attention more than email
  • Send a priority mail, or fedex letter to newspaper editors: that will also catch their attention
  • Send a hardcopy of the PMWatch Talking points package via priority mail to newspaper editors
  • File a complaint with the Better Business Bureau over sloppy journalism
  • Take out a classified add in your local paper that calls on Americans to stop supporting the Israeli occupation
  • Post the PMW flyer in your local supermarket, public library, etc. (but only in public places where this is permitted)
  • Printout and send maps to newspaper editors
If you have an idea about little things people can do to make the media and the American people in general more sensitive to the Palestinian cause, please contact us at: pmwatch@zworg.com
