Stories on Israeli aggression against Khan Yunis refugee camp

Over 52 years have passed since Khan Yunis refugee camp was founded as "temporary shelter" for Palestinian refugees. The home of over 60,000 refugees is now one of the most crowded spots on earth. For a stranger to wander through its narrow alleyways and makeshift houses, by the mounting garbage piles and impoverished streets and markets, one would then realize that human misery is not an abstract concept, but a living reality.
