Background:

1-The involvement in establishing Environmental Education and Nature conservation concepts in the Palestinian curriculum with the Palestinian Ministry of Education to be started in the year 2001.

2-The involvement in founding The National Strategy for Palestine Bio-Diversity with the Palestinian Environment Authority.

3-The leader's training courses executed by CPNP included 450 leaders from different governmental and non-governmental Palestinian Associations on a local, regional and International level.

4-Providing Scientific, Educational, and documented Data on Palestinian Wildlife through the Production of Publications, Magazines, Photos and Audio-visuals.

5-The establishment of the first Environmental Educational Center in Palestine and the region with  different sections and goals.

6-The execution of scientific and environmental  research specially the wildlife survey such as the last IBA's Project according to International criteria.

7-Execution of Regional Palestinian-Arab and Palestinian-Israeli activities to enforce the concept of Peace.

We have the Capacity Building towards local, regional and international cooperation in executing Professional Projects to enforce and implement nature conservation, protection of wildlife, Bio-diversity and habitat as well as environmental education concepts.

Future Planning:

1-The establishment of the first Palestinian Ringing Station in Beit Jala.

2-Developing the educational concepts for the environmental educational center activities.

3-As the available premises are not sufficient, a new building should be added when funds are available.
