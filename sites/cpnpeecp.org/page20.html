As a joint project between the European Natural Heritage Fund (Euro-nature)/Germany and the CPNP an Environmental Exhibition has been developed. The White Stork "Abu Sa'ad " leads visitors through this exhibition. On his migration trip, that knows no boundaries, from Europe through the Middle East to Africa and back, the White Stork tells about the different environmental problems affecting both his life and the life of other creatures . It includes also a photo exhibition documenting the Flora and Fauna in Palestine and Environmental experiments on weather, recycling, water conservation, and other vital Ecological Concepts.
