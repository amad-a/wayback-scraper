The Sites

General Recommendations for IBA's in Palestine



Full Environmental Survey:

Full Data gathering on all the aspects of the Site including the species and the numbers of Birds, Plants and Animals and it's current status and the dangers facing it now and in the future.


Strategy and General Management of the Site:

The Study of the Site and concluding the general strategical goals to the specified area taking in consideration all the current and future considerations to involve the local habitants in the conservation aspect including hunting, grazing and water conservation and planning for plantation or Eco-tourism according to the area's special status.

All those procedures aim at conservation and preservation of the vital wildlife elements .


Training and Awareness:

Includes the training of Rangers , Guards and specialists in the area ,and executing general ecological awarness program to include the habitants of the area in particular and the Palestinian society in general in order to increase the number of interested people on Nature Conservation concepts.


Political Pressure:

The execution of an organized procedures to pressure the parties that has influence on the Bio-diversity in order to limit the destruction on the areas

using all the possibilities and with the help of governmental and non-governmental associations.
