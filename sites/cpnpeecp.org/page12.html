Key to criteria

1-  Regularly holds a significant number of globally threatened species.

2a-Regularly holds 1% or more of species' biogeographical or flyway

      or Middle Eastern population (water birds and seabirds only).

2b-Regularly holds 20,000 or more water birds.

2g-Migratory 'bottleneck' site: more than 3,000 raptors or more than

     5,000 storks or more than 2,000 cranes pass in a season.

3-   Regularly holds a significant number of a species which is threatened

      or declining within the Middle East.  Only the top five sites for each such

      species in the country are considered to qualify as IBA's

4- Regularly holds a significant number of a species 

      whose world population is wholly or largely restricted

      to the Middle East. Only the top five sites for each such   

      species in the country are considered to qualify as IBA's.

5a- Representative example of a habitat, associated with a   

       characteristic assemblage of bird species.

5b- Rare/threatened/unique habitat, associated with a   

       characteristic assemblage of bird species

6-   Site important for bird conservation through

       education/research/tourism.
