Dr. Sana Issa Atallah

Dr. Atallah - field research in Iran

Dr. Atallah's collection

Dr. Sana Atallah was born in Beit Sahour on May, 20, 1943 . He graduated from school in Bethlehem to continue his study in the American University in Beirut. In 1963 he had his B.A degree in Biology. He continued to work as an assistant researcher and  got his Master degree in 1965. He had his Ph.D. in 1969 from the University of Connecticut, USA. His thesis was "Mammals of the Middle East" and included three thousand specimens that he collected himself and a vital scientific study  as well as the discovery of sub-species such as the Golden spiny mouse "acomys rassatus harrisoni".


Dr. Sana Atallah was a phenomenon because of the enormous scientific attributes he provided to the world  at the age of 27.

Dr. Sana Atallah died in a car accident in Iran during his field work on January, 17, 1970.
