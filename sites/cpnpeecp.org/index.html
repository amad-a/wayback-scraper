CPNP ACTIVITIES

Children for The Protection of Nature in Palestine "CPNP" started as a pioneer project in 1992  by the Evangelical Lutheran Schools  in Jordan and Palestine and became today a leading provider for   environmental and educational facilities  as well as Nature Conservation issues for a wide base of Palestinian  Students as well as Researchers.


The "CPNP" established in September 1998 the Environmental Educational Center, The first in the region.
