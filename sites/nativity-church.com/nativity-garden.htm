To The Bethlehem  Refugee  Garden is a place where you could plant an Olive  Tree for yourself or for  lost loved ones so they may be remembered in The Bethlehem in eternity.
The loss of a loved one is always an extremely difficult and trying time for the family left behind. It is a time for reflection, emotion, memories and strength .

Planting a tree in the Bethlehem  Olive garden is a way of creating a living, lasting and enduring memorial to a lost loved in a place of spiritual and historical significance

There is no more meaningful or comforting gesture

Bethlehem Olive  Garden has been built to assist all people in their journey through life. It is a place of personal and spiritual growth. It has been built to give people a sense of comfort and knowing that they are not alone. It is a place for people to heal, grow, laugh, cry, resolve conflict and know that they are uniquely who they are and can be appreciated in that uniqueness.

 

For Universal Peace, Freedom and Justice.
Out of Love and Gratitude to our Creator.
