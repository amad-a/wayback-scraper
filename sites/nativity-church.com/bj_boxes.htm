Handmade Olive wood Boxes one with the Nativity Star and the word Bethlehem inscribed on the lid and the other with the Jerusalem Cross and the word Jerusalem inscribed on the lid

These beautiful Boxes are  hand made from the trimmings of the olive tress by The Bethlehem Bible Artists a group of Christian craftsmen living in Bethlehem. Environment friendly.

 Each Box is:  2.4 W" x 1.4 H" x 2.4 W"
