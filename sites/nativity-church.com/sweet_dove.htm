Justice and peace must kiss each other (Psalm 85:10)

This olive wood peace Dove is handmade with love by the Christian women and children in the village of Jesus birth. They have made it with patience, love and the greatest hope that the world will finally hear their voices. They ask only for peace.

 

Dimension : (H) 3"x (W) 4"

1.When the dark cloud of war
Enshrouded our land,
The war cry filling the Holy Land,
The Dove of  sweet world peace,
No longer could stay,
She spread her wings,
"And flew weeping away."

2. The rumbling of tanks,
And the beats of frightened  hearts,
The Dove of sweet peace,
 fled Bethlehem the cradle of Peace,
Till the war  shall cease.

3. She has flown to a distance,
In quiet to dwell,
There weeping and cooing
In her quiet lone cell,
A waiting the hour,
When the rainbow of peace
Shall burst the dark cloud,
And the war-cry shall cease.

4. When the dark cloud of war
Enshrouded our land,
And the war-cry resounding,
all over the Holy Land;
The Dove of world  peace,
No longer could stay,
On her light wings she --
"Flew weeping away."

Adopt the Bethlehem Peace Doves

Peace is the inspiration of every human being in this new century.

Send this Bethlehem Peace Dove for world Peace... to the political leaders on important anniversaries such as April 22 (Earth Day); the third Tuesday in September (International Day of Peace); October 24 (United Nations Day); and December 10 (Human Rights Day) Let these leaders know that you will accept nothing less than a world free of Hate and Wars.

 

"The effect of Justice is Peace and the result of Justice, quietness and
Security forever" Isaiah 32:17.
