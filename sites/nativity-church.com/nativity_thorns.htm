The Crown of Thorns
 

 

2000 years ago thorn bushes grew abundantly in the Holy Land -- today they still do but they are getting rare due to the high demand for them and from what is left this crown of thorns was made. It is symbolic of Christ' suffering and humiliation.

Size: Approximately 9''x 9''

And when they had platted a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand: and they bowed the knee before him, and mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!" (Matthew 27: 29)
