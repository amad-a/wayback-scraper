The city of AL-BIREH on the middle series of mountains, 15 kilometers north of jerusalem, and it is 860 meters above the sea level, Its area is 24.500 donums. Its name means "WATER WELLS" due to the existence of many water wells and springs. The name was given to it in the year 3500 BC. Located in the center of Palestine, AL-BIREH served as a service between the north and the south. Throughout history, AL-BIREH was the center for all caravans traveling from Jerusalem to Nablus. For almost six thousand years, AL-BIREH was the strategic location armies used to camp and rest, the great leader Salah El-Deen camped in AL-BIREH before he liberated Jerusalem from the Crusaders, stories tell us that Jesus passed through AL-BIREH with his mother when He was a child. In that trip Priest Hilana constructed a church whose remains can be seen in the city center today. Its also believed that Khalifa Omar Bin-Al-Khatab visited the city when he came to liberate it from the Byzantines, the city was also a political and adminidtrative center for the Ottmans until 1917. On July 6, 1967 the Israeli troops occupied the city until 1994. When the civil administration of the city turned over to the Palestinian agreement. 
AL-BIREH is one of the most beautiful cities in Palestine . The natural geographic features of the city the refreshing mild winds and the moderate temperatures make AL-BIREH and ideal place to spend summer vacations, you can find areas to hike in the country, parks to spend quality time with your family and cages to sit with your friends and smoke argilah during the beautiful summer nights. In addition you will enjoy the traditional wedding celebration that take place every year on summer weekends.
Having a population of over 45,000 people, AL-BIREH took under consideration the safety and health of its citizens, the red Crescent Hospital with an emergency department open 24 hours, also Al-Rahma hospital opened for women who perfer to be examined by women doctors.  Many clinical and doctors offices, pharmacies, and hospitals that provide advanced medical care and surgical operations are available in AL-BIREH.
