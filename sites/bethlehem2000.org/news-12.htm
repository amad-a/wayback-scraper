News Brief No. 12; May 21, 1999

 

The Bethlehem 2000 International Committee Grows

Infrastructure projects were the first priority for the Bethlehem 2000 Project Authority when work in Bethlehem began almost a year ago. Infrastructure projects and the associated Cultural heritage projects are the basis for making the entire Bethlehem 2000 Project preparations a success and thus they continue to be essential in the development and modernization of the district.  In order to update you on the progress of the Infrastructure sector of the Bethlehem 2000 Project, we have outlined the on-going projects below. Please note that this listing does not include the completed projects nor those for which we have not yet found funding.

 

Beit Sahour Main Road  Beit Sahour: 2km out of 3.2 of the road have been paved, work is beginning on the final Km. 

Expected date of completion: August 1999.
WB Loan funds project.

 

Jerusalem-Hebron Road - First Part: The construction of a 2km of a four lane divided highway including all necessary infrastructure.

Expected date of completion: before the end of Oct. 1999.
WB Loan funds project.

 

Jerusalem-Hebron Road - Second Part: Preparations to build the infrastructure and excavate in order to build the remaining 2.2km of the four lane highway has begun, however work in area C  of the project, (i.e. 700 m. in length) was suspended on 28/4/1999. This hold up is due to a decision taken by the Israeli authorities. Bethlehem 2000 is pursuing the issue with the Ministry of Civil Affairs.

Expected date of completion: Oct. 1999.
Project is funded by W B Loan.

 

Shepherds Field Beit Sahour Road:. Work is on-going for a 2.5km road leading to Shepherds Field. Expected date of completion: August 99.
Project funded by the Government of Norway.

 

Construction of Bethlehem University Water Reservoir - Bethlehem:  The floor and walls of this reservoir are completed.

Expected date of completion: End of June 1999.
Project funded by WB loan.

 

Solomons Pools-Artas-Bethlehem Roads: Final recommendations are being developed  to award this project for tendering.  Work on this project is expected to begin by the end of  May 99.

Expected date of completion: December 1999.
Project funded by the Government of Italy.

 

Water Distribution Networks ( Bethlehem District): Tendering for this project is completed. The agreement includes constructing Ductile Water pipes, and a Water pump in Bethlehem and Doha.

Expected date of completion: November 1999.
Project funded by WB loan.

 

Rehabilitation of Inner Roads  Bethlehem District:  The evaluation of tenders for rehabilitating  4km of the main inner roads ( which have been in Bethlehem, Beit Jala and Beit Sahour) opened on May 13, 1999 and are ongoing.

Expected date of completion: October 1999.
Project funded by World Bank Loan.

 

Doha Sewage - Doha: Tenders will be submitted by the end of 3 June 1999.  The works construction of 28km of Sewage pipes on Doha, Khader and Beit Jala.

Expected date of completion: February 2000.
Project funded by the Government of the United Kingdom.

 

Karkafa Road Bethlehem: Construction has started.

Expected date of completion: November 1999.
Project funded by USAID.

 

Bethlehem Southern Inner Ring

Final Tendering documents for this project are being developed.  Bid opening will be on May 27.

Expected date of completion: December 1999.
Project funded by USAID.

 

Star Road - Bethlehem: Rehabilitation work began on May 3, 1999.

Expected date of completion: November 1999.
Project funded by the Government of Spain.

 

Paul VI Road - Bethlehem: Work on the part of the road being financed by the Government of Sweden began on May 5, 1999. The tender of the rehabilitation of Madbasa Square and Farhiah Street, which is being financed by the Government of Germany were opened on May 15.

Expected date of completion: October 1999.
Project funded by the Governments of Sweden and Germany.

Rehabilitation of Milk Grotto Street - Bethlehem: Work on rehabilitation has begun.

Expected date of completion: October 1999.
Project funded by the Government of Greece.

 

Rehabilitation of Wasat al-Balad (City Center)  Beit Sahour: Implementation of the project has begun. Expected date of completion: November 1999.

Project funded by WB.

 

Rehabilitation of Dar Mansour  Bethlehem: Implementation has begun.

Expected date of completion: July 1999.
Project funded by the Government of France.

Rehabilitation of Wassat al-Balad (City Centre)  Beit Jala: Work is expected to start on June 1999.

Expected date of completion: November 1999.
WB funded first part of the project.

 

Rehabilitation of St. Marys Church  Bethlehem: Tenders are expected to be opened in late May 99. Work is expected to begin on June 1999.

Expected date of completion: September 1999.
Project funded by WB loan.

 

Construction of Peace Center (next to Manger Square  Bethlehem):  

Work began on November 1999 and is on going.
Expected date of completion: November 1999.
Project funded by the Government of Sweden.

Rehabilitation of the main Vegetable Market - Bethlehem: Work is on going.

Expected date of completion: End of May 1999.
Project funded by the Government of Sweden.

 

Traffic and Tourist Sign Package  Bethlehem district: Under design and tender. This project  includes street signs and historic site signs as well as traffic signs and tourist guiding signs.

Expected date of completion: November 1999.
Project funded by USAID and the Government of France.

 

Orthodox Patriarch Complex near Manger Square (Bethlehem):

This project consists of a commercial center which includes a car park.
The ceiling of the top floor has been completed.
The ground floor of the car park has still to be completed
Expected date of completion: Unknown
Project funded by Private Sector.

 

Rehabilitation of An-Najajre Quarter - Bethlehem: Tender stage. Work on this project is expected to begin on June 1999.

Expected date of completion: December 1999.
Project funded by Japan.

 

Bethlehem Transport Terminal: Digging and leveling of the area began on April 6, 1999. The four-story parking lot in the vicinity of Manger Square is expected to be the main parking lot for all tourist buses.  

Expected date of completion: December 1999.
Project funded by Private Sector.
