News Brief No. 20, Dec. 30, 1999

 

Bethlehem 2000 Project is proud to announce the official opening of the CHRISTMAS MARKET on Star Street in Bethlehem. This official opening will take place on Sunday 19th at 11.00 a.m. under the auspices of Mr. Maher Al-Masri, Palestinian Minister of Trade and Economy and the Minister and Co-ordinator General of Bethlehem 2000 Project, Dr. Nabeel Kassis. Attending, as invitees, are Manuel Salazar, the Consul General of Spain, and the Mayors of the cities of Bethlehem, Beit Sahour and Beit Jala.

 

The Christmas Market is open from 10.00 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily until the 6th of January. Star Street is the street through which the Patriarchs of Jerusalem enter Bethlehem on Christmas. The 20 renovated shops sell traditional souvenirs and handcrafts from Bethlehem area, Jerusalem, Gaza and other Palestinian cities and villages. The Christmas Market aims at revitalizing the economy on this street while inviting visitors and pilgrims to walk along this street on their way to Manger Square and the Church of the Nativity. Star Street has been renovated and rehabilitated with the help of the Spanish Cooperation within the framework of Bethlehem 2000 Project.

For the Fourth Advent Concert in Saint Catherine's Church on Manger Square, Bethlehem 2000 presents the Orchestra and Choir of Covilhã from Portugal, which will perform the "Bethlehem 2000 Christmas Oratorio" at 19:00
