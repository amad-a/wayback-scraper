H. H. Pope John Paul II in the Holy Land of Palestine

 

On March 22, 2000, Bethlehem welcomed a very special visitor: His Holiness Pope John Paul II. The pontiff's long awaited visit to Palestine was a historical event of cultural, political and of course spiritual import. Palestine is the Holy Land and, as such, the cradle of Christianity.  It is home to some of the oldest Christian congregations in the world and, over the centuries, there has been an uninterrupted Christian presence in the region. Palestinian Muslims also cherish the Christian element in their motherland, from both a cultural and a religious point of view. Jesus Christ is the only Palestinian prophet in Islam.

The papal visit included some particularly significant moments, such as the Mass on Manger Square and John Paul's visit to Deheishe refugee camp, were he addressed those who are still waiting for a future of peace and justice, so many times evoked by His Holiness John Paul II.
