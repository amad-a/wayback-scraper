SUCCESS HIGHLIGHTS

 

Bethlehem hosts the world

Since the inauguration of the Bethlehem 2000 celebrations on December 4, 1999 Bethlehem hosted a series of well publicized, well attended cultural and religious events in which leaders from the temporal and spiritual worlds met to honor Bethlehem as the town where modern times began. As guests of the Palestinian National Authority, world leaders representing all parts of Europe, the America's, Africa and the Middle East and  spiritual leaders of all Churches and high ranking representatives from the Islamic world gathered in Bethlehem  to commemorate the birth of Jesus Christ and usher in the new millennium. Cultural events, which included; world renowned groups performing classical and modern music, folkloric performances, religious and temporal themes, eastern and western artists, and Christian as well as Muslim performers graced Manger Square and the Churches of Bethlehem and brought together a world that had all too often stood separate and apart from one another. 

 

Successful Rebuilding and Edging towards realization of Economic Goals

Under the direct auspices and guidance of the Bethlehem 2000 Project, the old city cores of B e t h l e h e m , Beit Sahour and Beit Jala are being successfully renovated, rehabilitated and pedestrianised.  A

programme to promote private sector investment in the old city cores was initiated to revive and recreate a once vibrant commercial and cultural life.  Tools to promote an efficient mechanism to generate revenue and added value to Bethlehem's economy as well as the national economy were linked as part and parcel of long term national planning. The completion of five star venues which can host events, conferences and tourists.

 

Other success highlights

$100 million in ongoing private sector investments;  $58 million in funds for infrastructure projects; $20.6 million in funds for cultural heritage projects.

$2.3 million in funds for tourism development projects; UN General Assembly resolution in support of the Bethlehem 2000 Project (resolution 53/27 and resolution 54/36) Establishment of an International Committee with distinguished membership; l The participation of Palestine and Bethlehem 2000 at all major international tourism fairs. Featuring of Bethlehem 2000 at major international forums including the Annual Meeting of the World Economic Forum at Davos in 1999 and 2000.
