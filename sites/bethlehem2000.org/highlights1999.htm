HIGHLIGHTS of 1999

 

THE ADVENT CONCERTS

Every Sunday of Advent, first-class concerts were given in Bethlehem. Capella Ducale di Venezia (Italy), XVIII Century Orchestra (Netherlands) and Orchestra and Choir of Covilha (Portugal) took part in this remarkable concert series, which was inaugurated by the world famous Vienna Boys Choir (Austria). A special highlight was the premiere of the Bethlehem 2000 Oratorio, composed by L. Cipriano for the occasion of the Millennium in Bethlehem.

 

OFFICIAL INAUGURATION OF THE MILLENNIUM

In a powerful, moving moment on Bethlehem's Manger Square, the patriarchs, archbishops and bishops of the 13 Churches of Jerusalem gathered together with His Excellency President Yasser Arafat to send the world a message of peace and justice in the official inauguration of the Millennium.

CHRISTMAS EVE

In the presence of H.E. President Arafat and his guests, the prime ministers of Spain and Italy - as well as a number of high-ranking officials from different countries-, a festival of choirs from all over the world took place on Manger Square to

celebrate the most special night of the Millennium.

 

NEW YEAR'S EVE

With the world's attention focused on Bethlehem, 2000 doves were set free to fly over Manger Square, as a tribute to peace. Moroccan singer Amina Alaui and Algerian Raj performer Rashid Taha welcomed the new millennium together with the people of Bethlehem and thousands of visitors.

 

Highlights 2000

 

ORTHODOX CHRISTMAS

Hosted by H.E. President Yasser Arafat, attended a Palestinian and Russian Orthodox choir programme. The ensembles taking part included the Beit-Jala Choir; the Amman Choir from Jordan, Ossipov National Academic Orchestra, Sveshnikov State Academic Russian Choir; the Sirrin Sacred Music Company and many vocal and instrumental soloists. As a grand finale, renowned Greek singer DEMIS ROUSSOS performed on Manger Square.

 

ARMENIAN CHRISTMAS

Bethlehem was this year graced with the presence of the highest representation of the Armenian Church, and entertainment was provided by the St.Vartan's and Seminar Choirs.
