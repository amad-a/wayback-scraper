The "Nazareth 2000" Project

The Israeli Government identified the Holy City of Nazareth as having tremendous potential for tourism. Accordingly, the Government, in cooperation with Nazareth Municipality, the Tourism ministry, the Tourist Corporation, and other ministries, drew up a master plan, called "Nazareth 2000", to exploit this potential. A budget of some US$
100 million was allocated to the Project, culled from various public sources.

Part of the renovated old city

New traffic system

According to the "Nazareth 2000" Project, the streets of the old city, especially those linking the major historical sites, the churches and the old market are being transformed into beautiful promenades.

The main street of Nazareth , Paul VI Street, is being turned into a boulevard , with two-way traffic permitted for buses only. Other traffic will be diverted to Al-Salam Street, parallel to the main boulevard. This new traffic system is designed to facilitate the movement and parking of tourist buses.

The city's two main squares , the Mary's Well Square and the City Center Square, will be provided with tourist bus parks nearby, thereby enabling tourists to visit the holy sites and the old market, and to return to their buses afterwards.

The Old Market

Nearby the Church of the Annunciation lies the Old Market, a bustling and colorful bazaar, which sprawls over winding lanes up the Galilean hillside. It is comprised of many
secondary markets, such as the Brides market ( for bridal dresses and other needs), the Blacksmith 's, the Goldsmith's, and the Vegetable market.

Hidden among the shops are public squares, mansions and many other historical and architectural treasures.

The "Nazareth 2000" Project aims to turn the old market into one of the most attractive
sites in Israel. The process of conservation and restoration, which is ongoing, has already led many tourist operators and guides to recognize the majesty and historical significance of Nazareth's old buildings, its churches, its houses and its old shopping arcades.
 Increasingly, Nazareth's old market attracts T.V networks and journalists from around the world, to document the uniqueness of the city.

Nazareth Old Market is also growing as a favorite resort for Israelis, who spend their weekends and their holidays in Nazareth.

Mount of the Precipice

In order to develop Nazareth's tourist capacity more fully, the Israel Land Authority has allocated an area outside the city, to provide for more hotel accommodation. This new
complex is located on the slopes of the Mount of the Precipice, overlooking the Jezreel Plain, with Mount Tabor on one side and the old city on the other.

Other attractions

Over the years, the annual Liturgical Music Festival, which is held in Nazareth, has become a further point of attraction for both Israeli and foreign tourists. Much of the attraction lies in the fact that the Festival is held inside some of the city's beautiful old churches.

A specially designed programme of street lighting is being implemented, to enhance the charm of the old city. Historical sites will be illuminated in different colurs, both at night and during the day, using lasers.

Nearby the hotel complex, a special garden is under construction, which is intended to include model-sized reproductions of key buildings around the world associated with Christianity.The uniqueness of the garden is expected to attract thousands of visitors from all over the country.
