The objective is to create the spectacle of a town which is transformed at night.

The aim is not to reproduce what we see during the day, but on the contrary, to rouse an alternative emotion by bringing out and highlighting aspects which are lost in the daytime urban tissue.
NAZARETH's Lighting Program will cover all districts of the town. The natural sites, monuments, public places (squares, gardeps, parks) and the facades of buildings, numerous ancient dwellings, main streets and roads, pedestrian precincts and narrow streets will all be equally important factors of the program.

To achieve this, six main themes will have to followed to elaborate projects adapted to the town's identity.

 

1. Contribute to the beautifvinig of the town
Visual and dynamic support for our professional knowhow; lighting is today an important aspect and an opportunity for municipalities to provide:

  • a socially orientated impact because lighting is greatly appreciated by the public on a local level (everyday life),
  • an action to promote its image and its renown on a national and international level.
  • an economic and touristic impact.

The lighting enhancement of monuments and sites that Nazareth is so fortunate to have in such great numbers is one of the essential points of the project. Lighting can reveal all the architectural richness but remain as faithful as possible to the spirit and personality of the site. The Nanzareth 2000 project seeks to restore a rustic atmosphere by the lighting enhancement of hundreds of ancient dwellings.

2. Associate chosen sites and monuments

The Lighting Program is a nocturnal luminous counterpart to the light which illuminates the town by day. The light emitted faithfully respects colour tones and accentuates the profiles of mouldings and forms by lightly projected shadows.

The object of urban lighting is to adopt a method which will incite a more global reflection on the evolution of the town's nocturnal image, in accordance with its history, its development and its culture. Nazareth is fortunate to be surrounded by mountains on which buildings such as the Saint Gabriel hotel and the Salesian monastery lend the town a distinguished silhouette.

3. Draw forth an emotion  imagination

The old town, of great historical importance, will slowly transform itself as night falls into a theatre composed of shadow and light. At night, a new image and hidden expressions of the town will be revealed. The town will be transformed into a waltz of light and emotions.

Lighting has progressively become a creative and inventive material thanks to the multiple effects it can make and its strong visual impact which can induce people's imagination.

4. Beckon a deepening of people's knofledge of the town.

The lighting scheme should give people envy to discover and deepen their knowledge about the town, their town, by giving it a new urban dimension allowing it to reveal its history, its culture and its development.

5 Create a nocturnal

in the past urban lighting has been developed on a much more functional basis. renowned and appreciated for the aspects of secureness that it can bring. Today, however, this is no longer the case.
It can be seen that 'over last few years new lighting environments have appeared in towns, these are more comfortable and often allow forgotten heritage to be highlighted and enhanced. It also renders squares, public places, natural sites of meeting and communication more convivial.

6. Make Passersby feel in security

While revealing another dimension of the town, the Lighting Program also aims at making Nazareth more beautiful, more attractive and more secure.

It is important to conciliate the need to get around town easily in an agreeable and safe environment, with the notions of visual comfort and harmonious integration in the urban environment. To achieve this, new ambient lighting has been created by the generalized implementation of indirect or semidirect lights using structurally integrated projectors and new technology.

Lighting must not be considered as an isolated element but as a link between functions, citizens and the sites. it can be considered as a sort of guiding element in the city by the luminous path that it traces at night. The town can be considered and interpreted as a puzzle (formed by streets, by districts), as a whole (the territory, the landscapes) or as numerous reference points used to appropriate the town.
