The lighting program will englobe th whole of the town and its diffrent district. Religious sites, monuments, the facades of some houses, main avenues and the coherence of urban lighting furnishings will all be relevant components of this program.

Light will progressively recompose a more agreeable environment using dynamic, mixed, direct and indirect lighting.

Veritable new surroundings will be created by the astute treatment of ground surface materials, facades and architechtural structures.

The lighting system must take into account the daytime landscape by favouring the integration of lighting equipment in historical and contemporary public places.

The lighting, in the same manner as a script, will enhance the scenic capacities of the town's sites and heritage.

Light will create a special event.
