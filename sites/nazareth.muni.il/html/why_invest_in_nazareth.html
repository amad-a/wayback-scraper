THE HISTORICAL BACKGROUND OF THE CITY NAZARETH

The angel Gabriel was sent from God unto a city of Galilee called Nazareth, to a virgin spoused to a man whose name was Joseph .... , and the virgin's name was Mary...."

" And the angel said unto her: fear not, Mary, for though haste found favour with God. And , behold , though shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son, and shalt call him Jesus".

" And he came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth, in fulfillment of that which was spoken by the prophets. He (Jesus) shall be called a Nazarene."

...And indeed, it was in Nazareth that Jesus lived for the major part of his life.
The Church of St. Joseph's Carpentry contains ruins dating back to the first century Anno Domini. Tradition holds that this was the site of Joseph's workshop and of the Holy Family's home.
Nazareth is the biggest Palestinian Arab city in Israel, with a population of 60,000, Christian and Muslims population is a powerful historical example of religious coexistence.
Nazareth's geographical location makes it a perfect base from which to tour the rest of the Galilee. To the north of the city lies Cana where Jesus turned water into wine. Also nearby is Sephoris, Tiberias, Mount Tabor, the Jordanian river and many other historical locations.

Nazareth offers a unique experience to the visitor. Picturesque red roofs and white stone churches sit haphazardly on the Galilean hillside. The wonderful panorama on offer from the heights above Nazareth, encompasses in its scope the entire Jezreel Valley.

As one of the most important Christian holy sites in the world, Nazareth has always been a special haven for pilgrims. The visitor finds an exciting ambiance with exotic flavors and textures, imbued with the famed warmth and hospitality of the Arab population. At the same time, Nazareth retains many spiritual oases of serenity and peace, provided by the diverse Christian orders in their beautiful churches and monasteries.

Among the important sites in Nazareth are: the Church of the Annunciation, which is by far the largest in the area, incorporating remains of earlier churches from the Byzantine and Crusader periods; the church of St. Gabriel, where, according to tradition, Mary first received the Annunciation from angel Gabriel; Mary's Well; the ynagogue church, which is built over the site of the synagogue where, according to Christian tradition, Jesus prayed; the Church of the Redeemer; the Menza Christi Church, which is built over the rock on which Jesus is said to have eaten with his disciples.
