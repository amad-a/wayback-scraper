How to Invest in Nazareth

The following will help you to bring your investment into reality.


Investment in the Hotel Complex:

The 4 plots, will be sold by a tender, but companies, of which 51% or more of it's holdings, are under foreign control, are allowed
to purchase the plots directly from
the Israel Land Authority.

In order to achieve an " Approved
Enterprise " status, you should apply to the Ministry of Tourism. The application will include basic
plans and an economic study for the your project.

Before you start planning your project, it is recommended to consult ate The City Planning Department, so your plans will be in accordance with Nazareth Town
 Plan 4070/c.
Learning The Town Plan 4070/c, will help you recognize any special conditions or specific requirements of the chosen plot.

A basic chart of your project should be delivered to the Planning Dpt. for approval.
When the basic chart is approved,
you may proceed with the detailed
 planning. These plans, when ready, are to be delivered to The
City Engineer for licensing.


The Historic core

If you choose to make your investment in the historic core region, The Conservation and Antiquities Department in Nazareth
 municipality, has prepared for you
 detailed lists for most of the old buildings and houses that might be
 exploited.


Supportive Departments

The " Nazareth 2000 Project " is co-managed by the Nazareth Municipality and The Government
Tourist Company. The managing
department is responsible for
developing
the infra structure in the city.
You may contact the " Nazareth
2000 Managing Department ", in
order to learn more about the
development projects, and this
may help you plan your
investment in accordance.

For your convenience, the
Nazareth Municipality established
recently an Economic Department
which, provides you with any
information needed.
After learning of your project, the
Economic Dpt. will help you:
- Find the most suitable place for
your investment
- The best way to do your investment.
