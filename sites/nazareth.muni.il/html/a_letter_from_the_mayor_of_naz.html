A Letter from the Mayor of Nazareth

Nazareth, the city of the Annunciation, has tremendous tourism potential. In recent years, the Nazareth Municipality, the Ministry of Tourism and the Government Tourist orporation have collaborated to uncover the unique crypts and corners of the ancient city after many years of being a neglectedcity incapable of fulfiling its pilgrimage and tourism potential. Nazareth 2000 was designated to change the image of Nazareth by creating a tourism and municipal infrastructure of high international standards. So today Nazareth is once again shining its historical beauty on the world, as the third millennium approaches. The city is expecting millions of tourists, especially Christian pilgrims, to arrive over the next few years. It also means that the city can offer plenty of investment opportunities that will surely produce excellent returns.
I hereby invite you to take advantage of this golden opportunity to join the people of Nazareth in providing a suitable atmosphere for tourists coming to celebrate the end of the second millennium.
