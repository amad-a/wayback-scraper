Signs and Wonders

When we designed the new signs for Nazareth, the character of the city, with its various rhythms, presented us with a challenge. We worked in accordance with the fabric of the urban landscape, which is rich in architectonic elements; we also took the bold colors of the commercial signs into account, and the limitations imposed by the narrow sidewalks.

 Adherence to a specific design language - with regard to the sign's silhouette, colors, and typography - helps ensure prompt identification and makes the sign stand out in the colorful city setting.

  The use of thin pole provides a solution to the problem of the narrowness of the city's sidewalks and lanes. With an eye to the varying conditions in

the city, we created joints that allow the sign to be hung in different ways; the signs can also be posted on existing walls and columns in accordance with circumstances.  The urban landscape is

also taken into account in the style of the
details of the sign, such as use of images, production techniques, and materials.

 We chose materials that can remain in use for a long time and still retain their freshness, such as iron, cast iron, and enamel.
