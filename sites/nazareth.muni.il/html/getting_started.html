Getting Started

-``All the talk about the plans about  Nazareth 2000 was just an abstract idea until recently, when several organizational decisions were made," says David (Dei) Harnik, director general of the Israel Government Tourist Corporation.

  At the end of may 1995, a supervisory steering committee was established to oversee the implementation of the project. Its members are Ramiz Jaraisy, mayor of Nazareth, and Eli Gonen, Doron Shorer, Amos Rudin, directors general of the Tourism, Transport and Construction and Housing ministries respectively.
Working under the auspices of this committee will be a steering and implementation committee, headed by Harnik and comprised of public figures and representatives of various government ministries.

Bellow:
 the Galilee landscape of wheat and barely fields, fruit and olive groves, and rounded limestone hills - the scenery that gives Nazareth its unique charm. Some of these landscapes have been damaged by the massive development of the city in recent years and the construction of upper Nazareth on the surrounding hills.

According to victor Albaranes, the Israel Government Tourist Corporation's deputy director general for marketing, the aims of the project are as follow: to build a proper infrastructure for visits by tourists and pilgrims, to ensure comfort for the visitor today and during the expected wave of visitors in the year 2000, to enable all Christians communities to have access to the holy places, to highlight the charm of Nazareth, and to develop points of interest and long, varies touring routes.

  The project is also meant to raise the standard of living of the citizens of Nazareth, develop sources of employment, increase options for tourist accommodations and services in the city, and create conditions for investment in Nazareth's tourism industry.

  About a year ago, architect and town planner Arie Rahamimoff was asked to prepare a plan for the development of tourism infrastructures in Nazareth.

 Rahamimoff and his team identified sites of historical and cultural value that fit the description of ``national treasures." a term first coined in the master plan for Israel in the year 2020.

  On July 17th, Rahamimoff is to present his plan to tourism minister Uzi Baram, the Israel Government Tourist Corporation, and the Nazareth Municipality. if the plan is approved, the government will be asked to recognize Nazareth 2000 as a national project and allocate about NIS100 million to the program.

  But the work has already begun. The market has been renovated and a ceremony marking the completion of its face-lift is to take place soon.
