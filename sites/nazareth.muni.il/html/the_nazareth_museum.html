The Museum as a Total Experience:
The museum that, according to plan, will be located in the old Saraya building, will tell the tale of Nazareth.

By means of state-of-the-art presentation devices, such as environmental reconstruction, models, diorama, animation, displays, video, presentation and computerized simulation, the visitors will enjoy the reenactment of the dramatic events which unfolded on the stage of Nazareth and its surrounding countryside.

Indeed, they will participate in a total Galilean experience, getting a glimpse of life in Nazareth through the ages. They well also have the opportunity of experiencing the distinctive Galilean sounds and tastes.

An Orientation and Cultural  Center:
The Nazareth Museum will serve as an information and enrichment center for the throngs of tourism who visit the city every year - a focal point for visitors and residents alike.

The Nazareth museum will be a lively and active site; gone will be the image of silent building housing a collection of exhibits selected at random. The Museum will serve the educational system of the city, turning into a culture meeting-place for the whole of the population of the region - a source of pride to Nazareth's inhabitants.
