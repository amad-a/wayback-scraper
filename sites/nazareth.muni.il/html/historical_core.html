THE HISTORICAL CORE

The historical core consists mainly of the restored old market, a site visited by thousands of tourists every day, The visitors delight in the authentic Middle Eastern atmosphere, walking through the charming arcades, shopping and eating. They come away with a truly unforgettable experience.

Notable Homes

Nazareth's historic center includes many notable homes from the 19th century which once belonged to wealthy merchants and landowners. These outstanding dwellings feature wooden ceiling beams, red-tiled roofs, stained-glass windows, white marble floors, triple arches and painted ceilings - all influenced by both Ottoman and European architecture and ornamectation.
Some of these houses have already been converted into wonderful tourist attractions, such as hotels, resturants and galleries.
The city of Nazareth offers other similar investment opporunities.

Khan El-Pasha

One of the beautiful and important sites in Nazareth is Khan El-Pasha. Built many centuries ago, it was restored during the latter part of the 18th century by Abd-Alla Pasha, the Wally of Sidom
The khan is a family endowment belonging to the descendants of Nazareth's High Commissioner, Abdalla El Fahoum. It has recently been restored again by the tenth trustee, Atef Fahoum.

The Khan Consists of:

    1.  A 1,600 square-meter ground floor featuring a courtyard surrounded by arcaded cross-vaulted shops and a 1,200 square-meter outdoor commercial center. The proposal calls for turning the ground floor into special restaurants, cafeterias and other tourist facilities.

    2.  A first floor with 750 square-meters of rooms, and the option of adding a third floor containing 1,200 square meters of hotel rooms, terraces and restaurants.
