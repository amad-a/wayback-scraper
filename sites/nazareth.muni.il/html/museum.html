Introduction

Establishing a museum in Nazareth, which tells the story of the city, is not an ordinary event. The 2000 year old small village, scene of the annunciation and childhood of Jesus, significantly influenced the historical development of mankind.
The year 2000 celebrations first originated in Nazareth in 1991 - a project which, over the years, enjoyed the baking of the Israel Government. And earned international support.

Today, Nazareth is the largest Arab-populated City in Israel, the culture and spiritual capital of its Arab citizens. For centuries, Nazareth's residents - Moslems and Christians - have lived side by side in harmony. Large public budgets have been invested by the government, and the city to establish an appropriate infrastructure for the millions of tourists and pilgrims who visit Nazareth's sites yearly. Record numbers of visitors are expected during the year 2000 and afterward. But this is not enough. To achieve its objectives, the ``NAZARETH 2000" project will need worldwide support and aid.

The ``Nazareth Museum" planned by the Municipality of Nazareth and Israel Government Tourist Corporation, under the auspices of UNESCO, is one of the most important features of the ``NAZARETH 2000" projects. Its establishment will turn it into one of the ``NAZARETH 2000" project. Its establishment will turn it into one of international scale, and considerable relevance to heritage of mankind.

Ramiz Jaraisy
