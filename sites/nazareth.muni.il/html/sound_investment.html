Why Invest in Nazareth

A sound investment

According to the Central Bureau for Statistics, the number of tourists who visited Nazareth in 1994 was 850,000; in 1995, this figure grew to 1.5 million.

These figures are forecast to increase rapidly in the next few years, reaching a peak of 2 million tourists in the year 2000. Nazareth's significance as a major tourist venue will undoubtedly be enhanced following the Vatican's designation of Nazareth as one of the principal sites of the Jubilee celebrations, marking the year 2000.

Thus, Nazareth offers the investor the prospect of attractive returns within a limited time scale for those investors who are willing to take early decisions and to move ahead with their investment plans.

In order to achieve the maximum returns, investors will wish to have their projects
complete in time for the expected influx of tourists, as we move towards the end of the second millennium..
