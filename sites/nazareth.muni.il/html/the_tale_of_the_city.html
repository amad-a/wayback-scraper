The Tale of the City

Museological Concept:
The permanent exhibition of the museum will focus on subjects relating to Nazareth past, highlighting the role played by historical event for which it is famous and the traditions involved. The exhibition will give an account of Nazareth's history by means of a number of focal points, the central ones being the village, which existed 2000 years ago, and the annunciation episode.

State-of-the-art presentation will be used in the exhibition, as well as models and special technical effects, couple with original exhibits, ancient manuscripts, and textual and graphic material.


Galilean Countryside:
Olive trees, vines, pomegranates and herbs - all of them reminiscent of the Galilean countryside - will greet the visitors who have just left the commotion of the market behind them, as they enter the courtyard of the Saraya.
In one of the corners of the reconstructed yard, The visitors will suddenly come across an old carpentry workshop; suggestive of the Christian tradition associated with Nazareth.

``The Well" presentation
The visitor will be first introduced to Nazareth and its history by means of a spectacular multi-media presentation. Figures and events will emerge from the waters of the well, the central theme of his show. The well is Nazareth's source of life and tradition, serving as a connecting link between the successive periods of its history. For many years the well was situated on the outskirts of the city, a gathering-place for the local women who used to draw from its water. Eventually lost in a maze of streets and building as Nazareth developed and turned into a city, it nevertheless remained one of its most outstanding landmarks.

By means of sophisticated animation devices, changing figures and scenes will be reflected on the waters of the spring. Images of rulers and conquerors, clergymen and inhabitants, will ripple over the waters to then rise over the bare walls of the room. Local women too will be seen descending to the spring, as was the custom from the days of the Virgin Mary and through the ages.

This chronicle of fascinating historical events will serve to create a world of illusion in which the visitors will immerse themselves, while past and present, old and new, flash before their eyes. The 5-minute presentation will be accompanied by narrative and background music, which will start by describing modern -day Nazareth, and then work back into time.
