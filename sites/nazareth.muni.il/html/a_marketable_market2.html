A Marketable Market
 

   After the shops were put back in place, their facades were thoroughly renovated and many of the invasive additions were removed. The market was covered with a modern, uniform roof and its streets were repaved with Galilee stones and black Golan basalt. Rest areas were built, and some of the old squares were opened up. The vendors began to renovated their shop interiors, and many shops were given a total overhaul.

  Most of the infrastructure and development work has been completed, and additional areas are slated for expansion and renovation. Soon it will be possible to stroll through the market and pause in such plazas as Mosque Square, Cafe Museum Square, beside the former head quarter of the Turkish governor, which later served as City Hall and is to become the Nazareth Museum. Once again, visitors will be able to enjoy the unique atmosphere of a Mediterranean ``market and square" city.
