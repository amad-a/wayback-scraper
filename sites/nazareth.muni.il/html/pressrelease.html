PRESS RELEASE

During a visit to the Vatican, in September 1996, by Ramiz
Jaraisy,  the Mayor of Nazareth, Cardinal Roger Echegaray, President of the committee for ... AD 2000 Jubilee Celebrations declared that:

 "...through the preparations made for The Great Jubilee and in accordance with the wishes of the Pope John Paul II, The Holy Land, and specially Nazareth of the Annunciation together with Jerusalem and Bethlehem, will be one of the most important places where the coming Jubilee will take place ..."

I have pleasure in informing you that, in view of the importance attached by UNESCO to those celebrations, as already reflected in the Organization's support for the "Bethlehem 2000" project, I have decided to grant UNESCO's sponsorship and to offer is support for the project "Nazareth 2000".

Federico Mayor,
The Director General
of the Unesco
