Museum-Related Activities

Space will be allocated on the ground floor of the building for a number of special museum activities, for the benefit of visitors and local residents, who will use the museum framework as a venue for educational and cultural activities.

Educational Section and Temporary Exhibitions:
A multipurpose hall with educational devices and projection equipment will be at the disposal of pupils, local residents and groups of visitors. This venue will be used for lectures and seminars on museum related topics: the history of Nazareth, sects and religions, as well as subjects relating to the restoration and conservation of historical buildings.
This hall will also be used as a venue for temporary exhibitions on related topics.

Information Center:
A computerized information unit will be located in the vicinity of the museum shop, by means of which visitors ill be able to obtain details on tours available in the city and activities scheduled on site.

The Museum Shop.
The museum shop will serve both as an introductory and concluding feature of the tour, and will be an integral part of the interactive exhibition. The visitors will have the opportunity of experiencing the tastes of Galilee by means o number of light traditional dishes: fruit, spices spiked with olive oil, cheeses and baked savories and pastries which will be displayed in a traditional environment. Descriptive information will be available.

These items will be on sale, as well as other original souvenirs, memento of the visit to the museum.

Offices, Storerooms and support areas:
These units will be located in appropriate quarters of the building.
