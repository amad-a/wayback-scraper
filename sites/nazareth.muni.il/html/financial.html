Why Invest in Nazareth

Financial Grants and Tax Exemptions

To promote investment in Nazareth, the government has granted the city priority ``A" region status. This enables hotels and other tourist facilities to enjoy the benefits of being classified an ``Approved Enterprise".

As for the year 1997, The benefits consist of:
  • Grants of up to 24% of the investment in fixed assets and land preparaion.
  • Reduced tax rates to as low as 10% for a period of 10 years.
  • A full tax exemption for up to ten years, if the right to recive grants is waived.
  • Accelerated depreciation.
