Introduction

The historic city of Nazareth will be one of  the major venues for official celebrations marking the year AD 2000.

The Holy Land is expecting 5 million Christian pilgrims in the year 2000 and it is likely that the majority will visit Nazareth.

This great event will definitely put Nazareth on the global tourist map, making it one of the most visited holy sites in the Holy land. And the huge tourist influx is bound to grow in the years to follow.

All of this creates the need to significntly increase the scope of facilities offered to the multitude of visitors expected to arrive as the sun sets on the second millennium.
The new facilities will include hotels, hospices, restaurants, cafes, new touristic attractions and the like.

In order to meetthis need, Nazareth is offering incentives to investors interested in committing themselves and their resources to the task at hand.

The huge number of visitors expected to arrive in Nazareth promise handsome profits for the investor.
