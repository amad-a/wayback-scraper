Nazareth - Mount of Precipice Tourist Complex

" AND THE PEOPLE BROUGHT HIM (Jesus) TO THE PRECIPICE OF THE MOUNTAIN THAT THEIR
CITY (Nazareth) WAS BUILT UPON ". ( Luke 4)

When completed, the hotel complex on the slopes of The Holy Mount Of Precipice will include 2,000 rooms in 10 hotels.

The complex is planned as a city within a city, in keeping with the character of the existing town and with adequate parking
spaces. A promenade will link the complex to the Mount.

The buildings within the complex will be between 4-5 stories high. Following the topographical line of the hill upon which it is

to be built, the complex will encompass gardens and pools, with a network of paths overlooking Nazareth and its surroundings.

The complex has been designed and approved in accordance with Nazareth Town Plan 4070/c.

Detailed plans for the various plots of land, including guidelines as to the character of the project, proposed programmes
and building parameters for each plot have been drawn up. Individual planning of each hotel must be carried out in a manner
appropriate to the general character of this unique complex.

The plots of land will be leased to investors by the Israel Land Authority, (the owners of the land). Infrastructure development
will be carried out by the Israel Land Authority.
