Not so lona ago, Nazareth was a small picturesque village. Today. it is a town situated on the spur of the Galilee mountain where different religions peacefully cohabit and which attracts a great number of tourists and christian pilgrims

The town of Nazareth has undertaken important town planning works as the year 2000 approaches.

  • Construct an infrastructure adapted to touristic visits and pilgrimages for the comfort of today's and future visitors at the dawn of the year 2000,
  • Give the opportunity to christian communities to discover and have access to holy places,
  • Exalt the charm of Nazareth,
  • Develop points of interest and varied tourist itineraries,
  • Identify unique cultural, historical and architectural sites,

These are the objectives which have been fixed by the NAZARETH 2000 team.

An omnipresent nocturnal lighting scheme will be the natural continuation of these measures.

NAZARETH is a unique place and should be treated as such.

A global architectural lighting program will express the desire to no longer treat lighting as an individual act, as a distinct element of architectural and town planning consideration, or as an additional functional entity, but rather to integrate it as a specific component of urban projects in order to show one's willingness to adopt coherent measures for town planning.

Adopting an architectural and urban lighting program means that a complete diagnostic has to be made consisting of structured reflection concerning the whole of the town in order to redesign the layout of the main urban arteries, to reveal its architectural richness, as well as its natural green belt and mineral sites along with sites which privilege interesting views.

This method imposes certain priorities.

The town is a means of communication. When illuminated, it becomes a showcase which gives one envy to further discover it, to wander through it and get to know it better.

The lighting scheme should sharpen curiosity and urge people to stay.

Amongst other things, the way public places are illuminated is a reflection of the town's culture. It expresses the relationship between man, nature and the site. Lighting asserts continuity between the actual culture and that of tomorrow through the art of urban composition.

The vegetation found in the town of Nazareth, in all its forms; trees, shrubs, flowers, is not an element to be neglected, and will become one of the lighting's nocturnal supports. Research and the choice of suitable light furnishings for the global lighting program are of primordial importance because they must be the link and the interpretation support which will unite the entire urban tissue of the town of Nazareth.

The lighting equipment must reply to strict and precise qualitative criteria :

  • aby direct-indirect, integrated or axial lighting which will provide
  • maximum comfort and in every circumstance will be on a human scale to favour pedestrians,
  • The variety of light furnishings will be in perfect harmony with the rest of already created urban furnishings or those still to be created, as well as the slanalling system.
  • It will be of coherent design which will blend in well with the surroundings.
  • For example, lamp posts will be very refined, discrete and sober and of a dark shade.
  • Light sources will be vivid white to brighten up colours (Pedestria I nprecincts, vegetation, water) and soft golden white for general lighting.
