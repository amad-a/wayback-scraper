Daytime integrated light furnishings will be a nocturnal visual guide and veritable point of reference. They will be as follows :

1. Main streets and roads

8 to 9m high light furnishings with soft golden white light sources which will characterise the approach to the town (important traffic circulation).

2. Gateways into the town :

Neutral furnishings which are invisible during the day, they will be integrated in the facades and axial lighting will be employed in the more narrow streets. This will characterize the mixture of motor vehicles and pedestrians with a strong presence of the latter.

3. Limits of the, old historical town

Pilgrims and tourists want I to visit all the town's historical sites and they want to breath the atmosphere as it was at the time of Jesus.

wall mounted furnishings which remind one of torches and characterise the rustic atmosphere of olden days.
This equipment has a dual function : Highly comfortable qualitative ambient lighting, a vertical signal giving the impression of a flame
