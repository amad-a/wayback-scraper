     Welcome ..

    Ramez Jaraisy
    Mayor of
    Nazareth
    City

         As Mayor of the City of Nazareth, it is my pleasure to extend
     a warm welcome to you as a visitor to Nazareth, City of the Annunciation.
     It is exciting for Nazareth to play a part in this important technological advancement with travel and tourism in the informational age.

     During your Internet visit, I hope you will have time to see as much of Nazareth as possible. I hope that Nazareth City will be a vacation destination for you and your family. Nazareth is known for its warm hospitality and as an outstanding destination for visitors from all over the world.

    Nazareth has many wonderful sights, activities, fine hotels, motels, superlative restaurants, Mary's well, basilica of the annunciation and its remarkable market and many many more.. Our facilities can accommodate visitors and  conventioneers  alike. The warm memories of your time in Nazareth will remain with you for years to come. On behalf of our citizens, I welcome you to our City for what I am confident will be an enjoyable & memorable time.

    Ramez Jaraisy
    Mayor of Nazareth City
