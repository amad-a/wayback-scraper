Palestine Introduction



There are two different photographic essays on Palestine here. One has eight images, and it is a short, tight sequence of the best landscapes. It has a short text passage for each picture and is similar to the short essays on Egypt, Ladakh, and Ceylon. However, in order to make a wider variety of my photographs from Palestine available for those who may wish to use them for study or publications, I have also mounted here a sequence of over one hundred images. If you choose the first, short sequence, whlen you reach the last print there you may either exit, return the the home page, or keep right on going with the longer set.
