Deir Yassin Remembered Events: SCOTLAND

  • Thursday, 5 April 2001: Presentation of petition to Scottish Parliament at 4.30pm (organised by Palestine Solidarity Action)

  • Sunday, 8 April 2001: Memorial Gathering at the Commemorative Plaque at Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum, Glasgow at 10.30am

  • Monday, 9 April 2001: Memorial Gathering in the Peace Garden, Aberdeen, on the opening day of the Scottish Trades Union Congress.

Dan McGowan, American founder and Director of the worldwide campaign "Deir Yassin Remembered" will address events.
