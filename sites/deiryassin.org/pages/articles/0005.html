BBC Report on DY

15th March 1998

Transcript of part of the first programme in the six-part BBC series entitled The Fifty Years War: Israel and the Arabs, which starts transmission on 15th March

Narrator: The weary soldiers were defending the road to Jerusalem, under siege by the Palestinian Arabs. From the moment the British began to leave, saving the Jews of Jerusalem from starvation became the first priority of the Jewish Army, the Haganagh.

Rabin .... to make sure the road between Tel Aviv and Jerusalem would not be endangered by the big villages or towns that were along the road, where from came all the attacks on the convoys.

Narrator : the Haganagh stepped up its operations to prevent Palestinian villages from sheltering fighters.

Rabin: Well it was not pleasant, the .... was to attack the village, to give warning to the civilians, to destroy the village, and by the elimination of the villages along the .... the road, we were sure there would be no attacks.

Narrator: the events at Deir Yassin one month before independence would scar relations between Jews and Arabs for years to come. In the five months since the civil war began, Deir Yassin had kept out of the fighting, it was not on the Haganagh’s list of hostile villages. But the Haganagh had some dangerous allies, one a small terrorist group, the Stern gang, joined up with another, the Irgun, best known for blowing up the British military headquarters in the King David hotel. Deir Yassin was where they planned to prove themselves against the Palestinians.

Meir Pa’il, Haganagh officer: I ran into a man who had left us for the terrorists, he told me that the Irgun and Stern Gang had got permission from our commander to attack the village of Deir Yassin. He was very proud.

Ben Zion Cohen, Irgun Commander: We got orders from the Jerusalem command of the Irgun, Stern Gang and Haganagh. Our orders were - Take Deir Yassin.

Meir Pa’il: I ran to my commander and asked, Why did you allow it? He said, I suggested two other targets, they turned them down. He said, I can’t shoot them can I? So I decided to spy on them.

....(indisctinct) .. loudspeakers to clear the village before sunrise.

Abu Mamoud, Deir Yassin resident: the loudspeakers blared out, Lay down your arms, Run for your lives. Then I heard our machine gun, I was kneeling down like this, I lifted the curtain and saw the village ablaze, their attack lit up the whole village,

Narrator: the village was not the soft target the Jews had expected.

Ben Zion Cohen, Irgun Commander: From the windows of the houses, Arabs were shooting at our soldiers. And from a force of 132, we had 42 wounded and six dead.

Narrator; Ben Zion Cohen, a petty officer from the Royal Navy joined in the house to house attack.

Ben Zion Cohen: So I gave the order, before entering a house, throw a couple of grenades inside.

Old Man: They threw a grenade into one house, 28 were killed,

Ezra Yakhin (of the Stern Gang?) : it was impossible to attack the enemy without hurting their families, it was difficult, it was painful, and I am sorry we had to do it, but we had no choice.

Old Palestinian man: after the battle, they took 14 prisoners, they lined them up by the quarry And mowed them down. they threw their bodies in the quarry, that’s what happened.

Meir Pa’il: I saw 20 men taken to the quarry, they lined them up and they killed them. Just like that. Shot dead. While this was going on Jews came from the next village, most of them were religious by the way, they started yelling Bastards! Murderers! What are you doing! Some shouted in Hebrew, others in Yiddish, they stopped the massacre.

Narrator: every Arab was driven from Deir Yassin to Jerusalem. Here, their leaders would make a terrible mistake.

Abu Mahmoud (Deir Yassin resident): We gathered in Jerusalem at the Hebron Gate, we checked who was missing and who had survived, then the Palestinian leaders arrived, including Dr Khalidi.

Hazem Nusseibi, Palestine Broadcasting Service: I asked Dr Khalidi, “How should we cover the story?” He said, “We must make the most of this. So he wrote a press release stating that at Deir Yassin children were murdered, pregnant women were raped, all sorts of atrocities.

Narrator: Arab radio stations put out wildly exaggerated reports of what happened, ignoring the protests of the witnesses.

Old Guy: we said, “there was no rape.” He said we had to say this, so the Arab armies will come to liberate Palestine from the Jews.

Nusseibi (Palestine broadcaster): This was our biggest mistake, we did not realise how our people would react, as soon as they heard that women had been raped at Deir Yassin, Palestinians fled in terror. They ran away from all our villages
