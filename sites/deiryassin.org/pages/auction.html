9 April 1998 marked the 50th anniversary to the massacre at Deir Yassin on the hands of the Irgun and the Stern Gang.

Trigguring a mass exodus of over 700,000 Palestinians, that massacre changed the course of events in Palestine during 1948 affecting the history and lot of the Palestinian People for years to come. To commemorate the 50th anniversary, a book was published with the title:

Remembring Deir Yassin; The Future of Israel and Palestine.
Edited by Daniel McGowan and Marc Ellis.

The front cover of that book shows a beautiful oil painting by Ann Leggett of an original Arab building that exists today at Deir Yassin. The figures are Arab Women and are timeless and the painting is done with a 17th century style resin-oil medium. It measures 10" by 12" and is being offered by a silent auction with the final bid to benefit "Deir Yassin Remembered." The highest bid will be accepted on the 51st anniversary of the Deir Yassin Massacre on 9 April 1999.
