Palestine




There are two photographic essays on Palestine here. The Hills of Palestine has eight images; it is a short, tight sequence of the best landscapes. The pictures load rapidly and contain a brief text passage for each picture. These images were scanned from Dye Transfer prints that are part of a larger museum exhibit called Landscapes. In order to make a wider variety of photographs from Palestine available for those who may wish to use them for study or publications, a sequence of over one hundred images called Palestine Journey has also been mounted here. If you choose the first, short sequence, when you reach the last print you may either exit, return the the home page, or keep on going with the longer set.
