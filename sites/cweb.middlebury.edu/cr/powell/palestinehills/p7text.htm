I approached this village also on foot, from the hills behind, from the north. I used nineteenth-century maps made by the Palestine Exploration Fund; these British maps were detailed and included ancient trails and Roman Roads. This day I lost my way, and as I wandered along the edges of fields and terraces, I tried not to damage dry walls or destroy anything. It had rained recently, the earth was damp and heavy, and with every step I picked up huge clumps of mud on my boots. I felt like a clumsy child who has stumbled into a neighbors garden and is unsure where to step. These olive trees have been carefully tended for centuries. Such trees are many decades old before they bear fruit.
