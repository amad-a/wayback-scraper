According to the traditions of Islam, Nebi Musa is the burial site for Moses; it is a holy place. The Torah says that Moses died without entering the Land of Canaan and that no one knows the place of his burial, but according to the Moslems his body was carried across the river to be buried here in the "Promised Land." Judaism, Christianity, and Islam have all at one time or another considered themselves to be the legitimate heir to a promise given by God to Moses. In The Bible, the promise of the land was never merely a deed of property given to a favorite tribe but a conditional contract with the promise of expulsion from the land in the event of misbehavior. Over the last three thousand years there have been repeated occasions when new religious factions, seeing decadence around them, read again old texts and decided that the promise could apply to them if they upheld the laws. By this time we have many groups who believe themselves to be at home here, in the promised land.
