Sebastiya is the name of the contemporary village built on the ruins of what was known twenty-seven hundred years ago as "the great hill of Samaria," a city cursed by the Biblical prophets for luxury, idolatry, and political folly. Many different trees make up these orchards: almonds, peaches, and apricots. I returned here several times until the flowers and trees were in bloom and the light was right. Local children watched me work, brought me green almonds to eat, and roasted a small bird that they caught in a snare.
