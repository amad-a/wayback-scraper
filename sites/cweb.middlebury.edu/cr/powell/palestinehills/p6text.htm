When I came upon this village called Rabah in the late afternoon, I had hiked over a low mountain and approached from its back side. It had rained several times this day, and the colors were fresh and rich. After I came down the hill, I was fed by villagers on flat bread, local cheese and olives and driven back to my lodgings in Nablus. There I shared a large dormitory room with several school teachers who had come to town from village schools to attend a workshop. Often on my wanderings I was taken to meet the village school teacher, almost invariably a man who commands respect in the village.
