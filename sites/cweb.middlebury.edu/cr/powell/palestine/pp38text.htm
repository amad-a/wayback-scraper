The camel's owner was not much larger than I was. I probably weighed about one hundred and twenty pounds at the time. For the two of us a baby camel was a handfull, and when he said that we were going to carry the thing back up to the top of the cliff, I said that he was joking. In the end we did it, and he gave me not a moments rest all the way to the top. I have never before or since felt my heart pound like that, fast and hard, thud, thud, thud, thud.... Then a strange thing happened. I got over it very quickly. At a high altitude one gets out of breath rather easily, but here we were well below sea level, and in that thick air I was back to normal in a short time.
