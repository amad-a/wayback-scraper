Tamam
Aref Al-Akhal
1930  Born in the city of Lydda, Palestine. 
1948  Forced to abandon his birthplace and take refuge in a refugee camp in"Khan-Younes", Gaza Strip.
1950  Enrolled in the College of Fine Arts in Cairo.
1953  Set up his first exhibition in Gaza.
1954  Opened his major exhibition in Cairo, with the participation of the Palestinian artist Miss Tamam Aref Al-Akhal. The exhibition was sponsored and inaugurated by Egyptian President Jamal Abdul-Nasser.
1954  Joined the Academy of Fine Arts in Rome.
1959  Married his artist colleague, Tamam Al-Akhal.
1969  Elected as first Secretary General, Union of Palestinian Artists.
1971  Elected as first Secretary General, Union of Arab Artists.
1994  Settled in Amman, Jordan.

  1935  Born in the city of Jaffa, Palestine
1948  Forced to abandon her birthplace and take refuge in a refugee camp in Beirut, Lebanon.
1953  Enrolled in the Higher Institute of Fine Arts in Cairo.
1954  Took part in the exhibition of her colleague Ismail Shammout in Cairo.
1959  Married her artist colleague Ismail Shammout, shared with him the Exhibitions held in various countries of the world.
1994  Engaged full-time in art and is resident with her husband in Amman.
