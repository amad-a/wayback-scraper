Jaffa, the city of oranges and one of the oldest cities on the Mediterranean. The Canaanites called it "Yafi" meaning beautiful. In season, the colors of ripe oranges on the trees filled me with joy, as did the happy rituals associated with picking and shipping the fruit across the blue Mediterranean.
Oil painting, 1997, 165X200 cm.
Our home was in the old City of Jaffa; its windows framed the clear blue sea. The bustle of the harbor intertwined with the ancient mellowed stone of the old buildings to form a mosaic of color and beauty. In the late afternoons, when the fishing boats returned home with their catch the harbor teemed with life and excitement.
