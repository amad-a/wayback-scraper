Ismail & Tamam

e x h i b i t i o n s. . .



1953 Ismail set up his first exhibition in Gaza town, (29th July 1953) the first exhibition ever to be held by a Palestinian artist in Palestine (His brother, Jamil, took part in this exhibition).
1954 Opened his major exhibition in Cairo,with the participation of the Palestinian artist Mss. Tamam Aref Al-Akhal, a colleague then, and currently his wife. The exhibition was sponsored and inaugurated by Egyptian President Jamal Abdul-Nasser, on 21st July 1954.
1957 Nablus, Gaza & Ramallah (Palestine).
  All exhibitions since 1959 were held in association with his wife artist, Tamam Al-Akhal
