We Must Ask Ourselves "WHY?"

A letter to Congressional representatives,
and published as a local (Spokane) Letter to the Editor

by Marianne Torres

Following is the letter we sent to our Conressional representatives, and which was published in our local newspaper, The Local Planet

9/13/01

Senator Maria Cantwell
717 Hart Senate Office Building
Washington, DC 20510

Senator Cantwell:

Without wasting words:

  1. Secretary Colin Powell said it first: "this is not just an attack on America. It is an attack on NATO, and an attack on civilization". Who can deny that? If it is an attack on civilization, the civilized world is the place in which we should BRING THE PERPETRATORS TO JUSTICE: the WORLD COURT. This not only spreads the risk and the alliances, but will allow us to avoid an orgy of executions which I do not believe our social fabric can survive.
  2. CALL FOR AN INTENSIVE WORLDWIDE MANHUNT. Do not call for war. Do not call for bombing yet more innocent civilians. Call for action that will bring the perpetrators to justice.
  3. We must understand that our willingness to kill civilians, and to support other states who do the same, is at the very root of this horrible act.
    • - Vietnam was a war against civilians (58,000 American dead &endash; primarily soldiers, 3,000,000 (three million) Vietnamese dead, primarily civilians);
    • - the Gulf War was a war against civilians (carpet bombing, destruction of an entire country's infrastructure &endash; you can't do that without bombing population centers);
    • - the continuing sanctions against Iraq is a war against civilians (at least half a million Iraqi children dead since 1991 to starvation and to disease that needed only a simple medical response &endash; and that's just the children);
    • - we supported and literally paid for wars against civilians in Guatemala, Nicaragua, El Salvador, Chile, Indonesia. We supported Brazil's war against their civilians. We supported and partially paid for Indonesia's war against their civilians. The list is far longer;
    • - the war in Palestine/Israel is a war against civilians, conducted by Israel, paid for en toto by the United States &endash; the list of grievances there is so long, again I won't even list them. Suffice it to say that Israel has broken hundreds of international laws, flown in the face of several hundred UN resolutions (a transgression for which we have bombed other nations), and committed multitudes of what the world recognizes as war crimes, all with impunity, and with absolute political and massive economic support from the US;
    • - the war in Yugoslavia and Kosovo was and is a war against civilians.

    HOW MUCH MORE OF THIS WILL WE DO BEFORE WE UNDERSTAND THAT KILLING INNOCENT PEOPLE ENGENDERS NOT JUST RESISTANCE, BUT HATRED?

  4. CALL FOR A REASONED, RATIONAL, RESPONSE &endash; a manhunt, and justice in the World Court. STOP THE JINGO-ISM. This is not a time for empty words!
  5. DEMAND THAT CONGRESS RESCIND THE TAX CUT, AND MAKE NO MORE. How on earth are we going to pay for all this if we cut off the very supply of the funds?

In sorrow,

Marianne Torres
Spokane, WA 99216

AND BY THE WAY, please work to pass legislation that prevents Credit Card companies from reaping false profits (no pun intended) from the billions of dollars worth of late charges they will levy because the US Mail has been delayed.
