Works by Edward Said, 1976

Compiled by

Eddie Yeghiayan

  • "Arabs and Jews." Bulletin of Peace Proposals (1976), 7(4):295-296.

  • "Arabs, Islam and the Dogmas of the West." New York Times Book Review (October 31, 1976):4-5, 35-37. A review of 11 recent books on Islam, including the Cambridge History of Islam.

  • "Between Chance and Determinism." Review of Bela Kiralyfalvi's György Lukács. TLS [Times Literary Supplement] (February 6, 1976), 3856:126-127. See Martin Eve, J.W. Pa yne and Grahame White in Part B.

  • "Conrad and Nietzsche." In Norman Sherry, ed., Joseph Conrad: A Commemoration, pp. 65-76. Papers from the 1974 International Conference on Conrad. London: Macmillan, 1976.

  • "Interview with Diacritics." Diacritics (Fall 1976), 6(3):30-47.

  • "On Repetition." In Angus Fletcher, ed., The Literature of Fact: Selected Papers from the English Institute, pp. 135-158. Papers from the two sessions of the Institute held ln 1974 and New York: Columbia University Press, 1976.

  • "The Palestinians and American Policy." In Association of Arab-American University Graduates, Inc., Detroit, Michigan, Information Papers, No. 11, January 1976

  • "Two Studies on the Palestinians Today and American Policy," pp. 1976.

  • "Raymond Schwab and the Romance of Ideas." Daedalus (Winter 1976), 105(l):151167.

  • Reply to Letters on Review. New York Times Book Review (December 12, 1976):36. See "Arabs, Islam and the Dogmas of the West" (1976).

  • "Roads Taken and Not Taken in Contemporary Criticism." Contemporary Literature (1976), 17:327-348.

  • "Summary of Statement," "Prepared Statement of Edward W. Said," (with Abu Lughod) "Questions and Discussion." In U.S. Congress. House. Special Subcommittee on Investigations of the Committee on International Relations. The Palestini an Issue in Middle East Peace Efforts, pp. 28-31, 31-36, 36-62. Hearings. 94th Cong., 1st sess., September 30, 1975. Washington: U.S. Government Printing Office, 1976.

  • "Under Western Eyes." Review of M.M. Badawi's A Critical Introduction to Modern Arabic Poetry. TLS [Times Literary Supplement] (December 10, 1976), 3900:15591560. See M.M. Badawi and S.B. Bushrui in Part B.

  • "Vico on the Discipline of Bodies and Texts." MLN (October 1976), 91(5):817-826.
