Works by Edward Said, 1972

Compiled by

Eddie Yeghiayan

  • "Abecedarlum Culturae: Structuralism, Absence, Writing." In John K. Simon, ed., Modern French Criticism: From Proust and Valery to Structuralism, pp. 341-392. Chicago: University of Ch icago Press, 1972. See "Abecedarlum Culturae: Structuralism, Absence, Writing" (1971).

  • "Eclecticism and Orthodoxy in Criticism." Review of Ihab Hassan's The Dismemberment of Orpheus: Toward a Postmodern Literature. Diacritics (Spring 1972), 2(1):2-8. For Hassan's reply see Part B, and see also Said's "Response" (1973).

  • "A Good Man Against Theories." Review of Paul Goodman's Speaking and Language: Defence of Poetry. New York Times Book Review (February 20, 1972): 21-22.

  • "Introduction." In Arthur Conan Doyle's Adventures of Sherlock Holmes. New York: Washington Square Press, 1972.

  • "Michel Foucault as an Intellectual Imagination." Boundary 2 (Fall 1972), 1(1):1-36.

  • Review of Roland Barthes' Critical Essays and Mythologies. New York Times Book Review (July 30, 1972):5, 15.

  • "Al-Tamanu' wa al-Tajanub wa al-Ta'aruf." [Witholding Avoidance, Recognition] Mawagif (March 1972).

  • "Al-Yasar al-Amriki wa al-Qadaya al-Filastiniyyah." Shu'un Filastiniyyah (March 1972), 7:208-212.
