Works by Edward Said, 1990

Compiled by

Eddie Yeghiayan

  • "Again Palestinians Suffer." New York Times (March 6, 1990:A19(N), A23(L).

  • "Arabesque." New Statesman & Society (September 7, 1990), 3(117):31-32.

    Interview with Edward Ball.

  • "Comments." New York Times (August 13, 1990):A4.

    Telephone interview by Eric Pace and Alessandra Stanley on the Iraqi occupation of Kuwait and President Bush's actions in the Gulf.

  • "The Discourse of the Orient." In Dennis Walder, ed., Literature in the Modern World: Critical Essays and Documents, pp. 234-244. Oxford: Oxford University Press/Open University Press, 19 90.

    See Orientalism (1978), pp. 1-9, 226-230.

  • "Figures, Configurations, Transfigurations." Polygraph (1990), 4:9-34.

  • "Figures, Configurations, Transfigurations." Race and Class (July-September 1990), 32:1-16.

  • "`A Formula for More Husseins': An Interview with Edward W. Said." LA Weekly (August 31-September 6, 1990), 12(39):19, 25.

  • "Hommage to a Belly Dancer." London Review of Books (September 13, 1990), 12(17):6-7.

  • "Israel, Palestine and Intifida: Reply." Critical Inquiry (Spring 1990), 16(3):697-698.

  • "Narrative, Geography and Interpretation." New Left Review (March-April 1990):81-97.

  • Orientalismo. S.L.: Prodhufi, 1990.

    Spanish translation by María Luisa Fuentes of Orientalism (1978).

  • "Third-World Intellectuals and Metropolitan Culture." Raritan (Winter 1990), 9(3):27-50.

  • "Yeats and Decolonization." In Dennis Walder, ed., Literature in the Modern World: Critical Essays and Documents, pp. 34-41. Oxford: Oxford University Press/Open University Press, 1990.

    See Yeats and Decolonization (1988).
