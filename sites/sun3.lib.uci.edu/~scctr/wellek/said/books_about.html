Books about Edward Said

Compiled by

Eddie Yeghiayan

  • Ansell-Pearson Keith, Benita Parry, and Judith Squires, eds., Cultural Readings of Imperialism: Edward Said and the Gravity of History. London: Lawrence & Wishart, 1996; New York: St. Martin's Press, 1997.
          Chiefly papers presented at a conference held March 1994, Warwick University; two other papers were originally presented at a conference held January 1994, University of Bristol.

  • Ashcroft, Bill and Pal Ahluwalia. Edward Said: The Paradox of Identity London & New York: Routledge, 1999.

  • Bové, Paul A., ed. Edward Said and the Work of the Critic: Speaking Truth to Power. Durham & London: Duke University Press, 2000.
    Contents:
    Paul A. Bové. Introduction:1-8
    Jacqueline Rose. Edward Said Talks to Jacqueline Rose:9-30
    W.J.T. Mitchell. The Panic of the Visual: A Conversation with Edward W. Said:31-50
    Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak. Race before Racism: The Disappearance of the American:51-65, 283-285
    Jonathan Arac. Criticism between Opposition and Counterpoint:66-77, 285-286
    Terry Cochran. The Matter of Language:78-96, 286-288
    Lindsay Waters. In Responses Begins Responsibility: Music and Emotion:97-113, 289-290
    Jim Merod. The Sublime Lyrical Abstractions of Edward W. Said:114-138, 291
    Kojin Karatani. Uses of Aesthetics After Orientalism:139-151, 291-292
    Rashid I. Khalidi. Edward Said and the American Public Sphere: Speaking Truth to Power:152-164, 292-294
    Barbara Harlow. Sappers in the Stacks: Colonial Archives, Land Mines, and Truth Commissions:165-186, 294-296
    Mustapha Marrouchi. Counternarratives, Recoveries, Refusals:187-228, 297-302
    Aamir R. Mufti. Auerbach in Istanbul: Edward Said, Secular Criticism, and the Question of Minority Culture:229-256, 302-305
    Ralph P. Locke. Exoticism and Orientalism in Music: Problems for the Worldly Critic:257-281, 306-312

  • Hart, William D. Edward Said and the Religious Effects of Culture. Cambridge & New York: Cambridge University Press, 2000.

  • Sprinker, Michael, ed. Edward Said: A Critical Reader. Oxford & Cambridge: Blackwell, 1992.
    Contents:
    Nubar Hovsepian. Connections with Palestine
    Benita Parry. Overlapping Territories and Intertwined Histories: Edward Said's Postcolonial Cosmopolitanism
    Bruce Robbins. The East is a Career: Edward Said and the Logics of Professionalism
    Tim Brennan. Places of Mind, Occupied Lands: Edward Said and Philology
    Abdul R. JanMohamed. Worldliness-without-World, Homelessness-as-Home: Toward a Definition of the Specular Border Intellectual
    Ella Shohat. Antinomies of Exile: Said at the Frontiers of National Narrations
    Richard G. Fox. East of Said
    Ferial J. Ghazoul. The Resonance of the Arab-Islamic Heritage in the Work of Edward Said.
    Barbara Harlow. The Palestinian Intellectual and the Liberation of the Academy
    Partha Chatterjee. Their own Words? An Essay for Edward Said
    Jennifer Wicke and Michael Sprinker. Interview with Edward Said

  • Varadharajan, Asha. Exotic Parodies: Subjectivity in Adorno, Said, and Spivak. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1995.
