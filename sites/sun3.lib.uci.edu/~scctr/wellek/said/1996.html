Works by Edward Said, 1996

Compiled by

Eddie Yeghiayan

  • "Arafat's Palestine." [Letter] London Review of Books (October 17, 1996), 18(20):4.

    Response to Ira Katznelson's letter, October 3, 1996.

  • Cultura e Imperalismo. Colleccion Argumento, 187. Barcelona: Anagrama, 1996.

    Spanish translation by Nora Catelli of Culture and Imperialism (1993).

  • "Fantasy's Role in the Making of Nations." Review of Jacqueline Rose's States of Fantasy. TLS [Times Literary Supplement (August 9,1996), 4871:7-8.

  • "From Orientalism." In Padmini Mongia, ed., Contemporary Postcolonial Theory: A Reader, pp. 20-36. London: Arnold, 1996.

  • "Fury of the Dispossessed." Observer [London] (September 29, 1996):23.

  • Des intellectuels et du Pouvoir. Paris: Seuil, 1996.

    French translation by Paul Chemla and Dominique Edde of Representations of the Intellectual (1994).

  • "An Intifada Against Betrayal, Despair." Los Angeles Times (October 2, 1996):B9.

  • "Lost Between War and Peace: In Arafat's Palestine." London Review of Books (September 5, 1996), 18(17):10-15.

  • "The Morning After." In Jane Hindle, ed., London Review of Books: An Anthology, pp. 32-42. London & New York: Verso, 1996.

    Table of Contents title: "Arrafat Surrenders."
    Reprinted from the October 20, 1993 issue of London Review of Books.

  • Representaciones del Intelectual. Barcelona; Buenos Aires; Mexico: Paidos, 1996.

    Spanish translation of Representations of the Intellectual (1994).

  • Review of Judith Miller's God Has Ninety-Nine Names: Reporting from a Militant Middle East. Nation (August 12, 1996), 263(5):28-32.
