Raml Zayta

The settlement of Sde Yitzchaq on village land. The city of Chadera has expanded so that some of its suburbs are now on village land. Only two of the original village houses remain. One of them is still inhabited by the only Palestinian family that stayed in the village. The second house has now occupied by a Jewish family.
