The District of Tulkarm

Qaqun

Kibbutz ha-Mapil was built on village land in 1945. Gan Yoshiyya; Ometz; Channiel. The fortress on top of the hill, a well that belonged to the family of Abu Hantash, and the school building are all that remain of the village. The school building is still used as a school by Israelis.
