The District of Tulkarm

Miska

Sde Warburg was established in 1938 on lands that had traditionally belonged to the village. Mishmeret on village lands. The site is covered with citrus groves; The two- room school still stands and used as housing for the watchmen who guard the orchards. The mosque serves as a storehouse for bales of hay and agricultural tools. The large cement fragments of a demolished enclosure built around the village well are visible.
