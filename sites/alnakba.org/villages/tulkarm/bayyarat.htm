The District of Tulkarm

Bayyarat Hannun

The lands of Bayyarat Hannun have been merged with those that once belonged to another destroyed village, Ghabat Kafr Sur. Two Settlements have built on the lands of Ghabat Kafr Sur. The southern portion of the town of Netanya is located 0.5 km from the site of Bayyarat Hannun. One large, two-storey, deserted houses still stands on the site, although some sections of it have been destroyed. The rooms on the lower floor are used to store agricultural implements.
