The District of Hebron

Bayt Nattif


 


Four settlements were established on village land in 1949 and in 1958. Piles of rubble that have been cleared by bulldozers are scattered over a wide area. Steel beams stand in the midst  of the debris at the sites center. In the rubble one can also see the remnants of arched house entrances. There are two open graves on the northeast side; the bones in the graves are visible.
