Dayr al-Dubban

The settlement of Luzit, composed of Moroccan Jews, was established northeast of the village site in 1955. The site is overgrown with thorny plants, The villages old roads are easily identifiable. There are also remnants of stone terraces and a cave. Vegetables, and fruits are grown on the adjacent land by Israelis farmers.
