Goals

Goals and Objectives of the Society

  1. To empower women by working with them to become income earning and active participants and decision makers within their community.

  2. To provide high quality care and education for children.

  3. To lend support to victims of war, occupation and social circumstances

  4. To study and preserve Palestinian folklore and to develop rural handicrafts and industries .
