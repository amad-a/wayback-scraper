1. The Home.

In 1984 the Society inaugurated its Home for young girls victimized by occupation and social circumstances .This home accommodates up to 150 girls.

The Society provides them with board, lodging, health care and education in the respective schools and universities in town . The Society endeavors to develop their creative abilities and talents by providing them with the opportunity to participate in various cultural and social activities .

2. Medical Clinics.

The Society has a dental and a medical clinic which provide free services to the girls in the Home and to the beneficiaries and staff of the Society.

3. Financial Aid and Aid in - Kind.

The Society offers help and assistance through the following channels:

The Sponsorship Scheme: Palestinian children victimized by war and occupation receive support from Palestinians, Arabs, and friends from all over the world This network of solidarity has been a source of encouragement to the Palestinian people during difficult times . 2800 families have received assistance through this scheme

Contingency aid is offered to needy families which include food stuffs, clothing , medication and wheel chairs.

Scholarships to University Students: the Society offers assistance to Palestinian university students. 400 hundred students have already benefited from this project .

Rehabilitation Projects: Loans are given to needy people for specific individual productive projects such as poultry, cattle raising and small groceries. Fifty families are already benefiting from this project.

Supporting Prisoners in Israeli Jails ; The Society has always given special attention to Palestinian prisoners by providing them with pocket money , clothing or supporting their families morally and financially. 400 prisoners are provided with winter clothing annually.
