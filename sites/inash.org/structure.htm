Organizational Structure

The Society has a General Assembly which meets annually to lay the policy and approve the budget and administrative reports. It also elects an executive committee from amongst the volunteer members of the Society, which is responsible for implementing the policy and strategy of the Society and which, in turn elects its officers and appoints the required staff and a supporting committee to assist it in its work .

Moreover, the Society has several other committees from within the general membership which supervise the operations of the various centers , a building committee which includes engineers and prominent members of the community and a folklore committee which includes spacialists and academicians in the field.

The Society also has groups of friends and supporters in various parts of the world and a liaison office in Amman run by a committee of volunteers.
