Income Generating Centers

In order to maintain its vital services to women, children and the community in general, the Society has established productive projects that would guarantee a sustainable income for its operations.

  1. The Home Products Center.

  2. The Bakery .

  3. The Clothing Center.

  4. The Handicraft Center.

  5. The Beauty Parlor.

 

1. The Home Products Center offers the following services:

It prepares different food stuffs for storage including cheese, crushed wheat, lemonade and olives.

It caters for the public on a daily basis or on special occasions.

It runs the Society Cafeteria which can accommodate 200 people and serves the student body, the staff and members of the Society and the public at large.

2. The Bakery:

This Center produces different kinds of biscuits, pastries , birthday cakes and wedding cakes and meets the demands of the local market .

 

3. The Clothing Center:

This Center produces men’s and children’s pajamas, underwear, gym suits, women’s lingerie and baby layettes.

4. The Handicraft Center:

The Handicraft Center-Embroidery and Tricot :

This Center depends on the proficiency and artistic skills of the Palestinian women in the various villages and towns who have developed this traditional skill into an art form through their careful choice of colors and designs and the accuracy of their work . This Center has become a focal point for thousands of women who bring their products and interact with the experience and artistry of those charge of the Center . In each of the villages working with the Society, there is a woman in charge who receives the raw material , discusses the design and then distributes the work amongst her colleagues. The finishing touches are completed at the Center where the articles are sold.

Tricot and hand knit articles in this Center are produced by the staff of the Center for all age groups .

These products are exhibited and sold at the Society showroom or upon order.

5. The Beauty Parlor:

This Center offers services to women , rents out wedding dresses at moderate prices and markets cosmetics.
