Cultural Centers and Programs

Culture is given utmost attention by the society. The following are the Society centers that promote and expose Palestinian with culture:

  1. The Palestinian Folklore Center.

  2. The Library.

  3. Documentation Center and Archives.

  4. Adult Education Program.

  5. The Summer Club.

  6. Seminars and Lectures.

 

1. The Palestinian Folklore Center

This Center was established in 1972 with the objective of studying, understanding and safeguarding Palestinian folklore . It is run by a committee of members of the Society and experts in the field. The Center has the following functions:

  • It compiles folklore material such as songs , proverbs, tales, customs and tools.

  • It publishes books and the Journal “Heritage and Society”. It has already published 30 issues of the journal and the following books.

It has 4 sections: the Guest Room, (al-Madafeh), the Village Home, popular costumes , the Yard(al-Housh) where ancient farming tools are displayed ,over and above other collections. 2. The Library:

The library contains 12000 books and volumes that deal with various topics and is open to members, staff and student body of the Society. It has recently opened a special section for children which has 2000 books.

3. Documentation Center and Archives:

This Center compiles documents and data related to the Palestinian Cause, women’s issues, current affairs and human rights violations committed by the Israelis against the Palestinians .

4. Adult Education Program:

The Society has adult education programs which combat women’s illiteracy and teaches women practical skills and health awareness.

5. The Summer Club

The Society organizes each year a summer club for children of the Home and the community where they practice their hobbies and different skills through programs run by specialists in the field of art, music , karate, literature , popular dance and sports.

6. Seminars and Lectures:

Students at the different training centers hold seminars on special occasions with the assistance of their teachers .This program aims at encouraging their speech and debating skills and empowering them with information necessary for their development .

The Society also hosts Palestinian scholars and writers to lecture to students, staff and members on social, cultural , historical, religious and political topics.
