A camp called Jenin

 

 

 

 

 

 


 

 

 

It was only one squared kilometer,

Used to have a name yes always,

Ten hundreds poor roofs are there,

Where people last week roasted like stakes,

You need to know the story, just wait …

 

 

Kids were kept alone under fire,

Shivering and pray for help ten days,

Where did go the father and the mother?

Doors where smashed and no use of keys,

You need to know the story, just wait….

 

 

Shooting of the families was only by tanker!

Rain is around the clock for weeks,

No food, no light, no mercy or water,

But rain of death is there with no fees,

You need to know the story, just wait…

 

 

The blood did run strong like a river,

No screams no prays,

No voices would bother,

The world did close ears and eyes,

And now might write to them hi dear,

We heard you crying but please,

We are coming to check just wait.

 

 

 

Ayman Allabadi

 

24/4/2002
