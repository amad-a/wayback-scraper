Heart …

 

 

 

 

 

Heart is the heart,

Once he talks,

Once he walks,

Or dare to go and fly,

He might smile,

And be happy once,

In the dark yes,

And tries to be smart,

But heart is the heart,

In lights would cry,

 

 

 

 

Heart is the vision,

Where eyes should try,

Wide as the sea,

As far as the stars,

Never would stop,

Never might dry,

Needs time to grow,

Needs love and hope,

To stay fresh and high,

 

 

 

It can be the flash,

Even if lights die,

It can be the heavens,

If was no sky,

Fate is the sentence,

We have to create,

Love is the state,

Where heart is the master,

And the face is the spy….

 

 

 

 

Ayman Allabadi

24/3/2002
