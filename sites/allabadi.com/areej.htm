Who killed Areej?
 
Areej was a girl of only nineteenth,
She lived there in Hebron before of yesterday,
Her heart was pumping,
Her dreams dare to breath,
She never thought that soon,
She has to stop and leave ...
 
Areej was a girl,
Like millions in this world,
She looked for a house,
And a spouse with lovely kid,
And to get her breakfast,
And to leave her hair with wind,
She did not ask for takeaway death ...
 
 
Who were the killers? ,
The silence at your lips,
Sellers were members,
The rest were those hands,
Of that dumb world,
All should ask her to forgive ...
 
Ayman Allabadi
