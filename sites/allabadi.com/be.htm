Be your self…

 

 

 

 

 

 

 


 

 

 

Are we staring at fog?

Are we under the sun?

Or we claim infinity!

Or just run to none!

Why don’t you feel that?

The heart turns to ash,

The soul might smash,

I doubt one word,

Would mean it is done…

 

 

Keep repeating the same,

While time passed away,

For words to explain,

How we use the day,

Forget your thoughts for once,

Argue is not that fun….

 

 

Just keep the seed to grow,

Fruits would come green,

Don’t look inside the straw,

And drink the sweet unseen,

You might imagine and though,

Still the mind is clean,

Be your self and live….

 

 

 

8/10/2002
