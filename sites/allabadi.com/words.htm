Words ….

 

 

 

 

Words and only few words ,

Cannot be bigger,

Cannot be better,

But it can tell much,

Although words are still only words! …

 

 

Cannot cure my own wounds,

Cannot restrain my homeland,

Cannot bring back the leaving birds,

But still they are words! ……..

 

 

What then might do?

Over screens, papers and walls? ,

Oh.yah,

It can prove that my dream is true,

And the hopes never die,

As long as a live, are still those words…….

 

 

Ayman Allabadi

 

5/6/2001
