You don’t need to say,

Your smile is my gift,

You don’t have to pay,

For those feelings swift,

Love is the ocean,

Love makes the rest,

Can’t you see!

Just leave the eyes,

In my heart to lay,

Or here in my face!

 

 

No more shivering hearts,

No more worries and pain,

Travel in my eyes,

Where you are the rain,

Life is there as far as the stars,

And you will be for that as the chain,

Can’t you see!

Just leave the smile,

Flooding on the lips!

 

 

Ayman Allabadi

 

 

7/6/02
