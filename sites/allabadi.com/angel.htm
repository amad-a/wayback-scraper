The angel.

 

 

 

I do have an angel,

I do have the flower of mine,

I do have my inspiring temple,

I do have my fresh musical wine,

I do have an angel…

 

 

Whenever the road is full with mixed details,

And nothing is clear, as there is no sign,

The mind got shivering,

The heart dances as a lost orphan,

The eyes talk no words,

The smile of my angel would lead me just fine….

 

 

Wind does not stop to blow,

Snow will always clean the lost and foggy time,

Green will always grow up and stand high,

The sea can dive inside the long silence then climb,

Life always has its surprise,

Land continues the talks with the sky,

No worries should remain as I still do have the angel of mine….

 

 

Ayman

14/4/2002
