Time

 

 

 

 

Time is calling for us my friend,

We might leave but time never goes,

Time is not simply a single word,

Time is a fortune and time is a rose,

You see it also a doubled blade sword,

Time is for healings and who knows the dose?

Time is the truth that never could be faked…

 

 

 

Wait and I also would be there,

Trying to bridge oceans to the dream,

Were life is better for us and no sore,

And music plays freedom with no scream,

Till then we have to wait and dare,

To claim it’s ours without any blame,

We have the light and the time has the shade….

 

 

 

 

Ayman Allabadi

27/4/2002
