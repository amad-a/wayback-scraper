A letter to the president

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Good morning Mr. President,

Good morning Mr. Lies,

They told me that a sponsor,

Must be brave and wise,

He talks straightforwardly,

And only with his tongue,

He sees with his eyes,

He loves holy freedom,

And justice with no dyes…

 

 

You can get your coffee,

Relax and accuse,

Watching your movie,

Of shelling and amuse,

You can be so cheap,

To gain a single vote…

 

 

I have a lesson,

For you Mr. Clown,

The night will never freeze,

The night will burst the dawn,

The sun will surely rise,

Blood will be its tone,

You can share the murder,

You can share the shame,

“Give me liberty or give me death”,

That is also our game,

Freedom’s our aspiration,

Whatever may be the price…

 

 

29/8/2001
