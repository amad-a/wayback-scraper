** Abu-Nawwas Cafe where you can enjoy the Ancient Arabian Times **
 

 

   With its simple furniture and its offer of traditional drinks, light meals and water pipes, in addition to its relaxing atmosphere, Abu-Nawwas takes visitors back to the ancient orient.
