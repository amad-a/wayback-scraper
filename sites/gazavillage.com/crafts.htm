**  Welcome to the new site of The Arts and Crafts Village  **
 

 

    The Village includes 4 handicraft workshops which produce 4 different traditional famous crafts in Gaza city. These workshops are: The Rug House, Embroidery House, Copper House and Glass House. The Village seeks to support these traditional handicrafts, it supply the handicraftsmen with the workshops in return for a small ratio from their total sales value, the thing, which keeps the handicraftsmen operating their business without any tiring expenses.
 

Rug House

   The resident rug maker displays his wonderful skill, using an authentic loom to produce the most beautiful rugs, bags, cushion covers and bedspreads, Variety of other woolen products are created by hand from both sheep and goats wool, which has been dyed by hand.

 

   
 

Embroidery House

    Here the weaver can be seen at work producing hand made Majdalawi fabrics, using a loom which originates from Ashquelon and is over 70 years old.  This fabric is traditionally woven and embroidered by Palestinian women to make the most beautiful cushion covers, dresses, bags and table linen.

 

     
   
 

Copper House

   Copper work is one of the less popular art forms in the Gaza Strip. The village provides a venue where copper antiques dating from a variety of historical periods are exhibited with the aim of promoting this craft and its beautiful products.

 

 

 
 

Glass House

   The glass house in the village exhibits glass, which has been produced in Hebron and Nablus and painted in Gaza City. It is possible to request special orders. A variety of sizes, colors and designs to suit individual tastes.
