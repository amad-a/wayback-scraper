Smile & Colors:
The First Children Art Exhibition at The Gaza Municipality Arts and Crafts Village. The Exhibition included art works done by 65 children aging between 9 to 14 years old. These children participated in the first annual artistic summer club for children at the Village. The club was held for 2 weeks from 1 July 2002 till 11 July 2002. The club was held after three successive months of work with children in various schools. The children were chosen for their artistic talent from different schools.

This program, The Development of Art Teaching in Schools, was the first program for the Arts and Crafts Village to work with children. The idea of the project came from the Village's belief in the importance of art to help in building the future generation, the independent Palestinian state generation. Art is the school of creativity, work, patience and beauty. It is also the school of morals and humanity. It is a base for social rest and free expression. The program held several goals:

  • The development of art teaching in schools through providing needed training for future teachers from the art department in Al-Aqsa University, and varying the experience of art students through dealing with foreign artists. Also training the the art teachers in schools, who don't have a certificate in art education, through participation.

  • Mitigate the effect of Israeli violence on children.

  • To deliver the experiment to the largest possible target group through printing a book that documents the experiment and organizing activities in Gaza and Khan Younis.

  • Get to know the talented children and improve their talent.

  • The effective participation and cooperation of all parties within the program: The Ministry of Education, the education directorates of Gaza and Khan Younis, the school headmasters, Al-Aqsa University and the field trainers.

The trainers were: Dr. Khaled Dahalan; child psychiatrist from GCMHP, Ross Georgeson; Sccottish artist with UNAIS, Angela O'Hara; Canadian artist working at the American International school in Gaza and 12 final year students from Al-Aqsa University to be field trainers.

The work team also included the staff of the Arts and the Crafts Village, the overall supervisors of the program and their executives. After training, the field trainers were distributed to 6 governmental schools in Gaza and Khan Younis, two field trainers foe each schools. The training involved 20 classrooms and 900 children with an average of 45 children per class. Then 65 children were chosen to participate in the art summer camp.

Spanish Artistic Activity:
An entertaining artistic activity done by the Village with a group of Spanish artists from 27 Jul 2002 till 1 Aug 2002.

  

12x2
An artistic workshop donated by Kayali through Amideast to give the chance to beginner artists to explore themselves and show their talents and capabilities through a combined exhibition. During the workshop, the 12 artists had made a mural carried the same name (2 Years of Intifada) and the same message (One painting renders the feelings of Palestinian artists under the present circumstances). Each artist made 2 to 4 paintings, and the total paintings were 45.
 

  After the workshop, the Village held an exhibition includes all the works done in the previous 2 weeks on the occasion of the second year of the Intifada. All the works were showing the Palestinian suffering and there dream of having there independence and freedom.
