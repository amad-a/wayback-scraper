In Memoriam of Palestine?
By Lillie
Israel is 53 today; May 15, 2001.
Today is its “historied” birthday, commemorating
a catastrophe in humanity’s lifespan,
marking the barbarous kicking out
of an indigenous human race
from its native land,
celebrating the myriad of massacres,
ethnic cleansing, and suffering
of masses that have spent
all Israel’s illegitimate life
enduring, dreadful threats,
intentional bloodsheds,
round-the-clock humiliation,
and relentless vice.

This day,
announces the theft of Arab soil,
villages, townships, mounts, and vales,
it sheds light on Arab street signs
re-embossed in Hebrew, in blood,
showing the way to razed Arab homes,
bleeding death lines,
ravaged olive trees,
and beaten Arab lands.

Today,
In Memoriam of Palestine?

When the sun refuses to rise
and life vetoes its life
Palestine dies.

Even then,

When the devil will dance in circles,
on Israel’s birthday,

Invincible Heaven will change its name,
to PaleInvincible Heaven will change its name,
Invincible Heaven will change its name,
to Palestine.
