Rage
By Unknown Poet

Let our churches burn!
Bomb our schools!
Don't let the Children Learn!
Pollute our water!
Destroy our Crops!
Place economic sanctions!
BLOW UP THE DOME OF THE ROCK!!

Rip the streets!
Burn the books!
Deny the truth!
Build on the land you took!
Shoot our boys!
Beat our wives!
Rape our daughters!
END OUR LIVES!!!

And when the world asks why, begin to lie!
Say our rocks hurt bad, and infringe on your human rights!
So go ahead and shoot your guns!
Drop your bombs!
Aim A-K's at a group of the young!
But we'll never give up!
We'll never lose touch!
For every two you kill...
ANOTHER BUS WILL BLOW UP!!

"I WOULD RATHER DIE ON MY FEET,
THAN LIVE ON MY KNEES."
