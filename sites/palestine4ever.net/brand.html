His Brand of     Courage
 By Rebellious Poet
My hero continues:

"It's time you began to understand
That it's not a choice within my hands
To stand and fend for friends and land
Is a brand of courage that I did not plan!

"I'm not dirt cheap to wish I'd die
But I am not sheep to hustle and tie
I do have a dream of my Love to try
And live a good life, and rise up high

"But an untamed beast within my blood
Like the devil released in a fiery flood
Rules my heart, my thought, and head
And all I would do is to fight if I could

"For against me arrayed is a murdering jew
My sister he shot, and my brother he slew
With a cowardly bomb my home he blew
My Mom had a head, but in shreds it flew

"Do you blame me for hating the dirty jew
Who flamed my home with his cowardly crew
If I catch him I'll kill him; I swear I'll do
For little is to settle for the stone I threw

"My brand of courage you cannot share
For away you have strayed and you did not dare
To stay the fray and my burden to bare

So I wonder...my"friend"...if you really care?"
