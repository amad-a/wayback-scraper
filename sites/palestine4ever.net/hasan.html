Hasan Newash

Oil in Thyme and Hot Bread

Percussion rains on the memorial
Of Abdeen's mother
Near Kalkaska's Indian Reservation
In Mancelona, Northern Michigan
While you rush before the rain of bullets
In Wadi Essarar, outside Jerusalem

It seems to always rain in Wadi Essarar
Rubber bullets- they call them.  Rubber is softer
On the ear, as dumdum inside flares
Between the eyes, to bleed to death
Usama's dream of being first
In sixth grade, next September
Loud percussion

On Manger Street, a tank on guard
You eye the children only steps away
In a fateful war against the army teens
Who've just arrived
From "Operation Uproot"
A pre-dawn battle
Against a 100 trees- olive trees
Of Roman vintage, in Beit Sahour

Loud percussion
Here, has come for children
Road blocks, tire burnings
- The scene is not Sewato -
Sarar a blanket on every inch
Of Manger Square, where "Love
Thy neighbor..." now is stillborn

Their rights of passage- shabab
Will play the game
A barrage of stones against the enemy
Face and dodge
A rain of fire
Retreat behind
Bullet-poked walls, and re-emerge
A surprise- guerrilla style
Then do it all again

Percussion reigns
And children always lose:  A life- sometimes
A leg, an eye - still to grow inside
A blinding vision
Of purple grapes
In family's orchard
Stolen village waiting
Behind the electric fence
And dream of bolting shut
This prison- misnomered:  Deheishe Camp

Percussion, percussion
The game is on today
And sarar is everywhere
As bullets too, and tanks are watching
While the White House watches
The Philadelphia Eagles against the Redskin's
And the Washington Bullets against the Chippewa Indians
They are back to back, today, on NBC

You hear the cry from Wounded Knee
As "Red Skin's" beat the drums
A loud percussion
They carve a path in fire glows
Defiance
Oil in thyme is waiting
Just behind those hills
And bread is hot in all the taboons
Percussion fires
The wave of children singing
Waving all to follow
Their beacon home
