The Palestinian Central Council’s(PCC) Final Statement issued at the end of it’s

General Session held on the 2-3 July 2000

 

Having met in Gaza for two successive days and on the basis of our people’s natural and historical right in establishing their own state, the relevant UN resolutions especially 338 and 181 and the declaration of independence in 1988, the Central Council announces to the Palestinian people, the Arab world and all the peoples of the world its will and determination to realize the declaration of independence issued by the Palestinian National Council in its 1988 session through the establishment of an Independent Palestinian state with Jerusalem as its capital as well as the realization of its sovereignty over the Palestinian lands within the 4th of July 1967 borders by the end of the interim period on the 13th of September 2000.

In view of the lack of any substantial progress in the peace process as well as both the interim and final negotiations, the PCC appeals to The Arab Republic of Egypt, its president, all the Arab leaders, both sponsors of the peace process namely USA and the Russian Federation, the European union, China, Japan, the African and Islamic summit, the nonalignment countries, the UN Secretary General and the members of the International Security Council to move immediately on all levels for salvaging the peace process and supporting the Palestinian people in their determination to realize their sovereignty over all the Palestinian territories occupied in 1967 by the end of the agreed upon interim period. The PCC also requests the international community and both sponsors of the peace process to pressure the Israeli government to fulfil all its commitments and obligations which it had previously approved and signed especially the third phase of redeployment, the release of the Palestinian detainees and the return of all displaced persons.

In this respect, the PCC has put its decision of state realization and sovereignty into effect. Therefore, it has decided to re-activate the committees that were previously formed in order to finalize the political, economic, social and legal plans and prepare all the necessary legislation.The PCC entitles the executive committee, the presidency of the council and the PNA to follow up the activities of these committees.

The PCC reiterates its previous decisions considering the permanent status issues which are:

    1. The adherence to the Palestinian refugees’ right of return to their land and properties in accordance with the international resolution 194 and rejection of all attempts aiming at resettling these refugees and depriving them from their inalienable right to return to their homeland.
    2. The insistence on the complete and comprehensive Israeli withdrawal from all occupied Palestinian territories including Jerusalem to the 1967 borders as the peace process that started in Madrid stipulated the implementation of Security Council resolutions 242, 338 and affirmed the principle of Land for Peace.
    3. The insistence on the removal and dismantling of all Israeli settlements from the occupied Palestinian territories as well as the implementation of the Israeli and civil withdrawal to the June 4th, 1967 borders.
    4. The insistence on having Jerusalem - occupied in 1967- as the capital of the independent Palestinian state; for peace wouldn’t be achieved without its return and liberation from the Israeli occupation and the protection of all religious sites :Islamic and Christian from the continuous onslaught of settlement colonization. The PCC condemns the Israeli attempts to adopt a new law which prevents the Palestinian refugees from returning to their homeland and reinforces its decision of annexing Jerusalem. Both of which are invalid for they contradict with the relevant UN resolutions particularly 252,465 and 478.

Upon the approach of the interim agreement deadline on September 13th, the PCC entitles to the executive committee of the PLO during this period to take the appropriate measures for the declaration of the independent Palestinian state as well as the realization of its sovereignty over the Palestinian land with Jerusalem as its capital.

Proclaimig this historical decision with complete national responsibility towards a future of security, peace, and stability in the Middle East, the PCC appeals to all peace-loving countries and peoples to stand by the international legitimacy and decisions, respect the implementation of its resolutions and support the Palestinian people while exercising their legitimate right in self-determination and establishment of their independent state.This state should constitute the actual guarantee for a comprehensive, just and secure peace and prosperity in the Middle East.

The PCC calls upon the Palestinian people to join efforts ,unite and stand together.In this regard, the council also reviewed its decisions pertaining to the activation of all PLO’s institutions and departments here and in the diaspora.

The PCC, moreover, has valued the consistency of the executive committee activities, the convention of this session of the PCC and the entitlement of the executive committee and council’s presidency to present a proposal for the formation of the new National Council in conformity with the basic law provisions and the national council’s decisions in this respect.

Also, the PCC hails the steadfastness of our Palestinian masses in the diaspora especially those in Lebanon , their adherence to their right of return to their homeland from which they were displaced from in 1948 and their complete rejection to all resettling and displacement schemes. The PCC hails the Palestinian masses who expressed their full adherence to their right of independence through their sacrifices on the day of Al-Nakbah and solidarity with the detainees.

The PCC reiterates that democracy ,equality and the principle of seperation of powers are the sole choice for the Palestinian people to build their national authority and establish their independent state. In this regard, the PCC calls upon the executive committee and the PNA to reinforce democratic life and political pluralism, to respect the independence of justice, to develop the judicial system, to strengthen the principle of accountability and transparency, to ensure the rule of law, to preserve public liberties for citizens and to conduct municipal and local elections in a way that reinforces national responsibility and democratic life.

Based on the importance of the participation of all factions and forces and in order to reinforce the negotiation performance in view of accomplishing our national mission for independence and sovereignty, the PCC entitles the executive committee to reinforce the Higher National Steering Committee for Palestinian-Israeli negotiations. In this context, some of the PCC representatives suggested to make a comprehensive review of the peace process with the Israeli side and rebuild it on new basis.

Furthermore, the council addresses the Palestinian detainees, hails their patience and steadfastness and assures them that freedom is very close for the national independence constitutes the liberation of land and the release of all Palestinian detainees from the Israeli prisons. The PCC also hails the great sacrifices made by the Lebanese people to liberate Lebanon from the Israeli occupation and stresses that implementing UN resolution 425 in Southern Lebanon - embodied in the complete and comprehensive withdrawal - demonstrates the inevitability of the Israeli’s commitment to implement UN resolutions 242 and 338 which state that Israel should withdraw from all occupied Palestinian and Arab territories. It also emphasizes the importance of convening an Arab summit to restore and reinforce Arab solidarity.

The PCC has also stood for a moment of silence in honor of the late Syrian president Hafez Al-Asad and wished luck for the new Syrian leadership headed by Dr. Bashar Al-Asad and stability for his people.

The PCC hails the perseverance of the Palestinian refugees in the refugee camps in Lebanon and reiterates its full adhto the right of return of all these displaced according to UN resolutions 194. It also discussed the continuation of the Iraqi people’s suffering resulting from the unjust sanctions, hailed the steadfastness of the great Iraqi people and demanded that sanctions are lifted.

Finally, the Central Council glorifies the martyrs of our people who sacrificed their souls for the liberation of Palestine and ensures their pure souls that their sacrifices will never be in vain for the independent Palestinian State and Jerusalem as its capital is about to become a reality.

 

Long live Palestine as a free Arab State.

Long live the Independent Palestinian State with Jerusalem as it’s capital.

Long live our Eternal Arab Nation.

Long live the international solidarity with our people and its just struggle.
