MOPIC’S 1998 FIRST QUARTERLY MONITORING REPORT OF DONOR ASSISTANCE

March 31, 1998

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF DONOR ASSISTANCE

On behalf of the Palestinian National Authority, the Ministry of Planning and International Cooperation wishes to express and extend its sincere gratitude and deep appreciation for the assistance contribution to the Palestinian People by the International Donor Community.

Notice: Please note that any missing information in any donor’s profile would be due to receipt of information after the cut-of date for the First Quarter , and will certainly be included in the Second Quarter.   Please note, also, that figures appearing under the year 1995 represent the aggregate figure for the years 1994 and 1995 combined, and this is being done temporarily pending a new software development to the PACS system.

Introduction to the First Quarterly 1998 Monitoring Report of Donor Assistance
The Ministry of Planning and International Cooperation (MOPIC) compiles and produces these quarterly reports in order to:

  • Monitor the level of assistance to the Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza.
  • Measure donor activity and focus on accomplishments.
  • Assess objectively the directions and priorities of development.
  • Consolidate the Palestinian Authority’s strategy for donor assistance.
  • Contribute indicators for dialogue on international cooperation program.

The procedure currently used by MOPIC for compiling and producing these quarterly reports involves the following basic process:

  • Forwarding the current donation matrix to respective donors and implementing agencies along with a data collection form.

  • Meeting with various donors’ representative offices and implementing agencies to discuss respective donor profiles.

  • Gathering agreements pertinent to assistance programs for Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza.

  • Updating the Palestinian Aid Coordination System (PACS) with data collected from donors and implementing agencies.

  • Forwarding the updated matrix to respective donors and implementing agencies for verification of the new data.

  • Conducting objective data analysis and producing illustrative charts to clarify certain trends and sectoral development.

The accuracy, relevance, and integrity of the data presented in these quarterly reports are positively correlated with the following factors:

  • Cooperation from donors and implementing agencies in the provision of data by the deadline and according to the format set by MOPIC prior to the production of the report.

  • Quality control by MOPIC staff, that involves careful application of the PACS, data verification, and text editing.

  • Provision of internationally accepted definitions for pledges, commitments and disbursements.

  • Clarity about international currency exchange rates.

  • Provision of some cross-reference to the Palestinian Development Plan (PDP).
