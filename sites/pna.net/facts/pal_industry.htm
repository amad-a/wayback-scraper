Palestinian Industry

"An Introduction"

 

Restrictions imposed by the occupying authorities are designed to protect Israeli industry. Even advisors to the Israeli government admit that this policy cannot be justified in such terms because of the relatively small scale of the OPT economy compared to Israel and the benefits which could accrue for the Israeli economy if Israel switched to a more liberal policy. Israeli policy restricts Palestinian production directly, through restrictive licensing of production units and control imports and of marketing in Israel and abroad. All foreign trade must pass through Israeli ports or airports or the Israeli-controlled Jordan bridges. Israel demands that all exports and imports from the OPT require a permit, and imposes taxes and customs duties and bureaucratic delays. Israel also requires Hebrew labeling of the few products which are permitted to be marketed in Israel or annexed Jerusalem. 80% of industrial firms rely on imports of raw material
