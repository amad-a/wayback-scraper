The Rocard-Siegman Report and the Palestinian Response

          On June 28th, 1999, the Council on Foreign Relations (CFR) released a report of the Council sponsored Independent Task Force (ITF), "Strengthening Palestinian Public Institutions", chaired by Michel Rocard and directed by Henry Siegman.

The full text of the Report is accessible on the Council site

( www.foreignrelations.org/public/pubs/palinst.html ).

           On August 11th, 1999, President Yasser Arafat issued a decree to initiate a process of "administrative reform", taking specifically into account the CFR report, and establishing, to this effect, the Higher National Committee for Institutional Development (HNCID).
