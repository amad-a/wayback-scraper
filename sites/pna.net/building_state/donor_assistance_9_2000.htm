ANNEX 1

1.3 Definitions of Terms

1.3.1 Definitions of Donations Terms

Pledge:
It is an initial indication of Donors’ intention to provide financial Aid for Specified period. The announcements of Donors’ Pledges, normally, take place in the Consultative Group (CG) meetings and these pledges are not allocated to any specific program or project.

Commitment:
The total Donors’ financial obligations; funding that are allocated to specific program and/or project based on signed protocols, memoranda and agreements between the PNA and the donor country or agencies.

Disbursement:
The total amount of previously committed funds transferred (paid out) from donor’s headquarters to the implementing agencies or directly to the beneficiaries.

1.3.2 Definitions of Sub-Sectors

Agriculture:
Fishery, forestry, livestock, and agriculture production. Excludes food Processing

Children & Youth:
Projects that related to children & Youth

Democracy Development:
Legislative development and electoral activities.

Detainees / Returnees:
Training and rehabilitation programs.

Education:
Educational programs development, and schools construction.

Energy:
Projects related to electricity and power.

Environment:
Environmental laws, policies, and resources and projects, excludes solid waste.

Health:
Health care, clinics, hospitals and medical equipment.

Housing:
Housing projects, credits, mortgage facilities.

Human Rights & Civil Society:
Fortifying human rights, professional and civil society development.

Humanitarian Aid:
In kind assistance for humanitarian needs, for example, food and medical aid.

Industrial Development:
Assistance to the industrial sector development, Textiles, wood, and handicrafts.

Institution Building:
All institutional support and development, technical assistance (that is exclusive of being a project component), equipment supply, human resource management, and training and institutional scholarships. Excludes projects outside institutions.

Legal Affairs:
Judicial reforms, legal infrastructure development, harmonization of law, legal & regulatory frameworks, and arbitration.

Multiple Sectors:
Includes projects that relate to more than one sector, for example, World Bank ERP, MIDP or EHRP.

Police:
Technical assistance, equipment, and budget support for the Palestinian Authority Police.

Private Sector Development:
All kinds of support to industries, private business, small - scale enterprise development such as those through local banks. Excludes textile development, wood, and handicrafts.

Telecommunications:
Assistance given to promote the Telecommunication systems.

Tourism & Cultural Resources:
Touristic sites development and renovation , preservation and renovation of historical sights, and culture.

Transportation:
Harbor, airport, roads, and future train station.

Undefined:
Includes money that is not allocated to a specific sector.

Water and Sanitation:
All projects related to drinking water supply, wastewater treatment and sewerage systems.

Women:
All projects related to women and gender awareness development.

 

1.3.3 Categories Definitions

Employment Generation (EMG):
Funds allocated to create jobs for the Palestinian labor market through different employment generation programmes.

Equipment (EQ):
Funds allocated to purchasing equipment for the use of the PNA investment programmes and to assist the PNA in building their institution.

In Kind (IK):
Materials, Medicine, Food… etc. given as donations.

Private Sector Support (PSS):
Funds in the form of credit facilities used to support the private sector development.

Public Investment (PI):
All investments related to projects designated to be for Public; non-profitable projects outside the domain of private sector and NGO’s.

Technical Assistance (TA):
All training programmes for the development sectors and the foreign consultants activities are funded under this category.

Budgetary Assistance (BA):
Assistance given to the PNA’s budget such as Holst Funds contributions.

Various (VAR):
Funds allocated to NGO’s and/or to more than one of the pre-stated categories.
