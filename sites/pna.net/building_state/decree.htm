Decree

The Chairman of the Executive Committee of the Palestine Liberation
Organization
The President of the Palestinian NationalAuthority

Based on the Authorities endowed on him and based on requirements of
National Interest
The following has been decreed

 

1- The formation of a Committee on Strengthening the Palestinian Public Institutions consisting of:

*Dr. Nabeel Sha'ath    Chairman
*Dr. Mohamed Zohdi Nashashibi member
*Dr. Maher Almasri      member
*Mr. Ahmed Abdl-Rahman member
*Dr. Ramzi Khouri member
*Dr. Mohmed Abdl-Aziz Aboushrea   member
*Mr. Jerar Al Kodwa member
*Mr. Mohamed Saed Altamimi   member
*Mr. Mohamed Al Naser   member
*Mr. Jamal Alshobaki member
*Dr. Mohamed Alsamhouri coordinator
*Freih Abu Middein member
*Munther Salah member

The committee shall be in charge of following up reports related to strengthening the Palestinian public institutions that are issued by various national and international bodies, in particular, the Task Force Report (Rocard and Siegman). The committee shall also be responsible for taking any procedural measures needed for strengthening the Palestinian institutions and shall present them to the Cabinet of Ministers for approval.

2- The above-mentioned committee may benefit from the services of any specialized expert in the area of public administration

3- This decree is valid from August 11' 1999 and all parties involved should be informed about it.

 

Yaseer Arafat
Chairman of the Executive Committee of
the Palestine Liberation Organization
The President of the Palestinian National Authority
