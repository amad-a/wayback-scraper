Israel will complete Judaization of Jerusalem after five years- PLO official

Abu Dhabi- The PLO permanent representative in the Arab League, Mohammed Sabeeh, revealed that Israel plans to complete its plan to Judaize occupied Jerusalem by the year 2002. He added in an interview published in Al Ittihad Arabic daily of Abu Dhabi that the plan is being implemented without any political fuss and through various ways such as the confiscation of Jerusalem land, demolition of Arab houses and the progressive expatriation of Palestinian residents from the holy city.

Sabeeh said the most dangerous step taken by Israeli occupation authorities regarding Jerusalem was isolating the city from the West Bank. The Israeli government plans to scatter Palestinian communities by constructing main road networks in Jerusalem which would serve Jewish settlements and Israeli military activities, the PLO official added.
