PNA calls for an urgent International Meeting in Gaza to save the Palestinian Peace Process

Gaza, (Saturday 15 March 1997)




The Peace Process in the Middle East is primarily based on the Security Council Resolutions 242 and 338 which outlaw the Occupation of land by force and recognize the principle "land for peace".

The Process had started in Madrid on 30th October 1991, under the auspices of the two shepherds of the Madrid Conference: The United States and Russia. Needless to say that the negotiations at that time were not Palestinian -Israeli and not even Arab-Israeli merely because of the lack of trust between the two parties due to the state of billigeremy which prevailed the area for many years.

The situation remained as such in Oslo till the Norwegian mediator succeeded in narrowing the gap while the Americans have played an important role in reaching the successive agreements.

Based upon this, approaching other parties to discuss the peace process is rather logical.

The signing of the Declaration of Principles by the Government of Israel and the PLO, the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian People took place in the gardens of the white House on 13th September 1993 in the presence of President Clinton. The document was signed by the Secretary of State and the Russian Foreign Minister. The same has happened when the Cairo Agreement was accomplished on 4th May 1994 and the successive Washington Agreement on 28 September 1995 which was the result of lengthy arduous talks in Taba regarding the second phase of the process.

Besides President Clinton, President Arafat, the late Prime Minister Rabin, other leaders such as King Hussein of Jordan, President Mubarak, the Prime Minister of Spain and Foreign Ministers of US, Russia, Norway, Japan, had singed that document. It all indicates the international involvement and interest in the success of the Peace Process and the right implementation of the agreement to ensure a bright future for the peoples of the area.

The main philosophy of the Israeli-Palestinian peace process is based on generating trust between the two parties to replace tension and violence and to the acceptance of coexistence and mutual respect. It is also based on the promotion of credibility between the two parties to strengthen the significance of negotiation and peace.

The International Community has expressed its position towards Jerusalem in the Security Council Resolution 478/80 which considered any Israeli action in East Jerusalem as illegal and called upon the world not to recognize any Israeli actions that would change the status of occupied Jerusalem.

On the other hand, the PNA has always tried to convince the Israeli Government that it has not signed an agreement with political parties or Militias but with the Israeli Government. Hence it will be a very serious precedent in International law to renew negotiations with every new Israeli Government on the same issues which were already settled in the agreement concluded with the former government. The world has observed with concern the rigid attitude of the Netenyaho government towards the implementation of the agreement regarding the Redeployment in Hebron.

The PNA is also surprised to see a new fierce wave of settlements on its way to confiscate large areas of lands under the pretext of the construction of new roads and by adding thousands of units to the existing settlements and eventually the Israeli government has expressed its determination to establish a new settlement in Abu Ghniem Mountain . Such a decision is not only a very hard strike to the peace process but it also aims at shaping the future of Jerusalem before the negotiations on the final stage start. Together with that the legal advisor of the Prime Minister's office has recently issued a statement allowing Jewish extremists to pray in the Aqsa Mosque a matter that will increase tension.

While the Palestinians were waiting for the implementation of the first stage of Redeployment of the Israeli forces in the rural areas which was supposed to take place on 7th March 1997, the Israelis declared a probable pull out from 9% of the total occupied areas in West Bank without mentioning that 7% of that ratio of land is indeed under the PNA administration. In other words Israel was determined to pull out its forces from only 2% of the land.

Such an action dose not only stab the credibility in the peace process but also creates repugnance and tension.

Actions of the sort are planned and implemented unilateraly neglecting the principle of partnership in the peace process . The official behavior of the Israeli government encourages the Jewish settlers to continue their fierce actions towards the Palestinians and their properties. All such actions make any negotiations void of content.

The Israeli Media tries to convince the world that the Palestinian desire to discuss with other parties the possibility of an outlet to such a crisis created by Israelis themselves as a violation to the agreements concluded between the two parties.

Don't they see that their actions are indeed a real violation to the peace agreements?

Do they expect the PNA to become a policeman who will protect the Israeli bulldozers while they operate to uproot the olive trees from the Palestinian land to prepare for more settlements?

The call of President Arafat for an international meeting in Gaza is indeed a serious political step for a logical international solution to the crisis to avoid violence, bloodshed and to save the peace process.

The expected meeting in which Palestine, USA, Russia, the European Union, Norway, Japan, Jordan and Egypt will participate represent an International unanimous desire to save peace.
