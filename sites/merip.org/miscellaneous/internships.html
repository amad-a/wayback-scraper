MERIP Internships
 
 


Interested in the Middle East?

Join the five-person staff of the Middle East Research and Information Project (MERIP) publishers of Middle East Report for a fall, spring or summer internship. Interns become an integral part of our work, learn about the Middle East, and gain valuable training in editorial production, administration or promotion. Now in its 26th year of publication, Middle East Report has provided critical, independent analysis of the issues and policies shaping the region today.

Editorial Intern Responsibilities

The intern assists the editor in producing Middle East Report. Depending on skills, this may include light research, proofreading, inputting copy, making corrections and formatting articles for typesetting. Interns also assist in procuring photographs and securing reports and documents. For students interested in learning how to find information in Washington, DC, and in how a magazine is produced--from start to finish.

Outreach Intern Responsibilities

The intern assists in the development and implementation of outreach and promotion strategies, targeted towards primary and secondary school audiences, new subscribers and donors. Work includes developing plans for promotion of specific issues and the magazine in general, coordinating mailings and mailing lists, writing and problem solving. This is an excellent opportunity to learn outreach and promotion skills and provides hands-on experience in promoting alternative educational materials.

Administrative Intern Responsibilities

The intern assists the executive director, business manager and circulation manager in implementing business and circulation strategies. This includes processing orders for back issues from universities and individuals, maintaining the inventory and library databases, coordinating a sales presence at seminars and conferences and handling informational queries. Provides experience in administering the business side of a nonprofit organization.

General Responsibilities

All interns share in general office work: answering telephones, photocopying, preparing outgoing mail, running errands and assisting staff members in special projects. Interns also participate in staff lunches on Middle East topics and may be asked to attend and/or staff a table at events.

Minimum Commitment

Fall and Spring interns: one semester, 12 hours per week. Summer interns: 3 months, 20 hours per week. Internships are unpaid; however, MERIP does reimburse for daily travel expenses.
