  • www.Salam.org: Justice in the Middle East is the website of the Boston Committee on the Middle East. The site provides a monthly newsletter and separate pages on US policy, Congress, Culture, Palestine/Israel, Lebanon, and Iraq.

  • The North American Coordinating Committee for NGOs on the Question of Palestine (NACC) has a broad range of resources on current issues and activism on Palestine. The site has separate pages on Human Rights, Closures, Land Confiscations, Refugees, Jerusalem, Palestinian children's art, and other subjects.

  • Iraq Action Coalition(IAC) The IAC was formed in 1995 to protest the continuing UN blockade on Iraq. The website has links to published studies on the effects of the embargo on the general population. The IAC is also engaged in a grassroots campaign to lift the sanctions on Iraq.

  • VOICES IN THE WILDERNESS: A Campaign to Break the Immoral Sanctions Against the People of Iraq is also attempting to end the sanctions on Iraq.

  • GLAS, The Gay and Lesbian Arab Society serves as a network for Gays and Lesbians of Arab descent. The site hosts the society's newsletter, Ahbab and other resources.

  • The website of The Arab-American Anti-Discrimination Committee (ADC) includes pages devoted to news updates, Middle East sources, information about their legal services, political action bulletins and information about the activities of ADC's regional chapters.
