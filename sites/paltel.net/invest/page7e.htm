1. Company, Activities and Share Capital
Palestine Telecommunications Company P.L.C. (“the Company”), located in Nablus, was incorporated and registered at the Registrar of Companies in Jericho as of August 2, 1995 under registration No. 563201029. The Company’s founding general assembly was held on December 27, 1995, during which the establishment of the Company was announced. The Company’s authorized share capital consists of 45 million shares of JD 1 each.
The Company’s main activities are offering, managing, and selling wire and wireless telecommunication services, as well as constructing and operating telecommunication stations and telephone networks in Palestine and in other countries. This is in accordance with the “License Agreement to Construct, Manage, and Operate the Telephone and Mobile Telecommunications”, signed between the Company and the Palestinian National Authority (PNA). The Company operates under the License Agreement and the Telecommunication Law No. 3 of 1996 decreed by the PNA.

2. License Agreement to Construct, Manage, and Operate the Telephone and  Mobile Telecommunications
As of November 15, 1996, the PNA agreed to grant the Company the license to use, invest, and operate all telecommunications network assets and equipment within the PNA territories (the License). Hence, the license agreement to construct, manage, and operate the telephone and mobile telecommunications (the Agreement) was signed between the PNA and the Company. According to the Agreement terms and conditions, the Company has full right to construct, install, and operate the telephone and mobile network including the exchanges, earth and air cables and other related equipment, as well as the right to use this network for sending and receiving telecommunications. The License is valid for a period of twenty years, renewable.
As for the wire telephones, the Agreement grants the Company the full right for a period of ten years with effect from November 15,1996, to provide the telecommunication services between all locations inside Palestine, as well as between Palestine and elsewhere. Further, as per the Agreement, the Company has to pay a one-time U.S. $ 25 million and an annual fee of 7% of its annual operating revenues in exchange for transferring to the Company the ownership of the telecommunication lines and to be recognized as the sole Licensee in Palestine.

3. Summary of Significant Accounting Policies
The financial statements have been prepared under the historical cost convention.
The significant accounting policies are as follows:

Use of Estimates
The preparation of the financial statements in conformity with the International Accounting Standards requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities at the date of the financial statements, as well as revenues and expenses during the year. Actual results could differ from those estimates.

Revenues and Expenses Recognition
Revenues and expenses are recognized on the accrual basis of accounting. Revenues from telecommunication services are recognized during the period in which such services are rendered after adjusting for amounts due to or from other telecommunications companies, which amounted to JD 17,951,613 and 15,359,089 for the years 1998 and 1997 respectively.

Investments in Affiliates
Investments in affiliated companies are accounted for using the equity method. Share of profits or losses in the investee companies is recognised in the statement of profit and loss.

Materials in Stores
Materials in stores are stated at the lower of cost or market using the first in first out or the average cost method. Materials in stores are primarily used in projects for expanding or constructing telecommunications network.

Projects in Progress
Projects in progress comprise all costs incurred to expand and construct the national telecommunications network and other projects. These costs include costs of direct materials and contractors costs, except direct labour, for which the proper basis of capitalization to projects in progress will be determined in the future. After completion, projects in progress are added to property, plant and equipment.

License, Concession and Lines Costs
License, concession and lines costs are amortized over ten years using the straight line method.

Property, Plant and Equipment
Property, plant and equipment are stated at cost, and depreciated over their estimated useful lives using the straight line method at the following annual rates:

Foreign Currencies
The Company mainly deals with New Israeli Shekel (NIS), United States Dollar and Jordanian Dinar (JD). The Company’s reporting currency is Jordanian Dinar. During 1998, fixed exchange rates had been used to translate transactions dominated in other currencies to JD. Monetary assets and liabilities in the balance sheet, which are denominated in foreign currencies are translated into JD using the prevailing exchange rates at the balance sheet date. At year end, total revenues, expenses, property, plant and equipment, projects in progress and materials in stores that were paid in NIS have been re-translated to JD using the average exchange rate prevailing at the date of the transaction.

As a result, a loss of JD 2,534,720 was incurred, and reflected in the statement of profit and loss.

7. Investment in Affiliate
The Company owns 175,000 shares (JD 1 each) in Palestine Information Technology, a private shareholding limited liability company (Pal-Tech). The holding represents 25% of Pal-Tech share capital of which 50% of the par value has been paid up.

The investment in Pal-Tech is stated at cost, since Pal-Tech is just a start-up.
