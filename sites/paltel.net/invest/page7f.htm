20. Reserves

-Statutory Reserve— In accordance with the Company’s articles of association and the Companies Law, 10% of annual profit before tax should be transferred into a statutory reserve account until the reserve balance equals one half of the Company’s share capital. Further, the transfer may be continued by a Board of Directors resolution until the statutory reserve balance equals the Company’s share capital. The statutory reserve can not be appropriated to shareholders.

-Voluntary Reserve— In accordance with the Company’s articles of association and Companies Law, the Company’s general assembly, based on the board of directors recommendation, can appropriate up to 20% of the annual net profit before tax as voluntary reserve. The voluntary reserve balance should not exceed one half of the Company’s share capital. The appropriation to the voluntary reserve for 1998 and 1997 was 10% each.

-Special Reserve— Based on the board of directors’ recommendation, 20% of the Company’s net profit after tax for the year ended December 31, 1997 has been appropriated to a special reserve for future needs.

24. Foreign Currency Translation Loss
This item represents net losses resulted from translation of monetary assets and liabilities, which are denominated in foreign currencies to JD using the prevailing exchange rates at the balance sheet date. Further, it includes net losses which resulted from retranslation at year end of non-monetary transactions occurred in NIS during the year and translated to JD at the internal rates used by the Company throughout the year. The average exchange rates throughout the year was used for translation.

The main reason for this loss is the decrease in the NIS exchange rate against JD during 1998 by 14.5%. The NIS is the basic functional currency of the Company’s revenues and significant part of its expenses and purchases, where the Company’s reporting currency is JD.

26. Taxes and Customs

- According to the Ministry of Trade and Economy letter No. MOF 300/11, dated July 25, 1998, the Company was granted the right to benefit from the Palestinian Law for Encouragement of Investment for five years ending on December 31, 2001. In addition, according to the Palestine Law for Encouragement of Investment, PALTEL will be subject to a nominal income tax of 10% for an additional sixteen years.
- According to the license agreement between the Company and PNA to construct, manage and operate the telephone and mobile telecommunications, the Company was granted an exemption from customs on tools and telecommunications equipment required for the telecommunication infrastructure. Moreover, the Company has the right to benefit from exemptions stated in the Palestinian Law for the Encouragement of Investment.
