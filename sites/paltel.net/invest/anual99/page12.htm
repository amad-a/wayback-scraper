1999 Annual Report

Independent Auditors' Report

To the Shareholders of
Palestine Telecommunications Company P.L.C.
Nablus - Palestine

We have audited the accompanying balance sheets of Palestine Telecommunications Company P.L.C. (the "Company") as of December 31, 1999 and 1998 and the related statements of profit and loss, changes in equity and cash flows for the years then ended.
These financial statements are the responsibility of the Company's management.
Our responsibility is to express an opinion on these financial statements based on our audit, and the information and explanations that we obtained, which we considered necessary for our audit.
We conducted our audit in accordance with International Standards on Auditing.
Those standards require that we plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the financial statements are free of material misstatement.
An audit includes examining, on a test basis, evidence supporting the amounts and disclosures in the financial statements.
An audit also includes assessing the accounting principles used and significant estimates made by management, as well as evaluating the overall financial statement presentation.
We believe that our audit provides a reasonable basis for our opinion.
The Company maintains proper books of account and the accompanying financial statements are in agreement therewith.
As further explained in Note 28 to the accompanying financial statements, as of the financial statements date, the legal registration of the cellular phone company has not been completed and the ownership structure has not been finalized yet.
As a result, the cellular phone net loss for 1999 are included in the statement of profit and loss.
In our opinion, except for the effect of the preceding paragraph, the financial statements referred to above present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of Palestine Telecommunications Company P.L.C. as of December 31, 1999 and 1998 and the results of its operations, changes in equity and cash flows for the years then ended in conformity with International Accounting Standards.

Arthur Andersen
Ramallah - Palestine
February 29, 2000
