1999 Annual Report

Accomplishments: Offering Better Customer Service

Developing a strong management is one of our most important goals. Having a professional management cadre assures that our services will be delivered expertly and at the accelerated pace we desire. The first step was to adopt administrative guidelines for directing all activities and operations. Every facet of the operation is covered by these detailed guidelines: administrative, financial, purchasing, warehousing, etc., throughout the company, down to the individual work site.
Organizational charts are made for all departments, sections, and the positions within them. These charts are invaluable in assuring smooth work flow between units, and for recruitment, promotions, performance evaluations and future planning for human resources purposes.
Our management and staff are supported by investments in the latest technology effecting all phases of the business, including equipment, supply monitoring, billing, financial and administrative information and electronic archives. This combination of resources - a well trained and conscientious management, backed by some of the best technology the world can supply - makes it possible to accomplish the advances in installations and customer services chronicled in this report.
Finally, our efforts in implementing quality controls enabled PalTel to obtain the ISO 9002 certificate by the SGS UK, LTD., for International Certification Services, in recognition of the initiation of a company-wide Total Quality Management System (TQM), to improve service quality and customer satisfaction.
PalTel is the first quality assured telecommunication firm complying with ISO 9002 Standard in the Middle East region. This certificate is the first indicating the implementation of the TQM among 33,000 certificates awarded by the SGS for companies around the world.

Employees

Employees increased to 1,972 by the end of 1999, compared to 846 at the end 1997; an increase of about 133 percent. Specialized training programs have been executed throughout all technical, financial, and administrative fields; specifically 65,220 training hours for over 1,953 employees during 1999.
Phone line installations increase by 300 percent in three years PalTel succeeded in installing about 54,927 new lines during 1999. Total installations accomplished through the past three years reached about 138,679, surpassing the number of the operating lines available during the occupation period, which amounted to 83,331 lines at the end of 1996.

Offering Telephony Services to Rural Areas

PalTel's first and foremost concern is serving all the Palestinian citizens and reaching all localities without exception. Executing the switch and outside plant projects assisted in making this dream tangible. The results: 177 localities throughout Palestine have been connected to telephony services during 1999. In addition, further telephony networks in about 130 villages and localities are in preparation and are scheduled to be activated during 2000. The total for Palestinian localities served now rates 90 percent of total population.


Public Payphones
PalTel installed a total of 2,031 outdoor and indoor payphones by the end of 1999, distributed as 1,382 in the West Bank and 649 in Gaza. PalTel has awarded another contract for an additional 2,000 payphones to facilitate spreading this vital service.
