1998 Annual Report - Financial Highlights

This annual report outlines the Company’s comprehensive activities, achievements, and strategic directions. It also presents the Auditors’ Report, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and Profit and Loss Statements for the fiscal year ending December 31, 1998. Palestine Telecommunications Co. P.L.C. is a public shareholding company incorporated in 1995 under the Company Law in force in the West Bank by a group of private investment companies and institutional investors. The Company has an exclusive License Agreement with the Palestinian National Authority. In accordance with the terms of this License, PalTel is developing the Telecommunications Sector. PalTel commenced its operation on January 1, 1997 with a capitalization of forty-five million Jordanian Dinars (JD 45 million).
