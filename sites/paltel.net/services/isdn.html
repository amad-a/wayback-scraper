ISDN

There are two types of ISDN services available in Paltel, ISDN BRI and ISDN PRI. The BRI provides two data channels and one signaling channel or what is known as (2B + D). The PRI provides 30 channels or E1 (30 B + D).

ISDN PRI service is of great assistance to all ISPs and big companies working in West Bank and Gaza region. This service will promote the information technology in Palestine and will help the ISPs to spread the Internet services in Palestine.

ISDN PRI is available at the E1 level only and it's available in the following cities: Nablus, Ramallah, Hebron, Bethlehem and Gaza.

To get this service please visit one of our customer services departments in West Bank and Gaza.

Allocating Tel.Numbers

ISDN system enables routing of a call directly to one of the analog interfaces.
For each analog interface (line1 or line2), it is possible to program up to two Tel. Numbers.
Each interface receives only the calls that are directed to one of the numbers.
To allocate the first number e.g. 2370599 with an area code of 09 to line1:
(*11*92370599#) I.e (*first line - first no.*- code-number#)
(*22*92370674#) I.e (2nd line - 2nd no. * - code- number #)

Incoming Calls



When a call terminates into the first number, any telephone connected to “line 1” will ring unless defined otherwise.
All the incoming calls for all the numbers will also enter the s-bus (digital interface).
ISDN telephones will also ring, unless they were defined to ring only for a specific number.

Sub-Addresses

The sub-address feature may benefit you in the following manner:
When the same Tel. Number is defined for the two analog interfaces; (line1 and line2), it is possible to define a unique address for each line.
If the caller has added the sub-address to the Tel. Number that is defined for the analog interface, the call will be routed to one of the lines according to the sub-address.
For example: If number 2370599 was defined for line1 and line2 interfaces, and it was defined that the sub-address for line1 is 123 and the sub-address for line2 is 124, then to call equipment that is connected to line2, dial 2370599*124#
The sub-address has 1 to 3 digits.
