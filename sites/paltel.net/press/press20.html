September 7th, 2000

Paltel and Palcel Participating in MEXCOM 2000

State-of-the-art GSM technologies are being exhibited currently in MEXCOM 2000, which is being held in Jordan between the dates 5-8 September, at the Zara Expo Center. Palestine Telecommunications Company, Paltel and Palestine Cellular Company, Palcel, are participating through a special booth, in which papers, brochures, printed materials and information on both companies are being distributed. Paltel is marketing career opportunities there and is availing job applications to be filled and submitted by those interested.

MEXCOM 2000 is sponsored by Jordan Telecom and MobileCom. Various presentations on dynamic technologies and services are to be presented during the event by participant telecom companies. Visitors will be enjoying benefits parallel to those allowed for Europe and the rest of the world for years; cutting edge services with the highest quality at competitive prices, thus showcasing the future of wireless communications.
