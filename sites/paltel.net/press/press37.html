February 27, 2001

Get Your Telephone-Line NOW. by 6 Installments

Due to the current deteriorating economic conditions, and aiming at offering ultimate facilitation measures possible for customers per facing such tough political complications, Paltel is initiating new installments campaign.

Fixed-line installation charges are now easily settled through 6 installments, the first would be paid initially as down payment, and the remaining 5 will follow successively through subscribers' telephone bills without any interest rate what so ever.

Please note, this offer expires by 17 March 2001. For subscription or more information please contact the nearest Customer Service Center.
