About Us

Profile
Paltel is a public shareholding company, established by a Palestinian capital of Ninety Six Million US Dollars ($96 Millions) by the contribution of a group of investors, banks, national companies & institutions and over 8000 citizens.

Since its start of duties on 1/1/1997, Paltel managed to achieve distinguished and remarkable accomplishments, which had a profound impact on the uplifting of the Telecommunications Sector level and the construction of the Palestinian national & independent Telecommunications System.

Paltel's main objective is to develop, operate and manage the telecommunications sector in the Palestinian areas and to provide state-of-the-art and high qualitative telecom services aiming at a wide network coverage throughout Palestine. The company will focus on achieving customer satisfaction and building shareholder value by continuously increasing its market share, broadening client base, applying suitable tariffs, and developing data communication services, fixed and mobile telephone lines, along with data transmission, Internet, payphones, leased circuits and a plentiful range of value-added services.

Accomplishments

Telecommunications and IT sector has undergone greatest developments & changes in recent years with astonishing speed scope. So either we face the risk of being left behind or we strive to keep pace with technological changes, meet an ever increasing demand for a wide variety of services and derive maximum benefit from current technologies for an accelerated migration towards future growth. PALTEL has accepted the challenge & initiated a comprehensive investment in upgrading the telecom infrastructure of the West Bank and Gaza. Great strides have been made in the construction of the Information Technology infrastructure through the deployment of a full array of latest services per meeting the inevitable and tremendous requirements of the third millennium and subsequently achieving our overall goal "Telecommunications for All".

Paltel is committed to keeping progressive, proactive and highly differentiated in maintaining unique contribution to the Palestinian Telecommunications Sector. Latest technologies provision is an on-going process, services penetration throughout all localities is tremendous and accomplishments are countless. 970 Palestine international Code, which is now being utilized by most countries around the world, is the starting point. A wide range of valuable services followed: Intelligent Network (IN) with the series of new services offered by it, toll free service, prepaid international calling cards (Ahlan Cards), ISDN lines allowing high speed Internet access at rates 4 times faster than regular telephone lines. Phone line and Public payphone installations, which are vastly penetrating Gaza and the West Bank. Multi-Services Data Communications as Frame Relay, ATM and ADSL services, which offer: high speed Internet connection, distance learning and Tele-medicine. Completing the Internet infrastructure and connecting it with the backbone of the international network, thus providing ISP's with Internet services and hosting www.paltel.net with all latest news on Paltel. Jawwal, Palestine GSM Cellular Network, which is continually experiencing exceptional growth since launching in October 1999. Digital Leased Lines, voice mail service, video telephone and more and more of a wide spectrum have advanced telecommunication services.

The latest in this wide array of new services is Paltel's new portal, www.alburaq.net, ARABIC IN TONGUE…PALESTINIAN IN ORIGIN…GLOBAL IN IMPACT…which allows feasible connection to diverse services and information in Arabic:- as searching for topics in all fields, free email subscriptions, chatting forums, Dictionaries, Qur'an interpretations and many others. However, the most worthwhile service at alburaq is the telephone directory, in which accessing any telephone number throughout Palestine is now at a click of a mouse.

ISO Accreditation

Extensive efforts per implementing quality controls enabled PALTEL to obtain the ISO 9002 certificate by the SGS UK, LTD., for International Certification Services, in recognition of initiating a company-wide Total Quality Management System (TQM), to improve service quality and customer satisfaction. PALTEL is the first quality assured telecommunication firm complying with ISO 9002 Standard in the Middle East region.
