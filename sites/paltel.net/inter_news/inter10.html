Feb. 14, 2000
 
The Palestinian Cellular Phone Company (Jawwal) And The Israeli Partner (Orange) Implementthe Signed Agreement Today
 

 
Implementing the signed agreement, Jawwal Company has finished all technical issues related to operating the Palestinian Jawwal with Partner (Orange Operator) in Israel, now Palestinian subscribers can use their mobile phones inside Israel. Teams from both sides reviewed all technical issues and finished network connection works with Roaming Department in Orange Company.
The reason why Orange was chosen for this project is that both companies work with the same Digital Technology (G.S.M), the most developed in the world of mobile telecom. This agreement was a new step towards completing our system and fulfilling the needs of the Palestinian citizen, needs, so that Jawwal subscribers will be able to use their mobile phones in Palestine and Israel without feeling any change concerning coverage or calls. Furthermore, Jawwal Company cares for international agreements side by side with local ones, since the company is following up with operating roaming with new countries.
