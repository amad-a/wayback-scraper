  Tour Palestine With
Bob and Willie Cork

April 11-23, 1999

 

So which way do we go from here?
.

 

 It was an incredible vacation, visiting with gracious and generous people, seeing places and hearing sounds that we never expected to enjoy. The pictures here are representative of our trip. There are breathtaking open spaces. It seemed like we were always sitting down for tea with friends. We were so frustrated to see beautiful scenes that Palestinians never get to see. We were always aware, when looking down at historic places like the old city of Jerusalem, that somewhere, watching, was an Israeli soldier with a gun. (foreground) Impressions is a narrative summary of our trip, and we hope you like our pictures and comments on the other pages. Be sure to check out Cactus.
