About SEARCH

SEARCH is a national non-profit human rights and educational institution, founded in 1972, which seeks a just Israeli-Palestinian peace based on the inalienable rights of both peoples.

SEARCH's focus is on improving media coverage on Palestine/Israel.

SEARCH was founded and is directed by Dr. Edmund Hanauer


The SEARCH Perspective

SEARCH believes that justice for Palestinians and security for Israeli Jews are interdependent and not mutually exclusive.

SEARCH recognizes the right of resistance against occupation and believes non-violent means are the best form of resistance.

SEARCH seeks a US policy committed to the rights of both peoples.

SEARCH urges Americans to oppose anti-Jewish and anti-Arab prejudices.


SEARCH's Work

SEARCH emphasizes outreach to journalists and informational work with them, as well as with two other important constituencies: American Jews and human rights organizations.

SEARCH supplies journalists (and others) with fact sheets and the Palestine/Israel File.

SEARCH acts as a resource for journalists on various aspects of the Arab-Israeli conflict, including international law, peace, security, human rights, and US policy.

About Dr. Hanauer

Edmund Hanauer has a B.A. from Dartmouth College and a PhD. in Political Science from American University. His dissertation focused on the relationship of American Jews to the state of Israel.
Before starting SEARCH in 1972, Hanauer taught political science, history, and international relations at Babson College as well as at the Unversity of Maryland's European Division.
He has spoken twice to the Nieman Fellows in Journalism at Harvard and twice to the State Department's Open Forum of the Secretary of State. His address to the City Club of Cleveland was televisied nationally three times by C-SPAN.
Hanauer's articles on the Arab-Israeli conflict have appeared in 30 major newspapers, and he has been interviewed numerous times on TV and radio, including National Public Radio.
