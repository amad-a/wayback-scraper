13-year old Bilal tries to flag down a car between the towns of Deir al Balah and Khan Yunis on the main north-south highway in the Gaza Strip on 7 October 2001. Drivers will pick up boys such as Bilal and pay them one shekel (approximately 40 cents US) for the 15-minute trip that passes an Israeli military post. During the current conflict, the Israeli military prohibits drivers from traveling alone on this stretch of highway, supposedly to mitigate the potential of suicide bombers. Bilal is one of several boys that travel back and forth on the highway each day to make money to cope with the doubling in poverty rates since the onset of the current Intifada in September 2000.
