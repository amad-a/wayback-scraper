Photographer's Statement

While photographing, the light of these children registered on the sheets of my film with a face, a smile, and a presence that embraced and celebrated life. This is how life should normally be for children anywhere in the world. But we all know that such is not the case in this particular part of the world. These are photos of Palestinian children in the West Bank and Gaza. Though, they were photographed in the current Intifada, they hardly show the miserable conditions these kids are living in their everyday life. Sometimes you feel as if they were done in a different place and time. One might get confused because the viewer will attribute what he sees to what he knows - the tragic and forgotten reality of Palestinian children.
