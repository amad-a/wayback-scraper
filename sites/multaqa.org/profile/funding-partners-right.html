ATF's Funding Partners

As a non-government and non-profit organization, ATF believes in the importance of securing a broad funding base in order to achieve greater financial security and maintain intellectual independence.

Since its establishment in 1987, The ATF has cooperated with a number of local and international organizations in implementing its programs. Both its capital funding and program-linked expenditure ha come from grants through a variety of organizations and institutions.

ATF has a strong network of friendly and professional relations and enjoys credibility among local and international organizations. Currently, It is implementing a comprehensive program Democracy Development funded by USAID.
