Arab Thought Forum Publications

Since its establishment in 1977, the Arab Thought Forum issued more than 140 publications covering a wide variety of subjects. ATF’s publications list includes:

  • Books and Reports
  • Research Papers from the Conference on Development
  • Reports from the Agricultural Database
  • ATF’s Journal “Shu'un Tanmawiyyeh"
  • ATF’s Newsletter "Al-Multaqa"
