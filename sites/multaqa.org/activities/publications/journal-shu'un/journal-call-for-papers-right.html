"Shu'un Tanmawiyyeh" Call for Papers

Shu’un Tanmawiyyeh welcomes papers in the following topics:

  • The peace process, including the final status issues of Jerusalem, settlements, refugees and final borders.
  • Democratic and legal issues including human and citizens rights, international laws etc.
  • Development issues.
  • State building and reform of public institutions

Publication regulations

  1. Articles submitted for publication should be between 6-20 pages (2000-6000 words)
  2. Manuscripts should be typed double spaced and should include the author’s name, affiliation and mailing address.
  3. Articles are accepted in either Arabic or English languages, with an abstract preferably in the other language, when possible.
  4. The journal encourages compliance with the rules of scientific methods in wiring; objectivity and reliability based on documentation as well as reference as much as possible. All tables and figures must have a complete source. References must include, wherever relevant, the name of the author, the year, title, publishers and place of publication.
  5. The writer, or someone on his/her behalf, may present the topic of the paper in a special seminar prior to printing.
  6. The material cannot be recovered by the writer after submittal, even if it was not published. Authors should retain one complete copy of their manuscripts.
  7. The writer will be given a symbolic honorarium not exceeding $200.
  8. ATF reserves the right to copy/edit the articles accepted for publication.
  9. Submission of an article implies that it has not been published elsewhere.
  10. The deadline for receiving articles for the upcoming issue is one month before the termination of each quarter.
