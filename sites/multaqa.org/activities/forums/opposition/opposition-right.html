The Role Of The Opposition

As part of its DIALOGUE Program, ATF initiated a series of discussions on the role of the Palestinian opposition under the rubric “Palestinian Opposition between Negative Inactivity and Positive Interaction”. Through this series, ATF aimed at encouraging an active role for Palestinian opposition parties by having them participate effectively in the process of democratic change and the reconstruction of the country.

This approach is based on a realistic understanding of the important role played by the opposition in any political system. It is also based on the belief that political leadership may function more effectively and with greater transparency and credibility if an effective and responsible opposition existed. Such opposition can share in the responsibilities of control, guidance, constructive criticism and positive contribution to the development of the country.

Nine meetings, between high level representatives of all main political opposition parties, have taken place so far.

These meetings focused, in the first place, at creating opportunities for the different representatives to meet and discuss the role of their respective parties in the capacity of political opposition. Also discussed were issues related to reaching a unified political program, the role of the opposition at the current stage, and coordination between the opposition parties as well as with the PNA.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (9) 26/10/1998

Target: The Democratic Coalition at the PLC

Participants: Hasan Khreisheh, Rawya Shawa, Mu’awiya Masri.

The meeting focused on four issues. First, the political process. Second, relations with other opposition factions inside and outside the parliament. Third, description of the role of the democratic coalition and its political platform. Fourth, future projection of how all these elements are going to relate to one another. Participants also discussed other issues related to the democratic formation in Palestine.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (8) 19/8/1998

Target: The Islamic Resistance Movement (Hamas)

Participants: Sheikh Ahmad Yasin, Dr. Mahmoud Al Zahhar, Isma'il Abu Shanab and Isma'il Haneyyeh,

The meeting evolved around four main points: 1) Opposition between political factions and parliamentary structures; 2) the importance and mechanisms of institutionalizing the Palestinian political system; 3) how can the opposition perform an effective role; 3) the role of Islamic opposition, Hamas in particular, in the current national building process.

The meeting also served as a forum for exchange of views on recent developments in the democratization process in Palestine. Hamas leadership displayed a pragmatic position regarding its relation with the PNA and its involvement in the Palestinian life.

Representatives of Hamas highlighted their movement’s commitment to a concrete and meaningful national dialogue with the participation of all political forces in Palestine. They also stated that the movement will not challenge the base of legitimacy of the PNA, nor does it have any interest in taking control from the PNA as it rejects the Oslo Accords in principle. Hamas is willing however, and has even demonstrated this willingness, to participate in the Palestinian democratic life according to democratic and peaceful means. The movement is, however, careful that this participation does not undermine its position regarding the struggle against Israeli occupation. Most importantly, Hamas will not be drawn to any armed confrontation with the PNA.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic and English.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (7) 12/2/1998

Target: Political parties (Fatah movement, Al Sha’b party, Al Nidal Al Sha’by front, Fida party) and independent representatives within the PNA.

Participants: Ahmad Majdalani, Naim Ashab Talal Sidir, Saleh Ra’fat, Abdel Jawad Saleh, Suleiman Najab, Mamdouh Iker, Kamel Ihmeed, Hana Omera, Nabil Qabalani.

The theme of the meeting was based on the belief that every political system must have an opposition, and the more effective the role of this opposition the more is the evidence of the strength and stability of the political system. This meeting focused on the role of the different types of opposition, in their secular and religious forms, as well as parliamentary opposition, which is still in the formation stages. The meeting also addressed the issue of how the authority envisions opposition, and how can opposition perform an effective and positive role in the creation of a clear and efficient political system.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (6)   8/12/1997

Target: Fateh non-main stream members

Participants: Abbas Zaki, Hatem Abdelkader, Hasan Khreisheh, Kaddoura Fares, Salwa Hudeib.

The meeting contained Fatah movement members with independent opinions; those known not to always correspond to the official party line.

Discussion focused on four main issues:

  1. The importance of institutionalizing the Palestinian political system, and in specific the definition of the role and limitations of the opposition, the legislative Council, the Palestinian National Council and the Palestinian political factions in light of the Oslo Accords.
  2. Traditional and parliamentary opposition.
  3. What is required from the opposition in order to better interact with political circumstances and assume a more effective role.
  4. The role of the Fatah movement, being the largest and most politically influential faction in the Palestinian arena.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (5) 12/7/1997

Target: Left wing opposition parties.

Participants: Abdel Rahim Mulawah, Qais Abdel Karim, Abdel Latif Gheith, Ali Abu Hilal, Rakan Salem, Salim Hamdan

The meeting focused on the dilemma that the Palestinian opposition found themselves in due to current political circumstances. They are both in opposition with the Israeli occupation, on one side, and with the PNA, on the other. Also discussed in the meeting was the citizens’ confusion as whether the opposition was on the PNA’s side or not. Participants also discussed the theory that says that the secular opposition was able to coordinate amongst itself, but was unsuccessful in coordinating with the Islamic political opposition.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (4) 21/11/1996

Target: left wing political parties and Hamas Movement.

Participants: Qais Abdel Karim, Abdel Latif Gheith, Hasan Yousef, Daoud Talhami, Omar Assaf, Ali Abu Hilal, Nassif Awwad.

The meeting, including high ranking representatives of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine, the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine, the Arab Liberation Front and Hamas, included discussions of clear-cut issues concerning the principles of the common program suggested for the opposition. Issues discussed included: disengagement from the restrictions of the Oslo agreement; defending the vital interests of the Palestinian people, particularly economic and social ones; follow up the struggle against Israeli practices especially regarding settlements, Jerusalem and the right of return for refugees; and defending political freedom and civil society.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (3) 21/11/1996

Target: Parties representative and independents.

Participants: Waleed Al Omari, Ahmad Majdalani, Samiha Khalil, Abdel Latif Gheith, Fadel Tahboub, Mamdouh Al Iker.

The meeting focused on historic changes resulting form the Oslo Accord, which led to a dilemma situation difficult to overcome. The continuation of the status quo is dangerous, as it restricts the achievement of peace in the area and will lead to an outburst of the situation that will harm both the Palestinian and Israeli interests.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (2) 11/8/1996

Target: Parties representative and independents.

Participants: Samiha Khalil, Mir’i Abdel Rahman, Fadel Tahboub, Waleed Al Omari, Jamil Hamami, Ali Abu Hilal, Saleh Ra’fat, Saji Salameh.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.

The meeting concentrated on the diagnosis of the current stage and the nature of its challenges. Participants also discussed the role or roles of the opposition in view of these challenges and the possibility of coordination between the different opposition parties and between the opposition and the PNA.

The Role Of The Palestinian Opposition (1) 28/5/1996

Target: Parties representative and independents.

Participants: Samiha Khalil, Ali Abu Hilal, Fadel Tahboub, Ahmad Majdalani, Waleed Al Omari, Saji Salameh, Naim Al Ashhab, Abbas Abdel Haq, Riziq Shuqeir, Mir’i Abdel Rahman, Kamal Quba’a.

This first and preparatory meeting focused on the definition of the opposition and the nature of the current political situation. The meeting also focused on the role of the opposition within the results of the Oslo Accord. Also discussed were the different types of opposition. Participants concentrated their discussion on democratic opposition within the official framework with consideration to popular opposition in its broader sense.

Proceedings of the meeting are available in Arabic.
