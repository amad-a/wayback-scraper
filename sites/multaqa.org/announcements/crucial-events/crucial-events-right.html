New Series of Town Hall Meetings with Local Councils

During the last two years, ATF has organized over 40 town hall meetings, with more than 90% of PLC members participating. Drawing on the successful outcome of these meetings, ATF planed to introduce an additional series of meetings focusing on local councils. Now, citizens of each main town have the opportunity to discuss their affairs and concerns with PLC and city council representatives. For maximum benefits, representatives of other ministries and public departments will be invited to respond to specific issues that may be raised by citizens.

The objective of this approach is to provide a democratic platform whereby citizens can discuss the issues that are of daily concern to their lives. The process will encourage and consolidate the principle of transparency and oversight between citizens and their local representatives in particular. The new format also helps to contribute to the preparations for local council elections by highlighting the importance of relations between the citizen and his representative.

By July 2000 it is expected that more than 30 town hall meetings will have been held in the main cities and towns of the West Bank and Gaza Strip. In light of this goal, preparatory meetings have been held between ATF and the Ministry of Local Government and mayors of participating communities. Both parties welcomed the idea and ATF immediately started implementation.
