A Future Outlook

ATF’s programs and activities are designed to increase the participation of civil society in Palestinian public decision making. They aim to encourage, support, measure and directly intervene in this process; which ATF has went to great lengths to advocate and nurture over many years.

In this context, ATF will continue to engage the Palestinian people in a democratic process with the intention of achieving an increased public participation in the civil life and public decision making. ATF will continue to:

  • Organize extensive forums in which the Palestinian public will be given an opportunity to discuss and debate, with their elected representatives, the nature and management of their preferred democracy.
  • Organize conferences and symposia on a range of issues related to civil society and the democratic process.
  • Print and distribute direct and immediately accessible literature about elected representatives and institutions.
  • Canvass, lobby and encourage local and national government institutions to advertise the results of their work and their legislative proposals.
  • Publish and review public policy matters proposed or decided by local and national government.
  • Publish regular books giving biographical details of representatives, explanations of the workings of local and national legislative institutions and details of other agencies working in the field of civic, democratic and human rights.
  • Involve “opposition” parties in the democratic process and avoid their retreat into cynicism or resistance to the democratic process.
  • Operate the Citizens’ Rights Center to directly support the citizens’ democratic rights in the event of conflict with local or national government.

All future planning of ATF programs is directly related to the future of Palestine at large. That has been and will remain the case in the medium and long term planning of all ATF’s initiatives.

The number one aspiration of the Palestinian people is to achieve an independent democratic Palestinian State. ATF’s future projects will continue to reflect this national cause through the achievement of several broad-line objectives:

  • To positively influence the democratic formation process in Palestine.
  • To tackle different sovereignty and citizens’ rights related issues.
  • To define the status of Jerusalem as an open and peaceful city.
  • To promote the awareness of the Palestinian people in the development of their own country. A comprehensive development that includes all fields in the various sectors including culture, economy, infrastructure, environment and nature protection.
