The most significant IR military operations against the collaborators in Jezzine area

The successive and successful blows carried out by the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants against Israeli occupation troops and their collaborators in Jezzine area had forced the latter to refrain from serving at their. The direct consequence was a partial retreating from some of these outpost in the first stage.

Then, a comprehensive withdrawal of the Israeli proxy militia was achieved after the 20th Brigade, which control the area, had reached to the edge of total collapse.

The IR issued a table of the most significant military operations staged by its mujahedeen against the 20th Brigade including the attacks that targeted its commanders Raymond Abou Murad, Emile Nasr, Joseph Karam and Monah Touma respectively.
