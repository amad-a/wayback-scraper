17 Israeli allied militiamen have defected
 

"17 Israeli allied militiamen have defected and taken refuge in Sfarray's police station and requested the Lebanese government to provide them protection. The 17 are from Annan. The defections came two days after Israeli allied militiamen redeployed in the occupied Jezzine enclave. The militia played down the significance of the redeployment but reports said the move may be part of a rebellion. The militia has been in disarray and its militiamen are suffering from low morale after successful resistance operations".

"Two days ago, the Israeli allied militia redeployed its forces in the occupied south Lebanon enclave of Jezzine moving them closer to the town. Militia personnel evacuated the frontline Dahr al-Mashnaqa and Annan positions east of the port city of Sidon after 30 militiamen serving in the two positions refused to stay unless they received reinforcements. The militiamen reportedly complained to a regiment commander Joseph Karam, known as Allouche they can no longer defend their posts. The commander refused their request and told them to stay. But the militiamen refused and Karam decided to evacuate the positions after consulting with militia commander Antoine Lahd. The militia played down the redeployment saying it was part of a military plan which could be implemented at any time and not a withdrawal. Reports here said the withdrawal followed a series of bomb attacks by the resistance on militiamen travelling to and from the post of Annan. The 2,500 strong militia has lost 35 men killed and 42 wounded this year. It has also suffered a number of defections in recent months particularly from its intelligence services. Their morale is low since their fate is unknown once Israeli forces pull out of occupied areas. As a result of the withdrawal, the villages of Annan, Sfaray, Bisri, Mazraat al Mathaneh and Khirbet Bisri are no longer part of the militia controlled region and the most forward militia position is now in the village of Roum. The militia has reportedly reinforced its presence in Roum and was establishing a new crossing point to replace Kfar Falous. Roum is a strategic position because it overlooks the regions east of Sidon and Iqlim al Kharooub. The source said the militia did not destroy the fortifications in its evacuated positions as it would in the past and did not remove the obstacles and earth mounds from Kfar Falous Roum road. The militia closed all roads leading to Annan in a move apparently aimed at punishing village residents for not forcing their sons to stop the rebellion. It was the second outpost abandoned in the Jezzine region in past few months following the opening earlier in the year of the Kfar Falous. Lebanese newspapers said the redeployment might be the first step that will end militia control over the area".

"In September, the militia pulled out of the Toumat Niha position a strategic location south of Jezzine.

The Islamic Resistance had described that as a major victory and further evidence of the weakening resolve of the militia. Hizbullah said the repeated painful blows struck against Toumat Niha are the main cause of the collapse in the militiamen's morale. Toumat Niha was a hilltop position that had a commanding view over the western Bekaa to the east Jezzine to the west and the central sector to the south. Its primary role was to act as an observation post to provide early warnings of resistance activity to Israeli troops stationed in Kfar Houna five kilometers southwest of the Jezzine. Reports in Beirut said following the rebellion, the militia command is taking measures to fill the vacuum. There are also unconfirmed reports the Israelis may be pondering the option of replacing the militia's commander Antoine Lahd. Lahd has been in Paris with his family and some reports say he was angered by a statement by an Israeli deputy from the Likud party who questioned whether Israeli troops were in the south only to protect their local militia. Lahd demanded a formal apology but so far one was not made. Some reports said Israelis are studying the possibility of replacing Lahd with someone not from the ranks of the militia due to internal fighting".
