Lahd warns of trouble from SLA soldiers after pullout

    The commander of the South Lebanon Army (SLA), General Antoine Lahd, said yesterday that he will not be responsible for his soldiers' deeds in the event that Israel decides on a unilateral withdrawal from South Lebanon. He expected that, in such a case, "my soldiers will take all sorts of steps." Lahd made the statement yesterday at a ceremony for graduating SLA officers in South Lebanon.

    When he was asked whether he believed that Prime Minister Ehud Barak was intent on withdrawing from Lebanon and in what manner the pullout would be completed, Lahd responded "From my knowledge of Barak, he is a serious and ardent individual. But even his closest ministers don't know what's in his head, so who can expect me to know?" Lahd added that from his conversations with Barak and Israel security officials, he understood that Israel doesn't plan a unilateral withdrawal from Lebanon.

1- Haaretz, 26-11-1999.
