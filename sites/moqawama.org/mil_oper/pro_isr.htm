Pro-Israeli militia announces redeployment amid rebellion reports:
IR redeployment is another fruit of Mujahedeens operations:

Israels surrogate militia, the South Lebanon Army dismantled frontline positions in the Jezzine enclave and redeployed the militiamen closer to the town.

The Lebanese government dismissed the military movements as nothing more than a redeployment and renewed calls for a complete Israeli withdrawal from the occupied zone.

 SLA personnel evacuated the Dahr al-Mashnaqa and Anan positions east of Sidon Monday afternoon, after 30 SLA militiamen serving in the two positions refused to stay unless they received reinforcements.

A security source told The Daily Star that the militiamen complained to the commander of the 20th SLA regiment, Joseph Karam, known as Allouche, that they can no longer defend the posts.

Allouche asked the SLA command in Marjayoun to send the needed reinforcements, but the reply was negative and he was told to use every possible means to force the 30 militiamen to stay in their positions, the source said.

But in the face of stubborn rebellion from the militiamen, most of whom are from the village of Anan, Mr. Karam decided to evacuate the positions after consulting with SLA commander Antoine Lahd.
The militia played down the importance of its withdrawal from the two positions.

Our forces are carrying out a redeployment in the sector of Kfar Falous as part of a military plan which could be implemented at any time, an SLA spokesman said. In September, the SLA pulled out of the Toumat Niha position, a strategic location south of Jezzine.

The 2,500-strong militia has lost 35 men killed and 42 wounded this year. It has also suffered a number of defections in recent months, particularly from its intelligence services.

As a result of the withdrawal, the villages of Anan, Sfaray, Bisri, Mazraat al-Mathaneh, and Khirbet Bisri are no longer part of the SLA-controlled region and the most forward SLA position is now in the village of Roum.

The source said that the SLA had reinforced its presence in Roum and was establishing a new crossing point to replace Kfar Falous. Roum is an important position because it overlooks the regions east of Sidon and Iqlim al-Kharroub.

The source said that the militia did not destroy the fortifications in its evacuated positions as it would in the past, and did not remove the obstacles and earth mounds from the Kfar Falous-Roum road.
The SLA closed all roads leading to Anan in a move apparently aimed at punishing village residents for not forcing their sons to stop the rebellion.

The Sfaray police station told The Daily Star that the residents of the freed villages called for the deployment of the Lebanese Army.

They want the Army to enter the region from which the SLA has pulled out, said Gerges Qazzi, a policeman.

As traffic on the Kfar Falous crossing stopped, military sources said the Army took precautions to face new developments.

President Emile Lahoud said Lebanon would continue to insist on supporting the resistance.

The new developments in south Lebanon will only make us more attached to UN resolutions, the simultaneity of the Lebanese and Syrian negotiating tracks and the countrys right to resist Israeli occupation. We will continue to support our people in the occupied zone, General Lahoud said.

However, Hezbollah sources said "The militiamen in the town of Annan have disagreed with their leadership over refusal to man new outposts".

Head of Hezbollah's political council MP Mohammed Raad said Hezbollah is following up the developments in coordination with the Lebanese army "which decides how to deal with the new situation.

Al-Safir newspaper quoted Islamic Resistance sources as expressing relief at the additional achievement which has been the result of hard blows dealt to the leaders and soldiers of the proxy militia in the evacuated areas as part of an Islamic Resistance program which went on over the past few years."

The resistance which reiterated the continuation of such programs to annihilate the remainder of the militia in the area, "highly appreciates the stands of the local residents who has exerted pressure recently rejecting the existence of the militia in their villages and refusing pressgang conscription.

The resistance safeguards the welfare of the area's residents for whom the Mujahedeen have sacrificed a lot, the IR sources said.

The high-ranking resistance sources explained the military situation in the enclave affirming that the militia's twentieth brigade has completely collapsed, while other militia divisions are about to face the same fate.

In Jerusalem, Israeli radio acknowledged that collaborator Lahd's militia has evacuated the Annan outpost and al-Mashnaqa checkpoint retreating towards the town of Roum, but claimed the new positioning is part of a tactical military plan, and not withdrawal.

Reports said collaborators reinforced their position on the Roum Hilltop which overlooks the main road in the town before withdrawing from the Dahr al-Mashnaqa Hilltop (where they had been positioned since the opening of the Kfar Falus crossing. The main road is still closed with obstacles.

Meanwhile the Lebanese army units positioned in Kfar falus has taken precautionary measures to confront any development.
