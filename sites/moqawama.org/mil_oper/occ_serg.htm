Three occupation sergeants killed in an IR operation dedicated to martyrs and Mujahedeen in Palestine

"On the occasion of the Prophet Mohammed's (A.S.) Mission and Martyr's Day, the Islamic Resistance's (IR) Special Forces Mujahedeen were able to penetrate through the enemy's security measures, dealing a painful blow deep inside the occupied zone, at the entrance of the "Israeli" 70th battalion HQ near the border with occupied Palestine."

"At 11:30 yesterday, IR's squad of Martyr Haitham Ghamloush (Sadeq) in the Special Forces detonated a roadside bomb as an "Israeli" foot patrol passed by near the command HQ entrance in al-Qobaa (Markaba) near the shooting zone."

"The seven troops (in the patrol) were killed or wounded and were left in the battlefield for 40 minutes as the enemy soldiers did not dare to advance to pull the casualties back, an IR communiqué read.

As it affirms that it will proceed with the Jihad line until complete liberation, IR dedicates this successful operation to the martyrs and mujahedeen in occupied Palestine, IR's communiqué added.
"The collaborators' Middle East Television said three of the troops were in serious condition, noting that 17 "Israeli" occupation troops have been killed and 100 others were wounded by resistance operations since the beginning of this year."

"However, "Israeli" television in the evening quoted an "Israeli" military source as acknowledging that three "Israeli" sergeants of the Golani Brigade were killed in the attack."

"Security sources in the occupied zone said the "Israeli" forces were in a hysteric status when the explosion took place."

"The confused soldiers went on a rampage of shooting randomly into the air while the occupation's artillery pounded the hilltops surrounding the operation site", the sources said.

"After more than 30 minutes, two "Israeli" helicopters landed in the site and moved the enemy casualties to the borders before taking them to hospitals inside occupied Palestine", the sources added.

"Hezbollah's official in the south area Sheikh Nabil Qawooq said, "IR mujahedeen's operation today near occupied Palestine affirms once again that IR is able to strike the enemy wherever the resistance men wish and where the enemy expects or do not expect. It also verifies the failure of the occupation's policy of fortification in the occupied zone."

"If the enemy has taken the decision to escalate aggressions -- this appears through the semi-daily air raids carried out by the enemy -- the resistance's decision is to efficiently retaliate at deadly spots where the enemy troops and their morale break down. The dust of raids will not screen the reality of the enemy's defeat, nor will it get the enemy out of the targeting circle", Sheikh Qawooq said.
"The killing and injury of enemy troops yesterday is part of their inevitable fate; the victory made by the resistance and our people's pride and heroism represent our inevitable fate of confronting the usurpers and occupiers", he added.

"Moreover, IR's squad of Martyrs Youssef Ali Awdeh and Mohammed Ali Yassin at 15:35 yesterday attacked enemy movements at the Blat outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits."
