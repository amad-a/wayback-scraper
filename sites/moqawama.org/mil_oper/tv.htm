Hizbullah fighter killed by "Israelis" appears on TV
 

    "Praising its troops for a job well done, the "Israeli" Army televised video footage from a unpiloted reconnaissance drone, showing the supposed final moments of a Hizbullah guerrilla.

"The dramatic film, which was broadcast on all "Israeli" television channels Thursday night, clearly portrayed the fleeing guerrilla, helicopter gunships, explosions and gunfire.

"The "Israeli" Army, releasing the video two days after three officers were killed and five soldiers wounded in a Hizbullah ambush, clearly hoped the footage would boost morale.

Imagine the red faces all around, then, when the killed guerrilla turned up alive and well in the Hammoud hospital in Sidon 24 hours later, with a few scratches on his face and arm.

"Clearly exhausted by his lengthy ordeal, the guerrilla, given the nom de guerre Hadi Sayyed Hassan, quietly recounted to The Daily Star his astonishing escape, which saw him chased by soldiers, tanks, helicopter gunships and even a missile-firing F-16 fighter-bomber.

"He and three colleagues embarked on a mission Wednesday, creeping into the central sector of the zone through Wadi Salouqi and heading for Markaba, 3 kilometers away.

The Markaba operation was not a routine mission since we had to cross lengthy distances and pass between "Israeli" positions. Simply getting so close to the Palestinian border is a victory in itself, he said.

But on the way back from the mission, the squad ran into an "Israeli" patrol.

We adopted a fire-and-withdraw tactic. I lay on the ground to cover the withdrawal of the guys using my Kalashnikov and hand grenades so that the "Israelis" couldnt advance, he said.

The fighters had inadvertently stumbled upon a unit of Golani Brigade troops trained to operate at close quarters against guerrillas. Having secured the withdrawal of his comrades, Hadi found himself cut off and took cover in an abandoned house in Markaba.

I took all the precautionary measures. Crouching under a staircase, I covered my head with my arms as I clutched onto my hand grenades, he said.

"Trapped by Merkava tanks, troops and with a helicopter gunship circling above, Hadi said that, at one point, he believed his only choice was to commit an act of martyrdom and kill the greatest number of Zionists".

I decided to face them and not surrender at all, he said.

Suddenly, Hadi said, a Merkava tank fired shells at the house followed by a long burst of machine gun fire.

I remained in my position, still clutching the hand grenades, waiting for a chance to throw them.

When I decided to stand up and fire at a soldier standing on the Merkava, a bulldozer approached in an attempt to destroy the house, he said.

Hadi jumped off the back porch and crouched down to assess the situation.
I realized that I was in the tanks sights there too. I fired several shots and ran as fast as I could, he said.

"The drone continued to track Hadi as he raced down a street and into fields surrounding the village.
As he approached Wadi Salouqi, with the edge of the zone tantalizingly close, Hadi took cover momentarily to regain his breath. But at that moment a missile fired from an F-16 warplane exploded beside him".

Trees around me flew and I couldnt hear anything. I buried myself in the soil, knowing that none of the "Israeli" soldiers would dare come close to me, he said.

The "Israelis", apparently assuming Hadi had been killed, gave up the chase.

Hadi, meanwhile, discovered that his walkie-talkie was still working. He contacted his comrades and was guided out of the zone.

"Hospital officials said that Hadis condition was stable. He lost a lot of blood, but three people donated blood to him and he is fine now, an official said.
