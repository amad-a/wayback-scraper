The "Israeli" armys name, the "Israel" Defence Force*, has taken on a new connotation lately as the military appears to be spending more time defending its mistakes rather than the Jewish state after a series of embarrassments have prompted a flurry of internal inquiries.

The parents are also demanding the dismissal of the report into the air disaster which killed 73 soldiers and that criminal charges be brought against Eliahu.

The "Israeli" ministry of defence has criticised Gershonis move as a cynical exploitation of the crash.

Other than being attacked by the resistance in the occupation zone, the "Israeli" army has also had its fair share of self-inflicted mishaps which have led to individual inquiries.

These include the so-called Sojod fiasco when a lone Hizbullah guerrilla clambered into the post, took a walk and punched an "Israeli" soldier before departing. Others include the overturning of a helicopter at the Dabshe position which injured four soldiers, and the armoured personnel carrier which tumbled into the Litani River, killing two soldiers.

On the same weekend as the APC accident, an "Israeli" soldier was hit on the head by a traversing tank barrel and another soldier was accidentally shot in the leg by one of his colleagues at a firing range.

Even the bungled naval commando raid on Insarieh a year ago has come under further scrutiny with the re-opening of the inquiry in July, which could lead to the prosecution of senior army officers.

Nine "Israelis" Killed or Wounded in an IR Qualitative Operation
"Five "Israeli" occupation troops were killed and four others were wounded today in the Islamic Resistance's (IR) qualitative security operation deep into the occupied zone. The figures were confirmed by sources inside the occupied town of Hasbaya", the operation site.

"A new qualitative achievement was added today by the IR to its record which is full of military and security victories over the Zionist enemy, when IR's mujahedeen penetrated all the enemy's tight measures and infiltrated deep into the occupied zone, in Zaghleh", an IR communiqué read.

"The Mujahedeen targeted an "Israeli" command column in the "security square" of the 30th Battalion's command HQ. The "square" includes the "Israeli" liaison command HQ and the Hasbaya security HQ." "The operation resulted in killing and wounding a large number of the occupation troops", the communiqué added.

"Stating the details of the operation, IR said "At 15:00 today, as Moslems celebrate the anniversary of the Birth of Sayyeda al-Zahraa (A.S.) "The convoy was on its way to the Marjeyoun barracks. An armored Jeep and a command car were destroyed in the attack. Ten "Israeli" troops who were in the two vehicles were either killed or injured", IR said.

"The two vehicles were seen burning. An enemy force was seen evacuating casualties with helicopters' assistance", IR added.

"Five "Israeli" troops and four others were wounded, security sources in Hasbaya said, adding that five occupation troops were in the command car and four others were in the armored Jeep which were targeted by the two bombs."

'The "Israeli" troops were not able to evacuate the four casualties in the Jeep for 90 minutes", the sources said.

"They said the number of "Israelis" killed in the attack is likely to rise due to the critical conditions of the four troops who were wounded."

"There were two nearly simultaneous explosions as an "Israeli" convoy passed by on a road leading to Hasbaya", Agence France Presse quoted security sources as saying.

"The agency said the "Israeli" occupation forces immediately cordoned off the area. AFP's reporter said he saw two "Israeli" military Jeeps, 150 meters away from one another, burnt completely, while four ambulances were evacuating casualties to Marjeyoun, the site of the "Israeli" occupation's HQ."

"Earlier, IR's squad of Martyrs Ali Sirhal and Ali al-Anqouni at 12:30 today ambushed a motorized patrol belonging to the collaborators' militia at the Ain Majdaleen post road."

"When the patrol reached the ambush site, the Mujahedeen attacked it with their machine guns and rocket propelled grenades destroying a T-54 tank and a M-113 APC. Definite casualties were inflicted on the militiamen."

"Meanwhile, IR's squad of Martyr Amin Rizq in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) was pounding the Ain Majdaleen outpost with the appropriate weapons and scoring direct hits."

"At 15:00 today IR's squad of Martyrs Hussein Nhe'em and Yousef Bzeih targeted the same outpost with the appropriate weapons and scored definite hits".
