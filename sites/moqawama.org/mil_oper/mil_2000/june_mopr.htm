Military Operations

June Monday 05 -2000:


"Israeli" tank to become  monument in Ghobeiri(1)

Ghobeiri Mayor Mohammed Said Khansa said on Sunday that an abandoned "Israeli" tank would be placed in one of the towns squares to ensure that the humiliating ("Israeli") retreat is remembered forever.
A statement issued by the municipality indicated that the square, located off the airport road, would be renamed Resistance Square.
Khansa said the Interior Ministry and other government departments had granted the municipality permission to proceed with its plans for the tank, which was taken by Hizbullah.
Khansa, who is an official with Hizbullah, said the monument would be set up soon.
He also announced that the municipality would give seven Ghobeiri residents who were liberated from Khiam last month LL5 million each as a modest contribution to help them in this difficult economic period and in appreciation of their suffering.

1- The Daily Star, 5-6-2000.

June Wednesday 07 -2000:

Occupation army pulls out from another Lebanese pocket(1)

The "Israeli" occupation army withdrew on Tuesday from a strip of land which had remained under occupation after the liberation of the Lebanese occupied zone on May, Al-Manar Television correspondent said.

The area of agricultural plains extends between the town of Kfar Kila, the Marjeyoun Plateau, and the Wazzani, the site from which the "Israeli" occupation has been stealing Lebanese water for years.

The two-kilometer strip is two hundred meters wide and extends along the border with occupied Palestine from the center of the middle sector to the beginning of the eastern sector.

Up till today (Tuesday), the occupation has been stealing Lebanese soil from the aforementioned strip.

Engineering vehicles were seen erecting a new barbed fence and watchtowers along the Metulla colony.


"Israel" leaves fort on Lebanese border(2)

" The "Israeli" army left a fortified position straddling the Lebanese border Tuesday, permitting UN troops to mark off the international border in the area with blue paint, an AFP correspondent witnessed.

The position of Har-Duv, near the town of Rosh Hanikra, stretched in part beyond "Israel's" northern border.

June Thursday 08 -2000:

IR hands over booty to Lebanese army(1)

The Islamic Resistance (IR) declared that it had over the past few days paraded the booty captured from the Zionist enemy and its proxy militia "with the aim of enabling our people throughout Lebanon to rejoice over the unmistakable victory over the Zionist enemy, IR said in a communiqué.

After the end of these ceremonies, IR presents the booty to the Lebanese army hoping that a national museum will be established to expose the spoils as an immortal historic emblem and a live evidence for the coming generations to the enemy's defeat and a lesson for the present and the future, the communiqué added.


"Israel" leaves a position and blows up another in Lebanon(2)

"The "Israeli" army left a position on the Lebanese side of its northern border Wednesday and blew up a nearby redoubt, an AFP correspondent witnessed.

The "Israeli" army had remained in the position and at the Abbassiyeh redoubt after it ended its 22-year occupation of south Lebanon on May 24.

At dawn, "Israeli" troops left the position about a hundred meters (yards) from the source of the Ouazzani River, a tributary of the Hasbani which flows into northern "Israel", witnesses said.

The soldiers fell back some 300 meters, to near the village of Ghajar, located between Lebanon and the Syrian Golan, held by "Israel" since 1967. UN cartographers who mapped ""Israel's" border with Lebanon have called for Ghajar to be divided into two.

The "Israeli" army, which put up barbed wire around the Ouazzani River's source, did not disassemble a pump system that pipes water toward "Israel".

Overnight, the "Israelis" dynamited Abbassiyeh, a large redoubt near a destroyed Lebanese village of the same name which remains behind barbed wire.

Bulldozers flattened the dynamited area out Wednesday, while some 100 villagers assembled on the Lebanese side and tried to take down the barbed wire and return home.

Officers of the UN observer mission monitoring the 1949 ceasefire line came to Abbassiyeh to make a survey, an AFP correspondent witnessed.

The "Israeli" moves come as the United Nations begins final work to confirm that "Israel" had withdrawn to the international border in compliance with Resolution 425 following its evacuation from southern Lebanon.

1- Manar TV, 7-6-2000.

2- AFP, 7-6-2000.

June Monday 12 -2000:

"Israel" dismantles radar station along Lebanese border(1)

" The "Israeli" army dismantled a radar station Sunday located on the border with Lebanon, an AFP correspondent reported from the scene.

The station encroached slightly on to Lebanese territory near the fortified position of Tzipporen, near the Manara kibbutz in Upper Galilee.

The dismantling came after a request by UN experts in charge of marking the border to verify whether the "Israeli" withdrawal last month meets UN Resolution 425, which called on "Israel" to pull out of southern Lebanon.

AFP, 11-6-2000.
