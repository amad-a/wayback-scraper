Another IR victory; two occupation sergeants killed near Blat overnight
 

Two Israeli occupation soldiers were killed last night in the western sector of the occupied zone in a fresh blow dealt by the Islamic Resistance (IR) to the enemy forces.

Only hours after IR Mujahedeen's infiltration into Markaba and striking the Golani elite troops, IR struck again near the occupation's outpost in Blatt, only 400 meters away from the border with occupied Palestine.

An Israeli army spokesman this morning acknowledged that two occupation sergeants were killed in IR's operation last night, four others were wounded. The names of the killed occupation troops are: Sgt. Ayal Cohen, 20 from Beersheba, and Sgt. Ohad Nachmias , 20 from Akko

They brought the number of Israeli soldiers killed in IR operations in the occupation zone to seven in 11 days. This rises to 24 the number of Israeli soldiers killed and to 104 the number of those wounded in IR attacks in South Lebanon since the beginning of this year.

The two soldiers, on patrol in occupied South Lebanon, died Thursday when two bombs exploded under their armored vehicles, according to the Israeli army spokesman.

A source in a pro-Israeli militia said the mine blast ripped through an Israeli army convoy, killing the two soldiers and wounding four others

The two bombs were detonated when two Israeli armored personnel carriers passed by on the road of the village of Blatt in the eastern sector of the Israeli occupied zone in south Lebanon, IR said.

The bomb explosion also destroyed an Israeli Merkava tank and another armed vehicle.

Three Israeli tanks were destroyed in resistance operations against Israeli occupying troops in the last 10 days. 

So-called Israeli Northern Commander Major General Gabi Ashkenzi said there had been over 1,000 incidents so far this year almost double the number in 1997.

He said, "There is no doubt Hezbollah has significantly increased its attempts to hit the Israeli force through a variety of means."

Last week IR men were able to penetrate so close to an Israeli outpost to plant a bomb.

The soldiers were killed in a two-pronged bomb attack on Thursday night. The first roadside bomb was detonated Thursday night at about 9:00pm as an Israeli tank and armored vehicle were operating in the western sector of the security zone.

There were items in at least one of the damaged vehicles that could not be abandoned in the "security" zone so the decision was made to extricate the vehicles with the stranded soldiers, Israeli media said.

The rescue team came under heavy Hezbollah mortar fire and in the process of the operation detonated at least one more roadside bomb, an Israeli news agency said.

IR vowed to its people and martyrs to continue combating the Israeli enemy and its collaborators until victory is achieved and the occupation is defeated, an IR communiqué read.
