"Israel" Asked for US "Intelligence Aid" to Confront Resistance Operations
  
From the Lebanese Daily al-Kifah al-Arabi
 "Israel" has asked the US government to provide it with "intelligence aid" to confront the attacks of Hizbullah and other Lebanese resistance groups in south Lebanon."

"The "Israeli" request was presented through the secret contact channels between Israeli intelligence (Mossad) and US intelligence, especially CIA and the Defensive Security Agency."

"Israel" has acknowledged defects in its security and intelligence abilities in south Lebanon; this made its forces vulnerable to successful attacks by the Lebanese resistance in the last months", US sources said.

"It has become obvious to the "Israeli" northern command that the Lebanese resistance had enhanced its ability to know the timing of the movements and patrols of the "Israeli" forces in south Lebanon, the American sources added tracing this fact back to considerable cooperation by Lebanese civilians with the resistance while gathering information (espionage) by the "Israeli" army is getting more difficult."

"Israel's" request for assistance from US intelligence can be interpreted as:

"First, providing the "Israeli" army with more advanced electronic espionage equipment for use in south Lebanon."

"Second, "Israeli" access to the data obtained by American satellites on south Lebanon."
