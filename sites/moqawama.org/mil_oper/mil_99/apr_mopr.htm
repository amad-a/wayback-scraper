Cycling moms convince mayors to support Lebanon withdrawal(1)
"Carmiel Mayor Adi Eldar and Rishon Letzion Mayor Meir Nitzan have established a forum called "Leaving Lebanon" after meeting with two mothers who lost sons in Lebanon and who earlier this week completed a nationwide bicycle ride to call for the IDF*'s withdrawal from Lebanon".

"Mayor Nitzan, who is also the chair of the Council of Local Authorities, said, "As mayors, all of us have experience in using our influence." He added that meeting with the two bereaved mothers affected his decision".

"Shimoni, one of the cycling mothers, said that the forum's establishment was an example of how successful the "physically and emotionally difficult journey" had been.

"She said she hoped the forum would encourage former generals and professionals to call on the government to take action on withdrawal".

At the same time, the IR squads of martyrs Fadl Atwi, Khodor Khiami, Ahmad Cherri, Sami Melhem, Ali Madi, Hassan El-Amin, Ali Kassem, Youssef Bzeih, Moussa Salman, Ali Harmoush, Mohammad Faqih, Hussein Salman, Hussein Nahim and Hassan Salman and the teams of martyrs of Mansouri, Nabatieh, Jmeijmeh and Sohmor massacres staged attacks on the positions of Blat, Radar, Taibeh, Beaufort Castle, Rihan, Dabsheh, Tair Harfa, Mashaaroun, Qantara, Soweida, Bir Kallab, Kfar Houna, Ahmadieh, Dabboura, Kassaret Al-Oroush, Zaffateh, Shraifeh and Ain Taghra respectively.

The attack on Sojod outpost resulted in wounding eight "Israeli" soldiers and three collaborators as the "Israeli" Army acknowledged.

Earlier, the IR group of martyrs Bassel Alaeddine and Ali Mohammad Nasser attacked, at 1:05, the position of the 70th Lahdi Brigade Command (Qabaa) scoring direct and certain hits.

Confusion in occupation and collaborators' ranks following the Sojod operation(1)
A state of confusion prevails in the ranks of the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators following the qualitative operation carried out by the Islamic Resistance (IR) against the Sojod outpost.

IR Mujahedeen on Tuesday stormed the Sojod outpost and raised Hizbullah flags over the outpost's destroyed ramparts.

Reports from the occupied zone said the Israeli command were surprised by the Sojod operation and the series of operations that accompanied it, especially the timing (in the afternoon) and the heaviness of the fire used by the Resistance.

This had led to a semi-total paralysis in the Israeli and Lahdi outposts along a 10-km front, as IR was able to directly hit the outposts and inflict serious damage on their fortifications.

The greater shock for the occupation command was the precision of hits in the major Israeli artillery emplacements at the Rihan hilltop which damaged four 155 artillery emplacements and wounded eight of the enemy soldiers, especially as Rihan is one of the most fortified outposts, besides its housing the occupation's command in the sector and its being station for armored vehicles and major center for emergency intervention and long-range artillery, the reports from the occupied zone said.

In a bid to boost the morale of the occupation troops and their collaborators, an Israeli delegation including officers from the northern area command and the liaison unit yesterday inspected the Rihan outpost, the damage in the outpost, and how the troops there were wounded.

The northern area command has formed an investigation panel to find out how IR men were able to storm into the Sojod outpost and retreat so easily.

Sojod operation proves the real initiative is in the hand of Resistance(2)
Chief of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad said the Islamic Resistance's (IR) operations Tuesday were part of the continuous resistance activity against the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators all over the occupied zone.

"It is clear, from the operation which targeted the fortified outpost of Sojod and silenced around 22 Israeli outposts in the area all at once, that Israeli troops are still there, in contrast with the what has been announced about reducing occupation troops and using Lahd militiamen in front-line positions," Raad said.

"The second issue reflected by this operation is that the actual initiative is in the hands of the resistance."

The Israelis are frustrated whenever Resistance men attack outposts of theirs or their collaborators', Raad added.

He also stressed the qualitative advancement in the Resistance men's performance, their calmness, and certainty about the operation's outcome.

Everyone has seen (in the footage of the operation) how one of the Resistance men entered the outpost unarmed with only two flags in his hands, the Hizbullah MP said.

This shows a real field superiority and brings down the unreal superiority which the Israeli enemy has tried to claim, he concluded.
