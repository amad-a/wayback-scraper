Compared with Vietnam War
Israel's War of Attrition Costs Two U.S.A Million Dollars a Day
 

A French official source said the war of attrition in South Lebanon reflected, negatively, on the fighting-capacities of "Israeli" Army, after it had been failed in confronting about 600 of Hizbullah's fighters.

The French source hinted that in spite of many strategic experts consider Lebanon, from military viewpoint, as the weakest ring among the Arab countries adjacent to the Hebrew State, it managed to enforce itself as a sole hot front with "Israel" in 1990s. Besides, it imposes upon "Tel Aviv" the military rules of game frequently controlled by the latter since its birth in 1948.
Lebanon had managed to accomplish that without being militarily and economically exhausted as it had happened to several Arab countries. Consequently, Lebanon had continued its track of development and reconstruction.

The same source asserted that Lebanon had created a new equation made it, from the "Israeli" point of view, a critical problem that one can compare it to Vietnam with respect to U.S.A in 1960s and 1970s.

The French source mentioned that the western diplomatic and military reports proved that the Hizbullah forces operating in the area adjacent to the "Israeli"-occupied enclave do not exceed 600 militants. Nevertheless, these forces had managed to put all "Israel's" northern front troops in a continuous war costs a total of nearly two million dollars a day. These expenses multiply according to the severity and strength of the military confrontations. The "Israeli" military command fears that one day Hizbullah will be able to mobilize hundreds of its fighters stationed in Beirut and Beqaa to deploy them in South Lebanon opposite to the "Israeli" forces.

After following and fizzling out the various kinds of ways aimed at weakening and undermining Hizbullah, starting from the operations of assassinating its leaders, passing by the attempts of sowing dissension between its allies in Damascus and Tehran reaching to the efforts of penetration into its hierarchy especially on the military levels, Tel Aviv had learned that Hizbullah became a tough block in South Lebanon

The "Israeli" Army's morale is in continuous deterioration day after day as a result of the war of attrition staged by Hizbullah. "Israel's" senior political and military officials had expressed their concern over the impacts of increasing human casualties sustained by the "Israeli" Army in a conflict no one can put an end to it but by implementing, unconditionally, the resolution 425, the source ended.
