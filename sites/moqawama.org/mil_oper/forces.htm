140 operations against occupation forces in March

    "Lebanese Resistance staged 140 operations against "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators during the month of March 1999".

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) carried out 117 operations, (83,57%), Multi-faith Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) 10, (11,44%). Amal movement 6 (4,28%). One operation has not been claimed by any party".

"IR's painful blows dealt to the enemy during the last days of February affected the occupation forces' performance in March, as the so-called "security zone" became more insecure".

"The field confusion of the "Israeli" army was shown in the measures taken after the Hasbayya operations in which the "Israeli" commander in south Lebanon Major General Erez Gerestein was killed.

Following that operation, the enemy arrested a number of the collaborating militia's security officers, marking the downfall of this militia and how the "Israelis" do not trust its militiamen anymore".

"Furthermore, the Hasbayya Druze Sheikhs' meeting in al-Bayyada, in which they forbade
cooperating with the "Israeli" occupation, made the enemy's situation even more complicated".

"In March, the "Israeli" enemy admitted the injury of six of its soldiers, and four agents, claiming that three of the "Israelis" were wounded in a vehicle collision in the Sojod Rihan sector".

"For its part, Resistance sources confirmed the injury of three enemy soldiers and five agents."
Outstanding operations:

1- Wadi al-Houjeir operation:
IR's squad of Martyr Jamal Sh'heme at 4:30 a.m. Friday March 26 detonated a bomb against an "Israeli" foot patrol near Wadi al-Houjeir, scoring definite hits in "Israeli" troops' ranks. The enemy acknowledged the injury of an "Israeli" officer and a paratrooper.

2- Kroum al-Arez operation:
IR's Marksmen Unit on March 11 hunted down two agent militiamen at the collaborators' outpost in Kroum al-Arez. Militia sources acknowledged that one agent was injured.

3- Beir Kallab operation:
At 18:15 on March 23, an IR squad detonated a bomb against a motorized collaborating patrol on the Beir Kallab outpost road, destroying an M-113 and inflicting casualties in the ranks of the enemy troops.

4- Blatt outpost operation:
On March 31, IR's squad of Martyrs Mohammed Tehini and Hassan Balhas in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) attacked the occupation's outpost in Blatt with rocket propelled grenades, damaging a Merkava tank that was positioned at the outpost's entrance.

1- Al-Manar TV, 2-4-1999.
