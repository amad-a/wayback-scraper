The War We Fear to Admit With

Under this title, L'Arche, a Jewish-French magazine, reported: "Dozens of "Israeli" casualties are inflicting, in these days, in South Lebanon. Nevertheless, the "Israeli" military authorities don't issue any official statement lists their names or number".
"Perhaps, the military operations that hit the "Israeli" soldiers stationed in Lebanon are subjected to a very strict restraints by the "Israeli" military censorship. The goal is, certainly, to conceal off the public opinion the real human casualties and material damages sustained by the "Israeli" troops, or even to evade that they were inflicted for the sake of their homeland", it added.

The magazine had wondered about the substantial reasons that make the "Israeli" government not to mention the identities of "Israeli" soldiers killed in this absurd war. "So, why the security systems don't follow in the steps of Ministry of Interior which always rushes to issue official statements about traffic accidents are death of Aids disease in every time the soldiers inflicted in the battlefield?", it asked.

The magazine added: "Since the reign of late "Israeli" Premier Menahim Begin, who launched the most prominent war against Lebanon, the "Israeli" authorities had stopped revealing the real number and size of human military and civil casualties. What is going now is not but false statements which aim at ceasing the Jewish public opinion who doesn't understand how and why "Israel" insists on its soldiers to be in continuous danger in Lebanon. It would be easy to understand that the homeland's supreme interest requires offering victims and sacrifices had the staying in Lebanon helps on keeping "Israel's" security. But, unfortunately, the "Israeli" troops are not in an offensive or defensive position in a context of an imposed war. They are undergoing a guerrilla warfare and fighting high morale militants intended to expel the occupier outside of the Lebanese territories".

"Until the "Israeli" government understands the fruitlessness of staying in Lebanon, the "Israeli" citizen will continue watching the convoys of coffins bearing the bodies to the final place", it ended.
 

Al-Diyar
17-1-1998
