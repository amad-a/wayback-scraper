IR discloses more details of the Markaba operation
 

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) yesterday disclosed new details regarding the qualitative operation carried out by its Mujahedeen on Monday near the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Markaba in which three occupation sergeants were killed and four others were wounded."

"One of the operation commanders told al-Safir daily that the Mujahedeen were able to incapacitate the enemy's surveillance equipment planted around the military compound."

"The enemy's radar and infra rouge devices were disabled by using counter means, he said.
"The bomb planted by the IR men had a guided explosive wave which suits the target, especially concerning personnel armoring", he added.

"He affirmed that the bomb achieved its goals with extreme efficiency, noting that there were two resistance groups, one for execution and the other for protection."

"The Mujahedeen were positioned at a place allowing them to have clear view of what was going on, he said, adding that the power of the blow completely confused the enemy, "as reinforcement and ambulances arrived at the operation site 40 minutes after the attack for fear of other bombs."

The IR commander stated that "The resistance men withdrew calmly and swiftly after detonating the bomb, and they were able to deliver the videotape in which the operation had been filmed. The footage was distributed to Lebanese media at night."

"IR selects the sensitive spots which hurt the enemy at human, security, political, and morale levels. The Hasbayya operation followed the same pattern, and so did the Markaba attack, he told the newspaper."

"The enemy's military targets are under constant surveillance by IR around the clock, even after the ground-movement modifications made by the occupation to curb its losses, he added, stressing that IR had information regarding the time when the troops will go to the nearby training field."

"The enemy did not take precautions for two reasons; first, they did not expect the Mujahedeen to reach the threshold of a border post; second, the training field was so close to the outpost that the troops supposed that there was no need for any precautionary measures, the IR commander concluded".
