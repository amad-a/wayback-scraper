The tally of blood, the tally of votes
 
    "This morning, in his weekly interview on "Israel" Radio, after blaming the opposition for his slip at Bar-Ilan University, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu did not forget to remind us once again under whose governments the buses blew up in the streets, and who gave back to whom a sense of security. "At noontime, when four "Israelis" were killed in Lebanon, Netanyahu was busy convincing the Jordanians that only he can make peace with the Palestinians and also renew the negotiations with the Syrians. Ever since the suicide bombings of 1995, Netanyahu has been successfully enlisting fear and bereavement to his political battles. When he's in charge, it's a fact that "Israelis" aren't killed in buses.Labor Party candidate Ehud Barak MK, by contrast, has only once mentioned the terror attacks during Netanyahu's tenure: What difference does it make to the families if their loved ones were killed in a bus, in a cafe in Tel Aviv or on the Jerusalem pedestrian mall? But the experts have suggested to the Labor Party to keep its distance from the diplomatic-security debate. Therefore, Barak is keeping mum".

"Channel Two opened its Friday night news magazine with a touching report about the deathly fear felt by parents of soldiers in Lebanon. But no one from the opposition spoke on the program about pulling out of Lebanon. Until yesterday morning "Israel" Defense Forces (IDF) casualties in Lebanon were almost beyond the pale of the election campaign. But no longer - at least as far as Labor MK Yossi Beilin is concerned. Beilin has no inhibitions about chalking the blood tally up to Netanyahu, just as Netanyahu did to his predecessor in the government".

"Since the beginning of the Lebanon War (June 1982), 1,539 soldiers have been killed there. Of them, 319 were killed from the time of the withdrawal from Lebanon to the "security zone" to date (including the helicopter disaster). Finally, 162 of the casualties in Lebanon during those 14 years (that is, more than half of them), fell during the period of Netanyahu's government. Beilin accuses Netanyahu of the explosion in Lebanon: How can it be said, on the one hand, that "Israel" must not withdraw without security and normalization agreements with Syria, and then on the other, to waste three years without exchanging a single word with the Syrians?"
