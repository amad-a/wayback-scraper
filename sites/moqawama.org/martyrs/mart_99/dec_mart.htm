Martyrs

December Saturday 11-1999:

Hizbullah announces martyrdom of two IR men killed during Thursday confrontation(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announced the martyrdom of two of its men who had engaged in heroic confrontations for hours on Thursday against the Israeli occupation forces and their proxy militia at the Rob Thlathine area deep inside the occupied zone and near the international borders with occupied Palestine, an IR communiqué read.

Moussa Mahmoud Ahmed (al-Sheikh Merza), 26, was born in Meis al-Jabal, married, joined IR in 1992, participated in several military and educational training courses, besides taking part in many qualitative Jihad operations, obtained encomium from Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah. He was martyred on Thursday, December 9, during the heroic confrontations fought by IR against the occupation forces in the vicinity of Rob Thlathine.

Martyr Hassan Ali Salameh (Ammar), 23, was born in Qaaqaiyet al-Jisr, single, joined IR in 1994, participated in several military and educational training courses, besides taking part in many qualitative Jihad operations, obtained encomium from Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah. He was martyred on Thursday, December 9, during the heroic confrontations fought by IR against the occupation forces in the vicinity of Rob Thlathine.

IR had engaged in fierce clashes for hours on Thursday with the forces of the Israeli occupation and its proxy militia in the Rob Thlathine area. IR targeted patrols, motorized forces, and foot forces belonging to the occupation forces and the collaborators. The close-quarter confrontations led to definite casualties in the ranks of the enemy forces.

Following hours of silence concerning the losses, the Israeli radio acknowledged that one Israeli soldier was wounded in one of the occupation's outpost in the eastern sector.

1- Al-Manar TV, 10-12-1999.
December Thursday 23-1999:

Hizbullah recovers martyrs' bodies(2)
"Hizbullah today recovered the bodies of four martyrs who had been in the battlefield since August 1999.
"The recovery followed search that went on Wednesday morning by ICRC teams in cooperation with UNIFIL troops at the areas of confrontation with the "Israeli" enemy in Wadi al-Slouqi.

The 5-hour search in which a UNIFIL helicopter took part ended up with finding the bodies of Martyr Hussein Mahmoud Qabalan (from Meis al-Jabal), Martyr Ahmed Abdel-Hussein Foani (Houla) and Martyr Radwan Moheiddeen Dogha (Riaq, Beqaa).

"The three were martyred during the heroic confrontations against the "Israeli" occupation forces on August 17, 1999. The enemy then acknowledged the killing of two of its troops and the injury of seven others.

"More search was conducted in the afternoon by ICRC that was able to withdraw the body of a fourth Resistance man: Martyr Youssef Ahmed Baddah from Bait Leef who had been martyred during the confrontations with the occupation forces in the town of Shamaa on November 24, 1997.

"The Martyrs' coffins that were wrapped with Hizbullah's yellow flags reached the Mount Amel Hospital at 15:15. They were received by a number of Hizbullah officials, including south area official Sheikh Nabeel Qawooq, journalists and ICRC representatives.

Qawooq underlined that recovering the martyrs' bodies was the result of continuous efforts made by Hizbullah and ICRC along the past few months "away from any political development, and within a humanitarian framework".

"Recovering our martyrs enhances our hope to proceed with the course of resistance to liberate all our land, and to keep Hizbullah as the quagmire to ""Israel"", Qawooq said.

We will not spare any chance to attack the enemy that will have no room for reassurance within any framework during the negotiations, he added.

"ICRC's search will continue in the next few days to find the bodies of martyrs Hussein Jameel Hammoud (of Bait Leef) and Youssef Abbas Rihan (from Maifadoun)".

1- Al-Manar TV, 22-12-1999. 
December Friday 24-1999:

Hizbullah martyrs' funerals, another IR man remains recovered(2)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) escorted Martyrs Youssef Baddah, Ahmed Abdel-Hussein Foani, and Hussein Qabalan to their final resting place.

"The Martyrs' bodies were recovered yesterday by the International Committee for the Red Cross (ICRC) from Wadi al-Shuhada.

"A number of Hizbullah officials, MPs, Ulama and figures along with thousands of people took part in the funeral which set out from Imam Mahdi's Mosque (Ghobeiri, Beirut) to the Shahidayn Cemetery in Shiah.

"Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem delivered a speech in which he praised the sacrifices of the Martyrs "who have humiliated the Zionists."

"The Martyrs are the pride of Lebanon; they are the ones who made the country strong, Qassem said.
A party that offers such sacrifices will always be firm under all circumstances and conditions, he added.
The Zionist entity is a permanent threat to our region, for it builds its existence at the expense of other peoples, he concluded.

"In Baalback, IR's supporters escorted Martyr Radwan Mohieddeen Dougha to his final resting place in a massive procession.

"Imam Khomenei's representative in Lebanon his Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek took part in the funeral along with Ulama and residents of Baalbek and the neighboring villages.

"The procession toured Baalback's main streets as mourners chanted slogans against the "Israeli" enemy and backing the Resistance's method.

"Sheikh Yazbek stressed that the Resistance will continue until the land is liberated.

Earlier, IR had recovered the remains of Martyr Abbas Youssef Rihan following a search that was conducted by ICRC teams.

"The body that arrived at al-Najda al-Shaabia Hospital in Nabatieh at 15:30 was received by a number of Hizbullah officials and ICRC's Representative in Lebanon Henri Fournier.

"Martyr Rihan will be laid to rest at 13:30 Friday in his hometown of Maifadoun".

1- Al-Manar TV, 23-12-1999. 
December Saturday 25-1999:

Martyr Rihan laid to rest(1)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) Friday escorted Martyr Abbas Rihan to his final resting place in his hometown of Maifadoun.

"Rihan was martyred during confrontations with the "Israeli" occupation forces in the vicinity of the Beaufort Castle outpost on August 22, 1999.

"Mourners moved in cars from Sayyida Fatima's (A.S.) Complex in Sidon to the entrance of Maifadoun. Several Hundreds of people were waiting to carry the coffin in the town's streets amidst slogans insisting on the continuation of the Resistance until the occupation in removed.

Chief of Hizbullah's Political council MP Mohammed Raad, MP Abdallah Qassir, party officials, political, social, educational and religious figures took part in the funeral.

"These martyrs have proven to the Ummah that they are able no only to defeat the enemy at the military and morale level but also to inject defeat into the "Israeli" society as well, Qassir said in a speech he delivered during the funeral.

"This Resistance will continue as long as one Zionist is on our soil. Operations will go on until our people return to all the occupied villages and we recover all our detainees and prisoners, he added.

"The Martyr was then laid to rest at the town's cemetery".

1- Al-Manar TV, 24-12-1999.

December Thursday 30-1999:

Recovered martyrs' remains laid to rest(1)
"Supporters of the Islamic Resistance (IR) took part in the funeral procession of returning martyrs Moussa Mahmoud Ahmed Qarout and Hassan Ali Salame who were martyred during confrontations with the "Israeli" occupation forces in Rob Thlathine on December 9, 1999.

"Hizbullah recovered the martyrs' remains yesterday through the International Committee of the Red Cross.

"Hizbullah's Political Council Chief MP Mohammed Raad, former MPs Ali Ammar and Mohammed Birjawi took part in the funeral along with Ulama and a large crowd of people who chanted slogans condemning "Israel" and stressing the option of Jihad and Resistance against the "Israeli" occupation until full liberation is achieved.

"The body of Martyr Qarout was taken to the Shahedain Cemetery where his Eminence Sheikh Yousef Qarout led the prayer of the dead on the martyr's body before he was laid to rest.

"Martyr Salame was taken to the south in a motorcade. He was laid to rest in his hometown of Qaaqayeit al-Jisr".

1- Al-Manar TV, 29-12-1999.
