Martyrs
 
  June Tuesday 15-1999:

Martyr(2)
The Islamic Resistance announced, proudly, the martyrdom of the martyr Imad Hussein Haidar Ahmad, born at Ras Osta-Jbeil in 1975, single, subjected to a number of military and cultural courses, participated with several military operations. He was martyred on 12-6-1999 in the course of the heroic operation carried out at Mount Abou Rkab.

1- IR statement, 14-6-1999. 
  June Thursday 17-1999:

IR Martyr escorted to resting place(1)
"Martyr Imad Hussein Haidar of the Islamic Resistance (IR) was escorted to his final resting place at his hometown Ras Ousta in the Jbeil district in Kissirwan".

"Faithfulness to the Resistance Bloc member MP Ammar al-Musawi, MP Mahmoud Awwad, MP Nouhad Souaid and a crowd of religious figures took part in the massed funeral procession.

"The crowds chanted slogans in support of Lebanon and the Resistance and lifted banners reading "death to the US and "Israel"".

"MP al-Musawi delivered a speech on behalf of Hizbullah, stressing on continuing the Jihad and Resistance until full liberation.

"Martyr Haidar Ahmed is single, born in 1975 at Ras Ousta, studied religious science, joined the Islamic Resistance in 1993.

"He participated in many Jihad operations and was injured last week in IR's confrontations with the "Israeli" occupation forces in Mount Abu Rkab in western Beqaa and martyred this week".

1- Al-Manar TV, 16-6-1999.
