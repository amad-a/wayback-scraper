November Saturday 06-1999:

Martyrs(1)
The IR announced, proudly, the martyrdom of Hussein Ali Mazloum and Ali Najib Medlej as they were confronting the Israeli enemy on Tuesday October 2,1999.

Hussein Ali Mazloum, born at Brital in 1971, married with four children, joined the IR in 1988, shared with several qualitative military operations and subjected to a number of military an cultural courses. He had gained several tributes from Hizbullahs Secretary General.

Ali Najib Medlej, born at Baalback in 1978, single, joined the IR in 1997 and participated with a number of military and cultural courses. He had obtained several tributes from Hizbullahs Secretary General.

1- IR statement, 5-11-1999. 
November Sunday 14-1999:

Martyr's Day celebrations continue
Hizbullah SG: Lebanon will decide what to do after the pullout(1)
Hizbullah and the Islamic Resistance organized more rallies to mark Martyr's Day in various Lebanese areas. One rally was held in Martyr Ahmed Qassir's hometown Deir Qanoun al-Nahr.

Qassir was the martyrdom freedom fighter who blew up the Israeli occupation's military governor HQ in Tyre in November 1983.

Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah reiterated the invariable positions which he had announced at the central Martyr's Day rally in Bir al-Abed on Thursday.

At a rally to commemorate Martyrs Hussein Mazloum and Ali Medlej in Baalbak, Sayyed Nasrallah underlined that the Israeli enemy has reached the stage at which it has opted to pullout as a result of the resistance and the steadfastness.

"What we must obtain is an unconditional withdrawal without any implicit or explicit conditions," he said.

Lebanon is the one that will decide what to do after the (Israeli) pullout. Whether to deploy its army or Internal Security Forces in the occupied zone; just as it did following the withdrawal from Jezzine, he added.

The secretary General stressed that "Lebanon is the one that decides what is within its national interest.

Maintaining Lebanon's security is an internal affair and part of the country's sovereignty."

No body must impose anything on Lebanon in this regard, he concluded.

IR announces martyrdom of 3 of its men(2)
The Islamic Resistance proudly announced the martyrdom of three of its courageous men who were martyred during the heroic confrontations that IR men fought against the Israeli occupation forces along the front lines on Shaabaan 4, 1420 A.H. - November 12, 1999.

The martyrs are:
Martyr Ali Zoheir Zahri (Abdel - Rasoul), 28, born in Nabatieh, married, joined IR in 1987, participated in several military and educational courses, took part in several qualitative jihad operations, got several encomiums from Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

Martyr Ahmed Fayyad Sbeiti (Jihad), 21, born in Kfar Seir, single, joined IR in 1987, participated in several military and educational courses, took part in several qualitative jihad operations, got several encomiums from Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

Martyr Saed Fawzi Faran (Hamzeh), 22, born in Nabatieh, single, one of the Military Information Department members, joined IR in 1996, participated in several military, artistic and educational courses, took part in several qualitative jihad operations.

1- Al-Manar TV, 13-11-1999.
2- Same source.
November Thursday 18-1999:

IR announces martyrdom of two of its men(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announced the martyrdom of Jihad Ali Hijazi (Sajed) and Ali Salman al-Khatib (al-Horr al-Amili) who died on Shaaban 8, 1420 A.H. - November 16, 1999, of wounds sustained during the recent confrontations fought by IR against the Israeli occupation forces.

Martyr Hijazi, 26, born in Houla, married, joined IR in 1992, participated in educational and military training courses, took part in qualitative Jihad operations, obtained encomiums from Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

Martyr al-Khatib, 22, was born in Deir Sirian, single, joined IR in 1993, participated in educational and military training courses, took part in qualitative Jihad operations, obtained encomiums from Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

The two martyrs were escorted to their final resting place at 15:00 Wednesday. The funeral procession set out from Imam Mahdi's Mosque in Ghobeiry and ended in the Rawdat al-Shahedein cemetery where the two martyrs were laid to rest.

1- Al-Manar TV, 17-11-1999.
