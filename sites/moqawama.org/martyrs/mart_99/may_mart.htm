Martyrs
 
  May Thursday 27-1999:
 
 Two IR Mujahedeen martyred during confrontations with enemy(1)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) announced today (yesterday) the martyrdom of two Mujahedeen who have joined the glorious Martyrs' march."

"Hussein Hassan Hayek (Ragheb) and Ahmed Ali Rida (Baqer) were martyred Tuesday night while defending the freedom and dignity of the Lebanese sacrificing steadfast people, an IR statement read.

Martyr Hayek was born in Adsheit in 1973, single, joined IR in 1996, underwent educational and military training courses, participated in several Jihad operations. He was martyred while performing his Jihad duty.

"Martyr Hayek was escorted to his final resting place today (yesterday) in a motorized convoy from the al-Zahraa compound (A.S.) in Sidon to the graveyard in his hometown of Adsheit where he was laid to rest.

"Martyr Rida was born in Teffahta in 1973, single, joined IR in 1991, underwent educational and military training courses, participated in several Jihad operations. He was martyred during the heroic confrontations with the enemy last night.

"Martyr Rida was escorted to his final resting place at the same time with his fellow Martyr Hayek from the same place in Sidon to his hometown's graveyard in Teffahta.

"As the convoy entered Teffahta, thousands of residents joined the funeral procession, forming a huge march that roamed the town's streets chanting slogans of denunciation against the US and ""Israel"". "Martyr Rida was laid to rest after prayer for his soul was performed.

"During the ceremony, Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc member MP Abdallah Qassir praised the two martyrs' heroism and bravery."

"Any withdrawal, whether from Jezzine or elsewhere, is the result of Resistance, the Mujahedeen and the Martyrs' blood, MP Qassir said, adding "We will not depend on international resolutions nor on a settlement which has already fallen, but on the Resistance which will always be our only choice."

"MP Qassir stated that IR's operations staged during the last few months, resulted in the ailing status of the collaborating militia".

"In Adsheit, residents joined the convoy of Martyr Hayek. The massive funeral that crossed the town's streets ended at the town's graveyard.

"During the ceremony Hizbullah's Central Council member Sheikh Khodor Noureddine delivered a speech stressing on armed Resistance as the only option to liberate the land from "Israeli" occupation".

1- Al-Manar TV, 26-5-1999. 
  May Monday 31-1999:

Martyrs

The IR announced, proudly, the martyrdom of Hussein Ahmad Trabolssi, born at Teffahta in 1973, single, he joined the IR in 1992, subjected to several military and cultural courses and participated with a number of qualitative military operations. He was martyred on 30-5-1999 in the course of performing his sacred Islamic duties.
