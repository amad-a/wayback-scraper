Martyrs
 
  February Wednesday 17-1999:

Beqaa marked memory of Sayyed Abbas Musawi (1)
"Beqaa and the town of Nabi Sheet today (yesterday) marked the anniversary of Sayyed Abbas al-Musawi's martyrdom.

"Former secretary general of Hezbollah, Sayyed al-Musawi, was martyred, along with his wife and toddler son Hussein, by an "Israeli" raid.

"Hezbollah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah attended the rally along with a large number of politicians and figures besides tens of thousands of people.

"Partial solutions and humiliating settlements will go to hell along with those who make them, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding that "I don't say this out of enthusiasm, but out of certainty".

We are confronting an enemy (the "Israelis") that can be defeated and humiliated. They can't sacrifice a lot for an illusion called "a national homeland for the Jews", he said .

"His Eminence called for creating a real resistance wherever there is occupation.

I am sure that these "Israelis" will rush into planes to leave (occupied Palestine), because they are seeking the life in this world, while we demand our right, he said.

"What we need in order to achieve victory are those people who are ready to be martyred, a little time, and a lot of faith, his Eminence concluded".

Children march demanding a UN condemnation of "Israeli" crimes (2)
"Hundreds of children today (yesterday) staged a silent march in Beirut on the martyrdom anniversary of toddler Hussein Abbas al-Musawi who was martyred along with his father, former Hezbollah Secretary General Sayyed Abbas al-Musawi, and his mother in an "Israeli" raid.

"The children marched to the UN HQ in Beir Hassan. They handed officials there a memorandum to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan demanding condemnation of the "Israeli" enemy's crimes against children and working on securing "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon."

"Head of Hezbollah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad said this annual gathering of Lebanese children is a live witness to the "Israeli" crimes and massacres against our children in the southern villages while the world turns a deaf ear to these aggressions".

"What has the world given to our childhood? Is it the Mother's Day gift of the Nabatieh bus massacre?", "How has the world protected out rights? Has it protected them with the missiles which blew up the Mansouri ambulance?", "Is it international law for our rights to be ravished?" read some of the signs which the children carried during the march".

March Sunday 28-1999:

Thousands take part in IR Martyrs' funeral processions in Jmaijme and Beirut(1)
"Thousands of Islamic Resistance (IR) supporters escorted Martyr Hassan Mehideen Hamza to his final resting place in his southern village, Jmaijme".

"The huge procession was an occasion for participants to affirm that resistance is the only choice to free the occupied land.

"The funeral procession set out at 9.30 with a large convoy of cars.

"All along the way to Jmaijme, villagers came out of their houses to pay their last respects to the Martyr who had heroically confronted the enemy air and ground war machinery along with his fellow IR Mujahedeen.

"As the convoy entered Jmaijme, villagers rushed out to carry the Martyr's coffin, chanting "Allah is great" and voicing their support to the Resistance and its Jihad path.

Former MP Mohammed Berjawi and Hezbollah Educational Official in the South Sheikh Hassan Medlej took part in the funeral procession.

Sheikh Medlej talked about IR's great resolve and strike force in confronting the "Israeli" enemy.
"The enemy warplanes and shells will only make us more determined to confront the occupation.

They will never be able to terrorize us, Sheikh Medlej stressed.

Jmaijme's Imam Sheikh Ramez Hamza led the funeral prayer, before the martyr was laid to
rest.

"In Beirut, thousands of IR supporters escorted Martyrs Ihab Haidar Medlej and Rabih Ibrahim
Marzouk to their final resting place in Raodat al-Shahedein cemetery".

"The procession began at Imam al-Rida Mosque in Beir al-Abed and swept the streets of
Beirut's southern suburbs.

"Hezbollah Deputy Secretary General Sheikh Naem Qassem commended the Mujahedeen who
were martyred inside the occupied zone.

"The Mujahedeen's heroic acts and sacrifices scared the enemy that deployed great numbers of
air and ground forces but could not find the courage to confront them", Sheikh Qassem said.

He also stressed that IR has turned into a great revival act for the whole nation and Ummah.
Sheikh Qassem said, "the storming of Arnoun is a victory for the Resistance and its people and a good lesson for "Israel" and the United States."

Martyrs(2)
The IR announced, proudly, the death of three martyrs. The martyrs are:

"Martyr Hassan Moheiddeen Hamza, 24, of al-Jmaijme, single, joined IR in 1993, took part in several educational and military courses, participated in many qualitative Jihad operations. He was martyred two days ago during the heroic Taibe confrontations which IR fought against the "Israeli" occupation forces."

"Martyr Ihab Haidar Medlej, 23, of Timneen al-Tahta, single, joined IR in 1994, took part in several educational and military courses, participated in many qualitative Jihad operations. He was martyred two days ago during the heroic Taibe confrontations which IR fought against the "Israeli" occupation forces".

"Martyr Rabieh Ibrahim Marzouq, 19, of Beirut, single, joined IR in 1994, took part in several
educational and military courses, participated in many qualitative Jihad operations. He was martyred two days ago during the heroic Taibe confrontations which IR fought against the "Israeli" occupation forces".
