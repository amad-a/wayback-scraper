Martyrs
 
April Wednesday 14-1999:

IR escorts Martyr Faqih to final resting place(1)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) in the south escorted Martyr Haitham Mohammed Faqih to his final resting place in a car convoy that set out from the Mount Amel Hospital (Tyre)."

"Martyr Faqih was killed yesterday while performing his Jihad duty".

"A scout ceremony was held at the Majadel village for Martyr Faqih, a scout leader himself.

When the funeral reached the martyr's hometown, Kfardouneen, Hizbullah Political Council Member Sheikh Khodor Nooredeen delivered a speech."

"IR's operation yesterday near the Beaufort outpost in which an "Israeli" soldier was killed confirms IR's success against the occupation and its determination to confront the enemy, he told mourners."

1- Al-Manar TV, 13-4-1999.
