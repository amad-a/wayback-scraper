Martyrs
 
  July Tuesday 6-1999:

Hizbullah escorted Martyr Tfaili(1)
"Hizbullah today (Monday) escorted Martyr Abbass Mohammed Tfaili to his final resting place in a crowded convoy, attended by party officials, his family and a huge crowd of Baalback residents".

"Martyr Tfaili was hit during the "Israeli" air raids against the town of Baalback ten days ago.

"He was born in Baalback in 1970, joined Hizbullah's general recruitment in 1988, underwent several military and educational courses, participated in many confrontations against the "Israeli" occupation and was wounded in one of them in 1995".

1- Al-Manar TV, 5-7-1999. 
  July Sunday 11-1999:

Resistance Martyrs equal to army Martyrs(1)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss has informed the chairman of "Faithfulness to the Resistance Bloc" MP Sayyed Ibrahim Amine al-Sayyed the decision of equaling the sons of the Resistance Martyrs with the sons of the Lebanese army Martyrs".

"The decision says the benefits and privileges of the army Martyrs' sons will be from now on enjoyed by the Resistance Martyrs' sons, including hospitalization, education and a monthly income for the families of Martyrs who served for more than three years".

"Moreover the wounded of the Resistance were also equaled with the injured soldiers of the army".

1- Al-Manar TV, 10-7-1999.
