Martyrs
 
  June Thursday 25-1998:

Martyr Hadi Nassrallahs mother celebrates
For the second time respectively, the strengthness itself of Hadis mother (Om Hadi) surprises her visitors! What she had been fortified with on the day of martyrdom of her eldest son Hadi Nassrallah, nine months ago, escorted her few hours before receiving his body marked by the honor of martyrdom.

The steadfastness you seek to be prepared for, on your way to meet with Om Hadi, to hold your feelings as you console a mother bereaved of a child, transfers to moments of relaxation just when you enter the house.

Surmounted by two photographs for the martyr, Om Hadi asserted that the hostages had taken her first interest, while the spirits of corpses had lifted to God. At times, she was looking stealthily at Manar TV to know about the arriving of martyrs corpses at Beirut Airport.

The questions raised by As-Safir either made her smiles or nodes, in a gesture that the question is obvious. When asked about the place of console, she rushed to correct and substitute the expression by congratulation.

Today, we will celebrate with their coming back at Husseinieh Lady Zeinab (peace be upon her). It is really a celebration!

Q: Are you ready for making the last sight on the martyr Hadi?
A: (She smiles) As you see. I am very happy for every mother will be pleased as her son returns At any way, and to be accurate, it willnt be the last sight for he is inside me and his spirit is fluttering among us But, if I said that I dont miss him, I will be liar, especially he was dynamic, and sometimes, he was helping me in some home works. But however, our feeling with the honor and dignity fills this gap.

Q: Do you expect that any change in his complexions had happened?
A: I dont think so for he was put, along with another martyr, in a refrigerator at Kiryat Shimona autopsy room. This makes the body as it was before. I had seen him on the battlefield on the day of his martyrdom. Today, I am not afraid or under an illusion. On the contrary, I am longing for seeing him.

Q: What do you see about the form of exchanging the hostages with the remains?
A: This is Sayeds (means H.E. Sayed Hassan Nassrallah) own business But I think that revoking Israels offer over exchanging martyrs with remains only and amending it to be exchanging hostages with remains is a positive point.

Q: Did you expect that it will take this period to receive Hadis corpse.
A: If we had accepted Israels initial offer, the problem would had been ended for long time. But our insistence on the hostages and other martyrs corpses made the Israelis changing their mind.
For the Jews, Hadi is a son of Hizbullahs Secretary General, but for us he is as any other martyr.

Q: Do you expect that you will be calm and self-possessed during the last meeting?
A: God willing He who was placid on the day of martyrdom, naturally, will be the same on the day of meeting. Today, I will embrace and press him to my bosom and whisper in his ear to mediate me for the family of Prophet Mohammad (Gods blessing and peace be upon them) and I will recite the Fatha openning, verses of the glorious Koran.

Q: What about his brothers: Jawad, Ali and Zeinab?
A: They are happy also Jawad had compiled and meldized him an anthem.
From the beginning, he followed the same track. Upon Hadis martyrdom, his interest in the Islamic Struggle Jihad had mounted. Certainly, when a person martyrizes, the family doesnt sink in despair, but on the contrary, its persistence increases so as to complete the track of Jihad.
