Martyrs
 
  September Thursday 3 -1998:

City Names one of its Autostrads after Martyr Hadi Nassrallah (1)
"The municipality of Beirut today decided to name the Autostrad between Shatila and Kafaat after Martyr Sayyed Hadi Nassrallah".

"Nassrallah is the son of Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah. He was martyred in September 1997 in the Jabal al-Rafih area during confrontations with Israeli occupation forces."

"Hizbullah secretary general today received Mayor of Beirut Mr. Abdle-Monem al-Arees who headed a delegation of the town's municipal council."

"The delegation discussed with his Eminence the proposals to develop the capital.  Sayyed Nassrallah expressed readiness to cooperate with all efforts that are in the public interest.  He thanked the City for the naming of the Autostrad "a step of a representative meaning which reaches all the resistance martyrs who are sacrificing their blood and their lives for the sake of this homeland and its just causes."

1- Al-Manar TV, 3-9-1998.
  September Friday 25 -1998:

ACSIR Celebrates Martyr Hadi Nasrallah's Anniversary in Cairo (1)
"Cairo-based Arab Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance in Lebanon (ACSIR) held a symposium last night to commemorate the anniversary of Martyr Sayed Hadi Hassan Nasrallah and to honor Egyptian Martyr Mahmoud Nour Eddeen."

"It is one year after the martyrdom of the young symbol freedom fighter Hadi Nasrallah, whose martyrdom while fighting the Zionists was a turning point in Hizbullah's history, for the martyr was a symbol of the ability of the party's leadership, his Eminence Sayed Hassan Nasrallah in particular, to resist and sacrifice his son", ACSIR said.

1- Al-Manar TV, 25-9-1998.
