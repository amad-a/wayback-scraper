  March Saturday 04-2000:


Resistance's Doctor honored(1)

"The Association of the AUB Graduates - the South Branch commemorated the late doctor Jaafar Ramal, one of the physicians of the Islamic Resistance.

The commemoration was attended by Health Minister Karam Karam, president of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad, and the family and colleagues of the deceased.

Karam delivered a speech in which he commended the man praising the medical services he rendered to civilians and Resistance men.

The Islamic Resistance will pursue the message of Dr. Ramal who had combined in his life the humanitarian services at the American University's Hospital with commitment to the country's causes, as he served as a physician at the front lines with the Islamic Resistance men, MP Raad said.

"The Hizbullah deputy talked about the miserable status of the occupation's army at this stage".

1-        Al-Manar TV, 3-3-2000.
