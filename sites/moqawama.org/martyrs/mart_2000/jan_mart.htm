Martyrs
 
  January Sunday 02-2000:

 Hizbullah Secretary General announces martyr's name(1)
Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah announced that the martyr who carried out Thursday's martyrdom attack against the "Israeli" occupation forces was Ammar Hussein Hammoud.

Ammar is the brother of Martyr Mohammed Hammoud who was martyred a year ago and his body is still held by the occupation forces, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"Ammar was born in the occupied town of Dibbeen (Marjeyoun district) in 1979, he joined IR in 1995, was member of the martyrdom brigades since these brigades were established. He was martyred while fasting on al-Qadr Night in 1420 A.H. - January 30, 1999.

I would like to say something about this martyr's family which I had talked about at an earlier rally at the Secretariat Square when we received the half-track which was captured by IR men from the occupation forces' outpost in Bait Yahoun.

"I told you then that I was visited by the father of a martyr who had six sons, one of them was martyred.

"The father, who came along with his wife and sons, told me, 'Martyr Mohammed has gone to his Lord; we are satisfied and proud about that. But we are here, my wife, my sons, and myself, to tell you that my five sons are to be offered as sacrifices to this course. They are at your service. If all the children are martyred, I am ready to be martyred and so is my wife.'

"Only a few months after this meeting, one of the five came to be the Martyr of al-Qadr Night.

"As for martyr Ammar, when I bid him farewell he told me, "I heard that if a family offers two martyrs, the rest of the sons are not allowed to take part in Resistance operations. I want my brothers excluded from this decision and allowed to go along the path of Jihad and Martyrdom, for I don't want to shut this great door in the faces of my four brothers whom I love and want to be resistance men and martyrs."

"As for the father, when he received the news he said, 'I have fulfilled my pledge and I keep the vow I made along with my four sons.'

Sayyed Nasrallah commented, "these are our martyrs and these are their families."

1- Al-Manar TV, 1-1-2000.

  January Thursday 20-2000:

Former prisoner dies of disease sustained during imprisonment in occupation prisons(1)
"Hizbullah proudly announced the death of freed prisoner Martyr Mahdi Abdel-Hassan Ibrahim who died of disease sustained during his imprisonment in the "Israeli" notorious al-Khiam detention center as a result of the physical and psychological torture he was subjected to at the hands of the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators.

"Ibrahim was born in 1968, married, joined IR in 1987, worked in the Islamic Health Society's Civil Defense Department.

"He was detained by the "Israeli" occupation forces on February 10, 1988 from his house in occupied al-Khiam and released on March 30, 1993 due to the deterioration of his condition as a result of the brutal torture he was subjected to and that had him sustain chronic diseases of which he died in Italy where he was being treated on Wednesday January 18, 2000.

"As we draw the world's attention to the suffering and brutality that our brothers are being subjected to in the "Israeli" and Lahdi detention centers in Lebanon and occupied Palestine, we call on the human rights' groups to exert the maximum pressures on the Zionist enemy to halt its arbitrary measures against the detainees that must be released immediately," a Hizbullah communiqué read.

1- Al-Manar TV, 19-1-2000.

  January Thursday 27-2000:

Martyr (1)

The IR announced proudly the martyrdom of Mahmoud Anani, born at Kounine in 1978, single, joined the IR ranks in 1996, subjected to a number of military and cultural courses and shared with several military attacks. He was martyred on 26-1-2000 during the heroic confrontations against the "Israeli" occupation forces and their agents at the areas of Shamaa and Hardoun.

1- IR statement, 26-1-2000.

  January Friday 28-2000:

Hizbullah escorts martyr Harb to final resting place(1)
"Supporters of the Islamic Resistance Wednesday took part in Martyr Taleb Hussein Harb's funeral which started in a motorcade that set out from the Hammoud Hospital in Sidon to the martyr's hometown of Jibsheit.

"Thousands of people filled the town's streets in spite of the heavy rain. MP Hajj Abdallah Qassir, Ulama, and supporters took part in the funeral procession. Mourners carried the martyr's coffin while chanting slogans supporting the Resistance and condemning the Zionist occupation. They also carried Hizbullah and IR flags.

"When the procession reached the town's Husseiniet, Sheikh Ismail Harb led the prayer of the dead. Then, MP Qassir delivered a speech in which he stressed that the resistance will continue until the occupation is removed.

"The recent qualitative operations prove this fact, he said, adding, "the continuation of IR's operations is not related whatsoever to the pattern of the ongoing (Syrian-"Israeli") negotiations which we see stumbling and staggering, for this enemy has never been used to offer concessions through negotiations."

"This enemy understands nothing but the force logic; therefore, the Resistance men's Jihad and the Ummah's embrace of these men is the only path and bet that can impose an unconditional withdrawal on the enemy, the Hizbullah MP stressed.

"The martyr was then laid to rest in the Jibsheit Cemetery which already houses 71 martyrs including the Martyrs' Sheikh, Ragheb Harb".

1- Al-Manar TV, 27-1-2000.

  January Saturday 29-2000:

IR escorts Martyr Anani to final resting place(1)
"Supporters of the Islamic Resistance Friday took part in Martyr Wael Anani's funeral procession which set out from the Husseiniet al-Zahraa (A. S.) in Beirut.

"The procession toured the capital's streets with mourners carrying the martyr's coffin and chanting slogans supporting the Resistance and condemning the Zionist occupation.

"Hizbullah's Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc member MP Nazih Mansour took part in the procession along with former MPs Hajj Ali Ammar and Hajj Mohammed Berjawi, who is also Hizbullah's Social Affairs Official.

"A motorcade then set out to the Shahidain Cemetery where the martyr was laid to rest".

1- Al-Manar TV, 28-1-2000.

  January Monday 31-2000:

Martyrs(1)
The IR announced proudly the martyrdom of two of its heroic mujahedeen. The martyr Mohammad Hussein Jaber, born in 1978, Kfar Tibnite, single, joined the IR ranks in 1996, subjected to several military and cultural courses and shared with a number of qualitative attacks. He was martyred on 28-1-2000 during the confrontations against the "Israeli" enemy forces deep inside the occupied zone.

The martyr Farouq Mustafa Ismail, born in 1977 at Deir Qanoun-Ras El-Ain, married, joined the IR ranks in 1996, underwent a number of military and cultural courses and participated in several qualitative military operations. He was martyred on 28-1-2000 in the course of fighting the "Israeli" enemy deep inside the occupied area.

1- IR statement, 30-1-2000.
