Martyrs
 
  April Friday 21-2000:

IR announces martyrdom of two of its men(1)

"With all pride, in the holy month of Moharram, the Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announces the martyrdom of two of its men who were martyred during the heroic confrontation fought by IR in the evening of Wednesday, April 19, 2000 against the Zionist enemy in the vicinity of the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Taibeh," an IR communiqué read.

"Martyr Haidar Qassem al-Taweel (Abu Ragheb), 25, married with two children, member of the Martyrdom Brigades, took part in several military and educational courses besides several qualitative operations, has encomium from Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, was martyred during IR's heroic confrontations against the "Israeli" occupation forces in the vicinity of the Taibeh outpost."

Martyr Fadi Mohammed Ghazal (Qassem), 23, single, member of the Martyrdom Brigades, took part in several military and educational courses besides several military operations, was martyred during IR's heroic confrontations against the "Israeli" occupation forces in the vicinity of the Taibeh outpost.

1- Al-Manar TV, 20-4-2000.

  April Friday 28-2000:

Another IR man joins martyrs' convoy(1)

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announced to the Islamic Ummah and the Lebanese people the martyrdom of one of its men.

"Martyr Hassanto Rizq (Hassan al-Sheikh), 24, born in Mashghara (Western Beqaa), married, joined IR in 1993, participated in several military and educational courses, took part in several operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, had encomium from Hizbullah's Secretary General, was martyred "Thursday morning of wounds sustained while intercepting the enemy's warplanes in the Western Beqaa Hilltops".

1-          Manar TV, 27-4-2000.

  April Saturday 29-2000:

IR announces martyrdom of one of its men(1)

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announces to its honorable people the martyrdom of Fouad Abbas Shehab (Abu Ali) who was martyred during the heroic confrontations fought by IR against the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators on Friday, April 28, 2000, an IR communiqué read.

"Martyr Shehab, 32, married, joined IR in 1989, participated in several military training courses and qualitative jihad operations including storming the outposts of Soweida and Ilman al-Shoumarieh," the communiqué added .

"He was wounded several times during confrontations with the Zionist enemy and its collaborators, received encomium from Hizbullah's secretary general, was martyred during the confrontations fought by IR against the outposts of the Zionist enemy in al-Ahmadieh and Ain Qinya in the Western Beqaa".

1-        Manar TV, 28-4-2000.
