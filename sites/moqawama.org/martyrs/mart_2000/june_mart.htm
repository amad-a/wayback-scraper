Martyrs
 
  June Friday 02-2000:

Resistance fighters remains returned(1)

The Lebanese Committee for the Red Cross handed over to Hizbullah the remains of two fighters, exhumed from a cemetery in Hasbaya, on Thursday.
A Hizbullah statement identified the men as Hassan Abbas and Mohammed Musa, killed in Zillaya, in the Western Beqaa, in 1985.
Druze clerics said that the cemetery still contained the remains of four fighters, including Wafaa Noureddine, Maryam Kheireddine and Assaf Farah. The fourth was an unidentified fighter with the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine.
Hizbullah said it had also reclaimed the remains of Hussein Jamil Hammoud, killed in Shamaa in 1997.
In Jerusalem, the "Israeli" military said it was carrying out DNA tests to identify the bodies of 16 Hizbullah fighters who had been killed by "Israeli" troops and buried in "Israel".

1-         The Daily Star, 2-6-2000.
