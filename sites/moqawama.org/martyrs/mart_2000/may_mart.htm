Martyrs
 
  May Saturday 06-2000:

IR martyrs laid to rest (1)

Hezbollah on Friday escorted two of its martyrs to their final resting place.  The Islamic Resistance had issued a statement announcing the martyrdom of Jalal Hassan Rammal and Hussein Mahdi Mohammed Ali who were killed during the clashes with the Israeli occupation forces in the evening of Wednesday May 3, 2000.

The enemy did not dare to confront the two Resistance men although they were in the territory it occupies.  Therefore, the occupation used its helicopters and warplanes to stage raids against them, the IR communiqué read.

 

Martyr Ali (Karbala), 32, born in Bidnayel (Beqaa), married with two children, joined IR in 1988, took part in several high military courses, progressed in several Jihad responsibilities, participated in scores of surveillance and qualitative operations against the occupation forces, was wounded several times, obtained encomium from Hezbollah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

 

Martyr Rammal, 35, born in Dwair (South), married with two children, joined IR when it first set out, took part in several high military courses, participated in scores of Jihad operations against the occupation forces, was wounded several times.

  May Friday 26-2000:

IR announces martyrdom of two of its men who fell during liberation days(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) and Hizbullah on Resistance and Liberation Day presented two martyrs who died of wounds sustained during the process of liberating the zone which was occupied by the "Israeli" forces.

Martyr Ibrahim Ali al-Zein was martyred of wounds sustained on Tuesday, May 23, when the enemy aircraft targeted his car, among other civilian cars, in the vicinity of the Blat outpost.

Martyr al-Zein, 30, was married and had four children.

IR also announced that one of its Ulama, Sheikh Abu Zar, was martyred on Liberation day.

Sheikh Ahmed Hamad Yahia (Abu Zar) was martyred on Thursday, May 25, of wounds sustained during the clashes between the residents, on the one hand, and the occupation and its collaborators, on the other, on Monday in the outskirts of his hometown of Rshaf.

Martyr Sheikh Abu Zar, 42, married with 4 children, joined IR in 1985, received religious courses in Iran, took part in several high military training courses, participated in many qualitative operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, obtained encomium from Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.
