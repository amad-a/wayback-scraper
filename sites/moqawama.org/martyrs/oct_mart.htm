Martyrs
 
  October Sunday 25 -1998:

MP Qassir: Bill of embracing martyrs' families is a remarkable legislative precedent (1)
"Parliament's Administration and Justice Committee is to proceed on Monday with studying the bill for sponsoring the families of those martyred, wounded or detained while fighting the "Israeli" occupation.

The committee has started a detailed examination of the bill which was drafted by Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc member MP Nazih Mansour."

"There are several defects in the state's presentations to the martyrs, detainees and the wounded of the Resistance," FTR MP Hajj Abdullah Qassir said.

"By drafting the bill, FTR wanted the parliament which represents the nation to endorse a bill acknowledging the deeds of those who are martyred, wounded, or captured while resisting the "Israeli" occupation", Qassir added.

"He stressed that the law itself is a legislative precedent which boosts the resistance's cause.
This is the first time in Lebanon's legislative history the resistance is recognized with such clarity and transparency", the Hizbullah MP said.

"The law states that "a Lebanese civilian who is martyred while fighting the "Israeli" occupation militarily to liberate the land from the occupation will be regarded as a martyr".

1- Al-Manar TV, 24-10-1998.
