Martyrs
 
  November Tuesday 17 -1998:

Martyr (1)
The IR announced before Imam Mahdi (peace be upon him) and with proud the death of Afif Ghamloush born at Jibaa in 1971 and married with three children. He had joined Hizbullah in 1986 and IR ranks in 1990, subjected to a number of military and cultural courses and took part a lot of military qualitative operations. Ghamloush was martyred on 15-11-1998 during performing his sacred Islamic duties at the IR front lines.

1- IR statement, 16-11-1998.
