Land of Immunity for Criminals
 
 
By Munif Al-Haj
Monday 5 Dec. 1998
 

According to a report dated November 1997, by Stephanie Nebehay of Reuters, a judge in Geneva issued international arrest warrants for seven Israelis, several of them former army officers, in connection with an alleged plot to kidnap 12-year-old Athina Roussel, sole surviving heiress to the Greek shipping tycoon Aristotle Onasis. At the age of 18 Athina will inherit a some estimated at $3bn, according to legal sources.

Judge Jacques Deleiutraz called the suspects agents who had done the dirty work of preparing the abduction plan by photographing the 12-year-old and her security entourage in Switzerland.

The Judge issued the warrants after conducting questioning in September and October, he confirmed reports that an Israeli named Eyal Ehrlich had been questioned. Eyal allegedly sold information to Athinas father Roussel on kidnapping plans by an Israeli ex-general, and an Israeli ex-colonel and others.

These Israelis from Eyal to the ex-general, the ex-colonel and others, how were their lives lived before this incident had occurred? Where were they trained ? In what sort of an establishment had they gone through to come out such low lives? What sort of training did they receive?

If you buy a product, and find out that the good is rotten, you immediately look at the label of that product to find out from which country or factory it was manufactured in.
Eyal the Israeli, the Israeli ex-general, the Israeli ex-colonel and their other Israeli colleagues are products of the Israeli military establishment that has no history in any humane act what so ever, history has never recorded any act by this establishment that was for the good of the human race, other than inhumane acts such as massacres, torture etc. etc. Look up all the newspapers, all the media, where ever you find the words massacres, killing, kidnapping, assassination, torture etc. you will find the Israeli military establishment next to it.

Eyal the Israeli had obviously betrayed his army colleagues by selling the kidnapping plans. A disagreement must have occurred, if that establishment had taught them anything it should have at least taught them not to betray one another. What can society expect from one who betrays for money?

We wish for the world to know the truth about these Israelis, and I say all Israelis because every single Israeli has gone through that establishment and is let loose in society free to do as they please, and Athina Roussel is a perfect example of what they are pleased in doing.
