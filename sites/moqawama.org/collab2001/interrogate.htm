Military prosecutors interrogate detained journalists accused of collaborating with Israel

 

Investigations into alleged links between two journalists and Israeli officials have resumed. Military prosecutors have been interrogating Habib Younis and Antoine Bassil on charges of contacting Israeli officials and hurting Lebanese-Syrian relations. MP Boutros Harb, whose law offices are defending Younis said he will demand the release of the al-Hayat newspaper journalist on bail. His offices had made a similar appeal on behalf of Toufic Hindi, the advisor to the dissolved Lebanese Forces militia. He remains in custody.
