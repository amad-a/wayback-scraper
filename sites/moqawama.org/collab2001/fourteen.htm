Fourteen Lebanese sentenced for collaborating with Israel

 

 Beirut's military court sentenced 14 more Lebanese for collaborating with the "Israeli enemy", court sources said Tuesday.

Thirteen people received sentences ranging from one to 18 months in jail for collaboration, entering "enemy territory", and serving with the now-disbanded Israeli-allied South Lebanon Army militia, they said.

A 14th SLA member was sentenced in absentia to seven years in jail for collaboration and serving with the militia.

The court also sentenced to seven and a half years in jail two men who were part of a group of Lebanese prisoners released by Syria in December, for collaboration with Israel.

Raja Salman Kabalan and Elias Lutfallah Tanios, had served prison terms of respectively ten and eight and a half years for the same reasons.

On Saturday, the court sentenced 21 Lebanese for collaboration with Israel, including 11 former SLA members to death in absentia for having tortured Lebanese resistants in the notorious Khiam prison in the former Israeli-occupied border zone in southern Lebanon.

The trials against those accused of collaborating with Israel started 10 days after the May 24 pullout of Israeli troops from Southern Lebanon. Since then, Beirut's military court has tried 2,478 suspected collaborators.

To this day, 30 have been sentenced to death, all of them in absentia.

AFP, 10-4-2001.
