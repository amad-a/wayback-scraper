Hizbullah Warns from “Israeli” Meddling in Internal Lebanese Affairs

 

Commenting on the late developments on the internal level, Hizbullah issued a communiqué in which it expressed concern about the information talking about the return of “Israeli” meddling in Lebanese internal affairs, "manifested through schemes and plans to spread tense atmosphere which would disturb the security situation and maintain necessary conditions for the “Israelis” to manipulate the fate of some Lebanese". The communiqué highlighted that this comes after the historical victory in which the resistance achieved liberation and severed the “Israeli” hand. Hizbullah's communiqué pointed further that relying on the Zionist variable is a suicidal choice in the full sense of the word and represents a high treason even in the “Israeli” logic itself, and "we could see now the isolated situation of the collaborators even by their “Israeli” masters, whom they deal with them as a stain of disgrace and shame. And this is more than enough to prevent others from having the same humiliation." The communiqué added that the speeches and statements made by some political and religious officials especially since the victory until now, whether those justifying collaboration with the enemy or the calls to deploy the Lebanese army in the South to serve the “Israeli” security, served as political and psychological motives for some to put hopes on the enemy. The communiqué concluded by calling on all the forces to unite efforts in order to face “Israeli” threats and prevent any “Israeli” penetration and take every possible measure to prevent its spreading through the Lebanese body.

Manar TV, 17-8-2001.
