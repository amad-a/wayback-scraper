Three jailed for dealing with Jewish state

A three-man gang attempting to sell information to Israel on the whereabouts of missing Israeli aviator Ron Arad has been arrested by the authorities on charges of spying for the Jewish state.
The gang was apparently unable to unearth genuine information and made it up instead. But the Israelis were apparently unconvinced and only paid a few hundred dollars for the information.
Arads fighter-bomber was downed over south Lebanon in 1986.
The three gang members were identified as Maroun Youssef Hajj, George Jean Hajj, and Abdullah Khaled Masri. They were tried by the military tribunal and convicted of establishing contacts with Israel, entering the Jewish state and relaying information to Israel. They were sentenced to 18-months in prison each.

The Daily Star 22-8-2001
