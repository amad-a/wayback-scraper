Lebanese MP Fares Buwaiz Denies he Collaborated with the Israeli Enemy

In Lebanon, the interrogations issue regarding contacts and collaboration with the Israeli enemy is expected to be back in focus amid information about possible summoning of names mentioned in the interrogations. The names list includes, as being circulated, a former minister and a current deputy who was a presidential candidate whom some information revealed was Fares Buwaiz. Judicial information mentioned that Buwaiz will be summoned but after his parliamentary immunities are lifted. Local newspapers mentioned that a meeting had been held between the presidential candidate, aboard a yacht he owned, and Antoine Bassil who received 2500 US dollars from the candidate to work out contacts with the Israelis to help him win the presidency. Buwaiz hurried to deny the accusations against him and accused persons of pushing Bassil to fabricate the story.
