Court sentences 22 more collaborators

Beiruts Military Tribunal has handed down 22 new sentences against  collaborators with Israel.
Four people received sentences in absentia ranging between 15 years and life for collaboration with the enemy, giving information about resistance fighters and kidnapping, according to a Wednesday night statement.
Two people were sentenced to one month in jail for entering enemy territory. The remainder received sentences of between six months and five years in jail for collaboration with or enrollment in the now-disbanded Israeli-allied South Lebanon Army.
The trials of Israeli collaborators started 10 days after the May 24, 2000, withdrawal of Israeli troops from south Lebanon. Since then, Beiruts Military Tribunal has tried 2,698 suspected collaborators.
