Australia to Host Lebanese Collaborators with Israel

The Israeli daily Haaretz said 200  Lebanese members of the former Lahdi  militia, which collaborated with the  Israeli occupation army in south  Lebanon, were given the permission by the  Israeli authorities to emigrate to Australia  in a month time.

The newspaper clarified that the emigration of the collaborators is part of an agreement between Canberra  and the Israeli foreign ministry, which allows  those having relatives in Australia to seek  asylum.

Australia is the second country after Germany to have received Lahdi collaborators and their families.

There are still about 3000 collaborators living in the Zionist Entity.

Manar TV, 12-3-2001.
