Lebanon sentences another 25 people for collaborating with Israel

 

 Beirut's military court sentenced 25 more Lebanese for collaborating with the Israeli enemy, judicial sources said Thursday.

Two were sentenced in absentia to jail, one for a month and a half and the other for six months for "entering enemy territory," the source said.

The remainder received prison sentences ranging between two weeks and seven years for collaboration with Israel, for entering "enemy territory" or for killing resitsance fighters.

The trials against those accused of collaborating with Israel started 10 days after the May 24, 2000 pullout of Israeli troops from south Lebanon. Since then, Beirut's military court has tried 2.595 suspected collaborators.

So far, 30 have been sentenced to death, all of them in absentia.

AFP, 3-5-2001.
