Israel extends collaborator mens residencies

 

Former Israeli-proxy South Lebanon Army militiamen and their families who fled to Israel in May 2000 have had their residency visas renewed for another two-and-a-half years, Israels Haaretz daily reported Monday.
The former militiamen were forbidden, however, from receiving Israeli license plates on their cars and have to make do with the original Lebanese plates. In May, SLA representatives complained to Israeli officials that the Lebanese plates are causing them great inconvenience, because it automatically marks them as Lebanese.
The Israeli Interior Ministry has ruled out the possibility of naturalizing the SLA men and their families for the moment.
Haaretz said there were 334 SLA cars on the roads in Israel. The remaining 240 are still waiting in a huge parking lot at the Israeli Armys Abbassieh outpost just south of the Lebanese border. The owners cannot afford to pay the required taxes.

The Daily Star, 3-7-2001.
