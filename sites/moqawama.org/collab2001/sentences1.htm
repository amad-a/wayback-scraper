Lebanon sentences another 20 for collaboration with Israel

 A Beirut military court handed down 20 more verdicts Thursday against Lebanese accused of helping Israel during its 22-year occupation of the south of the country, judicial sources said.

Among the sentences, three former members of Israels proxy South Lebanon Army were each sentenced in absentia to 15 years in prison.

A total of 2,478 people have been sentenced since the trial of collaborators began in early June, just 10 days after Israels pullout from south Lebanon.

Thirty people have been sentenced to death, all of them in absentia.

AFP, 19-4-2001.
