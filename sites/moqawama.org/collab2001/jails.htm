Lebanon jails 29 over collaboration with Israel

A military court on Saturday sentenced 29 people to prison terms ranging from one month to three years after they were found guilty of collaborating with Israel during its 22-year occupation of southern Lebanon.
Four defendants received three-year sentences for collaboration with the Israeli-backed South Lebanon Army, while 25 convicted of varying degrees of collaboration.
Lebanon is technically at war with Israel, and it bans any dealings or contact with the Jewish state.
Charges against two other defendants were dropped, the court said in a judgment posted on its bulletin board.
