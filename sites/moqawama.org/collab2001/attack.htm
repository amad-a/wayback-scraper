Former militiaman targeted in south Lebanon attack

 

A car belonging to the brother of a former Israeli allied militiaman has been set ablaze in southern Lebanon in the eighth such attack targeting members of Israels now defunct force in the zone Israel occupied. Lebanese Police said the car of Mohsen Nakhle, the brother of Mohammed Nakhle currently serving a prison sentence for collaborating with Israel, was burnt by unknown attackers in Taibeh village. A number of unknown groups have been claiming responsibility for similar incidents since March. They are calling on former militiamen to leave southern Lebanon.
