About 30 minutes later, LRB men scored hits in the Beir Kallab outpost.

Another LRB attack was staged on the same outpost with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades.

 

Also the LRB fighters attacked, at 11:05, the Israeli post of Blat with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
