February 2

12:00
The IR team of martyrs Eid Sobhi El-Khansa and Ahmad Ali El-Hajj Hassan detonated, at 9:50, a roadside bomb as a Lahdi motorized patrol was traveling on the road linking between Houla and Markaba villages. The mujahedeen managed to inflict several casualties among its ranks.

Also, the IR militants targeted an enemy engineering force as it was fortifying Shayar Azour position. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct hits.


In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Wa'il Anani and Habib Sharif Tarhini was rushing upon an "Israeli" motorized patrol near Blat post. The mujahedeen, who used the adequate arms, managed to chalk up direct goals.


Ten minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Haitham Mohammad Faqih and Hassan Aqil was falling upon Mashaaroun compound with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.

February 4

7:30
The Islamic Resistance (IR)  Thursday staged a series of operations against the outposts and  forces of the Israeli occupation forces and their proxy militia scoring accurate hits.

IR's squad of Martyrs Wael Anani and Rida Farhat staged an unexpected rocket attack on the Israeli occupation's outpost of Radar scoring accurate hits.

Meanwhile, squad of Martyrs Ahmed Abed-Ali and Mohammed al-Dirani in IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) pounded the same outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring more hits; squad of Martyrs Eid al-Khansa and Ahmed al-Hajj Hassan attacked the enemy's outpost in Shamaa destroying some of its major ramparts; squad of Martyrs Mahdi Sh'hade and Youness al-Husseini pounded al-Hardoun outpost barring it from intervention and reinforcement; and squad of Martyrs Ibrahim Nehme and Hassan Qassir targeted and paralyzed al-Bayyada outpost.

12:00
IR spotted enemy human and vehicle forces inside the Israeli occupation's outpost in al-Qalaa. Squad of Martyrs Mostafa Mzannar and Hassan Harb of IR's ARU targeted the enemy forces with the appropriate rocket propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.

20:05
The IR team of martyrs Taleb Harb and Ghassan Zaiter ambushed a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was traveling on the road linking between Rihan and Sojod villages. As the patrol reached to the determined spot, the IR men set off a big roadside bomb striking one military vehicle and inflicting all its occupants.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Hassan and Hussein Abdallah Moghnieh was showering the rest of the patrol with its rocket-propelled grenades inflicting more casualties among its ranks.

Fifteen minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Bahjat Dakroub and Ihab Haidar Medlej rushed upon the main fortification of Mashaaroun outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the militants reported.

Furthermore, following the attacking of Lahdi patrol earlier on the road between Sojod and Rihan, the enemy brought an engineering force of two bulldozers. Then, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Shouman and Hussein Wehbi detonated an explosive device striking one of the bulldozers.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Mohieddine and Ihab Haidar Medlej fell upon Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades as it was trying to back up the attacked force.

13:30
Amal Movement fired five 81mm rounds at Salaah position and four artillery shells at Qssair post.

February 5

In a new victory achieved by the Islamic Resistance (IR) against the ""Israeli"" enemy and its proxy militia, the IR succeeded, as it had promised before and due to its successive and successful blows, to liberate Sojod compound.


Considered as one of the most significant positions overlooking Iqleem Al-Toffah area, the mujahedeen had managed many times to tread it with their feet and plant IR's flags on its "fortified" ramparts. Also, the IR men were able to disgrace the ""Israeli"" Army's elite units when an IR militant managed to walk through its bunkers, fist fight with one ""Israeli"" unit soldier leaving him injured and return safely to his base.


Today on Friday ‎4‎ ‎February‎ 2000, the ""Israeli"" collaborators pulled out of Sojod outpost after evacuating the military vehicles and destroying its fortifications and installations.


The concerned outpost witnessed a long tight blockade imposed by the IR which has a high profile of ambushes and attacks against it in which a great number of ""Israeli"" soldiers and their allied militiamen were killed or wounded. The last ambush made by IR occurred on yesterday night as a Lahdi patrol was traveling on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan destroying two military vehicles and inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.


Later on, more detailed information will be released by the IR.
