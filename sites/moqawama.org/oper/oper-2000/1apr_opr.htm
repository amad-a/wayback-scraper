April 1

The Islamic Resistance (IR) retaliated for the Israeli occupation's aggressions. IR's squad of Martyrs Youssef Bzaieh and Moussa Salman Friday morning attacked the Israeli occupation's outpost of Radar with rocket propelled grenades in a violent and concentrated manner.

In the meantime, squad of Martyrs Khodor Khiami and Hassan Balhas staged a similar attack on al-Hardoun outpost while squad of Martyrs Ali Harmosh and Mohammed Faqih targeted an Israeli tank in the vicinity of al-Hardoun outpost. IR men forced the enemy tank, which was staging aggressions on the populated villages, to retreat.

Forty five minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Haidar Saleh Mazloum and Assadallah Morched Shamas rushed upon Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Dohour El-Kassara position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades barring it from achieving its goal.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Ali Awdi and Akil Moslem Ayoub targeted, at 16:15, Bir Kallab outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Ali Ahmad Zaarour and Ali Said Shaaban was falling upon Mothallath position with the proper weapons chalking up direct and definite goals.

For its part, Amal Movement shelled, at 14:00, Baraasheit compound.

April 10

In the framework of chasing the enemy soldiers and penetrating through the military measures taken around their outposts and fortifications, the IR team of martyrs Abbas Saleh and Sami Salman Melhem attacked, at 6:05, the Israeli outpost of Dabsheh with the heavy artillery shells destroying its western fortification, whereas the blaze columns were seen emanating out of it.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Hussein Moghnieh and Ali Harb was rushing upon the same outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits, whereas the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Balhas and Ghassan Khalifeh fell upon Beaufort Castle with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Youssef Badran and Abdallah Rammal ambushed, at 14:15, an enemy patrol as it was traveling on the road leading to Tohorra outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to destroy a military command car and inflicting all of its occupants.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Yassine Chehab and Ossama Hammoudi targeted Soweida outpost barring it from the intervention.

Thirty minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Hussein Mortada and Ammar Haidar Bitar staged a swift attack at Rshaf position. The mujahedeen, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, were able to hit a Lahdi engineering force as it was reinforcing the position scoring direct goals.

In response to the enemy's going too far in targeting the secured villages and wounding civilians at Arab-Salim, however, the IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded, at 16:50, the posts of Berkat Risha, Dahr El-Jamal, Dawawir and Rmeish with the rockets and heavy artillery shells. The militants managed to chalk up direct hits warning the enemy that they will not sit on their hands in the case of more aggressions.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, at 13:25, several shells at Baraasheit compound.
