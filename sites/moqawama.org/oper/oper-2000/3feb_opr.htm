February 22

11:00
The IR team of martyrs Ibrahim Mohammad Murad and Ahmad Hassan Khazaal attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Baraasheit compound with the proper weapons. Direct and certain hits were scored, the militants reported.


1915
Also, the IR squad of martyrs Mohsen Hassan Melhem and Rida Mohammad Rida El-Shaher targeted Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

February 23

11:10
The IR team of martyrs Wissam Ali Fsahi and Tahseen Ali Sharifeh attacked Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades. Certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Hani Obeid and Abdallah Qassem Yassine rushed upon enemy movements inside the 80th Brigade HQ of Jamoussa with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.


In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Ahmad Baalbacki and Ali Habib Jaber fell upon Haddatha compound with the proper weapons chalking up direct goals.


Also, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Dib Al-Dirani and Ismail Ahmad Ismail targeted, at 15:40, an "Israeli" motorized force near Taibeh position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct hits.

February 24

16:45
The IR team of martyrs Hassan Hussein Al-Sabbah and Ali Zein Qassir attacked enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost with the adequate arms. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.


In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Mohammad Qtaish and Hassan Mohammad Chokr rushed upon a Lahdi engineering force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound chalking up direct goals.

February 25

13:40
The IR team of martyrs Samir Mohammad Mattout and Mustafa Khalil Kourani attacked Mohaisbat as a number of Lahdi militiamen were inside its southern fortification. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.


Five minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Mahmoud Khodor Baghdadi and Ali Mustafa Fadel rushed upon the "Israeli" outpost of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades, where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.


7:20
The fighters of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted the "Israeli" position of Blat with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

February 26

The IR team of martyrs Moussa Mohammad Hammoud and Zein Assi attacked, at 6:20, Radar outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying its western fortification.

 

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Hassan Mezher and Mhamoud Saber Najem bombarded the same outpost scoring direct and certain hits.

 

Also, the IR group of martyrs Al-Abed Ali Malak and Abbas Ali Hijazi rushed upon Shamaa position with the rocket-propelled grenades.

 

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Abdallah Ali Rida Dib and Qassem Hassan El-Tawil fell upon Hardoun post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying a 12.7mm heavy machine gun.

 

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Ali Hussein Khalifeh and Ali Qassem Qobeissi targeted, at 13:15, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost with the suitable weapons. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen told.

 

Two hours later, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Kamel Kamal and Akram Ali Mansour rushed upon the "Israeli" post of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

 

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Abbas Mohammad Kamaleddine and Amin Abdel-Menhem Haidoura attacked, at 15:30, the 70th Brigade HQ of Qobaa. The militants, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score direct goals.

 

Five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Bilal Ali Mohammad and Daoud Mohammad Shreifa fell upon a Lahdi engineering force at Hemayed position. Direct hits were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

 

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Fayad Hijazi and Ali Mohammad Nasser rushed upon Haddatha compound with the appropriate weapons scoring direct goals.
