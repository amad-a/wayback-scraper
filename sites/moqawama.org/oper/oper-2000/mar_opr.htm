March 1

The Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Martyrs Mahmoud Sha'hade and Mohammad Haidar on Tuesday attacked enemy movements in the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Kassaret al-Oroush in a violent and concentrated manner inflicting definite casualties in the ranks of the enemy.


In the meantime, IR's squad of Martyrs Ahmed Khawaja and Hassan Wehbi targeted a Zionist 
infantry force in the Kassaret al-Oroush outpost while squad of Martyrs Saed Darweesh and Hussein Moghnieh attacked the Dhour al-Kassara outpost with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades scoring definite hits.


Also, the IR team of martyrs Wafic Zahwi Zehri and Abdel-Rassoul Rodwan Baydoun detonated, at 20:00, a powerful roadside bomb as a Lahdi motorized patrol was traveling on the road linking between the positions of Qabaa and Mashaaroun striking one military vehicle and inflicting all of its occupants.

March 4

11:45
The IR men spotted enemy military vehicles entering Baraasheit compound. Then, the IR team of martyrs Mahmoud Mohammad Jaber and Ahmad Mohsen Haroun attacked them with the suitable weapons scoring direct hits.

Half an hour later, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Hassan Hachem and Youssef Abdallah Nasrallah rushed upon enemy movements inside the same compound with the proper arms chalking up direct goals.

March 6

The IR team men spotted, at 00:00, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost. Then, the IR team of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Ali Badran and Hassan Mohammad Zahreddine rushed upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.


Also, the IR squad of martyrs Moussa Karout and Hassan Salameh targeted, at 9:10, from close range Qssair position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.


Meanwhile, as a 54-tank tried to back up the targeted position, the IR group of martyrs Ismail Abdel-Amir Hamzeh and Hussein Kamal Younes fell upon it with the rocket-propelled grenades enforcing it to retreat.


Five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Ali Fouani and Hussein Anis Ayoub rushed upon Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring definite hits.


In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Ali Hassan Atwi and Jamal Ali Chehimi was attacking enemy movements inside Salaah post. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to chalk up direct goals.


Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Monif Ashmar and Ibrahim Khalil Awada targeted fiercely, at 9:35, the "Israeli" post of Taibeh scoring direct and certain goals.


Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Ali Mahdi Waheb and Majed Mahmoud Kobeissi targeted, at 13:05, a Lahdi force as it was beefing up Mashaaroun position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct hits.


Also, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Hussein Haidar and Ahmad Bashir El-Hassan targeted, at 16:20, enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct hits.


Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Ali Jawad and Hussein Ali Haidar attacked, at 21:15, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct goals.


At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ramzi Daifallah Abou Salim and Hachem Abdel-Monhem Mohanna fell upon and "Israeli" force at Taibeh outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants told.


Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Mahmoud Jaber and Ahmad Mohsen Haroun reiterated, at 22:00, targeting Bir Kallab outpost as enemy movements were seen inside it. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct hits.

March 10

The IR team of martyrs Hussein Mahmoud Reslan and Haitham Qassem El-Bazzal attacked, at 14:30, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and racket- propelled grenades striking its northern and southern fortifications. 

Also, The IR squad of martyrs Hussein Ali Mistrah and Bilal Hassan Kobeissi reiterated, at 21:35, targeting Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals.
