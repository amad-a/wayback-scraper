February 14

10:15
The IR team of martyrs Mohammad Mahmoud Fahs and Habib Sharif Tarhini attacked Soweida outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.


12:00
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Abdel-Amir Hamid and Soheil Mohammad Diab targeted Dabsheh compound with the suitable weapons. Direct and definite goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

February 15

23:20
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters attacked enemy squad at Dabsheh outpost scoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties among the enemy ranks.


At the same time, another group of (LRB) rushed upon the posts of Tohorra and Soweida with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.


23:20
Amal Movement fired two 81mm rounds at Tohorra outpost.

February 16

The IR team of martyrs Hassan Abdel-Aala Qassir and Assi Mohammad Zeineddine attacked, at 11:25, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying the northern rampart and inflicting a number of casualties.


Fifteen minutes later, the IR squad of martyr Hassan Trabolssi reiterated targeting Bir Kallab outpost destroying a 12.7mm heavy machine gun as it was firing at Loweizeh village.


Also, the IR group of martyrs Talal Abdel-Rassoul Nasser and Hussein Suleiman Chaitou attacked fiercely, at 14:35, an enemy force as it was beefing up the "Israeli" post of Radar. Using the proper weapons, the mujahedeen managed to inflict several casualties, whereas the smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

February 18

15:25
The IR team of martyrs Hussein Ali Mazloum and Ali Medlej detonated a roadside bomb as a Lahdi foot-patrol was traveling on the road leading to Ain Qinya position inflicting a number of casualties. The Israeli-allied militia acknowledged that one of its members was injured as a result of this attack.


21:50
The fighters of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted enemy movements at Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades striking its fortifications.

February 20

14:20
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Noureddine and Jaafar Hassan El-Mawla targeted at the main fortification of Houla position with a number of rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties among the Lahdi militiamen.
