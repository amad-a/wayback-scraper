January 21

15:00
The IR team of martyrs attacked Shayar Azour position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.


At the same time, the IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded the "Israeli" post of Rihan chalking up accurate goals.
20:00
The IR squad of martyrs Adnan Hassan Farhat and Youssef Mehdi ambushed a Lahdi patrol of M-113 military vehicle and bulldozer as it was traveling on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan villages. As the patrol reached to the determined spot, the mujahedeen detonated a big roadside bomb destroying the M-113 military vehicle and inflicting all its occupants.


Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Ibrahim Alaoueih and Fouad Aoun was showering the rest of the patrol with its machine guns and rocket suffering more casualties among the enemy ranks.


Five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Hani Mustafa Chokr and Nazih Hachem Qobrossli rushed upon Sojod outpost as it was trying to back up the attacked patrol.


20:10
The IR team of martyrs Lutfi Mohammad Haidar and Ali Ahmad Baddah targeted enemy movements at Haddatha compound. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.


21:15
The Artillery Reinforcement Company (ARC) of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) bombarded enemy squad at Rshaf outpost inflicting several casualties among its ranks.


At the same time, another group of the ARC in LRB fired a number of artillery shells at Hemayed position befalling several casualties.
