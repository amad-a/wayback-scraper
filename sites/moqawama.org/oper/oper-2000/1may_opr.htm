May 5

In retaliation for the Zionist enemy's going too far in its repeated aggressions against our unarmed people in South Lebanon and Western Beqaa, following our long-lived patience, after the continued aggressions against our villages and towns of Arab Salim (its school), Mansouri, Majdal Selem, Majdal Zoun, Shaqra, Kfar Romman, Libbaya and others reaching to Habboush, yesterday, and Qotrani today, where a lot of civilians were inflicted and a number of houses were damaged bearing in mind the "Israeli" destroying of Lebanon's infrastructure in the near past. The last aggression occurred today (yesterday) when the "Israeli" shelling of Qotrani resulted in killing two women.

 

Accordingly, the IR was compelled to practice the right to defend its people and deter the aggression by any available means. So, the IR bombarded on Thursday 4/5/2000 the "Israeli" settlement of Kiryat Shmona with barrages of Katyusha rockets pledging its people to sacrifice the souls for the sake of protecting them

May 9

The Islamic Resistance (IR) and the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) proceeded with dealing blows to the outposts of the "Israeli" occupation forces and the remains of agent Antoine Lahd's militia.


IR's squad of martyrs Hassan Mahdi Alaeddeen and Ahmed Ali Shahla at 8:00 a.m. Monday attacked the enemy's outpost in Houla with direct rocket propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.


Also, the IR team of martyrs Ali Hussein Shamas and Hassan Mahdi Alaeddine targeted, at 13:00, Radar post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.


Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Rida Yassine and Ali Mahdi El-Hajj Hassan attacked from a close range, at 15:45, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct goals.


Half an hour later, the IR group of martyrs Imad Mohammad Qansou and Hussein Wajih Mshaik rushed upon Dohour El-Kassara compound with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.


Furthermore, the IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Mohammad Abdallah and Hassan Ali Chokr attacked, at 16:45, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.


Quarter an hour later, the IR team of martyrs Hisham Qassem Haidar Ahmad and Hussein Hadi Mortada fell upon the "Israeli" outpost of Dabsheh with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.


Also, the IR squad of martyrs Zeid Jamil El-Mousawi and Haidar Hassan El-Mousawi reiterated targeting, at 17:05, Dohour El-Kassara compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to score direct hits.


Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Adel Hussein El-Mousawi and Malek Ali El-Mousawi was rushing upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.


Moreover, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Ismail Qamar and Mahmoud Dib Chokair targeted, at 21:00, a Lahdi motorized force as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score definite goals.


Quarter and hour later, the IR team of martyrs Ghaleb Riad Mazloum and Ali Qassem El-Anqouni fell upon enemy movements inside Tair Harfa position. Using the adequate arms, the militants were able to chalk up direct hits.


Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Amir Qassem Rizk and Ahmad Hussein Serhan attacked, at 21:45, enemy military vehicles inside Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct goals.


Twenty five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Samir Sobhi El-Harakeh and Mohammad Hassan Jaber rushed upon enemy motorized movements inside Dohour El-Kassara compound. Using the appropriate weapons, the militants were able to chalk up direct hits.


In its turn, LRB squads Monday morning staged three attacks against the occupation's outposts.


An LRB squad at 6:00 a.m. targeted the al-Hardoun outpost with direct rocket propelled grenades while a second squad targeted the Shamaa outpost and a third, at 6:10 a.m., pounded the Blat outpost with rocket propelled grenades.

Definite hits were scored in the three LRB attacks.
