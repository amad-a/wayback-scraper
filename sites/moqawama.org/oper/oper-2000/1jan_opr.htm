January 1

00:30
The IR team of martyrs Mohammad Haidar El-Hajj Ali and Hani Ali Taha attacked enemy military vehicles near Bir Kallab outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the militants told.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ibrahim Mohammad Qassfeh and Fadi Taissir Batsh was rushing upon a Lahdi engineering force near Sojod compound. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct goals.

January 2

00:00
The IR team of martyrs Ali Mahmoud Assi and Shaaban Youssef El-Mokdad attacked Sojod compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades igniting a blaze.


Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Majed Taleb Jaffal and Ali Kamel Jahjah was rushing upon an "Israeli" infantry force at Rihan hilltops. The militants, who used the adequate arms, were able score direct hits.

7:30
The IR group of martyrs Hussein Mazloum and Ali Midlej detonated a powerful explosive device at one of Ahmadieh position's fortification. This was accompanied by a close-range attack in which the militants used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying a number of its fortifications.

Ten minutes later, the IR team of martyr Bilal El-Bazzal in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded the "Israeli" emplacements of Abou Qamha and Ahmadieh with the proper arms chalking up direct and accurate goals.

19:30
The IR men observed enemy movements near Haddatha compound. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Samer Hassan Ghassani and Adnan Hassan Farhat rushed upon them with the appropriate weapons scoring direct goals.

At the same time, the IR mujahedeen spotted enemy military vehicles as they were traveling on the road linking between Rihan and Sojod. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Shouman and Abbas El-Ansari targeted them with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up definite hits.

Three hours later, the militants spotted enemy movements at Haddatha compound. Then, the IR team of martyrs Ali Mustafa Kahin and Mohammad Hussein Awada fell upon them with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain goals.

 
January 3
7:55
The IR team of martyrs Hussein Moussa and Abdel-Rassoul Rodwan Baydoun attacked an "Israeli" motorized force at Mount Wardeh. The mujahedeen, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score direct hits.

14:50
The IR squad of martyrs Qassem Fadl Khreis and Ali Mahmoud Gharib targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Youssef Abdallah and Ibrahim Ali Issa rushed upon Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite hits were scored, the militants told.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Fouad Fayez Moghnieh and Salem Mohammad Abboud fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals.

In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Ahmad Radi El-Fann and Ibrahim Ali Issa was rushing upon an "Israeli" force as it was moving at Rihan hilltops. The militants managed to score direct hits.

 21:15
Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Zahi Nahim Haidar Ahmad and Salim Salim Ahmad and Salim Salim Ahmad attacked an "Israeli" infantry force near Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct and definite goals.

6:30
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters staged a swift attack against an enemy squad inside Dabsheh compound scoring direct goals.

Ten minutes later, another group of the LRB fell upon Beaufort Castle. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the fighters were able to chalk up direct hits.
