 December 1
01:55 
The group of two martyrs Youssef Baddah and Hussein Hammoud detonated a big roadside bomb as an "Israeli" agents car was traveling on the road of Houla and about 50 m only from Houla position destroying it completely and killing and wounding its members. Lahd's Militia admitted that three "Israeli" collaborators were wounded , two of them seriously, when their car was destroyed by a roadside bomb near Houla position. The two seriously wounded were airlifted for treatment to a hospital in "Israel". 

However, in its Tuesday 2-Dec-1997 edition, the Daily Star reported that "the resistance conducted 92 operations against the occupation forces last month, wounding eight "Israelis", Hizbullah said in a monthly report released yesterday. Three SLA militiamen had been killed and eight wounded, it said, adding that resistance casualties totaled eight, with four fighters from each of Amal and Hizbullah killed, raising total Hizbullah fatalities this year to 15. 

"This month was characterized by the revival of new resistance nits", the report said. 

"The Amal movement staged 17 operations compared to nine in October. The Syrian Social National party conducted three and the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine one." 

The report listed 138 separate "Israeli" assaults last month, down 11 from October. 

Its figures for the year recorded 35 civilians killed and 122 wounded; 29 civilians expelled from the zone, 243 civilians arrested by occupation forces, and 163 houses targeted by shelling and air raid.

 December 2

The group of two martyrs Youssef Baddah and Hussein Hammoud attacked Bayad radar station post with the various weapons scoring direct and certain hits.

At the same time, the group of two martyrs Hussein Hammoud and Hassan Chokr fell upon the same post, also, with the appropriate weapons scoring direct and certain hits where the columns of smoke were seen from the nearby area.

 December 7

The group of two martyrs Mohammad Nassereddine and Assaad El-Atat attacked an "Israeli" force stationed at Bir Kallab outpost with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits. 

The "Israeli" Radio admitted that one officer was wounded as a result of these operations and airlifted by an "Israeli" helicopters to Rambam Hospital in Haifa.

 December 10
14:15 
The group of two martyrs Mohammad Kammoun and Hassan Nassrallah fell upon a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was traveling on the road between Rihan barracks and Kfar Houna position with machine guns and rockets destroying one troop carrier at least. Five minutes later, the group of two martyrs Abdel-Amir Fadel and Khalil Hammoud attacked Kfar Houna position with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from providing reinforcements to the Lahdi motorized patrol. After a short time, it was seen a lot of ambulances traveling between the scene and Kfar Houna and Aramta villages.
