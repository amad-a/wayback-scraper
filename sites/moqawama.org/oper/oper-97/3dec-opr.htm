December 21

08:20
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Hassan Shamas blew up a roadside bomb as a Lahdi militiamen Toyota-car driven by the agent Joseph Elias Issa was traveling between Kfar Houna and Jezzine destroying it completely. 

 December 22

06:35
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Youssef Bzaih attacked Tair Harfa position with the proper weapons causing damage to some of its fortifications. The Islamic Resistance militants observed a Lahdi militiamen squad at Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of both martyrs Mohsen Zraik and Ismail Youness fell upon it with the proper weapons scoring direct and certain hits.

Meanwhile, the group of two martyrs Mahmoud Borji and Hussein Ghassani fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades causing damage to its fortifications. 

 December 23

03:30
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Salim Salim Ahmad exploded a roadside bomb when a Zionist patrol was traveling on the road between Rihan and Sojod inflicting a number of casualties among its members. One hour later, the "Israeli" helicopters evacuated the casualties from the scene. However, the "Israeli" Army confirmed that one officer and four soldiers were injured as a result of this blast.
