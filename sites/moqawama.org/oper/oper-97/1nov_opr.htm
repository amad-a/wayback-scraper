 November 1
8:00 
The group of two martyrs Haidar El-Jawhari and Mahdi Shamas ambusheda Lahdi motorized patrol on the road of Ahmadieh position, showering itwith a barrage of rocket-propelled grenades, destroying it and inflictinga number of casualties among its members. 

At the same time, the group of two martyrs Imad Hussein and MohammadMoussa bombarded Ahmadieh position with machine guns and rocket-propelledgrenades scoring direct hits. 

16:00 
The Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Mohammad Srour and JaafarEl-Mawla rushed upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelledgrenades scoring direct hits. 

 November 2
11:45 
The Israeli enemy troops tried to station at one of the sensitive pointsof Kassaret Al-Oroush position, but the group of martyr Abdallah Kassemattacked them with the proper weapons scoring direct hits. 

 November 3
16:45 
On Imam El-Hadi (peace be upon him) martyrdom memory, the Islamic Resistancegroup of two martyrs Mohannad Fouani and Mohammad Hamid blew up a roadsidebomb as a big Zionist infantry patrol was stationing near Talloussa villageinflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. Then, the Israeli gunnersshelled the area around, whereas the helicopters hovered over the areaof operation. An hour and half after detonating the bomb, a Zionist backingforce came to Mashaaroun area to evacuate the casualties and to back thetargeting force. Then, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Abou Khalil Kassfitargeted it with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. 

 November 5
10:45 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Hassan Shalhoub blew up a roadsidebomb as a Lahdi motorized patrol was passing on the road of Debel-Rshafscoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties among its members. 
At the same time, the group of martyr Hassan Salman attacked Rshafposition scoring direct hits. Meantime, the group of martyr Youssef Makkirushed upon Hemayed position with the proper weapons scoring direct hits,whereas the group of martyr Ali Hassan and the Artillery Unit group ofmartyr Ahmad Ali fell upon the two positions of Jamousa and Haddatha withthe appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. 

 November 7
15:00 
The Special Force Group of martyr Hadi Nassrallah ambushed a Lahdimotorized patrol as it passed on the road of Bir Kallab  Rihan and aboutonly 150 m from Bir Kallab outpost by detonating a roadside bomb and firingit with the machine guns and rockets. Mujahedeen managed to approach tothe Lahdi personnel carrier where they saw the parts of Lahdi agents atthe road. 

Ten minutes after two Mirkava tanks tried to back the attacking forcecoming from Kassaret Al-Oroush position, then Tank Snipers Unit the groupof Jabal El-Rafih martyrs fired at one of them destroying it completelyas it was seen by the naked eye and inflicting all its members. 

At the same time, the group of two martyrs Nimr Ftouni and Ramzi Daifallahattacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelledgrenades causing the destruction of some of its fortifications. 

After half an hour, the "Israeli" enemy helicopters tried to land atthe area of Kassaret Al-Oroush in order to evacuate the casualties, butthe Artillery Unit group of martyr Hadi Nassrallah drove them off. 

3:30 
The group of martyr Hussein Mohsen Nasser attacked an "Israeli" motorizedinfantry force coming from Rihan area with the proper weapons scoring itsgoals. 

 November 8
15:00 
The Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Khalil Madi and Ali Melhemset off a roadside bomb as Lahdi agents car passed on the road of KroumAlArz-Jezzine. The explosion led to the killing and wounding of all itsmembers. 

However, Lahd's Voice of South admitted that one militiaman was woundedas a result of this Operation.

 November 10
8:10
