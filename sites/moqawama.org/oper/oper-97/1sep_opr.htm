 September 1
11:45 
The group of martyr Ali El-Assaad rushed upon Rshaf position with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct hits. At the same time, the Snipers Unit group of martyr Ali Farhat targeted its members found on the position block resulted in inflicting one of them who was seen falling on the sand block. The clashes continued until 12:15 where the Artillery Unit bombarded Rshaf and Hemayed position with artillery shells scoring definite hits. 

17:45 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Riyad Dimashqi blew up a roadside bomb as a Lahdi infantry patrol was passing on the road of Haddatha position-the 17 th HQ killing or wounding all its six members. Immediately afterwards, the Israeli enemy, suffering a lot of casualties, shelled randomly the area. The Israeli dead bodies stayed at the venue for about one hour before the Israeli enemy had managed to close to them. 

18:00 
The Islamic Resistance militants ambushed Lahdi agents force with machine guns scoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties. This force was in a mission of drawing back the killed and wounded collaborators inflicting by a previous operation on the rood of Haddatha post-17th Headquarters. 

 September 3
22:55 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Ghassan Chaaitou attacked a Zionist force at Baraacheit position inflicting a number of casualties. 

23:50 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Ziad Zahwi attacked a Zionist infantry patrol as it was passing through a valley between Adsheit Al-Qsair and Qantra villages scoring direct hits. 

 September 5
1:00
The Islamic Resistance militants hit,heavily, an Israel's elite marine commandos in a pre-dawn four-hour fierced battle near Ansarieh killing twelve of the intruders in an Israel's worst casualties in a single operation since mid-1985. 

In details, a Zionist commandos force was landed after thirty minutes of midnight at the outskirts of Ansarieh village on the coastline between Saida and Tyre. This force was found out by Mujahedeen in the first minutes of landing. Then, the Islamic Resistance groups besieged the infiltrated commandos.  

 

At one O'clock dawn, Mujahedeen fired, intensively, at the enemy force. Immediately afterwards, a harsh clashes broke out and it was not long until the Israeli helicopters introduced to support the marine commandos which came under a swift strike killing and wounding all of its members estimated to be up of seventeen soldiers.

The chronology of vehement battles which continued until 4:30 am can be stated as follows:

2:00
Eight Israeli attempts to land further forces on the purpose of rescuing the marine commandos at the outskirts of Ansarieh and Khaizaran playfield were registered. These desperate attempts intensified the confontrations in which Mujagedeen attacked with mortar shells, heavy machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades.

2:20
Two Islamic Resistance fighters were wounded whereas the Lebanese Army anti-aircrafts fired, intensively, at the Israeli helicopters.  

 

2:45
The enemy helicopters blitzed the area with rapid firing "chain" guns and rockets in an attempt to evacuate its dead and wounded men.

3:00
The helicopters fired heavy machine gun rounds at Adloun-Ansarieh villages.

3:15
The warships fired heavy missiles at Loubieh, Ansareih and Adloun as a cover to evacuate the casualties.

3:30
The enemy helicopters renewed their attempt to land again on Ansarieh village, but Islamic Resistance anti-aircrafts fired, intensively, at them.

3:45
The Israeli enemy brought further air-borne forces because of heavy losses among the marine commandos.  

 

3:50
The Artillery Unit of Islamic Resistance bombarded, heavily, the new area of landing and violent clashes broke out between Mujahedeen and enemy forces.

4:15 until 4:30
The enemy helicopters searched for the inflicted and missing soldiers whereas the rubber boats came near to the seashore of Loubieh to draw the remaining soldiers leaving behind them the remains of at least one soldier as well as wetsuits and weapons.

As-Safir Interviewed Two of Islamic Resistance Militants "We found them out.... and fight them face-to-face"

In its September 6/1997 edition, As-Safir published an interview with two of Islamic Resistance militants who shared with Ansarieh Operation. Here are the details:

The triumphant Abou Shomran, Hizbullah's Command of Ansarieh Operation, and the fighter Bilal talked about the events of confantrations:

"Avoiding the mistakes committed in Kfour incident led the Islamic Resistance to take more preemptive measures such as supporting the guarding points at the villages, towns and sites which could be probable targets for Israeli enemy. So, the guarding groups were backed up by roadside bombs in order to enable them to dominate, militarily, on their area of deployment and to hit as great as possible the enemy soldiers ".

" This was the case of third guarding group of Ansarieh. So at 1:45 am, the members of this guarding group, about 2 km from the seashore and 100 m from Ansarieh itself, noticed remarkable motions which indicated to a probable landing since these motions came after flying the Israeli helicopters over Ansarieh and the neighboring villages ".

" These motions became closer and it was not possible to identify exactly the intruders until they reached to 20 or 25 m from our point. Then and in addition to the uniforms and talking in Hebrew, we knew, precisely, that they were Israelis ".

" After they had arrived in the mined spot, we blew up the six roadside bombs simultaneously ".

" From the first moment, the Israelis were terribly shocked. The casualties estimated 20 to 25 between dead and wounded men. The guarding members divided into two groups. The first group fought face-to-face the survived four or five soldiers. During this clash, two of the first group militants were, slightly, injured. So, the second guarding group managed to withdraw the two casualties and then engaged in battle with the remaining soldiers ".

" At that moment, the helicopters blitzed the area with rapid firing 'chain' guns and rockets ".

" Meanwhile, the anti-aircrafts of Islamic Resistance and Lebanese Army were firing, intensively, at the Israeli enemy ".

" Abou Shomran asserted that the only duty for the supporting Israeli planes represented in withdrawing the corpses from the battlefield. This continued about three and half an hour i.e. from 1:00 until 4:30 am ".
