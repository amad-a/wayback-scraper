07:40
The group of martyr Salim Salim Ahmad exploded a roadside bomb when a Lahdi foot patrol was traveling on the road of Anan killing and wounding all its members. However, Lahd's Militia admitted that one militiaman was killed and another wounded.

The Islamic Resistance announced, before Imam Mahdi (peace be upon him) and the Grand Ayatollah Imam Khamenai, the martyrdom of Salim Salim Ahmad (Gharib), born on 20/2/1976 at Kfour, single, joined to the Islamic Resistance ranks in 1994, shared in several military operations and subjected to a number of military and cultural courses. He was martyrized on 17/12/1997 during performing his Islamic duties.
