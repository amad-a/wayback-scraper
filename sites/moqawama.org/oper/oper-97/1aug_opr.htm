 August 9
On Saturday August 9,1997, the Islamic Resistance attacked Bir Kallab garrison occupied by Israeli forces, and managed to destroy a radar platform using medium range missiles. 

The Islamic Resistance attacked also Alman-Shoumaria garrison in the occupied security zone. 

On the other hand, and according to Agence France Press (AFP), the Islamic Resistance ambushed an Israeli occupation force in Rihan, Northeast of Nabatiyeh, as it was trying to infiltrate into the liberated zone in South Lebanon. 

The ambush resulted in the killing of one Israeli soldier and wounding three others in severe condition, after 6 hours of non-stop confrontation. 

The Islamic Resistance confirmed that non of its Mujahideen was killed or wounded in the attack. 

5:00 
The Islamic Resistance militants attacked a common Zionist-Lahdi force at the surroundings of Zumrayah position with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties among the enemy force. On the other hand, Lahd's Voice of South radio acknowledged that one Lahdi members was injured as a result of an attack launched by Islamic Resistance fighters at Baraasheet position.

 August 10
In the afternoon, the Islamic Resistance militants repelled an Israeli Unit as it was infiltrating to plant bombs inside the liberated areas. 

Also, after observing Israeli motions inside Al-Salaah post and its surroundings, the Islamic Resistance bombarded, heavily, the concerned post scoring direct hits. 

However, the news agencies said that one Israeli soldier was killed and three others were wounded, their cases described as serious, in a fierce clash with a group of the Islamic Resistance militants on the road of Kfar Houna in the afternoon. 

It is worth mentioning that two Israeli soldiers were injured, on Saturday 9/8/1997, by their colleagues as a result of the fears lived by the occupation forces. The two soldiers, Khatib Al-Moadi and Nizar Aver belong to Durze Special Unit formed recently, were part of a group ambushed at the surroundings of Aishieh position. Another Israeli group fired at them as it suspected that they are resistance fighters.

 August 18
In response to the Israeli shelling of Saida, the Artillery Unit of Islamic Resistance fired a salvo of katyusha rocket at Israeli Command Headquarters of Marjeyoun, Khiam and Jezzine killing one Israeli soldier and wounding several others. 

Early this day at 6:00 am, the Islamic Resistance militants attacked Dabsheh position with canon and heavy machine gun rounds and rocket-propelled grenades causing destruction of its reinforcements. 

In Al-Quds, an Israeli military spokesman admitted that ten katyusha rockets slammed into Israeli Command Headquarters of Marjeyoun and the neighboring area causing heavy material damage in the concerned site. 

On the other hand, the Islamic resistance captured two collaborators Ahmad Toufic Salameh and Nairoud Salah Daher at Debbin plain after a group of Mujahedeen had besieged them. Jaber Al-Massri, Shibaa village, another collaborator had surrenderred to the Lebanese Army.

This confrontation resulted in three casualties among the Israeli soldiers. The pro-Israeli Voice of South radio admitted that one Israeli soldier was wounded.

 August 27
As-Safir interviewed two of Ramaish-Blat operation commanders "Israeli screaming looked like the wailing and we saw definite casualties"

In its August 27/1997 edition, As-Safir published an interview with two of Rmaish-Blat operation commanders after few hours only of getting back to their base. Suffering three-day-blockade, nonetheless, they involved in qualitative operations targeted Israeli-Lahdi forces, at less than one kilometers from Lebanese-Palestinian international borders. The Islamic Resistance militants, who deeply infiltrated to the occupied zone, divided into three groups, each one included three members. 

Here are some excerpts : 

Abou Hassa : The duty of second group was to ambush at the nearest point of Israeli-occupied Blat post, only 800 m From Zareit Israeli settlement. At 2:00 pm, of Thursday, the militants fired, suddenly and intensively, as the Israeli patrol approached to the ambush point. We, completely, controlled the situation in which we threw seven hand grenades and fired more than 100 machine gun rounds. We, easily, heard the soldiers screaming but wailing and saw definite casualties among its members. 

Q : How did the enemy deal with the surprise factor ? 

A : Abou Hassan : As a result of shock and panic, they were firing overhead and randomly. It was clear that they were avoiding to fire, horizontally, simply because not to hit each other as repeatedly happened in the past. 

Q : How long did the clash take ? 

A : Abou hassan : Between thirty and forty five minutes. 

Q : And then ? 

A : Abou hassan : About 2:55 pm of Thursday, the first group blew up roadside bomb as two Israeli-Lahdi jeep cars were passing on the road of Rmaish - 17 th Headquarters killing and wounding all of their members. 

Abou Hassan : Of course, the enemy brought further reinforcements from Rami position towards Salhani area and Mozlem valley. Also, it was not long time until that the helicopters began to fly overhead, but they were repelled by Islamic Resistance anti-aircrafts out of the occupied zone. Meanwhile, the Artillery Unit of Islamic Resistance bombarded, heavily and accurately, the enemy targets at the surroundings of Blat position leading the enemy to redeploy again. 

Q : What about the circumstances of Israeli siege of one of your groups and lifting it later on ? 

A : Abou hassan : On Thursday evening, we went forward in order to lift the siege of Abou Hussein group where I contact him on Saturday dawn to inform him that the backing group was divided into two two-member groups in an effort to surround the Israeli ambush near Blat position. On Friday morning, the first backing group reached to the nearest point north of Israeli ambush at an area known by Khallat Al-Maharem waiting for fire orders. The second backing group reached to the eastern part of Blat position. So, at 7:40 am, the two backing groups fired, simultaneously, at the Israeli soldier and demanded the Artillery Unit of Islamic Resistance to bombard, heavily, the operations field, thereby, lifting the Israeli blockade of first group. Then, we began to retreat whereas the shells of Artillery Unit started to rain on the Israeli-occupied posts, beginning from Salhani passing by Blat and reaching to Jabal Bassil. 

Q : How did Hassan Elias Fakhereddine martyrize ? 

A : Abou hassan : During the withdrawal of our backing group, we met an Israeli patrol moving, quickly, on the road of Rami-Salhani so as to cut the road of our retreat. We had two choices : whether the entire group should involve in a fierce, face-to-face and unequivocal fight with the Israeli patrol or one of the group should sacrifice by himself for sake of the whole group. 

Here, Hassan Fakhreddine, 21, chosed the path of martyrdom presenting his soul in order to rescue the remaining members. It is worth mentioning, that the martyr Fakhreddine belongs to detachment of martyr Ali Qashmar. 

On the way of our withdrawal, we heard the sounds of light machine gun rounds and number of hand grenades. Then, we knew that martyr Fakhreddine's soul was on the way to the Great Lord. His body remained at the venue. 

Q : Before his martyrdom, How did Fakhreddine seem to be ? 

A : Abou hassan : From time to time, I had seen him washing with clean sand for prayer. One time, I had asked him:" Why are you praying not in time ? " he answered :" I am praying humbly to God in order to award me the martyrdom honor ". 

Q : Later on, What happened to the retreating group ? 

A : Abou hassan : After completing the retreat from the venue, we found that it is better to us to utilize from the remaining bombs. So, we planted a roadside bomb on the road of 17 th Headquarter - Ain Ibel - Rmaish and detonated it when an enemy infantry patrol, accompanied by armored vehicles, was passing. Then, we continued our march to the liberated areas. 

Q : How many casualties did the enemy inflict ? 

A : Abou hassan : I can say that there are more 15 casualties. 

Q : How did you spend the three days within the occupied zone ? What about the foods supplies ? 

A : Abou hassan : Yes, we suffered a sharp decrease in food supplies, since we took foods for one or two days at maximum, but we stay three days. Some of us put pebbles in their mouths as a "field" tool to met the thirst. One of our commrades was stung by a scorpion and we made the necessary first aids for him. 

Abou Hussein : At any way, the decrease in food supplies is not a big problem and we don't care much about this point since one tuna tin may be enough for the whole day. 

Q : Did you feel for a moment that you had lost the hope of survival ? 

A : Abou hussein : No, we lived in peacefulness since we were moving through the roadside and near houses. At worst, we thought that we could take refuge in one of these houses. 

Q : Could you film the different stages of operation ? 

A : Abou hussein : The martyr Fakhreddine was filming the operation by small video-camera. The film remained with him after his martyrdom. Later on, we brought another camera to film the detonation of roadside bomb as the enemy patrol was passing on the road of Rami-Salhani.
