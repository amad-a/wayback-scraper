 October 1
5:20 
The Islamic Resistance group of Houla martyrs launched a swift attack of Jabal El-Jamaah near Aitaroun, 3 km east of Bent Jbeil and 2 km from the Palestinian land, with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. 

The Resistance men struck a Zionist motorized platoon and a number of Lahdi members during their presence at the concerned area causing the destruction of its principal entrenchment and inflicting a number of casualties among their ranks. 

18:40 
The Islamic Resistance men observed a common Israeli - Lahdi armored force at Ahmadieh post. Then, the Artillery Unit bombarded it scoring direct and definite hits. The columns of blazes were seen emanating from the post.
 

Details 5:20 

The pro-Israeli Voice of South admitted that a member of Lahdi militia was killed by a resistance attack at Jabal El-Jamaah position, west of Aitaroun village. The Lahdi Radio indicated, first, that he was seriously wounded. Later on, he was died from his wounds during airlifting him by an Israeli helicopter to Palestine hospitals. 

However, the Islamic Resistance publish a C.V. related the killed Israeli agent: Mohammad Jawad Abdel-Menhem, born at Aitaroun in 1955, known by "Abou Jawad", joined with the Israeli agents militia in 1985, as a member of 95 th Battalion, served at most of Lahdi positions, subjected to a course of artillery in the occupied Palestine and, finally, he is a sergeant in Lahdi Artillery. 

 October 3
5:30 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Saeid El-Ali attacked Qantara post with machine guns and rockets causing complete destruction of its entrenchments. 

12:30 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Soheil Diab attack Mashaaroun position with the appropriate weapons causing the destruction of two of its entrenchments and inflicting a number of casualties among its members. 

 October 4
5:50 
The Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Hassan El-Akhras and Hassan Hayek launched a swift attack at Ali Al-Taher, with the appropriate weapons causing destruction of its entrenchments. Also, the group of Jabal El-Rafih martyrs attacked Dabsheh outpost causing destruction of its entrenchments and inflicting a number of casualties. At this time, several military vehicles were seen moving between the two positions, then, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Hadi Nassrallah targeted Merkava tank near to Ali-Taher outpost as it was bombarding the civil regions. The tank was destroyed as it was seen by the naked eye. 

Meantime, the group of martyr Ali Kawtharani shelled Ali Al-Taher outpost scoring direct hits. Also, the group of martyr Haitham Moghnieh bombarded Dabsheh outpost with the proper weapons scoring definite hits where the columns of fire were seen emanating from it. 

15:30

19:45 
The Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Kassem and Hussein Zahreddine attacked Sojod post with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades.

 October 8
5:50 
The Islamic Resistance men observed a Zionist squad stationed at Qalaah position. Then, the Artillery Unit group of two martyrs Hazzaa and Ibrahim Makki attacked it with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. 


11:50 
Overtaking the tightened military and security procedures, in the Israeli-occupied region, alleged by Amiram Levin, Zionist Entity's Northern Front Command, and which include ambushes, Merkava-type tanks and sophisticated day and night equipment, especially at the operation venue, the Islamic Resistance militants carried out a qualitative and big operations. 

Gathering an accurate and significant intelligence information, including an appointment for senior officers meeting of Zionist Entity's Northern Front Command, the Islamic Resistance men set off a roadside bomb as a convoy of three Mercedes cars related to the Command of the70 th platoon of Zionist Western Brigade was coming from Odaisseh village. The convoy was directly and definitely, hit. 

At the same time, another parade of four Mercedes cars containing a number of senior Zionist officers, were targeted by the Islamic Resistance men between Dawawir border crossing and Israeli-occupied Abbad position. Mujahedeen attacked it with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. 

Meanwhile, the Islamic Resistance militants attacked a common top-level Zionist-Lahdi meeting convened at the 70 th Platoon Headquarters scoring direct hits, inflicting a number of casualties and sparking a huge blazes seen from the nearby villages. 

At the same time, the Islamic Resistance men attacked Houla and Mashaaroun posts with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. 

This series of qualitative operations was on the honor of martyr Dr. Fat'hi Al-Shouqaqi, the former Jihad Islami Movement's Secretary General in his second anniversary of martyrdom. 
Details 11:50 

The international new agencies and independent security sources asserted that the first roadside bomb resulted in wounding two Israeli soldiers, their cases were described as serious. Also, the attack on the Zionist four-Mercedes-car convoy led to killing four soldiers and wounding seven others. 

However, the bombardment of Markaba barracks, Lahdi 70 Th Brigade Headquarters by more than twenty Katyusha rockets resulted in wounding, at least, one Israeli agent. This agent reported to be the Lahdi security officer Zohair Shoqair who, seriously, inflicted in his head and eye. 

During the bombardment, Markaba barracks was a scene of a top-level common Zionist-Lahdi meeting included the Command of Zionist Liaison Unit Elie Emitay and the command of Lahdi 70 th Brigade Robin Abboud, the security sources said. 

This is the fourth time for about two years, Elie Emitay had been a direct target by the Islamic Resistance men. 

In the past year, he, miraculously, survived two times and was wounded in the third time when several shells hit a position he was inside it.
