July 20

7:30,
In the framework of operations of July Aggression martyrs anniversary, the Islamic Resistance militants observed a Zionist force at Mothallath Al-Qsair & Ilman positions. Immediately, the group of martyr Abdallah Rammal of Artillery Unit Fired at the post with proper weapons scoring definite hits.

7:50,
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Ghaik Ibrahim Murad Abou Khalil ambushed to Lahdi infantry patrol on the road of Kounin-Beit Yahoun firing it with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting it a number of casualties.


July 23

18:45,
The Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Bilal Bahmad & Hussein Al-Tawil blew up a road side bomb as a jeep of special force of 90 Th. Brigade passed on Al-Homsieh-Jezzine road killing and wounding all of its members. Immediately afterwards, the enemy brought further reinforcements to the venue and moved the casualties, whereas another force fired a barrage of heavy machine gun rounds in addition to bombarding the area with several 120 mm shells emanating from Jezzine town.

23:05,
On the occasion of Prophet Mohammad (peace be upon him) Birthday and carrying out of Hizbollah's Secretary General orders concerning annihilation of collaborators militia, group of two martyrs Rabih Wehbi & Hassan Mraish ambushed a 90 Th. Brigade's mortorized patrol and detonating it on the road of Kfar Houna - Jezzine, killing and wounding all of its members.

The wail and cries of the collaborators were heard at a distance of hundreds of meters. After that, the area came under heavy bombarding from Jezzine and Al-Rihan regions.
