 June 1
15:00 
The Islamic Resistance Group of Martyr Hassan Mraish ambushed to SLA's motorized infantry patrol coming from Al-Rihan barracks and going to Bir Kallab position. Upon arriving at the ambush point (crossroad of Aramta, Bir Kallab and Al-Rihan ), the Islamic Resistance fighters fired it with machine guns and propelled rockets causing direct casualties. After a quarter an hour, the Israeli enemy brought more troops and reinforcement to the same area, but the Islamic Resistance repelled it with proper arms. 

19:00 
The Islamic Resistance Group of Martyr Redwan Rashid attacked an Israeli group near to Houla village causing direct casualties. 

At the same time, the Islamic Resistance Group of Martyr Ali Suleiman attacked an Israeli group, near to Al-Qantara village, with the proper arms causing direct casualties among the Israeli soldiers.

The Islamic Resistance Group of Martyr Majed El-Zein attacked an Israeli group located near to Houla village causing direct casualties. At the same time, the Islamic Resistance Group of Martyr Ahmad Chehab attacked a Zionist group located near to Bani Hayan village causing direct casualties.

The concerned convoy estimated to be consisted of two armored carriers, three armered wagons, three jeep cars, five half-trucks, three tankers and one tank of Mirkava sort.

 June 9

At the same time, the Islamic Resistance Group of Martyr Mohammad Melhem attacked Bir Kallab position with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades whereas Group of Martyr Suleiman Yehia attacked Sojod position causing direct casualties. 

 June 14
At 15:45 
the Islamic Resistance militants engaged with an Israeli infantry force ambush of twelve soldiers at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades for about seventeen minutes causing definite casualties. At 16:00, an armored Israeli force tried to support the infantry ambush, but the Islamic Resistance militants repelled it with the proper weapons causing direct casualties and preventing it from realizing its aggressive actions. 

Later one at 18:30, another group of Islamic Resistance ambushed to an Israeli infantry patrol, which was coming with the aim of giving aid to Israeli soldiers fell in the first operation at Kassaret Al-Oroush, causing further casualties among the Israeli soldiers. 

At 20:20, 
and upon passing an Israeli armored vehicle accompanied by walking patrol on the oad of Haddatha village, the Islamic Resistance fighters blew up a roadside bomb, wounding three Israeli soldiers, one of them was, seriously, injured. 

Furthermore and upon passing another enemy patrol, which was going to inspect the place of first bomb, the Islamic Resistance militants defonated second bomb causing further casualties among the Israeli sloldiers. 

Later on, the Israeli occupation forces admitted that one of its soldiers was killed and three were wounded. 

On the other hand, the Israeli Radio mentioned that while an Israeli walking patrol, supported by Mirkava tank which was in a mission of fire shower at Haddatha sector, a bomb was detonated killing one soldier and wounding three others. 

The Second Channel of Israeli Television announced that the Israeli detonated tank crew found themselves besieged inside the tank. The investigation, made by Northern Region Command of Israeli Army, showed that the electric system inside the tank was damaged. Also, the back gate of tank was twisted and closed in a way soldiers could not rescue themselves by getting out of the tank.Besides, the rescuing team needed two hours to arrive at the place of explosion. 

During this time, Yaakoud El-Jamis, the Israeli soldier who died later was alive and said that he couldn't feel with his leg. On the way to the hospital he died.

 June 17
11:20 
The Islamic Resistance group of the two martyrs Ahmad Al-Mousawi & Mahdi Shamas detonated a roadside bomb as the car of collaborator Youssef Jirjis Al-Nammour was passing killing him and his comrades who were accompanying him. All the killed collaborators are belonged to 20 th Brigade of Lahd's Militia. Also, Al-Nammour was working as a maintenance Officer at Jezzine Barracks. 

13:30 
The Islamic Resistance fighters observed a group of Lahd's Militiamen at Toumat Niha position. Then, group martyr Issa Ismail of Artillery Unit shelled the concerned position scoring direct hits. After that, the enemy artillery emplacements at Jezzine & Al-Shraifeh regions shelled the surroundings of Toumat Niha position, Al-Maramel & ain Al-Aman areas. 

 June 18
At 11:30 
the Islamic Resistance detonated a roadside bomb as a car of the Maintanence Officer of SLA's 2oth Brigade Yossef Jirjis Ak-Nammour passed on the road of Zahr Al-Ramla killing him immediately in addition to another woman conscript of the same Brigade. 

Also, the Islamic Resistance militants observed, at 13:00, a pro-Israeli militiamen at Toumat Niha position, then, an Artillery Unit of Islamic Resistance, heavily, shelled it causing direct casualties. 

Later on at 21:30, an Islamic Resistance group blew up a roadside bomb as a car of Israel's collaborators approached on the road of Bkasine-Jezzine, killing Lahd's Security Officer of Jezzine Nimr Nammour and Claude Khalil, the officer in charge of the Bater crossing. 

The pro-Israel Voice of South mentioned that a roadside bomb exploded when a car was passing near to Jezzine. The car burst into flames killing an Lahd's officer and a militiaman.
