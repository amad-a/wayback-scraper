Details 14:45
In the occupied Al-Quds, an "Israeli" military spokesman confirmed that a senior "Israeli" officer called lieutenant-colonel Yair, who serves as a liaison officer in South Lebanon, was wounded in his chest and leg by the Islamic Resistance fighters as he was in an inspecting visit to the "Israeli" positions.

Two days later on, "Israeli" newspaper Ha'aretz reported that "the colonel Yair, "Israeli" Commander of Eastern Sector of South Lebanon, was wounded moderately in a clash with Hizbullah unit near Sojod outpost . Yair, accompanied by a number of "Israeli" officers and soldiers, was in a pre-decided inspecting visit to Sojod position. On getting out of the position, he came under light machine gun fire from short distance".

"The primary investigation made by "Israeli" Army showed that a unobserved small unit ambushed near the position. On its way of retreating, the unit got support from Hizbullah's mortars. One of these shells fell near colonel Yair wounding him by shrapnels in his chest and leg", as it added.

"During the clashes, including air raids staged by the "Israeli" fighters and artillery shells, the colonel received first aids. Then, an "Israeli" helicopter landed at the scene and moved him to Rambam Hospital at Haifa where he subjected to a surgical operation", it reported.

The newspaper added: "The "Israeli" Army is inspecting whether Hizbullah had a prior information over Yair's visit or not. For, Hizbullah has repeatedly made its best to assassinate senior "Israeli" military officers within the occupied area".

Details 00:40
Five "Israeli" soldiers were wounded and three South Lebanon Army militiamen killed in two separate attacks over the weekend.

The blast which wounded the "Israelis" occurred at 12,30 am on Saturday while the soldiers were traveling in a Merkava tank between Tiri and Kounin, 2 km north of Bint Jbeil.

It was unclear what caused the explosion, but Unifil sources suggested that the Merkava hit an anti-tank mine.

The Merkava, which was disabled in the explosion, was believed to have been conducting a routine mine clearance operation along a tank track running beside the road, and was fitted with a 'chain flail' to detonate any mines in front of the tank.

The five soldiers were evacuated by helicopter to hospitals in "Israel".

"Israeli" artillery fired four 155 mm shells at the surrounding area and a nearby "Israeli" position and helicopters machine-gunned the vicinity of the blast.

Hizbullah claimed responsibility for the attack, which has raised to 90 the number of "Israelis" wounded in the south so far this year. A further 39 soldiers have been killed.

The attack was confirmed by an "Israeli" army spokesman in Jerusalem. "An "Israel" army force on operational duty traveling in an armoured vehicle ran over an explosive device which went off, wounding one soldier moderately and four others lightly", he said.
