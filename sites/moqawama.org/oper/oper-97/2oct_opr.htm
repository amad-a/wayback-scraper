Details 17:10

The Israeli Radio admitted that one Israeli soldier, Rounin Hounin, 19, was killed and two others were wounded as the Islamic Resistance fired anti-tank rocket at a Merkava tank stationed at Kassaret El-Oroush position.

Haaretz said that an Hizbullah unit fired several anti-tank rockets at a Merkava tank stationed at a certain position in the Eastern Sector. At least, two rockets hit the tank, thereby, killing the Isreali sergeant Rounin Hounin and injuring two others.

The Israeli newspaper added that Hizbullah fighters have knocked out at least three Merkavas in the last two months, two of them within the last week.

Also, it clarified that the Isreali Army asserted that Hizbullah is widely believed to have acquired more advanced rocket which has much greater velocity and can penetrate the armor to detonate inside the tank.

However, the Daily Star reported that according to a senior officer of Israel's Northern Command, quoted by the Israeli news agency Item, the accuracy of the Hizbullah fighters "improves by the day".

A military source in Lebanon said that the fighters proficiency has grown to the extant that they fire the rockets at the sides of the tanks, where the armor is less effective, to kill the driver.

He noted that most Merkavas, rarely, patrol the zone but instead remain stationary beside Israeli positions where their night-vision equipment can be used.

The Israeli are considering withdrawing their Merkavas from the zone, the source said : "As the Israelis have nothing superior, it could mean tanks will no longer be used in the zone, he said.

On the other hand, the Israeli Army annouced the death of the sergeant Mahdi Khatib, Beit Jenn, who was inflicted in the Southern Lebanon a month ago.

Khatib's father said that his son was died as a result of the ill-treatment subjected to it and because of the delay in moving him to the hospital.
