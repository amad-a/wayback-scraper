 July 1

18:50, 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed Israeli activities & soldiers at Tair Harfa post and it surroundings. Then, group of martyr Mohammad Moqallid attacked them with proper weapons scoring direct hits. 
 

 July 2

2:20, 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed a Lahd's force working in reinforcement out of Al-Sowaida position. then, group of martyr Zohair kobeisi attacked it with the proper weapons whereas group of martyr Riad Akhdar attacked Al-Sowaida position itself scoring direct hits. 

13:00, 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Ismail Younis attacked a big Israeli convoy, which was moving towards Beaufort Castle, on the road of Arnoun-Al Khardaleh with the proper weapons scoring direct hits. One of its vehicles was seen buning, thus, the twelve members, who were inside of it, were either wounded or killed. At the same time, the group of martyr Zohair Kobeisi bombarded Beaufort Castle whereas the group of martyr Riad Akhdar attacked Ali Al-Taher post with the proper weapons destroying part of its reinforcement and scoring definite hits. 

21:20, 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Zohair Kobeisi ambushed a Lahd's collaborators parade on the road of Haidab-Sidon attacking it with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties. Immediately afterwards, Israeli gunners unleashed a ferocious artillary barrage of 120, 155 & 160 mm caliber emanating from Jezzine and Roum areas. At the same time, group of martyr Mohammad Hamadeh attacked Al-Borj post with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits into its reinforcement. 

23:50, 
The group of martyr Ibrahim Hussein carried out the second phase of the abovementioned ambush against command of 20 th Brigade on the road of Haitoura-Daher Al-Ramleh, wounding him with his three officers. Immediately afterwards, the Israeli gunners unleashed several shells of 155 & 175 mm caliber emanating from Al-Zaffata position and Jezzine town into the area around. 
 

 July 3

5:30, 
The Islamic Resistance militants attacked Al-Abbad, Berkat Risha & Blat positions scoring direct hits. 

7:10, 
The Islamic Resistance militants unleashed a salvo of rockets into Blat post scoring direct hits. 

8:20, 
The Islamic Resistance fighters unleashed another salvo of rockets into Al-Dimashkieh position scoring direct hits. 

17:15, 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Abbas Hijazi attacked a squad of Israeli soldiers inside Bir Kallab post with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct &amp; definte hits. 
 

 July 5

9:30, 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Hussein Nasser ambushed an Israeli patrol near Sojod position killing & wounding all patrol members. Later on, the Israeli helicoptors tried, fruitlessly, to transport the casualties but they were replled by the Islamic Resistance militants. Then, it was reported that the enemy was inflicted by other two casualties. 

10:35, 
The Islamic Resistance fighters attacked Bir Kallab and Kassaret Al-Oroush positions with the proper weanpons. 

10:50, 
The Islamice Resistnace group of martyr Zohair Kobeisi managed to infiltrate to the northern part of Sojod position where it ambushed an Israeli reinforcemants unit and attacked it with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting the enemy two casualties at least. 
 

 July 6

22:00, In retaliation to shelling the civilians the Islamic Resistance militants unleashed four solvoes of rockets; The first at Blat's Israeli position, the second at Berkat Risha position and the other two at Al-Zaffata position scoring direct hits. 
 

 July 7

11:30, 
The Islamic Resistance fighters observed an Israeli & Lahdi motorized and infantry squad inside of Sojod position. Then, the group of martyr Youssef Mahdi attacked them with machine guns & rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct htis and inflicting a number of casualties. 
 

 July 8

23:45, 
The Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Ali Tomeh & Abdallah Choaito attacked, with the proper weapans, an Israeli force at Haddatha post scoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.
 

 July 10

23:00, 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed an Israeli force infiltrating into Hafour-Daraya-Al Qotrani area. Immediately afterward, the Artillery Unit targeted it scoring direct hits.
