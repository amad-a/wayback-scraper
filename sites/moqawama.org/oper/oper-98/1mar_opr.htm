 March 2

14:10 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Fadl Soweidan attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits. 
 

  March 3

8:00 
The group of martyr Amin Mohammad Termos attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. 

12:50 
The group of two martyrs Hussein Munir Kansou and Ahmad Melhem Chahrour fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits as the "Israeli" soldiers were reinforcing it. 

15:00 
The Islamic Resistance fighters observed an enemy force inside and outside of Dabsheh outpost. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of two martyrs Hassan Harb and Hassan Obeid targeted it with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of martyr Ali Hussein Sbeiti rushed upon Blat position with the proper arms chalking up direct and certain hits. 

16:20 
The group of two martyrs Fadl Mahmoud Soweidan and Hassan Issa Harqous rushed upon a Zionist motorized force stationed at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. 
 

 March 5

9:45 
The group of two martyrs Ibrahim Tarhini and Hassan Fahs attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying completely the western fortification. 

20:30 
The group of two martyrs Khalil Abdel-Amir Kamal and Nidal Ali Al-Hock fell upon Blat post with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of both martyrs Mustafa Abdel-Karim and Hussein Karrout fired at the "Israeli" helicopters forcing them to retreat. 

21:30 
The Islamic Resistance observed an "Israeli" force near Qantara post and Hojeir Valley. Shortly afterwards, the group of two martyrs Hassan Shamma and Moussa Abou Taam targeted it with the appropriate arms scoring direct and certain hits. 

22:45 
The group of both martyrs Salem Halawi and Ramzi Mehdi rushed upon an "Israeli" unit en route to reinforce the attacked force near Qantara post and Hojeir Valley with the adequate weapons chalking up direct hits. 
 

 March 6

10:00 
The Islamic Resistance staged a series of attacks at the Lahd’s militia posts and barracks where the group of two martyrs Mohammad Hussein Mazloum and Kassem Younes, Mohammad Merhej Osman and Zouelfikar Al-Iffi, Hassan Hachem and Redwan Anqouni, Issa Moussa and Al- El-Hok, Hussein Bakr and Khalil Bazzal, Assadallah Shamas and Hussein Audeh attacked Batter-Jezzine crossing point, Kroum El-Arez post, Jezzine barracks, Toumat Niha position, the 20th Brigade Operational Center and Ain Thaghra position, respectively with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties, scoring direct and definite hits, causing damage to some of their fortifications and igniting a fire with a half-track and military lorry. 

10:50 
The Islamic Resistance militants fell upon Toumat Niha post with proper arms where the smoke columns were seen by the naked eye emanating from the concerned post. 

13:00 
The group of both martyrs Hassan Shamma and Moussa Abou Taam detonated a roadside bomb as a Lahdi patrol composed of six members and a troops carrier was in exchange mission with Haddatha position inflicting all of its members. 

17:55 
The group of two martyrs Mohsen Fneish and Mahmoud Salman fell upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying its northern fortification and inflicting all its members. 

19:10 
The group of martyr Bassel Alaeddine targeted Mashaaroun outpost with adequate arms inflicting a number of casualties. 

22:00 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed an "Israeli" foot-patrol between Houla and Mashaaroun posts. Immediately afterwards, the group of both martyrs Talal Al-Saghir and Moussa Boussi targeted it with the proper arms chalking up direct and definite hits. 
 

 March 7

7:45 
The group of two martyrs Hassan Malek Harb and Hassan Hani Obeid a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was travelling on the road between Rihan and Sojod villages with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants asserted that they destroyed an M-113 armoured troop carrier inflicting all of its members. 

14:20 
The group of martyr Hussein Bashir hit a Lahdi force near Haddatha position with the proper weapons scoring direct hits. 

16:10 
The group of two martyrs Nabil Akhdar and Jihad Shbib fell upon a Lahdi motorized patrol at entrance gate of Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. 
 

 March 10

14:10 
The group of two martyrs Ali Kaoutharani and Haitham Moghnieh fell upon a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was passing on the road between Sojod and Rihan. As a result an M-113 armored vehicle was hit and burnt, whereas all its members either killed or wounded. 

14:15 
The group of two martyrs Ibrahim Alaoueih and Youssef Mehdi attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. 
14:30 
The Artillery Unit group of both martyrs Ali Sbeiti and Rabih Akhdar targeted Radar post chalking up direct hits.
