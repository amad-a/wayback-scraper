October 11

 The IR announced that the squad of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Hammoud and Karim Maarouf Atwi targeted, at 14:55, Rihan barracks with the adequate arms. Direct and accurate hits were scored, the Mujahedeen reported.

  October 12

 The IR squad of two martyrs Abed Hashem and Mohammad Zeidan launched, at 12:00, a swift attack and from several sides at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The ten-minute close range firefight cause the destruction of its main fortification located at the post's entrance. The Mujahedeen heard the enemy soldiers screaming and wailing.

  October 13

 "In spite of all the "Israeli" occupation's security measures to protect its troops, the Islamic Resistance (IR) staged a new qualitative operation in the Beaufort Castle area."

"IR's squad of Martyrs Karim Atwi and Mohammed Hammoud at 18:00 today detonated a large roadside bomb near an "Israeli" foot patrol at the Zaffateh - Beaufort Castle outpost road, an IR communiqué read."

"The attack resulted in killing or wounding the patrol troops. Some enemy troops were seen killed or wounded at the operation side. They remained there for over 30 minutes and were evacuated after bringing more reinforcement", the communiqué added.

  October 14

 "In retaliation for the "Israeli" enemy's aggressions against civilians and firing incendiary bombs to ignite fires, the Islamic Resistance (IR) at dawn today launched a warning attack with the appropriate weapons on the enemy's outpost of al-Abbad (at the border with occupied Palestine), an IR communiqué read."

"IR warned the "Israeli" enemy of going too far with its aggression, "for the retaliation will be comprehensive and concentrated."

"An "Israeli" military source acknowledged that the outpost was directly hit and that one "Israeli" soldier was injured when a shell hit the compound which is near the Kiryat Shmona colony."

  October 15

"In retaliation for the "Israeli" enemy's use of internationally banned phosphorous Napalm and incendiary bombs which have ignited fires in the south and the western Beqaa, the Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Martyr Zoheir Qobaissi bombarded the enemy's outpost of Berkat Risha (at the Lebanese-Palestinian border), scoring definite hits."

"IR vowed to preserve its people's security and interests while warning the enemy against using internationally banned bombs."

"IR is completely ready to retaliate for any aggression", an IR communiqué read.

Also, at 20:50, the IR men spotted enemy movements inside the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Then, the squad of martyr Ali Wahab Hijazi targeted them with the adequate weapons chalking up direct hits.

For its part, Amal movement shelled, at 18:00, Salaah position with a number of 120mm missiles.

"On the other hand, "Israeli" daily Yedioth Ahronot said the Israeli occupation troops in south Lebanon use drugs while on duty."

"(Israeli) military police has filed lists of charges against paratroopers who uses drugs, the newspaper said, adding that Similar lists have been filed recently against troops in the 202 battalion of the paratrooper brigade."

"Comments made by troops and their parents indicated the magnitude of the phenomenon.

A father of one of the troops convicted of using drugs while on duty asked "How can it be that tens of troops in the battalion smoke drugs nearly every night without their commanders knowing anything?"

  October 18

The Islamic Resistance spotted, at 17:45, enemy movements inside Blat position. Then, the squad of two martyrs Ahmad Dbouk and Hussein Kansou targeted them with the suitable arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of martyr Hassan Hammoudi attacked Radar-Ras El-Bayad post with the adequate weapons. Direct goals were scored, whereas the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Shortly afterwards, the squad of martyrs Ahmad Dbouk and Hussein Kansou attacked them with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals. In the meantime, the group of martyr Hassan Hammoudi targeted Radar-Ras El-Bayad position with the suitable arms. Direct hits were scored whereas the smoke columns were seen emanating from it.

  October 20

The IR spotted, at 20:40, enemy movements inside and outside Beit Yahoun outpost. Then, the squad of martyr Ahmad Ismail Ossaili targeted them with the adequate arms. Direct hits were scored, the Mujahedeen reported.
