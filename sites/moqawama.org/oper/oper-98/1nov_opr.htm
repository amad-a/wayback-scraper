 November 1

1:15 
The squad of martyr Mahmoud El-Hajj Ali targeted enemy movement near Dabsheh outpost with the adequate weapons scoring direct and certain goals. 

16:35 
The group of martyrs Ali Ahmad Chehab and Monir Mohammad Smaha targeted Radar launching pad stationed near Rihan barracks destroying it completely. At the same time, the squad of two martyrs Mohammad Ali Mohanna and Nasser Hussein Tleis attacked Sojod outpost from a close range and several sides with the machine guns destroying a number of its main defences. 

17:30 
The group of both martyrs Rabih Wehbi and Hassan Mreish attacked Kassaret Al-Oroush with the proper arms chalking up direct hits. Meanwhile, the squad of two martyrs Hussein Merhi and Ahmad Mshawrab attacked enemy movement inside Bir Kallab outpost from a close range with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. 
 

  November 2
19:00 
The IR squad of two martyrs Issam Chouman and Hussein Wehbi ambushed an "Israeli" foot-patrol as it was heading for Sojod village and attacked it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. At the same time, the group of martyrs Allam Soweidan and Youssef Awdeh showered the area of clashes with the rocket-propelled grenades. As Sojod outpost tried to reinforce the attacked force, the squad of martyrs Mohammad Ali Yassine and Riyad Youssef Dimashqi targeted it with the proper arms scoring direct and certain hits and preventing it from achieving its goals. 

In the meantime, the group of two martyrs Abdel-Amir Ahmad Fadel and Khalil Moussa Mahmoud attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. The Mujahedeen hit directly its fortifications and prevented it from backing up the attacked force. These clashes lasted until 20:30. 

20:30 
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades spotted enemy movements inside Dabsheh outpost. Shortly afterwards, its fighters targeted it with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals. Also, the columns of smoke were seen emanating from it.
