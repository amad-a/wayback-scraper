November 21
9:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Ali Al-Husseini and Ali Kawtharani detonated a roadside bomb as an enemy force was seen near Sojod outpost. In the meantime, the group of two martyrs Ahmad Khalifeh and Rabih Wehbeh attacked the same position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. 

12:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Haitham Ghamloush and Zoheir Kobeissi attacked an "Israeli" foot force, backed by a Merkaba tank, at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals. At the same time, the group of both martyrs Riyad Akhdar and Assaad Shabshoul was attacking Bir Kallab outpost with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from reinforcing the targeted force and hitting its main fortification. In the meantime, the squad of two martyrs Rameh Mahdi and Mustafa Nazar was targeting Sojod outpost. The Mujahedeen using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades managed to hit its defences. 

Twenty minutes later, the "Israeli" enemy brought a motorized force to back up the attacked force. Immediately afterwards, the group of two martyrs Karim Atwi and Mohammad Hammoud targeted it with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. The enemy troops were seen fleeing the battlefield. 

16:30 
Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Moussa Harqous and Abbad Sawli attacked enemy motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush with the appropriate arms chalking up direct goals. Meanwhile, the squad of two martyrs Ibrahim Cherri and Mustafa Harakeh was targeting another force at Bir Kallab outpost. Direct goals were scored, the Mujahedeen reported. 
 
 November 22
15:00 
The IR squad of martyrs Hussein Hammoud and Youssef Baddah attacked an enemy engineering force as it was fortifying Baraasheit compound. The militants used the proper arms and scored direct goals. 

 

 November 23
Half an hour after the midnight, the IR group of martyrs Naameh Habballah and Amin Mohammad Tormos attacked enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost with the suitable weapons scoring direct hits. 

On the early morning of Lebanon's Independence Day, the LRB underlines its firm persistence in Lebanon's sovereignty and sacrifices for the sake of safeguarding the homeland's soil purified from the impurity of the "Israeli" occupation force. While, the Lebanese Army continues confronting the occupation forces and offering martyrs and wounded, its efforts and sacrifices join with that of LRB to form together an axis unifying all various Lebanese sects and affiliations and to realize a new victory exactly as their early unity made the independence. 

On the occasion of Independence Day and asserting on the liberation and fighting the "Israeli" enemy and its collaborators, an LRB squad attacked on the morning of 22-11-1998 Qantara position with the adequate weapons scoring direct goals. 

Another squad, also, targeted Rshaf post with the various weapons chalking up accurate hits. 

At the same time, a third group targeted Hmayed position causing hits. 

6:20 
A fourth LRB squad attacked Tair Harfa outpost striking it directly. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it. 

Ten minutes later, another group of LRB fighters attacked Mothallath-Qsair position chalking up direct goals. 
 
 November 29
Forty five hours after midnight, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Sbeiti and Allam Soweidan targeted an "Israeli" force as it was trying to evacuate, the destroyed military vehicle on the road of Blat position. The mujahedeen used the adequate arms and chalked up direct hits. 

Five minutes later, the IR group of two martyrs Akram Khatoun and Haitham Abou Daya attacked an another "Israeli" force as it was trying to reinforce the first one with the suitable weapons scoring direct hits. 

22:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Ali Ahmad Al-Mistrah and Nasser Hussein Al-Mismar attacked enemy movements at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

 November 30
A group of Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 6:15, Rshaf position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting directly and certainly a number of its fortifications. 

At the same time, another squad of LRB targeted Hemayed post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. 

Moreover, a LRB squad attacked, at 6;30, Dabsheh outpost with the suitable weapons scoring direct and definite goals. 

However, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Krayem and Haidar Ismail spotted, at 16:30, enemy movements inside Ahmadieh post. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted them with the appropriate arms scoring direct hits.
