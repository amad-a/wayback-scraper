Details 13:20 
The Israeli news reported that an "Israeli" force of Givaati Brigade was in an operational mission near Blat post about 3 km only from the "Israeli" settlement Zareit when three roadside bombs detonated by the force. Then, the "Israeli" troops came under the fire of machine guns, rocket propelled grenades and mortars killing one soldier and wounding seven others, two seriously. 

Later on and upon bringing another "Israeli" unit to reinforce the ambushed one, the fighters fired at it with the assault rifles and rockets. Meanwhile, the "Israeli" helicopters fired heavy machine gun rounds at the fighters in an attempt to airlifts the "Israeli" casualties from the scene, but they were drawn groundfire from the fighters. So the process of evacuating the casualties delayed several hours. 

The "Israels" Radio reported that the Israeli Commander of Western Brigade said the clashes were severe and hard. The killed Sergeant called Nicolai Rapapour, 23, Russian immigrant. Upon the request of his mother who lives in Russian, "Israel" had consented to be buried in Russia. Later on, and because "Israel" had refused to be barred at a Jewish cemetery for his mother is not Jew by Christian, Rapapours family decided to go back to leave "Israel" for Russia completely. 

The Radio hinted that because of intense groundfire made by the fighters and the badness in weather, the helicopters had barely airlifted the casualties. After that, the "Israeli" Army had reinforced Blat post and its vicinity with ten armored vehicles and 75 soldiers. Also, a curfew was imposed on five nearby villages, the Radio added. 

However, Haaretz newspaper reported that one of the "Israeli" wounded said: "We were walking in a line when we heard three blasts one after another. Then, a very intense fire targeted us. At that moment, I thought that it is my end and that I will not get out of this situation harmlessly. It was really a terrible moment". 

In its 9/2/1998 edition, As-Safir, Lebanese newspaper said: "The explosions that echoed throughout the area around scene frightened the population of Zareit settlement who sought shelter for about 24 hours".
