  June 1

13:45 
The group of martyr Mohammad Ali Husseini targeted an enemy force near Jamousa position with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of two martyrs Anwar Al-Mir and Ibrahim Hussein attacked Anan post with the machine guns and rockets. 

19:15 
The Islamic Resistance watched an enemy force near Blat position. Immediately afterwards, the group of martyr Hossam Mohammad Ali Hallal fell upon it with the appropriate weapons. 

24:00 
The group of martyr Chadi Al-Zein rushed upon an enemy motorized force inside and outside of Jamousa position with the proper arms chalking up direct and certain goals. 

  June 2
21:00 
The Artillery Unit group of two martyrs Ali and Hassan Al-Sabah targeted Dabsheh outpost with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals where the smoke columns were seen emanating from it. 

22:30 
The group of both martyrs Ali Sharif Osman and Aiman Baalbacki targeted several military vehicles stationed at Sojod outpost with the proper weapons scoring direct hits. 
 

  June 4
6:30 
The group of two martyrs Fadi Ahmad Hmadi and Adham Nazih Hazimeh attacked vehemently Sojod outpost from several sides with various kinds of machine guns and rockets destroying two main fortifications and igniting several blazes. 

2:00 
As an Israeli force was in an exchange mission with another Lahdi one, the group of martyr Ibrahim Fakih rushed upon it inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. The Israeli enemy admitted that one soldier was wounded. 

23:20 
The group of two martyrs Hussein Adnan Farhat and Hussein Mohammad Nasser targeted Haddatha position with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. 

23:45 
The group of both martyrs Youssef Ismail Mahdi and Abbas Ali Hijazi attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. 
 

  June 6
24:40 
Despite the fact that the Israeli occupation forces had adopted complicated steps in order to protect Mirkava tanks stationed in Southern Lebanon, the Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Zoheir Tabatabahi and Hazzaa Makki broke through all the Israeli military steps and ambushed at only 250ms from Dabsheh outpost. As an Israeli Mirkava tank was moving on the road between Ali Taher and Dabsheh outposts, the militants set off a big roadside bomb destroying the tank and suffering all its crew. The blast was echoed obviously in the nearby villages. The Israeli troops couldnt evacuate it only at 2:10. 

2:10 
An Israeli force tried to evacuate the destroyed tank, but the Artillery Unit group of both martyrs Ali Rida Yassine and Ali Hassan Daoun attacked it with the appropriate weapons prevented it from achieving its goal. At the same time, an Israeli motorized force, stationed at Dabsheh outpost, attempted to backup the attacked force, but the group of two martyrs Riyad Akhdar and Moussa Habib Fahs attacked it scoring direct hits. 

10:45 
The group of both martyrs Hassan Hijazi and Ali Bostani attacked vehemently Sojod outpost with various kinds of machine guns and rockets chalking up direct and definite hits. Meanwhile, the group of two martyrs Majed Jaffal and Ali Abdallah Moghnieh rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct and certain goals. 
 

  June 9
5:30 
The group of two martyrs Ismail Younes and Riad Bjeiji attacked Toumat Niha with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying completely some of its defenses and chalking up direct goals. 

6:00 
The Artillery Unit of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades shelled Soweida outpost with the proper arms scoring direct hits. 

20:30 
The group of martyr Commander Hussein Bahij Nasser attacked the Israeli-occupied Blat post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. Five minutes later and as the Israeli troops opened fire at Jarjoua village, the group of martyr Mohammad Ali El-Husseini set off a big roadside bomb destroying the main fortification and inflicting a number of casualties among the Israeli soldiers. 
 

  June 10
19:25 
The group of martyr Abbas Al-Wazwaz targeted Blat position chalking up direct goals and enkindling several blazes. 

21:00 
The group of martyr Amin Mustafa Melhem attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the rockets scoring direct hits. 

21:55 
The group of martyr Wissam Hassan Hijazi watched an Israeli motorized force behind Kassaret Al-Oroush position. Then, the militants attacked it with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.
