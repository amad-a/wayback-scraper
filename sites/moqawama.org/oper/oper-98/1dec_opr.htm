 December 1

15:45 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Melhem and Ali Yassine attacked the "Israeli"-occupied Qabaa post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits. The columns of smoke were seen emanating from it. 

20:10 
The IR group of martyrs Abdel-Amir Fadel and Khalil Hammoud attacked enemy military vehicles inside Sojod outpost. The mujahedeen used the suitable weapons and chalked up direct and certain hits.
 

  December 3
12:00 
The IR militants spotted enemy movements inside Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit squad of martyr Nizar Awdeh bombarded them chalking up direct and certain hits. 

15:30 
The IR group of martyr Abdel-Amir Ahmad Fadel attacked enemy movements inside Blat post with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.
 

 December 4
12:45 
The IR men spotted fortification works inside Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit squad of martyr Ali Krayem bombarded the position scoring direct goals. 

16:55 
The IR group of martyrs Mohammad Yassine and Riyad Dimashq targeted Blat post with the proper arms chalking up direct goals. 

Moreover, the IR fighters observed enemy military vehicles at Ahmadieh position. Then, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Abdo Fahdeh attacked them with the adequate weapons chalking up direct hits. 

15:45 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Najem and Bassem Faour targeted enemy movements near Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to score direct hits. In the meantime, the group of martyrs Mohammad Kammoun and Hussein Jaber was attacking Sojod outpost with the adequate weapons chalking up certain goals. 

19:30 
The IR men spotted  an Israeli force near Ahmadieh position and enemy movements inside it. Then, the squad of martyr Mohammad Rafik El-Youssef targeted them with the suitable arms. Direct and accurate goals were scored where a quarter-hour blaze was erupted. 

22:00 
The IR militants observed an Israeli force as it was trying to infiltrate into Qilya village. Then, the group of martyr Ahmad Taleb attacked it with the proper weapons forcing it to retreat after scoring direct goals. 
 

 December 5

The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mohammad Sawli and Ibrahim Mohammad Kassfi targeted  enemy motorized movements near the "Israeli" post of Qabaa. Using the adequate arms, the militants were able to chalk up direct and certain hits.

The IR group of martyr Wissam Zayour attacked an "Israeli" collaborator force as it was fortifying Tohorra outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to hit its southern main fortification. At the same time, the squad of martyr Talal Assaghir targeted the same post with the suitable weapons scoring direct and definite goals. 

11:25 
The IR group of martyrs Hassan Sawli and Ibrahim Kassfi shelled  enemy movements at Blat position with the proper arms. Direct and definite hits were scored, the militants reported.

The IR group of martyrs Mohammad Melhem and Rida Al-Shaher spotted enemy movements near Ahmadieh position. Then, the mujahedeen attacked them with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct and definite goals. 

6:30 
A group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked Qantara compound from the entrance side. The fighters managed to hit directly the main fortification. Meanwhile, another group was targeting the same compound from the northern side striking certainly its main fortification. 

7:00 
A squad of LRB attacked Jamoussa position, which is the headquarter of the 80th Lahdi Brigade, with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits. In the meantime, another LRB group was targeting Baraasheit position with the suitable arms. Direct and definite goals were scored, the fighters reported.

 December 7
6:30 
On the occasion of the Birthday of the Awaited Imam Mahdi (peace be upon him), the IR squad of two martyrs Hassan Yassine and Hassan Nassrallah attacked and from a close range Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. 

The post's main fortification was hit directly. In the meantime, the group of martyrs Haidar Haidar and Wehbi Wehbi was attacking, from a close range also, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket propelled grenades. The fortification at the its entrance was certainly hit. At the same time, the group of martyrs Bahjat Dakroub and Hassan Saghir attacked "Israeli" movements near Kassaret Al-Oroush with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite hits. 

8:15 
The IR group of martyrs Ghassan Ghanem and Abdallah Rammal targeted enemy movements at Blat post. The mujahedeen managed to score direct goals. Meanwhile, the squad of martyr Mohammad Zaidan targeted Tair Harfa position as its members were fortifying it. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported. 

13:00 
The IR squad of martyr Mohammad Bashir attacked Jamoussa position with the adequate weapons destroying its western fortification. In the meantime, the group of martyr Ibrahim Faqih was attacking an "Israeli" force near Jamoussa position. Direct hits were scored. 

23:45 
The IR group of martyrs Mohammad Kamel Abbas and Kassem Zein-El Abideen Yassine observed fortification works near the Israeli-occupied post of Qabaa. Then, the militants targeted them with rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
