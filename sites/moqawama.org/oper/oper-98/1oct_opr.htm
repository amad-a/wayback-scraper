 October 1
 
9:25 
The IR militants spotted enemy members fortifying the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Then, the squad of martyr Fouad Baalbacki targeted them with the suitable arms scoring direct hits. 

15:00 
The IR men observed again the members manned the old Beit Yahoun outpost performing fortification works. Shortly afterwards, the group of martyr Abdallah Mohammad Chaitou targeted them with the adequate weapons. The mujahedeen scored direct hits. 
 
 
 
 October 2
 
15:25 
A close range attack at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. 

At the same time, another group of Artillery Company shelled the enemy troops of the same outpost. Direct hits were scored as the fighters reported. 

21:40 
The IR men observed enemy military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Then, the group of two martyrs Ghassan Ali Ghanem and Nabil Mohammad Akhdar attacked them with the suitable weapons chalking up direct and certain hits.
 
 
 October 3
 
17:45 
The IR spotted  enemy movements inside Blat post. Then, the group of martyrs Ali Al-Tawil and Nizar Kansou targeted them with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. 
 
 
 October 4
 
18:30 
The IR men spotted enemy troops fortifying the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Immediately afterwards, the squad of martyr Ali Mohammad Kawtharani targeted them with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits. 

23:00 
The IR fighters observed enemy movements near Bir Kallab outpost. Them, the group of two martyrs Issa Ktaish and Mohsen Fneish attacked them with the suitable arms chalking up direct goals.
 
 October 5
 
Also, at 18:30, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mohammad Jaghbir and Hussein Monir Kansou attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. 

Moreover, at 19:10, the IR militants observed enemy movements inside Radar post. Then, the group of martyr Akram Khatoun targeted them with the proper arms chalking up direct hits. 

However, Amal Movement shelled Salaah outpost and the road between Qantara and Deir Siryan with a number of 120mm missiles 
 
 October 6
 
The IR squad of two martyrs Majed Jaffal and Ali Moghnieh attacked, at 17:00, a collaborator engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost. The militants asserted that a bulldozer was hit directly. 

However, a group of Amal Movement shelled, at 17:00, Baraasheit outpost.
 
 October 7
 
The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Ali Ayoub and Kassem Abdel-Hassan Falah spotted a collaborator engineering force near Sojod outpost. Then, the militants targeted it with the adequate arms. A bulldozer was hit directly whereas the members escaped. 

At 19:50, the IR militants spotted enemy motorized movement near Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the group of martyr Mohammad Sawli targeted them with the adequate arms. At least one military vehicle was hit directly.
 
 
 October 8
 
The IR squad of martyr Mohammad Sawli attacked, at 14:30, Sojod outpost destroying the main fortification at its entrance. Meanwhile, the group of martyr Abbas Hammoud attacked its western side with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Several defences were certainly destroyed as the Mujahedeen reported.
 
 October 10
 
The IR squad of two martyrs Zaher Hmadeh and Mohammad El-Fann attacked at 18:00, an Israeli force from a close range in the vicinity of Rihan barracks. 

Following half an hour fierce clashes, the Artillery Unit of IR bombarded Rihan barracks scoring direct hits. Also, the IR men observed, at 18:45, an Israeli force inside Al-Afandi forest near Rihan barracks. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit pounded it chalking up direct and definite goals. 

Earlier at 17:50, two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades attacked Bir Kallab outpost. The first group opened fire from a close range at the soldiers manning the fortification while the other one attacked the post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring certain goals. The smoke columns were seen emanating from it. The embarrassed and frightened Israeli soldiers began firing randomly in all sides.
