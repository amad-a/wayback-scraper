 September 1

7:15 
In response to the "Israeli" aggressions on Mashghara, the IR squad of two martyrs Jamil Skaff and Abbas Bjeiji bombarded Lahd's Twentieth Brigade HQ scoring direct hits. One armored personnel carrier and one 155mm canon were damages. 

13:15 
The IR fighters observed enemy movements at Bassil area. Then, the group of both martyrs Hassan Kamal Hayek and Youssef Abdel-Karim Yassine targeted them with the various kinds of arms chalking up direct goals. 

At the same time, the squad of two martyrs Moussa Habib Fahs and Samer Ahmad Bshara attacked them also with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits. In the meantime, the group of both martyrs Mohammad Ibrahim Atieh and Mohammad Mustafa Kassas opened its fire at the same force by using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. A number of "Israeli" soldiers were seen fleeing the scene of clashes. 

14:20 
The "Israeli" helicopters tried to evacuate the casualties from the scene. Then, the IR Artillery Unit repelled them with the anti-aircraft guns. 

 


 September 3

13:35 
The IR men spotted fortification activities made by the enemy forces at Haddatha compound. Then, the squad of martyr Saeid Mustafa El-Ali targeted them with the adequate weapons. The militants were able to score direct goals. At the same time, the IR fighters observed, also, fortification works made by the enemy troops at Baraasheit position. Then, the group of martyr Hussein Abdel-Karim attached them with the suitable arms chalking up direct hits.

 


  September 4

15:15 
The IR squad of two martyrs Mohammad Abed Kammoun and Ahmad Hussein Freijeh staged a swift attack on Sojod outpost destroying completely its main fortification. At the same time, the group of two martyrs Hussein Ali Jaber and Hussein Monir Kansou backed up the first squad from the opposite side. The Mujahedeen used in this 17-minute clashe the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. The columns of smoke were seen emanating from the outpost. 

15:45 
As a Merkava tank tried to reinforce the attacked outpost, the squad of two martyrs Hassan Jawad Nassrallah and Haidar Mustafa Haidar Ahmad targeted it with the proper arms preventing it from achieving its goal. 

22:00, the IR men observed enemy movements near Sojod outpost. Immediately afterwards, the squad of two martyrs Wehbi Wehbi and Bahjat Dakroub targeted them with the proper arms. Direct hits were scored as the Mujahedeen reported.

 


 September 6

22:10 
Motorized movements on the road between Sojod and Rihan. Then, the group of two martyrs Hassan Jaghbir and Ali Kamel Jahjah targeted them with the proper rockets scoring direct hits.

 


 September 7

9:45am 
Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades striking its fortifications.

 


 September 8

19:45 
The IR men observed enemy movements near the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Then, the group of martyr Saeid El-Ali targeted it with the proper weapons chalking up certain goals. 

21:00 
The IR militants spotted military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Then, the squad of two martyrs Hassan Salman Krayem and Mortada Rammal attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits.

 

 September 9

00:40 
The IR squad of two martyrs Youssef Yassine and Kassem Kaoun observed enemy movements near and inside Beaufort Castle. Then, the squad targeted them with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals. 

15:30 
The IR militants spotted "Israeli" reinforcements entering Ahmadieh post. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Mohammad Mustafa Haidar targeted them with the suitable weapons scoring direct hits. 

15:35 
The IR fighters observed motorized movements and fortification works at the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Then, the squad of both martyrs Kassem Ahmad Naameh and Rabih Mohammad Wehbi attacked the outpost with the proper arms. 
The fighters were able to score direct hits. Also, the smoke columns were seen emanating from it. 

16:30 
The IR squad of two martyrs Mahdi Shamas and Ahmad El-Mousawi blew up a roadside bomb as a chief common collaborators "Israeli" convoy was entering into Ahmadieh post. The members were either killed or injured. At the same time, the group of both martyrs Mohammad Haidar Ahmad and Haitham Abou Daya attacked from close range the same convoy with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting more casualties. Among the inflicted was Nadim Abou Rafeh. 

 


 September 10

12:10 
The IR squad of two martyrs Faraj Ballouk and Hassan Saalouk blew up a powerful explosive device as military vehicle related to the command of the Both Bridge of Lahd's Militia was travelling on the road of Toumat Niha killing all its members. 

16:55 
The IR men ambushed a motorized collaborator patrol as its was passing on the road leading to Bir Kallab outpost. The group of both martyrs Fouad Rida Aoun and Nadim Skaiki attacked it with the machine gun and rocket-propelled grenades destroying an M-113 military vehicle and inflicting all its members. 

11:00 
The Islamic Resistance men observed enemy movements inside Tair Harfa outpost. Then, the squad of two martyrs Ali Baalbacki and Hussein Borji attacked them with the adequate weapons chalking up direct goals. 

11:25 
The IR militants observed enemy movements and fortification works at the entrance of Blat post. Immediately afterwards, the group of two martyrs Mohammad Abdel-Karim and Haitham Moghnieh attacked them with the suitable weapons. The militants reported that they scored direct and certain goals.
