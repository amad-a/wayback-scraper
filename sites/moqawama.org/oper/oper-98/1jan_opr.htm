However, The Daily Star newspaper reported in its January 10-1998 edition the following: 

The South Lebanon Army officer believed to have ordered the shelling of Sidon last August, in which seven civilians died, was the target yesterday of a roadside bomb ambush by Hizbullah guerrillas near Jezzine. 

The 4,40pm blast occurred as Emile Nasr was passing along the Roum-Jezzine road in an armoured column accompanied by other militiamen. An armoured personnel carrier was destroyed in the blast. 

Nasr, who commands the SLA in the Jezzine enclave, allegedly ordered the August 18 shelling of Sidon after his nephew and niece were killed in a road side bomb blast the same day. The bombardment, the first to hit central Sidon since the war, wounded 42 civilians.
