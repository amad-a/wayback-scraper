 August 4
11:45 
The squad of two martyrs Samir Ahmad Akil and Abbas Ahmad Hammoudi spotted enemy movements inside Blat position. 
Then, the militants targeted them with the suitable arms chalking up direct goals. 

15:55 
The group of both martyrs Mohammad Ibrahim Atieh and Abbas El-Wazwaz attacked Blat position with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits. 

18:30 
The group of two martyrs Youssef Yassine and Hussein Krayem targeted Blat post with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. 

23:45 
As two enemy bulldozers were performing fortification actions on the road linking the villages of Sojod and Rihan, the group of both martyrs Abbas Mohammad Hammoud and Fouad Saeid Abdallah attacked it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting directly one of them. 

At the same time, Sojod outpost tried to back up the attacked vehicles, but the squad of martyr Fadi Hassan Tawil targeted it with the adequate arms preventing it from releasing its goals. 

23:55 
The squad of martyr Mohammad Ali Najem attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket propelled-grenades destroying its main fortification at the entrance and preventing it from giving support to the attacked vehicles. 
 

 August 5
21:30 
The group of martyr Mohammad Mustafa Kassas set off a big explosive device as a patrol of Lahds militiamen was travelling on the road between Rihan and Bir Kallab outpost destroying military vehicle and inflicting all its members. 

21:40 
As a motorized patrol was coming from Rihan barracks in order to evacuate the casualties and pull back the destroyed vehicle, the squad of two martyrs Hijazi Mahmoud Hijazi and Aiman Mahmoud Hamdar targeted it with the adequate arms and rockets scoring direct hits. 

21:45 
Bir Kallab outpost tried to back up the attacked patrol. Then, the squad of both martyrs Hassan Kamal Hayek and Youssef Abdel-Karim Yassine attacked it with the rockets chalking up definite goals. 

 

  August 6
1:20 
IR men spotted enemy movements near Borj position. Immediately afterwards, the squad of martyr Hassan Hammoudi attacked the position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. 

1:25 
Ali Taher outpost tried to give support to Borj position. Then, the group of two martyrs Moussa Habib Fahs and Ozaina Jawdat Kansou targeted it with the suitable arms destroying its southern main fortification completely. 

8:30 
In retaliation for the repeated "Israeli" aggressions on the civilians, the IR squad of martyr Hassan Hammoudi attacked Zohaira position with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. 
In the meantime, the group of martyr Ali Najib Choaib targeted Ramya position with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals. 

13:30 
In the framework of retaliation for the "Israeli" aggressions on the civilians, the IR's Artillery Unit group of martyr Hassan Hammoudi bombarded Abbad position scoring direct hits. 

15:20 
The squad of both martyrs Bilal Ibrahim Darnouh and Mohsen Hussein Zraik targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. 
 

 August 8
17:35 
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades fighters attacked Mashaaroun position with the adequate weapons scoring definite goals. In the meantime, another squad targeted Houla position with the suitable arms chalking up direct hits. 

19:30 
The IR squad of martyr Fouad Rida Aoun attacked a radar installation at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.
 August 9
13:20 
The group of two martyrs Adnan Maarouf Kashmar and Mohammad Youssef Hazimeh spotted enemy movements inside Blat post. Then, the militants attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals. 

16:50 
The squad of martyr Hassan Hammoudi targeted Blat post with the suitable weapons chalking up direct hits and destroying some of fortifications. 

19:00 
The squad of martyr Hassan Hammoudi infiltrated to Sojod outpost and destroyed its western fortification upon which a radar system was installed. The Mujahedeen who returned safely to their base later, asserted that two "Israeli" soldiers were killed. It is worth noting that the IR men had destroyed the day before a radar installation. 

19:30 
As "Israeli" military reinforcements, coming from Rihan area, arrived at the entrance of Sojod outpost, the group of martyr Ghazi El-Husseini attacked them with the rockets scoring direct hits. 

21:30 
The IR squad of martyr Hassan Hammoudi ambushed an "Israeli" patrol at Bassil area, the militants set off an explosive device and, then, targeted the patrol with the adequate arms inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. 

23:45 
As an "Israeli" patrol reached to the place of the former blasts at Bassil area in a mission to move the casualties, the group of martyr Assaad Berro blew up a roadside bomb scoring definite goals. 
 

 August 10
14:00 
The squad of two martyrs Faraj Ballouk and Hassan Saalouk detonated an explosive device as a patrol of Lahd's Militia was in exchange mission on the road linking between Kroum El-Arz position (HQ of the 20th Brigade) and Jezzine city. Shortly afterwards, the enemy tanks and mortars stationed at Zohour Ramleh emplacement shelled the site of blast. 

14:45 
As the enemy soldiers were installing a radar pad at Sojod outpost, the group of martyr Ibrahim Alaouieh targeted them with the adequate weapons inflicting one soldier at least.
