  May 1
6:00 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force near Radar position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of two martyrs Ali Badawi and Ali Salman targeted it scoring direct hits. 

12:30 
The Islamic Resistance group of both martyrs Youssef Bzeih and Moussa Salman attacked Hardoun outpost with the proper arms chalking up direct hits. 

16:00 
The Islamic Resistance fighters observed a squad of "Israeli" agents inspecting the damages that hit Hardoun outpost. Shortly afterwards, the group of two martyrs Khodor Khiami and Youssef Barakat targeted them with the appropriate arms inflicting a number of casualties among their ranks. 
 

  May 2
5:15 
The Islamic Resistance group of martyr Ahmad Al-Mousawi rushed upon Ahmadieh position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals. 

7:00 
The group of two martyrs Salah Chaitou and Nidal El-Hoq fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. 

13:15 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed an "Israeli" armored force inside Ahmadieh position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Mahdi Shamas bombarded it intensely chalking up direct goals. The Islamic Resistance traded the artillery shelling with the enemy until after 14:25. 

18:25 
The Islamic Resistance men observed an "Israeli" force inside Zemraya position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of both martyrs Hassan Al-Saalouk and Faraj Ballouq shelled it scoring direct and accurate goals. 
 

  May 3
8:50 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed an "Israeli"-Lahdi common convoy entering into Zemraya position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Mohammad Moussa pounded it with the adequate arms scoring definite goals. 

13:00 
The group of martyr Ibrahim Murad targeted Jabal Jamaah post which is located between Aitaroun and Mohaibib scoring direct hits. 

14:30 
The group of two martyrs Hassan Zahwi and Mohammad Assaf rushed upon an "Israeli" foot-patrol near Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the machine guns and rockets chalking up direct and certain goals. 

At the same time, the group of both martyrs Moslem El-Khalil and Khalil Rmeiti attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rockets destroying its main defenses. In the meantime, the group of two martyrs Nazih Nassereddine and Osama El-Husseini fell upon Sojod outpost from several sides with the machine guns and rockets destroying its northern and western fortifications. The clashes lasted for about twenty minutes. 

18:35 
The Islamic Resistance men observed an enemy force performing fortification works inside Hardoun outpost. Immediately afterwards, the group of martyr Mohammad Abdel-Hussein Moselmani targeted it the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits. Also, the columns of smoke were seen emanating from it. 

18:45 
The Islamic Resistance fighters observed a Lahdi convoy entering into Zemraya position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Ahmad Taleb bombarded it intensely destroying one Jeep and one Mercedes car manned by Lahd's militiamen. 

22:25 
The Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force near Kassaret Al-Oroush position. Then, the group of both martyrs Abdallah Cherri and Salman Yehia targeted it with the proper arms scoring direct hits. 
 

  May 4
16:15 
The group of two martyrs Ali Jahjah and Ali Nazzal attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and destroying its eastern fortification. At the same time, the group of both martyrs Rameh Mahdi and Hassan Jaghbir rushed upon an "Israeli" force stationed at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
