For its part, the Lebanese National Resistance Front (LNRF) at dawn (Friday) carried out a complex qualitative operation against the outpost of the Israeli occupation and its collaborators in Shwayya (at the western foot of Mount Hermon), an LNRF communiqué read.

Squad of Arqoub Martyrs of Martyr George Nasrallah's Unit attacked the outpost and clashed with the troops manning it for 30 minutes with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, LNRF added.

It said that following the retreat of the first LNRF squad, another squad attacked the outpost with missiles scoring accurate hits.

"The confrontations intensified as the occupation forces and those of the proxy militia in the outposts of Ain Qinia and Zaghleh intervened. The occupation forces raked the near areas and lit up the Mount Hermon foot and the surrounding wadis with flare shells, the communiqué concluded, affirming that LNRF men returned to their bases safely
