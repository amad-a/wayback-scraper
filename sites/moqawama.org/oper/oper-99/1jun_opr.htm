 Jun 4
6:00 
The IR team of martyr Amin Mustafa Melhem attacked an "Israeli" foot-patrol at Mount Abou Rkab with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and definite goals. 

10:30 
The IR men spotted enemy movements inside Zimraya post. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyr Hassan Shamas in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded them chalking up direct hits. 

Five minutes later, the IR group of martyr Hassan Ali Al-Zein reiterated targeting the "Israeli" foot-patrol at Mount Abou Rkab with the proper arms. Direct and certain hits were scored, the militants reported. 

00:15 
The fighters of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked Soweida outpost with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits. 

One hour later, the fighters of LRB spotted enemy movements inside Dabsheh outpost. Shortly afterwards, the fighters targeted them with the appropriate arms chalking up direct goals. 
 
 Jun 5
14:30 
The IR group Imad Al-Salman attacked an "Israeli" infantry force near Mlikh village with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were chalked up.
 Jun 6
5:10 
The IR team of martyrs of Hussein Trabolssi and Hussein Hassan Hayek targeted Radar post with the appropriate weapons striking a radar launching pad and causing damage to its fortifications. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Ali Rida attacked violently Tair Harfa position where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it. 

6:30 
The IR group of martyr Imad Al-Salman ambushed an "Israeli" foot-squad near Mlikh village. The mujahedeen opened fire from their machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades from a close range inflicting several casualties. 

16:05 
The IR team of martyrs Ali Hussein Shibli and Ali Moussa Abdel-Karim attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Haddatha compound. The militants, who used the suitable arms, were able to chalk up direct hits. 

19:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mahmoud Zaraket and Bassel Ala-eddine targeted an "Israeli" collaborator patrol as its traveling on the road leading to Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct goals. 

The IR group of martyrs Zoheir Kobeissi and Hussein Trabolssi attacked, at midnight exactly, enemy movements near Blat post with the proper arms scoring direct hits. 
 
 Jun 7
14:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Hassan Bachir and Youssef Ali Nassereddine attacked enemy movements near Haddatha compound. The militants, who used the adequate arms, managed to chalk up direct goals. 

19:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Rodwan Sami Rashid and Khalil Ibrahim Said attacked enemy movements near Tair Harfa outpost with the suitable weapons. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct goals.
 Jun 8
20:15 
The IR group of martyrs Ahmad Ali Rida and Hussein Trabolsi attacked enemy movements near Hardoun position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades causing damage to several of its fortifications. Also, the smoke columns were see billowing out of it. 

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Hassan Atwi and Aiman Hamzeh Baalbacki targeted enemy force as it was beefing up Baraasheit compound. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct and certain goals.
 Jun 9
6:00 
The IR squad of martyrs Habib Tarhini and Hussein Ballout attacked Dabsheh outpost with the adequate arms striking its southern fortification and igniting a blaze inside it. 

17:50 
The IR team of martyrs Ali Sharif Osman and Youssef Ali Nassereddine targeted an "Israeli" infantry force near Mlikh village. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score direct hits. 

5:30 
Earlier, two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked the positions of Dabsheh and Soweida with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct and definite goals.
