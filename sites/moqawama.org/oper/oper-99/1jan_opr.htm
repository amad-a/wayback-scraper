 January 1
2:35 
The IR group of martyr Adib Ahmad Bahsoun targeted enemy movements at Baraasheit position with the proper weapons scoring direct goals. 

20:25 
The IR group of martyrs Faraj Ballouq and Mohammad Al-Mazbouh attacked Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct hits. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Kassem Harb and Kassem Yassine targeted Bir Kallab outposts. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to chalk up direct goals. 

 January 2
15:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Nizar Slim and Abdel-Aziz Kourani attacked enemy movements inside Blat post with the appropriate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of martyrs Youssef Barakat and Khodor Khiami targeted Hardoun outpost with the adequate weapons chalking up direct goals. 

19:15 
The IR group of martyrs Mustafa Nazar and Rameh Mahdi targeted motorized movements at Sojod outpost with the proper weapons chalking up certain goals. 
 
 January 3
11:00 
The IR squad of martyrs Hussein Mohanna and Moussa Yassine attacked an enemy motorized patrol on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Ghassani and Ibrahim Moghnieh targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from supporting the attacked patrol. Also, direct and certain hits were scored, the militants reported. 
 
 January 4
19:25 
The IR squad of martyrs Ibrahim Alaweih and Youssef Mahdi attacked Israeli enemy movements near Bir Kallab outpost. In the meantime, the IR group Ghassan Zaatar and Zahi Haidar Ahmad targeted Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct hits. 

23:15 
The IR group of martyrs Abbas Hijazi and Mohsen Fneish attacked enemy movements at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct goals.

 January 5
6:45 
The IR squad of martyr Abdel-Aziz Kourani attacked enemy movements in and around Radar post with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyr Mohammad Fayad was targeting Hardoun position with the suitable weapons. The position's main fortification was hit directly as the militants reported. 

13:15 
The IR squad of martyrs Abdel-Rassoul Baydoun and Rafik Zahwi targeted an "Israeli" force of two military vehicles near Qabaa post with the rocket-propelled grenades. The force which was fortifying the post was hit directly and the militants were able to damage one of the vehicles. 

16:30 
The IR group of martyrs Zein El-Abedeen Suleiman and Radwan El-Anqouni ambushed the convoy of the 20th Brigade's commander known by Alloush as it was travelling on the road leading to Ain Majdaleen. The mujahedeen blew up a powerful explosive device destroying his car and inflicting a number of his bodyguards. 

20:20 
The IR squad of martyrs Mahmoud Hussein Salman and Ali Kawtharani attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. 
 
 January 6
00:45 
The IR group of martyr Haitham Moghnieh targeted an "Israeli" motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Ghanem and Nabil Akhdar targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct hits. 

6:30 
A group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades attacked Haddatha compound with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. In the meantime, another group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades was targeting Baraasheit compound. The mujahedeen, who used the proper arms, were able to chalk up direct goals 

14:40 
On the occasion of Badr Grand Battle, the IR militants penetrated all the enemy's military procedures when the Engineering Unit's group of martyr Zahi Haidar Ahmad detonated a roadside bomb as an enemy inspection patrol was passing near Beaufort Castle. 

20:30 
The same group set off another powerful explosive device 150 meters only away from the entrance of Beaufort Castle. A command car was hit and burnt immediately whereas its passengers were either killed or injured. Several "Israeli" helicopters landed at the scene to evacuate the casualties. 
 
 January 7
14:15 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Abdallah Sawli and Hussein Mannaa attacked an "Israeli" engineering force as it was fortifying Kassaret Al-Oroush. The mujahedeen who used the rocket-propelled grenades managed to chalk up direct goals 
 
 January 10
The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mreish and Ali Abdel-Wahed attacked at midnight enemy motorized movements at Sojod outpost with the appropriate arms. The militants managed to score direct hits. 

6:30 
The IR group of martyrs Ali Ahmad Nazzal and Ali Sharara targeted enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to chalk up direct goals. 

10:35 
The IR group of martyr Malek Al-Mousawi targeted Ahmadieh position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate goals.
