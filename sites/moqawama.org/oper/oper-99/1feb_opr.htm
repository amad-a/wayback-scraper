 February 1
13:45 
The IR men spotted enemy movements at Ahmadieh position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit group of martyr Mohammad Bjeiji targeted them with the appropriate arms. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain goals. 

Five minutes later, the IR fighters observed enemy movements inside Zimraya position. Shortly afterwards, the ARU squad of martyr Abou Ali Shahla targeted the position with the adequate weapons chalking up accurate hits. 

16:15 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Fayad and Hassan Abou Raya attacked enemy movements inside Soweida outpost with the suitable arms scoring direct and definite hits. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it. 

Five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Tohini and Hassan Slim targeted enemy movements in and around Blat post. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the militants reported. 

16:55 
The IR squad of martyr Youssef Soueli reiterated targeting Blat post with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits. 

23:05 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Abdel-Hassan Qatbay and Soheil Mohammad Diab targeted enemy movements near Dabsheh outpost with the adequate arms. Direct and definite goals were scored the mujahedeen reported. 
Five minutes later, the martyr Hadi Nassrallah Special Forces Brigade's group of martyr Mohammad Hazimeh detonated an explosive device at about one hundred meters away from the "Israeli" posts of Abbad and Dawawir. The explosion occurred as an Israeli foot-patrol was passing nearby.

 February 9
10:45 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Ghazi Al-Husseini and Ghassan Zaatar in the Special Forces of Martyr Hadi Nasrallah's Brigade penetrated into Sojod outpost and killed, certainly, three collaborators, whereas the IR group of martyr Ibrahim Dakdouk managed to inflict two "Israeli" agents and destroy the northern and western fortifications. 12:45 
Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Zahi Haidar Ahmad and Mustafa Chokair detonated  a roadside bomb as an enemy motorized patrol was in its way to back up Sojod outpost. An M-113 armored personnel carrier was destroyed and burned completely whereas another military vehicle was hit also. The enemy troops suffered hysterical state and were heard screaming loudly. 

13l:10 
The IR group of martyrs Fadi Al-Tawil and Hussein Merhi targeted Sojod outpost with the suitable weapons chalking up direct and accurate goals . 

For its part, The Daily Star covered the IR storming of Sojod outpost as follows: 
"In fierce clashes that left five members of the South Lebanon Army* wounded, Hizbullah guerrillas Monday attacked and overran the hilltop Sojod outpost". 

10:45 
"Claiming a major accomplishment, Hizbullah said that a special forces squad at  assaulted the Sojod compound in the Iqlim al-Touffah overlooking the villages of Arab Salim and Jarjoue" 

"At the same time, other teams advanced toward the nearby outposts at Bir Kallab and Rihan to prevent reinforcements from supporting the SLA in Sojod." 

"The statement said that one squad was able to breach the defenses of Sojod and enter the post while a second team destroyed the northern and western fortifications. Two SLA militiamen were wounded in the gun battle". 

"Bir Kallab came under assault from machine guns and anti-tank rockets from one Hizbullah team while another ambushed an "Israeli" patrol as it headed for Sojod", the statement said. 

"Two hours after the fighting began, the guerrillas detonated a roadside bomb near Sojod as an SLA armored patrol passed by, wounding another three militiamen and destroying an armored personnel carrier". 

"Hizbullah said that the successful outcome of the attack came despite intensive measures adopted by the "Israelis" to prevent the resistance from entering the post again. 

"Last August, a Hizbullah guerrilla broke into the Sojod compound, stole some equipment, punched an "Israeli" soldier and departed. The incident embarrassed the "Israeli" army and was a powerful propaganda coup for Hizbullah". 
"The attack on Sojod came two days after an SLA militiaman was killed and another wounded in a roadside bomb blast in 
Houla". 

"Monday night, the SLA position in Houla came under heavy mortar fire by the resistance. Some rounds were reported to have hit "Israeli" border posts. No casualties were immediately reported. Security sources believe that Hizbullah is in the process of stepping up operations after last months lull, with the SLA being the favored target over "Israeli" troops". 

"Elsewhere, the Kfar Falous crossing linking the Jezzine enclave with Sidon is expected to re-open Tuesday morning having been closed since the end of December, security sources said. The road was closed after 17 militiamen deserted their posts in Anan Dec. 31, declaring they were no longer willing to serve in the SLA due to the threat of roadside bomb attacks perpetrated by Hizbullah. Faced with the shortage of manpower, the SLA command decided to redeploy its front line from 
Safaray to Roum and close the Kfar Falous crossing". 

"The pullback extended the no-mans land between the state and the militia from 3 kilometers to around 6 kilometers. A new gate has been installed in Roum which now marks the border of the SLA-controlled enclave" .
