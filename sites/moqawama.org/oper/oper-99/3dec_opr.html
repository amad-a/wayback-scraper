December 21
12:30 
The IR team of martyrs Ahmad Youssef Hammoud and Abdel-Hassan Berro targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled scoring direct hits. 

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Zahreddine and Adnan Fadl Khreis was rushing upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up. 

18:40 
The IR militants observed a Lahdi engineering force, accompanied by a bulldozers as it was beefing up Hemayed position. Then, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Khreibani and Ali Abdallah Krayem targeted it with the adequate weapons barring it from achieving its goals. 

Forty minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Mustafa Kamel Nazar and Fouad Madani ambushed a Lahdi patrol as it was traveling on the road leading to Soweida outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to hit a military vehicle. 

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Saad and Othaina Qansou was targeting the same position with the rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from backing the attacked force. 

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Daoud Fahs and Ali Moussa Nassour rushed upon Tohorra outpost scoring direct goals. 

20:00 
The IR squad of martyrs Qassem Ali Harb and Hassan Khalil Mansour targeted violently Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen mentioned. 

Thirty minutes later, the IR militants spotted enemy military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Youssef Hammoud and Abdel Hassan Berro fell upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled chalking up direct hits. 

20:35 
The IR mujahedeen observed enemy military vehicles moving inside Blat post. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Rida Jaafar Khalil and Ahmad Hassan Trad rushed upon them with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring accurate goals. 

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Farid Said Bazzi and Ali Hassan El-Refahi was attacking enemy motorized movements at Mount Bassil with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to score direct hits. 

Twenty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Nayef Ahmad Rammal and Rida Jaafar Khalil fell upon Sojod compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct and definite goals. 
 
December 26
13:30
The IR team of martyr Hussein Mazloum staged a swift attack against Kfar Houna position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying three of its main fortifications and inflicting a number of casualties. Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyr Ali Midlej was falling upon Shayar Azour with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate hits. 

At the same time, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit in the IR bombarded emplacements at Abou Qamha and Rihan barracks with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals. 

21:15
The IR group of martyrs Mohammad Kamel Abbas and Zaher Fakhri Hemade targeted an "Israeli" motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct hits. 

In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Ibrahim Ali Issa and Salem Mohammad Abboud was falling upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ayad Hussein Ghandour and Ali Mahmoud Assi targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring definite goals. 

"Israeli" radio last night acknowledged that an "Israeli" soldier was wounded in IR's attack on the Blat outpost. 

The radio also admitted that a Lahdi militiaman was wounded in the Sojod outpost. The militiaman was taken to a hospital in occupied Palestine due to his serious injury.
