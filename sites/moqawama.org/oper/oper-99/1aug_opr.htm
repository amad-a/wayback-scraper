 August 3
00:15 
The group of the martyrs Wafic Zahwi and Abed Al-Rasool Baydoun in the Islamic Resistance attacked Talat Al-Mohaisbat on the road towards Al-Taibe Al-Qantara with rockets inflicting direct hits. 

04:30 
The group of the martyrs Kamel Abdullah Abed Al-Rida in the Islamic Resistance attacked the Kalaa Zionist post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits. 

15:10 
The group of the martyrs Saleh Mohamed Harb and Abed Al-Salam Nayef Ayoub in the Islamic Resistance attacked the Hemayid post with appropriate weapons achieving direct and accurate hits, clouds of smoke was witnessed rising from the post. At the meantime the group of the martyrs Hassan Ahmed Jaber and Hasan Mohamed Karaki were attacking Rshaf post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits. 

At the same time the group of the martyrs Mustafa Mohamed Karaki and Mohamed Hasan Yehya attacked the Hadatha post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits in the northern bunkers of the post. 

Also, the group of the martyrs Raad Hussien Fares and Hussien Afif Wehbi attacked the Baraasheet post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits. 

17:40 
The group of the martyr Musa Habib Fahes in the Islamic Resistance resumed its attack on the Hemayid post after the surveillance observed Laheds engineers fortifying their bunkers, the attacks achieved direct hits. 

21:15 
The IR group of martyrs Mustafa Chokair and Ahmad Fadlallah attacked an enemy motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to score direct goals. 

Quarter an hour later, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Krayem and Hussein Ghassani targeted military vehicles inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported. 
 
 August 4
11:05 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Ahmad Freiji and Ghassan Ali Ghanem targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western fortification. 

14:10 
The IR squad of martyrs Sami Ali Al-Takesh and Hadi Mahmoud Mosheimesh attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Rshaf outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct and definite hits. 

23:00 
The IR squad of martyrs Khodor Jouni and Fares Hawi attacked Beaufort Castle with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
 August 5
00:30 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Hassan and Ahmad Haroun attacked enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported. 

3:00 
The IR unit of martyr Nasser Hassan Mansour targeted, at 3:00, "Israeli" military vehicles at the entrance of Radar post burning a number of them. 

At the same time, the IR group of martyr Abdel-Hassan Izzeddine attacked Radar post with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying a number of its fortification. 

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyr Hussein Abdallah Fawaz attacked Hardoun position, whereas the IR team of martyr Mohammad El-Zein rushed upon Shamaa outpost. In the meantime, the IR unit of martyr Ahmad Abdallah Khalifeh was attacking Bayada position. 

3:15 
The IR squad of martyr Hassan Ali El-Sahli targeted enemy groups near Hardoun outpost chalking up direct goals. 

At the same time, the IR team of martyr Hassan Mahmoud Krayem reiterated targeting fiercely Radar post destroying its fortifications where several blazes were seen sparking for more than one hour. 

6:30 
The IR group of martyrs Rida Khreis and Youssef Khalifeh attacked Tair Harfa outpost with the proper weapons. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and certain hits. 

7:45 
The IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Rida and Hussein Trabolssi targeted violently Bayada post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking most of its defenses. 

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Nidal El-Hoq and Raad Fares attacked the same post scoring direct goals. 

Twenty minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Ali Chahla and Mohammad Ali Farhat fell upon Hardoun outpost with the machine guns chalking up direct and certain goals. 

13:00 
The IR group of martyrs Youssef Mahmoud Olleik and Hussein Ali Abdallah attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Rshaf position with the adequate arms. The militants managed to score direct goals. 

23:05 
The IR group of martyrs Abdel-Amir Fadel and Hussein Mosheimesh attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was reinforcing Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to score direct hits and prevent the enemy force from achieving its goals. 
 
 August 6
17:35 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Mohammad Hassan Yahia and Ali Krayem targeted enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct and certain hits. 

22:10 
The IR unit of martyrs Hussein Abdel-Hassan Cherry and Khodor Rida Harb attacked an enemy engineering force as it was fortifying Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the suitable weapons, were able to chalk up direct hits and bar the force from achieving its goals.
 August 7
22:05 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) group of martyrs Hussein Haidar Khazaal and Issa Abdel-Rahim Khazaal attacked swiftly the Israeli post of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Youssef Ahmad El-Husseini and Youssef Haidar Ahmad targeted Salaah position with the proper arms. Direct goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.
 August 8
15:40 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) group of martyrs Bilal Mustafa Hemadi and Mohammad Hassan Jaber attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported. 

20:45 
The IR men spotted a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was traveling on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan. Shortly afterwards, the IR unit of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Hassan and Ahmad Trabolssi targeted it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite hits. 

23:00 
The IR team of martyrs Ghassan Hassan Kobeissi and Hussein Mohammad Haidar attacked an engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able score direct hits and prevent it from achieving its goals. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Issam Kamel Kansou and Bassam Akanan targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported. 
 August 9
1:05 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Ahmad Fadlallah and Ali Krayem targeted a Lahdi force at an area between Qantara and Taibeh towns with the rocket-propelled rockets scoring direct hits. 

12:30 
The IR team of martyrs Mahmoud Sami Abdallah and Mahmoud Taleb attacked enemy movements in and around Haddatha compound with the adequate weapons. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported. 

20:30 
The IR team of martyrs Abbas El-Wazwaz and Samir Akil attacked Blat post with the proper arms as an Israeli helicopter was landing inside it scoring direct hits. 

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Nimr Ali Hemayed and Hassan Kamel Hayek targeted Tair Harfa outpost with the suitable weapons. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported. 

Thirty five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Haidar El-Jouni and Mohammad Hassan Zilzli rushed upon a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain goals. 

23:20 
The a fore said group attacked enemy movements at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking direct hits. 
 
 August 10
20:10 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Mustafa Dib Khodor and Moussa Mohammad Harqous attacked Blat post with the adequate arms scoring accurate hits. 

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Ibrahim Abdel-Jaleel Cherri and Bachir Fouad El-Khalil targeted Hardoun outpost with the appropriate weapons. Accurate goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

The IR unit of martyrs Haitham Dbouk and Assaad Hussein Berro attacked a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to chalk up direct hits. 

One hour later, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Mannaa and Hassan Mahmoud Ghorabi attacked enemy motorized movements near Dabsheh outpost with the suitable weapons. The mujahedeen were able to score direct hits.
