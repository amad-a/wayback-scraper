 March 1
6:25 
The IR team of martyrs Moussa Diab Yehia and Fadl Mohammad Atwi made a swift attack on the Israeli outpost of Dabsheh striking its western fortification. 

12:00 
The IR squad of martyrs Mahmoud Kurani and Hassan Haidous targeted enemy movements at Blat post with the adequate arms scoring accurate hits. 

13:20 
The IR group of martyrs Sharif Akkash and Fadl Soweidan reiterated targeting enemy movements at Blat post chalking up accurate goals. An Israeli bulldozer was defused also. 

18:50 
The IR group of martyrs Mohammad Tehini and Hassan Balhas targeted an "Israel" Merkava tank at the entrance of Blat post with the appropriate arms striking it directly. 
 
 March 2
00:20 
The IR team of martyrs Majed Mahmoud Kobeissi and Ali Hassan Atwi attacked enemy movements at Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals. The smoke columns were seen by the naked eye emanating from it. 

13:45 
The IR squad of martyrs Fadl Mahmoud Atwi and Khodor Ibrahim Khiami targeted enemy movements at Bir Kallab with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goals were chalked up, the militants reported. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Mahmoud El-Amin and Ahmad Abdel-Jalil Cherri attacked an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain hits. 

Half an hour later, the IR team of martyrs Moussa Ahmad Salman and Youssef Rida Bzeih targeted an enemy group coming from Bir Kallab outpost to reinforce the attacked force. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct and certain goals. 

10:30 
A team of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked enemy movements in and around Tair Harfa outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. In the meantime, another two teams of (LRB) targeted Hardoun outpost with the proper weapons chalking up direct and accurate goals. 

 

 March 4
13:20 
The IR team of martyrs Youssef Bzeih and Moussa Salman attacked Shamaa position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and accurate goals. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Khodor Khiami and Ahmad Ossaili targeted Hardoun outpost with the appropriate arms scoring direct and definite hits. 

6:30 
A team of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted enemy movements at Qsair position with the proper arms. Definite goals were scored, the fighters reported.
 March 5
13:45 
The IR men observed enemy movements inside Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyr Mohammad El-Mazbouh targeted the concerned position with the appropriate arms. Accurate hits were chalked up, the militants reported. 

Twenty minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Salman and Moussa Yehia attacked enemy movements near Blat post with the proper weapons. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and certain goals. 

16:10 
The IR squad of martyrs Ali Soueli and Habib Tarhini made a swift attack on the "Israeli" post of Dabsheh with the rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western fortifications. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it as well. 

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Chahrour and Ahmad El-Hariri detonated a roadside bomb as an "Israeli" foot-patrol was passing on the road leading to Beaufort Castle scoring direct goals. 
 
 March 6
15:10 
The IR team of martyr Hassan Ibrahim Rida attacked enemy movements in and around Hemayed position with the suitable weapons scoring direct hits. 

17:30 
The IR squad of martyr Abdallah Ahmad Rammal reiterated targeting Hemayed position with the adequate arms. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported. 

18:30 
The IR group of martyrs Ali Harmoush and Ahmad Kamal attacked enemy movements inside the Israeli post of Radar with the appropriate weapons scoring direct and certain hits. 

One hour and a half, the IR team of martyrs Khodor Khiami and Hassan El-Amin targeted enemy movements in and around Dabsheh position with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals. 
 
 March 7
6:00 
Abbad post. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Rameh Hussein Mahdi and Ahmad Abdel-Jalil Cherri targeted Ramieh position scoring direct hits. 

20:30 
The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mustafa Salman and Mohammad Abbas Faqih attacked an enemy motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the militants reported. 

Ten minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Ali Mahmoud Harmoush and Hussein Nimr Nahim targeted enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the adequate arms. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and definite hits.
 March 8
22:30 
The IR mujahedeen observed enemy movements near Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyr Ismail Youness in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted them scoring direct hits. 

At the same time, the IR men spotted enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost. Immediately afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Olayan and Hussein Salman attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Nazzal and Hassan Balhas targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported. 

22:00 
A group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked Hemayed position with the adequate arms chalking up direct hits. 

At the same time, another group of LRB targeted Rshaf position with the appropriate weapons. The fighters were able to score certain goals. 

 
 March 9
12:45 
The IR team of martyrs Hassan Ghamloush and Rameh Mahdi attacked enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were chalked up. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Abdel-Amir Fadel and Khalil Hammoud targeted an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct hits. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Rabih Wehbi and Jihad Shbeeb attacked Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled greandes. The mujahedeen were able to score direct goals. 

18:30 
The IR team of martyrs Moussa Diab Yehia and Youssef Abdel-Hussein Badran attacked Hardoun outpost with the proper weapons. Accurate goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported. 

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Moussa Nassrallah and Abdallah Ahmad Rammal targeted Tair Harfa position with the suitable arms. The militants were able strike its western fortification. 

20:00 
The IR team of martyrs Ali and Mohammad Ashmar attacked an "Israeli" force near Taibeh position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals. 

20:35 
The IR squad of martyr Wafiq Zahwi targeted intensely, at 20:35, Taibeh position with the rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and definite hits. 

At the same time, the IR group of martyr Abdel-Rassoul Baydoun targeted an "Israeli" Merkava tank as it was shelling Slouqi Valley forcing it to retreat. 

 
 March 10
15:15 
The IR squad of martyrs Hussein Mohammad Salman and Hassan Ibrahim Rida attacked Taibeh position chalking up direct and definite goals. At the same time, the IR team of martyrs 
Ahmad Mohammad Olayan and Ali Ahmad Nazzal targeted enemy movements near Taibeh position. The militants scored direct and certain hits. 

Half an hour later, the IR group of martyr Hussein Kassem blew up an explosive device as a Lahdi foot-patrol was traveling on the road linking between Roum and Azour inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.
 March 11
2:15 
The IR squad of martyr Fadl Atwi targeted Haddatha compound with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals. Meanwhile, the IR team of martyr Hassan Mahmoud El-Amin was attacking the old Beit Yahoun outpost with the appropriate weapons. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported. 

21:30 
The IR group of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Hussein and Hussein Abdallah Moghnieh attacked a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was passing on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct hits. 

6:20 
Two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns. The fighters were able to chalk up direct goals.
