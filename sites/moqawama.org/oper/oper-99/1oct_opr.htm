October 1
22:50
The Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen observed enemy movements near Zimraya position. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Haidar Ismail and Nouh Hassan targeted them with the adequate arms scoring direct goals.
October 2
17:45
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Hassan Issa and Ismail Abdel-Jalil targeted a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking its southern fortification.
October 3
9:30
The Islamic Resistance (IR) men spotted a Lahdi engineering force which was fortifying Zimraya outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Mahmoud Hammoud and Abdo Fahd in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded it with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate hits.

6:50
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters staged a swift attack on Hardoun compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying its main fortifications.

However, Amal Movement fired four 107mm rocket-propelled grenades at Salaah position.
October 4
Amal Movement fired three 81mm mortar rounds at the surroundings of Hemayed position.
October 5
12:10
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Hassan Khalil Cherri and Abdel-Melhem Qassir attacked fiercely the "Israeli" post of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Berrou and Ali Mohammad Serhal rushed upon Salaah position with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

15:30
The IR squad of martyrs Imad Ali Balhas and Hassan Youssef Balhas targeted Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to hit a 12.7mm heavy machine gun and the northern fortification.
October 6
14:15
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Ahmad Isamil Ossaili and Ali Ibrahim Hassan fell upon Blat position with the appropriate weapons. Certain hits were scored, the militants reported. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Fadlallah Chalhoub and Karim Maarouf Atwi attacked the aforesaid position with the rocket-propelled grenades hitting its eastern fortification. 

5:45
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters targeted enemy squad at Soweida outpost with the suitable arms scoring direct goals. 

12:20
The LRB fighters attacked enemy movements at the "Israeli" post of Dabsheh with the proper weapons chalking up direct and accurate hits.

18:30
The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Khalil Cherri and Abdel-Menhem Qassir attacked fiercely Blat post with the adequate weapons scoring direct and accurate goals.

21:15
The IR group of martyrs Hussein Qabalan and Ahmad Fouani targeted Houla post with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking direct hits.

23:30
The IR team of martyrs Ghassan Ghanem and Ahmad Jalloul attacked Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and accurate hits.
 
October 7
6:05
The IR team of martyrs Ibrahim Abbas Zoheir and Hussein Mohammad El-Tawil attacked fiercely the Israeli post of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain goals.

21:00
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters targeted enemy squad inside Mashaaroun outpost with rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.
October 10
17:15
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Hassan Ali Abdallah and Mohammad Ali Darwish attacked an Israeli Infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush area with the machine guns and hand grenades. Certain casualties among the force were inflicted, the militants reported.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Hassan Mohammad Jaghbir and Mohammad Hussein Hammoud targeted the Israeli barracks of Rihan with the rocket-propelled grenades, whereas the IR squad of martyr Naameh Mohammad Hseiki fell upon the collaborator barracks of the 10th Brigade Command with the rocket-propelled grenades. The smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

Fifteen minutes later, an Israeli tank near Rihan tried to back up the attacked force, but the IR group of martyr Rifaat Ali Olleik targeted it with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

22:45
The IR men spotted an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush area. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Nabil Mohammad Akhdar and Hussein Khalil Cherri rushed upon it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
