 May 1

19:25 
The IR group of martyr Mohammad Hussein Kassem attacked an Israeli force at Rihan compound scoring direct and certain hits. 

22:00 
The IR squad of martyr Hassan Hani Obeid targeted enemy motorized movements at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct and definite goals. At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Salman and Mohammad Faqih attacked enemy movements inside Blat post. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.
 

 May 2

00:30 
The IR group of martyr Ali Najib Choaib targeted Kfar Houna position with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits. 

11:45 
The IR teams of martyrs Ahmad Ali Ramadan and Abbas Kazem Hoteit attacked a Lahdi force as it was beefing up Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and certain goals. 

22:50 
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Tanana and Kassem Jaber attacked enemy movements near Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits. 

At the same time, the IR group of martyr Assaad Shabshoul targeted Hardoun outpost with the proper weapons destroying some of its installations. 

Meanwhile, the IR team of martyr Mohammad El-Horr attacked Tair Harfa position with the appropriate arms. Direct and definite goals were scored, the militants reported. 
 

 May 3

8:00 
The IR team of martyrs Ahmad Yassine and Hassan Kheireddine Hassan in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted enemy movements inside Hardoun outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported. 

9:15 
The IR squad of martyrs Abbas Hassan and Ali Moussa Nassrallah attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to bar it from achieving its goals. 

21:45 
The IR team of martyr Hassan El-Akhras targeted a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.
 

 May 4

18:00 
The IR men spotted enemy movements inside Ahmadieh position. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyr Ahmad Al-Mousawi attacked them with the adequate arms. Direct and accurate goals were chalked up, the militants reported. 

23:20 
The IR squad of martyr Ghassan Ghanem attacked enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct hits. 

Ten minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Abbas Hassan and Osama Hakim attacked enemy movements inside Beaufort Castle with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. 

23:40 
The IR team of martyr Hassan Adnan Kheireddine attacked enemy movements in and around Haddatha compound with the suitable arms chalking up direct goals.
 

 May 5

21:50 
The IR team of martyrs Talal Assaghir and Zoheir Tabatabai attacked motorized convoy as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported. 

23:55 
The IR squad of martyr Hassan Hussein Balhas targeted, at 23:55, enemy movements in and around Hemayed position with the proper arms chalking up direct goals. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyr Fadi Hassan Melhem was attacking enemy movements in and around Haddatha compound. The militants, who used the adequate weapons, were able to chalk up direct hits.
 

 May 6

The IR militants observed, at midnight exactly, an "Israeli" proxy force beefing up Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Abdel-Karim Yassine and Adnan Kheireddine targeted it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits and barring it from achieving its goals. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Mohammad Hussein Kassem attacked enemy movements in and around Baraasheit compound with the adequate arms. The mujahedeen managed to score direct hits. 

2:35 
The IR group of martyr Abbas Ali Hassan attacked enemy movements in and around Haddatha compound with the suitable weapons. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported. 

Five minutes later, the IR team of martyr Asaad Mohammad Shabshoul targeted enemy movements at the old Beit Yahoun position with the proper arms. The mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct goals. 

13:50 
The IR men spotted enemy movements inside Zimraya position. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyr Mahdi Shamas targeted them with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. 

15:40 
The IR mujahedeen observed enemy movements near Zimraya position. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyr Ismail Younes in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit bombarded them chalking up direct goals.

The IR team of martyrs Mohammad Atwi and Hassan Balhas targeted enemy movements inside Blat post with the suitable weapons. Direct and certain goals were chalked up.
