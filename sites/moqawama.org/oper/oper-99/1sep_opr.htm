15:00

12:45 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Hassan Qassem Aqil and Ossama Rida Hammoudi attacked enemy movements at Jamoussa post, HQ of the 80th Brigade, with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

The IR team of martyrs Hassan Mohammad Abdel-Jalil and Ibrahim Khalil Farhat targeted an "Israeli" infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct and definite goals.

September 10
17:55 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Issa Makki and Ismail Bilon attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were scored, the militants reported. 

19:00 
The IR unit of martyrs Abdel-Hamid Tokko and Zakariah Harb targeted an "Israeli" motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and definite hits. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Jawad Ali Zein and Hussein Hammoud fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were scored. 

20:35 
The IR militants spotted enemy military vehicles entering Bir Kallab outpost. Immediately afterwards, the 
IR group of martyrs Hussein Shouman and Abbas El-Ansari rushed upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals. 

23:00
