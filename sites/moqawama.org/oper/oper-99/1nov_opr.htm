November 1
15:30 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Mohammad Mahdi El-Husseini and Hassan Adel El-Akhras attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and certain goals. 

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Chaouki Ali El-Jawhar and Bilal Ahmad Taha fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.
November 2
16:50 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Qassem Jaber and Abdallah Chaitou attacked an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain hits. 

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Abdel-Mawla Ismail and Ali Wahab Hijazi fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and definite goals. 

5:45 
A group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted an enemy squad inside Qsair position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring hits. 

At the same time, another group of LRB attacked Salaah position with the artillery mortars chalking up direct goals. 

5:40 
Amal Movement fired several 81mm rounds at Salaah position. Also, the Movement attacked, at 12:25, Taibeh outpost with two rocket-propelled grenades.

14:10

Twenty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Ali Yassine and Riad Youssef Dimashq fell upon enemy movements inside Hemayed position. Using the proper weapons, the militants managed to score direct hits.

Two hours later, the IR team of martyrs Safa Naamat Baydoun and Younes Youssef El-Bazzal rushed upon Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and definite hits.

The IR team of martyrs Khodor Hussein Obeid and Ahmad Ismail Ossaili attacked an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants told.
