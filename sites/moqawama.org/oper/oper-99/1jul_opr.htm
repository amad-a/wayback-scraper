 Jun 2
18:10 
The IR squad of martyrs Khodor Zawadi and Ali Youssef Baalbacki attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain goals where a blaze was enkindled.
 Jun 3
1:15 
The IR team of martyrs Hussein Hassan Hayek and Riyad Akhdar attacked an "Israeli" infantry force near Beaufort Castle with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.
 Jun 4
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants spotted, at midnight exactly, military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Al-Borji and Haidar Salhab attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals. 

14:00 
Also, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Ali Ahmad and Yasser Nayef Waridan attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was building a road near Mount Abou Rkab. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to strike a bulldozer in which a blaze was sparked.
 Jun 6
22:30 
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Haidar Youssef Al-Borji and Yasser Nayef Waridan attacked enemy movements at Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct goals.

 Jun 8
8:30 
The IR team of martyrs Hassan Ibrahim Sbeiti and Mohammad Ali Safieddine attacked enemy movements inside Bir Kallab with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain hits. 

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Nayef Assaad and Mohammad Ali Soueidan targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported. 

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Malek Ali Akhdar and Ali Hussein Ghamloush was attacking an "Israeli" enemy force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and definite goals.
 Jun 9
21:55 
The IR squad of martyrs Nimr Ftouni and Nasser Hawi detonated a powerful roadside bomb as an "Israeli" armored force was passing near Baraasheit compound disrupting a military vehicle and inflicting a number of casualties among its crew.
