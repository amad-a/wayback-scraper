December 2
17:25
The IR team of martyrs Jawdat Ali Olleik and Ahmad Chehab attacked Lahdi enemy movements inside Baraasheit compound with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.

20:00
The IR squad of martyrs Mustafa Haidar and Abbas Nazar attacked a Lahdi motorized force as it was traveling on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct and certain hits.

22:05
The IR mean observed enemy movements inside Sojod outpost. The IR group of martyrs Mohammad Tanana and Abbas El-Wazwaz fell upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.
December 3
6:15
For its part, The IR team of martyrs Hussein Abdel-Latif Jawad and Wissam Abdel-Hussein Zayour attacked the Israeli post of Dabsheh with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate hits. The smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Daoud Fahs and Talal Mohammad Hassan El-Saghir fell upon Beaufort Castle with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen told.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Jaafar Habib Harb and Hassan Mohammad El-Hajj Ali rushed upon Soweida outpost. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to hit its western and northern fortifications.

Quarter an hour later, the IR unit of martyrs Hassan Hallal and Ali Chehab staged a swift attack at the Israeli post of Radar causing damage to its equipments and western fortifications.

Meanwhile, the IR team of martyrs Youssef Baddah and Hussein Hammoud was targeting Hardoun outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Zaatar and Hussein Ahmad Trabolssi rushed upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking its northern fortification.

5:45
Amal Movement shelled Baraasheit compound with several artillery rounds

December 8
16:25 
In retaliation FOR the massacre committed by the Israeli occupation forces against Mansouri village and its population, the IR group of martyrs Ghassan Mohammad Zaatar and Haitham Afif Ghamloush ambushed an Israeli infantry force at Radar position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to inflict a number of casualties among its ranks. 

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Allam Soueidan and Ali Abdallah Bakri in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) rushed upon Radar position with the artillery shells. A radar installation was damaged whereas the blazes were seen enkindling the western fortification. Also, it was reported that several casualties were suffered. 

12:35 
The IR team of martyrs Khalil Mohammad Madi and Ahmad Mahmoud Mansour attacked an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported. 

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Mohammad Harb and Majed Mohsen Kansou was rushing upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. 

6:30 
The groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked the positions of Shamaa, Hardoun and Bayada with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying a 12.7mm heavy machine gun and scoring direct goals. 

Fifteen minutes later, the artillery unit of the (LRB) bombarded the positions of Radar and Blat damaging a radar installation and scoring other goals.

15:50

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Samer Hussein Ghizzawi and Adnan Hassan Farhat rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying its western fortification.
