 January 1

2:35 
Three 155mm missiles fell on the area between Baraasheit and Rshaf vilalges, whereas the surroundings of Baraasheit compound exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. 

2:40 
Four 155mm smoke missiles struck the perimeters of Baraasheit, while at the same time, four 81mm rounds hit the surroundings of Baraasheit compound. 

2:55 
Baraasheit town subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. 

5:45 
The surroundings of Baraasheit compound came under 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire. 

6:00 
A barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds hit Beit Yahoun outpost. 

19:50 
Mount Sojod was bombarded by twelve 120mm shells. 

20:00 
Five 120mm shells slammed Mount Al-Rafih. 

20:05 
Five 120mm rounds struck Mount Sojod. 

21:30 
The area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem towns was targeted by seven 155mm missiles. 

22:45 
Two 105mm rounds and one 120mm shell hit the area of Rous Al-Ifranj in Jezzine District in addition to firing several flare shells overhead. 

23:15 
Four rounds fell on Rous Al-Ifranj, whereas Owali River banks exposed to one 120mm shell. 
 

 January 2

The surroundings of Kassaret Al-Oroush compound subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. 

14:40 
Three 155mm missiles hit west of Yater village. 

15:40 
Seventeen 155mm missiles pounded south of Jibal El-Botom village. 

16:00 
The perimeters of Jibal El-Botom came under five 155mm missiles. 

16:45 
Mount Sojod was bombarded by ten 120mm shells. 

16:50 
Three 120mm shells landed the peripheries of Loweizeh village.

Six 81mm rounds hit Hala Hill.

The surroundings of Sojod outpost subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

21:25 
Mount Al-Jabbour came under twenty 105 and 120mm rounds.

 January 5

Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by thirteen 155mm missiles. 

4:05 
Twelve 155mm missiles pounded the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village. 

13:35 
Qaissieh Valley came under five 155mm missiles.

Sixty 81 and 120mm shells slammed the hilltops of Ain El-Tineh, south of Mashghara, the 1700th Hill, Zawarib area and Bayada heights.
