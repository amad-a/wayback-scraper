 February 5
7:20 
An "Israeli" fighter bomber raided southern Lebanon. The jet fired two missiles near the village of Mansouri east of the port city of Tyre and just north of the zone "Israel" occupies. A warplane also fired a missile near the village of Majdel Zoun. The house of Abdullah Kassab was damaged. Lebanon is expected to file a complaint to the monitoring group. The cease-fire bans attacks on and from civilian areas but permits the Lebanese resistance to attack "Israeli" occupation troops in the south. No casualties were inflicted in either raid" .
