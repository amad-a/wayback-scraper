September 1
11:15 
One 120mm shell hit Mansouri plain igniting a blaze. 

11:25 
The "Israeli" fighter-bombers raided into the area between Jibaa and Arab Salim towns.

Between 17:15 and 17:40 
The perimeters of Loweizeh came under seven 120mm shells and five 155mm missiles. 

17:15 
Five 120mm shells struck the peripheries of Jibaa. 

Between 17:25 and 18:25 
Arid El-Zennar area was bombarded by thirty five 120mm shells. 

17:30 
Three 120mm shells landed Mount Abou Rkab. 

17:40 
Mount Sojod was shelled by four 155mm missiles. 
 
September 2
Between 00:25 and 1:30 
Mount Bassil was shelled by twelve 155mm missiles. 

9:05 
Mount Sojod was bombarded by fifteen missiles and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets, whereas Tasseh Well watercourse exposed to two shells. 

16:30 
Twenty five 155mm missiles slammed into the 910th area and the hilltops of Bir Zahr and Daidaba. 

Between 16:55 and 17:25 
Libbaya village and its perimeters came under more than twenty five 130 and 155mm missiles. The "Israeli" shells killed the civilians Fatima Ibrahim Al-Jabli, 35, Hassan Nimr Akel, 18, and wounded Mona Haidar El-Aouta, 35, and her daughter Aya Hussein Akel, 15, and Hassan Ali Akel.
September 3
1:35

1:45 
The west of Yater village was shelled by seven 155mm missiles. 

1:50 
Three 155mm missiles landed Jib Soweid area. 

1:55 
Qaissieh Valley was targeted by seven 155mm missiles. 

Between 2:10 and 3:05 
The peripheries of Kfar Dounine town came under fifteen 155mm missiles. 

2:30 
The "Israeli" fighter-bombers staged a sortie into the perimeters of Kfar Dounine. 

2:45 
Five 155mm missiles struck the peripheries of Choukine-Jibsheit.

1:45

18:50

19:20 
It was reported that an "Israeli" warboat was seen cruising off the seashore of Ghazieh town. 

21:00
