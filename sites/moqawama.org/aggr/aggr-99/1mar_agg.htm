 March 1

00:10 
Two 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Markaba. 

Between 5:30 and 6:30 
Tasseh Well watercourse was bombarded by thirty two different rounds. 

6:30 
Mount Sojod was shelled by seven 155mm missiles. 

Between 11:30 and 12:15 
The perimeters of Qilya and Zillaya, the 910th Hill, Daidaba hilltops and Litani River watercourse came under about one hundred and fifty 105,155 and 160mm missiles. 

Between 11:45 and 13:10 
Fifteen 155mm missiles slammed into the south of Jibal El-Botom village. 

12:15 
One 155mm missile hit west of Yater village. 

12:55 
The west of Yater was targeted by one 155mm missile. 

13:15 
Beaufort Castle showered its surroundings and the eastern quarter of Arnoun with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds wounding the civilian Mohammad Nasser Ollaiq. 

14:05 
The "Israeli" warplanes raided into Litani River watercourse. 

15:00 
Several 160mm missiles hit Litani River banks striking the water station that supplies the villages of Zillaya, Qilya and Dallafeh. 

15:30 
Several rounds fell on Litani River watercourse and the perimeters of Qilya. 

17:25 
Slouqi Valley was pounded by eight 155mm missiles. 

17:30 
Four 81mm rounds struck Bayada area beneath to Qabaa post. 

20:05
Ten 155mm missiles fell on the outskirts of Majdal Zoun and five others on Zibqeen ravine. In the meantime, the west of Yater village exposed to three 155mm missiles.

Between 20:25 and 22:55
The "Israeli" warplanes staged more than ten sorties into the outskirts of Khorbet Selem town.

20:25
Three 155mm missiles struck Slouqi Valley.

20:30
The "Israeli" jets raided in Khawam Hotel at Baalbeck.

20:35
The "Israeli" bombers staged a sortie into the perimeters of Jibaa town.

20:35
Mazraat Hamra was shelled by 155mm missiles, whereas the area between Zibqeen and Remadieh came under four 155mm missiles.

20:45
The "Israeli" warplanes dropped one air-to-ground rocket on Naameh area.

Between 20:48 and 21:20
The "Israeli" bombers staged three raids into the perimeters of Jibaa town.

20:50
The "Israeli" warplanes raided into a ravine between Kafra and Deir Intar.

22:35
Srairi hilltops came under five 155mm missiles.

23:20
Four 155mm missiles were fired at the west of Zibqeen village.
