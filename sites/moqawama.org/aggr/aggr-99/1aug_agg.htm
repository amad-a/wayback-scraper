 August 3
06:20 
Two 120mm shells landed on the Sujud mountain and a 155mm shell from Al-Zafateh on the same place. 

07:35 
Four 155mm shells on Abu Rkab mountain. 

07:55 
Six 155mm shells on Abu Rkab mountain. 

15:15 
Sporadic firing within the vicinity of the Hadatha post and between Hadatha and Aita. 

15:20 
Bombing of Jal Al-Tayaran with 155mm shells. 

15:20 
Five 155mm shells landed in the area of Teir Harma west of Yater. 

15:25 
Two 81mm shells landed inside the village of Barasheet and very close to civilian homes, another two shells of the same caliber landed in Kalat Aita as well as firing 12.7mm caliber machine guns towards the outskirts of Aita. 

15:30 
Sporadic firing between Hadatha and Aita. 

15:45 
Sporadic firing with 12.7mm machine guns inside the village of Barasheet which resulted in the burning of agriculture in the area of Al-Ain as well as firing the same caliber inside the village of Hadatha. 

16:55 
"Israeli war planes raided the outskirts of Kafra between Harees and Kafra which resulted in the destruction of the deserted home of Hasan Mohamed Hijazi as well as damaging the homes of Mohamed Abed Al-Hussien Hijazi and Mohamed Abed Al-Hussien Gharabi. 

18:10 
Four 155mm missiles hit the perimeters of Haddatha. 

Between 20:45 and 21:15 
Mount Sojod was targeted by three 155mm missiles and one 120mm shell. 

21:15 
The peripheries of Mlikh subjected to three 155mm missiles. 

Between 21:30 and 21:50 
Seven 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Jibaa.
