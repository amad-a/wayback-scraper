  May 1
 
00:30 
Soweida outpost combed the outskirts of Habboush with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire. 

00:35 
Tohorra outpost raked the perimeters of Kfar Romman with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. 

7:50 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Nabatieh region. 

8:00 
An M.K. reconnaissance plane flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights. 

8:05 
The reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Central Beqaa areas. 

8:25 
The "Israeli" choppers and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights. 

9:45 
Two helicopters hovered over Taibeh Project. 

10:20 
The warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region. 

11:10 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over the area between Touline and Qalaouieh villages. 

12:45 
The peripheries of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri came under eight 120mm shells emanating from Hardoun outpost and fifteen others emanating from Shamaa position. 

16:15 
Tair Harfa outpost unleashed seven 120mm shells at Jib Soweid area. 

16:20 
The "Israeli" helicopters gunships raided into the area between Mansouri and Majdal Zoun firing one air-to-ground rocket. 

18:25 
Tair Harfa bombarded the area of Zibqeen Crushing Plant with thirteen 130mm shells. 

18:30 
The perimeters of Zibqeen and Qlayleh villages came under thirteen 81 and 120mm shells emanating from the positions of Tair Harfa and Hardoun. 

19:10 
It was reported that an "Israeli" warship was seen cruising off the seashore of Tyre heading northward. 

20;15 
A reconnaissance plane flew over Nabatieh region. 

21:20 
Baraasheit position fired two 60mm flash shells over Baraasheit village. 

22:35 
Shraifeh position bombarded Durzi Fort and Bir Zahr with 20 missiles. 

23:05 
Bir Zahr Durzi Fort came under ten shells emanating from Shraifeh position. 
 
 
 
  May 2
 
4:00 and until 5:00 
Zaghleh position bombarded the areas of Naameh and Dallafeh Bridge with twenty 160mm missiles. 

5:30 
Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire. 

5:35 
Bir Kallab outpost showered its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. 

6:05 
Six 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Safi Mountain. 

6:20 
Tair Harfa outpost targeted Jib Soweid area with three 130mm shells. 

6:25 
Four 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan fell on Safi Mountain. 

6:45 
Zohaira position shelled Tair Harfa with two 155mm missiles. 

6:50 
Zaffateh position unleashed eight 155mm missiles at the peripheries of Jibaa village. 

10:05 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Zibqeen and Majdal Zoun villages. 

13:15 and until 14:25 
The "Israeli"-occupied posts of Zaghleh, Shraifeh and Abou Qamha bombarded Litani River bands and the villages of Zillaya and Qilya with one hundred and fifty 155 and 175mm missiles. 

13:40 
The "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights. 

14:05 
An "Israeli" warboat was seen patrolling opposite to the coast of Khaizaran and Tyre. 

15:10 
Shraifeh position fired twelve 155mm missiles at Litani River bands. 

16:05 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Malkieh and Chaitieh villages. 

16:55 
Al-Rafih Mountain was bombarded by twenty 155mm missiles emanating from Shraifeh position. 

19:10 
Shraifeh position fired twelve 155mm missiles at Litani River bands. 

20:45 
Safi Mountain came under three 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position. 

20:55 
Zaffateh position targeted Safi Mountain with four 155mm missiles and the peripheries of Jibaa village with several others. 

21:55 
A drone reconnaissance plane flew, at low altitude, over Jibsheit and Adsheit villages. 

22:30 
Hardoun position fired 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire at the road of Majdal Zoun. At the same time, a drone reconnaissance aircraft soared over Western Sector of South Lebanon, whereas another reconnaissance plane flew over Shaqra, Baraasheit and the nearby areas. 

23:40 
A drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle height, over Borj Qalaouieh, Qalaouieh, Ghandourieh, Froun and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr villages. 
 
 
 
  May 3
 
3:15 
The "Israeli" jets flew in the airspace of Beirut. 

7:00 
Zaffateh position targeted Safi Mountain with three 155mm missiles. 

7:25 
Two 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan village struck Mlita Hill. 

7:30 
Shraifeh position pounded Durzi Fort with five 155mm missiles. 

7:40 
Sojod outpost targeted Sojod Mountain with three 81mm shells. 

8:50 
Litani River was bombarded by fifteen 155 and 175mm missiles emanating from Shraifeh position. 

9:20 
Eight 155mm shells emanating from Shraifeh position struck Bir Zahr hilltops and Durzi Fort. 

9:25 
Abou Qamha position unleashed nine 160mm missiles at Durzi Fort and Bir Zahr hills. 

10:40 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights. 

11:20 
The "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region. 

11:30 
The warplanes flew over Nabatieh region. 

13:05 
Berkat Naqieh near Shaqra village came under thirty five 105 and 120mm shells, several 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire emanating from Jamaah position. 

13:00 
One 105mm shell struck between the houses of Sultanieh village. Meanwhile, Mohaibib outpost combed its surroundings and the southern part of Shaqra village where there were farmers tendering tobacco plants. 

13:20 
Houla position fired ten 105mm rounds at Maghilan area, while Mashaaroun outpost unleashed nine 120mm shells at Aissieh Valley. 

13:30 
One 120mm shell struck Sultanieh village near the house of Mohammad Ali Yassine which hit by several shrapnels. 

15:00 
Safi Mountain exposed to four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area. 

16:30 
Mohaibib outpost unleashed more than six 105 and 120mm shells at Berkat Naqieh and four 105 tank rounds at the southern part of Shaqra village. 

18:45 
Shamaa post bombarded Majdal Zoun with twenty 155mm missiles, two of which were cluster. The missiles exploded between its houses but no casualties were reported. At the same time, Shraifeh position bombarded Litani River bands and the surroundings of Zillaya and Qilya villages with more than sixty 155 and 175mm missiles. 

20:15 
Hardoun and Shamaa positions raked Majdal Zoun with heavy machine gun fire hitting the house of Rida Ali Salman whose family survived miraculously. 

22:05 
A drone reconnaissance plane flew over Majdal Zoun village. 

22:20 
A drone reconnaissance plane flew over Kafra and Yater villages, when on the other hand, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared high over Aiteit, Qana and Hanaoueih villages. 

22:25 
A drone reconnaissance plane flew over the Southern coast, whereas an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights. 

22:45 
Five 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Safi Mountain. 

23:00 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Northern Beqaa areas. 

23:30 
Zaffateh position fired sixteen 155mm missiles at the perimeters of Jibaa village. 

23:35 
Nine 155mm shells emanating from Zaffateh position fell on the peripheries of Jibaa village. 

23:40 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Maifadoun villages. 

23:50 
The "Israeli" choppers hovered, at middle altitude, over Litani River. 
 
 
 
  May 4
 
00:15 
A drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Deir Ames and Mahrouna areas. 

1:25 
A reconnaissance plane flew over Froun and Ghandourieh villages. 

6:00 
Zaffateh position targeted Safi Mountain with three 155mm missiles. 

6:45 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah. 

10:05 
The "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of South Lebanon, in general, and, at low altitude, over Nabatieh region, in particular. 

10:30 and until 11:15 
An M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Touline, Qabrikha and Majdal Selem villages. 

10:55 
The warplanes flew in the airspace of South Lebanon. 

11:10 
The "Israeli" jets flew, at middle and low altitudes, over Central Sector of South Lebanon. 

11:45 
The warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah, East Saida and Nabatieh regions. 

12:55 
The "Israeli" jets flew low over Nabatieh region. 

13:50 
The warplanes flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah, Froun and Ghandourieh and conducted mock air strikes over Nabatieh area. At the same time, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Saida region. 

16:30 
A reconnaissance plane flew over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Kfar Seir villages. 

16:40 
Zaffateh position fired nine 155mm missiles at Safi Mountains and Mlita hilltops. 

16:45 
Two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Arid Zennar area. 

16:50 
Aqmata area exposed to two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
