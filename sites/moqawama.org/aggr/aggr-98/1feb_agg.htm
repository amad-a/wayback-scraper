Israeli Repressive Actions: 

The Israeli soldiers and their collaborators stormed into Hibbarieh and Fardis, Hasbaya District, and took the civilians Khazai Barakat, Talih Hassan Abou Rashed, 25, and Aiman Laqqis to Khiam detention center. At Odaisseh village also, the woman civilian Hanan Hassan El-Assaad 45, was arrested for questioning. El-Assaad is a wife of the Civil Administrative Official of Marjeyoun Kamel Abdallah, 55, who was arrested & accused, five days ago, of drug dealing between the occupied zone and Palestine. 

However, the Israeli occupation forces closed, suddenly, Zaghleh-Meimes crossing point which is dedicated for the university students who were surprized by this repressive step, knowing that this crossing point had been  opened a month ago after a closure for twenty years. 
The students appealed the International and local officials to work quickly with the aim of reopening it as soon as possible. 

Also, Odaisseh population reported, in a statement, that Israeli troops had besieged the village since January-14-1998 leading to a critical health and economical situations. Furthermore, the Israeli soldiers & their collaborators have been harassing the residents through interrogating and beating them and through searching the houses and damaging their furniture. 
The population called the officials, MPs and International Red Cross Committee to do their best in order to lift the siege of Odaisseh.
