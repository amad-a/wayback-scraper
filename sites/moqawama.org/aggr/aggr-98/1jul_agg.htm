 July 2

6:00 
Beit Yahoun fired several mortar 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at the perimeters of Aita El-Jabal village. Meanwhile, the helicopters hovered over Beit Yahoun and Haddatha positions. 

6:15 
Zaffateh position bombarded Mlita hill with ten 155mm missiles and Aqmata area with four others. 

6:30 
The batteries stationed at the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal shelled Haddatha woodlands with several 155mm missiles and Ain Al-Mizrab area with three others, whereas Zaffateh position shelled Mlita hill with five 155mm missiles. 

At the same time, the 17th Israeli-Lahdi common center unleashed ten 120mm shells at Bayad quarter of Haddatha village, while the perimeters of Safad El-Battikh and Aita El-Jabal came under a lot of shells emanating from Meis El-Jabal position and the 17th Center. 
In the meantime, it was reported that various kinds of shells struck the peripheries of Baraasheit, Safad El- Battikh, Aita El-Jabal, Haris Valley and Haddatha, whereas a tank stationed at Qawzah position fired several 105mm missiles at Yater village killing the citizen Abdel-Menhem Sweidan, 65. 

7:00 
The Israeli fighters and reconnaissance planes flew very low over the Central Sector of South Lebanon. This was accompanied by staging mock raids overhead. At the same time, the warplanes raided into the western perimeters of Baraasheit village. 

7:15 
Zaffateh position shelled the peripheries of Jibaa with four 155mm missiles, whereas five others emanating from Meis El-Jabal area fell on Haddatha forest.
