Details 19:00 

The "Israeli" fighters launched two air raids into Naameh, their 12th air strike on the town, 16 km south of Beirut. The "Israeli" jet airplanes fired four air-to-ground rockets at the social, human and health offices of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-General Command (PFLP-GC). The two sorties, 10 minutes apart, caused damage but no casualties, a PFLP-GC spokesman said. These raids drew fire from the anti-aircraft guns of the Lebanese Army.
