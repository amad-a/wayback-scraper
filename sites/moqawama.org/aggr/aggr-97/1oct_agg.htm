Details 6:00 
The Zionist occupation gunners unleashed several 155 mm shells at the outskirts of Arnoun, Hamra, Kfar Romman, Kfar Tibbnit and Litani River, whereas Nabatieh El-Fawqa came under several flechette shells causing damage of four houses related to the civilians: Abdel-Hassan Qoaik, Mohammad Yehia Jawad Ramadan, Mohammad Ali Ghandour and Hisham Yehia Jawad. 

Flechette shells, which contain hundreds of inch-long darts and are anti-personnel weapons, are banned by a Geneva Convention, yet Israeli enemy, routinely, fires them into populated areas in the South and Western Beqaa. 

Mohammad Ali Ghandour's family were, miraculously, survived as dozen shrapnels of the flechette shells struck everywhere of their home whereas Hisham Yehia Jawad's BMW - 525 brown car was damaged. The Israeli shelling hit, also, the electricity network of the area.
