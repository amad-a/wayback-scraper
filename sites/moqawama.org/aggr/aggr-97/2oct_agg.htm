The Israeli warplanes rocketed a Palestinian civil offices in Naameh, the second raid in three days.

Two missiles were fired at a civil office belonging to the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-General Command . No casualties were reported. The Lebanese Army units fired at the warplanes with its anti-aircraft guns deployed in the region.

PFLP-GC issued a statement in which it said that an Israeli squadron of American-made F-16 a raided into human and social centers located at Naameh launching several heavy air-to-surface rockets sparking blazes and causing material damage only.

The Daily Star reported that the air raid came as the Israeli army is, increasingly, worried over its mounting casualties in the south, 39 so far this year. Of immediate concern is the destruction of two Merkava tanks in the last week by Hizbullah fighters.
