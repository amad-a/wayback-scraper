15:10
Two Israeli warplanes launched two air raids firing a total of four rockets into hill of Toumat Niha in the South-Western part of Mashghara village. The Israeli warplanes were faced by anti-aircraft artilleries of Lebanese Army and Islamic Resistance.

In occupied Al-Quds, an Israeli military spokesman said that the air raid aimed at the Islamic Resistance's positions. It was the 39 th air raid into Lebanon this year.

" There was no immediate word on casualties in the raid, which was followed by heavy Israeli shelling of area", Lebanese security sources said.

" Shelling is spreading into the outskirts of Mashghara town, coming increasingly closer to homes', a security source added.

However, the Israeli occupation forces shelled Al-Wadi Al-Akhadar, Al-Nabatieh, and Litani River, Iqleem Al-Tuffah region, causing a blaze near Arabsalim village.

In Central Sector, hill of Al-Arish, located between Haddatha and Reshaf, came under artillery fire emanating from the Israeli forces located at Jabal Hemayed.

On morning, the warplanes had been flying in the airspace of South, Western Beqaa and Beirut, the capital, where they broke the sound barrier.

On the other hand, Mohammad Ramadan was injured by shrapnel from an old bomb which exploded while he was gardening at Yarin, a village in the Western Sector of Israeli-occupied zone.

Ramadan, 62, was rushed to a hospital in saida and his condition was described as serious after undergoing several surgical operations for was injured by several shrapnels in his head, belly and limbs. So, he will stay at intensive care unit (ICU) for a long time.

It is to be pointed out that the occupying Israeli forces kept to put mines and bombs at the occupied zone and green-line regions.

However, comings from the Israeli-occupied zone reported that the Israeli forces stormed into Al-Tiri village, Western Sector, and arrested the Head of Municipality Lutfi Kassem and his wife Fatima Cheaito.

June 19


The occupying Israeli forces, located at Al-Zaffata, Al-Mahmoudieh and Sojod positions, shelled the surroundings of the Lebanese Army's barracks at Al-Nabatieh, eastern hills of Al-Nabatieh and Kfar Romman, Al-Rafih mountain and outskirts of Aqmata, Sojod, Al-Rihan,Al-Aishieh, Mleikh, Al-Lowaizeh and Safi mountain. The shelling, also, spread to the eastern part of populated Jarjoua.

Furthermore, the Israeli warplanes flew in the airspace of South and Western Beqaa making several mock air raids and reconnaissance missions in addition to breaking the sound barrier.

On the other hand, security sources mentioned that a bomb exploded at Al-Tohorrars Israeli position, which overlooks at Kfar Romman village, resulting in a black smoke cloumn raised over the concerned position. The same sources said that the explosion might be resulting from blowing a quantity of ammunition found in the position.

At Eastern Sector, unordinary military activities were registered at Jezzine, Kfar Houna and Al-Aishieh Sector concurrently with an immediate military meeting convened at Marjeyoun Barracks. This meeting included the commands of Israeli Liaison Unit, Israel's Northern Region and Lahd's Militia. It was informed , also, that lahd's command of Jezzine was sent for this meeting which was devoted for discussing the military situation of Jezzine region, particularly, in the light of mounting the Islamic Resistance attacks on Lahd's officers and members. At this meeting, Jezzine-based Lahd's command demanded reinforcement from the Israeli forces. In response to this demand, the Israeli forces said that Jezzine area is out of the Israeli occupied zone, and thus, it is not possible to locate at it. The possible best act is to send some of reinforcement to the hills that overlook on the south-eastern part of Jezzine.

Comings from Jezzine said that the tension and fear dominate on Lahd's officers and members. Also, it was rumored that delegation of Jezzine's resident informed the command of Israeli Liaison and Lahd's Militia " with the fears of being threatened, at every moment, in our lives, homes, and cars. We are living in alienation and we do not know what, the future hides us ", the delegation said.
