Details 17:15 

A Lebanese civilian was killed yesterday during an "Israeli" artillery bombardment between Khorbet Selem and Jmaijmeh, 25km south east of Tyre. 

Abbas Ghazleh, 30 , was hit in the stomach by shrapnel. He was taken to Tibbnine hospital where he later died from his injuries. Ghazleh brings to 49 the number of civilian fatalities in the south this year. 

He was the first civilian fatality since December 3 when three Lebanese were killed by a roadside bomb blast in Majdal Selem.
