Smoke without Fire?
 
By Munif Al-Haj
15 Nov. 1997
 

The following are excerpts from the article Zionism written by Prof. Benyamin Neuberger.

(Time and again, the Jews of Europe, and to a lesser extent Jews in Muslim countries, were discriminated against, persecuted and massacred. This happened sometimes on religious grounds, sometimes for economic reasons, sometimes on social pretexts, and sometimes for national and "racial" rationales. Jews were slaughtered by the Crusaders when the latter made their way across Europe to the Holy Land (11th-12th centuries), massacred during the Black Death for allegedly poisoning wells (14th century), burned at the stake in the Spanish Inquisition (15th century) and murdered by Chmelnicki's Cossacks in the Ukraine (17th century). Hundreds of thousands of Jews were killed by the armies of Danikin and Petlura in the Russian civil war which followed World War I. The most infamous atrocity of all, the Nazi Holocaust in which some 6 million Jews were systematically annihilated mainly on "racial" grounds, was perpetrated by Germans, in whose country the Jews had made their most serious attempt to achieve acceptance and social assimilation.) (Over the centuries Jews were expelled from almost every European country - Germany and France, Portugal and Spain, England and Wales - a cumulative experience which had a profound impact, especially in the 19th century when Jews had abandoned hope of fundamental change in their lives.) (1)

What this Prof. Is trying to tell us is that because Jews were persecuted so much over their long history , they now have to have a homeland of their own, (anybody's homeland will do nicely.)

Did it not ever occur to anyone to ask why were they (Jews) always persecuted? Why are the Jews the only ones being persecuted and expelled? Why not the Muslims or Christians, or any other race for that matter? Excuse me, but I do not think that the whole of humanity throughout history were wrong and the Jews were right.(Sorry the Jews do think so by the way.)

If we really want to know what went on in history and if we do believe that history repeats itself we only have to look at how history has repeated itself today and witnessed the return of the Jews to Palestine. This return is an identical return of the past (history). So if we really want to find out the truth about what went on in history and why the Jews were expelled, we only have to look at the way the Jews are behaving in Palestine today.

It is not my intention in this article to list the atrocities of the Zionist entity. Whom so ever is interested in this subject can look it up himself to find out, or let me say to wake up to the atrocities. Instead, what I am trying to say is that we should ask the question, why?

Lets look at it this way; a person walks into your office for a job, you look at his references which says that he was expelled 23 times from his 23 previous jobs, no matter what excuses he has he must be doing something wrong. Lets say you employ him without looking at his references, he starts making trouble, you then look into his references and find out that he has always been a trouble maker in the past. What do you do?

Lets look at this article again:

    (Over the centuries Jews were expelled from almost every European country - Germany and France, Portugal and Spain, England and Wales)

Now what did almost every European country have in common to expel the Jews? The only reason I can find is the Jews themselves. Or if you like lets blame almost every European country.

What I am trying to say is so simple, but because our minds have been manipulated by the Jewish controlled media we have really believed that there is smoke without fire. And that is what they (Zionist) keep trying to convince us that, there is smoke without fire. This statement insults any ones intelligence, does it not?
