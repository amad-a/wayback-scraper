January Sunday 02 -2000:

Hizbullah on al-Quds Day: the Holy city is alive(1)
Hizbullah Secretary his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah underlined that al-Quds (Jerusalem) is an eternal historical truth that can never be abolished, forged, lost or forgotten. 

Speaking at the annual parade held by Hizbullah in Beirut to mark al-Quds Day, Sayyed Nasrallah said, "Marking this occasion every year has two sides: marking al-Quds Day and marking the course and method of al-Quds' imam, the Ummah's imam, inspirer, leader, the revolution's maker, the resurrection creator, the one who restored religion and hope in the twentieth century and on the eve of the third millennium: the Great Imam Khomeini."

"On al-Quds Day, we need to stress some facts:
First, when we say al-Quds, we don't mean Eastern al-Quds, or the holy city itself, for al-Quds is a symbol that stands for all of Palestine and all the Ummah that is targeted by the Zionist arrogant project which has been underway in our countries for over 50 years.

"Let the whole world hear these final words, before and after the settlement process: "Israel" is a cancerous usurping entity that has no legitimacy or legality; it was established on terrorism, usurpation, injustice, suppression, expulsion of peoples, and perpetrating massacres.

"Israel can never become legal or legitimate as a result of signatures, settlements, or accords. No one on this earth has the right to give one grain of sand from Palestine to these Jewish usurpers.

"As for us, the Ummah of Hizbullah, as part of this opposed and targeted nation on the eve of the third millennium, we say it publicly, with our voices and blood, with all strength: we do not recognize that a state, even village or farm, called Israel exists, even if the whole world recognizes it; Israel, will always be the enemy.

"It can never become friend or neighbor, one can not talk about coexistence or neighborhood with the criminals, racists, killers, massacre perpetrators in Lebanon, Palestine and the Arab world. These facts can not be abolished by accords; "Israel" is still an enemy in the eyes of the people in Egypt and Jordan. It will always be as such as far as the peoples of our Arab and Islamic Ummah are concerned.

"Lebanon, all of Lebanon, will not forsake one grain of its national soil or any of its national rights: our soil is not only the patches which have been added recently to the fence area, or the Shibaa farmsteads, for the seven villages are also part and parcel of Lebanon. No Lebanese, no matter what his position is, has the right to give up these territories.

"We don't have to make concessions or pay prices, for our territories are being recovered with the blood of our martyrs, with the sweat of our resistance men, with the patience of the martyrs' mothers, with the orphans' tears.

"The Ummah has to examine two models: that of the resistance in Lebanon and that of Arafat's negotiations, then the Ummah can choose.
