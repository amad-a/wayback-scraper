Collaborators complain from Israels maltreatment

After the defunct collaborators militia, so-called Southern Lebanese Army, bound their fate to that of Israel, the latter denies the collaborators services and rejects their presence.

About 500 collaborators with their families, who fled Lebanon along with the Israeli troops, plead before the Israeli Supreme Court to oblige the Israeli government to grant them the Israeli nationality in addition to the privileges enjoyed by the regular Israeli army. They also asked to be compensated for the losses they bore since the Israeli withdrawal from Lebanon.

The collaborators complained that the Israeli authorities not only ignore and ridicule them but also deal with them as inferiors.

They stated that Israel is neither providing them with suitable living conditions nor schools for their children.

It was also mentioned in the plea that the collaborators (with the former Israeli occupation in South Lebanon) fear returning to their homes because their lives are endangered, as they said.

However, Israel is proposing money for the collaborators for them to leave, which clearly shows the Israeli policy, stated the plea.

Manar TV, 9-12-2000.
