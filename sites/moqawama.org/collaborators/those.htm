Border prayer: those who fled to the occupation's entity want to return

Agence France Presse said twenty Lebanese including women and children who had fled to the entity of the Israeli occupation following the withdrawal of the enemy forces from South Lebanon gathered on Tuesday for prayer at the Lebanese-Palestinian border.

"We have come to ask the Virgin, on her day, to ease our pains," one of the Lebanese told the reporters at the Lebanese side.

The young man who was a member of the Israeli occupation's proxy militia asked, "What are the crimes that our women and children committed to lead miserable lives as refugees from one country to another?"

We want to return to Lebanon even if they are going to throw us in prison, he said.

Manar TV, 15-8-2000.
