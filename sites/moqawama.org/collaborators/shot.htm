Red Cross retrieves body of woman shot during pullout

The International Committee of the Red Cross on Monday repatriated the body of a Lebanese woman who died in Israel, where she had been receiving medical treatment after being shot by Israeli soldiers during their May withdrawal.
Raymonda Musa, 31, was hit in the back by a bullet May 24 when Israeli troops fired in the air to disperse a crowd of terrified Lebanese who were hoping to flee to Israel during the hurried withdrawal.
Musas parents, brother, sister and daughter, who had all fled to Israel, accompanied her body to Lebanon. Musas husband, however, remains in Sweden, where he is seeking political asylum for his family, the police said.
Her father, Raymond Musa, 48, said he planned to sue Israel for his daughters death.
I had to dig up my daughter with no help from the Israelis. They didnt even give me a coffin for her body, he said.
Ive been pleading for six weeks to return but they (the Israelis) have banned me because I file a lawsuit against them and accused them of killing my daughter, Musa said.
He added that the Israelis even accused me of being a Hizbullah agent.
The situation of Lebanese there is a disaster. I used to meet with many of them. Its better to be in jail than to live in conditions like these, he said.
We used to get exaggerated news that the people who returned to Lebanon are having their eyes gouged out and are being beaten and tortured. But all this is lies, he said
All Lebanese prefer to return and be jailed in their country but the Israelis are not allowing them. Theyre not giving them exit permits, he said.
Musa added that most Lebanese protested against sending their children to Israeli schools. There are families who stage hunger strikes because Israel is lying to us.
I call on all who stayed there to return quickly to Lebanon. I tell you from my home here that the treatment and reception I had were the most honorable, Musa said.
Funeral services for Musa were held in her native town of Marjayoun.

The Daily Star, 26-9-2000.
