48 collaborators sentenced to prison

 

The Military Tribunal handed down prison terms ranging between six months and seven years against 48 Israeli-allied militiamen on Thursday.
The militiamen were also banned from entering the area where they fought from after they are released from prison. They will be banned from those areas for the same amount of time as they serve in prison. In addition, several were ordered to pay a LL400,000 fine.
The former militiamen were convicted of collaborating with Israel, joining an enemy army, and entering Israel without permission. Over 1,100 former militiamen have been convicted.

The Daily Star, 13-10-2000.
