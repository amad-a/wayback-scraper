30 collaborators tried by military

The trial of 30 people on charges of collaborating with Israel during its 22-year occupation of the South opened at a military court on Monday afternoon.
The defendants, all of whom were in custody, were mostly members of Israels now-defunct proxy militia. Others were civilians who traded with Israel or traveled to the Jewish state.
The batch of suspects was the 29th since the trials of collaborators started on June 5, 12 days after Israel completed its pullout from the South.
Presided over by Brigadier Maher Safieddine, the court ­ which has ruled in more than 1,100 cases ­ has handed down prison terms of up to 15 years and is due to study around 1,400 other cases.
