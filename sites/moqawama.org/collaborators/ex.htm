Ex-Israeli-allied militiamen go to Germany

Former members of the disbanded Israeli-proxy militia who fled their country  arrived in Germany on Monday, where they will be given refuge.
The militiamen members were among 12 Lebanese nationals from three families who arrived at Berlins Schoenefeld airport en route to resettlement in the nothern port city of Hamburg.
 The collaborators men were initially given refuge in Israel, which has, however, asked other nations like Germany, Canada, Chile, France and the United States to accept some of the Lebanese refugees. ­
