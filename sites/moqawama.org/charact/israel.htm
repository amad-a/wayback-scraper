The "Israeli" occupation of Lebanon

      The whole world must know every single detail about the appropriation, by "Israel", of the southern territories bordering Palestine.

    Many territories have been seized since before the creation of state of "Israel" until now, i.e, since 75 years. The appropriation process expanded after the invasion of 1978. The border strip was created and accounted then for 9% of the overall surface of the Lebanon Republic.

  "Israel" seized huge parts of Mtulleh, Manarah and Houla since the end of the 19th century until the period of the French mandate over Lebanon and the English mandate over Palestine.

    The seven villages were joined to the lands playing under the British influence, then were wiped out during the Great Defeat, in 1948.

  The international borders were modified at Lebanons expense, conformity with the treaty of Paulet_ New camp  (1922). Later on, France disclaimed the Lebanese part of Howla valley in 1923, and the villages of that very region were destroyed between the two world wars. After the "Israeli" Arab war of 1948, "Israel" held  sway over large parts of the Lebanese villages spread along the international borders. Everyday, those were continuously modified.

  Lebanon did not enter the war of 1967. Never the less, "Israel" declared, unilaterally, the estinguishment of the truce with Lebanon. That was a violation of the International Low. Then "Israel" seized the farms of Sheba Locality in Arkoub, and other lands near el-cheikh mountain (since 1970), where it placed military bases.

  After the invasion of 1978, the security strip as well as the before strip were created.

  The "Israeli" army got close to the Litani Rivers the Resolution 425 of the International Security Council was not implemented.

  The international emergency forces were unable to stop the "Israeli" aggressions which culminated in the invasion of 1982. As a result of the invasion, the border strip was expanded, the economic, social and political structure of the Lebanese people was destroyed.

   Along with the occupation, "Israel" endeavored to take parts of the Litani River, after it seized the source of Wazzani and the groundwater of Hasbani River. It also took possession of new land in Khiam and in other border Lebanese localities, while moving the barbed wires inside the Lebanese territories more and more.

  The appropriation of the southern territories took place in violation of the international laws and resolutions. It was accompanied by the aggravation of the ternal crisis in Lebanon, crisis which imperiled Lebanons existence as a nation and as a state. The appropriation process led to the appearance of new geopolitical realities which undermined Lebanons utility, sovereignty and independence. It also worsened the position of the Lebanese negotiator who claimed "Israel" retreat to behind the international borders. We think that this borders are the once determined by the official document of the French mandate, or they many be the very borders of Greater Lebanon (1920).

  Furthermore, all the resolution of the Security Council concerning the retreat of "Israel" from Lebanon has not been implemented. "Israeli" troops are still attacking and engaging wars against Lebanon and the Lebanese people.

  These very facts justify the birth and the development of the Lebanese resistance against "Israel".

  This resistance is justified on the legal level, and also because the occupation still exist, as well as the "Israeli" aggressions against the Lebanese people and institutions.

  In order to carry out this study, we referent to different documents. The first is mainly based on interviews with many persons who are concerned with the appropriation question. We adopted an analytical approach and relied on facts.

  Chapter 2 is concerned with the "Israeli" retreat from the Lebanese; the nature of the Resolution 425 and its consequence, the powerful and effective American role in the "Israeli" Arab conflict, as well as the development of the peace process which started with Madrid Conference in Autumn 1991.

  We faced political and material complications as we were carrying out our study. Yet virtue of the scientific responsibility, we must unveil the truth, scrutinize information and issue, very carefully, conclusions. We tried to deal the best we could with the appropriation and the retreat issues. We wish that our study would raise a serious discussion within all circles, mainly among the Lebanese
officials whose mission is to bring this document to the international scene.

Chapter I

The appropriation process
A lot of people do not know the overall surface of the Lebanese seized by "Israel", since the creation of Greater Lebanon in 1920, until the creation of what was called the state of "Israel"  in 1948.

   Many dont know either the repercussions of these events on the Palestinian cause and the "Israeli" Arab conflict. In fact, when the Zionist settlers seized large lands in Mtalleh, Manarah, Hawla and Khiam at the end of the 19th century, they started to modify the international borders. Aim in view, they passed agreements with the British mandate which engaged protracted negotiations with the French mandate, concerning South Lebanons territories. (1)

   The agreement of the Truce concluded by "Israel" and Lebanon on 23/3/1949 made provision for that the truce would be a bide for on he international borders between Lebanon and Palestine. Nevertheless, the "Israeli" troops did not retreat from the territories, which they had seized before and after the agreement. That led to the seizure of more Lebanese territories; the barbed wires penetrated more and more inside Lebanon and "Israel" had a complete role over the zone of the borer strip. After the invention of the 1978 and 1982, the occupation went on. In fact, "Israeli" seized tens of thousands of miles of agricultural lands close to the international borders and sprawling from Cheikh mountain to the Mediterranean.

In fact, the settlement was not limited to Palestinian territories, since Jews appropriated land owned by Lebanese people, inhabited by Lebanese families and known for their abundance in water as well as for their strategic site.

   From 1896 onwards, Jewish settlers established the colony of Mtalleh one large surfaces of the village of the Mtalleh, after the expulsion of their Lebanese owners and inhabitants. Until 1912, that colony was inhabited by 310 Jews(3). The setters booted Arabs out of the villages of Khan Douair, Mdahel, Manshiyeh, Dafneh and Hkab.

  The Zionist settlers also dominated Manarah, near the border sever villages, for its sttegic importance. It was high located at the end of the series mountain Jabal Amel.

  The Ottoman authorities conceded to a Lebanese family the privilege of improving 55000miles of the land of Hawla. When that family failed to implement the content of the privilege, it sold it to the Zionist who booted the Arab in habitant out of: Khan Douair, Mdahel, Manshiyeh, Dafneh, and Hkab. Later on, they destroyed those villages after occupation of Palestine in 1948.

  At the most of the French mandate over Lebanon and the British mandate over Palestine, Jewish settlers held sway over nearly 65000miles of Hawla valley. Only 45000 miles remained to the Arabs, and were soon seized by the settlers in 1948.

2-Modification of the International Borders.

  The demarcation of the international borders between Lebanon and Palestine was affected by the Zionist appropriation of many Lebanese territories, especially after the declaration of the state of Greater Lebanon, and since Franco-British negotiations had been held during the first world war to treat the demarcation question. In fact, since 1916, the British and French state departments had been exchanging diplomatic notes, in order to shore the rule over the Arab Mashrek.

   The international borders (between Lebanon and Palestine), determined by the official document of the British mandate over Palestine and the French mandate over Lebanon in 1920, were modified 2 years later, 3/2/1922(5), in virtue of the treaty of Paulet- New comb. The treaty consecrated a new Franco-British agreement. It made provision for that new seven Lebanese villages would be placed under the British mandate, which soon gave them to the Jewish settlers.

  From that time onwards, the question of the seven villages is a major preoccupation for both Lebanon and the Arab world. It caused great problem to the inhabitants who the Lebanese nationality.

  The seven villages are: Hounin, Abel komh, Kadas, Malkiyeh, Tarbikha, Salhe and Nabi Youcha. They spread from the Eastern side of JabalAmel until the road of ras Nakorah near Ayta chab. These villages spread along the international borders and are 360m to 690m above the sea.

  The seven villages are known for their water and their arable lands. Until 1948, they were inhabited by 5000 persons, mostly by the Lebanese people who were killed and lost their homes during the war of 1948 after the destruction of their villages. "Israeli" troops established military sites and colonialist points in these villages. We mention among them:

 - The colony of Moshaf Marjaliot (Hounin)
 - The colony of Moshaf Yofal (Abel Kameh)
 - The stronghold of Yaftah (Kadas)
 - The Kipoutz of Malkiyah (Malkieh)
 - The control post of Metsodt yesha (Nabi Yousha)
 - The colony of Yeeron (Salhah)

  Later on, the Paulet-New comb treaty led to the modification of the international border for the benefit of "Israel".

  In June 1923, France ceded the major surface of the Lebanese region in Hawleh Plain to the British mandate. Again, the borders were modified, and Lebanon lost arable lands abundant in water (2300 miles) and belonging to the Lebanese owners(6). The land are joined to the villages and location of: Marjyoun, Kafarkela, Deir Meimas, Hasbaya, Kleia, and numerous forms(7).

  The Jewish settlement had a lot of repercussions on the Lebanese territories, and on the demarcation of the international borders. The remaining Lebanese villages (according to the statistics of 1921) were destroyed later, between the two world wars. A great number of these villages are located in Hawla Plain. We mention among them: Nameh, Khalsa,khsas, Dafneh, Doubra, upper Zouk,Down Zouk, Zwaweh, Mansuora, Lazzazeh, Salhiyeh, Khar Duoir,Shawka, Hanuta, Ikret, and Kforborom(8).

  Until the 4th decade of the 20th century, the population of those villages was estimates at 11000 Muslim and Christian (9).they are known for their arable lands which "Israel" had seized since 1948.when the inhabitants of Ikref and Kfarbor'om claimed to return to their land the "Israeli" government (the labor) refused their demand in 1972, and the next government (the Likoud) refuted the demand again in 1977. This fact is a very prove that the original inhabitants of those land claimed their right to return to their home (10).

    The problem of the border Lebanese sites and localities was difficult to solve because of the geopolitical overlapping between Lebanon and Palestine. Yet the Franco-British agreements added large lands to Palestine and, consequently, to the Israeli zone of influence. These lands became part of the confiscated Lebanese lands.

   Furthermore, Israel took advantage of the military and security developments which resulted from the Israeli-Arab war in 1948. It kept huge surfaces of the Lebanese territories, in violation of the simplest international rules (11), and although Lebanon had signed the treaty of the truce on 23-3-1949.

   The seized lands were distributed as follows: in the villages of Yaroun, Rmeich, Aytaroun, Blidah, Meis El-Jabal, Hawla, Odayseh and Kferkila. As a result, the international borders were once again modified on the benefit of Israel which got closer to the course of the Litani, 2 km away from the lowest geographic spot (12). Israel practiced also the military aggressions which led to:

 - The establishment of the colony of Mescav Am" in the vicinity of Odayseh near Manarah, in order to control the water of the region and drag it to the Israeli colonies of Galileo (North Palestine).
 - The full security control upon the road of Marjeyoun - Bint Jbeil.
 - The control of the farmers' movement in their own villages (near the international borders).
   We notice that the borders' modification continued - during the Frensh mandate and after the war of 1948 - for the benefit of Israel.

   The border were continuously modified because Zionists and Israelis never stop confiscating the Lebanese territories which were spread along the borders. More particularly, the territories bordering the "seven villages".

3 - The Appropriation Process Continues

  It is an international paradox that Israel declares, unilaterally, the extinguishment of the truce treaty with Lebanon. It is also a paradox that Israel violates the Lebanese air-space during the war of June 1967 although Lebanon hadn't entered the war.

   Later on, the Israeli army seized 14 farms in the neighborhood of Shebaa, the Lebanese village located in the region of Arkoub on the Western versant of el-Sheikh mountin. Then the farms were joined to a paved road which spreads from Houla valley to the entryway of Shebaa, passing by Kfar Souba.

   These farms are 25 km long. Their width varies between 7 and 13km, their surface is 250 is 250 km2 and they were owned by nearly 1000 Lebanese families.

   The population of the farms was 3500 persons when they were booted out in 1967. The farms are: El-Maghbar, Fashkoul, Zibdin, Harat Afwa, Bertaya, El-Raba, Beit El-Barak, Brakhta, Kfar Dawra, jov'at el Marah el Mloul.

   The estimations concerning the number of houses in these farms vary, yet they are in hundreds (14). These houses are built upon fertile arable lands. They are largely covered with olive trees and are very abundant in fresh water. The farms of Chab'a overtop a part of "Jabal Amel", the upper Galileo, the Syrian Horan and the Golan plateau. This is why these farms are geographically important.

   Israelis dredged the fields of these farms and changed their geographic aspect. They also wiped out a religious sanctuary, the shrine of Abraham, known as "Mashhad Teir".

   They disposed of religious properties which were part of the Muslim Wakfs and of the Orthodox church. Then, in 1985, they started to build colonies by the initiation of the Rabbi Mair Kahana (15). The new settlers soon began to aggress the neighboring villages and localities of Arkoub.

   Furthermore, Israel appropriated new Lebanese lands collateral to Habbaria and Kfarshouba (Arkoub) in order to get closer to the top of El-Cheikh mountin. From 1970 onwards, it controlled parts of: Nakkar, Shaml, Sawaki, Jourat Elleik. Soldiers circulate permanently in these lands (16) where military sites and long-ranged artillery emplacements are installed.

   Besides those military actions, the Israeli army installed a control center at the hill of Sadana near Shab'a. then it occupied the mountain of Shmeis and the east of Kfarshouba and encircled their regions and sites with barbed wires. It also constructed roads which join the regions of Wazzani, the hill of Wazzani and the hill of Nhas (on the international borders) together.

   Before the invasion of 1978, the appropriate process expanded. Military sites were installed within the Lebanese territories in the 1970, on the pretext of the necessity of facing the military actions which were launched by the factions of the Palestinian Resistance from Lebanon. As a result, the borders were effectively modified in the neighborhood of Aitaroun at the length of 3km and the depth of 300m, in a surface cultivated with cereals and to (17). An other modification occurred in the neighborhood of Dhayra where the barbed wires were moved in 1976 (18).

Many sites were installed in the districts of Tyre, Bint Jbeil and Marjeyoun as follows (19):

 - In the district of Tyre, the sites of Dhayra, Yarin, Mrouhin, Boustan, Ramiah and Nakoura.
 - In the district of Bint Jbeil, the sites of Aita Chaab and Blida.
 - In the district of Marjeyoun, the sites of Meis El-Jabal, the hill of Nhas, Kfar Kela and Qilia.

  The mentioned sites sprawl along the international borders, from Qilia at east to Nakoura at west the military presence is permanent, as well as the construction of roads within the Lebanese territories.

 
   Israel pretexted of security along the international borders, whereas it had been appropriating lands before the start of the Palestinian resistance from Lebanon (the South). It had also been committing aggressive acts against Lebanon's territories and sovereignty. In other words, Israel disposed of Lebanese territories although Lebanon didn't participate in the war. It used armed force to impose the accomplished fact along the international borders until the great military invasion occurred in 1978 and degenerated in new realities, among which we mention the "region of the border strip".
4 - The Appropriation of Lands within the "Border Strip".

   The region of "the border strip", i.e. "the security strip" was created on 15 March 1978 in the morning because of the Israeli invasion of South Lebanon.

   The Israeli army launched "the Litani Operation" which enabled the army to hold sway until the Litani River which spreads to the North of Tyre on the Mediterranean. Undoubtedly, Israeli leaders still dream of seizing the water of Litani. The operation took place "geographically" from Rashaya Fakhar at East until Ras Bayada on the Mediterranean at west. The front was 100km long and 17km wide.

   30,000 Israeli soldiers well armed and equipped with tanks, artillery, helicopters and gunboats participated in that operation.

  The military operations were launched against 358 villages in the districts of Hasbaya, Bent Jbeil, Tyre and Nabatieh, as a result, the Israeli army seized 1100km of the Lebanese territory, destroyed thousands of houses, schools, establishments and worship houses; spoiled agricultural lands and crops, killed and injured hundreds of civil people (20).

   "The Litani Operation" let to the creation of "the state of free Lebanon", which was backed up by Israel. It was created in a region bordering the international borders, that was known later as the "border strip" (21). That event obstructed the application of the Resolution  425 of the international security council, which stipulates the isreli retreat from Lebanon and gives the legitimate Lebanese authority the right to extend its leverage until the international borders. The international emergency forces as well as the Lebanese army were unable to cross the border strip and to execute the Resolution 425.

The damages of the aggression were estimated at 7 billions Lebanese pounds (about 3 million $). 23 villages and city, and 16 Palestinian camps were destroyed. 73000 Lebanese and Palestinian were either killed or wounded (25).

   Despite numerous Israeli official declarations about the Israeli intention to retreat from Lebanon, the appropriation process never stopped. It continued since the invasion of 1982, in parallel to the talk about a peaceful settlement in the Middle East. Here are some details:

 - the barbed wires along the international borders were moved from the colony of "Mtalleh" to 600m to the North (5 km long).

 -  The bordering barbed wires were continuously moved in the western sector of the border step, near the village of Yareen and in the face of the industrial colony of Beram in Galilea. This mean that the expropriation process never stopped, and that the international border were continuously modified by face, under the pressure of the "Israeli" force and the militias collaborating with them.

   We conclude that the expropriation of the Lebanese territories is a permanent "Israeli" goal, before and after the presence of the armed Palestinian resistance in Lebanon. The expropriated lands spread from Jabal Chkif at east to the Mediterranean near the south of Tyr at west.    The expropriation didnt aim only at the annexing of new Lebanese territories, but also at dominating them or, at least, dominating a part of them. This "Israeli" goal is no longer hidden.

   The "Israeli" argument is to protect the security of the Galilae or the security was to be guaranteed at the expense of the territories of an independent sovereign state.

   The nature of this security is determined by the authorities of the "Israeli" occupation, outside the framework of the international conventions and rules.

   That is an example of the "Israeli" expansiennise through the expropriation of a part of the Lebanese territories and the violation of the international laws calling to the retreat of the occupation forces from south Lebanon and the west Bekaa.
