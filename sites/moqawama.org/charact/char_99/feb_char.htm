  February Saturday 6-1999:

Lebanon clamps down on smuggling operations from occupied zone  (1)
"Customs officials in southern Lebanon have been clamping down on smuggling operations taking place in valleys just north of the zone "Israel" occupies in the south. The campaign coincided with reports that the head of the "Israeli" allied militia in the south, Antoine Lahd, has been building a fortune from such illegal activities".

"This was the catch - tobacco smuggled from the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon. Customs officials in Sidon on Tuesday night rounded up the goods and those smuggling the 900 cigarette packets on backs of donkeys through the Bisri-Meydan valley just north of the occupied Jezzine enclave. After shooting rounds, the smugglers managed to escape, but customs officials ceased the tobacco, with the help of the Lebanese Army intelligence. Customs officers in Sidon said the operation is part of a campaign to clamp down on smuggling originating from the occupied zone. This coincided with news reports in "Israel" unveiling the chief of the "Israeli" allied militia in occupied south Lebanon, Antoine Lahd, has profited handsomely from "Israel's" occupation of the area through a network of bribery, smuggling and crooked business dealings.

Reports first appeared in Western embassies that the "Israeli"-protected and commissioned Lahd has been imposing taxes on imports of stolen cars from Europe via the occupied port of Naqoura. Paris reports also said Lahd is making a daily profit of some 7,000 US dollars from imported gasoline to the occupied zone. Lahd has also been charging a 40,000 dollar tax per shipload of smuggled cigarettes through occupied Naqoura. Lahd has been condemned to death by Lebanese authorities for collaborating with the enemy, and aiding "Israel" in its occupation of Lebanese land. He heads the three thousand strong militia financed and trained by "Israel"".

-Future TV, 5-2-1999. 
  February Wednesday 10-1999:

Children cling to life after "Israeli" assault (1)
"Zeinab Maaz, 8, was lying prone in her Geitawi Hospital bed swathed in gauze, only her eyes and mouth uncovered."
"Nearby, her brother Abbas, 2, his little face swollen, his body covered in bandages, fretted for his absent mother."
Zeinab and Abbas are two of the three children who were severely burned when "Israeli" soldiers bombarded Majdal Silm with phosphorous and shrapnel shells Saturday. The children are still in critical condition".

"Zeinab, Abbas, and their older brother Mohammed, 10, were admitted to the Center for Intensive Burns at Geitawi Hospital Saturday after Tibnine Hospital and Sidons Hammoud Hospital were unable to cope with the extent of their wounds".
"The children were visiting their grandmother, Fatima Maaz ­ who was also wounded in the attack ­ when a mortar shell ripped through the living room, showering phosphorous over the house and its occupants".

"The "Israeli"s" and their South Lebanon Army proxies regularly use shells filled with white phosphorous in the south. Although the weapons are designed to produce an immediate smoke screen to provide cover for troops, the "Israeli"s" frequently use them against personnel. The phosphorous ignites spontaneously on contact with air and is very difficult to douse".

"Mohammed, who was in the kitchen at the time of the attack and sustained the lightest wounds, was discharged from hospital Sunday. But Zeinab and Abbas remain there, struggling for their lives".

"Dr. Georges Ghanimeh, the burns specialist who is treating the children, told The Daily Star that their condition was grim. Theyre suffering from deep third-degree burns and their situation is critical, he said.

"Jamileh Maaz, the childrens mother, has not been allowed to be with them due to the risk of infection. I can only look at them through the window, even though the babys crying for me to be near him, she said.

"She said she had been kept in the dark about the condition of her children and all she can do is wait.

She had been tending cattle with her husband in fields near Majdal Silm when the mortar attack occurred. They learned of their childrens wounds later."

My husband and I came here by taxi yesterday after we found out where they had taken our children, she said.

"Mrs. Maaz is worried that her daughter may be scarred for life. Zeinab opened one eye and looked at me earlier, she said. But she cant speak, she can only murmur and nod. Shes in a lot of pain.

She also fears for her childrens eyesight and hearing. Phosphorous bombs burn, their smoke blinds and suffocates, said Mrs. Maaz who is well versed in the different types of shells used by the "Israeli"s" and the havoc they wreak".

"Majdal Silm is on the edge of the central sector of the occupation zone and the surroundings area is shelled by the "Israeli"s" on an almost daily basis. But Saturdays bombardment was the first time the center of the village had been hit since the April war in 1996"

"Like many others, Mrs. Maaz has adopted a fatalistic attitude to help her cope with living in a war zone. Despite the fact that three of her six children have been wounded and two of them lie battling against death, she refuses to succumb to self-pity"
Whatever happens to a person in life is written by God, she said. All Im asking for is that with Gods will, my children will get better.

1- The Daily Star, 9-2-1999. 
  February Saturday 27-1999:

"Israeli" attacks increasingly target children (1)
" The annexation of Arnoun was only one of many illegal acts "Israel" committed in Lebanon. Children have increasingly become targets and victims in south Lebanon. Our next report is about their plight.

Report: Almost three years ago, the world was shocked by this site - a young girl lying dead in an ambulance that was taking her to safety. The ambulance was targeted by an "Israeli" air to ground missile. But attacks like this did not stop and over recent weeks, children have increasingly become targets of "Israeli" aggressions. So they decided to raise their voices, hoping that this time they will be heard. Children from across Lebanon took to the streets to ask the world for protection. Eight year old Zeinab and her two year old brother Abbas are still in hospital three weeks after "Israeli" forces who occupy land in south Lebanon shelled their village of Majdel Selm. They were severely burned when phosphorous and shrapnel shells landed in their living room. Doctors say they are still in critical condition. Not too long after that attack, one child was killed and another wounded when they stumbled on a cluster bomb planted by "Israeli" forces in the southern village of Nabatiyeh. Five year old Ahmed Mokalled died of his wounds. His father holds back his tears as he recalls the events of that fateful day. Raed Mouqalled, father said: "I took Ahmed and his friends on a picnic. They were playing. And suddenly we heard an explosion. Ahmed died and his friend was wounded". Cluster bombs are frequently used by "Israeli" troops in their attacks despite their ban in international conventions signed by "Israel". Ahmed's mother has still not come to terms with her son's death. His father, Raed said his wife has still not been able to go home - everything there serves as reminders of her youngest child. His toys still lie on his bed and his clothes still hang in the closet. Ahmed was a playful and loving kid and that is how his father remembers him. And memories are all he has left.

Ahmed was buried on his sixth birthday. Ahmed was not a terrorist nor did he carry arms. He was a young boy who had a future ahead of him. He was not the first to be deprived of fulfilling his dreams. And he may not be the last".

1- Future TV, 26-2-1999.
