Israel Building 10,000 West Bank Homes
 
 

Jerusalem: Israel currently has 10,500 new homes under construction in the West Bank Jewish settlements and another 4,500 are planned to begin this year, Haaretz said yesterday.

The new construction, which does not include building in settlements around east Jerusalem, would provide homes for another 60,000 Israelis in the West Bank, the newspaper said.

Currently some 160,000 settlements in the West Bank and Gaza Strip, which Israel occupied in 1967.

Out of the homes now under construction, 6,500 were authorised by prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu since he came to office in June 1996, while the remainder were authorised under his Labour Party predecessors.

Haaretz said the current building would allow a ten to 15 per cent increase in the settler population over the coming years above the natural birthrate in the settlements.
The figures do not include the new settlement of Har Homa, in Jebel Abu Ghneim in east Jerusalem, which is due to have 6,500 homes and whose groundbreaking in March 1996 threw the Palestinian-Israeli talks into crisis.

Haaretz reported last week that Israel planned to double the number of homes in West Bank settlements with the construction of 30,000 new homes over the coming years.
