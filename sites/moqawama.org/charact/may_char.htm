Torture in Israel (1)
Once again, the terrorist features of the Zionist entity had been confirmed especially by the styles adopted against the Palestinian civilians in the occupied territories.

In this regard, the BTselem Human Rights Group called for reconsidering the Israeli Supreme Courts decision over using the torture with the detainees. Knowing that this decision had come under denouncement from a lot of human rights groups. This Court had, already also, permitted detaining Lebanese innocent people as hostages and bargaining cards.

BTselem added that there are at least 850 Palestinians are subjected per year to the torture in Israel.

Prior to the hearing session convened by Israels Supreme Count in order to look into the calls about the prohibition of using violence during the interrogation, BTselem said in press conference that Israels Public Security Apparatus which known by Shin Bet interrogates between 1000 and 1500 Palestinians a year.

It added that 85% of are subjected to torture processes. The group depended in its statistics on official sources, lawyers and human rights organizations.

The group called on Israel to rule out the styles of dark ages. These styles included blindfolding, handcuffing, depriving from sleeping, shaking violently and putting the detainees in a critical and difficult positions and situations.

It should be noted that in 1987, an official committee headed by Mosheh Landaw, a retired judge of the Supreme Court, had authorized the Israeli Public Security Apparatus to use moderate physical torture and under specific conditions with the suspect.

At the same time, the international human rights groups decried the decision as it gives the green light to the torture sponsored by the state.

It is expected today that nine judges of Israels Supreme Court will hear and consider six calls presented by several Israeli human rights groups and Palestinian individuals who were subjected to the interrogations of Shin Bet requesting for prohibition of the violence during the interrogation.
In an affidavit made by Ami Ayalon, Head of Shin Bet, he considered the physical torture styles as essential means to struggle against the terrorism and foil the terrorist attacks.

However, an Israeli lawyer had presented previously a petition to the Israeli Supreme Court in which she said that this excuse should be rejected for it had been used routinely. Furthermore, it didnt preclude the terrorist attacks. She added that a lot of European countries are contending with success against the terrorism without resorting to these styles.*

BTselem said it is up to the Supreme Court now for Israel to be considered as a democratic state that strives the terrorism without using inhuman styles in the interrogation processes.

May Tuesday 21-1998:

"Israel" Tortures the children (1):
In an ugliest and flagrant violation of human rights and childhood, the Israeli occupation forces are still detaining, without any legal reason, eleven Lebanese children. In protest to this brutal step, Thursdays Mothers made a sit-in before the Headquarters of the International Committee for the Red Cross in the Lebanese capital Beirut.

State Minister for Administrative Reform Affairs Bshara Merhej, the Follow-up Committee for the Support of Detainees in Israels Jails and several leaderships of the parties had participated with this sit-in.

Merhej expressed his anxiety over their situations after Israel had prevented the International Committee for the Red Cross from visiting Khiam detention camp.

At the end of the sit-in, a memorandum was addressed to the Committee of Child Rights, International Committee for the Red Cross, UNs Human Rights Committee, Amnesty International and all the international and Arab organizations. We would like to inform you that the Israeli occupation forces had kidnapped eleven Lebanese children from their villages and schools and taken them to Khiam detention camp. As you know this step contradicts with all international laws especially the Children Rights Convention declared by the United Nations on November 20-1989 and endorsed by the International Summit of Child which convened at the United Nations Headquarters in New York on September 1990, the memorandum reported. It added that the detained children are:
 - Rabah Fayez Abou Faour, Khelwat, Hasbaya District, 16.
 - Mohammad Hussein Kazan, a student of Taibeh Official School, 15.
 - Ali Hussein Kazan (brother of Mohammad), 17, a student of Taibeh Official School.
 - Ali Mustafa Tawbeh, 14, Yohmor El-Shakeif, detained with his father Mustafa Tawbeh.
 - Mazen Abdallah, 12, Khiam.
 - Khanjar Choaib, 16, Khiam.
 - Ali Fadl Jahjah, 13, Kfar Kila.
 - Ahmad Barakat, 15, Rob-Thlathine.
 - Ali Ahmad Kashmar, 14, Khiam.

In Khiam detention camp and Israels jails, there have been many children under eighteen years detained since fourteen years such as Hassan Sadreddine Hijazi, Ahmad Ammar, Akram Alaoueih and others.

The memorandum added: Detaining children is a flagrant violations of child rights and a crime against the childhood and humanity where these children had been deprived from protection, growth, education and maternity. These children had been prevented from meeting with their families and with the International Committee for Red Cross. Also, they are suffering malnutrition and subjected to critical situations. Israel hold full liability for it controls Khiam detention camp which contains about one hundred and sixty others who many of them are detained without trial and in conditions contradict with the all international laws.

The Follow-up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detains in Israels Jails and families of the detained children described taking the children to Khiam detention camp as the most terrible violation of human and child rights. It called also all international and human organizations and especially the United Nations Child Rights Committee which is convening now its eightieth session to:

1- Prevent any interrogation with the detained children unless in the presence of their lawyers, responsible managers or a representatives of the International Committee for Red Cross.

2- Secure the mental and physical safety for the detained child through building up detention installations specialized for the children and separating them from the adults as confirmed by the Article no:10 of the International Convention over the civil and political rights.

3- Treat the children in a good and suitable manner especially in meals and clothes.

4- Permit them to meet with their families and exchanging the letters with them.

5- Permit the International Committee for Red Cross to visit and inspect them.

6- Regard the detaining of the children in Khiam detention camp as war crimes committed against
the humanity and trying the executors as war criminals.

7- Ask Israel for an unconditional and prompt release for the all children who should be considered as hostages.

After the sit-in, a meeting was held by the Minister Merhej, Head of the International Committee for Red Cross Jane Jack Fresard and the Follow-up Committee in which Merhej exposed the tragic conditions suffered by the detainees in Khiam detention camp. Also, he demanded that efforts exerted in order to release them must be continued and expressed his fearness concerning their situations.

In his turn, Fresard emphasized the persistence of the International Committee pertaining to exerting various kinds of pressures for the purpose of re-accessing the Khiam detention camp and reducing the pains of the detainees.

Finally, the mothers of the detained children had delivered Fresard the memorandum.
