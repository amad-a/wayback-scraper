"Israeli" Press Report: Islamic Resistance is waging a real war
 

"An analysis in the "Israeli" daily Ha'aretz said the reason behind the successful operations launched by the Islamic Resistance against the "Israeli" occupation forces lately was "the excellent intelligence Hizbullah had in the face of an "Israeli" army intelligence weakness".

"It took the "Israeli" army a long time to understand that it is no longer an issue of "Shiite suicide bombers" or "terrorists threatening the northern settlements" but a guerilla war launched by a well-tranied and well-armed force on its own soil against an occupation force, the daily's military analyst Tsefi Bar Il wrote".

"It took politicians even longer to understand that if they do not swiftly pull out of Lebanon, "Israel" will be engaged in an eternal war in Lebanon, a war whose first pretext was forgotten a long time ago and which turned to a series of survival battles for the "Israeli" army troops along with panels to investigate the fiascoes which accompany such wars", he added.

"In the absence of accurate information about the movements of the Hizbullah fighters' movements; nevertheless, such a demand is like asking a blind man to construct a colored panel, for this is a clandestine closed organization which sends operation men on motorcycles for far places, an organization that always gathers information about "Israeli" troops' movements; an organization which takes lessons quickly and all the time. Moreover, it doesn't have the build reinforced shelters in its outposts or to erect these shelters on its APCs.

"The military analyst said "Israeli" military leaders who worked in Lebanon "and probably the new OC Northern Command Gabi Ashkinazi know that the security one is moving day after day and part by part to become part of Hizbullah's ammunition".
