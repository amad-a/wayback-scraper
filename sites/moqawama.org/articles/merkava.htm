Merkava Tank
 
 
Ali Kawtharani
 

The Israeli-made Merkava tank is now a title of a new crisis suffered by the Hebrew state. In its semi-annual meeting convened on October 1997 , the Israeli security leadership, including Chief of Staff and his deputy, Head of Military Intelligence Apparatus, Command of North Brigade and other senior officers, focused on studying and analyzing the consequences of last events concerning the vulnerability of Merkava tank.

Until recent days, Merkava tank, classified by NATO as one of the most qualified tanks around the world, was considered as the key combat tank in Israeli Army. It was subjected to essential amendments leading to Merkava-2 and Merkava-3 types. It is well equipped that more than one country of NATO, in addition to Turkey, had seriously intended to purchase a lot of them.

Now and after being knocked out repeatedly , all these countries are rethinking their plans of purchasing such as tanks.

Technically, Merkava is equipped by 105mm caliber gun, middle machine guns, laser system and thermal and night-vision binoculars. Also, it can take several positions in order to change the track of rocket and send out camouflage smoke to mislead the enemy and thus minimizing the damages.

The Islamic Resistance fighters have a complete knowledge of its weakness and power points and thus facilating the mission of Tank Snipers Unit to hunt and knock out these kinds of tanks.

A senior commander of Islamic Resistance said : the first and basic weakness point is between its body and tower. This point requires very accurate hitting for it is very small. The second one is represented in its ability to be penetrated by hitting two anti-tank rockets at the same point.

The senior commander confirmed that the fighters are using classical rocket its destructive power depends on conic load found in the tip of rocket. So, upon striking the tank, it releases a thermal power reaches to 3000?c with velocity ranges between 5000 and 8000 m/s.

According to these realities a senior Israeli military source told AFP : The Israeli Army is worried over the vulnerability of its tanks stationed in South Lebanon after the Islamic Resistance had knocked out, repeatedly, several Merkava-type tanks by piercing through their armored sides. Knowing that the Merkava tank is considered as the most sophisticated one.

AFP added: The Israeli Army intends to rethink in its tactics of using Merkava tanks in South Lebanon and in the procedures that should be carried out to be more safety.
A senior Israeli officer of Northern Region Command confirmed that the aiming made by the Islamic militants is steadily, become more and more accurate and thus the tanks become , increasingly, easy targets in Lebanon.

In its October 20,1997 edition,  Haaretz, a widespread Israeli newspaper, said: The Israeli Army is worried over the truth that Hizbullahs men are succeeding in targeting Merkava tanks in South Lebanon where three of which were knocked out recently. The Israeli Army is, currently, investigating the circumstances of targeting, easily, this kind of sophisticated tanks.

Haaretz added that a source of Northern Region Command said: One time, Hizbullahs militants get closed to about 200m only from one of the tanks. Then, they hit it by a rocket destroying it and killing the lieutenant Iyal Shemon and Sergeant Ronin Hayoun.

General Israel Tal, architect of Merkava, is studying the circumstances of striking the tank, it concluded.

On the other Hand, Israeli TV said: In contrast with our belief, Merkava tank is seemingly apt to be hit. The ironic paradox here is that Channel-2 of Israeli TV had broadcasted a reportage shows its camouflage capabilities, operational tactics and the possibility to be defended. But n the next day, the Israeli TV displayed another reportage shows how the same Merkava tank exposed in the first reportage was hit. The comment made by the correspondent said: It is unbelievable , it is the same tank and the same crew are now  hitting by rockets launched by Hizbullah fighters. One Israeli was killed and the another was waiting his unknown fate at the hospital
In an article published in Haaretz October 21,1997, Amir Ourn wondered : After a series of fiascoes in Lebanon and after Hamas had humiliated Mossad apparatus , is it really that Hizbullah had routed Merkava tank?

These kinds of reports were confirmed by the acknowledgment of Israeli military commanders. The reservist general Youssi Ben Hanan declared: Merkava tank which is manufactured in Israel can not provide a perfect protection.

One of the setbacks that afflicted Merkava tank at the hands of Hizbullah men was on June 15,1997 when its exit gate was defused as a result of striking it by a rocket of huge explosive power. The electronic and mechanic system of tank were defused imposing the Israelis to take a risk by blowing up the gate in order to get the inflicted soldiers out of it.

Between 26-6-1996 and 18-10-1997, Hizbullah hit about twenty four Merkava-type tanks.

On the other hand, in fact, there is something not to be neglected or ignored. This was indicated by Rajeh Khoury in an article published in Nahar Lebanese newspaper on 21-10-1997, under a title Genus of  angels and genus of armored Plates : There is someone wants to limit the increasing problems of Israeli occupation forces with the vulnerable tanks only. The point is not the crispness of Merkava rather than the crispness of policy and politicians who draw the tracks of these military vehicles. The problem, treated here, touches the pillars of Israeli entity. This reminds us with what was written by Zaif Shef in 1985 : In Lebanon, all the weakness points of Israeli Army such as greediness, indulging in narcotics, smuggling , inability to contain the surprises, forthright acknowledging of fear, refusing the orders, depressionetc, appear obviously.
