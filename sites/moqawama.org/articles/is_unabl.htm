"Israel is Unable to Defeat Hizbullah"
 
 

The following is a Report from "Reuters" published in "Al-Safir" Daily Newspaper on Saturday 27/9/97 .

Yesterday Reuter published a report admitting that they and their agents are unable to defeat Hizbullah.

Reuter also mentions a description by a foreign military expert regarding the attacks executed by Hizbullah fighters on 25 Israeli targets by saying: They are not just an insignificant group of terrorists.

Reuter also added: The time has long passed when Hizbullah was considered merely a group of bearded fanatics fighting Israel and the West. Hizbullah is Israels fiercest enemy and have become highly trained not to forget the fact that each individual is willing to sacrifice his life for the protection of his beliefs.

The number of dead this year on the Israeli side that occupies the South of Lebanon tells the true full story, where as Hizbullah has the highest rate of loses in life among the Resistance fighters that reached the number of 48 this year most of them due to the air raids that they have no means of facing. But Israel with all its advanced weapons lost 34 soldiers, and the Militia the Lebanese Southern Army that is armed by Israel pays a price for its alliance with Israel at loss of 13 soldiers.

A Diplomat described Hizbullah by saying they are a small fighting force that has a limited amount of stored ammunition and weapons, but they have a high level of respect in this field.

He summed up his view regarding them as saying: They enjoy a good leadership and a strong motivation.

He added: Hizbullah does not own heavy artillery that Israel has which it uses to shell the fighters targets, instead they use mortar shelling size 82 mm. that its executed action is highly precise. And after years of experience they are now able casually to hit the Israeli defenses on the top of the hills from a distance of 5 km. Precisely from their first group of shells.

It also said, Israel secures its occupation of the South from its Bunkers on top of the high hills, plus a thousand or so Israeli Soldiers along with 3000 of the Militia the Lebanese Southern Army, all that does not show any sign of an ability to defeat Hizbullah.
