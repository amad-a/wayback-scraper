Whos The Birdbrain?
 
 
By Munif Al-Haj
Friday 6 Nov. 1997
 

Major General Matan Vilnai is a name to look out for in the Israeli Army. Vilnai, deputy
chief of staff until earlier this year when he took a leave of absence, has never hidden his desire to replace Chief of staff Amnon Shahak, whose term is due to end in summer 1998.

When a terrorist hears whining and arguments within Israeli society, it gives him the feeling that he has succeeded.(1). He said, speaking to students at Goldwater High School in Eilat. He also added that Israelis should not buckle under attacks by, Palestinians with brains like birds and the knowledge of nursery school children.(1).

Vilnai, a sample of what is coming up the pipeline in  Israels military  leadership is quoted as saying, There is nothing more impressive than someone who stands strong and tall, even when attacked,(1). I never knew Israelis were being attacked in Palestine and the South of Lebanon, I always had the idea they were being hunted like rabbits (remember Ansariya?). So if we replace the word attacked in his speech with the word hunted we should have something like this, There is nothing more impressive than someone who stands strong and tall, even when hunted. Now I ask you, whos the birdbrain? Vinlai and his like in the military are such geniuses that they cannot prevent a single blow planned by the masterminds of the Islamic Jihad, Hamas and Hezbullah. Whos the birdbrain?

A society that puts such human garbage  as its leaders should be blamed also. Leaders of a country reflect their society that put them there in the first place, so when we have leaders who commit massacres every single day we should know that that society had a great part in it as well. Not one single government in Israel fell due to any of its history of massacres, (Sabra, Shatila, Kana, Mansouriya etc. etc)

Vinlai is preparing himself for the summer of 1998, so he has to prepare all his sadistic macho speeches in order to succeed. If he does, I suggest like the present birdbrain, that that birdbrain keep his rabbits in the South of Lebanon, with rabbit hunting season kept open till dooms day.

(1) Haaretz 5 Nov. 1997
