An Israeli From The Moment He Murderedor Israel a State Created by Murderers to Provide Immunity for Murderers
 
   By Munif Al-Haj
Sunday 31 Oct. 1997
 

 An Israeli from the moment he murdered was the headline of an article in the Haaretz
newspaper dated 9 Oct. 1997.

A gruesome killing occurred in Montgomery County in the USA. Samuel Sheinbein 17, murdered  his Hispanic friend ,19 year old Alfredo Enrique Tello Jr.,after the murder his parents and brother assisted in disposing of the body by cutting it up to pieces and burning it(1).

Samuel Sheinbein who has never lived in Israel decided to move there to seek immunity simply because he was Jewish, knowing that the penalty would be less harsher in Israel for criminals than in the USA.

US authorities are demanding the extradition of the young criminal back to the USA to stand trial there. Israel on its part is studying the case because there is a dispute over whether the criminals father is infact an Israeli. If he turns out to be an Israeli then his son automatically becomes an Israeli, then according to the Israeli constitution there is no way for the criminal to be extradited, even when the Israeli government knows the real reason for his application for an Israeli citizenship.

The Haaretz writer of the article  An Israeli from the moment he murdered wonders at the state to which his country ( Israel ) has reached, for it to give immunity to murderers, he says  it touches on the debate between Israels role as a state of its citizens or a state of the Jewish People (2). I myself wonder why he should wonder at all, is there a state in the world that gives its citizenship based on ones religious background,  Israeli citizenship is both easy to acquire and permanent, and any Jew can receive it upon arrival in the country. This in effect turns Israel into a country offering total immunity for Jews. (2)

Finally, let me remind Mr. Haaretz writer and our dear readers of Menachim Begin as just one example, was he not one of the most wanted criminals by the British Government in Palestine. What happened when the state of Israel was created? Not only was he immune from punishment, but he also went on to become Prime Minister of that country, ofcourse a country of criminals can only be run by criminals.

So Mr. Haaretz writer stop your wondering about Samuel Sheinbein and remember that  Israel is a state created by murderers to provide immunity for murderers
 

( 1) Washington Post  20 Oct. 1997.
( 2 ) Haaretz                 9 Oct. 1997.
