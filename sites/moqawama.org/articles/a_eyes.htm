All Eyes on Lebanon
A Hizbullah victory
 
By Ze'ev Schiff  
Thursday, March 5,1998
 
 In the information war between the Hizbullah and "Israel", the Shi'ite party has the upper hand, although "Israel" does have some points to its credit. This battle is taking place in parallel to the warfare with artillery and explosives. There is no better illustration of the current Hizbullah propaganda advantage than the pictures on "Israel's" television screens. The Hizbullah is directing its public relations campaign at an "Israeli" society divided over the question of withdrawal from Lebanon. It is not operating according to the old-fashioned system the Arabs used to employ, which exposed their ignorance about "Israeli" society. The films produced by photographers who accompany Hizbullah sorties against the "Israel" Defense Forces (IDF)* and the Southern Lebanese Army (SLA)* are intended to show the determination and aggressiveness of the Hizbullah fighters. The films do not present the poor Lebanese victims of "Israeli" fire. Lebanon does that. These information-propaganda films are screened by the two "Israeli" television stations.

The Hizbullah propagandists are confident that the "Zionist enemy" will snap up its films. When these are shown in conjunction with scenes from the funerals in "Israel", there is no greater propaganda success for the Hizbullah. The "Israeli" media highlight "Israel's" weakness, its failures and the blunders of its forces, while emphasizing the boldness of those who fight against it.


* This term is absolutely unacceptable. "Israel's" Army is that of war and aggression for its protects land gained by force, confiscation and war in contradiction with the simplest rights of human confessed by the international standards.

* It is an "Israeli" nomination we absolutely reject for it contradicts with Lebanon's independence and sovereignty. This army is not but an "Israeli" agent working for realizing "Israel's" goals in Lebanon.
