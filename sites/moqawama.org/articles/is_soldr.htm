The Israeli Soldier
 
 
Munif Al-Haj 

The Israeli soldier Shemon Zarihan, 22 , is a new witness on the Zionist ferocity of Hebrew State and on the big gap that separates the sectors of this democratic society.

Zarihan, of Ethiopian descent, had emigrated to Israel ten years ago. In Ethiopia, his country he had been two years in a camp away from his family, before he had airlifted to Israel.

In a documentary film, made by an Israeli director, which highlights the reality of Jews Phalasha and the morale state of Israeli soldiers, Zarihan said: Throughout my life in Israel , I have been in a boarding school and then in the Israeli Army as a soldier, but now, I am living and sleeping in the street.

He added: When I get a vacation from the army, I walk around the streets until the midnight. Then, I sleep on a certain seat at the public gardens or bus stations. On the morning, I wake up to continue my touring in the streets.

On winter, I do my best to take refuge in a hidden corner far away from the eyes of military police men for they will prevent me under the penalty of law, he explained .

He continued: In Ethiopia, I was very happy when I was a child. I have never felt with the inferiority complex that I am suffering now in Israel.

The soldier asserted that he has no friends and that the only occupation he had taught in the Israeli Army is how to kill, for he serves in the Golani Brigade - an elite unit - in the Israeli - occupied Southern Lebanon.

Zarihan hints that he deals in narcotics. What I am doing is that what every soldier is doing in the Lebanese front to overcome the hell he lives there. You all know that our salaries are spent there, he said.
