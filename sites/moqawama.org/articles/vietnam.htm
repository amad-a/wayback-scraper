"ISRAEL'S" VIETNAM
 
 
By Eric Margolis  
15 Sept.1997 
 
NEW YORK - Last week, twelve elite "Israeli" naval commandos landed on Lebanon's coast, north of Sidon. Their mission: to assassinate a senior Shia Muslim cleric of the Hizbullah movement. Fighters from Hizbullah ambushed the "Israelis", killing all.

Just north of "Israel's" self-declared 'Security Zone,' a chunk of southern Lebanon annexed by "Israel" in 1982, five "Israeli" soldiers died in a brush fire ignited by "Israeli" shells. So far this year, 30 "Israeli" soldiers have been killed in combat in Lebanon. Another 73 died in a helicopter crash. Over the same period, some 200 Lebanese civilians were killed by "Israeli" shelling and air attacks.
Southern Lebanon has become "Israel's" Vietnam. Since 1982, "Israel" has lost 1,200 dead there.

The latest casualties are produced calls across "Israel" for withdrawal from the hell of Lebanon, a move advocated for the past decade by this column. Even Likud Party superhawk Ariel Sharon, the architect of "Israel's" 1982 disastrous Lebanese invasion, is now calling for "Israel" to get out.

The aim of the 1982 invasion was 1. to eradicate Yasser Arafat's PLO, which dominated the south and harassed "Israel's" northern border; 2. turn Lebanon into an "Israeli" protectorate, run by a Maronite warlord, Bashir Gemayel; 3. use Lebanon as a base from which to first destabilize, then fragment, hostile Syria. Mossad, "Israel's" crack intelligence agency, warned against the invasion.
Things went terribly wrong from the beginning. PLO units escaped intact.

Stiff resistance by Syrian troops in Lebanon killed 650 "Israel" troops. "Israeli" bombs and shells shattered the southern Lebanon cities of Tyre and Sidon.

Beirut was blasted into rubble by "Israeli" heavy guns.

"Israeli" forces killed 15,700 Lebanese civilians. Bashir Gemayel was blown up by a bomb. The UN ordered "Israel" to withdraw -a resolution which "Israel" ignores to this day.

I went into southern Lebanon with the "Israeli" Army to cover the war. PLO snipers and mines made movement extremely perilous. The "Israeli" convoy in which I rode was entering the market town of Nabatieh, when we were ordered to withdraw. There, earlier in the day, an "Israeli" armored units had bulldozed and then shot its way through a crowd celebrating Ashura, the most important and emotional Shia religious festival. The enraged Shia fired back.

That moment was the genesis of "Israel's" long, bloody woes with Hizbullah the Shia movement that dominates southern Lebanon. The Nabatieh incident, and arrogant "Israeli" behavior in Lebanon, quickly turned Shia villagers against "Israel".

Fired by the zeal of their Shia faith, Lebanon's formerly meek farmers turned into lions. Hizbullah guerillas drove the "Israeli" army south. When the United States foolishly tried to intervene in Lebanon to set up its own client regime in Beirut -under cover of a 'peacekeeping' mission - Hizbullah guerrillas blew up the US Embassy and Marine headquarters, and ran the stunned Americans out of Lebanon.

"Israel" and the US routinely denounce Hizbullah as a terrorist organization. Others see Hizbullah as a legitimate resistance movement battling against foreign occupation. Hizbullah also runs schools, hospitals, and orphanages.

Claims by "Israel" that it must retain the 'Security Zone,' garrisoned by 1,000 "Israeli" troops and 2,500 Lebanese mercenaries, are wearing thin. Hizbullah has repeatedly stated it won't attack northern "Israel" with rockets if "Israel" stops shelling Lebanese villages. Hizbullah wants "Israel" out of Lebanon and says it has no interest in attacking the Jewish state.

In spite of mounting pressure to withdraw, "Israel" hesitates for two reasons. First, Lebanon could fall under total Syrian control, giving "Israel's" main foe, President Hafez Asad, a tremendous moral boost.. Syria's 40,000 troops already control two-thirds of Lebanon. Second, an "Israeli" retreat would inevitably be feted across the Arab World and Iran as a major military defeat by for the previously invincible "Israelis".

Low-tech Hizbullah showed, however, that mighty "Israel" could be defeated by a combination of fighting skill, disregard of casualties, and suicidal courage. China and the North Vietnamese Army successfully used the same tactics against US forces in Korea and Vietnam.

Retreating from bloody Lebanon would endanger "Israel's" aura of military invincibility. It might encourage Palestinians, a most unwarlike race of shopkeepers, to adopt similar tactics. For Likud hardliners, a pullout from Lebanon means giving up the coveted waters of the Litani River, hopes of dominating Lebanon - and, worst, having to listen to intolerable crowing from Tehran and Damascus.

A diplomatic, face-saving way can surely be found out of the Lebanese quagmire. Time to end this nightmare. For "Israel", the best course is to declare victory and leave its little Vietnam.
