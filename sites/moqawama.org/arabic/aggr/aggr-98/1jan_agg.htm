January 1
Between 00:30 and 6:45
Mount Sojod was bombarded by fourteen 120mm shells.

2:20
The perimeters of Yohmor village came under a number of 60mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

2:35
A barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck Arnoun town.

3:15
Habboush River watercourse exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

4:25
Six 155mm missiles slammed into Sawan area between the villages of Choukine and Jibsheit.

4:35
Six 155mm missiles hit Jib Soweid area, east of Majdal Zoun village.

Between 4:35 and 4:45
Qaissieh Valley was targeted by six 155mm missiles.

4:40
Two 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Touline and one another on the peripheries of Khorbet Selem.

Between 4:40 and 7:55
The outskirts of Jibaa came under eleven 155mm missiles.

6:30
Tthe west of Yater village was hit by six 155mm missiles.

12:35
Five 120mm shells were fired at Hassan ravine.

14:40
A barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the residential neighborhoods of Majdal Zoun.
 
January 2

00:10
The surroundings of Zaffateh compound came under three 155mm missiles and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

00:45
Mount Sojod was bombarded by twenty eight 120 and 155mm missiles, whereas the surroundings of Sojod outpost exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

1:10
One 155mm missile fell on Mount Zaatar and another one on Qaissieh Valley.

14:10
Six 120mm shells were fired at Abou Rashed heights.

14:40
A volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the residential neighborhoods of Majdal Zoun village.

Between 19:40 and 23:00
The perimeters of Haddatha town came under fourteen 155mm missiles, more than four 120mm shells and several 81mm rounds.

19:40
The outskirts of Haddatha-Aita El-Jabal exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

19:45
The surroundings of Beit Yahoun outpost exposed to heavy machine gun bullets.

22:05
A barrage of heavy machine gun rounds targeted the surroundings of Haddatha compound.

22:35
Mount Zaatar was shelled by a number of 155mm missiles. 
January 3
00:45
One 120mm shell landed near the 17th compound, whereas the surroundings of Haddatha outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

7:45
The perimeters of Qilya, Zillaya and Naameh, Dallafeh crossroad, the 910th area and Litani River watercourse were bombarded by one hundred and fifty 120mm,155mm,160mm and 175mm missiles.

8:50
Four 155mm missiles hit Qaissieh Valley.
