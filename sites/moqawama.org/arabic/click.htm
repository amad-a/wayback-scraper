In order to read our Arabic version correctly you have three choices:
 
    1 - Install the Arabic Netscape Browser called Sindibad. (Highly Recommended).
    2 - Install the Arabic Microsoft-explorer (version 3.02 is the best).
    3 - Or if You have Arabic Windows 95, go to the preferences inside your Browser: 
      choose fonts, then choose Arabic Ariel font, then click (choose my preference), then restart your computer.
If you still have problems send us email.
