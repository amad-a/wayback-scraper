"Israeli" aggressors intercept a cargo ship near Tyre(1)
"The "Israeli" occupation forces at dawn today (Tuesday) escalated the aggressions against south Lebanon, as the "Israeli" gunboats intercepted the commercial cargo ship "Mondial" near the Tyre sea- front and kept it for 2 hours.

"The ship sailed from Hamburg-Germany carrying 118 cars aboard. The cars belong to Lebanese businessmen who said that the "Israeli" enemy often does such interception to collect money (tributes) that will be later used to finance the ailing collaborating militia.

But the Lebanese businessmen refused to reveal the sum of money they were forced to pay for the "Israeli" aggressors.

"On the other hand, the "Israeli" warplanes renewed their raids against the liberated villages, dropping several air-to-ground missiles on the vicinity of Jibaa.

"Earlier, the warplanes intensively hovered over the areas of al-Nabatieh and Iqleem al-Toffah, staging mock raids.

"Moreover, the "Israeli" artillery bombarded the outskirts of al-Loweizeh in Iqleem al-Toffah and the woods of Oqmata and Sojod with 120 and 155mm rounds from the enemy's emplacement in al-Zafatteh and al-Rihan hills.

"Al-Loweizeh residents spent all last night in bomb shelters after being targeted by "Israeli" heavy machine gun fire from the occupation's outpost in Bir Kallab. Several houses inside the town were reported damaged.

"Furthermore, civilian Mariam Srour, 45, was injured when her house in al-Mansouri was struck by an "Israeli" bomb".

Aita al-Shaab still suffering from blockade(2)
"The steadfast residents of Aita al-Shaab are still suffering from the blockade imposed by the "Israeli" occupation forces against their peaceful town.

"This morning (Tuesday), after a one-month blockade, the expelled inhabitants of Aita staged a sit in front of the UNIFIL'S HQ in Tyre, lifting banners condemning the blockade and calling for the release of all the detainees.

"They also delivered a memorandum to the UNIFIL's command, explaining the circumstances of the siege and the arrests of elders, women and children, in addition to the town's youth.

"Moreover, the protesters denounced the cut of water supply, the shootings inside the houses and the threats of destroying the houses of the steadfast residents who refuse to cooperate with the occupation forces.

"The demonstrators called on the UNIFIL to interfere and lift the blockade to allow the passage of food and medical supplies, in addition to the return of confiscated mobile phones".

Hizbullah: Aita al-Shaab siege fortifies steadfastness(3)
"Hizbullah today (Tuesday) called on all human and international committees to reveal the atrocities of the "Israeli" occupation forces and work for the lifting of the blockade imposed against Aita al-Shaab to allow humanitarian aid inside the town.

Blida, after Aita al-Shaab, Seiged(4)
"Information from the occupied zone in south Lebanon said a seige is being imposed against the town of Blida, by what is left of the collaborating militia, to force the residents to join the dismantled militia.

"ISRAELI" SAILORS PELT LEBANESE FISHERMAN WITH PRICKLY PEARS(1)
" An "Israeli" navy ship opened fire on a Lebanese fishing boat in Lebanese waters before hosing the crew with water canon and then pelting them with prickly pears, port authorities in the southern city of Tyre said Sunday. The incident occurred Saturday at dawn when Mahmud Hussein, 26, and Ali Zaatar, 24, were fishing two kilometers off the coast near the southern Lebanese port of Tyre, the same sources said. The "Israeli" vessel came up close to the boat, which was hit by a machine-gun bullet, and the sailors aimed their water canon at the two young men. They then lobbed rotten potatoes and prickly pears -- a fruit with long spikes on its skin -- at Mahmud, who protested their right to fish the waters and waved a Lebanese flag. He was later treated for bruising at a hospital in Tyre, the port authorities said. "Israel" has maintained a partial blockade on Lebanon's ports for several years and fires on fishing boats that venture more than two kilometers off the coast. In the past six years, dozens of Lebanese fishermen have been injured in encounters with the "Israeli" navy, with 21 one of them being left disabled, the Tyre fishermens' union says".

The five-nation group monitoring a cease-fire in the south is expected to meet on Thursday to discuss Lebanese and "Israeli" complaints of alleged truce violations".

" Six Lebanese, including a five-year-old child and a Hizbullah resistance fighter, were wounded by gunfire from "Israel" and its allies and an air attack in southern Lebanon Sunday. Police said the child, both his parents and another man were hurt when 15 "Israeli" shells hit the village of Mansouri, 11 kilometers south of the coastal city of Tyre, outside the "Israeli"-occupied zone. The Hizbullah member was wounded in the air strike, which also occurred outside the occupied zone. A middle-aged woman, Fatima Ramadan, was hurt when "Israel's" proxy militia machine-gunned her car on her way to the village of Yohmor. The villages of Zibqeen, Kafra, Majdal Zoun and Yater, on the western and central edges of the occupied zone were also raided. Later two "Israeli" fighter-bombers fired four missiles on the edge of Yater, adjoining the occupation zone. According to AFP, so far this year 20 civilians have been killed and 137 wounded in southern Lebanon. "Israel" has constantly targeted residential areas in southern Lebanon, in clear breach of a 1996 internationally-monitored accord which bans attacks on and from civilian targets but permits resistance operations against "Israeli" occupation troops".
