Child wounded in "Israeli" crackdown near Arnoun
"A young girl was wounded Friday when a mortar shell fired from Beaufort Castle exploded beside her in Yohmor as "Israeli" troops imposed a clampdown on the village, denying access to all traffic by machine-gunning the approach road.

"Zeinab Hamzi, 7, was struck by shrapnel in the stomach in the early evening and was admitted to hospital in Nabatieh. As she was being driven past Arnoun, SLA* militiamen manning the outpost in the occupied village fired shots near the car.

"The harassment meted out to the residents of Yohmor highlights the villages susceptibility to "Israeli" assaults after it found itself largely cut off from the rest of Lebanon following "Israel's" annexation of neighboring Arnoun in April.

""Israeli" troops manning Beaufort Castle began spraying machine-gun fire along the 1-kilometer road between Arnoun and Yohmor at around 5am, Hizbullah fighters ambushed another "Israeli" patrol at 6am. No casualties were reported.

"Although vehicles were allowed to pass between midday and early evening, "Israeli" troops in the castle fired 81mm mortar shells and SLA militiamen based in Arnoun continued to rake the surrounding countryside with heavy machine-gun fire.

"Yorge Gasser, the deputy head of the International Committee for the Red Cross in Lebanon, said that Yohmor residents requested the organizations help in transporting 19 students who were due to take their exams.

There were also two women who needed check-ups at hospital ­ one was pregnant and the other needed dialysis treatment, Gasser said. We contacted the SLA and asked if we could transport them to Nabatieh.

Yohmor resident Hussein Alleiq, 22, said that it was the first time the "Israelis" had blocked the road by machine-gun fire since Arnoun was incorporated into the occupation zone.

Yohmor is one of the more isolated villages along the front line with the Litani River Gorge, to the south and east, separating it from the occupation zone. The frequently shelled open grassland around the Hamra farmstead lies to the west and Beaufort Castle to the north.

Despite its proximity to the zone and "Israeli" soldiers in the castle, the village has largely escaped the rigors of occupation.

Only two or three families have left the village since April, Alleiq said. Theyre people who work in Nabatieh and cant afford the risk of being trapped in Yohmor.

The residents held a sit-in at the villages husseiniyeh in the afternoon to protest the "Israeli" harassment. Mohammed Ayoub, 52, said he was grateful for the assistance provided by the ICRC to ease the suffering of residents living along the frontline including Yohmor and Arnoun.

Gasser said that the events in Yohmor Friday were rather exceptional, although the ICRC is used to helping the inhabitants of frontline areas. Its not every week that a road is closed and under attack, he said.

The ICRC regularly contacts the "Israelis" and the SLA to allow residents permission to work in their fields.

Gasser said that life in Arnoun had returned more or less to normal despite a constant SLA presence. Residents didnt have access to their fields but we worked it out with the SLA. The residents can also come and go through a passageway in the barbed wire, he said.

"Other than the families that departed immediately after the "Israelis" re-took the village, no one else has left", Gasser said(1)

Daily Israeli aggression report
The Israeli occupation's warplanes at 11:00 this morning (Monday) raided against the vicinity of al-Sawaneh launching four air-to-ground missiles that damaged plantation fields in the town.

Minutes later, the enemy's warplanes retargeted the same area with another four missiles and again at 13:00, causing damage to more agricultural land.

In the meantime, the Israeli artillery emplacement at the hill of occupied Meiss fiercely bombarded Wadi al-Qaissieh and the vicinities of Touleen, Froun, al-Ghandourieh, Khorbet Silem, al-Sawaneh, Qabrikha, Wadi a'Slouqi and Wadi al-Houjeir, while the warplanes intensively hovered over the area.

Elsewhere, residents in Baraasheet lived a hard night yesterday when the Israeli occupation fired heavy machine guns and mortar rounds against the town's residential neighborhood.

For three consecutive hours, the enemy's soldiers at the Baraasheet outpost targeted the town and struck the houses of Diab Mehdi, Jamil Shebab, Hussein Shehab, Hanna Yaacoub, Abu Akram Farhat, Ahmed Farhat, Adnan Farhat, Fawzi Audi, Mohamad Audi, Hussein Yassine.
