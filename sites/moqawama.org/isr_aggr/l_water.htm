After admitting to soil theft, "Israeli" enemy gathers and steals Lebanese water
 

"After a rare admission of soil theft, ""Israel"" is now carrying out another plan to transfer Lebanon's water to territories in occupied Palestine."

"Lebanese residents living inside the occupied zone in south Lebanon were quoted as saying that ""Israel"" is carrying out a plan to steal Lebanon's water resources in al-Khiam, a border area where 90,000 cubic meters of soil has been taken away by "Israeli" occupation forces."

""Israelis" have gathered waters and springs on the al-Khiam plain into several tunnels which lead to northern areas in occupied Palestine, the Orient radio said."

"A similar report was published by the Lebanese daily as-Safir."

"Five "Israeli" bulldozers and more than 30 workers and experts embarked upon changing the terrain and linking al-Khiam plain to the Mutulla colony in northern occupied Palestine, the newspaper said.

The "Israeli" plan includes building three large reservoirs in which the rich groundwater is gathered and transferred through three huge tunnels into occupied Palestine", as-Safir added.

"Lebanon has always affirmed that ""Israel"" is stealing its water but the charge has never been admitted by the "Israeli" entity."

"On Friday, United Nations Interim Forces in Lebanon (UNIFIL) said the "Israeli" occupation army admitted that "Israeli" "civilians" are indeed removing Lebanon's soil for agricultural use in occupied Palestine."
