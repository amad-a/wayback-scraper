"Israeli" surfers assault Hizbullah internet site
 

" "Israeli" Internet surfers have been bombarding the web site of the Hizbullah, the main resistance group fighting "Israel's" occupation of south Lebanon, a Beirut daily said Monday. "Death to Hizbullah. We will kill you all," read an extract of an e-mail sent by an "Israeli" surfer on December 8, a day after a Hizbullah attack, the As-Safir newspaper reported. The number of e-mails and their tone, mostly threatening, varies according to the military situation in southern Lebanon, the paper said. "Please stop using explosive charges. Death is following us at every step that we make in southern Lebanon," said an e-mail signed by an ""Israeli" soldier in southern Lebanon" sent December 17, a day after a Hizbullah bombing in the area killed three soldiers and wounded four others. But there are also e-mails carrying hostile religious attacks: "The Jews are the rulers of the world. Death to our enemies ... we will liquidate all the Arabs," said one of the messages. A Hizbullah spokesman said the group received several messages from "Israel" nearly every day on its site (www.Hizbullah.org), mostly signed by "Israeli" soldiers. But it is a "one-way" conversation, as Hizbullah which does not recognize the "Zionist entity," refuses to answer any of the messages. "We only receive the messages, and we never answer. It helps us to have a feel of the "Israeli" public opinion and of the size of the opposition there to the occupation of southern Lebanon," said the Hizbullah spokesman".
