Daily Report
 
 
September Saturday 26 -1998:
 

Reports Expect a Sudden Withdrawal from Jezzine (1)
"An-Nahar newspaper quoted southern sources as saying that "due to the inability of the collaborators' militia to stay in the Jezzine area; as it is impossible to deploy "Israeli" units permanently in the area due to political and military reasons; and in the absence of a minimum Jezzine cover for the collaborators, the proxy militia's command, and consequently the "Israeli" occupation, has no choice other than a sudden withdrawal without guarantees or agreements."

"Jezzine has actually turned from a card in the hands of "Israel" and its collaborators to pressure the resistance and the Lebanese state into a card in the hands of the resistance and the state to use against the occupation", the daily quoted the sources as saying.

"The newspaper said "if and when it takes place, a withdrawal is expected to include all the Jezzine villages, except for the Kfar Houna Gate, which is regarded by the occupation forces as a strategic outpost overlooking the Mount Barouk and as a stage for launching aggression in the event of certain developments."

"In its issue, an-Nahar quoted sources as expecting a sudden withdrawal by the collaborators from Jezzine."

"For its part as-Safir daily said the "Israeli" occupation forces' aggressions at the Rihan-Kfar Houna -Ain Majdaleen came in the aftermath of unusual "Israeli" movements at the Rihan sector as vehicles were moving between the Jezzine area posts and those in the Rihan - Aishieh area. "

"Observer sources believe the occupation army is reducing its vehicles and equipment at the Jezzine front towards the Aishieh - Rihan", the daily said.

"The sources estimate that the "Israeli" presence in the Jezzine area has been reduced to a quarter, which may indicate a complete "Israeli" withdrawal from Jezzine is to take place within the next few days", the newspaper added.

"The possibility is enhanced by the fact that some Lahd militia officers of the Jezzine battalion have registered their children in schools in Marjeyoun and its surroundings. They have also moved to these areas as well" the paper concluded.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The IR issued a communiqué in which it recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions:
2:00, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Beirut.
6:00, Sojod outpost unleashed six 81mm shells at its surroundings.
7:15, three 120mm shells originating from Aishieh area struck Mount-Al Rafih.
12:00, the "Israeli" fighters flew, at middle altitude, in the sky of Beirut and its suburbs.
14:15, Rihan emplacement fired four 120mm shells at Arid El-Zennar area, four at Mount-Al Rafih and three others at Mount-Abou Rkab.
14:35, Arid El-Zennar came under four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan emplacement, whereas Sojod outpost targeted its surroundings with a number of grenades.
14:40, three 120mm shells originating from Rihan emplacement fell on Aqmata area.
15:10, Mount-Sojod exposed to eight 120mm shells emanating from Rihan emplacement and two 155mm from Zaffateh compound.
15:25, several 120mm shells originating from Rihan emplacement fell on the perimeters of Jarjoua town, whereas Zaffateh compound hit Mlita with one another.
16:15, Zaffateh compound targeted the northern perimeters of Jarjoua with a number of 155mm missiles enkindling several blazes.
17:10, the northern peripheries of Touline village subjected to 155mm missiles originating from Meis El-Jabal emplacement.
17:15, the old Beit Yahoun outpost showered its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
18:25, Zohaira position shelled west of Yater with five 155mm missiles.

Military Operations (3)
The IR announced that, at 18:15, its militants observed enemy movements inside Blat post. Then, the squad of two martyrs Mohammad Abdel-Karim and Said El-Ali targeted them with the appropriate weapons scoring direct and certain hits.
