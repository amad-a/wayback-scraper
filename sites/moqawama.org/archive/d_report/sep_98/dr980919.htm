Daily Report
 
 
September Saturday 19 -1998:
 

Introduction
The commander of "Israelis" proxy militia said the collaborators would be ready to retreat from Jezzine area, a enclave on the edge of the "Israeli"-occupied region in South Lebanon area on condition that the Islamic Resistance (IR) fighters should stop their attacks on the "Israeli" troops and his members.

In this connection, the news reported that the collaborator militia destroyed another outpost near Kfar Houna crossing point two days after detonating the strategic Toumat Niha outpost.

Lahd repeats offer to pull out of Jezzine ­ with some conditions (1)
"The commander of the (so-called) South Lebanon Army, Antoine Lahd, said that his militia was prepared to pull out of Jezzine and return the enclave to the government.

However, a withdrawal would be contingent on the armys assuming control of Jezzine and preventing the resistance from staging attacks on the (collaborators) from the district.

The South Lebanon Army is prepared to pull out from Jezzine if the Lebanese state takes over and commits to banning access to armed groups and preventing the region from being used as a base for operations against us, Lahd told Agence France Presse.

"Lahds comments came days after the ("Israel's" agents) redeployed from the Toumat Niha position, southeast of Jezzine, after eight militiamen were killed this year in an upsurge of attacks on the post by Hizbullah guerrillas."

"The militiamen have established a position at Ain Majdaleen, 2km to the south, which will act as a new early warning observation post for the "Israeli" troops stationed at nearby Kfar Houna, whose numbers have been reinforced in the past few days. A dirt track has reportedly been constructed between Ain Majdaleen and Kfar Houna. "

"However, a senior security source pointed out that there was nothing radically new in Lahds position and that ultimately any decision to withdraw from Jezzine was up to "Israel"".

Lahd is Israels messenger boy and his remarks are basically what "Israel" wants, the source said.
Lahd's proposal was seen by the Lebanese government as not but a mere reiteration of "Israeli" Jezzine First offer.

"A Lebanese official said "Lahd's proposal will not pass. What is required is a withdrawal similar to the one from Toumat Niha".

"The Lebanese government knows what to do in the event of a withdrawal by the collaborator militia", the official added.

After the Toumat Niha retreat, proxy militia destroys another outpost near Kfar Houna (2)
"The Lebanese daily al-Nahar today said the collaborator militia destroyed another outpost near the Kfar Houna Crossing two days after dynamiting the strategic outpost of Toumat Niha."

"The "Israeli" enemy's engineering force reinforced the fortifications at the Kfar Houna Crossing, the newspaper added in a clear indication of direct existence by the occupation troops in the Jezzine enclave. "

"The "Israeli"-occupation forces and their proxy militia's withdrawal from the Toumat Niha outpost (western Beqaa) was a prelude for withdrawal from other villages at different stages. The "Israeli" plan for withdrawal from Jezzine has begun, and some of this plan's features are starting to be clear," security sources said.

"Meanwhile, a Hizbullah senior official said evacuating the Toumat Niha outpost was evidence to the proxy militia's extreme weakness as it is no longer able to secure militiamen to defend this outpost which was the target of several successful operations by the Islamic Resistance (IR) killing and wounding a large number of the collaborators."

"Evacuation of the outpost can be viewed as a new victory by IR which was able to force the collaborators to abandon the outpost; a move which nears the Resistance and its people to full liberation," the Hizbullah official said.

"The 1,700-meter-high hilltop post, which was established in 1985 by the "Israeli" occupation forces, has commanding views of the western Beqaa to the east, Jezzine to the west and the central sector to the south."

"Toumat Niha's primary role was to act as an observation post to provide early warning of resistance activity to "Israeli" occupation troops stationed at Kfar Houna, 5km southwest at the southern end of the Jezzine enclave."

"Forty-four attacks had been staged by IR on or around the post since February 1987. Eleven of them were carried out this year in which eight militiamen were killed and three wounded," a Hezbollah statement read.

"Meanwhile, security sources disclosed that 150 militiamen, most of them from the disbanded "Lebanese Forces" militia, were brought from Cyprus last Wednesday by the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators to cover the shortage of militiamen, al-Kifah al-Arabi daily said today."
"The militiamen disembarked at the Naqoura port and were distributed to the proxy militia's barracks. They will be deployed in Jezzine next month after undergoing a training course," the newspaper quoted security sources as saying.

"The daily said that only 63 militiamen remained in Jezzine as many collaborators either fled the occupied zone or resigned from the militia."

Mohajerani Met Sfeir: Iran stands by the Lebanese government and people (4)
"Resistance in Lebanon has become comprehensive and National. The Lebanese government and people, with all its sects, stand by the Resistance to defeat the (Israeli) occupation, Iranian Culture and Islamic Guidance Minister Ataollah Mohajerani said after meeting the head of the Maronite Church in Lebanon."

"Mohajerani and Lebanese Maronite Patriarch Nasrallah Sfeir explored ways of expanding Tehran-Beirut cultural and religious cooperation as well as co-existence among faiths."

"Sfeir thanked the Islamic Republic of Iran for supporting the Lebanese government and its anti-Zionist resistance. "

"The Islamic Republic is a progressive and model system in the region," Sfeir said.

IR Operations (5)
13:45, the IR group of martyr Nasser Hussein Hawi attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants scored direct hits whereas the smoke columns were seen emanating from it.
20:00, the IR group of martyr Rabih Mohammad Wehbi attacked a collaborator motorized patrol of three M-113 armored personal carriers as it was traveling on the road leading to Bir Kallab outpost. Upon reaching to the entrance of the outpost, the mujahedeen showered it with the rockets striking directly one military vehicle.
