Daily Report
 
 
September Monday 14 -1998:
 

Introduction
The successful and powerful strikes staged recently by the Islamic Resistance militants are still reacting among the ranks of the "Israeli" allied militia.

Joseph Karam, the newly appointed as the collaborators' militia chief in Jezzine area intends to impose new measures against Jezzine's residents.

Meanwhile, several Lebanese figures stressed that concerning to the "Israeli" agents militiamen one cann't distinguish between the Maronite in Jezzine and the Shiite in Nabatieh.

However, at least one Lebanese citizen was held prisoner for he had been spying for the Jewish state for over 18 months.

Collaborators' measures to boost their morale in Jezzine (1)
"The reports from the occupied enclave of Jezzine said the collaborators' militia chief there, Joseph Karam, in coordination with the proxy militia's chief Antoine Lahd, intends to take new measures against the residents of Jezzine, including conscription of young men and taking them to the collaborators' training camps."

"The proxy militia plans also to take other measures in an attempt to boost the deteriorating morale of its militiamen in Jezzine as a result of the Islamic Resistance's painful operations. These measures include increasing the collaborators' salaries", the reports said.

"Agent Karam, alias Alloush, wants an "Israeli" force to be deployed at the entrances of Jezzine to intervene swiftly in case the collaborators are attacked by IR men", the reports added.

In the Face of Arbitrary Measures Against the Occupied Zone Residents, Lebanon Underlines "Lahd and his Militiamen are Collaborators (2)
"Lahd's militia is one collaborating with "Israel" the talk about integrating its militiamen in the Lebanese army is impossible', Prime Minister Rafiq Hariri said.

"Asked by the Egyptian weekly magazine Rose al-Youssef how Lebanon will deal with the militiamen, Hariri said "There is an area occupied by "Israel", and the militiamen joined (this collaborator) militia voluntarily, rather enthusiastically; others were forced to do so.  Every case will be examined separately."

"Kataeb (Plalange) Party Secretary General Karim Pakradouni stressed that "the Resistance and the army are the guarantee for liberation."

"One can't compare those who are collaborating with the occupier with the resistance men who are being martyred for the sake of the homeland', Pakradouni said.

"Lebanon's resistance against the "Israeli" occupation is the appropriate retaliation for expansion acts, for "Israel" wants to depopulate Jezzine because it wants the land without its people, as it wants the south without its people, no difference between the Marounite in Jezzine and the Shiite in Nabatieh", he added.

"In turn, the Moslem Ulama's Association (MUA) commended "the Islamic Resistance's recent heroic operations against the Lahd collaborator militiamen in Jezzine" condemning the practices of the occupation and its agents against the residents of Jezzine."

"MUA called on Christians to be aware of the "threats and be sure of one fact: Jezzine has been and is still occupied."

"Resistance is the only solution", an MUA communiqué said.

"The Lahd collaborators in Jezzine are enemies of Lebanon since they made themselves sand bags to protect the (Israeli) occupation", the communiqué added.

Traitor helped Mossad for 18 months before Amin hit (4)
"Akif Wehbi, the owner of a auto-body shop on Masbagha Street in Shiyah, a southern suburb of Beirut, was arrested at the beginning of the month by military intelligence agents."

"He has been accused of leaking information to "Israel" on the whereabouts of Amin which led to the deadly helicopter gunship ambush south of Tyre on August 25 in which Amin was killed."

"Wehbi was said to have shown unusual signs of wealth incompatible with his income from his garage."

"Nineteen people, including a woman, who had been arrested concurrently on suspicion that they had links to Amins killing were set free after investigations disproved the allegations."

"They were arrested on the basis of a false tip-off by people who had personal grudges against them", the source said.

"Wehbi has admitted to the charges, adding that he had been spying since early last year by leaking information on the positions of Lebanese troops and resistance fighters in the south to the "Israeli" Mossad intelligence agency", the source added.

"These latest arrests are considered separate from a spy ring that the government said it uncovered last month."

"In that case, 77 people were charged with passing information to "Israel" on Lebanese and Syrian troop movements and the activities of the resistance."

"At the time, officials said 17 of the suspects were under arrest and the remaining 60 were believed to be living in the Jezzine area, which is under (the so-called) South Lebanon Army control. - with agencies."

Military Operations:
The IR announced that, at 17:40, the squad of two martyrs Ali Khalil and Ahmad Hariri attacked a motorized collaborator patrol at the entrance of Bir Kallab outpost destroying an armored personnel carrier (5)

18:35, the IR squad of both martyrs Samir Wahab and Hassan Bazzi attacked Tair Harfa position with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of two martyrs Nizar Kansou and Mustafa Shamas fell upon Blat post with the suitable weapons chalking up direct goals.
18:30, the IR squad of two martyrs Ahmad Melhem Shahrour and Mhohammad Ali Assaf targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct and definite hits (5)

"However, Amal Movement before noon today attacked the "Israeli" occupation outposts in al-Hardoun, Haddatha, and al-Salaah with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, scoring direct hits."

"For its part, s quad of Martyr Pierre Abu Jawdeh in the Lebanese National Resistance Front (LNRF) at 1:45 (Sunday) raided the enemy outpost in Rshaf Hilltop", a Communist Party communiqué read.

"The Resistance men used machine guns and rocket propelled grenades in the attack. They clashed with the troops manning the outpost for over 20 minutes, scoring direct hits in the outpost's fortifications and inflicting several casualties in the ranks of the enemy troops", the communiqué added stating that the resistance men returned safely to their bases (6)

Monitoring group meets tomorrow in Naqoura (7)
"The international monitoring group is expected to meet tomorrow at the UNIFIL's headquarters in Naqoura. The panel will look into two complaints filed by Lebanon and "Israel" over violations of the April 96 Understanding."

"The Lebanese complaint centers on recent "Israeli" attacks on civilian areas in the villages of Marj Al-Zohour and Zillaya that resulted in the injury of a number of Lebanese civilians there. The "Israeli" complaint was lodged over alleged resistance shelling of "Israeli" outposts from civilian areas in freed Southern villages and towns."

Arab League Council to discuss "Israeli" attacks on South Lebanon (8)
"Arab Foreign Ministers will tomorrow meet in Cairo under the 110th Arab League Council meeting. Discussions are expected to focus on the implementation of UN Security Council Resolution 425.

The Arab officials will discuss the subject of Israel's occupation of South Lebanon and the Western Beqaa and the situation of "Israeli" detainees in Israeli jails."

"Lebanon's Foreign Minister, Faris Boueiz heads to Cairo tomorrow to represent his country in the two-day session. Boueiz is expected to present a detailed report explaining the real situation in the South and the importance of Arab support to Lebanon's call for the unconditional implementation of UN Resolution 425."
