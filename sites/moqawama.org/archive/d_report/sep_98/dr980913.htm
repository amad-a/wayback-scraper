Daily Report
 
 
September Sunday 13 -1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
The Islamic Resistance released a statement in which it recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions:
2:00, Meis El-Jabal emplacement pounded the outskirts of Haddatha town with twelve 155mm missiles.
8:10, Zaffateh compound targeted Aqmata area with three 155mm missiles.
10:40, Mount-Al Rafih came under five 120mm shells originating from Rihan emplacement.
12:05, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa areas.
12:20, the "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa.
15:00, the warplanes flew low over Nabatieh and Iqleem Al-Toffah and staged low mock raids over East Saida region.
15:15, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa areas.
15:25, the "Israeli" jets conducted low mock air raids over the areas of Western Sector.
15:40, the jet airplanes launched mock raids over Saida areas. In the meantime, Rihan emplacement targeted three 105mm shells at Mount-Sojod.
17:05, Rihan emplacement bombarded Mount-Sojod with ten 120mm shells.
20:10, the helicopters hovered over Harouf, Zawtar Gharbieh and Zawtar Sharqieh towns.
20:40, Mount-Sojod subjected to four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan emplacement.
21:20, Rshaf post showered Haqban Hill with Mag middle machine gun rounds.
23:00, the reconnaissance planes circled, at middle height, in the airspace of Nabatieh, Iqleem Al-Toffah and East Saida regions.
23:10, an M.K. surveillance aircraft soared over Kfar Seir, Ibba, Jibsheit and Adsheit villages.
23:15, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Froun, Ghandourieh, Hmairi and Hallousieh villages.
23:30, a drone reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Ghassanieh and the nearby areas.

IR Operations (2)
The IR adopted in its communiqué the following attacks on the "Israeli" enemy forces and their agents:
14:55, the IR squad of both of martyrs Salem Mohammad Abboud and Ibrahim Ali Issa attacked Sojod outpost with the proper rockets. The Mujahedeen asserted that they scored direct hits and destroyed two of its main fortifications at the entrance of the post.
15:00, the IR group of two martyrs Ibrahim Abdel-Hassan Hariri and Hussein Abbas Yassine fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct goals through destroying its main fortifications. Also, the smoke columns were seen emanating from within it.
15:55, the IR men spotted fortification works at the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Shortly afterwards, the group of martyr Kassem Ahmad Nahim attacked them with the suitable arms scoring direct hits.
