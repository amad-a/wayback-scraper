Daily Report
 
 
September Saturday 5 -1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
in its September 5,1998 communiqué the Islamic Resistance (IR) recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions:
6:45, Rihan emplacement fired four 120mm shells at Mount-Al-Rafih.
7:00, Zaffateh compound shelled Aqmata area with three 155mm missiles.
14:20, six 155mm missiles originating from Zaffateh position struck Mlita village, whereas Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
15:15, the warplanes flew low in the airspace of Central Sector, Nabatieh and East Saida areas and conducted low mock raids over Iqleem Al-Toffah.
15:20, the "Israeli" fighters raided into Mlita Hill and the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
15:35, Zaffateh compound targeted Arid El-Zennar area with two 155mm missiles.
15:50, an espionage plane circled over the areas of Iqleem Al-Toffah.
18:05, west of Yater village exposed to three 155mm missiles originating from Zohaira post.
18:10, Sojod outpost showered its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:45, an espionage aircraft hovered over Kfour ravine then turned towards the east.
18:55, Shraifeh position pounded Hamra area with five 155mm missiles.
19:00, Shraifeh post bombarded the surroundings of Qantara with seven 155mm missiles.
19:05, an M.K. surveillance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah heights.
19:10, Meis El-Jabal emplacement shelled Aissieh Valley with a number of 155mm missiles, when on the other hand, Qantara outpost raked Qabrikha town with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
19:20, Salaah and Mothallath positions showered their surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire, whereas a smoke shell unleashed over Mothallath position.
19:35, four 155mm missiles emanating from Shraifeh position fell on Sawan area, while at the same time, Zaffateh compound targeted the peripheries of Zawtar Gharbieh and Zawtar Sharqieh with several 155mm shells.
20:00, two 81mm flash shells fired over Salaah post.
20:05, the western perimeters of Touline village subjected to a number of 155mm missiles originating from Meis El-Jabal emplacement.
20:20, Zaffateh compound hit Aqmata area with three 155mm missiles, whereas Zohaira position targeted west of Yater with three others, one of which is phosphoric.
20:25, five 155mm shells originating from Zohaira position struck west of Yater.
22:10, several flare shells lightened west of Shamaa village.
22:20, Shamaa post unleashed a number of 120mm flash shells over Hamra-Bayad area.
23:20, one lightened helicopter hovered over the towns of Houla and Blida.
