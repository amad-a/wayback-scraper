October Saturday 24-1998:

Introduction

Besides the military aggressions on the liberated areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" occupation forces detained a Lebanese civilian and took him to Khiam detention center and distrained a trade ship near the port of Tyre.

Meanwhile, the Follow-up Committee for the Lebanese Detainees in "Israel's" detention centers called on the Lebanese to commemorate the Detained Teacher's Day on the 7th of November.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

"The "Israeli" occupation proceeded with their aggressions against the residents of the occupied zone.

In the village of Qlaileh, "Israeli" forces detained resident Bassam Gorgeous Naeem and led him to the "Israeli"-run al-Khiam detention center."

'Naeem is a taxi driver who works between Beirut and Bater. "

"Moreover, "Israeli" navy forces yesterday detained a trade ship near the port of Tyre. The ship, carrying cars imported from Belgium, was searched for more than half an hour before it was allowed to enter the port"… (1)

However, the IR mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:

00:10, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Froun, Ghandourieh and Litani River watercourse.

00:20, Zaffateh compound pounded the outskirts of Habboush with thirteen 155mm missiles.

00:35, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Khorbet Selem and Aissieh Valley.

00:55, Mount-Safi exposed to four 155mm rounds emanating from Zaffateh compound.

6:35, Rihan emplacement unleashed three 120mm shells at Mount-Sojod.

11:50, Zohaira position targeted south of Jibal El-Botom with four 155mm missiles. At the same time, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Beirut, whereas an M.K. surveillance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah highlands.

12:10, the "Israeli" fighters reiterated flying in the sky of Beirut areas.

16:05, Rihan emplacement fired three 120mm shells at Mount-Sojod and the outskirts of Loweizeh and two others at Aqmata plains.

16:20, Zaffateh compound bombarded the perimeters of Loweizeh with seven 155mm missiles... (2)

FCLD's activities on Detained Teacher's Day
"The Follow-up Committee for the Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" prisons (FCLD) called on the Lebanese to mark the Detained Teacher's Day on the 7th of November.'

"In a press conference the committee announced the activities program. These will include the declaration of a new document on the conditions of the Lebanese hostages in al-Khiam detention center and in prisons inside occupied Palestine, in addition to holding seminars, photo exhibitions, and meetings in different Lebanese towns in solidarity with the detainees."

"The activity program was declared by liberated detainee Suha Bshara who called on Lebanese teachers to concern themselves with the causes of detainees, resistance and liberation in order to be able to liberate the coming generations and inspire them with the spirit of resistance."

"During the conference, FCLD Secretary Mohammed Safa talked about the issue of the Lebanese hostages in the "Israeli" prisons."

"We have reports that the health conditions of some detainees in the al-Khiam prison is deteriorating," Safa said.

"Among those who are in critical condition is detainee Ali Hejazee whose wife confirmed, after visiting him two days ago, that he has been suffering complete health collapse," he added.

"Safa affirmed that detainee Mustafa Jawad Tawbeh was admitted to a hospital in Marjeyoun while in a coma. He called on the parliament to speed up the endorsement of the bill that sponsors the Martyrs' families which was drafted by Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc member MP Nazeeh Mansour. The bill is currently being examined by the parliament's administration and justice committee"… (3)

"The day’s campaign will call in particular for the release of children, the sick and the elderly detained in Khiam, according to Mohammed Safa".

"A year ago, Ali Touba, now 15, was taken to Khiam from the village of Arnoun with his father Mustafa, who has since become paralysed."
“My son can’t walk anymore,” said Mustafa’s father Jawad Touba, 68, who visited him four days ago at Marjayoun hospital where he is recovering from blood pressure problems.
“To make it harder for him, they’ve refused to let his imprisoned son see him for two-and-a-half months,” Touba said at the conference. “They were peaceful tobacco farmers. Why are they keeping them away from me?”
"Detainee Ali Hijazi’s health has deteriorated considerably since he was first imprisoned nine years ago."
"One of his legs has been amputated and he suffers from severe back pains, an ulcer and an eye disorder."
“Over the past five years it has taken more than 160 tranquilliser shots to relieve him of his intolerable pain,” said his wife, Sana, who had visited him on Wednesday."
“Two guards carry him, one on either side. He told me that he can’t stand the pain any longer,” she added.
"On the Thursday before the national day, public and private schools will dedicate two hours of class time to remembering the prisoners", a statement from the education ministry said.
“Teachers will describe the suffering of detainees in Khiam camp and other prisons housing Lebanese in "Israel",” it added.
"Students will also be asked to sign a petition calling for the prisoners’ release, to be submitted to the UN and other international organisations"… (4)

"Elsewhere, the Australian Group for Defending South Lebanon and the Western Beqaa - Lebanon Watch, yesterday called on Australia to pressure ""Israel"" to release Lebanese child, Ali Moustafa Tawbeh.

"The group yesterday issued a statement demanding the immediate release of Tawbeh and all Lebanese children who are being held in "Israeli" jails. Tawbeh, who was born in the Southern village of Arnoun, was detained on the 3rd of October 1997"… (5)
