October Thursday 8-1998:

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)

The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas:

5:30, Bir Kallab and Sojod outposts showered their surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

11:45, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah heights.

12:30, the warplanes flew over the areas of Western Sector, whereas the helicopters soared over Rihan region.

12:40, the "Israeli" fighters circled high in the sky of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

14:45, Rihan emplacement fired four 120mm shells at Mount-Sojod.

15:00, Zaffateh compound targeted Tasseh Well watercourse with five 155mm missiles and Mount-Sojod with three others, when on the other hand, Rihan emplacement unleashed four 120mm shells at the surroundings of Sojod outpost. Meanwhile, the helicopters hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

Between 15:10 and 15:40, Mlita village came under nineteen 155mm missiles originating from Zaffateh compound.

16:25, an M.K. surveillance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over the areas of Iqleem Al-Toffah.

16:35, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered over the towns of Bir Al-Salassel and Khorbet Selem.

17:30, the warplanes circled in the sky of Western Sector areas.

18:40, west of Yater subjected to seven 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position.

19:00, Zohaira position pounded west of Yater and the outskirts of Majdal Zoun with ten 155mm missiles.

21:30, Rihan emplacement bombarded Mount-Sojod with seven 120mm shells.

21:35, Meshnaqa post showered its surroundings with heavy machine gun fire and Roum post raked Azour Hill with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

21:40, the drone reconnaissance planes flew over the villages of Chaitieh, Zibqeen, Majdal Zoun, Debbaal, Barish, Sreifa, Chohour, Jibsheit, Harouf, Ibba, Kfar Seir and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr.

21:45, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered over Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

22:15, four 155mm missiles originating from Zaffateh compound fell on Habboush River banks.

22:20, Rihan emplacement targeted Mount-Sojod with six 120mm shells.

22:25, Sojod outpost combed intensely its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

23:20, a drone reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over Kafra, Yater and Deir Ames.

Military Operations (2)

The IR squad of martyr Mohammad Sawli attacked, at 14:30, Sojod outpost destroying the main fortification at its entrance. Meanwhile, the group of martyr Abbas Hammoud attacked its western side with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Several defences were certainly destroyed as the Mujahedeen reported.

18:35, the IR squad of martyrs Abdallah Mahmoud Chaitou and Abdel-Mawla Hussein Ismail observed enemy movements inside Radar- Ras El Bayad post. Shortly afterwards, the militants targeted them chalking up direct and certain hits. At the same time, the group of two martyrs Ali Wahab Hijazi and Hassan Mahmoud Jaghbir hit Blat post with the adequate arms scoring direct and definite goals.

21:30, the IR men spotted military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. The squad of both martyrs Fouad Said Abdallah and Mohammad Ali Najem attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the Mujahedeen reported.

22:30, as an enemy force intervened to reinforce Sojod outpost, the group of martyrs Fadi Hassan Tawil and Anwar Ali Al-Mir attacked it with the suitable weapons preventing it from achieving its goals.
