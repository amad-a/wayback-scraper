October Wednesday 14-1998:

"Israel" violates the Understanding

"Israeli" occupation forces violated the April understanding only a short while after the monitoring panel concluded its meeting"

"Starting at 17:30, the occupation's artillery in Shraifeh pounded the outskirts of Qilya, Zillaya and Mazraat al-Dallafeh."

"Mohammed Ibrahim Issa's family narrowly escaped death when an "Israeli" shell hit their house while they were inside."

"Lebanese security bodies are now preparing a file on the recent shelling as prelude to lodge a complaint with the monitoring group."

"The April Understanding Monitoring Group upheld a Lebanese complaint and blamed "Israel" for violating the understanding by shelling Baraasheit"… (1)

Military Operations… (3)

"In retaliation for the "Israeli" enemy's aggressions against civilians and firing incendiary bombs to ignite fires, the Islamic Resistance (IR) at dawn today launched a warning attack with the appropriate weapons on the enemy's outpost of al-Abbad (at the border with occupied Palestine), an IR communiqué read."

"IR warned the "Israeli" enemy of going too far with its aggression, "for the retaliation will be comprehensive and concentrated."

"An "Israeli" military source acknowledged that the outpost was directly hit and that one "Israeli" soldier was injured when a shell hit the compound which is near the Kiryat Shmona colony."

"The incident raised to 82 the number of "Israeli" occupation soldiers wounded in Resistance operations in south Lebanon since the start of 1998. Twelve have been killed in the period."
