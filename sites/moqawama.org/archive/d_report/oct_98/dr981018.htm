October Sunday 18-1998:

Occupation forces block off Hasbaya and train collaborators to do their dirty jobs… (1)

The "Israeli" occupation forces have imposed a total closure on the occupied town of Hasbaya where a total curfew was still in force yesterday. The move followed several arrests over dawn."

"Meanwhile, the Zimraya crossing leading into the occupied zone has been closed to civilian traffic without explanation from the occupation forces."

"On the other hand, Syrian news Agency (SANA) quoted Lebanese military sources as saying that the "Israeli" occupation army has almost finished training 120 collaborator militiamen to fly and use military helicopters."

"The training course started last July inside occupied Palestine; it will continue until the end of November", the Lebanese sources said.

"The militiamen will be based in Marjeyoun. They will get their orders directly from the "Israeli" military command, the sources stated, adding that three helicopters will be used by the collaborator militiamen to stage swift aggressions in south Lebanon."

"In the liberated areas, Lebanese security forces have arrested Mohammed Raslan on suspicion of spying for Israeli enemy."

"Security sources said that Raslan, along with Mohammed Jaber, known as "Abu Alloush", had formed a spy ring aiming to collect information on Lebanese army positions and resistance men movements."

"Israeli" Aggressions… (2)

The "Israeli" forces launched the following "Israeli" aggressions on Lebanon's lands:

00:05, Meis El-Jabal emplacement fired several 155mm rounds at Nahleh ravine between Shaqra and Majdal Selem towns.

10:20, the espionage planes hovered over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River watercourse.

10:35, one 120mm round emanating from Rihan emplacement fell on Mount-Sojod.

10:55, the reconnaissance planes circled in the sky of East Saida region.

13:15, the surveillance planes flew over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River watercourse.

22:50, the helicopters soared, at middle height, over Houla and Jabal El-Jamaah.

Military Operations… (3)

The Islamic Resistance spotted, at 17:45, enemy movements inside Blat position. Then, the squad of two martyrs Ahmad Dbouk and Hussein Kansou targeted them with the suitable arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of martyr Hassan Hammoudi attacked Radar-Ras El-Bayad post with the adequate weapons. Direct goals were scored, whereas the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Arab bar union recommends stopping normalization with the "Israeli" enemy… (4)

"The Permanent Office for the Arab Lawyers' Union concluded its second session for the year 1998 in Tripoli. The session was held at an invitation by the Lebanese Bar in the North with the slogan "The session for Backing Lebanon and Implementation of Resolution 425."

"Participants issued a statement stressing their solidarity with Lebanon's people and Resistance against the "Israeli" occupation."

"Lawyers stressed their support for the implementation of UN security Council Resolution 425 which calls for unconditional "Israeli" withdrawal from Lebanon, condemning the military and security "Israeli"-Turkish alliance which is sponsored by the US."
