October Tuesday 6-1998:

Introduction

The heavy human losses which inflicted on the "Israeli" troops in southern Lebanon have fuelled a debate in "Israel" about the wisdom of its occupation of Lebanese territories. The attack is yet another setback for the "Israeli" army which has suffered severe losses at the hands of the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants who have been improving their military tactics and intelligence networks. The string of explosive devices forced the command of "Israel's" Army to open investigation into the circumstances of the roadside bombing.

"Israeli" Enemy Investigates IR's Qualitative Infiltration into Hasbaya

""Israeli" occupation forces last midnight acknowledged that two paratrooper sergeants were killed and six others were wounded in the Islamic Resistance's qualitative operation near Hasbaya."

"The attack shocked the "Israeli" occupation's command due to the security penetration of the Mujahedeen who had been able to infiltrate through the tight "Israeli" measures into an area of command and security centers belonging to the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators."

"Regarding the execution of the operation, planting the bombs and the timing of targeting the command column unveiled a new security achievement by IR which once again proved that all the enemy troops and officers are vulnerable to its attacks."

"This (vulnerability) was behind the occupation command's not announcing its losses immediately. This, in turn, indicated an indirect acknowledgment by the enemy of the futility of its measures and the hi-tech armoring methods used to protect the occupations troops and convoys."

"The Hebrew speaking "Israeli" radio's corespondent disclosed that an investigation panel has been formed to examine the kind of bombs used by IR mujahedeen and their explosion power which destroyed two vehicles killing or wounding those in them."

"The "Israeli" radio said the bombs targeted 4 vehicles which were on their way to the Marjeyoun area to inspect collaborator militia outposts in the Hasbaya area."

"A string of explosive devices were detonated one after another as a convoy of four "Israeli" army vehicles - three lightly armored Storm (Sufa) jeeps and a bigger Abir jeep - were driving past, "Israeli" Jerusalem Post said."

"OC Northern Command Maj.-Gen. Gabi Ashkenazi said the second and last vehicles in the convoy were hit by the explosives, which were apparently detonated by remote control, the newspaper added.

It seems clear that Hizbullah had accurate information about the movement of the "Israeli" convoy", the paper concluded.

"Jerusalem Post quoted Ashkenazi as saying that the attack had been carefully planned and that the roadside bombs exploded one after another.

"Hizbullah is continuing to use roadside bombs to stage pinpoint attacks against "Israeli" army and (proxy militia) targets in the (occupied) zone and in the Jezzine enclave north of the zone", the "Israeli" daily said… (1)

"Meretz Party leader Yossi Sarid said the casualties in Lebanon were neither the result of fate nor of heavenly decree, but rather of policy made by real live politicians, who sanctify the Golan Heights and block the road to negotiations with Syria. Sarid called on all moderates to pressure the prime minister to renew the talks in order to make possible a quick withdrawal from Lebanon."
"As long as "Israel" refuses to deal with Syria, Sarid said, "the responsibility for the blood spilled in Lebanon is on [the government]… (2)

"Israeli" MKs Accuse Occupation Army of Covering up Investigations… (3)

"The "field incidents" of the "Israeli" army in south Lebanon are on the rise; and the "Israeli" army command often cover up investigations", a French Jewish newspaper said.

"Official information noted an increase of the incidents against the "Israeli" army since the beginning of the year", L'Arche newspaper said.

"In other statistics "La Tribune de Jueiffe" said 55 "field incidents" took place during the last Jewish year".

"Informed "Israeli" military sources said attacks against the "Israeli" forces have been increasing over the last year."

"Although the rise is slight, it is certainly worrying", the sources said.

"Meanwhile, "Israeli" parliamentarians accused the "Israeli" army of covering up and changing the facts about deadly incidents."

"MK Ran Cohen (Meretz) said the "Israeli" Knesset's Foreign Affairs and Security committee strongly fears that the "Israeli" army is covering up the outcome of investigations on deadly incidents which hit the army."

Hizbullah Rejects Agent Lahd's Recent Proposal… (4)
"Agent Antoine Lahd's recent mediation proposal is a modified version of the "Jezzine first" plan", Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General Sheikh Naem Qassem said.

"In an interview with al-Safir daily, his Eminence underlined that "Lahd wants a dialogue with the other side at any cost, be it direct or indirect, to make a deal on withdrawal from Jezzine. Lahd doesn't aim to pull out of Jezzine and have the Lebanese army replace his militiamen without any price; he wants the local or foreign mediation to drag us into security arrangements, even if these were restricted to Jezzine alone."

"We reject any form of negotiation or security arrangements (with the "Israeli" occupation or its collaborators). The only thing we accept is for Lahd to pull out unconditionally and for the Lebanese army to replace his militiamen", Sheikh Qassem said.

"The Resistance which is targeting the "Israeli" occupation troops and their collaborators will consider such a withdrawal as an additional achievement to be added to previous victories of imposing withdrawals from Toumat Niha, Sidon and Kfar Falous", he added.

"Israeli" Aggressions… (5)

The "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas:

5:35, Sojod outpost raked its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

8:55, an espionage aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

9:05, Zaffateh compound targeted Mount-Sojod with three 155mm missiles.

9:30, the helicopters soared over the area of Aishieh town.

11:30, Hardoun position fired ten 60mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun at the perimeters of Majdal Zoun village.

13:50, Baraasheit outpost combed its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

14:10, Baraasheit outpost hits Baraasheit town with three 81mm shells damaging the houses of Mohammad Ahmad Chehab, whose shops were hit also, and Rida Chehab and the car of Hussein Chehab.

14:40, one 155mm missile emanating from Zaffateh compound struck Mount-Safi.

14:45, Rihan emplacement unleashed three 120mm shells at Mount-Sojod.

17:00, two 120mm shells originating from Jamousa position fell on Haddatha woodland.

19:35, the warplanes flew, at middle altitude, over Kafra, Yater and the coast line between Mansouri and Ras El-Ain releasing anti-missile flares.

19:45, the jet airplanes circled, at middle height, in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

19:50, the "Israeli" fighters flew in the sky of Western Beqaa, Nabatieh and Jibsheit regions.

22:10, Sojod outpost raked its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

21:15, the helicopters hovered over Insarieh and Adloun towns.

Lebanese protest expulsion of elderly couples from occupation zone… (6)

"A sit-in was held this morning at the institution of the Druze community in Beirut to protest the expulsion of two elderly Druze religious sheikhs and their wives from the "Israeli" occupied zone in south Lebanon. It was attended by the Druze spiritual leader Sheikh Bahjat Gheith who stressed the steadfastness of the residents of Rashaya. On Friday, the "Israeli" allied militia expelled the parents of two men who recently defected from the militia. The parents of Raja Ward from the town of Hasbaya and the parents of Naji Munzer from the village of Ible el Saqi were ordered to leave the occupied zone. Raja Ward was an intelligence chief in the militia and turned himself in to the Lebanese army in July and his revelations led to the arrest of a pro "Israeli" spy network. Naji had shot dead a militia intelligence official in the zone before fleeing into government held territory."

Military Operations… (7)

The IR squad of two martyrs Majed Jaffal and Ali Moghnieh attacked, at 17:00, a collaborator engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost. The militants asserted that a bulldozer was hit directly.

However, a group of Amal Movement shelled, at 17:00, Baraasheit outpost.

"NCLD asked the international community to take action and pressure ""Israel"" to release them."
