Daily Report
 
 
October Wednesday 28 -1998:
 
Introduction
A member of Lahd's collaborator militia was killed and another was wounded as the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants bombarded Hmayed post with artillery shells.

Elsewhere, an official of the Palestinian Islamic Jihad Movement, his wife and child were critically injured when a car bomb exploded on the eastern highway of the southern city of Saida suggesting to an Israeli hand in this new crime.

In the meantime, Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah said that every inch of our occupied land is stage for the IR operations.

One collaborator militiaman killed another wounded in an IR operation (1)
"The Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Martyr Ahmed Hikmat Dbouq at 12:20 today targeted a collaborator militia force which was fortifying the outpost of Hmayed."

"The mujahedeen used direct rocket propelled grenades and were able to inflict definite casualties in the ranks of the proxy-militia force."

"One militiaman was killed and another was wounded in the Hmayed attack, security sources inside the occupied zone said."

"The attack brought to 35 the number of collaborator militiamen killed in resistance operations in south Lebanon since the beginning of 1998, according to enemy sources."

"The Amal Movement announced that one of its squads targeted "Israeli" reinforcement forces inside the Hardoun outpost."

An Islamic Jihad official escapes terrorist car bombing (2)
"The repercussions of the Arafat-"Israeli" deal and the intelligence coordination between the US, "Israel" and Arafat have started to appear in transferring tension and terrorism to areas other than the occupied Palestinian territories."

"As a translation of the secret security documents which had been prepared by the Americans and the "Israelis" in Washington to launch terrorist attacks against Palestinians opposing the humiliating settlement whether inside or outside occupied Palestine, a car bomb exploded in the southern Lebanon city of Sidon on Tuesday, wounding an official of the Islamic Jihad in Palestine along with his wife and son."

"Abu Hamza al-Mazjoub, his wife, Noha al-Rawwas, and five-year-old child Hussein were hurt when the bomb exploded as he started his car on Saida's eastern highway. A passerby was also wounded in the blast."

"The bombing took place the day after the Lebanese army and police units tightened security in Saida following the killing of two policemen by unidentified gunmen on Sunday night."

"At around 9:00 this morning, the bomb which was placed in al-Majzoub's car went off as he and his family entered the vehicle. The car was parked next to Majzoub's house."

"Majzoub, 37, who is Lebanese, his wife and their son were immediately taken to the Hammoud hospital. Hassan Maki who worked in a printing press close to where Majzoub's Volvo was parked was also hurt in the explosion."

"An Islamic Jihad representative, Abu Imad al-Rifai, accused "Israel" intelligence services (Mossad) of attempting to assassinate Majzoub".

"The crime was carried out by the Mossad and is a direct consequence of the Wye plantation agreement between "Israel" and the Palestinian Authority which was meant to destabilize the situation in Palestine and abroad", Abu Imad said.

 Hizbullah SG: Every inch in the occupied zone is stage for resistance operations (3)
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah honored a group of Islamic Resistance (IR) officers and members of Martyr Sayyed Abbas al-Musawi's squad."

"The squad, named after the late Secretary General of Hizbullah who was martyred along with his wife and five-year-old son in an "Israeli" helicopter attack in 1993, has recently carried out a series of courageous operations against the Israeli occupation forces, including the attacks on the Rihan barracks, Ain Majdaleen, Toumat Niha, al-Ahmadieh, and Zagleh outposts."

"Sayyed Nasrallah presented the honored Mujahedeen with gifts and delivered a speech stressing that the Jihad option is the only way to liberate the land."

"These courageous operations came under political and military circumstances which enriched their Jihad and morale dimension", his Eminence said.

"Every inch of our occupied land is stage for the Islamic Resistance operations", he added.

MP Mansour: Endorsing a bill to sponsor the martyrs families is only a first step (5)
"Faithfulness to the Resistance Bloc Member MP Nazih Mansour said the endorsement of the bill to sponsor the families of those martyred, detained, or disabled while resisting the "Israeli" occupation by the parliamentary Justice and Administration Committee is only one step towards solving the problems of these families, adding that these problems should have been tackled long time ago."

"Mansour also said the bill awaits approval from the general assembly before it goes into effect
"After the parliamentary approval, the cabinet has to ratify the law's practical regulations," the Hizbullah MP added.
