October Tuesday 13-1998:

Introduction

The "Israeli" occupying forces exploited the unusual rise in the heat of the atmosphere in Lebanon and launched a campaign of firing phosphorous and flare shells on the forested lands of South Lebanon. A lot of brush blazes were erupted destroying huge amounts of woodlands and groves.

Meanwhile, the five-nation monitoring committee blamed "Israel" for breaching the April 1996 Understanding by shelling Baraasheit town.

"Israeli" shelling fuels fires

"A yellow haze hung over Akkar, Mount Lebanon and the south as tinder-dry undergrowth and a strong breeze fuelled dozens of brush fires, destroying huge amounts of cedar and pine forests and olive and citrus groves."
"As if the natural elements were not enough to exacerbate the nation-wide fires, "Israeli" gunners made matters worse. In a calculated attempt to denude the densely forested slopes that provide cover for resistance fighters infiltrating the central sector of the occupation zone, "Israeli" gunners went into action firing phosphorous rounds into Iqleem al-Toffah, sparking a huge blaze."
"Despite the absence of resistance activity in the area, at 2.15pm, "Israeli" guns began shelling the eastern slopes of Wadi Zahrani at the southern end of Arab Salim, with an average of one phosphorous shell exploding on the heights in a ball of soft, white smoke every five minutes. Within a quarter of an hour, a huge fire had broken out, carried northwards by the stiff breeze and casting a thick yellow pall over Iqleem al-Toffah."
"Similar shelling on Monday had already turned vast areas of the hillside into a charred expanse of rock and earth, almost reaching the "Israeli" Sojod post, clearly visible on the ridge above Arab Salim."
“They’ve burned the lower slopes and now they’re concentrating on the upper part of the wadi,” said "Arab Salim’s mukhtar, Mustafa Moussa, as he watched the fire spread. “They know the resistance uses the undergrowth to creep into the zone so they’re deliberately burning it all,” he added.
"Further south, "Israeli" phosphorous shells had ignited the dry brush around Kafra and Yater. The blaze spread rapidly to Majdal Zoun, 10km further west. Hundreds Tyre’s residents gathered on the beach to watch the blaze on the hills to the east as Nepalese Unifil soldiers struggled with local villagers to contain the fires which, by nightfall, had got out of control."
"By nighfall, brush fires had spread to Mansouri, facing the western sector of the "Israeli" occupation zone, trapping some 4,000 inhabitants."
"The blaze quickly spread to Unifil’s post in the village, which was promptly evacuated by its Nepalese occupants."
"Looking east from Jbaa in Iqleem al-Toffah, dozens of brush fires were visible as civil defence personnel and soldiers rallied to contain the blazes."
"In Wadi Jenneh, just south of Kfar Melki, a huge fire had erupted out of control, destroying hundreds of olive trees as soldiers looked on helplessly. Several fires had broken out in Qraiye, with one threatening a roadside petrol station. The owner hosed his fuel pumps with water but the fire petered out before reaching the station"… (1)

Military Operations… (3)

"In spite of all the "Israeli" occupation's security measures to protect its troops, the Islamic Resistance (IR) staged a new qualitative operation in the Beaufort Castle area."

"IR's squad of Martyrs Karim Atwi and Mohammed Hammoud at 18:00 today detonated a large roadside bomb near an "Israeli" foot patrol at the Zaffateh - Beaufort Castle outpost road, an IR communiqué read."

"The attack resulted in killing or wounding the patrol troops. Some enemy troops were seen killed or wounded at the operation side. They remained there for over 30 minutes and were evacuated after bringing more reinforcement", the communiqué added.

"Israel" found to have violated April accord… (4)

"The five-nation monitoring group upheld a Lebanese complaint and blamed "Israel" for violating the April agreement by shelling Baraasheit."
"A single 81mm mortar round exploded in Baraasheit on October 6, damaging a shop and two houses “in violation of the understanding”, the statement from the group said.
""Israel" also filed a complaint over allegations that the resistance fired mortar rounds from within populated areas northwest of Majdal Zoun."
"However, the group concluded that “there was no agreement on whether the launching site was in a civilian populated area”.
"The group, comprising delegates from Lebanon, Syria, France, "Israel" and the US, welcomed “the constructive attitude of the parties, in the presentation and evaluation of the evidence and noted that such an attitude reinforces the effectiveness of the understanding”.

Lebanese teenager held by "Israelis" for a year: Rights Group… (5)

"A group which campaigns on behalf of Lebanese prisoners held by "Israel" called for the release of a 15-year-old who it said has been held for a year in "Israel's" occupation zone in southern Lebanon. Ali Tawbeh was abducted from the village of Arnoun, in the central part of the occupation zone, by the "Israeli" proxy militia in October 1997, the Committee for the Release of Lebanese Prisoners said in a statement. "Ali, (then) a 14-year old schoolboy, was accused of being an international terrorist," the group said. Tawbeh was taken to the "Israeli"-run Khiam prison where his father Mustapha, accused of supporting the resistance against the "Israeli" occupation of the occupation zone, is also being held. Mustapha Tawbeh had been kidnapped two weeks earlier by the "Israeli"-backed militia along with his wife who was released shortly afterwards", it said.

"Several other teenagers are among the 124 Lebanese being held in Khiam without trial, the group said, while 42 other Lebanese are being held in "Israel" itself. Maltreatment and torture are systematic in "Israeli" jails and this has been documented by international human rights groups like Amnesty International. The Lebanese government has been working hard to lobby the international community to pressure "Israel" to release these prisoners but the Jewish state continues to defy international laws and standards of human rights."

Lebanese detainee in poor health: Amnesty… (6)

"Amnesty International today issued a letter seeking information on Suleiman Hassan Ramadan from "Israel" and its allied militia in South Lebanon. Ramadan, has been held without charge by "Israel's" allied militia in the Khiam detention center in occupied South Lebanon since September 1985. "He is reported to have been detained in incommunicado detention for three years and to have been beaten and tortured by electric shock," Amnesty's statement said. The group warned that it has received reports that Ramadan is in poor health and may be denied access to adequate medial care. Amnesty said it is seeking more information on his current state of health, and wants Ramdan to be brought to trial "in accordance with international standards". Amnesty also described in detail violation of human rights practiced in the notorious detention center. Some 145 Lebanese and other Arabs are detained in Khiam."
