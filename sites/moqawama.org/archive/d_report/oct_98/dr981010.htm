October Saturday 10-1998:

Introduction

Al-Nahar Lebanese newspaper made an interview with the commander of the Islamic Resistance IR squads which carried out last Monday’s qualitative operation at Zaghleh area, Hasbaya District. This powerful attack resulted in killing two “Israeli” soldiers and injuring seven others as “Israel’s” Army acknowledged.

Meanwhile, the (IR) announced the martyrdom of two of its fighters during a confrontation with the “Israeli” troops in the vicinity of Rihan barracks.

The Hasbaya operation commander: We chose the right time and spot… (1)
“Last Monday's qualitative operation by the Resistance in the Zaghleh area (near Hasbaya) which killed two Israelis and wounded seven others was carried out by the Special Forces Unit of the Islamic Resistance.”

“Experts believe the attack shocked the military and security bodies of the “Israeli” forces, for the Resistance was able - for the first time - to stage such an operation in a dangerous security square, particularly since the “Israelis” and the "South Lebanon Army" (the collaborators' militia) regard Hasbaya and its outskirts as "friendly areas".

“An-Nahar met the commander of the squads which carried out the operation, in one of Hizbullah's centers in the southern suburbs; he had just returned from his military activity in the south.”

"The Zaghleh operation is more than qualitative because we staged it in a site where the enemy does not expect attacks, so we used the surprise element," said the young man who is in his thirties.”

“If the “Israelis” think that that area is friendly, we tell them all the occupied zone is unsafe for them”, he added.

“The commander said the operation was carried out by three squads.”

“We were three squads, one for execution, another for attack, the third for protection. Prior to the attack, we had already conducted a scan and surveillance of the site before planting the bombs. It is the first time we use this type of bombs against armored vehicles”, he said.

“He affirmed that "a group of collaborator militiamen was among the “Israeli” column which was made of three armored jeeps and an armored military truck. The column was on its way to the “Israeli” command HQ in Marjeyoun. It was equipped with anti bomb jamming devices."

“Regarding execution and timing he said: At exactly 15:00 last Monday, the targeted column reached the ambush site. We had set it 100 meters to the south of the 30th battalion Command HQ in Zaghleh, and 50 meters away from the “Israeli” - Lahdi liaison center."

"I gave the order to detonate the bombs; the task was conducted by two resistance men from the squads.”
“The attack squad advanced and was only 15 meters away from the target. They finished off every enemy movement which appeared before them," he said.

"We peppered the column with fire rounds for minutes; not even a bullet was shot on us. The squads pulled back according to the set plan. The enemy cordoned off the area to look for us. However, we managed to return to base safely in spite of the measures against us”, the commander added.

“We took into account the residents' safety and not inflicting any harm on them, for carrying out such operations must be very accurate. In brief our young men are skillful marksmen”, he said.

“The Resistance squad commander would not state the number of the squads' members or the weight of the bombs. He only said "they are in harmony with the size of the target".

“Asked about the way the Resistance men entered the town and sneaked between the houses, he said "these are secrets which we do not disclose; what we want to say is that the place, the target, and the time are ours".

“We were also able to videotape the operation in order to refute the enemy's potential attempts to say it did not happen”, he said.

“The importance of the Hasbaya operation lies in its site, deep inside the territories occupied by the enemy. The Resistance men infiltrated through the enemy's defense network after the occupation's withdrawal from Toumat Niha”, the commander added.

“One must not forget that we carried out the operation in a dangerous zone surrounded by several military and security centers, he said adding that "the operation also meant that the Resistance was also able to threaten the back lines used by the enemy and its collaborators. This proves our ability to select the proper time and place to strike the enemy."

Military Operations… (3)

The IR squad of two martyrs Zaher Hmadeh and Mohammad El-Fann attacked at 18:00, an “Israeli” force from a close range in the vicinity of Rihan barracks.

Following half an hour fierce clashes, the Artillery Unit of IR bombarded Rihan barracks scoring direct hits. Also, the IR men observed, at 18:45, an “Israeli” force inside Al-Afandi forest near Rihan barracks. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit pounded it chalking up direct and definite goals.

Earlier at 17:50, two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades attacked Bir Kallab outpost. The first group opened fire from a close range at the soldiers manning the fortification while the other one attacked the post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring certain goals. The smoke columns were seen emanating from it. The embarrassed and frightened “Israeli” soldiers began firing randomly in all sides.

Martyrs… (4)

The IR announced the martyrdom of two militants during a fierce firefight with the “Israeli” troops in the vicinity of Rihan barracks. The marytrs are:

  1. Mujahed Karim Maarouf Atwi, born at Jibaa town in 1969, joined the IR ranks in 1986, took part with a lot of qualitative military operations and gained a number of tributes of Hizbullah’s Secretary General H.E. Sayed Hassan Nassrallah. He was married with two children.
  2. Mujahed Mohammad Hussein Hammoud, born at Debbine in 1975 single, joined the IR ranks in 1995, shared with a lot of qualitative military operations and gained a number of tributes of Hizbullah’s Secretary General H.E. Sayed Hassan Nassrallah.

Pope Shnoda asserts legitimacy of resistance and welcomes choosing Gen Lahoud… (5)
“Egyptian Copts' Patriarch Shnoda III who is currently visiting Lebanon hoped the Lebanese will recover their sovereignty on the territories occupied by the “Israeli” enemy.”

“Shnoda stressed that resistance against the occupation is "the fruitful and legitimate option, for political solutions have led to nothing".

“The Patriarch is in Lebanon to attend the meeting of the Mid-East Churches' Council's Executive Committee.”

Four Mothers Movement: Pull out of the quagmire… (6)

“The Hizbullah has recently intensified its roadside bomb operations against IDF* and SLA* troops in the security zone and had increased its attacks on SLA soldiers in the Christian Jezzine enclave north of the zone.”

“Two soldiers, St.-Sgt. Zohar Doron, 21, from Kibbutz Lahav in the south, and St.-Sgt. Magen Friedman, 24, of Moshav Nir Etzion on Mt. Carmel, were killed and six others wounded in a roadside bomb attack in the Druse-populated Hasbayah village last Monday.”

“On that occasion, the perpetrators used an armor-piercing hollow-charge device and an anti-personnel explosive charge designed to cause maximum casualties.”

“The deaths of the two soldiers prompted a demonstration by the Four Mothers movement which has mounted a public campaign for an IDF withdrawal from south Lebanon.”

“Around 30 members of the movement staged a demonstration outside the auditorium in Haifa's Central Carmel district, where the main events in the annual Haifa Film Festival are taking place.”

“The demonstrators issued a personal call to Ariel Sharon to help bring about a solution to the Lebanon problem.”

"It was Arik Sharon as defense minister who took “Israel” into Lebanon. We are now calling on him as foreign minister to help pull us out of the quagmire there," Four Mothers activist Masha Sheindorf said.
