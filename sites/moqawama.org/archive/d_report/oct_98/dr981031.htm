Daily Report
 
 
October Saturday 31 -1998:
 
Introduction
As its usual, "Israel's" Army proceeded with its aggressions on Lebanon where its batteries shelled several villages and towns in South Lebanon. In retaliation for these assaults, the Islamic Resistance IR launched a number of military attacks on the "Israeli" and collaborators posts scoring direct and accurate hits.

Meanwhile, a former pro-"Israeli" militiamen was expelled yesterday from the occupied region after being detained for two months at the notorious Khiam detention center.

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
"Israel's" Army continued its aggressions on the Lebanese areas and staged the following assaults:
7:00, five 120mm shells fell on Mount-Al Rafih.
10:40, Mount-Al Rafih came under seven 120mm rounds.
11:25, four 155mm missiles struck Jib Soweid area.
14:30, more than three 155mm missiles fell on the west of Yater.
15:15, the perimeters of Baraasheit town exposed to three 82mm rounds.
15:20, one 155mm missile struck Mount-Zaatar.
15:35, Baraasheit subjected to two 81m shells.
15:40, several 155mm rounds slammed into Mount-Zaatar.
16:00, the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom was bombarded by eleven 155mm missiles.
16:45, the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom were pounded by twelve 155mm missiles.
19:15, ten 120mm shells struck Mount-AL Rafih.
19:30, Aissieh village exposed to four 155mm missiles.
Between 21:00, and 22:00, Mount Sojod was pounded by about forty 105mm 60 rounds.
22:00, western parts of Qilya and the perimeters of Zillaya came under about eighty five 155 and 175 mm missiles.

Military Operations (2)
The IR militants spotted, at 13:55, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost. Then the squad of two martyrs Mustafa Chokair and Mohammad Kamel Abbas attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain hits.

Also, at 15:10, the IR men observed enemy force at Baraasheit compound. Immediately afterwards, the group of martyr Abdel-Mawla Ismail targeted it with the appropriate weapons. Direct and accurate hits were scored, whereas several fortifications were damaged.

Furthermore, the IR men observed, at 15:50, enemy movements inside Blat post. Then, the group of martyr Hussein Chalhoub targeted them with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. Also, the smoke columns were seen emanating from it.

Moreover, the IR fighter observed, at 16:40, an "Israeli" force entering Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the group of martyr Ahmad Ossaili attacked it with the suitable weapons. The Mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct goals.

As a common "Israeli"-Lahdi motorized force tried to head for the crossroad of Dallafeh coming from Ahmadieh position, the IR militants attacked it, at 20:35, with the appropriate arms forcing it to retreat. Meanwhile, another group bombarded the position scoring direct and accurate hits.
Also, the IR militant shelled, at 22:00, the common force as well as Ahmadieh position.

At 23:20, the IR men reiterated targeting Ahmadieh position with the proper weapons chalking up direct goals. At the same time, the IR fighters spotted enemy troops on Naameh hilltops. Then, they attacked them with the suitable arms defusing an "Israeli" tank.

"Israeli" allied militiamen expelled from occupation zone (3)
"A former "Israeli"-allied militiaman was expelled from the occupation zone in south Lebanon. Najeeb Salman Sallam was driven from the Khiam detention center to the Zimraya checkpoint, one of five passageways linking the occupied area to the rest of Lebanon. "Israel's" proxy militia accused Sallam, of spying on the militia."

Arnoun's residents live life of fear due to "Israeli" occupation (4)
"Arnoun is a front line village in south Lebanon. But unlike other areas that border the zone "Israel" occupies, Arnoun has a different predicament. It is practically besieged and often visited by "Israeli" patrols."

"The last Lebanese army checkpoint is a little bit over a kilometer from the southern village of Arnoun. Arnoun is not technically inside the zone "Israel" occupies in the south but it lies just beneath the Beaufort castle, one of the "Israeli" manned outposts. Cars are not allowed to enter the village forcing residents to cross by foot."

"Man from village said: Our village was a paradise, this man says. But it is not heaven anymore. It is a ghost town. He was right. We crossed into the village and the tragedy was evident. Most of the homes have been abandoned and it took a while before we came across people. There were some 30,000 people here before "Israel" carved out its occupation zone. Today the village only has some 30 residents and most of them are old."

"Woman from village said: Most of the people have fled because of the appealing conditions here, she explained. But my husband and I do not have any other choice. We had to stay. But young men cannot stay here. The "Israelis" will take them. At times, "Israeli" occupation forces set up temporary checkpoints right here at the entrance of the village. They search homes, check cars and detain residents who they suspect assist the Lebanese resistance before returning to their hilltop position.

"Israeli" forces constantly keep a close eye on the village. And its residents have to deal with constant harassment."

"Man in village said: "Israeli" patrols enter the village. They searched my home, this man said. They look through the drawers, the closets, everywhere. They also besiege the village. When night falls, no one is allowed to leave their homes. This family lost their five-year old brother". "He needed urgent medical treatment but they had to wait for morning to leave the village. And it just was too late. But life has to go on. Arnoun's residents, or to be more accurate, the remaining few, mainly rely on tobacco cultivation and olive harvests for their livelihood. The serenity of this village is deceptive. The people live in fear. And the hardships they face are all too obvious. As this woman put it: the only thing people here want is to live a normal life".
