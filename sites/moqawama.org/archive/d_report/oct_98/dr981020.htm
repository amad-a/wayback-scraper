October Tuesday 20-1998:

Introduction

A state of panic prevailed among the "Israeli" forces and their collaborators in the occupied area of Hasbaya following a series of operations carried out recently by the Islamic Resistance (IR).

The panic was clearly manifested through the measures taken by the "Israeli" forces which stormed into and ransacked houses in the area.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" occupation forces breached the April 1996 Understanding as a 12-year-old civilian girl was injured by a shrapnel of an "Israeli" shell in Eastern Beqaa.

A 12-year-old Girl Wounded by "Israeli" Mortars

"A 12-year-old civilian girl was wounded by "Israeli" occupation's mortar fire in Lebanon's eastern Beqaa Valley on Monday", a security source said.

"The source said Fatima Abdullah was wounded by a mortar bomb which came from the direction of "Israel's" south Lebanon occupation zone."

"The assault at Qilya raised to 60 the number of civilians wounded by "Israeli" aggressions in south Lebanon since the start of 1998. It also damaged two houses", the source said… (1)

However, the IR recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions:

2:40, the helicopters hovered low over Beit Leef and Oyoun Valley.

8:45, the reconnaissance planes flew over the area of East Saida.

9:30, a drone reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over Baraasheit town.

10:45, the warplanes flew, at middle height, in the airspace of South Lebanon.

11:25, a drone reconnaissance aircraft had been flying, at middle altitude, for more than one hour over East Saida region.

11:30, the "Israeli" jets broke the sound barrier over the villages of Central Sector, whereas a surveillance plane circled over Khorbet Selem town.

11:40, the jet airplanes broke the sound barrier over Beirut region.

11:45, a reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle height, over Kafra, Yater and Haris villages.

11:55, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered low over Ghassanieh, Baissarieh and Teffahta villages.

12:10, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River watercourse.

14:00, a reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle height, over Shaqra town and the nearby areas. It is to be pointed out here that the "Israeli" fighters and surveillance planes had been flying for the morning over the villages of Central Sector.

16:30, two 155mm missiles emanating from Shraifeh position struck the area between Arnoun and Mazraat Hamra.

16:35, Shraifeh position targeted Sawan area with four 155mm missiles.

16:50, Meis El-Jabal emplacement fired five 155mm rounds of the surroundings of Touline village.

17:05, the area between Touline and Qalaweih exposed to a number of 155mm missiles originating from Meis El-Jabal emplacement.

17:55, two helicopters hovered, at middle altitude, over Blat, Bassil and Berkat Risha areas…

18:00, Ahmadieh position pounded Qilya village with ten 81mm rounds. The civilian Jawdat Ali Nasser's family members were miraculously escaped death as his house was hit by an "Israeli" shell. Also, several close houses were damaged.

18:50, Ahmadieh position showered intensely the perimeters of Qilya, Bir Zahr, Naameh Hill and the 910th hilltop with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

21:25, the helicopters soared, at middle altitude, over Taibeh town.

22:05, the "Israeli" choppers hovered, at middle height, over Taibeh town and Litani River watercourse.

22:15, Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

22:20, Rihan emplacement targeted Mount-Sojod with three 120mm shells.

23:10, Shraifeh position pounded the road of Sawan with seven 155mm missiles.

23:30, Houla and Mashaaroun compounds raked Slouqi Valley and the peripheries of Shaqra with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds… (2)

Occupation holds tens of cars at Zimraya crossing… (3)

"The forces searched the woods between the Zaghleh hilltop and the eastern part of the town of Hasbaya till the western entrance of the town of Ain Qinya."

"Reports said the occupation forces also stormed into several houses in the district of al-Ain in the northeastern part of the Hasbaya area."

"Continuing their arbitrary acts, the occupation forces have been holding about 30 cars at the Zimraya gateway since last Friday. The "Israelis" claim that the cars are not allowed to cross into the liberated areas due to "security reasons".

"The measure forced the drivers, particularly those with pick-ups loaded with vegetables and fruit to remove their loads and transfer them in cars brought from the zone."

"Moreover, for the fourth consecutive week, the occupation forces barred the Hasbaya farmers in villages at the confrontation line from harvesting their crops, especially olives."

"This means that more than 1000 farmers will not be able to benefit from their produce."

Mofaz paid inspection visit to South Lebanon… (4)

The "Israeli" chief of staff Gen Shaul Mofaz reviewed some "Israeli" outposts in the occupied zone. He was accompanied by the "Israeli" chief of the liaison unit Gen Gabi Ashkenazi", pro-"Israeli" militia's radio said.

"The radio said that Mofaz held a meeting with the chief of the liaison unit and his officers. He also listened to field reports about the military situation and the situation of the occupied zone."

"On the Other hand, Lebanon filed two complaints with the April Understanding Monitoring Group (AUMG); the first over "Israeli" occupation forces' bombardment of the town of Bnaafool (Iqleem al-Toffah) which resulted in damaging several houses. The second Lebanese complaint protests the Israeli shelling of the town of Dallafeh (western Beqaa)."

"As usual the "Israeli" enemy resorted to lodging a complaint to balance the Lebanese complaint. The enemy claimed in its complaint that resistance operations were launched from populated areas."

Military Operations… (5)

The IR spotted, at 20:40, enemy movements inside and outside Beit Yahoun outpost. Then, the squad of martyr Ahmad Ismail Ossaili targeted them with the adequate arms. Direct hits were scored, the Mujahedeen reported.

The Houla massacre anniversary marked

"The people of the occupied town of Houla marked the 50th anniversary of the brutal massacre perpetrated by the "Israeli" enemy in their town in 1948."

"The rally, at the Hai al-Sollom Hussainyiet, was attended by several political figures and Ulama from the occupied zone and other Lebanese areas, including Representative of Imam Khamenei in Lebanon Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek, MP Hassan Khalil, MP Ahmed Swaid, and the Imam of Houla Sayyed Hassan Yassin."

"The meetings and conferences which are held here or there, in Washington or elsewhere, aim to extinguish the remaining light in this Ummah; however, we will be on the alert, Sheikh Yazbek said.

We will confront these plots with all our might, having united all as one to be in one front aiming the rifle at the enemy's head", he added.

"We want the liberation of Lebanon to top the forthcoming (cabinet's) agenda along with supporting the steadfastness of our people", Sheikh Yazbek said.

"Those who have been steadfast in the face of the enemy must be rewarded and the Mujahedeen must be backed, because there can be no solution with this enemy; the only connection between us will be one of blood," he added.

"More than 100 Lebanese civilians were slaughtered when "Israeli" terrorist gangs attacked them in Houla in 1948"… (6)

FCLD discussed conditions of detainees in "Israeli" prisons with ICRC… (7)
"Secretary-general of the Follow-up committee for the Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" prisons (FCLD) Mohammed Safa discussed with the representative of the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) in Lebanon, Henry Forneau the conditions of the Lebanese hostages in the al-Khiam detention center and other "Israeli" prisons."

"The discussions focused specifically on the children detained in al-Khiam and the detainees who suffer severe health conditions such as Sulaiman Ramadan, Mostafa Arabeyaa and Riyadh Kalakesh.

Safa asked ICRC to deploy efforts to secure the release of the children, the sick and the women."

"The ICRC official said the international organization will continue its efforts in order to alleviate the detainees' pains and to try to make the visits by the detainees' families and ICRC representatives to al-Khiam more organized on schedule."

"On the other hand, FCLD said it will hold a press conference and other activities on the Day of the Detained Teacher."
