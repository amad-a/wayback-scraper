October Friday 23-1998:

Introduction

The "Israeli" Army continued its aggressions on the populated areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa. A Lebanese national was injured yesterday as an "Israeli" round fell on Kfar Romman town.

Meanwhile, and as a result of the brutal "Israeli" siege of Arnoun town, a six-year-old boy was died because of suffering severe stomachache and intestinal problems.

"Israeli" shelling injures civilian near Nabatieh
"A civilian was wounded yesterday when an "Israeli" tank fired a shell into Kfar Roumman, outside Nabatieh."
"Mohammed Atwi, 38, was hurt in the shoulder when the round exploded beside his house. He was taken to the Najdi hospital in Nabatieh for treatment."
"On Monday, two civilians were wounded in Qilya in the western Beqaa in a mortar bombardment. So far this year, 15 civilians have been killed and 80 wounded in the south."
"Lebanon is expected to file a complaint over the attack with the five-nation monitoring group, adding to two complaints lodged earlier this week. The group is scheduled to convene in Naqoura on Monday to hear the Lebanese complaints and one protest filed by "Israel""… (1)

Human tragedy in the South: Child dies due to occupation… (3)
"A new tragedy has shaken the south and its residents as it took the life of a southern boy whose only fault was to live under the yoke of "Israeli" occupation.'

"Ali Ghazal, of Arnoun, was detained by the "Israeli" occupation forces three month ago, he left a wife and 12 children. As the father was put in al-Khiam detention center, the family was living in terrible conditions."

"On Tuesday Ali's son, Ibrahim, 6, had severe stomachache due to intestinal problems.

Due to the blockade imposed by the "Israeli" occupation forces on the town, the family had to keep Ibrahim at home, for there are no clinics in the town to treat such cases."

"The mother could not sleep all night long; with the first morning hours she took her son to Nabatieh as his condition worsened."

"When she reached the Popular Relief Hospital in Nabatieh, the child had already passed away.

The mother asked what her son's fault was to lose his life due to "Israeli" blockade."

Military Operations… (4)

The IR men observed, at 17:30, enemy movements inside Ahmadieh position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit squad of martyrs Karim Atwi and Haitham Abou Daya targeted them scoring direct and certain goals.

Parliamentary Justice Committee approves sponsoring resistance Martyrs' families…(5)
"In the presence of Justice Minister Bahij Tabaraa, the parliamentary Justice and Administration committee yesterday approved two articles of a draft law to sponsor the families of those who were martyred, detained, or handicapped during resistance operations against the "Israeli" occupation."

"The committee which adjourned the meeting until next Monday to complete the study of the bill listened to reviews by some Non-Governmental Organization concerned in the issue of martyrs and detainees."

"Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc member MP Nazeeh Mansour was the one who drafted the bill which demands that the government sponsors martyrs' families."

"During the discussions, the committee listened to the director general of the South Council Hashem Haidar and the representative of the Civil Service Council Assaad Doumet, the director of the Charitable Foundation for martyrs Hajj Qassem Oulaiq in addition to the secretary of the Follow-up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons Mohammed Safa and the representative of the National Council for the Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons Jamal Mahroom."

Jewish settlers find unusual way to expand settlement… (6)

"Jewish extremists in a West Bank settlement have tried to expand its boundaries by burying their dead on the outskirts of their land. Zeev Altmann, a 70-year-old Jewish-American, has been buried several hundred meters (yards) outside the boundaries of Kfar Tapoah, a settlement of 95 families near Nablus, a settlement official told AFP on Thursday. The Palestinian villagers in nearby Yassuf and Salfit have protested against this "cemetery" on land which they claim as their own and have asked the "Israeli" government to order the exhumation of the body. The burial was carried out by a handful of extremists from the settlement who are members of the anti-Arab Kach movement, which was banned in 1994", the settlement official said.
"Altmann had visited the colony eight years ago and befriended the extremists, to whom he willed part of his wealth. He planned to finance a hotel at the settlement and asked to be buried there. "Israeli" soldiers and police have stopped Altmann's friends holding prayers at his tomb."
