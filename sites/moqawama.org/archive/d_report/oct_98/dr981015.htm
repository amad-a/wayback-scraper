October Thursday 15-1998:

Introduction

For the second day successively, the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants responded for the "Israeli" shelling of internationally banned phosphoric and Napalm rounds which sparked huge brush fires.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" occupying forces along with their collaborators stormed into several villages in Hasbaya District conducting house-to-house search.

IR Attacks Enemy Border Outpost of Berkat Risha… (1)
"In retaliation for the "Israeli" enemy's use of internationally banned phosphorous Napalm and incendiary bombs which have ignited fires in the south and the western Beqaa, the Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Martyr Zoheir Qobaissi bombarded the enemy's outpost of Berkat Risha (at the Lebanese-Palestinian border), scoring definite hits."

Also, at 20:50, the IR men spotted enemy movements inside the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Then, the squad of martyr Ali Wahab Hijazi targeted them with the adequate weapons chalking up direct hits.

For its part, Amal movement shelled, at 18:00, Salaah position with a number of 120mm missiles.

"IR vowed to preserve its people's security and interests while warning the enemy against using internationally banned bombs."

"IR is completely ready to retaliate for any aggression", an IR communiqué read.

"On the other hand, "Israeli" daily Yedioth Ahronot said the Israeli occupation troops in south Lebanon use drugs while on duty."

"(Israeli) military police has filed lists of charges against paratroopers who uses drugs, the newspaper said, adding that Similar lists have been filed recently against troops in the 202 battalion of the paratrooper brigade."

"Comments made by troops and their parents indicated the magnitude of the phenomenon.

A father of one of the troops convicted of using drugs while on duty asked "How can it be that tens of troops in the battalion smoke drugs nearly every night without their commanders knowing anything?"

More arbitrary measures by the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators… (3)
"A collaborator militia force stormed into the under-construction public hospital in the town of Hasbaya", reports from the occupied zone said.

"The militiamen gathered the hospital employees and workmen and questioned them for a long time. Then the collaborators searched the rooms and ransacked their contents, thus putting work at the hospital to a halt for several hours", the reports added.

"Two employees were taken to the militia's security HQ in Tallet Zaghleh. After being questioned, the men were taken to the notorious al-Khiam detention center", as-Safir newspaper said.

"Moreover, reports from the liberated villages in the Hasbaya area said an "Israeli"-militia joint force conducted house-to-house search in the occupied villages of Ain Qinya, Hasbaya and Shwayya."

"Thirteen residents were summoned to the security center in Zaghleh where they were questioned before being released. Four members of the militia's security body in Hasbaya were also questioned.

Furthermore, a proxy-militia force entered the town of Shebaa and searched three houses under the pretext of looking for weapons and ammunition."

Hizbullah SG Received ICRC Representative in Lebanon… (4)
Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayed Hassan Nasrallah today discussed the issue of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" enemy's prisons besides the International Committee of the Red Cross' (ICRC) activity with the international organization's chief in Lebanon Ourni Faurnie."

Faurnie who visited the Hizbullah Secretariat HQ in Haret Hreik this afternoon said the secretary general explained to him the situation of the detainees in "Israeli" detention centers."

"ICRC's chief said he explained to Sayed Nasrallah some details of ICRC's activity in South Lebanon and the efforts made by the international organization to maintain its credibility."

"The meeting with his Eminence was fruitful and successful", Faurnie said.
