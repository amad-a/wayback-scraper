October Monday 19-1998:

"Israeli" Aggressions:

Yesterday, the "Israeli" occupying forces staged the following aggressions on South Lebanon and Western Beqaa areas:

3:30, Mashaaroun position showered its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

3:45, one 155mm missile emanating from Meis El-Jabal struck Aissieh Valley.

7:00, Meis El-Jabal pounded Aissieh Valley with twenty 155mm missiles.

10:05, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River watercourse.

10:35, an M.K. surveillance aircraft soared over Majdal Selem, Aissieh Valley and the neighboring areas.

11:15, an M.K. surveillance aircraft circled, at middle height, in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

12:20, two "Israeli" warboats were seen sailing off the seashore of Maallieh and Qlayleh villages.

12:40, the espionage planes flew, at middle height, over East Saida region.

13:40, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh, Maallieh, Semmaieh and Litani River watercourse.

13:55, the reconnaissance planes circled in the sky of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

15:45, the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost came under two 120mm shells originating from Rihan emplacement and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds from Bir Kallab outpost.

15:50, Bir Kallab targeted Arid El-Zennar area with two 40mm rounds.

16:05, the warplanes flew, at middle height, in the airspace of Beirut and Western Beqaa areas.

17:30, a drone reconnaissance plane hovered over the villages of Central Sector of South Lebanon.

17:45, the surveillance planes circled over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River watercourse.

17:55, Zohaira position unleashed four 155mm missiles at the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom.

18:05, Yarin post pounded the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village with eight 155mm rounds, when on the other hand, west of Yater exposed to five 155mm missiles emanating from Aita plain. At the same time, a reconnaissance plane circled, at middle altitude, over Zibqeen and Jibal El-Botom villages.

18:15, Zohaira position fired four 155mm rounds at Jib Soweid area. Meanwhile, two enemy warboats were seen sailing off the west of Ras El-Ain.

18:20, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan emplacement fell on Arid El-Zennar.

18:25, a drone reconnaissance aircraft soared in the sky of Iqleem Al-Toffah highlands.

19:10, a drone reconnaissance plane circled, at middle altitude, over Shaqra and Baraasheit towns.

19:30, an M.K. surveillance aircraft flew in the sky of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

19:50, Zohaira position bombarded Jib Soweid with eight 155mm rounds.

20:15, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered over Iqleem Al-Toffah regions.

20:20, a reconnaissance plane circled, at middle height, over Kafra, Yater and Haris villages.

20:35, the helicopters hovered over Berkat Naqieh and the perimeters of Shaqra town.

21:10, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle height, over Aiteit and the neighboring areas.

21:20, Meis El-Jabal emplacement pounded Mount-Zaatar with seven 155mm rounds, whereas Berkat Risha bombarded the area between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun with ten 155mm missiles.

23:05, a drone reconnaissance plane circled low over East Saida, Jibsheit and Harouf areas.

23:10, the "Israeli" choppers and surveillance planes flew in the sky of Iqleem Al-Toffah region.

23:45, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered low over Ghassanieh and Teffahta towns.

Military Operations (2)

The IR observed, at 17:45, enemy movements inside Blat position. Shortly afterwards, the squad of martyrs Ahmad Dbouk and Hussein Kansou attacked them with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals. In the meantime, the group of martyr Hassan Hammoudi targeted Radar-Ras El-Bayad position with the suitable arms. Direct hits were scored whereas the smoke columns were seen emanating from it.
