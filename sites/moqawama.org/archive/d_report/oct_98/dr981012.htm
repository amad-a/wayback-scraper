October Monday 12-1998:

Introduction

Following the Islamic Resistance's (IR) qualitative penetration of "Israeli" military and security measures which represented in last Monday's attack at an "Israeli" convoy in Hasbaya region, the "Israeli" troops and their allied militia launched a campaign of arrests among Hasbaya's population. About six people, including a security collaborator officer, were detained today.

However, "Israel's" Army had paid raise and reduced working hours for its proxy militiamen in an attempt to boost their deteriorating morale.

"Israeli" allied militia detains six Lebanese… (1)

"South Lebanon Army* militiamen are conducting a campaign of arrests in the Hasbaya district, questioning and detaining those suspected of having aided Hizbullah guerrillas in the two roadside bomb attacks in the town last week."
"Among those arrested was an SLA security official, Samir Sanan of Ain Qenya. Sanan, along with Ali Zeineddine, also of Ain Qinya, and Marwan Saab of Shebaa were taken to Khiam prison."
"Three workers were snatched by SLA militiamen on Sunday from a new government hospital under construction in Hasbaya. They were released after four hours of interrogation."
"Wafaa Melhem, 23, and her three-year-old daughter Nourman were denied entry into the occupation zone via the Zimraya crossing by the SLA. Melhem had been visiting relatives in the western Bekaa and was returning to her home in Shebaa. The militiamen also prevented all residents of Shebaa, at the eastern extremity of the zone, from entering or leaving the village."
"The clampdown in the district was prompted by last week’s attacks, in which two "Israeli" soldiers were killed and six wounded on Monday while an SLA officer had a narrow escape on Friday."

"Israeli" Aggressions:

""Israeli" occupying forces in South Lebanon today shelled the fields of Sojod, Rihan, and Mazraat Okmata in Iqleem Al-Toffah. The area of Jabal Al-Rafih was also subject to heavy artillery shelling originating from "Israeli" outposts in Zaffateh and Aishieh."

"The "Israeli" shells targeted the villages of Nabatieh, Nabatieh Al-Fawqa, Kfar Romman and areas in the Iqleem Al-Toffah Mountain Ridges"… (2)

"The bombardment sparked a forest fire in which dozens of oak trees burned. Civil defence firefighters were unable to reach all parts of the blaze due to its proximity to the Sojod post"… (3)

For further details over the "Israeli" aggressions on Lebanon's areas, the IR issued the following communiqué (4):

00: 45, the warplanes soared high in the airspace of Beirut.

8:35, the warplanes reiterated their flights in the sky of Beirut.

9:00, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered over the areas of Iqleem Al-Toffah, whereas the helicopters circled over Sojod and Rihan areas.

Between 12:00 and 13:50, Rihan emplacement pounded Mount-Sojod with thirty 120mm shells.

12:15, Sojod outpost raked its surroundings with Mag and 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds enkindling forest fire.

13:50, two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan emplacement struck Tasseh Well, when on the other hand, Sojod outpost showered its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

14:45, Mount-Safi exposed to three 155mm missiles originating from Zaffateh compound.

14:55, Zaffateh compound fired nine 155mm missiles at Mount-Sojod and six others at Tasseh Well watercourse.

18:00, several flare shells unleashed at Sojod and Al-Rafih heights igniting blazes.

21:15, Meis El-Jabal emplacement fired more than four 155mm missiles at Aissieh Valley.

21:40, Rihan emplacement targeted Mount-Sojod with three 155mm phosphoric missiles and three 120mm shells.

23:00, an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered over the areas of Jezzine, Kfar Houna, Abou Rashed and Jabbour.

23:45, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Toffah highlands.

"Israeli"-backed militiamen granted pay raise, shorter hours to boost sagging morale… (5)

"In a bid to reverse the declining morale of the SLA, the Israelis have apparently given the militiamen a pay raise and reduced working hours."
"Each militiamen has been given a $75 raise, bringing the monthly basic salary up to $575, while time on duty has been reduced from 15 days a month to 12."
"Elsewhere, the five-nation monitoring group convenes in Naqoura this morning to hear two complaints filed by Lebanon and "Israel"".
"The Lebanese complaint concerns damage to a house in Baraasheit by SLA mortar rounds fired from the position above the village last Tuesday. The "Israeli" complaint is over the occupation forces’ allegation that the resistance staged an attack from within a populated area near Mansouri."

Military Operations… (6)

The IR squad of two martyrs Abed Hashem and Mohammad Zeidan launched, at 12:00, a swift attack and from several sides at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The ten-minute close range firefight cause the destruction of its main fortification located at the post's entrance. The Mujahedeen heard the enemy soldiers screaming and wailing.

21:00, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Abbas Bahmad and Mohammad Ismail Ismail attacked an enemy engineering force near Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying one military vehicle.
