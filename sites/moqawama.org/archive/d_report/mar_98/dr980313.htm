March 14,1978 Remembrance:
Between 1976 and 1978 several military operations such as kidnapping civilian buses and taking hostages from tourist facilities had been launched across the Lebanese-Palestinian borders. These attacks had been exploited very well by the Zionist media which mobilized the international public opinion against the Palestinian organizations which stood behind them. So, when the blood conflict erupted between the Syrian forces serving in the framework of Arab Deterrent Forces on one side and Phalanges Party and its allies on the other, the "Israeli" enemy take this opportunity through invading South Lebanon and occupying an area stretching from Qasmieh bridge north to Tyre city until the perimeters of Kawkaba in Western Beqaa. Between 14 and 22 March 1978, the "Israeli" forces captured about 2400 km2 from the Lebanese territories.

This Resolution stated that an international force should be formed with the aim of overlooking the "Israeli" retreat. Then, on May 3-1998, the Resolution 427 asked "Israel" to complete without any delay its retreat from the whole Lebanese territories. Also, on September 18-1978, the Security Council issued the Resolution 434 in which it reminded of the Resolution 425 after "Israel" had stopped completing its implementation and called for "respecting fully Lebanons security, sovereignty and political independence within its internationally recognized borders".
