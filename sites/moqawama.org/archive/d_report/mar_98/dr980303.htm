Daily Report
 

Monday March 3-1998:

The South Lebanon witnessed a series of "Israeli" military aggressions starting from the intensified flying of reconnaissance planes passing by the artillery shelling and firing heavy machine gun fire reaching to air raids staged by the "Israeli" fighters. The artillery shelling spread to include the areas of Western Beqaa.

However, the Islamic Resistance men responded to these "Israeli" aggressions through staging five separate attacks on Sojod, Bir Kallab, Blat, Dabsheh and Kassaret Al-Oroush posts.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The "Israeli" aggressions involved Iqleem Al-Tuffah region where the "Israeli" fighters fired several air-to-ground rockets in addition to staging mock raids.
The sorties were preceded by intense flying of pilotless reconnaissance planes in the airspace of South Lebanon.

In the meantime, the enemy batteries stationed at Zaffateh position pounded into the heights of Iqleem Al-Tuffah, especially Al-Rafih mountain, Sojod woodlands, Mazraat Aqmata and the perimeters of Loweizeh village.

At 3pm, the "Israeli"-occupied Zaffateh post targeted the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh El-Fawqa, the area between Maifadoun and Choukine villages and the perimeters of Kfar Tebbnite with a number of 122mm shells. The "Israeli" shelling extended to Habboush River and the surroundings of Sit Zbeideh Bridge which links Iqleem Al-Tuffah with Nabatieh region.

In Western Beqaa, the occupation forces bombarded the heights of Ain Al-Tineh, Qatraneh and Sraireh, whereas the heavy machine gun rounds struck the perimeters of Dallafeh, Loussi, Mashghara forests and Al-Amin pool (3).

Siege of Taibeh

However, the "Israeli" occupation forces still beleaguer Taibeh village carrying out a search campaign in its houses. Also, it was reported that the "Israeli" intelligence men took the civilians Abbas and Mohammad Abdallah Ghaith, Samir and Jamal Kousan and the son of Khalil Sawli to Khiam detention camp.

On the other hand, the "Israeli" soldiers and their collaborators burst into hundreds of commercial shops throughout the occupied region searching for Syrian commodities to be confiscated.
Also, the "Israeli" intelligence apparatus warned the owners of stores which market these goods such as clothes and candies that they should get rid of them within fifteen days of the current month under the penalty of punishment, such as closing the stores and confiscating their contents.

Some of the merchants clarified that the "Israeli" campaign aims at enforcing them to promote the "Israeli" goods. Also, they hinted that the prices of Syrian commodities are 70% less than those similar to "Israeli" ones (5).

On the other side, the family of Hussein Ali Rammal, Odaiseh village, arrested at Khiam detention camp, disproved releasing their son as the "Israeli" media claimed. Rammal's family demanded the International Red Cross Committee to exert its efforts to release their son (6).

Islamic Resistance Operations:

In its retaliation to the "Israeli" aggressions, the Islamic Resistance men launched five attacks on the "Israeli"- occupied posts at Sojod, Bir Kallab, Blat, Dabsheh and Kassaret Al-Oroush. The following is the statement of Islamic Resistance (7):
8:00, the group of martyr Amin Mohammad Termos attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
12:50, the group of two martyrs Hussein Munir Kansou and Ahmad Melhem Chahrour fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits as the "Israeli" soldiers were reinforcing it.
15:00, the Islamic Resistance fighters observed an enemy force inside and outside of Dabsheh outpost. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of two martyrs Hassan Harb and Hassan Obeid targeted it with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of martyr Ali Hussein Sbeiti rushed upon Blat position with the proper arms chalking up direct and certain hits.
16:20, the group of two martyrs Fadl Mahmoud Soweidan and Hassan Issa Harqous rushed upon a Zionist motorized force stationed at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Resolution 425:

As to the implementation of Resolution 425 issued by the Security Council of United Nations, deputy Secretary General of Hizbullah Sheikh Naim Kassem stressed: "The way of unconditional "Israeli" pull-out of Southern Lebanon and Western Beqaa is opened. The occupier has no any right to demand with disarming the resistance. We are the people of this country and there is no place or role for the aggressor to take decisions on our own affairs".

Also, he warned of being entangled into the booby-trapped project of "Israeli" government which is trying to utilize from International political climate to exert pressures on Lebanon and Syria.

"Having politically and popularly unanimous, the Islamic Resistance is much more exalted and significant to be a part of bargaining process with the enemy. It is in a position enabled it to enforce the "Israeli" troops to pull out of Lebanon", he said.

"The exceptional "Israeli" government interest in its willingness of carrying out the Resolution 425 is not but a political maneuver to decoy Lebanon into the security arrangements. Were it not the consecutive and successive military operations of the Islamic Resistance, the "Israeli" officials would not express their inclination of withdrawal", Sheikh Kassem adde.

He asserted: "Nowadays, the "Israeli" goals represents in: Embarrassing Lebanon and Syria and throwing the ball in their court, involving a certain European country into the American-"Israeli" plan for the purpose of reducing the international pressures exerted on "Israel", diverting attention from the complications of Palestinian-"Israeli" track and exposing some kinds of flexibility for domestic purposes".

In this connection, the Loyalty for Islamic Resistance bloc said the "Israeli" maneuvers represented in getting ready for conditional fulfilling the Resolution 425 aim at throwing the ball in Lebanese-Syrian court. No one can offer security guarantees to the enemy or accept a conditional pull-out from Lebanon".

However, and in "Israel" itself, many of the "Israelis" do not believe Netanyahu's statements about fulfilling the Resolution 425. Youssi Belin told the "Israeli" newspaper Haaretz: "If we supposed that the premier's statements reflect a new reality and not mere a information show, the issue could be then a political change and that the "Israeli" withdrawal would be also imminent".

In his turn, MP Haim Ramoun accused the "Israeli" government with deception and added: "It feels that the public is restless and uncomfortable. This is why it accepted with the resolution 425".

On the other hand, Youssi Sarid, Head of Meritz Party, raised a doubt about Netanyahu's attitude by saying: "Since the "Israeli" government does not move itself towards the table of negotiations with Syria, any compromise will not be settled in Lebanon".

Defeatist Song:

Yediot Ahronot, an "Israeli" newspaper, mentioned that every soldier serving in South Lebanon had chanted a defeatist song.

Mini Biger, compiler of the song, said: "I had written it during 1982 Invasion of Lebanon and recorded it since three months only. Today I, definitely, say that something didn't change", Yediot Ahronot ended.

            The first stanza of the song says:
            Years of rattling and woes
            Years of mourning and blues
            A full destruction
            Between the river and Lebanon

It is to be pointed out that the increasing "Israeli" casualties in Lebanon (39 "Israeli" killed in 1997 and four since the beginning of this year) raised again the heat debate inside "Israel" about the presence of its Army in Lebanon.

Previously, several similar songs spread among the "Israeli" forces during the period between 1982 Invasion of Lebanon and the "Israeli" partial retreat in 1985.

Since 1982, a lot of things hadn't changed. The "Israeli" Army, is not but an aggressive force occupies lands of a free and internationally recognized country called Lebanon which is a founder member for United Nations before a state called "Israel" had emerged. This "Israeli" aggression, couldn't be and, will not be repelled but by arms. Now, it become well known, as the song said, that the sword unsheathed by it against the peaceful people retracted at it through disasters and grief.
