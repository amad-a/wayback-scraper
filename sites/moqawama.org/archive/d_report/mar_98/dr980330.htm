Daily Report
 
March Monday 30-1998:

Despite all the "Israeli" rhetoric over a withdrawal from South Lebanon, the "Israeli" aggressions pursued against the Lebanese people.

"Israeli" Aggressions :
The "Israeli" occupation forces targeted at dawn Qatraneh heights with a number of 175mm shells whereas Kawkaba and Toumat Niha positions raked Jabal Zahr, Mazraat Ahmadieh and Abou Rashed areas with heavy machine gun fire. Also, the road between Hasbaya and Zemraya crossing point exposed for about two hours to heavy machine gun fire.

However, the following are the "Israeli" aggressions according to the Islamic Resistance statement :
7:00, Zohaira position hit west of Yater with eight 155mm missiles, whereas Tair Harfa outpost targeted Jib Soweid with four 120mm shells.
15:15, Jib Soweid came under four 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position.
15:20, Zohaira position unleashed five 155mm missiles at Jib Soweid area.
15:25, the west of Yater exposed to four 155mm shells emanating from Zohaira position.
21:10, two "Israeli" helicopters hovered, at low altitude, over Salhani and Blat areas.
23:15, Beit Yahoun outpost fired several 81mm flash bombs over the area between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun villages.

"Israel's" attack on Lebanese government:
On the other hand and in the context of "Israeli" aggression on Lebanon, the "Israeli" General Irez Gerstein  decried the Lebanese State as he was in Bent Jbeil considering "the whole Lebanese official systems and services inimical to the state of 'Israel' ". He added: "He who indulges in the hope to deal with it would hare committed a fatal mistake".

As to the Lebanese, this "Israeli" threat is not something new, for they are well aware of the fact that "Israel" since its birth in 1948 had adopted and established on rejecting everything relating to other entities on all human, political, economical, cultural, physical levels. The "Israelis" had showed a special skills surpassed those of the Nazis concerning the genocides committed by them in Palestine and Lebanon (massacres of Khiam, Zrarieh, Qana, Houla, Deir Yassine, Kfar Kassemetc).

In contradiction with all international laws over organizing the occupation affairs, "Israel" makes nowadays every effort to break down the structure of Lebanese government in the occupied region.

Also, it prevents the connections and visits between the population of the occupied regions and their other relatives natives in the liberated areas in addition to the abuses and repressive actions committed against the residents of occupied areas such as detention without trial or charge.

About the "Israeli" retreat:
Russian Foreign Minister Yevghini Brimakov  called on "Israel" to implement unconditionally the UN Resolution 425.

In the meantime, American spokesman of State Department James Robin  announced that "the Resolution 425 does not call for negotiations but certain discussions between he two parties". This American stance represents a progress if it were not called for discussions which usually, as American often used to, developed into negotiations later.

Accordingly, it seems that the agreement between the "Israeli" and American foreign ministers had settled down on the basis of focusing on the substantial issues aiming at the achieving security arrangements between Lebanon and "Israel" and not on the formalities which may fail to take this advantage.
