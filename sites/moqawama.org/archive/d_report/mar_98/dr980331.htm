Daily Report

March Tuesday 31-1998:

Expulsion :
The "Israeli" troops and their collaborators expatriated the woman civilian Ghazaleh Moussa Issa, 40, and her child, 4, to the out of the occupied region. Ghazaleh clarified that she was surprised as a number of "Israeli" agents came to our home at night and demanded her to leave once and for all her village Ramieh. They didn't let me leave the house until they confiscated the keys, burglarized the furnitures, stole the pickup truck and broke the windows and doors".

It is to be pointed out that Ghazaleh had been over last two years under almost house arrest for the "Israeli" intelligence members had rejected to give her a permission in order to leave her village.

Detention :
However, it was reported that the "Israeli" soldiers and agents had abducted the citizen Fadi Kassem Hamdan and taken him to Khiam detention camp.

"Israeli" Aggressions :
In the meantime, the "Israeli" occupation forces pursued their aggressive actions against the liberated areas. In this regard, the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Radar, Hardoun and Blat bombarded the outskirts of Beyout Siyad, Majdal Zoun, Wadi Nafha, Rahrah and Jib Soweid.

In Nabatieh region, the surroundings of Maifadoun, Choukine, Zawtar Gharbieh and Kfar Dajjal came under a number of shells.

Also, the "Israeli" gunners targeted Mazraat Aqmata, Al-Rafih Mountain and the woodlands of Sojod and Rihan with several missiles.

In Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" shelling extended to include at dawn the heights of Ain Tineh and Rihayat, whereas Ain El-Amin area and Mashghara forests exposed to a number of mortar shells and barrage of heavy machine gun fire.
