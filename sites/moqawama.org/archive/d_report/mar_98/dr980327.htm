Daily Report

March Friday 27-1998:

"Israeli" Aggressions  :
Once again, the "Israeli" occupation forces breached April 1996 Understanding, which prevented targeting the civilians, as they blew up three roadside bombs on the highway of Borj Qalaouieh, Bent Jbeil District, and near the house of citizen Ahmad Yassine Tawbeh.
The explosion of bombs which disguised as soil caused three craters each of 1.5m depth along with damages to the adjacent houses of Ahmad Yassine Tawbeh, Ali Tawbeh, Hussein Rmeiti and Ali Malkani.

Unifil's Finland Battalion serving in the region put on the alert, patrolled on the highway, condoned the scene and examined the bombs.

Security sources asserted the bombs held the hallmarks of previous ones planted by the "Israeli" forces. Hebrew scriptures were seen on the shrapnels. The sources clarified that the bombs were detonated by a reconnaissance aircraft which hovered overhead throughout the night.

However, the Engineering Unit of Islamic Resistance defused two others, each of 12kg and full of flechettes, on the road of Ghandourieh-Froun. Both bombs were disguised as rocks.

The explosives were intended as in former cases to be detonated by the civilians for they planted inside a residential area and near the houses. Both bombs are very identical to the bombs planted by the "Israeli" troops in the villages of Qlayleh (19/6/1997), Zillaya (4/6/1997), Nabatieh (8/11/1997), Majdal Selem (6/12/1997), highway of Dorodghaya-Hmeiri (20/10/1996), road of Froun-Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr (20/2/1997), road of Majdal Zoun-Mansouri (23/2/1997), Nabatieh El-Fawqa (6/5/1997), highway of Tyre-Baflay (17/7/1997) and Semmaieh (19/11/1996). Some of these roadside bombs had been discovered on time while the others exploded to kill and injure many civilians.
Shelling :

In Tyre, the reporters said the villages of Mansouri, Majdal Zoun, Froun, Qsair and Litani River came at night under intermittent "Israeli" shelling which resumed at dawn to include Slouqi Valley, Qalaouieh and the outskirts of Froun and Ghandourieh. Also, at 6:30am, the shells extended to involve the eastern quarters of Froun village.

As of 7:00am, the concentrated artillery shelling emanating from the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Hardoun, Radar and Shamaa pounded into Mansouri, Majdal Zoun and Byout El-Siyad, whereas two missiles fell near Hamra crossing point.

In Nabatieh region, it was reported that the "Israeli" batteries bombarded at morning Mazraat Aqmata and the heights of Sojod, Rihan and Aishieh villages.

"Israeli" penetration :
A common "Israeli"-Lahdi force of twenty five soldiers, three military, vehicles and one bulldozer came out of Zaghleh position, north of Hasbaya and penetrated three kms deep inside the liberated area towards Meimess valley to position finally near a sand block separating between the two liberated and occupied regions in Western Beqaa.

This "Israeli" military move was met by a mobilization among the Lebanese Army ranks stationed at Meimess village and its surroundings.

The "Israeli" bulldozer improved and heightened the sand block and closed two sand roads, one leads to Ain Debleh area and the other leads to Ain Qinya and Showaya villages.
The force lasted for about two hours and returned then to its previous position.

Islamic Resistance Operations :
In response to the "Israeli" aggressions, the Islamic Resistance militants attacked at 7:40am Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying two of its main fortifications.

Canadian-French delegation at Qana :
A common media delegation of Human Rights Committees in Canada and France paid a visit to Qana's memorial where it put a wreath.

Representative of Canadian Human Rights Committee Nova Victja said: "We are surprised that a state like "Israel", which presents itself before the international public opinion as a democratic one, had committed such a savage and barbaric massacres. What I have seen today will enforce me to transfer the real image of this state which lifts the democracy slogan while committing massacre more horrible than those committed by the Nazis".

Families of detainees :
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayed Hassan Nassrallah had received a delegation of families held in "Israeli" detention camps. Nassrallah explained the latest developments of the potential exchange process maintaining that the internees and detainees "are the conscience and heart of Hizbullah leadership, and that the issue of releasing them is our first priority".

It is to be pointed out that Hizbullah had managed to liberate a great number of detainees and martyr bodies through exchanging them by two "Israeli" corpses in 1997.

About the "Israeli" retreat:
"Israeli" Minister of Defense Yitzhac Mordechai continued peddling his plan in United States of America which until now did not adopt it. Nevertheless, it seems not to classify the "Israeli" activities in South Lebanon as aggressions and occupation actions that violate a sovereign state as this was determined and defined by the international laws . So, when asked whether "Israel" is a state occupying Lebanese territories, the American Minister of Defense William Cohen replied: "We do not have this stance. At the same time, we encourage complying with the UN resolutions".

This American stance may had been highlightened by the statement of Iranian Minister of Culture and Guidance Atallah Mohajirani  who said the consecutive "Israeli" governments "did not demarcate the final borders of Zionist entity" where "Israel's" constitution, unlike the whole countries around the world, does not mention this issue at all.

In the meantime, the news reported that the Americans  well aware that Mordechai, who seeks after the "Israeli" premiership, is trying through Lebanese matter to improve his image and status inside "Israel" by saving it from the Lebanese mess. Nonetheless, he did not bear any thing new until now.
Some commentators say that the reasons of flexibility expressed by Mordechai such as his interest in getting out of the Lebanese quagmire, satisfying the "Israeli" public opinion, improving regionally and internationally the image of ruling coalition in "Israel" and diverting the American's attention through a certain move in Lebanon so as to make it flexible concerning Netanyahu's intransigence over the Palestinian track are all correct and right. Besides, there is something else, as the western diplomatic sources hint at, represented in the personal political ambitions of a number of the "Israeli senior officials especially Netanyahu, Mordechai and Sharon.

On the way to this office, the first had managed to overcome two rivals Dan Meridor and Begin the son. Meridor's competition emerged from the ability to invest his economical performance, so to speak, in order to hold Likud's command and subsequently the premiership, whereas Begin's rivalry stemmed from the political legacy his father Menachem Begin left behind.

But the real and serious rivalry to Netanyahu is that which arises from the security field for it is a precondition of achieving the peace as perceived by the latter on one hand and it is the field where Mordechai and Sharon have a long history and special experience on the other. Their slogan always was to secure the stability and peace for the northern "Israeli" localities and to halt the bleeding sustained by the "Israeli" collaborators.

It is worthmentioning that the competition is, first, between Mordechai and Sharon. This competition pushed the latter to put forward his plan in an attempt to knock out that of Mordechai.

Also, the competition is, second, between Netanyahu and Mordechai. So, despite the fact that Netanyahu prefers Mordechai's plan, for this reason he freehanded Mordechai to make the last tour, but he cannot permit Mordechai to go too far and endanger his command of Likud Party and, consequently, the premiership. The core point here is Lebanon's situation which an issue of polarization between Netanyahu, Mordechai and Sharon.
