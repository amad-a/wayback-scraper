Daily Report
 
March Friday 20-1998:

Upon arriving at Beirut via its international airport, the families of Lebanese detainees and internees arrested in "Israeli" jails rushed to receive the United Nations Secretary General Kofi Annan who addressed them by saying : "I know very well what a mother or sister feels upon missing a husband, son or brother".

The families, who exposed big photographs related to their detainees and lifted posters explaining their suffering and demanding their release, interrupted Annan frequently through expressing their fearness.

Annan then added:  "We will fight for you and for the human rights. I will do my best for the International Committee for Red Cross to be permitted to enter into "Israels" concentration camps and particularly Khiam detention camp, especially the whole world commemorates in these days with the fiftieth memory of establishing the United Nations Organization and the International Declaration of Human Rights".

But Annan did not take up a promise for releasing the detainees whom "Israel" uses them as bargaining cards as it was stated by the "Israeli" Supreme Court. So, this situation does not satisfy the minimum level of rights which should be granted to the detainees and their families.

Nevertheless, the exciting point in Annans statement is that he considered Khiam detention camp as an "Israeli" one. "Israel" repeatedly had rejected responsible for Khiam detention camp whereas all concerned parties such as detainees, observers and jurists underline that "Israeli" intelligence apparatus is responsible for it.

On the other hand, the National Association for the Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons  handed over the United Nations Secretary General a memorandum in which it pointed out to the flagrant "Israeli" violations & the ill-treatment against the Lebanese detainees held in "Israels" jails. For if they were considered as internees they would be protected by the third Geneva Convention, otherwise they would be protected by the Fourth Geneva Convention".

The memorandum clarified: " The few number of the detainees who had been released following 1992 were in miserable health situation that some of them had died after a short period of their release. At the same time, there are dozens of the Lebanese detainees moved into "Israel" had been subjected to sentences varying between 10 and 30 years imprisonment, thus, discreting the international laws and mores".

"Despite "Israels" claims that has no any relation with Khiam detention camp, all evidences and testimonies made by the released detainees, even the testimony of a former "Israeli" soldier, underline without any doubt that Khiam detention camp is under full control of the "Israeli" intelligence apparatus and its collaborators", the memorandum continued.

It concluded: "There are more than 250 Lebanese citizens, including several children and women, are falling now as victims of the international laws and human rights. Also, since the United Nations first priority is to maintain the peace and human rights in the world, then, it should work hard for the Resolution 425 to be implemented unconditionally. In the meantime, the United Nations must exert its best efforts for "Israel" to abide completely by the Geneva conventions and promote the detention conditions of the detainees".

However, the Lebanese Jurist Group  delivered Annan a memorandum in which it mentioned: "The Lebanese people is looking for an effective and basic role for Annan to exert the possible pressures on "Israel" in order to retreat from main parts of Lebanese lands".

"Israel" must not be permitted to impose its own interpretation of the Resolution 425, it emphasized.

"Annan should promptly send an international inquiry committee to unmask and disclose "Israels" abuses and violations for Human Rights Charter and Geneva and Lahai conventions. This move should be in the context of the efforts which must be exerted to release immediately the detainees and hostages", it ended.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The "Israeli" occupation forces arrested the civilian Mohammad El-Sayyed Ali From Aita El-Shaab which had been seized since the last Monday .

On the other hand, the recent "Israeli" abuses, deterioration in Lahds morale along with the increasing debates over a possible "Israeli" pull-out of Lebanon made the "Israeli" collaborators in a critical situation especially after Lebanon had refused to be joined the Lebanese security forces or the political life, knowing that many of them had been sentenced by Lebanons judicial authorities for cooperating with the enemy.

However, the South Lebanon had witnessed several "Israeli" aggressions which had been recorded in a statement issued by the Islamic Resistance :
13:25, two helicopters hovered over Blat post.
14:25, Tair Harfa position targeted Jib Soweid with three 120mm shells.
14:30, Zohaira position hit Jib Soweid with a number of 155mm missiles.
15:30, the east of Zibqeen came under four 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position.
17:10, the valley between Mansouri and Majdal Zoun subjected to four 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position.
17:35, Zohaira position hit the western perimeters of Yater with thirteen 155mm missiles. The shells exploded 50m from the houses whereas one of the shells fell near a military vehicle related Unifil.
18:00, Tair Harfa position hit the area between Majdal Zoun and Zibqeen with four 120mm shells.
21:25, Meshnaqa position targeted the surroundings of Anan post with two 81mm shells.

Islamic Resistance Operations:

The attacks launched by the Islamic Resistance fighters on the "Israeli"-occupied positions in Southern Lebanon resulted in wounding six "Israeli" soldiers at Radar position  as it was acknowledged by the "Israeli" occupation forces. The Islamic Resistance issued the following statement :
14:40, the group of two martyrs Mohammad Rafik Al-Youssef and Mohammad Ahmad Moussa targeted Zemraya position with the adequate arms scoring direct and definite hits.
16:30, the group of both martyrs Wissam Fsahi and Mahmoud El-Assaad attacked Radar post with the proper weapons chalking up direct and certain hits.
17:30, the Islamic Resistance men observed a Zionist squad inside Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the group of two martyrs Sheikh Khalil Said and Rodwan Rashid attacked it with the proper arms scoring direct hits.

On the other hand and in an attempt to raise the morale his agents, the "Israeli" Prime Minister met with Antoine Lahd so as to assure and give him confidence that he will not abandon him at all. In the meantime, Lahds Militia arrested some of Taibehs youngmen because they had refused to join the compulsory military service and beleaguered Shebaa village for the same purpose.

 Lahds Militia threatened also he who will cooperate with the Lebanese security systems inside the occupied region of Southern Lebanon  after it had revealed that many of citizens are offering significant information about the military motions of "Israeli" forces and their collaborators.

These Lahdi threats indicate how the critical situation "Israeli" agents suffer.

The Resolution 425 and "Israeli" withdrawal

Tens of the "Israelis" demonstrated today demanding the "Israeli" troops to pull out of Lebanon. The demonstrators covered their faces with mud  indicating the great losses sustained by the "Israeli" forces as a result of occupying Southern Lebanon.

In the meantime, the demonstrators demands contradicted with the decisions taken by the "Israeli" minimized cabinet headed by Benjamin Netanyahu. The cabinet adopted Netanyahus plan which stipulates negotiations as a necessary step to conclude security arrangements in return for an "Israeli" withdrawal from South Lebanon, knowing that these arrangements are completely rejected by Lebanon which demands strongly the unconditional fulfillment of the Resolution 425 .

In this regard, Osmat Abdel-Majid, the secretary General of Arab League declared : "Any "Israeli" condition in return for carrying out the Resolution 425 is not acceptable at all", pointing out to the fact that "Israel" is obliged to implement the concerned Resolution for the area which aimed to be a safe and calm region for its occupation forces had become a dangerous and disastrous area because of the Lebanese people resistance".

When the Resolution 425 came to light twenty years ago, Abdel-Majid was then an Ambassador of his country to the United Nations.

Taking this point into the consideration, he stressed that "this Resolution states an unconditional withdrawal from South Lebanon" and that the mechanism adopted later relates to Unifils mission and not to any guarantees that might be granted for "Israel". So. Lebanons stance in this respect is strong and rightful".

However, "Israeli" political analysts  said that the real cause which stands behind Sharons plan is that he is affraid that Mordechais feelers will pave the way in front of the resumption of the negotiations between "Israel" and  Syria and thus causing an "Israeli" withdrawal from Golan Heights.

Also, the observers said that the main goal of highlighting the Lebanese issue from Netanyahus side is to evade the requirements of Palestinian track especially in the light of the increasing debates over an imminent American initiation which might call for an "Israeli" retreat from West Bank with a rate greater than that suggested by Netanyahus government.
