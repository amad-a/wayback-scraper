Daily Report
 

Tuesday March 10-1998:
 

Finally, the British Foreign Ministry  expressed its concerns over a March 4 "Israeli" Supreme Court ruling authorizing the "Israeli" government to hold Lebanese detainees as bargaining cards in negotiations over servicemen missed in action on the Lebanese territories.

Spokesman of the British Foreign Ministry said: "We had always expressed our opposition to the administrative detention and final rulings", adding, "Britain rejects detaining any person in the region without a fair trial".

It is something strange that despite the British Foreign Ministry and American State Department had been knowing well that "Israel" is holding Lebanese nationals as hostages without fair trial and under an illegal procedure called "administrative detention" , they had never exerted any adequate pressure on "Israels" government in order to release the concerned hostages.

Americas pressure exerted from time to time on a selected countries, often opposing to its tyrantnic policy, is a flagrant siding with the repressive and unfair "Israeli" logic of power.

Likewise, Britain had blasted, previously and in different cases and times, holding persons as hostages in many areas around the world. So, what is the matter with the British today to express shyly their concern only.

On the other hand, the comment on television over the weekend by Labor Party leader Ehud Barak - that had he been born a Palestinian he would have probably joined a terror organization-continued to provoke heated discussion in "Israel".

So, "Israeli" Mr. Michael Kleiner (Gesher) caused an uproar with his statement: "According to Baraks twisted logic, its interesting what Barak would have told the interviewer had he been asked what he would have done had he been born to German parents during World War II" .

Kleiner also said he "would not be surprised if Barak, as a potential heir to the premiership and as a former chief of general stall, find, himself as the chief marketing factor in the recruitment commercials of Haman"  .

The storm broke out again when MK Haggai Merom (Labor) called out to the Likud: "You have the culture of terrorists within a democracy" .

Answering on behalf of the government, Deputy Defense Minister Silvan Shalom said Labor had
created the Likuds next election campaign slogan: "With Barak well win!" Shalom said: he had been waiting for three days for Barak to apologize and admit he had made a mistake and put an end to the episode. "Instead, Barak hides behind his words and adds to them an attack on the Likud and its leader. Was it they who said these words? "Shalom asked .

He quoted a poll that 90 percent of the public is convinced Barak had made a mistake and said Baraks words had shown a serious defect in someone who is a candidate for prime minister. Shalom said Barak had legitimized terror by his statement .

Throughout the discussion MKs. traded insults, with Naomi Blumenthal (Likud) telling Barak: "You are the copywriter of the terror organizations". Micha Goldman (Labor) responded: "You are the duty idiot for the Likud". When MK Ori Orr (Labor), a former major-general, told Blumenthal she knew nothing about fighting terror, she replied calling him a Ghauvinists .

Furthermore, Knesset members from both sides of the political aisle lashed out at each other after Hamas activist Ayman Kafissa used remarks made by Labor leader Ehud Barak to defend his activities .

Kafissa shocked a Military Court in Lod yesterday when he said, "Mr. Barak said that if he was Palestinian he would have been a soldier for Hamas, Fatah or Hizbullah. We are Hamas soldiers who justifiably want to liberate Palestinian land" .

"Israeli" Aggressions:

Today, the "Israeli" aggressions accompanied by abuses towards the population of Arqoub area. So, it was reported that the "Israeli" troops and their agents had called in for more than thirty of Shebaa notables  to the HQ of Security Apparatus at Marjeyoun. The summon was not justified and the reasons remained unknown, knowing that this move came after the citizens had refused paying illegal taxes imposed by the so-called Civil Administration Board . But the independent sources noticed that the purpose of this "Israeli" move  is to exert a pressure on Shebaa residents to join Lahds Militia and the Civil Administration Board. Also, it seems that this issue is a condition for the occupation forces to lift the siege of Shebaa and Hibbarieh villages which had been imposed for about two months. During this siege, the "Israeli" troops prevented citizens less than fifty years old from moving towards the liberated areas causing a food shortage.

However, the "Israeli" fighters continued in flying over Hasbaya and Western Beqaa regions , whereas the "Israeli" shelling spread to involve Majdal Zoun, Mansouri, Beyout El-Siyyad, Jib Soweid and Mazraat Aqmata, the heights of Jabal Zahr, Dellafeh and Qatraneh and woodlands of Sojod and Rihan .

Resolution 425 and "Israels" Pull-out:

Deputy Lebanese Prime Minister and Minister of Interior Michael Murr asserted that "the Resolution 425 is clear and forthright and needs no further explanations" and that "the Israeli interpretation is not but a booby-trapped return to the May 17 Agreement. The Resolution 425 emphasizes, clearly, an unconditional retreat", he added.

"Israels" plan aims at disengaging the Lebanese-Syrian track and striking the national unity" painting out that "the correlation between the two tracks in peace and war circumstances prevents breaking down, weakening and isolating Lebanon on which "Israel" wants to impose a surrender accord, he concluded .

Ali El-Khalil, Chief of Lebanese Parliament Foreign Affairs Committee focused on "Lebanons firm stance over an unconditional implementation for the Resolution 425" rejecting "the negotiation about the means of fulfilling it" highlighting the correlation between the Lebanese and Syrian tracks & calling on strengthening "the national unity against the "Israeli" booby-trapped maneuver" .
