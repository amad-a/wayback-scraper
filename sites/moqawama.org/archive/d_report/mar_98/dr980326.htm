Daily Report
 
Thursday March 26-1998
 

Detainees:

The "Israeli" newspaper Haaretz mentioned that the Paris-based Solidarity League for the Lebanese Detainee Soha Bshara and the "Israeli" League for Banning Torture had appealed for the release of Soha Bshara before the "Israeli" Supreme Court. Bshara has been arrested for about ten years in Khiam detention camp.
The petition presented by the left-wing "Israeli" lawyer Lina Tsmil on behalf of the abovementioned leagues who reported: "So far, any judicial verdict against Soha Bshara has not been taken while she is spending her days in an "Israeli" solitary confinement in a manner contradicts with the international laws. The "Israeli" authorities prevented the International Committee for the Red Cross, human rights organizations and lawyers to visit the detainees and to say nothing of their families. Also, the Amnesty International considered Soha Bshara" as a hostage detained illegally".
The petition demanded the "Israeli" Court to enforce Chief of Staff and Minister of Defense in order to release her .

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The "Israeli" Occupation forces breached once again the April 1996 Understanding as their batteries bombarded most of areas adjacent to the green line wounding two civilians and causing damage to a number of Baraasheit's houses. Also, it was informed that a sergeant of the Lebanese Interior Public Security was detained at Hamra crossing point.

Breaching April 1996 Understanding :

In Tyre, it was reported that as of eight O'clock at morning, the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Haddatha and Baraasheit shelled the two liberated villages of Shaqra and Baraasheit. The "Israeli" shelling wounded two civilians: Abbas Lutfi Zahwi, 31, and Hussein Kayed Melhem, 24. Both are from Majdal Selem but they were working at Baraasheit. They were moved to Tibbnine Hospital for treatment.
At 10:30am, the "Israeli" Shelling extended to involve the villages of Tibbnine, Haddatha, Jabal El-Zaatar, Baraasheit, Shaqra, Safad El-Battikh and Majdal Selem.
The bombardment paralyzed the daily life whereas the schools in the region suspended their courses and classes.
For its parts the Irish battalion of Unifil put itself on the alert and patrolled its military vehicles along the road stretching between Siddiqeen and Tibbnine villages.
Complaint : Familiar sources reported that the Lebanese delegation will present a complaint to the Chief of Five-Nation Wonitering Committee the American Ambassador Joseph Sullivan concerning an "Israel's" violation of the April 1996 Understanding when it shelled Baraasheit village wounding two civilians Abbas Zahwi and Hussein Melhem.
In Nabatieh, the "Israeli" gunners shelled intensely as of 4 O'clock at dawn the surroundings of the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh, the eastern hilltops of Kfar Romman and Nabatieh El-Fawqa, Zahrani River bands and Wadi El-Akhdar. The "Israeli" shelling was accompanied by firing heavy and middle machine guns rounds emanating from the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Zaffateh, Dabsheh and Tohorra.
After 6:00am, the "Israeli" bombardment spread to include the perimeters of Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh, Froun, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr, Yohmor and Litani River.
In Eastern Sector, the heights of Qatraneh and the area stretching from the western part of Sraireh village until Mazraat Shbeel inside the occupied region, came under the "Israeli" bombardment. Later on, the "Israeli" missiles extended for about three hours to Litani River from Dellafeh side.
In Western Beqaa, Berkat Jabbour, Abou Rashed forests and Ain Tineh heights came under several missiles whereas the woodlands of Mashghara village exposed to a number of mortar shells and barrage of heavy machine gun fire.
On the other hand, the "Israeli" troops abducted the sergeant of Interior Public Security Marwan Abi Samara at Hamra crossing point as he was coming from his occupied village of Zohaira in Western Sector and took him to Khiam detention camp .

"Israeli" collaborators and the asylum

However, four "Israeli" agents of Lahd's militia requested asylum in the Jewish state in fear of reprisals following a pullout underlying the collapsing morale of the militia.
"Israeli" lawyer Zvi Rish was quoted as telling "Israel" radio: "I have been contacted by a senior officer and three fighters of Lahd's Militia, all from the same family, who asked me to seek political asylum and even, eventually, "Israeli" citizen ship for them and their families" .
The four militiamen wrote to Netanyahu and Defense Minister Yitzhak Mordechai, requesting refuge. "We tied our fate to the state and we protected the settlements of northern "Israel" (not their own homeland!) with our bodies. "Israel" is deeply obligate to us", the letter said. According to Rish, the four "are all very worried, even panicked, by the possibility of an "Israeli" withdrawal from the 'security  zone' because they think the arrival in the area of Syrian troops and members of Hizbullah could lead to massacres" .
This request of asylum reflects the reality of depression suffered by these who often committed crimes against their own people and homeland for the favor of "Israeli" enemy. Also, it reflects the truth of slogans raised by them in the face of the Lebanese national forces claiming that they are the only force which would liberate Lebanon, but from whom? And for the favor of whom?.
These who belong to the "Israeli" agent Antoine Lahd are not but mercenaries that fight side by side with the enemy in return for money paid by "Israel".
In the meantime, it was reported  that several families of the "Israeli" agents senior officers had fled to "Israel" then to Australia and Canada as a primary step for those officers to follow them, whereas Antoine Lahd  is the only "Israeli" agent who secured himself by refuging to France which approved his political asylum.

About the "Israeli" retreat:

Amid the debates over a possible "Israeli" pull-out from result South of Lebanon as a result of increasing pressure exerted by the Islamic Resistance, Austrian Chancellor Victor Klima  declared that Austria and European Union (EU) call for an unconditional implementation of UN Resolution 425. The Chancellor did not confirm the "Israeli" withdrawal, adding: "We have heard a lot of rumors. We are waiting for a decision pertaining to the retreat when the "Israeli government will meet this Sunday".
In the meantime, the Lebanese Foreign Affairs Minister Fares Boueiz  expressed his satisfaction over the stance of Arab foreign ministers assembly concerning Lebanon where he said: "It is very significant that the Arab Laeague had adopted the Lebanese stand pertaining to the "Israeli" propositions about the situation in South Lebanon".
"After we had explained in detail the differences between the "Israeli" proposition and the Resolution 425, a statement issued by the assembly in which the Arab foreign ministers considered the "Israeli" proposals disagreeing the Resolution 425 for these proposals imply preconditions which may blew up its content and core. The Arab League foreign ministers assembly asked for an immediate and unconditional withdrawal from South Lebanon, for it is unreasonable to negotiate over a piecemeal withdrawal", Boueiz concluded.
