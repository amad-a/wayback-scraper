Daily Report
 
Thursday March 12,1998:
 

Following 27 hours of continued meetings of Five-Nation Monitoring Committee during which complaints presented by the Lebanese and "Israeli" delegations were discussed. The Lebanese and Syrian delegations refuted the "Israeli" claims concerning the resistance attack on Jezzine barracks. The Committee  said the resistance attack was legal and causing no casualties among the civilian ranks. Also, the Lebanese and Syrian delegations managed to affirm the resistance right to use anti-aircraft guns.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

In the meantime, the "Israeli" artillery bombarded the heights and woodlands of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh and Mazraat Aqmata. The "Israeli" shelling spread to include Jibal El-Botom, Zibqeen, Yater, Majdal Zoun and Izzieh valleys .

On the other side, it was reported that the "Israeli" troops stormed into the villages of Taibeh, Odaisseh, Markaba and Kfar Kila searching their houses. This campaign resulted in detaining a lot of youngsters in order to join the compulsory military service within Lahds Militia. Some of the detainees were identified as: Hassan Rida Moussa, Mohammad Mahdi Fawaz, Fares Khodor Fares, Abbas Fares, Ali Fares and Ossama Chith .

However, the Lebanese Red Cross passed through the siege of Taibeh and Rob-Thlathine villages and distributed five hundred food packages among their population.

Islamic Resistance Operations:

On the level of military operations, the Islamic Resistance carried out several attacks on the positions of the "Israeli" occupation forces. Here are the details according to the statement issued by Islamic Resistance :
15:15, the group of two martyrs Abbas Hammoud and Fouad Abdallah attacked an "Israeli" patrol near Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rockets chalking up direct and certain hits. At the same time, the group of both martyrs Mohammad Najem and Fadi Tawil rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rockets preventing it from reinforcing the attacked patrol, scoring direct and definite hits and destroying its main fortification near the entrance gate.
17:25, the Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force inside Blat post. Immediately afterwards, the group of two martyrs Khalil Olayan and Ghaleb Ali Harb fell upon it with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits. The smoke columns were seen by the naked eye emanating from the post.
17:40, the group of martyr Ahmad Mahdi attacked an "Israeli" force near Haddatha position with the proper arms chalking up direct hits.
18:00, the group of martyr Bassel Alaeddine bombarded Haddatha position with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits. The smoke columns were seen emanating from the position it self.
20:15, the group of martyr Mohammad El-Hadi bombarded Haddatha position with the appropriate arms inflicting a number of casualties among its members and causing damages to some of its fortifications.

Detainees:

The Thursdays Mothers carried out its weekly sit-in, which coincides with the memory of March 14, 1978 "Israeli "Invasion of Lebanon, before the Headquarters of International Committee for Red Cross-Hamra, in the presence of Secretary of Committee for Supporting the Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" prisons and a number of its members.

During the sit-in, several speeches were delivered demanding the releasing of detainees and the pull-out of "Israeli" troops from Lebanon .

March 14 Remembrance and Resolution 425

In the context of debate over the implementation of Resolution 425 stating on retreating the "Israeli" forces from South Lebanon which invaded by these forces on March 14,1998, the Lebanese demanded, through festivals held upon callings made by several political, student and union forces, the "Israeli" occupation forces to get out of Lebanon  after it had refused to comply with the international Resolution 425 issued by the Security Council.

However, Maronite League raised a doubt about the "Israeli" intentions towards fulfilling the Resolution 425. "The expectations raised recently over the Resolution 425 particularly the "Israeli" feelers which are often elusive and make confusion for the international public opinion do not prompt comfort and satisfaction. This situation requires a warning campaign among the Lebanese people to enhance the national unity with the aim of undermining the "Israeli" proposal through maintaining the
correlation between the Lebanese and Syrian tracks .

The Casuist (Mufti) of Republic of Lebanon Mohammad Rachid Qabbani said: "Qanas massacre and March 14 memory are exceptional evidences on the "Israeli" terror which extended over the past fifty years. Since its birth in 1948, "Israels" gangs such as Hagana had been committing the massacres, one after another, in Deir Yassine, Jafa and Haifa. Qanas massacre is not but a new confirmation about the terroristic nature of "Israels" intentions. Likewise, "Israels" recent feeler over conditional retreating from Lebanon is not but a new play to separate the Lebanese track from Syrian one. In fact, the correlation between the two tracks is only way to confront "Israels" ambitions and deception .

In the meantime, Assistant Secretary of State for Middle East Affairs, Martin Indyk disclaimed  that "Israel" and United States had discussed the recent proposals about the withdrawal from Lebanon" and refused to discuss, also, "Israels" motives.

It is something strange that the "Israelis", who are in strategic relations with the Americans concerning the policy in Middle East, did not really discussed their proposal with them. Besides, "Israels" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu had declared recently that the Europeans, unlike Americans, know nothing about this region. Adding what preceded to the Secretary General of United Nations Koufi Anans denial about any proposals concerning the "Israeli" withdrawal from South Lebanon. The perspective will be as French well acquainted sources  depicted it that there was no any new "Israeli" stance. The whole story is not but a trial balloon and an attempt to embarrass the Lebanese and Syrian sides.
