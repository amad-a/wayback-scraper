Daily Report
 

Wednesday March 4-1998:

As "Israel" pursued its aggressions against the Lebanese people and lands, the Lebanese Minister of Foreign affairs and his Syrian counterpart discussed the "Israeli" stands over the possibility of fulfilling the Resolution 425. The discussion resulted in emphasizing the correlation of both Lebanese and Syrian tracks in the negotiations with the "Israeli" occupier about its withdrawal from South Lebanon and Golan Heights.

The news of both sides1 reported that Syria still absolutely bolster Lebanons stand concerning the forthright text of the Resolution 425 issued before the Resolution 426 which determine the mechanism of implementing the first one. It is unnegotiable and irrevocable ruling. If there are any security arrangements, they should be as a result and complement of the retreat. Also, they should be between the United Nations and "Israel", from one side, so that the latter must hand over the occupied Lebanese lands to the first, and also should be between the United Nations and Lebanon, from the other side, so that the latter can receive its freed lands.

Furthermore, the Armistice Agreement, concluded in 1949 and torn by 1978 "Israeli" Invasion, must regulate the relations between Lebanon and "Israel" after its retreat from Lebanon.

Also, it was underlined on the connection between the Resolution 425 and other international resolutions such as 242 and 338, which adopted by Madrid Peace Congress to pave the way for a natural peace because the Resolution 425 handles a specified point like the 1978 "Israeli" Invasion of Lebanon. So it cannot alone realize the comprehensive and fair peace. The other issues such as: Palestinian future and presence in Lebanon, the seven Lebanese occupied villages and the Lebanese water captured by "Israel" should be treated by the peace process and negotiations.

Concerning the post-withdrawal phase, it is up to the Lebanese authorities to take decisions on issues such as the future of Hizbullah and the fate of Lahds militiamen.

Going back to Boueiz-Sharaa discussions, it was transpired that France did not adopt or propagate the "Israels" stand or rather "Israels" maneuver. Revealing the inseriousness of the "Israeli" maneuver, Ariel Sharon, "Israeli" Minister of Infrastructure, pointed out that "Israeli" government did not take any decision over carrying out the United Nations Resolution 425 confirming that any discussions inside the sessions of "Israeli" cabinet headed by Benjamin Netanyahu did not occur2.

Daniel Jwanou3, the French Ambassador in Lebanon, said after a meeting held with Lebanese Premier and Minister of Foreign affairs, France is not involved in any special "Israeli" feeler. Also, it is not about to be an intermediary out of the context of the resolutions issued by the United Nations & International Security Council unless the concerned parties agreed unanimously. These statements indicate, from French viewpoint, that the International community cannot approve the "Israeli" maneuver.

In this regard, the "Israeli" aggressions on Lebanon continued as usual. So, the "Israeli" gunners stationed at Zaffateh post shelled Mazraat Aqmata and the heights of Sojod, Rihan and Aishieh in Iqleem Al-Tuffah. Moreover, a shepherd was survived whereas a number of his livestock were died as tank rounds fired by a tank stationed at Ain Qiniah Hill at 1:00pm.
At 4:00pm and while two "Israeli" helicopter gunships were showering the area between Qatraneh and Loussi with heavy machine gun fire, the "Israeli" artillery shelled the same area with several 122mm missiles.

In Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" occupation forces bombarded the surroundings of Jabbour Pool and the perimeters of Abou Rashed in addition to combing Maramel heights and Mashghara woodland4.

Occupiers and student-shielding:

In the occupied region7, the correspondents reported that the occupation forces had stationed at a newly constructed position inside Shamaa village and very close to the houses and about less than 100m away from the village school. The move was interpreted as a protection shield for the "Israeli" troops. The population started to look for another residence centers far away from the forces positioned there.

It is to be pointed out that April 1996 Understanding stated that the civilians should be kept away from any direct or indirect military actions.

Lebanese detainees and "Israeli" Judiciary

"Israels" Supreme Court8  passed a ruling in which it authorized the "Israeli" government to "hold ten Lebanese detainees as a bargaining card. They are not targets, but they can serve to achieve a goal".

The ten have appealed for release, but the Court refused. The judgement was not promulgated except after eliminating their names and how they were detained from the courts record.

Aharon Barak, Chief of the Supreme Court, said: "Releasing the Lebanese detainees may crumple the negotiations over the "Israeli" missing men", claiming "arresting a group or individual in return for releasing our missing and captured men is a vital interest for State of "Israel".

The London-based Amnesty International said the Courts, decision is intolerable, adding that "those held as hostages include people who were only 16 when they were taken from their village and have now spent up to 11 years in detention, often in secret and incommunicado9.

Also, it added: "Some have been there without charge, trial or any legal status since 1986. Since September 1997, they have had no access to the International Committee for Red Cross10.
