Daily Report
 
Saturday March 14,1998:
 

Like this day before twenty years, "Israel" brushed aside the international laws through invading the Lebanese territories reaching to Litani River. Neither International Security Council nor argent calls managed to stop its invasion. Also, it did not carry out the Resolution 425 concerning the unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon.

Since then, the "Israeli" aggressions continued against Lebanon and its nationals. Day after day, these aggressions are in constant increase through using different kinds of weapons such as warplanes, tanks, mortars and machine guns. Nothing to say about the horrible massacres such as Qana massacre where more than one hundred civilians, most of them are children, women and old men, were killed as they were taking shelter inside one of United Nations Headquarters.

Today, the "Israeli" batteries shelled, like the past days throughout the last twenty years, the villages of Tibbnine, Baraasheit, Haddatha, Aita El-Jabal, Haris, Srobbin, Rshaf, Majdal Zoun, Mansouri and Beyout El-Seyyad . Also, the "Israeli" shelling extended to include Slouqi Valley, Aishieh, Zibqeen, Jabal Zahr and the perimeters of Sraireh .

Islamic Resistance & Lebanese Brigades Operations:

The Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it states the following :
11:15, the Islamic Resistance men observed an enemy force carrying out certain fortifications inside the "Israeli"-occupied Radar post. Immediately afterwards, the group of two martyrs Ali Rida Yassine and Ibrahim Dakdouk rushed upon the concerned post with various kinds of weapons scoring direct hits. The columns of smoke were seen emanating from it.
11:25, the group of two martyrs Rida Hariri and Yassine Chehab attacked Hardoun outpost which is the Headquarters of 81th Brigade, with different kinds of arms chalking up direct hits.

However, the Lebanese Brigades for Resisting the "Israeli" forces issued its first statement in which it states the following :

Four months and seventeen days ago, exactly on November 9,1997, Hizbullahs Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah announced the birth of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades. Today March 14-1998, the 20th memory of 1978 "Israeli" Invasion of Lebanon, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades goes into action against the "Israeli" occupation forces and issues its first statement:

"We had wanted the deeds to precede the works. Also, we had intended to announce our existence by blood, rifle and operational presence in the battlefield defending our homeland, people and dignity".

"We have decided to take the action, on this day particularly, to maintain that the "Israeli" enemy does not know the logic of law and international resolutions. The only logic the enemy can catch is that of force. So far, this logic proved that it caused a defeat for the "Israeli" enemy and enforced it to retreat in many positions and attitudes".

On this day, and on the basis of the operational action, we declare the birth of Lebanese Brigades for Resisting "Israeli" Enemy which went into action following several months of preparation and habilitation, behind the scenes and apart from the illusions, to express the whole Lebanese sects trust of the resistance option and their great hope of the imminent victory, God willing.

The Lebanese Resistance Brigades maintains, from the first bullet, its absolute commitment by this way whatever the sacrifices were. Also, it will be, hopefully, the struggle-framework that can contain every Lebanese national very interested in his people, homeland and dignity.

With the beginning of the military operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades announces its first military statement:

On Monday March 14-1998 and at 5:30am, one of our teams attacked the "Israeli"-occupied Baraasheit outpost with the machine guns, rockets and mortars. The fighters managed to destroy an observation post, heavy machine guns and some of its fortifications scoring direct and certain hits.

At 5:45am, a second team rushed upon Haddatha outpost with the machine guns and mortars destroying some of its fortifications, a number of heavy arms and chalking up direct and certain hits.
Later on at 6:20am, a third team attacked Soweida position with various kinds of light and middle arms inflicting a number  of its members and destroying some of its fortifications.
