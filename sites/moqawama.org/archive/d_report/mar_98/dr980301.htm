Daily Report
 

Sunday March 1-1998:
 
The "Israeli" troops employed batteries of 155 and 120mm caliber and mortars of 81mm caliber in addition to heavy machine gun of 12.7mm caliber in their aggression on the Lebanese territories . The "Israeli" shelling included Sojod mountain, Aqmata, Safi mountain, the outskirts of Jibaa (1), the perimeters of Loweizeh and Kfar Houna plain (2).

In the meantime, the "Israeli" enemy returned to its old manner by carrying out the piracy activities along the Southern Lebanon coast holding the ship Mondial Rorou (3) at the dawn for five hours before it had been released. The abovementioned ship was coming from Hamburg port to Tyre port bearing on its board 314 second hand cars.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The following are the "Israeli" aggressions in accordance with the Islamic Resistance statement:
8:35, Sojod mountain came under four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area, whereas Sojod outpost showered its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
13:25, Zaffateh position fired seven 155mm missiles at Aqmata plain.
13:30, Zaffateh position targeted Safi mountain with three 155mm missiles and the perimeters of Jibaa with three others.
13:35, the perimeters of Loweizeh subjected to eight 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
14:20, Sojod outpost raked its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:00, Kfar Houna position unleashed three 105mm tank rounds at Kfar Houna plain.
23:55, Houla position fired two 81mm flare bombs on the area between Houla and Shaqra villages and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at Slouqi valley.

Attitudes over the Islamic Resistance:
The Lebanese political partys cables of support of Islamic Resistance for its struggle against the "Israeli" occupation forces had continued considering this struggle as the Islamic Nations sole option. So, the Head of Arab Socialist Union sent  to the Secretary General of Hizbullah Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah a cable in which he asserted that "the militants are the conscience of our nation" condemning the "Israeli" massacres committed against the Lebanese civilians.

Islamic Monotheism Movement blessed the heroic military operations staged by the Islamic Resistance militants. "There are no options before the "Israeli" occupation forces but the immediate quick, and an  unconditional withdrawal from the Arab and Islamic territories", the Movement said.
The Movement demanded the United Nations to implement its resolutions confirming that "the United Nations prestige and reputation will be distorted and falsified unless it can carry out these resolutions by force as it had done with Iraq and its miserable people".

However, the Head of Hizbullahs Politburo MP Mohammad Road noticed that "there had been more than ten "Israeli" proposals suggesting a unilateral withdrawal from Lebanon. This insistence doesnt mean but an admittance with the defeat".

"He who pushes the "Israeli" to remember the Resolution 425 after it had been forgotten for twenty
years, can certainly, enforce him within a short period to retreat unconditionally".
