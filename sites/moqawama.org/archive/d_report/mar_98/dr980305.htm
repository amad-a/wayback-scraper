Daily Report
 

Thursday March 5-1998:

The tragedy of the Lebanese detainees in "Israels" prisons continues as long as "Israel" exercises the state terrorism through detaining, arbitrarily, Lebanese nationals as it was reported by Amnesty International  human associations and jurists. "Israels" Supreme Court justified the detention actions as they touch the "Israeli" security vital interests . This justification did not convince one of the three-judge panel Dalia Dorner  who said: "It had not been confirmed certainly and reasonably that releasing the detainees would foil the possibility of releasing the "Israeli" missing and captured men".
However, Amnesty International issued a report in which it said: "The "Israeli" troops or their collaborators are still detaining a number of Lebanese civilians. Most of them have been detained for years because of their suspected relations with the resistance activities waged at the "Israeli" occupation forces in South Lebanon. Some of them were arrested and put in solitary confinements without charge, trial or any legal status. The others were taken to Khiam detention camp, run by the South Lebanon Army, the "Israeli"-backed militia, and located in the "Israeli"-occupied area of South Lebanon".

Amnestys report added: "Not less than 21 Lebanese citizens, all of them had been apprehended, seized or abducted between 1986 and 1994, were taken, in secret, to the "Israeli" detention camps. Some of them have been there without charge and others are still captured despite of serving their sentences. Currently, no one can know the cause for which they were put in confidential administrative detention. Most of them have had no visits paid by their families throughout their period of detention in "Israel" whereas two of them were taken to a clandestine detention center and have had no access to the representatives of the International Committee for the Red Cross".

"About 130 detainees, among them one woman only, are now held in Khiam detention camp. Since "Israel" is considered as an occupation authority in the South Lebanon, so, it should hold the full responsibility for Khiam detention camp in which a lot of detainees have been seized for more than 12 years without being charged, tried or had any legal status", the report wrote.

"Confused and troubled by the long period of forgetfulness, these Lebanese detainees do not know when will be released", Amnesty mentioned.

Those who were captured and taken to "Israel" or Khiam detention camp after serving their sentences or without charge or trial are considered as hostages by the Amnesty International. They may be used as a bargaining chip in a prisoner exchange as it was reportedly mentioned by "Israel" and its agents.

In 1996, Amnestys delegates asked for an explanation of Lebanese nationals apprehended in secret. Uri Or, then deputy "Israeli" Minister of Defense replied: "we will release them as soon as we receive further information about Ron Arad, an "Israeli" Air Force navigator who was shot down over South Lebanon on Oct. 16,1986. Some of the detainees had been released from Khiam detention camp in return for offering information about the "Israeli" soldiers missed in Lebanon.

Amnesty International concluded that "Israel" should release the detainees if it did not want to try them in accordance with international laws which state that it is not acceptable at all for any person to be held as a hostage. Also, every one was apprehended without any reason but in return for offering information over missing persons or enforcing others to release certain detainees should be released unconditionally and promptly.

However, Raif Sigal , professor of law at Tel Aviv University and judicial affairs editor for Haaretz pointed out that the decision of "Israels" Supreme Court breaches a basic legal principle which states that confiscating a freedom of a certain person may be justifiable only if it is related to his personal behavior. Also, this detention violates the principle which states that the man is responsible for the consequence of his work.

Demanding the release of those apprehended in the "Israeli" jails,  Thursdays Mothers made, as usual on every Thursday, a sit-in before the headquarters of the International Committee for the Red Cross in Beirut.

In this sit-in, the Head of the Follow-up Committee for the Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli Jails called on the Lebanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs to file an urgent complaint to the United Nations, International Security Council and competent international organizations to protest against the detention of Lebanese citizens as hostages in "Israels" prisons. Also, he called upon organizing an official and popular international information campaign to scandalize the flagrant violations of the human rights committed and admitted by "Israel".

Resolution 425 and "Israels" Maneuver

However, after Lebanon and Syrian had refused the scandalized "Israeli" maneuver over a conditional implementation of the Resolution 425, "Israels" Premier Benjamin Netanyahu is seeking after marketing his plan which failed at the European level.

So far, Netanyahu has not been given yet an ear by the Europeans. This case pushed him to blast them by saying: "They do not know anything about the Middle East affairs"  . Particularly, he got anger after the French government had not adopted and marketed the "Israeli" project before both Lebanese and Syrian governments . It seems that France, supporting for fulfilling the Resolution 425 since it had been issued in 1978, thinks, as many do in the region, that the only purpose of the "Israeli" step is to separate the Lebanese track from the Syrian one. This purpose repeated through raising "Lebanon First" and "Jezzine First" plans . Together with the separation of both Lebanese and Syrian tracks, raising those two plans aimed at gaining security arrangements, disarming the Islamic

Resistance and monitoring and espionage centers inside Lebanon to keep an eye on Syria.
No doubt, the Islamic Resistance strikes had made the mission of the "Israeli" troops positioned in Southern Lebanon very difficult. Also, they pushed "Israels" President Azer Weisman , reflecting thereby the "Israeli" public opinion over questioning about the advantage of killing their sons on lands of others, to wonder about how to get out of Lebanon.

The "Israeli" project had received another big strike, for all the Arab countries had supported Lebanon and Syria in their confrontation against "Israel". Mohammad Ali Nohaimi , the Qatarian Ambassador and Dean of Arab Diplomatic Corps in Beirut, said the goals of the "Israeli" propose are: separating both Lebanese and Syrian tracks, diverting the attention from the Palestinian-"Israeli" track and pretensing before the "Israeli" public opinion that "Israel" whishes to pull out of South Lebanon.

Egyptian Ambassador to Beirut, Adel Khodary  agreed to his Qatarian Ambassador by saying: "All Arab Ambassadors back up Lebanons stand concerning the implementation of Resolution 425. We are coming into a hard stage because of "Israels" obstinacy".
