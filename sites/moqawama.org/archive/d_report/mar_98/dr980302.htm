Daily Report
 

Monday March 2-1998:

The Islamic Resistance pursued its strife against the "Israeli" occupier on the various levels disclosing its lies just as it had well dealt before with its troops who are now under the mercy of the militants (Mujahedeen) in the battlefield. The Islamic Resistances last swift attack on the "Israeli"-occupied Bir Kallab outpost, in which the militants held complete capture, raised and heated again the debates inside the "Israeli" circles.

In the details, the "Israeli" occupation forces had claimed that the fighters couldnt storm into the post and that the film displayed about the operation was not but an old film for an old operation launched by the resistance previously. Thus, the Islamic Resistance made an information ambush to the "Israeli" Army, especially to its so-called Liaison Unit Officer in South Lebanon Arez Golestein who alleged that the militants did not arrive at the post. The information ambush had represented in displaying films confirm the seizure of Bir Kallab post and planting flags Hizbullah and Islamic Resistance by Mujahedeen who issued the following statement (1):

"Once again, the enemy leadership veils the realities from its society. It seems that propagating lies became the only way to raise the morale of its soldiers serving in the Southern Lebanon and persuade the Zionist society with the significance of staying in the occupied region of South Lebanon".

"From his first days, it was appeared that Arez Golestein, newly appointed as a Liaison Unit Officer, had set up the alleged new strategy against the Islamic Resistance on lies. It seemed that he had not learned from the previous lessons. So, he had been caught in the same trap his leaders ensnared before in two former incidents . The first came when they denied, in the day, storming into Dabsheh outpost and planting Hizbullahs flags by the Islamic Resistance fighters. At night, a video tape displayed by Hizbullah refuted the "Israeli" allegations", the statement added.

"The second time occurred when Elie Emetai, the former officer of "Israeli" Liaison Unit, waited for broadcasting a video tape about storming into Sojod at the evening of 12/5/1997, but this did not happen. Then, he disclaimed that the fighters couldnt intrude into the concerned post. But on next day, the film displayed by the Islamic Resistance proved that the fighters were inside Sojod outpost", it reported.

The statement added: "Today, "Israels" media took their time by keeping silent for over two days in which Hizbullahs Manar TV broadcasted only external and distant scenes about Bir Kallab operation. So, "Israels" media thought that the displayed video tape had filmed a former military operation, as they claimed before the "Israeli" society".

"But disseminating scenes about the enemy and its collaboraters activities inside Bir Kallab outpost before one day of attacking it  and about the militants as they stormed into the post planting Hizbullahs flags on Manar TV had disgraced and unveiled the lies of "Israeli" enemy who is sustaining the military strikes".

However, Nahar, a Lebanese newspaper, interviewed some of the members who intruded into the post. Here are the excerpts:

Four fighters of the group which attacked Bir Kallab outpost told Nahar the details of the military operation carried out by the Islamic Resistance few days before. It is worthmentioning that this attack resulted in killing three "Israeli" soldiers.

The commander of the group known by Abou Yasser is a father of two children, has a Bachelors Degree in Chemistry from Lebanese University-Faculty of science and worked in many fields before he had joined the Islamic Resistance ranks. His comrade known by Hamzeh has three children and was a student at the Faculty of Science at mid of 1980s. Since then, "I devoted myself for the resistance activities", he said. In his turn, Zou El-Fikar completed only the courses of the intermediate classes and participated with several cultural and religious courses. The fourth, Salah, was inflicted in his foot in the firefight. He will get married in the coming few months.

Abou Yasser said: "We obtained information that a squad of "Israeli" paratroopers had entered into Bir Kallab outpost possibly preparing to conduct an aggressive actions against the liberated areas. So, a decision was taken to launch a swift attack on the post.

Salah continued that after he had been ordered to film the attack, he coated his body and dressed clothes suitable to the soil. "I was about only fifteen meters away from Lahds militiamen before they had been replaced by the squad of "Israeli" soldiers. I laid there for nearly four hours during which I shooted three films and informed my commander about the last events. From this very near point, I heard the sounds of agents and witnessed a group of them drinking tea. Also, I watched clearly the exchange of "Israeli" and Lahdi soldiers".

He clarified that the militants had utilized much from these films.

In his turn, Hamzeh told that the "Israeli" troops were provided with night and day-vision equipment and rockets launcher. "Fearing of any attack launched by the Islamic Resistance men, the "Israeli" soldiers were, barely, coming out of their fortifications", he said.

Abou Yasser hinted that there is a special room confined only to the "Israeli" soldiers , in  every post , prohibited by Lahds militiamen to enter it at all".

Salah reported: "After going into the post, we planted the bombs carried by the martyr Hassan Harb inside the whole barricades and destroyed them. Upon pulling out, one "Israeli" missile struck the post resulted in killing the militant Harb and I wounded in my leg. I tried to transport his blessed body, but the commander ordered me to retreat after he had found out with certainty that he was dead".

"Israeli" Aggressions (3):

The "Israeli" enemy launched further aggressions on the Lebanese liberated lands. The Islamic Resistance stated the following:
10:15, Meshnaqa position targeted the surroundings of Anan post with a number of 81mm shells.
12:40, an "Israeli" warboat was seen cruising off the seashore of Adloun.
16:25, Aqmata plain subjected to two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
18:40, the helicopters hovered, at middle altitude, over Blat area.
18:50, Hemayed position hit the area between Haddatha and Haris villages with several 60mm shells.
19:00, the helicopters soared over Rshaf village, whereas Hemayed position fired a number of 81mm shells at the Shobroq area.
20:55, a drone reconnaissance aircraft flew over Qlayleh and its vicinity.
21:20, four "Israeli" warboats were seen patrolling about 2km from the seashore of Adloun and Abou El-Asswad.
21:25, Meshnaqa position targeted the surroundings of Anan post with two 81mm shells.

In this respect, the "Israeli" troops sealed of Taibeh preventing its population from going in and out of the village and arresting eleven citizens. Also, the population called for a sit-in before the International Red Cross Committee HQ-Beirut on Tuesday 3-3-1998.

Meanwhile, MP Nazih Mansour, a member of Islamic Resistance Loyalty bloc, payed a visit to the Lebanese Minister of Foreign Affairs Fares Boueiz explaining the situation in the village and demanding the competent political and diplomatic circles to exert the pressure to lift the siege of the village. "In Taibeh, there are no but children, women and old men", he declared.

Islamic Resistance Operations (4):

14:10, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Fadl Soweidan attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits.
