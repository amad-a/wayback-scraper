Daily Report
 
Wednesday March 11-1998:
 

The Lebanese comments continued over "Israels" Supreme Court judgement concerning holding Lebanese citizens as a bargaining cards in return for retrieving "Israeli" soldiers missed in action in Lebanon.

Farouq Barbir, the Lebanese Minister of Technical and Occupational Education, said the decision "is a clear evidence of a flagrant violation for Geneva Convention and human rights .
Arab Committee for Human Rights  issued a statement in which it condemned "the "Israeli" barbarian decision which undermines the concept of individual responsibility and makes the man apt to blackmail", asking for "the prompt releasing for all Arab detainees and hostages held in the "Israeli" prisons".

The statement reported that "there are sixteen hostages in Alon prison subjected to administrative detention, six others kidnapped by the banned "Lebanese Forces" which delivered them to "Israel" in 1990 in addition to sixteen hostages in Ashkalon jail, four youngsters in Kfaryouna prison and twenty six in Sarafand and Nafha prison", all these prisons locate inside "Israel".

The former Head of Bar Association  said "Israels Supreme Court ruling disclosed the false claims which depict "Israel" as a state of law", wondering "which law permits holding an innocent to be used as a bargaining chip".

"Zionist media had worked hard to depict the Hebrew State as a state of law amid an Arab environment lacking the minimum level of legal-guarantees. By these actions, "Israel" confirms it is something sharply contradicts with the propaganda it broadcasts. Perhaps, the seriousness of the abovementioned ruling enforced "Israel" to keep it as a military secret. It took three months from its issuance to be made public".

In this regard, the Secretary General of Egyptian Organization for Human Rights Mohammad Monib  is visiting Lebanon. Monib is trying to complete a file about "Israels" war crimes to be presented to
the United Nations in a context of an international criminal trial for the "Israeli" war criminals.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The "Israeli" Army continued the siege of Shebaa village after it had sent for dozens of its residents to the security office in Marjeyoun where they remained for more than three hours. The "Israeli" soldiers demanded Shebaas notables to support the Civil Administration office at the village, pay new taxes, offer the youngmen to be joined  Lahds Militia, collaborate with the "Israeli" and Lahdi security systems and inform them with any exceptional and abnormal motions in and around the village. Otherwise, the residents will be suffered a complete siege i.e. preventing them to leave the village and this will cause a shortage in foodstuffs and fuel oil as well .

It was reported that Shebaas population delegation refused al these demands informing the "Israeli" forces that the village is under the protection of United Nations Norwegian force. The "Israeli" officers responded that rejecting the demands will be very costly.

In the meantime, the "Israeli" batteries shelled the heights of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh, Mlikh, Loweizeh and Mazraat Aqmata in Iqleem Al-Tuffah region .

Also, the "Israeli" gunners shelled afternoon the area between Choukine, Maifadoun and Zawtar El-Sharqieh after it had fired heavy machine gun rounds at the quarters of Kfar Tibbnite village . The "Israeli" shelling extended to include at about 4pm. Zawtar Al-Gharbieh, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr, Maifadoun, Choukine and Kfar Dajjal farm.

In Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" artillery bombarded the hilltops of Rihayat, Maramel, Ain El-Amen and Mashghara .

At the same time, the "Israeli" fighters crisscrossed, at middle altitude, throughout the day in the airspace of South Lebanon conducting mock raids .

Islamic Resistance Operations:

The Islamic Resistance fighters responded to "Israels" political maneuver concerning the implementation of Resolution 425 by staging several concentrated attacks at the "Israeli" forces. The militants destroyed one Merkava tank inflicting all its members. The "Israelis" admitted that two soldiers were wounded one of them his case was described as serious . A statement issued by the
Islamic Resistance recorded the following events :
14:10, the group of two martyrs Ali Kaoutharani and Haitham Moghnieh attacked a motorized Lahdi patrol as it was travelling on the road between Sojod and Rihan destroying an M-113 armoured vehicle and inflicting all its members. The vehicle was seen burning.
14:15, the group of both martyrs Ibrahim Alaouieh and Youssef Mahdi fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct hits.
14:20, the Islamic Resistance men observed an enemy force inside Radar post. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of two martyrs Ali Sbeiti and Rabih Akhdar targeted it chalking up direct hits.

Resolution 425 and "Israeli" retreat:

Twenty years ago, United Nations Security Council issued a Resolution in which it ordered the "Israeli" forces to pullout of South Lebanon. So far, this international law hasnt been carried out. At the same time, "Israel" still violates a sovereignty of a free, independent and internationally recognized stated as one of United Nations founders.

How can the Resolution 425 be executed while United States of America prevents any international movements to oblige "Israel" to abide by the international resolutions.

So, fighting against an occupied force which destroyed the economical, agricultural and residential infrastructure of the Southern Lebanon, and consequently Lebanons role in the region, was inevitable.

"Israels" constant obstinacy and arrogance sparked a new war between the "Israeli" forces and Lebanese nationals from all sects, trends and classes. This war resulted in hundreds of "Israeli" soldiers who either killed or wounded and convoys of destroyed tanks, troop carriers, guns and armoured vehicles.

It is something confusing, ironical and paradoxical that Rami Ben Dour had entered into Lebanon as an aggressor between 1978 and 1982 without any feeling of qualm. But today after their son had grown enough to serve in Lebanon within the "Israeli" forces, he and his wife Rachel are living in fear and anxious.

At a time where more than 900 "Israeli" soldiers have been killed since 1978 Litani Operation, Rachel Ben Dour established a women association its main purpose is to achieve an unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon. "The "Israeli" government thinks that it does not matter for soldiers to be killed. This is their national duty. Also, it thinks that I have to send my son as a pawn in chess", she said.

"The Security Zone in south Lebanon has become a fatal ambush for the "Israeli" soldiers as a result of the daily attacks launched by Hizbullahs fighters accusing the "Israeli" officials by following up a bad policy" , she added.

Rachel concluded by saying: "Israels policy-makers are trying to evade critical explanation over how a tremendous number of soldiers killed fruitlessly in the past years".

Youssi Bilin , an M.K. and member of Labor Party, called for a unilateral retreat from Lebanon and said: "The danger that may emerge as a result of leaving Lebanon will be much less than of staying in it". Also, he called for implementing the Resolution 425 and criticized Netanyahu by saying: "Netanyahus conditions of carrying out the Resolution change its core", knowing that the Resolution 425 focused on an unconditional withdrawal of the "Israeli" forces. The Resolution did not call for any kind of negotiations or security arrangements.

The Security Council is the only authority that can amend this international Resolution. So, any "Israeli" proposal to negotiate over the Resolution is not but a fruitless maneuver. This interpretation coincides with Bilins view which came after great losses sustained by the "Israeli" forces as a result of the Islamic Resistance strikes.
