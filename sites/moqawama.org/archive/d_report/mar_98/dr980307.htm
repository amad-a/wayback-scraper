Daily Report
 

Saturday March 7-1998:

Labor Party Chairman Ehud Baraks  statements raised sharp critiques and resentment among the "Israeli" circles. He said: "If he were a Palestinian of the appropriate age, he would probably join a terrorist organization".

Barack made this remark on the cable television program "Pgisha Ishit" (Personal Encounter).
Also, he told Haaretz (March, 5-1998) that his statement was not a gaffe. "What else could I say? That if I were a young Palestinian immersed from birth in the Palestinian ethos, Id become a third grade teacher?".

Doubtless, these statements legitimize the Palestinian armed struggle against the "Israeli" entity. So, he came under a sharp attack by both "Israeli" right-wing and left-wing.

A Likud spokeswoman said that Barak had gone too far, and that he is no longer a fitting representative of a Zionist political party in the State of "Israel" .

A senior official of Labor Party said that Barak "talked sincerely but unwisely" .
Minister of Agriculture Itan told Yediot Ahronot, an "Israeli" newspaper: "In this case, Barak is legitimizing the Palestinian to kill the Jew"  .

Forgetting that he was the head of the extremist underground Lavy  Movement which fought the Palestinian forces and British rule in Palestine and that he had detonated David Hotel in Quds for the purpose of killing United Nations Sweden delegate Count Bernadot, Yitzhak Shamir, the former "Israeli" Premier, noticed that Baraks statements are considered as "a propaganda to join terrorist organizations".

Barak, in his turn, clarified the difference between the deeds and words noticing, in a personal interview, that Netanyahus policy towards peace process on the Palestinian track enhances the terrorist organizations much more than my remarks .

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The "Israeli" troops shelled Haddatha village and its perimeters causing damages to the house of the citizen Abdel-Karim Sabra. Also, the "Israeli" shelling extended to include Slouqi, Hojeir and Qalaoeuih  valleys, the eastern quarter of Froun and the perimeters of Haris, Aita El-Jabal  and Ghandourieh, whereas two helicopters hovered intensely overhead.

Besides, the Islamic Resistance statement  reported the following:
7:55, Sojod mountain subjected to two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
14:10, Aqmata plain came under four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
14:25, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Aqmata plain.
14:30, the perimeters of Haddatha subjected to four 155mm missiles emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal, whereas Arid El-Zennar area came under three 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
14:35, Beit Yahoun hit Abou Shbib Sabras house, Haddatha village, with one 81mm shell, but no casualties were reported.
16:00, the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost subjected to two 105mm tank rounds, whereas Bir Kallab position hit Arid El-Zennar area with three 81mm shells.
19:20, Kaissieh valley was hit by several 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area.
20:00, Roum post fired six 120mm shells, a twelve 105mm missiles, twenty one 120 and 81mm flare shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire, Mag and Makler rounds at the surroundings of Meshnaqa position.
21:00, Beit Yahoun combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Islamic Resistance Operations:

 In Central Sector of South Lebanon , an "Israeli" soldier was wounded as a result of the Islamic Resistance attacks on the position of "Israeli" troops and their collaborators .
However, the Islamic Resistance statement  recorded the following military operations:
