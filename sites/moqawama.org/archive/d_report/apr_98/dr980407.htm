Daily Report
 
 
April Tuesday 7-1998:

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli"-occupied post of Qantara bombarded at dawn Kaissieh Valley and the outskirts of Froun, Ghandourieh, Qalaouieh and Sawaneh villages. Meanwhile, the "Israeli" occupation forces combed Hojeir Valley and the nearby valleys with heavy machine gun fire.

Also, the villages of Qabrikha, Majdal Selem, Baraasheit and Sawaneh came under several shells. In Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" shelling extended to involve the perimeters of Berkat Jabbour and forest of Abou Rashed.

On the other hand, the Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it mentioned the following
