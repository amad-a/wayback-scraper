Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 30-1998:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions (1):
The "Israeli" gunners shelled at 5:00 Mazraat Ahmadieh and Zahr Mountain, while at 10:00, the "Israeli" shelling spread to include Qatraneh heights and the peripheries of Sraireh area. This was accompanied by firing heavy machine gun fire at the perimeters of Qilya and Dallafeh areas.

In Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" shells targeted the heights of Moqawama, Rihayat, Mashghara and Ain Tineh and the surroundings of Ain Amin area.

In its statement, the Islamic Resistance (2) recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions:
6:40, Zaffateh position unleashed four 155mm shells at Safi Mountain.
8:10, the "Israeli" choppers and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights, while an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Nabatieh region.
9:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Qlayleh and Maallieh villages.
9:40, an M.K. reconnaissance plane flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah areas.
11:00, the "Israeli" fighters and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights, whereas the warplanes only flew over Beqaa region.
11:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Shaqra and Khorbet Selem villages.
11:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Nabatieh region.
11:35, an "Israeli" warboat was seen cruising off the seashore of Adloun, Sarafand and Insarieh villages.
17:15, Baraasheit outpost fired six 81mm shells at the houses of Baraasheit village, but no casualties were reported.
17:35, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over Qana and its vicinity.
21:55, Houla position shelled Slouqi Valley with six 120mm missiles.
20:40, the helicopters hovered over the area of Qsair village and Mothallath position.
22:40, the surroundings of Sojod outpost came under eight 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
22:45, Beit Yahoun position showered the peripheries and woods of Aita El-Jabal with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire, while Baraasheit outpost raked the perimeters of Baraasheit village with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. In the meantime, three 120mm shells fell on Rafih Mountain.
22:50, a drone reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Haris village. Meanwhile, Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Islamic Resistance Operations (3):
The Islamic Resistance announced that at 17:20, the group of both martyrs Rameh Mahdi and Ali Assaad attacked a Lahdi force as it was setting up fortification works at Baraasheit outpost with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits.

"Israel's" Maneuver:
While the "Israeli" aggressions followed up on South Lebanon, the diplomatic movement of European Community via its special delegate Miguel Moratinos still lacks the effectiveness as the Lebanese Foreign Affairs Minister Fares Boueiz pointed out (4). He made it clear that the European delegate "had came here as a listener questioning about the possibilities of certain solution and hoping that he will explore the possibility of achieving a specific future work but this is not definite".

Boueiz added that Moratinos is trying to make the discussions over finding solutions under way although he hasn't real and tangible capacities empowered by the European countries.

In this regard, Lebanese military sources (5) denied the press information which talked about travelling a Lebanese-Syrian common military delegation in response to an American invitation with the aim of discussing the arrangements of the "Israeli" withdrawal from South Lebanon.

For his part, the Secretary General of Francophone Organization Dr. Butros Butros Ghali reiterated his attitude over the Resolution 425.

"In my capacity as a former Secretary General for United Nations, I welcome "Israel's" retreat, but it should be unconditional for the Resolution is clear and needs no further interpretations or amendments" he said (6).
