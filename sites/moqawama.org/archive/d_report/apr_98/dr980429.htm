Daily Report
 
 
April Wednesday 29-1998:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" batteries stationed at Soweida outpost shelled in the morning the road of Habboush-Arab Salim (1). It is worth noting that this road has been shelled for about two weeks.

Also, the "Israeli" gunners (2) targeted the outskirts of Mansouri and Zibqeen villages with 120mm shells causing damage to a house in Mansouri.

In the meantime, it was reported that the "Israeli" occupation forces (3) had deployed several tanks and armored vehicles at the positions of Kawkaba, Rihan, Zaghleh, Ain Qinya and Sojod. Also, the "Israeli" troops intensified the observation works and surveillance patrols along the front line and inside the occupied region.

However, the Islamic Resistance issued the following statement (4):
24:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Borj Shimali, Bazourieh and the neighboring areas.
1:25, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Jbeil region.
5:45, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over the areas of East Saida.
5:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
6:40, three 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Safi Mountain.
6:45, Safi Mountain exposed to two 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
7:05, one 120mm shell emanating from Rihan area struck Safi Mountain.
9:40, the "Israeli" choppers hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
10:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah and Nabatieh regions.
11:35, a reconnaissance plane flew low over Zibqeen and Jibal El-Botom villages.
11:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Khorbet Selem.
12:40, the "Israeli" fighters and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Nabatieh and Central Sector, whereas the warplanes flew over Ghandourieh, Froun and Qalaouieh villages. At the same time, the "Israeli" jets and M.K. reconnaissance flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
12:50, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa areas.
12:55, the jet airplanes flew, at middle height, in the airspace of Beirut.

13:15, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Aqmata plains, while Bir Kallab outpost showered its perimeters with heavy machine gun fire. At the same time, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
16:30, the reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Western Sector of Southern Lebanon.
19:45, Hardoun outpost bombarded Izzieh valley and the peripheries of Majdal Zoun with twelve 120mm shells.
20:00, Zohaira position fired one 155mm cluster missile over Majdal Zoun village.
20:15, the perimeters of Jibaa village came under several 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.

Ghali's Visit
The Secretary General of Phrancophone Organization Dr. Butros Butros Ghali paid a visit for the cemetery of victims of Qana massacre in South Lebanon where he placed a wreath and stood silently for one minute as an honor for the souls of victims-martyrs. "I would like to consider me as one of Qana's sons. I really belong to it in spirit. I am sticking together with it and with the whole Lebanese people for I trust with you. We are doing our best with Lebanon for 'Israel' to withdraw from its land in accordance with the Resolution 425", he said.

Resistance Results:
19:20, the Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Ali Farhat and Mohammad Moqallid targeted Tair Harfa outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct hits where the columns of smoke were seen emanating from it.

"Israel's" Army disclosed (5) that the name of the lieutenant who wounded as a result of the attack launched by the Islamic Resistance fighters on Monday 27-1998 is Sal Lanzman and that he was put in the intensive care room at Rambam Hospital of Haifa. It is note worthy that five soldiers others were injured in the same operation, three of them their cases were described as serious.

For his part, Amnon Shahak, "Israel's" Chief of Staff, tried to raise the morale of his soldiers by saying (6): "There is a difference between the competence of the "Israeli" soldiers and the combatant performance of Hizbullah fighters during the confrontations which occur in South Lebanon, claiming that "Israel's Army is making observation works much better than Hizbullah" (7).

The Resolution 425
While the ambassadors of Pakistan (8) and Coba (9) to Lebanon supported the implementation of the Resolution 425, Arab familiar diplomatic sources in Washington told As-Safir Lebanese newspaper that the meeting between UN Secretary General Kofi Annan and US Coordinator Denis Ross concluded to an agreement that "Israel's" offer over the Resolution 425 "should remain movable, in public and under control". The aim is that any party should not be put in a critical situation until reactivating the Syrian and Palestinian tracks.

The sources made it clear that Ross had put Annan in the climate "what is possible and impossible" concerning the Resolution 425. "So, we don't expect any surprise from Annan", the sources ended.
This means that "Israel's" offer about the Resolution 425 and the withdrawal from Lebanon will be put in a course much bigger than what had been circulated until now. It is obvious that it is connected to the settlement administrated by the United States of America in the region.

Therefore, the issue is not that "Israel" wants to abide by the international resolutions but the matter is how to exploit these resolutions it will achieve private objects.
