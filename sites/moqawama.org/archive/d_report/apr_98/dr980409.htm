Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 9-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" Army pursued violating the Lebanese sovereignty by launching further aggressions on South Lebanon. These aggressions had been recorded in a statement issued by Islamic Resistance :
00:15, the "Israeli" fighters flew over Qana area and Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights where they were driven off by the Lebanese Army anti-aircraft guns.
11:45, the jet airplanes flew in the airspace of Nabatieh and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions.
12:10, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa areas.
14:50, the "Israeli" helicopters and M.K. reconnaissance planes flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
15:30, the helicopters hovered over Taibeh and Markaba villages.
15:40, two helicopters soared, at middle altitude, over Houla area.
16:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Yater, Siddiqeen and Kafra villages.
18:00, two helicopters flew over Qantara villages.
18:55, two helicopters soared, at middle height, over Salhani area.
19:00, two helicopters soared, at high altitude, over Blat, Hemayed and Salhani areas.
23:15, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft flew over Kafra and the neighboring areas.
23:30, the warplanes flew, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Tyre city and its suburbs.
23:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Kfar Seir and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr villages.

The Resolution 425
The Lebanese President Elias Hrawi  asserted that Lebanon will not accept any amendments concerning the resolutions 425 and 426.

Meanwhile, Arab League's Secretary General Osmat Abdel-Majid  reassured that the Resolution 425 is in no need to "any interpretation, explanation or amendment" for it, clearly and frankly, calls for a complete retreat from Lebanon's territories. Abdel-Majid also regarded "the 'Israeli' efforts in this aspect as an attempt to thwart a real implementation of the concerned Resolution".

Martyr :
The Islamic Resistance announced proudly the martyrdom of militant Hassan Hassan Ghamloush, born in 1979 at Shaqra village. He had joined the Islamic Resistance ranks in 1996 and subjected to several cultural and military courses. Ghamloush was inflicted on April 3-1998 as he was carrying out his Islamic and sacred duties. Yesterday, he martyrized of his injuries.
