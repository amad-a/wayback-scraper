Daily Report
 
 
April Sunday 26-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" artillery shelled roughly at dawn the villages of Yater, Mansouri, Zibqeen and Biyout Siyad (1). This was accompanied by firing heavy machine gun rounds.

In Nabatieh region, the outskirts of Choukine, Maifadoun and Mazraat Kfar Dajal came at 12:50 under a number of missiles, while in the morning the road between Habboush and Arab Salim villages exposed to another barrage of missiles.

It should be noted that it is the fourteenth time within two weeks this road exposes to the "Israeli" shelling. The population of Arab Salim in particular and Iqleem Al-Tuffah in general expressed their fears over the possibility of cutting this road and paralyzing the traffic on it.

They issued a statement in which it called on the international and humanitarian organizations to interfere with the aim of preventing "Israel" from pursuing its aggressive actions against them. Also, they demanded the Lebanese government to raise their issue before the competent and concerned parties.

At about 4:30pm, the "Israeli" batteries stationed at Abbad and Taibeh Project posts shelled Hojeir Valley, Litani River and the surroundings of Froun village.

The "Israeli" occupation forces shelled also Zahr Mountain and the surroundings of Qilya village after they had combed before noon the surroundings of Berkat Shebaa.

In this regard, the Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it reported the following "Israeli" Aggressions (2):
5:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
6:40, Safi Mountain was bombarded by seven 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan village.
6:45, Zohaira position shelled Jib Soweid area with nine 155mm missiles.
6:50, Zohaira position bombarded west of Yater with nine 155mm missiles and the peripheries of Majdal Zoun with eleven others. Meanwhile, the choppers hovered over Blat and Chihine areas.
7:00, the helicopters soared, at middle and low altitudes, over Yater where they dropped anti-missile flares.
7:05, the perimeters of Loweizeh village exposed to seven shells emanating from Rihan area. At the same time, two helicopters soared, at middle height, over Mariamine area.
7:10, Aissieh Valley was bombarded by ten 120mm shells emanating from Mashaaroun position.
9:10, the reconnaissance planes flew low over Aiteit village.
11:05, the "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of Nabatieh region and staged mock air strikes over Bent Jbeil.
11:30, the warplanes flew, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Beirut.
11:35, the jet airplanes were still flying in the airspace of Bent Jbeil region.
12:50, Shraifeh position unleashed four 155mm missiles at the road between Zawtar and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr villages.
16:15, Mothallath position combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
17:05, Shraifeh position bombarded Litani River bands with twelve 155mm missiles.
17:15, Shraifeh position fired ten 155mm missiles at the perimeters of Zawtar Sharqieh and twenty others at Litani River bands, when on the other hand, Salaah and Mothallath posts targeted Litani River and Raj area with twelve 81 and 120mm shells.
18:30, the "Israeli" positions at Mashaaroun and Meis El-Jabal bombarded the perimeters of Majdal Selem, Touline, Sawaneh and Tamerieh villages as well as Kaissieh Valley with fifteen 120 and 155mm missiles. The shrapnels of one of these missiles struck the house of the citizen Mahmoud Ibrahim Awali at Tamerieh village.
18:45, the perimeters of Majdal Selem, Sawaneh and Tamerieh villages came under several 120mm shells emanating from Mashaaroun post and a number of 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area. Several missiles fell between the houses of Tamerieh village.
19:55, Zohaira position targeted west of Yater with six 155mm missiles.
20:15, Jib Soweid area exposed to six 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
On the level of the military operations, the Islamic Resistance recorded the following attacks:
6:15, the group of two martyrs Mustafa Abdel-Karim and Hussein Karout targeted the "Israeli"-occupied Blat post with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.
6:25, the group of both martyrs Hassan Balhas and Hussein Naim attacked Radar position with the adequate arms chalking up direct hits.
16:45, the Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force inside Mothallath position. Immediately afterwards, the group of two martyrs Issa Shouman and Hussein Wehbi attacked it the appropriate arms scoring direct hits.
16:50, the Islamic Resistance fighters observed an enemy force near Salaah position. Shortly afterwards, the group of both martyrs Suleiman Awada and Abdel-Karim Ayoub fell upon it with the proper arms chalking up direct goals.

"Israeli" Terrorism (4):
On the sixteenth anniversary of killing the Irish soldier Peter Port who was serving in the ranks of UN Irish Battalion operating in Lebanon, his family paid visit to Tibbnine village and put a wreath on his monument. It is worth noting that Port was killed in 1982 with two of his comrades at a checkpoint set up at village of Tibbnine.

It should be noted that the "Israeli" Army which is characterized by an expansionist settlement doctrine had used to launch aggressions on Unifils operating on South Lebanon. One of the most remarkable of these aggressions was the bombarding of Fijian Battalion Headquarters at Qana village which resulted in killing 109 civilians. The real object that stands behind the "Israeli" aggressions perpetrated against the Unifil emerges from the fact that "Israel" had always rejected the resolutions of United Nations. David Ben Gourion, "Israel's" first Prime Minister, was always avoiding naming it and instead he was referring to it by saying "that thing".

"Israeli" Retreat:
Butros Butros Ghali, the current Secretary General of Francophones Organization and the former UN Secretary General, said: "There is no reason to amend the Resolution 425 which should be implemented unconditionally" (5).

In the meantime, the Syrian-Iranian Supreme Board issued a common statement in which it considered the recent "Israeli" proposal over a conditional pullout from South Lebanon is a ploy aiming at the misleading of the international public opinion, making commotion inside Lebanon and harming the common destiny of Syria and Lebanon.

The two parties assured their supporting for Lebanon's stance concerning an unconditional and prompt withdrawal from the Lebanese territories. Also, they called on the Security Council and the UN Secretary General to do everything for the purpose of ending the "Israeli" occupation of all Arab and Muslim territories including the sacred Quds for the peace and stability to be achieved in the region (6).

Furthermore, both sides expressed their firm support for the Islamic and National Resistance regarding its struggle against the occupation forces as a legitimate right ensured by the principles of International Law, UN Charter and the Islamic Resolutions.

As to the domestic attitudes, MP Mohammad Fneish, a member of Loyalty to the Islamic Resistance Bloc, maintained: "The 'Israeli' offer pertaining to the withdrawal from South Lebanon is serious for it is a result of the predicament suffered by the resistance actions and not by reconsidering its methods over dealing with the international resolutions or human rights organizations. The "Israeli" enemy must not be rewarded for its withdrawal", he said.

Fneish concluded: "Any 'Israeli' withdrawal doesn't revoke our right to resist and continue the attacks as long as there is one inch lays under the enemy control".
