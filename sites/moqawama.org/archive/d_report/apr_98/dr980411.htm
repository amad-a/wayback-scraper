Daily Report
 
 
April Saturday 11-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions :
According to a statement issued by the Islamic Resistance, the "Israeli" aggressions are as follows:
5:45, four 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan fell on Aqmata plain. Also, Sojod and Bir Kallab outposts combed their surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
13:30, Zaffateh position unleashed five 155mm missiles at Aqmata plain.
13:50, the perimeters of Loweizeh village exposed to five 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh post.
18:10, Haddatha outpost raked its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
19:25, Mothallath position showered its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
19:40, Mothallath position fired heavy machine gun fire at the civilian cars travelling on the road between Froun and Ghandourieh villages.
22:00, three "Israeli" helicopters hovered, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Zahrani and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions.

Islamic Resistance Operations :
At 12:15, the Islamic Resistance militants observed a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was about coming into Bir Kallab outpost. Shortly afterwards, the group of two martyrs Mohammad Khalil and Ibrahim Dakdouk rushed upon it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
