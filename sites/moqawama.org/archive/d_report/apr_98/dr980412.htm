Daily Report
 
 
April Sunday 12-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions :
The "Israeli" Army escalated military the situation in South Lebanon when its fighters staged over two hours commencing from 9:00 am six sorties.

The warplanes fired sixteen air-to-ground rockets at the deserted Sojod's Husseinieh and its surroundings. The anti-aircraft guns of Lebanese Army and Islamic Resistance deployed in the area there fired at the "Israeli" fighters.

However, the Islamic Resistance recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions :
6:25, Sojod outpost fired hand grenades and 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings. Meanwhile, three 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan fell on Aqmata plain.
9:00, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Nabatieh and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions.
9:15, 9:20 and 9:25, the "Israeli" fighters raided on Sojod's Husseinieh building.
9:30, Toumat Niha outpost raked the perimeters of Mashghara and Ain Tineh with several shells and a barrage of heavy machine gun fire.
9:35, 9:50 and 9:51, the "Israeli" jets raided on Sojod's Husseinieh building.
12:30, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle height, over Mazraat Meshref, Siddiqeen, Jibal El-Botom and the nearby areas.
13:20, the "Israeli" jets and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Northern Beqaa areas.
15:30, seven 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area fell on Aissieh Valley, whereas Mohaibib outpost hit the perimeters of Froun and Ghandourieh villages with three 155mm shells.
15:40, Aissieh Valley exposed to five 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area.
15:50, the "Israeli" helicopters hovered over Markaba and Houla villages. In the meantime, Slouqi Valley came under ten 155mm shells emanating from Meis El-Jabal area.
16:05, about thirty 155mm missiles emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal fell on the area between Touline and Ghandourieh villages and the perimeters of Khorbet Selem and Sawaneh, whereas Taibeh position fired a lot of 120mm shells at Slouqi Valley. At the same time, the "Israeli" helicopters hovered overhead.
16:50, two helicopters soared over Jibal El-Botom area.
17:05, several 120mm shells struck Slouqi Valley, whereas the helicopters flew, at middle height, over Jibal El-Botom area.
17:50, Bayada outpost targeted Majdal Zoun with two shells wounding the civilian Hussein Ali Merhi. Meanwhile, Zohaira post shelled a number of 155mm missiles at Jib Soweid area.
22:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Majdal Selem and Slouqi valley.
22:10, Tair Harfa fired several 81mm flare shells overhead. In the meantime, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah, Nabatieh and the nearby areas.
22:35, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft flew low over Zahrani River.
23:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Harouf and Toul areas.
22:30, Baraasheit outpost unleashed a number of 81mm flash shells over the area between Baraasheit and Shaqra villages.
23:45, the "Israeli" jets and helicopters flew, at middle altitude, over Tyre city.
23:55, Dabsheh outpost fired 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire at the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh. Meanwhile, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared overhead.

Islamic Resistance Operations :
9:30, the group of martyr Adel Al-Mousawi targeted a half-track on the road leading to Toumat Niha outpost with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct hits.
17:40, the Islamic Resistance men observed an "Israeli" force near Bani Hayan area. Then, the group of martyr Ziad Zahwi targeted it with the proper arms scoring direct and certain hits.
