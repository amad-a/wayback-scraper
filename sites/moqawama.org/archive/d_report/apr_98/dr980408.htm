Daily Report
 
 
April Wednesday 8-1998:
 

The Lebanese people hadn't enjoyed the holiday of Greater Bairam for the "Israeli" occupation forces pursued their aggressions on the villages of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa which are adjacent to the greenline.

Meanwhile, "Israel's" Minister of Defense went on marketing his proposal of withdrawing from South Lebanon describing, at the same time, he who talks about a unilateral retreat as being bluffed.

"Israeli" Aggressions :
The "Israeli" troops bombarded in the morning the woodlands of Qatraneh, perimeters of Sraireh and Litani River with several 155mm missiles.

The "Israeli" batteries shelled at night Mazraat El-Ahmadieh and the surroundings of Dallafeh.
In Western Beqaa, the heights and forests of Mashghara in addition to the area of Mashghara Vocational School came under a number of shells.

Monitoring Committee's Statement :
Over 32 continued hours, Five-Nation Monitoring Committee, which oversees the implementation of April 1996 Understanding, looked into six complaints presented equally by Lebanon and "Israel".

Monitoring Committee condemned the "Israeli" forces and their collaborators for killing the citizen Assaad Hattab considering this act as a breach for the Understanding. Also, it called on the collaborators to take the necessary precautions so as such incidents not to be occurred again.
Moreover, the Committee blamed "Israel" for bombarding two houses at Baraasheit and urged it to respect the provisions of the Understanding for "under no circumstances the civilians and their properties shall be targets".

Also, it denounced killing the six civilians on the road of Kawkaba demanding "Israel" not to use civilians at military positions for they might be military targets.

Mordechai's visit to South Lebanon :
In a clear violation for Lebanon's sovereignty, "Israel's" Minister of Defense Yitzhak Mordechai made a tour in the occupied region of South Lebanon. He reiterated during the tour his saying: " 'Israel' is still undertaking to ensure the security and safety of its northern region" thus disregarding, the problem of who will ensure the security of the "Israeli"-occupied region of Southern Lebanon upon which "Israel" continues in exercising all various kinds of human rights violations and in infringing Lebanon's sovereignty.

"Israel's" alleged retreat:
While the Islamic Resistance militants are still confronting the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators in South Lebanon, the Lebanese government followed up its efforts in refuting "Israel's" maneuver pertaining to its conditional acceptance of the Resolution 425.

For his part, Lebanese Prime Minister Rafik Hariri  rejected the "Israeli" offer by saying: "After it had been refused the Resolution 425 for a long time, "Israel" had given recently a conditional approval discharging it from its substantial contents. So, if we sat to negotiate the "Israeli" preconditions, we would enter into a bargaining process over the size of security arrangements intended to be offered by Lebanon to "Israel". To sit on the negotiations table this means that Lebanon had lost his cause in an unconditional implementation of the Resolution 425. I think that all the world including Americans and French share us this point of view".

In this regard, Head of French Senate delegation Senator Adrian Gottern , who visited Patriarck Sfeir, declared: "France had always upheld an unconditional implementation of Resolution 425".

Amnesty Law for Lahd's members :
The Lebanese newspapers took an interest in the amnesty draft law submitted previously by the Loyalty for Islamic Resistance Bloc in Lebanese Parliament.

Sources of Bloc made it clear that the draft law is related to the members but not officers of Lahd's Militia in the event of surrendering themselves within a period of three months from publishing it in the Official Journal. The main purpose of this law is to settle in legal manner the cases of those who had been press-ganged with the "Israelis".

The sources maintained the importance of the draft law for it doesn't deal discretionally with those who will react positively. So, the law permits, rarely happened in the contemporary history of occupations, the Lahdi members to confirm their nationality through getting out of the circle of collaborating with the "Israeli" enemy. Also, the proposal aims, at this very stage, to embarrass and weaken the "Israeli" enemy's movements in order to compel it to retreat with as much as possible losses and as less as possible preconditions and earnings.

It is worth mentioning that the draft law is not something new for it had been submitted to the Lebanese Speaker several months ago.

So, highlighting the draft law might be attributed, in these days, to what the "Israeli" offer had raised over a conditional implementation for the Resolution 425 especially the point related to the situation of the "Israeli" agents.

It is worth noting that any party in the world, either in the past or present days, cannot accept to reward a general amnesty for the collaborators. For instance, the Palestinian Authority had refused to receive them in the area under its control enforcing the "Israelis" to evacuate them to special areas under its control where they were granted the "Israeli" nationality.
