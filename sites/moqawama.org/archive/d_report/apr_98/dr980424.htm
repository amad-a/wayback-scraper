Daily Report
 
 
April Friday 24-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
"Israel's" Army pursued its aggressions on South Lebanon. The reporters (1) told that the "Israeli" tanks bombarded at dawn Zahr Mountain and Ahmadieh Hill. This was accompanied by firing heavy machine gun rounds at the peripheries of Qilya and Zillaya villages.

Also, the "Israeli" batteries shelled in the morning the heights of Jabbour, Rihayat and Maramel, Abou Rashed woods and the surroundings of Jabbour pool.

In Tyre, the "Israeli" gunners targeted at dawn the outskirts of Shaqra and Baraasheit in addition to Zaatar Mountain. At 10:15am, the "Israeli" shelling, accompanied by heavy machine gun fire, spread to involve the villages of Qabrikha and Shaqra and valleys of Talloussa and Slouqi.

However, the Islamic Resistance statement recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions:
24:50, one "Israeli" chopper hovered over Naqoura and headed for north, whereas an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over the villages of Tyre District and its vicinity.
6:35, Sojod village exposed to three 81mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan village.
6:40, Zohaira position bombarded seven 155mm missiles at Jib Soweid area.
6:50, two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Safi Mountain.
7:00, Safi Mountain came under two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
7:10, Zohaira position targeted Jib Soweid area with three 155mm missiles.
8:35, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Zibqeen and the nearby areas.
10:00, five 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Sojod village.
10:30, Shraifeh position targeted Litani River with one 155mm missile.
10:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over Kfar Seir, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Litani River. Meanwhile, Shraifeh position shelled Litani River bands with three 155mm missiles.
11:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Zrarieh area. It is be pointed out that this aircraft had been hovered overhead since the night before.
11:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle height, over Qabrikha, Majdal Selem, Shaqra and Aissieh valley area.
12:00, the choppers hovered over the positions of Qalaah and Borj areas. In the meantime, Shraifeh position targeted Litani River bands with three missiles.
12:15, the helicopters hovered, at middle height, over Houla, Markaba and Meis El-Jabal villages when on the other hand the "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
12:35, two choppers soared, at middle altitude, over Blat and Ramieh areas.
16:30, Aqmata area came under four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
16:40, the helicopters soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
16:55, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
18:45, the "Israeli" jets flew in the airspace of Bent Jbeil region.
20:05, Zohaira position targeted west of Yater village with three 155mm missiles.
20:20, Jib Soweid area exposed to two 155mm shells emanating from Zohaira position.

Monitoring Committee (3):
The Five-Nation Monitoring Committee which oversees the implementing of April 1996 Understanding hold in its 24-4-1998 meeting, the "Israeli" occupation forces the responsibility for wounding a Lebanese civilian at Nabatieh El-Fawqa and for hitting several houses at Shaqra village few days ago.

Islamic Resistance Operations (4):
5:30, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Mohammad Fouani attacked a Lahdi force to the south of Houla village with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.
10:25, the group of martyr Fadi Melhem targeted once again the Lahdi force south of Houla village chalking up direct hits.
18:50, The Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Zoheir and Mohammad Hazimeh attacked Blat position with the proper weapons scoring direct hits. Also, the columns of smoke were seen emanating from the abovementioned position.
