Daily Report
 
 
April Saturday 25-1998:
 

Siege of Taibeh:
The news reported that the "Israeli" troops had beleaguered Taibeh village as an attempt to take revenge on population's rejection of establishing a civil administration apparatus in their village.

The "Israeli" soldiers set up checkpoints on its roads preventing the residents from coming into or out of the village. Also, the medical materials and food substances were forbidden.

The population called on the humanitarian and international organizations to place pressures on the occupation forces to stop their aggressive actions.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
Once again, the "Israeli" soldiers breached April 1996 Understanding 24 hours before the five-Nation Monitoring Committee had called "Israel" for excluding the civilians in South Lebanon from its military actions.

The "Israeli" gunners shelled the internal quarters of Kafra village wounding the children Nassim Ali Hijazi, 12, and Abbas Ahmad Hamdan, 13, as they were playing at the playground of the village near Nipal's Battalion Headquarters. The two injured children were transported to Tibbnine Hospital and Najem Hospital.

The "Israeli" shells emanating from Blat post at 5:00 pm, ignited also several blazes at Kafra's groves.

On the other hand, the Islamic Resistance recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions:
7:00, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Safi Mountain.
7:30, three 120mm shells fell on Aqmata plain and two others struck Safi Mountain.
10:00, the warplanes flew in the airspace of northern Beqaa areas.
14:10, Zaghleh position fired six 160mm missiles at Darazi Fort and Zellaya area.
14:30, Shraifeh position bombarded Litani River bands with ten 155mm missiles.
16:00, the area between Kafra and Haris villages came under five 120mm missiles emanating from Hemayed position.
16:05, Zohaira position unleashed four 155mm shells at Kafra village inflicting the boy Nassim Ali Hijazi, 12, in his shoulder and causing damage to the houses of Amin Hijazi and Sanieh Hijazi.
17:25, the outskirts of Yater village exposed to two 120mm shells emanating from Blat post.
18:20, Mashaaroun position targeted Aissieh Valley with several 120mm shells including a smoke one fell on a house at Sawaneh village.
1:35, the jet airplanes flew in the airspace of Northern Beqaa areas.
20:15, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the peripheries of Shaqra village.
20:20, Houla old post fired four 81mm flare shells overhead and one over Tabel Valley to the south. Also, the same post combed Tabel Valley with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
20:40, five 155mm missiles emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal struck the west of Shaqra village.
20:55, the perimeters of Majdal Selem exposed to three 155mm shells emanating from Meis El-Jabal area.
21:00, Houla new position fired one 81mm flash shell over Houla old position.

Islamic Resistance Operations (4):
13:45, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Mohammad Al-Youssef targeted Ahmadieh position with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
15:45, the Islamic Resistance militants observed enemy forces inside and around Rshaf position. Then, the group of two martyrs Khalil Kamal and Nidal El-Hoq targeted them with the adequate weapons chalking up direct and definite hits.
18:20, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Khalil Olayan attacked Qabaa position, the "Israeli"-Lahdi 17th Brigade Headquarters, with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
20:00, the Islamic Resistance men observed a Zionist force near Mashaaroun position. Immediately afterwards, the group of martyr Ali Moussa Tohmeh targeted it with the appropriate arms chalking up direct hits.
