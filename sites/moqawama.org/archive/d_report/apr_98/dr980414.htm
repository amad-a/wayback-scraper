Daily Report
 
 
April Tuesday 14-1998:
 

Lebanese detainees:
The "Israeli" army is to challenge Monday's court decision to acquit a Lebanese man arrested two years ago and charged with firing rockets into "Israel".

Ali Ahmad Banjak, from Tyre, had been accused of helping launch Katyusha rockets across the border during "Israel's" Grapes of Wrath offensive in April 1996. The charge was thrown out due to lack of evidence.

But a spokesman for the "Israeli" army said: "The army prosecutor will file an appeal to arrest Ali Ahmad Banjak".

The court had ordered Banjak deported back to Lebanon within two days, except in the case of an appeal. A military court outside Tel Aviv is expected to hear the challenge on Wednesday.
Banjak, 21, said he was "astounded at the acquittal" in the light of the heavy sentences usually imposed on Lebanese taken to "Israel".

"Israel" has admitted to holding a group of Lebanese detainees without trial to use as bargaining chips for the return of the remains of "Israeli" troops who were killed in the south - a practice approved by "Israel's" Supreme Court.

The decision drew condemnation from Amnesty International, which says that at least 150 other Lebanese nationals are being held in the South Lebanon Army detention centre at Khiam, in the occupied zone (1).

Yesterday, Amnesty announced the release of one detainee on April 7, Huda Hamada, but expressed grace concern over the maltreatment of four others.

Khalil Karnib, 26, and his brother Suleiman, 23, who were transferred to Marjeyoun hospital about a fortnight ago, are both thought to be suffering from torture-inflicted illnesses.

"Khalil is reportedly suffering from concussion and bleeding inside his skull, and it is understood he has also become epileptic since his detention", the report by the human rights group said (2).

"Suleiman is said to be very ill with a liver disease and thalassaemia (a blood disorder)".

The group also named Ghassan Ali Issa, 20, and Haydar Ali Issa as being held without trial since July 1997, and appealed for them to be treated according to international standards.

"Detainees at Khiam have been held incommunicado since September 1997 when family members and delegates of the International Committee of the Red Cross were denied access", it said, urging their "unconditional release if they are not to be charged with criminal offences and tried according to international standards". - with agencies (3).

Islamic Resistance Operations (7):
The statement issued by the Islamic Resistance mentioned that at 16:00, a group of two martyrs Hassan Balhas and Hassan Ahmad attacked Blat post with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits. Also, the columns of smoke were seen emanating from inside it.

However, security sources (8) mentioned that two "Israeli" soldiers were wounded, one in serious case, during a training on armored vehicles and ammunition at an area near to the border of Lebanon and Palestine.

It is note worthy that 'Israeli" and French Jewish press sources had reportedly asserted that the "Israeli" Army always conceals the real number of its casualties in South Lebanon to be attributed then to traffic and training accidents.

Memory of Qana Massacre:
On the second anniversary of April 1996 Aggression and Qana Massacre committed by the "Israeli" enemy, the Follow-up Committee of Lebanese Parties (9) issued a statement in which it reported "The enemy is still pursuing his military aggression against our people in South and Western Beqaa. Recently, it had launched a maneuver in order to create troubles among the Lebanese society. But this maneuver was met, as it was happened in April 1996 Aggression, by a unity of Lebanon's government and people and by supporting the brave resistance which as it managed to drop down what so-called Operation Grapes of Wrath, it will knock down also the "Israeli" attempts aiming at shaking the domestic peace and creating an new internal commotion.

For his part and commenting on "Israel's" offer pertaining to fulfilling the Resolution 425, the Lebanese Foreign Affairs Minister Fares Boueiz (10) said: "Concerning the recent 'Israel's' proposals, we would like to emphasize that 'Israel' is in no position to change the nature of Resolution 425 and 426 or set conditions not found originally in their texts. So, we support an accurate implementation of these resolutions".

However, Hubert Vedrine, the French Foreign Minister, considered the "Israeli" government acknowledgement of the Resolution 425 as a progress that cann't be ignored. Also, we cann't ignore that the Resolution 425 doesn't contain any conditions. The executive conditions, which are sufficient in themselves, can be found only in the Resolution 425. So, while we think that there is a real progress, we also see that both resolutions should be fully respected (11).
