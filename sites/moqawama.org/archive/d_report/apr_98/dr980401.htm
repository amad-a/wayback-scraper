Daily Report
 
 
April Wednesday 1-1998:

About the "Israeli" withdrawal:
Anything new hadn't been noticed in the decree passed by "Israel's" inner cabinet pertaining to the "Israeli" retreat from South Lebanon. Also, "Israel's" approval over a conditional implementation of the Resolution 425 didn't enforce Lebanon to change its rejecting attitude to any kind of amendments, maneuvers or alternations concerning the implementation of the UN Resolution 425 and 426.

The spokeswoman of French Foreign Ministry reminded of the true nature of Resolution 425 confirming the necessity of "an immediate and unconditional fulfillment of the concerned Resolution" .
France's affirmation this reflects full satisfaction with Lebanon's attitude especially towards the goal for which the resolution 425 had passed.

The French diplomats dealt cautiously and have no confidence in "Israel's" proposal despite the fact that praising "Israeli" government for its approval on the Resolution 425  , as some of these diplomats commented on this approval by saying "It is a good step", will encourage this government to stick strongly with it and go far in this regard.

In "Israel", sentiment over the occupation remains split. Yossi Beilin , a member of the opposition Labour Party, said Netanyahu plan was doomed to failure and instead proposed his own scheme to end the occupation. He also considered that the "Israeli" precondition, that Lebanon should provide security arrangements for "Israel", is not true.

For his part, the former "Israeli" forces commander in South Lebanon Ifraim Sennieh stressed that government's decision is not but a "trick". The Netanyahu's main goal is to divert the world attention from the impasse of peace process on the level of Palestinian track" .

On the Lebanese side, President Elias Hrawi reiterated his stand  that "Lebanon will never negotiate the implementation of UN Resolution 425. If "Israel" really wants to withdraw, it only has to do so".
He ended: "What 'Israel' raises is not but a trial balloon. We have an explicit resolution that only needs help from the United Nations to be applied".

Speaker Nabih Berri  said: "It is not a decree that shows a will to implement 425. It is a plan to make assault on South Lebanon".

Foreign Minister Fares Boueiz  reported: "The 'Israeli' proposal is not aimed at implementing 425 but at burying it", adding that "Israel" is looking to lure us into negotiations that will not lead but to arrangements identical to those of May 17 Accord".

He maintained that there is not something new in "Israel's" decision. On the contrary, it added further preconditions. "Israel" tries to blew it up", he said.

As to the fate of "Israeli"-backed militia, he mentioned: "It is Lebanon's own business. "Israel" has no any right to put conditions of any kind especially in this sensitive matter".
"Israeli" Aggressions :

Once again, "Israel" had breached April 1996 Understanding in several areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa.

In Nabatieh region, the "Israeli"-occupied post of Zaffateh bombarded commencing from 1:00pm the heights and woodlands of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh, Loweizeh and Mazraat Aqmata.

Also, Ali Taher position targeted before midnight Aqidah neighborhood of Nabatieh El-Fawqa with two tanks missiles which hit, directly, the citizen Ali Youssef Houmani's two-story house causing gross material damages and igniting a blaze continued until the morning and destroyed completely its furnitures.

In Eastern Sector, the "Israeli" gunners shelled Mazraat Loussi, Litani River and Sraireh forests. At 7:30pm, two "Israeli" helicopters raked the heights of Abou Rashed and Qatraneh and the perimeters of Sraireh with heavy machine gun fire but they were drawn fire from the Lebanese Army anti-aircraft guns.

In Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" shelling spread to include Mashghara and the hilltops of Maramel, Rihayat and Ain Tineh areas.

"Israeli" military reinforcements :
It was reported that the "Israeli" troops and their collaborators were put on the alert on the front of Western Beqaa-Hasbaya. The "Israeli" forces also brought armored reinforcements to their positions at Kawkaba, Ain Qiniah, Toumat Niha, Kfar Houna and Rihan areas.

However, it was seen a Unifil's Norwegian patrol inspecting the site where a blast killed six persons and wounded another who were working in fortifying Kawkaba position.

The "Israeli"-backed Lahd's Militia Command charged the Norwegian Battalion of impeding the evacuation of casualties especially the site of explosion is about only 75mm from a Norwegian observation post which is located at the western part of Kawkaba village.

Eyewitnesses mentioned that the "Israeli" troops and Lahd's Militia had left the casualties on the ground for about one hour and half because of fearing of other undetonated bombs. Also, it was noticed that the "Israeli" helicopters landed far away from the scene whereas an ambulance transported the casualties to Marjeyoun area then airlifted to Rambam Hospital at Haifa.

Detainees :
The Paris-based Committee for the Support of Detainee Soha Bshara delivered the French President Jack Chirac in which it requested eliminating Khiam detention camp and releasing all the detainees. Also, it demands Liya Tsimil, lawyer of Bshara and a number of other detainees to do everything in order to try her for she has been ten years in detention without trial. In her turn, Tsimil expressed her fearness that "Israel" may took the detainees from Khiam detention camp to the jails inside the occupied Palestine in the event of its withdrawal from South Lebanon.

An "Israeli" clerk and extremists' threats :
The "Israeli" clerk and director Ronitte Broocovits, 44, said she had been threatened by Jews extremists because claims that she had glorified and praised "the Palestinian fighters in a TV serial story over the history of 'Israel'". It is worthmentioning that this program was broadcasted by "Israeli"-TV Channel one on the fifties anniversary of establishing "Israel".

"The program was about the terrorism. I had been accused of giving the terrorists a forum to express their ideas. But I was doing my job as a journalist", she said.

Brocovits mentioned: "I don't regard the Palestinians as terrorists but fighters for the sake of freedom. This is why the "Israeli" society cannot accept the matter", adding that "the fanaticism led to the assassination of the former "Israeli" Premier Yitzhac Rabin in 1995 on the hand of a right-wing extremist".

She concluded: "I don't know where the terror will lead us".

The documentary serial story, which focused on the issues of "Israeli"-Arab conflict, raised a hot debate inside "Israel" concerning its past and present days.
