Daily Report
 
 
April Monday 6-1998:

"Israeli" Aggressions:
According to a statement issued by the Islamic Resistance, the "Israeli" aggressions are as follows:
18:05, Taibeh Project post fired seven 155mm missiles at the outskirts of Ghandourieh village.
18:30, about ten 155mm shells emanating from Meis El-Jabal area struck the perimeters of Touline, Qalaouieh and Hojeir Valley.
18:40, the area between Qalaouieh and Touline villages came under seven 155mm missiles emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal.
19:10, Bir Kallab outpost raked its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
19:40, Zohaira position targeted Jib Soweid with four 155mm missiles.
19:45, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa.
20:10, the "Israeli" fighters flew low in the airspace of Western Beqaa areas.
20:25, seven 155mm missiles emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal fell on Aissieh Valley.
20:40, the "Israeli" jets conducted mock raids over Yater village.
20:45, Tannour Valley-Salhani area came under three 105mm tank rounds emanating from Blat post and three 155mm shells emanating from Yarin post.
20:55, Tohorra outpost bombarded southwest of Yater village with eighteen 155mm missiles, whereas Zaffateh position hit the perimeters of Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr with several 155mm shells. Meanwhile, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Litani River.
22:35, the M.K. reconnaissance planes had continued in flying since one and half and hour over Litani River.
22:40, three helicopters soared, at middle altitude, over Radar area.
23:00, six helicopters hovered at low and middle altitude, over Tair Harfa and Alma villages, whereas an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Shaqra, Baraasheit and Majdal Selem areas. At the same time, the warplanes flew, at middle height, in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
23:35, the "Israeli" jet airplanes flew in the airspace of Nabatieh and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions. In the meantime, Hemayed post showered its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it recorded the following attacks:
1:00, the group of martyr Youssef Baddah attacked Blat post with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
2:15, the group of martyr Hassan Hammoud targeted again Blat post with the adequate weapons chalking up direct and definite hits.
6:30, the group of two martyrs Taha Tohan and Mohammad Jaafar fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and destroying the northern and southern ramparts.

 However, the Islamic Resistance announced proudly the martyrdom of Hassan Hussein Balhas, born in 1975 at Siddiqeen. He had joined a number of military courses, shared with several attacks on the "Israeli" enemy and martyrized on 6/4/1998 during performing his Islamic and sacred duties.
