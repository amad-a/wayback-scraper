Daily Report
 
 
April Sunday 19-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
24:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Sreifa and the neighboring areas.
1:30, Shraifeh position shelled the road of Sawaneh with three 155mm missiles.
6:40, Zohaira position targeted west of Yater village with three 155mm missiles.
6:45, the perimeters of Jibaa came under five 155mm shells emanating from Zaffateh position.
6:50, Zohaira post shelled Jib Soweid area with several 155mm missiles, whereas the perimeters of Touline village exposed to a number of 122mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal.
6:55, several 155mm missiles emanating from the area of Meis El-Jabal fell on Aissieh Valley.
7:05, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Sojod mountain.
9:35, the "Israeli" jets flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
9:55, the warplanes flew, at middle height, over the Western Sector of South Lebanon.
13:5, the jet airplanes flew over Ghandourieh, Nabatieh and Central Sector of Southern Lebanon, whereas an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over Qana, Hanaouieh and the nearby areas.
14:05, two helicopters over Blat and Bassil areas.
15:30, Berkat Naqieh was hit by four 120mm shells emanating from Meis El-Jabal area.
16:10, Sojod outpost raked its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
16:20, Zohaira position bombarded west of Yater village with about fifteen 155mm missiles.
16:40, two choppers hovered low over Jibbain and Chihine areas.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
5:45, the Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Ahmad Abdel-Jalil Cherri and Ibrahim Qassfi attacked the "Israeli"-occupied Taibeh post with the proper arms scoring direct hits and causing damage to its outer defenses. Also, the columns of smoke were seen emanating from inside it.
13:40, the group of both martyrs Khodor Ghadboun and Abdallah Kassem fired at Radar post with the proper arms chalking up direct hits.
15:15, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Hussein Jaber attacked Mohaibib post with the adequate weapons inflicting a number of casualties among its members and chalking up direct hits.
15:45, the group of two martyrs Samih Ahmad Soweidan and Hassan Ahmad Moshawrab fell upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.
16:10, the Islamic Resistance fighters observed an enemy force near Blat position. Shortly afterwards, the group of both martyrs Ali Badawi and Ali Suleiman targeted it with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.
16:30, the group of two martyrs Moussa Boussi and Majed Jaffal fired at Mohaibib post with the adequate arms scoring direct and accurate hits.
