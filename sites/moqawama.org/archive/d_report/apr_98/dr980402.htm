Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 2-1998:

The peaceful image, "Israel" tries hardly to imbue itself, did not manage to conceal the true nature of "Israeli" aggressions on South Lebanon. Once again, "Israel" had breached the April 1996 Understanding when its air and ground forces bombarded Iqleem Al-Tuffah region especially Loweizeh village where one Lebanese civilian had martyrized.

"Israeli" Aggressions :
The "Israeli" fighters staged two sorties at 12:50 and 12:55pm on the road between Jarjoua and Loweizeh villages killing the civilian Assaad Mohammad Hattab, 35, Arab-Salim, as he was driving his tanker on the abovementioned road. The martyr Hattab is married and had three children.
The two sorties were accompanied by bombarding the hilltops of Sojod, Rihan, Loweizeh and Aqmata.

Earlier of this day, the "Israeli" batteries had shelled Zahrani Well, surroundings of the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh and the eastern hilltops of Nabatieh El-Fawqa and Kfar Romman.

An "Israeli" campaign on Unifil  :
The pro-"Israeli" Voice of South said an "Israeli" official accused the Unifil's Norwegian Battalion operating in Kawkaba area of being responsible for the blast which killed six workmen who were fortifying a military post in that area. This "Israeli" accusation came because the Norwegian Battalion is taking charge of the site where the blast occurred as the "Israeli" official considered.

This "Israeli" stance reflects the idea that "Israel" rejects the international mediation in which it is a party. It only relies on the force and support of United States of America which has the right to "veto" any resolution that may contradict with "Israel's" interests.

It is worthmentioning that the road on which the explosion had occurred had been built forcibly by the "Israeli" force five years ago inside the area controlled by the Norwegian force. "Israel's" goal was to avoid its military vehicles to travel on the main road for its is opened to fire of the Islamic Resistance militants.

On the other hand the "Israeli" agents serving at Zemraya crossing point banned the ice cream products to be entered into the occupied areas by the Lebanese merchants permitting at the same time the "Israeli" ice cream products to be imported.
