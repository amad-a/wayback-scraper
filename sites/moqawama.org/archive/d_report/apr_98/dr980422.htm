Daily Report
 
 
April Wednesday 22-1998:
 

Lebanese Detainees:
Another Lebanese hostage joins the darkness of "Israeli" jails. The Lebanese citizen Ali Ahmad Banjak had failed to get his personal freedom although an "Israeli" First Instance Court had ordered the release of Banjak on April 13-1998 and returning him to Lebanon within 48 hours. "Israel's" Military Prosecutor had appealed to the concerned Court requesting that the hostage Ali Ahmad Banjak should be under arrest (1).

In a number of interviews made by several "Israeli" newspapers, Banjak had talked about his barbaric torture the weeks followed his detention. This panic and terrific situation compelled him to acknowledge with actions he didn't do them (2).

"Israeli" Aggressions:
Slouqi Valley and the peripheries of Baraasheit and Shaqra came, at 8:15am, under "Israeli" shelling which spread to include on the afternoon Nafha Valley and the surroundings of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri villages (3).

Also, the Islamic Resistance statement reported the following "Israeli" aggressions (4):
7:00, Zaffateh position targeted Safi Mountain with two 155mm missiles, whereas Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
7:05, Sojod outpost showered its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
7:30, Shraifeh position fired four 155mm shells at the road between Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Zawtar villages.
7:35, nine 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Safi Mountain.
8:00, Zaffateh position shelled Safi Mountain with one 155mm missile.
12:25, two choppers hovered over Kounine, Tiri and the 17th Center areas.
12:30, an Apatchi helicopter landed beyond Manara area, when on the other hand Zohaira position targeted Jib Soweid area with Nabatieh, Iqleem Al-Tuffah and the neighboring areas.
12:45, two helicopters hovered over Markaba and Houla villages.
12:50, Zohaira position shelled Jib Soweid area with three 155mm missiles.
12:55, Meshnaqa position unleashed four 81mm shells at Kfar Falous Valley.
13:55, Roum post bombarded the road of Roum village with ten 120mm shells.
19:50, the "Israeli" fighters flew, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Saida and its suburbs.
20:05, Habboush River bands were bombarded by ten 155mm missiles emanating from Mahmoudieh position and several others emanating from Zaffateh position.
20:35, the road between Zawtar and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr came under three 155mm shells emanating from Shraifeh position.
20:50, one "Israeli" chopper soared, at middle height, over Qasmieh area.
21:30, Litani River, west of Zillaya, Bir Zahr area, Darazi Forte and martyr Abou Hassan Bjaiji's Hill exposed to twenty five 120 and 155mm shells emanating from the "Israeli"-occupied positions of Zemraya and Shraifeh.

On the other hand, the "Israeli" troops abducted the woman citizen Intisar Ibrahim Abdallah, 22, from her village and took her to Khiam detention camp after she had questioned at the 17th Center of Bent Jbeil (5).

For their parts, security sources located at the occupied region of South Lebanon reported that two "Israeli" bulldozers were seen participating with setting up a new position south of Houla village after the position which located at its west had came under several military strikes by the Islamic Resistance militants (6).

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The "Israeli" occupation forces admitted that one soldier was injured as a result an attack launched by the Islamic Resistance at Radar position. Also, the "Israeli" agents acknowledged that one member was wounded after the Islamic Resistance fighters blew up a roadside bomb when a Lahdi patrol had passed by (7).

However, the Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it recorded the following attacks (8):
24:00, the Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force inside and around Qalaah post. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Khodor Wahab targeted it with the rockets chalking up direct and definite hits.
12:30, the group of martyr Rabih Maatouk attacked Soweida outpost with the adequate rockets scoring direct hits.
12:35, the Islamic Resistance fighters observed an enemy force inside Radar position. Immediately afterwards, the group of martyr Akram Khatoun targeted it with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits where the smoke columns were seen emanating from the position.
12:50, on the occasion of Zoul-Hijja, a month of homage, and despite the security and military procedures, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Salah Ghandour detonated a roadside bomb as a car belonging to the "Israeli" collaborators was travelling on the road of Roum village. The explosion resulted in destroying completely the concerned car and inflicting all its members. Then, an enemy motorized force attended to the scene and evacuated the casualties. At the same time, the "Israeli" batteries bombarded the site of blast with a number of 81 and 120mm shells.
20:00, the group of martyr Hussein targeted Ahmadieh post with the proper arms scoring direct hits.

Attitudes over Resistance:
Kuwaiti parliamentary speaker Ahmad Abdel Aziz Saadoun said that "Israel" had formally recognized UN Resolution 425 after 20 years due to the efforts of the resistance and the courage of the Lebanese people (9).

Commenting on the recent "Israel's" proposal for implementing Resolution 425, Saadoun pointed out that "twenty years after the resolution was issued, "Israel" has finally agreed to implement it with conditions, thanks to the resistance and the Lebanese people".

He added that "neither Lebanon nor the Arab nation can accept such conditions", saying that "the only acceptable condition is that "Israel" withdraws without conditions" (10).
Over "Israel's" Offer:

In Los Angelos, UN Secretary General Kofi Anan said that "Israel" has to prove its readiness for fulfilling the Resolution 425 which calls for the withdrawal from Lebanon" (11).

It should be noted that Anan's statement this comes following doubts raised over his real stance concerning "Israel's" offer and Lebanon's response, also over the role of the United Nations and its Secretary General pertaining to the American policy in the region.

In the meantime, the Lebanese Foreign Affairs Minister Fares Boueiz (12) sent a message to the foreign ministers of states which are members of Security Council explaining the real nature of Lebanon's stand and analysis with regard to the recent "Israeli" proposals. It stated that "Israel's" delegate to the United Nations had sent on 6-4-1998 a message to the Head of Security Council in which he informed him about the decisions of the "Israeli" Government National Security Committee" concerning this subject. "Israel's" delegate asked for distributing it "as an official document on all the countries".

"This document is considered as a first official attitude issued by "Israel", in spite of the fact that it was not issued by the government but by the National Security Committee. So far, the "Israeli" government hasn't passed an obvious decision pertaining to this subject".

"First: The Lebanese government abides by the necessity of carrying out the UN Resolution 425 as well as the Resolution 426 which is considered as its executive mechanism".

"Second: As in the positions raised through media, this message set up several terms though "Israel"
had denied that. If these terms were adopted, there would be basic and substantial amendments on the Resolution 425 and 426".

"These amendments will be regarded as legal and political change in the Resolution's structure bit in all its goals".

"Therefore, these attitudes and practices don't indicate that "Israel's" government which ask to negotiate the Resolution 425, where it isn't necessary, while it rejects to negotiate, where it is necessary, the Resolution 242 and 338, is not too far from what we have mentioned before".
