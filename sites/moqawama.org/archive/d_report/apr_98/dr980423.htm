Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 23-1998:
 

Amnesty International:
In spite of the fact that Amnesty International's reports will not lead to the release of the Lebanese detainees in "Israel's" jails, these reports form as an assistant factor in raising their causes and thus in releasing them.

In this field, Amnesty International addressed a letter to both "Israel's" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defense Minister Yitzhac Mordechai and the commander of "Israeli" collaborators militia Antoine Lahd in which it demanded that the Lebanese nationals Adnan Yehia, Hussein Merhi, Bilal Abou Taam and Hussein Nasr should be released soon and without conditions in the case of not charging them (1). Also, it asked about their fate after they had been detained since March 3-1998.

The Amnesty called on the "Israelis" and their agents "to present guarantees that the detainees are treated well and that their families and lawyers should meet them". Also, it expressed fears of the possibility of subjecting them to torture and ill-treatment" (2).

In the meantime, the Follow-up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in "Israel's" Jails (3) disclosed that the contacts with the internee Abdel-Majid Kassem Zaytoun, Deir Kifa village, who apprehended on 2/3/1993 and took to a prison in "Israel" had been stopped since the early 1997. Asked about him, the International Committee for Red Cross answered that the concerned internee had been released, but the Committee didn't received him.

The Committee regarded this as a strange case holding, at the same time, "Israel" the full responsibility for the internee and expressing its fearness of being assassinated by "Israel" upon claiming his release. Finally, it called on all the international organizations to do everything with the aim of disclosing Zaytoun's fate and bringing him back to his family.

However, the Secretary General of Arab Organization for the Human Right Mr. Mohammad Faiq (4) said his Organization had engaged itself to defend the rights of Arab detainees and internees in the "Israeli" prisons, including the Lebanese internees considering that the detention actions are contradicted with the international agreements and conventions.

Also, he called on the International Committee for Red Cross and other international organizations to bear their responsibility over the judgement passed recently by the "Israeli" Supreme Court which considered a number of the Lebanese hostages as bargaining cards. He demanded that all the Lebanese internees held in the "Israeli" jails should be promptly released.

At the end, he called for a wide popular lobby campaign in order to draw the attention that "Israel" is the only state throughout the world that its law authorizes practicing torture.
On the other hand, Archbishop of Baalback's Greek Orthodox Kirmess Bustross accused the United States of America of granting "Israel" an absolute support (5).

Bustross's statement this came in the course of a speech delivered before the Synod dedicated for Asia which has been holding since the last Sunday (May 3-1998). "The christians' message addressed for Moslims has been always handicapped by the absolute support granted by US. to "Israel's", he concluded.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
In the Central Sector, the "Israeli" batteries stationed at Qantara position shelled at 1:00pm the villages of Qabrikha, Majdal Selem and Froun. The "Israeli" shelling spread to include at 1:30pm Hojeir Valley and the villages of Mansouri, Biyout Siyad and Majdal Zoun, whereas the surroundings of Hamra crossing point exposed to three missiles (6).

In the Eastern Sector, several 155mm missiles fell on Litani River bands and the valleys located west of Zillaya and Loussi whereas Zahr Mountain came under a number of mortar rounds. At the same time, these areas exposed to heavy machine gun fire (7).

On the other hand, the Islamic Resistance recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions (8):
6:45, Zaffateh position unleashed five 155mm missiles at Safi Mountain.
8:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Nabatieh city.
9:15, Yarin position shelled the perimeters of Haddatha village with several 155mm missiles.
9:30, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft flew low over Mansouri village and the nearby areas.
11:35, Salaah position fired several 81 and 120mm smoke shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
11:45, Salaah position targeted Litani River with four 120mm shells.
12:20, Jib Soweid area came under four 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position.
12:35, Salaah position bombarded the surroundings of Mothallath and Alman position with ten 120mm shells.
12:40, Shraifeh post targeted Sawan area with one 155mm missile.
12:45, Zaghleh and Shraifeh positions fired fifteen 155 and 160mm missiles at Litani River, west of Zillaya and Bir Zahr area, whereas the hill between Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Zawtar Gharbieh villages exposed to four 155mm missiles emanating from Shraifeh position.
14:40, two "Israeli" choppers hovered at middle altitude, over Salhani area.
13:20, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over East Saida areas. Meanwhile, Roum position combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
19:20, more than three 122mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal fell on the western peripheries of Touline village.
19:25, the perimeters of Shaqra village were shelled by three 122mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area.
19:30, several 122mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal struck the area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem villages.
20:25, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
22:35, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Shaqra and Baraasheit villages.
22:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Tair Felsay, Hallousieh and Hmeiri villages.
23:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft flew low in the airspace of Central and Western Sectors of South Lebanon.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Islamic Resistance issued at statement in which it record the following attacks (9):
10:25, the group of two martyrs Mohammad Abdo Kammoun and Hussein Ali Jaber attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.
11:15, the Islamic Resistance men observed an enemy force inside Sojod outpost. Immediately afterwards, the group of both martyrs Sheikh Hussein Kansou and Hassan Jawad Nassrallah fell upon it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.
12:15, the group of two martyrs Ali Sbeiti and Haitham Abou Diah attacked Radar position with the adequate arms chalking up direct and certain goals.
13;15, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Munir Smaha targeted Ahmadieh position with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.
18:00, the Islamic Resistance fighters observed an enemy force inside Kassaret Al-Oroush. Shortly afterwards, the group of two martyrs Haidar Mustafa Haidar and Wehbi Wehbi attacked it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
19:15, the Islamic Resistance fighters observed a Lahdi force performing fortification works inside Houla position south of Houla village. Then, the group of martyr Yehia Kassem targeted it with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

Fleeing a Lahdi militiamen (10):
In this regard, and after carrying out several advanced steps on the way of breaking down Lahd's Militia such as the Amnesty Law presented by the Loyalty to the Islamic Resistance Bloc and the condensed security and military attacks launched by the Islamic Resistance militants, it was registered that a number of "Israeli" agents had been fled to the liberated areas.

Fearing of a sudden "Israeli" pull-out from Jezzine area, many of the "Israeli" collaborators displaced from it to Marjeyoun and its nearby areas. In this connection, it was reported that 95% of Lahd's officers had left their houses in Jezzine area for other areas.

On the other hand, security sources reported that the "Israeli" intelligence apparatus (11) had detained three brothers from Taibeh village. The three, well known by their collaborating with the "Israeli" troops, are identified as Abbas Kawsan, the security official in the village, and his brothers Mohammad, wounded previously as a member in Lahd's Militia, and Mohsen, now cooperating with Lahd's Militia. All of them were taken to Khiam detention camp.

"Israel's" proposals (12):
Speaker of Kuwaiti Parliament Ahmad Abdel-Aziz Saadoun announced that the latest "Israeli" offer couldn't be considered as a case of complying with the international resolutions but rather it is withdrawal project in accordance with the "Israeli" conditions.

Saadoun assured that "Israel's" mere acceptance of the Resolution 425 means nothing more than that it is unable to pursue its occupation of the Lebanese territories because of the actions of the Lebanese resistance which is supported by the Lebanese people. "At the same time, it becomes clear that the Arab position is unified and there is an absolute rejection of the "Israeli" conditions. It is somehow a success that "Israel" had, finally, accepted the Resolution 425 after twenty years of rejection", he concluded.

However, the Qatari Ambassador and the Dean of Arab Diplomatic Corps (13), described the "Israeli" offer as not serious. "The Resolution 425 is concrete and tight. Though it had been passed twenty years ago, the Resolution 425 hadn't lost its value and legality", he told.

Also, he considered that "Israel's" offer is not but a result of the resistance panic strikes and aims at making a certain commotion among the Lebanese society. "The Arab supports the Lebanese stance pertaining to the "Israeli" proposed, he concluded.

For his part, the Saudi Ambassador Ahmad Koheimi said, after meeting with the Lebanese Foreign Affairs Minister, Lebanon's stand is honorable and we, as a Saudi Arabia Kingdom, uphold it absolutely.
