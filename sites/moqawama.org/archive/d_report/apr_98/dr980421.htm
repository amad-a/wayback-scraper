Daily Report
 
 
April Tuesday 21-1998:
 

While the talks and statements concentrate on the "Israeli" proposal over carrying out a pull-out of South Lebanon, the "Israeli" occupation forces breached the April 1996 Understanding as they shelled the Lebanese civilians and Unifil troops stationed at South Lebanon.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The Ghanaian Headquarter at Qabrikha village came under "Israeli" shelling which caused damages, to its observation post (1). Also, the "Israeli" shelling spread to include the villages of Shaqra, Yater, Kafra, Rshaf and Srobbin at 6:30am and Slouqi Valley at 9:00am. Several houses at Shaqra village damaged as a result of this shelling.

The "Israeli" gunners repeated their shelling of Shaqra village at 5:00pm where a number of its houses were damaged, including houses of Mahmoud Amin Ismail and Hachem Fouani (2).

Plea against "Israel's" violations (5):
Two years following Qana massacre (109 butchered), its repercussions continue in expanding as a ball of ice. In this regard, three American lawyers applied a plea to UN's Human Rights Organization in which they asked for the investigation of "Israel's" human rights violations during "its aggressive military campaign on Lebanon on April 1996 where 170 Lebanese civilians killed, 350 wounded and 350 thousands displaced in addition to the destruction of hundreds of houses". The plea concentrated basically on Qana massacre.

It stated: " 'Israel' had committed blatant breaches for human rights including the deliberated shelling of the refugees who took resort at Unifil's base at Qana on April 18-1998". Also, 'Israel' had intentionally targeted the civilian installations including electric and water stations as well as residential buildings.

The plea divided into eight chapters as follows:
- The first relates to those who applied the plea Haidar Bitar basically who two of his sons butchered at Qana massacre.
 - The second includes a brief statement of the whole contents of the plea.
 - The third and fourth expose the realities of the massacre.
 - The fifth is about the legal responsibility of "Israel".
 - The sixth states the United Nations' Secretariat General investigation.
 - The seventh relates to the absence of local solutions of the petitioners.
 - The eighth is about an urgent request for doing something.

In this connection, the plea told: " 'Israel' had violated not only those who applied this plea but also thousands others terrified by its forces in South Lebanon". Moreover, it stressed: "There is a need to an immediate and quick efforts to prevent further killing of the civilians in South Lebanon. 'Israel' will follow-up its aggressive actions if anyone cann't stop it".

The plea asked for making quickly the necessary investigation about 'Israel's' attack on the civilians who sought refuge in UN's Qana base and issuing the proper recommendations to the competent authorities of United Nations.

Also, it requested that Human Rights Organization should perform the following steps:
 - Making interviews with those who survived from Qana massacre and another "Israeli" murdering processes, Unifil's and Lebanese Army officers, eyewitnesses, journalists and Lebanese and "Israeli" government officials.
 - Performing full investigation over the conditions of mass murder at Qana (Uns' base of Fijian Battalion), Nabatieh (destroying populated building), Mansouri (rocketing the ambulance on April 16) and Majdal Zoun (Unifil's base on April 17) in addition to the processes of impeding the lorries that were bearing humanitarian aids to the "Israeli"-shelled populated areas.
 - Drafting a comprehensive report over "Israel's" legal liability for the actions mentioned hereabove as well as the adequate compensations for victims and harmed peoples in addition to taking specific procedures for these kinds of actions not be repeated.

About "Israel's" offer (6):
The Lebanese Prime Minister Rafik Hariri reiterated his refusing of any compromise over the conditions of UN Resolution 425 which called for a unilateral withdrawal". Netanyahu's terms are unacceptable and unnegotiable at all", he said.

Hariri maintained that it is not Netanyahu's power to ask guarantees "for disarming Hizbullah's fighters" backing at the same time their struggle for the sake of the homeland.
Also, he considered that he and his government "cann't run after fighters risking their lives in fighting who occupies their land as if they are criminals, while at the same time, disregard and ignore mercenaries put themselves in serving the occupier".

He refused to reward the "Israeli" agents for their treason confirming that "neither the Lebanese government nor the Army are ready to fight the honest people".
