Daily Report
 
 
April Friday 10-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions :
The "Israeli" occupation forces carried out a series of aggressions against South Lebanon and Western Beqaa. At dawn, the "Israeli" batteries shelled Mazraat Loussi, Litani River and the perimeters of Qatraneh. Then, the liberated areas facing the two positions Kawkaba and Toumat Niha came under heavy machine gun fire. Meanwhile, the "Israeli" jets crisscrossed and conducted intensive mock raids throughout the airspace of South Lebanon drawing attention and raising doubts among the security sources.

Also, the Southern Lebanon witnessed the following "Israeli" aggressions :
00:05, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over the sea opposite to Adloun town.
5:45, Bir Kallab outpost fired five 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
9:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over Nabatieh region.
11:10, an M.K. reconnaissance plane flew in the airspace of Nabatieh city.
20:30, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
21:55, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Aqmata plain.
In the frame work of "Israeli" abuses practiced on the population of the occupied region of South Lebanon , the "Israeli" soldiers are still preventing the Lebanese University students from leaving the occupied region to follow up their studies at the Lebanese University. So, the students called on the Five-Nation Monitoring Committee to exert pressures on the "Israeli" occupation forces in order to reopen Zaghleh-Meimess crossing point which is allocated for the students knowing that the examinations will begin in a short time.

Islamic Resistance Operations :
The Islamic Resistance announced that at 15:15, the group of two martyrs Mohammad Najem and Bassem Faour attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and destroying the northern barricade.

 Lebanese Detainees in "Israel's" prisons :
The Spring University of Human Rights in Morocco issued a statement in which it declared its support for the rights of the Lebanese detainees-hostages held in the "Israel's" jails. It regarded the cause of Arab and Lebanese hostages as "an international human cause for their detention contradicts with and violates Geneva Convention and other international laws".

The statement denounced "Israel's" Supreme Court decision issued on 4-3-1998 on the chance that the Lebanese detainees could be used as hostages and bargaining chips in a prisoner exchange. It demands "Israel" to permit the detainees to meet with the International Committee for Red Cross, lawyers, human organizations and their families. Finally, the statement announced its support to the struggle of Lebanese people for the sake of liberating its lands and fulfilling the Resolution 425.

For their part, the participants of International and Arab Human and Youth Organizations symposium called for organizing an international solidarity campaign to exert pressure on "Israel" for the purpose of releasing all the Arab detainees held in "Israeli" jails.

The alleged "Israeli" retreat:
The American Institute for Foreign Political Researches issued a report in which it regards the optimism over a conditional implementation of "Israeli" withdrawal from South Lebanon is not justified and real .

However, Karim Baqradouni, the former Secretary General of Lebanese Phalanges Party  asserted: "Unlike to the Resolution 242, the Resolution 425 is so clear where it states an unconditional and full pull-out to the internationally recognized borders. There is no possibility to any confusion or uproar pertaining to either the seven villages, the occupied enclave or exploiting the waters".

He added: "Kourt Valdheim, the former UN's Secretary General wrote on March 1978 a report about how to carry out the Resolution 425. The sections no 6,7 and 8 stated respectively the necessity of "Israeli" withdrawal to the international borders, creating a region under the control of Unifil and implementing the Armistice Agreement concluded between Lebanon and "Israel" in 1949.
About "Israel's" offer he said: "There is no need to any kind of negotiations. The Resolution 425 speaks about the principle of retreat while the Resolution 426 talks about the mechanism which certifies that it is up to Unifil to organize the security arrangements and the process of "Israeli" pull-out from the Lebanese lands so as to be handed over finally to Lebanon's government.
