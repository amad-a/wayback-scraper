Daily Report
 

Saturday February 28-1998:
 

The Islamic Resistance men carried out three "Israeli"-occupied posts including a military squad near Bir Kallab outpost. Meanwhile, the "Israeli" troops followed up their aggressions against the liberated areas of Lebanon, particularly the heights of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh and Aqmata.

However, the Islamic Resistance announced, proudly, the martyrdom of the militants: Hani Hassan Obeid, Hassan Ahmad Shamma and Moussa Mohammad Abou Taam.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The Islamic Resistance statement registered the following aggressions (1):
7:00, Sojod outpost hit its surroundings with a number of 81mm shells, whereas Bir Kallab outpost combed its vicinity with heavy machine gun fire.
7:10, Sojod mountain subjected to 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area. Also, Zaffateh position hit Aqmata area with five 155mm missiles.
7:15, five 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan struck Sojod mountain.
7:20, Aqmata area subjected to one 120mm shell emanating from Rihan area.
7:35, one 120mm shell emanating from the outskirts of Rihan town struck Aqmata area.
7:45, Zaffateh position targeted the perimeters of Jibaa with two 155mm missiles.
20:00, Beit Yahoun fired three 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
20:15, Mohaibib outpost unleashed four 155mm missiles at Zaatar mountain.

Islamic Resistance Operations:

The Islamic Resistance statement reported the following (2):
6:55, the group of both martyrs Kassem Zein El-Abedeen Yassine and Fadl Mahmoud Soweid fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct hits.
13:00, the Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Hassan Obeid and Hassan Harb rushed upon a motorized patrol on the road leading to Bir Kallab outpost with the rockets and machine guns. The militants asserted that one military truck was destroyed. The truck was transporting equipment and construction materials for rebuilding Bir Kallab outpost which was destroyed by Mujahedeen the day before. At the same time, the group of two martyrs Hassan Shamma and Moussa Abou Taam attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. The position came under a fierce and concentrated attack while its soldiers were rebuilding the destroyed barricades and fortifications. The attack prevented its soldiers from supporting the attacked patrol.
Martyrs:
The Islamic Resistance announced, proudly, the martyrdom of three of its militants (3):


Martyr Hassan Hani Obeid, born at Jibsheit in 1976, single, joined Hizbullahs ranks in 1994 and Islamic Resistance in 1995, subjected to several military and cultural courses, carried out a lot of military operations and participated in confronting the April-1996 Aggressions. He had martyrized on 24-2-1998 in firefight with an "Israeli" force near Abou Rkab mountain.

Martyr Hassan Ahmad Shamma, born at Meis El-Jabal in 1969, married and has one son, joined Hizbullahs ranks in 1986 and Islamic Resistance in 1994, shared with several military and cultural courses and carried out a number of military operations. He had martyrized on 26-2-1998 in a confrontation with the "Israeli" enemy at Slouqi valley.

Martyr Mohammad Abou Taam, born in 1971, single, joined Hizbullahs party in 1993, passed through military and cultural courses, participated with a number of military operations. He had martyrized on 26-2-1998 in a confrontation with the "Israeli" enemy at Slouqi valley.
