Daily Report
 

Friday February 27-1998:
 

Amid the series of "Israeli" aggressions against the Lebanese civilians and allegations of senior "Israeli" officers by saying Hizbullah had lost its balance, the Islamic Resistance militants launched an attack on the "Israeli"-manned Bir Kallab outpost. The militants took over it after they had destroyed its barricades and fortifications and inflicted, certainly, a number of its soldiers.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The "Israeli" occupation forces pursued their aggressions against the Southern Lebanon and Western Beqaa through targeting the population of the liberated areas with various kinds of arms in addition to flying fighters, helicopters and reconnaissance planes and staging several air raids overhead(1). The aggressions climaxed when the "Israeli" fighters bombarded the road between Mlikh and Loweizeh villages with six air-to-ground rockets (2). The "Israeli" shelling spread to Loweizeh perimeters Zohaira, Aqmata, Maifadoun, Zawtar  Gharbieh outskirts, Mazraat El-Hamra, Nabatieh, Arab-Salim, Habboush, Sojod, Mlikh, Jabal El-Rafih (3), Zawtar Sharqieh, Nabatieh, Zahrani River, Litani River, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr, Kfar Dajal farm (4) and Iqleem Al-Tuffah areas.

Also, the "Israeli" batteries shelled the perimeters of Kafra, Yater, Srobbin, Jibal El-Botom, Zibqeen, Majdal Zoun, Byout El-Seyyad and heights of Mansouri (5).

In Western Beqaa, also, the "Israeli" gunners pounded into Litani River and the heights of Jabal El-Dahr, Abou Rashed, Birkat Jabbour, Ain El-Tineh & Mashghara, whereas the perimeters of Maidoun, Qilya and Meimess subjected to a barrage of heavy machine gun fire (6).

Islamic Resistance Operations:

Today also, the Islamic Resistance men rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost at 1:00pm wounding four "Israeli" soldiers (8) and destroying its fortifications and barricades in fierce clashes lasted until the evening.

According to the statement of Islamic Resistance, the details came as follows (9).
On the occasion of Islamic Resistance Week, honoring Sayyed Abbas Al-Mousawi and Sheikh Ragheb Harb and emphasizing the course by fighting the "Israeli" enemy, the Islamic Resistance frustrated the enemy leaders and confirmed that the "Israeli" military procedures are fruitless. Few hours after the "Israeli" enemy had boasted with its alleged effective arrangements aiming at minimizing the casualties, the Islamic Resistance militants, in an intelligence accomplishment, had learned that Lahds militiamen manning Bir Kallab outpost had been replaced by a squad of "Israeli" paratroopers from the elite Golani brigade, possibly preparing to conduct an operation into the liberated and populated areas. So, confirming the military capabilities of the militants, a decision was taken to attack it in a broad daylight.

Therefore, and at 13:10pm, the Islamic Resistance fighters made a swift attack throughout the front line of the occupied region including Radar, Blat, Qalaah, Dabsheh, Zaffateh, Sojod, Kassaret Al-Oroush, Kfar Houna and Bir Kallab posts in addition to "Israeli" squads near the latest one.

So, the groups of martyrs Fouad Rida Aoun, Ibrahim Ali Issa, Zoheir Kobeissi, Nimr Ftouni, Salem Aboud, Mohammad Kassem, Ali Yehia and Ahmad Hariri attacked the positions of Blat, Radar, Dabsheh, Qalaah, Zaffateh, Kassaret Al-Oroush, Kfar Houna and Bir Kallab respectively.
Also, the group of two martyrs Mohammad Ashmar and Kassem Hamdan rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost from its entrance side inflicting a number of casualties, destroying the main fortification and planting the Islamic Resistance and Hizbullah flags on the compound.

At the same time, the group of both martyrs Hussein Bahij Nasser and Kamel El-Beiruti attacked the northern part of the compound destroying the barricades there and inflicting its soldiers. In the meantime, the group of two martyrs Hassan Ghassan Ghasssani and Ghassan Ghanem fell upon its southern barricades destroying it completely. The militants held their ground for a period destroying the remaining barricades, fortifications and installations.

At 13:30, the group of both martyrs Mohammad Itani and Safi Bashir targeted a Merkava tank while it was leaving Kassaret Al-Oroush position to provide support to the beleaguered soldiers at Bir Kallab. Meanwhile, the group of two martyrs Rabih Wehbi and Haitham Nasser rushed upon a motorized force as it was coming from Rihan area to reinforce the attacked post with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying completely one military vehicle where it was seen burning by the naked eye.
At 14:00, two "Israeli" helicopter gunships tried to provide support to Bir Kallab outpost, but they were drawn fire from anti-aircraft guns of the Islamic Resistance group of both martyrs Nabil Akhdar and Mohammad Kamel Abbas.
At 14:15, an armoured column attempted to assist the attacked outpost but was ambushed en route by the group of two martyrs Hassan Hijazi and Kassem Khreis with the rocket-propelled grenades.

One military vehicle caught fire and burned for several hours.
At 15:30, the Islamic Resistance anti-aircraft guns fired at the "Israeli" fighters while they were flying in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
At 23:30, the Islamic Resistance observed an "Israeli" force at Hojeir valley. Shortly afterwards, the group of both martyrs Hassan Harb and Mustafa Nazar fell upon it with the adequate arms chalking up direct and certain hits. Thus, the enemy was enforced to bring further reinforcements to back up the attacked force and evacuate the casualties.

The Islamic Resistance announced, proudly, the martyrdom of the militant Hassan Malek Harb (Murtada), born at Jibsheit in 1978, single, joined the Islamic Resistance ranks in 1994, participated in several military and cultural courses and carried out a lot of military qualitative operations. He had martyrized on 27-2-1998 during in the firelight at Bir Kallab outpost.
