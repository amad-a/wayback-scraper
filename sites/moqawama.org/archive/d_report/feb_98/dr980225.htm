Daily Report

Wednesday February 25-1998:
 

The "Israeli" occupation forces followed up the aggressions on South Lebanon and Western Beqaa shelling them with mortars and rockets. Meanwhile, the Islamic Resistance men fell upon nine "Israeli" and Lahdi posts and patrols.

"Israeli" Aggressions:

The "Israeli" gunners shelled the perimeters of Sraireh and Qatrani, whereas the helicopters hovered overhead. Also, the occupiers unleashed several rockets at Majdal Selem, Central Sector, damaging a number of houses related to the citizens Hussein Ali Maaz, Hassan Mohammad Maaz and Abbas Alaeddine. Furthermore, the "Israeli" enemy bombarded the heights of Sojod woodlands, Rihan, Aqmata reaching to Jibaa and Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.

Islamic Resistance Operations:

The Islamic Resistance militants retaliated against the "Israeli" aggressions by targeting the enemy posts and patrols. So at 11:15am a team of resistance ambushed a joint "Israeli"-Lahdi patrol as it was traveling on the road between Aramta and Kfar Houna firing it with the proper weapons, destroying one armored troop carrier and inflicting all its members.
Also, at 11:30am, a second team attacked Kfar Houna post destroying its main fortification which overlooks at the scene preventing it from supporting the attacked Lahdi force.
Furthermore, and at 11:50am, the Islamic Resistance men attacked once more the motorized patrol at the road of Aramta-Kfar Houna scoring direct hits. In the meantime, the militants observed a common "Israeli"-Lahdi military squad inside Kfar Houna post. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit of Islamic Resistance targeted it with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits.
At 4:00pm, the Islamic Resistance fighters staged a one-hour attack at an "Israeli" foot-patrol near Rihan village scoring direct definite hits. Then, the "Israeli" occupation forces brought a number of military vehicles but they were repelled by the proper weapons scoring direct hits. Also, a common "Israeli"-Lahdi squad stationed to the west of Kfar Houna post came under fire by the Islamic Resistance men who attacked a security convoy as it was coming out of the abovementioned post inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks as well.

Obituary:
Meanwhile, the Islamic Resistance announced, proudly, the martyrdom of the militant Kassem Ali Hamdan, born at Houmine El-Tahta in 1979, single, joined the Islamic Resistance ranks in 1995, subjected to several military courses and participated with a lot of military operations. He had martyrized, on 23/2/1998, during performing his Islamic sacred duties.

Detainees:
On the other side, and while the hostages Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid and Mustafa Dirani (1) continued their hunger strike for the second week successively, Diranis wife and children presented a memorandum to the International Committee of Lebanese Red Cross. The memorandum detailed the health situation suffered by both hostages after the hunger strike carried out by them as a protest to mistreatment, discrimination and the physical and psychological torture they met along with their inmates.

"Suffering paresis that he cannot move without a stick, Dirani is subjecting now to various kinds of physical and psychological torture. Also, he is arrested in a solitary confinement, banned to contact with his family. Moreover, the human organizations are prohibited to be informed about his health situation and detention conditions", the memorandum said.

"We, the children of Hajj Abou Ali Dirani, support our father in his strike inside the detention camp
and ask the Secretary General of United Nations, Amnesty International and International Red Cross to perform the following:

      1 -  Enforcing "Israel" to permit us to meet with Hajj Mustafa Dirani and Sheikh Abdel Karim Obeid and to be acquainted with their health state and arrest conditions.
      2 -  Permitting the lawyer of Hajj Mustafa Dirani to meet with him and promote his detention conditions.
      3 -  Exchanging letters via International Red Cross Committee", the memorandum added.
    "Considering our father as an hostage kidnapped by "Israel", we ask for releasing him promptly", the memorandum concluded.
In this connection, the Follow-up Committee of Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Detention Camps and family of detainees Ibrahim Iskandar Abou Zaid and his brother Boulos and wife Ivon Soweidi called for sharing with the strike of Thursday Mothers at 11:00am of Thursday 23/2/1998 to be united in solidarity with the three detainees.

In the meantime, Jack Fresard, representative of International Red Cross in Lebanon, delivered the Lebanese Premier Mr. Rafik Hariri a letter related to the exchange of "Israeli" soldier remains for the Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" detention camps. In his turn, Hariri submitted Fresard a letter its contents remained unknown.

Resistance Information:
In a bid to support the resistance activities against the "Israeli" enemy, Hizbullahs Information Unit presented the Lebanese Information Minister Mr. Bassem Sabaa with the proposal of creating Hebrew-speaking satellite station with the aim of broadcasting Hebrew programs addressing the "Israeli" public opinion and lobbying the enemy government and army to pull out of Lebanon.(2) Sabaa had praised the proposal asserting that the project should be carried out.
