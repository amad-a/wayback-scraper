Daily Report
 

Thursday February 24-1998:
 

The "Israeli" occupation forces pursued their aggressions against the Lebanese areas adjacent to the occupied region of Southern Lebanon and Western Beqaa. So, the villages of Western Beqaa and Iqleem Al-Tuffah were shelled, whereas the "Israeli" fighters, helicopters and reconnaissance planes flew in the Lebanese airspace. Also, the occupation forces kidnapped one civilian upon storming into Odaisseh village.
However, the Islamic Resistance men chased the occupiers through attacking their posts with the machine guns, rockets and mortars.

The Islamic Resistance stated the series of "Israeli" aggressions as follows:
20:25, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Semmaieh, Ras El-Ain and Deir Qanoun villages.
20:45, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Kafra, Yater, Siddiqeen and the neighboring areas.
21:30, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Borj Al-Shimali and Tyre areas.
22:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle height, over Sreifa and the nearby areas.
22:10, the drone and M.K. reconnaissance planes continued in flying, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Western Sector of South Lebanon.
23:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Kfar Seir, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Maifadoun villages.
23:15, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Baraasheit and Qabrikha areas.
23:30, the helicopters hovered over Houla village.
23:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Aiteit and the nearby areas.
7:05, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Litani River.
9:40, the "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
9:55, the warplanes and M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Baraasheit and the neighboring areas.
10:45, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa.
12:05, three 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Aqmata area.
12:20, Zaffateh position bombarded Aqmata area with twenty 155mm missiles.
12:25, Blat post targeted Wadi Salhani with two 120mm shells.
12:30, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of East Saida.
12:40, Blat post hit Jabal Bassil with one 120mm shell.
12:45, Blat post raked Salhani area and its vicinity.
12:55, Aqmata area came under fourteen 105mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan. In the meantime, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Yater area.
13:35, the jet airplanes flew low over Nabatieh and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions.
13:40, the warplanes flew over Western Beqaa areas.
14:45, the "Israeli" fighters flew, at middle height, over Western Beqaa.
16:50, Kfar Houna plain subjected to twelve 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
On the other side, the "Israeli" soldiers stormed into Odaisseh village kidnapping the citizen Ali Assaad Rammal who was taken him to Khiam detention camp.

Islamic Resistance Operations:

The Islamic Resistance reported in a statement the following operations:
16:00, the group of both martyrs Ali Khalil and Ahmad Hariri launched a 50-minute attack at a Zionist foot-patrol near Jabal Bou Rkab with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.
17:30, the Islamic Resistance group of two martyrs Ahmad Chahrour and Mohammad Assaf targeted the military reinforcement coming from Rihan barracks with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits. The intermittent clashes continued for about one hour.
18:30, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Munir Smaha observed a common "Israeli"-Lahdi force to the south of Kfar Houna village. Then, the Artillery Unit shelled it with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.
19:05, the group of two martyrs Mohammad Hassan and Nasser Hawi rushed upon an "Israeli" security convoy of several cars as they were coming out of Kfar Houna post.

The Islamic Resistance announced, proudly, the martyrdom of militant Abdallah Kassem Yassine. He died after years of suffering for he was inflicted during a battle with the Zionist enemy at Jabal Safi. He was born at Younine in 1958, married with six children and joined Hizbullah in 1982.
