Daily Report
 

Thursday February 26-1998:
 

No sooner had Amiram Levine, "Israels" Northern Region Commander, said: "The "Israeli" soldiers will remain in Lebanon until the day Hizbullah lays down its arms" and that "the peace in the region willnt be presented as a gift but as a result of our military strength", (1) than the Islamic Resistance teams launched an attack at the "Israeli"-occupied Blat post killing three soldiers and wounding two others (2). The successful and qualitative operation formed a clear evidence that the Lebanese will never give up their lands and legitimate rights in freedom and sovereignty.

In the meantime, the Islamic Resistance groups staged nine attacks on the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Sojod, Bir Kallab, Dabsheh, Kassaret Al-Oroush, Radar and Bayada (3) killing three soldiers and wounding two, also, Nahar reported Soweida and Ghezlan posts came under attacks of resistance (4).

"Israeli" Aggressions:
Early this day the "Israeli" batteries shelled Yater, Majdal Zoun, Loweizeh, Aqmata, Jabal Sojod and its surroundings (5), Mansouri, Jibal El-Botom, Izzieh, Zibqeen, Nabatieh and the perimeters of Nabatieh El-Fawqa and Habboush. Also, Amerieh plain and Kfar Romman came under the "Israeli" shelling, whereas the area stretching from Kfar Houna until the perimeters of Sraireh combed with heavy machine gun fire (7).

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" helicopters and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah and the occupied region. Besides, the "Israeli" warboats violated the Lebanese marine sovereignty through cruising off the coast between Naqoura and Aqbieh of the South Lebanon.

According to the statement issued by the Islamic Resistance, the "Israeli" aggressions occurred as follows:
00:20, Zaffateh position hit Jabal Sojod with three 155mm missiles.
00:35, three 120mm flare bombs lightened Markaba area.
00:55, a lightened "Israeli" warboat was seen cruising off seashore of Aqbieh.
1:45, Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
6:30, Sojod outpost fired seven 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire at its surroundings.
6:35, Zaffateh position shelled Aqmata plain with four 155mm missiles. Meanwhile, Sojod area subjected to three 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area, whereas Sojod outpost hit its surroundings with three 81mm shells.
7:10, Zaffateh position unleashed four 155mm missiles at the perimeters of Loweizeh and four others at Jabal Sojod.
7:30, the perimeters of Loweizeh came under five 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
8:10, an Awaks reconnaissance plane and one helicopter flew over Chihine and Rahrah villages.
8:15, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
12:25, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
15:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah area.
16:35, two helicopters hovered over Abbad area.
17:05, Zohaira position bombarded west of Yater with thirty five 155mm missiles.
17:30, Tair Harfa outpost unleashed five 120mm shells at the perimeters of Majdal Zoun.
17:45, Jabal Sojod came under ten 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area, whereas Sojod outpost raked its surroundings with a barrage of middle machine gun fire.
17:50, Sojod outpost targeted Jabal Sojod with ten 81mm shells.
18:30, Zaffateh position bombarded Sojod mountain with ten 155mm missiles.
18:35, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Sojod mountain.
21:00, the helicopters hovered, at middle altitude, over Markaba Rob-Thlathine and Taibeh villages. In the meantime, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Tibbnine and Baraasheit areas.
21:15, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Tamerieh and Aissieh valley.

Islamic Resistance Operations:

The Islamic Resistance men followed up the attacks on the "Israeli" troops as follows (9):
6:00, the Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force near and inside Dabsheh outpost. Immediately afterwards, the group of martyr Abdo Agha Zadeh rushed upon it with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits.
6:05, the group of two martyrs Kassem Hamdan and Abdallah Yassine fell upon a Zionist force near Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct hits.
At the same time, the group of both martyrs Ahmad Abdel-Karim Yassine and Hassan Ahmad Saad attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying its main southwestern fortification.
6:15, the group of two martyrs Moussa Ali Dib and Hachem Abdel-Menhem Mohanna rushed upon Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying its main western fortification.
6:55, the group of two martyrs Ramzi Daifallah Abou Salim and Murtada Ahmad Rammal attacked Kassaret Al-Oroush position chalking up direct hits.
16:45, the Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force inside Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of both martyrs Rabih Maatouk and Khalil Olayan targeted it with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.
17:15, the group of martyr Kassem Hamdan attacked the "Israeli"-occupied Radar post with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying its Radar station and two main fortifications.
17:20, the group of both martyrs Haitham Abou Dia and Ali Sbeiti renewed the attack on Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct, definite and concentrated hits. The smoke columns were seen emanating from within the fortifications, installations and military vehicles of the abovementioned post.
Also, after several minutes, an infantry force was seen coming out of the post. Then, the militants opened fire inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. Meanwhile, a helicopter landed at the post to evacuate the "Israeli" casualties. AFP reported that three "Israeli" soldiers were killed and two others wounded.
17:35, the group of both martyrs Mohammad Ali Khalil and Mohammad Ahmad Omais ambushed at a distance close to the northern principal fortification of Sojod outpost. So, when several of its members gathered near to the concerned fortification, the group rushed upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties.
19:45, the group of martyr Bassel Alaeddine fell upon an "Israeli" ambush at Wadi Slouqi and near Mashaaroun position with the appropriate weapons inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks and scoring direct hits with the abovementioned position. Immediately afterwards, the helicopters hovered over Majdal Selem, Khorbet Selem, Touline, Houla and Shaqra villages whereas an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Baraasheit, Tibbnine, Shaqra and Tamerieh villages.

Detainees:
The mothers of detainees, arrested in "Israeli" prison, known by "Thursdays Mothers", held the weekly sit-in on February 2-1998 in front of the International Red Cross Committee Headquarters in Lebanon asking for releasing their relatives kidnapped in unlegal and unfair circumstances. The sit-in this Thursday was very impressive for the detainees Ibrahim Iskandar Abou Zaids (arrested on November 1997) wife, son and daughter cried bitterly. The latest sustained a nervous breakdown after she had told about suffering detainees, in general, and torturing her relatives who are subjecting to a military trial in "Israel", in particular. (10)

The head of Committee for Support of Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons Mohammad Safa, called the "officials to exert effective and serious efforts on United Nations in order to protest against the "Israeli" steps and demanded Maronite Patriarck Cardinal Nassrallah Sfeir, religious spiritual authorities and Middle East Churchs Council to raise this issue before Pope John Paul II".

The wife of the detained Ali Hijazi addressed a memorandum to the Secretary General of United Nations, International Red Cross Committee and the Arab and international organizations in which she said: "On January 1-1998, the "Israeli" occupation forces kidnapped three Lebanese citizens Boulos Iskandar Abou Zaid and his wife Ivon Soweidi and brother Ibrahim, Mlikh village, from Jezzine city, took them to Lishon prison, North "Israel", sustained torture and mistreatment, subjected to military trial and not permitted to be visited by the representative of International Red Cross only after three months. All these procedures contradict and breach the international of conventions".

Also, Anis Abou Zaids brother invited the Lebanese government so as the international organizations exert pressure on "Israel" to release the Lebanese detainees.

The memorandum handed over to the International Red Cross Committee (11).
