Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 26 -1998:
 
Introduction
The heavy and harsh repercussions of striking Israel with batches of Katyusha rockets staged by the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants are still resounding throughout the enemy entity.

The rocket attack left Israel and its officials amid a confusing and puzzling situation. The domestic debate over an Israeli withdrawal from South Lebanon was heated up again. Questions about the wisdom of targeting the Lebanese civilians were highly raised among the enemy leaders.

Israeli Aggressions:
The worst crisis in the south since the 1996 April war plunged the region into uncertainty as fighting continued unabated with a Lebanese soldier wounded in an Israeli helicopter attack in the western Beqaa and two members of the South Lebanon Army killed in clashes with the resistance.

As the south remained at the boiling point, the Israeli inner security cabinet weighed its options for responding to Tuesday nights mass Katyusha attack on northern Galilee, in which 12 Israelis were wounded. 

 

  The Israeli governments dilemma was compounded by an unexpected backlash on the domestic front, which blamed the rocket strike on the assassination of a senior Amal official hours earlier.

With the south holding its breath in anticipation of a retaliation, senior security sources said Israel appears to be favoring an operation against the resistance, which could come in the next few days. But they are ruling out another air and artillery blitz in the south similar to the April war.

The Israelis are really scratching their heads to the point of bleeding trying to come up with a response to their recent casualties and setbacks in Lebanon. Attacking Hizbullah directly is the most viable option open to them, one source said.

Ahmad Othman, a conscript manning a Lebanese army position in the abandoned village of Maidoun in the western Beqaa, was seriously wounded evening when his unit was targeted by missiles fired from Israeli helicopter gunships.

An armored personnel carrier was damaged in the attack. The helicopters had come under anti-aircraft fire from the unit while flying in the vicinity of the Bir Kallab post, 5km to the west, which had earlier been attacked by Hizbullah guerrillas...

Whatever the Israelis are planning, fears of a full-scale assault on south Lebanon are unjustified, security sources said. They know therell be a backlash on the domestic front if they go over the top in the south and cause further Lebanese casualties, a source said, adding that the resistance would retaliate with more Katyusha strikes into northern Galilee.

The much-discussed option of targeting Lebanons infrastructure remains a possibility but again, Hizbullah could claim justification for retaliating in kind with mortar and rocket attacks on Israel. (1)

Israeli officials question wisdom behind the bombardment of the Lebanese civilians.
Mounting Israeli casualties in the South and heightened tension along the border with Lebanon have renewed a debate inside Israel on the wisdom of maintaining the occupation in Lebanon.

There was also a rift among Israeli officials today on whether Hussam Amin, the Amal military official, should have been assassinated.

Israel's so-called inner-cabinet today met and discussed the situation in southern Lebanon, amid mounting calls to withdraw troops from the occupied zone. The security cabinet said it considered the Katyusha attack to be quote a severe violation of the 1996 April cease-fire understanding, reached two years.  A number of hawkish ministers, notably the architect of Israel' deadly invasion of Lebanon in 1982, Ariel Sharon and Rafael Eitan, called for attacks on civilian infrastructure targets inside Lebanon to pressure the Lebanese government to curb resistance attacks against Israeli occupying troops in the South. Sharon and Eitan said Israel should respond forcefully to last night's Katyusha attacks on its northern settlements. But Labor Party member of parliament Yossi Beilin criticized Israel's continued presence in the South, describing it as absurd. Beilin stressed that continued occupation in southern Lebanon is constantly threatening northern Israel and not protecting the area.

Senior Israeli officials also criticized the assassination of Hussam Amin, the Amal military official, south of Tyre yesterday. President Ezer Weizmann, who toured northern Israel today,  said  he hoped those who took the decision to assassinate Amin had first weighed the consequences.

Opposition Labor legislators, namely Ori Orr, also criticized Amin's assassination. Orr said Israel should have thought twice if such an operation was worthwhile, adding this would keep tens of thousands of people in shelters. Meanwhile, the head of Knesset's foreign and security committee, Ozi Landau, maintained his hard-line stance, saying Syria should pay the price of the Katyusha attacks on northern Israel if Damascus was involved in their launch (3)

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Hizbullah resistance movement had also announced it launched Katyusha rockets on northern Israel in retaliation for increased Lebanese civilian casualties from Israeli attacks. Security sources in Lebanon said 26 Katyusha rockets were launched on northern Israel last night.

Hizbullah stressed it launched the attack and said quote if our civilians will not be safe then their civilians will not be safe as well. A Hizbullah statement also said the group has run out of patience with Israel's relentless assaults and systematic killing of Lebanese citizens. Israeli planes fired rockets at residential areas in the West Beqaa town of Mashghara, wounding four Lebanese, including a child.

Ten Israelis were reportedly injured in the attacks on northern Israel. Residents of Kiryat Shmona and Nahariya in northern Israel had spent their night in shelters. Israeli artillery and helicopters equipped with machine-guns last night rained down shells on the Lebanese villages on the edge of the occupation zone. Israeli defense minister Yitzhak Mordechai who had praised the assassination of Amin visited the Galilee and said the rocket attacks would not deter Israel form striking against fighters in Lebanon (4)

However, the IR squad of two martyrs Abbas Soueli and Ibrahim Cherri rushed upon an Israeli foot-patrol as it was in an operational mission inside Bir Kallab outpost. The Mujahedeen targeted it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks where two soldiers were seen laying on the ground (5)

Peoples view in IR rocket retaliation:
The residents of South Lebanon were very proud of the IR qualitative strike which represented in firing salvos of Katyusha rockets deep into Israel.

In the village of Zibqeen, Samir Bzieh, 28, said that the sound of rockets being fired towards Israel was one of sweet revenge. I was delighted when I heard the rockets overhead because the Israelis are always hitting us. But this time we were hitting them, he said.

His wife, Samar, nodded in agreement as she threaded green tobacco leaves together prior to hanging them up to dry in the sun. Life here is normal, she said. Those with weak hearts are afraid but we are used to Israeli threats.

Behind her and clearly visible through the shimmering heat haze was the Blat position, one of the most heavily targeted Israeli compounds in the south.

Whenever Blat is attacked, Zibqeen is regularly hit in the reprisal bombardment.

Samir said that around five families had left the village overnight, although some people had since returned.

Asked if he was prepared to leave Zibqeen should the Israelis choose to launch an offensive, Samir shrugged.

Depends on the heat of the Israelis, he said. But it will take more than a few shells to move us.
In the neighboring village of Jibal Botom, Fatmeh Balhas, 27, was adamant that nothing would move her from her home.

Balhas lost her brother, her husband and her three children, including her 17-day-old baby, Mohammed, in the Qana massacre when Israeli shells slaughtered 107 civilians who were seeking refuge in the Fijian battalion headquarters.

For Gods sake, I would never go back to the UN. I will stay in my house, she said.

Balhas said she was pleased that the rocket strike had resulted in Israeli casualties.

We have not forgotten our losses. Its time the Israelis also learned to suffer, she said.

Her mother, Hasna, said she saw a Katyusha launcher trundling past her house during the previous night.

I could not believe it ­ I almost fainted, she said (6)

EU urges Israel not to build more on Golan Heights (7)
 The European Union on Wednesday urged Israel not to expand settlements on the Golan Heights, saying it would raise questions about the country's commitment to the Middle East peace process. "If such a plan is implemented it would represent a significant setback to the efforts to resume the latest Israeli-Syrian negotiations," the EU said in a statement.

Israel captured the Golan Heights from Syria in 1967. "It would also constitute a further complication for the implementation of the principle of 'land for peace' and raise questions about Israel's" commitment to the peace process," the EU said. "In this context, the European Union reiterates its position that settlements in the occupied territories are illegal and in contravention of international law," the EU said in a statement.
