Daily Report
 
 
August Sunday 2-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" occupation emplacements of Zaffateh and Mahmoudieh (1) shelled Tasseh Well watercourse and the heights and woodlands of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh, Mazraat Aqmata and Loweizeh.

Also, the outskirts of Mazraat Hima and Mazraat Hamra, the plains of Yohmor Shkeif and Kfar Tibbnit, Zawtar Sharqieh, Abou Salem Valley and Litani River banks came under a lot of shells.

In Eastern Sector, the "Israeli"-occupied compounds of Zaghleh and Abou Qamha shelled in the morning Dahr Mountain, Meimess sector and the road between Zimraya and Marj Zohour igniting a fire in olive groves owned by the citizen Samih Sabah from Meimess.

As of 9 O'clock in the morning, an "Israeli" force combed the areas of Rihan, Sojod and Kfar Houna and cut off for more than one hour the highway between Marjeyoun and Jezzine. Also, it was known that an "Israeli" military delegation made an inspection tour in this sector.

Smuggling (3)
The reports said that the "Israeli" military police had arrested an "Israeli" soldier and a member of Lahd's Militia with an accusation of smuggling weapons and narcotic from the occupied enclave into "Israel".

Islamic Resistance Operations (4):
A statement issued by the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported that 107 attacks had been carried out during July, 74 of which, i.e. 70%, were launched by IR men.

The Lebanese Resistance Brigades LRB staged fifteen attacks, whereas Zoubaah (Whirlwind) Organization made three military operations.

The attacks resulted in killing one "Israeli" soldier and wounding eight others, all of them were inflicted by IR attacks. Also, four members of Lahd's Militia were killed, two of them by IR attacks & the third as a result of LRB attacks whereas the fourth was killed by detonating an un-adopted explosive device on the road between Roum and Anan villages.

The statement added the IR men wounded five others of Lahd's Militia. During this month, one IR militant, Hadi Msheimesh, was martyrized on 2-7-1998.

The most prominent military operations were: storming into Baraasheit compound on 2-7-1998, falling upon Shkeif Namel - Baraasheit positions on 16-7-1998 and attacking Rihan barracks on 30-7-1998, the statement ended.

Lebanon Watch-Australia: Calls for Condemning "Israel" (5)
An Australian watchdog which follows up the situation in Lebanon called on the US and Australia to condemn "Israel'" aggressive policy.

In a memorandum addressed to US secretary of state Madeleine Albright and Australian foreign minister Alexander Doner on the occasion of the "Ozman 98" meeting between the two officials, Lebanon Watch-Australia called on Washington and Sydney to pressure "Israel" to withdraw from the territories it occupies and to release all prisoners.

The group called on Albright and Doner to adopt the recommendations of the Australian parliamentary report which was the fruit of a fact-finding mission made by an Australian parliamentary delegation headed by Australian speaker Ayan Sinclair. The recommendations condemned the aggressive policies of the "Israeli" government.

The delegation visited Syria, Lebanon, Jordan and occupied Palestine.

The memorandum stressed the "Israeli" human rights violations and its carelessness about international laws and conventions. It noted the suffering of Lebanese and Arab detainees in "Israeli" occupation prisons calling for their immediate release.

"Israeli" Political Debate Over the Usefulness of the Occupation of South Lebanon (6)
"Israeli" radio yesterday aired more discussions over the Islamic Resistance's operation last week in the Rihan area. The debate focused on the usefulness of the occupation's stay in Lebanon after the successive losses inflicted on Israeli troops by the Islamic Resistance.

One "Israeli' sergeant was killed and five troops were wounded in the IR's operation in Rihan last Thursday.

"Israeli" Labor party officials warned of the "costly price "Israel" is paying" for its occupation of south Lebanon. They expected Hizbullah to increase its activities against "Israeli" occupation's targets especially after concluding its general congress.

The Labor officials, including Knesset members, said the "Israeli" government's policy towards "the peace process" may lead to further tension in south Lebanon, "Israeli" radio said.

They questioned the usefulness of the "Israeli" army's stay in Lebanon in the aftermath of the successive physical and morale losses inflicted on "Israeli" troops, the radio added.

The Labor officials demanded that the "Israeli" government adopt a swift plan to withdraw from south Lebanon in order to "prevent more Israeli losses".

Elsewhere, "Israeli" groups prepare to resume their activities to pressure the Israeli government in order to pull out of south Lebanon.

The activists told "Israeli" media that they will take some steps within a few days along the Lebanese Palestinian borders in cooperation with parents of "Israeli" occupation troops who are in Lebanon and relatives of "Israeli" troops killed in South Lebanon.

"Israeli" radio's military correspondent in occupied south Lebanon said the occupation army may receive more attacks in the future after Hizbullah's success in penetrating the so-called security zone and "dealing a painful blow to an "Israeli" outpost."

Meanwhile, president of the "Israeli" entity Ezer Weizman said Sunday that "Israel" must withdraw from south Lebanon, adding this has to be carried out "with wisdom."

Weizman made the remarks during visits to the "Israeli" occupation soldiers wounded last week in the occupied zone.

But he did not elaborate his views on how to carry out the pullout.

The latest death brought to seven the death toll of  "Israeli" soldiers who have been killed in occupied south Lebanon since the beginning of the year, according to "Israeli" acknowledgment.

An annual average of 35 "Israeli" soldiers have been killed in the zone in fighting with Lebanese Resistance men in the past years. This has fueled a heated domestic debate and a strong call for a unilateral withdrawal.
