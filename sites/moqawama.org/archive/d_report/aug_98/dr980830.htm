Daily Report
 
 
August Sunday 30 -1998:
 
"Hizbullah has the last word" (1)
"Commenting in yesterdays edition of Haaretz, Zvi Barel asserted that the tactical gains achieved by assassinating Hussam Amin were undermined by the strategic damage the operation caused".

In the past, anyone who launched rockets into "Israel" could count on a counter-strike by the "Israel" Defense Forces; nowadays, Hizbullah has the last word. The primitive Katyusha rocket has become a strategic weapon in Lebanese hands, Barel wrote.

"Significantly, most "Israelis" believe that the Katyusha attack was in response to Amins death, a point propagated by the Israeli army, which sought plaudits for the skill with which the assassination was carried out. Only The Jerusalem Post noted that Hizbullah said they fired the rockets because the SLA had wounded six civilians in Mashghara the same day".

"Ironically, this may lead the "Israeli" army to think twice before conducting similar precision strikes, fearing a backlash directed at settlements in northern "Israel".

Wrote Barel: Every skirmish in Lebanon is now perceived as a potential threat to the well-being of "Israels" northern communities... The problem is that the IDF is no longer able to forgo these killings, or other offensive operations, because its prestige and its deterrent capability are at stake.
However, a security source said: They (Israelis) were looking for guerrilla targets. They found none and there was nothing they could do".

For his part, a western diplomat was quoted as saying: I believe this episode has come to an end. The "Israelis" backed off because the options were not good for them, the diplomat said.

"Although the majority of "Israels" inner security cabinet last Wednesday voted to hit Lebanons infrastructure in reprisal for the Katyusha attack, "Israeli" premier Benjamin Netanyahu and defense minister Yitzhak Mordechai both reportedly ruled out this option. An attack on civilian targets would effectively end the April agreement, opening up northern "Israel" to further
strikes.

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
The Islamic Resistance released at statement in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" Aggressions:
00:35, Rshaf compound raked the perimeters of Haddatha village with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
1:15, Qalaah position combed its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds, while at the same time, Dabsheh and Ali Taher outposts showered the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh and Ali Taher forest with another barrage of heavy machine gun rounds.
5:00, Baraasheit compounds combed the perimeters of Baraasheit village with 12.7mm and Mag machine gun fire, when on the other hand, Beit Yahoun outpost showered the peripheries of Aita El-Jabal village a 12.7mm heavy machine grounds only.
6:45 and until 7:20, Zaffateh and Rihan emplacements pounded Mount-Sojod with twenty 120 and 155mm shells.
7:30, Mount-Zaatar exposed to seven 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal emplacement.
7:40, Meis El-Jabal emplacement hit Mount-Zaatar and the peripheries of Haddatha village with seven 155mm shells.
10;10, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Qana, Aiteit, Siddiqeen and the neighboring areas.
11:40, the warplanes circled, at middle height, over Qlayleh, Hennieh and the nearby areas.
17:40, Izzieh post showered the perimeters of Yohmor with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
21:00, Rihan emplacement hit Mount-Sojod with two 120mm shells and the perimeters of Loweizeh with three others, whereas Sojod outpost fired two 105mm shells and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
Between 21:30 and 21:45, Zaffateh compound shelled the peripheries of Jibaa town with six 155mm missiles.
22:15, Hassan ravine which is located between Tair Harfa and Majdal Zoun came under four 60mm shells emanating from Tair Harfa outpost.
22:35, Meis El-Jabal emplacement targeted Aissieh Valley with five 155mm missiles.
22:50, two flare shells of unknown source and caliber lightened the area of Zhalta.
23:45, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Sector.
23:50, the "Israeli" jets flew, at middle altitude, over the areas of Western Sector.
