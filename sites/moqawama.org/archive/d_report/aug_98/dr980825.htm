Daily Report
 
 
August Tuesday 25 -1998:
 
Introduction
Following successive strikes made by the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants, the "Israeli" occupation forces and their agents of what so-called South Lebanon Army (SLA) resorted to targeting the civilians, an indication of their repeated failures and fiascoes in fighting the Mujahedeen.

Once again, "Israel" made a blatant contravention of the April 1996 Understanding as its troops assassinated a senior Amal officer in a helicopter ambush and inflicted, through bombarding the populated areas, six Lebanese civilians, including a child in Western Beqaa.

However, the IR men retaliated swiftly with a cross-border rocket salvos injuring up to dozen "Israelis".

Hizbullah hammers "Israel" with rockets
The Katyusha rounds capped a day of alarming violence in which the "Israeli"-proxy South Lebanon Army militiaman was killed and six Lebanese civilians, including a child were wounded, in the Western Beqaa.

More than 60 Katyusha rockets, fired in two batches, struck Nahariya and Kiryat Shmona starting at 9.45pm in the biggest attack on targets inside "Israel" since the April 1996 understanding that ended "Israel's" so-called Grapes of Wrath onslaught.

A Hizbullah spokesman told The Daily Star that the Katyushas were in response to the killing of Hussam Amin, Amal's southern commander, as well as the wounding of civilians in Mashghara.

In claiming responsibility, Hizbullah said it had run out of patience with "Israel's" "relentless assaults, destruction, terrorism and systematic killing of our citizens."

In a statement, it said Hizbullah "had been warning "Israel" that its repeated violations of the April understanding would not go unpunished. Yet, it maintained its aggressive strategy leaving us with no choice but to remind the enemy that its civilians will never enjoy peace and security as long as our own are subject to attack."

Added the statement: "While we remain committed to the April understanding, we stand ready for any eventuality if "Israel" persisted in its assaults."

Anticipating retaliation after the morning helicopter raid, the "Israeli" military ordered public shelters to be opened in all Galilee. "Israel's" war minister Yitzhak Mordechai warned the Lebanese resistance against responding, saying: "I would not advise anyone on the other side to take action in response."

But the resistance made good on its pledge not to heed any "Israeli" threat, sending the rockets zooming in nine hours after Amin's assassination and catching Galilee residents by surprise.

The "Israeli" army said that electricity was out in dozens of settlements as a result of the strike. A spokesman for the Kiryat Shmona area hospital said 12 people were being treated for slight injuries, which included trauma and shock (1)

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
Houssam El-Amin, an Amal officer, was killed when Apache helicopters fired two missiles at a blue Mercedes he was driving some 500m from the coastal road at Ras Al-Ain.  The first missile missed and buried itself deep in the road before exploding. The second blew the vehicle off the road, killing Amin instantly.

Arriving at the scene minutes later, Daily Star reporter Nicholas Blanford saw youngsters scrambling through the undergrowth to retrieve pieces of the car and of Amin. Wreckage from the car was strewn over the road and the surrounding banana groves and orange orchard. The vehicle had been transformed into a blackened lump of misshapen metal.

Amal said Amin was 31, born in Matarieh, married with three children and had joined the group in 1982.

Earlier, the residential neighborhoods of the towns of Mashghara, Sohmor and Ain Al-Tineh (western Beqaa) were suddenly shelled from "Israeli" occupation outposts. Child Qassem Hussein Soboh and civilians Mohammed Mahmoud Soboh, Jamal Hachem, Jihad Fawzi Al-Qaq (in Ain al-Tineh), Fadi Haidar Ahmad, and Ali Moussa (in Mashghara) were all wounded by the "Israeli" bombardment.

Furthermore, the houses of Asaad Soboh, Ali Hussein Khozaie, Jamal Nassar, Hussein Hassan Hachem, Ahmed Khalil Najm, Hassan Ali Hachem, Jamal Abdel-Karin Hachem in Mashghara were damaged, besides the houses of Mohammed Ameen Hachem and Ibrahim Mohammad Soboh in Ain Al-Tineh.

The Ain Al-Tineh school was also damaged along with a number of cars.

The "Israeli" occupation's aggressions today included an air raid 14:20 on the Ain Al-Tineh hilltops.

After 18:00 the enemy artillery in the Zohaira outpost bombarded the western outskirts of the town of Yater with a number of 155mm rounds (3)

Netanyahu: Hizbullah's bombs create painful damage (7)
(So-called) "Israeli" occupation's OC Northern Command Gabi Ashkenazi ordered the "Israeli" troops in the outposts of the Iqleem Al-Tuffah and Nabatieh areas not to travel in unarmed military cars, security sources in the south told the Al-Sharq Al-Awsat newspaper.

Ashkenazi said only three occupation troops can board a vehicle in the occupied zone and that troops, not monitoring or guarding, must remain inside shelters and bunkers around the clock.

For his part. "Israeli" war minister Yitzhak Mordechai gave orders that military information must not be disclosed to the Lahd collaborator militia, especially the dates of "Israeli" units and patrols' movements, the daily added.

"Israeli" radio quoted enemy Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu as saying "it is difficult for me to say when we will be able to leave Lebanon with appropriate security arrangements."

However, I can say that we have never stopped our efforts and attempts, publicized and otherwise, to achieve this goal, Netanyahu said.

Until this goal is achieved "Israeli" troops will protect the north with being subjected to more Hizbullah bombs, he added.

The enemy Prime Minister talked about efforts to diminish "the painful and horrible damage" caused by these bombs.

Elsewhere, enemy internal security minister Avigdor Kahlani who advocated withdrawal from south Lebanon said Lebanon can extend its sovereignty on its southern territories. However, he stressed the need to guarantee the security of the residential communities in the north.

The Golan Heights is a security zone for "Israel", he said during a tour in Northern occupied Palestine, calling for not discussing land issues until a settlement is reached.

Sheikh Qassem: The Resistance is Much better than it was in 1996 (8)
Hizbullah deputy secretary general said the regional and southern situation does not enable the enemy leaders to mass political support for any large-scale aggression against Lebanon.

The enemy leadership does not have a plan for a feasible political plan especially after the failure of the Lebanon First project, the Jezzine First, the pressure to separate the Lebanese and Syrian tracks, and finally the failure of the proposal for implementing (UN security Council) Resolution 425 which was booby-trapped with security arrangements, he said.

Sheikh Qassem stressed that the performance and methods of the Islamic Resistance is much better than they were in 1996, whereas the enemy's status on the ground is not better than it was during the 1996 aggression.
