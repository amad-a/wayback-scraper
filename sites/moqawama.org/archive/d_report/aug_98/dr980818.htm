Daily Report
 
 
August Tuesday 18 -1998:
 

In spite of the inspection tour made by "Israelis" Minister of War Yitzhac Mordechai in the occupied enclave of South Lebanon and the military command orders for the "Israeli" soldiers deployed in Lebanon to be in full readiness, the "Israeli" Army didn't succeeded in restoring the confidence to its soldiers.

Nevertheless, the "Israeli" aggressions continued in land, sea and air violating the Lebanese sovereignty and the international standards.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" gunners shelled before noon Litani and Habboush rivers and the peripheries of Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr, Yohmor and Mazraat Hamra.

In Hasbaya region, Abou Qamha position targeted Dahr heights and Meimess Sector with several missiles igniting a number of blazes.

Also, it was reported that a Lahd's Militia foot-patrol had crossed over its Zaghleh position and advanced about two kms towards the northern part of Dabghi forest where it set up several ambushes. This was accompanied by firing heavy machine gun rounds from Kawkaba post at Dahr Mountain and Qilya perimeters.

In Western Sector, the outskirts of Sawaneh, Qabrikha and Majdal Selem exposed to "Israeli" artillery shelling.

Earlier, the sectors of Froun, Ghandourieh and Alman witnessed at night intermittent artillery shelling (1).

On the other hand, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it mentioned further "Israeli" aggressions (2):

1:45, the espionage planes circled in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
7:00, nine 155mm missiles originating from Shraifeh position struck Mazraat Hamra.

Meanwhile, Sojod Mountain subjected to four 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.

23:15, Meis El-Jabal emplacement fired six 155mm shells at Haddatha woodlands, whereas heavy machine gun rounds were unleashed at the village of Haddatha causing damage to Hussein Sabra's car.
23:45, Zaatar Mountain came under five 155mm missiles originating from Meis El-Jabal emplacement.

However, the "Israeli" soldiers deported the national Ahmad Saad to the out of the occupied Lebanese areas. Saad had been held prisoner in Khiam detention camp for forty days (3).

Islamic Resistance Operations (4):
The IR statement assumed the responsibility of the following attacks on the "Israeli" occupation forces:
1:00, the IR men observed enemy movements inside Dabsheh outpost. Shortly afterwards, the squad of martyr Mohammad Hazimeh targeted them with the suitable arms scoring direct hits.
13:45, the IR militants spotted an "Israeli force entering into Zimraya position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Bassam Abbas bombarded intensely the position chalking up direct goals.
18:15, the squad of two martyrs Youssef Baddah and Hassan Fakhreddine attacked Tair Harfa outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct and definite hits. Also, the smoke columns were seen emanating from the outpost.
18:40, the IR men spotted enemy movements inside Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the group of two martyrs Ali Olleik and Adnan Kashmar targeted the post with the suitable weapons chalking up direct and certain goals. Moreover, the smoke columns were seen, by the naked eye, emanating from inside it.
22:00, the IR militants observed two Mirkava tanks stationed at Sojod outpost. Then, the squad of both martyrs Abdallah Hassan Wehbi and Assaf Hassan Mohanna targeted them with the rockets scoring direct hits.
22:35, the IR fighters observed enemy movements inside Rshaf outpost. Then, the group of martyr Samir Akil hit them with the proper weapons scoring direct goals. At the same time, the squad of martyr Abbas Hammoudi attacked Haddatha compound with the appropriate arms chalking up direct goals.
