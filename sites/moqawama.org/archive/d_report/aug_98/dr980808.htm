Daily Report
 
 
August Saturday 8 -1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions (1):
The "Israeli" gunners shelled, at 5:30pm, Srobbin and west of Yater in Western Sector of South Lebanon.

Also, the southern part of Qilya village exposed to a number of missiles striking the houses of Mohammad Kamil Ali and Hassan Al-Youssefi with shrapnels.

Furthermore, Yohmor ravine, the surroundings of Berkat Jabbour and Litani River watercourse came under several 155mm missiles causing material losses only.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it recorded further information over the "Israeli" aggression:
4:30, Rihan emplacement targeted Sojod Mountain with three 120mm shells.
8:15, an M.K. espionage aircraft soared over the western areas of Chmestar town.
9:35, an M.K. espionage aircraft hovered in the airspace of Baalback city.
10:45, the reconnaissance plane flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
11:05, the reconnaissance planes flew over Zibqeen and Jibal El-Botom villages.
14:20, the warplanes flew low over Jbeil region.
14:30, the "Israeli" fighters circled over the Lebanon's Western Mountain Series.
17:00, Sojod outpost hit Sojod Mountain with seven 81mm shells, whereas Zohaira compound bombarded the perimeters of Zibqeen and Jibal El-Botom with fifteen 155mm missiles.
Meanwhile, the southern part of Zibqeen subjected to seven 155mm shells originating from Yarin emplacement.
17:10, Zaffateh position targeted the peripheries of Loweizeh village with two 155mm missiles, while at the same time, Zohaira post pounded the outskirts of Yater with five 155mm missiles.
17:15, Sojod Mountain subjected to three 81mm shells emanating from Sojod outpost and two 155mm missiles originating from Zaffateh position. At the same time, Zohaira position shelled the road between Siddiqeen and Kafra villages with ten 155mm missiles, whereas Yarin emplacement targeted the area between Yater and Zibqeen with six 155mm missiles.
Elsewhere, three 155mm missiles of unknown source struck the road linking between Kafra and Zibqeen.
20:00, Hardoun and Qalaah posts showered the peripheries of Majdal Zoun with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
20:10, the reconnaissance planes flew, at middle height, over Jibsheit and Harouf villages.
20:25, an M.K. espionage aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Anqoun, Bnaafoul and the nearby areas.
20:45, Shamaa position showered the perimeters of Shamaa village with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire, when on the other hand, Hardoun outpost raked its surroundings with the same caliber rounds.
20:50, a drone reconnaissance plane flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
22:00, an M.K. espionage aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
22:05, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Jowaya and its vicinity.

Islamic Resistance operations (3)
In its statement, the IR assumed the responsibility for the following attacks:
17:35, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades fighters attacked Mashaaroun position with the adequate weapons scoring definite goals. In the meantime, another squad targeted Houla position with the suitable arms chalking up direct hits.
19:30, the IR squad of martyr Fouad Rida Aoun attacked a radar installation at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.
