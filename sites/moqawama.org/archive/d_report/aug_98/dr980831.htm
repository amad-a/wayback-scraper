"Israeli" aggressions wound a boy in Nabatieh and damage houses
"In a fresh "Israeli" violation of the April Understanding which coincided with a meeting of the understanding's panel, "Israeli" artillery shelling of residential areas in the town of Kfar Romman at dawn damaged the house of Hani Khalil whose son, Mohammed, 16, was lightly wounded. The "Israeli" bombardment also damaged the houses of Mohammed Ali Assi and Qassem Bilal without causing any casualties".

"Moreover, at dawn and in the morning the woods of Nabatieh and Ali Al-Taher were hit by "Israeli" mortars while enemy warplanes and helicopters circled over these areas".

"Before noon, "Israeli" warplanes staged successive mock air raids over Nabatieh and Iqlim al-Touffah while Israeli 155mm rounds fired from the enemy outpost of Dhaireh fell on the area between Majdel Zoen and Zibqeen".

"Meanwhile, "Israeli" warplanes flew at various altitudes in the skies of the villages of the Tyre and Bint Jbeil areas. The occupation's artillery in Dhor Mais pounded the Jabal al-Zatar area (between the towns of Tibneen and Baraasheet) with a number of 155mm rounds".

"Shelling also targeted Wadi al-Qaisseh and the Jib Swaid area with similar rounds, as MK espionage drones circled in the area".

"In the afternoon, enemy artillery shelled the outskirts of Zawtar al-Gharbeih, Zawtar al-Sharqieh, al-Marbaa area, Ali al-Taher woods with 155mm rounds amidst several mock air raids in the Nabatieh and Iqlim al-Touffah skies and other southern areas".

"At 15:30, "Israeli" occupation forces pounded the outskirts of Majdal Zoen hitting a number of houses with shrapnel. The town's water reservoir was damaged for the second time within a few days. It is the only source of water for the town's residents" (1)

AUMG Discuss Eight Complaints
"The April Understanding Monitoring Group (AUMG) is currently meeting at the southern Lebanon UNIFIL HQ in Naqoura on the Lebanese-Palestinian border to discuss eight Lebanese and "Israeli" complaints".

"Lebanon filed three complaints with the panel over Tuesday's "Israeli" shelling of Mashghara, in the southern sector of the Beqaa Valley in eastern Lebanon, which left five civilians, including a six-year-old boy, wounded. Beirut also protested last week's shelling of the southern villages of Arab Salim and Jarjoua, which wounded one civilian and damaged several homes and cars.

Lebanon filed a fourth complaint today over the "Israeli" shelling of Kfar Romman at dawn today".

"The monitoring committee, which includes delegates from Lebanon, Syria, France, the US, and "Israel", was formed after the 1996 Israeli aggression.  The understanding states that combatants in south Lebanon must spare civilians the risks of attacks.  The Israelis, however, repeatedly breach the understanding. Twenty-three Lebanese civilians have been killed and 84 others wounded by "Israeli" fire since the beginning of 1998.

"Israel" lodged a complaint over a rocket attack by the Islamic Resistance on northern occupied Palestine colonies Tuesday which wounded 12 "Israelis". The Hizbullah attack was in retaliation for the repeated "Israeli" violations".

"The "Israeli" three other protests concerned shells which fell last week on the village of Ramieh, claims of mortar attacks from populated areas in Jibaa in southern Lebanon, and the injury of a 65-year-old teacher in a suspicious roadside bomb in the occupied Jezzine enclave" (3)

"However, August was notable for being the first month this year in which no Lebanese civilians were killed. The month also registered a rise in "Israeli" casualties and witnessed the first "Israelis" to be wounded since the 1996 April war".

"According to a Daily Star count, fatalities included two "Israeli" soldiers, one "Israeli" engineer working in the zone, one Hizbullah fighter, five SLA militiamen, one Amal guerrilla and one communist fighter. The wounded included 13 Lebanese civilians, 19 "Israelis", 11 "Israeli" soldiers, two Hizbullah fighters and one Polish Unifil soldier"...(4)

Nine Occupation Troops Wounded in an IR Operation and a Helicopter Crash
"There have been more "Israeli" measures reflecting fear of losses in the ranks of occupation troops that may create impact on "Israeli" public opinion", security sources said.

"An "Israeli" U.S.-made Bell 206 helicopter crash showed that the new "Israeli" measures include restricting occupation officers' movement between occupied Palestine and occupation outposts to late overnight times for fear of Islamic Resistance (IR) attacks", the sources added.

"The crash of the helicopter at dawn today near al-Dabsheh outpost injured four enemy troops, three officers, and a sergeant. Two of them were reported to be in serious condition".

"Meanwhile, enemy command remained silent about the operation which IR carried out at the Dabsheh outpost".

"AFP said five "Israeli" troops were wounded in the attack".

'The new losses are expected to heat up the debate in the enemy entity over the usefulness of occupying parts of Lebanon, especially as occupation forces have repeatedly proved to be helpless before IR's qualitative and increasing operations. This has recently been a source of embarrassment for the enemy's political and military command".

Nam countries back Beirut's rejection of "Israeli" 425 offer
DURBAN, South Africa: "Foreign minister Fares Boueiz welcomed the Non-Aligned Movements decision to back Beirut in rejecting "Israels" conditional offer to withdraw from the south".

'At a two-day meeting of the movements foreign ministers, a draft communiqué issued on Sunday said that "Israel" should implement UN security council Resolution 425 unconditionally as written".

"Israels" May proposal, which asked Lebanon to guarantee the security of its northern borders in return for a pullout, was rejected outright as an attempt to undermine Lebanons sovereignty".

"Boueiz contacted his Egyptian, Jordanian, Iraqi, Iranian and Kuwaiti counterparts, among others, to thank them for their support".

"He also told them of "Israels" recent threats to hit economic targets such as power stations, roads and bridges in Lebanon, in the event of attacks against its occupation forces"... (7)

FTR's MP Musawi Rules out an "Israeli" Large-Scale Aggression Soon
"The "Israeli" enemy is in a big quagmire due to the Islamic Resistance's (IR) increasing operations and the painful blows inflicted by the Resistance men in the ranks of the occupation troops, Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) member" MP Ammar Musawi said.

"In a radio interview, Musawi ruled out a large-scale "Israeli" aggression "at least in the near future".
"The enemy does not have a lot of options, and it cannot achieve any results by such aggressions; things may even backfire as the "Israeli" quagmire may get wider and deeper. We think the enemy command is trying to avoid this", he said.

"MP Musawi expected the enemy to return to observing the April Understanding  "with the permanent possibility of repeated violations by the "Israeli" side, IR is concerned with retaliation with similar attacks."

"Although the monitoring group is very limited, its existence is better than not having it, for it is witnessing the "Israeli" going-too-far aggressions, the Hizbullah MP said.

Irslan in US: Resistance is an Acquired Right for Every People Whose Land is Occupied
"Minister of Expatriates Talal Irslan called for the creation of a Lebanese lobby in the countries where there are Lebanese immigrants".

"Lebanon, the state and the people, stick to Resistance as means to liberate every inch occupied by "Israel", Irslan said.

"He was speaking at an honorary ceremony held by "the Lebanese Club" and "the Druze Association" in Detroit (Michigan).

"The Resistance has preserved not only the dignity of Lebanon but also that of all the Arabs", he said.

"This political national stand needs your support, he told Lebanese expatriates".

"They accuse the Resistance in Lebanon of being terrorism; is liberating the land terrorism? Is defending the homeland terrorism? Is resisting occupation terrorism?" Irslan asked.

"Resistance of occupation is a human right stated by the Human Rights' Charter. It is an acquired right for every people whose land is occupied", he said.

"The minister called on immigrant Lebanese to stick to their national identity, not the sectarian one, "along with adhering to our, heritage history and culture."
