Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 5 -1998:
 

"Israel" experiments new missiles on Lebanese Southerners (1)
Lebanese media today reported "Israel" is testing new weapons on civilian Southerners. Low-flying guided missiles fired by helicopter are being tested in South Lebanon before being placed on the international arms market. The "Israeli" "mini-cruise-missiles" have since May killed one civilian, wounded another, and injured three resistance men The Daily Star Newspaper today reported a striking editorial saying:

Perhaps we should not be overly surprised at the revelation that the "Israelis" are using Lebanese as human guinea pigs to test a new and still-secret "mini cruise missile" in the south.

For 30 years the Israeli army has deployed the most up-to-date armaments, both ground- and air-based- often courtesy of the US government - against Lebanese civilians which has resulted in tens of thousands of deaths.

Yet the "Israeli" assumption that Lebanese lives are so valueless that they can be used as cheap targets to perfect their latest weapons is still a demonstration of the Jewish state's appalling cynicism.

That the "Israelis" will some day profit from the deaths of Lebanese civilians once the Dandy missile is placed on the international arms market is additionally odious.

The Mouqalled brothers, who were targeted by the Dandy missile last May, were not members of the resistance but farmers who had been tending their beehives all day. Although the location of the hives is on the edge of a particularly volatile sector of the occupation zone, the area is often frequented by villagers of Arab Salim, who pick the abundant Zaatar which grows there.

If as appears likely, it was the "Israeli" soldiers manning the Soweida position, 3km away from the two men, who relayed their presence to the helicopter, the troops would have seen that the Mouqalleds were clearly unarmed. Resistance fighters would have attempted to conceal themselves.
The "Israelis" even had the temerity to admit they targeted the Mouqalleds with the missile, claiming the two men were helping the resistance.

An unimpressed monitoring group subsequently condemned "Israel" for violating the April agreement; although that will have little consequence on the Mouqalled family who lost one son in the attack and had another badly burned.

The missile has mainly been witnessed by Unifil personnel - at Majdal Selem and in the Norwegian area of the zone.

In a typical example of "Israeli" arrogance, they have ignored all Unifil's requests for information on the weapon and banned the peacekeepers from approaching the blast site in the Norwegian sector until they had had a chance to remove the missile's vital components.

There is little the Lebanese government alone can do to stop "Israel" from treating the zone as a convenient testing range. That, unfortunately, takes a political will from the international community. Judging from past experience, it will remain silent.

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
In a remarkable military development, the "Israeli" occupation forces pounded, at 5:45am, their heavy artillery shells at the road linking the villages of Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen. One civilian was killed and another one was wounded as their car was hit by an the "Israeli" shell. Also, several shells slammed on the perimeters of Zibqeen Majdal Zoun and Mansouri. The civilian Alwan Ghannam, 27, from Mansouri village was injured.

Between 6:30 and 8:30pm, the shells extended to include Majdal Selem, Khorbet Selem, Sawaneh and Qaissieh Valley. This was accompanied by firing heavy machine gun rounds at the surroundings of Baraasheit.

Elsewhere, Kawkaba position, manned by Lahd's militiamen, fired heavy machine gun rounds at the peripheries of Qilya, Mazraat Ahmadieh and Dallafeh. Then, a number of mortar missiles slammed on Litani River watercourse and the southern part of Mazraat Loussi.

Also, Daidabeh hill exposed, at 2:00pm, to three 130mm shells and other mortar missiles emanating from Ain Qinya position sparking a huge blaze at the outskirts of Meimess village.

Israel shut off a mosque in Jafa (5)
After the Israeli authorities had shut down Talbieh mosque in Jafa, the dignitaries of Jafa, which was occupied in 1948, called during a meeting held at the Islamic Club for a demonstration to be launched from Kabir (great) mosque to Talbieh mosque.

In the meantime, Israels Interior Security Minister Avigdor Kahlani had revoked his visit to Jafa in a bid not to raise the sentiments of its population.

Earlier, the dignitaries of Jafa asked Kahlani for concealing his visit for he is responsible for closing the mosque.

In this regard, a lot of Israeli policemen stormed, at dawn, into Talbieh mosque in virtue of an administrative order issued by the Israels official of absentees properties.
