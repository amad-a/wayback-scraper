Daily Report
 
 
August Saturday 1-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" occupation emplacements (1) of Zaffateh and Mahmoudieh shelled Tasseh Well watercourse and the heights and forests of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh, Mazraat Aqmata and Loweizeh villages.

Also, the "Israeli" batteries stationed at Shraifeh compound slammed, at midnight, Berkat Jabbour, Litani River banks and Qatraneh woodlands with twenty 155mm missiles. This was accompanied by firing flash shells over the western hilltops of Hermon Mountain, Loussi sector and Wastani Mountain.

However, the Islamic Resistance mentioned in a statement (2) the following "Israeli" aggressions:
00:15, Habboush bridge exposed to several 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds from Soweida outpost.
00:35, one flare shell of unknown origin and caliber lightened the area of Bissri.
1:05, Meis El-Jabal emplacement targeted the outskirts of Haddatha with one 155mm missiles.
5:15, the perimeters of Jibaa village subjected to three 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
11:50, Blat post fired two 120mm shells landed near Zibqeen village.
13:30, Bir Kallab outpost unleashed one 81mm shell at Aqmata plain and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings, whereas the batteries deployed at Aramta targeted Arid Zennar area with four 105mm shells.
14:35, Roum post shelled Azour hill with a number of 120mm missiles.
15:00, Zaffateh compound shelled Sojod Mountain with five 155mm missiles, when on the other hand, Bir Kallab outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
15:15, Zaffateh position bombarded the peripheries of Loweizeh village with two 155mm missiles and Arid Zennar area with three others.
16:50, Meshnaqa position hit Joune town with one 120mm shell.
16:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over East Saida areas.
19:15, Zaffateh position shelled Tasseh Well with three 155mm missiles.
19:20, Rihan emplacement shelled the road between Loweizeh and Jarjoua villages with four 120mm shells.
19:25, Habboush River watercourse came under three 120mm shells.
19:35, Meshnaqa position hit Joune town with one 120mm shell landed near the school.
21:15, Toumat Niha compound slammed Maramel heights and the 1700th Hill with fifteen 60 and 81mm shells and combed the southwestern heights of Mashghara, Maramel area and the 1700th Hill with 12.7 and 23mm heavy machine gun fire.
At the same time, Hmayed position unleashed several flare shells over Oyoun Valley.
23:00, Zaffateh position shelled Mazraat Hamra with three 155mm missiles.
23:05, Rshaf outpost targeted the perimeters of Haddatha village with three 81mm shells.
23:40, Haddatha compound raked its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Red Cross returns Arnoun man's remains for burial
The remains of Hussein Attieh, who was killed in a roadside bomb blast in Arnoun over a week ago, were collected on Saturday by representatives of the International Committee for the Red Cross.

Mohammad Bizri, the ICRC chief in Nabatieh, and two members of the Lebanese Red Cross spent an hour collecting the remains of Attieh which lay scattered in a field beside the scene of the explosion.

Attieh was blown to pieces on July 24 when a bomb exploded in Arnoun, 5km east of Nabatieh.
The village lies in a no-man's land beside the occupation zone and is overlooked by the "Israeli" position at Beaufort castle.

The "Israelis" refused permission for the ICRC to enter the village to collect Attieh's remains until Saturday.

The body parts, which were placed inside a plastic bag, were later returned to Attieh's parents for burial.
