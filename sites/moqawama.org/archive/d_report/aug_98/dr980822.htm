Daily Report
 
 
August Saturday 22 -1998:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions (1):
00:05, the "Israeli" choppers and espionage planes circled in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
00:10, one helicopter hovered over Qlayleh village, whereas an M.K. surveillance aircraft soared over Zahrani and its vicinity.
00:15, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over the areas of Jibsheit and Adsheit.

1:15, a drone reconnaissance plane hovered low over Nabatieh region.
1:30, the helicopters circled over the sea opposite to the seashore of Adloun and Sarafand towns.
1:40, the helicopters flew low over the villages of Sharqieh, Doueir, Jibsheit, Toul and Harouf, but they were attracted fire by the Lebanese Army anti-aircraft guns.
1:45, the helicopters tried to land at the area between Deir El-Zahrani and Kfour, but they were driven off by the anti-aircraft guns deployed by the Lebanese Army in the region.
1:55, the helicopters continued flying over Deir Zahrani, Bfarwa Valley and the coast line of Western Sector. The helicopters were met by the Lebanese Army anti-aircraft guns. At the same time, the "Israeli' fighters circled, at middle height, in the airspace of Beirut.
2:00, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Jibsheit village.

2:15, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Deir Keifa, Chehabieh, Hallousieh and Hmeiri villages.

17:30, an M.K. surveillance plane flew in the airspace of Baalback.

Islamic Resistance Operations (2)
In its statement, the IR reported that, at 15:15, the group of martyr Bilal Ahmad Zayour ambushed a motorized patrol belonging to Lahd's Militia as it was in its way to Bir Kallab outpost. When it became at about 100ms from the position, the militants attacked them with the machine guns and rockets hitting directly M-113 military vehicle.

At the same time, the squad of two martyrs Ali Kamel Jahjah and Hussein Ali Rida fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying its main fortification and barring it from giving support to the attacked force.
