Daily Report
 
 
August Friday 28 -1998:
 
Introduction
Following a day of relative calm, "Israel" today renewed its attacks against the liberated areas and unarmed people in South Lebanon.

In the meantime, many of Lebanese dignitaries expressed their support for the Islamic Resistance military operations especially the latest strong Katyusha rocket attack on the "Israeli" entity.

"Israeli" Aggressions (1):
00:15, the warplanes flew over Jibsheit area.
00:30, the jet airplanes circled in the airspace of Beirut.
1:00, a drone espionage plane flew over Ghassanieh town and its vicinity, whereas an M.K. surveillance aircraft hovered low over the area stretching between Loubieh and Kharayeb villages.
1:05, a drone espionage plane flew low over Semmaieh and Ras-El Ain areas.
1:45, Zaffateh compound pounded Litani River watercourse, with six 155mm missiles, when on the other hand, Abbad post targeted Hojeir Valley with three others. Meanwhile, the warplanes circled over the sea opposite to Saksakieh and Khaizaran coast towns.
1:50, three 155mm missiles originating from Meis El-Jabal emplacement slammed into the perimeters of Touline village.
2:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Naameh area.
2:35, a drone and M.K. surveillance planes flew over Naameh town.
2:40, the helicopters soared, at middle altitude, over Jibsheit and Nabatieh regions.
7:30, Blat post unleashed four 120mm shells at the area between Shamaa and Chihine, whereas Hardoun outpost showered the perimeters of Majdal Zoun with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. In the meantime, Shamaa post raked its surroundings with a barrage of the same heavy machine gun rounds.
7:50, Tair Harfa compound bombarded the peripheries of Majdal Zoun with about ten 105mm tank rounds hitting a water tank.
8:30, Zohaira position fired four 155mm missiles at the perimeters of Majdal Zoun and several others at the west of Yater village.
9;40, four 120mm shells emanating from Shamaa post struck the peripheries of Majdal Zoun, whereas the road linking between Zibqeen and Hennieh villages came under six 155mm missiles.
9:45, the northern perimeters of Zibqeen exposed to four 155mm missiles originating from Zohaira position.
10:45, Meis El-Jabal emplacement hit Ghordaleh area with a number of 155mm missiles.
11:15, the warplanes broke the sound barrier over Beirut and flew in the airspace of Central Beqaa areas.
11:20, the "Israeli" fighters circled over the Lebanese Western Mountain Series.
13:15, Mount-Sojod subjected to one 120mm shell originating from Rihan emplacement.
13:30, Rihan emplacement unleashed seven 120mm shells at Tasseh Well and one at Mount-Sojod.
13:45, Tasseh Well exposed to four 120mm phosphorous shells emanating from Rihan emplacement. The shelling ignited several blazes in the area. At the same time, the helicopters hovered over Rihan and Kassara area.
14:00, Rihan emplacement bombarded Mount-Sojod with nine 120mm shells and Tasseh Well with four others.
14:20, four 120mm shells originating from Rihan emplacement slammed on Mount-Sojod.
17:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Nabi Sheit town.
18:55, Rihan emplacement hit Mount-Sojod with four 120mm shells.
19:05, one 120mm shell emanating from Rihan emplacement struck Tasseh Well.
Between 19:10 and 19:25, Rihan emplacement pounded Mount-Sojod with seven 120mm shells.
20:10, the warplanes flew, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Beirut.
20:20, Mount-Sojod came under four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan emplacement.
20:45, Meis El-Jabal emplacement unleashed four 155mm missiles at the perimeters of Touline village.
23:30, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle height, over Semmaieh, Maallieh and the nearby areas.

IR Operations (2):
The IR announced, in its statement, that the squad of two martyrs Ahmad and Haidar Borji detonated a roadside bomb as a foot-patrol was moving on the road between Jibbain and Chihine. The militants reported that a number of casualties were inflicted.

In this regard, Lahd's Militia admitted that one of its members was wounded seriously in this operation. Agence France Presse quoted security source in Marjeyoun as saying that the militiaman was seriously wounded.

Attitudes over Katyusha rocket attack against "Israel's" enemy entity (3)

"Sheikh Yazbek Warns the Enemy: Aggressions will be Retaliated for Hizbullah official, representative of Ayatollah Sayed Ali Khamenei in Lebanon his Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek said "If the enemy's arm is long, the Resistance's arm is over the enemy's head. When the enemy messes with the security of our people, it will have no security."

"If the enemy shells our unarmed people, we will shell it, if they hit our infrastructure we will hit theirs", Sheikh Yazbek said.

Sheikh Qawooq: the Enemy's Security is Unstable while the Resistance is Strong and Invulnerable
"Hizbullah's official in the south area his Eminence Sheikh Nabil Qawooq said the "Israeli" enemy's security today is more unstable, while the Resistance is stronger and more invulnerable".

"Speaking after offering congratulations and condolences to the family of Amal's Martyr Hussam Al-Ameen, Qawooq said "The enemy wanted to address a message to Lebanon, the answer came from Hizbullah and all the Lebanese, stating that the whole of Lebanon is Resistance."

"Amal MP Ali Khreis who was at Al-Ameens' during Qawooq's visit commended the "struggle of the brothers in Hizbullah".

"He wanted Sheikh Qawooq to convey his greeting to Hizbullah's secretary general his Eminence Sayed Hassan Nassrallah and to the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen".

Ayatollah Fadlallah: Bombardment of Colonies is Clear Message that Aggression will not Go Unpunished

"Ayatollah Sayed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah talked about the heroic retaliation of the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen for "Israeli" aggressions against the occupation's settlements (in Northern Palestine)".

"It was a clear message that aggressions will not go unpunished," he said.

"In the Friday Khotba he delivered at Imamien Hassanien (A.S.) Mosque, Sayed Fadlallah said "The enemy tried to threaten with invasion or attacking infrastructure in Lebanon, however it restored balance to its rhetoric and lodged a complaint with the April Understanding monitoring group."

"Contrary to some analyses which said the enemy wanted to bring down or change the rules of the understanding to make it a negotiation channel, it is in no one's interest to annul the understanding; moreover, there is no chance to develop the understanding at the political level", Ayatollah Fadlallah said.

"The Resistance's strikes were able to maintain the "Israeli" quagmire at the internal level, with calls made for a unilateral withdrawal.  There are a lot of complications that bar the enemy from launching a large-scale aggression; however, we always need to be on the alert, for the enemy is treacherous, especially since the regional and international complications may bring about some security and political surprises", he added.

Ayatollah Shamseddeen: The Enemy Tried all Forms of War and Invasion and Failed
"Head of the Islamic Shiite Higher Council Ayatollah Sheikh Mohammed Mahdi Shamseddeen said the "Israeli" enemy has tried all forms of all-out invasions and wars against Lebanon and here we are, still standing up heads high with a strong national unity."

"Speaking at the Friday Khotba, Shamseddeen said there is unequaled consensus on Resistance, while ""Israel"" suffers internal divisions due to the shame and disgrace inflicted on its army by the resistance operations."

""Israel's" strength and nuclear bombs are of no avail in the confrontations with the Resistance men", he said.

Abdel-Meguid: "Israel" will Pull out Thanks to the Lebanese People's Steadfastness and Resistance":

"Secretary-general of the Arab League Dr Esmat Abdel-Meguid today described the situation in southern Lebanon as "dangerous" due to "Israeli" provocation".

"Speaking after a meeting with President Elias Hrawi in Baabda, Abdel-Meguid said he discussed with the president the situation in the south, adding "Israel" should implement UN Resolution 425 unconditionally".

"The resolution calls on "Israel" to immediately and unconditionally withdraw from Lebanese territories".

"The Arab League chief said "Israel" will withdraw from Lebanon due to the steadfastness of southerners and resistance operations. He also reiterated the Arab League's support to Lebanon."
"Abdel-Meguid condemned "Israel's" continuous attacks on civilians in South Lebanon".
