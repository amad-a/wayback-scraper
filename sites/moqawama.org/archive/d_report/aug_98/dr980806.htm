Introduction
The "Israeli" occupation forces violated today the April 1996 Understanding as they shelled Kfar Tibbnit and Froun villages inflicting a number of civilians and causing damages to certain houses and groves.

Also, the "Israeli" air force carried out its routine espionage flights breaching the Lebanese national sovereignty.

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
The "Israeli"-occupied outposts of Ali Taher and Borj shelled after midnight the eastern quarter of Kfar Tibbnit town. Abdel-Hassan Bashar, 110-year-old, suffered light lesions in his face and hands by shards of flying glass when a missile landed near his house at Kfar Tibbnit.

Furthermore, the houses of Abdel-Amir Fadel, Mohammad Hassan Ajram, Dr. Ali Hassan Ajram, Youssef Abdallah El-Akhras, Mohammad Ali Yassine and Nassib Abdo Bashar were damaged as a result of the "Israeli" shelling.

Also,several members of the families of Mohammad Ali Yassine, Amin Yassine and Nassib Abdo Bashar had escaped injury as a number of 60mm mortar shells landed around.

Besides, the electricity network in the village was damaged as well, but repairs were quickly made.
In the meantime, the southern quarter of Kfar Tibbnit was also hit by similar bombardment. One of the shells hit a house belonging to an Internal Security Forces officer, Ali Mohammad Hayek, but he, his wife and their five children all escaped unscathed.

Elsewhere, the heights and forests of Iqleem Al-Tuffah, the outskirts of Mazraat Hima and Mazraat Hamra and the plain of Yohmor Shkeif subjected before midnight to the "Israeli" shelling.

Meanwhile, the villages of Zibqeen, Mansouri, Hennieh, Yater, Srobbin, Qabrikha, Froun, Ghandourieh, Borj Qalaouieh, Sawaneh and Haris and Qaissieh Valley exposed throughout the night to an intermittent but random "Israeli" shells.

At 7 O'clock in the morning, Qantara outpost bombarded the outskirts of Froun village with a number of missiles and its internal quarters with a barrage of heavy machine gun rounds. Several fires were sparked in the tobacco field whereas windows glass of many houses were shattered.

In the meantime, Qatraneh Mountain, Mazraat Loussi, Dahr Mountain, Mazraat Ahmadieh, Sraireh perimeters and Litani River banks exposed throughout the night to mortar shells, 155mm heavy artillery missiles and tank rounds.

Moreover, the "Israeli" shelling extended to include Wastani Mountain, Dabghi plain and the peripheries of Maidoun, Qilya, Dallafeh and Mazraat Ain Fjour.

Vatican miffed at Netanyahu meddling in Choice of Bishop (4)
"Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has angered the Vatican by trying to influence us choice of a new bishop for "Israel's" Christian Arab minority, Catholic Church sources said Thursday.

Netanyahu tried to oppose the nomination of Bishop Boutros Mualem as the new bishop of the Greek Catholic church in Galilee, the sources said, apparently judging the cleric too pro-Palestinian. Instead, Netanyahu pressed the Vatican to appoint an Arab Israeli priest, Father Ernil Shufani, the sources said.

The Vatican rejected Shufani, who is not popular among Arab Israeli Christians and who has been targeted by allegations of mismanaging church property. Mualem, previously posted to Brazil, was subsequently chosen by the Greek Catholic synod to replace the current bishop of Galilee, Maximos Sollum, who is retiring. Aides to Netanyahu acknowledged Thursday that he had met with Shufani and discussed the appointment, but they would not elaborate. The Maariv newspaper quoted a Vatican spokesman, Chero Santaniti, as saying that any attempt by governments to influence Church appointments was "red flag" for relations. "Only dictatorial nations try to influence these decisions," he was quoted as saying.
