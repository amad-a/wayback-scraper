Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 19 -1998:
 

Introduction
After an inspection tour made by "Israel's" Minister of War yesterday, the South Lebanon witnessed today a military escalation during which "Israel" breached the April 1996 Understanding.

The "Israeli" shelling extended to include most of the areas adjacent to the green line. The aggressions which launched in land, sea and air caused heavy material losses, especially in the areas of Iqleem Al-Tuffah where several houses and cars were damaged and number of brush fires were sparked.

At Ain Tineh village in Western Beqaa, a five-member family were escaped unscathed as an "Israeli" missile hit their house.

Moreover, a Polish Unifil soldier was wounded when an "Israeli" shell hit his truck on the road linking between Zibqeen and Hennieh.

In retaliation for these savage aggressions, the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants carried out a series of military operations killing one soldier and wounding four others, two seriously.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" jets staged, at about 10:30am, a sortie on Mlita hilltops firing one air-to-surface rocket causing a blast echoed throughout the area. The intense smoke columns were seen emanating from the site of explosion. The "Israeli" jets were confronted by the anti-aircraft guns of the Lebanese Army and Islamic Resistance.

Earlier, the outskirts of Jarjoua, Ain Bousoir, Houmin El-Fawqa and Ain Qana and the heights and woodlands of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh, Loweizeh and Mazraat Aqmata came under a lot "Israeli" shells.

The nationals Mahmoud Waked Moqalled's house and Ali Jamil Moqalled's car had been burnt entirely. The "Israeli" shells hit, also, the house of Mohammad Moussa Moqalled, the head of Jarjoua Municipality, causing heavy material damages. Also, several blazes were ignited destroying olive and oak orchards at Mlita hill, whereas the electric cables along with the road between Jarjoua and Ain Bousoir were cut off.


In Western Beqaa, it was reported that Ahmad Hussein Zein's five-member family had escaped the death as an "Israeli" shell hit his house penetrating one of its rooms. Fortunately, the shell didn't explode.

Elsewhere, the "Israeli"-occupied position of Ain Qinya bombarded at noon Dahr Mountain and Meimess sector with twenty 130mm shells. This was followed by firing heavy machine gun rounds emanating from Zimraya post at Meimess sector.

Also, the news from Arkoub region reported that an "Israeli" force carried out a wide showering campaign at the forests stretching from the eastern hilltops of the two villages Hibbarieh and Kfar Shouba to Majidieh sector. During this campaign, the force had questioned a number of farmers and shepherds accidentally found in the area.

In Western Sector, the "Israeli" batteries situated at Qantara compound shelled, at 6:00am, the Valleys of Slouqi and Qaissieh and the outskirts of Froun.

Earlier at night, the villages of Baraasheit, Shaqra, Yater, Qabrikha and Froun came under a lot of 155mm missiles.

This was accompanied by combing Ghandourieh's square with a barrage of heavy machine gun rounds.

For its part, the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-General Command (PFLP-GC) reported that a squadron of "Israeli" fighters raided, at 9:05pm, into the PFLP-GC's clinic located at Naameh. The fighters unleashed several air-to-ground rockets destroying a medications store and ambulance (1).

In Tyre region, the reports said a Polish soldier was hit in his leg by Shrapnel near Zibqeen in the afternoon when the "Israelis" shelled the villages facing the western sector of the zone. The soldier was flown by Unifil helicopter to Naqoura where he received treatment at the peacekeeper's hospital. His condition was not serious(2)

Islamic Resistance Operations (4)
 As the "Israeli" occupation's command has not recovered yet from the shock of the Islamic Resistance's (IR) man's penetration into the Sojod outpost, the Mujahedeen today dealt a fresh blow to the occupation forces killing one "Israeli" soldier and wounding four others, according to "Israeli" acknowledgment.

In spite of the new tight security measures, including orders to occupation troops to keep their weapons chambered, and the tour made by the enemy war minister Yitzhak Mordechai in a desperate attempt to boost his troops' deteriorating morale, IR was able today to hit the occupation forces near the Sojod outpost itself inflicting more confusion and embarrassment on the occupation command especially as the Mujahedeen were able to penetrate once again all the declared measures, reach a spot near the outpost, ambush an "Israeli" patrol and target it with a large roadside bomb.

An "Israeli" soldier was killed and three others were injured on Wednesday in a Resistance attack in the occupied zone, Agency France Press quoted security sources as saying.

IR stated in a communiqué' the detailed course of operations today:
"IR's squad of Martyr Hassan Hammoudi at 8:25 this morning ambushed a Zionist foot patrol that was heading to the Sojod outpost vicinity."

"When the enemy patrol reached the ambush site, the Mujahedeen exploded a powerful bomb and blitzed the enemy troops with machine guns and hand grenades, killing or injuring the occupation troops."
"At 8:45, the enemy brought motorized reinforcements from the Rihan area to the clash site. IR's squad of Martyr Sheikh Taiseer Badran attacked the reinforcements with the adequate arms at the Sojod--Rihan road barring them from achieving their goals."
"At 8:50, an "Israeli" .Mirkava stationed in front of the Sojod outpost tried to back up the targeted forces, IR's squad of Martyr Mohammed Hazimeh targeted the tank with the appropriate weapons and hit it directly."
"At 10:45, while enemy vehicles were moving inside the outpost of Sojod, IR's squad of Martyr Qassem Quoun attacked the outpost with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
Earlier, IR's squad of Martyrs Abdallah Wehbi and Imad Mohanna at 22:00 (Tuesday) attacked two "Israeli" Mirkava tanks in the Sojod outpost.
"At 22:35 (Tuesday) IR's squad of Martyr Samir Aqil attacked enemy movements in the outpost of Rshaf with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades. Meanwhile, IR's squad of Martyr Abbass Hammoudi was targeting similar movements at the Haddatha outpost. The Mujahedeen scored direct hits in both outposts.
After several hours of silence, "Israeli" enemy radio this afternoon acknowledged that one "Israeli" soldier was killed and four others were wounded in IR's ambush today near Sojod.
Furthermore, at 14:00 (Wednesday) IR spotted enemy movements inside the occupation outpost in Blat. IR's squad of Martyrs Asaad and Mustafa Karaki targeted the outpost with the adequate arms.

In the meantime, IR's squad of Martyr Rida Shmais and Mohsen Zraiq targeted similar movements at the Tair Harfa outpost.

The Mujahedeen scored direct hits in both outposts.

"Israel" Secretary Testing Chemical and Biological Weapons
The London-based Foreign Report newsletter today said "Israel" is developing chemical and biological weapons at a secret research institute southeast of "Tel Aviv". The newsletter quoted sources as saying four serious accidents occurred at the institute in recent years killing four employees and injuring 25.
