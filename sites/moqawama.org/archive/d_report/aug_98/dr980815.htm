Daily Report
 
 
August Saturday 15 -1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" batteries stationed at Rihan emplacement fired flare shells over the greenline of Eastern Sector of South Lebanon.
Also, the helicopters hovered over the area stretched between Qatraneh Mountain and the heights of Kawkaba and Arkoub villages (1).
However, the Islamic Resistance IR released a statement in which it recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions (2):
9:05, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Rmadieh and Chaitieh villages.
10:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared at middle altitude, over Qalaouieh, Borj Qalaouieh, Froun and Ghandourieh villages.
13:45, Zaffateh position unleashed three 155mm missiles at Sojod Mountain, whereas Rihan emplacement targeted Tasseh Well with three 120mm shells. In the meantime, Bir Kallab outpost raked its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
14:55, the warplanes flew over the Lebanese Western Mountain Series.
17:45, the eastern part of Majdal Zoun subjected to three 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position.
17:50, Zohaira position unleashed one 155mm missile at the peripheries of Hennieh village and three others at the perimeters of Majdal Zoun.
18:15, one 120mm shell originating from Saf El-Hawa position fell on the perimeters of Baraasheit village.
18:45, Sojod outpost combed intensely its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
19:10, Rihan emplacement fired four 120mm shells, including one phosphoric shell, at Sojod Mountain, when on the other hand, Sojod outpost fired several 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
19:15, Haddatha position hit its surroundings with one 60mm shell.
19:40, Haddatha position fired a number of 60mm shells and barrage of 12.7mm and Mag machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
19:45, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Nabatieh, Iqleem Al-Tuffah and East Saida regions. Meanwhile, seven 155mm missiles originating from Meis El-Jabal emplacement struck the perimeters of Haddatha.
20:05, the jet airplanes flew, at middle altitude, over Nabatieh and the neighboring areas.
23:00, Haddatha woodlands exposed to several 81mm shells originating from Haddatha compound.
23:50, an espionage plane flew over the sea opposite to Insarieh town.

Islamic Resistance Operations (3):
The IR issued a statement in which it adopted the responsibility of the following attacks:
17:45, the IR spotted enemy movements inside Tair Harfa position. Then, the squad of two martyrs Hassan Hayek and Moussa Fahs targeted them with the suitable weapons chalking up direct hits.
18:20, the group of both martyrs Sami Abdel-Amir Kobeissi and Issam Kamel Kansou attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rockets destroying the main fortification at its entrance and scoring direct hits.
22:20, the IR militants observed military vehicles near Sojod outpost. Then, the group of two martyrs Bassam Abdallah and Mohammad Hassan Jaber targeted them with the rockets chalking up direct hits.
