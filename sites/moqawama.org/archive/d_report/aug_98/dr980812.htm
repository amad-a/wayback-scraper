Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 12 -1998:
 

Introduction
The more the "Israeli" occupation forces insist on staying in Southern Lebanon the more the Islamic Resistance IR militants increase their determination in fighting against the "Israeli" enemy. Yet, these militants will not be held back by the "Israeli" military, political and monetary influences, simply because they are armed by the right and faith. This appeared, clearly, when the IR man had penetrated successfuly the enemy military fortifications and ramparts of Sojod outpost last Sunday.

IR's Qualitative Penetration Worries the Occupation's Command (1)
IR's qualitative operation in the Sojod outpost and its exceptional occurrences remained a resounding and active event in the "Israeli" internal arena and a major topic in the enemy's media which described one of the Mujahedeen's entrance into the outpost as "disgrace" inflicted on the "Israeli" army.

Meanwhile, more confusion and embarrassment could be traced in the enemy military command's statements which tried to play down the humiliation, its elite troops have been subjected to, besides their complete helplessness in confronting one resistance man who stormed into one of the most important strongholds of the occupation and withdrew without one of the occupation troops daring to confront him.

The "Israeli" daily Ha'aretz" Hebrew edition quoted a senior "Israeli" officer as acknowledging that the party's elements enjoy great courage.

The reason behind the concern evoked by the incident is the fear that Hizbullah plans to abduct "Israeli" soldiers, the officer said.

The holes which appeared in the Sojod outpost's protection system would indicate that such abduction attempts may bear fruit, he added.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" occupation army is continuing to investigate how a Hizbullah Resistance man penetrated the Sojod outpost manned by "Israeli" elite paratroopers and escaped despite being shot by the soldiers.

The inquiry being led by Brig.-Gen. Erez Gerstein, head of the (Israeli occupation army's) liaison unit, is also concentrating on how the small Hizbullah squad managed to reach the area without being seen, the "Israeli" daily Jerusalem Post said.

One of the (Islamic Resistance) gunmen climbed a short but steep rock face to reach the ramparts of the stronghold and then either cut through or clambered over barbed wire fencing surrounding the position, also without being observed, the daily added.

Gerstein yesterday met and debriefed the soldiers and officers who were at post at the time of the incident and his findings will be submitted to OC Northern Command Maj.-Oen. Gaby Ashkenazi, who will turn them over to Chief of Staff Lt.-Gen. Shaul Mofaz, Jerusalem post said.

The incident was dubbed a debacle in some Hebrew newspapers. According to one report, the soldiers may have been complacent, and a few suffered from low motivation, it concluded.

Ha'aretz said the "Israeli" military command was annoyed by the comments of the "Israeli" media and considering the operation as disgrace to the "Israeli" army.

As the "Israeli" military command tried to ease the campaigns launched against it by claiming that the Resistance man did not enter the outpost (although "Israeli" officers acknowledged the fact earlier), IR retorted with a new qualitative move as it aired footage of the Resistance man's entrance and coming out of the outpost.

Meanwhile, "Israeli" internal security minister Avigdor Kahlani said what happened in Sojod calls for disgrace.

I have seen the photos in newspapers which described what had happened. In fact, what happened isn't nice; it even calls for disgrace, Kahlani said.

Speaking in an interview with the "Israeli" TV's second channel, Kahlani said "Hizbullah is always confusing and embarrassing us", calling for a serious investigation that would end up in learning lessons from the incident.

"Israeli" Aggressions (2):
The heights of Mashghara, Ain Tineh and Rihayat, were bombarded, at dawn, by nearly twenty missiles of different calibers.

The "Israeli" shelling caused material damages in properties and woodlands.
At night, the "Israeli" fighters staged low mock air raids over the areas adjacent to the front line. This was accompanied by hovering the helicopters overhead.

"Israeli" Disrupts Tyre Port Activity (3)
"Israel's" navy today stopped a cargo ship transporting used cars from Germany to the southern port city of Tyre. Occupation forces boarded the ship, a few kilometers away from the port, and searched its contents. They also inspected documents of the ship's crew, before allowing the ship to continue it's trip to Tyre. "Israel's" navy occasionally searches incoming ship to the southern port city.

Houla's Residents condemn "Israel" (4)
Houla residents yesterday urged the government to put an end to "Israel's" illegal annexation of property in the south after the Jewish State's forces recently seized several acres of land located along the joint border region.

A statement issued by local residents said that "Israeli" forces had taken over the cultivated plots located between Abbad and Dawawir a few days ago.

The statement added that the "Israelis" had planted land mines in the area, installed barbed wire, barricades and erased all demarcation lines to prevent residents from entering.

"We urge government officials at the executive and legislative levels to put an end to the annexation of Lebanese territory by exposing "Israel's" activities," they said.

The residents suggested the information and foreign ministries organize a media campaign aimed at exposing "Israel's" activities and to file a complaint at the UN Security Council. The residents complained that previous appeals and warnings to the government remained unanswered.

"The only way to expel "Israeli" troops would be for the government to assume its responsibilities and provide people in the occupied area with a means to resist. The government should also create an international lobby to stop "Israel's" repressive activities," the statement concluded.

Sayed Nassrallah: The agent has no faith, religion or color (7)
Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah yesterday pointed out that the Resistance will always be the watchful eye safeguarding the homeland, stressing that the Resistance men's bombs only target the "Israeli" occupation troops and their collaborators.

Sayyed Nassrallah said that Hizbullah will not attempt to enter the occupied enclave of Jezzine in the event of an "Israeli" withdrawal.

"We do not target Christians in Jezzine. We target the agents who have no faith, religion or color," his Eminence said.

The deaths of Shiite collaborators at the hands of the Islamic Resistance is proof of this, he added.
Accusations have been made recently that Hizbullah was trying to displace Christian residents of the occupied town which is manned by the Lahd collaborator militia on behalf of the "Israeli" occupation forces.

"We regret to hear certain people speaking on behalf of the Jezzine residents. We met with a large number of Jezzine officials who are satisfied with our position," the secretary general said.

"Hizbullah knows how to plant bombs and hunt "Israeli" occupation troops and their collaborators," Sayed Nassrallah said.

"We will protect the people of Jezzine who are our people and safeguard co-existence," he added.
He stressed that Hizbullah had no intention of taking up positions in Jezzine in the event of an "Israeli" withdrawal, adding that the army was perfectly capable of maintaining security in the area.

"We have an army which is up to the task. Let "Israel" withdraw and Hizbullah will not enter Jezzine," he said.

The calls, however, for putting an end to resistance operations in Jezzine and other areas would be tantamount to consolidating the occupation and its agents, his Eminence stated.
