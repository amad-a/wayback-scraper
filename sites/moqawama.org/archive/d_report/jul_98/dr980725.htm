Daily Report
 
 
July Saturday 25-1998:
 
Today, several areas of South Lebanon witnessed a number of "Israeli" aggressions. Islamic Resistance, however, retaliated by targeting the "Israeli"-occupied post of Blat.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
In its statement, the Islamic Resistance (1) mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:
1:10, a reconnaissance plane flew over Kafra, Yater, Qana, Aiteit and the nearby areas.
1:15, a drone espionage plane flew over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr village.
1:55, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude,, over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River watercourse.
5:50, a drone espionage aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Abbassieh, Ghandourieh and Hojeir areas.
6:25, two 155mm missiles emanating from the outskirts of Meis El-Jabal village struck Zaatar Mountain.
6:55, Zaffateh compound fired five 155mm missiles at Aqmata area and five others at Sojod Mountain.
7:05, Zaffateh position unleashed four 155mm missiles at Mlita village.
10:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Litani River watercourse.
10:55, Mothallath position targeted Mazraat Alman with two 81mm shells.
13:25, Zohaira position hit west of Yater with nine 155mm missiles.
13:45, Shraifeh compound shelled the road between Zawtar Gharbieh and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr villages with nine 155mm missiles.
14:35, four 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area struck the area between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun villages.
21:45, Haddatha post hit its surroundings with several 81mm shells.
22:30, Baraasheit position targeted the area between Baraasheit and Shaqra villages with one 120mm shell.
23:15, Baraasheit position fired four 81mm shells at the area between Beit Yahoun and Baraasheit and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings. In the meantime, Haddatha post combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Islamic Resistance Operations (2):
At 19:05, the group of two martyrs Hassan Mohammad Saad and Aiman Hamzeh Baalbacki targeted Blat post with the suitable arms scoring direct goals.
