Daily Report
 
 
July Friday 17-1998:
 

Introduction
As the "Israeli" aggressions continue against the Lebanese people and lands, Lebanon had lodged three complaints to the Five-nation Cease-Fire Monitoring Committee over the "Israeli" shelling of civilians and civil targets.

Meanwhile, Lebanon's Foreign Affairs Minister Fares Boueiz called on UN Secretary General Kofi Annan to work for releasing the detainees and internees with whom "Israel" infracts the international covenants especially Geneva covenant and Child Rights Agreement which declared on 20-11-1989.
However, the Lebanese President Elias Hirawi said that we have to be aware of "Israel's" intelligence systems that are doing everything for the purpose of undermining Lebanon and its unity.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" troops (1) stationed at Zaffateh compound shelled in the morning Mazraat Hamra and Mazraat Hima. Also, the highway of Habboush-Arab Salim came, at night and dawn, under several missiles.

The "Israeli" shelling caused damages to the electric network cutting of the electric current in the region.

Elsewhere, the "Israeli"-occupied Shraifeh position and Kawkaba post which is manned by Lahd's militiamen shelled, at 5:30am, Mazraat Loussi, Mazraat Ahmadieh, Zahr Mountain and the outskirts of Dallafeh and Qilya villages.

Meanwhile, Kawkaba post showered, at 7:30am, the area stretching from Kawkaba hill until Borghoz and Souk El-Khan with heavy machine gun fire.

On the other hand, Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it reported the following (2):
00:15, an M.K. reconnaissance planes flew over Ashour Valley, whereas a drone reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Beaufort Castle and Kfar Tibbnit.
1:15, Bayada outpost fired a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings and several flare shells overhead.
1:20, a number of "Israeli" warboats were seen patrolling opposite to the seashore Maallieh and Qlayleh villages.
5:10, Zaffateh compound unleashed eight 155mm missiles at Aqmata area.
5:30, four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Sojod Mountain.
5:45, Zohaira post targeted west of Yater village with seven 155mm missiles.
5:50, Bir Kallab outpost fired several grenades and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
10:45, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Western Sector of South Lebanon. Meanwhile, the helicopters flew over Aishieh area.
10:50, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
13:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft flew very low over Tyre city.
17:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Yater, Siddiqeen, Qana and Aiteit villages.
19:40, Houla post unleashed five tank rounds at the peripheries of Shaqra village and several 81mm artillery shells at the area between Shaqra and Houla.
19:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
19:55, an M.K. reconnaissance plane flew over Qana, Hanaouieh, Barish and Maaroub villages.
20:30, Zaffateh position bombarded Aqmata area with nine 155mm missiles.
20:35, the west of Yater exposed to six 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira compound.
20:45, the batteries stationed of Rihan area targeted Sojod village with three 120mm shells and Sojod Mountain with four others.
20:55, five 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area fell on the outskirts of Haddatha village.
21:00, Zaffateh position hit Litani River banks with six 155mm shells, whereas the west of Yater came under three others emanating from Meis El-Jabal. At the same time, Sojod village exposed to two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
21:05, Zaffateh post targeted the area between Arnoun and Mazraat Hamra with two 155mm missiles.
23:50, Zaffateh position hit the area between Kfar Jouz and Kfour with several 155mm missiles.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Islamic Resistance held in a statement the responsibility of the following attacks:
19:45, the group of martyr Mohammad Nassar attacked Houla post with the suitable arms scoring direct hits.
22:00, the group of martyr Soheil Diab targeted again Houla post with the proper weapons chalking up direct and certain goals.
23:30, the group of martyr Mustafa Nazar targeted enemy movements near Tohorra outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct and definite hits.

Monitoring Committee (3)
The Five-Nation Cease-Fire Monitoring Committee is expected to hold a meeting on the coming Tuesday to discuss three complaints presented by Lebanon. The complaints relate to the "Israeli" shelling that inflicted the citizen Ali Karnib, 65, on the past Monday at Haddatha village, damaged seven houses at the village itself and struck nine houses at Nabatieh on the last Wednesday where miraculously twenty five nationals were not hurt.

Boueiz asks Annan's assistance (4)
Lebanon foreign Affairs Minister Fares Boueiz sent a message to the United Nation's Secretary General Kofi Annan demanding the international bodies to interfere with the purpose of releasing the Lebanese detainees and internees. Here are some excerpts:

"The detention of these innocent Lebanese represents a blatant infraction of the human rights where none of them has been accused since their arrest. Also, they are sustained by various kinds of torture in contradiction with the Fourth Geneva Covenant stated in 1949. This Covenant and its first Protocol state that the civilians, subjected to a foreign occupation, shall not be underwent to any kind of torture or arbitrary detention"

" Furthermore, "Israel" recently apprehended dozens of Lebanese and since 1997 it had prohibited the representatives of the International Committee for the Red Cross and families to visit periodically the detainees"

" In addition to a number of patients, girls and old men, there are more than eleven minors violating, thus, the Article no:37 of the Child Rights Agreement declared by the United Nations on 20-11-1989"

" It is worthmentioning, that Lahd's Militia had kidnapped sixty Lebanese citizens on 29-5-1998 according to information provided by the international human bodies"

"The most clear sign of "Israeli" scorn for the international law is the decision taken by its Supreme Court on 4-3-1998 over the continuation of detaining the Lebanese as "bargaining chips" and without trial or being accused. This decision represents a flagrant violation of the International Declaration for the Human Rights"

" On the fifty anniversary of the International Declaration of the Human Rights and on the Lebanon's Day of Internee, we call you to exert your best efforts so as to put an end for the tragedy of these detainees and their sufferance"

" Our urgent demand today is to do everything in order to release the Lebanese detainees from the "Israeli" jails"

Settlements a War Crime (5)
The conference which was convened at the initiative of the UN General Assembly is debating the definition of crimes over which the court will have jurisdiction. The article to which "Israel" objects defines as a crime the transfer, directly or indirectly, by the occupying power, of parts of its "own civilian population into the territory it occupies, or the deportation or transfer of all or parts of the population of the occupied territory, within or outside this territory". Some 320,000 "Israelis" have settled in the Gaza Strip and the West Bank including east Jerusalem. More than 13,000 have settled on the Golan Heights.
