Daily Report
 
 
July Sunday 19-1998:
 
Introduction
Today, the "Israeli" aggressions against Lebanon varied to include artillery shelling, showering heavy machine gun rounds and flying the warplanes in the airspace of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa.
Meanwhile, the delegation of the Australian Parliament paid a visit to the cemetery of Qana massacre martyrs which will be always a witness of the "Israeli" savage in the end of the twentieth century.
Elsewhere, the pieces of leads show day after day that "Israel" has planned and carried out bombing Oklahoma City building in United States of America.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
In Eastern Sector, a tank related to Lahd's Militia (1) fired at 2:00pm four rounds at Mazraat Loussi and the heights of Daidaba and Dahr Mountain. Also, Zimraya position unleashed heavy machine gun rounds for about quarter an hour the highway of Meimess-Marj Zhour.

Earlier, the "Israeli" batteries targeted at dawn the woodlands of Jabbour and west of Ain Tineh, the heights of Rihayat Maramel and the hilltops of Mashghara and Ain Tineh villages. Several blazes were sparked as a number of phosphorous shells struck around.

The "Israeli" troops and Lahd's militiamen showered Litani River banks, Ain El-Amin surroundings, west of Mashghara, and hills of Moqawama and Touma.

In Iqleem Al-Tuffah, the "Israeli"-occupied positions of Zaffateh & Mahmoudieh shelled Litani River banks, Mazraat Aqmata, the forests of Sojod, Rihan, Aishieh and Loweizeh. The "Israeli" shelling, also, extended to include Mazraat Hamra, Mazraat Hima, Abou Salem Valley and the plains of Yohmor Chkeif and Kfar Tibbnit.

Islamic Resistance Operations

The groups of the Lebanese Brigades for the Resistance of "Israeli" Occupation attacked the new Marj Houla position with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits and destroying some of its ramparts and fortification. At the same time, the Artillery Company targeted Mashaaroun position scoring direct goals.

Australia's MPs visit Qana Cemetery (3)
John Hanna Fourd, Head of the Australian Parliamentary delegation, expressed his grief over the "Israeli" brutality during his visit to the cemetery of Qana massacre martyrs and requested for making it as an international sacred shrine

The Australian delegation, made up of the MPs Bari Ouvaril, Eddy Mikaliv, Patricia Fourstite and Steve Braks, put a wreath on the tombs of Qana's martyrs.

Mossad and Oklahoma Blast (4)
The American journalist David Hofman talked about a "potential role of the 'Israeli Mossad' in bombing the American Federal building in Oklahoma city which resulted in killing 240 persons and wounding hundreds others.

Hofman, who is compiling the most comprehensive book about the blast, stressed that the pieces of evidence lead to Mossad. Meanwhile, the American authorities and media continue hushing up this point. Furthermore, the American judiciary continue ruling out such possibility.

Hofman maintained that shortly before the explosion, there were three intelligence members in Oklahoma city. The first is Avi Kibkine who was the "Israeli" Premier's anti-terrorism affairs consultant. The second is Weil Northrob whom Hofman asked him about what was he doing in Oklahoma shortly before the blast? He replied "for things to be under control and before my eyes". The third is still unknown.

Also, Hofman assured that Mossad had controlled completely the investigation soon after arresting the American suspect Timothy Mc-vi who sentenced to death in this case.
