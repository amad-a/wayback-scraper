Daily Report
 
 
July Sunday 26-1998:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" occupation forces shelled the heights and woodlands of Iqleem Al-Tuffah, the outskirts of Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr, Mazraat Hamra, Mazraat Hima and Kfar Tibbnit villages, Litani River watercourse and Abou Salem valley. The shells originated from the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Zaffateh and Aishieh.

In Western sector, the "Israeli" gunners targeted the villages of Haddatha, Baraasheit, Shaqra and Aita El-Jabal where the citizen Mohammad Haidar's house was hit by one missile. The previous villages along with the area stretching to the outskirts of Tibbnine, Slouqi Valley and Qaissieh Valley were raked in the morning by heavy machine gun fire.

Also, Blat position bombarded at 6:00pm west of Yater with fifteen artillery shells sparking several blazes.

However, the ambiguity is still encompassing the causes of the blast that was occurred in the citizen Ali Tawbeh's house at Arnoun village the past Friday night. In this regard, the International Committee for the Red Cross is trying to contact with the "Israeli troops to get permission with the aim of having access to the scene so as to gather the remains of the citizen Atieh who killed in the blast.

When the explosion happened, a patrol of the Lebanese Interior Security Forces tried to enter Arnoun village for the purpose of making the necessary investigation, but the "Israeli"-occupied Beaufort Castle opened fire forcing if to retrace its steps.

Later on at the noon, an "Israeli" intelligence forces, coming from Beaufort Castle, headed for the place of explosion and searched the nearby houses.
