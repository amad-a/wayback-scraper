Daily Report
 
 
July Tuesday 7-1998:
 

Introduction:
As the dispute continues between "Israel's" President Azer Weizman and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, the "Israeli" occupation forces pursued launching assaults on the civilians of South Lebanon. Besides, the Lebanese hostages detained in the "Israeli" concentration camps are still suffering the repressive actions made by "Israel's" courts.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" gunners (1) shelled before noon the heights of Jabbour, Abou Rashed and Zahr Mountain.

For its part, the Lebanese Interior Security Forces (2) mentioned, in a report, that Lahd's militiamen stationed at Kfar Tibbnite crossing point opened randomly the fire at the civil cars which were in the way to pass across it last Saturday evening sparking blazes in ten cars that identified as follows:

  • Mercedes - 280 of no: 1382715 owned by Ghassan Hussein Mohammad Haidar, Taibeh village.
  • Mazda of no: 1306773 related to Ali Nahim Saad, Khiam.
  • Mercedes of no: 1437004, possessed by the policeman Mohammad Salman Sara, Mary.
  • Mercedes of no: 979832, possessed by Ali Amin Ayash, Khiam town.
  • Datsun of no:1148934, related to Kamel Hussein Kalakesh, Dibbin village.
  • Mercedes of no: 1440011, owned by Kamel Ali Yahia, Kfar Kila village.
  • B.M.W of no: 1634985 owned by Khaled Kamal Nassrallah, Taibeh village, in addition to two Mercedes cars of unknown owners.
The report added that the fires that broke up destroyed about fifteen Dunums of olive and wheat groves located to the southeast of the crossing point.

In this regard, it was known that a security source said a complaint intended to be filed by Lebanon so as to be presented to the Five-Nation Monitoring Committee which supervises the implementation of the April 1996 Understanding.

On the other hand, the Islamic Resistance mentioned, in a statement, the following "Israeli" Aggressions:
1:00, Saf El-Hawa barracks fired five 120mm shells at the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal.
1:15, Beit Yahoun new outpost unleashed six 60mm mortar shells at the surroundings of Beit Yahoun old outpost.
7:15 and until 8:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Baraasheit and the neighboring areas.
9:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle height, over Zahrani, Sarafand and Khaizaran areas, whereas another one hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
10:55, an M.K. reconnaissance plane flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
14:55, Aramta position fired six tank rounds and 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire at Abou Rkab Mountain, peripheries of Mlikh village and nearby areas.
15:35, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Sector.
15:45, the "Israeli" fighters and M.K. reconnaissance aircraft flew, at middle altitude, in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.

"Israeli" Inquiry over Haddatha Operation (4)
Following the Islamic Resistance militants success in storming into the fortified Haddatha outpost, the "Israeli" occupation forces made an inquiry and sent a committee of investigators in order to study the gaps and defects that enabled Hizbullah running over and occupying the position.

Normalization Camps?! (5)
Several "Israeli" youth organizations had proceeded carrying out a campaign with the aim of setting up student and scout camps that gather the "Israeli" and Lebanese residing at the occupied enclave.

"Israeli" Intelligence curbs Lahdi clashes (6)
Amid the confusion that befalls the "Israeli" forces and their collaborators, it was reported that clashes between conflicting groups inside Lahd's Militia had been broken out at night in Bent Jbeil town.

The clashes began as a number of Lahd's militiamen, not belonging to the Bent Jbeil, set up a checkpoint at the entrance of the town. The Town's "Israeli" collaborators tried to prevent them but the matter quickly developed into exchanging affronts and then to firefight with the machine guns.

The "Israeli" intelligence soldiers rushed to control scene and captured several members where one of them was identified as Ali Youness.

It should be noted here that the aforesaid militia, which composed by "Israel" in 1997 for the purpose of protecting it, is now suffering a state of disconnection because of the Islamic Resistance panic strikes. Dozens of its officials and members had fled to the liberated areas forming critical situation in its basic structure.

Twenty Lebanese under "Israel's" administrative detention (7)
France Press Agency reported that a court in Tel Aviv took a secret decision over the fate of twenty Lebanese hostages detained without trial in "Israel".

The Head of Court, Menahim Ilan banned publishing any information concerning these detainees who have been put under the administrative detention and without any charge.
Trying two "Israeli"-collaborated Terrorist Networks (8)
The Permanent Military Court headed by the Brigadier General Hussein Chahin postponed the trial of the two "Israeli"-collaborated terrorist rings to the second day of the coming September.

The first consisted of Fahim Youusef El-Alam (charged with dealing in narcotics), Joseph Badin, Sharbel Jerjis Assaf ( both accused of breaching the Law of boycotting Israel), Nazih Youssef Kalakesh and Saeid Maatouk El-Alam (both accused of dealing with "Israel").

The second composed of Joseph George Karam, Roukoz Ibrahim Roukoz, Chokri Elias El-Hayek, Michel Youssef Aoun and George Maroun Jurji who all were charged with collaborating with the enemy and an attempt of kidnapping and killing the sergeant major Bazil.L.

Lebanese Parties praise arresting the last terrorist networks (9)
 The Lebanese parties and forces praised apprehending, recently, the pro-"Israeli" terrorist ring. Also, they lauded the high performance of the Lebanese Army and Interior Security Forces which took the steps necessary for uncovering the rings especially that perpetrated actions of bombing and shaking the stability.

Canadian Senator criticizes "Israel's" Policy (10)
Canadian Senator Marsille Brodoum said "Israel" cann't persist in defying the United Nations resolution for it is emerged by virtue of its acknowledgement".

He wondered: "How do some countries apply the international resolutions, while others throw them aside? How does "Israel" authorize itself to negotiate over the Resolution 425? This resolution is unnegotiable. "Israel" violates the Lebanese airspace as it is playing a game".

Brodoum considered "Lebanon, in its uniqueness, as the only rival for "Israel" in the region", demanding the international community to push "Israel" in order to carry out the international resolutions.

"Israeli" Spying Satellite (11)
"Israel's" space agency is getting ready to launch a spying satellite which is dedicated for gathering military information about Syria, Iraq and Iran.

It is worth mentioning that this the third satellite of its kind.
