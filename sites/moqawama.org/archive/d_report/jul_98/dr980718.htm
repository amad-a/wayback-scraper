Daily Report
 
 
July Saturday 18-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions (1):
1:30, the warplanes flew over Bent Jbeil region.
3:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah area.
4:25, an M.K. reconnaissance plane soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
13:00, Zaffateh position shelled Arid Zennar area with eight 155mm missiles, while Bir Kallab outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
16:15, two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Arid Zennar area, whereas Bir Kallab outpost raked its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
16:40, Zaffateh compound targeted the peripheries of Loweizeh village with three 155mm missiles.
16:45, Zaffateh position hit Safi Mountain with two 155mm missiles.
17:15, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle altitude, over Qana, Aiteit, Siddiqeen and the neighboring areas.
18:10, Tasseh Well came under seven 120mm shells whereas Arid Zennar area exposed to two others emanating from Rihan region.
19:40, Bir Kallab outpost raked Arid Zennar area with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire, whereas Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with Mag middle machine gun rounds.
20:25, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Zahrani region while a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Bnaafoul and Erkay villages.
20:40, several 81 and 120mm shells emanating from Meis El-Jabal fell on the outskirts of Aita Al-Jabal, while Haddatha compound showered the perimeters of Aita Al-Jabal with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
21:15, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared high over Saida city. In the meantime, Dabsheh outpost fired twenty one 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at Khanouq, Kassarah and Tohorra areas.
22:45, a drone reconnaissance plane flew low over Jibsheit, Adsheit and Ibba villages.

Islamic Resistance Operations (2)
2:30, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Hussein Bashir targeted Beit Yahoun outpost with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct and definite hits.
