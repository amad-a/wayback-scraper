Daily Report
 
 
July Friday 31-1998:
 
Introduction
Either it was accused of violating the international covenant or not, "Israel" follows up its aggressions on Lebanon's people and land. Also, the use of torture methods inside "Israel's" jails and detention camps are going, without any legal or moral deterrent, side by side with pounding the Lebanese areas with the rockets and artillery shells.

The evidence had been provided by the United Nations' Human Rights Committee which denounced the unreasonable torture and unjustifiable detention actions made by the "Israeli" authorities in Lebanon and Palestine.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" jet airplanes raided into Joune town, Iqleem Al-Kharroub region, firing two air-to-surface rockets.

UN condemns "Israel's" practices (1)
The United Nations Human Rights Committee expressed its deep concern for "Israel's" rejection of applying the international covenant over the civil and political rights. "Israel" should apply the covenant in Lebanese and Palestinian occupied territories for they are under its "actual control", as the committee said.

The Human Rights Committee, made up of eighteen independent experts, denounced "Israel" during its meeting, held in Geneva, because of torture methods it uses  against the detainees during the interrogation. It called "Israel" to respect the principles of Human Rights in the occupied territories.

According to a report presented by the "Israeli" government and questions addressed by experts to an "Israeli" delegation, the Committee concluded its meeting with passing several recommendations and results.

Christin Chanite, Head of the Committee, considered that these recommendations and results imply a condemnation of the "Israeli" law and practice.

The Committee pointed out that "Israel's" delegation admitted with using the torture methods, such as shackling hands, using musks, exposing to violent shaking and depriving from sleeping as a technic to extract information from the detainees.

In this regard, it called "Israel" to waive such methods that are banned in accordance with the Article no: 7 of the Covenant.

Also, the committee expressed its concern over the administrative detention operations for security causes. It considered that some of these operation acquire a form of taking hostager in return for releasing soldiers missed in action.

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
In a remarkable development, the "Israeli" fighters launched, at about 9:00pm, two raids into the outskirts of Joune town, Iqleem Al-Kharroub, firing two air-to-ground rockets.

Earlier, the warplanes flew low in the airspace of Saida and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions releasing flash shells.

However, the "Israeli" gunners stationed at Qantara compound bombarded, at 6:00am, Qalaouieh ravine and the villages of Sawaneh, Qabrikha, Froun and Ghandourieh with up to twenty smoke missiles.

As of 7 O'clock in the morning, the shelling raged at the valleys of Slouqi and Qaissieh. Five shells struck Tamerieh crossroad between Touline and Qabrikha villages cutting off the electric wire leading to the village and halting the passing on the highway for about one hour.

Also, the "Israeli" troops targeted Joune with five missiles, but no casualties were reported.

Elsewhere, the outskirts of Mazraat Hamra and Mazraat Hima, the plains of Yohmor Shkeif and Kfar Tibbnit, the ravines of Abou Salem, Kfour and Kfar Jouze, the watercourses of Litani and Zahrani rivers, the heights of Iqleem Al-Tuffah and Zawtar Sharqieh came, before and after noon, under intermittent "Israeli" shelling.

In the meantime, an "Israeli" armored force headed by the so-called Commander of Liaison Unit, showered, for nearly four hours, the surroundings of Sojod, Rihan and Kfar Houna villages.

In Eastern Sector, the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Abou Qamha and Shraifeh targeted Dahr Mountain, Qatraneh heights and Litani River watercourse with a lot of artillery missiles.

The security sources reported that about ninety missiles struck, over three hours, these areas. A series of fires sparked and continued even until after noon.

Also, Zimraya and Kawkaba positions raked in the morning Mazraat Ahmadieh and Meimess sector with heavy machine gun fire.

In Western Beqaa, the enemy artillery shelling targeted, at dawn, Litani River watercourse and the eastern heights of Qilya village with several shells, including phosphorous ones, enkindling a lot of blazes which continued until the morning. Earlier, eleven missiles fell on the western heights of Ain Tineh town.

On the level of repressive practices, the "Israeli" intelligence soldiers expatriated the national Souad Hassan El-Assaad together with her three children Hamad: (17), Nassir (13) and Zolfa (10) from their village Odaisseh to the outside of the occupied enclave.

El-Assaad had been taken, on the last March, to Khiam detention camp where she was held prisoner for twenty days.

It is worth noting that El-Assaad is a wife of Kamel Youssef Abdallah, the Official of Civil Administration of Khiam town who had been taken by the "Israeli" occupation forces on the past January 25 to Khiam concentration camp.

An "Israeli" Inquiry Committee (4)
In the wake of the successful Islamic Resistance's attack in Rihan area, the "Israeli" Army Northern Command had begun investigating the incident. The "Israeli" Army has not ascertained whether the Hizbullah fighters ambushed the "Israeli" soldiers or met them by chance. The bomb points to a planned attack, but lately Hizbullah has not ambushed "Israeli" Army troops in daytime. The command will also investigate whether Hizbullah forces had planned an attack elsewhere in the area and decided to take advantage of the situation when they saw the "Israeli' soldiers nearby. The inquiry will also try to determine whether the "Israeli" forces were sufficiently equipped and whether the rescue forces acted properly.

A week ago, senior Hizbullah officials hinted that they would soon try to abduct "Israeli" Army soldiers to increase their bargaining power in negotiations for the release of Shi'ite prisoners held by "Israel".

Beating Ten "Israeli" soldiers (5):
Haifa - based weekly Colbou newspaper reported that ten "Israeli" soldiers had been beaten by four Arab youngsters.

The "Israeli" police forces "sought not to publish any information over this issue for it may be viewed as a national shame", the newspaper said.

It added that the soldiers engaged in hand-to-hand fighting on the past Tuesday night with the four Arab young men on Haifa seashore.

A police officer "preferred not to propagate any thing about this matter for it forms as a shame on the "Israeli" Army where four Arab young men managed to beat and overcome ten "Israeli" soldiers", the paper mentioned.

The hand-to-hand fighting resulted in wounding seriously three soldiers, who were moved to the hospital for treatment, whereas one Arab young man only was lightly injured.
