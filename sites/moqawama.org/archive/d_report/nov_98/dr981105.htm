Daily Report
 
 
November Thursday 5-1998:

Introduction
The Israeli occupation forces kept up their aggression against Lebanons areas. The Israeli enemy warplanes staged two sorties on an area in Western Beqaa.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) retaliated by detonating a powerful explosive device in Jezzine area killing three collaborators and wounding another.

At least 3 collaborators killed in IR operations (1)
As part of its continuous action to liberate the land and defeat the Israeli occupation, and on the occasion of Imam Ali's Birth Anniversary, the Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Martyr Karim Atwi at 10:40 today detonated a roadside bomb at the Dahr al-Ramleh road near a collaborator Lahd APC belonging to the proxy militia's Emergency Unit of the 20th Battalion. The collaborator's vehicle was completely destroyed, and those in it were immediately killed, an IR communiqué read.

At 11:15, when the enemy brought an infantry force to the operation site, IR's squad of Mohammed Mahmoud Kazem of Martyr Sami Melhem's Brigade detonated a roadside bomb. The patrol members were either killed or injured, the communiqué added.

Later the collaborator militia acknowledged that three of its militiamen were killed and another was wounded.

The killed collaborators were identified as Joseph Makhoul, Bassam Jabraiel, and Joseph Bajjani.
The wounded, Merhej Aakouri, was taken a hospital inside occupied Palestine owing to his critical condition; it was reported that his leg had been amputated.

The fresh casualties raised to 31 the number of collaborator militiamen killed in resistance operations in south Lebanon since the start of this year. Another 39 were wounded over the same period. Twelve Israeli occupation troops have been killed in the south in addition to the collaborators' casualties.

After the qualitative operation the collaborator militia closed Bater and Kfar falous crossings and declared a state of alert in its ranks.

The proxy militia also sealed off the Jezzine area by closing the road between Jezzine and Marjeyoun, the site of the occupation and collaborators' headquarters.

Moreover, IR at 14:45 spotted Israeli forces inside the Blat outpost. IR's squad of Martyr Ali Sh'hab targeted the forces with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

At 16:20, IR ambushed an Israeli-Lahdi security column as it was entering the Bir Kallab outpost.

The mujahedeen attacked the column with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades burning one of its vehicles.

In the meantime, another IR squad was attacking the Beir Kallab outpost preventing it from backing the targeted column.

Earlier, at 9:30 a.m. today IR spotted a joint Israeli-collaborator column which was on its way to the al-Ahmadieh outpost. IR's squad of Martyr Ali Kraiem in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted the enemy column with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

At 10:25, squad of Martyr Ahmed Oussaili targeted enemy movement at the Blat outpost scoring definite hits.

Israeli Aggressions
The Israeli enemy fighters launched yesterday two sorties on Zillaya Valley in Western Beqaa firing six air-to-surface rockets. Also, the Israeli batteries pounded the same area with up to one hundred and fifty 155mm missiles. However, the Israeli warplanes flew in the airspace of Nabatieh and Iqleem al-Toffah regions conducting mock air raids, but the anti-aircraft guns of the Lebanese Army repelled them.

For further details about the Israeli Aggression, the IR issued a statement and reported the following (2):

6:55, three 155mm missiles struck the area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem.
7:00, the perimeters of Shaqra exposed to three 120mm shells whereas the peripheries of Touline came under three 155mm missiles.
9:30, fifty 155 and 175mm missiles slammed into Litani River watercourse.

10:45, the outskirts of Mariameen subjected to six 155mm missiles.

15:00, two 155mm missiles landed on the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom.
15:25, seven 155mm missiles hit west of Yater village, whereas the area between Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr, Zawtar Gharbieh and Zawtar Sharqieh came under of four others.

18:20, five 155mm missiles landed on Aissieh Valley.

Hrawi raps Israeli theft (3)
President Elias Hrawi Wednesday urged the government to mount a major international diplomatic offensive to expose Israels theft of fertile soil from the occupied zone.

Mr. Hrawis call came at the start of the weekly Cabinet session. He said he expected international organizations and friendly nations to bring pressure to bear on Israel and force it stop this blatant aggression.

He also notified the Cabinet that the Foreign Ministry had formally complained to the UN Security Council, the command of Unifil, the European Union, and the United States.

The government, however, has not sought a Security Council meeting on the issue, to avoid a showdown with Washington, which is likely to veto any condemnation of Israel.
