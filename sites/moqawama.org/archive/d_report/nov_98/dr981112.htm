Daily Report
 
 
November Thursday 12-1998:
 
Introduction
The "Israeli" fighters raided into Iqleem Al-Toffah highlands for the fourth consecutive day while the "Israeli" gunners shelled the road linking between the towns of Habboush and Arab-Salim.

Meanwhile, Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah reaffirmed his determination to maintain the resistance war against the "Israeli" occupation troops in South Lebanon.

However, Lebanon's Foreign Minister Fares Boueiz appealed yesterday to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan calling on the United Nations to exert efforts to force "Israel" to return soil stolen from the occupied region or compensate for the ecological damage.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
""Israeli" warplanes Wednesday morning staged three raids on Iqlim al-Tuffah for the fourth day in a row."

"Security sources said that the three raids, which took place before dawn, had targeted the village of Sojod."

""Israeli" artillery in the Zaffateh position also shelled the banks of the Zahrani River and Iqlim al-Tuffah in the early hours of Wednesday" (1)

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
00:30, three 105mm rounds landed Mount-Safi.
5:05, the "Israeli" fighters raided two sorties on Mount-Sojod and one another on Mount-Al Rafih.
5:25, the warplanes raided into the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
Between 6:00 and 9:45, Mount-Sojod was bombarded by twelve 120mm shells.
6:10, Sojod and Bir Kallab outpost showered their surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
6:25, the surroundings of Tasseh Well exposed to one 155mm missile.
6:45, one 120mm round hit the surroundings of Tasseh Well.
13:30, the west of Yater village came under three 120mm shells.
Between 18:55 and 19:15, Mount-Al Rafih came under eight 120mm shells(2)

Hizbullah says martyrs die for Lebanon's dignity (3)
"Hizbullah yesterday commemorated Martyrs Day. The Secretary General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah stressed Lebanon's martyrs gave up their lives for the sake of regaining the country's sovereignty and dignity."

" Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, the secretary general of Hizbullah, the group which spearheads the resistance war to oust "Israeli" troops from areas they occupy in south Lebanon and the west Beqaa led a rally organized on the occasion of Martyrs Day. Sayyed Nasrallah praised Lebanon's martyrs saying they gave up their lives for the sake of the country, to liberate their land from "Israeli" occupation. The Hizbullah chief also launched a scathing attack against Palestinian Authority President Yasser Arafat for the latest peace deal he signed with "Israel". Arafat he said is a traitor who gave up the rights of the Palestinian people. Arafat he said is working with the "Israelis" and Americans to provide information on Hamas and Islamic Jihad groups he said were resisting "Israeli" occupation of their land."

"Israeli" occupation threatens Hasbayya and tells its dignitaries to condemn resistance operations (4)
""Israeli" occupation's command has started a campaign of intimidation on the clerics and dignitaries of Hasbayya urging them to condemn the resistance's operations against the occupation forces and their collaborators", reports from the occupied zone said.

"The occupation wanted the town's leaders to issue condemnation statement, call for strikes in the occupied villages and launch press counter-attacks", the reports added.

"The reports added that the occupation command has summoned to its HQ in the Marjayoun barracks four of Hasbayya's dignitaries and told them to condemn the resistance's activity in their area and coordinate an opposing position on these operations with the Jezzine dignitaries, "otherwise, tough measures" will be taken against them "including expulsion from the occupied zone."

"However, the Hasbayya delegation told the occupation command that "resistance is legitimate in Hasbayya, just as it is legitimate in Jezzine, Marjayoun and all the occupied zone's villages and towns".

Shocked by the delegation's response, the occupation command told the four men that tough measures will be taken against anyone who cooperates with the resistance men or any anti-occupation security bodies."

Occupation forces adopt new measures to curb their losses (5)
"UNIFIL disclosed that "Israeli" occupation forces have recently taken a series of measures including increased air raids with the aim of decreasing the losses in their ranks owing to the resistance's qualitative operations."

"A UNIFIL officer said "Israeli" troops rarely come out of their outposts whose fortifications have been boosted."

"Moreover, the occupation command ordered the soldiers to put on full protective outfits including helmets all the time, especially after one of the "Israeli" troops was hit by shrapnel in the head and lost sight in a resistance attack on the Blatt outpost last month."

"Another measure, the officer added, was to decrease motorized and foot patrols to the minimum.
We rarely see them on highways or roads", he said.

Military Operations (6)
The IR group of two martyrs Ali Hassan Darwish and Abbas Moussa Kobeissi attacked at midnight Sojod outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits. At the same time, the squad of martyrs Sami Abdel-Amir Kobeissi and Bilal Ali Bahmad attacked several military vehicles as they were setting up sand blocks near Sojod outpost with the proper weapons. The attack chalked up direct goals and thwarted the enemy troops to continue their mission.

Also, the IR squad of martyr Khalil Olayan targeted, at 13:10, an "Israeli" force as it was setting up a radar launching pad near Dabsheh outpost by using the appropriate arms. The Mujahedeen managed to score direct goals.

Furthermore, the IR men spotted enemy movements near Soweida outpost. Then, the group of martyr Mustafa Nazar targeted them with the proper weapons chalking up direct hits.

At 20:00, the IR group of martyrs Jaafar Ziwawi and Hussein Mannaa attacked enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the suitable arms. The Mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Lebanon asks UN to force "Israel" to return stolen soil (7)
"Lebanese Foreign Minister Fares Bweiz sent a letter Wednesday to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan calling on the United Nations to force "Israel" to return soil stolen from southern Lebanon. Bweiz said his letter called for adopting "measures to force "Israel" to return the stolen soil or pay compensation for damages to the owners and the environment.

"The "Israeli" foreign ministry formally admitted on Tuesday that civilian contractors from "Israel's" northern Galilee were systematically taking soil from the "Israeli"-occupied border zone in southern Lebanon. The "Israeli" army also acknowledged on Friday that "Israeli" civilians were bringing soil from Lebanon for use in "Israel" and said it wanted the practice stopped. A spokesman for UN peacekeeping troops in southern Lebanon, Timor Goksel, said Thursday that UN soldiers had seen "Israeli" civilians loading earth onto trucks in the border zone. Some 20 to 30 "Israeli" civilians, unaccompanied by "Israeli" troops, have been seen since September transporting soil into northern "Israel" where it is being spread over new agricultural terraces, he said. In a previous letter sent to Annan on Thursday, the Lebanese foreign minister had called on the United Nations to intervene with the "Israeli" authorities "to put a stop to a deplorable practice which is becoming more frequent and persistent."

Monitoring Group studies ten complaints (8)
"The Monitoring Committee for the April Understanding Wednesday began examining three Lebanese and seven "Israeli" complaints of truce violations", the Lebanese delegation said.

"Lebanon has accused "Israeli" troops of opening fire on a fishing boat, shelling the village of Braashit in the central sector, wounding a young man and injuring four houses, all since the beginning of November."

"In addition, "Israel" was responsible for shelling the village of Yater in the eastern sector and damaging three houses", the Lebanese complaint said.

""Israel" in turn has charged Hizbullah with conducting a number of attacks from civilian areas.
This is the 27th time the cease-fire panel has met to consider complaints about the illegal targeting of civilians."

"The panel, composed of delegates from France, "Israel", Lebanon, Syria and the United States, meets at the Unifil headquarters in Naqoura"
