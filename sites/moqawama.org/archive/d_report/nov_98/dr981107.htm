Daily Report
 
 
November Saturday 7-1998:

Introduction
The "Israelis" acknowledged yesterday that they are stealing fertile top soil from South Lebanon to the settlements in the occupied Palestine, a United Nations official said.

Meanwhile, seventeen Lebanese civilians have been abducted by "Israeli" occupation forces in Southern Lebanon including Sayyed Abaas Fadlallah, Imam of the occupied Kfar Kila town.

However, the Lebanese judicial authorities charged the Lebanese - American tycoon Roger Tamraz with conducting business deals with the "Israeli" businessmen and breaching the prohibition on travel to the Jewish entity.

Occupation forces attempt to disclaim responsibility for theft of Lebanese soil(1)
""Israel" yesterday admitted that its "citizens" are transferring fertile top soil from south Lebanon to colonies in occupied Palestine", a United Nations official said.

"Timur Goksel, spokesman of the U.N. Interim Forces in Lebanon (UNIFIL), said in an interview with the Orient radio that they were informed by "Israeli" army of the "result of investigation over the soil-transfer issue".

"Goksel said the "Israeli" army told the UNIFIL that it is true that some of "Israeli" citizens are transferring soil from south Lebanon, but it claimed that the operation was conducted by civilians for their civil purposes and was not a government-organized act."

""Israel"" had earlier dismissed reports of the soil theft as "nonsense".

"Over the past two weeks, Lebanon has been accusing the "Israeli" entity of trucking hundreds of tons of fertile soil in the eastern sector of the "Israeli"-occupied south Lebanon into "Israeli" colonies in Galilee (occupied Palestine)."

"Lebanon has sent letters to U.N. Secretary General Kofi Annan, the European Union, five permanent members of the U.N. Security Council and the Arab League, calling for an international intervention to stop the "Israeli" theft."

""Israeli" security and judicial authorities have started investigation into the case and "Israeli" army said it has taken measures to prevent the re-occurrence of the incident, Goksel said.

The spokesman said the UNIFIL has sent a judge to the site where Lebanon's soil are being removed to collect more information."

"Since its occupation of parts of south Lebanon, ""Israel"" has been stealing Lebanese water and using south Lebanon to test missiles."

"The "Israeli" army had established that indeed "Israeli" civilians had been removing the soil for civil purposes in "Israel"," he said.

"UNIFIL said earlier on Friday it had confirmed Beirut's claim that "Israelis" had dug up top soil in south Lebanon's occupation zone south of the town of Marjeyoun and shipped it into the Jewish entity."

"We have sent a report on the incident to the U.N. headquarters after the claims started 10 days ago," said Goksel. "Soil was being dug up and carried to the "Israeli" side."

"According to UNIFIL soil has been removed to a depth of two to three meters (six to 10 feet) in a 100 by 300 meter (yard) area south of Marjeyoun inside the occupation zone."

Arrested resistance aides include six collaborators (2)
""Israeli" occupation forces have arrested several people in the security zone, accusing them of collaborating with the resistance."

"Travellers from the security zone reported that "Israeli" security services arrested Mounif Nayef Hammoud, 33, and Faraj Ali Hammoud, 25, Thursday night in the village of Kfar Kila, Marjayoun, for collaborating with the resistance. The "Israelis" took the two men to the Marjayoun barracks for investigation."

"Kfar Kila is reportedly still under siege and "Israeli" forces are patrolling the village.
According to security sources, Saniyeh Nayef Abu Dalleh from the village of Zalloutiyeh and an unidentified girl from the village of Oum Toute, Tyre, were also abducted Thursday by "Israeli" forces for the same reason as the other two men."

"The sources said "Israeli" forces have also arrested a further 17 people from the village of Qlaiaa, Marjayoun, six of whom were members of the "Israeli"-backed South Lebanon Army (SLA) militia.
The 17 were accused of collaborating with Lebanese intelligence services."

"The sources identified some of those arrested as Antoinette Kesrwani and her husband Gerges Salim Karam, an SLA official, Abdel-Massih Gerges Nemr, Bassam Gerges Naim, and Nimr Kamel Nimr"

Tamraz charged for dealing with "Israel" Magistrate seeks five-year sentence for fugitive tycoon (4)
"Lebanese-American tycoon Roger Tamraz was officially charged Friday with conducting business deals with "Israeli" businessmen and violating the ban on travel to the Jewish state", a judicial official in Beirut said.

"The charges, carried in an indictment by Chief Military Investigator Riad Talih, were based on a report by the daily al-Anwar in September on Tamrazs alleged illicit business deals in "Israel"."

"The news report was essentially a translation of a story published the same month by the "Israeli" daily Haaretz on a reported visit by Tamraz to Tel Aviv in June to buy the "Israeli" fuel company Sonol.

According to the Haaretz website, Tamraz said that he tried to take over Sonol by backing the Shnap-Eran Group in its bid to appropriate Granit Hacarmel, Sonols parent company."

"He added that he had negotiated with Shnap-Erans two "Israeli" owners", who were identified as Moshe Shnap and Eli Eran.

In an interview with Haaretz yesterday (Sept. 5), Tamraz confirmed that he had wanted to acquire the controlling interest in Sonol, the English version of the newspapers website said.
He explained that his interest in Granit derived from the fact that he could market two million tons of products to Sonol every year, and added that what had made Sonol so attractive was its chain of gas stations, it added.

The group had bid $210 million for a 56 percent stake in Granit. Mr. Shnap therefore met a number of bankers and businessmen, trying to raise the money, including Tamraz, with whom Mr. Shnap met in Tel Aviv, it maintained.

"Magistrate Talih issued an arrest warrant against Tamraz, now a U.S. citizen who lives in Washington, and ordered his trial in absentia, demanding a sentence of at least five years in prison with hard labor."

"Tamraz, 58, had previously been sentenced in absentia to three years in jail for collaborating with "Israel". That case was based on a meeting in Paris between him and an "Israeli" diplomat, David Kimche.

Tamraz is also sought under two Lebanese international arrest warrants in connection with the $200m Intra financial company embezzlement case."

"A trial for Tamraz was ordered in France last year on charges of embezzling money from a bank in the West European country. He is believed to have brought the Paris-based Banque des Participations et de Placements to bankruptcy by mismanaging its assets."

"The case was brought by Intra, which Tamraz headed in the 1980s and is now predominantly owned by the Central Bank".

"A French investigation magistrate charged Tamraz with issuing a fake budget for the BBP in 1985 and embezzling at least 6,224,097 French francs (more than $1 million) from the financial institutions coffers."

"Tamraz was also at the center of a U.S. probe into election campaign donations for President Clinton. He admitted in a congressional hearing last year that he had donated $300,000 to Mr. Clintons Democratic Party in a bid to gain support for a project to build an oil pipeline from the Caspian to Turkish ports."

Sayyed Nasrallah: The enemy is looking for a way out (6)
"The Zionist enemy regrets taking the decision to invade and occupy Lebanon", Hezbollah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said.

"The enemy is looking for a formula that would save it from the great quagmire created by the Mujahedeen's blows", he added.

"Speaking at a reconciliation meeting between two families reached under his auspices, his Eminence said "We will not accept that the "Israeli" enemy achieve any gains at the expense of our people, land, or country".

"His Eminence stressed that resistance remains the option for liberation."

Sayyed Fadlallah calls for an overall stand to confront the enemy (7)
"Ayatollah Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah called for an overall stand by all the Lebanese to confront the "Israeli" going-too-far aggressions which are moving from one stage to another".
