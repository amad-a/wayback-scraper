Daily Report
 
 
November Monday 16 -1998:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions:
An uneasy calm reigned over the liberated areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa.
Sunday was the first day in a little over a week that "Israeli" warplanes have not raided the south. With the "Israeli" army maintaining a low profile in the occupation zone at the present time, the raids give the impression that "Israel" is taking a pro-active role. They also provide an opportunity for the flight crews to practice their targeting skills.

Nonetheless, the "Israeli" army spokesmans daily statements that warplanes have targeted terrorist bases in the south, disguise the fact that the only damage caused by the raids so far was to some rocks on a hillside in Iqleem al-Toffah (1)

For its part, the Islamic Resistance reported in its communiqué, the following "Israeli" aggressions (2):
6:05, the outskirts of Haddatha came under seven 155mm missiles and several 120mm rounds whereas the area between the towns of Haris and Rshaf exposed to seven 155mm missiles.
Meanwhile, ten 155mm missiles slammed into Aissieh Valley.
6:15, fifteen 155mm missiles fell on the deserted  Lebanese Army barracks and its surroundings.
6:30, three 155mm missiles struck Haddatha woodland, whereas Mount-Zaatar which is located between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun towns exposed to two others. At the same time, two 155mm missiles landed between Rshaf and Haris.
6:50, Mount-Sojod was bombarded by six 120mm rounds and four 155mm missiles whereas Mlita village came under twenty four others.
Between 7:00 and 7:15, the outskirts of Haddatha town, Slouqi Valley and the area between Haris and Haddatha came under eighty 120 and 155mm rounds.
7:10, seventy 120m 155 and 160mm shells pounded into Mount-Sojod, whereas Mlita village subjected to ten 155m missiles.
7:35, the eastern perimeters of Baraasheit town exposed to three 120mm shells.
8:00, seven 120mm rounds hit Mount-Sojod.
8:30, eight 155mm missiles struck Aissieh Valley.
8:55, Haddatha forest exposed to five 120mm shells.
22:25, two 155mm missiles hit the peripheries of Loweizeh village.

Military Operations (3)
In retaliation for the "Israeli" raids, the Islamic Resistance (IR) staged, at 6:00, a series of military operations on the "Israeli" and collaborator posts. The IR squads of Said Harb, Youssef Ali Awdeh, Ali Ahmad Shehab, Monir Smaha, Mohammad Ghazi El-Husseini, Mohammad Kamel Darbaj, Moussa Hammoud and Abdel-Amir Fadel attacked the positions of Radar, Blat, Taibeh Project, Beaufort Castle, Dabsheh, Tair Harfa, Hemayed and Jamoussa, respectively.

The Mujahedeen used the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.
At 8:20, and in response for the continued Zionist aggressions and "Israel's" violation of civilian security in the liberated areas by targeting the children, the IR group of martyr Hussein Adnan Farhat attacked Abbad post with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

 At the same time, the squad of martyr Moussa Fahs targeted Mashaaroun outpost whereas the group of martyr Kassem Kaoun attacked Qantara position. The Mujahedeen used the suitable arms chalking up direct hits.
