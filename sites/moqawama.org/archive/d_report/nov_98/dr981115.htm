Daily Report
 
 
November Sunday 15 -1998:
 
IR affirms: "Israeli" raids did not achieve goals (1)
"A UNIFIL source disclosed that "Israeli" occupation forces' intensive raids on southern areas aim at training new pilots to target resistance positions and confuse the resistance men in order to curb their attacks on the occupation's outposts in the south".

"For its part the Islamic Resistance (IR) affirmed that these raids did not achieve any of their intended goals."

"There have been no casualties in the ranks of the resistance men during these raids, nor did they affect the Mujahedeen's movement, an IR source told al-Manar noting that the number of attacks against the "Israeli" occupation forces and posts in the south is on the rise."

"These methods are nothing but desperate attempts by the occupation forces to boost their troops' flagging morale", the IR source added.

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
"Israel's" Army pursued it escalation against Lebanon and staged the following aggressions:
6:30, Mount-Zaatar was bombarded by eight 120mm shells.
5:50, several 155mm missiles hit Aissieh Valley.
5:55, the perimeters of Haddatha town exposed to a number of 155mm missiles.
6:40, ten 155mm missiles slammed into the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom.
6:55, the warplanes raided into the surroundings of Sojod village firing two air-to-ground rockets.
7:00, the warplanes staged another sortie on Mount-Sojod firing two air-to-surface rockets.
7:00, Mount-Al Rafih subjected to five 155mm missiles, whereas Mount-Sojod exposed to four 120mm rounds.
7:15, four 120mm rounds fell on Mount-Zaatar near Baraasheit town.
7:25, eight 155mm missiles landed Mount-Zaatar.
11:55, twenty 120mm shells slammed into Mount-Sojod.
19:35, the surroundings of Anan post was hit by five 81mm shells.
20:55, two 120mm shells fell on the perimeters of Majdal Zoun.
21:30, Bayada Hill was bombarded by fifteen 81mm shells.

Military Operations (3)
The IR squad of martyrs Taleb Ballouq and Mohammad Jawad El-Horr attacked at one O'clock dawn enemy vehicles as they were entering Sojod outpost. The fighters used machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades and scored direct and definite goals.

FCLD protests occupation's abduction of old couple (4)
"The Follow-up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons (FCLD) has protested the detention of an old couple and taking them to the notorious al-Khiam detention center."

""Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborator militia on September 23 abducted Hassan Saeed and his wife Abdeh Qassem Malkani from their house because their son refused to join the enemy's proxy militia, FCLD said in a letter addressed to Amnesty International."

"The committee demanded swift action and protest campaigns to secure the immediate release of the old prisoner couple."
