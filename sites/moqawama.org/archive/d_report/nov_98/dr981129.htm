Daily Report
 
 
November Sunday 29 -1998:
 
Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) successfully attacks deep inside the "Israeli"-occupied region twice within ten days only resulted in killing eight "Israeli" soldiers and wounding eleven others and galvanizing a nationwide debate over the "Israeli" occupation of Southern Lebanon.

Meanwhile, Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General Sheikh Naim Kassem warned that any attack against our infrastructure or any harm inflicted on our people or villages will be retaliated for.

 "Always ready" Hezbollah does not see a large-scale "Israeli" aggression (1)
"If the "Israeli" occupation forces target Lebanese civilians, the "Israeli" settlements will be in direct threat", Hezbollah Deputy Secretary General said.

"Sheikh Naim Qassem stressed "the enemy has only one option: to withdraw unconditionally".
Although Hezbollah does not see an imminent large-scale aggression (by the "Israeli" occupation forces), the party is always ready for any option the enemy may resort to, his Eminence told al-Noor Radio".

"Sheikh Qassem explained that an aggression is ruled out because ""Israel"" cannot guarantee the outcome of any offensive, politically and on the ground alike".

"We expect the achievements of the Islamic Resistance to be much better than those achieved in 1993 or 1996 in case of an "Israeli" aggression, for the resistance is in a better situation while the enemy is not", Sheikh Qassem said.

"On the political level, ""Israel"" does want to distract attention from the achievements made by the Arafat treason. ""Israel"" wants things to pass quietly and peacefully until all the Wye Plantation accord articles are implemented", he added.

"His Eminence underlined "Any attack against our infrastructures, or any harm inflicted on our people, or villages will be retaliated for."

"In other word the "Israeli" colonies will be in direct threat when our people or infrastructures are under any "Israeli" threat", he said.

Hezbollah says attacks humble ""Israel"" (2)
" Lebanon's Hezbollah guerrilla group, fighting a war of attrition to try to oust "Israel" from the country's south, said on Friday it had had a good week."

"It has penetrated the Jewish state's "security" zone twice in as many days, killed four "Israeli" soldiers in bomb and mine attacks and sparked a nationwide debate about "Israel's" presence in southern Lebanon".

"Sometimes we win some and sometimes the enemy does. With the grace of Allah, we've managed to jump ahead of "Israel's" sophisticated security this time," Hussein Khalil, a senior Hezbollah official, told Reuters.

"We've managed to put them in their place. We've taught them a lesson. "Israelis" don't pay attention to their daily crimes against Lebanon, or repent until they get lessons."

The latest attacks raised to 20 the number of "Israeli" soldiers killed so far this year in fighting in south Lebanon, which the Jewish state has partly occupied since 1978.

They were also Hezbollah's third foray this month into the 15-km (nine-mile) deep zone which "Israeli" set up in 1985 to protect its northern borders from potential attacks.

Khalil said better planning and more sophisticated tactics had enabled his group to "get right into the "Israelis"' homes and back them into a military corner."

On Wednesday, two "Israeli" soldiers were killed by a roadside bomb planted inside the zone. A day later, a mine explosion ripped through an "Israeli" patrol, killing another two soldiers.

A total of seven "Israeli" soldiers have been killed in the past 11 days. "Every one's asking what is "Israel" doing in the south," Khalil said. "They're under international and local pressure to pull out. So who's stopping them?"

In April, the "Israeli" cabinet adopted a 1978 U.N. resolution calling on its troops to withdraw "immediately" from southern Lebanon but said it would do so only with Lebanese guarantees.
"Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said on Friday his government was committed to staying in the south until its security demands were met.

Khalil reiterated Lebanon's stance that "Israel" must withdraw unconditionally and urged the Jewish state to cut its losses and pull back soon or face an escalation in violence.

""Israel" invaded Lebanon without asking anyone or asking for anything. So why must they get guarantees now to leave," Khalil asked. "We welcome any withdrawal, which is a victory for the resistance, but we will fight until "Israel" leaves."

Lebanon's south is the last active Arab-"Israeli" frontline. Last year, "Israel" lost 36 soldiers-- its highest death toll since it set up the security zone.

Netanyahu has often blasted the Lebanese government for not containing the southern conflict but Khalil said the Jewish state's own policies were responsible.

"These attacks are a natural response to the "Israeli" occupation. Their presence on our land is an aggression. It has been for 20 years. Lebanon has lost a great deal because of the "Israelis"...We paid with blood and now the enemy has to pay."

Roadside bombs: Achilles' heel of the "Israelis" (3)
"Military observers in the "Israeli" entity acknowledged that the enemy army feels completely helpless before the roadside bombs planted by the Islamic Resistance in the occupied zone which have taken the lives of many occupation troops."

"The "Israeli" radio which said this cited that four "Israeli" troops were killed in such bombs within 24 hours."

""Israeli" television's military correspondent said "Hezbollah has discovered the "Israeli" army's Achilles' heel, adding that the "Israeli" military has not been able to find a way to protect the soldiers from these bombs."

"Military observers noted that "Israeli" prime minister's space for maneuver is increasingly narrower, for a large-scale aggression against Hezbollah or Lebanese civilian targets will have the colonies in northern occupied Palestine showered by Katyushas".

"The Netanyahu cabinet will only take very limited measures", the military observers said.

There can't be a just and comprehensive peace without the return of southern Lebanon and the Golan Heights, says Gen Lahoud (4)
""Israel" may have decided to test the will of the new administration. We will not submit to blackmail and the "Israeli" escalation does not frighten us," said President Emile Lahoud, who was sworn in as head of state only four days ago.

"Our stand with Syria is clear and unchanged. There cannot be a just and comprehensive peace without the return of southern Lebanon and the (occupied Syrian) Golan Heights," Lahoud said at a meeting with MPs on Friday.

The "Israeli" occupation has threatened to hit Lebanese civilian targets after the killing of four "Israeli" occupation soldiers in Islamic Resistance attacks this week, according to the radio of the "Israeli" proxy militia.

Gen Lahoud rejected renewed "Israeli" demands for Beirut to provide security guarantees in return for a troop withdrawal by the "Israeli" entity, which has occupied southern Lebanon for 20 years despite UN resolutions calling for it to pull out.

"If "Israel" wants to withdraw, let it withdraw without conditions," Lahoud told the MPs during consultations to designate a new prime minister for his first government.

Foreign Minister Fares Boaez said on Saturday that Lebanon continued to reject Netanyahu's demands for security guarantees.

"Demanding guarantees aims at pushing Lebanon away from resolution 425 and entering negotiations to abolish it," said Boaez, referring to the 1978 UN Security Council resolution stipulating an immediate "Israeli" pullout.

In his inauguration speech on Tuesday, Lahoud accused ""Israel"" of having its own concept of implementing resolution 425. "Our answer is to refuse to give guarantees and arrangements for a pullout granting comfort to "Israel" at our expense."

He also gave implicit backing to the resistance groups, saluting "those who remain steadfast, those who are resisting and those who have fallen as martyrs."

Military Operations (6)
Forty five hours after midnight, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Sbeiti and Allam Soweidan targeted an "Israeli" force as it was trying to evacuate, the destroyed military vehicle on the road of Blat position. The mujahedeen used the adequate arms and chalked up direct hits.

Five minutes later, the IR group of two martyrs Akram Khatoun and Haitham Abou Daya attacked an another "Israeli" force as it was trying to reinforce the first one with the suitable weapons scoring direct hits.
