Daily Report
 
 
November Sunday 8-1998:
 
Introduction
Once again, the "Israeli" occupying forces violated the April 1996 Understanding as the cluster shells fell on the residential areas of Baraasheit town. Meanwhile, the "Israeli" jets fired two air-to-surface rockets at Al-Assi area.

In the "Israeli"-occupied region, the collaborators deported a 70-year-old woman after she had been beaten with a plastic hose and confiscated her olive groves.

In the meantime, a Hizbullah official source denounced the detention of Kfar Kila's Imam and the "Israeli" theft of rich soil from the occupied enclave.

"Israeli" enemy violates the April Understanding
"The outskirts of Haddatha, Aita al-Jabal, Sribbeen, and the residential areas in Baraasheet were bombarded from "Israeli" occupation outposts for over 30 minutes during which scores of rounds slammed the areas".

"In another violation of the April Understanding the shells which targeted the residential areas damaged the house of Abdel-Hassan Daher in Baraasheet when an 81mm cluster bomb exploded over the house while the family was inside."

"Moreover, an "Israeli" plane today fired two rockets at al-Assi area just north of the Western sector of the occupied zone."

"There were no immediate reports of casualties in the raid, the 90th by "Israeli" occupation's planes since the start of 1998" (1)

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
1:20, four 120mm shells landed on the perimeters of Sojod village.
4:00, Sojod outpost fired heavy machine gun rounds at it surroundings and flare shells overhead.
12:25, the west of Yater village came under eleven 155mm missiles, while at the same time, Mount-Zaatar was targeted by five 155mm missiles.
12:30, three 81mm shells fell on Baraasheit town.
14:35, Mount-Sojod was hit by two 120mm rounds.
16:25, four 155mm landed on west of Yater.
16:30, south of Zibqeen village exposed to four 120mm shells.
17:10, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom firing one air-to-ground rocket, and then continued flying overhead.
17:30, three 155mm missiles struck west of Yater.
17:45, the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom and Yater came under ten artillery rounds.
18:00, Rommaneh position raked its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
20:50, Mothallath post combed its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds (2)

Collaborator militia beats and expels a 70-year-old Woman (3)
"The "Israeli" proxy militia expelled Zahra Saleh Hammoud, 70, from the occupied town of Yareen after the militia official in the area, Ghazi abu Darraj, had beaten the elderly woman with a plastic hose and confiscated her olive groves."

"With cuts and bruises all over her face and body, Hammoud said Abu Darraj's goals were not political, but financial."

"He forces the residents to pay for every patch of land producing olives or oil, she said.
Collaborator Abu Darraj said I have to pay him in order to collect my olives but I refused. He forced me into his car and took me to the town square. He beat me with a big water hose in front of the residents, Hammoud stated, adding that none of the residents dared to interfere for fear of the armed collaborator's evil."

"After that Abu Darraj put her in his car and dropped her at the Hamra Crossing calling her names all the time; he told her never to think of returning to her land."

"Hammoud affirmed that the "Israeli" occupation's collaborators in Yareen seize crops and brutally steal from the residents".

Hizbullah condemned "Israeli" occupation's practices (4)
"A Hizbullah official source yesterday condemned the detention of the occupied town of Kfar Kila's imam, Sayyed Abbas Mohsen Fadlallah, by the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators."

"The party also denounced the theft of soil and other natural resources by the occupation forces besides the destruction of the environment and expulsion of residents from the occupied zone, the Hizbullah source said."

"He called on the "world's public opinion and human rights groups to condemn and deter the enemy from perpetrating criminal acts which stain humanity and the international community as a whole".

A Hizbullah delegation visits Druze religious leaders (5)
"A Hizbullah delegation, including Deputy Secretary General Sheikh Naem Qassem and Political Council member Sayyed Nawwaf al-Musawi, visited Sheikh Abu Mohammed Jawad Walieddeen of the Supreme Religious Council for Druze Monotheists."

"The Hizbullah delegation which first visited the Irfan (Knowledge) Monotheist Foundation was accompanied by the foundation's president, Sheikh Ali Zeinedden to Sheikh Walieddeen's residence.

The visit was part of efforts to enhance the national consensus on resisting the "Israeli" occupation and a gesture of appreciation for the religious leaders who are instructing their followers to reject the occupation and its collaborators while seeking to defeat the enemy and liberate the land."

"The delegation underlined that the resistance is an issue which concerns all the Lebanese. "It can be boosted with combined efforts by all, each according to his capabilities," the delegation said, commending the dignified stand taken by the residents of Hasbaya and the Golan Heights."

"For his part, Sheikh Walieddeen said, "Resistance has always been innate among the people of the Mountain and Wadi al-Taim. Druze Monotheists have always been struggling to defend the Ummah, its entity and dignity."

"He commended the Jihad of the Islamic Resistance stressing that those who collaborate with the "Israeli" occupation are "cursed" by all faiths."

Military Operations (6)
The IR group of martyr Youssef Baddah attacked, at 12:15, enemy force entering Hmayed position, while the squad of martyr Hussein Hammoud was attacking another force entering Baraasheit compound. Both groups scored direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR militants spotted, at 16:20, enemy movements inside Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit squad of martyrs Zoheir Kobeissi and Monir Smaha targeted them with the suitable weapons. The Mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct and definite hits.

Furthermore, the IR men observed, at 17:00, enemy movements near Sojod outpost. Then, the group of two martyrs Abbas Akil and Mohammad Ramadan targeted them with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals.
