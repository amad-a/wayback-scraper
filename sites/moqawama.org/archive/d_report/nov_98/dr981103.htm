Daily Report
 
 
November Tuesday 3-1998:

Fierce Clashes between IR and Occupation Troops in Oqmata and R'hayyat(1)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) yesterday afternoon engaged in fierce clashes with two "Israeli" forces inside the occupied zone in the south and the western Beqaa inflicting definite casualties in the ranks of the enemy troops."

"Owing to the ferocity of the confrontations, enemy warplanes had to intervene and raid western Beqaa twice to ease up IR's pressure on the targeted occupation force in R'hayyat."

"IR at 14:20 today ambushed an "Israeli" foot patrol in the Oqmata Plain area. IR's squad of Martyrs Ali Najib Sho'aib and Majed Jaffal attacked the enemy force with machine guns scoring definite hits in the enemy ranks."

"Meanwhile, IR's squad of Martyrs Ali Yahia Khalil and Mohammed Kamel Darbaj backed the first IR squad with rocket propelled grenades and scored direct hits in the ranks of the "Israeli" force.

Some "Israeli" troops were seen running away from operation site."

"Occupation forces shelled the area with smoke bombs to cover the evacuation of their casualties.
When the Beir Kallab outpost tried to intervene and back the "Israeli" defeated force, IR's squad of Martyrs Youssef Fakih and Nadim Skeiki attacked the outpost with the appropriate weapons and silenced it."

"IR's squad of Martyrs Fadi Oun and Ibrahim Alawieh attacked the Sojod outpost with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades also preventing it from intervening."

"In Western Beqaa, IR at 14:30 spotted an "Israeli" commando force in the area of R'hayyat. IR's squad of Martyr Hassan Shammas in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted the enemy force in a concentrated way. The Mujahedeen engaged in fierce clashes with the enemy force inflicting definite casualties in its ranks."

""Israeli" aircraft intervened at 15:30 raiding the Ain al-Teeneh hilltops in an attempt to ease the pressure off the targeted force which was still being pounded by IR Mujahedeen."

"At 16:50, enemy warplanes once again raided the Ain al-Teeneh hilltops which were also shelled by the occupation forces, along with the outskirts of neighboring Mashghara."

"Moreover, IR at 15:45 spotted enemy movements inside the occupation's outpost in Blat. IR's squad of Martyrs Youssef Badran and Youssef Baddah targeted the movements with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits."

"In the meantime, IR's squad of Martyrs Hussein Ghassani and Hussein Hammoud was attacking the Radar outpost. The Mujahedeen were able to hit a radar in the outpost's western fortification."

At 17:25 IR's squad of Martyr Nabil Akhdar targeted the Blat outpost and scored direct hits.
Finally, the IR men observed an "Israeli" force entering Zimraya position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Nassar Nassar bombarded the position scoring direct and definite hits.

"On the other hand, statistics prepared by the Consultative Center for Studies and Documentation showed that 137 operations were launched against "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators in October; 74 of the attacks were staged by IR men".

"The "Israeli" enemy acknowledged that two of its soldiers were killed and eight others were wounded in these operations, all of them in ones staged by IR."

"The occupation and its collaborators also acknowledged that one Lahd militia collaborator was killed and three others were wounded during October."

"Israeli" aggressions continue against lebanon
""Israeli" warplanes yesterday afternoon, fired two rockets into the mountains near the village of Ayn Teneh in the west Beqaa. There were no reports of casualties. "Israeli" artillery also pounded the hills of Iqlim al-Tuffah in south Lebanon. Earlier the Hizbullah resistance movement claimed responsibility for a resistance operation against an "Israeli" patrol near Iqlim al-Tuffah" (2)

For its part, the IR recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions (3)

The "Israeli" occupying forces continued their aggressions on Lebanon lands and staged the following assaults:
14:35, Mount-Sojod came under six 120mm shells.
15:10, about thirty 155mm missiles pounded into Maramel and Jaish hilltops.
15:35, the warplanes launched air raids into Ain El-Tineh highlands firing three air-to-surface rockets.
15:40, four 120mm rounds struck Mount-Sojod.
16:45, thirty 155mm missiles fell on the hilltops of Ain El-Tineh.
17:25, the west of Yater exposed to four 155mm shells.
17:30, Litani River watercourse in Western Beqaa was bombarded by seven 155mm missiles, west of Yater by five and Jal Tayaran by six others.
17:35, the old and new Beit Yahoun outposts showered its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:30, Jib Soweid subjected to four 155mm missiles, whereas the perimeters of Izzieh exposed to two 120mm shells.
20:30, several 155mm missiles hit Litani River banks.
21:10, one 120mm shell struck Slouqi Valley.
22:25, Soweida outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
23:10, one 120mm round fell on Slouqi Valley.

Exhibition of resistance and steadfastness in Damascus (4)
"Head of the Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) MP Sayyed Ibrahim Amin al-Sayyed to days ago inaugurated the Exhibition of Resistance and Steadfastness at the Assad's National Library in Damascus."

"The inauguration was attended by MPs Abdullah Qassir, Isamail Sokkarieh, Dr. Antoine Hatted, and Mahmoud Awwad besides head of Islamic Amal movement Hussein al-Musawi and Lebanese and Palestinian clerics and politicians."

"Visitors toured the exhibition wings which included 200 pictures and artistic constructions representing the heroism and sacrifice of the resistance men against the "Israeli" occupation. The fair will be open for ten days."

"Opening the exhibition in Syria is full of indications, due to the national and patriotic positions taken by Syria and its leader Hafez al-Assad on supporting Lebanon's unity", MP al-Sayyed said.
"He condemned the "feeble" Wye Plantation accord which "serves "Israel" and denies the national rights of Arab Palestinians to have their own state with al-Quds as its capital."
