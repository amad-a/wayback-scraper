Daily Report
 
 
November Monday 2-1998:

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
The "Israeli" occupying forces staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas:
18:40, eight 120mm shells slammed into Mount-Al Rafih.
18:50, Houla post showered its surroundings with a barrage of heavy machine gun rounds, whereas Mashaaroun outpost raked the perimeters of Majdal Zoun with another barrage. In the meantime, the peripheries of Sojod village came under six 120mm shells.
19:20, the west of Yater village exposed to five 155mm missiles.
20:30, nine rounds slammed into Mount-Al Rafih.
20:45, the surroundings of Sojod outpost subjected to eight 60mm rounds and three 120mm shells.
20:55, four 155mm missiles struck road of Sawan.
21:00, eleven 155mm missiles fell on the peripheries of Jibaa town whereas Mount-Al Rafih came under five 120mm shells.
22:00, Habboush River watercourse exposed to a number of 155mm missiles.

Military Operations (2)
The IR squad of two martyrs Issam Chouman and Hussein Wehbi ambushed, at 19:00, an "Israeli" foot-patrol as it was heading for Sojod village and attacked it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. At the same time, the group of martyrs Allam Soweidan and Youssef Awdeh showered the area of clashes with the rocket-propelled grenades. As Sojod outpost tried to reinforce the attacked force, the squad of martyrs Mohammad Ali Yassine and Riyad Youssef Dimashqi targeted it with the proper arms scoring direct and certain hits and preventing it from achieving its goals.

In the meantime, the group of two martyrs Abdel-Amir Ahmad Fadel and Khalil Moussa Mahmoud attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. The Mujahedeen hit directly its fortifications and prevented it from backing up the attacked force. These clashes lasted until 20:30.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades spotted, at 20:30, enemy movements inside Dabsheh outpost. Shortly afterwards, its fighters targeted it with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals. Also, the columns of smoke were seen emanating from it.
