Daily Report
 
 
November Sunday 22 -1998:
 
Introduction
As its usual, "Israel's" Army, occupying a part of South Lebanon, proceeded with its aggressions on the liberated areas.

However and in a move related to the results of Markaba operation launched by the Islamic Resistance IR, an "Israeli" officer and member of "Israel's" Northern Command has resigned his post. It is to be pointed out that Markaba operation resulted in killing three soldiers and wounding four others.

Elsewhere, Arnoun has become on empty village for almost all the remaining residents had left their houses as a result of "Israeli" repressive continued actions.

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
The "Israeli" occupying forces staged the following aggressions:
00:25, several flash shells lightened Majdal Selem town.
5:30, six 155mm missiles struck Habboush River watercourse.
6:30, Mount-Zaatar was targeted by three 155mm missiles.
7:45, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to two 155mm missiles.

10:05, several 155mm missiles fell on Qaissieh Valley.
15:15, three 155mm missiles hit Mount-Zaatar.
18:35, Haddatha woodland subjected to a number of 155mm missiles.
18:40, four 155mm rounds fell on the peripheries of Haddatha town.
21:15, the perimeters of Baraasheit came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
21:40, one 81mm shell hit west of Yater village.
23:30, four flare shells lightened the area of Houla.

Markaba operation's aftermath: Resigning an officer (2)
"In a related development, an "Israeli" officer and member of "Israel's" Northern Command, Col. Zayan, has resigned his post following last week's heavy losses sustained by the occupation forces at the outpost of Markaba."

"A powerful bomb planted by IR resulted in the death of three occupation sergeants and wounding another four. It was reported that one of them had his leg amputated today.

The "Israeli" colonel resigned after results of the special inquiry into the circumstances of IR's qualitative operation in Markaba were made public."

"On the other hand, in their weekly protest against the war in south Lebanon, "Israelis" calling for immediate withdrawal from South Lebanon led by Knesset member, Yossi Beilin, staged a demonstration in Tel Aviv renewing demands for an immediate and unilateral pullout from southern Lebanon."

Exodus from Arnoun due to occupation practices (3)
"Almost all the remaining residents of the town of Arnoun left the village, leaving their homes and livelihoods behind as a result of "Israeli" occupation's suppression and arbitrary measures."

"Nine families departed, leaving behind 15 residents who have refused to abandon their hometown.
Arnoun, five kilometers east of Nabatieh, is located in a no man's land between the occupation zone and the liberated territory."

"The "Israeli" occupation's post at Beaufort castle dominates the area and troops frequently enter the village to conduct house-to-house searches and detain residents."

"Even women and children were forced to leave Arnoun on foot due to an "Israeli" ban on the use of vehicles inside the village imposed in 1985. Cars were waiting outside the village to carry residents to Nabatieh and nearby villages."

"Fatima Hamdan, 55, said the exodus has gone on for 12 years. "Nobody is aware of us and nobody cares," she said.

"The last shop in the village has closed and the man who delivered drinking water has also left," said Hassan Qatabi, 55, "There's no longer any life in the village.

Mohammed Droubi, 60, said that most of the residents decided to leave when they learned that the Mokhtar, Afif Hamdan, and his son Abdullah had departed for Nabatieh.

"Mr. Hamdan said the treatment met out by occupation forces had become intolerable. "A child died last week on his way to the hospital and not even one official showed concern," he said. "Do we all have to die before they take action?"

Hamdan was referring to a five-year-old boy who needed urgent medical treatment but his family had to wait for morning to leave the village, but it was too late, for the child had passed away.

"We live at the mercy of the "Israelis" who are positioned in the Beaufort Castle a few kilometers away from us," a villager carrying his belongings said. "Whenever the "Israelis" lose a soldier in southern Lebanon they storm our village and arrest people and take them to the al-Khiam detention center."

"Cases of torture and maltreatment have been reported in the prison. One 14-year-old boy was snatched by "Israeli" troops from Arnoun earlier this year and taken to the notorious jail."

Military Operations (5)
The IR squad of martyrs Hussein Hammoud and Youssef Baddah attacked, at 15:00, an enemy engineering force as it was fortifying Baraasheit compound. The militants used the proper arms and scored direct goals.

Motivated by Defeat  (5)
"The "Israeli" Air Force has chalked up some 100 air raids this year, the highest number since 1985.
This appalling statistic should be greeted with outrage, condemnation and appeals to the international community to pressure the "Israelis" into calling a halt to this senseless aggression. But frankly, that "Israel" is being forced to rely on ineffective and money-wasting air raids neatly underlines the fact that the resistance is winning the war in the south."

"Generally, the air raids are only effective in destroying some boulders in remote wadis and have virtually no impact on the increasingly effective operational capabilities of the resistance.

Yet "Israel" must be seen to do something in its war against the resistance and given the growing reluctance of the "Israeli" General Staff to commit ground troops to counter the guerrillas, staging innumerable air raids appears to be the safest option."

"Despite the much-vaunted reputation of the "Israeli" Army, times have changed. These days, parents of new recruits have the cellular phone number of their children's commanding officer and can call him on a daily basis to ask how little Benjamin or Aaron is faring."

"The domestic backlash to military casualties in the occupation zone is possibly the biggest constraint on the "Israeli" Army's ability to perform efficiently."

"This year, we have watched the "Israeli" Army curtail commando operations against the resistance, reduce the number of foot patrols and build up the defenses of their hilltop compounds with reinforced concrete."

""Israeli" troops spend most of their time playing cards and listening to music inside their bunkers while mortar rounds fired by the resistance chip the concrete above their heads."

"The risk posed by air strikes to the guerrillas is minimal and attacking empty wadis ultimately means that civilians are spared from being harmed."

"Such air raids are, of course, totally reprehensible, but if the "Israelis" feel that the only way they can combat the resistance is to smash boulders in Iqlim al-Touffah with their missiles, then it only shows that they are facing defeat."
