Daily Report
 
 
November Wednesday 25 -1998:
 
Introduction
Hizbullah warned Jewish state that the Islamic Resistance (IR) cannot sit on its hands before its continued aggressions on the civilians and it is ready to put an end to these violations.

Lebanon yesterday lodged two complaints with the April Understanding Monitoring Group (AUMG) over the wounding of three civilians Monday and the damage to a number of houses at Kfar Tibbnit and Haris.

Meanwhile, the IR has released a videotaped footage of Monday's attack against the "Israeli"-occupied post of Dabsheh in which a Merkava-3 tank was destroyed.

In the occupied Palestine, the chief of the operations branch at "Israel's" Mossad intelligence service has resigned after the botched mission in which two of the agency's men were arrested in Cyprus.

Resistance warns enemy against targeting civilians:
"In response to the wounding of three civilians Monday and damage to a number of homes in Kfar Tibnit and Haris, Hizbullahs southern commander, Sheik Nabil Qaouk, accused "Israel" of breaching the April understanding in spirit and content and vowed retaliation."

The Islamic Resistance is committed to the maximum to the security of its people and the safety of its villages and civilians, he said. We would not spare any single chance to target the enemys patrols, by ambushing their positions and convoys.

"Sheik Qaouk met Mahmoud Yassine, 73, his son Hussein, 30, and Ali Yassine, 8, who were wounded when "Israeli" shells struck Kfar Tibnit. We warn the "Israeli" enemy  We cannot keep silent to these breaches  We salute the resistance men who are fighting the enemy. Today, we are having the perfect opportunity to target and exhaust the enemy army, he said.

"Israel" looking for a new formula to propose withdrawal from Lebanon
"A number of "Israeli" ministers are demanding once again reconsideration of the "Israeli" army's stay in South Lebanon having reached a conclusion that the "Israeli" proposal to pull out of south Lebanon according to UN Security Council Resolution 425 plus demanding security arrangements has failed", "Israeli" daily Ma'ariv said.

"During the "Israeli" cabinet's session, the ministers listened to a detailed review of the situation in south Lebanon."

"It was clear in this review that Hezbollah has recently developed its combat methods; this is apparent in the weapons which now hurt the "Israeli" army. It was also clear that the "Israeli" army cannot develop responses to these methods", the daily said.

""Israeli" war minister plans to travel to the US to meet UN Security General Kofi Annan and Senior US officials".

"Ma'ariv said "During recent secret discussions on this issue, Annan agreed to send a special envoy to shuttle in the region with the aim of accomplishing the "Israeli" army withdrawal a initiative."

It has been determined that the envoy will be Italian diplomat Jean Dominico Pico who had been behind several prisoner swaps between "Israel" and organizations" (1)

"During a meeting of "Israel's" cabinet Monday, Chief of Staff Lieutenant General Shaul Mofaz gave a briefing on developments in the south during which he said that Hizbullah had improved its operational capabilities. General Mofaz admitted that the "Israeli" army was having problems dealing with this."

" At the same time, the military front has stagnated, with "Israel" no longer willing to take the initiative against the resistance, for fear of incurring casualties. Having spent millions of dollars boosting the defenses of their bunkers, the "Israeli" soldiers are largely sitting tight under the protection of reinforced concrete while warplanes and artillery respond to guerrilla assaults" (2)

Hizbullah releases footage of attack against Dabsheh (3)
"The Hizbullah resistance movement has released videotaped footage of yesterday's attack against the "Israeli" outpost at Dabshe. Hizbullah resistance fighters had fired rockets on an "Israeli" tank outside the Dabshe military compound in the central sector of the zone "Israel" occupies in south Lebanon. A Merkava tank was destroyed. "Israel" said two soldiers were seriously wounded and one officer slightly injured by anti-tank fire. "Israeli" warplanes also went into action yesterday
launching raids against areas in the south".

Lebanon lodged complaints with AUMG (4)
"Lebanon yesterday filed a complaint with the April Understanding Monitoring Group (AUMG) over the injury of three civilians and the damage in houses, cars and property due to the "Israeli" occupation's bombardment of the town of Kfar Tibneet and Upper Nabatieh on Monday."

"The enemy aggressions, which targeted the towns' residential neighborhoods and its main street, resulted in wounding Ali Yassin, 8, Mahmoud Yassin, 75, and his son Hussein Yassin, 30, who is in a serious condition. The victims were hospitalized in the South hospital (Nabatieh)."

"The Lebanese delegation to AUMG has prepared a report including documents, photos, and a videotape showing the damage caused by the "Israeli" bombardment."

"The panel is expected to convene in a few days at the UNIFIL HQ in Naqoura."

"Meanwhile, the "Israeli" enemy lodged a complaint with the group claiming that resistance men has launched attacks from the towns of Jibaa and Nabatieh against the occupation's outposts."

"Israeli" Aggressions (5)
The IR communiqué mentioned the following Israeli aggressions:
6:05, ten 105mm rounds slammed Oqmata area.
6:30, Sojod and Bir Kallab outposts showered their surrounding with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
7:55, three 81mm rounds hit the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost and three others struck Mlikh village.
8:05, Mount-Sojod came under six 155mm missiles.
14:00, three 120mm shells fell on Mount-Al Rafih.
14:50, Mount-Sojod exposed to four 105mm rounds and one 120mm shell. Meanwhile, Tasseh Well watercourse was shelled by four 120mm missiles.
22:00, the ravine between Shaqra and Majdal Selem towns came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds whereas several flare shells were fired overhead. In the meantime, two flash shells were unleashed over Rshaf town.

Mufti calls on UN to secure release of religious leader abducted by "Israel" (6)
"The Mufti of the Republic, Sheikh Mohammed Rashid Qabbani called on the United Nations and the 1996 April cease-fire panel to pressure "Israel" to release the Imam or sheikh of the occupied southern village of Kfar Keela. Sheikh Qabbani was speaking during talks with a delegation representing the occupied village, who are holding contacts with top officials to win the release of Sayyid Abbas Fadlallah. He was kidnapped by "Israeli" occupation forces on October 27. The Mufti denounced "Israel's" abduction of the Kfar Keela cleric and promised to hold high level contacts to win his freedom."

Mossad operations chief quits over Cyprus fiasco
"The chief of the operations branch at "Israel's" Mossad intelligence service has resigned following a bungled mission in which two of the agency's men were arrested in Cyprus, the Haaretz newspaper reported Tuesday. The newspaper's military correspondent said the official, who can only be identified by his first initial, Y, handed in his resignation last week and it was accepted by Mossad director Ephraim Halevi. The two "Israelis" were arrested on November 7 near the southern coast of Cyprus and were charged with spying on sensitive military installations in the area According to the Haaretz, Y had planned to retire even before the Cyprus incident but moved up his resignation after taking responsibility for the bungled operation. The Cyprus fiasco was only the latest in a series of embarrassing missteps by the Mossad. A year ago two Mossad agents were caught in Amman after trying to assassinate a leading Palestinian Islamic official, Khaled Meshal, in the Jordanian capital. The agents were only released after "Israel" agreed to free the jailed spiritual leader of the Islamic Resistance Movement (HAMAS), Sheikh Ahmed Yassin. The Mossad's reputation was further tarnished when two of its agents were caught in Switzerland bugging the apartment in Bern of a Lebanese who they suspected was a militant. And in December 1997 a former star agent, Yehuda Gil, was arrested for having invented alarmist intelligence reports about Syria. The string of affairs led to the resignation in February of Mossad chief Danny Yatom and his replacement by Halevi, a former senior Mossad official turned diplomat" (7)

For its part, Ha'aretz, an "Israeli" daily newspaper, dealt with the event as the following:
"Mossad chief Efraim Halevy and deputy Amiram Levin have been asked to appear before a Knesset subcommittee to testify about the agency's internal investigations and personnel changes in the wake of the arrest of two Mossad men in Cyprus nearly three weeks ago."

"The two are expected to meet with members of the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee Subcommittee on Security Services within a few days."

"Halevy announced this week he was accepting the resignation of Y., a senior Mossad officer whose previous attempts to resign this year were rebuffed. Y., who is reportedly eyeing several opportunities in the business world, renewed his offer to resign when the Cyprus scandal broke."

"Internal inquiries and investigations at the highest levels of the Mossad regarding the Cyprus affair reportedly paint a picture of negligence, mistakes and overconfidence. The affair is the third high-profile Mossad snafu in the last 14 months. Previously, two Mossad agents were arrested in a botched attempt to assassinate Hamas official Khaled Meshal in Amman, and four agents were arrested during an attempt to bug the telephone of a Hezbollah activist in Bern, Switzerland."

"According to foreign reports, Y. heads a Mossad unit called "Neviot" or "Ma'ayanot." Those reports indicate the unit focuses on eavesdropping, wiretapping and break-ins."

"Y. and his unit were reportedly not involved in the Meshal affair; his previous resignation offer followed the Bern fiasco. Halevy chose to keep Y. on at that point, on the grounds that the failure had not been his fault."

"But once the men known as Igal Damary and Udi Hargov joined the list of arrested Mossad agents when they were caught by Cypriot officials on Nov. 7, Y. said he was no longer willing to continue in his present post, and that he recognizes his "ministerial" responsibility for the failure."

"Y. will continue at his present post until the Mossad decides who is to replace him. One of the leading candidates for the job is Y.'s deputy" (8)
