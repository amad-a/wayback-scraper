Daily Report
 
 
November Friday 20 -1998:
 
Introduction
Following the Islamic Resistance's (IR) Markaba operation on Monday, the calls inside the Jewish entity to withdraw the occupation forces from Lebanon had intensified.

A Knesset panel investigating the Markaba operation will discuss the details of the inquiry into the IR operation.

In the meantime, the "Israeli" sorties continued on the areas of South Lebanon. The "Israeli" strikes targeted a power station that supplies seven villages in areas outside the "Israeli"-occupied region.

More "Israeli" debate over occupying South Lebanon: We are paying a heavy price in blood (1)
""Israeli" war minister Yitzhak Mordechai yesterday inspected the "Israeli" occupation forces' outpost at al-Qobaa and told reporters that mistakes had been uncovered regarding the Islamic Resistance's roadside bombing in which three of his occupation soldiers were killed and four others were wounded on Monday."

"The incident is grave... because of things mentioned this morning in the field by the commanders... Some steps have already been taken and others will be taken," he said after visiting three wounded soldiers at Rebecca Sieff Hospital in Safed (occupied Palestine).

Mordechai later told the families of two of the dead soldiers that his army would investigate the incident and would update them.

"The Markaba operation has instigated calls in ""Israel"" to withdraw troops from Lebanon.
In a Knesset debate on Lebanon, science and technology minister Michael Eitan said "We are paying a heavy price in blood because the presence of our soldiers in south Lebanon is not a deterrent," noting that Hezbollah can fire Katyushas into occupied Northern Palestine any time it wants.

Eitan was responding to urgent motions to the agenda from five members of the "Israeli" Knesset of parties across the political spectrum calling for withdrawal or another security solution for northern Palestine.

"A Knesset panel will see the work of the military commission of inquiry that is investigating IR's qualitative operation in Markaba".

"Uzi Landau, who chairs the panel of the "Israeli" Knesset's foreign affairs and "defense" committee that is overseeing the investigation, said the panel will study the reports in order "to understand whether the incident at Tel al-Qobaa was indicative of the general behavior" of occupation army forces in occupied South Lebanon".

"Landau is expected to scrutinize reports related to the operation. He has lashed out at "Israeli" prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his war minister Yitzhak Mordechai, saying they should be investigated for adopting policies that have led to more "Israeli" casualties in occupied south Lebanon.

"Israeli" internal security minister Avigdor Kahalani accused his government of not doing enough to "solve the Lebanon problem," adding that "Israeli" occupation soldiers "are being used as paper targets in south Lebanon fields".

"MK Yossi Beilin stressed that ""Israel"" should withdraw its forces from Lebanese territories "bravely", and "implement UN Security Council Resolution 425, not just declare commitment to the resolution by words".

"UN Security Council Resolution 425 calls on ""Israel"" to immediately and unconditionally withdraw its occupation forces from Lebanese territories."

"Israeli" Aggressions:
""Israeli" helicopters raided overnight an area located at the edge of the zone the Jewish state occupies in southern Lebanon, hitting a power antenna that supplies villages with electricity. Two gunships fired three missiles on an area located between Jabal al Botum and Zibquin at the edge of the zone. One missile targeted a power antenna near Zibquine which provided electricity to seven villages in the area. Yesterday, two "Israeli" warplanes raided Mazraat Iqmata in the Iqlim al-Tuffah mountain ridge, just north of the occupation zone. It was the third "Israeli" strike against the south in three days. "Israel" intensified its air strikes after three of its elite occupation soldiers were killed and six others were wounded on Monday when Hizbullah resistance fighters detonated a roadside bomb along their foot patrol in Markaba inside the occupation zone, some three hundred meters away from the international border. Military Operations (4)
The IR group of martyr Ibrahim Faqih attacked, at 12:10, an enemy force as it was fortifying Baraasheit compound scoring direct and certain hits.

The IR squad of martyrs Suleiman Awada and Hussein Wehbeh attacked, at 21:10, motorized movements on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan towns. The militants used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades and chalked up direct and definite hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ziad and Hassan Zahwi infiltrated, at 17:30, to Qantara's entrance and then attacked the position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. The Mujahedeen opened fire from a close range inflicting a number of its members. In the meantime, the group of two martyrs Ali Tohmeh and Ahmad Dbouk was attacking the position from the southern and western sides. Several casualties were inflicted whereas the western main fortification was hit directly. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it, while at the same time, the soldiers were heard screaming as they were inflicted by a state of panic and terror.

'"Israelis" capable of claiming anything,' says angry sheik in Sidon shrine row (5)
"Angry reactions continued in Sidon Thursday over a report that "Israel" had asked the British Foreign Office to instruct its embassy in Beirut to inspect a shrine in Sidon reportedly belonging to a Hebrew prophet, Zevolon bin Yaacoub."

"Sheik Maher Hammoud, a leader of the Islamic Tawhid Movement (Islamic Unification) called for a mosque to be built on the site of the shrine to allow Muslims to pray in it and to prove the falseness of "Israel's" claims.

"Leading prayers at the shrine, Sheik Hammoud said: Those who have stolen fertile soil from south Lebanon and transported it to their settlements in occupied Palestine, and those who are bombing and killing innocent people every day are capable of claiming the property of anything, he said.
"The Islamic Waqf (Religious Endowment) has rejected "Israeli" claims, saying the shrine belonged to a pious Muslim known as Sidoun bin Yaacoub and that the Waqf has the deeds to the property.

Residents of the Dekermane region, where the shrine is sited, recently renovated the shrine and rebuilt the road leading to it."

Why are the "Israelis" now claiming this site, 13 years after their pullout from Sidon?, Sheik Hammoud asked.

"Sheik Mohammed Salim Jalaleddine, the Sunni Mufti of Sidon, said that "Israel's" claims are aimed at improving its international status."

"Israel", which has a long history of profaning sacred sites, is now trying to depict itself as a state which preserves such sites, he said.

"Israel" should release elderly couple or else Lebanon will file complaint to UN (6)
"Lebanon will file a complaint to the United Nations if "Israel" does not release two elderly civilians who are being held in the Khiam detention center in occupied south Lebanon. This was announced by the follow up committee for the support of detainees in "Israeli" jails. 75 year old Hasan Mohamed Saed and his wife 70 year old Alabda Malkani were detained by "Israeli" occupation troops in the south in September. They were taken from their homes in the occupied village of Asir after their son refused to join the ranks of the "Israeli" allied militia in the south. The committee called on the international community to secure their release. "Israel" holds some 180 Lebanese without trial or charge in clear breach of human rights conventions."
