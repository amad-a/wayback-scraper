Daily Report
 
 
November Friday 13-1998:
 
Introduction
The "Israeli" occupation troops pursued escalating the military situation in South Lebanon launching further aggressions on the liberated areas. The "Israeli" jets conducted a series of sorties on the highlands of Iqleem Al-Toffah.

Elsewhere, the news reported that the "Israeli" military command ordered the implementation of new plan in order to raise the morale of the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators.

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The Islamic Resistance (IR) released a communiqué in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:
12:50, fifteen 155mm missiles pounded into Litani River banks.
13:10, Aissieh Valley came under four 155mm missiles.
Between 15:05 and 15:30, the west of Yater village was bombarded by more than fifteen 155mm missiles.
16:30, the "Israeli" warplanes fired two air-to-ground rockets at Mount-Sojod.
16:50, two 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Jibaa town.
17:00, several 155mm missiles struck Jib Soweid area.
17:05, the "Israeli" jets fired two air-to-surface rockets at Mount-Sojod.
17:25, the warplanes raided into the peripheries of Zibqeen firing one air-to-ground rocket.
22:40, four 120mm shells struck Mount-Sojod while its surroundings exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
23:25, forty 4mm rounds pounded into Oqmata area(1)

An occupation plan to boost its troops' flagging morale, IR's counter plan (2)
"Resistance sources said the "Israeli" occupation forces a few weeks ago started implementing a new military plan against occupied south Lebanon", Lebanese daily al-Bairaq said.

"Enemy war minister Yitzhak Mordechai ordered the implementation of the plan to boost the flagging morale of the "Israeli" occupation troops and the proxy militiamen", the newspaper said.

"The plan includes increasing air raids on IR alleged posts in Iqlim al-Touffah and mid-sector plus intensifying artillery shelling of the liberated villages and launching large-scale detention campaigns against residents and clerics in the occupied villages "in an attempt to confuse the resistance and create a state of panic in the occupied zone."

"However, resistance sources affirmed that "steps have been taken to thwart the Zionist enemy's plan. In fact, the resistance has been successful in this regard."

"The newspaper said the resistance has adopted a series of measures in the front-line villages which are adjacent to the occupied zone in order to confront any large-scale "Israeli" aggression especially as the "Israeli" current ground, sea, and air escalation continues around the clock."

Military Operations (3)
The IR squad of two martyrs Mohammad Hassan Jaber and Hussein Mohammad Haidar attacked, at midnight, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite hits. Also, the smoke columns were seen emanating from it.
At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Issam Kamel Kansou and Bassam Abdallah Akanan attacked enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the Mujahedeen reported.
Also, the IR squad of martyr Mohsen Zraik opened fired, at 12:50, at an intelligence convoy as it was entering Zimraya post scoring direct goals.
At 16:20, the IR men spotted enemy movements inside Tair Harfa position. Then, the group of both martyrs Akram Khatoun and Ali Shehab attacked them with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.

Investigator urges death sentences for terrorists
"Military Investigator Riad Talih Thursday demanded the death sentence for 15 members of an alleged terrorist group believed to have links with the outlawed Lebanese Forces militia and the "Israeli" intelligence apparatus, Mossad, as the daily As-Safir and Al-Nahar newspapers reported (4)

"He also demanded the imprisonment of five others, including a lawyer, for three years with hard labor in connection with the same ring, which reportedly carried out terrorist attacks against Lebanese and Syrian targets in both countries."

"Those who face execution include 12 in custody and three who are believed to be living in Australia, where an LF office reportedly responsible for masterminding the attacks is located.

Two of those at large allegedly operated a computer company in Ain al-Roummaneh which liaised with the office in Australia through the Internet."

"Those who face imprisonment on charges of withholding information include one suspect in custody and four others who have been released on bail."

"The core of attacks took place in 1996 and included the bombing of a bus in Damascus which killed dozens of people and a machine gun attack on another Syrian bus in Tabarja which claimed the life of one person, according to the charges carried by Mr. Talihs indictment."

"The ring is also believed to have fired at a Syrian checkpoint in West Beirut, bombed a cafe frequented by Syrian workers in Boushrieh and a housing camp in Bourj Hammoud inhabited by Syrian workers."

"A bomb attack on two Syrian military positions in Dora and bomb attacks on Syrian Social Nationalist Party offices in Jdaideh and Bikfaya were also blamed on the ring. The incidents took place at various times in 1997 and 1998, the indictment said, without saying if any casualties were reported.

Two alleged members of the ring were killed in Dora in June when a bomb they were reportedly planning to plant at a Syrian military position in Nabaa, an eastern suburb of Beirut, exploded while they were carrying it."

"The ring reportedly also planned to kill Interior Minister Michel Murr and Water and Electricity Resources Minister Elie Hobeika and the head of Syrias intelligence in Lebanon, Major General Ghazi Kenaan, according to the charges."

"The men allegedly planned to kill Mr. Murr by detonating a booby-trapped car while his motorcade passed by on a road between Antelias and Bikfaya in Metn and to kill Mr. Hobeika in his private office in the Myrna Shalouhi Center from the top of an adjacent building."

"Neither of the assassinations were actually attempted."

"The indictment said its arguments were supported by suspects confessions, statements by witnesses and evidence collected by investigators."

"A trial date has yet to be decided on. However, it is due to take place at the Military Tribunal" (5)
