Daily Report
 
 
June Saturday 20 -1998:
 

Israeli Aggressions:
The Israeli batteries stationed at Abbad position shelled (1) in the morning Hojeir Valley, Slouqi Valley, Litani River bands and the perimeters of Touline and Qabrikha villages with a number of 155mm missiles.

However, the Islamic Resistance (2) recorded in its statement the following Israeli aggressions:
10:55, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Beirut.
14:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Kfar Dajjal areas.
14:30, Zaffateh position shelled four 155mm missiles at Aqmata area.
14:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Litani River, Zawtar Gharbieh and Zawtar Sharqieh.
15:15, Zaffateh position unleashed four 155mm missiles at the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
17:55, Zohaira position fired six 155mm missiles at west of Yater.
18:20, six 155mm missiles emanating from Zohaira position hit west of Yater.
20:20, Mothallath position targeted its surroundings with three 120mm shells.
20:35, Mohaibib position shelled Hojeir Valley with three 155mm missiles.
20:40, the area between Touline and Qalaouieh villages came under five 155mm shells.
21:20, Zohaira position bombarded west of Yater with ten 155mm missiles.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The statement issued by the Islamic Resistance (3) reported the following:
14:00, the Islamic Resistance militants watched an enemy force setting up sand blocks and detonating several rocks around Kassaret Al-Oroush position. Then, the group of two martyrs Riyad Mohammad Akhdar and Hassan Ali Zeineddine with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
14:45, the group of both martyrs Imad Kamal Salman and Hassan Mahmoud Zaraket attacked a motorized force as it was entering into Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.
