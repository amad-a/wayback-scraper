Daily Report
 
 
June Monday 1-1998:
 

The calls made by the Amnesty International for Israel to release the Lebanese detainees from its jails did nothing, knowing that these detainees have been apprehended for long time without trial or any legal excuse.

In the meantime, the Israeli occupation forces persisted in targeting the Lebanese civilians thus opening the way for Lebanese to use the possible various kinds of styles to expel the Israeli occupier and liberate their lands.

Israel tortures the Lebanese detainees (3):
The Amnesty International expressed its concern over sixty Lebanese, who have been detained by the Israeli agents in the wake of setting off a bomb near Markaba village on May 27-1998, for they are subjected to torture, ill-treatment and detention without trial.

If they werent subjected to fair trial, charged or tried in accordance with the international law, they should be released promptly, it added.

The Amnesty expressed its concern this in a letter addressed to Israels Prime Minister Benjamin Natanyahu, Minister of Defense Yitzhac Mordechai and the commander of the Israeli agents Antoine Lahd. Also, it demanded that the detainees should not be subjected to any kind of torture and ill-treatment and that they should be permitted to meet periodically with the International Committee for the Red Cross and their families.

On the other hand, UN Committee against Torture addressed the Israeli authorities a letter under a title of The Children Call. It called for taking immediately the necessary steps to secure the safety of the detainees physical and psychological circumstances in Khiam detention camp and release promptly Fayez Abou Faour, 16, and Mohammad Hassan Kazan, 15, for they are not accused of any crime.

13:45, the group of martyr Mohammad Ali Husseini targeted an enemy force near Jamousa position with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of two martyrs Anwar Al-Mir and Ibrahim Hussein attacked Anan post with the machine guns and rockets.
19:15, the Islamic Resistance watched an enemy force near Blat position. Immediately afterwards, the group of martyr Hossam Mohammad Ali Hallal fell upon it with the appropriate weapons.
24:00, the group of martyr Chadi Al-Zein rushed upon an enemy motorized force inside and outside of Jamousa position with the proper arms chalking up direct and certain goals.
