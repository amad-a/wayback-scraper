Daily Report
 
 
June Monday 15-1998:
 

Introduction:
The Israeli Army, stationed in South Lebanon, continued its aggression against the Lebanese violating the national sovereignty. Meanwhile, the Islamic Resistance pursued its activities, whereas the international stances over carrying out the Resolution 425 continued as well.

Elsewhere, the news reported that the exchange operation between Lebanon and Israel had become imminent.

Israeli Aggressions:
The Israeli troops (1) showered Zahr Mountain, the heights of Libbaya and Meimess and the perimeters of Qilya and Zillaya with heavy machine gun fire igniting several blazes. Also, the Israeli tanks shelled Litani River bands and Burghoz woods with a number of phosphorous missiles causing material damages only.

Furthermore, it was reported that an Israeli force had entered into Mazraat Burghoz, which is under the control of Unifil, searching a lot of its houses and questioning their owners.

The Resolution 425:
The Indian Minister of Foreign Affairs Fasbandra Raji (4) assured her support for an unconditional implementation of the Resolution 425 over Israels withdrawal from South Lebanon. This stance came in the context of message she sent it to her Lebanese counterpart Fares Boueiz.

For his part, Jordans Minister of Foreign Affairs Jawad Annani (5) declared that his country asserts that Israel should carry out an unconditional retreat from Lebanon in accordance with the
Resolutions 425 and 426.

Exchanging Hostages and Martyrs Corpses (6)
The Israeli newspaper Maarif reported that Israels Army is getting ready to carry out the operation of exchanging the Lebanese hostages with the Israeli soldiers remains.

It reported that the agreement concluded the last week will be carried out after the municipal elections. It estimated that it may be executed tomorrow or after tomorrow at most.

The swap, sponsored by France and supervised by the International Committee for the Red Cross, will be implemented at three stages, it added. At first, Israel should release fifty detainees from Khiam detention camp and deliver the fighters corpses. Then, the Lebanese government must submit Israel the remains of the soldier killed in the bungled operation at Insarieh. Finally and inspecting the bodily parts, Israel has to release ten detainees apprehended in Israels jails.

Mediterranean Agencies and Israel (7)
In a conference held yesterday in Limasoul in Cyprus, the League of Mediterranean Sea States Agencies rejected an Israeli application of membership.

The conference, in which sixteen countries are participating, ratified the agenda after it had dropped down the application applied by Israels Agency Itim. The rejection this came after Dr. Fayez Sayegh, the Syrian head of the delegation and Director General of Syrians Agency, Sana, had refused to enroll this issue on the agenda. The Syrian rejection was praised by the Lebanese delegation.
