Daily Report
 
 
June Sunday 14-1998:
 

Israeli Aggressions (1):
The following are the Israeli aggressions mentioned by the Islamic Resistance statement:
9:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
10:20, the jet airplanes flew, at middle altitude, over Western Beqaa areas.
10:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Qalaouieh, Borj Qalaouieh and Litani River.
10:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Litani River.
11:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle height, over Qana, Aiteit and Hanaouieh villages.
11:35, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Rmadieh, Hanaouieh and the coast line.
12:00, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered very low over Hmeiri and the nearby areas.
13:15, Zohaira position unleashed four 155mm missiles at Jib Soweid area.
13:35, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft flew low over Sreifa, Qalaouieh, Borj Qalaouieh and Froun villages.
16:15, the reconnaissance planes flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
16:20, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr village.
17:10, Zohaira position targeted west of Yater with four 155mm missiles.
17:15, the Israeli fighters flew, at middle altitude, over Nabatieh and Central Sector of South Lebanon.
17:20, the reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa region.
18:40, Zohaira position hit west of Yater with four 155mm missiles.
19:00, a reconnaissance plane flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
19:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Doweir and Sharqieh villages.
20:30, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Kafra and Haddatha villages.
20:35, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights. At the same time, Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
20:50,  a flying body was seen over Khartoum, Bablieh and Sharqieh villages, whereas the warplanes flew over Tibbnine and the nearby villages. Meanwhile, one 120mm shell emanating from Rihan area struck Tasseh Well.
20:55, Sojod Mountain came under seven 120mm missiles emanating from Rihan area.
21:00, the Israeli choppers hovered over Zahrani region.
21:15, one helicopter flew, at middle altitude, over Mohaibib village and Abbad position. At the same time, Sojod outpost fired one 81mm shell and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings.
21:45, Sojod Mountain exposed to four 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.
21:50, Zaffateh position unleashed six 155mm missiles at the perimeters of Jibaa village.
22:00, the helicopters hovered over the posts of Blat, Tair Harfa and Hamra.

Islamic Resistance Operations (2)
In its statement, the Islamic Resistance hold the responsibility of the following attacks:
13:10, the group of two martyrs Hassan Balhas and Mohammad Abdel-Hussein Moselmani targeted the Israeli-occupied Blat position with the adequate arms scoring direct goals.
18:40, the group of two martyrs Amin Mustafa Melhem and Ali Sharif Osman targeted Blat post with the proper weapons scoring direct hits where several smoke columns were seen emanating from the post.
20:30, the group of martyr Mahmoud Hussein El-Assaad attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the rockets chalking up direct goals. Meanwhile, the group of martyr Mohammad Hussein Mortada attacked Sojod outpost with the rockets scoring direct hits. Half on hour late, the Israeli choppers landed at the scene and evacuated the casualties.
