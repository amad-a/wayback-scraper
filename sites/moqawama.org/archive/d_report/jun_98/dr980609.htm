Daily Report
 
 
June Tuesday 9-1998:
 

Introduction:
The Israeli air force persisted in breaching the Lebanese airspace as its choppers and reconnaissance planes reached to Saida City, 45km away from Beirut.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance continued its confrontations with the Israeli troops. Meanwhile, the negotiations over the exchange of the Lebanese hostages and corpses with the body parts of the Israeli soldier is still in operation.

Israeli Aggressions (1):
The Israeli choppers violated the airspace of South Lebanon as they crisscrossed over the area stretching from the Lebanese-Palestinian borders and until Saida City.
However, the Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it recorded the following Israeli aggressions:
8:30, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Litani River, Zawtar Gharbieh and Zawtar Sharqieh areas.
8:40, two helicopters flew low over Berkat Risha and the peripheries of Majdal Zoun village.
8:45, the Israeli choppers soared over Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
9:50, the reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah and East Saida regions.
11:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Qana, Aiteit and the neighboring areas.
11:45, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Bazourieh and Borj Shimali villages.
11:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared, at middle height, over Qana, Hanaouieh, Rimadieh and Majdal Zoun villages.
12:30, the reconnaissance planes flew over Siddiqeen and the nearby areas.
14:25, the reconnaissance plane flew over Yater village.
17:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Haris areas.
18:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Yater and the neighboring areas.
18:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Yater, Aiteit, Qana and the nearby areas.
19:05, a drone reconnaissance plane over Kafra and Deir Ames, whereas and M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Qabrikha village.
19:15, the M.K. reconnaissance aircraft was still flying over Yater.
19:25, Zohaira position bombarded west of Yater with four 155mm missiles.
19:40, Zohaira position fired fourteen 155mm missiles at west of Yater.
20:20, the Israeli choppers flew over Houla and Doubay Fort areas.
20:45, the helicopters hovered over Bir Kallab.
20:50, heavy machine gun rounds emanating from Rihan area struck Sojod Mountain.
20:55, each of Rafih Mountain and Tasseh Well came under three 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area, while Sojod outpost showered the houses of Jarjoua with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
21:00, two helicopters soared over Blat and Ramieh areas.
21:20, Zaffateh position fired four 155mm missiles at Aqmata plain and four others at Mlita Hill.
22:25, the helicopters hovered low over Houla village.

Islamic Resistance Operations (2):
In its statement, the Islamic Resistance adopted the following attacks:
5:30, the group of two martyrs Ismail Younes and Riad Bjeiji attacked Toumat Niha with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying completely some of its defenses and chalking up direct goals.
6:00, the Artillery Unit of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades shelled Soweida outpost with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
20:30, the group of martyr Commander Hussein Bahij Nasser attacked the Israeli-occupied Blat post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. Five minutes later and as the Israeli troops opened fire at Jarjoua village, the group of martyr Mohammad Ali El-Husseini set off a big roadside bomb destroying the main fortification and inflicting a number of casualties among the Israeli soldiers.

Exchange of Hostages and Corpses:
Hizbullahs sources (3) made it clear that the negotiations run by the International Committee for the Red Cross to complete the exchange process between Hizbullah and Israel are now about the mechanism of the implementation. The swap is expected to release about sixty Lebanese detainees and forty resistance corpses in exchange for delivering Israel the bodily remains of an Israeli soldier who had been killed in Insarieh last year.

The Israeli Deputy Minister of Defense Siavan Shaloum said the negotiations over exchanging hostages and body parts are in operation.

For his part, Frances Ambassador to Lebanon Daniel Jwanou (4) disclaimed that his country has a role in the negotiations process, happened now between Israel and Hizbullah, over exchanging the Lebanese hostages captured in Israels prisons with the remains of an Israeli soldier killed on September 1997.

After a meeting held with the Secretary General of Foreign Affairs Ministry Zafer El-Hassan, Jwanou answered a question about the French role in the negotiations by saying: I reiterate what the President Jacques Chirac had said in his past press conference held in Beirut on May 31. The only negotiator in this process is the International Committee for the Red Cross.

On the other hand, the negotiations process over exchanging the hostages and corpses had taken too long time to settle dawn finally on the following principles (5):

 - Equating lives with the corpses and not corpses in return for corpses as Israel said before.

 - Releasing hostages detained at Khiam detention camp as well as those who are captured in the Israeli jails.

 - Liberating one third of the Lebanese hostages from Israels jails and two thirds from Khiam detention camp.

 - Applying the seniority principle in choosing the names of hostages.

Also, the exchange will not confine only to Hizbullahs hostages but will extend to include Communist Party, Amal Movement and other parties.

The negotiations finally, resulted in releasing sixty hostages whereas Israel had agreed, in the beginning, to release only twenty. However, Israel had acknowledged that it has forty resistance corpses raising, thus, another problem about the fate and whereabouts of the others. This problem is under negotiations now.
