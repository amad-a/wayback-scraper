Daily Report
 
 
May Wednesday 27-1998:
 

Israeli Violations (1):
The Five-Nation Monitoring Committee blamed the Israeli occupation forces for the violations of the April 1996 Understanding. The complaints presented by Lebanon to the Committee dealt with the Israeli aggressions that occurred on 21,23,25,26-5-1998.

Although the Monitoring Committee doesnt condemn obviously Israel concerning its aggressions on Lebanon because of the Israeli-American alliance, Lebanon is still presenting complaints against Israel in an effort to bring into view the Israeli flagrant violations of the internationally adopted laws and conventions, even though the international and regional competent bodies will not denounce the Israeli occupation.

Israeli Aggressions (2):
In a defiance to the international laws and conventions and following less than 24 hours of blaming Israel for breaching four times the April 1996 Understanding, the Israeli occupation forces carried out a new aggression against the Lebanese citizens.

These forces set off a bomb at 7:00pm at the outskirts of Nomeirieh village, Nabatieh District, killing the shepherd Saleh Atieh El-Khaled, 15, as he was grazing his sheeps.

A second bomb was detonated at 6:00pm at the outskirts of Maifadoun village, Nabatieh District, suffering the herdsman Ahmad Ali Safa, 35, with various injuries in his face and body and killing tens of his sheeps.

The third bomb which planted also by the Israeli troops at the same site didnt cause any human losses.

It should be noted that targeting the civilians by planting bombs is the most flagrant style concerning the breaching of the signed agreement especially the latest one which is known by the April 1996 Understanding, through which Lebanon tried to halt the Israeli aggressions.

In the meantime, the Israeli helicopter gunships (3) raided at 10:00am into the western part of Baraasheit village firing several air-to-ground rockets at a hill facing to the populated houses.

In Eastern Sector, the Israeli gunners (4) shelled at dawn flechette missiles at Zahr Mountain, then, targeted Litani River and the peripheries of Sraireh and Mazraat Loussi. At 5:00am, the areas of Meimess, Souq El-Khan, Kawkaba and Burgoz came under heavy machine gun fire.
