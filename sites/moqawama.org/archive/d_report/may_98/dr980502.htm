Daily Report
 
 
May Saturday 2-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The Islamic Resistance issued the following statement:
4:00 and until 5:00, Zaghleh position bombarded the areas of Naameh and Dallafeh Bridge with twenty 160mm missiles.
5:30, Sojod outpost combed its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
5:35, Bir Kallab outpost showered its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
6:05, six 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Safi Mountain.
6:20, Tair Harfa outpost targeted Jib Soweid area with three 130mm shells.
6:25, four 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan fell on Safi Mountain.
6:45, Zohaira position shelled Tair Harfa with two 155mm missiles.
6:50, Zaffateh position unleashed eight 155mm missiles at the peripheries of Jibaa village.
10:05, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Zibqeen and Majdal Zoun villages.
13:15 and until 14:25, the "Israeli"-occupied posts of Zaghleh, Shraifeh and Abou Qamha bombarded Litani River bands and the villages of Zillaya and Qilya with one hundred and fifty 155 and 175mm missiles.
13:40, the "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
14:05, an "Israeli" warboat was seen patrolling opposite to the coast of Khaizaran and Tyre.
15:10, Shraifeh position fired twelve 155mm missiles at Litani River bands.
16:05, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Malkieh and Chaitieh villages.
16:55, Al-Rafih Mountain was bombarded by twenty 155mm missiles emanating from Shraifeh position.
19:10, Shraifeh position fired twelve 155mm missiles at Litani River bands.
20:45, Safi Mountain came under three 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
20:55, Zaffateh position targeted Safi Mountain with four 155mm missiles and the peripheries of Jibaa village with several others.
21:55, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at low altitude, over Jibsheit and Adsheit villages.
22:30, Hardoun position fired 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire at the road of Majdal Zoun. At the same time, a drone reconnaissance aircraft soared over Western Sector of South Lebanon, whereas another reconnaissance plane flew over Shaqra, Baraasheit and the nearby areas.
23:40, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle height, over Borj Qalaouieh, Qalaouieh, Ghandourieh, Froun and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr villages.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it recorded the following attacks.
5:15, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Ahmad Al-Mousawi rushed upon Ahmadieh position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals.
7:00, the group of two martyrs Salah Chaitou and Nidal El-Hoq fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.
13:15, the Islamic Resistance militants observed an "Israeli" armored force inside Ahmadieh position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Mahdi Shamas bombarded it intensely chalking up direct goals. The Islamic Resistance traded the artillery shelling with the enemy until after 14:25.
18:25, the Islamic Resistance men observed an "Israeli" force inside Zemraya position. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of both martyrs Hassan Al-Saalouk and Faraj Ballouq shelled it scoring direct and accurate goals.

The Islamic Resistance announced proudly the martyrdom of the militant Mohammad Abdel-Hussein Moselmani born in 1979 at Chaitieh village.
