Daily Report
 
 
May Saturday 30-1998:
 

Israeli Aggressions:
The Southern Lebanon witnessed further Israeli aggressions that targeted the civilians inside their villages causing human and material damages. In this regard, the Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it mentioned the following Israeli aggressions:
00:15, two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Sojod Mountain.
00:45, an Israeli helicopter crisscrossed opposite to the seashore of Khairzaran.
1:05, one 120mm shell emanating from Rihan area fell on Sojod Mountain.
7:05, Zaffateh position bombarded Safi Mountain with eight 155mm missiles.
9:00, Aqmata plain came under three 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
13:55, the warplanes flew low in the airspace of Beirut, the capital.
16:25, Abou Qamha bombarded the hilltops of Daidabeh and Bir Zahr with twelve 160mm missiles, whereas Zaghleh post fired nine others at Durzi Fort and Bir Zahr areas. At the same time, Shraifeh post unleashed twenty five 155mm missiles at Litani River bands, while Ain Qinya position targeted Durzi Fort with four 105mm shells.
16:55, Bir Kallab outpost targeted Aqmata area with four 81mm shells.
17:10, two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Sojod Mountain.
18:10, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Lebanons Western Mountains Series.
18:15, the Israeli jets flew in the airspace of Hermel area.
20:10, Zaffateh position targeted Sojod Mountain with three 155mm missiles, while Zohaira post hit west of Yater with three 155mm missiles.
20:20, an Israeli warship was seen opposite to the coast of Zahrani.
20:25, Safi Mountain was shelled by three 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
21:05, Mashaaroun and Houla posts combed the perimeters of Shaqra, Majdal Selem and the adjacent valleys with 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. Meanwhile, Mashaaroun position targeted Qaissieh valley with two 120mm shells.
21:10, Houla post shelled the peripheries of Shaqra with several 105mm missiles fell between its houses, whereas four others emanating from Meis El-Jabal struck Majdal Selem and Shaqra villages. In the meantime, Mashaaroun position raked the adjacent valley with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
21:15, Bir Kallab outpost fired a number of Tow rockets at a civil car as it was traveling on the road between Loweizeh and Jarjoua villages burning it completely.
21:35, several 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Majdal Selem and Qaissieh Valley. At the same time, Houla post unleashed a number of tank rounds at the area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem. Meanwhile, Mashaaroun outpost fired 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at Majdal Selem cutting off the electric current which supplies the village.
23:00, the Israeli choppers hovered over Sojod area. Meanwhile, Jamousa position targeted the peripheries of Haddatha village with two 120mm shells.
23:50, Boslaya area was lightened by three flash shells.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
As regard to the attacks which launched by the Islamic Resistance, it reported in a statement that at 21:00pm, the group of martyr Yehia Kassem Abou Saleh attacked the newly built up Marj position scoring direct hits.
At the same time, the group of martyr Fadi Melhem targeted Mashaaroun position chalking up direct goals. Also, the group of martyr Bassel Ala-eddine fell upon an Israeli force near Mashaaroun position with the machine guns and rockets scoring direct goals.
For its part, Jihad Islamic Movement in Palestine announced that one of its groups attacked Dabsheh outpost and its surroundings inflicting a number of its members.
