Daily Report
 
 
May Sunday 31-1998:
 

The Israeli occupation forces didnt respect, once again, the April 1996 Understanding which is signed basically with the aim of protecting the civilians from the hostile activities. So, these forces pursued violating repeatedly the Understanding through killing the citizens and destroying the properties. In the meantime, the tremendous number of complaints presented to the Five-Nation Monitoring Committee didnt deterred the Israeli violence.

In response to the Israeli aggressions the Islamic Resistance militants carried out several military operations against the Israeli troops stationed in Lebanon.

Israeli Aggressions:
Once more, Israel had breached April 1996 Understanding when its forces stationed at Soweida outpost, fired, at 2:45pm, a Tow guided missile at the northeastern quarter of Arab-Salim village, Iqleem Al-Tuffah. As the rocket exploded at a field adjacent to Ahmad Moqallids house, Mohammad Moqallid, 19, son of the house owner, had martyrized promptly, whereas his brother Hussein Moqallid, 18, was wounded in several sides of his body. The two youngsters were inspecting their beehives placed there where the rocket exploded. Both were transported to Al-Janoub Hospital of Nabatieh for treatment.

At the same time, the Israeli troops targeted another civil car as it was passing on the highway of Jarjoua-Loweizeh with three Tow rockets burning it completely whereas its driver was miraculously saved.

In the meantime, the Israeli-occupied Dabsheh outpost combed in the morning the surroundings of Hamzeh family quarter and the deserted Al-Mustakbal school with heavy machine gun fire igniting a blaze in the nearby fields. Later on, Civil Defense members in Nabatieh city tried to put it out.
For his part, an Israeli military source admitted that an Israeli unit had carried out an operation against several houses in Sojod village out side the occupied region.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it adopted the following attacks:
8:45, following the brutal aggression that targeted a civil car on the road of Loweizeh-Jarjoua, the group of martyr Kamel Al-Beiruti rushed upon an Israeli force stationed at Bir Kallab outpost destroying the Tow launcher and inflicting a number of soldiers.
15:45, the group of martyr Mohammad Munif Ashmar attacked an Israeli force at Jabal Rafih area with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The clashes, in which the light, middle and heavy machine guns were used, lasted for about one hour.
16:30, the group of martyr Nasser Hawi attacked Bir Kallab outpost destroying its main fortification and chalking up direct and certain hits.
18:00, inspite the intensified security and military procedures taken by the enemy as its patrols move throughout the Israeli-occupied region, the Islamic Resistance militants managed to plant a big roadside bomb at the entrance gate of Bir Kallab outpost. Then, the group of martyr, Mohammad Ali Ghazi Husseini blew up the planted roadside bomb as a patrol of two military vehicles was coming from Rihan barracks to Bir Kallab outpost. One of the two vehicles was seen burning whereas all of its members were inflicted.
At 18:30, an enemy force attempted to give first aids and evacuate the casualties, but the group of martyr Ibrahim Ollaik showered them with the machine guns and rockets suffering a number of its soldiers.
At the same time, the group of martyr Ali Salman fell upon Bir Kallab with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from giving support to the attacked force.
19:15, several Israeli choppers tried to land near the scene in order to evacuate the casualties.
19:30, the group of martyr Mohammad Atwi fell upon an Israeli motorized patrol as its was passing on the road between Rihan and Bir Kallab crossroad with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.
22:50, the group of martyr Ali Shaaban attacked several military vehicles near Bir Kallab outpost and the scene with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
