Daily Report
 
 
May Friday 1-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
The Islamic Resistance issued the following statement:
00:30, Soweida outpost combed the outskirts of Habboush with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
00:35, Tohorra outpost raked the perimeters of Kfar Romman with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
7:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Nabatieh region.
8:00, an M.K. reconnaissance plane flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
8:05, the reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Central Beqaa areas.
8:25, the "Israeli" choppers and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
9:45, two helicopters hovered over Taibeh Project.
10:20, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
11:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over the area between Touline and Qalaouieh villages.
12:45, the peripheries of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri came under eight 120mm shells emanating from Hardoun outpost and fifteen others emanating from Shamaa position.
16:15, Tair Harfa outpost unleashed seven 120mm shells at Jib Soweid area.
16:20, the "Israeli" helicopters gunships raided into the area between Mansouri and Majdal Zoun firing one air-to-ground rocket.
18:25, Tair Harfa bombarded the area of Zibqeen Crushing Plant with thirteen 130mm shells.
18:30, the perimeters of Zibqeen and Qlayleh villages came under thirteen 81 and 120mm shells emanating from the positions of Tair Harfa and Hardoun.
19:10, it was reported that an "Israeli" warship was seen cruising off the seashore of Tyre heading northward.
20;15, a reconnaissance plane flew over Nabatieh region.
21:20, Baraasheit position fired two 60mm flash shells over Baraasheit village.
22:35, Shraifeh position bombarded Durzi Fort and Bir Zahr with 20 missiles.
23:05, Bir Zahr Durzi Fort came under ten shells emanating from Shraifeh position.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
On the level of the military operations, the Islamic Resistance issued the following statement:
6:00, the Islamic Resistance militants observed an enemy force near Radar position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Unit group of two martyrs Ali Badawi and Ali Salman targeted it scoring direct hits.
12:30, the Islamic Resistance group of both martyrs Youssef Bzeih and Moussa Salman attacked Hardoun outpost with the proper arms chalking up direct hits.
16:00, the Islamic Resistance fighters observed a squad of "Israeli" agents inspecting the damages that hit Hardoun outpost. Shortly afterwards, the group of two martyrs Khodor Khiami and Youssef Barakat targeted them with the appropriate arms inflicting a number of casualties among their ranks.
