Daily Report
 
 
May Saturday 23-1998:
 

Israeli Aggressions (1):
The Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it recorded the following Israeli aggressions:
00:45, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle altitude, over Nabatieh city and its vicinity.
1:45, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Beirut, the capital.
5:50, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Qana, Aiteit and Hanaouieh villages. At the same time, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle height, over Kfar Dounine and its nearby areas.
6:05, Zaffateh position shelled Safi Mountain with three 155mm missiles.
6:10, the perimeters of Jibaa village came under five 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh position.
6:15, eight 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Sojod Mountain, whereas Zaffateh position unleashed four 155mm missiles at Mlita area.
7:30, Tair Harfa outpost fired four 120mm shells fell near Mansouri tank.
7:45, the perimeters of Mansouri exposed to four 120mm shells emanating from Tair Harfa outpost.
8:00, Bayada outpost showered the peripheries of Mansouri with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
9:00, Shraifeh and Abou Qamha positions bombarded five missiles at the road between Yohmor and Zillaya and fifteen others at Zahr Mountain.
14:05, Bir Kallab outpost targeted Arid Zennar area with three 81mm shells.
15:35, Zohaira position bombarded Tair Harfa with fifteen 155mm missiles, while a drone reconnaissance plane hovered over Majdal Zoun, Zibqeen and Jibal El-Botom villages.
15:40, a drone reconnaissance plane flew in the airspace of Western Sector.
15:45, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Kfar Dounine and Chehabieh villages.
16:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Litani River.

As a result of bombarding the perimeters of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri villages, the houses of the citizens Izzat Taqi, Moussa Aqil, Hassan Slim Dorr and Ibrahim Mohammad Faqih were damaged.

16:55, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered, at middle height, over Shaqra area.
17:30, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
18:15, an M.K. reconnaissance plane flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
19:15, three 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Sojod Mountain.
19:25, Sojod outpost hit Sojod village with three 81mm shells.
19:50, the Israeli choppers flew over Aishieh and Rihan areas.
20:00, Shraifeh position bombarded Litani River with fifteen 155mm missiles, whereas Ain Qinya position shelled Bir Zahr area with five 105 tank rounds.
20:20, Zohaira position fired eight 155mm missiles at the west of Yater village.
20:40, the road of Sawan was shelled by three 155mm missiles emanating from Shraifeh position.
20:50, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
21:30, Bayada outpost fired two 81mm flash shells over Mansourieh and Biyout El-Siyad villages and 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds towards the sea.
23:35, a flying body was seen over Nabatieh city.
23:15, Blat post targeted Dabsheh area near Zibqeen village with four 120mm shells.

Islamic Resistance Operations (2) :
The Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it held the responsibility of the following attacks:
5:30, the group of martyr Salim Slim set off a roadside bomb as a Lahds foot-patrol was traveling on the road between Sojod and Rihan inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.
6:10, the group of two martyrs Moslem and Osama El-Khalil rushed upon Sojod outpost from several sides with the machine guns and rockets destroying the northern and western fortifications where the smoke columns were seen emanating from it.
8:20, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Mohammad El-Khatib targeted Ahmadieh position with the appropriate weapons scoring direct and certain goals.
7:35, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades attacked Tair Harfa outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.
19:25, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Ismail Younes shelled Ahmadieh position chalking up direct and definite hits.
19:30, the Artillery Unit group of martyr Nouh Hassan bombarded Zimraya position scoring direct and certain hits..
