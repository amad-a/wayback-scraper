Daily Report

Israeli Aggressions:
Few hours following the Israeli aggressions against Mansouri village where several houses damaged, the Israeli fighters (1) staged two sorties at 1:30pm into Mazraat Aqmata in Iqleem Al-Tuffah firing four air-to-ground rockets. Later on, the Israeli jets raided three times at 8:30pm into the same site firing further air-to-ground rockets.

The Israeli shelling spread to include at 10:00am Mansouri village causing damage to the houses of Moussa Taqi and Mohammad Zaanni. Also, several shells struck the outskirts of Biyout Siyad, Majdal Zoun, Zibqeen, Kafra, Yater and Mariamin Valley (2).

Detainees (5):
On the fourth anniversary of kidnapping Haj Mustafa Dirani, one of the senior leaders of Amal Movement-Faithful Resistance, the Secretary General of Hizbullah H.E. Sayed Hassan Nassrallah demanded that the detainees held in the Israeli prisons should be unconditionally released and regardless to the tension in the region. Also, he maintained that the Islamic Resistance would do everything in order to release them saying that the detainees are hostages and not internees.

We will use the force to thwart any treasonous plan similar to May 17 Accord. I have said this in public as well as in secret.

Israels Nuke (7):
The Israeli professor Israel Shahak disclosed that Israel has between sixty and eighty nuclear warheads directed to the all Arab capitals, Pakistans nuclear installations and some of the former Soviet sites. This information had been reported in his book under the title: The Public Secrets:

Israels Nuclear and Foreign Policies which had been documented by dozens of the Israeli press reports. It should be noted that this is the first time in which an Israeli opponent to the right-wing expansionist policies and an advocator for the peace process and self-determination of Palestinian people reveals such information.

Shahak, who is domiciling in United States of America, believes that Israel has an available plans for emergency states including the use of nuclear weapons to make massive destruction against the Arab civil gatherings and strategic sites such as Aswan Dam. He said the essential axis of Israels foreign policy is that the Hebrew State must be the only nuclear force in the region in order to dominate and has an easy access to Persian Gulf states where the oil wells and Americans vital interests. This is why the Israelis are worried about Indians nuclear experiments performed few days before.

Furthermore, he dealt with the international campaign led by Israel against the Iranian efforts to obtain mass destruction weapons and ballistic missiles from Russia and China. He made it clear that Israel, he described it as a bridgehead for the American and western capitalist interests especially in the field of arms and trade, is heading an international defamation campaign against Iran with the aim of destroying its radical regime which forms as a stumbling block in the face of the western markets just as Cuba was in the past.

He added that Israel is very interested in the earnings of the trading with several Arab countries, pointing out that there is inversely proportion relation between the public antagonism to Israel and the clandestine purchases from its commodities. It is worth noting that the trading exchange between the Arab countries and Israel reaches to about several billion Dollars, he reported. Also, he attributes the Israeli war against Lebanon to the commercial reasons to a great degree.

The writer accused Israel of spreading narcotics in exchange for security information in adjacent countries in order to weaken them and increase the negativity among the Arab peoples giving the example of Israels sponsoring of the Palestinian smugglers.

Shahak, severely, criticizes the Jewish organized comminuting in Canada and United States of America for they are more extremist than the Jewish citizens in Israel. The unconditional support for Israel and the defamation of any individual criticizes Israel is so extremist step to a degree that the Israelis have already known that the chauvinism and extremism of the Jewish organizations over supporting Israel in these two countries exceed extremely those of the normal Israeli Jews.
The concerned book was published by Blotou Publishing House. Shahak is a retired chemistry professor. When he was a teenager, he fled the Nazis prosecution in Europe and emigrated to Israel. As he his political and intellectual activities were impeded in Israel, he preferred to stay in the United States of America. He has very well reputation and credibility among the opponents of the Likud expansionist policy.

This book agreed with the nuclear expert Vanonos statements published last week over Israels nuclear weapons and reactors that threaten the stability and environment in the Middle East region.  It is worth noting that Vanono is arrested in Israels Jails.
