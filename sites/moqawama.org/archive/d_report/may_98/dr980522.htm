Daily Report
 
 
May Friday 22-1998:
 

Israeli Aggressions:
The Israeli batteries (1) shelled at dawn Yohmor valley and the heights of Jabbour, Abou Rashed and Qilya with more than twenty missiles. In the meantime, Litani River, Maidoun woodlands and the heights of Mashghara and Ain Tineh were hit by heavy machine gun fire.

At 6:30am, it was reported that five artillery shells fell near the road between Zillaya and Yohmor villages as a school bus bearing students was traveling from Qilya to Mashghara and Aitanit. The driver Salama El-Youssef and the students were miraculously survived.

Also, the Israeli gunners targeted before noon Zahr Mountain and Meimess area and at night the Israeli shelling spread to include Litani River, Qatraneh Mountain and Sraireh perimeters.
However, the Israeli helicopter gunships combed at 3:00am the area between Shbeil farm and Sraireh perimeters with heavy machine gun fire.

Moreover, it was reported that the Israeli fighters broke the sound barrier over Baalback, Hermel and Northern Beqaa areas and flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions.
On the other hand, the Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it mentioned the following Israeli aggressions (2):
6:00, Zohaira position shelled west of Yater with four 155mm missiles.
6:45, as a school bus was traveling on the road between Zillaya and Yohmor villages, Shraifeh position fired seven 155mm missiles struck only 30 ms from it, but the students were miraculously survived.
10:25, one warplane flew over Jbeil area, whereas several others broke the sound barrier over Iqleem Al-Tuffah and Nabatieh regions. Meanwhile, the Israeli fighters broke the sound barrier and conducted mock raids in the airspace of Central Beqaa where they were confronted by the Lebanese Army anti-aircraft guns.
10:30, the jet airplanes flew in the airspace of Jbeil region.
11:00, two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area struck Sojod Mountain.
11:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
11:50, an M.K. reconnaissance soared over Shaqra village.
12:50, four 120mm shells emanating from the outskirts of Rihan struck Sojod Mountain.
15:30, the M.K. reconnaissance planes flew, at middle altitude, over Qana, Deir Ames, Rishknanay and the neighboring areas.
16:00, Sojod Mountain came under fifteen 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area, while Sojod outpost showered its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
16:10, Zaffateh position targeted the road between Loweizeh and Jarjoua villages with four 155mm missiles.
16:30 and until 17:30, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Qana, Aiteit, Wadi Jilow and the nearby areas.
16:40, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Qlayleh-Nabha and in the airspace of Lebanons Western Mountains Series.
17:15, Kassaret Al-Oroush position unleashed three 105mm shells at Sojod Mountain.
17:55, three 155mm missiles emanating from Zaffateh post struck Mlita Hill.
18:15, Zaffateh position shelled Mlita areas with six 155mm missiles.
20:45, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Teffahta and Bablieh villages.
20:50, Sojod Mountain exposed to three 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area. At the same time, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
21:05, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Burghoz, Sraireh, Abou Rashed, Khallet Khazen and Kfar Houna area.
21:30, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Litani River, while an M.K. reconnaissance plane flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
21:40, Hamra crossing point fired 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at its surroundings and several 81mm flash shells overhead.
22:15, Zohaira position shelled west of Yater with a number of 155mm missiles.
22:20, M.K. reconnaissance planes flew over Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr, Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Iqleem Al-Tuffah where the Israeli fighters, also, flew overhead.
22:35, Zohaira position targeted the peripheries of Majdal Zoun with three 155mm missiles.
23:10, two 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal area fell on Qaissieh Valley.
23:15, the Israeli fighters and reconnaissance planes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights. Meanwhile, eleven 155mm missiles emanating from Meis El-Jabal struck Aissieh Valley.
However and for the fourth day, the Israeli troops have been continuing the closure of Kfar Houna crossing point (3) which is the only outlet for the population of Sraireh. The village locates at the dividing line between Western Beqaa and Iqleem Al-Tuffah regions and about twelve kms from the Israeli posts and five kms from the resistance positions.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Islamic Resistance issued a statement in which it adopted the following attacks (4):
6:00, the group of two martyrs Ahmad Al-Mousawi and Mahdi Shamas targeted Ahmadieh position with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits.
12:45, the Islamic Resistance militants observed Israeli bulldozer and Poclain machine behind Kassaret Al-Oroush position. Then, the group of martyr Moussa Jaber attacked them with the proper arms chalking up direct goals.

Torture in Israel (5):
Following the critiques made the Israeli human rights organization which is known by BTselem, the UN Committee against Torture, in its turn, criticized torturing the Palestinians detained in the Israeli prisons highlighting that under no circumstances shall the security excuse inside Israel be a justification for any kind of torture.

The Canadian Head of the Committee Peter Bournes said that defending the state is not considered as a justification for using the torture. He added: From our point of view, the security forces are torturing the Palestinian detainees.

Therefore, this statement assures the racial nature of the Zionist entity, known by Israel, which breaches at discretion the international conventions and human rights when conflict with its interest.

France & Resolution 425 (7)
Daniel Jwanou, Frances Ambassador to Lebanon, reiterated his support for the Resolution 425 and called for its an unconditional implementation. He, also, maintained that there is no peace without Syria.

He replied the questions of journalists saying: Frances stance is well known. Also, the Resolution 425 is very obvious. So, this Resolution must be carried out unconditionally and without any negotiation or delay.
