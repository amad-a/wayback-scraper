Daily Report
 
 
May Monday 18-1998:
 

Israeli Aggressions (1):
The Israeli occupation forces followed up their aggressive activities when the Israeli air force launched at dawn a sortie on Sojods Husseinieh building. The security sources said the jets fired four air-to-ground rockets their explosions echoed throughout the region.

In the morning, the Israeli gunners shelled intensely the outskirts of Choukine, Maifadoun, Zawtar Gharbieh and Mazraat Kfar Dajjal villages and Litani River bands.

In the Central Sector, the Israeli fighters crisscrossed overhead throwing extraneous substances over the areas of Hojeir and Slouqi valleys.

Also, Houla post targeted the perimeters of Qabrikha, Froun, Ghandourieh, Shaqra and Mjdal Selem with twenty 120mm missiles.

In Eastern Sector, the Israeli shelling extended to include Jabal Zahr heights, Qatraneh perimeters and Sraireh woodlands whereas at dawn the shells struck Mazraat Loussi and Litani River bands.

At 9:00am, the peripheries of Qilya, Mazraat Ahmadieh, Kfar Houna, Aishieh, Rihan, Hasbani and Zimraya villages came under heavy machine gun fire. Furthermore, the Israeli batteries bombarded before noon the outskirts of Maidoun, Yohmor valley and Litani River bands.
However, the Islamic Resistance recorded the following Israeli aggressions (2):
00:00, the Israeli fighters conducted low mock raids in the airspace of Nabatieh region, Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights and Central Sector areas. At the same time, Taibeh Project position fired three directed remote control rockets where the first struck the perimeters of Khorbet Selem, the second fell on the area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem and the third targeted the area between Khorbet Selem and Qalaouieh.
00:10, the warplanes flew low in the airspace of South Lebanon and staged mock air raids over Nabatieh and Jibsheit areas.
00:20, the jet airplanes flew in the airspace of Beirut and Bent Jbeil regions whereas several M.K. reconnaissance planes hovered over Beirut Southern Suburb, Ouzaei, Qabrikha, Khorbet Selem and Zawater. At the same time, the helicopters soared over Abbad and Markaba areas. In the meantime, Mohaibib position target Nahleh Valley between Majdal Selem and Shaqra villages with a number of 155mm missiles.
00:25, the Israeli fighters and reconnaissance planes flew over Haddatha and Baraasheit areas.
00:30, a drone reconnaissance plane flew over Kafra, Yater and Haris villages.
00:35, the Israeli fighters and M.K. reconnaissance aircraft over Qasmieh and the nearby areas.
1:30, the warplanes flew over Qalaouieh village throwing extraneous substances.
5:00, the jet airplanes flew low over Western Beirut.
7:00, the Israeli fighters raided into Sojod village firing two air-to-ground rockets.
7:25 and until 8:10, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
9:20, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft soared over Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
10:20, an M.K. reconnaissance plane hovered, at middle altitude, over Majdal Selem village.
10:30, Sojod and Bir Kallab outposts combed their surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
11:05, the Israeli choppers hovered over Rihan area.
14:05, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Qabrikha, Shaqra and Baraasheit villages.
14:10, a reconnaissance plane flew low over Shaqra village.
14:20, a reconnaissance plane flew over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River bands.
16:35, the helicopters soared over Aishieh area.
18:45, a reconnaissance plane flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
19:00, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Aqbieh and Beisarieh villages.
19:10, a reconnaissance plane flew over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh and Litani River bands.
20:00, the Israeli jet airplanes conducted very low mock air raids over Iqleem Al-Tuffah region.
20:10, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa.
20:15, the jet airplanes staged mock raids over Naameh area.
20:35, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa, Iqleem Al-Tuffah and Nabatieh regions.
20:40, the warplanes flew low in the airspace of Saida and its suburbs where they were met by the Lebanese Army anti-aircraft guns.
20:50, the Israeli fighters conducted low mock raids over the Central Sector of South Lebanon, East Saida and Iqleem Al-Tuffah.
21:05, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Western Beqaa.
21:10, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Central Beqaa.
23:10, the Israeli jets flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah heights.
23:50, Mashaaroun position fired two 120mm flash shells over Slouqi Valley.

Setting up new post (3):
The Israeli agent Antoine Lahds Militia set up a new position in replacement of another old one and near the houses of Houla village thus violating the international laws. This step would form a pretext for the occupation forces to claim that the resistance men are aiming at the civilians in every attack on this position.

Israeli repressive actions (4):
The population of Arkoub Sector asked the Unifils Norwegian Battalion to protect them from the Israeli abuses. From time to time, the Israeli troops and their agents had used to send for some people for questioning and expatriate others out of the occupied region as it was happened with the internee Mohammad Moussa Daher and his family last week.

Resistance Operations (5):
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades attacked Qantara position, at 5:20am, with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades and Mashaaroun position with the proper weapons scoring accurate hits.
For its part, Amal Movement announced that one of its groups observed at 7:30am an Israeli military reinforcements inside Beaufort Castle and on the road leading to it. Immediately afterwards, the fighters target them with mortar shells and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties among them. At the same time, the fighters came back safely to their bases.

Israeli morale (6):
As-Safir Lebanese newspaper observed Voice of Israel and reported the following:
The Israeli news broadcaster was reading in the course of program The Newspapers Today. When he reached to Yediot Ahronot he said:

In Ahronot today, there is a photo for an Israeli soldier exposing a tattoo man marking his shoulder. Under the photo there is an article talking about the increasing of tattoo phenomenon among the Israeli soldiers as a sign to take away the bad lack before going to serve in Lebanon.

The news broadcaster added: The poll showed that the symbol of dragon together with death angel is the most one used in tattooing.

It should be noted here that the Israeli has to know that there is no necessity for such tattoo or amulets. From the beginning the Israeli occupier had been demanded not to invade Lebanon, and now he ought to leave thus saving himself from the mounting losses. Now that he does it, so let the tattoo protects him from the resistance strikes!!.

Israel had seen a little of our strength, godwilling, it will see our real strength, as Sayed Abbas Al-Mousawi, Master of the Islamic Resistance martyrs, said.

Lubrani: No retreat (7):
The so-called Israeli Coordinator in Lebanon Uri Lubrani said: No retreat without security arrangements, for no one in Israel wants to come back again to Lebanon. If there were no security guarantees, we would stay in Lebanon.

France and the Resolution 425 (8)
After a meeting with the Lebanese Foreign Affairs Minister Fares Boueiz, the French Ambassador Daniel Jwanou maintained that Frances stance over the Resolution 425 is very clear. Although, it is correct that there is a new Israeli proposal. Also, it is right that Israel had acknowledged the Resolution 425 for the first time since twenty years. But the Israeli government put several conditions and asked for guarantees not noticed in its text.

He ended: We fully respect the Resolution 425 and all resolutions related to the Middle East cause that can serve the Syrians and Palestinians. We fully respect the international law.

For his part, the Germany State Minister Verner Huer (9) confirmed his support pertaining to the unconditional implementation of the Resolution 425. It is obvious that we uphold a complete and unconditional fulfilling of the Resolution 425 in order to secure the stability and peace among the peoples of this region, he reported.

Israel and Pollution (10):
Green Peace Office in Israel reiterated that the Israeli authorities are still burying toxic waste in the Mediterranean Sea.

It reported: The Israeli Environment Ministry had given the Israeli Electricity Company a private permission to bury 15 thousand tons of charcoal ashes polluted with other mineral materials in the international water 100km away from the seashore. It added that millions tons of toxic waste were buried at this site last decade.

Also, Israel has permitted for an American sterilization company called Haifa for Chemicals to bury 60 thousand tons of toxic waste in the Mediterranean Sea until the coming October, Green Peace mentioned.

It concluded: The Israeli practices are considered as direct violation for the international agreements Israel, so far, rejects to sign.
