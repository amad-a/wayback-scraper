Daily Report
 
 
May Wednesday 13-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions:
In a surprising development, "Israel" had escalated the military situation on the Lebanese front, as they warplanes raided Palestinian medical and management centers located at Deir Taanayel, at the outskirts of Shtoura in the Beqaa Valley, belonging to the Fatah Revolutionary Movement headed by Colonel Abou Moussa. The aggressive raids resulted in the martyrdom of 10 members of the movement, and wounding more than 25 other peoples in 3 raids conducted by "Israeli" warplanes at 1:15, when they launched many heavy missiles coupled with 4 loaded dams, each of them including 188 cluster bombs which began to explode between the peoples present in these facilities, inflicting heavy damages in the houses and tents inside this location.

The correspondent of local-based Lebanese daily newspaper "Al Safir", who was able to enter the targeted area, stated that 'It was sowed by missiles and bombs which created large holes, smashed a massive number of trees, uprooted the plants and destroyed the small houses and tents which constitute this outpost, notably the tents serving for medical recovery, whose beds and medicaments were dispersed in all directions after being completely smashed by the bombs and missiles.

Dr. Abou Mohamed, the center's responsible told "Al Safir" that "The facility is serving the medical and management work and isn't a military outpost, but a place where all kinds of health cases are treated from all places in the Lebanon".

The responsible affirmed that "All martyrs and injured peoples are either from the medical staff, or physician assistants or employees or guards of the center, which doesn't include anti-aircraft machine guns and weapons because it is not a military outpost".

The French Position Regarding the Raid:
The French Foreign Ministry stressed through its spokeswoman on the condemnation of the "Israeli" raid, adding that "France has no other option but to condemn this brutal military act".

The Russian Position:
The Russian emissary to the Middle-East region, Viktor Posovaliok, underlined his regret towards the "Israeli" aggression, stating that "We are against these kinds of acts and we regret very much the loss of human being  victims who fell as a result of this aggression".

As for the daily aggressions, it was reported that the "Israeli" occupation forces shelled early this morning by artillery and heavy machine guns the regions of Hasbaya and Mimas, as well as Al-Dahr Mountain Heights.

The Prisoners and Detainees in The "Israeli" Prisons:
In the file of prisoners and detainees, the head of International Red Cross delegation to the Lebanon, Jean-Jacques Frazer, announced that there are persistent contacts in order to resume the visits previously conducted by both the International Red Cross and the prisoners' relatives to these prisoners at Al-Khiam prison.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" occupation forces continued to maintain the liberated prisoner Houda Hamadeh under house arrest in her village of Maroun Al-Ras, following her release from Al-Khiam detainment center on April 7, 1998, despite her very ill health situation which was the prime reason behind her release.

Houda, who lives in her native village Maroun Al-Ras, had asked the follow-up committee which works in support for the Lebanese detainees cause in the "Israeli" prisons and Amnesty International Organization to act towards forcing the occupation forces to allow the prisoner Hamadeh to leave the occupied zone.

Around the Issue of Implementing the Resolution 425:
Russian emissary to the Middle-East region, Viktor Posovaliok, reiterated his country's position pertaining to the UN resolution 425, and stating that "The position of Russia is known, we are supporting the implementation of this resolution as clearly stipulated and as our obligation urges us to implement the international resolutions, there is no new happenings, thus the resolutions should be implemented".
