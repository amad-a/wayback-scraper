May Thursday 14-1998:
 

Lebanese Resentment from the American Ambassador's Statement
At the aftermath of the "Israeli" raids on the Taanayel area in the Lebanese Beqaa district, and instead of condemning the "Israeli" violation of the Lebanese national sovereignty, the attack that resulted in the martyrdom of 10 innocent peoples and the wounding of another 20 citizen, the American ambassador to Lebanon, Richard Jones, expressed his total surprise towards the presence of the Palestinians in the Lebanon, in a speech which was addressed to them, and never referring to the killing of some of them, and the wounding of the others.

This American sealing of the "Israeli" criminal act had prompted instant Lebanese denouncing reaction from different figures, mainly from former Lebanese Prime Minister, Dr. Salim Al-Hoss, the current deputy of Beirut, who replied to the American ambassador's question related to the reason behind the Palestinian presence in the Lebanon as following: "I would like to draw the attention of the ambassador that the Palestinians are spread I the Beqaa, in the North, in Beirut's suburbs and in the South, that is throughout Lebanon, for a very simple reason, which is related to their expulsion from their own homeland in Palestine and now evicted in all the Arab World, with the blessing of the world's super powers headed by the United States of America. Their case is still unsettled until this very day, and they will always be dispersed throughout the world as long as these countries remain supporting "Israel" by financial and weaponry means, providing it with political and diplomatic cover, and biasing "Israel" through preventing the establishment of the Palestinian homeland on the soil of Palestine.

"People are still wondering about the United States of America position towards Israel's use of American weaponry in its aggressive operations targeting Southern Lebanon, the Western Beqaa and refugee camps, but also during its invasion of the South in 1978, the comprehensive war launched in Lebanon in 1982, both large criminal aggressive operations conducted in 1993 and 1996, in addition to the various other aggressive acts which targeted the Arab nation throughout half a century of its presence".

Evicting a Lebanese Family from The Occupied Zone:
In an arbitrary procedure that totally contradicts the international laws and human rights, the "Israeli" occupation forces have evicted the Lebanese citizen Mohamed Moussa Daher  (70 years-old), his daughter Mona (27 years-old) and grand-daughter Maya (5 months-old) from Shaba'a village; Mohamed and Mona are the father and sister of the liberated prisoner Ghassan Daher, released by the occupation forces last week and expulsed from his native village after spending 12 years in the prison of Al-Khiam.

The newly-expulsed Daher affirmed that the agent Mohamed Naba'a, the security responsible of the collaborators in the occupied town of Shaba'a, ordered him and his daughter at 8:00 in the morning to move to the station of the security apparatus belonging to the collaborating militia in Hasbayya. The order was submitted by Naba'a to the father in his home in Shaba'a, while the daughter received the order in the classroom of the Shaba'a school. As both citizens arrived to the security station in Tal Zaghleh, one of its officers informed them that he has taken the decision to evict them outside the occupied zone, and asked them to move to Zimraya crossing point by the car of the daughter, Mona, a "BMW" with the number 1552957, escorted by a militia security car.

When arriving at the crossing point, the members of the agent militia get hold of their personal identity cards and the car's plate which enabling the car to move in the occupied zone, and ordered both citizens to drive away from he occupied zone and to never come back. Accordingly, the father and his daughter moved by car towards the opposite point of the Lebanese army, where they received the appropriate assistance for the baby girl which was brought by her mother right from her bed, without even getting hold of her milk can.

The evicted woman, Mona, said that she is a teacher at the Shaba'a school, and that her husband has emigrated to the kingdom of Saudi Arabia, that she knows nothing in politics, and that all she want to receive is the return to her marital household and the completion of the schooling season of her students. As for the father, he urged all concerned parties to work in order to secure his return to his native home.
