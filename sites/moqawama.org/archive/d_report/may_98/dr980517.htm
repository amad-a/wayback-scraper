Daily Report
 
 
May Sunday 17-1998:
 

"Israeli" Prime-Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced that "Israel is the only side able to set the terms pertaining to its withdrawal from Lebanon", while (Israeli) daily newspaper Ma'ariv quoted United Nations sources as saying "There is a chance for reaching a compromise in Lebanon", following the meeting between Netanyahu and UN Secretary General Kofi Anan.

"ISRAELI" Aggressions:
The "Israeli" obstinacy, clearly manifested in Netanyahu's speeches during his visit to Washington was echoed in the occupied region of Lebanon, where the "Israeli" occupation forces shelled the heights and hills of Jabal Al-Rafih and the woods of Sojod, Al-Rihan, Al-Ayshiyeh, Al-Lowayzeh, Safi Mountain and Aqmata plain in Iqleem Al-Tuffah region; the shelling also included the vicinity of Jba'a town, emanating from the occupation forces outposts in Zaffateh and Mahmoudiyeh.

In addition, the occupation forces have bombarded early I the morning the woods of Al-Qantarah, Al-Srirah, with 155 mm shells, in conjunction with rounds of heavy gunfire emanating from Kawkaba outposts towards Mazra'at Al-Ahmadiyeh.
