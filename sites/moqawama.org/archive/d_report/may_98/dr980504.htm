Daily Report
 
 
May Monday 4-1998:
 

"Israeli" Aggressions (1):
The "Israeli" enemy pursued its aggressions against South Lebanon and Western Beqaa especially Iqleem Al-Tuffah region and the Eastern and Central Sectors. Also, it was reported that several guns had been deployed into Shraifeh and Abou Qamha positions.

In Iqleem Al-Tuffah region, the "Israeli" gunners shelled in the morning the road between Habboush and Arab Salim villages and Zahrani River bands. This was accompanied by shelling the outskirts of Froun and Mazraat Hamra and Litani River in Central Sector.

Also, Daidabeh Hill, Zahr Mountain, Meimess area and Qatraneh hilltops and the peripheries of Dallafeh and Sraireh came under more than seventy heavy "Israeli" shells distributed into three salvos launched between the morning and afternoon.

It was reported that an "Israeli"-Lahdi common military delegation paid visit to Zemraya position where it inspected the damages caused by the Islamic Resistance batteries the day before.

On the other hand, the "Israeli" occupation forces brought ten armored vehicles borne by 175mm guns via Mutilla crossing point to Shraifeh position. So, the number of 175mm guns at the position reached to eighteen whereas the number of 155mm guns is twelve. Also, the position of Abou Qamha was supported by three 175mm guns, while several armored vehicles deployed into Rihan and Kfar Houna area.

However, the Islamic Resistance mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:
 00:15, a drone reconnaissance plane flew, at middle altitude, over Deir Ames and Mahrouna areas.
1:25, a reconnaissance plane flew over Froun and Ghandourieh villages.
6:00, Zaffateh position targeted Safi Mountain with three 155mm missiles.
6:45, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah.
10:05, the "Israeli" fighters flew in the airspace of South Lebanon, in general, and, at low altitude, over Nabatieh region, in particular.
10:30 and until 11:15 an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered over Touline, Qabrikha and Majdal Selem villages.
10:55, the warplanes flew in the airspace of South Lebanon.
11:10, the "Israeli" jets flew, at middle and low altitudes, over Central Sector of South Lebanon.
11:45, the warplanes flew in the airspace of Iqleem Al-Tuffah, East Saida and Nabatieh regions.
12:55, the "Israeli" jets flew low over Nabatieh region.
13:50, the warplanes flew over Iqleem Al-Tuffah, Froun and Ghandourieh and conducted mock air strikes over Nabatieh area. At the same time, an M.K. reconnaissance aircraft hovered low over Saida region.
16:30, a reconnaissance plane flew over Zawtar Gharbieh, Zawtar Sharqieh, Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr and Kfar Seir villages.
16:40, Zaffateh position fired nine 155mm missiles at Safi Mountains and Mlita hilltops.
16:45, two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area fell on Arid Zennar area.
16:50, Aqmata area exposed to two 120mm shells emanating from Rihan area.

Islamic Resistance Operations:
The Islamic Resistance announced its responsibility for the following attacks:
16:15, the group of two martyrs Ali Jahjah and Ali Nazzal attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and destroying its eastern fortification. At the same time, the group of both martyrs Rameh Mahdi and Hassan Jaghbir rushed upon an "Israeli" force stationed at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
