"Israeli" Aggressions:
"The "Israeli" occupation forces bombarded the areas of Tallet Abu Qamha, Wadi Zillaya, Villages of Qilia, Luci, Dallafeh and Jabal al-Dahr in (Western Beqaa). Over thirty rounds hit these areas.

Moreover, occupation forces raked the same areas with heavy machine-gun fire".

"Yesterday, "Israeli" occupation's warplanes fired rockets at areas in southwestern Lebanon.
Four "Israeli" jets fired 12 rockets at areas in the western part of occupied southern Lebanon. There were no reports of casualties."

"The raid raised to 109 the number of "Israeli" occupation's air aggressions on Lebanon since the start of 1998."

"Moreover, "Israeli" planes on Sunday broke the sound barrier over Beirut after a series of night flights during the week over the capital and other cities at supersonic speeds, shattering windows and scaring civilians".

"The mock raids, which were ""Israel's"" most intense activity over Beirut since its 1996 offensive that killed about 200 civilians, triggered Lebanese complaints to the U.N and the European Union" (1)

"Israel's" Four Moms calls for the retreat (4)
"The Four Mothers movement is urging President Bill Clinton to use his influence to help enable a peaceful IDF* withdrawal from the security zone".

"Movement chairwoman Rahel Ben-Dor said that representatives of the organization met with the president and his wife Hillary shortly before they left Jerusalem International Convention Center on Sunday night".

"The meeting was held in a side room. Our representatives spoke with president and his wife and gave them written material about our activities," said Ben-Dor, who was not present herself.
"They said the president listened carefully and nodded his head several times, while Hillary Clinton expressed a desire to help and said she was interested in reading the material they were given.

"Our message is very simple - to find a way to end the bloodshed in Lebanon, because if there is no solution, the situation there could easily deteriorate and disrupt if not undo the peace process and everything that has been achieved so far."
