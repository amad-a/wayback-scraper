Daily Report
 
 
December Friday 11 -1998:
 
Introduction
For the second day, the "Israeli" jets carried out mock air raids breaking the sound barrier several times over Beirut and the southern city of Sidon and Iqleem Al-Toffah.

Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah said Hizbullah wouldn't respond to a "political and psychological provocation" that aimed to raise the "Israeli" Army's deteriorated morale following the deadly Islamic Resistance (IR) attacks.

In the meantime, the families of Lebanese detained in "Israeli" detention centers, officials and representatives of non-governmental organizations protested "Israel's" continuous detention of Lebanese without trial or charge.

"Israelis" resort to second day of idle threats Low-level sorties rattle children and windows but little else
""Israel's" rage of impotence continued Thursday, with further low-level sonic booms rocking Beirut, Sidon and Tyre and prompting official complaints to the UN and the international group monitoring the April understanding".

"Four double blasts were heard in Beirut between 1 A.M. and 2:10 P.M. as warplanes roared over the city at an estimated altitude of 1,500­2,300 meters. Normal overflights are around 11,000 meters.

Beiruts stoic residents generally ignored day two of "Israel's" low-level sorties with an indifferent collective shrug, with descriptions of the raids varying from irritating to childish.

Following a phone conversation between President Emile Lahoud and Premier-designate Salim Hoss, the Foreign Ministry was instructed to draft a letter to UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan formally protesting "Israeli" aggression against Lebanese civilians.

Lebanon, a small country which has never sought to have sophisticated heavy weaponry and firmly believes in peace, justice, reconstruction and improving the living standards of its citizens, should not be subjected to this daily armed blackmail performed by a member state of the UN, the letter to Mr. Annan said.

After exposing "Israeli" aggressions and particularly "Israeli" mock raids and sonic booms aimed at terrorizing children and the elderly and at disrupting the daily life of innocent civilians, Lebanon is asking you to make all possible efforts to put an end to this aggression and violation of Lebanons sovereignty, the letter continued.

"Mr. Hoss informed the five ambassadors whose countries have permanent seats at the UN Security Council of the measures he has undertaken".

"The French Foreign Ministry issued a statement calling for a halt to the low-level flights.

In Sidon, several windows were broken in the citys National School and the state-run Sidon Modern School, causing panic among students and teachers alike".

Dalal Qasab, the principal of Sidons Dawhat al-Makassed School, said that staff had instructed children not to sit too close to plate glass windows and to assemble outside when the warplanes were overhead.

"Some teachers decided to take the opportunity provided by the mock raids to explain to their pupils how sonic booms are created."

We told them not to be afraid because "Israeli" warplanes are only trying to frighten kids, she said.
Tyre and the surrounding area were also subjected to the deafening blasts of swooping jets.

Unifils long-serving spokesman, Timur Goksel, admitted that the intensity of the blasts over the peacekeepers headquarters in Naqoura took him by surprise. All the pre-fabricated buildings shook when the planes went overhead, he said. It even had me ducking and Im the guy whos used to loud bangs in the south.

"Despite the aggressive posturing of the "Israelis" in the past two days, the situation is unlikely to deteriorate, according to observers. They said Benjamin Netanyahu was aware that replacing sonic booms with bombs and rockets would only result in Katyusha rockets descending on northern Galilee. This scenario would create an unwelcome domestic backlash for the embattled "Israeli" premier."

"On the other hand, low-level sorties provide good training for the "Israeli" aircrews and ­ with the planes remaining beyond the range of anti-aircraft guns ­ are relatively safe. Furthermore, the mock raids serve to highlight to the new president and government "Israel's" military superiority and ability to strike wherever and whenever it wishes" (1)

However, the IR reported further "Israeli" aggressions (2):
9:50, four 155mm missiles fell on Mount Sojod.
13:15, the west of Yater and Jib Soweid area came under eleven 155mm missiles.
17:25, two 81mm rounds hit Haddatha woodland.
17:40, Mount Sojod exposed to two 120mm shells.
21:15, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
21:55, Habboush River watercourse was targeted by a number of 155mm missiles.
23:00, several 60mm shells struck the peripheries of Kfar Romman town.

Nasrallah shrugs off sonic bombardment (3)
"Unruffled by "Israeli" warplanes rumbling overhead or the sonic booms shaking the capital, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said Thursday that Hizbullah would not respond to a political and psychological provocation that aimed to lift the morale of a military reeling from fatal resistance blows".

"Through provoking Lebanon, "Israel" was seeking to draw the new government and the resistance into a confrontation beyond the confines of the April 1996 understanding. But clearly, were not so inclined, Hizbullahs secretary-general told The Daily Star in an interview. Breaking the sound barrier is nothing new to us. Its been happening for years. Resistance attacks, including three over a 10-day period in November in which seven "Israelis" soldiers perished, have demoralized the "Israeli" army and renewed calls by "Israelis" for a withdrawal from the south."

Theyve been at a loss as to how to respond. They believe their options are limited. The shock created by the resistance was not solely over the loss of a few soldiers and officers. Its because the resistance was able to penetrate deep into the occupied zone, almost reaching the border. That was an achievement by the resistance because all the "Israeli" military superiority fell in the face of the resistance, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"He questioned the timing of the "Israeli" provocation on the eve of the arrival of President Clinton in the region to seal the Wye River Accord between the "Israelis" and the Palestinians.

The low-altitude supersonic flights over Lebanon, including a night-time sortie that jolted Beirutis out of bed at 1 A.M., coupled with threats by "Israeli" officials against the Palestinians are evidence of an "Israeli" desire to escalate tension in the region during Mr. Clintons stay in "Israel" and Gaza.

Although Mr. Clintons visit benefits the "Israelis", they may be seeking to undermine it or to strengthen their bargaining position by creating tension to add new conditions for peace.

Mr. Clinton, he said, is coming to witness the scrapping of the history of the Palestinian people and this is in the interest of "Israel". But "Israel's" greed and expansionist ambitions are such that all this is not good enough for them.

"The Hizbullah leader denied that operations in the south had ebbed since November to avoid wide-scale "Israeli" retaliation".

The pattern of resistance varies. Sometimes we lay traps for them and wait for days before they fall into the ambush. Our strategy is to strike whenever theres an opportunity to do so. Sometimes the operations are successful, other times theyre not and go unmentioned.

Lebanon denounces UN double standards (4)
" Lebanon commemorated the 50th anniversary of the universal declaration of human rights, by denouncing double standards applied against people in the region. A number of events were held on the occasion".

" On the 50th anniversary of the universal declaration of human rights "Israel" is holding Lebanese as hostages. Families of Lebanese detained in "Israeli" detention centers, officials, and the representatives of non-governmental organizations held a sit-in in front of UN House in downtown Beirut, to protest "Israel's" continuous detention of Lebanese without trial or charge. The newly-elected minister for telecommunications Issam Neeman took part in the protest. The follow-up committee for the release of detainees from "Israeli" detention centers delivered a letter to the UN Secretary General Kofi Annan, denouncing torture and human rights violations against Lebanese held without trial or charge in occupied southern Lebanon and inside "Israel". They also called for the closure of the notorious Khyam detention center where "Israel" holds 140 Lebanese as hostages. Forty-three others are detained inside "Israel". Speakers denounced quote double standards applied by major powers when dealing with the issue of human rights, saying some are ignored while other are highlighted depending on the interests of these states. They also vowed Lebanon will continue to resist "Israel's" 20-year old occupation of Lebanese territory. The protest was held as diplomats, delegates and UN officials were marking the 50th anniversary of the human rights declaration. The secretary-general of the Economic and Social Commission of Western Asia, Dr. Hazem Biblawi read the message UN Chief Kofi Annan prepared for the occasion."

"Biblawi said: It is the day to renew our commitment to globalizing justice in the age of globalization. I began this anniversary year by reaffirming the universality of human rights, and by arguing that human rights are foreign to no culture and native to all nations. From the streets of Asia to towns of Africa to the courts of Europe, this past year has been a year of protest and progress. But Deputy Ahmad Soueid warned some powerful nations have been using the slogan of human rights to oppress democracy in other weak countries. He criticized the ongoing UN embargo against Iraq, saying it is starving and killing Iraqi children. MP Soueid reminded delegates that "Israel" has been committing massacres against Lebanese for the past fifty years leading to the Qana bloodbath inside a UN compound in 1996, where more than 100 Lebanese taking shelter from "Israeli" shelling were slaughtered by "Israeli" gunners. He stressed a fair and strong force needs to protect the human rights declaration".

Military Operations (5)
The IR squad of martyrs Hussein Ghassani and Ghassan Ghanem attacked, at 12:15, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking a number of its fortifications.
