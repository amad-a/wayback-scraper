Daily Report
 
 
December Tuesday 29 -1998:
 
Introduction
The "Israeli" occupying troops and their collaborators stormed into Arnoun village, which lies just outside the occupied area of South Lebanon, and bulldozed six of its houses. This new aggression came less than a week after the "Israeli" warplanes slaughtered a mother and her six children in a raid on a farmhouse in Janta, Beqaa.

However, a senior Mossad veteran described Hizbullah's militants in their running after the liberation of their land as fighters of freedom.

"Israeli" bulldozers raze six houses in Arnoun (1)
""Israeli" troops have demolished six houses in the village of Arnoun after claiming to have unearthed an arms cache in one of the buildings".

"About 30 soldiers accompanied by several militiamen of the South Lebanon Army* entered the village, which lies just below Beaufort Castle, on Sunday and bulldozed the buildings without warning.

Some olive trees were also destroyed in the operation".

"Arnoun, 5 kilometers east of Nabatieh, lies in a no-mans land between free Lebanon and the occupation zone. "Israeli" troops regularly enter the village to search house and question and occasionally detain residents.

The owners of the destroyed houses are Ali Mohammed Qatbani, Ahmad Bashir, Afif Nayef Olleiq, Mohammed Salim Qatbani, Hussein Nayef Olleiq and Yassin Sharafeddine".

"The SLA-run Voice of the South radio claimed that the militiamen had discovered an arms cache and food used by Mujahedeen saboteurs in one of the houses."

"But the residents insisted that the destroyed homes were empty".

I was with my family when we heard a loud noise at 9 P.M. We thought that the "Israelis" had come out of Beaufort Castle to patrol the village as usual, said Zeinab Attieh, 55.

But we found out that bulldozers were razing our neighbors houses. Three hours later, the houses were destroyed.

Zeinab Assaf, 60, said the villagers were used to harassment from the "Israelis" and the SLA.
This is not the first time "Israeli" bulldozers have destroyed houses here but nobody seems to care about it, she said. Five of my sons left the village eight years ago, while my sixth son, Qassem has been detained in Khiam prison since 1997.

Arnouns mukhtar, Afif Hamdan, said the demolition of the houses was only the latest in a long list of woes.

Our plight continues but nobody cares. The whole village could become a ruin if this situation continues, he said.

Ha'aretz: Hezbollah men are freedom fighters (2)
"An "Israeli" journalist condemned the "Israeli" occupation of south Lebanon and the recent slaughtering of a Lebanese mother along with her six children by "Israeli" warplanes reciting a long list of "Israeli" massacres against Lebanese civilians".

"Nadwa Othman, who was killed last week with her six children, was not the first mother to have been killed by mistake by "Israel" in "the security zone." In April two years ago we killed a mother and her seven children, by mistake of course. Five days earlier, an "Israeli" attack helicopter fired on a Lebanese ambulance and two mothers and two girls were killed; in August of this year, a civilian named Abd al Hassan Bushar was injured. According to reports, he was 110 years old. Three children were wounded three months earlier, also in an "Israeli" bombing attack; a 17-year-old boy was killed last week, a few days before the Othman family met its death," Gideon Levy wrote in "Israeli" daily Ha'aretz yesterday.

The lives of the Lebanese citizens in the security zone - women, children and old people who found themselves in this situation through no fault of their own, - are far less secure than the lives of the "Israeli" mothers and children south of the border. This is the way it should be, according to the "Israeli" perception, when the alternatives are always either us or them. However, it is doubtful that this is the true alternative, he added..

As occupied territories go, the occupied territory in southern Lebanon couldn't be better from an "Israeli" point of view, because there, the profoundest wish of Yitzhak Rabin and many other "Israelis" is fulfilled: there's no High Court of Justice and no B'Tselem. Few take any interest in the danger to the lives of the mothers and children in the village of Janta where the Othmans lived; there are even fewer who care about the human and civil rights of the residents of this village and others like it, Levy wrote.

"The Khiyam prison is full of men and women whose illegal arrest and conditions of imprisonment are "Israel's" direct responsibility, just as it is responsible for everything else that is done in the security zone.

""Israel" grabs and arrests people, and transfers them for interrogation to within its own borders and detains them, finally, at Khiyam or in "Israeli" prisons, without trial and without time limits. "Israel" is allowed to imprison Lebanese citizens - even the High Court of Justice has said so - without limitations as "bargaining chips" to be used on a rainy day or in negotiations over "Israelis" missing in action about whom it has long been clear that they are no longer in Lebanon or perhaps even still alive.

""Israel" builds, paves and fortifies there, and confiscates and takes control of private property, but in contrast to the bypass roads in the West Bank, from there no voices are even raised in protest.

"The only protest that is heard is the violent protest of the Hezbollah. In "Israel", it is very convenient to depict this not as a protest against the military occupation, but rather as yet another expression of the desire to wipe us off the face of the earth".

"Reuven Merhav, a senior Mossad veteran and formerly the representative of the Foreign Ministry in Beirut, has said that if all the opponents to the withdrawal - Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and opposition leader MK Ehud Barak, Labor MK Dr. Ephraim Sneh and retired chief of staff Amnon Lipkin-Shahak - had been born Shi'ites, they would be fighting the same stubborn war against the occupier that the Hezbollah is fighting. "The Hezbollah," says Merhav, "are freedom fighters in every respect, an authentic expression of the deep desire to eject "Israel" from southern Lebanon."

"Freedom fighters? An authentic expression? Occupied territory? This is not convenient for "Israel" to see, neither for the Likud nor the Labor Party, who are united on the issue of Lebanon in a miserable alliance that only spells disaster. It is much more convenient to talk about "the security zone," "Hezbollah terrorists," and "they want to wipe us off the face of the earth," than to admit to reality, the "Israeli" journalist concluded.

Military conflicts among the "Israeli"-proxy militia (3)
"An extreme confusion prevails in the ranks of the collaborator militia in the occupied zone due to the growing conflicts among the militia's officials on one hand and the "Israeli" talk about withdrawal from south Lebanon on the other."

"These conflicts are sometimes expressed through assassinations, as was the case with collaborator official in Shibaa, Ghassan Daher, who was killed last Friday, reportedly by another militia security official called Mohammed Nabaa."

"Daher was about to flee the occupied zone after the "Israeli" statements about withdrawal from Lebanon", reports from the occupied zone said.

"Moreover, there was another clash Saturday night between agent Youssef Hashem and agent Akram Oulayyan over collecting coercive payments from occupied towns' residents", the reports added.

"In a related development, the "Israeli" blockade imposed on the village of Shibaa entered its third day. Sixteen of the village residents were taken to the "Israeli" occupation's position in Zagleh for interrogation over the killing of collaborator Daher".

Monitoring group equates "Israeli" massacre in Janta with resistance retaliation (4)
"The April Understanding Monitoring Group on Sunday equated the massacre perpetrated by "Israeli" enemy against a Lebanese family, whose torn limbs scattered around a farmhouse in Janta, with the resistance's retaliation against colonies in northern occupied Palestine".

"The group -- with representatives from Lebanon, Syria, France, the United States, and ""Israel"" -- said the "Israeli" entity had breached the agreement when it killed a woman and six of her children in an air raid on the Beqaa Valley on Tuesday".

"The monitoring group condemned this grave incident, which led to such tragic and unacceptable consequences, and called upon "Israel" to take effective measures to ensure that its military operations do not result in the loss of innocent civilian lives," the group said in a statement issued late on Saturday.

"The group also took up an "Israeli" complaint that Islamic Resistance men retaliated for the "Israeli" massacre by firing Katyusha rockets into "Israeli" colonies in northern occupied Palestine".

"The monitoring group was created in April 1996 after a 16-day "Israeli" aggression that killed at least 175 people, mostly Lebanese civilians".

"The understanding, which prohibits targeting civilians or firing from civilian areas, has been violated by the "Israeli" occupation forces frequently."

"Since the beginning of 1998, twenty-five Lebanese civilians have been killed and ninety-two wounded in "Israeli" occupation's shelling and raids in Lebanese areas".
