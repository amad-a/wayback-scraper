December Monday 14 -1998:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions (1)

7:30, nine 155mm missiles pounded west of Yater.
7:55, several 155mm rounds hit the peripheries of Jibaa.

12:40, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into a river watercourse near Zillaya village firing ten air-to-ground rockets.

14:00, twenty five 155mm missiles pounded Litani River banks near Zillaya.
14:10, three 155mm rounds fell on the peripheries of Zibqeen village.
14:15, nine 155mm missiles slammed the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
14:40, several 155mm missiles pounded the peripheries of Jibaa.

Military Operations (2)
The IR group of martyr Mahmoud Borji attacked, at 6:30, Beit Yahoun outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite hits were scored, the militants reported. At the same time, the squad of martyr Hussein Moussa Mohanna targeted Haddatha compound with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals. In the meantime, the group of martyr Ahmad Ismail Ossaili targeted Baraasheit compound with the appropriate weapons scoring direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Zaatar and Ahmad Melhem Chahrour attacked, at 8:00, an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the rocket-propelled grenades. A Merkava tank was hit directly whereas several casualties were inflicted.

Furthermore, the IR militants spotted, at 12:30, an enemy bulldozer fortifying Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the group of martyrs Rashid and Khalil El-Bazzal targeted it with the suitable weapons. The militants managed to chalk up certain goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Said Harb and Salem Abboud attacked, at 14:15, an "Israeli" force at it was coming to evacuate the damaged Merkava tank, which was hit earlier. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to inflict several casualties among its ranks.

Fifteen minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Haitham Afif Ghamloush and Ahmad Hariri attacked motorized movements at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain hits.
