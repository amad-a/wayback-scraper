Daily Report
 
 
December Wednesday 16 -1998:
 
Lebanon: resistance will not stop (1)
"Official sources said the American administration has conveyed to Lebanon messages including a request to calm the situation in the south."

"The world "calm" here means a call to stop the resistance's deadly attacks against the "Israeli" occupation forces, the sources added stressing that Americans heard an official reply stating that "resistance will not stop for a moment as long as the occupation remains."

"Israel" will be responsible for any violation or toppling of the April Understanding. Moreover, Washington is supposed to pressure "Israel"; not the other way round, the Lebanese reply stated according to the sources."

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
The "Israeli" occupation troops carried out the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas:
5:40, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal village exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
11:15, six 155mm missiles landed south of Jibal El-Botom.
14:05, fifteen 155mm missiles pounded the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen villages.
14:25, Jib Soweid area was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
14:45, south of Jibal El-Botom was bombarded by twenty rounds.
15:00, seven 155mm missiles struck the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen, one of which landed near the house of Khalil Mohanna at Jibal El-Botom.
17:45, three 155mm missiles hit Mount Al-Rafih.
17:55, the surroundings of Sojod came under several shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:10, Qaissieh Valley was hit by a number of 155mm missiles.
18:15, the perimeters of Khorbet Selem town came under several 155mm missiles.
18:20, seven 155mm rounds slammed the peripheries of Majdal Selem and Talloussa towns.
19:15, Shaqra exposed to one 155mm missiles whereas its outskirts was shelled by five others.
20:00, two 120mm rounds fell on Mount Al-Rafih.
20:50, the forest and perimeters of Haddatha town came under eight 155mm missies.

Prisoner Dakroub calls on International groups to break the isolation of detainees in enemy prisons (3)
"Follow up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" prisons (FCLD) received a message from prisoner Bilal Dakroub who is being held as a hostage, in the "Israeli" prison of Ayalon along with another 18 prisoners whose "sentences" ended ten years ago".

"Prisoner Dakroub demanded that the isolation imposed on the detainees be broken noting that they are denied the right to be visited by relatives or receive messages"

"The detainees are not allowed to meet with lawyers or human rights groups' representatives, Dakroub added in his letter."

Military Operations (4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Abdel-Hassan Ezze-Eddine and Abdel-Rahman Houmani attacked, at 11:00, enemy movements at Blat post with the appropriate arms. The militants managed to score certain goals.
Also, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Kazma and Hachem Fakhr-Eddine targeted, at 14:45, an enemy force as it was fortifying Blat position. Using the adequate weapons, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct and definite hits.
The IR squad of martyrs Amin Ballouq and Mohammad Jaafar Al-Mazbouh attacked, at 18:00, enemy motorized movements at Sojod outpost with the suitable weapons scoring accurate goals.
