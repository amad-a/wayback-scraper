December Thursday 24 -1998:
 
Introduction
"Force can't be met except by force- Blood cant be protected except by blood-The killers must pay the price". Yesterday, the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants carried out their pledge to protect the Lebanese territories and civilians. Several salvos of Katyusha rockets were fired into the "Israeli" settlements in retaliation for Tuesday's Janta massacre in which a mother and six of her children were slaughtered in an "Israeli" air raid.

Thousands of mourners and Hizbullah supporters yesterday buried the charred bodies of Osman family in Baalbeck after a funeral procession that turned into a protest against the '"Israeli"" entity and its sponsor, the United stated of America.

IR pounded "Israeli" colonies with Katyushas, collaborator officer was killed also (1)
Once again, the Islamic Resistance (IR) has confirmed its decision and pledge, and fulfilled its promise to protect our territory and our civilians, IR said in a statement.

"At 7:30 a.m. our fighters in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit pounded several Zionist colonies in northern occupied Palestine with Katyusha rockets, scoring direct hits," the statement added.
IR's attacks came hours after the horrible massacre in which "Israeli" enemy's warplanes slaughtered a mother and six of her children in Janta (Beqaa).

The woman, 35-year-old Nadwa Othman, and her three sons and three daughters aged between one and 16 years, were martyred when an "Israeli" missile hit a farm where they were preparing to break their fasting at Janta, five kilometres (three miles) from the Syrian border.

Twenty-eight Lebanese civilians have been killed by "Israeli" occupation forces in south Lebanon since the start of this year, setting off outrage across the country.

"Murderers must pay the price of their crimes," the IR statement said. ""Our fighters did not sleep last night after the tragic death of the civilians. We renew our pledge to protect our people and their villages, no matter how great the struggle."

"Violence must be answered back by violence; their blood must be spilled for ours," it added.
The Resistance men's retaliation covered six "Israeli" colonies injuring at least 13 "Israelis", one of them seriously, and causing extensive damage, particularly on electricity networks.

A Lebanese security source said IR men had fired about 60 Katyusha rockets into northern occupied Palestine. The rockets caused heavy property damage in the towns. The resistance fired about five to six volleys of about 12 rockets each.

"Since force cannot be confronted except with force and murderers must pay the price of their crimes ... the Islamic resistance rocketed Zionist colonies," IR's statement read.

"Most residents in colonies in northern occupied Palestine were in air raid shelters at the time of the rocket attacks. They were told to stay in their shelters for the second consecutive night".

However, an "officer" in the "Israeli" proxy militia was killed Wednesday in an attack by the Islamic Resistance men, collaborator sources acknowledged.

IR announced that its squad of Martyrs Wafiq Zahwi and Abdel-Rasoul Baydoun at 13:30 detonated a roadside bomb against a collaborator militia patrol at the Anan outpost road.

The patrol members were killed or injured, an IR communiqué read.

Thirty-four collaborator militiamen have been killed and another 40 wounded in resistance operations in occupied southern Lebanon since the beginning of the year, according to an Agence France Presse toll.

Earlier, IR's squad of Martyrs Hassan Mraish and Rabieh Wehbi at midnight (Tuesday) attacked the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Sojod with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, scoring direct hits in the outpost's fortifications.

In the meantime, IR's squad of Martyrs Mohammed Abbas and Qassem Yassin attacked the Kassaret al-Oroush with the adequate arms scoring accurate hits, and IR's squad of Martyrs Fadl Swaidan and Hussein Nasser attacked the occupation's outpost in Sojod with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, scoring direct hits.

On the other hand, the Jamaa al-Islamia (The Islamic Group) wing of Quwwat al-Fajr (Dawn Forces), a new resistance group, staged an attack against the occupation's outpost of Tallet Sweida.
The group said it had inflicted casualties in the mortar and rocket-propelled grenade attack which took place at 6:30 a.m. (Tuesday).

Qassem: bombardment of colonies is no violation of understanding (2)
Hezbollah's Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem confirmed that the Islamic Resistance's retaliation against "Israeli" colonies in Northern Palestine was not a violation of the April Understanding, "for this is the only way to deter the ("Israeli") enemy from launching aggressions against Lebanese civilians".

Qassem said the killing of a woman and six of her children in Janta by ""Israel"" was a violation of the April Understanding that bans attacks to and from civilian areas but permits the Lebanese resistance to attack "Israeli" occupation troops and their allies in the south.

In a telephone interview broadcast on Qatari satellite television Sheikh Qassem, said: "We carried out these attacks in response to the killing of civilians."

"We believe that "Israel" must pay a price every time it carries out such an action."

""Israel"" does not dare to launch a large-scale aggression on Lebanon at this stage owing to its current circumstances and the losses inflicted on it in previous wars, he said.

For his part, Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc member MP Mohammed Fneish told Reuters in an interview Hezbollah had launched the barrage to make ""Israel"" respect the April 1996 Understanding that bans attacks on civilians.

He dismissed "Israeli" statements that the civilian deaths were an accident.

""Israel" has once again resorted to its favorite weapon and committed a heinous crime against our people," Fneish said. "They deliberately targeted a civilian home in a residential area to terrify the people and weaken their resistance."

"We can't bear to see the "Israeli" war machine attack our civilians and our villages without retaliating. For the sake of our people and their protection, we launched rockets at "Israel" so that they respect the April understanding," he added.

The April understanding, which ended a 17-day "Israeli" aggression in 1996, bars "Israeli" or resistance attacks on civilian targets but not on military targets.

Fneish affirmed that Hezbollah did not have any facilities near the farmhouse which was hit and vowed to fight the "Israeli" occupation "as long as there is one "Israeli" soldier on our soil."
He said Hezbollah was on alert for "Israeli" retaliation.

"The "Israelis" are hysterical and this makes us expect the worst. We're cautious and on alert but they will never stop us from performing our duty," he added.

Fadlallah decries muted reaction to killings (3)
Leading Shiite cleric Ayatollah Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah has criticized the absence of any reaction from Western countries to the Baalbek massacre in which a woman and her six children were killed Tuesday during an "Israeli" air raid.

"Israel" is committing massacres against children and innocent civilians, but the United States, European countries and some Arab states did not even condemn these massacres, Ayatollah Fadlallah said during an iftar held by the Association of Charities (al-Mabarrat) Wednesday.

The iftar was attended by Prime Minister Salim Hoss, who represented President Emile Lahoud, along with other officials and clerics.

If those who were killed were Jews killed by our mujahedin, would the United States or the world have kept silent as they are doing now? he asked.

If a Jewish boy is killed in an attack in occupied Palestine, the world would rush to condemn the attack. But when we are killed, an ambassador of a Western country told us that all parties should show self-restraint.

The cleric called on the public to support the resistance to "Israeli" occupation.

We have to support the mujahedin who are the peoples army, which complements the states Army, because they have succeeded in creating a dilemma for "Israel" for the first time in Arab history, the cleric said.

Injured father and his sole surviving son bid a dazed and tearful farewell
Mohammed Othman, bruised and bandaged, was supported on the shoulders of a friend as he stared in dazed grief through the throngs of mourners and the yellow and red Hizbullah flags at the seven coffins which contained the remains of his family.

It took only seconds for his wife, who was preparing iftar, and six children to be wiped off this Earth when an "Israeli" fighter jet.

Only Mr. Othman and his son Alaa, 11, survived ­ though both were wounded ­ to be in Baalbek to bury their family beneath a gloomy sky. They survived to watch the procession of seven coffins, draped in wreaths, carrying the charred remains of Nadwa, Mr. Othmans wife, and his children: Souad, Aidi, Amina, Sobhi, Amin and Ali. The tiny coffin of Sobhi, an 18-month-old baby, led the procession from the Iman Khomeini Hospital to the Baalbek Serail, where a number of clergymen spoke.

After the speeches, a procession of ambulances traveled to Tofeil, the Othmans home village, where they were buried. They had to travel to Tofeil via Syria due to the lack of a direct road to the remote village.

Death to America and "Israel", "Israel", the enemy of Muslims were chanted by the crowd, familiar mantras at Hizbullah gatherings, an unmistakable anger filling their voices.

At the funeral, there was no question who was responsible for ""Israel's" mistake. This is a present from America and "Israel" for Ramadan, said Abdel-Rahman Sharqieh, a Sunni religious court judge.

What happened is the responsibility of the United States as much as it is the responsibility of "Israel", he said. The massacre is a follow-up to the atrocities perpetrated against the Iraqis during U.S.-British strikes on Iraq last week.

Netanyahu is not the only one responsible for the massacre. Clinton, whose lewd behavior is well-known, is also responsible, said Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek, a member of Hizbullahs politburo.

He also blamed the most conspicuous symbol of the U.S. presence in Lebanon, Ambassador David Satterfield. The ambassador does not carry solutions to Lebanese problems. He carries killing and bombing of women and children, he said.

Sheikh Yazbek concluded with a dire warning.

Right now we distinguish between the American people and their administration, he said. But if the American people do not move and say something to their criminal administration, they will be dealt with on the same footing as their officials (4)

Angry mourners marched from the Imam Khomeini Hospital in Baalbeck where the bodies of Nada Osman and her six children were taken following the attack. Thousands of Bekaa residents, officials, and relatives carried the bodies of the slain family to the village of Tfeyl, where they were buried. Mourners shouted anti-"Israeli" and anti-US slogans in the rally" (5)

Civilian deaths fuel Lebanon debate (6)
"Tuesday's bombing mistake that killed Lebanese mother Nadwa Osman and six of her children has reopened the debate on ""Israel's" role in Lebanon."

MK Yossi Beilin (Labor), who is outspoken on withdrawing from Lebanon, said yesterday that "the salvo of Katyushas that hit Kiryat Shmona is more proof that the security zone is not effective in protecting the northern residents." The zone itself, Beilin said, is a danger to residents of the North and does not allow them to lead normal lives.

According to Beilin, the conflict between the IDF* and Hizbullah is what is creating the attacks on Lebanese civilians and the response in the form of Katyushas on the Galilee.

"We must get out of there," he said. If a solution can't be reached via Syria, he said, an agreement with Lebanon isn't possible either, adding that unilateral withdrawal remains the only answer.

"If the IDF withdraws from Lebanon, and it's replaced by the Lebanese Army and UNIFIL soldiers, the estimation is that Hizbullah will continue to honor the Grapes of Wrath understandings and will not attack "Israel"," he said.

"MK Ran Cohen (Meretz) said that what happened to the Lebanese family was a disaster that shouldn't have happened. "It shows that we are too deeply involved in Lebanon," he said. "We should only be as deep in Lebanon as is needed to protect the northern settlements." This he estimated at 500 to 1,000 meters. "I am not saying that we should implement a unilateral withdrawal," he added.

MK Dedi Zucker (Meretz) said the residents of the North "are paying an unnecessary price for the 'human error' of the political echelons and not of a pilot."

Meanwhile, two mothers of soldiers killed in Lebanon met yesterday morning with members of the committee. Orna Shimoni said the mothers have been encamped outside Beit Hanassi for a month and their protest has only touched a few MKs. "I fear that during the election season we will have a deterioration in the situation," she added.

The MKs were told that a new Lebanon policy should be a priority and not only when soldiers are being killed by roadside bombs and Katyushas are falling.

Middle East expert Yossi Olmert said the Katyusha attacks and the rush to shelters are the latest symptom of ""Israel's" total lack of a coordinated policy in southern Lebanon and the understandings reached after the Grapes of Wrath operation.

Speaking on Arutz 7, Olmert declared that residents in the North and the entire country have become hostages of Hizbullah. The understandings reached in 'Grapes of Wrath' and Operation Accountability have totally collapsed, leaving the Hizbullah with a strategic edge.

Instead of launching an immediate strike against them, everyone is pushed into bomb shelters, causing panic and fear among residents. Olmert stressed that he now supports a full withdrawal from Lebanon, because there is no clear national policy concerning Lebanon.

He said it would be preferable to have a strong policy in northern "Israel" than to have no policy in southern Lebanon".

Iran and South Africa condemn "Israeli" massacre (7)
"Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Hamid Reza Asefi on Wednesday condemned "Israeli" air raids on civilian targets on the suburbs of Baalbek, eastern Lebanon.

Asefi said the criminal act by ""Israel"" is in line with its pre-meditated plan to exert pressure on the Lebanese government and nation and to destabilize Lebanon and the region, the Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA) reported."

"These criminal acts aim to put pressure on the government, people and the heroic resistance of Lebanon and to destabilize this country," the television quoted Asefi as saying.

"Israeli" warplanes on Tuesday afternoon targeted a farmhouse at Tufail village, Baalbek, slaughtering wife of a farmer and six other members of his family.

"Asefi said the air raids carried out by ""Israel"" are gross violation of the 1996 understanding that bans attacks on civilians by ""Israel"" and Lebanese resistance.

He called on the international organizations and other states to protest against the "Israeli" brutal raids on the civilian targets"

South Africa has also condemned the "Israeli" massacre, saying that such acts endangered lives of innocent civilians and would perpetuate the cycle of violence in the Middle East.

In a statement released Wednesday, the South African Foreign Ministry called on "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's government to stop air raids against population centers in Lebanon, South African Press Association reported Thursday.

South Africa regarded the attack as yet another in a litany of such incidents, including the inexcusable massacre of more than one hundred Lebanese civilians at Qana in 1996, said the statement.
Similar attacks in recent years have led to the deaths of large numbers of innocent civilians in Lebanon, it added.

"This will once again perpetuate the cycle of violence in the region and lead to further hostility in southern Lebanon and on the northern border of "Israel"," it said.
