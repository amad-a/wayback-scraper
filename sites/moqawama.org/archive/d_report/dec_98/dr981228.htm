Unilateral withdrawal from south Lebanon? Lebanons the only game in town as Netanyahu seeks to secure votes from doves (1)
""Israel's" security cabinet met late Sunday to examine its options in south Lebanon amid mounting speculation that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is on the verge of announcing an unconditional troop withdrawal from the occupied zone."

"Mr. Netanyahu is said to have lent his support to a variation of the plan advocated by "Israeli" Foreign Minister Ariel Sharon who has proposed a phased withdrawal accompanied by stern warnings of retribution if cross-border attacks continue".

"The new plan recommends an immediate rather than gradual withdrawal of "Israeli" forces from the south, while retaining the threat to retaliate against future attacks".

The south is the only game in town now. Netanyahu is not going to give anything on the Palestinian front and it looks like hes going for Lebanon instead. Something is definitely in the works and we should see what it is by Tuesday, said a senior security source.

"The office of the "Israeli" prime minister, however, denied that Mr. Netanyahu has decided to support an unconditional withdrawal"

"Speculation over a possible volte face by Mr. Netanyahu began after former Finance Minister Yacov Neeman, who resigned from the "Israeli" government two weeks ago, revealed the contents of a classified report on a recent meeting between Mr. Sharon and UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan".

"Mr. Sharon reportedly explained to Mr. Annan his proposal for ending the occupation as if it was official government policy".

"The "Israeli" governments policy up to now has been to propose adopting Resolution 425 in exchange for a number of security guarantees. However, Lebanons unwavering refusal to consider the conditional proposal coupled with rising domestic opposition to the occupation within "Israel" and the imminent elections could have forced the fickle Mr. Netanyahu to explore other alternatives".

"On Friday, Labor MP Ephraim Sneh said that Mr. Netanyahu was planning a populist move by staging an unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon to garner votes in the pending election."

"A spokesman for Mr. Sharon, quoted Sunday in the Jerusalem Post, said that the attempt to portray the proposal as an electoral move ... was pure evil.

"The elections in "Israel" are now unquestionably the determining factor over the fate of south Lebanon in the short- to medium-term. During the previous elections in 1996, Shimon Peres sought to boost his appeal to the right-wing by staging the Grapes of Wrath operation. Mr. Netanyahu, who has no need to prove his credentials to "Israel's" hawks, having frozen progress on the Palestinian front, may believe that a withdrawal from Lebanon would secure the votes of the peace lobby".

"Beirut and Damascus can do little but watch the developments unfold. However, the security source insisted that, contrary to the perceived wisdom in Lebanon, the Sharon plan was not intended to split the Lebanese-Syrian negotiating track in the peace process."

"It remains unclear, however, whether the "Israeli" army would support a withdrawal from the south unconditionally. "Israeli" Chief of Staff Lieutenant General Shaul Mofaz has repeatedly declared that the only option, short of a security arrangement with Lebanon, is to maintain the occupation".

Fathers of commandos appeal to High Court (4)
 "Two fathers of naval commandos killed in a Hezbollah ambush in South Lebanon last year asked the High Court of Justice yesterday to authorize a DNA analysis of the remains buried in their sons' graves".

"The two men, Nahshon Teby and Yoram Golan, are also asking that state funerals be held for their sons after the body parts are identified".

"Sgt. First Class Raz Teby and Staff Sgt. Guy Golan were among the 12 fatalities in the September 1997 Shayetet tragedy and were buried two days later. Ten months later, the body of commando Itamar Ilya was returned in a deal with Hezbollah. Other body parts were also returned and, after an analysis of their shape and color, were identified as those of Teby and Golan. These body parts were quietly buried without informing the families of the commandos".

"The Teby and Golan families also filed a complaint to the police against the IDF* chief rabbi, Major General Gad Navon, the former head of the IDF's personnel branch, Major General Gideon Shefer, and head of the casualties division, Brigadier General Drora Piar, for desecrating the graves of their sons. Yoram Golan told the court on Sunday that the families have been waiting for four months for the graves to be opened. "All of our requests have been rejected. We have lost faith in the system and therefore have come to the court," Golan explained.
