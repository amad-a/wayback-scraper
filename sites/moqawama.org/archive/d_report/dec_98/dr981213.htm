Daily Report
 
 
December Sunday 13 -1998:
 
"Israeli" occupation resumes escalation (1)
""Israeli" occupation's planes today raided an area in Iqlim al-Touffah and staged mock air raids over southern liberated areas"

"Two enemy warplanes fired two rockets on Mount Sojod (Iqlim al-Touffah) north of the occupation zone."

"The strike raised to 108 the number of "Israeli" occupation's raids on Lebanon since the start of 1998 and comes during a three-day visit to occupied Palestine by U.S. President Bill Clinton".

"There were no reports of casualties in the aggression which followed night flights by "Israeli" planes on Wednesday and Thursday over Beirut and other cities at supersonic speeds, shattering windows and scaring children."

"In earlier aggressions, the occupation forces bombarded the outskirts of Upper Nabatieh, damaging a number of houses and a parked car"

"Elsewhere, the areas of Sojod, Mleta, Mazraat Oqmata, Jabbour water reservoir and Abu-Rashed hilltop were shelled with dozens of rounds that emanated from occupation positions beyond Litani, al-Ahmadieh and Burghos."

"Moreover, in an act of piracy, "Israeli" gunboats intercepted a Ukrainian cargo ship with a load of 115 cars. After a thorough search, the ship was allowed into the Tyre port".

Military Operations (3)
The IR group of martyr Ali Choaib and Bilal Al-Darnouh observed, at 6:30, an "Israeli" force near Sojod village. As the force arrived  at the scene, the mujahedeen set off a roadside bomb inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.

Also, the IR squad of martyr Nadim Hassan Skeiki and Ibrahim Abdel-Hassan Hariri attacked, at 20:30, an "Israeli" foot-patrol near Sojod village. Using the proper weapons, the mujahedeen chalked up direct and certain hits.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Hani Taha and Mahmoud El-Hajj Ali attacked, at 20:40, an "Israeli" force as it was fortifying the surroundings of Qabaa post. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to score direct and definite goals.
