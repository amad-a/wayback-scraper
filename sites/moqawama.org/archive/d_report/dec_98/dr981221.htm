Daily Report
 
 
December Monday 21 -1998:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
The "Israeli" occupations forces carried out the following "Israeli" aggressions:
1:15, two 120mm rounds hit Kfar Falous River watercourse and two flare shells were fired overhead.
11:50, Haddatha forest was targeted by four 120mm rounds, whereas several flare shells were unleashed over Haddatha town. Also, the area between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun villages exposed to two 81mm shells.
Between 13:00 and 13:15, the west of Yater was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
16:45, the perimeters of Majdal Zoun came under a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:05, the surroundings of Salaah position came under a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
19:15, the surroundings of Salaah position exposed to three 81mm shells whereas the surroundings of Mothallath position subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
22:10, eleven 155mm missiles slammed the outskirts of Haddatha town.
23:25, the area of Sawan was bombarded by nine 155mm missiles.

Military Operations (2)
At 22:10, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Kamel Tarhini and Mohammad Hussein Kassem targeted enemy movements near Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to score direct and accurate goals.
