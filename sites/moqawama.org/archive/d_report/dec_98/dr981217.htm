Daily Report
 
 
December Thursday 17 -1998:
 
IR's military commander: the enemy has nothing new; we control its movements in the south (1)
"Military commander of the Islamic Resistance (IR) said the "Israeli" military command in occupied south Lebanon had nothing new when it decided to depend an air force to pursue the resistance men".

"Such decisions aim only to fool public opinion, especially the "Israeli" public opinion, he said, adding that the enemy has already staged around 100 raids but has not been able to hit one of the Mujahedeen".

"We are used to this tactic. It has no impact on us; on the contrary, the resistance managed to carry out successful operations, such as storming into outposts and combating with enemy and collaborator soldiers in broad daylight, the IR military commander said in an interview with al-Hayat newspaper".

"He stressed that the Zionist enemy, backed by all the US sophisticated technologies, had a technology hysteria as it involved its most sophisticated technologies in the battle, but only in vain."

"With our humble technology, we were able to thwart the enemy's technologies. This has been acknowledged by the enemy itself", he added.

"The military commander affirmed that IR does not rely on local residents in its military operations in the occupied zone."

"The resistance's military intelligence gets information from the enemy ranks and those close to it with several methods besides the surveillance patrols that get first-hand field information, he said underlining that IR has no problem in gathering information about the enemy".

"We know the enemy's movements as we know the palms of our hands", he said.

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
The "Israeli" occupation troops carried out the following aggressions:
2:30, Beit Yahoun outpost raked Aita El-Jabal forest with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
3:30, fifteen 155mm missiles struck the outskirts of Zibqeen and four others hit the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom.
13:30, west of Yater was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
13:35, three 130mm rounds fell on Izzieh ravine.
13:40, Jib Soweid area was hit by four 155mm missiles.
15:10, the peripheries of Loweizeh village exposed to three 155mm rounds.
Between 15:20 and 15:35, four 160mm missiles and more than three 155mm rounds slammed the peripheries of Jibaa town.
16:00, two 155mm missiles landed the perimeters of Loweizeh.
18:25, Bir Kallab outpost raked its surroundings with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Military Operations (3)
The IR squad of martyrs Amin Ballouq and Mohammad Jaafar Al-Mazbouh attacked, at 18:00, enemy motorized movements at Sojod outpost with the suitable weapons scoring accurate goals.

The IR squad of martyrs, Hassan Ibrahim Al-Saalouk and Raseel Ahmad Rahal targeted, at 15:30, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its fortifications. In the meantime, the group of martyrs Hussein Wafik Kazma and Abdel-Rahman Houmani was attacking an "Israeli" motorized at Kassaret Al-Oroush. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen chalked up certain goals. At the same time, the squad of martyrs Hachem Mohammad Fakhr-Eddine and Hussein Mustafa Wehbi targeted an "Israeli" force at Bir Kallab area with the adequate arms scoring direct goals.

At 19:05, the IR squad of martyrs Mustafa Nazar and Rameh Mahdi targeted military vehicles movements at Bir Kallab outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate hits.
