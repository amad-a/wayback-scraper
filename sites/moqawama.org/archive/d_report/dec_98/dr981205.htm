Daily Report
 
 
December Saturday 5 -1998:
 
No pullout means more resistance operations, says Qawooq (1)
"UNIFIL's political adviser and spokesman Timur Goksel, Friday held one of his regular meetings with Hezbollah's southern official Sheikh Nabil Qawooq".

"Sheik Qawooq confirmed that the recent spate of operations against the "Israeli" occupation troops would continue."

"The "Israeli" decision not to withdraw from the south and to try to reduce their casualties and all the threats made by "Israel" against Lebanon will lead to only one thing, which is more resistance operations," he said in a statement.

"If the April monitoring group is not able to stop "Israeli" aggressions ... the resistance is capable and ready to stop "Israel" and protect Lebanese civilians," he added.

Goksel said that he expected the occupation zone to quieten down for the time being after the escalation at the end of last month.

"The UN official added that Sheikh Qawooq had asked UNIFIL to mediate to allow the peacekeepers to retrieve the bodies of two Islamic Resistance fighters martyred in a clash with occupation troops a year ago near Shemaa in the western sector. Goksel said he would exert efforts in this regard".

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
The Israeli occupying troops staged the following aggressions on the South Lebanon areas:
Between 00:50 and 6:35, Qaissieh Valley was hit by more than 155mm missiles and several 120mm shells.
6:30, five 120mm rounds struck Mount Al-Rafih.
6:35, Qabrikha village was shelled by three 81mm rounds.
7:25, four 155mm missiles slammed the perimeters of Sojod.
7:30, several 155mm rounds fell on the peripheries of Haddatha causing damage to the house of Ali Mohammad Nasser whereas Mount Zaatar exposed to another shells.
8:30, the perimeters of Nabatieh and Kfar Romman came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
9:00, west of Yater was targeted by five 155mm missiles.
9:45, the "Israeli" jets carried out a sortie on an area between Arab Salim and Habboush towns.
9:55, the "Israeli" jets raided for the second time on the same area.
10:00, twenty five 120mm shells pounded Mount Sojod and Sojod village.
16:35, the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom exposed to five 155mm missiles.
17:30, two 155mm rounds struck Mozlem ravine.

Military Operations (3)
The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mohammad Sawli and Ibrahim Mohammad Kassfi targeted, at 00:50, enemy motorized movements near the "Israeli" post of Qabaa. Using the adequate arms, the militants were able to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Also, the IR group of martyr Wissam Zayour attacked, at 8:20, an "Israeli" collaborator force as it was fortifying Tohorra outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to hit its southern main fortification. At the same time, the squad of martyr Talal Assaghir targeted the same post with the suitable weapons scoring direct and definite goals.
Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Sawli and Ibrahim Kassfi shelled at 11:25, enemy movements at Blat position with the proper arms. Direct and definite hits were scored, the militants reported.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Melhem and Rida Al-Shaher spotted, at 17:30, enemy movements near Ahmadieh position. Then, the mujahedeen attacked them with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct and definite goals.

However, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 6:30, Qantara compound from the entrance side. The fighters managed to hit directly the main fortification. Meanwhile, another group was targeting the same compound from the northern side striking certainly its main fortification.

At 7:00 also, a squad of LRB attacked Jamoussa position, which is the headquarter of the 80th Lahdi Brigade, with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits. In the meantime, another LRB group was targeting Baraasheit position with the suitable arms. Direct and definite goals were scored, the fighters reported.
