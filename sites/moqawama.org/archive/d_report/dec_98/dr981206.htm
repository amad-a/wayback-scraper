December Sunday 6 -1998:
 
Introduction
The profound impact of the Islamic Resistance (IR) successful and panic strikes is appearing, nowadays, in the "Israeli" Army and its collaborator self-conducted setbasks. The latest one occurred on Friday when the "Israeli" proxy militiamen opened fire, by mistake, on "Israeli" soldiers.
Two "Israeli" soldiers and one collaborator were wounded in an exchange of fire near Majidieh in the Eastern Sector of the "Israeli" occupied area of South Lebanon.

However, Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayed Hassan Nassrallah said the "Israeli" military commanders stand helpless before the IR military attacks and say we can do nothing.

Three occupation troops wounded in "friendly fire" as "Israelis" and collaborators clash in the south  (1)
"Three "Israeli" occupation troops were wounded Friday when fighters from the "Israeli" proxy militia mistakenly opened fire on "Israeli" soldiers", security sources said.

"Two "Israeli" soldiers were critically wounded and a third was seriously injured, the security sources said.

"But in Jerusalem, a military spokesman said that two "Israeli" soldiers and a militiamen were wounded in the exchange of gunfire".

"The attack occurred when the collaborator militiamen mistook the "Israeli" occupation troops for Islamic Resistance (IR) men and opened fire on their patrol near Majidiyeh in the eastern sector of the "Israeli"-occupied zone in southern Lebanon", the security sources said.

"The "Israeli" military spokesman said that "a Lebanese shepherd who spotted an "Israeli" army patrol on a routine mission thought they were a group of Hezbollah and warned the militiamen on leave in the area."

"They opened fire on the patrol," he added.

"In the exchange of fire that followed, two "Israelis" were wounded and a third man from the SLA," he said.

"Senior "Israeli" officers rushed to the scene and managed to stop the shooting, the spokesman said, adding that the "Israeli" occupation army had begun an investigation"

"The two "Israelis" were taken to Rambam Hospital in the northern Palestinian city of Haifa where their conditions were listed as "moderately serious" while the Lahdi militiaman was taken to a Marjayoun hospital where he was said to be in "serious" condition, the spokesman added.

"At least twenty-four "Israeli" occupation soldiers have been killed and 91 wounded in resistance operations in southern Lebanon since the start of 1998. Seven "Israelis" have been killed in the last two weeks in attacks by IR Mujahedeen. The mounting casualties have prompted "Israel" to review its presence in southern Lebanon".

For its part, the "Israeli" newspaper Jerusalem post was quoted as saying:

"Northern Command has launched an inquiry into an ambush mission that went sour on Friday, when South Lebanese Army* troops opened fire by mistake on IDF** soldiers, believing them to be Hizbullah gunmen"

"Two IDF soldiers, one of them an officer, as well as a soldier from the SLA, were shot in the close range gun-battle Friday afternoon in the eastern sector of the security zone, only a few kilometers from the northern border".

"The two IDF soldiers were evacuated by an IAF helicopter to Haifa's Rambam Hospital, where the IDF Spokesman later reported that the soldiers were in serious condition. The wounded SLA soldier was in Marjayoun Hospital also in serious condition".

"The "friendly fire" clash occurred near the SLA's training base at Majadiyeh in the zone, northeast of Metulla. Soldiers from a Combat Engineers unit had taken up an ambush position as part of ongoing operations to kill Hizbullah infiltrators."

"A local shepherd spotted the ambush, but apparently thought that the soldiers were themselves members of a Hizbullah squad".

"The shepherd alerted off-duty SLA soldiers in nearby Halta village, north of Har Dov, who raced to the scene and started shooting at the IDF troops - believing they were gunmen".

"The IDF troops, thinking that they were coming under attack from hostile forces, returned fire. Two members of the IDF unit, the officer and a soldier, as well as one of the SLA soldiers, were wounded in the exchange."

"The shooting was brought to an end through the intervention of senior IDF officers, who rushed to the scene as soon as they received initial reports".

"The incident underlined the tension in the region as a result of the recent wave of Hizbullah attacks, in which seven IDF soldiers and a member of the SLA have been killed and several others wounded."

"Military sources said the inquiry will investigate the level of coordination between IDF and SLA units."

"The off-duty SLA soldiers cannot be blamed for thinking that they were thwarting a Hizbullah attack, but a check with their own commanders and/or IDF liaison officers in the region could have averted the mistaken clash", the sources noted.

"The IDF also has problems, however, over how to keep its missions secret and prevent Hizbullah collaborators inside and north of the zone from obtaining information that could help the organization lay bomb traps and ambushes".

"Ongoing inquiries into the two recent bomb attacks near the IDF's Tel Kabah outpost, in which five Golani Brigade soldiers were killed, have revealed that the Hizbullah squad responsible for planting and detonating the devices received assistance from residents of the zone".

"The interim report on the first Tel Kabah incident, in which three soldiers were killed by a claymore mine while they were walking to a nearby firing range, also revealed breakdowns in certain procedures and regulations at the outpost itself."

"Since the recent deadly pinpoint attacks, which appeared to have been carefully planned in advance, Hizbullah has reverted back to long-range mortar fire at IDF and SLA positions as well as bomb attacks, primarily against SLA targets".

"There was more shelling of positions in the western and eastern sectors on Friday morning, prior to the friendly fire incident. The IDF Spokesman said planes then struck at guerrilla targets in the northeastern sector of the security zone" (2)

"On the other hand, a Lebanese newspaper commented on the "friendly-fire" incident yesterday in which three occupation troops were injured by their collaborators' fire".

"While accidents and friendly- fire incidents are inevitable in a conflict, the episode occurred at perhaps the safest spot in the entire zone, near an "Israeli" army training camp at Majadiyeh, one kilometer from the Lebanese border with the Golan Heights. Hezbollah has never staged any operations in the area," The Daily Star said.

"Friday's debacle is only the latest in a series of embarrassments and setbacks in the past five months for the "Israeli" army in the occupation zone. Others include the lone Hezbollah guerrilla who punched an "Israeli" soldier during a walk in the Sojod compound; the helicopter crash in Dabshe, injuring four soldiers; the armored personnel carrier that plunged 300 meters into the Litani river valley, killing two soldiers; the arrest of a sergeant for gun-running and smuggling crack cocaine into "Israel"; the accidental shooting of a soldier by a colleague at a firing range; the soldier who was hit in the head by a rotating tank barrel", the daily added (3)

Demonstrators across occupied Palestine call for unconditional Lebanon withdrawal (5)
""Israeli" activists calling for immediate withdrawal from south Lebanon demonstrated across occupied Palestine Friday to call for a unilateral "Israeli" pullout from the region, a move supported by some 40 percent of the "Israeli" population, according to a newspaper poll."

"Several hundred protesters gathered near the "Israeli" war ministry for the biggest of the demonstrations, demanding an immediate pullout, but protests were also held in the border town of Qiryat Shemona and at around 20 road junctions across occupied Palestine".

"How much longer?" read one sign at the protest which was organized by the Four Mothers' Movement, a group of "Israeli" occupation soldiers' mothers.

"The ("Israeli" occupation) army high command has received a growing volume of mail from worried parents in recent weeks seeking to avoid their sons "serving" in Lebanon or to have them "brought home", the Hebrew daily Yedioth Ahranot reported Friday.

"The army received 15 letters of concern over the past two weeks as intensified attacks by resistance fighters in occupied southern Lebanon killed seven "Israeli" soldiers."

"An opinion poll published by the "Israeli" daily Ma'ariv on November 30 found that support for an unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon had nearly doubled to 40 percent since a similar poll conducted in March."

"At least twenty-four "Israeli" occupation soldiers have been killed and 91 wounded in resistance operations in southern Lebanon since the start of 1998. Seven "Israelis" have been killed in the last two weeks in attacks by IR Mujahedeen. The mounting casualties have prompted ""Israel"" to review its presence in southern Lebanon."

"Moreover, Yedioth Ahranot said two top "Israeli" military officials had suggested an unconditional troop withdrawal. But it said war minister Yitzhak Mordechai was adamant about receiving security guarantees from the Lebanese government before approving any withdrawal."

"Two top "Israeli" security officials recommended at a meeting of military policymakers that "Israel" withdraw troops from Lebanon unconditionally", the newspaper said.

"The daily said the head of ""Israel's"" domestic Shin Bet security service and the deputy chief of the Mossad intelligence agency both advocated a pullout during a meeting convened last week to discuss ""Israel's"" Lebanon policy."

"The newspaper, quoting officials who participated in the five-hour meeting, said Mordechai told the security forum he remained opposed to an unconditional withdrawal".

"Mordechai told parliament earlier this week that a majority of "Israeli" army commanders believed the best military response to the Lebanon question was to keep troops in place".

"The daily quoted one participant at the meeting as saying Shin Bet chief Ami Ayalon sharply criticized the army's strategy in Lebanon".

"Lebanon stresses an "Israeli" withdrawal must be unconditional"..
