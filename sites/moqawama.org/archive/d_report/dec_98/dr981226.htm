Daily Report
 
 
December Saturday 26 -1998:
 
Leader Khamenei condemns the Janta massacre, calls on Moslem governments and peoples to salvage Ummah from weakness (1)
"Moslems' Leader his Eminence Grand Ayatollah Sayyed Ali Khamenei condemned the brutal massacre perpetrated by the "Israeli" occupation forces in Janta"

"This massacre confirms this enemy's thirst for blood and committing crimes.

In the Friday Khotba he delivered in Tehran today, his Eminence said the "Zionist brutal assault on Lebanon and slaughtering women and children in the holy month of fasting, Ramadan, is one of the bitter acts which express the blood-thirsty and oppressive nature of the Zionist enemy."

"We call on all Moslem peoples and government to reconsider this tragic humiliating situation", his Eminence said.

"Islamic Iran, in its capacity as president of the Organization of Islamic Conference, calls not only on Moslem governments, but on Moslem peoples as well, especially the meritorious scholars, intellectuals, students and journalists to shoulder their responsibility in the face of the increasing raids staged by the arrogant and Zionist group against Islam and the revival of Islamic life. They need to salvage the Ummah from the weakness it is in", his Eminence said.

Egypt condemned "Israeli" brutality (2)
"Egyptian Foreign Minister Amr Moussa Thursday condemned the killing of Lebanese civilians by "Israeli" troops in south Lebanon".

"Commenting on a recent "Israeli" atrocity, Moussa underlined that the life of a Lebanese or Palestinian civilian is not in any way less important than that of an "Israeli"."

"Referring to "Israel's" "apology" for what it termed as "accidental" killing of Lebanese civilians, Moussa slammed the "Israeli" logic as setting a precedent that the "aggressor would kill civilians before saying sorry."

"He poured scorn on Israel's apology, saying that the "Israeli" entity will "always have to remember" that it has destroyed an entire Lebanese family".

""Israeli" warplanes launched an air raid last Tuesday in Beqaa, taking the lives of seven Lebanese civilians, a woman and her six children".

"International laws and conventions prohibit targeting civilians in times of war, he said, demanding "Israel's" respect for the international laws".

"Israel has claimed the killing of the family a "mistake," which was refuted by the U.N. peacekeeping force in south Lebanon".

"A spokesman for the U.N. force had said that the house in which the family was living is hundreds of kilometers away from the "Israeli" border and no military actions have been taken in its vicinity".

Sergeant in the occupation army dies of IR operation injury (3)
""Israel" radio said today 1st Sgt. Doron Benzigri, 26, a border policeman who had suffered head injuries in a clash with Islamic Resistance men more than three years ago, died without ever regaining consciousness".
