Introduction
Any Israeli aggression will not go unpunished. Any Israeli aggression will be retaliated for at the time and in the way that the resistance deems appropriate, Hizbullahs Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Naem Qassem said.

Meanwhile, the tension remained high today along the southern occupied region as Israeli warplanes intensified their flights and the areas adjacent to the occupied area were bombarded.

Hezbollah DSG: bombardment of colonies achieved goals(1)
Hezbollah's Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem said the resistance's rocket retaliation was necessary to affirm the resistance's constant readiness to defend its people.
It was a clear message on what "Israel" may get if such aggressions (as the Janta massacre) are repeated. We believe the retaliation has achieved its goals, Sheikh Qassem said.

What took place in Janta was an aggression. We don't discuss intentions but facts. As far as we are concerned a horrible massacre took place; even if it didn't happen, who gave "Israel" the right to fly its planes in Lebanese airspace and act as it wishes, he added.

The deputy secretary general stressed that any Israeli aggression will not go unpunished.
Any Israeli aggression will be retaliated for at the time and in the way that the resistance deems appropriate, he said.

The Israeli threats are nothing new; we got used to them, especially as "Israel" has always launched aggressions against our women and children with and without threats. They know that we are always ready to confront and retaliate for any aggression, he added.

He said the Israelis think one thousand times before launching such aggressions "although they need such threats to boost the morale of the Israeli internal arena, but if they commit any foolish act, they know that the retaliation will be painful and difficult for them."

His Eminence ruled out the possibility of an Israeli large-scale operation "because it will backfire on the Zionist enemy and its society, and they won't be able to dismantle the resistance's structure. Anyhow, we underline our preparedness to counter any possibility."

Sheikh Qassem highly estimated the Lebanese government's performance in this issue.

Filing a complaint with the UN security Council and the contacts made by Prime Minister Salim al-Hoss are positive steps. This is a good omen of more cooperation and better agreed-on positions. Such good relations are in the interest of Lebanon, he said.

Sidon Metropolitans denounce massacres in Lebanon and Iraq (2)
Roman Catholic and Roman Orthodox Metropolitans in Sidon Georges Quaiter, Elias Kfouri and Mtanious al-Khoury today condemned the "hideous massacres" that have been perpetrated recently in Lebanon and Iraq on the eve of Christmas and during the holy month of Ramadan.

The Metropolitans called on the world's conscience to review what is going on and to put an end to the violations of human rights at all levels.

The clergymen's position came after a meeting they held today at the Sidon Roman Catholic Metropolitan's house.

Arab American Group denounces Israel for raid that kills family (3)
 A top Arab American group denounced the Israeli air raid that killed a mother and six of her children in Janta Tuesday. The Arab American discrimination committee said it rejected the Israeli explanation of the attack as an accident because quote the systematic targeting and repeated massacres of Lebanese civilians by Israel makes their policy of terrorism crystal clear. The Israeli army claimed the civilians were killed because of what it called a human error. A military spokesman alleged one of the pilots had misjudged the target.

The true meaning of goodwill to all (4)
Jarjouh, a village in Iqlim al-Touffah just across the edge of the Israeli-occupied zone, is a frequent target of Israeli and SLA shells and has been deserted by most of its residents. But decorating a tree for Christmas continues to be a yearly custom.

Formerly the home of some 5,000 Christians and Muslims, only 300 residents are left in the village, whose mosque is still intact but whose church was partially destroyed in an Israeli air strike.

Seventy-year-old Haikal Habib Elias is a Christian and has refused to leave his village. Every year, he puts up a Christmas tree outside what is left of the St. George Greek Catholic Church. He sees the ritual as a responsibility to his community since he was entrusted with the keys to the church by the last priest to serve there.

On Christmas Eve, Mr. Elias placed a tree in the church courtyard and decorated it with the help of a Muslim neighbor, Zeinab Saade Bitar.

We are steadfast in our village. This serves as a kind of resistance against the occupiers, Mr. Elias said as he placed an icon of the Virgin Mary on top of the tree.

In the past, Muslim villagers helped their Christian neighbors ring the church bells on Christmas Day, he said, adding that most of the remaining Christians these days go to nearby Nabatieh or Sarba to celebrate religious occasions.

Funeral services are carried out outside the village, but burials still take place in the church cemetery.

Mrs. Bitar said Christians in Jarjouh also take part in Muslim festivals celebrated by their neighbors exactly as we did in the good old days when everyone was still here.

On Christmas Eve a joint dinner was held for everyone in the village, she added to a consenting nod from Mr. Elias.
