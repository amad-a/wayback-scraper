Introduction
The "Israeli" occupation troops pursued their aggressions of the Lebanese areas and the Islamic Resistance, in its turn, continued its strikes against them.

Four out of five "Israelis" want more done to get out of Lebanon (1)
"Four out of five "Israelis" believe the government should do more to get "Israeli" occupation troops out of Lebanon, where they have suffered mounting casualties by the Islamic Resistance (IR) in recent months, an "Israeli" opinion poll suggested Thursday".

"Of those polled, 81 percent said the government should make "greater efforts" to end its 20-year occupation of southern Lebanon, against 13 percent who said they should not and six percent who expressed no opinion"

"Thirty-five percent of those polled said ""Israel"" should pull out even without guarantees against future attacks on northern "Israel"."

"The poll was carried out by Bar Illan University in the week November 22-29 among a representative sample of 1,049 adults and carried a margin of error of three percent".

"Most respondents were surveyed before a pair of IR attacks on "Israeli" occupation troops in south Lebanon on the nights of November 28 and 29 which killed four "Israeli" soldiers prompting "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to cut short a European tour"

"Another opinion poll published by the Tel Aviv daily Maariv on November 30 found even higher support for an unconditional "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon."

"The survey, carried out by polling organization Gallup, found that support for an unconditional withdrawal had nearly doubled to 40 percent since a similar poll conducted by the organization in March."

"Netanyahu's government said earlier this year that it was prepared to implement a 20-year-old UN Security Council resolution calling for an "Israeli" withdrawal, but only in return for guarantees for the security of northern ""Israel"" and its militia allies."

"But Lebanon rejected Netanyahu's offer stressing that Resolution 425 requires ""Israel"" to withdraw unconditionally"
