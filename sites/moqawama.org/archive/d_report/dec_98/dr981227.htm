Daily Report
 
 
December Sunday 27 -1998:
 
Collaborator official killed in Shibaa, another collaborator killed in resistance attack (1)
"Security sources in Nabatieh said resistance men at dawn (Saturday) attacked the outpost of the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators in Tohorra."

"The "Israeli" radio acknowledged that a collaborator militiaman was killed in this attack.
In occupied Shibaa, the collaborator official in the town, Ghassan Daher, 32, was killed last night during a clash between his militia group and another group led by Mohammed Khalil Nabaa".

"The clash was viewed as part of ongoing inner conflicts among collaborator militia factions.
"Israeli" occupation forces have sealed off the town, imposed curfew, and conducted house-to-house searches. "Israeli" troops also cut off the road between the town and Hasbayya"

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
The "Israeli" occupation troops staged the following aggressions:
20:35, Jabbour Hill was bombarded by twenty 120mm rounds.
22:10, several flare shells were fired over Taibeh town.
23:55, five 120mm shells hit Hassan Valley and four others fell on Dallafeh area. In the meantime, several flare shells were fired over Mozlem ravine.

Military Operations (3)
The IR squad of martyrs Fadi Taissir Batesh and Hani Ali Taha targeted, at 19:00, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost. Using the adequate arms, the militants managed to chalk up certain goals.

Two mothers of killed soldiers with PM (4)
 " The two mothers who lost their sons in Lebanon and spent one month demonstrating in front of the president's residence in Jerusalem, demanding to "solve the problem of Lebanon," refused Friday to meet with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu"

"There is no use meeting with Netanyahu alone. Only a meeting including cabinet ministers can raise the issue of Lebanon on the national agenda," said Orna Shimoni, who lost her son Eyal, an armored brigade officer, about 15 months ago. According to Shimoni, Uri Elitzur, the manager of the prime minister's bureau, told them that such a meeting, with Netanyahu and the ministers, could be held this week."

"About one month ago, Shimoni, from Kibbutz Ashdot Ya'akov, and Lala Parnas from Moshav Kidron, who two years ago lost her son Tsur, an officer in the elite unit Egoz, launched a sit-in demonstration next to the president's residence in Jerusalem, demanding to meet with the cabinet ministers and Netanyahu."

"Last Friday, after such a meeting was not held, they decided to go home and continue their struggle from there. On Friday, Netanyahu told the both mothers he could meet them that day, at around noon, but they refused"

"Netanyahu and the cabinet humiliated us," Shimoni said yesterday. "For a whole month they didn't want to talk to us, to hear what we had to say."

"They were visited individually by ministers Yitzhak Mordechai, Rafael Eitan, Shaul Yahalom, and former minister Ya;akov Ne'eman, as well as former chief of staff Amnon Lipkin Sshahak".
