Daily Report
 
 
December Sunday 20 -1998:
 
Occupation forces stage more aggressions on liberated areas (1)
""Israeli" occupation forces once again increased tension in South Lebanon as the enemy artillery in Mashaaroun, Houla and Tallousa last night bombarded the areas of Tamerieh, Majdel Selim, Qabriekha and Toleen. Meanwhile, "Israeli" warplanes staged three raids on these areas cutting off electricity and destroying a car".

"In the morning (Saturday), the occupation's artillery in Zaffateh bombarded Mazraat al-Hamra, Hima Arnoon, the outskirts of Yohmor, the vicinity of Zawtar al-Sharqieh and Zawtar al-Gharbieh and the Litani River banks"

"Moreover, the enemy aggressions also targeted the woods of Sojod, al-Rihan, and Mazraat Oqmata in Iqlim al-Touffah".

"Before noon today, "Israeli" warplanes violated the Lebanese airspace and broke the sound barrier twice over Nabatieh, Iqlim al-Touffah and the capital Beirut".

"On the other hand, reports from the occupied zone said occupation intelligence elements accompanied by a number of collaborator Lahd's militiamen stormed into the occupied town of Ramia. The enemy troops abducted Raef Rahmeh Issa, 37, and took him to the al-Khiam detention center"

For its part, the Islamic Resistance recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions (2):
8:15, two 155mm missiles struck the peripheries of Jibaa town.
9:45, four 155mm missiles landed Mazraat Hamra area.
10:05, the area between Haris and Haddatha towns was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
11:10, Nahle ravine which is located between Shaqra and Majdal Selem towns was targeted by more than six 155mm missiles.
12:55, two 155mm missiles fell on the peripheries of Majdal Selem.
14:55, Joune valley was bombarded by eight 120 and 155mm rounds.
15:20, Haddatha woodland exposed to five 120mm rounds, whereas the perimeters of Haddatha came under five 81mm shells. At the same time, the west of Yater village subjected to four 155mm missiles and Mount Zaatar to five others.
15:25, ten 155mm missiles pounded west of Yater, when on the other hand, Jib Soweid area was shelled by three others.
15:30, Majdal Zoun was raked by a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds causing damage to the house of Habib Rashid.
15:55, eight 155mm missiles pounded the peripheries of Haddatha, while at the same time, three 120mm shells struck the outskirts of Haris.
16:45, four shells fell on Joune valley.
Between 17:20 and 18:00, Haddatha forest was shelled by more than twenty four 81, 120 and 155mm missiles.
18:00, four 155mm rounds slammed the perimeters of Majdal Selem.
18:10, the area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem was targeted by two 155mm missiles.
19:30, one 120mm round struck the surroundings of Anan.
Between 21:25 and 22:20, Slouqi Valley was bombarded by six 81mm shells and six 120mm rounds.
24:00, Slouqi Valley and its surroundings came under a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations (3)
The IR squad of martyrs Wissam Zayour and Talal Assaghir attacked, at 7:05, Sojod outpost with the proper rocket-propelled grenades. In the meantime, the group of martyrs Hassan El-Hajj Ali and Kassem Ali Harb was attacking Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. At the same time, the group of martyrs Hassan Sawli and Ibrahim Kassfi targeted an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the adequate arms, the militants managed to score accurate goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Abbas and Kassem Yassine attacked, at 9:45, Beaufort Castle with the appropriate rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goal were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyr Mohammad Atwi targeted, at 11:30, Mashaaroun position with the adequate propelled-grenades scoring direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Wafiq Zahwi and Abdel-Rassoul Baydoun attacked, at 15:20, Tair Harfa position with the suitable weapons. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Ali Assaf and Fadi Kassem Izzeddine attacked, at 19:00, motorized movements at Bir Kallab outpost with the proper weapons scoring accurate hits.

IR proudly announced two martyrs killed during confrontations with enemy (4)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) Mujahedeen received the holy month of Ramadan with the fragrance of their pure blood and offering more sacrifices to defend right and humanity against an unjust occupier that practices nothing but killing and terrorism."

"On the path of liberation and victory, the Islamic Resistance proudly announces to its steadfast and sacrificing people two of its heroes who were martyred while defending the pride and dignity of our nation, an IR communiqué read.

"Martyr Wafiq Zahwi Zahwi (Komiel) and Martyr Abdel-Rasoul Radwan Baydoun (Abu Hadi) were martyred Friday during the heroic confrontations which IR fought against the "Israeli" occupation forces in the vicinity of the Masharoun outpost", the communiqué added.

"Martyr Zahwi, 22, of Majdel Selim, single, joined IR in 1994, took part in several educational and military courses, participated in many Jihad operations".

"Martyr Baydoun, 21, of al-Sh'habieh, single, joined IR in 1994, took part in several educational and military courses, participated in many Jihad operations".
