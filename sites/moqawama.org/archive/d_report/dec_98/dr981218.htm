Daily Report
 
 
December Friday 18 -1998:
 
Occupied village wants its sayyed back (1)
"More than 40 people from the occupied village of Kfar Kila in the south appealed to the government Thursday to lodge an official complaint to the UN over the detention of the villages spiritual leader two months ago."

"Sayyed Abbas Fadlallah was detained by (the "Israeli"-proxy) South Lebanon Army militiamen Oct. 27 when he was offering condolences at a neighbors home. His uncle, Hadi Fadlallah, appealed to local and international communities for his nephews release".

Fifty days have passed since the crime  took place and  he is subjected to brutal torture for no reason, Mr. Fadlallah said during a news conference at the Press Federation.

In addition to Sayyed Fadlallah, 10 other people from Kfar Kila have been detained at the Khiam detention center.

"Mr. Fadlallah claimed that the "Israelis" were planning to abduct the few remaining religious leaders in the zone. Weve appealed to spiritual leaders first, since theyre the party most concerned with the issue, then to official and diplomatic officials. But Sayyed Fadlallah is still in detention, he said.
"Mr. Fadlallah called on the International Committee for the Red Cross to arrange a visit to Sayyed Fadlallah. The head of the ICRC delegation, Henry Fournier, told us last month that Sayyed Fadlallah was detained in Khiam and he promised to visit him within the week but were still waiting for a response, he said.

"Lebanon protested the detention to the UN, which instructed Unifil to gather further information from the "Israeli" army."

"Unifil sources said Thursday that the "Israelis" have still refused to comment on the story".
"There has been a suggestion that Sayyed Fadlallah could stand trial for his part in the alleged scheme". "But this has been dismissed because the SLA has no jurisdiction to hold trials and if Sayyed Fadlallah were transferred to the Jewish state, it would undermine Israels claims to have no authority over Khiam".

Occupation and collaborators on alert (2)
 "The collaborator's militia in the occupied zone was placed on alert and all the militiamen were ordered to join their positions. The new measure was linked with the US aggressions on Iraq and the general situation in the south".

"At the front-line positions in the Hasbayya sector there were "Israeli" armored movements. Bulldozers erected barricades and set up fortifications in the outpost of Zoghle, Tallet Ain Qinia, and Marj Shwayya."

"The collaborator militia increased its motorized movements on highways, especially the Hasbayya - Marjeyoun highway. Temporary checkpoints were erected".

"In the Marjeyoun - Khiam sector an "Israeli" armored force this morning raked the areas of the Marjeyoun Plain and Marj al-Khoukh with heavy machine guns".

However, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a communiqué in which it recorded the following "Israeli" aggressions (3):
Between 13:15 and 13:30, six 155mm missiles slammed west of Yater village.
13:20, Jib Soweid area was hit by five 155mm missiles.
14:45, five 120mm rounds struck Oqmata area.
16:55, Kassaret Al-Oroush showered its surroundings with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
17:45, the area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem towns was targeted by four 155mm missiles and several 120mm rounds.
17:50, Qaissieh Valley exposed to a number of 155mm missiles.
21:25, Slouqi Valley was shelled by five 81mm missiles and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
21:30, the perimeters of Shaqra town came under four 155mm missiles.
21:35, one 120mm round struck the house of the citizen Abdel-Raouf Khalaf, at Shaqra, inflicting his son Mohsen, 14, who was moved to the hospital for the treatment. In the meantime, Slouqi Valley was hit by six 120mm rounds.
22:20, two 81mm flare shells were fired over Haddatha.
