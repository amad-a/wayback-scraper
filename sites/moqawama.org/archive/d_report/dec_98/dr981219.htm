Daily Report
 
 
December Saturday 19 -1998:
 
Teenager killed by "Israeli" gunfire
"A 17-year-old boy on his way to collect provisions for his family was shot and killed late Thursday night by an "Israeli" commando unit that had advanced out of the zone to the outskirts of Shaqra village.

Mohsen Khallaf died instantly in a hail of gunfire beside his house on the edge of the village".
"Initial reports Friday said that he had been struck by an "Israeli" artillery shell during a bombardment of the area the night before"

"But evidence emerged Friday suggesting that a team of "Israeli" commandos had killed the boy.
The Khallaf house is the last in the village before reaching the zone. Beyond the house is the bleak and desolate expanse of the zones central sector and the infamous Wadi Slouqi, scene of countless gunbattles between the resistance and "Israeli" troops."

It was around 9:15 P.M. when Mohsen said he would go into the village and buy us some food for sohur (the pre-dawn meal during Ramadan), said Mohsens brother, Ali.

He had just left the house when we heard gunshots and the sound of helicopters in the air. My mother wanted to run outside to see if Mohsen was safe but I wouldnt let her go, he added.

The helicopter gunships remained in the area for half an hour while the family lay panic-stricken in their home. Shortly after 9:45 P.M., a car pulled up outside the Khallaf house and Ali heard the beep of a horn.

The driver told me there was somebody lying in the road by the house. I knew it must be Mohsen. When I went to him, his body was already cold, Ali said.

"The villagers at first believed he had been accidentally struck by bullets fired from the Houla position, about one kilometer away. But the tragedy became all the more sinister when several 5.56mm cartridge cases were discovered on a grassy hillock only 50 meters from where Mohsen died. The caliber is that of the M-16 assault rifle, a weapon favored by "Israeli" special forces units".

"Furthermore, on inspecting the site to gather evidence to back a possible complaint with the monitoring group, the Army found a grenade designed to be launched from an M-16. The grenade had Hebrew markings."

"There was further clear evidence of concentrated small arms fire from the bullet-holed cactus and chipped rocks next to where Mohsen died."

"Unifil confirmed the presence of helicopters over the area Thursday night although the Irish peacekeepers manning 6-28 position, about one kilometer from the village, did not report the sound of small arms fire. However, the weather was bad that night and the Irish position was on the other side of a hill from the scene of the shooting".

"The "Israeli" Army has made no comment on the killing. But it appears plausible that a commando unit, backed by helicopter gunships, had penetrated the village, possibly with the intention of planting roadside bombs, when it stumbled on Mohsen."

"Mohsen was buried Friday morning in Shaqras cemetery under a dreary gray sky and just a few meters from the graves of 16 resistance martyrs from the village" (1)
