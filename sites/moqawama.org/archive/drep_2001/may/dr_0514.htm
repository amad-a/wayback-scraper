 Thurs2ay 29-2000

 

Withdrawal from Lebanon Had The Greatest Impact on The Zionist Entity: Ben Eliezer…(1)

“Israeli” enemy war minister Benjamin Ben Eliezer in an interview with Ma’ariv daily said that the “Israeli” withdrawal from Lebanon had the greatest impact on the Zionist Entity. He said he was not sorry for this withdrawal and if he was in power then, he would have taken the same decision. In an indication of the influence of the withdrawal from Lebanon on the Palestinians, the “Israeli” war minister said that Arafat understood the withdrawal as “hundreds of people who found a situation where they succeeded to force “Israel” out of Lebanon”.

 

Renewed “Israeli” shelling in the Shibaa area of southern Lebanon…(2)

“ “Israeli” artillery shelled the area around the village of Shibaa in the eastern sector of southern Lebanon Saturday for the second time in four days, but caused no casualties, Lebanese police said.

Five shells fell on Birkit Nakkar and the Seddana hill to the southwest of the village, shortly after 3 PM (1200 GMT), they said.

The firing started without any military activity occurring on the Lebanese side, they added.

On Wednesday, a dozen “Israeli” shells targeted the same area, also without causing casualties, while “Israeli” helicopters overflew the Shibaa Farms.

Since the “Israeli” pullout from southern Lebanon in May 2000, Hizbullah, which spearheaded the resistance against the occupation, has continued its operations against the “Israeli” army in the Shibaa Farms area.

Hizbullah has killed three “Israeli” soldiers and captured three more in the area since October.

The Shibaa Farms is located beyond the "blue line" drawn by the United Nations to establish a border between the two countries.

 

Hizbullah launches first fish farm…(3)

Hizbullah’s agriculture cooperative launched its first fish-farming project on the Tayr Filsey River in Tyre on Saturday.
Tyre MP Abdullah Qassir, Iranian Ambassador Mohammad Sobhani, Iranian Agriculture Ministry official Khairallah Barwin, and other political and local figures were present.
Qassir said Hizbullah would “strive to ensure development and resistance requirements for all southern areas” and accused the government of “neglecting” deprived areas in the South.

 

Bomb wounds shepherd, kills goats…(4)

The Internal Security Forces said Saturday that an unexploded “Israeli” cluster bomb injured a shepherd in the Western Bekaa town of Jub Jennin In addition to wounding 25-year-old Ahmed Kattour on Friday, the bomb also killed a pair of his goats.

 

Refugees in Lebanon march in Hamas demo hailing anti-”Israel” martyrdom attacks…(5)

“ About 4,000 Palestinian refugees joined in a demonstration organized by the Hamas Islamic movement Sunday, which was intended to honor martyrdom attacks against “Israel”.

The protestors demonstrated in the Ain al-Helweh refugee camp -- Lebanon's biggest with around 70,000 people -- in the outskirts of the southern port city of Sidon, an AFP correspondent said.

The demonstration was led by representatives of Hamas and officials from various Islamic movements, the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-General Command of Ahmad Jibril and the Saiqa.

Youths, some in military fatigues and others in white tunics and wearing fake explosives marched around the camp while holding the Koran Muslim holy book.

Dozens of under-five's, wearing shrouds and fake explosive belts, marched with plastic weapons.

They chanted slogans for Arab and Muslim unity "against Zionism, Jews and +Sharonism+," after the policy of “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon.

A group of protestors held banners with the name of every one of some 40 Palestinian villages destroyed during the Nakba, or the "catastrophe" of the 1948 creation of the state of “Israel”.

The vast majority of the 367,000 Palestinian refugees in Lebanon are originally from territories on which the Jewish state was established.

The protestors marched toward the camp mosque where they listened to a live fiery speech via telephone and through a loudspeaker by Khaled Mashaal, the Damascus-based Hamas politburo president.

Mashaal warned against "attempts to suffocate the Intifada," or the Palestinian uprising against “Israel” that has been raging since September.

"It is unconceivable that Arabs serve as intermediaries between our people and the enemy," he added in reference to the recent Egyptian-Jordanian initiative to revive Palestinian-”Israeli” peace talks.

 

“Israel” enters Palestinian area, kills 5, pounds Gaza…(6)

“ “Israeli” troops entered a Palestinian area of the West Bank early on Monday hours after killing five Palestinian policemen and rocketing security targets in the Gaza Strip, Palestinian officials said.

In a night of intense aggression, “Israeli” soldiers demolished a Palestinian police station in a suburb of the West Bank Palestinian-ruled town of Tulkarm and then entered the town and destroyed a house, the governor of Tulkarm told Reuters.

Earlier, a Palestinian ambulance officer, who declined to be named, said his crew had found the bodies of five men from the Palestinian National Forces shot dead by heavy machinegun fire and dumped in a hole in the ground near Beitunia, west of Ramallah.

It was the highest Palestinian death toll since late March and brought to 418 the number of Palestinians killed since a revolt against “Israeli” occupation erupted last September after peace talks stalemated.

Earlier, “Israeli” helicopter gunships and naval boats destroyed at least eight armoured personnel carriers in a fierce missile strike on Palestinian security targets in Gaza.

Helicopters hovered over a security compound 300 metres (yards) from Palestinian President Yasser Arafat's Gaza City headquarters before they opened fire, the rockets glowing red against the black sky above Gaza.

Thousands of Gaza City residents awoke as the ground shuddered from the explosions, shortly after midnight on Monday, the eve of a Palestinian national day of mourning to mark the anniversary of “Israel’s” creation on May 15, 1948.

Tension was expected to rise before Tuesday's anniversary, which Palestinians mark as the Nakba or "Great Catastrophe" of their uprooting from towns and villages in the first Arab-”Israeli” war.

INCURSIONS CRITICISED

The “Israeli” incursion into the Palestinian-ruled area of the West Bank was the latest of several similar operations in the past weeks which have been criticised by the United States.

"(An “Israeli” force) entered (Palestinian) Area A in Tulkarm...and demolished a house" Tulkarm governor Izz al-Din al-Sharif said.

"This action is unjustified and is a mere demonstration of strength by (“Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel) Sharon which is unacceptable to us," he added.

The army said in a statement it had destroyed the house and police station after Palestinians failed to prevent several shooting incidents against “Israeli” citizens.

A Palestinian security source said the five men were killed when “Israeli” soldiers "shelled" a checkpoint they were guarding.

Ramallah governor Mustafa Liftawi accused soldiers of killing the men while some slept and others did guard duty as part of what Palestinians say is “Israel’s” policy of assassinating Palestinian activists.

An army spokeswoman said soldiers on "operational activity" west of Ramallah saw "suspicious figures who were where they were not supposed to be" and opened fire. She said this was the only incident in which soldiers fired at Palestinians in the area early on Monday. "It was probably them," she said.

GAZA STRIKE "UNPROVOKED"

Palestinian Public Security Chief Abdel-Razek al-Majaydeh called the missile strike in Gaza City, central and southern Gaza "an unprovoked attack". He said at least eight helicopters took part in the attack.

"The “Israeli” bombardment tonight in the Gaza Strip indicates that “Israel” is interested in escalating the tension and came without any reason," al-Majaydeh told Reuters.

At least three Palestinian policemen were wounded but none appeared to be in serious condition. It was not immediately clear whether buildings near the armoured vehicles were seriously damaged in the attack.

An army spokeswoman said the attack was the result of an "unprecedented number of mortar attacks" against Jewish settlements in Gaza and “Israeli” farming communities nearby in the last four days. She said 17 mortar bombs had been fired.

U.N. Secretary General Kofi Annan accused “Israel” of using "excessive force" against the Palestinian uprising, adding in an interview with Reuters in Brussels that talks between the two sides had produced meagre results.

 

“Israeli” Occupation Army Assassinates Two Palestinians, More Mortar Attacks in Retaliation…(7)

Continuing its assassination policy, four “Israeli” helicopters fired three rockets at a car, parked close to the offices of the Palestinian governor of the autonomous Palestinian town of Jenin in the northern Gaza Strip. Motassem al-Sabagh, an official of the Fatah faction, was in a car when it was hit. He died of his injuries while he was on his way to a Nablus hospital. In the same incident, a 21-year-old Palestinian policeman, Allam Jaludi, who was standing close to the car targeted by the helicopters, also died of injuries sustained in the attack. Thirteen other Palestinians were also injured. The U.S. Apache type helicopters fired numerous missiles and severely damaged five buildings. An “Israeli” enemy army spokesman refused to comment on the attack. Palestinian Mujaheddeen responded by firing three mortar bombs at the Jewish settlement of Neve Dekalim, slightly injuring one Jewish settler. Palestinian security sources told AFP that “Israeli” tanks moved dozens of meters (yards) inside a zone under full Palestinian authority in Khan Yunis before withdrawing. Elsewhere in the Gush Katif area, “Israeli” troops moved several meters from their army camp in Qarara village and seized an unused building inside Palestinian territory, security sources said. Meanwhile, three Palestinian teenagers were wounded, one of them seriously, when “Israeli” troops fired live rounds at a group of stone throwers at the Mintar crossing, close to Gaza City. On another level, hundreds of Palestinians began a funeral march Saturday morning for a 16-year-old boy killed in clashes with the “Israelis”. Hussam Tahfish was killed Friday at the al-Mintar crossing point between occupied territories and the Gaza Strip. Medical sources said he had been shot by a live round from an automatic weapon, with the shot entering his side before piercing his heart. Fatah movement had called for a strong turnout at the funeral of the boy whom they said was one of their members. More clashes erupted there Saturday, with one boy wounded. Meanwhile, the leader of the Islamic Hamas movement called on Palestinians to intensify their attacks on the Zionist Entity in the run-up to next week’s 53rd anniversary of the so-called “Israel’s” independence on May 15, which the Palestinians call "The Catastrophe". In an attack friday night, “Israeli” forces had shelled the village of Beit Lahya in the northern Gaza Strip. Chief of Palestinian public security in the Gaza Strip, Brigadier Abdel Razaq El Majayda, said four shells had been shot from the northern borders between Gaza and occupied Palestinian territories and had targeted civilians. At least three civilians were wounded. El Majayda warned that “targeting civilians is a very serious “Israeli” escalation against defenseless people".

 

“Israeli” Analyst Questions Policy Vis-A-Vis the Intifadah, Fearing Another Defeat…(8)

Why are we fighting? What victory is "“Israel”" seeking in exchange of the Intifadah? Under this title the political analyst in “Israeli” daily Ha'aretz, Aure Bar Yussef, wrote: From the start of the violent confrontations with the Palestinians, politicians mouthed assertions that we will win. But till now the signs of this definite victory are not seen. Bar Yussef considers every military institution, which fights for a long time without defining the reasons behind fighting, will eventually meet a destructive end. "This is what happened to the Americans who emerged from their crisis of Vietnam War with grave losses. And that was the case with the Soviets who ran away from Afghanistan. “Israel” as well behaved similarly in and retreated unilaterally from southern Lebanon," he said. Bar Yussef added, the price "“Israel”" is paying in the current Intifadah is not cheap. He defined four aims for which "“Israel”" is fighting: the settlements, West bank and the Gaza Strip, Al-Quds, and annulling the right to return of the Palestinian refugees. As for settlements, Palestinian insist on freezing their construction now to remove them permanently in the future, and for that they are willing to die. On the other hand, Bar Yussef wondered: "How many among the “Israelis” would be ready to sacrifice and defend the settlements of Netzarim or Nissit or the settlements in Hebron? How many mothers are willing to push their children for doing that?" The “Israeli” analyst mentioned the growing death toll of “Israeli” soldiers and settlers. He maintained that most of the “Israelis” believe they need nothing in that region and they favor a unilateral separation, though they find it difficult to go behind the 1967 borders in the frame of a comprehensive solution. Bar Yussef noticed that the issue of occupied Quds (Al-Quds) is very intricate. But both the Palestinians and the “Israelis” were on the verge of solving it before the break of confrontations. Former US President Bill Clinton had proposed a possible solution before leaving office. As for the fourth issue, "“Israel”" finds it difficult should the Palestinian refugees return. Suffering heavily from two Palestinian fronts in the Gaza Strip and the West Bank, " “Israel”" warned the danger will be graver if the refugees return. The “Israeli” analyst concluded, "all the attempts to praise the victory-to-be are making the “Israelis” less and less supportive of the continuation of war in the future, and to avoid the repetition of questions asked now concerning the war against Lebanon, a discussion pertaining to the Palestinian Intifadah must start as soon as possible."
