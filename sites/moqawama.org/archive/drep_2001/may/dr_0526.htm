Liberation Not Complete Without the Shibaa Farms Says Lebanese Officials On First Anniversary of Liberation

Nasrallah: Resistance is the major weapon in Lebanon’s hand…(1)

The day the “Israeli” occupation army was defeated and forced out of southern Lebanon and Western Bekaa thanks to Lebanon's resistance, was marked in the UNESCO Palace in Beirut. The ceremony took place under the auspices of Lebanese President Emile Lahoud and was attended by Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, Lebanese Speaker Nabih Berri, Prime Minister Rafiq Hariri, and other Lebanese officials and foreign diplomats. Sayyed Nasrallah praised the honorable role of the resistance in ending the 22-year-old Zionist occupation. Nasrallah warned that the Zionist enemy is still threatening the whole region with war, and is still occupying part of Lebanon and still holds Lebanese detainees. He said the resistance is the major weapon in Lebanon's hand and a rational and threatened person never throws his weapons amid a battle. Sayyed Nasrallah also revealed that Hizbullah has received tempting offers to stop its resistance. Lebanese speaker Nabih Berri considered that UN resolution 425 is still not implemented until Shibaa Farms is liberated. Berri said the blue-line delineated by the United Nations means nothing to the southerners. He added, "We are only interested in our country's full sovereignty". He said, "As long as the Shibaa Farms are occupied, Lebanon has the right to resist. And resolutions saying that Shibaa Farms are not subject to Resolution 425, do not concern us. Any “Israeli” attack on Syrian troops in Lebanon or Syria will be considered an attack against the south and will give the resistance the full right to retaliate by hitting the “Israeli” northern settlements. These are the new rules of the game. The time “Israel” imposes its rules has ended." Prime Minister Rafiq Hariri said the 25th of May is a national day par excellence, indicating a number of challenges facing the state, especially the reconstruction of the liberated areas. Head of the committee which organized the liberation celebrations, deputy Prime Minister Issam Fares, considered that liberation Lebanese territories will not be complete unless the Shibaa Farms is recovered and the detainees released.

 

Country set to mark the day the “Israelis” were driven out
Qassem : It’s our duty to support Intifada..(2)

Gearing up for the May 25 anniversary of “Israel”’s withdrawal from the South, natives of the liberated villages marched on Thursday from Marjayoun to the Fatima Gate, where they staged an anti-”Israeli” sit-in.
They waved Lebanese and Hizbullah flags and burned American and “Israeli” flags while chanting anti-”Israeli” and anti-American slogans. The march was headed by the cleric, Imam Sheikh Hassan Taleb, from Kfour, near Nabatieh.
Ragheb Harb, son of Sheikh Ragheb Harb, who was killed by “Israeli” forces, addressed the crowd.
“The victory over the enemy was only possible thanks to the blood of our martyrs and their patience and steadfastness,” he said against a background of “Israeli” warplanes hovering at low altitudes overhead.
Meanwhile, a conference opened on Thursday at the Commodore Hotel to express solidarity with the Palestinian intifada in the West Bank and Gaza. The conference was organized by the Arab Committee for Helping the Intifada.
Hizbullah’s deputy secretary-general, Sheikh Naim Qassem, addressed the audience and congratulated them on the occasion of the first anniversary celebration of Liberation Day.
“Our victory against the enemy has opened a new chapter in the Arab-”Israeli” conflict because we now speak from a position of strength after years of speaking from a position of weakness,” he said. He added that “it’s our duty to support the intifada because we are directly affected by the developments in the West Bank and Gaza.”
He stressed that “it’s not strange for us to be involved in the events in Palestine. In fact, it would be strange if we weren’t involved in such events.”
He accused “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon of wasting time in merely talking about regional peace.
“Just as we were capable of defeating the enemy in Lebanon, the Palestinians too can deal a heavy blow to the Zionists,” Qassem said.
Meanwhile, Iranian Education Minister Hussein Muzaffar
visited Hizbullah’s secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, on Thursday to discuss bilateral relations.
“I met with Sheikh Nasrallah, the great military leader who scored a great victory against the Zionists,” he said after the visit. “We hope our Palestinian brothers are capable of inflicting a similar defeat against this enemy soon.”
He added that “the victory that was achieved by a small number of fighters against a massively better-equipped enemy shows that great things can be achieved thanks to self-confidence and reliance on God.”
On Liberation Day, Nabatieh Governor Mahmoud Mawla is due to inaugurate a marble plaque commemorating the occasion. The plaque, erected on the Western side of Beaufort Castle, bears the inscription:, “On May 25, 2000, the dawn of liberation came during President Emile Lahoud’s term in office.”
The plaque, donated by the Nabatieh governor’s office, measures 4 meters in height and 3 meters in width and features a cedar tree in the middle.
For the occasion, Hizbullah set up several arches to entrances of southern villages. Placards were also on display bearing anti-”Israeli” slogans. Hizbullah and Amal flags and pictures of Nasrallah were placed on the roofs of houses and power poles.
Minister of State Beshara Merhej described Liberation Day as a “landmark in Lebanon’s history.”
Speaking at a political forum organized on the occasion of Liberation Day, he said that “victory against the enemy was the crowning of decades of effort and sacrifices.”
Tyre MP Ali Khreis said that “total victory will be achieved when all the land is liberated including the Shibaa Farms and the Golan Heights and when the Lebanese prisoners in “Israel” are released.”
Western Bekaa MP Sami Khatib paid tribute to the Lebanese people for the liberation. He called for more international pressure to be exerted on “Israel”.
In a statement issued on the occasion of Liberation Day, the Progressive Socialist Party said that “Liberation Day was an occasion for celebration for all Lebanese because everyone played a part in this victory.”

 

Lahoud warns north “Israel” could be threatened if it strikes Lebanon…(3)

Lebanese President Emile Lahoud said Thursday that northern “Israel” would be threatened if the Jewish state launched strikes on Lebanon in retaliation for anti-”Israeli” attacks by the Lebanese group Hizbullah.

"“Israel” was not able to change the rules of the game and any new equation it would attempt to impose would jeopardise its security in the north", Lahoud told the London-based newspaper Al-Wassat.

Extracts from the interview were published by the Al-Hayat daily on the anniversary of “Israel”'s troop pull-out from southern Lebanon after 22 years of occupation.

On May 18, Syrian Foreign Minister Faruq al-Shara said that "if “Israel” tried to change the rules of the game, we will put back the rules that we want for this game. Time is on our side."

 

“Israeli” Defense (War) Minister Binyamin Ben Eliezer, speaking on army radio on Wednesday, again warned that its military would fire on Syrian targets in Lebanon to retaliate for any attacks by Hizbullah.

On April 16 “Israeli” planes destroyed a Syrian radar station in Lebanon, killing a Syrian soldier in the wake of an attack by Hizbullah on the Shibaa Farms area.

 

Lahoud: Resistance is our best weapon to face “Israeli” aggression
President warns of more dangerous days as Jewish state ‘gets murkier’…(4)

President Emile Lahoud warned Thursday that the region was “on the brink of an all-out conflagration” and called on the Lebanese to remain steadfast.
In a national address on the eve of Liberation Day, Lahoud called on the international community to renew efforts to restart the Middle East peace process.
“The region finds itself on the brink of an all-out conflagration as a direct consequence of “Israel”’s intransigence,” he said. “We believe that the world community needs to instill a sense of renewed activism and undertake a major reassessment of the peace process. This should first and foremost sternly demand a halt in over half a century of “Israeli” aggression.”
Lahoud said that “while this review takes place and due to the volatility of the situation it behooves us to retain the resistance and steadfastness as the  weapons of choice facing “Israeli” aggression.”
“Time is on our side,” he said. “The “Israeli” conundrum gets murkier by the day. And no matter how arduous our journeys seem and no matter how some  continue to hedge their bets on unrealistic scenarios we believe that we are on the right path toward salvation and that the course we charted contrary, to the misconceptions of some, is the least weary for Lebanon and the Lebanese.
“In these dire times and while our enemy assimilates the necessary lessons that will induce him to return to the negotiating table, we need, as Lebanese citizenry and institutions, to brace ourselves with a sense of national unity, responsibility, perseverance and faith.”
 
 

 “Israel” will be bombarded if Syrian army hit in Lebanon: Berri ..(5)

Lebanese Speaker Nabih Berri said Friday that northern “Israel” would be bombarded if the Jewish army carries out more attacks on Syrian positions in Lebanon.

 "“Israel” says it wants to change the rules of the game but we are telling it that if it attacks the Syrian army in Lebanon, the (Lebanese) resistance will consider it its right to retaliate against the whole of “Israel”'s north", he said during a ceremony in Beirut marking the first anniversary of “Israel”'s pull out from south Lebanon.

It was the first time Lebanese or Syrian threats were so clear in the current tension between “Israel” and its northern neighbours.

Berri added, "Lebanon has the right to continue its resistance ... as long as “Israel” continues to occupy the Shibaa Farms”.

Berri said that United Nations resolution 425 on the “Israeli” pull out from south Lebanon will only be fully implemented when “Israel” withdraws from the Shibaa Farms.

 

Iran says Lebanese resistance model for Palestinians..(6)

Iran said Friday that the Islamic resistance which helped force “Israeli” troops out of south Lebanon a year ago was serving as a model for Palestinians in their uprising against the Jewish state.

"The victory of the Lebanese Islamic resistance against the Zionist regime's occupiers was unprecedented in the history of Arab-”Israeli” wars," the foreign ministry said in a statement to mark the anniversary of the “Israeli” pullout.

"The Palestinian people now believe that continuing their jihad (holy war) and resistance is the only way for them to end the occupation of their lands by “Israel” and regain their legitimate rights," it said.

Iran does not recognise the state of “Israel” and has wholeheartedly supported the latest Palestinian uprising or intifada.

Foreign Minister Kamal Kharazi called on Islamic countries on Wednesday to bankroll the intifada, saying: "There is no hope for peace as long as the racist and aggressive regime exists in the heart of the Middle East."

Foreign ministers of the Organisation of the Islamic Conference, currently chaired by Qatar, are to meet in Doha on Saturday to consider the Middle East crisis.

 

“Israeli” Occupation Troops Shoot, Wound Two Civilians Along the Border…(7)

A 14-year-old Lebanese civilian Bassam Jaafar was wounded after being shot at from “Israeli” enemy positions at al-Abbad occupation border post. Earlier, “Israeli” occupation troops shot and wounded a Palestinian near the border fence in a demonstration marking the first anniversary of the “Israeli” occupation army withdrawal from southern Lebanon. Mohammad al-Assadi, 25, was hit by two bullets in the legs and taken to hospital in the town of Nabatiyeh. He was among the many groups of people to have gathered on the border to celebrate the anniversary.

 

Hizbullah organises women's protest on border with “Israel” …(8)

More than 1,200 women gathered here by the old “Israeli”-Lebanese border crossing in a protest organised by the Lebanese movement Hizbullah to mark the one-year anniversary of “Israel”'s withdrawal from south Lebanon.

But the organisers called by loudspeakers for demonstrators not to throw rocks at the “Israeli” military watchtower across the border.

Fatima Gate was one of five operating border crossings between “Israel” and Lebanon before the Jewish state pulled out from Lebanon last May 24 after 22 years.

It has since become a pilgrimage site for Lebanese and Arabs to throw a symbolic stones at “Israeli” military positions across the border.

The women held aloft Lebanese and Palestinian flags, as well as pennants with the initials of Hizbullah, and burned “Israeli” and US flags, while shouting in support of the Islamic Resistance, Hizbullah's armed wing, which spearheaded the rebellion against “Israeli” occupation.

“Israel” is on alert for a possible attack by Hizbullah, which has killed three “Israeli” soldiers and captured another three in skirmishes on a part of the border which Lebanon still claims.

 

Liberation stamp says it all…(9)

To mark the first anniversary of liberation, Libanpost and the Telecommunications Ministry launched a commemorative stamp depicting the struggle against the “Israelis” on Thursday .
“This stamp captures an important event in Lebanon’s history,” Telecommunications Minister Jean-Louis Qordahi told a news conference, where he unveiled the new stamp.
“It spreads knowledge about Lebanon,” he added.
A year ago, the ministry coordinated with Libanpost to issue the stamp in time to mark the first anniversary of the South’s liberation from 22 years of “Israeli” occupation.
Stamp designer Hiba Mikdashi, 24, used four essential features in designing the stamp ­ the human, which symbolizes the nation and resistance, patriotism, seen through the Lebanese flag, and a map of Lebanon, with the former occupation zone highlighted.
“Hands are pointing to the sky, voices are silently raised, eyes are looking up to heaven and the flag stands high and mighty,” Mikdashi told The Daily Star.
She said the stamp would show future generations what was achieved to enable them to live freely. She said it would also trigger foreign interest in the resistance.
“The stamp practically speaks for itself. All the emotions shown on the stamp illustrate war (pain) and victory (relief), and give the observer a strong dose of hope and confidence,” said Mikdashi.
She said that designing the stamp in such a way was a result of the struggle she witnessed and experienced during the “Israeli” occupation.
Libanpost chairman Nassib Husseini described his efforts to attract collectors.
“We’re trying to help seven stamp collectors in forming a club,  and hope to attract more of them,” he explained.
Mikdashi, who graduated in graphic design from the American University of Beirut, received a certificate of appreciation from Libanpost, along with an unspecified “symbolic” financial award for designing the stamp.
Qordahi said that he planned to reactivate the ministry’s postal stamp committee.
“Stamps are a marketing tool for the country’s products,” he said, expressing his intentions to prepare a project plan to revive the body.

 

Court sentences collaborators …(10)

The Military Tribunal issued sentences Thursday against collaborators with “Israel”.
The court’s sentences included jail terms of three years, while others were subject to the statute of limitations.
The court also issued four sentences, in absentia, of life with hard labor against Ibrahim Abdel-Aaal, Mohammed Salah Atieh Awad, and Ihsan Hussein Hassan for detonating explosives in the Nahr al-Bared refugee camp and dealing in military weapons.
Suspects Shawqi Suleiman Hajj and Fakhry Muhyi were both acquitted for lack of evidence.
The court also issued 44 sentences for army desertion, impersonating a security official, illegal possession of military weapons, threats, battery and robbery.
Sentences for these charges varied between one-year jail terms and fines ranging from LL200,000 to LL400,000.

 

Dedicated to Lebanon on Liberation Day, Palestinian Martyrdom Operation Injures 60 “Israelis”

Palestinian mujahedeen resumed self-sacrifice operations against the Zionist Entity. The Islamic Jihad movement issued a statement saying the two martyrs Ossama Darwiche Abol Haija and Alaa Hilal Abdul-Sattar Sabbah blew themselves up along with their car in the Khdeira town in northern Tel Aviv, this afternoon injuring about 60 “Israelis”. The explosion occurred near the bus station in the northern town of Khdeira. One report from the scene said a booby-trapped car crashed into a bus setting off the explosion. The Islamic Jihad dedicated the operation to the Lebanese people and to Hizbullah's resistance and leadership in the first anniversary of liberation. Earlier, the self-sacrifice martyr Hussein Abu Nasr from the Islamic Hamas movement blew himself up in an attack on an “Israeli” enemy position in the central Gaza Strip Friday. Hamas’s armed wing, the Ezzedine al-Qassam Brigades, said in a statement that “this operation is a response to “Israeli” attacks.” The martyr Abu Nasr was driving a bomb-laden truck which exploded near an “Israeli” occupation position. Occupation forces said the attack caused no injuries among its soldiers. A military spokesman said the bomb-laden vehicle was in a line of cars waiting to be allowed to pass the military position at Netzarim, when suddenly the driver accelerated towards the post, and the vehicle blew up. The spokesman also said that a roadside bomb had been found on a road leading to Netzarim, close to the al-Mintar crossing point. The spiritual head of Hamas Sheikh Ahmad Yassin warned that operations would continue against the “Israelis”. “If the “Israeli” army thinks its positions are impregnable it is mistaken, because we have candidates for martyrdom who will know how to hit the “Israelis” wherever they are,” Sheikh Ahmad Yassin added that “The martyr Abu Nasr wanted to tell the “Israelis” that every aggression demands a riposte.” “Israeli” tanks later entered two areas of autonomous Palestinian territory, including one near Netzarim. Palestinian police general Saeb el Ajeez said that “Israeli” tanks opened fire and advanced some 500 metres into Palestinian-controlled territory, in violation of autonomy accords. Five Palestinian policemen were injured, one of whom was in a moderate to serious condition. Three tanks and a bulldozer later mounted another incursion into an area in the eastern part of Gaza City.
