Fadlallah: “Israel” is too weak to strike…(1)

Senior cleric Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah said Monday that “Israel” would not wage war against Lebanon and Syria because it was facing security problems.
““Israel” won’t venture into a war in Lebanon against the Lebanese or the Syrian Army or the region, since it is at the peak of the security crisis created by the Palestinian intifada,” Fadlallah said during a lecture at Rashid Karami Fairgrounds in Tripoli.
On hand were Public Works and Transport Minister Najib Mikati, Minyeh MP Saleh Kheir, North Lebanon Governor Nassif Qaloush, Tawhid Movement head Bilal Shaaban and several scholars.

 

Marjayoun rocked by early morning bomb…(2) 
Eighth attack in the South targeted shop owned by collaborators tax collector

A bomb exploded in Marjayoun early Monday morning, causing no injuries but extensive damage to the shop of a suspected “Israeli” collaborator.
The shop, which sold bathroom products, is owned by Maroun Abu Rizk and his sons.
However, Abu Rizk’s sons, Camille and Youssef, insist that their father “has nothing to do with the shop.”
The pair say they have owned and operated it by themselves for over a decade.
Abu Rizk, the brother of former General Labor Confederation president Elias Abu Rizk, was in charge of levying taxes for the civil administration body of the “Israeli”-proxy South Lebanon Army militia during the occupation.
However, he has not been charged with collaboration because a Beirut court ruled that at 72, he is too old to stand trial.
Security sources who arrived on the scene said the bomb, which was placed at the shop entrance, contained one kilogram of TNT and was “extremely explosive.” The resulting blast created a 7-centimeter hole in the ground, in addition to damaging several neighboring shops.
A patrol from UNIFIL’s Indian battalion also inspected the scene and prepared a report in collaboration with members of the Internal Security Forces.
The explosion came following the distribution of a series of threatening flyers, warning collaborators to leave the region.
Despite the other attacks, Monday’s explosion is the first time a shop had been targeted in Marjayoun. Booby-traps were previously restricted to cars in the towns of Houla, Aitaroun, and Kfar Kila.
Nabatieh Public Prosecutor Ghassan Rabah arrived at the shop at noon to say he “was following up the issue,” adding that the case could be referred to the military court if the motives are determined to be political.
Another attack took place on Sunday, when unknown assailants burned the car of Ahmad Jahjah, near his home in the border village of Kfar Kila.
Jahjah, who was an employee of “Israel’s” civil administration during the occupation, fled following last May’s withdrawal, but later returned and handed himself over to authorities here.
He was subsequently released after serving a six-month jail term.

 

Palestinian baby killed in shelling of Gaza Strip camp…(3)

A 4-month-old Palestinian girl died yesterday when “Israeli” shellfire landed in the yard of her home. She was the youngest person killed in the current Mideast bloodletting.

Iman Hijo was killed, and her 19-year-old mother, her grandmother, and three more children belonging to the extended family were all seriously injured by shrapnel when a shell crashed in the small backyard of the family's cinder-block house in the Khan Yunis refugee camp in the Gaza Strip, a relative said.

At Nasser Hospital, where surgeons operated on several members of the Hijo family, relatives dressed in black sat on the floor outside the operating theater, weeping and wailing.

Palestinian doctors said shrapnel from the shell went through her abdomen and out her back.

Iman was killed instantly when a shell landed in the yard where family members were sitting, according to her uncle Wael Hijo.

In the hospital emergency room, the baby's 7-year-old aunt, Dunya, sat on a bed with a dazed look on her face, her frilly white-and-green dress pulled up above cut and bandaged legs.

"They killed the baby," Dunya said, then burst into tears.

Another family member, 18-month-old Mahmoud Hijo, was in intensive care with shrapnel wounds, doctors said.

Overall, 24 Palestinians were hurt at the camp, including 10 children, according to Palestinian doctors. Also, a Palestinian police officer was killed in a clash in the West Bank, the Palestinians said, after “Israeli” troops briefly entered three Palestinian-controlled towns in pursuit of gunmen.

 

Two Days of “Israeli” Aggressions Leave 3 Palestinian Martyrs, Including 4-Months-Old Iman, 65 Injured…(4)

Four months-old Iman Hajjour is the latest victim of Zionist barbarism and atrocity against the Palestinian people. The baby-girl intifadah martyr was killed, and her mother, 19-year old sister, 14-year-old brother were all wounded by “Israeli” enemy tank shells that hit the Palestinian refugee camp of Khan Younis in the Gaza Strip. At least 22 other Palestinian civilians were injured in the aggression. The “Israeli” occupation army shelling damaged a school, two apartment buildings and a number of houses. “Israeli” tanks also shelled a Palestinian military intelligence building in the southern Gaza Strip town of Deir al-Ballah. Earlier, “Israeli” enemy tanks launched an incursion into Palestinian-controlled territory in the West Bank for the second time in 24 hours, triggering renewed fighting between the two sides, and Palestinian mujahedeen responded by targeting the Settlements of Neve Dekalim and Atzona with mortar shells. A 45-year-old Palestinian, Hussein Abu Taman, was killed and 19 others were injured during the heavy “Israeli” machine gun and tank fire on the West Bank town of Tulkorm, which the “Israeli” occupation troops partly invaded before being rebuffed by Palestinian security forces. Several buildings, including an apartment block, a college, and a school, were damaged in the barrage. The raid followed a green light from “Israeli” war minister Binyamin Ben-Eliezer for the occupation army to enter Palestinian-ruled territories when it sees fit in fighting the seven-month old intifadah. In the West Bank town of Beit Laham, thousands of Palestinians, including Hamas members, took part in the funeral procession for a Fatah activist killed by Zionist troops, a day earlier, when it entered the Palestinian-ruled village of Beit Jallah for about 8 hours. 20 Palestinians, including 4 children were wounded in the aggression. The Palestinian Authority outraged by the killing of defenseless civilians said Palestinian territories is witnessing a serious “Israeli” military escalation against the Palestinian people. The secretary of the Palestinian presidency, Tayeb Abdel Rahim, called for the immediate deployment of an international protection force following the “Israeli” bombardments that killed a four-month-old girl in the Gaza Strip. Abdel Rahim said, “After this butchery, we call on the international community to provide immediate international protection to the Palestinian people”. Abdel Rahim charged saying “this aggression comes after the “Israeli” government gave the green light to the army to act without restraint”.

 

“Israeli” settlement guard murdered a day after Palestinian baby killed…(5)

An “Israeli” was killed on Tuesday at a West Bank settlement in an attack security forces blamed on Palestinians. On the same day, the Palestinian Authority accused “Israel” of escalating the conflict with the killing of a Palestinian baby.

The “Israeli” was shot several times and stabbed as he guarded orchards at the Itamar settlement where he lived, police said. Discarded rifle cartridges were found on the ground near the body, they told “Israel” radios. Security forces sealed off the area and conducted searches for the attackers, whose footprints lead to a nearby Palestinian village.

The murder came the day after a 4-month-old Palestinian baby girl was killed when “Israeli” shells landed in the yard of her home in the Khan Yunis refugee camp in the Gaza Strip. The “Israeli” army said the strike came in response to mortar shelling from the area on Jewish settlements.

The Palestinian baby, Iman Hijo, was killed when a piece of shrapnel hit her in the back. She became the youngest person killed in the seven months of fighting between “Israel” and the Palestinians. Hijo's mother, grandmother and three more children belonging to the extended family were all seriously injured by shrapnel when a shell crashed near the cinderblock home, a relative said. Women relatives of the baby wept, wailed and cursed “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon as they waited outside the operating room at the Nasser Hospital where surgeons operated on several members of the Hijo family.

Overall, 24 Palestinians were hurt, including 10 children, at the Gaza camp, according to Palestinian doctors. The Palestinian Authority called on the international community to help stop "this military escalation by Sharon and his bloody handed soldiers," according to a statement released by the official Palestinian news service, WAFA.

Most of the shells targeted a market area that was only a few yards from the Hijo family home and from which “Israel” said the Palestinians had fired. Iman Hijo was the youngest person killed by fire in the fighting, which erupted in September and has claimed 436 lives on the Palestinian side, and 73 people on the “Israeli” side.

 

“Israeli” Occupation to Build New Settlements in Palestinian Territories…(6)

“Israeli” enemy Premier Ariel Sharon gave orders to build five new settlement blocs ready to contain 20,000 settlers in the northwest of Negev desert. The “Israeli” government said that the creation of these new towns in the arid Halutza sector, near the Gaza Strip and the “Israeli”-Egyptian border, was aimed at stopping any future government from exchanging land with the Palestinian Authority as envisaged by previous administration of Ehud Barak. Moreover, the “Israeli” municipality in occupied Qods (Al-Quds) is also planning to build a new Jewish quarter consisting of 300 homes in the Arab eastern part of the city. Senior Palestinian official Khalil Toufadji said Monday the homes would be built on land confiscated by enemy Sharon in 1990 near the Sheikh Jarrah neighborhood north of the old city. He added that the work has already started on the infrastructure for a police station on the site. The official warned that this will handicap any chance of finding a solution to the problems of Quds. The “Israeli” enemy occupied and annexed the eastern part of Quds in June 1967, and considers the whole of the city its "eternal and united capital."
