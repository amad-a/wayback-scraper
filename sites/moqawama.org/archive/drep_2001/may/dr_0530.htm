Hizbullah vows to soon free Shibaa Farms "under fire"…(1)

“ The armed Lebanese movement Hizbullah vowed Tuesday to soon force the end of “Israel’s” occupation of the border Shibaa Farms "under fire."

"The resistance insists that the “Israeli” withdrawal from the Shibaa Farms take place under fire," said Sheikh Nabil Kawook, Hizbullah's top official in southern Lebanon.

"Hizbullah's fighters will plant their flags on the hills of Shibaa Farms, and this time is not far away," he said during a ceremony to lay the cornerstone of a cultural center in this southern village.

On Monday, Nawaf Moussawi, a member of Hizbullah's political leadership, warned that 2.5 million “Israelis” are in range of the group's Katyusha rockets.

Moussawi also said Hizbullah "considers itself a partner to the Palestinians and not simply a supporter in the intifada," the eight-month Palestinian uprising against “Israeli” occupation of the West Bank and Gaza Strip.

During “Israel’s” occupation of south Lebanon, which ended last May, Hizbullah frequently pounded northern “Israel” with Katyusha rockets.

Since October, Hizbullah has launched attacks on the Shibaa Farms, capturing three “Israeli” soldiers and killing three more.

 

Lahoud: 'Our Troops Won't be at Nose Distance From “Israeli” Army'…(2)

President Lahoud has vowed to refrain from deploying the Lebanese army at a 'nose's distance' from “Israeli” troops on the southern border until a comprehensive Middle East peace is concluded by Lebanon, Syria and the Jewish state.
"There is a state of war between Lebanon and “Israel”. Therefore it is impossible from a military standpoint to put the Lebanese army at a nose's distance from the “Israeli” army," Lahoud told reporters in Paris after his talks with French President Jacques Chirac on Monday.
Lahoud said units of the Lebanese army were stationed in south Lebanon at a 15-minute from the border with “Israel” and this has ensured full security in the region. "No one has been killed there since the “Israeli” withdrawal," he said.
Lahoud also asserted that the Shibaa farms belonged to Lebanon.
"The international law stipulates that the border between two countries is defined by these two countries, not the United Nations or any other third party," Lahoud said.
 

“Israeli” planes overfly southern Lebanon…(3)

“ “Israeli” warplanes were seen overflying southern Lebanon's border zone with “Israel” Tuesday but stopped short of probing deep into Lebanese airspace.

Fighters flew over the Arkub sector in the east and over the villages of Shibaa and Kfarshouba, next to the disputed Shibaa Farms area, occupied by “Israel” but claimed by Lebanon.

The “Israeli” army also fired off several machine gun bursts to the Lebanese side of the "blue line" drawn in the sector by the UN to act as a border between Lebanon and “Israel”, although no one was hurt.

“Israeli” planes violated Lebanese airspace Sunday for the 10th straight day, breaking the sound barrier over the Lebanese capital, and flying over the Bekaa valley, where Syrian anti-aircraft defences are based.

Staffan de Mistura, representative of the UN secretary general in southern Lebanon, described the flights as "violations of the blue line" and said they would likely "increase tension in the region."

 

UN says vehicle came under “Israeli” fire in broder zone…(4)

“ A UN vehicle patrolling in the Shibaa farms area between “Israel”, Syria and Lebanon, came under “Israeli” fire "several days ago," the UN announced Tuesday.

The United Nations Disengagement Observer Force (UNDOF) added in a statement read over the telephone that there were no casualties and that an inquiry into the incident had been launched.

"UNDOF can confirm that a UN vehicle was shot at by the “Israeli” Army while they were conducting a routine patrol in accordance with the 1974 ceasefire agreement," the statement said.

"UNDOF would like to emphasize that there were no casualties and the matter is currently under investigation," it continued, adding "the incident happened several days ago, in the general area of the Shibaa Farms."

UNDOF has been deployed since 1974 on the Golan Heights, which have been occupied by “Israel” since 1967.

The Lebanese daily As-Safir reported Tuesday that the incident happened last week, and that a wheel of the UN vehicle was damaged by “Israeli” automatic fire.

 

Three “Israeli” killed by Palestinian fighters…(5)
” Three “Israelis” were shot dead and four others wounded in two separate attacks in the West Bank yesterday. In addition, two soldiers suffered light to moderate wounds when two Palestinian suicide (martyrdom) bombers detonated explosives near an army post in the southern Gaza Strip.
Gilad Zar, 40, the security officer of the Samaria Regional Council, was killed as he travelled between Yitzhar and Kedumim early yesterday morning.
Sarah Blaustein, 53, of Efrat, a new immigrant from New York, and Esther Alvan, 20, were killed, in an attack on the Al-Quds-Gush Etzion (tunnel) road, just north of Neveh Daniel. The two, along with five others, were headed for Al-Quds to attend Zar's funeral.
Alvan died hours later from the severe head wounds she sustained. Blaustein's husband Norman, 52, and her son Samuel Berg, 28, who was visiting from the US, were wounded, as was Yisrael Deri, 20, also from Efrat. Ada Moran, 39, suffered severe anxiety. The other passenger in the car was unscathed. The wounded were taken to Al-Quds's Hadassah-University Hospital, Ein Kerem, and Shaare Zedek Hospital.
Zar, 40, father of eight children aged three to 15, lived with his wife Hagar in Itamar and was the son of Moshe Zar, one of the first pioneers in Samaria. Shortly after 7, he was driving near the Gitt junction, between Kedumim and Yitzhar, when fighters in a parked car shot at his windshield, causing his car to overturn. Two fighters then got out, walked to Zar's car, and shot him in the head at point-blank range. Zar was buried in Karnei Shomron last night.
Security officials did not rule out the possibility that Zar was specifically targeted, but noted that it is possible that the fighters planned to shoot at an “Israeli” vehicle and Zar's just came along.
After the attack, the “Israeli” Army tightened security on villages in the Nablus area and a blockade was reimposed on the city. OC Judea and Samaria Brig.-Gen. Benny Ganz told reporters that despite the restrictions imposed on the area, it could not totally be sealed off.
"No matter how painful such incidents are, we must breathe and count to 10 and decide what to do," he said.
Yesterday afternoon, Blaustein, her husband, son, and the others headed for Al-Quds to attend Zar's funeral when Palestinians in a passing car sprayed their vehicle with gunfire before fleeing toward Bethlehem. The Blaustein's made aliya from New York last August and lived on the same Efrat street as her brother, David Unterberg, and his wife Cheryl. Their daughter Atara, 14, was not in the car. Berg is Blaustein's son from a previous marriage.
 

Anger as minister approves plan for 500 homes on Palestinian land…(6)

“Israel’s” attempts to convince the world that it has embraced the Mitchell Commission's blueprint for peace were severely undermined yesterday when a senior cabinet minister confirmed that he had approved plans for hundreds of new settler homes in the occupied West Bank.

The announcement, by Natan Sharansky, the Housing minister, came during a violent day in which two Jewish settlers were killed by Palestinian guerrillas on the West Bank, two Palestinians were shot dead by “Israeli” troops, and a Palestinian suicide martyrdom bomber blew himself up at an “Israeli” checkpoint in southern Gaza, injuring two “Israeli” soldiers.

“Israel” insists that it has accepted the Mitchell findings, containing a plan for restoring calm, starting with an unconditional ceasefire. This is despite the fact that Ariel Sharon, the “Israeli” Prime Minister, rejects a key Mitchell recommendation considered critical by the report's authors and unnegotiable by the Palestinian leadership: a complete freeze on all settlement construction in the occupied territories.

This position was confirmed yesterday when Mr Sharansky told an “Israeli” radio station that he had approved construction bids for 496 new homes within Ma'ale Adumim, near Al-Quds, the largest illegal “Israeli” settlement in the occupied territories, where home sales have plummeted since the start of the intifada. He also approved bids for 217 homes in Alfei Menashe, near the West Bank Palestinian city of Qalqilya. Both projects were set in motion by the previous Barak government.

Under an agreement with his coalition partners, Mr Sharon is insisting on continuing so-called "natural growth" construction inside settlements, despite thousands of empty homes on the West Bank, and “Israel’s” record of using this as a cover for occupying more land. He also refuses to rule out the building of by-pass roads ­ an issue which his spokesman, Ra'anan Gissin, said was "not related to settlements but to security".

Exploratory talks by William Burns, the US's Middle East envoy, seem doomed, despite the resumption of security talks between both sides last night. Mr Burns's task has not been made any easier by his own Secretary of State, Colin Powell, who has angered the Palestinians by implying that the Mitchell Commission's call for a total settlement freeze is open to negotiation.

 

A Palestinian culture of resistance is re-born…(7)

The Al-Quds Post’s website reported that “IAF (“Israeli” Air Force) F-16 warplanes may have dropped munitions as large as 250 kilograms on their targets” (May 18, 2001). Among these targets were a ministry building, police stations, a TV station and a prison, all in civilian neighborhoods in several Palestinian cities under “Israeli” military occupation for the past 34 years. The warped justification for “Israel’s” latest war crime is that it is a response to yet another Palestinian suicide martyrdom bomber who hours earlier took the lives of seven “Israeli” citizens in a shopping center in the “Israeli” city of Netanya.
The thundering roar of a US-made “Israeli” F-16 was still in the air when I jumped from my computer to our front porch to identify where the latest bombs fell. My wife rushed to check on our two daughters, Areen, 7, and Nadine, 1, who were both luckily napping at the time. My family was not in any immediate danger, the targets were about one kilometer away. However, what I did find on the street in front of our house was revealing.
Not knowing if the single blast was the end of this latest “Israeli” terror raid, six neighborhood children who were playing ball in the street on this quiet Friday, started running toward their homes. These were kids from about six to 12 years old. As they ran, one started to chant: “We shall sacrifice for the sake for Palestine,” and the others replied in chorus as the rumbling sound of the “Israeli” warplane faded into the horizon.
In the minutes to follow, we made and received no less than a dozen phone calls. The frantic routine of rushing to check if any friends or family members were killed or injured, in this now daily event, has returned Palestinian society to a dynamic which, since the Oslo peace accords, has slowly deteriorated: a sense of national community and a culture of resistance to “Israeli” occupation.
For this, we owe “Israel”, and “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon specifically, a thank you. Since the Oslo peace accords, nearly 10 years ago, Palestinian society was rushing to assimilate with the global trends of free nations: individualism, focusing on trade and business, redefining civil society from a resistance culture to a state-building culture, creating national institutions. At one point, one could even say that many Palestinians, and even more “Israelis”, were feeling that “Israeli” occupation had ended. Many were convinced that both sides, “Israelis” and Palestinians, had proceeded past the point-of-no-return and that time was the only factor before full peace, stability and security blessed both peoples.
Then, thanks to hawkish war criminal Sharon, things changed overnight.
As Sharon provoked Palestinians with his infamous visit to Al-Haram al-Sharif in the “Israeli”-occupied Old City of East Al-Quds, Palestinians started reverting to that which had characterized Palestinian society ever since the first intifada in 1987. A culture of resistance to “Israeli” occupation is taking root, once again. But this time the culture is multi-faceted.
There are those Palestinians who have brushed shoulders with the racist “Israeli” occupation in its rawest form ­ poverty, displacement, imprisonment, torture, land confiscation, deportations, closures, unemployment, political assassinations, the list goes on. Many of these defiant souls can be found at today’s flashpoints using any meager tools available to them to resist occupation. Others, sadly, have equated life under “Israeli” occupation to death and have chosen to take the lives of innocent “Israelis” as well as their own. This is tragic, but reality. Yet other Palestinians, despite “Israel’s” continued terror bombing campaign, are engaged in building the emerging State of Palestine and refuse to stop building the foundations of a viable, independent and democratic country.
Instead of a normal life, “Israel” has now successfully exposed Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza to the same military might that our brethren in Lebanon have become so accustomed to. Instead of youngsters memorizing sport and music celebrities, Palestinian children can now tell if gunfire is from a 5.56mm or 7.62mm machine gun or if an air raid was from an F-16 warplane or helicopter gunship. For this lesson, Palestinian parents will never forgive “Israel”.
Sharon and the “Israeli” government are to be thanked for revealing the real “Israel” to the world. An “Israel” that is not only not ready for peace, but also accepts returning to a struggle of existence, instead of pursuing an era of co-existence.
The world is witnessing “Israel” blunder in an attempt to politically dodge multiple international commissions that are condemning this most recent state terror campaign. Orchestrated by “Israeli” Foreign Minister Shimon Peres, “Israel” attempts to solve the conflict by inventing new political jargon and by engaging well-designed public relations blitzes instead of facing its core international obligation of ending occupation. The truth is becoming harder to hide with every passing “Israeli” warplane. The world has spoken ­ “Israeli” occupation is the source of contention and must end, illegal “Israeli” settlements must end, imprisonment of Palestinian political prisoners must end. There is no other way.
Sharon has returned Palestinian society to a culture of resistance. Soon, he will move the international community to a new culture of responsibility toward protecting Palestinian civilians and realizing a negotiated solution to the conflict based on peace with justice. In the meantime, a new generation of Palestinians will learn and live the meaning of intifada while the state of Palestine continues to be built amongst the backdrop of “Israeli” bombings.

Sam Bahour is a Palestinian-American businessman living in the besieged Palestinian city of al-Bireh in the West Bank

 

B'Tselem, Amnesty slam “Israel”…(8)
” “Israel’s” policies in the intifada received a tongue-lashing yesterday from two human rights watchdog groups, Amnesty International and B'Tselem.
According to Amnesty's annual report for 2000 which outlines human rights violations in 149 countries, more than 300 Palestinians have been killed, most of them unlawfully, since the October uprising began.
The intifada began, according to the report, when “Israeli” Police "used excessive force" on September 29 at the Aksa Mosque where five Palestinians were killed and 200 injured during rioting. The incident "sparked daily demonstrations and riots in “Israel” and the Occupied Territories," the report, which was issued this morning, continued.
The majority of the 300 Palestinians killed and 10,000 injured since October were demonstrators throwing stones, the report states, adding that “Israel” security forces "used excessive lethal force, firing rubber-coated metal bullets and live ammunition including high-velocity bullets at demonstrators." Some Palestinians were deliberately targeted and extrajudicially executed, the report claims.
The report also targeted the nature of “Israeli” arrests of Palestinians on intifada-related charges, claiming that some of the 2,000 arrested "reported that they were beaten or kicked immediately after arrest."
The “Israeli” human rights organization B'Tselem, in a report released yesterday called 'Beatings and Abuse of Palestinians by “Israeli” Security Forces during the Aksa Intifada,' cites 12 Palestinian testimonies that describe the beatings and abuse of 24 Palestinians that took place in recent months throughout the West Bank.
