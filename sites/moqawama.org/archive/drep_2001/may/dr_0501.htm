Experts defuse “Israel’s” deadly legacy…(1)

Explosives experts from the Internal Security Forces and UNIFIL personnel detonated dozens of unexploded shells and grenades in a field in Mar Elias, Marjayoun on Monday.
The experts inspected the field and gathered the ordnance amid tight security measures.
Residents were prevented from approaching the area.
The various types of shells bore Hebrew inscriptions. During the occupation, the area was used by “Israel’s” South Lebanon Army members to test “Israeli” munitions.
Yacoub Nabaa, a local resident, was killed on Sunday when an unexploded “Israeli” shell he was trying to carry exploded.

 

7 Palestinians Killed in Palestine…(2)

“ Explosions, one near Yasser Arafat's headquarters, killed six Palestinians in the Gaza Strip and the West Bank on Monday. A seventh Palestinian died in a clash with “Israeli” soldiers.

The deadly blasts, whose victims included two children, were followed by two shooting attacks on West Bank roads Tuesday. One killed an “Israeli” man whose van overturned after it was fired upon and the other slightly injured an “Israeli”.

In the West Bank town of Ramallah, a huge blast leveled a two-story building not far from the headquarters of the Palestinian leader, Arafat, killing at least three Palestinians and injuring six others. Doctors had previously said a Palestinian seriously injured in the blast had been killed. Arafat was in his building at the time of the explosion.

Palestinians blamed “Israel” for the blast. One of the dead was Hassan el-Qady, whom “Israel” suspected of shooting dead a 16-year-old “Israeli” boy who had been lured to the Ramallah area by a 26-year-old Palestinian woman in an exchange of e-mail messages.

Palestinians said an 8-year-old boy and his 6-year-old sister were killed. The mother of the two children and her 5-year-old daughter were injured but were in stable condition, a hospital official said.

The governor of Ramallah, Mustafa Eissa, said a bomb was placed under the stairs, and he believed the “Israelis” were responsible. Hussein al-Sheikh, a leader of Arafat's Fatah movement, charged, ''This is one of the continuous “Israeli” policy of assassinating Palestinian leaders.''

In Gaza City, an explosion ripped through a building, killing two Palestinians and injuring five others. A neighbor said the building belonged to an activist from the militant Hamas.

The “Israeli” military spokesman's office said the military had nothing to do with the explosions and had no information about them.

Also Monday, a bomb exploded inside a Jewish settlement in the Gaza Strip, killing one Palestinian worker and wounding another, the “Israeli” army said.

The bomb attack in Gaza apparently targeted a Jewish man and his daughter, who were in a car at a greenhouse on the Rafiah Yam settlement in the southern Gaza Strip, said Yehoshua Mor-Yosef, a spokesman for settlers, but the blast killed the Palestinian man and injured a second.

Also, a Palestinian wanted by “Israel” was shot and killed overnight by “Israeli” troops who tried to arrest him near the West Bank city of Qalqilya, “Israel” radio reported. The Palestinian, Adnan Odeh, was shot as he tried to flee the soldiers, the radio said.

In the southern Gaza Strip Palestinians fired two mortar rounds at the Jewish settlement of Gadid from the Palestinian city of Khan Yunis, the “Israeli” army spokesman said. “Israeli” tanks responded with shell fire. Five Palestinians suffered moderate injuries from the shelling, a Palestinian police spokesman said.

Since the fighting began last September, 428 people have been killed on the Palestinian side and 72 on the “Israeli” side.

 

PA blames “Israel” for Ramallah blast

The Palestinian Authority is holding “Israel” to blame for the blast last night in Ramallah that killed three people, including a brother and sister, aged 8 and 6.
"We are talking about an unprecedented war against the Palestinians on the part of “Israel”," PA cabinet member Nabil Amar said this morning.
"“Israel” has an interest in killing members of Palestinian organizations," Amin el-Hindi, head of PA General Security Service said in an interview with Palestinian radio.
Palestinians wanting to organize and take part in attacks against “Israel” must first receive permission of the PA, and operate according to general guidelines laid out by PA Chairman Yasser Arafat, el Hindi said, according to an “Israel” Radio report.
Palestinians operating within the framework of the PA's security apparatus were forbidden from taking part in "popular committee" militias, el Hindi said.

 

Jewish settler killed after blast near Arafat HQ…(4)

Assaf Hershkovitz , 31, was murdered in an ambush earlier this morning after his vehicle was fired upon and overturned shortly before 06:30 at the "t" - junction between Ofra and Beit El on bypass road number 60.
The man, married and father of two is the son of Arieh Hershkovitz, 55, who died in a hail of bullets fired from a passing vehicle as he drove on the highway between the Rama junction and Adam (Geva Binyamin) on January 29th.
The “Israeli” Army has imposed a closure on the nearby village of Bittin and army forces are combing the area searching for the assailants.
Two Palestinians carried out the lethal attack and fled into Palestinian-held territory, according to a report on the Yediot Ahronot Hebrew daily website.
An abandoned car, believed used in the attack, was found in the vicinity.
Another “Israeli” motorist, from Beit Shean, was lightly wounded after Palestinians opened fire on his car in a drive-by shooting earlier this morning.
The attack took place adjacent to the northern Samarian community of Alon Moreh near Nablus, “Israel” Radio reports.
The man was wounded in both legs in the shooting.

Proposed ceasefire under attack as intifada leaders reject call for truce…(5)

PALESTINIAN officials leading the uprising against “Israel” have vowed to sabotage a proposed ceasefire and resumption of the peace talks.

The rejection of the initiative proposed by Egypt and Jordan has come even from members of the Palestinian leader Yasser Arafat's Fatah faction. They include Marwan Barghouti, head of Fatah in the West Bank. Fatah is the dominant group within the Palestine Liberation Organisation.

"The only ceasefire agreement that the Palestinians will accept is one that leads to an end to the (“Israeli”) occupation within the shortest period of time," he said. Mr Barghouti, a strong supporter of the intifada, said the uprising would continue until “Israeli” troops withdrew from the West Bank and Gaza Strip. "Intifada should not be stopped until “Israel” flees as it did from Lebanon, within a few hours," he said, referring to “Israel’s” withdrawal from southern Lebanon last May, ending two decades of occupation.

The proposed ceasefire was made public after a meeting on Sunday in Cairo between Shimon Peres, the “Israeli” Foreign Minister, and Egyptian officials.
