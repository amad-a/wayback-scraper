Tens of Thousands of Lebanese answered Hizbullah's Call to Demonstrate for the Palestinians…(1)

Tens of  thousands of  Lebanese took to the streets late Friday to commemorate the "Land Day" in  compliance with Hizbullah Secretary- General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah`s  summon to express support for the  Palestinian people.

The marches were launched after the Ashoura  reminiscence  gatherings from different regions.

 The crowded masses chanted  anti-”Israeli” slogans and promised to  support the Palestinians against  “Israeli” aggressions.

Earlier thousands of Palestinians  in various refugee camps in Lebanon demonstrated against the “Israeli” Occupation and the US support for “Israel”.

 

Aridi demands more help for South…(2)

Information Minister Ghazi Aridi urged the government on Saturday to give more attention to the South’s border region.
“The state has failed to launch enough development projects in the south to fill the vacuum left behind by the “Israeli” occupation,” Aridi said in a statement after visiting Khiam prison for the first time since the liberation in May.
He called for more public works projects to be implemented in the south to boost the economy once again.
He added that “Tel Aviv used to hide, from the “Israeli” public, the truth about what went on in Khiam and everywhere else in the south. But when the Lebanese resistance forces started to launch attacks against the occupation forces, the resistance’s media began to reveal facts about everything that went on in the South

 

“Israeli”-allied militia spy chief sentenced to death…(3)

The former head of the militia’s intelligence wing in Bint Jbeil has been sentenced to death by the Military Tribunal for working against the resistance.
Kamal Fayyad, who fled Lebanon when “Israel” ended its occupation of the south last May, was convicted in absentia of “weaving intrigues” for the ““Israeli” enemy and its agents” and providing them with information about the resistance.
Fayyad, who has moved to the US state of Michigan, was reportedly badly beaten in January by Lebanese seeking revenge for his activities in the South.
In sentences handed down late Friday and posted on a noticeboard Saturday, the court meted out prison terms ranging from six months to 15 years to 23 collaborators.
The court also imposed fines of LL400,000 on most of the defendants. A woman who visited “Israel” received a sentence of one month in prison and a fine of LL1 million.
The court, whose verdicts cannot be appealed, has sentenced more than 2,300 people to jail terms of up to 25 years since “Israel’s” withdrawal from Lebanon. Hundreds more are awaiting trial.
At least 18 collaborators have been sentenced to death in absentia for killing resistance fighters.

 

6,000 Palestinians rally at refugee camp to support uprising against “Israel”…(4)

“ Some 6,000 people gathered at a Palestinian refugee camp here Sunday to call for support of the Palestinian intifada, or uprising, against “Israel” and an end to sanctions against Iraq.

About 100 armed guerrillas staged a military parade during the rally, organized by Palestinian leader Yasser Arafat's Fatah faction.

In a rare event at a Palestinian refugee camp, the demonstrators held up portraits of Arafat next to those of Syrian President Bashar al-Assad and his Lebanese and Iraqi counterparts, Emile Lahoud and Saddam Hussein.

On Friday, Palestinian refugees at the Ain al-Helweh refugee camp, near the southern port city of Sidon, for the first time held together portraits of Arafat and Assad, who put an end to decades of strained relations with a historic meeting Tuesday in Amman on the sidelines of an Arab summit.

During the rally at the Rashidiyeh camp on the outskirts of the southern city of Tyre, Sultan Abul Aynain, the head of the Fatah movement in Lebanon and Arafat's personal representative, called on Arab leaders to "ask the United States to abolish all sanctions imposed on Iraq."

He also called for "coordination between the Syrian, Iraqi and Lebanese armies to help the Palestinians who are fighting against the “Israeli” occupation."

Abul Aynain has been holed up in the camp since he was sentenced to death in absentia in October 1999 for "setting up an armed gang and damaging the Lebanese state and its authority."

The rally was attended by representatives of a number of Lebanese parties, including Amal movement, the Progressive Socialist Party of Druze leader Walid Jumblatt and the Nasserite Popular Organisation.

 

A Palestinian Martyr, 35 Injured in More “Israeli” Occupation Aggressions…(5)

54-year-old Wahid Nasri al-Deek died from injuries he sustained when the “Israeli” occupation army gunships attacked Palestinian Chairman Yasser Arafat's personal guard force 17 in Ramallah Wednesday.

In the West Bank town of Nablus more than 40 thousand people participated in the funerals of six Palestinian martyrs, victims of  Friday's “Israeli” aggressions against the Palestinians who marked Land Day by hurling stones at the occupier.

Carrying the blessed bodies of the martyrs in the West Bank, the crowd voiced threats to “Israeli” PM Ariel Sharon. Palestinian fighters fired shots in the air and others waved the Palestinian and Iraqi flags, as well as that of Hizbullah, the symbol of resistance.

In the Gaza Strip, two little girls aged two and a half and seven, and an emergency medical worker were hit with “Israeli” bullets during an exchange of fire between occupation soldiers and Palestinian intifadah fighters in the Brazil refugee camp, near the border with Egypt.

Four Palestinians were injured when the enemy troops fired on Palestinian stone throwers at the Mintar crossing in the Gaza Strip. Medical sources said three other Palestinians were injured during confrontations between Palestinians and an enemy post in the Jewish settlement of Netzarim, south of Gaza Strip.

The southern West Bank town of al-Khalil(Hebron) came under heavy “Israeli” army artillery and machine-gunfire overnight, in what witnesses described as the worst shelling in six months of “Israeli” aggressions.

28 Palestinians were injured and dozens of houses were damaged in the attack which lasted almost 12 hours.

Meanwhile, high-ranking Palestinian officials said the national security forces conducted training and maneuvers to prepare for a potential “Israeli” assault. Ahmed Helis, secretary general of Fatah movement said, " Every point in “Israel” will become a legitimate target for our fighters if “Israel” attacks our cities."

“Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon promised to restore security in “Israel”, saying in a statement he will continue to strike those who "attack us and those who send them".

The Palestinian Authority denounced the enemy's crackdown on Land Day's demonstrators as a barbaric crime that shames humanity and demanded  international action.

In a statement, the  Authority accused the Sharon  government of "putting into  application its 100-day plan  seeking to bring the Palestinian people  to their knees by force of arms."  It said that “Israeli” soldiers "fired live  rounds targeting the head and the  chest with the deliberate goal of  killing, and this when they're not  under threat." The Palestinian Authority  said another 226 Palestinians were  wounded in protests.

The  Palestinians also criticized the United  States for its veto of a UN resolution to  authorize international  observers in the  occupied territories, saying the  move "encouraged the Sharon  government to continue this plan  and its war against the Palestinians in  violation of the Geneva convention"  on the rules of war.

 

“Israel” arrests Arafat's elite guards…(6)

“Israel” arrested six members of Yasser Arafat's elite Force 17 guard, Palestinians said.

Tension was high in the West Bank and Gaza after two days of intense clashes. Today, Palestinians buried seven people killed in confrontations with “Israeli” troops.

“Israeli” police and security remained on high alert for attacks inside “Israel” by Palestinian militants, after a string of bombings.

In Ramallah, an 11–year–old boy, Mohammed Tamini, died today of wounds suffered in a clash with “Israeli” forces on March 15, hospital doctors said. Palestinians were preparing a midday funeral for him.

“Israeli” special forces arrested the six Palestinians, including a regional commander of Force 17, at the entrance to the village of Jilijiyeh, north of Ramallah, said Palestinian official Ribhi Arafat.

An “Israeli” official, insisting on anonymity, confirmed the arrests, but said that only five were Force 17 members. On Wednesday, “Israeli” helicopters attacked Force 17 headquarters and buildings in Ramallah and the Gaza Strip, after “Israel” charged that commanders of the elite force were directly involved in attacks against “Israelis”.

“Israeli” officials pledged to continue their operations against Force 17, considered the best–trained force in Yasser Arafat's Palestinian police and responsible for his personal safety.

The Palestinians said “Israeli” forces raided a Force 17 roadblock and arrested everyone there. Ribhi Arafat said “Israel” is holding at least 35 members of Palestinian security forces, accusing them of gunfire attacks against “Israelis”.

Palestinian leaders stepped up security measures, expecting “Israeli” attempts on their lives, Palestinian security officials said. The leader of Yasser Arafat's Fatah organization in the West Bank, Marwan Barghouti, and Force 17 commander Mahmoud Damara added bodyguards, and neither is sleeping at home, they said.

Palestinians say “Israel” has targeted and killed at least a dozen Palestinian activists since the current uprising erupted on September 28. “Israel” has acknowledged some of the killings and refused comment on others.

Cabinet Minister Ephraim Sneh said Sunday that the policy of targeting activists has not changed. "As soon as there is a target, and hitting it will prevent new terrorist attacks, it has to be hit," he told “Israel” radio. Gideon Ezra, the deputy internal security minister, said the main targets would be "middle–rank officials" who supply materials for attacks.

 

“Israeli” commandos kidnap five of Arafat's presidential guards…(7)

The Palestinian Authority has demanded that “Israel” return five members of Yasser Arafat's Force 17 presidential guard kidnapped by undercover commandos from a West Bank village early yesterday.

Mr Arafat has urged European governments and America to put pressure on “Israel” to comply.

Saeb Erakat, a senior Palestinian peace negotiator, told The Independent from his home in Jericho: "Abducting our security personnel is an escalation we haven't seen before. This is piracy. It cannot be tolerated."

The raid came at the end of a week of mounting violence, which saw three “Israeli” children killed in shootings and suicide bombings. The Red Crescent reported that 18 Palestinians were killed last week.

An “Israeli” military source reported that one of those captured in yesterday's undercover operation was a Force 17 commander. He said the force was singled out because it had "transformed itself from a security service to a terrorist group". He accused it of responsibility for more than 40 per cent of recent attacks on “Israelis”.

The kidnapping followed a declaration by the Defence (War) Minister, Binyamin Ben-Eliezer, that "the era of restraint is over". After last week's spate of bombings inside “Israel”, Ariel Sharon's national-unity government is striving to seize the initiative and keep the Palestinians guessing. During a cabinet meeting yesterday, an extreme right-wing minister, Rehavam Ze'evi, called for Mr Sharon to bomb Mr Arafat's home in Gaza. The idea was denounced by Likud and Labour ministers.

There are persistent reports, however, that the security services have drawn up a hit-list of Palestinian military and political leaders. The Transport Minister, Ephraim Sneh, who served as deputy defence (war) minister in the previous government, pledged: "As soon as there is a target, and hitting it will prevent new terrorist attacks, it has to be hit."

Saeb Erakat retorted: "For “Israel” to declare that it has a list of candidates for assassination is state terrorism. It must be stopped. At a time when Egypt, Jordan and others are trying to restart negotiations, this government has no plans for peace. It has plans for escalation."

 

“Israeli” shot after PA warns red line was crossed in Area A raid…(8)

As the Palestinian Authority issued a warning to “Israel” last night that it has "crossed a red line" by entering Area A (under Palestinian control) to arrest six Palestinians, an “Israeli” citizen was shot dead near Nablus. No other details were released for publication at press time.
The six Palestinians, including five members of Yasser Arafat's presidential guard and another Palestinian settler, were arrested late Saturday night near the village of Sinjil.
Security sources said the Palestinians may respond to the arrests by attempting to kidnap “Israeli” soldiers. The head of the PA's preventative security apparatus in the West Bank, Jibril Rajoub, said yesterday that the arrests were a "clear violation of the Oslo Accords. The “Israelis” have crossed a red line."
Chief of Staff Shaul Mofaz told Channel Two in response last night that "the Palestinians are the ones who have crossed the red lines," adding that, "the “Israeli” Army will act wherever it is needed to harm the terrorists."
In addition to increased attempts to kidnap soldiers, “Israeli” sources say that the Palestinians may also respond to the arrests by holding onto “Israelis” who enter areas under PA control, such as the teenage boy and girl who were found in Ramallah on Friday and returned unharmed to “Israel”.
