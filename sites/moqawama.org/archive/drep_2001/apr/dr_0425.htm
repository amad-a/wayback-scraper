Complete text of Leader's speech at Int'l conference on Palestine…(1)

-         Palestinian intifada is an expanded model of Islamic Resistance

-         Israel entity is not able to engage itself in a long confrontation 

Following is the full text of a speech delivered by the Supreme Leader of Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Ali Khamenei here Tuesday at the International Conference on Palestinian  Intifada.                                                             


    "Speakers and representatives of the national assemblies of Islamic countries, leaders and representatives of Mujahid groups and combatants of the front line of Islamic defense, honored guests and distinguished audience:                                               


    "Welcome you all to Iran and pray for the blessings and the guidance of Allah for you.                                            


    "The decision to organize this meeting is indeed very auspicious. With the grace of the Almighty, your meeting will strengthen the commitment and solidarity of Islamic states and peoples for the uprising Palestinian people. Such meetings have practically proven that the question of Palestine is an Islamic issue and belongs to the entire Islamic world. The occupation of Palestine is a part of a satanic design by the world domineering powers, perpetrated by the British in the past and being carried out today by the United States to weaken the solidarity of the Islamic world and to sow the seeds of disunity among us.                                                 


    "Our enemies have consistently endeavored to prevent the solidarity and unity of Muslims through the policy of divide and rule and promoting ethnic division. In the beginning of the occupation of Palestine, Muslim ulemas, such as Sheikh Azedin Ghassan and Haj Amin al-Hosseini appealed to Muslims to join hands in helping the  Palestinian people in their just struggle. The great religious leader, Kashef Al-Gheta, even called for jihad against the Zionist.   


But, with much regret, the Islamic color of the Palestinian struggle gradually faded and its ethnic content has become stronger.           


    "The victory of the Islamic Revolution in Iran, under the able leadership of Imam Khomeini, a courageous and learned descendent of the prophet, was a watershed event in Islamic awakening in the entire world, especially in the countries of our region. The victory of Muslims in south Lebanon, in the apparently unequal encounter with stronger enemy, once more proved the justness of the Islamic struggle and clearly showed that if Muslims put their trust in Allah, their victory will be certain, the astonishing victory of the Islamic resistance in south Lebanon, and the scandalous failure of all the compromising plans proved good lessons for our region.


"The triumph of Muslim combatants in south Lebanon, and the failure of compromising plans encouraged Muslim Palestinian people to resort to Intifada again. But this time the voices of surrender and compromise inside Palestine and the region will not change the mind of the brave and enduring people of Palestine. They are determined to bring their struggle to victory. The first Intifada was stopped as a  


result of insinuations by the Zionists and their supporters and pressures from the Americans and the West, promising to meet the demands of Palestine through peaceful means. Passage of 10 years has shown that all efforts by the supporters of the Zionists were aimed at rescuing Israelis from the heavy pressures stemming from the relentless struggle of Muslims.                                       


    "Time has also shown that rosy promises given to Palestinian negotiators were nothing but mere illusions.                          


     "The occupation, expansionism and savagery by Israel today were all predictable for those with insight and for the sympathizers of the Palestinian cause in the Islamic nations.                         


     "From the very inception of Israel, this fictitious and usurper has consistently violated inalienable rigths of Palestinian people.   


Moreover, some Western governments, especially the Americans have justified and supported these atrocious actions and international bodies have also tried to give legitimacy to the identity and aggressions of this regime by justifying its gross aggression.        


    "Western powers have always shown territorial ambitions toward Palestine and Beit-Al-Moqadas. Imposition of Crusade Wars on Muslims is a vivid proof of their greedy ambitions toward this sacred land.   


Military commanders of the Allied forces, after defeating the Ottoman Empire and marching into Palestine said that, today the Crusade Wars have come to an end.                                     


    "The occupation of this land was in accordance with a complex scheme, designed to prevent the unity and solidarity of Muslims and re-establishment of powerful Muslim states.                          


    "There are documents showing close collaboration of the Zionists with the Nazi Germany, and exaggerated numbers relating to the Jewish holocaust were fabricated to solicit sympathy of the world public opinion, lay the ground for the occupation of Palestine and to justify the atrocities of the Zionists.  


"There is even evidence on hand that a large number of non-Jewish hooligans and thugs of Eastern Europe were forced to migrate to Palestine as Jews. The purpose was to install in the heart of the Islamic world an anti-Islamic state under the guise of supporting the victims of racism and to create a rift between the East and the West of the Islamic world after 1400 years of unity between those two parts.                                                            


    "In the beginning the Muslims were caught by surprise, since they were not aware of the depth of the scheme designed by the Zionists and their Western supporters. The Ottoman Empire was defeated and the Sykes-Pico Treaty was secretly concluded to divide the Islamic territories among the victors of war, The League of Nations placed Palestine under the trusteeship of the British. It promised to support the Zionists and brought Jews to Palestine at the expense of displacing and forcing out Muslims from their motherland, in this long war, on one side of which are the West and the Zionists, and on the other side, the newly established Arab states, the enemies of Islam have made use of complicated instruments, including the power of international media and forums.                                       


    "One one hand, they invited Muslims to show patience, tolerance and engage in peace talks, and on the other hand, continued to arm Israel. Their strategic goal in these unequal and discriminatory interactions between Muslim countries and Israel has been to preserve the military superiority of Israel over Islamic countries.   


They have consistently supported Israel at international bodies, and have justified its atrocities by employing their mass media that keeps telling us victory over Israel is a dream that will never be realized.                                                             


    "The Israeli regime has carried on its aggressions for more than fifty years with impunity since its formal recognition by the United Nations and no one and nothing has been able to stop her. But the situation has changed; the Lebanon Islamic Resistance, comprising of a few thousands of young combatants, armed with a strong faith, have become a nightmare for this regime and its supporters. These beloved young combatants forced Israeli to retreat from South Lebanon in humiliation. Their victory became a guiding torch for other Muslim combatants and today we are witnessing the Al-Aqsa Intifada, which is akin to the Lebanon Islamic Resistance but in a broader scale.                                             


     "Now that you have gathered here to show your commitment to the Intifada, there is a heavy responsibility on your shoulder. You should, above all other things, demonstrate that the Islamic world is determined to revive the good traditions in its glorious history.     


    "Uppermost among these traditions was the unity of Muslims that brought the victory of Muslims in their decisive battles against the aggressor Crusaders. In those great historic events, Muslims from all corners of the Islamic world joined their defenses in that decisive and long jihad between the believers and unbelievers.                  


    "Today all Muslims of the world have pinned their eyes on this destiny-making struggle of Palestinian people. They have greater hope for this Intifada. In the first Intifada, ten years ago, the climate of compromise and surrender gradually prevailed in the region. There were those who overly depended on American promises, and there were also those who believed that we cannot resist and would ultimately succumb to international political pressure and there is no way out but acceptance of compromise under American and Israel terms.         


    "After the events that unfolded at that time in the region, the ground was then more conducive for acceptance of their terms. But, this year as you meet for this conference, the road to humiliating compromise is closed.                                                 


    "Even those who had put all their hope on the United States openly admit the current impasse.                                            


    "In 1991, Arabs and Muslims became disheartened as a result of a series of successive defeats in the course of the Persian Gulf War.   


Their internal unity was also exposed to serious threat of collapse. Conditions have changed now, especially after the great historic Islamic resistance in South Lebanon that put fresh hope in the hearts of Muslims.                                                    


    "At one time, there were two ways to deal with Israel: military encounter of Arab armies with Israel and compromising approach that would lead to giving in to Israeli demands by peaceful settlements.  


    "The previous experiences of military encounters proved to be failure for Islamic countries and the so-called 'land for peace' scheme meant continued military weakness of Arab countries. A clear case in point was the Camp David Accord.                              


    "During those days, there was no talk of resistance. Resistance was said to be universally unacceptable. Now we have a successful model of resistance before us that was able to liberate occupied territories without making any concessions to Israel and stopped it from hoisting its flags in the capital of Lebanon.                    


    "With the Camp David Accord, Israel withdrew on the condition that Egypt would not send her army to north of Sinai. In Lebanon, Israel begged Lebanon to send its army to its border with occupied Palestine, fearing the Islamic resistance. The resistance was able to restore full sovereignty to South Lebanon and other occupied territories.     


     "This Intifada is a popular uprising, Palestinians have lost hope in all schemes aimed at striking a compromise, and have realized that victory is solely dependent on their resistance.                      


    "The people of Palestine suffered much in their previous Intifada and offered many martyrs and casualties for Islam and liberation of Islamic lands.                                                        


    "The Oslo talks ultimately brought an end to that uprising. What was the outcome of Oslo? Today, even the Palestinian designers and supporters of Oslo Accord have stopped defending it. They have realized that Israel wanted to solve her own problem by freeing  herself from facing the stone-throwing fighters and reducing her exposure.                                                             


    "Little, if any, that was given to the Palestinians, was to extinguish the fire of Intifada, When Israel saw her problem resolved and mistakenly felt that Palestinians do not have the power to resume Intifada, they even stopped the little concession they had made and revealed their true expansionist nature.                              


    "The so-called peace process and the Oslo scheme placed the Palestinians in a situation that made them realize there is no other way but an all-out popular uprising.                                  


    "The Al-Aqsa Intifada primarily revolves around Beit-Al-Moqadas.  


The spark that ignited the anger of Palestinian people stemmed from gross insult of the Zionists to Al-Aqsa Mosque, by realizing their mission to protect one of the most sacred religious places of Muslims, the people of Palestine, entered the arena of struggle and by their devotion and sacrifices kept the sacred flame of resistance against the Zionist occupiers burning.                                        


    "The compromising process, and more specially the Oslo plan, divided the ranks of Palestinians. But this sacred Intifada was able to bring back and restore national Palestinian unity. As you have all seen, all segments of people are present in this struggle and  Islamists and nationalists are fighting side by side. Even those whose


hearts are at another place have no choice but to go along with this huge wave.                                                            


    "The Islamic uprising, or in another word Islamic awakening, began in Iran after the victory of Islamic Revolution under the leadership of Imam Khomeini and has appeared with great force in all other Islamic regions.                                                      


    "Today, this movement predominately revolves around the question of Palestine. The Al-Aqsa Intifada was able to bring all Muslims and Arab nations, even those beyond the immediate borders of Palestine, to the arena of struggle.                                                


    "Demonstrations by millions of Muslims from east and west of the Islamic world showed that Palestinian people can count on their support and this issue is pivotal to unity of Muslims.         

 

Nasrallah puts “Israel” on notice…(2)

Hizbullah’s secretary-general warned “Israel” on Tuesday to expect attacks from Palestinians, a declaration that won applause at a conference in Tehran.
“The fully armed Zionist military should wait for surprise attacks by Palestinian resistance groups,” Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah told an audience that included Iran’s top leaders as well as Khaled Meshaal, a leader of the Hamas movement, and Abdullah Ramadan Shalah, the head of Palestinian Islamic Jihad.
“Victory is ours. I confirm to you that the resistance in Lebanon will continue its operations,” Nasrallah added, asserting that Hizbullah would not submit to pressure.
He said his group would prove that Palestinians were not alone in their fight against “Israel”. “I say to the Zionists ­ and I mean it ­ wait for us, we’ll come to you from everywhere,” he warned.
Parliamentary leaders from over 30 Islamic countries are attending the two-day conference Iran is hosting on the intifada. Iran’s supreme leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, opened the conference Tuesday by declaring that combat, not dialogue, was the language understood by “Israel”.
Khamenei called on the rest of the Muslim world to support the Palestinians, even if that meant defying the United States and the West.
“Supporting the Palestinian people is one of our important Islamic duties,” Khamenei said.
Also in attendance was Speaker Nabih Berri, who cautioned that “Israel” was still determined to occupy parts of Lebanon, notably the South.
““Israel” is working hard to re-enter parts of the South that it left after 22 years of occupation,” he said. ““Israel” has launched a substantial provocation campaign aimed at blaming the Lebanese for any attacks  launched on their territory.”
“Israel” claims Lebanese resistance forces have received a fresh batch of long-range rockets from Tehran and other weapons aimed at undermining the security of northern “Israel”.
“The recent air raids against Dahr al-Baidar weren’t a reply to the Lebanese resistance activities,” the speaker argued, “but were aimed at intimidating both Lebanon and Syria.”
Berri added that “the resistance is a consequence of the “Israeli” occupation of our land. Hence, this resistance will remain as long as the occupation continues. The resistance forces have already launched attacks against the Shibaa Farms and will continue to do so.
““Israel” also aimed, during its latest air raid on April 16, to cut the supply route for the Lebanese resistance from Syria,” he said, “and prevent further attempts to normalize relations between Damascus and the Palestine Liberation Organization.”
He stated that “the raids were also aimed to achieve another “Israeli” objective ­ halting the Syrian peace initiative in Lebanon after it successfully stopped fratricidal bloodshed in our country. “Israel” won’t end its attacks on Lebanon until it succeeds in breaking Lebanon’s solidarity with Syria and launching an international diplomatic campaign to force the Beirut government to deploy the Lebanese Army to the southern border.”
He predicted that “Israel’s” next objective was to “disarm the resistance forces as a prelude to making south Lebanon a free-for-all once again.”
Berri and Nasrallah were chosen to be part of the conference’s presiding committee, along with five other members.
Former Prime Minister Salim Hoss told the conference that supporting the intifada was “an historic responsibility,” adding that the uprising’s failure would be a “disaster” similar to that of “Israel’s” 1948 creation.

 

International Conference in Support of Palestinian Intifadah Opened in Tehran…(3)

A major 2-day International Conference in Support of the Palestinian Intifadah started today morning in the Islamic Republic of
Iran, attended by thirty-four parliamentary delegations comprising close to 300 political and religious officials from Arab and
Islamic nations. They are expected to adopt a resolution in full support of the Palestinian cause and denounce the Zionist
Entity's aggressions.

Imam Ali Khamenei, in the opening speech, said the victorious Islamic Resistance in Lebanon must serve as a model, for it
pushed out the “Israeli” occupation army completely defeated, and now the Zionists are begging for the deployment of the
Lebanese army in South Lebanon.

Imam Khamenei said the ongoing Palestinian intifadah is an expanded model of that of the Islamic Resistance, and called on the
Arab and Islamic world to assume their responsibility in supporting the intifadah, on which a lot of hope is placed upon now
more than any other time. He added that the submission track of Oslo led the Palestinian people to the one and only option of
Intifadah, which reunited the Palestinian factions after the submission track has divided them. Imam Khamenei said the Zionist
Entity is not able to engage itself in a long confrontation with the Palestinians. He added the Zionist regime is rotting on the
inside and the new generation of “Israelis” is no longer motivated to defend it. So the Arab and Islamic peoples are stronger than any other time.

His eminence called for practical measures and "First of all the Zionist regime must be isolated in the occupied territories, its political and economic lifelines severed and the armed resistance must be continued." The Islamic leader moreover, criticized the arrogant powers, especially the United States of America, for attempting to break the united ranks of the Muslim people of Iran due to Iran's support for the Palestinian cause. He revealed that those powers have said very clearly that if Iran stops its support for the Lebanese and Palestinian combatants and people, they will cease their hostile action against the Islamic Republic. However, Imam Khamenei said Iran will remain committed to the intifadah and resistance against Zionists and their supporter and expressed confidence that Palestine will be liberated with the continuation of the intifadah and support of the Islamic world.

Iran's President Sayyed Mohammed Khatami called on the formation of a U.N war crimes tribunal in Palestine to help halt the current “Israeli”-created violence.

He said, "the main cause of the current instability in the Middle East region is the Zionists' denial of the basic rights of the
Palestinian people, and therefore Intifadah should be acknowledged not only among Moslem states but also in international
forums as a legitimate movement," the president added.

Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, addressed the audience saying "We are amid a historical opportunity
to destroy completely the Zionist scheme which has been threatening us for more than 50 years."

He called for an intensification of the sacred Intifadah and ruled out any hope of a successful United States role in negotiating a
so-called peace settlement. Islamic countries must assure their responsibilities, said Nasrallah, adding that any retreat or
submission will have a catastrophic impact on the region and the nation as a whole. Hizbullah Secreatry-General vowed that
the Islamic Resistance in Lebanon will continue its operations and said that the new formulas the “Israeli” enemy Premier Ariel
Sharon is trying to implement by force will be destroyed, and he will be defeated like his predecessors. Sayyed Nasrallah
pledged: "In the Name of Hezbullah and the Resistance, we shall not stay idle, and we shall assume our moral and legitimate
responsibilities, and prove to the Zionists that the Palestinians are not alone. We tell the Zionists be prepared to face us in
places that you expect or do not expect."

In turn, the political director of the Palestinian Hamas movement Khaled Meshaal called for more self-sacrifice operations and
mortar attacks against the “Israeli” occupation and affirmed the Palestinian people categorically reject any return to the negotiating
table.

The Palestinian Authority represented by Selim al-Zaanoun, President of the Palestinian Central Committee, denounced the
Oslo accords as a "Sandcastle" of illusion saying negotiations with the “Israelis” were at an impasse.

Lebanon Speaker Nabih Berri called on the Arab and Islamic world to build a strategy to confront the Zionist Entity, saying the
“Israelis” are working on sowing division in Lebanon. 

 

Parliament blames US, UN for attacks…(4)

Parliament’s Foreign Relations Committee has blamed the United Nations and Washington for the recent military escalation between Lebanon and “Israel” and cautioned against turning the Blue Line into an “official border.”
Tyre MP Ali Khalil, who chaired the committee’s Tuesday meeting, described the UN-drawn Blue Line as a “fallacy aimed at replacing the official border with a false one that does not incorporate Lebanese land in Shibaa Farms and three other positions located on the other side of the line.”
Khalil told reporters that “Lebanon expects UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan to draft an appropriate report on the escalation and not repeat the same mistakes as his aides, especially concerning the Blue Line, which was drawn by (UN Special Coordinator for the Middle East Peace Process) Terje Roed-Larsen and his team, and later endorsed by Annan himself.”
The committee condemned the April 16 “Israeli” attack that targeted Syrian radar positions in Dahr al-Baidar and cautioned against “the air raid’s negative and destructive repercussions for the Middle East peace process as a whole.”

 

Collaborators face new threat
‘Revolutionary Vengence Cells’ target those who ‘helped occupation’…(5)

A previously unknown group warned suspected collaborators in the former occupied zone on Tuesday to leave their homes by the month’s end or face “the worst acts of vengeance.”
Meanwhile, a car belonging to a suspected agent for “Israel’s” Mossad intelligence service, currently held by security authorities, was blown up Tuesday in the village of Khirbet Silm.
The group’s warning was the latest in a growing number of incidents in which mainly Muslim residents of the border area have been subjected to beatings, and bomb and arson attacks against vehicles and homes.
The group, calling itself the Revolutionary Vengeance Cells, distributed leaflets in several villages along the border, including Addaisseh and Houla.
The leaflet, entitled “Statement Number One,” targeted “all those who worked in “Israel”, took part in the consolidation of its military positions and its civil administration.”
Describing collaborators as “pigs and monkeys,” the leaflet accused them of continuing to work “publicly and with insolence” for the “Israelis”. It said that the collaborators were “expressing their readiness to deal with the enemy at any time.”
Former members of the “Israeli”-proxy South Lebanon Army militia who fled to “Israel” during the withdrawal last May subsequently received compensation from the “Israeli” government, amounting in some cases to over $40,000.
The several hundred militiamen who later returned to Lebanon brought their compensation packages, which they gave to their families before being taken into custody by the authorities.
The statement ordered “all collaborators and members of their families” to leave their homes by April 30.
“The hired agents who leave Roumieh Palace (the central prison near Beirut), whatever their sentences are and however long they have been banned for, should not in any case return to their native villages in south Lebanon,” the group warned. “If they do, even after a certain time, the gates of hell will open up in front of them.”
Many Muslim residents of the former occupation zone have expressed anger at what they perceive to be overly lenient sentences handed down by the Military Tribunal in Beirut to former SLA militiamen, members of the SLA’s civil administration, and those who had jobs in “Israel”.
In a statement, Hizbullah denied any involvement with the Revolutionary Vengeance Cells and warned against anyone attempting to use the party’s name to carry out acts of revenge.
The car blown up Tuesday belonged to Imad Hajj Ali Majed, 36, who was detained in January by Hizbullah members. Another car parked nearby was damaged in the blast. Nobody was hurt. Majed, the half-brother of Khirbet Silm’s mukhtar, Hussein Majed, is believed to have been recruited by Mossad while living in Germany.
The distribution of the leaflets came just three days after two cars in Houla belonging to suspected collaborators were blown up. Two other bomb attacks against vehicles have occurred in Houla since March 26.
Another car was destroyed in an explosion over the weekend in the village of Aita Shaab. Since the beginning of the year, there have been at least 13 attacks.

 

Intifadah Fighters Continue Mortar Attacks Against “Israeli” Occupation, “Israeli” Aggressions Leave One Martyr, Several Wounded…(6)

A Palestinian man was shot dead by “Israeli” occupation troops about 200 metres (yards) from the border near Beit Hanun, in the northern Gaza Strip. He shot several times in the chest and the hand, Palestinian medical and security sources said.
The body of Yusef Abu Hamda, 40, was handed over to the Palestinian liaison committee by the “Israeli” army, the sources said. His death brings to about  401  the total number of  Palestinians killed during the seven months of  intifadah.

Moreover, “Israeli” enemy forces fired Tuesday a number of shells towards the Bawabat Salaheddin residential area in Rafah in the southern Gaza Strip. Four Palestinians, including a 75-year-old man were wounded by “Israeli” fire. Medical sources and witnesses said, three, including the elderly man, were in a moderate to serious condition after being hit by pieces of shrapnel.

And at an “Israeli” checkpoint on the road between the West Bank town of Ramallah and occupied Quds (Al-Quds), a 40-year-old Palestinian taxi driver was wounded when he was shot by “Israeli” guards.

Meanwhile, Intifadah operations continued and the “Israeli” occupation army said that three Palestinian mortar shells were fired on a Jewish settlement in the Gaza Strip, claiming no injuries were caused. The bombs fell on the settlement of Gadid, part of the Gush Khatif bloc in the southern Gaza Strip, an army spokesman told AFP. It was the first incident of mortar fire since Sunday and came despite an order by Palestinian leader Yasser Arafat for a halt to such attacks.

“Israeli” security sources said it dismantled a huge bomb at a gas station in the northern al-Quds.

On Monday, and as Palestinian mourners were attending the funeral of Madi Khalil Madi,25, who died Sunday from wounds sustained during an “Israeli” raid on Deir el-Balah last week, “Israeli” enemy forces shot dead deliberately an 11-year-old Palestinian boy, and injured 12 others near Khan Yunis in the southern Gaza Strip.

Palestinian security liaison officer with “Israel” for the southern Gaza Strip, Colonel Khaled Abul Ulaa, said “Only the “Israeli” army fired bullets towards the mourners, without any justification.” Abul Ulaa referred to the “Israeli” army which denied involvement in the death as a claim which has no foundation in truth. According to Palestinian medical sources and witnesses, the boy Mohammed Muharib was killed by a bullet to the head. Clashes with enemy forces broke out during the funeral in the southern Gaza Strip.  Witnesses said the “Israeli” troops stationed at the Neve Dekalim Jewish settlement, not far from a cemetery, also fired two tank shells at the crowd of mourners, raising the number of injuries. Some of the crowd threw stones in retaliation.

Tensions have been heightened in the Khan Yunis refugee camp since “Israeli” troops raided the area two weeks ago, destroying
dozens of homes and leaving hundreds homeless.

 

Zionist Entity’s airline to sell off planes in face of intifada losses: report…(7)

“Israeli” national airline El Al will sell off several of its jets and cut services to a number of international destinations as it struggles to cope with plummetting business borne of seven months of regional confrontations, “Israeli” newspapers reported on Tuesday.

According to the Haaretz newspaper, El Al’s board of directors has decided to sell off six of its airplanes and to cut flights to destinations including Copenhagen, Chicago and Manchester after taking a severe economic drubbing since the outbreak of the intifada, or Palestinian uprising, in late September.

The Al-Quds Post newspaper said the planes to be put on the market are El Al’s workhorse Boeing 747s. The state-owned airline could not be reached for comment. El Al signed a deal in December 1999 to buy three Boeing 777s, manufactured in the United Sates, to replace its aging 747s after delaying a decision on a potential 350 million dollar order for European Airbus A330s which had aroused US ire.

Hundreds of El Al employees are likely to find themselves out of work as a result of the aircraft sales, with proceeds from the transactions going partly toward the purchase of a new 777 plane, the Al-Quds Post reported.

An initial draft report on El Al’s financial performance in 2000 found a net loss of 109.4 million dollars, as compared to a 16 million dollar profit in 1999, Haaretz said.

The report cited a severe tourism slump following the outbreak of "violence" as an “immediate” cause of El Al’s economic plight, according to Haaretz. “These events have a significant influence on the company’s revenues and profits,” Haaretz quotes the report as saying.

“According to assessments at the time of the preparation of the report, the aforementioned events are likely to have an effect on the company’s business results in 2001 as well.”

Some 64,000 “Israelis” have already been thrown out of work because of the economic crunch caused by the ongoind conflict, according to government statistics.

“Israel’s” tourist industry was the hardest hit with hotel revenue dropping by 27-percent during the first three months of the intifada, and hotel employment slipping by 5,100 jobs at the end of 2000 as compared with the nine months before the unrest erupted, according to official statistics.

The industry is unlikely to take an upswing in the near future, as clashes rages on and countries like the United States and Britain have issued travel warnings for parts of “Israel” and the Palestinian territories.

Syrian press called for the United  Nations to resurrect an obsolete resolution that equates Zionism, the Jewish nationalist ideology, with racism.

UN General Assembly resolution 3379, passed in 1975 by a coalition of Arab nations and Third World countries, was repealed in 1991 following the end of the Cold War and the launching of the Arab-”Israeli” so called peace process.

Syria Times said:  ““Israel” is seen by the whole world committing acts of aggression, launching a mass annihilation war against the Palestinian people, building settlements, blockading Palestinian cities and villages, rejecting to implement UN- related resolutions. Therefore, there is a need for an Arab and international move to revive the resolution 3379."
