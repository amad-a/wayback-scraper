Lebanon: 'Sharon fabricates report of Iranian missiles to wage massive onslaught'…(1)

The Beirut government has accused “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon of "fabricating" a report about an Iranian missile base in south Lebanon to set the stage for a large-scale offensive against Lebanon.
The charge came in a statement released by a foreign ministry official in Beirut Wednesday evening, denying Sharon's claim that the projectiles and long-range Katyusha missiles in the alleged base could hit central “Israel”.
Iran's foreign ministry spokesman Hamid-Riza Assefi has also put out a denial reported by start-run Radio Tehran. "These charges are baseless. They are all wrong," Assefi said.
Sharon's claim was reported by the Al-Quds Post newspaper. "The most dangerous factor in the region is terror and the center of terror is the area between Afghanistan and Lebanon," Sharon said.
"Iran has started building a base in Lebanon from which they can rocket and long-range Katyushas that can reach the center of the country," he said.
Sharon said he had discussed "ways to combat this threat" with US President George W. Bush and his senior aides when Sharon was in Washington last month.
When he returned from the US capital, Sharon opened up a barrage of claims that Iran was airlifting massive missile supplies to Hizbullah in Lebanon via Syria to strike at Haifa, Nahariya and the Galilee.

 

Collaborators sentenced to death…(2)

Five former members of the “Israeli”-backed South Lebanon Army (SLA) have been sentenced to death in absentia for collaboration with “Israel”, a military court source said Thursday.
Daniel Youssef Louka, Hussein Mortada Mohana, Jean Ibrahim Helou, Maroun Elisa Abu Ghanam and Elie Khalil Dalah were convicted of joining the now disbanded SLA and killing Lebanese resistance fighters.
The sentences were handed down late Wednesday and were posted on a court bulletin board Thursday.
The death sentence brought to about 35 the number of former SLA militiamen who have been sentenced to death in absentia.
A man and two brothers were also sentenced to six years in prison for serving with the SLA and involvement in the killing of Lebanese guerrillas as well as entering “Israel”.
The court dropped collaboration charges against five defendants, while four others were sentenced to jail terms ranging from eight to 18 months for serving with the SLA.

 

More “Israeli” aggressions leave 5 Palestinian martyrs, several wounded…(3)

Fatah vows revenge for death of four activists

“Israeli” enemy forces opened fire, as usual, at Palestinian civilians at Bureij refugee camp in the northern Gaza Strip early Thursday. Ahmad Wahdan-40-was shot dead as he was harvesting orange. Commander of the Palestinian border guards in the northern Gaza strip, colonel Mohammed Rajab, assured that the soldiers had shot without any reason on the farm workers.

Late Wednesday, four Palestinians, including three members of the Fatah movement and a teenager boy, were killed in a bomb planted by the enemy forces near the Rafah crossing point between Gaza Strip and Egypt. Six other Palestinians were wounded in the explosion.  A Rafah hospital source later said the body was found after the blast threw him several meters away.

Thursday, Fatah movement  issued a statement threatening to avenge the death of the three members, including one official, Ramadan Azzam. "This crime was orchestrated by “Israel’s” terrorist government and occupation forces and it will not go unpunished", the statement said.

Palestinian chief negotiator said Arafat underlined the need for an international force in the Palestinian territories, amid “Israeli” violence and terrorism.

According to witnesses the explosion happened just as the Palestinians were passing close to the barbed wire and concrete
making the border between the Gaza Strip and Egypt. The “Israeli” army control tower, is located some 500 meter form
the site of the blast.

Following the blast, violent clashes erupted between Palestinian mujaheddeen and occupation forces.  The “Israelis” shelled
residential areas in Al-beireh town of Jabal al-Tawil.

A Palestinian post at the Ain Kina town was also shelled with mortar shells and heavy machine-guns, causing severe damages
to the possessions.

Local sources said that security forces have failed an “Israeli” attempt to break into the town.

In the Kfar Younis camp Ibrahim Hammad al-Queili (20) died from wounds sustained when “Israelis” fired explosive bullets into his chest, on April 7th and in Al-Khalil, Palestinian sources said that “Israeli” forces attempt to assassinate Wael Shasif an activist of the Fatah movement but he was not hurt.

For his its part, the Popular Resistance Force, the military arm of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine, issued a statement saying that it fired mortar shells on Ajderot Settlement late Wednesday.
