Hizbullah Secretary-General Said Hizbullah Not Holding A Fifth “Israeli” Captive…(1)

 Hizbullah Secretary-General  Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah denied  Hizbullah captured a fifth “Israeli” soldier,  commenting on “Israeli” War Minister  Benyamin Ben Eliezer's  announcement that  a fifth “Israeli” soldier had gone missing a year ago and that he might be  in Hizbullah hands.

Nasrallah said Hizbullah was holding only four “Israeli” captives - the three soldier who were kidnapped in an ambush last October 7 and a reserve army colonel who was seized in a seperate operation.

Asked by the Sharq al-Awsat daily on the readiness of the “Israelis” to release Sheikh Abdul Karim Obeid and Hajj  Mustapha al-Dirani in return for the release of the “Israeli” soldiers  held by  Hizbullah, Sayyed Nasrallah said, "What the “Israeli” officials say in the media is not important. What’s important is what they send through the intermediaries in the serious negotiations."

Ben-Eliezer is reported to have said in an interview carried by the “Israeli” daily Yedioth Ahronoth that the “Israeli” government was willing to consider the release of Sheikh Abdul Karim Obeid and Hajj Mustafa Dirani if that would help to obtain the freedom of the four “Israelis” held by Hizbullah.

 

The "Settlement" Process Is An Illusion: Hizbullah…(2)

Member of Hizbullah political  politburo, Hajj Hassan Hudroj, said  the settlement process in the Middle East is just an illusion about  realizing peace and security in the region.

Hodroj was speaking during the anniversary of the Dair Yassin massacre of Palestinians perpetrated by the Zionists in 1948, as well as the  assassination of Mohammed Youssef  al-Najjar, and Ahmad Kamal Odwan in their  homes in Beirut by an “Israeli” commando unit.

Representing the Palestinian Fatah  Intifadah Movement, Abou Fadi Hamad  was surprised about the holding of  “Israeli”-Palestinian meetings, and called  for the continuation of the intifadah until  Palestine is liberated.

For his part, the  representative of the Islamic Jihad Movement, Abou Imad al-Rifaii, condemned the  secret and open negotiations between  the Palestinian Authority and the “Israeli” enemy while Palestinian children, and women are being shelled. 

 

Germany Suspends Swap Efforts, Says Sharon's Priority is to Attack Hizbullah's Missiles…(3)

Germany reportedly has put its mediation to arrange a prisoners swap between Hizbullah and “Israel” on hold because Prime Minister Ariel Sharon's priority is to attack Lebanon and destroy Hizbullah's missile power.
"This was made clear by Sharon to the German mediator who recently met him in Jerusalem," said Prime Minister Rafik Hariri's Al Mustaqbal newspaper, without citing a specific source for its report.
The report, which failed to identify the mediator by name, said he heard threats from an angry Sharon to mount a military operation to wipe out the missiles that he claims were supplied by Iran to the Party of God.
The mediator drew the conclusion that the prisoners swap was no more a priority for Sharon. The mediator has since appealed to the German government to exert the maximum possible effort to head off a drastic deterioration in the region.

Failed bomb attempt causes fire in former “Israeli”-occupied south Lebanon…(4)

“ A bomb placed in a tractor failed to explode Tuesday, in the third such booby-trapped attempt in about two weeks in the former “Israeli”-occupied border zone of southern Lebanon, police said.

The explosive charge apparently failed to blow up and merely provoked fire in the tractor owned by contractor Saadallah Daher at his cement factory on the main road of the town of Marjayoun, they said.

The driver of the tractor escaped unharmed but the vehicle was damaged, they said.

A bomb's detonator can cause fire even if the bomb itself fails to explode, usually because it is either poorly set or because it has expired.

Police said the bomb carried Hebrew inscriptions.

“Israel” pulled its troops from southern Lebanon on May 24, 2000, after 22 years of occupation.

More than 6,000 Lebanese fled to “Israel” at the time of the withdrawal, fearing revenge attacks. Many of them have since returned to Lebanon where some have been tried for collaboration with the "enemy “Israel”."

Fourteen Lebanese sentenced for collaborating with “Israel”…(5)

“ Beirut's military court sentenced 14 more Lebanese for collaborating with the "“Israeli” enemy", court sources said Tuesday.

Thirteen people received sentences ranging from one to 18 months in jail for collaboration, entering "enemy territory", and serving with the now-disbanded “Israeli”-allied South Lebanon Army militia, they said.

A 14th SLA member was sentenced in absentia to seven years in jail for collaboration and serving with the militia.

The court also sentenced to seven and a half years in jail two men who were part of a group of Lebanese prisoners released by Syria in December, for collaboration with “Israel”.

Raja Salman Kabalan and Elias Lutfallah Tanios, had served prison terms of respectively ten and eight and a half years for the same reasons.

On Saturday, the court sentenced 21 Lebanese for collaboration with “Israel”, including 11 former SLA members to death in absentia for having tortured Lebanese resistants in the notorious Khiam prison in the former “Israeli”-occupied border zone in southern Lebanon.

The trials against those accused of collaborating with “Israel” started 10 days after the May 24 pullout of “Israeli” troops from Southern Lebanon. Since then, Beirut's military court has tried 2,478 suspected collaborators.

To this day, 30 have been sentenced to death, all of them in absentia.

 

One Palestinian Martyr, 18 Injured On Another Day of “Israeli” Aggressions…(6)

A Palestinian military doctor was killed and 18 other people injured Tuesday when “Israeli” missiles pounded targets at Deir Al-Balah in the Gaza Strip.

Palestinian medical and security sources said Wail Khuweita, 29 years old, who held the rank of lieutenant, was killed when
the medical clinic inside the naval headquarters in the northern Gaza Strip was hit by ground-to-ground missiles.

Two other military doctors including the chief military doctor at the position and two naval officers were also injured in the attack.

The “Israeli” army said anti-tank missiles were used in the strikes.

At a central Gaza Strip military police position, another 10 people were wounded including the commander of the position when a missile struck the building setting it on fire.

The Palestinian Intifadah, however, continues despite the threats. Three Palestinian mortar bombs fell on a Jewish settlement in
the southern Gaza Strip on Tuesday morning.

Two of the shells hit the Katif settlement, part of the Gush Katif settlement bloc in the southern Gaza Strip. The army said it did not know where the third landed.

Palestinians fighting the “Israeli” occupation of the West Bank and Gaza Strip for the past six months have been stepping up their use of mortars in recent weeks, in retaliation to “Israeli” aggressive attacks.

Meanwhile,  “Israeli” enemy Prime Minister Ariel Sharon claimed that "“Israel”" had a “clear plan” to restore alleged security as he toured the border with the Gaza Strip just before an “Israeli” army strike.

But Palestinian presidential secretary Tayeb Abdel Rahim said "“Israel’s”" strikes which left one person dead were all-out “declared war,” which demanded the international community to provide protection to the Palestinian people.
