Iran, forerunner in the struggle against Zionist regime: Nasrallah…(1)

" Secretary-General of the Lebanese Hizbullah Movement, Seyed Hassan Nasrallah, here on Wednesday evening stressed that the Islamic Republic of Iran is the forerunner in the struggle  

against the Zionist regime.                                          

   At a meeting with Majlis Vice-Speaker Mohammad Reza Khatami, he said that despite the enemies' one-sided propaganda, Iranian officials are steadfast in their support of the resistance movement of the Palestinian nation, consistent in their policy in this regard over the past 22 years.                                                       

   Lauding the initiative of the Iranian Majlis of holding the International Conference on Palestinian Intifada which ended its work Wednesday night, he expressed his observing that Iran is making great progress domestically along with its people.                   

   He stressed that notwithstanding close ties between the Lebanese Hizbullah Movement and the Islamic Republic of Iran, the movement is independent in operation and has no links with Iranian domestic factions.                                                            

   Nasrallah also invited the Iranian vice-speaker to visit Lebanon in the near future.                                                     

   Referring to recent victories gained by the Lebanese Hizbullah forces in the occupied lands, Khatami said that such victories are the positive results of the unity and solidarity embraced by the Lebanese people in their untiring struggle against the Zionist regime.                                                              

    "Although there may be some minor differences among Iranian officials on various internal issues, all authorities of the country share a common view regarding support for the resistance movement of  the Palestinian nation against "Israel"," he added.                    

    In a related development, Mohammad Reza Khatami met and conferred with a member of the political bureau of the Islamic Movement of Palestine (Hamas), Khaled Mashaal, yesterday.              

    "Support for Palestine began with the victory of the 1979 Islamic Revolution in Iran and will continue," Khatami told the Hamas official, adding that all political parties in Islamic Iran support the Palestinian Intifada.                                             

    Praising the great support of the Islamic Republic for the Palestinian resistance movement, Mashaal lauded the holding of the Tehran conference as a great boost for the movement and reiterated his conviction that continuation of the Intifada is the only solution to the Palestinian problem.                                          

    He further briefed the Iranian parliamentarian on the current situation in the occupied territories.

 

Nasrallah, Khatami Discuss Palestinian Intifadah, Regional Situation…(2)

Hizbullah Secretary General Sayyed  Hassan Nasrallah met today with  Iranian President Sayyed Mohammed  Khatami.

The latest developments  especially the Palestinian Intifadah and means to support it were discussed during the meeting.

Sayyed  Khatami  renewed the Iranian support to Lebanon and the Lebanese Resistance to liberate all its occupied lands. He  added that the Tehran  Summit unites the Muslims' position  from the Palestinian issue.

Khatami said if  the Zionist Entity's presence is the major problem  facing the Islamic and the Arab world,  the Intifadah is the major problem facing  this entity. Hence it must be supported by all  possible means and capabilities so as to guarantee its continuation. He  maintained that "Should the Zionist Entity  be defeated once, surely it will be defeated  another time."

Meanwhile, Sayyed Nasrallah met today with Palestinians injured by "Israeli" occupation army bullets  receiving treatment in Iran.

The  heroes of the Intifadah enthusiastically  welcomed  Sayyed Nasrallah and hailed and cheered for Hizbullah demanding  it to keep its struggle against the Zionist  enemy. They also took some memorial  photographs with Sayyed Nasrallah.

 

Iranian FM praises Hizbullah's role in anti-"Israel" battles…(3)

" Iranian Foreign Minister Kamal Kharrazi here Thursday highlighted the Lebanese Hizbullah's instrumental role in driving "Israeli" troops from southern Lebanon after an 18-year occupation, and said it ushered in a new atmosphere for regional combats.                                                             

    "The Lebanese victory in southern Lebanon has made the regional states more determined to obtain legitimate rights (long) denied them," Kharrazi told the visiting Lebanese Hizbullah leader Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.                                                    

    "Iran has always supported the brave nation of Palestine and is in favor of driving the occupiers from all occupied territories," he noted.                                                               

    For his part, Nasrallah said that the recently-concluded Tehran conference to support the seven-month-old Palestinian Intifada (uprising) would further encourage the Muslim nations to support Palestinians in their anti-"Israeli" combats.                          

    "The Zionist regime is trying to sow terror in the region to achieve its own goals," he said, and "to this end, it is seeking to tarnish the real image of regional combats (against "Israel")."        

    "But," he noted, "the Lebanese nation has found the key to victory against the occupiers after driving "Israeli" troops from southern Lebanon."                                                   

    On Wednesday, he told the Tehran conference in support of the Intifada, "I tell you...we in Hizbullah and the Islamic resistance in Lebanon will not stand idly by...We will prove to them (Zionists) that the Palestinians are not alone in this battle."                  

    The two-day conference--attended by the Palestinian group Hamas, Hizbullah of Lebanon and delegations from 30 Arab and Muslim states--

was aimed to help attract financial and other support for the Palestinian cause and to give a fresh impetus to its policy of "no compromise with "Israel"."                                             

    Iran has repeatedly called for the destruction of "Israel" as the only solution to the conflict in the Middle East.                    

    At least 387 Palestinians, 13 Arabs and 74 other "Israelis" have been killed in what is now known as the second Intifada against "Israeli" occupation which began in late September.

 

International Conference in Support of the Palestinian Intifadah Condemns "Israeli" Aggressions…(4)

The International Conference in Support of the Palestinian Intifadah, on its second and last day, was a scene to more Arab and

Islamic calls to efficiently support the Palestinian people in their struggle against the "Israeli" occupation. The conference held in

Tehran is the biggest conference in the last 50 years against the Zionists and in favor of Palestine, said Ali Akbar Mohtashami,

the conference chairman. Referring to the conferences final resolution, Mohtashami said the main themes would be support for

the Palestinian uprising and a continuing struggle against "Israeli" violations.

The draft statement of the conference, attended by Moslem and Arab officials from 37 countries, severely condemns the Zionist

Entity for its terrorist acts and aggressive and expansionist policies which reached its peak in recent months, by committing

more massacres against the Palestinian people, including women and children, and the assassination of Palestinian personalities.

The conference, therefore, calls upon the UN Security council to assume its responsibilities and form an international war

crime tribunal to try the Zionist criminals.

The Conference will also call for the severing of all relations with the "Israeli" enemy, and condemn the US biased support which

has expelled thousands of Palestinians from their land, continuing in settlement expansion, destroying Palestinian properties, and imposing an economic siege on  the Palestinians.

On the second day of the conference, the vice-president of the Higher Islamic Shiite Council in Lebanon Sheikh Abdel-Amir

Qabalan urged the participants to take decisions which answers the current challenges. While the head of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine General Command, Ahmad Jibril, called for an intensification of the armed struggle against the "Israeli" occupation.

Issa Ben Majed al-Ghanem from Qatar affirmed his countries support for the Palestinian people's rights.

And the Emir of the Islamic Jamaa in Pakistan, judge Hussein Ahmad, hailed Hizbullah's efforts in Lebanon which pushed and

encouraged the Palestinian people to uprise. He also called on Arab and Islamic countries to cut relations with the Zionist

Entity.

As to the Secretary-General of the Islamic Council in Britain, Youssef Faylock, he announced the support of 2 million British

Muslims to the aims of the Intifadah conference.

Representing Fatah Intifadah Movement, Abu Moussa said the conference is of great importance of the intifadah which highly

needs the support of Arab peoples.

The Presidents of the Indonesian parliament Nour Eskandar al-Bursan conveyed the will of the Muslim Indonesian people to

participate in the confrontations against the Zionist occupations.

And the Bahrain Deputy, Mohammed Youssef al-Sayyed said the Islamic nations should take Hezbullah in Lebanon and its

victories as a model, adding the "Israeli" show of power is not only threatening an Arab country but the whole region.

The former Jordanian Speaker Abdel-Latif Arabiyyat said the Conference should be named the Nation's Conference for it

expresses the hopes and Opinions of the whole nation.

The Syrian representative Brigadier Mahmoud Amman conveyed Syrian President's Salutes and proposed pressuring on the

Arab countries for it to carry out the resolutions of the past Islamic and Arab Summits.

As to the Palestinian official responsible for the Quds file, Faisal Al-Husseini said the current intifadah is that of Liberation not

protest. He demanded an aid of 35 million dollars to protect and build Palestinian residence in occupied Quds.

 

2ndConference to support Intifada wound up, releases final statement…(5)

"The Second Conference to Support the Intifada of the Palestinian Nation which was held in Tehran within April 24 and 25, issued a final statement Wednesday evening.         

   The conference ended its two-day activities following forty-eight hours of lengthy discussions among over 30 Muslim nations PMs.       

    Excerpts from the final statement of the conference, read by its Secretary Hojatoleslam Ali-Akbar Mohtashami pour are as follows:     

    1- The participants at the conference believe that the Palestinian nations' Intifada is a brave and righteous response, based on international laws, to the racist, brutal genocide staged by the  occupiers of their motherland.                                       

    2- The participants at the conference condemn resorting to all types of means of terrorism, brutality and expansionism by the Zionist Regime against the Palestinian nation, particularly during the recent months.                                                              

    3- The organized crimes of the Zionists, aimed at the defenseless people of Palestine, particularly women and children reveal the racist nature of the Zionist Regime and are regards as war crimes.   

    4- The conference regards Zionism as a type of racism and resorts to all possible means to implement UN Resolution 3379 of 1975.

    5- The conference emphasizes that its participants believe the Zionists have no right or legitimacy within the occupied lands.      

    6- The conference strongly condemns the military, economic and political support of the US administration for the Zionist Regime.   

    7- The conference invites all countries, particularly the Islamic governments to severe all ties with the Zionist Regime and stop the naturalization process, as a sign of solidarity in fight against the Zionist usurpers.                                                    

    8- The conference condemns the Zionists moves in demolishing signs of the Islamic and Christian civilizations in Beit-ul-Muqaddas and condemns the Zionists efforts aimed at changing the population ratio of that holy city in favor of the Zionists.                          

    9- The conference supports the Palestinians in establishment of  their independent country whose capital should be the holy Quds,     

asking for international denouncement of any move against the solidarity and sanctity of Quds.                                     

    10- Freedom of the occupied southern parts of Lebanon proved it to all Muslims that strong faith and perseverance of the Lebanese who fought a sacred Jihad against the occupiers of their lands gained superiority over the heavily armed merciless Zionist Army's  phony   

might.                                                               

    11- The conference appreciates the practical solidarity existing among all Palestinian groups, as well as the whole Muslim Umma as a result of Intifada.                                                  

    12- The conference condemns the Zionists recent attack at the Syrian bases in southern parts of Lebanon, against all international onventions, considering it a threat against peace and stability in the region.                                                          

    13- The existance of atomic warheads at the disposal of a wayward regime that yields to no international treaties, like the Zionist Regime is a serious threat against peace in the Middle East.         

    14- The Palestinian refugees have a natural, legitimate and definite right to return to their occupied lands.                    

    15- The conference appreciates establishment of the valuable funds within the Organization of Islamic Conference (OIC) and the Arab League (AL) in support of Intifada.                                  

    16- The conference encourages the OIC and the AL to activate committees commissioned to monitor systematic sanction against "Israel".

    17- The conference suggests that cooperation and friendship groups with the Palestinian nation should be established.                   

    18- An inter parliamentary association comprising of Muslim nations' present and previous MPs should be established as a non-government organization.                                         

    19- The conference decided to establish a secretariat office to see into proper implementation of its adopted stands and decisions.  

    20- The conference pays due respect to the sacred soul of Imam Khomeini, the late founder of the Islamic Republic of Iran, who adopted such resolute stands against the Zionist usurpers.            

    It also praised the current stands of Iran on the Palestinian issue, announced by Ayatollah Seyed Ali Khamenei and sincerely thanked Iran's Majlis (Parliament) and President Khatami

Iran Denies "Israeli" Allegation on Military Buildup in Lebanon…(7)

Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Hamid Reza Asefi here on Wednesday rejected as "sheer baseless" an allegation by "Israel"i Prime Minister Ariel Sharon that Iran had set up a missile base in Lebanon.

Asefi was quoted by the official IRNA news agency as saying that "Israel" has repeatedly launched such allegations against Iran in an effort to cover up its aggressions from air and ground against Lebanon.

Asefi noted that what remains questionable is the Zionist ("Israeli") regime's illegal identity and its deplorable expansionist policies against the interests of regional Muslim nations.

The spokesman pointed out that the time of such allegation is aimed at diverting the world's attention from the ongoing pro- Palestine Intifada (uprising) conference held in Tehran.

The Islamic republic is currently hosting a two-day international forum to support the Palestinian Intifada against "Israeli" occupation.

The conference is expected to provide financial and moral support for the Palestinians and put pressure on "Israel" to stop its aggressive policy against the Palestinians.

Echoing remarks made by Iranian leaders at the opening ceremony of the conference, Asefi stressed Iran's political and spiritual support for the struggles of the Palestinians, Lebanese and Syrians to restore their rights and liberate their lands occupied by "Israel".

Iran does not recognize "Israel" and has repeatedly called for the destruction of the Jewish state as the only solution to the conflict in the Middle East.

 

Benefits law for ex-detainees to be fast-tracked…(8)

The Social Affairs Ministry and representatives of former detainees of "Israeli" prisons agreed Wednesday to complete a draft law on former detainees' demands within the week and send it to Cabinet for review before liberation day on May 25.

Minister Asaad Diab and six former detainees will draft a detailed report outlining the detainees' social assistance needs by the deadline set by Prime Minister Rafik Hariri.

On April 14, Hariri responded to former detainees' protests and hunger strikes by promising to reactivate the ministerial committee concerned with monitoring the situation of ex-detainees.

In February and April, 25 former detainees of "Israeli" prisons held two protests and hunger strikes at the Grand Serail to call attention to their plight.

The ministry's family affairs department head, Hussein Majed, discussed with the ex-detainees their three major demands: employment, financial compensation for their years of imprisonment in "Israel", and tax exemption.

Majed said there were 5,232 former detainees on ministry records according to the number of filed applications.

Noting the importance of considering education level in employment assignments, Majed said "60 percent have reached the secondary level, 10 percent studied at universities, and 18 percent are illiterate."

"Around 48 percent of them suffer from chronic diseases," Majed continued.

He also discussed the benefits criteria the government is considering, including the duration of detention and the detainees' personal health and social conditions.

Despite the new agreement, the six former detainees left the meeting at the ministry pessimistic of the government's ability to meet the deadline.

"We don't believe the Cabinet will take any decision before May 25," said ex-detainee and  spokesman for the former detainees, Mohammed Yassin.

Former detainees announced a May 20 counter-deadline for the government.

"We chose this date to embarrass officials who will celebrate the first anniversary of the liberation," Yassin added. He did not disclose the ex-detainees' plans if their deadline were not met.

"Despite my pessimism, we'll work on completing this report with the ministry," former detainee Ibrahim Kalash said.

Yet he wondered why they had to prepare a report when their demands had been presented several times before.

The completed report will be presented to the ministerial committee following the situation of former detainees. Upon the committee's review, the report will be sent to Cabinet for approval by the May 25 deadline.

A Palestinian Martyr and Several wounded in More "Israeli" Aggressions…(10)

A number of Palestinian civilians were wounded by "Israeli" occupation soldiers in different clashes in the Palestinian territories, while the "Israeli" enemy has heightened  restrictions on the Palestinian people and enforced the blockade,  fearing Palestinian attacks during the celebration of the 53rd anniversary of  its occupation of Palestine.

The fiercest clashes erupted in Abu Sneinah neighborhood in the West Bank town of al-Khalil (Hebron), where a Palestinian child was injured when Jewish settlers aggressed Palestinian civilians in the town. Palestinian fighters answered by targeting occupation forces protecting the settlers. Grenades were also launched at occupation posts in Rafah, in the Gaza Strip.

In the West  Bank town of Qalqilya, a 20- year-old Palestinian was shot dead by the  occupation forces, and in the Gaza Strip  another Palestinian-25- was shot while climbing a border fence. Meanwhile, Palestinian  mujaheddeen fired another round of  mortar shells on the Gadid Jewish  settlement, part of the Gush Khatif  bloc south of the Gaza Strip.

Meanwhile the "Israeli" occupation continued the destruction of Palestinian homes, and the razing of plantation.

Overnight, two Palestinian civilians were  wounded by shrapnel from shells fired by  "Israeli" tanks in the southern Gaza Strip  town of Rafah. A house in the  Salaheddin gate area of the town near the  Egyptian border suffered damage and  burns when it was hit by a shell.

 

Activists urge Washington to curb "Israeli" human rights violations in occupied territories…(11)

Palestinian and "Israeli" human rights activists have called on the George W. Bush administration to speak out against "Israeli" violations of Palestinian rights. They accused the US administration of not having the courage nor vision to be even-handed and stand up to domestic pressure. "Israeli" and Palestinian activists, meeting with senior and mid-level officials in Washington, said despite the Bush administration's open criticism of "Israel's" siege of the occupied territories and the construction of Jewish settlements, the change has only been marginal from the previous Bill Clinton team. They said Washington is still letting "Israel" off the hook. Human rights activists urged Bush to support a deployment of international rights monitors in the territories, a proposal the US had vetoed at the UN Security Council.  Human rights groups have been stepping up their condemnation of "Israeli" oppressive measures against Palestinians.
