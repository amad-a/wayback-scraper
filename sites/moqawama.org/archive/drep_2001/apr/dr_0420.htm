Resistance: “Israel” will pay for attack
South commander warns that “Israeli” PM ‘miscalculated’ with radar strike…(1)

Hizbullah’s southern commander suggested Thursday that “Israel’s” air strike against a Syrian military radar base earlier this week had not been unexpected and warned that the organization would hit back in a “specific way.”
Sheikh Nabil Qaouk accused “Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon of “pushing the region toward a large-scale war for which all the Arab countries should prepare.”
Qaouk said that Hizbullah had been studying Sharon closely since the former “Israeli” general took power in February.
“We knew he was a very careless man and does not calculate well.
“We were ready for all possibilities and probabilities … (The air raid) did not go beyond what we had calculated Sharon capable of,” Qaouk told The Daily Star in an interview.
“Israel’s” air raid against what was the largest Syrian Army radar installation on Lebanese soil has raised the stakes in the tit-for-tat exchanges between Hizbullah and the “Israeli” Army in the Shibaa Farms.
“Israel” appears to have set a new precedent by which Syrian military positions will be targeted in response to Hizbullah attacks in the Shibaa Farms or elsewhere along the border.
Faced with this prospect, will Syria pressure Hizbullah to curtail its military operations?
“This has never happened in any former period and will not happen in the future,” Qaouk said. “Syria fully supports our right to resist.”
The last time a Syrian position of any importance was attacked was during “Israel’s” invasion of 1982 when “Israeli” warplanes destroyed Syria’s anti-aircraft missile system in the Bekaa. The only deliberate attack against Syrian troops since then was on the second day of the Grapes of Wrath operation in April 1996, when “Israeli” helicopter gunships fired missiles at a Syrian anti-aircraft position near Beirut Airport.
Could Damascus really stand back and allow “Israel” to destroy its military positions so that Hizbullah could continue attacking “Israeli” troops?
“Sharon and his government will discover that Syria is able to resist and maintain steadfastness,” Qaouk said. “Hizbullah is determined not to allow the “Israelis” to impose any equation. Sharon committed a very stupid action. Syria has said through the comments of President Bashar Assad that it will defend itself and it will not be long before the “Israelis” discover they have created their own tragedy.”
Qaouk argued that the air raid had backfired on Sharon because it had strengthened Syria’s resolve and rallied the Arab world against “Israel”.
“The “Israeli” people will soon discover that they have put their faith in a very stupid man,” he said.
The deadly anti-tank missile attack on Saturday in the Shibaa Farms which killed an “Israeli” soldier and prompted the retaliatory air raid was indirectly criticized by Prime Minister Rafik Hariri through an editorial in his Al-Mustaqbal newspaper. The editorial ­ which questioned the timing of the operation ­ underlined the continuing rift between the premier and Hizbullah.
Qaouk conceded that Hariri “had opinions” on the wisdom of Hizbullah’s operations before the “Israeli” air strike, but after the raid “the issue is different.”
“He has trust in our operations,” he said, “and all Lebanese have the same opinion in Hizbullah’s ability.”
Should we expect another unfavorable editorial in Al-Mustaqbal if Hizbullah was to stage a further operation?
“Wait and see,” Qaouk replied.
Hizbullah appears determined to proceed with its hit-and-run operations against the “Israeli” Army in the Shibaa Farms, regardless of whether Hariri or the Lebanese in general approve.
Qaouk said that Hizbullah has “decided to make the “Israelis” very sorry for what they did (by attacking the radar base).”
“Our (military) program was not affected by the “Israeli” raid … Saturday’s operation was definitely not the last. We will deal with this raid in a very specific manner,” he warned without elaborating.
He would not be drawn into giving a time-frame for any response to the air raid, but his comments suggested Hizbullah will continue to confine its actions to military targets in the Shibaa Farms rather than expand the theater of conflict elsewhere along the border.

 

Lebanon rejects “Israeli” offer in return for constant Syrian presence
Lahoud: Resistance will continue…(2)

President Emile Lahoud said Thursday “Israel” should know “clearly and once and for all” that it would not achieve security by using force and disclosed “secret” offers to barter protection for “Israel” in exchange for a permanent Syrian presence in Lebanon.
Speaking at the opening of a weekly Cabinet session, Lahoud dwelt on recent developments, especially “Israel’s” air strike against a Syrian radar installation in the Bekaa on Monday.
“All this will not prevent us from adhering to our firm position based on doing away with “Israeli” occupation of territory, a return of Palestinian refugees to their homes and releasing prisoners held in “Israeli” jails,” he said.
“Without all of these rights and without resorting to the principles of a comprehensive and just peace in the region, “Israel” should know clearly and once and for all that it will not achieve security by force, regardless of its aggression and escalation.”
The president said “Israel” would be held responsible for its “aggression and escalation in occupied territory as well as for its attempts to introduce new formulas which will be rejected by us, through resistance and diplomatic and other legitimate means.”
Lahoud said that more than two months ago, both Lebanon and Syria had rejected offers through diplomatic channels proposing a quid pro quo ­ “a permanent Syrian presence in Lebanon in exchange for permanent Lebanese and Syrian protection for the security of northern “Israel”.
“We rejected these offers and said at the time that such a presence is linked to the strategy of confrontation, not with an exchange between “Israel’s” security and Syria remaining here.
“We say the same thing about an offer made openly by “Israeli” Foreign Minister Shimon Peres two days ago and affirm that the resistance is continuing and the exchange is rejected,” the president said.
““Israel’s” interior will not be safe if our own interior is subject to any aggression,” he warned.
In turn, Premier Rafik Hariri said that since Ariel Sharon came to power this year, “Israel” was following a “suicidal policy that would not lead to a solution.”
He added that the “Israeli” air strike Monday had been “denounced by everyone” and that “Israel” was trying to escalate tension and violence in the region.
Such a policy will have “negative effects” on “Israel”, he said.
Hariri reiterated the government’s support of the resistance as long as “Israel” occupied Lebanese territory and committed “aggression” against it.
The session was preceded by a separate meeting grouping Lahoud and Hariri lasting about 30 minutes.
Ministerial sources said Economy and Trade Minister Basil Fuleihan told the Cabinet that he was notified during a visit to Tehran earlier this week of Iran’s approval to place a deposit with the Lebanese Central Bank, earlier requested by Hariri.
No amount was set, but some ministers felt the figure would be “a considerable one.”
It also approved a German government grant to help remove mines from the South.

Assad vows retaliation for “Israeli” attack on Syrian station…(3)

“Syrian President Bashar al-Assad has vowed to retaliate against the strike. Assad did not indicate how Damascus might respond but said “Israel’s” actions have raised a "feeling of anger in the Arab street".

“President Bashar al-Assad, in his strongest reaction so far to the “Israeli” strike against a Syrian radar base in Lebanon, said the country would not stand idly by as “Israel” mounted attacks. Presidential Spokesman Joubran Kourieh said the President's remarks were made during a meeting of the ruling Baath Party's Regional Command, the party's highest policy making body, held on Tuesday under Assad's chairmanship. The meeting was the first since an “Israeli” air raid early on Monday killed at least one Syrian soldier and wounded six others in eastern Lebanon. “Israel” claimed it launched the raid in retaliation for the killing of one of its soldiers by the Hizbollah resistance movement on Saturday.  Official Syrian press had urged the US to quote "put the brakes on “Israel’s” aggressive drive". The director of Syria's official radio and television called on Arab countries to "put pressure on American interests," without elaborating. Official newspapers said Arabs must use the oil weapons in the struggle against “Israel”, adding Damascus has quote numerous means at its disposal to make “Israel” regret its Monday bombardment of the Syrian radar station. The US has been coming under increased Arab criticism and condemnation for what they say is Washington's support for “Israel” in its confrontation with Palestinians, Lebanon and Syria. The Syrian presidential spokesman quoted Assad as expressing an interest in meeting the demands of the Syrian people, who want to extend support and backing to the Palestinian uprising against “Israeli” occupation in the West Bank and Gaza Strip. At UN headquarters in New York, UN spokesman Fred Eckhard said Assad had a long telephone conversation with Secretary General Kofi Annan to discuss his concerns about escalating violence. Eckhard said Assad was quote firm in his views that what the “Israeli” are carrying out is a form of aggression. Damascus had warned “Israel” it would pay "a heavy price for the attack at the convenient and appropriate time". More countries have been condemning the raid. The European Union presidency described it as excessive. Sweden which holds the rotating presidency of the 15 nation-bloc said the “Israeli” raid which it stressed was in retaliation for Hizbollah attacks on Shibaa Farms was a disproportionate reply. The EU urged maximum restraint to prevent any military escalation.

 UN Human Rights group denounces “Israeli” violations of Lebanese sovereignty…(4)

“The United Nations Commission on Human Rights has denounced “Israeli” violations of Lebanese sovereignty. At its annual six-week session in Geneva, the UN rights forum, also demanded “Israel” release at least 19 Lebanese detainees it has been holding, most without trial or charge. The 52-member state body condemned an “Israeli” high supreme court decision allowing “Israel” to detain Lebanese as hostages and bargaining chips for any future negotiations on the fate of missing “Israeli” servicemen. The UN commission is in session until the end of April to examine abuses worldwide. It has adopted a resolution condemning “Israeli” atrocities against Palestinians. Only the US and Guatemala voted against the resolution.

“Israeli” warplanes overfly south Lebanon for fourth consecutive day…(5)

““Israeli” warplanes flew over south Lebanon Thursday for the fourth consecutive day, said Lebanese security sources.

The planes patrolled “Israel’s” former security zone in south Lebanon and the Shibaa Farms territory on the “Israeli” and Lebanese border, the sources said.

On Monday, “Israeli” jets broke the sound barrier over Beirut and southern regions, following an overnight air strike on a Syrian army radar station in eastern Lebanon that left at least one Syrian soldier killed and four wounded.

Lebanon has filed several complaints with the United Nations about “Israeli” planes breaching its airspace, saying the flyovers violate UN Security Council resolution 425, which called for the “Israeli” pullout from southern Lebanon.

“Israel” withdrew from Lebanon last May, ending 22 years of occupation.

 

UAE delegation visits South as part of demining pledge…(6)

A delegation from the United Arab Emirates’ army inspected minefields in the South for the first time Thursday, following the UAE’s pledged support for Lebanon’s demining campaign.
“Our delegation is touring the South to get a better feel of the effects of the occupation and evaluate the mission that needs to be carried out on this level,” said General Obeid al-Kutubi, the head of the delegation.
Earlier this month, the UAE said it would finance mine-clearing efforts in the South, where an estimated 130,000 mines were planted by “Israel” “Israeli” proxy.
Accompanied by General George Sawaya, the delegation toured the Maidaneh Plain in eastern Kfar Roummane, Nabatieh, and visited Beaufort Castle, a former post manned by the “Israeli” proxy South Lebanon Army militia.
The group then visited the “Israeli” and SLA-run Khiam prison where a Hizbullah delegation showed it around the former detention camp.
A Hizbullah member and a former detainee then briefed the delegation on “Israel’s” torture methods on Lebanese and other detainees held in the camp.
“This visit has enabled us to form a realistic idea of the extent of destruction” in all previously occupied areas in the South, Kutubi said. He said the UAE team would start clearing land mines soon after coordinating with the Lebanese Army.

 

Lebanon sentences another 20 for collaboration with “Israel”…(7)

“ A Beirut military court handed down 20 more verdicts Thursday against Lebanese accused of helping “Israel” during its 22-year occupation of the south of the country, judicial sources said.

Among the sentences, three former members of “Israel’s” proxy South Lebanon Army were each sentenced in absentia to 15 years in prison.

A total of 2,478 people have been sentenced since the trial of collaborators began in early June, just 10 days after “Israel’s” pullout from south Lebanon.

Thirty people have been sentenced to death, all of them in absentia.

 

Military fires media salvo at “Israel’s” leaders over Gaza climbdown…(8)

“Israel’s” armed forces, condemned internationally for the persistent use of excessive force against the Palestinians, are facing a fierce internal storm over this week's botched reoccupation of part of the Gaza Strip. Anger is simmering over the operation that sent “Israeli” troops, tanks and bulldozers in to seize Palestinian land close to Beit Hanoun, in the north-east of the strip ­only to be hastily pulled out less than a day later.

The retreat has placed strain on relations between Ariel Sharon, the former general whose election in February was applauded by many in uniform, and military officers who believe the government capitulated embarrassingly under American pressure.

The “Israeli” media carried reports from anonymous officers saying the plan had been to reoccupy the territory, bulldozing land and destroying orchards, for as long as was necessary to ensure it was impossible for the Palestinians to fire mortars from the area. On Wednesday night, less than a day after the force was withdrawn, five mortar bombs were fired from the Strip into an “Israeli” kibbutz, demonstrating that the expedition had been militarily ineffective as well as internationally damaging and causing American ire.

The army outrage was fanned by a statement from the “Israel” Defence (War) Forces claiming the commander in the Gaza Strip, Brigadier-General Yair Naveh, had exceeded his authority in saying the troops may stay for "days, weeks, or months", if necessary.

Mr Sharon's aides and his Defence (War) Minister, Benjamin Ben-Eliezer, say the invasion, the fourth in a week by “Israel” into Palestinian territory, was planned to last only 24 hours. Much of the “Israeli” media said the expedition was a failure. "None of those involved in the short-term “Israeli” Army occupation ... has anything to be proud of," said Ha'aretz newspaper.

 

Mortar attacks the latest tactic against “Israel”…(9)

“ A snapshot of three hooded Palestinians firing a mortar was attached as proof of a claim of responsibility Thursday by the militant group Hamas for an attack on a Jewish settlement. The message was delivered by leaflet and e-mail -- and later in a video that was played over and over on the “Israeli” TV news.

Hamas has become increasingly boastful recently about its use of mortars -- often simple homemade devices. They have further destabilized the region by drawing “Israeli” reprisals, including a brief occupation of a slice of the Gaza Strip that earned “Israel” a U.S. rebuke this week.

“Israel” holds the Palestinian Authority responsible for the dozens of mortar attacks of recent weeks, saying Palestinian leader Yasser Arafat has done nothing to stop the firing and asserting that some of his officers are directly involved.

Yet for all its military might, “Israel” has been unable to prevent the attacks -- a source of frustration for Prime Minister Ariel Sharon, who has promised to restore security to “Israelis”.

The mortar attacks have injured two “Israelis” and rattled many others. Hours after “Israel” seized two Palestinian areas in Gaza on Tuesday and Wednesday, mortar shells fell near Jewish settlements and an “Israeli” community farm.

“Israeli” Transport Minister Ephraim Sneh, an ex-general, acknowledged that the army had no answers. Mortars can be fired by small crews hiding in the fields and citrus groves of Gaza, he said. "Even if the entire army searches, it won't find them," he told “Israel” Radio.

Hizbullah guerrillas based in Lebanon have trained Hamas activists in making and firing mortars, said Barry Rubin, an “Israeli” political scientist, who estimates that only about a dozen Hamas members are involved.
