Mine conference set for May…(1)

Lebanon will host an international conference in May to draw up a plan to deal with the tens of thousands of land mines still planted in the South.
Western Bekaa MP Sami Khatib, the head of Parliament’s National Defense Committee, said Defense Minister Khalil Hrawi told the committee that a number of European countries had been invited to attend the conference, which is scheduled for May 21.
The announcement comes as French Ambassador Philippe Le Courtier has urged Lebanon to address the mine issue and decide whether aid was needed from Europe and France.

 

“Israeli” Occupation Army Assassinates An Islamic Jihad Leader, Kills 15-Year-Old Boy…(2)

A Palestinian movement Islamic Jihad leader was assassinated by the “Israeli” occupation in a blast in the West Bank on Thursday, Palestinian security sources said.

Martyr Iyyad Hardan, aged around 30, was killed while he was standing in a public telephone booth in the northern West Bank town of Jenin, a Palestinian security source said, adding that the telephone had probably been booby-trapped.

Hardan was the second Islamic Jihad activist to be slain in four days following warnings by “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon’s government that it would go on the offensive against those its suspects of “terrorism.”

“Islamic Jihad will not stay silent about these “Israeli” crimes against our people and will respond strongly and as swiftly as
possible to these crimes,” a Jihad official who asked not to be named told AFP in Gaza City.

Another activist from Islamic Jihad was slain in an “Israeli” helicopter attack in the Gaza Strip on Monday, a killing “Israel” said was a “preventative” operation to avert a major attack.

The assassination of Mohammed Abdel Al, who "“Israel”" accused of involvement in several deadly attacks over the past few years, was the first such assassination since Sharon took office a month ago.

The “Israeli” Occupation Army  liquidated more than 20 militants since the launch of the uprising, or intifada, against the “Israeli” occupation.

Islamic Jihad on Wednesday warned of the “danger” of security meetings between “Israel” and the Palestinians, saying in a statement: “We reject any contact, official or secret, with the Zionist enemy.”

The latest death brings to about 384 the number of  Palestinian martyrs since the uprising  broke out in late September.

On the other hand, a 15-year-old Palestinian boy was shot dead by “Israeli” troops and another 13 injured in clashes around the Gaza Strip on Thursday, medical sources said.

Ahmed al-Assar was shot in the chest by a live bullet during a clash between a small number of Palestinian stone throwers and the army near the Jewish settlement of Netzarim in the central Gaza Strip, they said.

Another five Palestinian youths were wounded in the northern Gaza Strip at the Erez crossing  by gunfire in a clash between the “Israeli” army and dozens of stone throwers, other Palestinian medical sources said.

One of the wounded was seriously hurt in the leg during the clash, they said.

In other clashes, two Palestinians were wounded at the Mintar crossing with in the north and one near the Kfar Darom Jewish settlement towards the south.

 

Occupation Authorities Go On With Settlement Constructions…(3)

The Zionist Entity called Thursday for tenders for the construction of more than 700 new homes for Jewish settlers in the occupied West Bank, a move that sparked an outcry from Palestinians.

The publication of tender offers for 708 homes by the housing ministry is the first since Prime Minister Ariel Sharon, a champion of Jewish settlements in the occupied territories, took office almost a month ago.

They cover 496 units in Maale Adumim, the largest settlement that lies east of occupied Quds, and 212 in Alfei Menasheh near Qalqilya in the northern West Bank.

Senior Palestinian negotiator Saeb Erakat said the tenders were part of an “Israeli” “policy of aggression.”

“These tenders are destructive measures aimed at the whole peace process,” he told AFP. “The true policy of the Sharon government is a policy of settlement, a policy of ignoring agreements and a policy of aggression against the Palestinian people and the whole world, including the United States of America.”

Palestinian leader Yasser Arafat’s top adviser Nabil Abu Rudeina said the move “will increase the levels of violence and tension in the whole region.” He called the tenders part of an “Israeli” strategy to scuttle “American efforts to revive the process,” the day after an “Israeli”-Palestinian security meeting took place with US participation.

The settlements, which are illegal under international law, have been at the flashpoint of the six-month Palestinian intifada or uprising against the “Israeli” occupation.

An official in Sharon’s office cited by the radio said the move did not contradict the prime minister’s commitment “not to build new settlements but allow the natural development of those already existing.”

Sharon spearheaded settlement construction drives as a cabinet minister in the 1970s and again in the 1990s and there are now some 200,000 Jews living in the West Bank and Gaza Strip.

The European Union called for an immediate halt to the settlement policy in the occupied territories, including east Quds, which "“Israel”" annexed after capturing it in 1967.

“All settlement activities are illegal and constitute a major obstacle to peace,” it said in a statement. It urged “Israel” to stop the building of 2,800 new units in the  Har Homa settlement on the edge of east Quds and the creation of a new settlement in the Gush Etzion bloc in the West Bank called Givaot, with 6,000 housing units.

 

“Israel” gunships hit Gaza…(4)

“Israeli” helicopter gunships attacked Palestinian security forces target in the Gaza Strip early on Friday, hours after the United States issued a sharp rebuke to “Israel” over its Jewish settlement policy.

Palestinian Public Security Chief Major-General Abdel Razek al-Majaydeh said “Israeli” helicopters bombarded four Palestinian security posts near Jabalya refugee camp in Gaza. Five people were slightly wounded in the attack, Gaza hospital sources said.

The “Israeli” army said its forces had attacked targets in response to "many" mortar shells that were fired earlier in the evening on villages inside “Israel” and in the Gaza Strip.

In the Palestinian village of Beit Lahia close to the border, witnesses said the local police station had sustained serious damage during the helicopter attack, with its walls blasted out and missile holes cratering floors.

Majaydeh told Reuters there had been "grave damage to several buildings" in the area.

 

Mortars fired at “Israeli” villages…(5)

Palestinians fired five mortar bombs from the Gaza Strip into three villages in “Israel” proper on Thursday, the “Israeli” army said. Mortar attacks inside “Israel” have been reported only once before in more than six months of the Palestinian uprising.

The army said no injuries were reported. Palestinian witnesses at the Erez border crossing between “Israel” and Gaza said “Israeli” troops responded with mortar fire of their own.

Intense fighting was also reported on Thursday night at the Erez border crossing between “Israel” and Gaza. Palestinian police said “Israeli” soldiers near Erez bombarded four Palestinian security targets, including two police posts, with sniper fire and heavy weapons.

 

UAE Says The US, “Israel” Are Terrorists…(6)

The Information Minister of the United  Arab Emirates Abdallah Bin Zayid Al  Nahyan described the United States as the first terrorist.

The UAE minister said during the  opening of a photo exhibition of Intifada  Martyrs, the US which issues an annual report of countries that support  terrorism should not forget to add itself on  top of the list along with “Israel”.

Al  Nahyan described the Zionists “Israelis”  as  the new Nazis adding that the “Israelis” are practicing all kinds of  terrorism against the Palestinian people.
