Hizbullah and SSNP stick to strategic pact…(1)

A delegation representing Hizbullah visited the Syrian Social Nationalist Party Friday to discuss local and regional developments.
Hizbullah’s delegation, headed by Nabatieh MP Mohammed Raad, called for continuing the resistance path until all Lebanese objectives are met, including the liberation of the Shibaa Farms and the release of Lebanese prisoners held in “Israel”, where they remain held without trial.
The two parties agreed on the need to maintain the strategic alliance with Syria until the Golan Heights are liberated. They also agreed to coordinate their efforts to secure development plans for liberated areas in the South.
The two sides welcomed the Syrian redeployment “that showed how eager Syria was to consolidate civil peace in Lebanon.”

DFLP stage protests against Annan visit
Palestinian faction rejects US involvement, demands implementation of UN resolution…(2)
 Palestinians across Lebanon gathered to protest against UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan’s brief visit here on Friday.
In downtown Beirut, supporters of the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine protested outside UN House, urging Annan to adopt a fairer view of the Palestinian struggle.
“Listen up Kofi Annan, we’re Palestinians from Lebanon. We don’t want decisions or schemes. We want to blow up buildings, tanks and airplanes,” chanted young DFLP members.
Some 250 Palestinian refugees, among them small children and the elderly, echoed these words as they briefly marched around Khalil Gibran Garden at Riad Solh Square.
Baalbek-Hermel MP Marwan Fares, who heads the parliamentary Committee for Human Rights, was among those who attended the protest. Fares said the intifada offered Palestinians a chance to regain their human rights, and predicted that neither the UN nor the United States would be able to stop them.
Ali Faisal, a member of DFLP’s political section, maintained that CIA Director George Tenet’s efforts on Wednesday to broker a cease-fire was only an excuse for the US to “crush the intifada and shield “Israel”.”
Meanwhile, Mustafa maintained that the US’s recent decision to get involved again in Arab-”Israeli” affairs was motivated by its desire to rescue “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon from failing to bring about the peace which he promised his voters.
“We wish the international community would begin to see the Palestinians in a different light, and stop picturing us on the same footing as “Israel”, which has an army,” said Huda Ajouz, a member of DFLP’s women’s organization and a Shatila refugee camp resident.
At the Ain al-Hilweh refugee camp near Sidon, Yasser Arafat’s Fatah party also organized a large-scale demonstration to mark Annan’s one-day visit.
Hundreds of people marched behind a row of refugee children who carried Palestinian flags and pictures of children who were killed in the intifada.
Demonstrators waved banners and shouted slogans such as, “George Tenet, leave us alone, you and your damned schemes,” and “George Tenet is Sharon’s other face.”
Young Palestinian men staged a sit-in at the camp’s entrance, where they used blood, which they extracted with syringes, to sign their names on a banner reading, “The right to return is a right sanctified by blood, O Kofi Annan.”
Relaying a message on behalf of all Palestinian factions, DFLP member Tayseer Ammar called on Annan and the international community to pressure “Israel” into withdrawing from Palestinian territory, including Jerusalem, and dismantle its settlements.
The message also asked for the UN’s “temporary protection of Palestinian people until the occupation is terminated,” and it called for efforts to coerce “Israel” to “stop its policy of siege, economic strangulation, and killing.”

As “Israeli” Occupation Forces Wound Several Palestinian Youths, Intifadah Fighters Fire More Mortar Shells…(3)
Convinced that resistance alone will liberate occupied Palestine, Palestinian resistance fighters challenged the US-brokered cease-fire and carried out more intifadah operations overnight against the “Israeli” occupation. The Fatah Movement said it fired 6 mortar shells on the Morag settlement in the Gaza Strip. And the “Israeli” enemy radio admitted that 10 mortar shells targeted the Gosh Katif settlements in the Gaza Strip. Late Thursday, as well, intifadah fighters shot and wounded three Zionist settlers. The settlers were driving to a settlement east of the West Bank city of Ramallah. Their injuries were described as moderate to critical. In the West Bank town of Khalil, 12 firebombs were hurled at occupation patrols. For its part, the Zionist government translated the ceasefire agreement by carrying out more aggressions against Palestinian civilians. The occupation troops shot and wounded six Palestinians, among them a 12-year old boy who was seriously wounded near the Neve Dekalim settlement in the Gaza Strip town of Khan Younis. Eyewitnesses said the occupation soldiers fired live-bullets to disperse the teenage boys who threw stones at them after the soldiers assaulted 3 other boys. A medical source in the Amel hospital reported that 3 young boys, whose ages ranged between 4 and 9 were severely beaten by the Zionist soldiers. Moreover, groups of Zionist settlers under the army's protection attacked several Palestinian villages in Ramallah. Meanwhile, Palestinians in Khalil (Hebron), marched in the funeral of a Palestinian who was killed late on Wednesday by settlers in a drive-by-shooting on a West Bank road. Concerning the implementation of US-CIA Chief George Tenet's ceasefire plan, the occupation said its tanks were being moved back, borders reopened and road blocks removed in the Gaza strip and the international border with Egypt was opened. However, the Palestinian Authority said instead of lifting the siege and closures of Palestinian cities and towns and implement other measures reacquired by the work plan, the Zionist Entity has taken a number of actions to further the suffering of the Palestinian people. The Authority added that the occupation army tightened the closure on Beit Laham, Khalil, Nablus and Ramallah in the West Bank, destroying roads and setting up new checkpoints in several areas.

Zionist, Palestinian Officials Trade Accusations On Implementation of Tenet's Plan…(4)
“Israeli” officials contended that they would only apply the terms of the Tenet accord selectively. “Israeli” War Minister Benyamin Ben Eliezer accused the Palestinians of ignoring the US-brokered ceasefire which he claimed the “Israelis” are committed to. Nevertheless, he expressed his hope concerning opening all the cross points in the very near future. However, a senior official in “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon's office proved the opposite when he said the “Israelis” will only implement the agreement where the Palestinians are respecting it. In his Statement the official was echoing a statement by Sharon Thursday who said the “Israeli” forces had been directed to maintaining the ceasefire and implement all measures whenever there is a complete cessation of what he called violence, shooting and terrorism. For his part, Palestinian International Cooperation Minister Nabil Shaath said the “Israelis” are dragging their feet reimposing measures which were supposed to have been annulled. He added, Tenet accord obliges the “Israelis” to visibly withdraw their forces from the edges of Palestinian - controlled areas, but that is not seen on the ground.

Palestinian People Took to the Streets in Protest Against Tenet's Plan…(5)
Upon the call from the Intifadah Follow-up Committee, the Palestinian national and Islamic forces organized a massive rally in Gaza city to protest the Palestinian Authority acceptance of US proposals, who came to be known as Tenet's plan. Thousands of Palestinians from different political trends participated in the march. They carried banners protesting the US-ceasefire plan. Some carried a coffin on which was written "security coordination" in a clear indication that the Palestinian street opposes any security coordination with the “Israeli” enemy. "I am taking part in the demonstration because I reject all policies which allow the stealing of the Palestinian's rights and prevents the Palestinians people from gaining the least of their rights. We here refuse these agreements which was proposed by Tenet because it deprives us from our rights," said a protester. "We do not accept this solution. Let the settlers leave us alone and go away. The occupation tanks are in front of our homes. Our children are imprisoned by the siege. There is no peace with “Israel”. The intifadah should go on until death or until victory by God's will," added an old lady. On another location, Palestinian boys were throwing stones at “Israeli” occupation soldiers, not fearing their weapons. The children stone-throwers whom many of them were killed for the sake of the Aqsa mosque expressed their own way of rejection. "Our officials are the ones to have accepted. But the people did not accept (Tenet's plan)," said a boy stone-thrower to the Manar's camera. Another boy said, "I am here to throw stones, return Quds and liberate Palestine with our stones. The Jews oppressed us, they took our land, arrested our young men. We want to continue the intifadah."

Zionists Urge Americans to Save Plummeting Tourism Sector Due to Palestinian Intifadah…(6)
Three major US Jewish organizations urged Americans Friday to visit the Zionist Entity this summer to show solidarity as tourism there has plummeted amid months of confrontations with the Palestinians. The groups—the Conference of Presidents of Major Americans Jewish Organizations, State of “Israel” Bonds and United Jewish Communities The Federation of North America—said their members would travel to "“Israel”" and called on others to do the same. “We are at a critical moment in the history of “Israel”,” they said in a full-page advertisement in The New York Times. “It is time to show our solidarity with the people and state of “Israel”." “WE ... ARE GOING TO “ISRAEL” THIS SUMMER. JOIN US,” the text reads in bold, capital letters, adding that such trips are important to show “full support and unwavering commitment to the Jewish homeland and honor the generations of Jews who came before us and made “Israel” a reality with their prayers and their lives.” The advertisement appeared less than a week after “Israeli” officials expressed outrage that some Jewish groups were cancelling visits to the Zionist Entity fearing intifadah oprations, including martyrdom bombings. Foreign visits to the Zionist Entity have dropped by about a third since then costing the “Israeli” economy some two billion dollars in tourism, export and construction revenues, according to a study published last month. In one particularly virulent rebuke, occupied Quds Mayor Ehud Olmert accused the US Reform Movement—one of the major branches of Judaism in the United States—of “spitting in the face” of "“Israel”" with its decision to cancel youth trips to the Zionist Entity this summer. The Reform Movement cancelled its annual summer trips following the June 1 martyrdom operation at a Tel Aviv beachfront nightclub that killed 20 settlers and the bomber, the deadliest anti-”Israeli” attack in years. Meanwhile, several airlines have rearranged schedules so their crews will not have to stay overnight in the Zionist Entity following the Tel Aviv bombing, prompting consternation among aviation officials.
