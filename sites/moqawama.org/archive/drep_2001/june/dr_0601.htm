“Israel” violates Blue Line in Ghajar again
Observation post was erected Saturday, 20 meters into Lebanese territory…(1)

UNIFIL confirmed on Thursday that “Israeli” troops have established an observation post north of the Blue Line in Ghajar, the third breach of the UN-delineated boundary in the divided village since January.
UNIFIL senior adviser Timur Goksel said that last Saturday, “Israeli” soldiers erected a sandbagged observation post 20 meters into Lebanese territory overlooking the Hasbani River.
“We saw it and have been protesting it to the “Israelis” since then,” Goksel told The Daily Star. “They said they’re studying it and they haven’t yet given a response (on removing the post).”
Ghajar is split by the Blue Line with two-thirds falling inside Lebanon and the remainder in “Israeli”-occupied Syria.
The “Israelis” are in the process of erecting a sophisticated electrified fence along the Blue Line from Ras Naqoura on the coast to opposite the village of Shibaa at the northern end of the Shibaa Farms. Following protests by Ghajar’s residents, the “Israelis” have so far agreed to refrain from running the fence through the middle of the village.
But the “Israeli” Army believes that the absence of a fence in Ghajar poses a security risk. It tried to circumvent this dilemma in January by swinging the new fence around the northern tip of Ghajar on Lebanese territory. “Israel” was forced to abandon the plan after the violation was protested by Lebanon and the UN.
In April, the “Israelis” erected a camera mounted on a pole about 20 meters north of the Blue Line on the western edge of the village overlooking a deep wadi through which the Hasbani River flows. The pole was taken down two weeks ago after protests by UNIFIL.
The new observation post has been built on the same spot.
Part of the Hasbani River Gorge ­ the bend near the new water-pumping station ­ cannot be seen from the “Israeli” side of the Blue Line.
A UNIFIL officer said that the camera and observation post were set up to allow the “Israelis” to monitor the wadi, which they consider a potential route for guerrillas to infiltrate the “Israeli” side of the line, even though the surveillance measures violate the Blue Line.
 

“Israeli” helicopters strike Lebanon after explosions in disputed area…(2)

“ “Israeli” helicopters fired Thursday night on positions in south Lebanon following five explosions in the “Israeli”-held Shibaa Farms area, Lebanese police said. They gave no indication of any casualties.

The helicopters launched heavy machine-gun fire for half an hour around three “Israeli” positions in the Shibaa Farms, after five blasts, the origins of which were not clear, were heard from the area, police said.

The “Israeli” air force, apparently fearing a guerrilla infiltration, also struck an entry point into the Shibaa Farms from Lebanon.

The militant movement Hizbullah has since October launched a series of attacks on the area, capturing three “Israeli” soldiers and killing three others.

 

Jewish state’s troops threaten farmers…(3)

Farmers harvesting wheat near the border with “Israel” at Metulla were threatened by “Israeli” troops Thursday, who warned them at gunpoint to leave the area.
Mehdi Fawaz and two brothers, Ahmad and Mohammed Daher, were harvesting wheat in a field just north of the border beside Metulla when  an “Israeli” military vehicle stopped opposite them. The soldiers climbed out of their vehicle and, pointing their rifles at the three farmers, yelled at them in Arabic to leave their field.
The three men initially ignored the order and continued working. But they abandoned their equipment and left the field when the “Israeli” soldiers were joined by extra troops and took up firing positions on the other side of the fence.
The “Israeli” soldiers left the area shortly afterward when the farmers returned with several local reporters. Lebanese Army officers also arrived.
“We ploughed the land in October and the “Israelis” are preventing us from harvesting our crop,” Fawaz told The Daily Star.
He called on UNIFIL to “protect us from the “Israeli” soldiers, so that we can return to harvesting our wheat.”
 

Army not on border for ‘military’ reasons…(4)

Defense Minister Khalil Hrawi said on Thursday that the government’s refusal to deploy the army along the border with “Israel” was a “military strategy.” He also said that tampering with security in the region was “prohibited,” especially in liberated areas.
Speaking during a visit to the Melchite League in Achrafieh, where he reviewed domestic and regional developments, Hrawi said the security situation in liberated areas was “under state control.”
“Recent attacks on citizens’ property serve no one, and the perpetrators have been arrested and referred to court for trial,” he added.
In explaining the government’s reasons for not deploying the army at the border ­ though he said it had a “presence” in the South ­ Hrawi was clear: “We are still in a state of war with the “Israelis”,” he said. “The presence of the army on the Blue Line would make daily friction with the enemy likely, and so the military strategy in such a situation … is to avoid any confrontation.”

 

Hoss says ‘no’ to normalization…(5)

Former Prime Minister Salim Hoss urged Jordan and Egypt on Thursday to close down their embassies in Tel Aviv, calling it “the capital of barbarism.” He then urged both countries to expel “Israel’s” ambassadors there “until the Hebrew state recognizes full Arab rights.”
Hoss was addressing the Second Annual Conference to Combat Normalization with the Zionist Entity in Kuwait on behalf of Arab delegations participating in the opening session.
“No, no, a thousand times no to normalization,” he said. “No to normalizing relations with “Israel”, no to liaison offices or commercial offices opened by certain Arab countries with “Israel”, and no to any meeting with “Israeli” officials on the pretext of personal friendship or openness.”

 

Intifadah Operation Leaves A Zionist Settler Dead, Targets More Settlements…(6)
Several mortar bombs hit today the Gosh Katif settlement in the Gaza Strip. The popular resistance committees claimed responsibility for the shelling which is dedicated to the Lebanese people on the first anniversary of the Liberation. “Israeli” enemy army reported that the Palestinians have launched a mortar shell near the CRD settlement in the Gosh Katif bloc but reported no injuries. In the Gaza Strip, Palestinian mujahedeen opened fire at an enemy post near the Neve Dekalim settlement, part of the Gosh Katif settlements bloc, and another post was hit at Rafah close to the borders with Egypt. Elsewhere, a Palestinian stabbed a Jewish settler several times at Tilbot neighborhood in southern occupied Quds, and was transferred to the hospital. Another Jewish settler was shot in the head while driving, north of Tulkarm in the West Bank on Thursday. Enemy army Radio reported that the “Israeli” settler, Zvi Shelef, was evacuated to a hospital in Haifa but he died before. The Zionist Entity resumed its aggression against the Palestinian-controlled area. Overnight occupation forces shelled a Palestinian security position at Deir al-Balah central Gaza Strip, causing severe damage. Also in Qarara town, a Palestinian security post was destroyed. The attack left four Palestinians wounded.
