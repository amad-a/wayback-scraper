June Thursday 07-2001

 Thurs2ay 29-2000

 

Nasrallah warns “Israel” not to invade…(1)

Hizbullah’s secretary-general said Wednesday that “Israel” should not “imagine” it can again invade the country as it did in 1982.
Speaking at a ceremony to honor the Lebanese Units to Resist “Israeli” Occupation, an event held on the occasion of the first anniversary of the liberation of the South, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah urged constant readiness, warning that “the battle is still on.”
A statement issued by the group’s information department said Nasrallah pledged that resistance will continue until all “Israeli”-occupied territory, including the Shibaa Farms, has been liberated.
“Zionist escalation and threats make it imperative to remain active as we are committed to protecting our country and defending our people,” the statement quoted Nasrallah as saying.
The statement also said Nasrallah praised the Units’ “steadfastness” and that he backed their demands to join in operations to liberate Shibaa Farms.
The Units’ commander presented Nasrallah with a symbolic shield in appreciation of his support.

 

Hizbullah urges Palestinians to keep up struggle…(2)

Hizbullah Secretary General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah has called for more suicide martyrdom bombings against “Israel” to end its occupation of the Palestinian territories. Nasrallah, speaking at a rally in Beirut that marked the twelfth anniversary of the death of Iranian revolutionary leader Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini late on Tuesday, said “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon and the US cannot prevent Palestinian suicide martyrdom bombers from breaking through “Israeli” guard posts. The Hizbullah leader's victory over “Israel” is cited as inspiration for the Palestinian uprising against the Jewish state's occupation of the West Bank and Gaza Strip. He urged Palestinian bombers to take their example from Iran and quote "wipe out" “Israel”. The Jewish state withdrew from south Lebanon last May following a series of successful resistance operations. “Israel” has accused Hizbullah of involvement in the Palestinian uprising, a charge the group categorically denies. 

Fadlallah: the intifada needs help…(3)

Arab countries came under fire from the cleric Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah on Wednesday for their failure to support the Palestinian intifada.
“The problem with Arab countries is that they regard the intifada as a burden and a crisis that they want to sweep away by any means,” Fadlallah said in his weekly press conference. “So they are trying to shut all doors the Palestinians are using to continue their intifada.”
He condemned the American peace initiative, describing it as “one of the West’s serious attempts to end “Israel’s” crisis with the Palestinians without forcing Tel Aviv to pay the price exacted by the Palestinian people and authority.”
Fadlallah said that “the Americans imposed a ceiling on Palestinian ambitions by giving Palestinian leaders a choice between accepting the recommendations of the Mitchell Commission report or preparing themselves for a new war that both the Americans and “Israelis” promise to be bitter.”

 

“Israeli” warplanes again violate Lebanese airspace…(4)

“Israeli” warplanes again violated Lebanese airspace Wednesday, breaking the sound barrier over Beirut, reporters said.

Two warplanes flew at medium altitude over south Lebanon before continuing over the Lebanese capital, the same route “Israeli” planes took Monday.

The Lebanese army Sunday fired anti-aircraft batteries on intruding “Israeli” aircraft for the first time since “Israel” ended its 22-year occupation of southern Lebanon in May 2000.

 

“Israel” expells four Lebanese asylum seekers: police…(5)

“Israel” expelled Wednesday four Lebanese who had secretly crossed during the night into the Jewish state to seek asylum, police said.

An “Israeli” military jeep dropped the four in a deserted border region, just ouside the disputed Shibaa Farms, from where they walked toward the village of Shibaa where they were seized by Lebanese police, they said.

They were identified as Sandid Amasha, 22, Akram Saleh Hamed, 27, Hassan Kamal Shehaib, 27, and Wassim Qassem Ghasham, 36.

The four had requested asylum in “Israel” after crossing into the Jewish state late Tuesday, according to the “Israeli” army.

The four, from the town of Hasbaya in southern Lebanon, made a risky entrance into “Israel” over the line of control with Syria by Mount Hermon, near Lebanon, they said.

Lebanese security sources said the four were former members of the South Lebanon Army (SLA), “Israel’s” proxy militia during its 22-year occupation of southern Lebanon until its troop withdrawal in May 2000.

Three of them - Amasha, Hamed and Shehaib - had recently completed terms of between two months and four months in jail in Lebanon for collaboration with the enemy, they said.

The fourth was awaiting trial on the same charges.

When the “Israeli” army pulled out of Lebanon, about 6,000 Lebanese collaborators left the country in fear of retaliation from the local population.

But several hundred of them have now returned to Lebanon and more than 3,000 have been put on trial in Beirut for collaborating with the “Israeli” enemy.

 

High-profile visitor at Khiam…(6)

Syrian comedian Doreid Lahham toured the South in his capacity as a UNICEF goodwill ambassador Wednesday, viewing the Khiam detention center and other relics of the “Israeli” occupation.
The renowned artist, accompanied by his wife Hala, was in the South at Hizbullah’s invitation. In Khiam, a group of former detainees were waiting for him. The actor listened as Mohammed Nayef, an ex-detainee, recounted experiences of torture suffered by prisoners at the hands of “Israelis” and their collaborators.
Lahham said the resistance and Hizbullah “made us understand that nothing is impossible to man’s will. All Arabs should know that those who have no will should sit aside and not hinder other’s will.”
Asked by reporters about his mission as a goodwill ambassador, Lahham said his role consisted in supporting any people who have suffered torture or oppression. “UNICEF is a humanitarian rather than a political organization,” he said. “It cannot stop a war or a conflict, but tries to treat the results of a war.”

 

Court sentences collaborators…(7)

The Military Tribunal sentenced in absentia on Wednesday a former senior Lebanese Forces official to life in prison with hard labor.
Ghassan Touma was convicted of “committing crimes against people and property, undermining state authority, and mounting terrorist acts against citizens and Syrian troops in Lebanon.”
The tribunal also issued two 15-year jail terms in absentia against Elias Ibrahim Haddad and Qassem Ismail Alyan for collaborating with “Israel”, and sentenced 15 people on charges of joining the ranks of the now-defunct SLA, entering “Israel”, and contact with the enemy.

 

Muslim Community in Ukraine Celebrates Days of Liberation…(8)

The Ahlul Beit Association in Odessa and Sanfarubble in Ukraine held in both cities exhibitions and ceremonies to mark the days of liberation and resistance against the “Israeli” occupation in southern Lebanon. On the occasion, a number of Arab and Islamic organizations representatives stressed on the need to support the Palestinian intifadah, and not yield to Zionist and American pressure. They also said Hezbullah’s resistance, which defeated the Zionist enemy, is to be taken as a model.

 

Zionist Settlers With Occupation Army Cover Went on Rampage in Palestinian Areas…(9)

“Israeli” armed settlers went on the rampage in the village of Assamiya on the main road between Ramallah and Nablus, setting fire to homes, greenhouses and a school. Palestinian villager Ihsan Salem, said the settlers had taken up positions on the roofs of Palestinian homes shouting in rage "Death to Arabs". He added the arsenary had cost him over 100,000 dollars. The “Israeli” enemy army and police sent reinforcements to the area opening fire and tear gas on hundreds of villagers who turned out to protect their cultivation after failing to impose a curfew. Two Palestinians were injured, one by a bullet and the other after being beaten. In al-Khalil too, settlers broke into the Palestinian neighborhood beating and stoning the Palestinians. “Israeli” army troops joined in, hitting Palestinians with the butts of their rifles. Two photo journalists were injured in the incident. A settler leader disclosed, a demonstration was planned for tonight, in al-Quds. He demasked the “Israeli” intentions saying "There are those among us who are ashamed to say the truth, the demand is to declare a state of war." Moreover, “Israeli” security officials threatened if the Palestinian Authority did not rearrest what they called "extremists" referring to the Palestinian Mujahedeen, "“Israel”" will take care of them". They added they gave the Palestinians a list including 120 names for the men to be arrested. Meanwhile, the Aqsa Martyrs Brigade reiterated its refusal of “Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon's cabinet threats, reminding that Sharon lies behind several massacres against the Palestinian people. The brigade issued a statement, saying it carried out a series of operations including targeting an occupation army post in Jenin, a settlers' car on the road to Qalqilia and another near Aride Settlement. Al-Aqsa Brigade reaffirmed that what was circulated by media about a joint ceasefire statement is no more than a conspiracy carried out by those who still abuse the power of the Palestinian people. On another level, Palestinian officials affirmed that the measures announced by the “Israeli” enemy to ease the blockade imposed on the West Bank and Gaza Strip had not been fully implemented. A Palestinian official said the “Israeli” army is still closing the Rafah border with Egypt and the Karameh crossing with Jordan. A Palestinian oil company official complained that imports of oil and gas were still not allowed in the Gaza Strip adding that the situation on the ground had become difficult due to fuel shorting. Still more, the occupation barred the transfer of Palestinian Bank deposits in “Israeli” Banks in the Palestinian self-controlled territories what prevented the Palestinian Banks from paying back their dues. Banking sources said the Palestinian Authority filed a complaint against the enemy demanding it to call off this illegal measure. It is worth mentioning that the Palestinian deposits in the “Israeli” banks is estimated by about 25 million dollars.

 

Pro-Palestinian protests in us and France…(10) 

A group of US-Muslim leaders held a news conference and sit-in outside the State Department headquarters in Washington, protesting American support for what they described as “Israel’s” apartheid-like policies toward Palestinians. The consortium of six US-Muslim groups said in a statement it opposes Washington's continued and uncritical support for “Israel’s” quote "immoral, illegal and unjust" policies toward Palestinians in the occupied West Bank and Gaza Strip. They plan more protests, including a demonstration outside the White House later this week. Meanwhile in Paris, some 2,000 pro-Palestinian protestors marched through the French capital calling for a continuation of the intifada despite calls by French President Jacques Chirac for restraint in the Middle East. The demonstrators held banners describing Sharon as a murderer who should be put on trial.
