Hizbullah ‘will shoot back’ if “Israelis” fire across border…(1)

Hizbullah will now retaliate directly against any “Israeli” troops who shoot civilians on the Lebanese side of the border, a party source told The Daily Star Wednesday, raising the specter of cross-border gunbattles at key flashpoints along the frontier.
The warning came a day after Hizbullah’s anti-aircraft batteries fired on “Israeli” warplanes flying over the western sector of the border district.
A UNIFIL officer said that one of the two reported incidents involved an aircraft being targeted by 8 to 10 rounds of anti-aircraft fire from just south of At-Tiri village near Bint Jbeil.
It was the first time that “Israeli” warplanes have been shot at by the resistance since overflights resumed last October and, according to the Hizbullah source, marks the beginning of a new retaliatory policy.
“We will return fire if the “Israelis” violate our border,” the source said. “This is the decision we have taken. We cannot keep silent on the daily violations any longer and we want to make the “Israelis” understand this.”
He said that retaliation would not just be confined to “Israeli” aircraft flying in Lebanese airspace.
“If the “Israelis” kill or wound Lebanese civilians from one of their outposts, then we are obliged to return fire directly,” the source said. “If they fired from their post on Sheikh Abbad Hill, for example, then we will return fire at the Abbad Hill post.”
“Israeli” troops have killed three civilians and wounded at least 29, four of them this year, in border demonstrations, mainly at the former Fatima Gate crossing in Kfar Kila and at Sheikh Abbad Hill.
The latest incident occurred on May 26 when a civilian was shot in the leg at Sheikh Abbad Hill during a demonstration marking the first anniversary of the “Israeli” withdrawal.
The prospect of shootouts blazing across the border fence, however, is likely to increase the level of tension beyond the existing theater of conflict in the Shibaa Farms to along the border with “Israel”.
Since the “Israeli” withdrawal, Hizbullah has refrained from responding to border shooting incidents and has been careful to limit its activities to the remote Shibaa Farms territory rather than launching operations directly into “Israel”.
Combatting “Israeli” violations of the UN-delineated Blue Line might restore some of the international legitimacy Hizbullah enjoyed during the occupation of the South but lost when it launched the Shibaa Farms campaign last October.
Of more importance to Hizbullah, however, is the ability to maintain pressure on the “Israeli” Army beyond the confines of the Shibaa Farms, particularly at a time when the stakes may be unacceptably high for further attacks in the disputed territory.
“Israel” reportedly conveyed strong warnings to Damascus last month that Syrian military positions in Lebanon would be targeted if Hizbullah continued attacking “Israeli” forces occupying the farms.
In mid-April, a Syrian radar station was destroyed by “Israeli” warplanes in response to a Hizbullah missile attack in which an “Israeli” soldier was killed.

Hizbullah Fields Strella Missiles Against “Israel’s” Air Force…(2)

Hizbullah has fielded a new air defense system to confront “Israel’s” unrelenting 'sonic war' in advance of U.N. Secretary-General Kofi Annan's visit to Lebanon.
A Hizbullah communiqué highlighted by the Beirut media Wednesday said Party of God's air defense unit had fired twice on intruding “Israeli” fighter jets over Bint Jbeil and Tyre Tuesday.
No hits were claimed in the communiqué that reported the first Hizbullah interception of “Israeli” fighter jets since the Jewish state terminated its 22-year occupation of south Lebanon nearly 13 months ago.
An-Nahar said two surface-to-air missiles were shoulder-fired on the planes over Zibkin near Tyre in addition to automatic fire from anti-aircraft guns.
The Soviet-designed Sam-7 and Sam-9 missiles are the only heat-seeking anti-aircraft rockets that are shoulder-fired. They are known as Strella missiles.
“Israeli” pilots have a standard practice of releasing heat balloons to deflect ground-to-air projectiles.
Media reports said the development indicated that Hizbullah fighters would henceforth be firing at intruding “Israeli” warplanes and navy gunboats in Lebanon's territorial waters hostilities with “Israel” well beyond the Shabaa farms.
Media commentators said the aerial hostilities were expected to figure high in Annan's talks with President Lahoud, Premier Hariri, Speaker Berri and Foreign Minister Mahmoud Hammoud on Friday and Saturday.
Annan's personal representative Steffan de Mistura disclosed on Tuesday that the secretary-general would not be visiting the UNIFIL peacekeeping force in south Lebanon or inspecting the Blue Line as the local media had widely anticipated.

 

“Israeli” Enemy Rejoices that Captives Are Still Alive, But Considers Nasrallah's Speech As Part of Psychological War…(3)

Hizbullah Secretary-General’s speech was turned into a subject of debate and analysis by the Zionist Channel One television. The channel considered Nasrallah’s words as part of the psychological war Hizbullah launches against the Zionist Entity. The Channel One news reader said for the first time Nasrallah says that there are living “Israeli” soldiers in Hizbullah’s hands. This should be valued, he added. For his part, the Zionist analyst for Arab affairs Oded Gernot said Sayyed Nasrallah spoke in plural that their are living captives, and advised "“Israel”" not to take risks because those can die.

 

Hammoud seeks Arab unity to face “Israel”…(4)

Foreign Minister Mahmoud Hammoud will seek Arab support for devising ways to confront repeated “Israeli” threats, informed sources said on Wednesday.
Hammoud will raise the issue at a meeting of the Arab Follow-Up Committee in Amman on Monday. The Committee was set up in Cairo last year to discuss support measures for the Palestinian intifada. The sources said other issues to be raised included support for the release of Lebanese detainees held in “Israeli” jails, participation in Lebanon’s de-mining operations, and backing the liberation of the Shibaa Farms.

 

“Israeli” dead in shooting attack south of Al-Quds…(5)

 An “Israeli” security official, was killed in a shooting attack that took place a shortly after 10:00 on the Bethlehem bypass road.
Another passenger in the vehicle, reportedly a guard in his 20's, sustained moderate wounds to the neck and jaw in the attack.
A third person in the vehicle was apparently uninjured in the attack.
Initial reports say the vehicle was cut off and stopped by a Palestinian car in an apparently planned assassination as it traveled southbound on the road some 300 meters before the entrance to the first tunnel, according to an “Israel” Radio report.
The gunman got out of his vehicle, approached the “Israeli” car and shot and killed the “Israeli”, then fleeing into a nearby olive grove.
The third passenger, who was sitting in the back seat of the vehicle, ran after the attacker and shot and killed him.
The guard was transferred to the trauma department of Hadassah-University Hospital, Ein Kerem.
The road connects Al-Quds to the Gush Etzion bloc of communities to the south.
The attack took place some 20 hours after the beginning of the cease-fire between “Israel” and the Palestinian Authority.

 

Palestinian Resistance Fighters Challenge Security Talks, Continue Operations Against the Occupation…(6)

Despite a US-brokered ceasefire deal, Palestinian mujahedeen continued their struggle against the Zionist Entity, and targeted an “Israeli” settler's car while he was passing in Irtah in Tolkarm. Also in the Gaza Strip, Palestinians fire machine guns at an occupation post close to the Neve Dekalim settlement. No injuries were reported. Earlier morning, “Israeli” enemy army reported that Palestinians opened fired from a car at Ofra settlement in the West Bank leaving an “Israeli” woman injured in the neck. The shooting followed the killing of an “Israeli” official by Palestinian gunfire Tuesday night in the West Bank. An “Israeli” army spokesman claimed that the man was a Greek Orthodox monk. Overnight, a Jewish settler was shot dead in east Quds (Al-Quds). Also an “Israeli” official was injured during clashes. “Israeli” enemy radio reported that occupation forces had come under fire at the Rafah border, but reported no injuries. Also in Nablus, an “Israeli” soldier was wounded when an army checkpoint at the Jewish settlement of Shavei Shomron in the northern West Bank, came under Palestinian fire, the radio said. The radio added that troops also came under fire near the Itamar settlement south of Nablus. Meanwhile, “Israeli” occupation troops continued their aggressions against Palestinians. Three Palestinians were wounded by shrapnel from “Israeli” tank shells fired on residential areas in the southern Gaza Strip near the “Israeli”-controlled border with Egypt. The shelling set fire to a station gas and damaged several homes in Salaheddeen Gate region and al-Salam residential area. Palestinian witnesses also said the “Israeli” army opened fire with machine guns on residential areas in Khan Yunis in the southern Gaza Strip, causing damage to buildings.

 

Barghouti: The intifada will continue…(7)

The Palestinian people cannot accept the cease-fire arrangement brokered by CIA Director George Tenet, Fatah General Secretary Marwan Barghouti said yesterday.
Speaking to Al-Quds Post Radio, Barghouti claimed Palestinian Authority Chairman Yasser Arafat was pressured into agreeing to what he called an understanding.
"The Palestinians are not convinced and of course we refuse any agreement, any understanding which will not guarantee the “Israelis”' full withdrawal from the occupied territories," he said. "The intifada will continue and it will represent the will of the Palestinian people."
The intifada did not start by an order, said Barghouti, and it will not be ended by an order.
He made it absolutely clear Fatah will only operate in the territories and in Al-Quds.
Asked who was responsible for yesterday's shooting attacks in Samaria in which two “Israelis” were wounded, Barghouti said he understood Fatah's military wing had taken responsibility for the acts.
Hamas and Islamic Jihad said yesterday they would not abide by Arafat's acceptance of the cease-fire and would continue their fight against “Israel”.
"This is the will of the Palestinian people, and the future will witness a continuation of resistance and intifada," senior Hamas official Abdel Aziz Rantissi said.
Islamic Jihad, in a faxed statement to Reuters in Beirut, said it regretted the PA's acceptance of the truce.
"We in Islamic Jihad regard this step by the leadership of the Palestinian Authority... as a dangerous disappointment that holds grave danger for the unity of our people," the statement said.
The deal, it said, "could waste all their sacrifices and the blood of hundreds of martyrs and tens of thousands of wounded through... courageous months of uprising."

Palestinians Voice Opposition to Tenet's Plan, Vows to Continue Intifadah…(8)

Islamic Palestinian movements Hamas and Jihad vowed Wednesday to continue their armed struggle against the “Israeli” enemy despite the ceasefire deal accepted by the Palestinian Authority. Hamas leader in the Gaza Strip Ismail Abou Shanab said that whatever the pressures exercised by the Palestinian Authority, the intifadah will continue because the occupation and the settlers remain. Likewise the Islamic Jihad rejected the Tenet plan, which constitutes an American plot to “abort the intifadah”. Jihad member Mohammed Hindi said there will only be a lull for a few weeks, but a new explosion will follow on the ground. In the West Bank of Ramallah, more than a thousand Palestinians held a rally Tuesday night to protest against Tenet’s plan. The Protestors headed to the office of Palestinian Chairman, Yasser Arafat, who was convening with CIA Chief George Tenet. They reiterated anti-CIA slogans. In Gaza, and upon the request of Islamic and National forces, hundreds of Palestinians set to the streets of Alshateh refugee camp to denounce the American pressure exerted on the Palestinian Authority.

 

Jibril vows to continue arms shipments…(9)

Palestinian militant Ahmad Jibril has vowed his group will keep trying to smuggle weapons to Palestinians for their uprising against “Israel”.
“We are going to continue sending weapons as long as the “Israeli” occupation of the Palestinian territories continues,” the head of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-General Command (PFLP-GC) told al-Mustaqbal newspaper. “It is the duty of all Palestinians and Arabs to support the intifada by all means,” he said.
The “Israeli” military said in May it had intercepted a Lebanese fishing boat bound for Gaza with 40 tons of weapons on board, including anti-aircraft and anti-tank missiles, as well as Katyusha rockets. Jibril claimed responsibility for the failed arms delivery, but said the shipment was only four tons, and vowed to continue his efforts.
Later in the month Jibril urged the oil-rich emirate of Kuwait to finance him in order to buy weapons for the Palestinian territories.

 

Sharon Admits the Zionist Entity Faces Economic Hardships Due to Intifadah…(10)

“Israeli” enemy Premier Ariel Sharon admitted that his country is facing huge economic hardships as a result of the Palestinian Intifada. He pledged to allot whatever money needed to guarantee the security of the “Israelis”, but Sharon said this requires lowering the spending in other areas. Sharon called on his ministers to stop asking for more financial allotments saying “we are in a contingent situation and we need to set the priorities”. He clarified that military spending has increased to confront the Palestinian intifada and this has forced the government to lower its spending in other areas.
