Hizbullah: ‘no Iranians on border’…(1)

Hizbullah on Monday denied “Israeli” press reports claiming that Iranian intelligence forces had deployed along the border to gather information in preparation for guerrilla attacks against the Jewish state.
“Israel’s” Yediot Ahronot daily reported Monday that dozens of Iranian Revolutionary Guards arrived in Beirut a month ago for briefings with members of Hizbullah.
The Iranian intelligence men, being more professional than their Hizbullah counterparts, decided to check the situation on the ground for themselves and devise possible plans of action, Yediot said, quoting a report on “Israel’s” Channel 2 television.
The newspaper claimed that the Iranian agents had established an observation post on Sheikh Abbad Hill, facing a new “Israeli” outpost.
“From time to time the “Israeli” press works on fabricating baseless news,” Hizbullah said in a statement. “We have learned from the “Israeli” enemy that whenever they want to pave the way for an aggression they document such lies.”
Security sources in the South also dismissed the report.
“If anything, it’s a case of Hizbullah feeding the Iranians information, not the other way around,” said a UNIFIL officer who spoke on condition of anonymity.

 

“Israeli” soldiers threaten shepherd…(2)

An “Israeli” patrol threatened to shoot a shepherd Monday as he approached the border near Metulla, the second such incident in the past two weeks.
Four “Israeli” soldiers pointed their weapons at Taher Hussein Merhi, 65, when he refused to leave his field. Merhi then collected his flock and moved away.
Earlier in the month, three farmers were ordered by “Israeli” troops to stop harvesting their wheat and leave their field opposite Metulla.

 

Lebanese lawyer wants to bring Sharon and Peres before the courts…(3)

“ A Lebanese lawyer close to the fundamentalist Shiite group Hizbullah asked Lebanese courts Monday to put “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon and his foreign minister, Shimon Peres, on trial for war crimes against Lebanon.

May Khansa filed a complaint with State Prosecutor Adnan Addoum against Sharon and Peres for 73 "crimes" committed against Lebanon since the creation of the state of “Israel” in 1948.

Addoum told AFP the complaint had been registered by his office but added that he did not yet know how he would handle it.

He nevertheless said that if the two were found guilty, an international arrest warrant would be issued against them, via Interpol.

The prosecutor also said he had forwarded the complaint to the military intelligence services, adding he had asked them to send him the results of their investigation as quickly as possible.

Among the "crimes" Khansa blames on the two “Israeli” leaders are the invasion of Lebanon and the Sabra and Shatila Palestinian refugee camp massacres in 1982, the capture of two fundamentalist leaders, Abdel Karim Obeid in 1989 and Moustapha Dirani in 1994, the assassination of the secretary general of the Shiite movement Hizbullah, Abbas Moussaoui, in 1992, and the massacre of 105 Lebanese civilians in Qana during the shelling of a UN base in

Under Lebanese law, “Israel’s” premier and the former Nobel peace prize winner could incur the death penalty.

Khansa wants the trial to be held on central Beirut's Martyrs Square, which was devastated by the 1975-1990 civil war, where she suggested gallows should be put up.

 

Failed “Israeli” Occupation Assassination Attempt of Islamic Jihad Movement Follows the Killing of 3…(4)

In a new “Israeli” occupation assassination attempt, a member of the Islamic Jihad Movement was seriously injured in a car blast in the West Bank on Monday. Imad Abu Diab, 22, was driving the car when it blew up inside the Tulkarem refugee camp in the northern West Bank, witnesses said, adding that a passer-by was also injured. The Palestinian Authority has accused "“Israel”" of carrying out more than 30 assassinations of Palestinian activists since the start of the uprising against “Israeli” occupation in late September. The Zionist Entity is demanding that the Palestinian Authority put back behind bars dozens of intifadah activists from Islamic Jihad and Hamas. But Palestinian international cooperation minister Nabil Shaath told a press conference in Luxembourg after meeting EU foreign ministers that the Palestinian Authority would not “serve as a policeman for “Israel”.” On the other hand, several attacks were launched against “Israeli” occupation forces after the brutal killing of three Palestinian women in the central Gaza Strip. “Israeli” enemy forces reported that four mortar shells hit the Kfar Darom settlement but no damages or injuries were reported. Also enemy bomb expertise dismantled a bomb which was planted close to Ramim settlement east Tolkarm. In east Quds (Al-Quds) occupation forces demolished a Palestinian home. The owner of the house, Faraj Hirbawi, said he had not been warned about the demolition order and said he was in the process of gaining authorization for the building. The “Israelis” continued their attacks as they razed Palestinian land close to the Deir Al-Balah camp. They also closed the road with sand bags banning Palestinians from going in and out. “Israeli” enemy military source said nine mortar shells were fired late Sunday on the Jewish settlement of Kfar Darom in the southern Gaza Strip. In a separate incident, Palestinians opened fire with automatic weapons on the Netzarim settlement, south of Gaza City, late Sunday. An “Israeli” army patrol also came under fire near the locality of Beitunia, near the West Bank town of Ramallah. A Palestinian source said occupation forces and Palestinians exchanged fire near the village of Beit Rima, also near Ramallah, but said no injuries had been reported. Meanwhile, a military source said a bomb exploded early Monday at an “Israeli” roadblock on the border with the West Bank near the town of Um el Fahem. The blast occurred near two jeeps belonging to border guards preventing Palestinians from entering the occupied territories under the current blockade. This comes after three Palestinian women from the same family were killed in an “Israeli” enemy shelling on homes in the village of Sheikh Ajlin in the central Gaza Strip late Saturday. Palestinian information minister Yasser Abed Rabbo said the killing is a clear sign that “Israeli” enemy Premier Ariel Sharon and his government are not striving for a serious ceasefire.” He added that the killing of three women is an indication to the world that the occupation military is continuing the killing and terror against the Palestinian people and that the people are practicing their natural right of resisting this occupation and defending themselves. Palestinians in the Gaza Strip found it difficult to hold back their anger, as they buried the three women. Fatah, Jihad and Hamas factions vowed to fight until the end, with stones, with everything they‘ve got.

 

Fatah West Bank Leader Says One Can Not Depend On US Role For It Lies Behind the Blockade…(5)

Secretary of the Fatah movement in the West Bank Marwan Barghouti said that the Intifadah has put the security coordination issue and the series of arrests behind its back. In a phone call with al-Manar Channel, Barghouti assured that Fatah disapproves arresting any Palestinian whether he belongs to Hamas, Fatah, Jihad or any other Palestinian group. He added that as long as the “Israeli” occupation forces keep on attacking the Palestinian people, then there will be no ceasefire and the Intifadah will go on until the occupation ends. Barghouti said that the Palestinian people do not reverberate to the Mitchell report, or any other initiative, adding that its impossible to bet on the American role in the region because it is the one responsible for the blockade on the Palestinians.

 

Knesset Member Bishara's Call For Greater Popular Resistance Causes Zionist Uproar…(6)

The “Israeli” Knesset member Azmi Bishara was at the centre of a storm Monday over his call for greater popular resistance against the Zionist Entity during the ceremony in Syria for the late president Hafez Assad. Bishara faces a possible police investigation over his remarks, which prompted one right-wing “Israeli” Knesset member to call for him to be put before a firing squad. The leader of the left-wing “Israeli” Arab Balad party accused the rightist dominated government of “Israeli” enemy Prime Minister Ariel Sharon of leading towards war, saying “Today, “Israel” puts forth a choice -- either accept “Israel’s” dictates - or full- scale war.” Zionist Attorney General Eliyakim Rubinstein said that when Bishara crosses back into "“Israel”" from Syria, police and the state prosecutor's office will consider taking action against him. Bishara however, scoffed at the controversy, saying in a statement that he had made similar remarks “countless times” in "“Israel”", even in parliament.

 

Palestinian survey: 90 percent of youth support suicide martyrdom attacks…(7)

A survey conducted by researchers at the Bir Zeit University in the West Bank revealed that 90 percent of Palestinian youth support suicide martyrdom attacks and that amongst Palestinians there has been a 50 percent increase in support for suicide martyrdom bombings, “Israel” Radio reported Tuesday.

In the general population, support for suicide martyrdom attacks stands at some 75 percent.

 

B'Tselem: “Israeli” roadblocks cost Palestinian lives…(8)

The “Israeli” Army continues to detain sick Palestinians on their way to hospital - some with life threatening conditions - at roadblocks inside the territories, according to a report by the humans rights organization, B'Tselem, to be released today.
The report claims “Israel” is continuing to hamper the administration of medical treatment within the territories with its policy of closures .
B'Tselem's report contains a list of such incidents recorded by its researchers. In some of the cases documented, patients even died after being held up at the roadblocks - and these are not isolated incidents according to the organization.
The “Israeli” troops had promised there are strict guidelines in place to prevent ambulances being held up at roadblocks and checkpoints in times of emergency. But B'Tselem says that ambulances carrying sick or injured Palestinians continue to be help up unnecessarily.
According to the report, the “Israeli” troops prevents patients from receiving vital medical attention by setting up roadblocks made up of mounds of earth or concrete blocks that area placed across the road, or with roadblocks manned constantly by “Israeli” soldiers. Palestinians are thus forced to take lengthy and convoluted detours to avoid the roadblocks and reach the medical centers.
In those instances where the “Israeli” Army leaves one road - under the tight supervision of soldiers - open, the ambulances often lose precious time looking for the chosen road, since it changes often without the residents being informed.
In one incident recorded by B'Tselem on June 7, an “Israeli” soldier manning a roadblock ordered a Palestinian on his way to dialysis treatment to turn back, saying there was no entry.
In another case, an 11-year-old girl in need of hospitalization was held up at a roadblock near Nablus for 45-minutes, even though the soldiers saw her vomit and that she was obviously in pain. The girl died before she reached the hospital, B'Tselem said.
The report states further that doctors frequently encounter problems and delays on their way to work, and that the road closures make it even more difficult to get water to remote areas. There are also problems with regular refuse collection, exposing the residents of the territories to serious health risks, they added.
B'Tselem's report calls “Israel’s” policy of restricting the movement of Palestinians "collective punishment" and charges it with "directly inflicting suffering on the civilian population which is not involved in acts of violence."
The report says that one can only imagine the sorts of problems people who "only" want to get from one place to another must encounter, if ambulances and sick people encounter so many delays and problems.

 

“Israeli” ruse creates a deadly ‘Wonderland’…(9)

In 1865, the British author Lewis Carroll wrote the classic Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland. On the surface, it was a children’s book but, in fact, it was a clever satire mocking the absurdity and mendacity of authority.
I was reminded of Alice this week as I read “Israeli” press reports noting that the United States was close to achieving an “historic agreement” with “Israel” regarding a “settlement freeze.”
According to the reports, “construction beyond current built-up areas will be frozen.” Other points in the reported agreement state that: No new settlements will be created; no additional land will be expropriated for the purpose of construction; but exceptions will still be allowed for new roads.
By definition, then, this freeze is no freeze at all. This “historic agreement” appears to be nothing more than a repetition of past “Israeli” commitments which have been honored more in the way they have been broken than in their observance.
It is quite bizarre that we are even discussing such a settlement freeze in 2001. In fact it was about one decade ago that then Secretary of State James Baker achieved a historic compromise of his own. In an effort to build the confidence necessary to coax Arabs and “Israelis” toward peace, Baker succeeded in getting a number of major Arab governments to agree to terminate enforcement of their secondary economic boycott of “Israel” in exchange for “Israel’s” agreement to a settlement freeze.
Since that time “Israel’s” settlement presence in the Occupied Territories has doubled! While the “Israelis” continue to claim they have not built new settlements and have only allowed for natural growth of existing settlements, their claims are patently false. Today, what “Israel” calls “greater Al-Quds” is virtually surrounded by a “Great Wall” of new concrete settlements, which “Israel” calls “extensions of existing neighborhoods.” In fact they spread from hill to hill, ringing the city. Palestinian villages have been trapped within these “Israeli” compounds and strangled.
And while the “Israelis” continue to claim that they will not confiscate “new land” on which to build these extensions, this too is an artful fabrication. Large areas of Palestinian land surrounding settlements have already been seized by “Israel” and are defined as “state” (that is, “Israeli”-controlled) lands.
Aside from all of this, there are, in fact, dozens of new settlements that have been developed in the past decade, some as so-called “private” ventures, a practice by which successive “Israeli” governments have attempted to absolve themselves of responsibility. But all receive government assistance in the form of infrastructure support and protection.
As damaging as this cancerous growth of new housing has been to the Palestinians, the dramatic expansion of the network of “Jewish only” security roads, in reality super highways connecting many of the settlements to Al-Quds and Tel Aviv, has been even worse. Large swatches of Palestinian land have been taken and declared off-limits to facilitate the construction and operation of these roads. The impact of this network of roads has been to cut the Occupied Territories into pieces.
All of this has been in direct fulfillment of the original Likud plan for the Occupied Territories first defined in an October 1978 World Zionist Organization “white paper” entitled Master Plan for Development of Settlement in Judea and Samaria 1979-1983. Written by Matityahu Drobles, it was described as a plan “making concrete and realizing our right to Eretz “Israel”.” In the paper, Drobles lays out in detail a plan to construct “settlements and roads around the settlements of the minorities (i.e. the Palestinians), but also in between them, this in accordance with the settlement policy adopted in the Galilee.”
Describing the intent of this plan, the then- economics editor of the Al-Quds Post, Meir Merhav, told Time Magazine in March of 1980 that the West Bank “is to be carved up by a grid of roads, settlements and strongholds into a score of little bantustans so that (the Palestinians) shall never coalesce again into a contiguous area that can support autonomous, let alone, independent existence.”
All of this, of course, was verbally opposed by successive US Presidents from Carter to Clinton. Whether decrying the settlements as “illegal,” “obstacles to peace,” “unhelpful unilateral acts”, or as “provocative” and as “impediments,” opposition was clear ­ but no action, forceful and definitive, was taken to bring about a halt in their growth.
So here we are today, 20 years after Merhav’s fateful analysis and Carter’s stern warnings, 10 years after Madrid and Baker’s “historic compromise,” and the Drobles Plan is near completion. In violation of unenforced international law, 400,000 “Israelis” have been settled in the Occupied Territories (including what “Israel” calls “annexed” East Al-Quds), and the Territories have been carved up into pieces. More than that, tens of thousands of these settlers are a hostile and dangerous vigilante presence in the Occupied Territories armed not only with weapons but an uncompromising theologically based ideology of entitlement and conquest.
Many “Israelis” understand the dangers posed by this situation. They know that despite the rhetoric of a new “freeze,” the intent of the government remains the same.
As Yossi Sarid, a leader of the Meretz Party noted, “when you build new settlements or expand existing settlements, it means that you want to persist with the occupation and deepen it.” And prominent “Israeli” military analyst Ze’ev Shiff observed that “most of “Israel’s” governments have tried various ploys regarding the settlements … the excuses were absurd, because everyone understood that there was only one interpretation for settlement expansion: the creation of facts intended to help “Israel” close off the option of the Palestinians being able to establish a viable state.”
And the former deputy mayor of Al-Quds, Meron Benvenisti, dismissed the ruse of a “freeze” by noting the settlement enterprise is “not a demographic-physical matter that can be measured by the number of populated apartments and the increase in the number of settlers. It is a multi-headed hydra controlling millions of acres, water sources, roads, economic endeavors, huge budgets and especially unlimited real estate potential.”
The Mitchell report was quite clear. “A cessation of Palestinian-”Israeli” violence will be politically hard to sustain unless the government of “Israel” freezes all settlement activity … including ‘natural growth’ of existing settlements.” The reports of the “historic agreement” are a dramatic step back from Mitchell’s recommendation and, as such, represent an accommodation to “Israel’s” intent to strengthen and entrench its presence in the Occupied Territories.
So excuse me if I remain unconvinced by this latest “settlement freeze,” full as it is of loopholes and exceptions. I’ve visited this “Wonderland” before.

James J. Zogby, president of the Arab American Institute, writes a syndicated column
