Two Belgian suits pending against Sharon for 1982 Lebanon massacres…(1)

“ Two class action suits are pending in Belgium against “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon, charging him with responsibility in the 1982 massacres of hundreds of Palestinian refugees in Lebanon, the public prosecutor said Wednesday.

The first suit, lodged early this month by an ad hoc committee of Palestinians, Lebanese, Moroccans and Belgians, alleges Sharon was responsible for the 1982 massacres of up to 2,000 Palestinian civilians in the Sabra and Chatila refugee camps.

The second, charging crimes against humanity, genocide and war crimes, was filed on Monday by 23 alleged survivors of, and five witnesses to, the massacres.

An investigating magistrate has been assigned to determine whether the cases are admissible under a unique 1993 Belgian law that makes it possible for Belgian courts to try cases of genocide and other crimes against humanity that took place in foreign countries.

The two suits accuse Sharon of allowing Christian militias to slaughter between 800 and 2,000 Palestinian refugees at the Sabra and Shatila camps located in an area controlled by the “Israeli” military after “Israel’s” 1982 invasion of Lebanon, when Sharon was defense minister.

An “Israeli” commission of enquiry in 1983 found Sharon indirectly responsible for the killings, a finding that forced him to resign his post.

Last January, when he was campaigning for prime minister, Sharon expressed his regrets for the "terrible tragedy" of the 1982 massacres, but refused to apologise.

"What it was," he said in a press interview, "was an act of killing carried out by Arab Christians against Arab Muslims."

Arabs also blame Sharon for provoking the current intifada or Palestinian uprising with his visit to east Al-Quds's al-Aqsa mosque compound, the third holiest site in Islam, last September 28.

 

Collaborator security chief gives himself up to Lebanese authorities…(2)

“ A man who had been a senior security official in the now-defunct “Israeli” proxy militia in south Lebanon gave himself up to Lebanese authorities Wednesday, police said.

Fouad Habani, 65, who had been security chief for the South Lebanon Army (SLA) in the Marjayoun-Qlaiaa sector, and his wife, Mariam Fares, crossed the border from “Israel” at Naqoura. They were met by officials of the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon and taken to a Lebanese army post.

Fouad and his wife were among the some 6,500 former members of the SLA and family members who, fearing reprisals, fled to “Israel” in May 2000 following the “Israeli” withdrawal from southern Lebanon after a 22-year-occupation.

Many of these people have complained about their living conditions in “Israel”, and some have returned home.

A Beirut military court began trying people for collaborating with the "“Israeli” enemy" only 10 days after the “Israeli” pullout, and more than 2,600 people have gone before the tribunal.

So far, 31 have been sentenced to death, all of them in absentia, and many other sent to jail.

 

A Palestinian Martyr, Several Injured in More Zionist Aggressions, Resistance Fighters Retaliate By…(3)

A Zionist Settler was shot dead by Palestinian fighters near the settlement of Homesh in the northern West Bank, the same area where two Jewish settlers were gunned down by Palestinians in separate attacks on Monday, settler sources said. Earlier, a Palestinian man died after being shot by “Israeli” occupation troops near a military checkpoint in the West Bank northwest of Ramallah, “Israeli” army radio reported. The shooting occurred near the settlement of Kiryat . Also a pipe bomb exploded in the city of Khadera Wednesday morning, in occupied Palestinian territories since 1948, “Israeli” enemy Radio claimed no injuries. The bomb had been hidden in a trash can on a street corner in the center of the city, one block away from the central bus station. Large numbers of “Israeli” occupation police rushed to the scene and sappers began checking the area in case more bombs had been planted. An organization called the Popular Army Vanguards claimed responsibility of the attack. A spokesman of the organization told al-Manar T.V, via a phone call, the attack came in retaliation of the assassination of Palestinian Intifadah activists that the “Israeli” enemy is perpetrating and to stress the continuation of the resistance. Khadera has been the scene of several bombings by Palestinian Mujahedeen, most recently three weeks ago, when a car bomb exploded in the city center, causing several casualties. This is the first attack in the territories occupied in 1948 since the cease-fire engineered by CIA Director George Tennet on June 13. In a new “Israeli” assassination attempt, an explosion went off in a store that sells cellular phones injuring the store owner who is believed to be one of Fatah movement cadres. Sources said an explosion went off on Faisal Street in a store lightly injuring the store owner Ahmad al-Ma’ani 45 years. Police and Sappers rushed to the scene. Meanwhile, two “Israeli” enemy tanks drove 200 meters into a Palestinian-ruled area in the south of the Gaza Strip today. According to Palestinian security sources, the tanks halted east of Qarara, near the Gush Katif Jewish settlements, and were still there an hour later, around midday. The sources added, that large numbers of occupation troops blockaded the Ras-el-Bustan area in al-Aizareyyeh east of the occupied Quds. “Israeli” occupation forces today broke into the village of Kfar Silvit and raided several civilian houses and tampered its contents. The occupation troops also injured last night four Palestinian youths in Khan Younis south of Gaza. At the meantime, armed settlers from the Ma’ali Afrayeem settlement set up a camp on a road north of the West Bank demanding that occupation forces build a permanent military post there. Palestinian security officials reiterated their orders to increase search operations aiming to stop mortar attacks on Jewish settlements.

 

Arafat: “Israel” trying to fool public opinion…(4)

Palestinian Authority Chairman Yasser Arafat yesterday accused “Israel” of deceiving the international community and lying about its intention to abide by the US brokered cease-fire.
"It is an attempt to deceive international public opinion," Arafat told reporters. "They [the “Israelis”] are still firing from their tanks and machine guns and are still using internationally-banned weapons, and the settlers are pursuing their crimes under the protection of the “Israeli” army. So their claims they are committed to a cease-fire is a lie."
Arafat was responding to a declaration by the “Israeli” security cabinet yesterday which held him responsible for the recent wave of fatal Palestinian shooting attacks, but stated that “Israel” would remain committed to the cease-fire plan of CIA director George Tenet.
The PA issued a statement last night condemning the killing of “Israeli” or Palestinian citizens, and expressed its commitment to the Sharm e-Sheikh Agreement, the Mitchell Report and the Tenet document. In addition it called on “Israel” to lift the closure and all restrictions imposed on Palestinian areas in the West Bank and Gaza.
One of the two scheduled security meetings dealing with setting a timetable for the implementation of all the Tenet paper was canceled. However, the higher security committee met in Al-Quds last night in the presence of the CIA. “Israel” was expected to present a timetable for the redeployment of its troops to the pre-intifada positions, once the shooting attacks stop.
Bassam Abu Sharif, an adviser to Arafat, said before the meeting that the sides were likely to discuss the timetable even if it would not yet be implemented.
A senior Palestinian security official condemned yesterday “Israel’s” large scale arrests of Palestinians at “Israeli” road blocks, and said that releasing these prisoners would help calm the situation. The issue was to be raised by the Palestinians at the security meeting. The security official said “Israel” was trying to detain the largest possible number of Palestinian civilians to use them as leverage in future negotiations.
Palestinian security officials said “Israel” was pressing the issue of arrest of Palestinian gunmen and militants but were not willing to do anything to relieve the Palestinian suffering simultaneously, like ease the closure.
"As long as the “Israelis” only look at their suffering, ignoring the suffering of the Palestinians, we will not succeed in implementing the Tenet paper," West Bank Preventive Security chief Jibril Rajoub told The Jerusalem Post.
"The sudden increase of shooting attacks is due to the settlers' attacks on Palestinians...the Palestinians will defend themselves even if this will cost them 10,000 martyrs", Abu Sharif said.
He claimed that Prime Minister Ariel Sharon gave an order to protect armed settlers when they attack Palestinian villages.
The Palestinians are still refusing to arrest those who violate the cease-fire in areas B and C and then hide in area A, and refuse to re-arrest Hamas and Islamic Jihad members unless “Israel” does its part as outlined in the Tenet paper and agrees to move forward with the implementation of the Mitchell Report. Arafat wants to link the cease-fire to the resumption of talks, arguing that the cease-fire is part of the Mitchell package.
Earlier yesterday in Cairo, Arafat accused “Israel” of purposefully delaying peace talks.
He met with Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak and briefed him on the status of the cease-fire. The two also discussed ways Egypt can help to push for the implementation of the Mitchell Report and the resumption of final status talks.
"The “Israelis” are still besieging [Palestinian] villages, cities and camps," Arafat said. "The “Israelis” were supposed to pull out within 48 hours to their former positions and, at the same time, [the two sides] were supposed to have begun the diplomatic process."
In Gaza, Palestinian security officials renewed their call to halt all mortar attacks. The security officers met through Tuesday night and decided to confiscate mortars and renew the order to stop such attacks, Palestinian security sources said.
Palestinian official also denied that the PA's cease-fire order applies only to area A, but said the PA can only enforce it there, since “Israel” does not permit the Palestinian security to act and move in areas still under “Israeli” security control.

 

British PM refuses to meet Sharon…(5)

British Prime Minister Tony Blair has refused to meet with Prime Minister Ariel Sharon during a layover on the latter's visit to the United States next week.
Blair reportedly refuses to meet with the “Israeli” leader in the wake of a BBC investigative program documenting Sharon's role in the 1982 Sabra and Shatilla massacres in Lebanon.
Sharon's request to meet with Blair during a stop in London next week was turned down by the British due to what was called Blair's busy schedule, Army Radio reported.
However, the British reportedly fear that certain parties may issue legal motions in the British court system requesting Sharon's arrest.

Under Pressure from the Intifadah Zionist Settlers Consider Leaving Palestinian Territories…(6)

The intifadah has pushed Zionist settlers to reconsider their presence in Palestinian territories. According to a poll published in the Zionist daily Yediot Aharonot, a total of 19 percent of the settlers questioned would consider pulling out from the Gaza Strip and the West Bank “on condition of being able to obtain a home to replace that which they would have to abandon”. Another 24 percent said they would be unlikely to leave while the majority, 54 percent, were adamant that they were not going anywhere. These are religious settlers, who have ideological reasons for choosing to occupy Palestinian land. Some 200,000 Jews live in 150 settlements in the West Bank and Gaza Strip.

 

Jose Bove takes his magic potion to the West Bank…(7)

Jose Bove thrust out his walrus moustache, threw a defiant scowl at the “Israeli” policemen and soldiers barring his way, linked arms with his friends and then started to shove at the wall of muscle and guns before him.

The celebrated French sheep farmer ­ wrecker of his local McDonalds fast food outlet, destroyer of genetically-modified crops and commando-in-chief in the anti-globalisation war ­ arrived in the West Bank yesterday to serve notice that he was in favour of globalisation, after all.

"We want to show the world what is happening here," he explained, during a break in the shoving-match, "We need people and governments everywhere to understand that there must be international protection for the Palestinian people. What is happening to them is a crime," he said.

The French media has given the short and stocky Mr Bovéthe soubriquet of Asterix after the diminutive cartoon character who spends his time duffing up the hapless Roman occupiers, aided by slugs of Magic Potion.

Yesterday, Asterix was in action anew, although this time the occupiers ­ no less daunting and imperious than Caesar's men ­ were equipped not with spears and swords but M-16s, riot batons, tear gas canisters, flak jackets and a fleet of armoured jeeps. And the magic potion? That was us, the reporters and cameramen on whom the 48-year-old Frenchman depends to get his message across to the outside world.

Mr Bové arrived in Al-Khader, a Palestinian village on the southern edge of Bethlehem, accompanied by about 70 activists, including a group of “Israelis”. He was among the crowd who clambered by foot over the giant mound of rubble with which “Israeli” army bulldozers have blockaded the village, in a vain attempt to walk up a nearby hill to a cluster of three cabins ­ the beginnings of another Jewish settlement, erected in contravention to international law on land stolen from Al-Khader, and now defended by “Israel’s” security forces.

Among the protesters was 23-year-old Liad Kantorowicz, a graduate of philosophy who returned home to “Israel” seven months ago after 11 years in the US in order to fight for justice for the Palestinians. She, too, knows about the powers of Magic Potion. Why else, if not to secure headlines, would she have admitted to us that her last job was as a dominatrix in a Seattle sex parlour, strutting around in her stilettos and smalls and brandishing a whip.

And the crowd also included Neta Golan, the fearless “Israeli” radical who has spent the last eight months out in the battle-zones, tirelessly trying to stop “Israel’s” soldiers and settlers from tearing up Palestinian olive orchards, shooting into Arab villages, or taking land for settlements.

In a protest in the same village on Friday, an “Israeli” policeman broke her arm at the elbow, yanking it behind her back. Yesterday, in sling and plaster, she was back on the frontline.

It started peacefully enough. Mr Bové was greeted by the local “Israeli” police commander, Ephriam Arditi, plus around 60 police and soldiers. Surrounded by cameras, the Frenchman put his case. What were the “Israelis” doing defending land that was not theirs? If it was ­ as the police claim ­ a closed military area, then why were settlers allowed access to it? Did they not realise this was a crime?

"I'm a farmer, and these (Palestinian) people are farmers too. So I am fighting with them to help them protect their land," Mr Bové told the watching media.

With Mr Arditi caught in the middle of the crowd, the soldiers started elbowing reporters out of the way, and the shoving began. Minutes later one of the activists was arrested and dragged away. "No violence! No violence!" chanted Mr Bové and his friends. A group of Palestinians nearby took up chanting but maybe misheard the French accent as they shouted: "No peace! No peace!"

Then, Mr Bové and his friend changed their slogan: "We want to be arrested! We want to be arrested!" The Frenchman got his wish as did seven others amid a haze of “Israeli” tear gas. It was Mr Bove's first visit to the occupied territories to drum up publicity for the Palestinians' plight, and for the need for an international protection force.

He arrived five days ago, and has seen enough violence to be dismissive of the current ceasefire, which officially remained in place yesterday despite two more deaths. "If this is a ceasefire, then war here must be truly horrible," remarked the Asterix of the peace movement.

 

Washington Considers Boycotting UN Conference on Racism…(8)

The US State Department said proposed calls for slavery reparations and a condemnation of Zionism threaten US participation in the upcoming UN Conference on Racism, Racial Discrimination, Xenophobia and Related Intolerance set to begin in Durban, South Africa next August. State Department Spokesman Richard Boucher said talks on Monday between Secretary of State Colin Powell, other top US diplomats and UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Mary Robinson had failed to address Washington's concerns on the two issues. Boucher maintained that calls for a statement condemning Zionism as racism and African demands for Europe and other western countries to pay reparations and formally apologize for slavery did not fit the formulation of “forward-looking”. Washington has skipped the two previous UN racism conferences—in 1978 and 1983 -- because of the Zionism clause.
