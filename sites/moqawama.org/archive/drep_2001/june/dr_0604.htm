Sharon faces suit in Belgian court (1)

"israeli" Prime Minister Ariel Sharon is being sued in a Belgian court for the 1982 massacres in the Sabra and Shatila refugee camps, Belgium’s daily Le Soir reported Saturday.
The suit was filed under a unique 1993 law that allows Belgian courts to try people, regardless of their nationality, for genocide and other crimes against humanity committed abroad.
Sharon was due to visit Brussels next week, but "israeli" television reported he had canceled the trip in the wake of Friday’s suicide bomb attack in Tel Aviv.
The newspaper said Belgian judicial authorities were studying whether the suit against him was admissible under terms of the law.
The suit’s plaintiffs, a mix of Palestinians, Lebanese, Moroccans and Belgians, have accused Sharon of allowing the 1982 slaughter in the Sabra and Shatila camps. Sharon was Israel’s defense minister at the time.
Although the United Nations has officially classified the killings as acts of genocide, it was only last January that Sharon ­ who refused to apologize ­ expressed any regret for the “terrible tragedy.”
“What it was,” he said in a press interview, “was an act of killing carried out by Arab Christians against Arab Muslims.”

 

Lebanese gunners fire on "israeli" aircraft (2)

Lebanese anti-aircraft batteries fired on intruding "israeli" aircraft Sunday for the first time since Israel's withdrawal from southern Lebanon more than a year ago.

They opened up three times as "israeli" warplanes flew low over the coastal city of Sidon, 40 kilometres (25 miles) south of Beirut, but failed to hit their targets, an AFP correspondent said.

In what has become an almost daily occurrence, formations of "israeli" planes flew over the border area from east to west, the coast as far as Beirut and the southern part of the eastern Bekaa valley.

Correspondents said they broke the sound barrier and simulated attacks for more than an hour and a half.

"israeli" violations of Lebanese airspace, the subject of numerous complaints by Beirut to the United Nations, have intensified since an attack by Hezbollah on "israeli" troops in the Lebanese Shebaa Farms area.

"israeli" troops withdrew on May 24 last year from an area of southern Lebanon they had occupied for 22 years, but still hold the Shebaa Farms.

 

UNIFIL protests "israeli" violation of Lebanon border (3)

The United Nations Interim Forces in southern Lebanon (UNIFIL) said Friday it filed a complaint against "israel" for having violated the UN-drawn blue line dividing "israel" from Lebanon.

UNIFIL spokesman Timor Goksel told AFP that the complaint was lodged earlier this week after the "israeli" army set up an observation post at about 20 yards into Lebanese territory.

Goksel said it was a "temporary observation post" set up to replace an antenna with electronic observation devices, which "israel" had dismantled on May 22 following a similar UN complaint.

On March 1, UNIFIL peacekeepers dismantled a cement fence which "israel" had set up about 300 yards into Lebanese territory in the same area.

The Alawite village of Ghajar is divided in two by the "blue line" and is on the border between Lebanon and the Syrian Golan Heights, occupied by "israel" since 1967.

Ghajar residents had protested against the UN line dividing their village and succeeded in having no border fencing in the village itself.

So the blue line is not visibly drawn in Ghajar, as it is in other areas along the border where even asphalted roads are painted blue.

The blue line was drawn to mark the line of Israel's withdrawal from south Lebanon last May 24, which ended a 22-year occupation.

 

The Zionist Cabinet Completely Seals Off Palestinian Territories Following the Operation (4)
The Zionist Entity's full security cabinet met Saturday to consider a response to the martyrdom operation at a Tel Aviv night club overnight, that left 18 people dead and more than 100 injured. 

The 12-member cabinet, met after an inner security cabinet meeting of enemy Premier Ariel Sharon, War Minister Binyamin Ben Eliezer and Foreign Minister Shimon Peres. 

The three were in telephone contact with each other all night, while the war ministry was in consultations with the military high command. "israeli" public television reported that the meeting decided to cancel Sharon’s trip to Europe, which was due to start on June 4. 

"israeli" cabinet secretary Gideon Saar said Saturday after the meeting that "Israel" will take “all necessary measures” to defend the "israelis". 

The Zionist Entity has also decided to seal off the Palestinian territories completely, including a sea blockade of the Gaza Strip, and to restrict the freedom of movement of Palestinian Chairman Arafat, possibly meaning that Gaza airport would be closed off. 

"israeli" Minister of Communications Reuvlen Rivlin suggested Saturday that "Israel" could force Arafat to leave the Palestinian territories if he cannot put an end to anti-"israeli" attacks. Rivlin said, “Arafat himself is saying that he is controlling everything on the Palestinian side. 

If he can't, let him leave Palestine.” "israeli" officials Saturday roundly held Arafat responsible for the bombing. Enemy President Moshe Katsav said from the United States that Arafat “believes that by provoking an escalation, the situation is going to improve for him. 

War Minister Binyamin Ben Eliezer accused Arafat of wanting “to provoke chaos in the Middle East.” Minister of Internal Security Uzi Landau said Arafat “is the one who has made it possible for these organizations, over which he has jurisdiction.” 

Sharon last week claimed a unilateral ceasefire but still launched several attacks on Palestinian territories, the matter which was voiced by the Palestinians as a public relations ploy.

 

Qassem applauds ‘martyr’ operations (5)

Hizbullah’s deputy secretary-general Sheikh Naim Qassem described recent “martyr” operations launched against the "israelis" by Palestinian fighters as “fine, superb and shaking the Zionist state right down to its roots.”
Speaking in the village of Taibe, Qassem said that “"israel" has been shaken from top to bottom because this is the first time "israel"’s tourism has been hit by a more than 50 percent reduction, and the country’s growth rate drops to 4 percent. This is the first fruit of the intifada.”
