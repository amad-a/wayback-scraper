Land mine maims man in Jezzine…(1)

A 20-year old man was wounded Thursday by the explosion of an anti-personnel mine left behind after “Israel’s” troop pullout from south Lebanon a year ago, police said.
Mohammed Salman, 20, lost a foot from the blast as he was gathering scrap metal near Kfar Falous, Jezzine.
His father, Salman, said that while driving toward their village of Ain Arab, his son got out of the car and stepped over barbed wire to gather the material, to be used in rebuilding their destroyed home.
Residents said it was the first known land-mine injury in the Jezzine district since the Kfar Falous-Jezzine road was re-opened in 1997.
Salman, who was also injured by shrapnel released by the mine in the explosion, was rushed to Qasab hospital in Abra for treatment, where he remained in intensive care.

 

Collaborators men jailed for collaboration…(2)

A military court has convicted 12 people, most of them former members of the “Israeli”- allied South Lebanon Army, of collaboration and contact with “Israel” and sentenced all but one to up to 15 years in prison.
The harshest sentences were handed down to Qassem Ismail Olayyan and Elias Ibrahim Haddad, in absentia.
According to the verdicts issued late Wednesday and posted on a court bulletin board Thursday, Abbas Hassan Hajj was sentenced to two years in prison and fined LL400,000. Seven others received prison terms ranging from six months to one year and were fined LL400,000 each for serving with the now-defunct SLA.
The court dropped collaboration charges against three people.

 

Brussels court to rule on war-crimes charges against Sharon…(3)

“Prosecutors in Belgium will decide in coming weeks whether a complaint filed in Brussels against “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon should go ahead. An ad hoc committee of Lebanese, Palestinians, Moroccans and Belgians has filed a lawsuit against Sharon holding him responsible for the 1982 Sabra and Chatila massacres of up to 2,000 Palestinians south of Beirut. Court officials in Brussels said the merits of the charge will be studied in depth, before the court decides if the case should proceed to trial. According to a 1993 law Belgian courts can try cases of genocide and other crimes against humanity that take place in foreign countries. The law is already being put into practice at the ongoing trial in Brussels of four Rwandans charged in connection with the 1994 massacre of Tutsis and moderate Hutus in the central African state. An “Israeli” government inquiry held Sharon "indirectly" responsible for the Sabra and Chatila massacres. He was supposed to visit Brussels on Wednesday but postponed his trip after last week's suicide martyrdom bombing in Tel Aviv. 
 

“Israeli” troops injure Palestinians as us spy chief attempts to bolster cease-fire…(4) 

“Israeli” troops have shot and injured two Palestinians in a third day of student protests over an “Israeli” blockade that is stopping them from reaching their university. The outbreak of fighting and deep distrust are threatening a fragile ceasefire before planned talks between US CIA chief George Tenet and “Israeli” and Palestinian leaders. 

CIA chief George Tenet is preparing for separate meetings with “Israeli” and Palestinian leaders, after holding talks with Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak in Cairo on the first stop of a regional tour aimed at shoring up the fragile Palestinian-”Israeli” ceasefire. Mubarak called for a more active US role to contain the Mideast crisis. There has been little comment on Tenet's discussions in Cairo and Amman, where he met senior Jordanian officials and US special Mideast envoy William Burns. Palestinian officials welcomed what they said was the gradual rise of Washington's involvement in the crisis. They want it to help lift “Israel’s” clamp down on the Palestinian territories, and not to limit its role to restraint. “Israel” wants Tenet to extract a pledge from Palestinians to end all confrontation. They accused Palestinian President Yasser Arafat of ending his quote historic role in the pursuit for peace. “Israel” said Tenet will activate the implementation of the Mitchell report. The US-led commission, chaired by former US Senator George Mitchell, calls for an end to fighting and an immediate freeze to settlement activity, a condition “Israel” categorically rejected. US officials said Tenet's main task is launching "serious" security discussions. Secretary of State Colin Powell telephoned Arafat and Sharon overnight to press them to solidify a prevailing ceasefire. A US official said Powell called on “Israel” to exercise restraint and urged Arafat to arrest those who have committed "terrorist" acts. Washington had denied any deal with “Israel” over Jewish settlements, which US officials described as provocative. They said the talks are ongoing but an agreement remains out of reach. The European Union also warned “Israel” against building new settlements on occupied land, saying settlements did not contribute to the establishment of a solid and just peace. The issue took center stage after Jewish settlers in the occupied Palestinian territories rampaged against Palestinian homes and farms, injuring civilians and destroying property. Thousands of settlers took to the streets demanding Sharon unleash “Israel’s” military might against Palestinians after a settler baby was injured in Palestinian gunfire. There were sporadic clashes across the West Bank despite the fragile cease-fire declared by both sides. “Israeli” troops shot and injured at least 13 Palestinian demonstrators in Ramallah. Three “Israelis” were wounded, two of them seriously, when their car was ambushed by Palestinian gunmen near a settlement in the area. The Brigades of the al-Aqsa Martyrs claimed responsibility for the attacks.
