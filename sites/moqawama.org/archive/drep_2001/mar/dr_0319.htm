Court says “Israel” must justify holding kidnapped Lebanese militants…(1)

“ An “Israeli” court on Sunday extended by one month the detention of two kidnapped Lebanese leaders held for years without charge or trial but said the government must justify their continued imprisonment.

Sheikh Abdel Karim Obeid and Mustapha Dirani were abducted by “Israeli” commandos during the Jewish state's occupation of Lebanon as bargaining chips in any negotiations on the release of missing “Israeli” servicemen.

They are being held in administrative detention, a system condemned by human rights groups under which “Israel” holds detainees without charge or trial for renewable six-month periods.

The prosecution called Sunday for their detention to be extended by three months, but the Tel Aviv district court agreed to just one month and said War Minister Binyamin Ben Eliezer must justify in that time “Israel’s” claim that freeing them would "constitute a danger for the security of the state of “Israel”."

Their lawyer Zvi Rish denounced their continued imprisonment, saying they were being held in isolation in a secret army detention centre and had never been allowed visits by representatives of the International Committee of the Red Cross.

"This decision can only encourage Hizbullah to try to kidnap “Israelis” as bargaining chips," he told public radio.

Hizbullah, the movement which spearheaded the armed campaign against “Israel’s” occupation of Lebanon, seized three “Israeli” soldiers in early October and said it had captured an “Israeli” colonel working for “Israeli” intelligence who had entered Lebanon on a false passport.

Sheikh Obeid, a local spiritual leader of Hizbullah, was kidnapped by “Israeli” commandos in 1989 while Dirani -- then a military commander for the rival Amal militia -- was seized in 1994.

Their continued detention is one of the lingering points of hostility between “Israel” and Lebanon since the Jewish state ended its 22-year occupation of its northern neighbour in May.

The two remain in custody despite a supreme court ruling in April last year ordering the release of another 13 Lebanese prisoners held as bargaining chips.

In particular, “Israel” is seeking information about the fate of Ron Arad, an “Israeli” airman who was shot down over Lebanon in 1986 and captured alive. Hizbullah denies all knowledge of his whereabouts.

Ben-Eliezer: We won't release Obeid or Dirani (2)

Defense (War) Minister Binyamin Ben-Eliezer yesterday flatly rejected the option of releasing Mustafa Dirani and Sheik Abdel-Karim Obeid, in effect preempting a court ruling giving him 30 days to decide on the issue.
Ben-Eliezer was speaking at his first meeting since taking office, with the families of the three soldiers kidnapped by Hizbullah in October.
He instructed those dealing the matter to relay this as the official position of the Defense (War) Ministry to the Tel Aviv District Court, which is considering a petition on the pair's freedom.
Ben-Eliezer met with the families of Sgt. Adi Avitan, St.-Sgt. Omar Sawayid, and St.-Sgt. Binyamin Avraham last night at the Tel Aviv ministry. He told the families that he sees as one of his principle tasks to work in every way to bring the soldiers home, a ministry statement said. Ben-Eliezer also told them that even before he took over as defense minister he had actively sought information on the fate of the three soldiers with figures abroad.
The families urged him to continue to detain Dirani and Obeid, and demanded that he increase efforts to return the missing soldiers.
"I feel the pain of the families," Ben-Eliezer was quoted as saying, adding that he asked the families to give their confidence to the defense establishment which is working to win their children's release.
Former OC Northern Command and deputy head of the Mossad, Amiram Levine, said over the weekend that there was no point in continuing to hold the two Lebanese who were captured as bargaining chips to negotiate for missing IAF navigator Ron Arad.
"Just as it was legitimate to abduct them to play out every chance to return Ron Arad and other missing people, the moment they ceased being a true bargaining chip we have to return them," Levine told Channel 2. The position drew the ire of the families of the three kidnapped soldiers.
"It is inconceivable that every person in the defense establishment comments on such sensitive issues. I think that the state of “Israel” has to take un-routine steps, think differently in order to return our boys home," said Chaim Avraham, the father of Binyamin Avraham.
Hizbullah guerrillas on October 7 fired rockets at an IDF* patrol near Mount Dov and riddled the three soldiers with bullets before carrying them, wounded, away. So far, no information on their condition has been relayed to “Israel”.
The Tel Aviv District Court yesterday gave Ben-Eliezer 30 days to decide whether “Israel” should continue to detain Dirani and Obeid.
Judge Ori Goren handed down the decision in response to a petition from Devora Chen of the Tel Aviv District Attorney's Office to extend the two men's administrative detention by six months because they pose a danger to the state.
Goren postponed ruling on Chen's request, deciding instead that the new defense minister should first decide if he wants to change the state's policy regarding the two. As a result, their detention will be extended for one month. If Ben-Eliezer does not submit a response, Goren will rule based on the previous government's policy.
Obeid, the former Hizbullah spiritual leader in southern Lebanon, was abducted by the IDF in 1986.
Dirani was abducted by the IDF in 1994. He is the former Amal security chief who captured Arad in Lebanon in 1986 and was involved in attacks on the IDF and SLA.
Attorney Zvi Rish, who represents Dirani and Obeid, argued that the state should release them as their continued detention will do nothing to further the return of the MIAs. He asked the court to view a videotape of Levine's Channel 2 interview.
Eyal Avitan, brother of Sgt. Adi Avitan, rejected Levine's statements.
"If it were his son in captivity he would not have been able to say that," Avitan said.

“Israeli” war planes cause sonic boom over Beirut…(3)

“ “Israeli” war planes buzzed Lebanon Sunday, breaking the sound barrier over Beirut with booms causing buildings' winwdows to vibrate, AFP reporters said.

The jets, which took off from northern “Israel”, flew at high altitude up south Lebanon's coastal plain, leaving vapour trails behind them.

Three weeks ago “Israeli” planes stirred a panic when they broke the sound barrier over the snow-covered western slopes of the Mount Lebanon range, which was crowded with skiers.

Since “Israel” ended its 22-year occupation of south Lebanon last May, Lebanon has lodged frequent complaints with the UN against “Israeli” violations of its airspace.

“Israeli” killed in shooting attack on West Bank road…(4)

An “Israeli” man was killed in a drive–by–shooting on a West Bank road by Palestinian gunmen this morning, the army said.

The “Israeli” was fatally wounded, lost control of his car and hit an oncoming truck, Jewish settler leaders said. He died shortly afterwards.

The attack occurred on an “Israeli” controlled road south of Al-Quds. According to the army, Palestinian gunmen shot on the car from a passing vehicle and then fled into nearby town of Bethlehem which is controlled by the Palestinian Authority.

Jewish settlers have been the targets of shooting attacks on West Bank and Gaza Strip roads since “Israeli”–Palestinian fighting broke out almost six months ago. To date, 429 people have been killed including 352 Palestinians, 58 “Israeli” Jews, and 19 others.

Palestinians fire mortar into “Israel”…(5)

An “Israeli” soldier was lightly injured yesterday by fragments of a mortar shell fired at an army base near Kibbutz Nahal Oz, adjacent to the Gaza Strip.
The mortar attack marks the first time Palestinian insurgents freedom fighters in Gaza have fired at “Israeli” targets located within “Israel’s” borders. Three mortar bombs were fired at the “Israeli” Army base. The soldier, who was stationed at the entrance to the base, received medical care on site.
Palestinians have recently aimed mortar fire at Jewish settlements in Gaza, such as Netzarim, but until yesterday they had refrained from shooting at targets on the other side of the Gaza border. The squad responsible for the mortar attacks that preceded yesterday's incident apparently takes orders from Hizbullah. The group also includes members of Yasser Arafat's presidential guard, Force 17. The squad's commander, Masoud Ayad, was killed more than a month ago by “Israel” Air Force helicopters.
