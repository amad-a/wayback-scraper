Lebanese farmer killed by “Israeli” mine…(1)

“ A Lebanese farmer was killed Monday when his tractor drove over a landmine left by “Israel” after its troop pullout from southern Lebanon, police said.

Ali Mohamad Aqiqi, 30, was driving on a road on the outskirts of the village of Shaqra, east of the southern port city of Tyre, when the mine exploded, they said.

Aqiqi was transported to hospital where he died from his wounds, they said.

Ten Lebanese civilians have been killed and 70 injured by mines left over since “Israel’s” troop withdrawal from southern Lebanon in May after 22 years of occupation.

A dozen Lebanese army troops have also been injured in mine-clearing operations. Two Syrian soldiers were wounded Thursday when a mine exploded while taking part in a demining operations in south Lebanon.

The United Nations estimates that at least 130,000 “Israeli” mines and unexploded shells remain in the region.

UN peacekeepers, who complain that “Israel” has not been forthcoming with maps of the mines, said January 15 that they have dealt with 2,159 mines and explosives in the area since the end of the “Israeli” occupation.

“Israeli” army fires heavy machine-guns into south Lebanon: police…(2)

“ “Israeli” soldiers in the Shibaa Farms fired heavy machine-guns into southern Lebanon overnight, without inflicting casualties, police said Monday.

“Israeli” soldiers fired from positions in Ramta, Sammaqa and Rowaissat Aalam in the Shibaa Farms, a mountainous region on the Lebanese-Syrian borders occupied by “Israel”, police said.

The shots targetted the areas of Bastara and Wadi al-Mughur as well as the outskirts of the village of Kfarshouba, they said.

The soldiers also fired 15 flares over the region as “Israeli” helicopters hovered over the borders, they added.

The police said no activity along the Lebanese side of the border had been reported before the incident.

On Friday, “Israeli” soldiers in the Shibaa Farms fired 10 shells into south Lebanon, according to the UN Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL), which said it was not aware of any previous activity along the Lebanese side of the border.

Since “Israel’s” troop pullout from southern Lebanon in May after 22 years of occupation, Hizbullah has launched several attacks on “Israeli” forces in the Shibaa Farms, captured by the Jewish state from Syria in 1967 and now claimed by Beirut.

The attacks have killed two “Israeli” soldiers captured three, who are still being held in a bid by Hizbullah to secure a prisoner swap.

“Israelis” fire in the air in border face-off…(3)

“Israeli” troops fired into the air Sunday to ward off Lebanese youths throwing stones across the border.
A group of youths threw stones at an “Israeli” Army post on Sheikh Abbad hill and violently shook the border fence. “Israeli” soldiers fired machine-guns into the air, driving the youths away. There were no casualties. Stone throwing, a popular activity last summer in the wake of “Israel’s” withdrawal, has diminished in recent months.

Lebanon judges another 16 for collaboration with “Israel”…(4)

“ A Beirut military court has passed judgment on 16 more former pro-”Israeli” militia members, bringing near completion the marathon trials of more than 3,000 Lebanese, the chief prosecutor said Saturday.

Nasri Lahoud, quoted by the press, said 2,326 of the 3,010 defendants have been judged and that the trial should be over by the end of the year.

Thirteen people were sentenced to one year in prison, one to two years and two others were released in the trial Friday.

The marathon trial began June 5, just 10 days after “Israel” hastily withdrew its forces, ending a bloody 22-year occupation of southern Lebanon.

So far, 15 people have been sentenced to death and three others to life in prison, all by absentia.

Holocaust Deniers Confirm Beirut Conference, Urge Lebanon to Shake off US Pressure…(5)

Organizers of an international conference of Holocaust deniers have confirmed that it will be held in Beirut on schedule at the end of the month despite protests by the U.S. government and Jewish lobbies in the United States and Europe.
The confirmation was made by the California-based Institute for Historical Review and the Swiss-based Organization "Verite et Justice." The Beirut meeting is dubbed the "Revisionist and Zionism Conference," As-Safir reported from Washington.
Verite et Justice President Mark Weber told As-Safir he was confident the Lebanese government would shake off pressure by the U.S. administration and Jewish lobbies and allow the conference to be held in Beirut from March 31 through April 4.
"We trust the Lebanese authorities will give the Zionist pressure the rejection it deserves. These shameful efforts to suppress the freedom of expression reiterates the need to holding such a conference," Weber said.
Asked why was Beirut chosen for a venue, Weber said there were two objectives:
1-Consolidating the cooperation between Western, Arab and Islamic historians.
2-Furthering the growing realization that Jews and “Israel” were manipulating the holocaust for their own political financial interests.
"“Israel” and Zionist organizations are using the Holocaust for blackmail to force European states and companies to pay hundreds of billion dollars to “Israel”," Weber said.
He said his organization "does not deny the occurrence of the holocaust, but rejected the manipulation of the Holocaust for political and financial blackmail."
He said many European historians and intellectuals would attend the Beirut conference. Among them will be French writers Roger Garaudi and Robert Forisson as well as German jurist Horst Maller

A Palestinian Martyr, Three Photographs Injured by “Israeli” Forces…(6)

A Palestinian young man was killed  last night when “Israeli” enemy soldiers shot  him dead near the Mintar crossing point in the  Gaza Strip Friday  night.

Medical  Palestinian sources said the corpse of the  unidentified martyr bears scars  caused by shrapnels of “Israeli” shells .

An officer in the Palestinian security  said the martyr was subject to heavy gun  shots and 2 tank shells when he was in  his land.

He added that the “Israeli” soldiers left  him to bleed until dawn and then dragged his body by a bulldozer.

Meanwhile, the “Israeli”  radio announced that  a blast blew up a military patrol near  Al-Boreij camp in Gaza Strip .

Also, a Palestinian man was shot and  wounded overnight in the vicinity of the  Jewish settlement of Sussia, near the West  Bank city of al-khalil, Palestinian police said  Saturday.

Nabil Ali, 35, from the village of Yatta,  near al-khalil, was hit in the foot with a live bullet and taken to the Ahli hospital in the area.

It was the second incident in less than  24 hours.

A Palestinian shepherd  was shot and seriously injured by a Jewish  settler near Sussia, as well.

 “Israel” seals off Ramallah to 'protect targets against guerrilla attacks'…(7)

“Israeli” troops tightened the blockade yesterday on Ramallah, the Palestinian Authority's West Bank administrative and security capital.

Links were cut with the villages of Anata and Aqab, to the north, as well as the Khalandia refugee camp to the south. Last week, army bulldozers dug a grid of wide ditches to prevent residents of 33 villages driving to Ramallah. Palestinian spokesmen accused Ariel Sharon's new government of destroying their economy and starving the population.

An “Israeli” military spokes man said last night the siege was designed to disrupt guerrilla plans to attack targets inside “Israel” and West Bank Jewish settlements.

Al-Quds and other “Israeli” cities remained under a heightened security alert throughout the weekend, when Jews were celebrating the Purim holiday. Vehicles arriving in town were searched and armed troops patrolled the streets.

“Israel” announced yesterday that it had arrested a senior Hamas activist, suspected of leading assaults on the Gaza settlement of Netzarim. Mohammed Dirawi, 23, was alleged to have filmed attacks by masked men in military uniforms, which were then shown on Arab television stations. Another cell was said to have been broken up in the village of Hussan, near Bethlehem.

Journalists beaten by militant Jews…(8)

Three Palestinian journalists were beaten by militant Jews in the West Bank Saturday, two days after a Reuters camera crew came under fire from an “Israeli” soldier in the Gaza Strip.
Reuters TV cameraman Mazen Daana and sound technician Nael Shiyoukhi, and Hussam Abu-Allan, aphotographer for French news agency Agence France-Presse, were beaten by militant Jews in the divided city of Hebron, eyewitnesses and a hospital official said.
Their injuries were light, although they had bruises all over their bodies, said Dr. Jamil Shehade of Alahile hospital.
About 500 militant Jewish live in Hebron, surrounded by more than 100,000 Palestinians. Groups of militant settlers took to the streets Saturday, throwing stones at Palestinians and attacking Palestinian journalists, eyewitnesses said.
Palestinian shopkeepers closed their stores and left the area.
On Thursday, a soldier at an “Israeli” army outpost at Netzarim in the Gaza Strip opened fire with a heavy machine gun toward a Reuters TV crew that was filming the outpost, but nobody was hit, the news agency and an army spokesman said.

Group demands “Israeli” Army try two soldiers for killing Palestinians…(9)

The Public Committee Against Torture yesterday demanded that the army court-martial soldiers involved in the killings of two Palestinian youths in Gaza last month. It also complained that the Military Police investigations unit is investigating only four of the 325 incidents in which Palestinians have been killed since the beginning of the intifada.
The letters were addressed to Attorney-General Elyakim Rubinstein, Judge Advocate-General Menahem Finkelstein, and Col. Miki Barel, head of Military Police investigations.
The first case involves Ibrahim Amur, 14, who was shot and killed on February 3 in the Gaza Strip. He was reportedly walking in the vicinity of a school situated near Kfar Darom when a sniper shot him in the abdomen. According to soldiers who witnessed the shooting, Amur did nothing to provoke the soldiers.
According to the Al-Quds weekly Kol Ha'ir, which first reported the incident, the sniper was tried in a disciplinary court before Givati Brigade commander Col. Lior Shalev, who sentenced him to 49 days in jail.
The other case involves Iman Abu Hawalli, 16, who was allegedly shot and killed from "point-blank range" on February 9, when he approached a group of Givati soldiers. The soldier who killed Hawalli testified that he had opened fire in accordance with open-fire regulations and aimed at the lower party of Hawalli's body. Hawalli, however, was struck in the upper part of his body.
An inquiry found that the soldiers acted according to regulations.
"They did everything they could to get the youth to leave the area but to no avail. They sent a Palestinian taxi driver to the youth with a warning he had to leave, and he did for a while, but came back. Then when he approached their position, they shouted at him to halt. He did not. They fired a warning shot into the air, and he kept coming. They finally fired at his legs and the youth was mortally wounded."
The Public Committee Against Torture said it is inconceivable that such cases should be investigated and tried according to disciplinary, rather than criminal procedures.
Military sources noted that the “Israeli” Army altered its open-fire regulations as a result of redefining its conflict with the Palestinians to an "armed struggle short of war." “Israel” believes that this absolves it of any claims for compensation for damage caused by the conflict and allows it to increase its fire power according to international law.
"We very rarely open investigations into shootings," said one senior Army officer. So far the “Israeli” Army has investigated only four cases of shooting violations and another 20 are pending.

Palestinians said “Israeli” Army using flechettes…(10)

A Palestinian farmer, buried in Gaza yesterday, was killed by flechettes, dart-like projectiles and anti-personnel ammunition not seen yet in the Intifada, but which Palestinians say was used by the “Israeli” Army in Lebanon.
Ziyad Ayad was admitted to Shifa hospital in Gaza before dawn with one of the projectiles in his left eye, two pointing in opposite directions in his pelvis and one in his left lung, x-rays showed.
His relatives said he was approaching his farmland on Saturday when he was shot. An army spokesman said he was seen in an area where terrorists had planted bombs in the past.
The spokesman refused to confirm the soldiers used flechettes, which are fired like cluster bombs from a rifle.
"The “Israeli” Army uses means best suited to the overall security conditions and specific threats in the area," said the spokesman.
Palestinian security sources said the projectiles, about the size of a carpenter's nail, were used by the “Israel” during the occupation of Lebanon.

Arab FMs: Try “Israeli” officials for war crimes…(11)

Foreign ministers from several Arab states meeting this week in Cairo will consider a resolution calling for “Israeli” officials to be tried for war crimes.
A recommendation asking the UN to send a fact finding committee to the territories will also be discussed, “Israel” Radio reported this morning.
A subcommittee of the Foreign Ministers Conference agreed to indemnify the Palestinians $40 million monthly.
The Arabic-language daily Al Hayat reported that Egypt and Jordan want to focus on economic issues, in opposition to other Arab countries seeking to carry out measures against “Israel”, the report said.
The United Nations Works and Relief Agency made its second emergency appeal yesterday to Arab foreign ministers at the conference in order to help combat increasing unemployment in Palestinian refugee camps.
Chief of Staff at UNWRA's headquarters in Gaza, Mian Qadruddin, denies the funds will fuel the current intifada.
