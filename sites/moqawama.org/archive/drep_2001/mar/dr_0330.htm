Claims of imported Iranian missiles fuel Sharon's latest 'fantasy'…(1)

Ariel Sharon has done it again. Nineteen years after he allowed “Israel’s” Phalangist militiamen to enter the Sabra and Chatila camps in Beirut to "eliminate" 2,000 fantasy Palestinian "terrorists", he now claims that Lebanon is "the centre of international terrorism". In 1982, “Israel’s” allies slaughtered up to 2,000 Palestinian civilians in their hunt for the mythical "terrorists".

So what, the Lebanese are asking, is coming now? It's a frightening question because “Israel’s” new Prime Minister is accusing the Iranians of transferring new long-range missiles to Lebanon ­ capable, so he claims, of hitting the centre of “Israel” ­ and accusing Syria of using Lebanon's airports to transfer these fantasy missiles. It's important to use the word fantasy. In the hundreds of miles I travel across Lebanon every month, I have yet to see a long-range missile, let alone a transporter. Satellite pictures would easily identify such a rocket and Beirut airport is these days so hide-bound with security that you couldn't move a rifle through its terminal.

So what, the Lebanese are asking, does the Sharon threat mean? Are the Israelis waiting for a provocation to attack Beirut's gleaming new civilian airport? Or, a favourite target, the civilian power stations? Almost every week now, a new crisis appears along the United Nations "blue line" in south Lebanon, the UN's ceasefire line that roughly marks the
“Israeli”-Lebanese frontier. Two months ago, the Israelis started building a "security" fence inside Lebanese territory. After UN protests, they halted.

Then came a series of “Israeli” threats over the Wazzani river, a lazy stream that meanders through south-eastern Lebanon and crosses into the “Israeli”-
occupied Syrian Golan Heights. After beginning the reconstruction of a village destroyed during “Israel’s” 22-year occupation of Lebanon, the Beirut authorities laid a small plastic pipeline from the Wazzani to the building site so that returning Lebanese refugees could have running water.

Within hours, the Israelis thundered that the "taking" of this water ­ by Lebanese, from a Lebanese river, for a Lebanese village ­ created a grave crisis. “Israel” was being deprived of water for its northern Galilee settlements. After UN protests, “Israel’s” Foreign Minister, Shimon Peres, promised the affair would be treated with "discretion".

On Wednesday, Lebanon began pumping water from the river as “Israeli” soldiers watched.

 

An “Israeli” Man Transmitted Hizbullah Broadcasts…(2)

“Israeli” security arrested an “Israeli”  accused of transmitting  broadcasts of Television and  Radio channels that belong to Hizbullah, Hamas and the Palestinian Authority.

Ma'ariv newspaper, which disclosed the  story, said Gabriel Hifr has been  indicted for using an Antenna atop a  building at the entrance of al- Quds(Al-Quds) holy city where he broadcasted these channels to a  satellite which in turn broadcasted  them to foreign channels.

The  suspect, whose equipments were confiscated, denied possessing any  broadcasting channels, and also denied the accusations saying  that some of his competitors are  trying to cause him harm and that he  worked according to the law.

 

“Israeli” soldiers fire mortar rounds into south Lebanon…(3)

“ “Israeli” soldiers fired two mortar rounds into southern Lebanon Thursday, but there were no reports of casualties, police said.

The mortars were fired from an “Israeli” position in Tallet Ramta in the Shibaa Farms territory, a mountainous region at the Lebanese-Syrian borders occupied by “Israel”, they said.

The rounds, accompanied by heavy machine-gun fire from “Israeli” posts in nearby Sammaqa and Rowaissat Aalam, slammed in a valley in Bastara on the other side of the border area, they said.

“Israeli” helicopters also hovered over the targeted region, they added.

The police said no activity along the Lebanese side of the border had been reported before the “Israeli” firing, the third such incident this month.

On March 9, the United Nations reported that the “Israeli” army had fired shells into the same region without inflicting casualties. Three days later, “Israeli” soldiers fired heavy machine guns into the area.

On both occasions, there were no casualties and no reports of activity on the Lebanese side of the border prior to the shootings.

Since “Israel’s” troop pullout from southern Lebanon in May after 22 years of occupation, Hizbullah has launched several attacks on “Israeli” forces in the Shibaa Farms, captured by the Jewish state from Syria in 1967 and now claimed by Beirut.

The attacks led to the death of two “Israeli” soldiers and the capture of three others in a bid by Hizbullah to secure a prisoner swap.

 

Man unharmed after mine explodes…(4)

A construction worker had a lucky escape Thursday when his bulldozer set off a land mine near Ibl es-Saqi.
Jean Nohra, 31, was not hurt in the incident.
At least 10 people have been killed and 35 wounded by land mines since “Israeli” troops withdrew from Lebanon last May.

 

Lebanon hands down 16 more sentences for collaboration with “Israel”…(5)

A Beirut military court handed down 16 more sentences against Lebanese accused of collaborating with “Israel” during its 22-year occupation of the country, judicial sources said Thursday.

Out of the 16 sentences ordered by the court late Wednesday, two -- a death penalty and a life sentence -- were handed down in absentia, they said.

The rest were given sentences ranging from five months to five years in prison, they said.

The marathon trials, which began just 10 days after “Israel’s” troop pullout from south Lebanon in May 2000, have so far led to the judgment of 2,412 people, out of some 3,010 Lebanese who were arrested on charges of collaboration or surrendered to authorities.

So far, 18 people have been sentenced to death and four others to life in prison, all in their absence.

The trials mainly concern members of the South Lebanon Army, a militia set up by “Israel” to assist the Jewish state's 22-year occupation. Others have been sentenced for crossing the border to work in “Israel”.

 

Hizbullah tells FTV group did not receive any funds from alleged US cell following federal court's indictment of Lebanese on charges of "conspiring" to aid organization…(6)

“US courts have indicted four alleged members of Hizbullah in the US on charges of giving cash and military equipment to the Lebanese group. The indictment said the men planned to acquire a variety of items that Hizbullah would use to quote "engage in violent attacks and to film such attacks for use in Hizbullah propaganda efforts". But a Hizbullah source told Future Television the group "did not receive any funds" and denied "having any knowledge of the" US court allegations. 

“ Federal prosecutors in the US have charged four Lebanese, described as members of a Hizbullah cell operating out of the state of North Carolina, with plotting to smuggle cash and military supplies to the resistance movement in Lebanon. Funded by counterfeit credit cards and a multi-million dollar cigarette smuggling operation, prosecutors said cell members conspired to obtain explosives and equipment for Hizbullah, which led the campaign to oust “Israeli” troops from southern Lebanon after a twenty-two year occupation which ended last May. The indictment said the men also conspired to provide the movement with night vision goggles, stun grenades, blasting equipment, global positioning devices, mine detection, radar equipment and cameras. The US State Department classifies Lebanon's Hizbullah as a quote terrorist organization. Prosecutors named the defendants as Ali Adham Amhaz, Mohammad Hassan Dbouk, Hassan Hilu Laqis and Said Mohammad Harb. Only Harb remains in custody without bond, while the remaining are not in custody. Prosecutors accused Amhaz of being the main purchasing agent for Hizbullah in North America. Eight other co-defendants were arrested last year and four were free on bond pending trial. The US government claims Mohammad Youseff Hammoud, named in the original indictment, led a Charlotte-based group of Lebanese immigrants who bought cases of cigarettes in North Carolina, which has a low cigarette tax and sold them at a profit in other states. The indictment capped a three-year investigation that came to light in July 2000, when police in Charlotte and Michigan arrested 17 so-called Hizbullah supporters, including one alleged to have funneled more than 1 million dollars to the group in Lebanon. The indictment also claimed the defendants sought insurance for Hizbullah fighters who could be killed during operations. 

5 Palestinian Martyrs, Tens of Wounded in “Israeli” Aggressions…(7)

The Palestinian people offered three more martyrs Thursday in their 6-month-old intifadah against the “Israeli” occupation.

A group of about 50 youths were hurling stones at “Israeli” occupations soldiers at the Erez crossing in the Gaza Strip, when the
enemy troops opened fire at them, killing Mohammed Salman Abu Shamla, 18, who was hit in the head and the heart and
15-year-old Mahmoud Abu Shahada, whose heart was also pierced with a bullet. Another 13 Palestinians were injured, two of
them seriously.

The third martyr, Hossam Ghanem al-Kranz, a 23-year-old member of the Palestinian preventive security forces in the Gaza
strip, was shot dead near the flashpoint Jewish settlement of Netzarim overnight. The “Israeli” army said al-Kramz opened fire on their position in the area.

Also, "“Israeli”"  tanks on Thursday fired shells at the Palestinian district in al-Khalil(Hebron) as tensions continued to run high in the divided West Bank city.

This comes just hours after “Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon ordered the first air strikes against the Palestinian territories since he
came to power.

The air raid killed a member of force 17 and a Palestinian women near the West Bank city of Ramallah and left more than 60
wounded in the Gaza strip.

Palestinian chairman Yasser Arafat's Gaza home was damaged during the targeting of his force 17 presidential security guard, headquarters.

Meanwhile, Palestinian resistance fighters hurled a hand grenade at  an “Israeli” occupation army outpost near Rafah, on the Palestinian-Egyptian border.

Fire was also opened at an “Israeli” bus near the Jewish settlement of Maali Levoneh in the West Bank.

 

Sharon Announces the "Days of Restraint Over"…(8)

"The days of restraint are over", announced “Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon following  Wednesday's air raids on Palestinian territories.

The “Israeli” occupation cabinet gave Sharon and war minister Benjamin Ben Eliezer the green light to order not only responses to Palestinian attacks, but also individual pre-emptive measures in the future.

Ben Eliezer said the raids are a message to Palestinian Chairman Yasser Arafat that "“Israel’s”" patience is at its end, announcing that it's a state of war, and that the “Israeli” army will prepare for a long lengthy struggle if Palestinians do not put down the gun.

As for the US position towards the escalation, the US State Department, siding with its strategic ally said it recognized "“Israel’s”" need to protect itself from what it labeled terrorist attacks. The US had just hours before vetoed a security Council resolution to send a UN observer force to protect Palestinians.

The “Israeli” air raid and the US veto came as a clear challenge to the Arab leaders which ended a two-day summit in Amman
during which they paid tribute to the heroic intifadah and slammed “Israel” but fell short of taking a firmer stand against the
54-year-old enemy. 

 

Arafat Says the Intifadah Will Continue Until Palestinian Flag Flies Over Quds…(9)

For his part, Arafat condemned the air strikes as part of a deliberate strategy by Sharon against the Palestinians.

"This is the beginning of a 100-day Sharon, plan", said Arafat warning that whatever the Israelis do the intifadah would continue
until the Palestinian flag flies over occupied Quds as the capital of an independent state.

Ahmed Helis, secretary general of Palestinian Arafat’s Fatah faction, threatened attacks inside “Israel” if the “Israeli” army enters areas under Palestinian self-rule.

“Every point in “Israel” will become a legitimate target for our fighters if “Israel” attacks our cities,” he said.

General Abdelrazek al-Majaida, the head of Palestinian public security for the Gaza Strip, accused the “Israeli” army “of preparing to launch a major offensive against the Palestinian territories.”

He also warned the army against any move “to enter the Palestinian territories.”

 

Targets of “Israeli” Occupation Army Listed in Ma'ariv…(10)

The Ma’ariv newspaper reported  according to top-level “Israeli” security  apparatus that “Israel” is continuing its policy of  elimination against the  Palestinians which will be resumed  immediately after the Day of Land.

The sources added that the policy will  not be limited to operations only, but  will be a constant policy against what  it described as terrorism.

Ma’ariv disclosed the goals planned  by the Zionist army within the mentioned policy. These goals are:

Second, high-ranking officers in  Palestinian security apparatuses, and  among those the organization  commander in the West Bank, Marwan  Barghouti, and Force-17  commander in Ramallah,  Mohammed Dimra.

Third, founders of Palestinian Security  apparatuses responsible for  operations such as the General  Intelligence Agency headed by Tawfiq Tirawi.

Fourth, Palestinian houses neighboring  Abu Sneinah area in al-Khalil, because  they were used to launch attacks  against the Jewish settlement quarter in al-Khalil.

Fifth, Palestinian houses in Bait Jala  which are used to launch attacks  against Jilo settlement.

 

UN human rights agency condemns “Israeli” atrocities…(11)

The United Nations Human Rights Commission has strongly condemned “Israel” for using excessive and disproportionate force against Palestinian civilians in the occupied territories. UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Mary Robinson called for a reduction of “Israeli” troops in the region and the immediate easing of “Israel’s” siege on Palestinian areas. Robinson urged the Palestinian leadership to halt what she described as violence from the Palestinian side. UN human rights investigators have expressed disappointment at Washington's veto of a Security Council resolution to send UN observers to protect civilians in the territories. A UN investigative team called for pressure on “Israel” to halt human rights violations, including extra-judicial executions which they said are being endorsed by top “Israeli” officials.
