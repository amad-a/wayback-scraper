Lebanon to develop water resources despite “Israeli” threats: Berri…(1)

“ Lebanese Parliament Speaker Nabih Berri vowed Friday that Lebanon will forge ahead with its plans to divert water from a prime water source for “Israel” despite threats from the Jewish state.

"Lebanon will pursue all of her projects destined to exploit hydraulic resources... and to finish the work undertaken on the Wazzani river," Berri told reporters. "The time has passed when Lebanese water is stolen by “Israel”."

Berri warned “Israel” "against all measures that it would take to prevent Lebanon from exploiting its national waters."

“Israeli” officials accused Lebanon Wednesday of trying to change the course of a river, known as the Hasbani or Wazzani, one of three tributaries of the Jordan river which feeds the Sea of Galilee, “Israel’s” prime source of fresh water.

“Israeli” officials condemned the setting up of pumps along the tributary and warned the Jewish state it would "defend its interests," but declined to say if that meant military action.

Berri also tagged the diversion of water as recompense for “Israel’s” 22-year occupation of southern Lebanon that ended last May.

On Thursday, “Israeli” army chief Shaul Mofaz sought to calm tension over the water row, urging “Israelis” "not to talk of war."

Scarce water resources are a key factor in resolving “Israel’s” stormy relations with its neighbours, including Syria and the Palestinians, and have been the cause of Arab-”Israeli” violence in the past.

Between 1963 and 1966, Syrian attempts to divert water from the Hasbani river led to heavy exchanges of fire with “Israel”.

MPs promise intifada support
Beirut conference seeks ways to unite Arab front against “Israel”…(2)

Several MPs attending a conference on Friday pledged to hold a parliamentary session at the end of the month dedicated to supporting the intifada.
The conference, organized by the Arab Nationalist Forum in reaction to “Israel’s” recent siege on the Palestinian territories, brought together elected officials, political party representatives and activists in anticipation of the Arab Summit, to be held in Amman at the end of the month.
Fatah official Marwan Barghouti phoned from a march in the West Bank as sirens sounded in the background. He added urgency to participants’ calls to support “protesters in the streets.”
He said “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon “is trying with all his might, with tanks and helicopters, to put out the intifada in our hearts, but people are still facing him despite the siege and all the hardships.”
Protests continued all over the occupied territories Friday that resulted in clashes with “Israeli” soldiers and more bloodshed.
“We want to look to the Arab nation, parties, civil institutions and intellectuals and see a real people’s movement that is supporting us with word and struggle and giving the people in the street support,” Barghouti said.
Beirut MP Mohammed Qabbani answered the call and pledged to form a parliamentary committee on the issue.
“On March 27, the same day the Arab summit begins, we’ll hold a parliamentary session to discuss supporting the intifada,” he said.
Qabbani also echoed many participants’ concerns and called for a more efficacious Arab summit this time around. He explained that the previous one held in Cairo at the start of the intifada almost six months ago resulted only in unfulfilled promises to cut off ties with “Israel” and deliver financial support.
Beirut MP Bassem Yamout proposed that the Arab summit create a three-pronged negotiating team, representing Lebanon, Syria and Palestine, to implement a “unified strategic plan” based on common interests of recovering occupied land and reclaiming Al-Quds.
“The “Israeli” people voiced their opinion loudly and clearly in the last elections ­ they said ‘no’ to a just and complete peace and ‘yes’ to … military means,” he said.
“We need to coordinate our efforts and present a united front.”
Other suggestions made during the conference included the founding of an independent Arab fund to finance an Arabic media project that will publicize the Palestinian point of view internationally.
Many applauded and cheered when one participant insisted that a square in Beirut be named after Mohammed al-Durra (a Palestinian child who was shot to death by “Israeli” soldiers in his father’s arms) and replace the title of “one of the many French generals who adorn this city.”
Most participants insisted that lifting sanctions on Iraq, the right of return for Palestinian refugees and defending water sources and water rights against “Israel” should be priorities for the upcoming Arab summit.
““Israel” is starting to feel the economic pain resulting from the intifada,” said Minister of State Beshara Merhej. “We have to step up efforts to support it because when we’re helping Al-Quds, we’re helping ourselves and the Arab nation.”
The head of the Al-Quds Institute in Beirut, Masoud Shabbis, said the recent trial of 600 “Israeli” citizens who refused to complete their required military service was a sign that “the end is near for “Israel”.”

500 Palestinians in Lebanon protest against “Israeli” blockade on territories…(3)

“500 Palestinians demonstrated Friday at the Ain al-Helweh refugee camp in Lebanon to denounce “Israel’s” blockade on the occupied terrorities and support the nearly six-month-old Palestinian uprising.

Carrying banners denouncing the blockade and "attempts to starve the Palestinian people," the demonstrators burned an effigy of “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon.

Holding up a map of Palestine bearing the names of 374 Palestinian and “Israeli” Arab martyrs of the uprising, the protesters also called for "international protection for the Palestinian people" and Arab support for the intifada.

The protesters had been called to the rally by the three major factions of the Palestine Liberation Organization: Palestinian Authority head Yasser Arafat's Fatah and the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine and Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine.

Sharon announced Wednesday the easing of some parts of the crippling blockade, in particular allowing goods to be sent into the territories and easing Palestinians' freedom of movement.

But Palestinians protested for a full lifting of the embargo, and Palestinian information minister Yasser Abed Rabbo said the “Israeli” moves were for show.

"These are lies to deflect international criticism," Abed Rabbo said.

“Israeli” Occupation Troops kill A Palestinian, Injures 20…(4)

“Israeli” troops shot and killed a Palestinian and injured three others Friday, one critically, at the Mintar crossing point between occupied Palestine and the Gaza Strip, medical officials said.

Mohammed Jumaa Abu Oun, 20, died after he was shot in the chest at Mintar, hospital officials said.

Witnesses said there had been no clashes taking place when the boy was shot.

Abu Oun’s death brought to about 400 the number of Palestinians killed since the uprising broke out nearly six months ago.

Separately, “Israeli” soldiers  injured another 17 Palestinians with rubber-coated steel bullets during clashes on the edge of the West Bank city of Ramallah, medical officials said.

The clashes in Ramallah followed a march of some 1,000 Palestinians who called for the continuation of their intifada, or uprising, against the “Israeli” occupation.

“Intifada all the way! On with the Intifada!” shouted marchers in Ramallah, some of them waving Palestinian and Iraqi flags.
Other demonstrators carried pictures of Hassan Nasrallah, Secretary-General  Hizbullah.

At a rally in the Gaza Strip, the militant group Islamic Jihad vowed to “teach “Israel” a lesson.”
“We much teach the Zionist enemy a lesson they will not forget,” said the secretary general of the Islamic Jihad Movement in Palestine, Ramadan Abdallah Shallah. He was speaking to the crowd of more than 2,000 supporters by telephone from the group’s headquarters in Damascus.

Marchers in Nablus said they planned more demonstrations on Saturday and would march on “Israeli” checkpoints on the outskirts of the city, an action that would likely trigger clashes with “Israeli” soldiers posted there to enforce the closure.

On the other hand, Al-Quds brigade, the military wing of the Islamic Jihad, announced today that  it has attacked a settler's car near Dair  Sharaf crosspoint in Nablus.

The brigade said in a statement that Martyr Ossama  Turkman's group attacked the car  where a settler died and another was  wounded.

“Israeli” occupation troops  admitted one settler was wounded and  was rushed to the hospital.
