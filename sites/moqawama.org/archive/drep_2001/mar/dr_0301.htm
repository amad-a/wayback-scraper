March Thursaday 01-2001

 Thurs2ay 29-2000

 

“Israeli” Occupation Army Admit Deficiency in Responding to Hizbullah Operations…(1)

The “Israeli” occupation army  once more acknowledged it  faces difficulties in confronting Hizbullah  operations in the occupied Lebanese  Shibaa Farms.

In a report on the investigations into the last Hizbullah operation in the  Shibaa Farms, the “Israeli” north army commander, Gabi Ashkinazi, admitted  his army was deficient in  responding to the Resistance missile  attack in Mount Rous, which led to the killing of an “Israeli” soldier and the  wounding of three others, two weeks  ago.

The report says the “Israeli” army did  not efficiently open fire following the attack.

Ashkinazi also admitted for the first  time that the head of the “Israeli” army unit  targeted in the Resistance operation  was among those injured.

Meanwhile, “Israeli” enemy warplanes violated  the Lebanese airspace once again Wednesday, when flying over the  regions of Hasbayya in the south and the West Bekaa.

Hammoud seeks aid for detainees…(2)

Foreign and Emigrant Affairs Minister Mahmoud Hammoud on Wednesday called on the Republic of South Africa to use its leverage on the Democratic Republic of Congo to get its government to release 12 Lebanese who have been in detention for over a month.
Hammoud had already asked the authorities in France, Belgium and Sudan to do the same.

“Israeli” Aggressions leaves more Palestinian Martyrs, Wounded…(3)

Palestinians fired shots Wednesday at an “Israeli” road construction crew,  wounding a settler in the leg and hand.

The shots came from the West Bank town of Qalqilya.

Also Intifadah fighters opened fire on an “Israeli” occupation army patrol south of the Kesovim Checkpoint in the Gaza Strip.

On the other hand, the “Israeli” Occupation army opened fire from an outpost in the Gaza Strip, wounding a 5-year-old girl in the thigh outside her nursery school.

A Palestinian security source said “Israeli” tanks fired at a  Palestinian police post in the southern Gaza town of Rafah, injuring two policemen.

An “Israeli” security source said there was heavy exchange of fire in the area.

This followed a night of fierce “Israeli” shelling on Palestinian areas.

A 55- year-old Palestinian man was killed when an enemy  shell hit his home in the West Bank town of Ramallah.

Another man was considered clinically dead.

Also a 13-year old Palestinian boy was critically wounded by heavy “Israeli” fire near the Mintar crossing.

Meanwhile, the Occupation army went on with its house demolition policy and used bulldozers to turn into debris a two-family Palestinian house in the West Bank village of Rafat.

As usual the demolition was carried out under the pretext that the Palestinians lacked construction permits.  The house is close to the site where Palestinian resistance fighters shot and wounded five Jewish settlers in two operations Tuesday night.
