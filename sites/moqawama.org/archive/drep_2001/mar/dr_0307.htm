“Israel” violates Lebanon's airspace; “Israeli” troops open gunfire on Lebanese demonstrators…(1)

“ “Israeli” troops on Tuesday opened gunfire on demonstrators along the Fatima Gate border fence, but there were no casualties. “Israeli” warplanes also breached Lebanese airspace. Warjets flew at low altitude over the Kfar Chouba frontier village and areas close to the occupied Shibaa Farms. 

Talks over kidnapped “Israelis” ‘at standstill’…(2)

“Israeli” Army was slamed for preventing the kidnapping bid

The relatives of three “Israeli” soldiers kidnapped by Hizbullah fighters last October have claimed that the negotiations mediated by Germany to win their release have broken down.
The relatives say “Israel” halted negotiations with Hizbullah that were being brokered by the head of Germany’s intelligence agency a month ago when outgoing “Israeli” Prime Minister Ehud Barak refused to accept Hizbullah’s demands.
The families said they could not make public the details of Hizbullah’s demands.
The brother of one of the kidnapped soldiers made a similar claim in December, which was denied at the time by “Israeli” Deputy (War) Defense Minister Ephraim Sneh.
Hizbullah’s chief demand is the return of Lebanese detainees in “Israeli” jails in exchange for the release of the three soldiers and a fourth “Israeli”, Elhanan Tannenbaum ­ claimed by Hizbullah to be a Mossad agent and by the “Israelis” as a businessman. But Hizbullah is also demanding the release of hundreds of Palestinian prisoners as well as “Israeli” Army maps depicting the location of land mines in south Lebanon. Hizbullah is reportedly also seeking the release of 10 Syrian prisoners.
An official with a humanitarian organization said that the negotiations have not been halted but were in a “static situation” until the new “Israeli” government takes office.
“I believe most probably there has been no progress until now … I don’t expect anything to happen soon,” the official said. “The German mediators can only go as fast as the two sides are willing to go.”
The relatives of the “Israeli” soldiers made the claim on Sunday following a visit to the site of the kidnapping in the Shibaa Farms.
They took the opportunity of the trip to slam the “Israeli” Army for failing to take greater precautions against a kidnapping bid.
“We see how badly the army failed. If the commanders had been more responsible and made the right decisions, then this kidnapping would not have happened,” Yaacov Avitan, the father of Sergeant Adi Avitan, was quoted as saying by The Al-Quds Post.
An “Israeli” Army inquiry into the kidnappings found fault with local commanders who were blamed for not taking sufficient precautions and for acting too slowly in the immediate aftermath of the abductions.

Italy ready to boost aid for nation…(3)

Italian officials are ready to boost assistance to Lebanon, including helping the South, President Emile Lahoud said.
The president, who returned on Sunday from a six-day official visit to Italy and the Vatican, said Italy would provide Lebanon with military assistance according to an agreement to be signed between the two countries.
The president said that “Italy has expressed readiness to increase its help to Lebanon with regard to the land mines still buried in the South.”
Lahoud said Italy and the Vatican supported Lebanon’s refusal to accept that Palestinian refugees be settled here.
“This showed once again that Lebanon was right in its choices regarding the future of Lebanese and their role in the region,” the president said.

Military wing of hamas claims responsibility for netanya bombing…(4)

The military wing of the Palestinian Hamas movement has claimed responsibility for the suicide attack that left a bomber and three “Israelis” killed in Netanya on Sunday. In a fax received by AFP in Beirut, the Ezzedine al-Qassam brigades said it has been making good on a pledge to unleash ten suicide bombers once “Israeli” hardline leader Ariel Sharon takes power. The group said Sunday's attack is the first. “Israel” has stepped up security as Sharon prepares to take office. 

Sharon's aides are scheduled to file his coalition agreement in the Knesset or parliament, officially declaring the Likud leader's intention to take power by presenting the government for approval by legislature on Wednesday. Knesset Speaker Avraham Burg said “Israelis” are ready to give the patch-quilt cabinet a chance. Sharon, reviled by Arabs for masterminding a number of wars and massacres, has kept his remarks low-key in the days before he takes office. Palestinians said they will take a wait-and-see approach when dealing with the hawk. Sharon's unity government will have a majority in the 120-member Knesset, combining outgoing Ehud Barak's Labor Party with ultra-nationalist blocs that favor expelling Palestinians from the occupied West Bank and Gaza Strip. Sharon has also wooed the ultra-Orthodox Shas party, notorious for its anti-peace policies. Labor's Shimon Peres, who will be foreign minister in Sharon's cabinet, called for a resumption of negotiations, saying it was the only way to resolve the 53-year-old conflict between the two sides. Peres called for new moves to restore security cooperation with the Palestinians and said help must be provided to impoverished Palestinian civilians. “Israel”'s closure on the Palestinian territories has been described as a time bomb after thousands of families lost their sources of income. The closure has been coupled with new “Israeli” admission of encroachment on Palestinian Authority land. “Israeli” army officers were quoted as saying they are nibbling on territories under Yasser Arafat's control to show Palestinians there is a quote territorial price for the intifada or uprising against occupation that erupted in late September. In an indirect threat, senior security officials warned they would capture three roads in Gaza leading to illegally-built Jewish settlements on grounds of bolstering security for the settlers. But outgoing Foreign Minister Shlomo Ben Ami said peace remains the only option.  He stressed that Barak's government had laid the framework for future peace talks. Sharon who triggered the violence ruled out a resumption of negotiations before the intifada ends. He also distanced himself from talks concluded with the Palestinians by his predecessor. The Palestinian Hamas movement vowed to unleash ten suicide bombers when the Likud leader takes office. “Israeli” troops have deployed all over the country, following Sunday's bomb attack in the coastal city of Netanya that killed the bomber and three “Israelis”.  Hamas's military wing, Ezzedine al-Qassam, has kept “Israel” on guard over more suicide attacks. 

Peres Quits as Foreign Minister if Sharon Re-invades Gaza…(5)

“Israel”'s Labor Party candidate for foreign minister in the upcoming Sharon government has warned that he would quit if the hardline Prime Minister attempts to re-invade the West Bank and Gaza.

"I will also resign if the new government revokes the (1993) Oslo Accords," Peres said in a lengthy interview published by the Tel Avis daily Maarev, which was reproduced by An-Nahar.

"I have learned that it is impossible to resolve a conflict between two people by their gun barrels," Peres said. "I have served long in making guns and now I want to work for good neighborhood."

Peres said “Israel” was obligated to lift the economic blockade of Gaza and the West Bank. "We have to abstain from pushing other people into terrorism. We have to understand that fire cannot be quenched by fire."
Peres said he still believed that an understanding could be reached with the Palestinians. "We have to sit together and try to devise ways to put the fire down," he said.
The incoming foreign minister called for the creation of "sentimental bonds" with the Palestinians. "I have frequently told Yasser Arafat he would get a better response if he spoke like King Hussein and President Anwar Sadat," Peres added.
He asserted that he would not run for the chairmanship of the Labor party to replace outgoing Prime Minister Ehud Barak.
"I have held that job for a long time. I want not to devote all my time to peace-making," Peres said. 

 More Palestinians than Jews in under 50 years…(6)

Figures published by the UN reveal that by 2050 the number of Palestinians living both within the Green Line and in the territories will exceed that of the Jewish inhabitants. The number of Palestinians will reach 11.8 million, compared to 10.1 million Jews.
The figures, published as part of a comprehensive report on world population, also show that the number of Iraqi citizens is expected to grow by 132% and that of Iranians by 62%.
