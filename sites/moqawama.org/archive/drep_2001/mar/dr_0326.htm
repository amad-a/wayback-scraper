Lebanon's Resistance ,People, Army's  Readiness Deters “Israeli” Attacks, Says Hizbullah…(1)

Hizbullah Deputy Secretary-General Sheikh Naim Qassem said Hizbullah wants to keep the “Israeli” file open since " we still have rights to recuperate. There are still detainees in (“Israeli”) prisons, and the Shibaa Farms is still occupied."

During a ceremony held in honor of  teachers in the city of Baalabak, Sheikh Qassem asserted that was it not for the continuous readiness of  the resistance, the people, and the army, “Israel” would have been satisfied by launching its threats to prevent Lebanon from using its water resources.

“Israel” May release Obeid, Dirani for Evidence That its 3 Soldiers with Hizbullah are Alive…(2)

“Israel’s” defense (war) minister has offered to consider the release of Sheikh Abdul Karim Obeid and Hajj Mustafa Dirani if he gets evidence that three “Israeli” soldiers in Hizbullah captivity were still alive.
Benyamin Ben-Eliezer made the offer shortly after Sharon's latest claim of an Iranian airlift of missile supplies to Hizbullah capable of hitting “Israel’s” center, An Nahar said Saturday. There was no immediate response from Hizbullah.
An “Israel” court on Sunday extended the detention of the two most prominent Lebanese prisoners in “Israel” for one more month to give the defense (war) minister the chance to decide whether or not they should remain in “Israeli” custody without trial.
Obeid was kidnapped by “Israeli” commandos from his home in Jibsheet in 1989 and Diran was grabbed by helicopter-borne “Israeli” paratroopers from his bed in the Bekaa village of Qsarnaba in 1994.
"I am not prepared to release any one as long as our children remain held there. Period," Ben-Eliezer told “Israel’s” state-run Channel-Two television station.
Asked whether he would release Obeid and Dirani if gets evidence that the three soldiers were alive, Ben Eliezer said: "I want an evidence that our children are still alive. I will then be naturally willing to discuss every thing."
“Israel” had for long been linking the release of Obeid and Dirani to the fate of missing “Israeli” airman Ron Arad, who was captured after his fighter-bomber was shot down near Sidon in 1986.
Hizbullah had said it would be willing to let intermediaries see and check on the three “Israeli” soldiers If “Israel” releases Obeid and Dirani.
It remains to be seen whether Ben-Eliezer's offer would lead to a resumption of mediation efforts by Germany to arrange a Hizbullah-”Israeli” exchange of prisoners. The Germans had suspended the mediation upon Sharon's accession to power in “Israel” last month.

Hizbullah Asserts to Use Force Against Any “Israeli” Aggression…(3)

Hizbullah's official of  southern Lebanon Sheikh Nabil Qawook said any “Israeli” attack on Lebanese infrastructures or the Wazzani River is a violation of Lebanese sovereignty and will be faced with force.

Addressing Lebanese tobacco farmers in liberated southern Lebanon, Sheikh Qawook stressed Lebanon will exercise its right concerning the use of the Wazzani waters  with nobody's favor.

Meanwhile, President of the South Council  Qabalan Qabalan said Lebanon will  start the coming Wednesday to  pump water from the Wazzani  River to the southern village of Wazzani,  after the pumping machine have been  set up a week earlier than  planned.

He said “Israeli” threats have  motivated the workers to end the  job quickly.

On the other hand, “Israeli” threats continued  against Lebanon's use of its own water  resources.

The 2nd “Israeli” Channel  reported a security source to have said  that if the Lebanese pump out a large  amount of water  from the river, than  “Israel” will be obliged to reconsider its response.

The Wazzani River gushes out from  Lebanon and flows into occupied  Palestine where it feeds the Jordan  River.

Missile claims dismissed…(4)

Hizbullah and Iran Sunday dismissed claims by “Israeli” Prime Minister Ariel Sharon that Iran has sent missiles into Lebanon which are capable of reaching central “Israel”.
Nabatieh MP and Hizbullah member Mohammed Raad said Sharon’s claims were “an attempt to win over international sympathy and bolster military assistance for the Zionist entity.”
Raad, who was speaking during a ceremony in Kfar Sir in Nabatieh, said that such claims were “an indication of the fear in which the enemy has lived since its defeat in Lebanon.”
Sharon accused Iran on Friday of sending missiles to Lebanon which could reach as far as central “Israel” and of turning Lebanon into an “international center for terrorism.”
Iran rejected Sharon’s claim Saturday. “The remarks by the Zionist regime’s prime minister are sheer lies,” Foreign Ministry spokesman Hamid-Reza Assefi told the official Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA).
“Such ridiculous claims are aimed to divert public opinion in the region from intensification of the Zionist regime’s policies of violence, intimidation and tension,” Assefi added.
Iranian Foreign Minister Kamal Kharrazi was in Lebanon last week to encourage a broad anti-”Israeli” alliance ahead of Tuesday’s Arab summit in Jordan.
Iranian Ambassador Mohammad Ali Subhani told IRNA on Sunday that the “Israeli” claims were “groundless.”
Subhani said “Israel” considered any resistance from occupied Arab lands as terrorist acts, while it committed the “worst and inhuman acts” in Lebanon and Palestine.
“Sharon should be tried as a war criminal,” he said.
Subhani said it was not unlikely that Sharon wanted to prepare for new crimes against humanity in the Occupied Territories, with American approval.
He stressed Lebanon’s right to adopt resistance until liberation was completed and all detainees in “Israeli” prisons released.
“Iran has always supported such a legitimate right,” he said.
Raad said it was unlikely that “Israel” would attack Lebanon because the Jewish state knows it would have to pay a high price.
He said Arab countries should take advantage of the “Israeli” crisis to “devise an offensive strategy” to abort “Israel’s” “plots,” instead of yielding to the US administration.

Hariri Rejects Sharon's Charges of Iranian Airlift of Missiles to Hizbullah…(5)

Prime Minister Hariri has rejected Ariel Sharon's barrage of charges, that Iran has armed Hizbullah with missiles capable of hitting the center of “Israel”, and that “Israel” was not, in a position to start a new Middle East war.
"We're not going to tell Sharon whether his charges of an ongoing Iranian airlift of missile supplies to Hizbullah via Syria are true or not. This is classified military information," Hariri said in an interview reported by the LBCI.
"But I want to marvel whether “Israel” does lack any weapons itself. Possessing nuclear bombs, ballistic missiles, and the biggest arsenal in the Middle East, “Israel” should be the last, to speak about arms airlifts," Hariri said.
Nothing that “Israel” has been adamantly refusing to sign an agreement to ban weapons of mass destruction in the Middle East, the premier said he believed the big powers would not let Sharon kindle a new Middle East war.

Beirut plans to dismantle “Israeli” pump on Hasbani…(6)

Council of the South president Qabalan Qabalan says Beirut plans to stop “Israel” from diverting water by dismantling an “Israeli”-built pump along the Hasbani River.
“We have completed the necessary contacts with the United Nations and we are about to take some practical steps to stop “Israelis” from stealing the water,” Qabalan told reporters at the new Wazzani pumping station Saturday.
The newly completed Lebanese pumping station will be opened in an official ceremony on Wednesday, Qabalan told The Daily Star. He said tests were successfully conducted Friday to ensure that the pump was operational. He also said final preparations have been under continuous “Israeli” surveillance.
The water pump will supply the village of Wazzani with water from the Hasbani.
In 1964, “Israeli” installed its own small pump at the Wazzani Springs on the west bank of the Hasbani. The pump feeds drinking water into the occupied village of Ghajar.
Earlier this month, “Israeli” officials condemned Lebanon for setting up their pump along the Wazzani, warning that “Israel” would “defend its interests.” Since then, “Israel’s” army chief, General Shaul Mofaz, has warned against “talk of war.”
UN peacekeepers responded to “Israeli” accusations that the Lebanese were trying to divert the river by saying workers were only building a four-inch pipe to carry water to “a poor village lacking water.”
“You don’t divert a river with a pipe so small,” said Timur Goksel, spokesman for the UN Interim Forces in Lebanon.
UN envoy Steffan de Mistura said Friday that he has been assured by “Israeli” Foreign Minister Shimon Peres “that the water issue should be dealt with cautiously and should not … provoke a crisis.” ­

Beirut complains to UN over foreigners crossing into “Israel”…(7)

“ Beirut accused “Israel” Sunday of allowing 15 Kurds to cross into its territory and complained to the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL).

"Lebanon has protested to the UNIFIL command following the exploitation by “Israel” of the particular case of Ghajar on the international border by “Israel”, which facilitated the passage of 15 Kurdish nationals into area," according to a presidential statement.

The village of Ghajar is split in two by the "blue line," drawn by the United Nations following the “Israeli” withdrawal from southern Lebanon in May 2000 and acts as a border point.

An “Israeli” military spokesman earlier said that 15 unarmed foreigners, who had entered “Israeli” territory from Lebanon, were arrested by the army and questioned by the security services.

According to the Lebanese police, the people concerned were daily farm workers in Sarada, a village located some two kilometres (1.2 mile) from the central sector of the border between Lebanon and “Israel”.

They went mid-morning to the banks of the Wazzani river and set up as if to have a picnic, then crossed the river to the adjacent village of Ghajar, which has been occupied by the Jewish state since 1967, the statement said.

“Israel” accepted that Lebanon would have sovereignty over the northern part of the village and decided that its troops would no longer carry out patrols.

The “Israeli” army has regularly fired on demonstrators at the border since its May 24 withdrawal from southern Lebanon after 22 years of occupation.

Arab grouping backs intifada…(8)

The General Secretariat for the Conference of Arab Parties, which convened in Beirut on Friday and Saturday, expressed support to the Palestinian intifada in the West Bank and Gaza.
In its final resolutions, the conference called on Arab governments to “continue their support to the Palestinian intifada at the political, media and financial levels.” The conference called for condemning ““Israeli” aggression against the Palestinians and the American blind bias in favor of “Israel” and against the Arab cause.”
The conference addressed an open letter to the heads of state meeting in Amman, urging them to stop seeking partial solutions to the Arab-”Israeli” conflict and to try to achieve maximum solidarity among Syria, Lebanon and the Palestinians as well as other Arab states immediately involved with the Arab-”Israeli” conflict.
The message urged the Arabs leaders to speak up in defense of Al-Quds’s Arab identity and called on them to honor the pledges they took in the Cairo summit in 2000, especially with regard to financial assistance they promised to Lebanon. 

Several Palestinians Injured in Anti-Siege Protest

“Israeli” occupation soldiers have fired into the air and thrown tear gas and stun grenades to break up protests against the continued blockade of Palestinian towns and villages.  The protests in the West Bank and Gaza Strip were the latest in nearly six months of a Palestinian uprising against “Israeli” occupation.

At the Al-Ram army checkpoint near Ramallah, soldiers fired tear gas and stun grenades to disperse a few hundred people marching and staging a sit-down protest. Several people were injured, witnesses said.

In the Gaza Strip, demonstrators were peacefully protesting  against the siege and lockout policy  followed by the “Israeli” authorities on  the Palestinian territories.

Members  from the Palestinian Legislative Council  participated in the procession. Upon  reaching the Martyrs crossroad  near Netzarim settlement, several  “Israeli” vehicles surrounded the  demonstrators while “Israeli” soldiers  targeted them with tear bombs and  robber bullets in an attempt to disperse  the crowds. Some suffocation cases  were reported.

Also, a Palestinian source said “Israeli” soldiers launched a  bomb at  the procession of  the  Amnesty International  Secretary-General Peter Sneih  while making a tour in Salaheddine crossing  near Rafah in the Gaza Strip.

The bomb was launched from  an “Israeli” outpost  near the Egyptian border.

The source said  that neither Sneih nor his delegation  members were injured. Sneih's visit  aims at briefing on the “Israeli” violations in the Palestinian territories.

“Israeli” occupation forces continued its  tight blockade over the Palestinian  cities ,villages and camps.

Information mentioned that  “Israeli” forces stormed into many  houses near the al-Khalil(Hebron) main street at  the western entrance of the Arroub camp, where it exercised  aggression against the residents and  searched the houses.

Meanwhile, the enemy forces continued to close the Martyrs' street  and many different quarters of the occupied city preventing the  Palestinians from using their cars.

Husseini: We will topple Sharon…(10)

A senior official with the Palestinian Authority said Saturday that “Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon’s government would fall if it was unable to halt the Palestinian intifada.
“There is a clear decision; we should bring down Sharon and his government on the security level to be able to bring them down politically,” said Faisal Husseini, the senior PLO official in Al-Quds.
Husseini, here to attend a conference of the Union of Arab Lawyers, made the comments during a visit to the Ain al-Hilweh refugee camp in Sidon.
He also urged participants in this week’s Arab summit to help topple Sharon and “refrain from giving him and his government a chance.”
“We want you to resist politically,” he said. “We will resist and then we will break him.”
Arab leaders are scheduled to meet in Amman on Tuesday and Wednesday to discuss ways to support to the Palestinian uprising. They are also expected to adopt a united position regarding the United Nation’s sanctions against Iraq.
Husseini urged the Arab leaders to send a clear message to Sharon that Palestinians will not negotiate with “Israel” unless the Jewish state “gives them their rights” and “stops practicing its racist policies.”
Husseini then warned of two possible dangers to the Palestinian cause. One would be to enter negotiations without the uprising, a move he called “useless.” The other would be for Palestinians to fight without having any clear political agenda.
Husseini said the intifada’s goals are “liberation and independence,” and that “neither “Israeli” oppression nor the United States will stop it.”
