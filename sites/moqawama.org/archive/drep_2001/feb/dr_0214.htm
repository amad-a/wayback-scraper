February Wednesday 14-2001

 Thurs2ay 29-2000

 

“Israeli” fighters break sound barrier over Lebanese port city…(1)

“ “Israeli” warplanes broke the sound barrier over the southern Lebanese port city of Tyre Tuesday, causing five supersonic bangs which unnerved local residents.

“Israeli” fighters also broke the sound barrier over Beirut on Sunday and overflew southern Lebanon on Monday.

Lebanon has lodged several complaints with the United Nations in recent months about “Israeli” planes breaching its airspace, saying these flyovers are a violation of the UN Security Council's 425 resolution which prompted the “Israeli” withdrawal of southern Lebanon in May, ending 22 years of occupation.

Lahoud calls for more Arab cooperation…(2)

Lebanese President Emile Lahoud asserted the importance of Arab solidarity and coordination, especially in the light of the ongoing events in the region.

Speaking to a delegation that participated in the 9th seminar of the Arab armed forces training associations, held in Beirut, Lahoud said  that regional developments reacquires cooperation similar to that between Lebanon and Syria, one of the elements which contributed to the liberation of the South and Western Bekaa from the “Israeli” occupation.

Hariri says “Israel” is behind instability…(3)

Lebanese Prime Minister Rafik Hariri is due to visit France on Tuesday to seek support and draw aid and investment in tackling the country's recession.

Hariri will sound out French President Jacque Chirac on the latest developments in the region.

Speaking ahead of a visit to Paris, the French daily “Le Monde” quoted Hariri as saying that "“Israel”" still harbors expansionist ideas, and Lebanon will not deploy the army on the Lebanese-Palestinian border until "“Israel”" resumes negotiations.

Hariri, who said that Lebanon is after peace instead of war, blamed "“Israel”" for the instability in the region.

“Israeli” occupation kills two Palestinians…(4)

Amid the continuation of gun battles in the Gaza Strip and the West Bank, Palestinian resistance fighters  detonated a roadside bomb, targeting an “Israeli” occupation army tank and jeep at the Rafah town near the Egyptian-Palestinian border. A number of “Israeli” soldiers were wounded.

Also the “Israeli” army reported that a bomb was launched at one of its forces on the Shalala street in the West Bank town of al-Khalil (Hebron).

Another day of “Israeli” aggressions against the Palestinians claimed the lives of a 14-year-old boy and a Fatah security official.

Massoud Ayad, 50, a lieutenant-colonel in Palestinian Chairman Yasser Arafat's elite Force 17 guard, was assassinated when “Israeli” combat helicopters fired four missiles at the car of Ayad as he was driving in northern Gaza city.

As a cover up for the operation, “Israeli” helicopters shelled a Force 17 camp and a base belonging to the Palestinian National Security Forces.

Palestinian security officials called the killing a "savage assassination" and "state terrorism", and announced that Ayad, was recently promoted to lieutenat-colonel from major in Arafat's elite Force 17 guard. He is also a member of the Palestinian preventive security.

The “Israeli” army claimed Ayad was setting up a Palestinian branch of the Lebanese Hizbullah group in the Gaza Strip and said he was behind a series of shootings, and attacks against Jewish Settlements in the Strip.

"“Israel”" has came under widespread criticism from human rights groups over a series of deliberate killings of Palestinian intifadah activists.

Also, “Israeli” soldiers, in a new barbaric act, shot dead a 14-yer-old Palestinian boy, Bilal Ramadan, on Tuesday. The boy was shot in the heart by a live bullet near the Mintar crossing point.

Another Palestinian,  Mohammed Abu Harz, was injured by “Israeli” bullets.

Clashes erupted Tuesday between “Israeli” troops and Palestinians near the Khan Younis refugee camp in the Gaza Strip. “Israeli” soldiers fired shots at U.N. vehicles who were in the area.

In the West Bank town of al-Khalil (Hebron), “Israeli” occupation troops fired tear gas at Palestinian school children, who were hurling stones at the occupiers.

These events follows battles that raged overnight in the West Bank and Gaza strip.

In the Gaza Strip 120 Palestinians were injured when the “Israeli” army launched its rockets and internationally banned gaz on resident areas.

Palestinian homes in both Gaza and al-Khalil were heavily damaged.
