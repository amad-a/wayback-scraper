UAE president meets Berri, vows to help de-mine South…(1)

The president of the United Arab Emirates, Sheikh Zayed Bin Sultan al-Nahyan, stressed the “need to support Lebanon and promote its development” on Monday
Zayed, who met Speaker Nabih Berri, said Lebanon should “regain its prominent economic and cultural role on the Arab and international scenes.”
The crown prince of Abu Dhabi, Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed al-Nahyan, expressed his country’s willingness to contribute to the demining in the South, with the cooperation of the United Nations.
Sheikh Khalifa said that the UAE granted $50 million to Lebanon, but that the Lebanese government had so far failed to use the assistance to implement certain projects.
l Berri said that “Israel” had surely been taught enough lessons by the resistance and would not “try its luck” again in Lebanon.
In a speech delivered in Abu Dhabi, where he flew last week to attend an Arab parliamentary conference, Berri predicted that “Israel” would not succeed in intimidating Lebanon. “Lebanon will not accept to be a victim of any “Israeli” show of force. We are confident of our unity and our national army and we are sure that “Israel” isn’t ready to try its luck with us once again.”
He stressed that Lebanon’s peace path would continue to be closely linked to that of Syria because Syria represented a decisive factor in the region’s military strategy.
Berri also called on the new American administration to “seek a peace accord which is built on comprehensive justice for all parties concerned with the Middle East conflict.”
He urged the United States “not to push toward a military escalation in the region and not to let its bias in favor of “Israel” blind it to the suffering of the Arab people in general and the Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza in particular.”
Berri concluded that the recent “Israeli” elections were nothing but a “comedy act” that the Likud and Labor parties played to further “Israel’s” strategic military advantage against the Arabs.


Intifadah Operations Hit Various “Israeli” Occupation Targets…(2)

Palestinian sources said an “Israeli” Apache gunship heavily opened fire at a Palestinian Security post east of Gaza city.

The source added that the attack followed a strong explosion which targeted an “Israeli” convoy passing on the eastern road of Gaza city.

Also the “Israeli” occupation army shelled the main electricity transformer post in the Gaza Strip refugee camp Al Shatteh, after a fierce exchange of gunfire with Palestinian intifadah fighters in Al-Sudaniyya area, north the Gaza city.

Meanwhile, an occupation army convoy was hit by Palestinian resistance fighters between the Jewish settlement of Netzarim and the Mintar crossing in the Strip.

Earlier in the morning, two enemy outposts were subjected to gunfire near the Gosh Katif settlement, and an “Israeli” car came under sniper fire near the settlement of Neve Dekelim, also in the Strip.

Gaza- Khan Younis road was blocked later on.

Palestinians also fired at an “Israeli” army roadblock close to the village of A-Rafat south of Ramallah in the West Bank.

Palestinian sources denied the “Israeli” occupation claims that the coastal road linking Gaza city to the middle refugee camps in the Gaza Strip has been opened.

The source said enemy tanks are still blocking the passage of civilians and cars. While vehicles were allowed to pass on the Salaheddine road under the Surveillance of “Israeli” tanks.

The occupation army had earlier said it ordered the removal of roadblocks which in effect divided the Gaza Strip in two, following an appeal from U.S Secretary of State Colin Powell to end the closure of Palestinian territories.
