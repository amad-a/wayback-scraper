Hizbullah and UNIFIL talk about border…(1)

Hizbullah’s southern commander, Sheikh Nabil Qaouk, met with UNIFIL’s senior adviser, Timur Goksel, Monday amid renewed tensions along the Lebanon-”Israel” border.
Qaouk accused the United States of pressuring Lebanon to give up attacks against “Israeli” troops in the Shibaa Farms.
“This won’t change the stand of the resistance as to the continuation of its operations in the Shibaa and stopping all “Israeli” violations,” he said. ““Israel’s” defeat in the South created an inferiority complex for them … If the “Israeli” Army were capable of doing something against Lebanon it wouldn’t have hesitated.”
Goksel said that he had discussed a number of issues “in light of regional tensions,” including the relationship between UNIFIL and Hizbullah.

Resistance's victory to be discussed in academic institutions…(2)

Lebanese Education Minister Abdul Raheem Mourad issued a communiqué to the schools and universities in which he asks them to dedicate one session on Tuesday to talk about the role of the resistance and its heroic actions.

The Resistance in Lebanon has been praised in the Arab and Islamic world for forcing the “Israeli” occupation army to end its 22-year occupation of south Lebanon.

 

“Israeli” occupation assasinates a Hamas leader…(3)

In latest developments, a member of the Hamas group, Mahmoud Madani was killed in an apparent assassination attempt in the Balata refugee camp near the West Bank city of Nablus.

A private source said Madani,25, was fired at from an “Israeli” military outpost at the hilltop near the refugee camp while returning from al-Fottooh mosque. His chest received three bullets.

Martyr Madani was arrested two times by the “Israeli” occupation in 1994.

12 Palestinians were shot by “Israeli” enemy gun fire in Khan Younis refugee camp in the Gaza Strip and near al-Khodor village in the West Bank.
Meanwhile, a roadside bomb exploded at a passing enemy convoy near Netzarim settlement.

Another bomb had exploded in an enemy patrol north of Kisovim military checkpoint.

At night, Palestinians stepped up their attacks and fired at “Israeli” military targets in Beitlaham and Baitjalla.

As a result the occupation forces deployed tanks south of the occupied al-Quds(Jerusalem) holy city.

Five Palestinians were injured during the clashes.
