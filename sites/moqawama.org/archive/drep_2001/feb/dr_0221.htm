 Thurs2ay 29-2000

German mediation between “Israel” and Hizbullah continues…(1)

The head of a visiting German parliamentary delegation to Lebanon MP Christian Sterzing said Tuesday the German mediation for the release of “Israeli” captured soldiers by Hizbullah and Arab detainees in “Israeli” prisons is going on smoothly and quietly and that Germany is doing its utmost to solve this issue.

Stifzing’s statement came after a meeting with Lebanese Foreign Minister Mahmoud Hammoud during which the situation in southern Lebanon , including the role Germany and Europe may play in the reconstruction of the formerly occupied South were discussed.

Army thanks Nasrallah for weapons…(2)

Hizbullah’s secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, met with representatives of Army Commander Michel Suleiman on Tuesday, who thanked him for a recent donation of captured “Israeli” weaponry.
Acting as personal representatives of Suleiman, the two officers conveyed his appreciation for Hizbullah’s decision to hand over equipment that was recovered from the “Israeli” Army and its militia following last May’s withdrawal.

Marathon trials of Lebanese collaborators with “Israel” near end…(3)

“Eight months of trials by a Lebanese military court of collaborators with “Israel” during the Jewish state's occupation of southern Lebanon are nearing their end.

Late Monday the court dealt with another 41 defendants, sentencing 21 of them to prison terms of between six months and three years for collaboration.

The remainder were sentenced to one month in jail and fines of half-a-million Lebanese pounds (330 dollars), for entering “Israel” to work.

The collaborators were largely former members of the South Lebanon Army (SLA), a militia formed by “Israel” whose collapse precipitated the “Israeli” withdrawal from southern Lebanon last May after a 22-year occupation.

The latest verdicts, plus 57 handed down last week, bring to 2,441 the number delivered since June 5 last year. A total of some 2,500 people have been charged.

They include 15 people sentenced to death and three to life imprisonment, all in their absence.

A failed “Israeli” attempt to kidnap a Hamas leader…(4)

Exclusive information to al-Manar Channel reported that  a special unit in the “Israeli” army executed an airdrop this morning on  zone (A), which is under the control of the Palestinian Authority in Jenine, in an attempt to abduct a member in Hamas called Nasr Jarar.

The “Israeli” unit stormed into Jarar’s house and detained his brother but sooner he was set free.

Following the airdrop, clashes broke between the “Israeli” soldiers and a group of Palestinian fighters who headed toward the region after hearing news of the “Israeli” attack.

On the other hand, armed confrontations between the Palestinian resistance fighters and the “Israeli” enemy continued today in Qods(Jerusalem) where two “Israeli” settlers were wounded. One of  the settlers was stabbed by a Palestinian girl.

Another Palestinian girl was wounded during morning armed clashes in the city of al-khalil(Hebron).

Sporadic clashes took place during the night in the West Bank, where Palestinians fighters targeted the enemy's camp in Shidima and Racheal’s Tomb.
“Israeli” troops in the settlement of Gilo and Psagot returned machine gun fire on the Palestinian village of Beit Jala and al-Biri where several Palestinians were wounded.

Two foreign workers one from Holland and the other from South Africa were wounded during clashes that took place in the southern entrance to Tulkarm.

 In Nablus, a vehicle carrying settlers was targeted. The enemy radio claimed that no casualties were reported.

Moreover, the occupation troops started today fortifying the houses in Gilo settlement. The project will cost more than ten million dollars.

Palestinian police arrests suspected “Israeli” collaborators…(5)
The Palestinian police announced the arrest of a number of Palestinians suspected to have provided the “Israeli” occupation with information that led to the assassination of Mahmoud Madani, a captain in the military wing of the Hamas Movement.

Meanwhile, thousands of Palestinians attended the funeral of Madani Tuesday in the West Bank town of Nablus.

Mourners carried banners calling for the avenge of Martyr Madani’s killing.

The Palestinian mujjahed was shot three times in the chest while standing in the Balata refugee camp near Nablus on Monday and died hours later in hospital.

Human rights groups have accused “Israel” of eliminating at least 15 known Palestinian activists since the start of the Palestinian intifadah late September.
