February Monday 12-2001

 Thurs2ay 29-2000

 

First Air Violation Since Sharon's Election…(1)

“Israeli” air force jets crashed the sound barrier over Beirut Sunday, hours after Prime Minister-elect Ariel Sharon made his first tour of the volatile border with Lebanon since his election victory five days ago.
The intruding fighter-bombers also flew supersonic runs over South Lebanon's port cities of Sidon and Tyre, in what Lebanese authorities described as the first air space violation in the Sharon era. A Lebanese protest is expected to be lodged with the United Nations.
Sharon, who defeated Labor Party ex-Premier Ehud Barak in “Israel’s” Feb. 6 elections, toured the border with Lebanon on Saturday, a day after Hizbullah Secretary-General Sheikh Hassan Nasrallah called the new leader of the Jewish state a "fool."
"This fool's premiership may well spell out the beginning of the end for the state of “Israel”," Nasrallah said in remarks highlighted by several Beirut dailies on Saturday.

Palestinians determined to continue the intifadah…(2)

Thousands of Palestinians attended the funeral of 16-year-old Ayman Abu Hawly who shot dead by “Israeli” Occupation forces Friday near the Jewish Settlement Kfar Darom in the Gaza Strip.

Chanting “We will avenge your death,” the Palestinian mourners marched in Gaza carrying the body of Abu-Hawly, a goat-herd.

They also vowed to go on with their uprising against the “Israeli” occupation until all Palestine is liberated.

Meanwhile, “Israeli” soldiers and Palestinian fighters exchanged gunfire in the West Bank and Gaza Strip.

Also, stone-throwing Palestinians have clashed with “Israeli” forces in the West Bank town of Ramallah.

This  followed a day of confrontations in various Palestinian territories, during which 26 Palestinians were wounded by “Israeli” bullets.

"The intifadah will defeat Sharon", Fatah Leader…(3)

Fatah leader in the West Bank , Marwan Barghouti, said the Palestinian uprising will go on even though right-wing Ariel Sharon was elected as “Israeli” Prime Minister.

Barghouti said Palestinians should not dread Sharon, asserting that the intifadah will continue and escalate until “Israel” withdraws from Palestinian land it has been occupying for more than three decades.

He went on saying that the uprising will lead to the downfall of Sharon as it did to his predecessor, Ehud Barak.

The Fatah leader was speaking during a Palestinian products exposition held to encourage the boycott of “Israeli” goods.
