Hizbullah not worried about Sharon election…(1)

Nasrallah: He could bring about the end of “Israel”

Hizbullah’s Secretary-General, Hassan Nasrallah, dispelled Arab fear Friday regarding the election of Ariel Sharon as “Israel’s” premier and said the party would continue fighting him as it fought his predecessors.
“So Sharon has won. Why all the fuss?” Nasrallah asked during a graduation ceremony at a school in Beirut’s southern suburbs. “Sharon’s arrival could actually be a blessing in disguise because it could be the beginning of the end of the Zionist entity.”
“What has made us victorious against all the previous “Israeli” leaders will again make us victorious over Sharon ­ our faith, our commitment to jihad and to sacrificing the blood of our martyrs remains alive,” he said.
Nasrallah said Arabs should stop wailing over Sharon’s victory and be happy instead, for it could herald the end of “Israel”.
“If you insist on bringing out the handkerchiefs to cry over the dead, bring them out (for tears of joy) and eat sweets over Sharon’s (death), God willing,” Nasrallah said.
He said Hizbullah would continue fighting against Sharon’s “Israel” just as it had battled the Jewish state under previous leaders.
“We aren’t living in the year 1982 any more,” Nasrallah said. “The “Israeli” Army that Sharon led to invade Lebanon has been defeated and fled from Lebanon like terrified rats.”
He was referring to “Israel’s” 1982 invasion of Lebanon ­ masterminded by then-Defense (War) Minister Sharon ­ and the massacre of hundreds of Palestinian refugees in the Sabra and Chatila refugee camps.
Nasrallah said Arabs have entered a stage of victories “after all the previous defeats,” arguing that the coming days would prove it.
“In what year are they living, all these people who are afraid of Sharon and who say that he’ll destroy Lebanon?” he said, referring to some Arab media and politicians who warned that Sharon would wage war in the Middle East.
“We strongly reject this media and political rhetoric which seeks to terrify the region and its people, particularly the Lebanese and Palestinians,” Nasrallah said.
He said that, to Hizbullah, Sharon was the same as his predecessor Ehud Barak, and the party did not support either.
Nasrallah said that as long as “Israelis” chose Sharon, “they must bear the consequences of their choice.”
Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek, a member of Hizbullah’s Shura Council, said Sharon’s election was not “a source of worry” to the party.
“The Zionist entity has fired the mercy shot at itself when it chose Sharon and shall not be tranquil from now on,” he said.
Yazbek said Arabs must realize that there would never be peace with “Israel”, “which only understands the language of resistance, jihad and intifada.”

Mosques call on Arabs to unite…(2)

With this week’s election of Ariel Sharon as “Israeli” prime minister still reverberating, Muslim clerics devoted their Friday sermons to warning of a “volcanic eruption” at any time and urging a united Arab stand to face any possible “dangers.”

Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah meanwhile said: “The Zionist society which elected Sharon is experiencing a nervous breakdown because of the security shake-up brought about by the intifada, and this made it resort to the brutal symbol of violence.”
He said that Arabs and Muslims “should not succumb to American deceit seeking to freeze the intifada,” noting that the US was “fully committed” to “Israel’s” security.
Fadlallah called on Arab leaders to draw up, at their next summit, a plan to “exert pressure on the situation in Palestine by supporting the intifada and the Palestinian people in their great cause.”
He said that the new situation should be confronted “with care, as it may prompt the enemy to intensify its aggression and try to flex its military muscles in Lebanon for intimidation purposes.”
Lebanon must be on a “maximum state of alert in full coordination between the Lebanese Army and the resistance to face all developments and threats and defeat on the enemy, should it launch any aggression,” he added.
The head of Jabal Amel clerics, Sheikh Afif al-Nabulsi, said Sharon was no different from his predecessors, recalling the “criminal role” played by the “Israeli” Labor Party, “especially the criminal (Ehud) Barak, against the Palestinians and the wars he launched on Lebanon.”
He affirmed that the intifada and the resistance movement in Lebanon “will topple Sharon and his government.”

Sheikh Abdel-Amir Qabalan, Vice-President of the Higher Shiite Council, told his congregation: “The election of the terrorist Sharon as prime minister in the Zionist entity has placed the region on the brink of the abyss and exposed it to a volcanic eruption any minute.
“It has also made the whole region face the possibilities of a new war sweeping away all efforts made so far to achieve a just, comprehensive and permanent peace,” he said.
Qabalan added that it was the international community’s responsibility to “avert the many dangers” resulting from electing Sharon in view of his “terrorist past which heralds a sanguine future.”
Qabalan added, however, that Lebanon, which “scored a huge victory against the “Israeli” enemy and forced it to withdraw from its territory in the South and Western Bekaa … will not be intimidated by Sharon’s threats and terrorism.”
Another Shiite cleric, Sheikh Abdel-Amir Shamseddine, said Sharon’s election as “head of the Zionist government puts a great responsibility on Arabs and Muslims to unite in confronting his aggressive machinations.”
Shamseddine, the dean of the Islamic Studies Institute, also said Sharon’s election “will not intimidate us and we are determined to confront him.”
He called for bringing Sharon to justice “to be tried for the massacres he committed in Sabra and Chatila” Palestinian refugee camps during the “Israeli” invasion of Lebanon in 1982.
“This criminal must face the same fate as that of war criminals,” Shamseddine said.
 

Clashes erupt on "Day of Palestinian Rage"…(3)

“Israeli” enemy troops and Palestinian gunmen traded fire in the West Bank town of Ramallah.

“Israeli” soldiers used tank mounted machine guns and heavy caliber weapons against Palestinian firing automatic rifles, from buildings under construction.

The troops also fired rubber-coated metal bullets on demonstrators.

At least two demonstrators were wounded during the clashes.

The battle flared after more than 200 Palestinians took to the streets protesting against “Israeli” occupation.

Meanwhile, stone-throwing Palestinian youths clashed with “Israeli” troops in the West Bank town of Khalil (Hebron).

The troops fired rubber coated metal bullets and stun grenades.

On the other hand, “Israeli” bulldozers continued overnight until Friday to level large areas of Palestinian agricultural lands, in the Mawasi area north of the Gaza Strip city of Khan Younis.

Also a group of Jewish Settlers, under heavy protection from the occupation army, gave written warnings to 22 Palesinian families in the area telling them to evacuate their houses before Sunday.

“Israeli” authorities, who gave the evacuation orders, justified the expelling of Palestinian families from their lands, saying that it wants to  set up a security fence to protect nearby Jewish settlements.
