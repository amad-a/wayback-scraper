Daily Report
 

 
May Monday 10-1999:

Outcome of "Israeli" vote will not change south(1)
"Sheikh Naim Qassem, Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, insisted Sunday that the goal of the resistance was liberation and that the outcome of "Israels" general elections has no relevance to Lebanon".

Our resistance is one of liberation and not for political use or to sell an idea or position, he said during a religious ceremony in Ainata.

"Qassem added that he did not care which "Israeli" candidate won the May 17 election because none of them is working for our interest.

"Tyre MP Abdullah Qasir, who is a member of Hizbullahs Loyalty to the Resistance parliamentary bloc, ruled out the possibility of any large-scale "Israeli" offensive in the near future in which occupation forces would overstep the red lines drawn by the resistance".

Netanyahu is living within narrow boundaries and realizes that venturing into any new attack is similar to entering a dark tunnel where he can do nothing but bend to the resistance attacks and withdraw unconditionally from our land, he said during a religious ceremony in Baraasheit.

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions:
4:55, four 120mm shells hit Mount Sojod.
Between 12:50 and 13:40, eleven 155mm missiles fell on Ain Tineh hilltops and nine others on Maidoun highlands.
17:45, west of Yater village came under ten 160mm missiles.
17:50, three 155mm missiles struck the perimeters of Rmadieh and Zibqeen villages.
18:25, Mazraat Hamra was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
20:30, four 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Jibaa town.

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants observed, at 12:40, an "Israeli" collaborator force as it was beefing up Kfar Houna position. Immediately afterwards, the IR group of martyr Mustafa Haidar in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit bombarded the position with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Atwi and Hassan Balhas targeted, at 17:25, enemy movements inside Blat post with the suitable weapons. Direct and certain goals were chalked up.

For its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 6:00, enemy movements inside Soweida outpost with the appropriate arms scoring direct and definite hits.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 20:25, a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Kassem and Fadi Hassan Melhem attacked it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.
