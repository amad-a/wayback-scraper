Daily Report

 
May Monday 17-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese liberated areas as reported by the IR statement:
5:00, the south of Jibal El-Botom was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
Between 5:00 and 5:15, eight 120mm shells were fired at Mount Sojod.
5:25, the perimeters of Loweizeh village exposed to four 155mm missiles.
Between 5:30 and 5:45, four 155mm missiles were unleashed at the outskirts of Jibaa town.
16:45, the surroundings of Ali Taher and Borj posts subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
17:30, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the surroundings of the new Beit Yahoun position.

Military Operations(2)
The IR team of martyr Ibrahim Mohammad Faqih attacked, at 21:40, a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying the old Beit Yahoun position with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.
