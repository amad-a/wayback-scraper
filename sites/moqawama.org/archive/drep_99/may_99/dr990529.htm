Daily Report
 
May Saturday 29-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah asserted that the security of Jezzine enclave rests with the government alone. The resistance will not replace the government because its task is to dismiss the "Israeli" enemy and its agents out of our land humiliated.

Elsewhere, "Israeli" Ma'arive said the troops have been deserting their units over the last few months because their parents have threatened to expel them from their parents houses if they returned to Lebanon.

State's security authority is the one to hold militiamen accountable, Hizbullah(1)
"Withdrawal (from Jezzine) is not a favor from those who are pulling out, but because they are forced to do so, the daily al-Sharq al-Aossat quoted a Hizbullah source as saying".

"The source said, "there are some 185 collaborating militiamen in Jezzine," stressing that "Hizbullah is not, in any way, concerned about the security situation, for this is the duty of the Lebanese army and the state's security services."

"However, Hizbullah does not acknowledge any "closed areas", the source emphasized, refusing the idea of closing Jezzine in the face of the Resistance men".

"Moreover, the source pointed out "the Jezzine residents' cooperation and sympathy with the Resistance has had the most impact in the supremacy over the "Israeli" occupation."

"He found it strange to hear that residents in Jezzine have some fears as the town is returning to be a part of the nation again".

"The source reiterated that the Lebanese authority, not Hizbullah, is the one entitled to hold Jezzine's militiamen accountable after the withdrawal. But he did not rule out the possibility of information cooperation with the concerned services in this matter".

"Elsewhere, "Israeli" commentator Ofer Shelah said the decision to withdraw from Jezzine highlighted the ambiguity in ""Israel's"" strategy".

"Hizbullah has won another battle,'' Shelah wrote in the daily Ma'ariv. "The SLA* is showing signs of collapse, and "Israel", which has not yet prepared a real withdrawal program ... will find it even more difficult to close the gaps.''

President Lahoud: No guarantees or arrangements for any kind of withdrawal(2)
"President Emile Lahoud was quoted by his visitors as saying that Lebanon has always made it clear that it will not offer security guarantees or arrangements for any "Israeli" withdrawal outside the framework of a just peace which includes Lebanon and Syria equally".

"The President added that the security of Jezzine and its people was threatened by the presence of "Israeli" and SLA fighters and not by the end of the occupation, contrary to what some are trying to say.

Goksel: The withdrawal is direct result of militia collapse(3)
"Timur Goksel, Unifils political adviser, dismissed speculation of a hidden purpose behind the pullback, arguing that the militia had collapsed in Jezzine and that a withdrawal was therefore inevitable."

It isnt Jezzine First or Jezzine Last. Its Jezzine Must, he said, referring to the ill-fated U.S. proposal for a phased withdrawal. The SLA folks simply couldnt hold it any longer.

"Israeli" occupation soldiers fleeing Lebanon(4)
"A large number of "Israeli" (occupation) troops are fleeing their units that are positioned in Lebanon, an "Israeli" daily said yesterday".

""Israeli" Ma'ariv said the troops have been deserting their units over the last few months because their parents have threatened to expel them from their (parents) houses if they returned to Lebanon".

"Investigations conducted by these "Israeli" units' commanders revealed that this phenomenon is spreading widely.

"However, "Israeli" high-ranking officers said, "It is hard to explain to the soldiers, what they are doing in Lebanon, while prime minister-elect (Ehud Barak) announces that he will pull out the army (from Lebanon) within one year. It gets even harder when we try to explain our presence in Lebanon to the soldiers' parents".

Occupation bars Mimes residents from crossing into Hasbaya, "Israeli" gunboats fired at Tyre fishermen(5)
""Israeli" warplanes Friday hovered over al-Nabatieh and Iqleem al-Toffah at medium altitude, staging mock raids".
"Elsewhere, "Israeli" gunboats at dawn targeted with machine-gun fire Lebanese fishermen's boats near the coast of Tyre. No casualties were reported.

"On the other hand, the "Israeli" occupation forces barred twenty Mimes residents from traveling into the town of Hasbaya via the Zimraya crossing "until further notice". The reasons behind the "Israeli" measure were not unveiled.

"The residents were on their way to Hasbaya to do some administrative paperwork at official departments.

"They called on the government and the district's MPs to set up administrative departments outside the occupied zone, annexing their area to the Rashayya district.

"In a fresh violation of the April Understanding, the "Israeli" occupation's artillery emplacements in Aishieh and Zaffateh, Friday afternoon, bombarded the western neighborhood in Jibaa and the vicinity of Ain Bousoir in Iqleem al-Toffah with several 155 mm rounds.

"The bombardment against Jibaa damaged 15 houses belonging to Rida Wehbe, Mohammed Ali Wehbe, Salim Jezzini, Ahmad Jezzini, Mohammed Saleh Karaki, Mohammed Raad, Abbas Dhaini, Hassan Shehab, Adnan Nehmeh, Mohammed Kammoun and Abdallah Ali Ahmed".

"Israeli" Aggressions(6)
The "Israeli" occupation troops staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas as mentioned in a communiqué issued by the IR:

5:30, the surroundings of Qantara compound came under two 60mm shells and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
8:40, the outskirts of Qabrikha village exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
13:30, Ain El-Tineh town was bombarded by fifteen 155mm missiles.
Between 15:15, Jibaa town and its perimeters came under twenty 120mm shells and nineteen 155mm missiles shutting off the electric current.
16:45, Mazraat Hamra was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
17:50, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the formerly-evacuated position of Toumat Niha firing two air-to-surface rockets.
19:15, nine 155mm missiles slammed into the outskirts of Yater.
19:40, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into Shohada (Martyrs) Valley, near Yater village, firing ten air-to-surface rockets.
19:45, the peripheries of Baraasheit town exposed to heavy machine gun fire.
21:45, the surroundings of Rmadieh and Roum posts subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
21:50, thirteen 81mm rounds struck the surroundings of Jezzine's military operation center.
Between 21:55 and 22:10, Sojod village was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
22:05, three 155mm missiles hit Mount Al-Rafih.
22:10, the perimeters of Arnoun town exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Military Operations(7)
The IR team of martyrs Bilal El-Akhras and Ali Najib Choaib attacked, at 1:00, an "Israeli" infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Fadi Al-Tawil and Hussein Haidar targeted, at 15:05, the artillery emplacement of the 20th Brigade at Ain Thaghra with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite hits were scored, the militants reported.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Ali Rida and Hussein Hayek attacked, at 19:00, enemy movements in and around Blat post with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.
