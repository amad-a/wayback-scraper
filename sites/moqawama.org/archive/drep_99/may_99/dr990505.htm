Daily Report  
 
May Wednesday 5-1999:

Introduction
The newly-appointed commander of "Israel's" proxy militia in Jezzine enclave and his driver were killed as the Islamic Resistance (IR) men detonated a roadside bomb when his car was traveling on the road of Dahr Ramleh in the occupied Jezzine.

In a pre-dawn mortar barrage also, three "Israeli" occupation troops were wounded when several shells fired by IR gunners landed inside Beaufort Castle, overlooking Arnoun.

In the meantime, the "Israeli" jets swooped low on a residential area behind Imam Ali (Peace be upon him) mosque in Baalback to bomb a building. At least two civilians were injured, the residents reported.

IR hits the collaborators' command in Jezzine again; new 20th regiment chief killed…(1)
"Only a few days after targeting the chief of the 20th regiment in the collaborating militia agent Joseph Karam (alias Alloush), the Islamic Resistance (IR) this afternoon dealt another hard blow to the command of the same regiment as a powerful roadside bomb exploded against a car belonging to the regiment killing and injuring all those in it".

"A collaborating source confirmed that the attack took place and said that agent Mounah Toumah, who was appointed a few days ago as the chief of the militia's security in Jezzine (replacing agent Alloush), was instantly killed and his bodyguard who was driving the car was wounded"

"For its part, IR issued a statement about the operation, saying that after targeting the former 20th regiment chief agent Joseph Karam, IR dealt another painful blow to the enemy's militia as the squad of Martyr Mohammed Haidar al-Jaohari detonated a large bomb against the car of one of the high ranking officers in this regiment."

"The explosion, the statement continued, took place at 14:45 as the car was passing on the road of Dahr al-Ramleh-Jezzine, killing the officer and his bodyguard".

"The enemy's radio acknowledged the IR operation and said that it killed a "senior commander" in the collaborating militia and seriously injured his bodyguard".

Enemy acknowledged injury of three soldiers near Beaufort Castle by IR attack…(2)
"An "Israeli" military spokesman today (yesterday) affirmed the injury of three "Israeli" occupation soldiers in the Islamic Resistance's (IR) attack last night on the occupation's outpost near the Beaufort Castle.

One of the injured was reported in serious conditions and the other two in moderate condition, the spokesman said, pointing out that they were transported by a helicopter to the Rambam hospital in Haifa, inside occupied Palestine".

"IR's squad of martyrs Abbas Hassan and Oussama al-Hakim at Monday midnight attacked the occupation's outpost at the Beaufort Castle with the appropriate weapons, inflicting many casualties in the ranks of the enemy troops manning the outpost".
"IR also targeted enemy movements inside the occupation's outposts in Sojod and Haddatha.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Talal Assaghir and Zoheir Tabatabai attacked, at 21:50, motorized convoy as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyr Hassan Hussein Balhas targeted, at 23:55, enemy movements in and around Hemayed position with the proper arms chalking up direct goals. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyr Fadi Hassan Melhem was attacking enemy movements in and around Haddatha compound. The militants, who used the adequate weapons, were able to chalk up direct hits.

"On the other hand, security information from the occupied zone said the "Israeli" occupation army's northern command and the "Israeli" intelligence services are conducting a largescale investigation to find out how the IR Mujahedeen infiltrated through the occupied zone in al-Bayada Naqoura and planted a bomb then detonated it against an "Israeli" foot patrol killing a staff sergeant and wounding three soldiers, especially as the area is unusually fortified by measures taken in land and at sea.

"Reports stated that the enemy's command deployed tens of soldiers in the area and the surrounding villages. They set up flying checkpoints and thoroughly searched the cars of residents and those of the collaborating officers.

"For his part, UNIFIL spokesman Timor Goksel responded to reports published by the "Israeli" daily Ha'aretz which said the "Israeli" army is conducting an investigation to know whether UNIFIL is involved in the Bayada-Naqoura bombing which killed an "Israeli" staff-sergeant and injured three soldiers.

"We regret to see that accusations against UNIFIL are becoming traditional when "Israeli" soldiers are dead and when things do not go the way they like them to go," Goksel said.

"A UNIFIL officer said, on condition of anonymity, "This is not new, when the "Israeli" liaison unit commander Major General Erez Gerestein was killed 300 meters away from a UNIFIL post and on a main road used by hundreds of cars daily, the UNIFIL was accused."

"The "Israelis" pretend to have forgotten that bombs are sometimes planted at the entrances of their outposts and positions," the UNIFIL officer added.

Alleged spies arrested by army intelligence…(3)
Two Lebanese men accused of cooperating with "Israel" were arrested by army intelligence, security sources said Tuesday.

"Souheil Barakat and Faisal Aridi are accused of forming an espionage and sabotage ring that coordinated with Unit 504, the foreign branch of "Israel's" military intelligence.

“They gathered information on movements of the army, the resistance and various political personalities in Lebanon,” one source said without providing further details.

Both men are residents of Baysour in Aley. They were arrested after their alleged activities were uncovered by army intelligence.

"Sources disclosed that prior to their arrest the two men were allegedly plotting to assassinate Raja Ward, a former South Lebanon Army* militiaman who turned himself in to authorities in July last year.

Acting on information given by Ward, authorities were able to crack a spy ring and arrest 77 agents accused of collaborating with "Israel"".

Monthly report on "Israeli" aggressions in April 1999…(5)
"There were 127 "Israeli" air and ground aggressions in the month of April. Aerial aggressions constituted 33.7%, of the gross aggressions, in comparison with the 25.4% air aggressions constituted in March 1999.

"Arnoun was once again a victim of "Israeli" brutality. The electoral considerations in the "Israeli" entity has moved the military machine into the town which was annexed to the occupied zone amidst international indifference and blatant US bias covered by feeble pretexts.

However, the Resistance men have proven the enemy claims wrong, repeatedly striking the occupation’s outpost in Beaufort Castle and its road and wounding "Israeli" troops.

"Meanwhile, the Shibaa residents’ uprising went on amidst "Israeli" suppressive measures against the steadfast people.

""Israeli" warplanes carried out 29 raids on areas in the south and the western Beqaa targeting Wadi Zillaya and a Wadi between Jibal al-Bottom and Zibqeen twice on April 4; the woods of Jarjoua, Ain Boudoir, Mlita Hilltop, and Wadis between Jibal al-Bottom and Chaitieh twice on April 10; a Wadi between Jibal al-Bottom, Zibqeen and Yater on April 11 and 12; the area just before Zibqeen and al-Chaitieh on April 15; an area between Jibal al-Bottom and Zibqeen, and an area between al-Chaitieh and Zibqeen on April 17 and 18; the Chaitieh hilltops on April 23; the Mlita Hilltops, Mazraat Oqmata, woods of Jibaa, Western Hilltops of Toumat Niha, woods of Zibqeen, Baton Hilltop, and a wadi between Jibal al-Bottom and al-Chaitieh on April 23; the Mlita Hilltop, Mount al-Rafih, Ain Boudoir, Birket Jabbour Hilltops, northern Zibqeen, and a Wadi between al-Chaitieh and Jibal al-Bottom on April 27; and the Loweizeh Hilltops on April 28.

"The town of Habboush narrowly escaped a horrible massacre on April 4, when the "Israeli" enemy bombarded the town’s residential neighborhoods wounding eight civilians including six children.

"The victims were Fatima Ali Halwani, 2, Zeinab Khdeir, 8, Fatima Qassem Ayoub, 7, Alaa Qassem Ayoub, 6, Mostafa Habib Hjeij, 5, Khadija Ibrahim al-Homsi, 9, Latifa Shreif, 52, and Moussa Atriss, 27.

"The brutal aggression coincided with a similar bombardment that targeted the southern neighborhood in Arab Salim wounding eight-year-old Ali Khalil Hassan.

"On April 17, Abu Dhabi Television’s correspondent Qassim Dergham was hit in the back by an "Israeli" rubber bullet as reporters were taking pictures of occupied Arnoun residents.

"Furthermore, "Israeli" enemy on April 18 bombarded the southern neighborhood of Arab Salim wounding Jawad Mohammed Mosusa, 35.

"Three civilians were also wounded on April 23 when the occupation targeted the town of Kfar Tibet.

Regarding the "Israeli" suppressive practices inside the occupied zone, occupation forces on April 6 collectively expelled 18 people from the town of Shibaa, some of them are elderly, children, and women.

"The mass expulsion ignited an uprising in the occupied town led by its imam, Sheikh Ismail Dallah who called for “confronting this new campaign that aims at displacing the town’s residents”.

Dallah’s ensuing detention by the occupation’s proxy militia outraged the residents who gathered in front of the militia’s security HQ and tried to storm it.

The residents clashed with the militiamen manning the office using sticks and stones while collaborators used rifle butts. The residents were eventually able to free the Sheikh.

"The Shibaa deportees are: Khalil Ahmed Nabaa, 67, his wife Zobeida Mohsneh, 65, Hanaa Hareth Shehab, 35, her sons Maher Nabaa, 9, and Abdallah Nabaa, 6, and Ihsan Mohammed Nabaa, 16, Khalil Saab, 60, Radi Khalil Abu Hwaili, 67, his wife Fatima Abu Hwaili, 68, Waed Khalil Abu Hwaili, 7, Walaa Khalil Abu Hwaili, 35, Ayan Nasser, 25, Hourieh Hamdan, 50, Khadija Saeb, 55, Adib Rahal, 55, his wife Hanaa Abu Rizq, 45, and Bassam Rahal, 14.

"Moreover, occupation forces on April 12 expelled three children from Shibaa. They joined their parents who had been deported earlier.

""Israeli" occupation forces on April summoned for investigation three residents of Ibl al-Saqi which has been sealed off for six weeks.

"Three Lahd militia officers from al-Qlaia’ were also detained along with an "Israeli" officer following the theft of the Marjeyoun barracks office of former "Israeli" liaison unit commander Erez Gerstein who had been killed by the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen.

"On April 22, occupation detained Abdallah Mohammed Obeid Seif, 56, and his son Hassan Abdallah Seif, 25, following the occupation’s storming into the occupied town of Um al-Toot, a farm between the towns of Marwaheen and Yareen (western sector).

the Beaufort Castle outpost.

"Occupation created roads and broadened others in Marjeyoun, Bint Jbeil and Jezzine. "Israeli" forces cut trees for fear of bombs planted by the Islamic Resistance.
