Daily Report
 

 
May Friday 7-1999:

Introduction

The "Israeli" occupying troops stepped up their aggressions against the Lebanese liberated areas. In a clear violation of the internationally-monitored April 1996 Understanding, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into populated areas in South Lebanon.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) announced that it bombarded the border posts as a warning response to "Israel" not to launch further aggressions on Lebanon.

Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah met with Colonel Dr. Bashar Al-Assad in Damascus. Both sides hailed the large official and public embrace of IR's dignified role in confronting the "Israeli" occupation.

Meanwhile, President General Emile Lahoud reiterated that the Resistance is the only choice for liberating the land with the support of the Lebanese Army.

"ISRAEL" INTENSIFIES ATTACKS ON SOUTH LEBANON

""Israeli" warplanes raided hills and valleys in southern Lebanon for the third consecutive day. "Israeli" jets fired about eight rockets at Yater village in the western sector of southern Lebanon, north of the zone "Israel" occupies in the south. Shells also slammed into populated neighborhoods in the village of Arab Salim in the Iqleem al-Toufah mountain ridge, north of the occupation zone. One home, belonging to Hussein Saleh was damaged in the attack. The western Beqaa was not spared the "Israeli" shelling, especially the village of Mashghara. The Zahrani River banks also came under intense "Israeli" shelling. But no casualties were reported. "Israeli" warplanes flew at low altitude over the south and reached the capital".

""Israeli" navy gunboats, meanwhile, opened machinegun fire on Lebanese fishing boats which had sailed off the coasts of the southern city of Tyre. The gunboats have been spotted in different areas close to the coast in the south"(1)

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a communiqué in which it reported the following aggressions staged by "Israeli" occupation troops on the liberated areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa:

00:45, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to three 120mm shells and two 81mm rounds.
2:25, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the surroundings of Haddatha compound.
5:05, eight 155mm missiles were fired at the area between Shaqra and Majdal Selem towns.
5:55, Qaissieh Valley and the peripheries of Touleen were shelled by a number of 155mm missiles.
7:35, the "Israeli" bombers raided into the west of Yater village.
8:45, the perimeters of Majdal Selem subjected to four 155mm missiles.
11:35, ten 155mm missiles slammed into the areas of Zawarib and Marmala.
12:05, Markaba ravine was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
13:40, the outskirts of Habboush town came under several 155mm missiles.
13:45, four 120mm shells hit the perimeters of Arab Salim town.
15:45, the surroundings of Taibeh position subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
15:55, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost
18:05, five 155mm missiles struck north of Zibqeen village.
18:20, the road linking between the villages of Qlaileh and Zibqeen was bombarded by twelve 155mm missiles, while at the same time, west of Yater exposed three others. Meanwhile, three 120mm shells fell on Jib Soweid area between Majdal Zoun and Mansouri villages.
19:00, the "Israeli" jets raided into the outskirts of Qlaileh.
20:15, eight 81mm rounds landed in and around the area of Toumat position(2)

IR warns against further aggressions:

In a primary warning response to "Israel's" going too far in violating April 1996 Understanding and to air raids, the IR team of martyr Mohammad Ali Ghazi Al-Husseini in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded at 6:00, the border post of Abbad scoring direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Ibrahim Mohammad Faqih in the (ARU) targeted the border position of Ramia chalking up direct hits. Several blazes were seen erupting inside it.

Also, the IR mujahedeen spotted, at 11:15, enemy movements inside Kfar Houna position. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyr Mohammad Hammoud in the (ARU) bombarded the position. Direct and accurate hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported(3)

""Israel's" military radio claimed mortar bombs and rockets fired from southern Lebanon landed in "Israeli" territory early today. UN peacekeeping sources in occupied southern Lebanon rejected the "Israeli" claims. They stressed, all mortar bombs and Katyusha rockets fired by the resistance landed in Lebanese territory"(4)

"ISRAELI" ALLIED MILITIAMEN STORM VILLAGE AND STOP COMMUTERS OUTSIDE OCCUPIED VILLAGE(5)

"Israeli" allied militiamen today (yesterday) installed a checkpoint outside the newly occupied village of Arnoun. They stopped cars and pedestrians travelling to neighboring Yohmor, which is outside the zone "Israel" occupies in the south. "Israeli" allied militiamen also stormed homes in Arnoun. They questioned residents and conducted searches. Most villagers fled Arnoun when occupation troops annexed the village last month to the zone "Israel" occupies in the south. Meanwhile, allied militiamen set a store on fire in occupied Jezzine. The store's owner Maroun Harfoush reportedly refused to abide by a strike ordered by militiamen following the recent killing of their commander in occupied Jezzine, Monah Toumah, at the hands of the resistance".

Sayyed Nasrallah met Col. Bashar al-Assad(6)

"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah this morning met with Colonel Dr. Bashar al-Assad in the Syrian capital, Damascus, in the presence of Sayyed Nasrallah's political Advisor Hajj Hussein al-Khalil."

"Discussions focused on the latest political developments in Lebanon and the region, in addition to commending the Islamic Resistance's great sacrifices in south Lebanon and western Beqaa.

Both sides, hailed the large official and public embrace of IR's dignified role in confronting the "Israeli" occupation to complete the liberation of the sacred land"

"Talks also pointed out to the importance of staying alert to all the suspicious "Israeli" traps and suggestions which the enemy is trying to sell from time to time".

President stresses Resistance option(7)

"President General Emile Lahoud today (yesterday) reiterated that the Resistance is the only choice for liberating the land with the support of the Lebanese army".

"Representing the president at a celebration at the Martyrs' square to mark Martyrs' Day, Interior Minister Michel al-Murr said the Resistance that had acquired the world's admiration and a national consensus is also offering martyrs to achieve liberation.

"We will not give up our right in any inch of our land, or our legitimate right to resist the "Israeli" occupation, Mr. al-Murr stated.

"He added, "We will never be guards for the occupation which has only one choice: to withdraw."

Moreover, al-Murr noted that Lebanon will never be secluded, stressing on the unity of the Lebanese Syrian track and fate".

Premier stressed on steadfastness in the face "Israeli" aggressions(8)

"Press and Editors' Syndicates held a reception at the Press Syndicate Headquarters to mark the Press Martyrs' Day".

"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss represented President Gen. Emile Lahoud at the reception.

The premier said the Lebanese people and government will always be steadfast in the face of "Israeli" aggressions".

"We will do everything we can and we will make all necessary contacts to prevent the enemy from proceeding with its aggressions, and, if necessary, we will go to the April Understanding Monitoring Group". Mr. al-Hoss added.
