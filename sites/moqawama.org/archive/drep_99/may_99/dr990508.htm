Daily Report
 

 
May Saturday 8-1999:

Introduction
 The Islamic Resistance (IR) leveled another painful blow against the Israeli allied militia when four militants set up a roadblock outside the village of Mashmoushi and captured the Israeli collaborator Sergeant Staff Botros Wehbe and brought him to the liberated areas.

Meanwhile, the five-nation Monitoring Group denounced the Israeli occupation forces for violating the April 1996 Understanding when Israels warplanes and artillery staged aggressions on the Lebanese civilian areas.

However, UNIFIL spokesman Timor Goksel narrowly escaped death as an Israeli mortar round landed meters away from his car near Bayada crossing point.

RESISTANCE FIGHTERS CAPTURE TOP ISRAELI ALLIED MILITIA OFFICER FROM OCCUPATION ZONE
Following an accurate survey of the area and obtaining the necessary information about the Israeli agent Botros Jirjis Wehbe, the IR team of Al-Khiam Detainees in the Special Forces trapped, at 7:45, on the road of Deir Mashmoushi, Al-Sabah in Jezzine area to the collaborator Wehbe and took him to the liberated areas (1).

For its part, Future TV reported that the " Lebanese resistance fighters have dealt Israeli occupation troops and their allied militia in southern Lebanon yet another painful blow. Ten days after capturing an Israeli allied militiaman from occupied Jezzine and bringing him to liberated territories, four fighters from Hizbullahs special units seized an officer in the Israeli allied militia. Resistance guerrillas infiltrated the zone Israel occupies and captured Botros Jirjis Wehbi, a fifty-year old lieutenant, as he was driving towards occupied Jezzine. They brought him to areas outside the zone. Morale in the Israeli-financed and maintained militia has been running low, following severe losses at the hands of the resistance"(2

Also, the IR team of martyr Ibrahim Mohammad Faqih attacked, at 00:30, enemy movements inside headquarters of the 80th Brigade Command at Jamoussa with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain goals.

Half an hour later, the IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Abdallah Qaranbash and Nouh Abdel-Razek Ayash attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goals were chalked up.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Said Youssef Ayoub and Kamel Hassan Badreddine targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and certain hits.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyr Mohammad Abdel-Hussein Moselmani attacked, at 15:00, enemy movements inside Blat post. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to chalk up direct and definite hits.
For its part, Amal Movement fired several 120mm shells at Salaah position(3)

INTERNATIONAL MONITORS CONDEMN ISRAELI ATTACKS ON CIVILIANS(4)
 The five-nation group monitoring a 1996 truce understanding in south Lebanon blamed Israel for breaching the truce deal by launching air and artillery attacks on civilian areas. Delegates called on Israel to abide by the cease-fire and to avoid a repeat performance of its attacks on civilians.

In a statement issued late last night, the international group monitoring the April cease-fire understanding, condemned Israels air raids on the southern village of Zibqeen and the northern town of Baalbak this week, which injured at least two Lebanese civilians and damaged several homes. The raids also led to the destruction of a cultural center in Baalbak. The monitors also denounced Israeli artillery attacks which damaged homes in villages near zone Israel occupies in southern Lebanon. But the representatives disagreed on charging Lebanese resistance fighters with violating the accord for allegedly firing at Israeli occupation soldiers from populated villages, as Israel claimed. The April Understanding cease-fire agreement bans attacks on or from civilian areas but does not condemn resistance operations against Israeli occupation troops. The group, which is made up of delegates from the US, France, Israel, Syria, and Lebanon, said the Israeli attacks were a breach of the April Understanding, and put civilians at risk. Delegates also urged Israel to avoid a repeat performance and to implement the deal carefully. The monitoring group was set up to oversee the April Understanding accord reached between Israel and Lebanon after Israels April 1996 Aggression Grapes of Wrath Operation three years ago.

 Israeli Aggressions(5)
The IR released a communiqué in which it mentioned the following aggressions staged by the Israeli occupation troops:

00:35, Mount Al-Rafih was targeted by four 120mm shells and two 155mm missiles.
Between 00:45 and 15:00, Mount Sojod was bombarded by more than sixteen 120mm shells.
00:55, Haris ravine was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
11:00, thirteen 120mm shells slammed into Tasseh Well.
12:40, Jib Soweid area between the villages of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri came under ten 155mm missiles.
13:05, eight 155mm missiles were fired at the outskirts of Majdal Zoun.
15:10, the west of Yater village was bombarded by twenty seven 155mm missiles.
Between 15:42 and 16:10, the Israeli jets dropped twenty one bombs on west of Yater.
