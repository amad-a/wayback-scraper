Daily Report
 

 
May Sunday 9-1999:

Hoss denies receiving threat message(1)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss denied that Lebanon had received an "Israeli" message in which the enemy threatened to escalate the situation in south Lebanon".

"PM al-Hoss reiterated that the solution is realized when the "Israeli" occupation forces withdraw, stressing that the Resistance is the natural outcome of "Israeli" aggressions not vice versa".

"These aggressions are related to the upcoming "Israeli" elections," Mr. al-Hoss added.

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)
The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions:

00:35, one 120mm shell struck the surroundings of Taibeh position.
7:10, Mount Sojod was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
Between 14:25 and 14:50, west of Yater village exposed to more than six 120mm shells.
17:45, the area between Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen villages was bombarded by eight 155mm missiles.
23:05, four 81mm rounds fell on Bayada area and three others on Berkat Jabbour.

Footnotes:
1- Al-Manar TV, 8-5-1999.
2- IR statement, 8-5-1999.
