Daily Report
 
May Sunday 30-1999:

Introduction
"Israeli" occupation's militia is proceeding with dismantling its outposts and facilities in Jezzine area as a prelude for the withdrawal due to the state of collapse it has reached as a result of the Islamic Resistance (IR) successive operations against the collaborators.

Head of Hizbullah's Politburo MP Mohammad Raad stressed that the "areas that will be liberated will be under the control of the Lebanese government.

Meanwhile, MP Pierre Dakkash said withdrawal from Jezzine is the outcome of the resistance blows and the steadfastness of the enclave residents.

Elsewhere, the "Israeli" occupation forces, for the second day in a row, continued their brutal aggressions against residential neighborhoods in the town of Jibaa, in violation of the April Understanding.

Militia dismantles another outpost in Jezzine(1)
""Israeli" occupation's militia is proceeding with dismantling its outposts and facilities in the Jezzine enclave as a prelude for this militia's retreat due to the state of collapse it has reached as a result of the Islamic Resistance's successive operations against the collaborators.

"High-ranking sources expected the withdrawal of the enemy and its collaborators from Jezzine to take place within two or three days.

"The proxy militia this morning evacuated an advanced position in Kroum al-Arz that overlooks the Bater-Shouf crossing, security reports said.

"Large military trucks were seen moving equipment from the outpost to the Kfarhouna crossing; most of the outpost's ramparts and fortifications have been dynamited, the reports added.

The militia yesterday dismantled the communications and surveillance devices at the Daher al-Ramla outpost; these were transferred to a hilltop overlooking the Kfarhouna crossing.

"On the other hand, reports said a dispute has erupted between the militia chief agent Antoine Lahd and the "Israeli" military northern area command over the positions that the militia is to evacuate.

Reports said the occupation army prefers a partial withdrawal from Jezzine to guarantee that "Israeli" positions remain in back lines, away from the resistance's attacks.

"Elsewhere, a meeting in Marjayoun that was called upon by agent Lahd was aborted, as the Jezzine notables who had been summoned by the collaborators' chief refused to attend the meeting.

Some of the summoned left Jezzine to Beirut, including the Roum Mayor Jirji Haddad.

"It was reported that Lahd will hold a press conference in Marjayoun on Monday to explain the decision of withdrawal from Jezzine"

"On the other hand, collaborating militiamen last night arrested a Jezzine teacher.

Nabil Arheel was on is way back home when the militiamen abducted him because he called up Al-Manar Television's "Hadith al-Sa'aa" talk show.

"During the talk show which hosted Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General and former Jezzine MP Edmund Rizq, Arheel praised the Islamic Resistance "whose operations have liberated the enclave."

The militia questioned Arheel and kept him in custody until this morning.

MP Raad: newly liberated areas will be under state control(2)
"Head of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Hajj Mohammed Raad stressed that the "areas that will be liberated as a result of the Islamic Resistance's (IR) operations will be under the control of the Lebanese government, which has the authority to take the measures and steps it deems appropriate."

"MP Raad expressed his pride in this big victory achieved by the Mujahedeen, calling on collaborators in the occupied zone to learn the Jezzine lesson and to give themselves up quickly before it is too late.

IR will continue the struggle to expel the "Israeli" occupation from our precious occupied land", MP Raad added.

Resistance blows, residents' steadfastness brought about Jezzine withdrawal, says MP Dakkash(3)
"MP Pierre Dakkash Saturday said the withdrawal from Jezzine is the outcome of the Resistance blows and the steadfastness of the enclave residents".

"Precious Jezzine will soon return to the nation, after all the suffering that cost the residents a lot, MP Dakkash stated.

"The pullout would have never happened if we had not had two essential factors: the steadfastness of the Jezzine residents and the Resistance, which has been accomplishing one victory after another, confusing the enemy and astonishing the world, he added.

Mr. Dakkash called on the "government to make up its mind and take the initiative, undertake all its responsibilities, and reassure the people of Jezzine about their safety and security, in addition to embracing them."

"Moreover, he called on everyone to be cautious, not to slide into any mazes designed by "Israel" and to avoid all the traps that might be set up by the "Israeli" enemy".

For the second day in a row, Jbaa neighborhoods bombarded by "Israeli" occupation
""Israeli" occupation forces, for the second day in a row, continued their brutal aggressions against residential neighborhoods in the town of Jbaa, in violation of the April understanding."

"The "Israeli" artillery emplacements in al-Zaffateh Saturday bombarded the northern outskirts of Jbaa for more than two hours, targeting residential neighborhoods with tens of 155mm rounds that damaged the houses of Ahmed Mohammed Youssef Karaki and Abbas Hourshi.

"The area between Upper Houmine and Habboush in addition to Mazraat Oqmata, Mount Sojod, the Zahrani River gorge and Wadi al-Qaissieh were also bombarded by a number of 155 mm rounds.

"Moreover, the southern neighborhood of the town of Arab Salim was struck by "Israeli" artillery bombardment that damaged the houses of Hussein Saleh and Mohammed Salim Abboud, in addition to the town's public school which was hit by five shells as children were leaving classes. No casualties were reported

"Furthermore, "Israeli" warplanes staged two raids on Mount al-Akhdar on the northern side of Beslaya and the vicinity of the Toumat Neha outpost, which had been evacuated by the collaborating militia.

"Earlier, the warplanes intensively hovered over southern areas, staging several mock raids.

"The "Israeli" bombardment, Saturday afternoon, targeted the Hilltops of Sojod and al-Rihan, Jabal al-Rafieh and the main roads of Arab Salim, Habboush, and Houmeen-Habboush".

"The bombardment ignited a huge fire in the Wadis of the Zahrani River gorge. Firefighters in Nabatieh managed to put out the fire"(4)

Military Operations(6)
The IR team of martyrs Hussein Hassan Hayek and Ahmad Ali Rida attacked, at 6:45, Soweida outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

Two hours later, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Rida Yassine and Ali Hassan Daoun detonated a powerful explosive device as enemy members were at the ramparts of Soweida outpost. Several casualties were inflicted, the mujahedeen reported.

Also, the IR gunners bombarded, in a response to "Israeli" shelling of Jibaa town, the emplacement of Toghra destroying one 122mm artillery cannon.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Bahij Nasser and Kamel Hassan Badreddine targeted, at 13:45, the enemy emplacement of Zaffateh with the appropriate arms. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Moreover, the IR team of martyr Rida Al-Shaher targeted, at 20:15, "Israeli" squads at Kfar Houna position scoring direct and definite hits.

Twenty minutes later, the IR mujahedeen observed enemy military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Fouad Khalifeh and Hussein Hijazi targeted them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals and inflicting a number of casualties.
