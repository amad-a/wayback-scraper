Daily Report
 
 
May Sunday 2-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) issued a statement in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions on Lebanon's liberated areas:

00:45, the surroundings of Mashaaroun position subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
3:50, three 155mm missiles hit the peripheries of Zibqeen village.
Between 11:55 and 12:15, Mount Sojod was bombarded by thirteen 120mm shells.
12:10, four 155mm missiles struck Mlita area.
12:15, Mount Sojod subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
13:10, twenty five 155mm missiles slammed into the bridge of Berkat Jabbour.
21:15, Hassan ravine between Tair Harfa and Majdal Zoun villages came under eleven 120mm shells.
21:40, a flare shell hit the house of Hussein Saleh Salman at Majdal Zoun burning the kitchen completely.
23:00, Jib Soweid area between Mansouri and Majdal Zoun subjected to six 155mm missiles.
23:05, the surroundings of Radar post exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
23:15, the surroundings of Hardoun outpost came under four 60mm shells and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations(2)
The IR group of martyr Ali Najib Choaib targeted, at 00:30, Kfar Houna position with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits.

Furthermore, the IR teams of martyrs Ahmad Ali Ramadan and Abbas Kazem Hoteit attacked, at 11:45, a Lahdi force as it was beefing up Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Tanana and Kassem Jaber attacked, at 22:50, enemy movements near Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits.

At the same time, the IR group of martyr Assaad Shabshoul targeted Hardoun outpost with the proper weapons destroying some of its installations.

Meanwhile, the IR team of martyr Mohammad El-Horr attacked Tair Harfa position with the appropriate arms. Direct and definite goals were scored, the militants reported.
