Daily Report

 
May Sunday 23-1999:

"Israel" able to withdraw from Lebanon in a year, say Barak intimates(1)
"An "Israeli" pullout from southern Lebanon within a year is quite "feasible", two "Israeli" retired generals close to enemy prime minister-elect Ehud Barak said Saturday".

"The withdrawal of our troops from Lebanon within the next year is a perfectly feasible goal," Yossi Peled told "Israeli" army radio.

"Peled, as a former commander of "Israel's" northern region, was at one time in charge of the occupation forces' military activities in Lebanon.

"Dany Yatom, a former head of the "Israeli" Mossad secret service, told "Israels" public radio he hoped an agreement with Lebanon and Syria would make the withdrawal possible, and lead to further "peace" moves with the two countries".

"Peled and Yatom are members of a circle of former officers who advised Barak during his election campaign".

"A pullout from Lebanon was one of Barak's key election promises. He did not provide specifics other than to say it would involve renewing settlement negotiations with Syria".

France cautious of Barak's plan for withdrawal from Lebanon(2)
"The French government yesterday expressed extreme caution of "Israeli" enemy prime minister-elect Ehud Barak's plan for withdrawal from Lebanon within a year".

"I think we need to be very cautious. These are press reports that we had no word about from other sources, French Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Anne Gazeau-Secret said.

"France's stance is still represented in the "necessity of implementing (UN Security Council's) Resolution 425, which calls for "Israeli" unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon," she added, stressing that opening negotiation tracks with the concerned parties, especially Syria, is very important, and "if we can facilitate things, we will."

"Gazeau-Secret regretted the "Israeli" delegate's walkout of the April Understanding Monitoring Group.

We regret this decision, but the seriousness of the delay should not be exaggerated, she said.

We believe the group will be able to meet quickly, probably next week. It is more important than ever to honor the 1996 April arrangement", she added.

Lahd militia disintegrating(3)
"British Foreign Report , which is specialized in security affairs, reported that collaborator Antoine Lahd's militia is disintegrating".

"This militia is unable to recruit members to man its outposts, while some of the militia's officers and members are "suspected" of cooperating with Hizbullah, the report said.

"It stated that "Israel" had failed to find a replacement for the militia's chief agent Antoine Lahd and pointed out that the commander of the militia's training camp, in addition to hundreds of militiamen, immigrated to Sweden."

"A number of militiamen in the militia's security service benefited from the amnesty offer and deserted the militia along with the names of their collaborators to Hizbullah, the report added.

"It concluded that although the "Israeli" occupation is still paying the collaborators' wages in addition to feeding, training and arming these militiamen, many of those have shifted their loyalty to Hizbullah".

Military Operations(5)
The IR group of martyr Al-Horr Kamel El-Beiruti attacked, at 7:00 an "Israeli" foot patrol near Aramta village with the machine guns and grenades inflicting several members among its ranks.

At the same time, the IR team of martyr Hassan Jawad Nassrallah targeted the same force with mortars and rocket-propelled grenades.

Five minutes later, the IR team of martyr Ghassan Ghanem targeted an "Israeli" squad as it was trying to back up the attacked force with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from achieving its goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Ghassan Zaiter and Hassan Malek Harb attacked, at 12:5, a collaborator motorized force as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR team of martyr Mohammad Ali Ghazi and Ibrahim Mohammad Faqih targeted enemy movements near Blat post with the proper weapons. The militants managed to score direct and accurate hits.

Moreover, the IR men spotted, at 22:20, enemy movements inside Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyr Abbas Kazem Hoteit attacked them with the adequate arms. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and certain goals.

For its part, two squads of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 23:40, violently Qantara compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The fighters achieved their goals.
