Daily Report
 

 
May Tuesday 11-1999:

"Israeli" WARPLANES RAID THE OUTSKIRTS OF ARAB SALEEM AND Habboush(1)
""Israeli" warplanes today (yesterday) shelled the outskirts of the southern villages of Arab Salim and Habboush. They are located on the edge of the zone Israel occupies in southern Lebanon. Two shells landed in residential neighborhoods, but there were no reports of casualties.

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)
For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:

00:20, six 81mm rounds hit Sakhrieh Hill.
2:45, Haitoura and Zhalta hilltops subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations(3)
The IR team of martyrs Othaina Kansou and Mustafa Nazar attacked, at 6:00, Soweida outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades striking its western fortification. The smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.
