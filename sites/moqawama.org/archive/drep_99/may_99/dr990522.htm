Daily Report

 
May Saturday 22-1999:

Introduction
Six days following the outpost was over-run by the Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen and in spite of the exceptional security precautions and military plans supervised by high-ranking "Israeli" officers, the IR militants managed, once again, managed to storm into Beit Yahoun outpost, plant a series of powerful explosives and, then, dynamite it. The security sources said that considerable damage was done, especially, to the northern fortifications.

However, Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah received, in the presence of the heroes of Beit Yahoun operation, the Presidential Guards' Brigade Commander Col. Mustafa Hamdan, representing President Gen. Emile Lahoud. The meeting was devoted to honor the squad that carried out the operation.

IR tears down the Beit Yahoun outpost over enemy troops' heads, attacks several other outposts
"In a great security achievement and infiltration, an Islamic Resistance's (IR) engineering squad at 10:30 a.m. Friday detonated several powerful bombs which were planted inside and around the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Beit Yahoun, destroying the outpost over the heads of the manning troops who were either killed or injured".

"Residents in liberated frontline villages said they heard an extremely powerful explosion and saw condensed clouds of smoke covering the outpost's area.

"When the smoke cleared out, most of the outpost's ramparts and reinforcements were completely destroyed by the blast, residents added.

"IR issued the following statement:

"In a new qualitative achievement, and in spite of the "Israeli" enemy's exceptional precautions in the outpost of Beit Yahoun following last week's storming of the outpost and the crossing of Beit Yahoun by IR Mujahedeen, in spite of the military plans supervised by high-ranking "Israeli" officers, including the commander of the western brigade, in addition to the increase in the number of troops and Merkava tanks' deployment in the surrounding hills, in spite of the alert and readiness inside the neighboring outposts of Haddatha and Baraasheit, in spite of the state of caution and maximum alert inside the outpost itself, and in a measure aimed at defying the enemy, IR's Mujahedeen attacked the occupation's outpost in Beit Yahoun for the second time in a week, placing the "Israeli" enemy's arrogance under their feet, as IR's squad of Martyr Zahi Haidar Ahmed from the Brigade of Martyr Hadi Nasrallah in the Special Forces infiltrated through all these measures and planted several powerful bombs inside the Beit Yahoun outpost.

"At 10:30 Friday, the squad detonated the bombs, destroying the outpost, killing and injuring the manning troops.

"IR distributed a videotape of the qualitative operation, which was shot by an IR cameraman, to local television stations.

"On the other hand, "Israeli" radio Friday admitted the injury of one "Israeli" soldier, saying he was hit when an "Israeli" occupation force found itself trapped in an IR ambush in the Eastern sector.

"The clash resulted in the injury of the occupation soldier who was hit by a hand grenade shrapnel.

"The "Israeli" occupation is now investigating the incident, the Radio added.

"Moreover, IR's squad of Martyr Abdel-Amir Fadel at dawn Friday attacked an "Israeli" motorized force that was attempting to take positions on the al-Rihan Hilltops with the appropriate weapons.

"The squad of Martyr Ali Oleiq, meanwhile, staged a similar attack against an enemy force in Kassaret al-Oroush.

"The Mujahedeen scored definite hits in both attacks.

"In addition, squad of Martyrs Mustafa Nathar and Ahmed Yassine targeted enemy movements inside the occupation's outpost in Sojod with the adequate arms, scoring definite hits"…(1)

For its part, The Daily Star reported " Hizbullah guerrillas blew up the “old” Beit Yahoun outpost in the western sector, six days after the position was over-run by the resistance.

"Security sources said that considerable damage was done to the northern fortifications.

"Two militiamen standing near the outpost at the time of the blast were seen running for cover. A short while later a car drove out of the position. No casualties were reported.

"The South Lebanon Army*-run Voice of the South radio claimed that the post had been empty for two years, apparently forgetting that one of their militiamen was killed, another captured and seven wounded when the post was stormed last Saturday.

"Security sources said that three 106mm recoilless rifle rounds were fired at both the “old” and “new” Beit Yahoun outposts Thursday night, providing cover for fighters to penetrate the perimeter and plant the charges. Al-Manar broadcast video footage Friday night of the fighters approaching the outpost and planting the explosives...(2)

President Lahoud dispatches Col Hamdan to meet Sayyed Nasrallah and hail Bait Yahoun operation heroes…(3)
"Representing President Gen Emile Lahoud, Presidential Guards' Brigade (PGB) Commander Col Mustafa Hamdan visited Hizbullah Secretariat HQ in Haret Hreik where he met Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah and the heroes who staged the qualitative operation against "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Bait Yahoun and seized an "Israeli" M-113 Armored Personnel Carrier.

"In appreciation and respect of the new victory over the "Israeli" occupation, Col Hamdan presented the commander of the squad that carried out the operation with a precious pistol from the PGB museum. "The other Mujahedeen were granted the PGB Honor Shield.

"Sayyed Nasrallah held a meeting with Col Hamdan, PGB officers and the Mujahedeen who staged the glorious operation. Col Hamdan conveyed to the Mujahedeen the President's greetings, appreciation of their heroism and their sacrifices, "for by capturing the "Israeli" APC, they have shown what the "Israeli" occupation is doomed to face in Lebanon".

"This heroic operation has had a great morale boost and incentives to achieve victory among all the Lebanese, Col Hamdan said, stressing the heroic role played by the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen in deterring the "Israeli" aggression and liberating the occupied land."

"He underlined the Lebanese official stand stressing "the unity of the army, the Resistance and the Lebanese people in the face of the occupation."

"For his part, Sayyed Nasrallah thanked the president for 'the kind gesture" and commended his firm national stances in confronting the occupation's plots and projects, besides his backing to the Resistance and interest in the martyrs' families.

"Lebanon, its Resistance, army and people have never been stronger, firmer, or more steadfast in the face of the "Israeli" occupation since 1982, Sayyed Nasrallah stated, pointing out that the president's generous move will fuel the Mujahedeen with more resolve and drive to confront the occupation.

"The move also shows that the Lebanese loyal people appreciates the Martyrs' blood and the Mujahedeen's sacrifices, with which victory and freedom will inevitably come inshallah," Sayyed Nasrallah added.

"Col Hamdan presented Hizbullah Secretary General with a PGB honorary shield as a sign of loyalty and appreciation.

"On the other hand, Sayyed Nasrallah hosted a delegation from the al-Tawheed Islamic Movement headed by the movement's Secretary General Sheikh Bilal Saed Sha'aban.

"The TIM delegation congratulated his Eminence on IR's latest victorious operations against the "Israeli" occupation in the South, especially the Bait Yahoun operation".

"Discussions focused on the importance of backing the Resistance in order to complement the liberation process and deter the occupation's aggressions.

 FRANCE WANTS "ISRAEL" TO WITHDRAW UNCONDITIONALLY…(4)
"France has reiterated its support for Lebanese demands for an unconditional "Israeli" pullout from south Lebanon. Paris has also said it is prepared to lend its diplomatic support to break the deadlock in peace negotiations".

" According to press reports, Prime Minister Lionel Jospin restated the French position in a letter delivered to his Lebanese counterpart Salim Hoss by the junior minister for foreign trade, Jacques Dondoux, on Wednesday. The reports said France believes a just and comprehensive peace can only be achieved when "Israel" leaves south Lebanon unconditionally in line with UN resolution 425. "Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak has vowed to end the occupation within a year but has so far given scant details of his plans. Defeated right-wing incumbent Benjamin Netanyahu had always demanded prior guarantees for the safety of "Israel's" northern border and its militia ally which Lebanon rejected as conditions not called for by Resolution 425. Jospin hoped that following the "Israeli" elections, it will be possible to rapidly relaunch negotiations on the Lebanese and Syrian tracks of the Mideast peace process. Peace talks between Lebanon and "Israel" have been frozen since February 1994 -- those between "Israel" and Syria have been frozen since February 1996. Syria insists it will only resume peace talks where they left off -- with an "Israeli" agreement in principle to withdraw from the Golan Heights which it occupied in 1967".
