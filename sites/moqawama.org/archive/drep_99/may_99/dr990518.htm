Daily Report

 
May Tuesday 18-1999:

Introduction
Dozens of thousands of people moved out of their homes for the reception of the armored personal carrier captured by the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants as a result of storming into the "Israeli" allied militiamen-manned outpost of Beit Yahoun.

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah told the crowds that: "whoever the winner is in the "Israeli" elections, they are all the same and their only difference is the way they lie. Their only choice, also, is an unconditional retreat from south Lebanon and Western Beqaa.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" jets staged a lot of sorties into the areas of Jibal El-Botom, Chaitieh, Zibqeen and Siddiqeen firing tens of air-to-ground rockets.

 Hizbullah SG: Mujahedeen trod on an "Israeli" outpost on Nakba day(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah hailed the Mujahedeen of the Islamic Resistance who have "trodden with their feet and shoes on an "Israeli" occupation outpost and on the head of the occupation in Bait Yahoun".

"Speaking before tens of thousands of people who gathered at the Hizbullah Shura Square to receive the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen who had seized an "Israeli" Armored Personnel Carrier, Sayyed Nasrallah said, "I thank and appreciate the Mujahedeen; we kiss the foreheads and hands of these heroes who have presented the Ummah with a great and terrific model."

"We have seen their movements on television, and so did the whole world. They were examples of the brave reassured Mujahedeen who do not fear death".

"The Secretary General also hailed the Mujahedeen who have taken part in the Islamic Resistance since it was launched in 1982. He greeted the martyrs' fathers, mothers, wives and children "with whose blessing the Resistance proceeds and defeat the Zionist enemy".

Moreover, his Eminence commended all those who took to the streets from Bait Yahoun, Tibneen and Sidon on the day the Bait Yahoun operation took place to welcome the Mujahedeen.

"He also thanked those who gathered today along the streets from Uzai to Haret Hreik to greet the Mujahedeen and stress the method of Resistance.

"On Nakba day (the 1948 Catastrophe - the day the "Israeli" entity was created) the Mujahedeen overran an "Israeli" occupation outpost, for our hearts are no longer haunted by fear nor are they living the spirit of the Nakba defeat," he said.

"On the contrary, we will be creating Nakba for the occupation and its collaborators, until all that was lost in 1949, 1967, and 1982 is recovered," he added.

"The Hizbullah Secretary General stressed that "no matter who wins today's "Israeli" elections, they are all the same, they only differ in slyness and maneuvering, no matter who wins, he has only one choice before the Islamic Resistance's sacrifices: to withdraw unconditionally".

His Eminence launched a call to exchange the Lahdi prisoners in Hizbullah's possession for prisoners in enemy prisons."

"I announce that the Resistance has four Lahdis who are offered for exchange for as many prisoner brethren as possible, he said.

"At the beginning of the honorary ceremony, one of the Islamic Resistance's Mujahedeen, the one who has driven the APC which was seized by the Resistance, saluted the Secretary General and presented him with a machine-gun".

"Sayyed Nasrallah saluted the crowds, raising the gun, before granting the Resistance man a medal and a sword with a koranic verse inscribed on it".

Captured APC, infiltrated through the "Israeli" air blockade and reached Beirut(2)
"Cooperating with the Lebanese army, Hizbullah succeeded in bringing to Beirut the collaborating militia's M113 armored personnel carrier (APC) captured by the Mujahedeen during the qualitative operation against the Beit Yahoun crossing and outpost last Saturday".

"Bringing the APC from the south to Beirut required a task of maximum security precautions and alert.

At 22:30 Saturday, the M113 arrived at the city of Sidon aboard a bulldozer carrier covered by pieces of nylon and fabric to make it look like blocks of imported marbles in order to hide it from the "Israeli" surveillance aircraft, especially MK drones."

"As soon as the APC's engine was started up in a coastal southern village, the southern Lebanese skies were full of "Israeli" MK drones and helicopters which intensively hovered over the coastal area from Tyre to Sidon, according to sources who followed up the process step by step"

"The bulldozer carrier departed with the M113 aboard, accompanied by seven cars full of Mujahedeen equipped with SAM 7 missiles and wireless communication devices to secure a safe passage for the carrier, the sources stated".

"Due to the importance of the APC, the carrier stopped in Sidon near former MP Nazih Bizri's residence and disappeared between buildings, while the Lebanese army air defense artillery conducted a special deployment along the Sidon coast and intensively fired to intercept the "Israeli" helicopters and gunboats," the sources added.

"At 2:00 a.m. Sunday, after the sky and the sea became clear, orders were given to the bulldozer carrier to proceed with its journey, crossing Maksar al-Abed in Sidon then al-Awali River, heading to Beirut with the escort of the Lebanese army until it reached the designated destination".

Southern residents indifferent to polls as air raids continue(3)
"The sixth "Israeli" general election to occur since south Lebanon was occupied was greeted Monday with complete indifference by the residents of front line villages."

"As the "Israeli" electorate voted to decide the bitterly contested election, Monday was just another day of shelling and air raids for the villagers living opposite the western sector.

One pig leaves and another comes in, said Fatmeh Mohanna, 30, in the village of Jibal El-Botom. Whos going to win? I dont know and I dont care.

Fatmeh was sitting in the shade with her niece, 1-year-old Rayan, and her mother, Zeina, watching an air raid on the edge of the village.

We thought it would be quiet with all of them voting in their election. We werent expecting raids today, Fatmeh said.

"One of two "Israeli" F-16 fighter-bombers dived toward the outskirts of the village, with anti-missile flares bobbing in its wake, before banking steeply to the right and climbing rapidly having released its deadly cargo".

"A moment later came a deep, resonant thump, that was felt rather than heard, as the bomb struck Wadi Awar, a few hundred meters to the north. A pall of white smoke rose gracefully into the blue sky before disintegrating in the breeze, as the warplanes circled around for another run.

"There was no resistance activity in the area prior to the raids but the warplanes returned six times to drop a total of eight bombs into the wadi.

Were all used to this, from babies to old men, Fatmeh said nonchalantly as she bounced Rayan on her knee.

"In March, Labor Party leader Ehud Barak ­ who was poised Monday to oust incumbent Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu ­ promised to withdraw troops from Lebanon by June 2000 if elected premier".

"But the pledge by "Israel's" former chief of staff meant little to the residents of Jbal Botom.

They are not honest about pulling out. If they were, they would have done it already, said a young man, who identified himself only as Mohammed.

"In Aita El-Jabal, 10 kilometers further east, the residents were clearing up debris after several buildings were struck by shrapnel during an "Israeli" bombardment of the area, hours earlier.

Two shells hit the center of the village, damaging the walls of houses, a mosque and a school.

"The shelling was an ill omen for Mustafa Zahar, 70, a shopkeeper. Every time one "Israeli" prime minister leaves, we get someone worse, he said.

"Zahar believed that the "Israelis" do not genuinely want to leave Lebanon but would have little choice in the face of mounting troop losses at the hands of the resistance".

"Aita El-Jabal lies in a gently sloping valley on the edge of the occupation zone between the South Lebanon Army* outposts at Haddatha and Beit Yahoun".

"Hussein Saad, 24, gained a grandstand view of Hizbullahs assault on the Beit Yahoun compound last Saturday, when the position was over-run and a militiaman and an armored personnel carrier were captured"

I was pleased and proud to see Hizbullah attack the post like that as it sends a message to the "Israelis" that we are winning, he said.
