Daily Report

 
May Tuesday 25--1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah asserted that Lebanon would offer no concessions for a troop pullout, adding that "we believe that the enemy will be forced to pullout unconditionally, while at the same time, our weapon is our national unity and solidarity with Syria".

In a clear indication that the ""Israeli"" proxy militia has reached its lowest ebb, however, the collaborators refused to reoccupy the old outpost of Beit Yahoun. This came after the Islamic Resistance (IR) men briefly occupied the position earlier this month and captured a militiaman and an armored personnel carrier.

Sayyed Nasrallah: we are close to victory(1)
"Hizbullah's Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said, "We are now close to victory, and the daily qualitative resistance operations are nothing but a prelude to drive out the "Israeli" enemy from the south".

"With the rifle and word, the Resistance succeeded in creating in the Zionist Society a pressurizing public opinion that is urging the "Israeli" government to withdraw from Lebanon unconditionally, Sayyed Nasrallah stated".

"His Eminence was speaking during a rally in Qsarnaba on Sunday, marking the fifth anniversary of abducting Hajj Abu Ali Mustafa al-Dirani from his house by an "Israeli" commando unit..

"We have recently heard about a renewed US initiative concerning the settlement process. I just want to remind everyone that if a pullout occurs, it won't be the result of a favor from anyone. The US has always provided the "Israeli" enemy with nothing but support and assistance, the Secretary General stressed.

"He added, "Barak's winning of the election in "Israel" will make us shoulder great responsibilities. We must warn the people against this stage's dangers and lies. The Labor party is the first one responsible for the largest crimes and massacres against the Arab and Islamic people."

"Until now, Barak has not made a statement concerning a withdrawal, and all that is being said is leaked by his circles. As to the US ambassador (David) Satterfield's words, they represent a maneuver."

"Syria and Lebanon will never accept any conditions for any "Israeli" pullout, because there is no (Yasser) Arafat in Lebanon as long as the latter is the den of (Syrian President) Hafez al-Assad", Sayyed Nasrallah concluded.

"Israeli" occupation permanently shuts down Beit Yahoun crossing(2)
""Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators Monday permanently shut down the Beit Yahoun crossing and replaced it with the al-Hamra crossing, following the refusal of the militiamen and their officers to return to the outpost, which was lately attacked and destroyed by large quantities of explosives planted by the Islamic Resistance (IR), reports from the occupied zone said.

"The reports stated that the "Israeli" occupation has adopted tight security measures around the al-Hamra crossing which the enemy is reinforcing with personnel and surveillance equipment to prevent further IR attacks similar to the one staged in Beit Yahoun".

"The crossing is one of six linking the liberated areas to the occupied zone. It is used by people crossing to and from the Bint Jbeil district and Marjeyoun area villages".

"Security sources in Jezzine said the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators have used a bulldozer to broaden the roadsides form Roum to Azour, in a desperate attempt to curb the number of "Israeli" and collaborators' casualties inflicted by IR bombs".

"These roadside bombs have recently killed and injured a large number of collaborating officers and militiamen".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupation troops launched the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas as reported in a communiqué released by the IR.
12:10, four 155mm missiles and four 120mm shells fell on Jib Soweid area and five 60mm rounds landed near Hardoun outpost, whereas the west of Majdal Zoun exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
12:30, the perimeters of Mansouri village subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations(4)
The IR militants observed, at 12:05, enemy movements inside Hardoun outpost. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Moussa Adib and Hussein Chalhoub attacked them violently with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals.

GUN-TOTING "BLOND" WHO IS "Israel's" NEW LEADER: A VICTIM(5)
" Munem Abul Muna nearly fell out of his chair. He had just found out that the blond woman who tried to kill him and his family 26 years ago was in fact a man and not just any man -- but the new prime minister of "Israel", Ehud Barak. "I am in a state of shock and still cannot believe it," said Abul Muna, 60. But whether he believes it or not it, the fact is that he was a collateral casualty; the chance victim of an "Israeli" commando raid in Beirut, on the night of April 9-10, 1973, and the raid was headed by the young Barak, disguised as a woman in a blond wig. "I can still feel the shrapnel from the bullets. But until now I did not know that it was Barak who tried to kill me and my family," said Abul Muna, proudly displaying the small black marks on his right shoulder. Abul Muna, of course, was not the "Israelis"' target. Barak and his men were after three top leaders of the Palestine Liberation Organization. It was just bad luck that Abul Muna happened to be sleeping in a small ground floor room with his wife and two small children in one of the buildings struck by the "Israelis". "I jumped up in terror and looked through the window, and what I thought was a blond woman opened up into the room with a machinegun," he said. "I dived under the bed with my son while my wife covered our daughter with her body on the other bed," he added. Umm Fadi, his wife, interrupted him to add that more than 40 bullets were later recovered in the room while the elevator on the other side of the wall was completely destroyed. The operation, dubbed the "hippy" raid by the western media because of the disguise of the commandos, was one of Barak's most sensational operations in a string of military feats. The glowing reputation he built up in the army has stood him in such good stead in his political life that it helped to propel him last Monday to the prime minister's seat. For the Palestinians, who had established their power base in Lebanon after being forced out of Jordan as a result of the bloody Black September incidents in 1970, Barak's operation was a serious blow. His squad burst into three apartments in the fashionable Verdun neighborhood, shooting dead three key Palestinian officials, Mohammad Youssef Najjar, the number two man in Yasser Arafat's Fatah movement, Kamal Adwan, the director of Fatah underground activities in "Israel" and the occupied territories, and chief PLO spokesman Kamal Nasser. The operation also seriously destabilized Lebanon, as then Prime Minister Saeb Salam resigned few days later in protest over the Lebanese army's failure to prevent the attack. It all began that night when a large "Israeli" commando landed stealthily at a sandy beach in Beirut, jumped into rented cars parked nearby by agents who had arrived in Lebanon a few days earlier and sped off into the city where they struck seven different targets, killing a total of 25 people. The other casualties in the Verdun operation included Najjar's wife who was mown down after trying to cover her husband with her body, two Lebanese policemen, a guard and a 70-year Italian woman. "It was a merciless killing operation, a professional James Bond-style operation with explosives, machine guns and all," Abul Muna recalled. It is true that the raid occurred 26 years ago and might have been overshadowed by other horrific scenes during the long years of the civil war in Lebanon that broke out two years later, but it is striking how vivid memories of the incident still remain in Verdun for those who were there. "It was very symbolic. It was a great defeat for the Palestinians, the Lebanese and the Arabs. No wonder Barak later became prime minister," said a barber across the street. But Abul Muna is not very impressed with Barak's achievements. "If one day Barak makes peace with us as he promised when he was elected and if he then comes to Beirut, I will surely take my revenge," he said, sliding his finger meaningfully across his throat".
