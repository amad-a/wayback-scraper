Daily Report
 
 
May Monday 3-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
In its communiqué, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following aggressions on the liberated areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa:

1:15, six 120mm shells were unleashed at the ravine between Shamaa and Majdal Zoun villages.
8:05, Jib Soweid area was targeted by three 155mm missiles.
8:10, four 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Majdal Zoun.
Between 9:40 and 17:20, Mount Sojod was bombarded by forty one 120mm shells.
9:50, the south of Sojod came under a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
14:05, the surroundings of Mashaaroun and the new Houla positions exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
16:35, four 155mm missiles struck Sraira hilltops.
17:30, the surroundings of Mashaaroun position subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations(2)
The IR team of martyrs Ahmad Yassine and Hassan Kheireddine Hassan in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted, at 8:00, enemy movements inside Hardoun outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Hassan and Ali Moussa Nassrallah attacked, at 9:15, a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to bar it from achieving its goals.


Footnotes:
