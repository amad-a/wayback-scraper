Daily Report

 
May Wednesday 19-1999:

Introduction
Confirming their total readiness for preventive retaliation at any time, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen retaliated, swiftly, on the "Israeli" savage aggression on the town of Zawtar Sharqieh in which two civilians were killed.

Dozens of Katyusha rockets were fired into "Israel's" northern settlements exactly three hours following the "Israeli" blatant violation of the April 1996 Understanding.

Meanwhile, Head of Hizbullah's Executive Council H.E. Sayyed Hashem Safieddine stressed that the "Israeli" community admittance of its army's defeat in the south will force Barak to find a way to get out of this quagmire.

IR avenges death of two civilians by shelling Katyusha rockets:
"Tension escalated in south Lebanon Monday night as the "Israeli" occupation forces bombarded the town of Eastern Zawtar killing two young men in a blatant violation of the April Understanding.

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) swiftly and fiercely retaliated by launching Katyusha rockets and mortars on the Galilee area north of occupied Palestine".

"In retaliation for the "Israeli" occupation forces' persistence in violating the April Understanding, the Islamic Resistance (IR) at 1:35 a.m. Tuesday launched Katyusha rockets against the "Israeli" settlements of Kiryat Shmona, Naharaya, Doreen and Shlomi, scoring accurate hits, an IR communiqué read".

"The retaliation came in reprisal to the "Israeli" occupation's air raids and bombardment against the city of Baalback, Zibqeen, Khorbet Selem and Eastern Zawtar where two civilians were martyred as the "Israeli" artillery shelled the town last night", the statement added.

"IR renewed its commitment and readiness to pay back and deter the enemy at any time.

We will not let our people and villages be targeted. We pledge God to defend them", the communiqué concluded.

"Hizbullah official in the south Sheikh Nabil Qawooq said the "Israeli" enemy will always be targeted as long as it is occupying our land, pointing out that the enemy's violations and aggressions will not benefit it, but will fire back and result in more losses in the enemy's ranks".

"The "Israeli" radio acknowledged that nine "Israeli" settlers and four soldiers were injured during the Katyusha attacks on "Israeli" settlements.

"Moreover, the radio admitted that four housing units were damaged while schools were shut down and settlers went into raid shelters where they hid overnight"(1)

Also, the IR group of martyr Kamel Hassan Badran attacked, at 17:00, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Baraasheit compound with the adequate arms. The attack came as several trucks, carrying construction materials, were entering the same post. The militants managed to score direct hits

Also, the IR group of martyr Hussein Zahreddine targeted, at 20:00, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported. At the same time, the IR team of martyr Mohammad Hussein Kassem attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain hits (2)

"Israeli" occupation massacred two young men at Zawtar Sharqieh:
"With 13 minutes left before the polling booths closed in "Israel" Monday, an "Israeli" F-16 fighter-bomber flew high over the occupation zones central sector". "In the village of Zawtar Sharqieh, 10 kilometers further north, Farid Harb, 20, and his cousin Saddam, 19, were chatting beside a tobacco field. They had less than a minute to live".

"Finnish Unifil soldiers near the village of Qsair, 2 kilometers south, glanced up as they heard the approaching jet. The F-16 dived toward the ground, passed over the Finnish position and launched a missile 2,200 meters from the Harbs.

"The missile took only 11 seconds to cross the Litani River gorge, skim the olive plantations on the edge of the village and home in for its final run. The two boys were standing beneath a streetlight, said Hassan Fawaz who saw the missiles approach. The missile flew in a straight line ­ I could see the tail flame in the darkness. Then it plunged to the ground and exploded.
"The missile struck a concrete wall beside the Harb cousins.

I thought there would be more shelling so I stayed inside for five minutes, said Dalal Rammal. Then I heard Farids mother screaming that her son was dead.

"Hizbullahs reply was swift. Exactly three hours later, a ferocious barrage was unleashed on northern "Israel", combining Katyusha rockets, artillery shells and mortar rounds. In all, some 140 rounds were fired, with 30 landing inside "Israel".

"Questions were raised Tuesday about why such an apparently motiveless attack had occurred. The Harb family is widely respected in the south. Sheikh Ragheb Harb was assassinated in 1984, a year after founding the resistance.

"A security source said the "Israelis" took a monumental risk by targeting the Harbs.

The "Israelis" must have known that by killing the two cousins at their homes, especially because they were Harbs, Katyushas were bound to hit "Israel" quicker than usual. But for some reason they clearly thought the risk was worthwhile, the source said.

"The missile fired bore a similarity to the long-range NT Dandy or Long Spike anti-tank weapon, developed by "Israel's" state-owned Rafael armaments firm and known to have been test-fired in the south for more than a year. The missile fragments scattered across the tobacco field in Zawtar Sharqieh consisted of the same lightweight metal alloys as had been found in Arab Salim and Majdal Selem following earlier Dandy attacks.

"The Dandy, however, is designed for use by helicopters rather than fixed-wing aircraft.

"According to a military source, the pinpoint accuracy of the missile suggests that the Harb cousins were painted by a laser designator, either by "Israeli" troops, possibly in the Salaah outpost opposite the village, or more probably a pilotless reconnaissance drone.

"The Finnish peacekeepers reported the presence of a drone above the central sector shortly after the attack".

"Meanwhile, two civilians were reportedly wounded when "Israeli" troops in Arnoun opened fire on them Tuesday night".

"Red Cross officials were negotiating with the "Israelis" for permission to enter the area and evacuate brothers Hassan and Hussein Mohammed Ollaik".

Sayyed Safieddine: "Israeli" society's admitted defeat in Lebanon will force Barak out(5)
"Head of Hizbullah's Executive Council his Eminence Sayyed Hashem Safieddine stressed that the "Resistance's activity is the result of the Mujahedeen's resolve and options according to field facts".

"The flagrant violations of the April Understanding by the "Israeli" enemy Monday and the earlier aggressions required a reprisal from the Islamic Resistance at the right time to make the enemy feel the importance of observing the Understanding", he said.

"Sayyed Safieddine stated during an interview with al-Manar TV that Hizbullah is committed to the Understanding, pointing out that the "Israeli" society unanimously supports a withdrawal from south Lebanon to escape the quagmire".

"The "Israeli" community admittance of its army's defeat in the south will force Barak, or anyone else in charge, to find a way to get out of this quagmire", his Eminence added.

Barak reiterates pledge to withdraw from Lebanon within a year(6)
""Israeli" enemy's elected prime minister Ehud Barak said he will "end the conflict in Lebanon" within a year".

"He did not provide specifics of his plan, other than to say it would involve renewing negotiations with Syria"
"Barak was speaking barely an hour after occupied northern Palestine was struck by dozens of rockets fired by the Islamic Resistance in retaliation for the killing of two Lebanese civilians by "Israeli" shelling on Monday".

"We are here in Rabin Square thinking of the thousands of citizens who are in shelters on the northern border, and I hereby commit myself to do everything to take the army out of Lebanon within a year, Barak said.

"For his part, the "Israeli" entity's president Ezer Weizman said he backs reaching a speedy settlement, adding that Barak has appropriate ideas in this regard".

""Israeli" enemy's radio said the "Israeli" army viewed the rocket attacks on Galilee "as very serious".
