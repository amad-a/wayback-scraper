Daily Report
 
 
May Saturday 1-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it mentioned the following aggressions:

00:10, the surroundings of Taibeh position exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
00:15, one 120mm shell struck the perimeters of Mlikh village.
12:10, four 155mm missiles fell on the outskirts of Jibal El-Botom village and one another on Sawan area.
12:20, the south of Zibqeen village subjected to three 155mm missiles.
12:30, one 155mm missiles hit west of Yater.
12:45, Hamra area was targeted by two 155mm missiles.
16:05, ten 160mm missiles slammed into Sojod village.
Between 19:40 and 23:10, Mount Sojod was bombarded by eleven 155mm missiles and seven 120mm shells.
20:10, one 81mm round landed Haddatha forest.
20:30, Arid El-Zennar area subjected to five 120mm shells.
22:10, four 155mm missiles were fired at north of Touleen village.
22:30, Qalawani village exposed to five 155mm missiles.
22:50, the surroundings of Houla position came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
23:15, four 155mm missiles slammed into the area between Zibqeen and Jibal El-Botom villages.
23:55, the surroundings of Qobaa post exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Military Operations(2)
The IR group of martyr Mohammad Hussein Kassem attacked, at 19:25, an Israeli force at Rihan compound scoring direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyr Hassan Hani Obeid targeted, at 22:00, enemy motorized movements at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct and definite goals. At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Salman and Mohammad Faqih attacked enemy movements inside Blat post. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.
