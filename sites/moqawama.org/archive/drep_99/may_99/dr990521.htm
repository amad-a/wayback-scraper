Daily Report

 
May Friday 21-1999:

Introduction
Following the statements, over a withdrawal from South Lebanon within a year which made by Israels Prime Minister-elect Ehud Barak, a state of embarrassment and fear prevailed the Israeli allied militiamen.

Meanwhile, the talks of the five-nation monitoring group had collapsed on the third day of meeting at Naqoura after the Israeli delegations storming out of the session.

However, the Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Al-Hoss said that there is no difference between Barak and his predecessor Benjamin Netenyahu and that the bet should be on our Resistance and unity.

Walkout mars Monitoring Group talks(1)
Speculation of progress on a withdrawal from the south contrasted Thursday with the first-ever walkout by the Israeli delegation to the five-nation Monitoring Group after talks collapsed on the third day of a meeting in Naqoura to discuss a record 26 complaints.

In a move that has reportedly angered the U.S. and French co-chairs of the group, the Israeli delegation stormed out of the meeting, claiming that Lebanon was refusing to accept responsibility for the deaths of Jezzine civilians from a roadside bomb attack.

A statement from the Foreign Ministry said that Israel had failed to provide evidence that Lebanon was responsible for the bomb blast.

The co-chairs released a statement, saying that the meeting was temporarily adjourned because of the need of the Israeli delegation to consult with its authorities.

Confusion in collaborators' ranks follow Barak's statements(2)
A state of confusion and concern has been overtaking the collaborating militiamen and Lebanese laborers who work inside occupied Palestine following the Israeli elections and the statements made by Israeli prime minister-elect Ehud Barak about a withdrawal from south Lebanon within a year, as-Safir daily reported today (yesterday).

The newspaper stated that tens of militiamen held meetings away from the Israeli occupation and its security services to discuss their future after Barak's statements which they think will be at their own expense, especially that the Lebanese authorities had already issued arrest warrants against many collaborators.

The militiamen discussed a number of options, including formation of groups that declare intention to join the Lebanese legitimacy besides organizing mass desertion operations to liberated areas in the south, preceded by pleas of mercy to the Lebanese officials in an attempt to ease potential punishment that they will face, the report added.

It was also reported that tens of militiamen took decisions to resign form the so-called "South Lebanon Army" militia as a first step to settle their legal status in the eyes of the Lebanese state.

Observers expect that the coming period will witness major "shakeups" inside the so-called "SLA" in Jezzine, Hasbaya, Bint Jbeil and Marjeyoun.

They also expected a rise in the number of deserting militiamen who will flee to liberated areas.

PM al-Hoss reiterates; no difference between Barak and Netanyahu(3)
Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc member MP Ammar al-Musawi quoted Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss as saying, "There is no difference between Israeli prime minister-elect Ehud Barak and his predecessor Benjamin Netanyahu.

PM al-Hoss does not expect a breakthrough in the settlement negotiations, especially as the period separating the formation of an Israeli cabinet and the start of preparations for the US presidential elections is very short, MP al-Musawi said.

The premier noted that Israeli Labor had lost the elections as a party, for its head is now the prime minister and has to make alliances with extremists which will be tying him up.

The Labor party is the one that staged most the wars and massacres against Arabs, PM al-Hoss pointed out, saying "the bet should be on our national Resistance and Unity, on our internal stability and continuous cooperation with Syria."

On the other hand, the cabinet yesterday passed a decree exempting brothers and sons of Resistance martyrs and those martyred in Israeli aggressions from military conscription.

Military Operations(5)
The IR squad of martyrs Assaad Mohammad Chabshoul and Ahmad Jadallah Qaranbash attacked, at 00:30, enemy movements near Soweida outpost with the appropriate arms. The militants managed to chalk up direct goals.

Ten minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Ali Chour and Mohammad Zeidan targeted Kfar Houna position and crossing with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Also, the IR team of martyr Nouh Abdel-Razzak attacked, at 18:40, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Beit Yahoun outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain hits. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyr Mohammad Ali Al-Husseini targeted Baraasheit compound as it was trying to reinforce the concerned compound barring it from achieving its goals.

Besides, the IR group of martyrs Bilal El-Akhras and Ossama Mohammad El-Hakim attacked, at 21:15, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Haidar El-Jawhari and Ibrahim Mohammad Faqih targeted Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score direct and certain goals.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Bahij Nasser and Kamel Hassan Badreddine attacked, at 22:00, enemy movements in and around Soweida outpost. The attack, in which the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades were used, resulted in scoring direct and certain hits.

Fifty minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Ghassani and Ghassan Ghanem targeted a collaborator motorized patrol as it was entering Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able chalk up direct and definite goals.
