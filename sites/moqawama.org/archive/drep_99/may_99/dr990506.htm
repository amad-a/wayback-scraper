Daily Report  


 
May Thursday 6-1999:

Introduction

The Lebanese Prime Minister had condemned "Israel's" aggression on the eastern city of Baalback. Four civilians were injured in the air raid. Lebanon reiterated its legitimate right to resist the "Israeli" occupation troops.

Lebanon lodged two more complaints against the "Israeli" enemy army to the Monitoring Group. Also, "Israel's" helicopter gunships fired at least eight missiles on valleys near the village of Zibqeen.

Hizbullah senior source announced that "Hizbullah is currently examining its options to determine how to deal with these aggressions in line with its Jihad and ethical responsibilities".

"ISRAEL" LAUNCHES DEEPEST ATTACK INSIDE LEBANON SINCE 1980S…(1)

"Tuesday night "Israel" launched its deepest attack since the 1980s on a populated area. "Israeli" warjets raided the center of Baalbeck in eastern Lebanon. Four people were injured and five homes were damaged."

" "Israeli" fighter jets attacked a building near the Imam Ali mosque in the center of the eastern city of Baalbeck last night. It was the first so far deep attack into a populated Lebanese city by "Israel" since the 1980s. Sources said "Israeli" warjets fired two rockets at a cultural center and destroyed it. "Israel" claimed the building was a Hizbullah headquarters. Senior Hizbullah officials categorically denied the "Israeli" allegations that the building belonged to the party and that those injured were Hizbullah members. They stressed, it was a cultural center belonging to an adjacent mosque. Civilians were rushed to the city hospital following the attack. Relatives packed the emergency room entrance. Fatmeh Amhaz was one of the four civilians injured in the raid, which "Israel" claimed targeted Hizbullah officials. Her injuries are moderate. But her son, Ali, is suffering from head wounds.

His situation is stable. Ali said he was having dinner with his mother when they heard loud explosions. They felt the injuries right away. Ali added the shells landed about 200 meters away from the family's dining room. Hussein Jammal was also injured in the "Israeli" raid. The young man said he was passing by when the shells landed not far from him. Hussein is also suffering from head injuries. Hizbullah's cultural officer in Baalbeck, Sheikh Hatem Abu Daya confirmed, the casualties were civilians. He also denounced the "Israeli" raids, saying they violate the 1996 truce understanding which bans attacks on civilian targets. He accused "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of ordering the attack on the heart of city to boost the morale of his troops and gain support from "Israelis". He warned that the resistance will strike where it is painful for "Israel" at the appropriate time and place. The raid on Baalbeck shattered glass in several buildings and cut electricity in the neighborhood. Five homes were damaged. The "Israeli" air force last bombed Baalbeck in February".

"Minutes after the raid on Baalbeck, "Israeli" helicopters fired at least eight missiles on valleys near the village of Zibqeen, east of the southern port city of Tyre. Seven homes were damaged and one civilian was injured. The area is outside the zone "Israel" occupies. Hizbullah said resistance fighters retaliated by attacking a number of occupation outposts in the area. "Israeli" occupation troops have been reeling from heavy military losses at the hands of the Lebanese resistance. The commander of "Israel's" allied militia in occupied Jezzine, Monah Touma was killed in a resistance roadside bombing yesterday. Three "Israeli" soldiers were also injured during an overnight clash with Hizbullah early yesterday."

PM Hoss condemns "Israel's" latest April Accord violations…(3)

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss Wednesday denounced an "Israeli" air raid on a civilian area of Baalback this week.

“This is yet another criminal act by "Israel" against Lebanon and its people,” he told reporters. “I believe that this escalation outside the occupation zone is related to the "Israeli" elections.” The "Israeli" polls are due May 17.

"Late Tuesday, "Israeli" warplanes struck deep into Lebanon, bombarding a building adjacent to a mosque in Baalback. Security sources said two civilians were wounded in the attack. "The government filed two complaints to the monitoring group in the south, one over the strike on Baalback and another on an "Israeli" attack that damaged houses and wounded a civilian in Zibqeen the same day.

"The committee was already in session to look into four Lebanese and three "Israeli" complaints about alleged violations of the April Understanding during the last 10 days. The meeting was still going on after midnight.

"Lebanon lodged a complaint regarding the wounding of a woman and her child in the "Israeli" shelling on Jarjoua April 27 and also protested the targeting of civilian houses in the shelling on the same day of Haddatha, Nabatieh and Jarjoua, which resulted in destruction of at least three houses.

"Israeli" complaints accused Hizbullah of launching attacks from civilian areas, like Haddatha and Majdal Selem.
"The "Israeli" delegation to the committee also accused Hizbullah of damaging a house in the district of Jezzine during one of its attacks last week".

Hizbullah source: We are examining our options of retaliation…(4)

"Hizbullah condemned "Israel's" attacks Tuesday and sent a veiled warning to "Israel". “We consider this attack and yesterday’s attack on the Imam Ali's library and a house in the village of Zibqeen a rampant violation,” a Hizbullah statement said.

“We’re studying our options and deciding the appropriate method to deal with this aggression,” it added.

Collaborators force store owners in Jezzine to shut down "in protest" for killing agent Touma…(5)

" Reports from the occupied zone said collaborating militiamen in Jezzine today (yesterday) forced store owners to shut down their shops to make it look as if they were protesting the killing of agent Mounah Touma yesterday in a roadside bomb detonated by the Islamic Resistance (IR)".

"Touma recently replaced agent Joseph Karam (Alloush) as commander of the 20th regiment. Karam had also been hit by an IR bomb last week.

"At gunpoint, the militiamen threatened to arrest owners if they does not close their stores and insulted some of them".

"In addition, the collaborators closed down the crossings of Kfar Falous and Bater which link the Jezzine enclave to the liberated areas".

"The occupation's militia has been living in a state of confusion and insecurity following the consecutive powerful IR blows".

Military Operations…(6)

The IR militants observed, at midnight exactly, an "Israeli" proxy force beefing up Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Abdel-Karim Yassine and Adnan Kheireddine targeted it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits and barring it from achieving its goals.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Mohammad Hussein Kassem attacked enemy movements in and around Baraasheit compound with the adequate arms. The mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyr Abbas Ali Hassan attacked, at 2:35, enemy movements in and around Haddatha compound with the suitable weapons. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported.

Five minutes later, the IR team of martyr Asaad Mohammad Shabshoul targeted enemy movements at the old Beit Yahoun position with the proper arms. The mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct goals.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 13:50, enemy movements inside Zimraya position. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyr Mahdi Shamas targeted them with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR mujahedeen observed, at 15:40, enemy movements near Zimraya position. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyr Ismail Younes in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit bombarded them chalking up direct goals.

FOUR LEBANESE FREED FROM KHIAM DETENTION CENTER…(7)

""Israeli" troops and their allied militiamen today (yesterday) released four Lebanese detainees from the Khiam detention center in occupied southern Lebanon. "Israel's" allied militia said Nabil Abdallah, Nadim Saab, Ayman Mais, and Samir Alaadine were released for "humanitarian reasons". Some 124 Lebanese remain in Khiam without charges filed against them or trials planned. Dozens of other Lebanese are also being held in "Israeli" jails.

Amnesty International and other human rights groups have reported cases of torture and maltreatment at "Israeli"-run detention centers".
