Daily Report
 
 
August Sunday 29-1999:

Following the towns of Aita Al-Shaab and Bint Jbiel the occupation forces put the towns of Mais Al-Jabal and Hula under siege  (1)
The occupation forces ten days ago put the occupied towns of Mais Al-Jabal and Hula under siege. It was noted that the traitor Zuhair Shkier led a group of collaborators and imposed a siege on the town of Mais Al-Jabal, Shkier also confiscated all travel permits belonging to residents of the town. The group stormed a number of houses in the town damaging personal belongings as they carried on their practice humiliation.

In the town of Hula the traitors Kamal Shriem, Hussien Musa Ibrahim and Hassan Abdullah Mahmoud and others imposed a siege on the town. The group also prohibited the inhabitants from leaving the town as well as storming and searching many houses.

PM Salim Al-Hus confirmed that the government spares no effort in presenting the situation of the Israeli occupation to the General Council of the UN or to the Arab League.

The PM also added that efforts to lift the siege are still under way until our goal is reached.

Israeli Aggression  (2)
The IR issued a statement in which it reported the following aggression which were staged by the Israeli occupation troops on Lebanons villages and towns:
Between 6:20 and 7:00 , Majdal Zoun was bombarded by twenty nine 81 mm rounds, several 105 mm tank shells and 12.7 mm heavy machine gun fire damaging the house of Hussein Ali Fayad.
6:50, six 155 mm missiles struck Jib Soweid area .
7:00, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by five 155 mm missiles.
7.05, one 155-mm missile hit Hamra area.
11:10,  Ilmam area was targeted by two 81 mm rounds and two smoke shells, whereas Litani River watercourse near Qsair crossroad was hit by one 120 mm shell.
11:35, seven 155 mm missiles slammed into the west of Yater village.
11:45, Hassan Valley near Majdal Zoun, came under six 81 mm rounds.

Military Operations  (3)
The IR team of martyrs Youssef Rihan and Hussein Hassan Hayek attacked, at 12:50, Beaufort Castle with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits.
