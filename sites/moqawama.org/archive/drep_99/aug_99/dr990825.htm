Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 25-1999:

ISRAEL CARRIES OUT RAIDS AGAINST SOUTHERN(1)
""Israel" carried out air raids over valleys just outside the occupied zone. Police did not mention any casualties in the raids. The planes fired three air-to-ground missiles at 9:00 p.m. (1800 GMT) at an area between Zibqeen and Chaitieh, on the western edge of the zone. They returned 10 minutes later and fired three more. The planes resumed the attack an hour later on the edge of the central sector of the occupied zone. The "Israeli" planes fired four missiles close to Jibaa, in the Iqleem al-Toffah range".

An enemy espionage network was disclosed(2)
It was revealed that a spy network, planted by the "Israeli" enemy in the Lebanese liberated areas, was dismantled yesterday.

The cell gathered security information about Hizbullah's cadres, centres and activities.

The security sources reported that on the first half of the present month, the Lebanese Intelligence apparatus arrested the "Israeli" agent H.A.N., who is a construction worker, from one of the villages of Marjeyoun District. He was detained on one of crossing points between the liberated and occupied areas.

The "Israeli" agent, quickly, acknowledged his relation with the "Israeli" intelligence through which he leaked information over Hizbullah's members and offices in Beirut, Beqaa and South Lebanon.

The security sources said that "Israeli" troops searched the agent's house in the occupied zone and interrogated a number of his relatives at Marjeyoun Barracks. In this regard, the "Israeli" intelligence questioned the collaborator security official of Dibbeen - Blat Sector for three successive days.

Israeli Aggressions(3)
The Israeli occupation forces launched the following aggressions on the Lebanese liberated areas:
8:50, the surroundings of Salaah position subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
19:10, the perimeters of Yater came under seven 155mm missiles.
19:35, the Israeli war jets raided into the crossroad of Jibal El-Botom, Zibqeen and Chaitieh villages firing eight air-to-surface rockets.
19:38, the Israeli fighter-bomber dropped four air-to-ground rockets into the same area.
19:35, the Israeli warplanes staged a sorties into the peripheries of Jibaa.
21:00, Mount Sojod was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
21:05, six 81mm rounds fell on Arid El-Zennar area and three 155mm missiles on Litani River watercourse between Zawtar and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr.
21:10, the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost exposed to an intense heavy machine gun fire.
21:20, Sawan area was targeted by four 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(4)
The IR group of martyrs Jaafar Abdel-Hassan Daher and Mohammad Merhej Osman attacked, at 19:10, enemy movements near Blat post with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits.

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Abdallah Fawaz and Ahmad Abdallah Khalifeh rushed upon Radar post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and accurate goals.
