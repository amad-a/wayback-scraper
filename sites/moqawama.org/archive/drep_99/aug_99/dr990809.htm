Daily Report
 
 
August Monday 9-1999:

MP Fneish: Resistance is our only guarantee to liberate our occupied land(1)
"Bint Jbeil MP Mohammed Fneish, warned that "Israeli" premier Ehud Barak was more dangerous than his predecessors because he is attempting to apply political and diplomatic pressure to halt resistance operations against the occupation forces. Describing the resistance as our only guarantee to liberate our occupied land, the Hizbullah MP vowed the armed struggle would continue"

We reject all Zionist and American calls to stop the resistance. We stick to our right in the resistance until the unconditional liberation and withdrawal, Fneish said during a religious ceremony in Yater".

Forget normalization with "Israel", focus on inter-Arab ties ­ speaker(2)
"The parliamentary speaker injected a note of cold reality into the general mood of optimism over the prospects of advancing the peace process on Sunday, saying an improvement in inter-Arab relations was the key to regional harmony rather than normalization with "Israel".

Arabs are now shutting their eyes, closing their ears, wiping their memories and starting anew as if the Arab-"Israeli" conflict has just been born, and as if the issues of Jerusalem, the return of refugees, the return of Lebanon, the Golan and Arab territories, are simple headlines that one can overstep to normalize relations with "Israel", he told a rally in Zahrani.

We tell Arabs that peace is born from the normalization of inter-Arab relations and from raising the level of solidarity and common Arab work, and not from the Arab-"Israeli" normalization of relations, he said.

"Describing peace as a Lebanese-Syrian need, Berri reiterated Lebanons conditions for a treaty with "Israel": What we want is a just and comprehensive peace based on international resolutions which leads to a full and unconditional "Israeli" withdrawal from Lebanese territories; to a similar withdrawal from the Golan to the June (1967) borders; and to granting Palestinian refugees the right to return in addition to solving the issue of Jerusalem, he said.

"The speaker insisted Lebanon would continue to support the resistance until "Israel" withdrew: We reject all attempts to depict resistance as terrorism. There is a big difference between the rights of our people through the resistance and the state terrorism of "Israel", he said. Our deep-rooted national union, our union of fate and the resistance is our constant trinity to face the unknowns of the future, Berri added.

1:25, nine 155mm missiles slammed into the peripheries of Khorbet Selem town.
1:50, the surroundings of Houla position subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
6:30, Qaissieh Valley was targeted by four 155mm missiles.
Between 7:05 and 9:05, Mount Sojod was bombarded by eight 155m missiles and four 120mm shells.
9:30, the perimeters of Mlikh village exposed to eight 155mm missiles.
14:20, the surroundings of Shamaa position subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
20:45, six 155mm missiles struck the perimeters of Yater.
 21:15, one 120mm shell hit Mount Sojod.
21:25, one tank round landed Mount Bayad near Deir El-Zahrani town.
Between 21:25 and 21:35, the peripheries of Jibaa came under six 155mm missiles.
21:31, the Israeli war jets raided into the area between Zibqeen and Izzieh.
21:50, the Israeli warplanes staged a sorties into the south of Jibal El-Botom.
21:54, the Israeli fighter-bombers dropped twelve air-to-ground rockets into Intari ravine between Yater and Jibal El-Botom.
22:00, the Israeli war jets launched a sortie into the perimeters of Jibaa.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Ahmad Fadlallah and Ali Krayem targeted, at 1:05, a Lahdi force at an area between Qantara and Taibeh towns with the rocket-propelled rockets scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Mahmoud Sami Abdallah and Mahmoud Taleb attacked, at 12:30, enemy movements in and around Haddatha compound with the adequate weapons. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Abbas El-Wazwaz and Samir Akil attacked, at 20:30, Blat post with the proper arms as an Israeli helicopter was landing inside it scoring direct hits.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Nimr Ali Hemayed and Hassan Kamel Hayek targeted Tair Harfa outpost with the suitable weapons. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Thirty five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Haidar El-Jouni and Mohammad Hassan Zilzli rushed upon a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain goals.

Furthermore, the a fore said group attacked, at 23:20, enemy movements at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking direct hits.
