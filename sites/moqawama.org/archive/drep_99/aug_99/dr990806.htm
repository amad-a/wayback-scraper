Daily Report
 
 
August Friday 6-1999:

Introduction
After seiging Aita Al-Shaab and Blida, it is now the turn of Bent Jbeil, the largest city in the mid sector of the occupied zone in South Lebanon. The "Israeli" occupation forces use this kind of pressure to recruit young men from the occupied zone to the ailing collaborating militia.

However, the "Israeli" troops released four Lebanese detainees from the "Israeli" run Khiam detention center.

LEBANON CALLS ON UN TO PUT AN END TO "ISRAELI" SEIGE OF THE BINT JBEIL VILLAGE(1)
" "Israel's" blockade of the occupied southern village of Bint Jbeil has entered its second week. Bint Jbeil residents called on the international community to intervene and pressure "Israel" to lift the siege. The government has also demanded United Nations intervention to end the blockade".

" Another southern Lebanese village is under "Israeli" blockade. Bint Jbeil, deep inside the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon, has been under an "Israeli" siege for eleven days. No one is allowed to enter or leave the village, and food and medicine shortages are imminent. Today, Bint Jbeil inhabitants living in Beirut, held a sit-in at the headquarters of the press syndicate in Beirut. They are demanding international pressure on "Israeli" to lift the blockade on the occupied southern village. The rally was attended by deputies, human rights delegates, and a number of NGO's. Deputy Abdel Hamid Beydoon said the coercive measures applied by "Israeli" occupation troops in the south, including Bint Jbeil, amount to what he called terrorism.

The outside world, he added, must know about the suffering of southerners under "Israeli" occupation. MP Mohammed Fneish warned that as long as "Israeli" occupation persists, southerners can expect suffering and torture, but the Hizbullah deputy pledged resistance attacks will drive "Israelis" out of Lebanon. Human rights group called on the international community to pressure "Israel" to lift the siege on Bint Jbeil, along with the occupied village of Aita Shaab which has been under a similar blockade for weeks now. Lebanon's Prime Minister Selim Hoss, in his capacity as foreign minister, asked Beirut's representative at the United Nations headquarters in New York, Ambassador Samir Mubarak, to demand pressure on "Israel" by United Nations Secretary-General Kofi Annan to lift the blockade. Ambassador Mubarak was asked to contact diplomats in New York and explain to them this latest "Israeli" aggression. UN peace-keepers and International Red Cross Committee volunteers have been able to deliver much needed humanitarian to the occupied and besieged villages of Bint Jbeil and Aita Shaab. "Israel" has been occupying parts of southern Lebanon since 1978 in clear violation of United Nations Security Council Resolution 425, which calls on the Jewish state to immediately and unconditionally withdraw its occupation forces from Lebanese territory".

Occupation released four Lebanese as parents visit sons at Khiam prison(2)
"The "Israeli" occupation forces today (Thursday) released four Lebanese citizens after detaining them at the Khiam prison".
"One week after Hizbullah's initiative of releasing two collaborating militiamen, the "Israeli" occupation's command gave direct orders to the collaborating militia to release four Lebanese nationals detained at the Khiam prison, on condition of staying inside the zone occupied by the "Israeli" army in south Lebanon."

"The released are: Georges Abu Kheir, from al-Aashieh and was detained for 19 months - Castro Khalil Baydoun, from Aitaroun, detained for 30 months- Hussein Ali Sadek, from al-Khiam, detained for 14 months - Hussein Ali Habsi, detained for 18 months".

"On the other hand, 28 detainees' parents today (yesterday)visited their sons at the Khiam prison when an International Red Cross bus transported them to the occupied zone via the Kfar Tibneet crossing.

The visited detainees are: Wajeeh, Hussein and Samer Hijazi (Three brothers), Mustafa Alawyeh, Kamel Abdallah, Taisseer Shaaban, Khanjar Shouaib and Ali Ibrahim Qassem".

"To their surprise the parents of detainee Castro Baydoun arrived at the prison to find out that their son has been just released."

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupying forces staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese liberated areas:

5:20, five 155mm missiles struck Mount Zaatar.
5:50, the surroundings of Mothallath position came under several 60mm rounds and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 6:30 and 11:20, Mount Sojod was bombarded by four 155mm missiles and one 120mm shell.
Between 16:20 and 16:45, Yater and its outskirts exposed to ten 155mm missiles and three 120mm shells.
21:15, three 155mm missiles hit Mount Sojod.
22:35, the perimeters of Jibaa subjected to three 155mm missiles.
23:05, one 155mm missile landed the house of national Hussein Olleik at Zawtar Gharbieh. His family narrowly escaped death whereas his house was damaged.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Mohammad Hassan Yahia and Ali Krayem targeted, at 17:35, enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Abdel-Hassan Cherry and Khodor Rida Harb attacked, at 22:10, an enemy engineering force as it was fortifying Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the suitable weapons, were able to chalk up direct hits and bar the force from achieving its goals.
