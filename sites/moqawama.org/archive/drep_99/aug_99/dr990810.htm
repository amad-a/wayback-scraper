IR scored new victory "Israeli" allied militia redeploys deeper into occupied zone(1)
"Battered by months of resistance assaults, the SLA* militia has abandoned its frontline position at Qantara in the central sector and is preparing to redeploy to a new post deeper inside the occupation zone.

"The militias departure from the outpost comes two months after the SLA vacated the old Beit Yahoun compound in the western sector. The move is part of a re-organization of the militias frontline outposts during the unpredictable final stages of the occupation.

"The pullout began Saturday evening and by dawn Sunday the estimated 10 militiamen departed having dismantled the post and removed all weapons, ammunition, surveillance equipment and electrical fittings.

"The decision to redeploy, however, has left the SLA post at Qsair, 3 kilometers north of Qantara, completely isolated. The SLA withdrew from the Alman position, 2 kilometers east of Qsair in 1994. "Now, with the departure of the militia from Qantara, Qsair is the only remaining outpost in the area.

A security source admitted that the decision in this respect was puzzling.

Qsair has been left extremely isolated. There will be no reinforcements in case of attack, the source said. The militiamen in the Qsair outpost ­ which is located in the corner formed by Wadi Hojeir running into the Litani river gorge ­ cannot expect reinforcements if they come under full frontal attack as the nearest post at Salaah is 5 kilometers to the east, the source said.

"During the past two months, the resistance has concentrated its attacks on Qantara and the "Israeli"-manned outpost at Taibeh. Even during Julys lull in military activity, Qantara was still subject to almost daily mortar attacks.

"However, SLA casualties in Qantara were not particularly high given the number of assaults. In the past two years, three militiamen have been killed and seven wounded. One "Israeli" soldier was also wounded in Qantara.

""Israel" Radio confirmed the withdrawal of the militia from Qantara and said the unit would man a new position which is easier to defend and better equipped. The occupants are expected to redeploy to a new outpost under construction on a small hill just east of the "Israeli" Taibeh post. The new SLA position will have views of several villages facing the central sector as well the open grassland between Qantara and Taibeh.

"The success of the resistance in forcing the militia to abandon the Qantara post will likely encourage increased attacks on Qsair in the next few weeks in the hope of forcing a further SLA withdrawal.

Hizbullahs commander in the south, Sheikh Nabil Qaouk, described the withdrawal as a new victory for the resistance.

By forcing the enemy to pull back from its position in Qantara, which was turned into a real hell-hole, the resistance has scored a new victory, he said in a statement.

This headlong flight is the inevitable result of the heroic actions and sacrifices by our fighters who have managed to paralyze the enemys military force and its agents and prevent them holding this position, he said.

"The redeployment from Qantara is the most significant development so far as the SLA militia undergoes a reorganization program for the final round of the south Lebanon conflict.

"Many of the most heavily hit frontline positions manned by the SLA are being refortified with the addition of underground bunkers and reinforced concrete roofs over previously open areas.

"The move is an attempt by the "Israelis" to bolster the flagging morale of the militia. At the end of 1997, the "Israeli" Army launched a multi-million dollar program to beef up the defenses of its outposts in the occupation zone but neglected the SLA positions".

"ISRAELI" SAILORS PELT LEBANESE FISHERMAN WITH PRICKLY PEARS(4)
" An "Israeli" navy ship opened fire on a Lebanese fishing boat in Lebanese waters before hosing the crew with water canon and then pelting them with prickly pears, port authorities in the southern city of Tyre said Sunday. The incident occurred Saturday at dawn when Mahmud Hussein, 26, and Ali Zaatar, 24, were fishing two kilometers off the coast near the southern Lebanese port of Tyre, the same sources said. The "Israeli" vessel came up close to the boat, which was hit by a machine-gun bullet, and the sailors aimed their water canon at the two young men. They then lobbed rotten potatoes and prickly pears -- a fruit with long spikes on its skin -- at Mahmud, who protested their right to fish the waters and waved a Lebanese flag. He was later treated for bruising at a hospital in Tyre, the port authorities said. "Israel" has maintained a partial blockade on Lebanon's ports for several years and fires on fishing boats that venture more than two kilometers off the coast. In the past six years, dozens of Lebanese fishermen have been injured in encounters with the "Israeli" navy, with 21 one of them being left disabled, the Tyre fishermens' union says".

Collaborators get terms for collaborating(5)
"Five former members of the "Israeli"-backed South Lebanon Army militia were sentenced Monday to imprisonment terms after they were found guilty of collaborating with an enemy state. A sixth was acquitted.

"The Military Tribunal sentenced Hani Aoun to three months in prison and Pierre Aoun, Robert Hajj, George Haddad and Jean Harb to each serve one-year sentences. Pierre Aoun was also fined LL1 million, while Jean Saliba was acquitted.

"The court, presided over by Brigadier Maher Safieddine, heard the cases last Wednesday along with 14 others for which verdicts and sentences will be issued at a later date.

"The defendants were among 220 SLA militiamen who surrendered to the authorities after their militia pulled out of Jezzine in June.

"They all claimed that they were forced into the SLA, either coerced by the militia or unable to find other employment. But they denied supplying "Israel" with intelligence information.

"The court, which will meet every Wednesday to hear the cases of SLA defectors, has ruled on 25 cases. It will not meet this Wednesday because of the 11am closure of government offices for the solar eclipse".
