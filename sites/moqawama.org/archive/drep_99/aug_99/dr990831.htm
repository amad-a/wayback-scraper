Daily Report
 
 
August Tuesday 31-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General Sheikh Nahim Qassem confirmed that the Islamic Resistance (IR) would continue facing the Zionist enemy and warned that: "I tell the "Israeli" government, army and especially the settlers, as of today prepare to be in the shelters whenever one civilian is wounded".

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" occupation troops proceeded with their aggressions yesterday as the warplanes and ground forces attacked areas north of the zone "Israel" occupies. Six civilians, including a five-year-old, were injured in the "Israeli" assaults.

Hizbullah vows to avenge further attacks(1)
"The threat of imminent cross-border Katyusha rocket attacks was rekindled Monday in a blistering speech by Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, Naim Qassem, who warned "Israel" that the resistance would not tolerate further civilian casualties in south Lebanon.

We announce with no reservations that the Katyusha rockets are ready for launching the minute we think suitable. These rockets will be launched by our brothers and will fall on the settlements (in "Israel") in retaliation for the Zionist attacks, Qassem said during the funeral of a Hizbullah fighter, Karim Daqdouq, killed in an air raid on Sunday.

Qassems fiery speech was prompted by the wounding of five civilians on Sunday, including a three-year-old child and a nine-month-old baby, in "Israeli" and SLA* shelling. Several houses were also damaged in the bombardments.

Whats happening in the south is very dangerous from our point of view. We have registered what happened (Sunday) and its for you to remember so that no blame can be cast when the retaliation is launched at a time we will decide from now on. No one must assume that we will remain silent, he said.

Qassem insisted that the resistance would not be intimidated by the prospect of further attacks similar to the wide-ranging "Israeli" air raids on June 24.

If we practiced self-restraint (on Sunday), it was not in preparation for (U.S. Secretary of State Madeleine) Albrights visit or because were scared that the infrastructure may be bombed ... Let everyone know that our finger is on the trigger. Let them beware of the resistance fighters anger and their response at the right time and place, he said.

The "Israeli" Army has said on several occasions that the June air raids are the reason why Hizbullah has refrained from firing Katyushas across the border. However, Hizbullah usually only launches mass cross-border rocket assaults in response to a spate of civilian fatalities over a short period of time. Since June 24, the circumstances have not arisen for Hizbullah to unleash the Katyushas.

Qassem hinted that Syria has placed no restraints on Hizbullahs military operations and warned the "Israeli" inhabitants of northern Galilee to prepare their bomb shelters.

No one has spoken to us and no one will accept "Israel's" arbitrary and aggressive acts. We have not remained silent because were scared, but because were weighing matters to decide whats best to protect our people. But I tell the "Israeli" government, army and especially the settlers, as of today prepare to be in the shelters whenever one civilian is wounded, he said.

Qassems remarks are certain to alarm the "Israeli" government at a time when the arrival of Albright to the region on Wednesday is expected to inject a new impetus into relaunching the peace process.

"Hizbullah has consistently declared it will continue fighting the occupation forces until there is a full withdrawal from the south, regardless of progress in the regional peace talks. But Qassems speech was the most bellicose uttered by a senior member of Hizbullah since the election of "Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak in May raised hopes for a peace settlement and an end to the occupation. It was also the first time since June 24 that the threat of Katyusha attacks against northern "Israel" has been made so forcefully".

"ISRAEL" ESCALATES ATTACKS ON SOUTH INJURING CIVILIANS(2)
"Tensions have risen in southern Lebanon as "Israeli" warplanes and ground troops today (yesterday) attacked areas north of the zone "Israel" occupies. Six civilians, including a five-year-old boy, were injured in "Israeli" attacks on the south yesterday.

The five-nation group monitoring a cease-fire in the south is expected to meet on Thursday to discuss Lebanese and "Israeli" complaints of alleged truce violations".

" Six Lebanese, including a five-year-old child and a Hizbullah resistance fighter, were wounded by gunfire from "Israel" and its allies and an air attack in southern Lebanon Sunday. Police said the child, both his parents and another man were hurt when 15 "Israeli" shells hit the village of Mansouri, 11 kilometers south of the coastal city of Tyre, outside the "Israeli"-occupied zone.

The Hizbullah member was wounded in the air strike, which also occurred outside the occupied zone. A middle-aged woman, Fatima Ramadan, was hurt when "Israel's" proxy militia machine-gunned her car on her way to the village of Yohmor. The villages of Zibqeen, Kafra, Majdal Zoun and Yater, on the western and central edges of the occupied zone were also raided. Later two "Israeli" fighter-bombers fired four missiles on the edge of Yater, adjoining the occupation zone. According to AFP, so far this year 20 civilians have been killed and 137 wounded in southern Lebanon. "Israel" has constantly targeted residential areas in southern Lebanon, in clear breach of a 1996 internationally-monitored accord which bans attacks on and from civilian targets but permits resistance operations against "Israeli" occupation troops".

Military Operations
"The group of the martyrs Ghasan and Zuhair Kbiesi in the Islamic Resistance attacked at 10:30 hours Sunday morning, enemy movements in the Blat post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits. At the same time the group of the martyrs Issam Kanso and Hassan Elias Fakherdinne attacked an enemy motorized troop in the vicinity of the post inflicting direct hits".

And at 10:50 hours of the same day the group of the martyrs Nidal Ali Al-Hiq and Raad Hussien Fares observed fortifying works at the Hemayed post, the group immediately attacked the post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits"

Also, the IR militants spotted, at 19:30, enemy movements at Baraasheit compound. Immediately afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Ali Sakr and abdallah Hadi rushed upon them with the suitable arms chalking up direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR mujahedeen observed, at 22:00, military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Ghassani and Ghassan Ghanem targeted them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite goals(3)

"Meanwhile, Amal continued its attacks on "Israeli" and SLA militia outposts in commemoration of the 21st anniversary of the disappearance of its founder Imam Musa Sadr.

At 6.30am, the movements fighters fired mortar rounds at the northern sector posts of Sojod and Soweida and the central sector position of Salaah.

Thirty minutes later, SLA outposts at Qsair and Mheisbat were attacked. A militiaman, named as Abed Halawi, was reportedly wounded in Qsair and taken to hospital in "Israel". There was no confirmation of the casualty.

At 7.30am, the "Israeli"-manned Taibeh outpost and the radar station in Bayada were shelled. "Israeli" tank fire from Bayada damaged several homes in Mansouri. No casualties were reported(4)

Wounded Golani battalion commander not returning to battalion(5)
" Lieutenant-Colonel Ilan, who was wounded two weeks ago in Lebanon by Hizbullah guerillas, will not be returning to command of the Golani 51st battalion, because his injuries are more serious than first thought".

"Lt.-Col. Ilan was wounded on August 17th when a group of IDF soldiers encountered the Hizbullah guerillas. Ilan was part of the unit that chased the guerillas, killing two. He was shot in both hands and was evacuated by helicopter to Rambam Hospital in moderate condition, thinking that he would soon return to his battalion. However, his right hand requires months of rehabilitation, during which Ilan must curb his activities."

"Therefore, a new commander was appointed for the Golani 51st battalion, Major Avi, who previously was an intelligence officer in Golani. Ilan, who served as battalion commander for a year and a half, will return to the IDF at the conclusion of his rehabilitation, and will receive a new appointment at that time"

"Israel" tightens its grip on the towns that are under siege(6)
"The towns of Aita Al-Shaab, Bint Jbeil and Al-Mari are still under siege despite the close arrival of the UN secretary generals envoy to the area. The envoys mission will be to inspect the situation of the towns that are under "Israeli" occupation.

"In the town of Hula only three of its inhabitants collaborate with the enemy, Imad Shriem, Hussien Musa Ibrahim and Hassan Abdullah Hamoud. The collaborators prevented the inhabitants from leaving the town. While searching the houses the collaborators fires bullets in the air in an effort to scare the inhabitants".

A new martyr falls in defense of our homeland(7)
"With great pride and a firm resolute the Islamic Resistance announces that another hero has followed in the footsteps of the righteous:

"The martyr Abdel Karim Mustafa Dakduk (Ali) achieved martyrdom on the 26th of August 1999 in defense of his homeland. He was born in Aita Al-Shaab 1972, married. He joined the Islamic Resistance in 1995, underwent various military and cultural courses, had a part in many military operations.

"The burial ceremony will take place today at 17:00 hours starting from Imam Al-Mahdi Mosque, Al-Ghobieri".
