HIZBOLLAH RELEASES VIDEO OF MARTYRED FIGHTERS
The Clashes erupted on Tuesday as Hezbollah fighters, avenging the death of one of their commanders, killed two Israeli soldiers and wounded six others. Three Hezbollah soldiers were also killed, and immediately hailed as martyrs by the group, which is leading a resistance war to oust Israel from occupied southern Lebanon. Despite the confrontations, the first since Ehud Barak became Israel's prime minister, Israel says it will stick to promises to withdraw from Lebanon within a year. Hezbollah has released a video of its soldiers going into battle. It shows Hizbollah fighters in the field. The Hizbollah fighters who were killed in the clashes were also shown on video praying before going to fight occupation forces. Hizbollah said the pictures were filmed on Tuesday before fatal clashes with Israeli occupation forces in South Lebanon. Israel sent its air force on several bombing runs during a fierce four-hour artillery, mortar and rocket battle in Wadi Slouki, a valley across from the zone Israel has been occupying for 21 years in southern Lebanon. The Hezbollah soldiers prayed before leaving to fight, pledging loyalty to the leader of the Hezbollah in Lebanon, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah. Later in the day the three fighters died. Two Israeli occupation troops were also killed. However Israel's government says its losses will not stop it from fulfilling its campaign pledge to pull out of Lebanon within a year, even if no peace agreement is in place by then. Tuesday's first Israeli combat fatalities in Lebanon since Prime Minister Ehud Barak took office in July would certainly increase calls in Israel for a withdrawal.
Israel claims it occupies Lebanese land to "protect" its settlements in northern Israel from guerrilla attacks, but Lebanese, ncluding senior officials and experts, believe Israel has deep-seated interests in the south, including its water-rich rivers. There have been numerous reports of Israeli diversion of Litani River water into Israel. In an unprecedented move, Israel this year acknowledged stealing fertile top soil from the south, after the United Nations confirmed Israel's systematic transportation of tons of top soil to the northern Galillee. But mounting Israeli casualties on the hands of the resistance have pressured Israeli leaders to publicly pledge to pullout from Lebanon.

Since 1985 about 250 soldiers have died in southern Lebanon. Many Israelis - among them, leaders of Barak's Labour party - now argue that the cost of securing the occupied zone is too high and that the northern settlements are defensible from the international border.

President tells people of Ainata "we're with you"
President Gen Emile Lahoud told a delegation from the occupied town of Aita al-Shaab that the suffering of  their hometown and the occupied area is "daily evidence to the Israeli arrogance and to the enemy's carelessness when it comes to human values and international conventions."

The state does not use words alone, the president said, adding that the government is using all its capabilities to exert international pressure on the Israeli enemy to force it to stop its oppressive and arbitrary measures against unarmed residents.
