The wadi Slouqi epic from the occupation's side: cowardice and feebleness(1)
"The physical and material impacts of the Wadi al-Slouqi confrontations have gone beyond the human and material losses in the ranks of the enemy to reach the "Israeli" troops' morale.

"The enemy media talked elaborately about the feebleness, cowardice, defeatism that was shown clearly in the ranks of "Israeli" troops during and after the confrontations.

"Two "Israeli" dailies, Ha'aretz and Yediot Ahronot, published the details of the clashes, the fact that the three Hizbullah men discovered the Golani force which was lying in wait for them, how the Hizbullah men managed to approach the "Israeli" force silently and shoot and hurl grenades at the elite of the "Israeli" army.

"Yediot Ahronot said the circumstances of the clashes raised a lot of question marks regarding the performance of the elite troops.

"Ha'aretz asked how three Hizbullah fighters were able to take aback the huge and trained Golani force, and how the few defeated the many despite the fact that the battle took place at very close distance in a rocky and complicated terrain. The newspaper noted the precision of Hizbullah's artillery that, in spite of the "Israeli" air raids, was able to prevent the "Israelis" from evacuating their casualties, besides "weakening the efficiency of the "Israeli" aircraft" with the aid of anti-aircraft fire.

"In a bid to contain the repercussions of the Wadi al-Slouqi confrontations, the occupation command formed a panel headed by the occupation commander in Lebanon Gabi Ashkenazi. The committee that also included a number of "Israeli" senior officers is supposed to investigate the details of the confrontations and how three Hizbullah men were able to defeat 15 of the Golani elite troops (later became 75 troops at the end of the clashes) backed by helicopters and warplanes besides the most sophisticated weaponry.

"The panel aims also to attempt to spare the "Israeli" army more accusations and mockery by the "Israeli" press which is talking about the "cowardice and feebleness of the troops" and the inability of their command to urge them to perform their duties in the batterfield".

Jewish state faces storm after the calm(2)
"The killing of two "Israeli" soldiers in fierce clashes with Hizbullah fighters this week has provided a rude awakening for "Israel" which had been lulled into a false belief that Syria had instructed the organization to tone down its resistance activities in preparation for a resumption of the peace process.

"Tuesdays close-quarter battle in Wadi Slouqi was a clear indication that Hizbullah has no intention of de-escalating its drive to oust the "Israeli" Army from the south.

""Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak has committed himself to a withdrawal by June next year by first striking a deal with Syria. This policy has received the endorsement of a new report undertaken by the Tel Aviv Universitys Jaffe Center for Strategic Studies, which concludes that the occupation must be maintained until a peace treaty is signed with Syria. The report, entitled "Israel" and Southern Lebanon, was submitted to Barak on Wednesday.

"According to the Haaretz daily, it examined all options that have been raised by previous "Israeli" governments. These include expanding the zone to the Litani River ­ a drastic measure advocated by the former "Israeli" agriculture minister Rafael Eitan in the mistaken belief that such a move would place northern "Israel" beyond the 22-kilometer range of Katyusha rockets.

"Other measures include the perennial favorite of bombing Lebanons infrastructure every time an "Israeli" soldier was killed in the south, hitting Syrian military targets in Lebanon, and a unilateral withdrawal following security guarantees from Beirut.

"The report acknowledged that a unilateral withdrawal would reduce military casualties but at the increased risk of danger to the residents of northern "Israel". It assumed that Syria would encourage Hizbullah to continue attacking northern "Israel" to place pressure on the "Israeli" government to hand back the Golan Heights.

"The most startling recommendation is that if Barak intends to abide by his pre-election withdrawal pledge ­ and peace has yet to be agreed with Syria ­ "Israel" should establish a channel of indirect communication with Hizbullah to assess the organizations post-withdrawal plans".

IR which always announces its martyrs with pride and dignity, presents to its patient Ummah and people the three martyrs:

- Martyr Hussein Mahmoud Qabalan (Sadeq), born in Meis al-Jabal 1975, married, joined IR in 1994, shared with several military and educational courses, took part in many Jihad and qualitative operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, obtained encomium from the Hizbullah Secretary General.

- Martyr Ahmed Abdel-Hassan Foani (Mahmoud), born in Houla 1975, married, joined IR in 1994, participated with several military and educational courses, took part in many Jihad and qualitative operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, obtained encomium from the Hizbullah Secretary General.

- Martyr Radwan Muhieddeen Dogha (Moussa), born in Reyaq 1980, single, joined IR in 1998, belonged to the martyrdom squad, participated with several military and educational courses, took part in many Jihad and qualitative operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, obtained encomium from the Hizbullah Secretary General.

"The three Mujahedeen were martyred on Tuesday 17, 1999 during the qualitative operation in Wadi al-Slouqi".

Cabinet: "Israeli" aggressions won't affect stability(5)
"The Council of Ministers held its ordinary session yesterday afternoon at its headquarters near the National Museum. The session was headed by President Gen Emile Lahoud and attended by Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss and ministers.

"At the opening of the session, President Lahoud stressed the coherence of the Lebanese stand in the face of the "Israeli" attempts that aim to harm Lebanon's stability.

"Assassinating one of the Resistance's leaders will not affect the firmness of this stand or the right to resist the occupation until it is removed, Lahoud said.

"The current phase requires unity of efforts and reduction of confusions and competitions at the internal level, he added, calling for the establishment of a democratic atmosphere through an objective opposition that would not harm the national morale at political, economic, or administrative levels.

Political rhetoric must be elevated to express democratic awareness and responsible freedom", the president concluded.

BARAK'S LEBANON POLICY INCLUDES ASSASSINATIONS, COMMANDO OPERATIONS, AND INFRASTRUCTURE BOMBING: KHALIL(8)
"The head of parliament's foreign relations committee, Dr. Ali Khalil today (yesterday) warned that "Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak intends to mount assassination attempts and commando operations on Lebanese territory, as part of his general policy for the country. "Israel" has been occupying parts of southern Lebanon since 1978, and has launched scores of invasions against Lebanon, and committed numerous massacres against civilians. Days before Barak took office, "Israel" launched a bombing campaign on June 24 that targeted Beirut's electricity stations and several bridges throughout the country".

Ex-"Israel's" militiamen get sentences(9)
"Twenty-three former members of the "Israeli"-backed South Lebanon Army were sentenced Thursday to prison terms ranging from six months to two years for collaborating with an enemy state.

"The Military Tribunal sentenced two defendants to two years in prison, one to 18 months, 12 to one year and eight to six months, contradicting indictments that requested at least seven-year sentences for all defendants.

"The court, presided over by Brigadier Maher Safieddine, referred the case of one defendant to a special court for juvenile delinquents because he is under 18 years old. The court acquitted four others because they are over 60 years old".

"All of the former SLA members said they joined the militia because they were under pressure from the SLA or they could not find other jobs. But they denied supplying "Israel" with information about the positions of the army or resistance fighters. The defendants are among 220 SLA militiamen who turned themselves in to authorities after their militia pulled out of Jezzine in June".

"Israel" breaches the international law systematically(10)
"The follow up committee for the release of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" detention centers today appealed to Prime Minister Selim Hoss, in his capacity as foreign minister, to address the issue of detentions at the UN General Assembly later this fall. They called on Hoss to inform the international community of "Israel's" systematic breach of international law, by kidnapping Lebanese civilians and keeping them as hostages in occupied southern Lebanon and inside "Israel". They said any resumption of peace talks with "Israel" should be conditional on "Israel's" willingness to free some 128 Lebanese detained in its jails. Several human rights groups, including Amnesty International, have condemned the appealing living conditions at the detention centers".
