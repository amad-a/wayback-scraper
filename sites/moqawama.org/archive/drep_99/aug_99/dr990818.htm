Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 18-1999:

Introduction
As Hizbullah promised the day of assassinating one of the senior military Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen that the "Israeli" crime will not go unpunished, the IR militants launched series of qualitative military operations against the "Israeli" occupying troops in the Central Sector of South Lebanon. Three "Israeli" soldiers were killed and five others were wounded including a lieutenant colonel.

In the meantime, the "Israeli" enemy proceeded with its violation of the April 1996 Understanding by bombarding the residential areas in South Lebanon, thus inflicting four Lebanese civilians.

However, Hizbullah members and supporters escorted, yesterday,. Martyr Ali Deeb to his final resting place in a massive procession that set out from the square of Hizbullah Secretariat HQ in Haret Hreik.

Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah took part in the procession and said before thousands that "our open war against the occupation will remain as such and we will proceed with all the strength, firmness and resolve that the battle requires".

Three "Israelis" killed in fierce south fighting Resistance keeps its vow to avenge killing of chief(1)
"Fulfilling its pledge to avenge the assassination of a senior resistance commander, Hizbullah fighters on Tuesday engaged "Israeli" forces in the most intense and prolonged battle of the year, killing three soldiers and wounding five.

""Israel" denied that it was behind Mondays bombing in Sidon in which Ali Deeb, a Hizbullah security officer, was killed.
But the government and the resistance remained adamant that only "Israel" would benefit from the assault, which jolted Sidon while still reeling from the assassination of the four judges in June. Despite the "Israeli" denials, politicians in Beirut have linked the two events to a purported "Israeli" scheme to destabilize the country.

"On Tuesday, gun battles raged in the south as mourners in Beiruts suburb of Haret Hreik turned out in the thousands to bury Deeb. The fighting was the first serious outbreak in the south since "Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak took office last month.
""Israeli" warplanes staged at least three raids on areas facing the central sector Monday night and Tuesday morning as Hizbullah mortar and short-range Katyusha rocket teams traded blows with "Israeli" artillery for most of the day. Four Lebanese civilians were also wounded in the clashes.

The fighting began with a series of standard mortar attacks overnight on the Salaah, Taibeh and Mheisbat outposts at the northern end of the central sector. "Israeli" warplanes fired missiles at midnight into Wadi Qaissieh between Majdal Selem and Khorbet Selem. "But the fighting markedly escalated at about 7.30am when a Hizbullah squad ambushed an "Israeli" commando patrol in Wadi Slouqi. A statement from the group said that fierce battles broke out as the fighters hurled hand grenades and fired machine guns at the "Israelis".

""Israeli" artillery pounded the area and fired smoke rounds in an attempt to provide cover while extracting the dead and wounded. A second Hizbullah team attacked "Israeli" reinforcements, preventing them from reaching their beleaguered comrades who were pinned down in the wadi by heavy mortar fire.

"An "Israeli" Merkava tank which was shelling the areas opposite the zone came under fire from the resistance. The statement said that the fighters forced the tank to cease firing.

""Israeli" helicopter gunships began strafing the area while Hizbullah guerrillas targeted "Israeli" soldiers retreating toward Wadi Istabl on the outskirts of the Houla position.

"Heavy shelling continued in the area accompanied by up to six air raids as "Israeli" warplanes fired missiles into Wadi Qaissieh as well as the area around Jmaijmeh and Shaqra, 2 kilometers to the south.

"At 11.35am, Hizbullah said that six Hummer and Rio all-terrain vehicles, transporting a large number of "Israeli" soldiers to the old Houla position, were targeted with rocket-propelled grenades. At the same time, resistance mortar teams stepped up the shelling of the "Israeli" troops trapped in Wadi Slouqi.

The cries and screams of the Zionist soldiers were heard clearly, the statement said.

It was unclear when the "Israeli" casualties occurred. They raised the "Israeli" death toll this year to 12, with another 44 wounded.

"The "Israeli" casualties were the highest in a single incident this year since three officers were killed and five wounded in a similar ambush near Maidoun in February.

Barak, who has pledged to withdraw "Israeli" troops from Lebanon within a year, suggested that Tuesdays fighting will be what makes it possible in the end to remove the "Israeli" Army from Lebanon by agreement and deploy it on the international border.

Security sources, remarking on the intensity of the fighting, said that more than 200 artillery rounds were fired into the Shaqra area by the "Israelis" with Hizbullahs mortar teams responding every few minutes with salvoes of between 10 and 15 rounds each. The sources said that 139 high explosive and smoke rounds of 155mm caliber alone hit the area around Shaqra and Wadi Slouqi.

Theres a genuine battle being fought on military lines out there, a security source said.

"Israeli" troops were spotted carrying three stretchers in Wadi Slouqi at one point"(1)

"For its part, the Islamic Resistance's (IR) issued a statement in which it announced that the mujahedeen Monday night and Tuesday dawn staged a series of operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators inflicting definite casualties in their ranks.

IR once again affirmed that it possesses the initiative and that the martyr leaders' blood only adds up to the Resistance men's resolution, strength and efficiency in the grounds of Jihad and confrontation.

"At 7:30 Tuesday, squad of Martyr Commander Hajj Ali Deeb attacked a Zionist infantry force in Wadi al-Slouqi with machine guns and hand grenades. Violent clashes ensued in which a number of the invading troops were either killed or injured.

"The enemy had to bring in reinforcements to evacuate its casualties under a heavy cover of bombardment. Squad of Martyr Commander Mohammed Hazimeh intercepted the reinforcements with machine-gun fire and rocket propelled grenades preventing them from performing their mission.

"At 8:15, as the occupation troops were retreating towards Wadi al-Istabl (the vicinity of the Houla outpost), they came under fire from the squad of Martyr Commander Hussein Bahij Nasser. The Mujahedeen used the appropriate weapons in the attack, scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, another IR squad targeted and silenced an "Israeli" Merkava tank that had tried to provide cover for the retreat of the defeated troops.

"Then, the enemy engaged its helicopter gunships in the clashes to ease the pressures imposed on its troops by the IR Mujahedeen. Moreover, "Israeli" warplanes staged several raids while enemy artillery fired scores of rounds on the vicinity of the targeted area.

"At 9:30, the enemy brought in another force to evacuate the casualties; this one was targeted by the squad of Martyr Mohammed Habhab that used the appropriate weapons and scored direct hits.

At 11:30, the enemy brought additional reinforcements that included six Hammer and Rio vehicles that carried a large number of troops to the vicinity of the old Houla outpost. They came under attack from the squad of Martyr Hassan Zaraqet that used rocket propelled grenades and direct rounds, scoring several hits.

"Five minutes later, squad of Martyr Nimr Ftouni of the Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted the Zionist besieged force in wadi al-Slouqi with direct rounds following the repeated failures by the enemy to lift the siege imposed on it by the Resistance men. More casualties were inflicted in the enemy ranks. "Israeli" troops were heard crying in the operation site.

Meanwhile, Zionist warplanes staged several raids on Wadi al-Qaissieh, and the outskirts of Shaqra and al-Jmeijmeh"(2)

Also, the IR militants observed, at 15:15, enemy movements inside Baraasheit compound. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Saleh Mohammad Harb and Mustafa Mohammad Karaki targeted them with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.

Moreover, the IR unit of martyrs Bilal El-Akhras and Ghassan Ali Ghanem attacked, at 16:20, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Forty minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Trabolssi and Adnan Mohammad Hassan fell upon an "Israeli" force near Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct and certain goals(3)

However, the "Israeli" daily newspaper Ha'aretz covered the event by reporting that "two Golani soldiers, Staff Sergeant Eyal Gueta and Sergeant Doron Hershkovitz, were killed yesterday and seven soldiers were wounded in clashes with two Hizbullah cells in South Lebanon. Two of the hurt soldiers suffered serious wounds and a battalion commander was among the lightly wounded".

"The clashes yesterday occurred at about 7:30 A.M. The Golani force was operating near the border of the security zone in the central sector. A three-member Hizbullah cell opened fire at the force at short range as it passed by the village of Shaqra.

"The guerrillas fired light weapons and hurled hand grenades, and the soldiers returned fire and stormed toward the cell.

"Staff Sergeant Eyal Gueta was killed during the chase, and some soldiers were wounded. A rescue force alerted from "Israel", headed by a battalion commander, Lieutenant Colonel Ilan, treated the wounded and joined in the chase. After a short while, the battalion commander's force encountered "Hizbullah guerrillas, again at short range. Doron Hershkovitz was killed in the exchange of fire, and the battalion commander was lightly hurt in his hand.See background, Page 2.Soldiers who participated in the fighting said the battalion commander was "very lucky" to escape without more serious injuries. "The fighting with the two Hizbullah cells lasted about six hours and ended in the afternoon, when the force returned to retrieve the body of one of the soldiers.

"The seven wounded soldiers were evacuated to Rambam Hospital in Haifa. Two are in serious condition, two suffered medium injuries and three, including the lieutenant colonel, were lightly hurt. "Five of the soldiers underwent surgery yesterday. The wounds were all caused by bullets and shrapnel.

The air force set out on two bombing runs during the fierce artillery, mortar and rocket battles, which raged for nearly four hours in Wadi Slouqi, a valley in a no-man's -land across from the security zone in southern Lebanon.

"Fighter jets fired two missiles at the area, which is believed to be used by Hizbullah guerrillas as a base to launch attacks on "Israeli" troops and their allied Lebanese militiamen in the occupied zone.

Wadi Slouqi lies between the village of Khorbet Selem and the villages of Houla and Markaba, about three kilometers (two miles) from the border.

"The same area was targeted by "Israeli" jets and helicopter gunships early yesterday before the ground fighting erupted.

"There was no word on casualties from the air strikes and no independent confirmation that any guerrilla targets were hit"(4)

Hizbullah SG at martyr Deeb's funeral: There is no place for the occupation in Lebanon
"Thousands of Hizbullah partisans and sympathizers, chanting Death to "Israel" and America is the Great Satan, crowded Haret Hreik on Tuesday for the funeral of Ali Deeb, a resistance officer slain in a roadside bomb attack in Sidon a day earlier.

"The crowd assembled in front of Hizbullahs Shura Council headquarters in Haret Hreik to honor Deeb, alias Abu Hassan.

"The partys secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, led the mourners. In a brief address he repeated Hizbullahs pledge to keep up the jihad (holy war) until "Israel" is ousted from Lebanese territory.

We are not wasting time. We are leading a huge war to liberate our land, declared Nasrallah.

The crowd silently repeated after him as he recited the prayer of the dead in front of Deebs coffin, draped with a yellow Hizbullah flag. Death to America; death to "Israel"! "Israel" is the enemy of Islam, they shouted.

Finally, Abu Hassans body has rested and his soul ascended to meet that of his son who also died a martyr several months ago, Nasrallah said. It is not surprising that Abu Hassan was martyred. It is more surprising that he remained alive until now, he said.

Deeb joined Hizbullah when the party was established in 1982. He was the target of at least six assassination attempts. While no group has yet to accept responsibility for his death, Hizbullah has blamed "Israel" for the assassination.

The procession passed through the blocked-off streets of Haret Hreik, causing a traffic jam that lasted two hours. Deeb was buried in Beirut at the Rawdat Shahidein cemetery because his body could not be taken to his native village of Kounine in the occupation zone.

The procession included a van draped in Hizbullah flags that was covered with posters of Nasrallah and late party leader Sayyed Abbas Musawi, as well as a stream of sheikhs who carried the coffin.

Deebs murder was widely condemned by government officials, with President Emile Lahoud paying condolences to Hizbullah. This crime, committed by the enemy, can never falter Lebanons determination to defend its territory and its rights, he said(5)

Nassrallah said: "Our open war against the occupation will remain as such; we have started this war with full awareness, realizing whom we are fighting, what road we are taking, whom we are confronting, the injustice, estrangement, killing. displacement, perils, accusations, intimidation and threats that await us, he said.

"Let no one think that the falling of martyrs and killing our leaders, cadres, Mujahedeen, brothers, and families may reduce our resolve in this battle, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding "Abu Hassan (Martyr Ali Deeb), was martyred, so did Sayyed Abbas al-Musawi and Sheikh Ragheb Harb, along with their sons and brothers. Others will be martyred, for we are not playing games here, nor are we wasting time; we are engaged in a real war, the only real war fought by the Ummah. We will proceed with all the strength, firmness, resolve that the battle requires."

"The enemy will understand, as we have made it understand in the past months and years, that there is no room for the occupation in Lebanon, the Secretary General concluded(6)

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss also condemned the attack, saying it not only targeted the resistance, but every Lebanese.

"Tyre MP and Amal Movement member Mohammed Abdel-Hamid Beydoun accused "Israel" of murdering Deeb, saying it was an attack on Lebanons sovereignty and a violation of all international laws.

"Beydoun called on the government not to let the crime go unpunished especially on the legal level.

We heard that "Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak has prepared a long list of names of resistance fighters he wants dead, he said(7)

Fresh "Israeli" violation, sea and ground bombardment
"In a fresh "Israeli" violation of the April Understanding, Abbas and Fatima Bzieh were wounded in the town of Zibqeen when an "Israeli" round exploded in their field.

"The man and his wife were collecting the carob harvest of their grove near the Nepalese UNIFIL battalion in the town.

"The occupation's artillery widened the area of their aggressions today; setting the mid-sector ablaze with bombs and raids.

"Spokesman for the UNIFIL Timur Goksel said the "Israelis" bombarded in the morning (during the Wadi al-Slouqi confrontations) the outskirts of Majdal Selem, Shaqra, al-Jmaijmeh, Wadi al-Qaissieh, and Houla.

"Lebanese security sources said more than 100 rounds hit the outskirts of the targeted areas while the occupation's warplanes staged two raids at 9:55 and 13:45, firing eight air-to-ground missiles.

"The "Israeli" escalation started at night, as enemy warplanes staged raids on the outskirts of Khorbet Selem and the Wadi al-Qaissieh gorge.

"Lebanon has filed a number of complaints with the April Understanding Monitoring Group over the injuries of four civilians and the damage of several houses in "Israeli" aggressions"(8)

LEBANESE FISHERMAN WOUNDED BY "ISRAELI" NAVY FIRE(10)
"A Lebanese fisherman was seriously wounded this morning when "Israeli" navy gunboats opened machine-gun fire on fishing boats off the coast of the southern port city in Tyre. 66 year old Ibrahim Arid received a bullet in his back and was evacuated by other fishermen toward the port of Tyre, where he was transported to hospital for surgery. According to the fishermen, an "Israeli" navy gunboat opened machine-gun fire without warning on four fishing boats off the coasts of Tyre. "Arid was wounded in the back and his boat was pierced by a hail of bullets. He managed to return to shore with the help of two fellow fishermen.

The three other boats also headed back," one fisherman told AFP. The "Israeli" navy has been imposing a sea blockade for several years on Lebanese fishermen in the Tyre area, preventing them from sailing more than one nautical mile off the coast. On March 1, the "Israeli" navy fired similarly without warning on fishing boats off Tyre, without inflicting casualties".

People of Qantara want to return to their town(11)
"The town of Qantara is located on a set of hills and wadis 15 kms to the south west of Tyre. The town was doomed to have the "Israeli" occupation forces positioned on one of the opposite hilltops for 21 years.

"This fact has led to the displacement of 99% of the 45000 people who used to live in the town. Since the "Israeli" invasion of south Lebanon in 1982, the town has lost 15 martyrs; 30 of its residents were wounded, 25 others are held in the notorious al-Khiam detention center, 150 were houses destroyed, scores of groves were burned. "Israeli" forces, moreover, deliberately burned the woods surrounding the town.

"These figures came in a memorandum sent from the town's people to the UN Secretary General Kofi Annan. It was handed over to the UNIFIL's Liaison Officer Maj Eric Bodlen by a delegation from the town.

"The memorandum called for assisting the al-Qantara's people to return to the town following the withdrawal of the "Israeli" occupation forces from the Qantara Hilltop last week. It also wanted UNIFIL to set two checkpoints at the town's eastern side (opposite the occupation's outpost in Taibeh) and to the west facing Wadi al-Hojeir".
