Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 4-1999:

Introduction
A Lebanese civilian was wounded when "Israeli" shells exploded in the coastal village of Mansouri in the Western Sector. Also, the "Israeli" war jets raided into an area in Iqleem Al-Toffah mountain ridge in southern Lebanon.

In the meantime, Hizbullah called on all human and international committees to reveal the atrocities of the "Israeli" occupation forces and work for lifting the blockade imposed on the village of Aita Al-Shaab to allow humanitarian aid inside the town.

"Israeli" aggressors intercept a cargo ship near Tyre(1)
"The "Israeli" occupation forces at dawn today (Tuesday) escalated the aggressions against south Lebanon, as the "Israeli" gunboats intercepted the commercial cargo ship "Mondial" near the Tyre sea- front and kept it for 2 hours.

"The ship sailed from Hamburg-Germany carrying 118 cars aboard. The cars belong to Lebanese businessmen who said that the "Israeli" enemy often does such interception to collect money (tributes) that will be later used to finance the ailing collaborating militia.

But the Lebanese businessmen refused to reveal the sum of money they were forced to pay for the "Israeli" aggressors.

"On the other hand, the "Israeli" warplanes renewed their raids against the liberated villages, dropping several air-to-ground missiles on the vicinity of Jibaa.

"Earlier, the warplanes intensively hovered over the areas of al-Nabatieh and Iqleem al-Toffah, staging mock raids.

"Moreover, the "Israeli" artillery bombarded the outskirts of al-Loweizeh in Iqleem al-Toffah and the woods of Oqmata and Sojod with 120 and 155mm rounds from the enemy's emplacement in al-Zafatteh and al-Rihan hills.

"Al-Loweizeh residents spent all last night in bomb shelters after being targeted by "Israeli" heavy machine gun fire from the occupation's outpost in Bir Kallab. Several houses inside the town were reported damaged.

"Furthermore, civilian Mariam Srour, 45, was injured when her house in al-Mansouri was struck by an "Israeli" bomb".

Aita al-Shaab still suffering from blockade(2)
"The steadfast residents of Aita al-Shaab are still suffering from the blockade imposed by the "Israeli" occupation forces against their peaceful town.

"This morning (Tuesday), after a one-month blockade, the expelled inhabitants of Aita staged a sit in front of the UNIFIL'S HQ in Tyre, lifting banners condemning the blockade and calling for the release of all the detainees.

"They also delivered a memorandum to the UNIFIL's command, explaining the circumstances of the siege and the arrests of elders, women and children, in addition to the town's youth.

"Moreover, the protesters denounced the cut of water supply, the shootings inside the houses and the threats of destroying the houses of the steadfast residents who refuse to cooperate with the occupation forces.

"The demonstrators called on the UNIFIL to interfere and lift the blockade to allow the passage of food and medical supplies, in addition to the return of confiscated mobile phones".

Hizbullah: Aita al-Shaab siege fortifies steadfastness(3)
"Hizbullah today (Tuesday) called on all human and international committees to reveal the atrocities of the "Israeli" occupation forces and work for the lifting of the blockade imposed against Aita al-Shaab to allow humanitarian aid inside the town.

"These repeated atrocities show that the "Israeli" occupation is aiming at breaking the steadfastness of our people, a Hizbullah statement stressed.

"The statement said these aggressions reveal the troubles the "Israeli" occupation is suffering from on the occupied Lebanese soil.

"The "Israeli" acts and measures, adding to the steadfast of our people, are strengthening the determination of our Mujahedeen to resist for the return of the land and to force the enemy to pay a high price for its aggressive measures against our innocent civilians", the statement added.

Blida, after Aita al-Shaab, Seiged(4)
"Information from the occupied zone in south Lebanon said a seige is being imposed against the town of Blida, by what is left of the collaborating militia, to force the residents to join the dismantled militia.

"According to the information, the "Israeli" occupation ordered the collaborators to recruit young men to reinforce the ailing militia.

"At once, collaborators Abu Burhan, Hassib Farhat, Youssef Ali Ibrahim, Jamal Moussa Ali Hussein, Mohamad Hussien Hijazi and Hussein Ali Hassan surrounded the town, preventing everyone from going in or out.

"Following this act, the collaborators stormed the town's houses and counted all men and youth who have the capability to carry a weapon, telling them they should apply at the recruitment camps of the collaborators or pay a sum of $10,000 to be excused from the obligatory recruitment.

"The families of these young men refused this move and told the collaborators that they will not sacrifice their children for the service of the Zionist enemy who is occupying our land in south Lebanon", the information added.

Military Operations(6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Ahmad Freiji and Ghassan Ali Ghanem targeted, at 11:05, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western fortification.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Sami Ali Al-Takesh and Hadi Mahmoud Mosheimesh attacked, at 14:10, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Rshaf outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct and definite hits.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Khodor Jouni and Fares Hawi attacked, at 23:00, Beaufort Castle with the proper arms scoring direct hits.

SENSE OF NORMALCY NOT RESTORED TO Kfar Houna(7)
" When "Israeli"-backed forces withdrew from the Jezzine enclave in southern Lebanon last June, it was uncertain how long it would take before normalcy is restored to villages in the area. We visited the front-line village of Kfar Houna, and as our report suggests, the shadow of occupation still haunts the region. And there is a clear security vacuum there".

These elevated rolling hills are an ideal escape from the sweltering summer heat. They are also a haven for "Israeli" allied militiamen whose presence in this front-line region continues to haunt Kfar Houna residents. The village was liberated from "Israeli" occupation in June, and there is a lot of this around: Concrete, sand, and machinery. People are returning after a seventeen year absence.

(NS: Lutfi Habhab, Kfar Houna resident, telling FTV: "we will rebuild this village, people have been returning, mainly for weekends, the reason villagers left in the first place was because "Israeli" allied militiamen wanted to recruit young men to their ranks, everyone refused, so the militiamen kidnapped two families, and people were scared"). Kfar Houna witnessed some of the worst clashes between Lebanese resistance fighters and retreating occupation forces in June. (BRIDGE: the first occupation checkpoint is less than 200 meters away from here but there are rumors in Kfar Houna that "Israeli" allied militiamen have been vacating the post) The post is a short drive from the village square. But there have been many reports indicating that "Israel's" proxy force is retreating into outposts deep in the zone "Israel" occupies. Few minutes after he went through a militia checkpoint, we asked this cab driver whether occupation troops are manning the checkpoint, he said 'yes'. But Kfar Houna residents insist militiamen are on the move".

 "(NS: Lutfi Habhab, telling FTV: "Militiamen have left the Kfar Houna post, they are in Rihan and Aramta, there is no one at the post, the further they get from the village, the more stable life becomes") The village square is bustling with people. The center of activity is around the top of an "Israeli" tank which was blown up by the resistance during clashes in the village years back. Today, children, including those here for the weekend, can once more enjoy their summer vacation.

(NS: Zeinab Abbas, Kfar Houna native, saying: "...three weeks ago, was the first time I came here, and it was very great thing to discover my village for the first time because my grandpa used to tell me lots of things about it (CUTAWAY OF KIDS PLAYING) today and after I discovered all the beauty, I feel I am ready to fight any occupation to live free in my village..") Young men who avoided coming here during the occupation for fear of detention and forced recruitment to the "Israeli" militia force, today enter and leave the village freely.

(NS: Tarek Mohsen, Kfar Houna native, saying: "I only returned here a month and a half ago, many people died or got sick during our absence, but we could not come here due to the occupation, but thanks to resistance fighters who spared their blood for our freedom we are here today... but the main problems is that there is no presence for police or security forces here)
In contrast to other areas of Kfar Houna, this neighborhood which is only a glance away from occupation troops, lay deserted. A few days ago, machine gun fire rained down on these alleys. Residents said a number of roadside bombs have also been detonated in this area, blaming the incidents on occupation troops.

(Standup: No sense of normalcy will be restored to this village before occupation forces who are stationed just down this road withdraw from the area. For now, residents of Kfar Houna have to live with the fact that their village marks the beginning of the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon. Mohalhel Fakih, Future Television, Kfar Houna, South Lebanon)".
