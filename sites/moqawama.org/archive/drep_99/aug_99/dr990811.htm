Daily Report
 
 
August Wednesday 11-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said that the "Israeli" occupation's withdrawal from Qantara outpost resulted from the fact that the enemy command was no longer able to bear the Islamic Resistance's (IR) incessant military pressure.

Elsewhere, the residents of the occupation zone gathered outside the Escwa building in downtown Beirut to protest the continued crackdown by the occupation troops of village in the Western Sector. The protesters delivered a memorandum for UN Secretary General Kofi Annan in which they called for an end to the month-long blockade of Bent Jbeil, Aita El-Shaab, Ainata and Blida.

Resistance forced occupation to withdraw, says Sheikh Qassem
The "Israeli" occupation's withdrawal from the Qantara outpost resulted from the fact that the enemy command was no longer able to bear the Islamic Resistance's incessant military pressure, Hizbullah "Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem said.

His Eminence expected "more partial withdrawals by the enemy and its proxy militia, thanks to the "Mujahedeen's activity and the painful blows they are dealing to the occupation."

"Speaking at a rally in Beqaa, Sheikh Qassem said ""Israel"" is a threat to our present and future"(1)
"Also, he launched a scathing attack on the concept of peace with "Israel" which he described as a present and future danger to our nation.

How can anyone believe that a solution is possible with this enemy? he asked. "Israel" is a state which rose through war and is an aggressive country created with the support from the so-called great nations on the basis of imposing terms rather than on the basis of peoples rights.

Who will be able to match the nuclear development and military capabilities with which the Zionist entity is going to impose its terms anytime, and on any group or state.

Qassem derided the United Nations, the leadership of which benefits the powerful at the expense of the weak.

He added that the UN Security Council was incapable of issuing a resolution condemning "Israel".

"Israel" represents and harbors occupation schemes which we cannot accept. The violation which brought in the Jews from the different parts of the world wants to violate while asking Arabs to acknowledge its legitimacy as a friendly state, he said(2)

SOUTHERNERS APPEAL TO THE WORLD TO LIFT SEIGE ON VILLAGES(3)
"About a month ago, "Israel" besieged a number of villages in occupied southern Lebanon, not allowing anyone to enter or leave them. Beirut has appealed to the United Nations for help, and the government was informed that United Nations peace-keeping officials based in New York are holding contacts with "Israel" over the issue. Today (yesterday), residents of the besieged villages held a sit-in and called on the international community to intervene".

" (woman singing: "tanks are besieging us and we resist....") This old woman from occupied and besieged Bint Jbeil in southern Lebanon wants the world to listen to her and help ease the plight of her village which has been under an "Israeli" siege for the past month. Mariam Bazzi along with tens of people from the occupied villages of Bint Jbeil, Aita Shaab, Ainata, Ramyeh, Houla, Srayra, and some Arkoub towns, are protesting against "Israel's" siege of the region. "Israeli" occupation forces have imposed a total blockade on the region, not allowing anyone to enter or leave the villages. Mariam Bazzi, whose relatives are in Bint Jbeil, called on US President Bill Clinton and UN Chief Kofi Annan, to decide on who is a "terrorist", people defending their rights and suffering or occupiers imposing hardships and breaching human rights declarations on daily basis? A committee representing the besieged villages gathered at UN House in downtown Beirut where they handed United Nations representatives a letter addressed to Secretary-General Kofi Annan, describing the plight of Lebanese under the "Israeli" blockade. The letter says occupation forces have been demolishing homes of innocent civilians, especially in Bint Jbeil, where Adnan Bazzi's home was a few days ago leveled. The letter details how "Israeli" forces have been parading their tanks in the villages, threatening villagers with expulsions, or detention if young men refuse to join the ranks of the "Israel's" proxy militia. The committee warned that there is a shortage of food and medicine as a result of the siege, adding "Israeli" forces have deliberately been burning agricultural produce in the villages. Tens of young men and elderly, the letter said, have been rounded up and detained by occupation troops, on grounds that they aid Lebanese fighters resisting "Israeli" occupation in southern Lebanon. Youssef who is from besieged Ainata, said his relatives there are under a physical and psychological blockade. "Israel", he said, has forbidden people there from contacting their relatives in Beirut. (NS: Youssef telling FTV: "it prevents people from entering, it closed the crossing points, it arrested many people and put them in Khiam, it burned the agricultural produce, which caused a decrease in the food supplies, and many people are suffering from illnesses...). A number of deputies attended the rally and voiced support to the villagers. Speakers compared the siege of villagers to hostage-taking. These villagers hope the United Nations, which issued Security Council resolution 425, calling on "Israelis" to withdraw from Lebanon, will intervene and pressure "Israel" to end its latest atrocity against southerners. The committee representing the besieged villages is planning to hold a support rally for villagers in the occupied zone at the Beit Yahoun border-crossing which links the zone to the rest of Lebanon on Sunday".

"ISRAEL" DESTORYS HOME AND CUTS POWER TO FIVE SOUTHERN VILLAGES(4)
(israeli Aggressions)
""Israeli" shelling on the front-line village of Baraacheit in southern Lebanon destroyed a house belonging to Najib Farhat. Lebanese police reported that five shells fired by occupation troops fell inside the village, but caused no casualties. Some twenty shells also landed at the outskirts of Baraacheit, destroying an electricity grid leaving four other villages without power".
