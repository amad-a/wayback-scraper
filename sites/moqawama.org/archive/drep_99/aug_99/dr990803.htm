Daily Report
 
 
August Tuesday 3-1999:

Introduction
The anticipation of the commander of the Irish battalion in south Lebanon regarding the expected withdrawal of Israeli troops comes at a time when the enemy military leaders are in confusion as to how to face the Islamic Resistance.

Islamic Resistance operations (1)
The Islamic Resistance announced the following:

00:15 the group of the martyrs Wafic Zahwi and Abed Al-Rasool Baydoun in the Islamic Resistance attacked Talat Al-Mohaisbat on the road towards Al-Taibe Al-Qantara with rockets inflicting direct hits.

04:30 the group of the martyrs Kamel Abdullah Abed Al-Rida in the Islamic Resistance attacked the Kalaa Zionist post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits.

15:10 the group of the martyrs Saleh Mohamed Harb and Abed Al-Salam Nayef Ayoub in the Islamic Resistance attacked the Hemayid post with appropriate weapons achieving direct and accurate hits, clouds of smoke was witnessed rising from the post. At the meantime the group of the martyrs Hassan Ahmed Jaber and Hasan Mohamed Karaki were attacking Rshaf post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits.

At the same time the group of the martyrs Mustafa Mohamed Karaki and Mohamed Hasan Yehya attacked the Hadatha post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits in the northern bunkers of the post.

Also, the group of the martyrs Raad Hussien Fares and Hussien Afif Wehbi attacked the Baraasheet post with appropriate weapons achieving direct hits.

17:40 the group of the martyr Musa Habib Fahes in the Islamic Resistance resumed its attack on the Hemayid post after the surveillance observed Laheds engineers fortifying their bunkers, the attacks achieved direct hits.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Mustafa Chokair and Ahmad Fadlallah attacked, at 21:15, an enemy motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to score direct goals.

Quarter an hour later, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Krayem and Hussein Ghassani targeted military vehicles inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Israeli aggressions (2)
The Islamic Resistance announced the following:
06:20 two 120mm shells landed on the Sujud mountain and a 155mm shell from Al-Zafateh on the same place.
07:35 four 155mm shells on Abu Rkab mountain.
07:55 six 155mm shells on Abu Rkab mountain.
15:15 sporadic firing within the vicinity of the Hadatha post and between Hadatha and Aita.
15:20  bombing of Jal Al-Tayaran with 155mm shells.
15:20 five 155mm shells landed in the area of Teir Harma west of Yater.
15:25 two 81mm shells landed inside the village of Barasheet and very close to civilian homes, another two shells of the same caliber landed in Kalat Aita as well as firing 12.7mm caliber machine guns towards the outskirts of Aita.
15:30 sporadic firing between Hadatha and Aita.
15:45 sporadic firing with 12.7mm machine guns inside the village of Barasheet which resulted in the burning of agriculture in the area of Al-Ain as well as firing the same caliber inside the village of Hadatha.
16:55 Israeli war planes raided the outskirts of Kafra between Harees and Kafra which resulted in the destruction of the deserted home of Hasan Mohamed Hijazi as well as damaging the homes of Mohamed Abed Al-Hussien Hijazi and Mohamed Abed Al-Hussien Gharabi.
18:10, four 155mm missiles hit the perimeters of Haddatha.
Between 20:45 and 21:15, Mount Sojod was targeted by three 155mm missiles and one 120mm shell.
21:15, the peripheries of Mlikh subjected to three 155mm missiles.
Between 21:30 and 21:50, seven 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Jibaa.

Significant withdrawals in the near future (3)
The commander of the Irish battalion in the south of Lebanon Colonel Nash expected significant withdrawals in the near future. His comments came during a get together meeting called on by Colonel Nash with heads of municipalities and mayors of the region in Tibneen.

Nash also said during his speech, the Irish Battalion will always support security, the environment and services in this region. He also mentioned his great hope in achieving peace, hoping that the next Irish Battalion would be the last one to serve on Lebanese soil, expecting significant withdrawals in the near future.

The April understanding committee meets to discuss two Lebanese complaints (3)
The April understanding committee meets this morning at Al-Nakoura to discuss two Lebanese complaints regarding the bombing by Israel a number of Lebanese villages especially the villages of Barasheet, Touleen and Zibqeen which resulted in various damages.

The inhabitants of Aita Al-Shaab continue their movement in order to liberate it from its siege

A delegation from Aita Al-Shaab headed by Shiekh Ali Surour visited the President of the Lebanese parliament Nabih Beri to discuss the sufferings of their fellow villagers who have been under siege for over three weeks.

The delegation pointed out to the president that the Israelis have abducted tens of the their fellow villagers stressing that most of them are under fourteen years of age. The Israelis also vandalize homes, fire live bullets in the air in order to frighten women and children, they also do not allow farmers to go to work in order for them to get their daily bread, while threatening villagers that they will destroy their homes.
