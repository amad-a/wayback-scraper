Daily Report
 
 
August Thursday 12-1999:

"Israeli" jets bomb wadis in Beqaa(1)
""Israeli" warplanes dropped bombs into wadis in the western Beqaa".
"The jets fired eight missiles into the area. No casualties were reported. The resistance fired three missiles at the planes but missed".

However, the "Israeli" occupation troops launched the following aggressions (2):
00:10, four 155mm missiles hit Tasseh Well watercourse.
1:30, four 155mm missiles landed Majdal Selem town.
3:20, the west of Yater subjected to five 155mm missiles.
11:15, Mount Sojod was targeted by four 120mm shells.
12:35, six 155mm missiles slammed into the east of Majdal Zoun village.
14:25, Mount Zaatar was bombarded by nine 155mm missiles.
15:35, seven 155mm missiles were fired at Qaissieh Valley and the perimeters of Khorbet Selem town.
At 17:50 and 18:00, the "Israeli" fighter-bombers dropped eight air-to-ground rockets into Litani River watercourse - Zillaya village, but the anti-aircraft guns of the IR and Lebanese Army intercepted them.
Between 18:20 and 19:15, Aishieh area was targeted by three 155mm missiles and one 120mm shell igniting a blaze.
18:40, the Israeli war jets dropped four air-to-surface into Litani River watercourse.
19:10, one 120mm smoke shell hit the perimeters of Sawaneh village sparking a fire.
19:15, the area between Sawaneh and Majdal Selem exposed to one smoke shell.
20:05, Mount Zaatar was bombarded by eight 155mm missiles.
Between 20:20 and 20:40, eleven 155mm missiles slammed into Qaissieh Valley.
20:30, several 120mm shells were fired at the peripheries of Touline.
Between 21:00 and 23:00, Mount Sojod was pounded be eleven 120mm shells and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
23:15, the perimeters of Loweizeh came under five 120mm shells.
23:35, one 81mm round fell on the southern peripheries of Haddatha.

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Ali Nadim Hassan and Hussein Ali Haidar attacked, at 00:10, enemy movements at Baraasheit compound with the appropriate arms scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Hallal and Mohammad Abdel-Hassan Hattab targeted, at 8:35, an "Israeli" force at the hilltops of Rihan town with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Ali Saqr and Abdallah Hadi El-Asheq rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Mustafa Tleis and Mohammad Ahmad Najem attacked, fiercely, at 10:45, Sojod outpost chalking up direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Rafik Youssef and Haitham El-Bazzal in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted an "Israeli" convoy as it was entering Zimraya post. The mujahedeen were able to score direct goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Ali Hassan and Ali Ibrahim attacked, at 19:05, a collaborator force as it was beefing up Mohaisbat Hill. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militant were able to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Kammoun and Hassan Fahda targeted, at 22:50, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Bir Kallab outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades managed to score direct hits and prevent it from achieving its goals.

At the Same time, the IR unit of martyrs Ibrahim Taher and Hassan Abdel-Hussein rushed upon the same outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals whereas the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.
