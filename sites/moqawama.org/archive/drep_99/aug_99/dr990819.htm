Daily Report
 
 
August Thursday 19-1999:

Introduction
Relative peace and calm descended on south Lebanon Wednesday following a day of the heaviest fighting this year between the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants and the "Israeli" occupying troops which resulted in killing three "Israeli" soldiers and wounding six others.

In "Israel", the radio reported that the "Israeli" Religious Affairs Minister Yitzhak Cohen of the Sephardi-ultra Orthodox Shas party has called for an immediate withdrawal from Lebanon without further delay.

However, "Israel" has decided to increase the security in its embassies, consulates and official institutions worldwide after the assassination Monday of one of the IR senior commanders Ali Hassan Deeb.

In this regard, the Lebanese President Gen. Emile Lahoud was quoted as saying "the martyr sacrificed himself for all of Lebanon. The unity and strength of Lebanon increase with the fall of every martyr".

Occupation command on its painful defeat in Tuesday clashes(1)
"Relative calm prevailed in the western and mid sectors in south Lebanon following the violent clashes between the Islamic Resistance (IR) Mujahedeen and the "Israeli" occupation forces that resulted in the killing of three "Israeli" troops and wounding six others.

"The occupation command delayed the acknowledgment of its losses until last midnight "in order to study the situation and investigate the circumstances of the incident" according to the "Israeli" radio which said that the clashes started when a Golani patrol spotted a Hizbullah squad that was trying to infiltrate into the occupied zone to plant bombs. But the Hizbullah men were faster than the "Israeli" troops in opening fire and wounding three "Israelis". One "Israeli" soldier was killed during the ensuing chase.

"The enemy radio added that another Golani force rushed to the battlefield, but was intercepted by a Hizbullah ambush. Another "Israeli" soldier was killed during the clash and three others were wounded.

"The radio quoted Maj Gen Gabi Over as saying that the Hizbullah men were three. The occupation's so-called northern area commander Gabi Ashkenazi told the radio that the clashes went on for several hours in a tough terrain. The heavy fire exchange prevented the rescue troops from moving the casualties to the hospitals swiftly.

"Five Lebanese civilians were injured in the "Israeli" occupation's violent shelling of Lebanese villages and towns besides four air raids against the targeted areas".

"ISRAELI" MINISTER CALLS FOR IMMEDIATE WITHDRAWAL FROM Lebanon(2)
""Israeli" radio today (yesterday) reported that "Israeli" Religious Affairs Minister Yitzhak Cohen of the Sephardi ultra-Orthodox Shas party has called for an immediate withdrawal from Lebanon following deadly clashes between "Israel" and the Hizbullah resistance movement. Cohen said that "Israel" must withdraw unilaterally from Lebanon without further delay. "I think that "Israel" should withdraw from the Lebanon immediately. I am going to raise the issue of the immediate unilateral withdrawal in the cabinet meeting on Sunday," Cohen told the radio. "Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak said in response that he will withdraw "Israeli" forces from Lebanon as he promised, within a year after he took office on July 6, in a responsible manner and not in a haphazard manner, the radio said. A spokesman for Barak added that the issue of Lebanon was discussed by the government Wednesday, but she refused to give any substantive information. "Israel's" occupation of Lebanese land is one of the most contentious political issues in "Israel", especially after painful resistance attacks against enemy forces. Renewed opposition to the 21-year occupation of southern Lebanese surfaced when three "Israeli" occupation soldiers were killed in clashes with Hizbullah on Tuesday and seven soldiers were wounded. The toll is the highest in a single incident since eight "Israelis", including Brigadier General Erez Gerstein who was the highest-ranking officer in Lebanon, were killed in Hizbullah attacks in February. The clash came a day after Hizbullah leader Ali Dib, also known as Khodr Salame, was blown up in his car in Sidon, the main city of southern Lebanon. "Israel" welcomed the assassination Tuesday, without claiming the responsibility attributed to it by the Hizbullah. A senior "Israeli" military official was quoted by AFP on Wednesday that he was "100 percent certain" there was no connection between the assassination of Salame and the attack on "Israeli" forces. But Hizbullah is not buying "Israel's" denials. Blaming "Israel" for Salame's death, Hizbullah swore to be avenged on its leaders. The resistance fighters who attacked the "Israeli" patrol on Tuesday called themselves "the martyr Ali Dib" commando", security services in Lebanon said.

Condolences offered on Martyr Deeb, President Lahoud: what is happening in south is source of pride for all(3)
"Commander of the Republican Guards' Brigade Col Mustafa Hamdan represented President Gen Emile Lahoud in offering condolences on the martyrdom of Hajj Ali Deeb. Hamdan quoted the president as saying "the martyr sacrificed himself for all of Lebanon. The unity and strength of Lebanon increase with the fall of every martyr."

"Earlier, President Lahoud told visitors, "We are optimistic. What is happening in the south and the Western Beqaa, the heroic acts there, is a source of pride for us all; it also restores the dignity of the Arabs."

"A lot of delegations and personalities offered condolences at the Hizbullah Secretariat including Director General of the Army Intelligence Col Raymond Azar, Representative of Army Commander Col Wafiq Jezzini, Col Foad Sh'hade along with an army delegation, a delegation from Internal Security Forces' officers headed by the Deputy Commander of the Mount Lebanon area Col Roger Salem, a delegation from Hamas headed by the movement's representative in Lebanon Usama Hamdan, delegations from the PLO, the Tawheed Movement, the Moslem Ulama Association, and Ansarullah"

"ISRAEL" INCREASES SECURITY WORLDWIDE AFTER DEATH OF HIZBULLAH LEADER(4)
" "Israel" has decided to increase the security in its embassies, consulates and official institutions worldwide, following the assassination Monday of Khodor Salameh, a senior official of the Lebanese Hizbullah organization. Hizbullah has accused "Israel" of being behind the death of Salameh, and the "Israeli" Ma'ariv daily reported Wednesday that "Israeli" security officials believe some groups, including from Hizbullah, will try to take revenge on the Jewish state, either in south Lebanon or elsewhere in the world. "Israel" believes Islamists were behind the bombing of the "Israeli" Embassy in Argentina in March 1992, in revenge for an "Israeli" army attack which had killed top Hizbullah leader Abbas Musawai one month previously. Salameh was killed Monday when the car he was driving exploded in the southern port of Sidon. Hizbullah, which is the leading group that is resisting "Israeli" occupation of southern Lebanon, immediately accused "Israel" of responsibility and vowed to avenge the death of the official. "Israel" has yet to admit responsibility for the killing".

"Israeli" Aggressions(5)
The "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions:
00:35, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by eight 15mm missiles.
00:55, six 155mm missiles struck the area between Houla and Shaqra villages.
Between 1:00 and 3:50, Mount Sojod was targeted by eight 81mm rounds and six 120mm shells.
1:05, four 120mm flare shells were fired over the surroundings of Hardoun outpost.
5:05, Habboush river watercourse was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
5:35, the surroundings of Sojod outpost subjected to heavy machine gun fire.
6:40, six 120mm shells fell on the perimeters of Loweizeh village and four 155mm missiles on Mlita hilltops.
Between 7:20 and 7:40, the peripheries of Jibaa town exposed to three 120mm shells and one 155mm missile.
Between 11:40 and 12:15, Baraasheit town and its perimeters came under ten 81mm rounds, several 120mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets. The shells landed near the Unifil post and town's school building and houses damaging the citizen Najib Farhat's home.
20:40, three 120mm shells hit Oqmata area.
Between 22:20 and 22:50, Mazraat Hamra was bombarded by nine 155mm missiles.
23:20, one 155mm missile landed Sawan area.

"ISRAELI" ALLIED SOLDIER INJURED IN LEBANON RESISTANCE ATTACK
"A member of "Israel's" proxy militia was slightly injured Wednesday in a Lebanese guerrilla attack on the "Israeli"-occupied zone of south Lebanon. The soldier was injured when guerrillas of the Amal resistance movement fired rockets on a militia post in Baraasheit, in the central sector of the occupation zone. Amal said its guerrillas fired mortars, rocket-propelled grenades and machineguns at the position in a 20-minute clash with militiamen manning the post. It added an ambulance for the collaborators was seen evacuating a casualty. On Monday, an SLA militiaman was seriously injured by a sharpshooter belonging to the
Hizbullah movement in Sojod, at the edge of the central sector of the zone"(6)

For its part, The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyr Ali Kaoutharani observed, at 00:20, enemy movements at Slouqi Valley. Immediately afterwards, the IR unit fell upon them with the proper weapons scoring direct and certain hits.

Thirty minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Tarhini and Ibrahim Hariri rushed upon a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Sojod outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to prevent it from achieving its goals

Also, the IR group of martyrs Jamal Saleh and Youssef Doheini attacked, at 19:30, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported(7)
