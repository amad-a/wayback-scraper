Daily Report
 
 
August Sunday 15-1999:

15 collaborators flee, 3 detained by occupation  (1)
Reports from the south and western Beqaa areas that are occupied by the Israeli forces affirmed that a number of Lahd militiamen has recently fled the occupied zone.

Fifteen militiamen have been able to flee to France and Canada via the Zionist entity under the heading of "visits for rest and recreation", the reports said, adding that these militiamen never spoke about the possibility of their quitting the militia as they did not want the Israeli intelligence to find out about and foil the escape.

Most of those who fled managed to take their families with them, others left their houses that can be inhabited, the reports said.
On the other hand, three collaborator militiamen have been detained recently in the Shibaa area by the Israeli intelligence on charges of observing the movements of the occupation army and giving the information to the Resistance.

Reports said the three were among those who had repented following the Fatwa

Israeli aggressions  (2)

21:00, three 120 mm. shells hit Mount Sojod .
23:20 the eastern neighborhood of Kfar Tibneet exposed to a valley of 12.7 mm heavy machine gun bullets damaging several houses .
23:45 , one 120 mm. shell landed Arid El- Zennar area
23:55 , six 120 mm. shells struck the perimeters of Mlikh village .

IR operations against occupation forces (3)
The Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Assad Shabshoul and Mohammed Qsas at 12:00 Saturday attacked a Lahdi collaborators' force that was fortifying the Mhaisibat outpost.

The Mujahedeen used the appropriate rocket propelled grenades in the attack, scoring definite and direct hits. Smoke was seen billowing from the outpost.

Earlier, IR's squad of Martyrs Hussein Qassir and Rida Shmais at 23:10 Friday spotted enemy vehicles entering the outpost of Beir Kallab.

The Mujahedeen immediately attacked the force with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades scoring definite hits.

Moreover, IR's squad of Martyrs Adnan Qashmar and Mohammed Atieh at 21:30 Friday targeted other vehicles that were entering the Sojod outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring definite hits.
