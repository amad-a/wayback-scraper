 Israel prepares statistics of official employees in the occupied territory (1)
An official report stated that Israeli intelligence have recently commenced preparing a statistical report of all Lebanese official employees in the occupied area. In order to achieve this, the Israelis threatened the civilians who refuse to cooperate, with strict measures such as not allowing them to leave their towns into the liberated areas or even imprisonment in Al-Khiyam prison.

The statistics will concentrate on employees in the fields of water, electricity, communications, schoolteachers, police officers and officials of the courts.

LEBANESE WARRANT OFFICER ARRESTED FOR "CONTACTS WITH THE ENEMY (3)
A warrant officer of the Lebanese army has been arrested on charges of providing information to Israel, which helped it assassinate resistance leaders, officials said here Thursday. Hussein Ali Oleyyan "confessed to having contacted the enemy, and providing them with information about positions of the Lebanese army and of the Syrian army in Lebanese territory, as well as the sites of weapons belonging to resistance forces in southern Lebanon," the official ANI news agency said. He also gave the Israelis information about the homes and offices of some resistance officials, and details of the cars they used, it added. "This information enabled the Israeli enemy to assassinate them by using helicopters or with explosives planted on the roads they used," the agency said. It added that Oleyyan had admitted "meeting enemy officers in Israel, where he used to travel by plane via Greece or the Netherlands, and sometimes by crossing the (Israeli-) occupied zone" of southern Lebanon. He used wireless equipment given him by the Israelis to transmit the information, it said. His wife and mother have both been arrested for complicity, the agency added. It did not say when Oleyyan was arrested, nor did it say whether he was involved in the assassination of top Hezbollah official Ali Dib, who was killed August 16 when his car was blown up in the southern port city of Sidon. Hezbollah blamed Dib's death on Israel. Since June 1998 defection of Raja Ward, the Lebanese head of the Israeli security services in southern Lebanon, at least three "espionage networks" working for Israel, involving more than 200 people, have been broken up by the Lebanese authorities.
