August Friday 27-1999:

Introduction
Once more, the "Israeli" occupying troops breached the April Understanding by shelling the residential neighborhoods of Baraasheit town with various kinds of missiles. Several houses were damaged, but fortunately no casualties were reported.
Meanwhile, the Lebanese intelligence sources revealed that the number of fled collaborators has reached to 65 after recent statistics prepared by the official sources.

Elsewhere, the Lebanese military court sentenced 29 of the collaborators, who surrendered to the competent authorities, to prison terms ranging from six months to two years for collaboration with the "Israeli" enemy.

"At around ten oclock this (yesterday) morning the "Israeli" forces broke the April understanding by bombing with various caliber the town of Baraasheit. The bombing resulted in the damage of many houses, while the inhabitants were unharmed as the result of the steps that they undertake during such bombings like moving to the lower floors of their homes. "The "Israeli" bombings also reached the outskirts of the towns of Al-Mansouri, Majdal Zoun and Wadi Al-Qaissieh, which led to the breaking out of fires in many farms.

"As their usual tyrannical practice the "Israelis" prevented cars belonging to the International Red Cross to enter the town of Yohmor Al-Shkif to transport a seriously ill civilian to the nearest hospital. "The enemy informed the inhabitants that this practice would remain so until further notice, noting that this step violates the Geneva Convention".

65 militia collaborators fled(3)
"The steps taken by the "Israelis" to stop the fleeing of its collaborators have failed. Intelligence sources revealed that the number has reached 65, following recent statistics prepared by official Lebanese sources. The collaborators gave themselves in to Lebanese authorities or to the Islamic "Resistance following the recent "Israeli" withdrawal from Jezzine.

"And according to the following up sources that Ehud Barraks promise to the collaborators of keeping them in mind in any deal for withdrawal have failed. The reason being that the Lebanese government has refused to discuss this matter unless through the Lebanese courts.

"The sources also added that the collaborators who have given themselves in have noticed that the courts have been lenient so long as they did not shed any Lebanese blood".

Another 29 surrendered collaborators sentenced(4)
"A military court sentenced 29 former pro-"Israeli" militiamen Thursday to prison terms ranging from six months to two years for collaboration with the enemy.

"The sentences raised to 92 the number of former South Lebanon Army* member who have stood trial since "Israel" withdrew from the Jezzine area in June.

"They are among 220 militiamen who voluntarily surrendered to the authorities after the withdrawal, preferring to stay in their hometowns rather than retreat with the SLA to new positions in the "Israeli"-occupied zone further south.

"Of the 92 militiamen put on trial, 83 have received jail terms of three months to two years. Another nine have been acquitted or
had their cases dropped because they were elderly.

"The cases are heard by the Military Tribunal, which holds weekly sessions in Beirut under Brigadier General Maher Safieddine, the presiding judge.

"The trials have usually opened on a Wednesday afternoon and ended around midnight with the handing down of verdicts and sentences.

"Relatives of the militiamen have complained that the sentences are too harsh.

Defense lawyers have argued that the defendants were victims of circumstance, not traitors, who joined the militia under duress or financial straits.

"But the sentences imposed on the convicts have been considerably more lenient than the seven-year sentences proposed by the indictments.

"Meanwhile, a Lebanese soldier and a former bodyguard of Speaker Nabih Berri was accused of spying for "Israel" along with members of his family.

"First Adjutant Hussein Alayyan, who was arrested at his home in the South on Aug. 9; his mother Khadijeh Hussein Mroue; and his wife Rola Ali Hassan, are in custody.

His brother Hassan; father Ali Mustafa Alayyan; and a friend, Youssef Siblini, are still being sought by the authorities.
"They have all been accused of contacting "Israel's" Mossad foreign intelligence service and providing information on the activities of resistance guerrillas, as well as the Lebanese and Syrian arm.

For its part, Amal Movement attacked, at 8:10 and 9:30, the positions of Taibeh and Baraasheit respectively.
