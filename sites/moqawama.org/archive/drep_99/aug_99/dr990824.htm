The town of Al-Mari under siege (3)
The correspondence of the Al-Diyar daily newspaper reported through civilians coming from Hasbaya that the town of Al-Mari has been under siege for more than a weak by Israeli forces. The Israelis prevent the inhabitants from leaving or entering the town except those who are transported by the International Red Cross for serious health problems.

The families of the town called upon all to put an end to the siege of their town.

The Israeli forces resumes its policy of expelling (4)
The Israeli forces resumed its policy of expelling innocent civilians from their homes. While the committee of the April understanding was meeting yesterday afternoon it expelled the citizen Fadi Kamal Al-Kalut (25yrs) his wife Jinan Nasserdinne Dallah (21yrs) and their son Ali (5months) from their town of Al-Khiyam through the crossing point of Kfartibneet. Al-Kalut explained that the collaborators lead him and his family to the crossing point and warned them not to return to their town without giving any explanations. The Lebanese army transported Al-Kalut and his family from the crossing point to the Nabatiyeh police station and from there to their relatives in the city of Sidon. The citizen Al-Kalut mentioned that he worked as an ordinary citizen in a bakery in his village of Al-Khiyam and that he had intended to open his private bakery at the end of this month.
