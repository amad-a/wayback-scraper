Daily Report
 
 
August Saturday 7-1999:

Occupation forces violated understanding again(1)
Israeli occupation forces have once again resorted to their attempts to pressurize Lebanon by violating the April Understanding and deliberately targeting civilians, as Israeli rounds last night rained down the village of Zawtar al-Gharbieh.
"A 155 mm round hit the house of Hussein Ouleiq (in the village's western neighborhood).

UN Interim forces in Lebanon (UNIFIL) has repeatedly affirmed that only the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators use this type of shells.

"Ouleiq and his family narrowly escaped death, for they had left the house minutes before it was hit.

Israeli artillery this morning fired-scores of 155 mm and 120 mm rounds on the villages of Nabatieh and Iqleem al-Toffah, especially Wadi al-Hojeir, al-Ghandourieh, Oqmata and the Sojod Hilltops.

"Meanwhile, Israeli MK espionage drones hovered over southern areas, while the occupation outposts overlooking Nabatieh combed their surroundings with heavy machine gun fire".

Lebanon moves to stop siege of towns(2)
"Lebanese diplomacy has managed- through contacts with regional and world capitals - to lift the siege imposed by Israeli occupation forces on the villages of Aita al-Shaab, Blida, and Bint Jbeil.

"UNIFIL troops' efforts to distribute food supplies in the towns were blocked by the occupation's militia.

"The insistence of the international force, however, forced the agents to retreat and allow the UNIFIL to enter the villages that are still partially under siege.

"Israeli occupation forces are trying to benefit from the clampdown they are imposing on the towns, Lebanese security sources said.

"The occupation has told their so-called civil administration apparatus in the Hasbaya - Arqoub area to conduct accurate records of all the mobile phones and their owners, under the heading of "regulations and administration", the sources added.

"The occupation's step is in fact a prelude to confiscate the phones in case of any new siege, to prevent any leakage of information about enemy movements or the deteriorating morale of the occupation's militia, the sources concluded.

"The occupation command warned, through its "civil administration, against concealing any mobile phones, otherwise, the phones will be confiscated and their owners punished. It also demanded detailed information from the phone stores about the sales and the names of the customers".

21:45, the surroundings of Qabaa position subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
22:25, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by seven 155mm missiles.
22:45, four 155mm missiles hit the peripheries of Khorbet Selem.
21:10, one 155mm missile landed Hojeir Valley.
23:30, the perimeters of Mlikh came under four 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) group of martyrs Hussein Haidar Khazaal and Issa Abdel-Rahim Khazaal attacked swiftly, at 22:05, the Israeli post of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.
At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Youssef Ahmad El-Husseini and Youssef Haidar Ahmad targeted Salaah position with the proper arms. Direct goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.
