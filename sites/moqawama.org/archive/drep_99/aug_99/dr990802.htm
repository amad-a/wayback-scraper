Daily Report
 
 
August Monday 2-1999:

A Zionist soldier and two of their collaborators hit by the Islamic Resistances operations
A reporter of Al-Safir newspaper announced the wounding of a member of the Israeli army and two of its collaborators on the dawn of Saturday morning. This occurred during attacks executed by the Islamic Resistance on a number of military outposts along the front line of the eastern sector.
The collaborators radio station mentioned the incident adding that the wounded Israeli soldier was lifted by helicopter from the vicinity of Al-Shkif castle where the mortar exploded to Rambam hospital in occupied Palestine. The radio station claimed that the two collaborators wounds were mild and were treated in their posts.

A Zionist military delegation inspects the situation in the security zone
Reports from inside the security zone stated that a delegation of Israeli military personnel inspected early today Israeli troops at Talat Al-Sharika east of the city of Al-Khiyam. The delegation met with commanders of the post to discuss the military situation there following a shoot out. The exchange of fire occurred a few days earlier between a group of Israeli soldiers and their collaborators in the vicinity of the post. The incident led to the death of a collaborator with three wounded as well as the wounding of an Israeli officer.

New verdicts against the collaborators
The permanent military court headed by brigadier general Maher Safiedinne issued the following severe verdicts against individuals who collaborated with the Israeli enemy:
1)  15 years hard labor in absentia against Ahmed Hussein Abed Al-Latif (known as Ahmed Tanous), Mohamed Saadallah Al-Abdullah, Mohamed Hassan Al-Abdullah and Mohamed Jawdat Al-Abdullah for collaborating with the enemys intelligence.
2)  15 years hard labor in absentia against the intelligence officer of the central sector Ahmed Abed Al-Jalil Sheet (born at Kfar Kila 1944, mothers name, Abda Al-Zahraa) and Fadllalah Ahmed Suliaman for communicating with the enemy, collaborating with them and for passing on secret intelligence information.
Two years imprisonment in absentia plus a fine of 2m Lebanese pounds each, against Rafik Kassem Harajli Ahmed Hassan Fayad and Ali Kamal Fayad for entering occupied Palestine without permission.

20 years hard labor in absentia against Kheir Mkhayel Mansour (born Ras Baalbak 1954, mothers name, Saraya) for collaborating with the enemys collaborators and for using a forged ID card under the name of Yousuf Isber Azar.

Verdicts against the collaborators of the town Jezzine
Last Wednesday the permanent military court issued new verdicts on 17 collaborators with Israel who gave themselves in to the courts after the withdrawal from Jezzine as follows:
One year nine months imprisonment against the detained Tanous Elias Tanous (born at Anan 1958, mothers name Rose) for collaborating with the enemy and joining the militia in 1997 reaching the position of major with his duty being the assistant to the commander of the 20th regiment Emil Nasr. Judge Joseph Maamari commented on Tanouss remarks on suffering from weak hearing by saying, he cannot hear well due to all the bombardment he executed towards the liberated peaceful areas. He was responsible for a while of the eavesdropping room in the Jezzine barracks.
One year imprisonment against the collaborators Shadi Elias Mansour (born at Saida 1977, mothers name, Sayeda), his brother Wissam Elias Mansour (born at Anan 1978, mothers name, Sayeda) and George Yousuf Mkheybir (born at Beirut 1963, mothers name Salima) for collaborating with the enemy. Wissam confessed that he was injured during an operation by the Islamic Resistance and was taken to Haifa hospital for treatment. As for Mkheybir who was a sergeant in the militia, he served from 1989 till 1998 at various cannon emplacements. Fadi Mansour joined the enemys militia in 1995 and under went training at Al-Mojaydiya camp and was transferred to serve at several military centers such as Saidoun, he left the militia two months prior to the withdrawal from Jezzine.
One year imprisonment for each of the following: Rony Said Aoun (born at Al-Hilaliya 1974, mothers name Sayeda), George Elias Sulaiman (born at Beirut 1958, mothers name Aziza), Elias Maroun Hana (born at Ein Al-Mir 1952), Tanyous Jerjis Aoun (born at Al-Hilaliya 1973, mothers name Mary), Hassib Eid Abdo (born at Ein Majdalin 1966, mothers name Zubeida), George Eid Aoun (born at Al-Hilaliya 1971, mothers name Hadla), Shirbil Halim Musa (born at Ein majdalin 1973, mothers name Terez), Alber Abdullah Salim (born at Al-Miye Miye 1972, mothers name Josephene), Yusouf Maroun Bu Sader (born at Saida 1966, mothers name Zahiya), Nabil Aoun Aoun (born at Jezzine 1970, mothers name Sayeda), all for collaborating with the enemy.
One year two months imprisonment against Rober Elias Abu Rashed (born at Iblih 1963, mothers name Samira), a verdict of innocence for Naim Yusouf Bu Nader (born at Jezzine 1972, mothers name Sabah).
The court also ordered the end of the Pursuit against Joseph Elias Sulaiman who turned out to be working in favor of the Lebanese army intelligence since 1992, he was the driver of the previous commander of the 20th regiment Emile Nasr, he was injured with him in a road bomb planted by the resistance.
One year imprisonment against the detained Paul Sharbil Yunous (born at Mrjaoun, mothers name Marina) for communicating with the enemys collaborators and working in their broadcasting organizations.
