Daily Report
 
 
August Monday 23-1999:

Introduction:
The Islamic Resistance delivered a new blow to the Zionists by executing heroic operations during the past two days in the South and in the West Bekaa. The Israelis were forced to admit of two casualties one of which being critical.

At the same time Hezbullahs Secretary General Sayed Hassan Nassrallah assured that the operations against the Zionist enemy could continue.

Islamic Resistance operations (1)
01:40 the group of the martyrs Ali Ismail Oliek and Hussien Kamal Hayek in the Islamic Resistance attacked Al-Qalaa Israeli post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits.

At the same time the group of the martyrs Yusuf Abdel Karim Yassin and Musa Habib Fahes were attacking military movements outside the Al-Qalaa post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits.

06:10 the group of the martyrs Samir Akil and Adnan Kashmar in the Islamic Resistance attacked military movements inside the Al-Shkif post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits.

Remains of Israels war machine injures a civilian (3)
A heat balloon, which Israel uses continuously over the skies of Lebanon, landed nearby the civilian Mohamed Hussien Shalhub a shepherd from the town of Kilya in the Bikaa. The fire caused by the balloon killed a number of Mohameds herds while he sustained serious burn injuries to his hands, feet and face.

Israel uses heat balloons to avoid being hit by the Lebanese army and the Islamic Resistances anti air firing.
