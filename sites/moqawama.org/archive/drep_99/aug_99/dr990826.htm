Daily Report
 
 
August Thursday 26-1999:

Hizbullahs Secretary General in a series of receptions..(1)
"The political situation in the area and in Lebanon especially the South and the West Beqaa under the "Israeli" aggressions were discussed today (yesterday) by Hizbullahs Secretary General Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah and his visitors. Among the visitors was the Minister of Information Anwar Al-Khalil. Mr. Khalil said after the meeting that: under the leadership of President Emil Lahoud, and Prime Minister Salim Al-Hus who confirm the role of the Resistance as being the foundation does not part at all with the out look of the government. The Minister also added that the support of the resistance will remain great, and that this support will surely increase confidence in the role of Lebanon in the future, reassuring that liberation will come God willing".

"ISRAELI" JETS ATTACK EDGE OF SOUTH LEBANON VILLAGE(2)
""Israeli" warplanes attacked the edges of a village close to "Israel's" south Lebanon occupation zone for the second consecutive day. Reports said two jets fired 16 rockets at a valley near Zibqeen southeast of the port city of Tyre. The jets raided the same valley on Tuesday. There were no apparent casualties from the air attacks, almost a daily occurrence in a war of attrition between the Hizbullah resistance movement and "Israeli" occupation forces. Hizbullah spearheads the resistance war. "Israel" has occupied different parts of Lebanon since 1978 and has refused to implement UN resolution 425 which calls on the Jewish state to unconditionally withdraw from Lebanese territory".

However, the "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese liberated areas as the Islamic Resistance statement reported (3):

1:20, three 120mm shells hit the deserted Lebanese Army barracks, whereas the surroundings of Dabsheh post exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

The occupation forces impose curfew inside the towns that are under siege(4)
"Weeks and months have passed on the siege that was imposed by the "Israeli" forces on a number of towns in the South and the West Beqaa. Sources from inside the occupied territory say that the "Israelis" have yet again changed the rules to others that are stricter on the civilians. The "Israeli" forces first allowed citizens over the age of 40 years for men and the over 50 for women to leave their towns only for serious health reasons. It then imposed a night curfew on the inhabitants with orders to shoot at the violators".

"The siege of the town of Al-Mari is in its fifth day. It was noted the reason of the siege was because of the fleeing of one of the inhabitants of the town Wassim Fares Judiyeh to the liberated side after refusing to collaborate with the "Israelis". Mr. Judiyeh fled 24 hours before a meeting that was set for him by "Israeli" intelligence officers at Marjeyoun in order to try and force him to collaborate with them".

Military Operations(5)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Ali Olleik and Habib Tarhini attacked the "Israeli" position of Dabsheh with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits. The smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Ali Chahla and Rida Mohammad Shmeis targeted, at 11:00, a collaborator engineering force as it was fortifying Jamoussa post of the 80th Brigade Command. Using the suitable arms, the militants were able to score direct and certain hits and prevent it from achieving its goals.

Two hours later, the IR unit of martyrs Nimr Ali Hemayed and Mohammad Mustafa Kassas reiterated targeting the collaborator engineering force which was beefing up Jamoussa post. The mujahedeen, who used the proper arms, managed to chalk up direct and accurate hits.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Nasri Tleis and Bilal Ali Bahmad attacked, at 18:25, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Thirty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Hassan Ali Al-Sahli and Hassan Ahmad Jaber rushed upon enemy movements inside Blat post. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to chalk up direct and accurate goals.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Saleh Mohammad Harb and Mustafa Mohammad Karaki targeted, at 19:40, Blat post with the adequate weapons scoring accurate goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Mohsen Hussein Zreik and Assaad Mohammad Shabshoul attacked, at 22:20, Beaufort Castle with the suitable arms chalking up direct hits.
