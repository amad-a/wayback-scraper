Daily Report
 
 
August Tuesday 17-1999:

"Israel" assassinated an IR heroe(1)
Hizbullah: The crime will not go unpunished
Yesterday, "Israel" added a new crime to its record which is fraught of massacres and crimes. Khodor Salameh, one of the Islamic Resistance heroes, was martyred when the car he was driving exploded in the southern port city of Saida.

The Daily Star said that "the bombing, coming days after a military officer attached to Parliaments security was arrested in Zrarieh on suspicion of spying for "Israel", prompted soldiers and Internal Security Forces personnel to set up checkpoints throughout Sidon.

"Hizbullah accused "Israel" of being behind the killing and vowed revenge.

"Ali Hassan Deeb, known as Abu Hassan, was killed instantly when two roadside bombs exploded simultaneously at 5.20pm on the northern side of the road between Hilalieh and Abra on the eastern edge of Sidon. His BMW saloon was blown to pieces, with the bulk of the vehicle hurled 20 meters down the road. The blast ignited the tinder-dry undergrowth on the side of the road.

"The explosion cast a huge pall of smoke into the air and the sound of the detonation startled residents throughout Sidon, with many hurrying into the streets to find out what had happened.

Civil Defense rescuers and firefighters rushed to the scene of the blast and extinguished the blazes. "Deebs body was taken to the Hammoud Hospital in Sidon. Troops and ISF personnel cordoned off the area and checked the identities of passing motorists. Searches of nearby rooftops were also ordered on the chance that the bombs were detonated by someone on the ground.

"Security sources said that the bombs were standard roadside charges, consisting of high explosives packed with ball bearings which act as shrapnel.

"As far as Hizbullah was concerned, there appeared to be no doubt that the assassination was carried out by the "Israelis", their first such operation in Lebanon since Ehud Baraks rise to power in May. The groups southern commander, Sheikh Nabil Qaouk, described Barak as the No. 1 terrorist.

This crime will not go unpunished and "Israel" will regret its crime, he said speaking at the scene of the attack.

"In a statement, Hizbullah said: Abu Hassan joined the Islamic Resistance when it started in 1982 and spent a life full of jihad and sacrifice and martyrdom for God and in fighting the Zionist enemy during all the phases of resistance until his own martyrdom.

"He survived a number of assassination attempts, most notably when an "Israeli" helicopter gunship targeted his car with a missile during the April war in 1996. Deeb was not in the car at the time but a male relative and a woman were killed. He was married with five children and was born in Kounine near Bint Jbeil in the occupation zone.

"The attack bore the hallmarks of an "Israeli" operation. Although roadside bombs are the favorite ­ and deadliest ­ weapon of the resistance, the "Israelis" also use them, usually planting the charges along known infiltration routes used by guerrillas into the occupation zone.

"The most telling evidence that indicated "Israeli" involvement in Mondays blast were the pilotless reconnaissance planes drones that were seen in the skies above Sidon both before and after the blast.

"The "Israelis" have used drones in the past to relay radio signals which detonate roadside bombs. The most notable previous incident was in August 1997, when a drone triggered a triple roadside bomb blast in the village of Kfour near Nabatieh in which five Hizbullah guerrillas were killed.

"The operator back in "Israel" would have seen Deebs car approaching the ambush spot via the television camera mounted beneath the drone. As the car passed the two bombs, the operator would have sent a radio signal via the drone to detonate the charges".

"In northern "Israel", troops were on alert, according to security sources there. But the military did not order residents to seek shelter ­ the normal practice when cross-border attacks are anticipated".

MP Raad: the US is hindering international institutions(2)
"Head of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad accused the US administration of obstructing international institutions and resolutions besides ignoring all international and human laws and conventions.

"The US is trying to control the oppressed and developing world because it feels there is no one to see what it is doing or to hold it accountable, MP Raad said.

"Speaking at a rally, Raad said, "The US intervenes and mediates when this is within its interests, and launches terrorist operations when it is its interest to do so. It adopts all methods and means to serve this interest, that is to impose its spearhead, the Zionist entity in the region."

"Washington is trying to market the Arab recognition of ""Israel"", and to snatch Arab legitimization of the Zionists' occupation of Palestine and al-Quds (Jerusalem) in order to enable the Zionists and the Americans to extend their control over the Arab World".

One "Israeli" collaborator wounded in clash(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyr Ali Al-Bostani and Moussa Jaber attacked, at 12:45, a collaborator engineering force near Mohaisbat position. The mujahedeen managed to strike directly a military vehicle.

Two hours later, the IR squad of martyrs Suleiman Yehia and Ali Chokair rushed upon enemy movements at Taibeh post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.

Furthermore the IR group of martyrs Rida Hammoudi and Haitham Sobhi Dbouk attacked, at 19:30, a collaborator engineering force as it was reinforcing Hemayed position. Using the suitable weapons, the militants were able to score direct hits.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 23:10, enemy movements near Mohaisabat position. Immediately afterwards, the IR unit of martyrs Kassem Khreis and Jihad Annani fell upon them with the proper arms chalking up direct goals.
