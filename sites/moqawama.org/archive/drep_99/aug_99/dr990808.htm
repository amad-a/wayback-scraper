Daily Report
 
 
August Sunday 8-1999:

Clampdown of Bint Jbeil and Aita al-Shaab continues; militiamen loot properties(1)
"Although the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators had confiscated the mobile phones in the towns they are besieging, information is still coming from the occupied zone in south Lebanon.

"Reports from the town of Bint Jbeil which has been under siege for two weeks said occupation troops and their collaborators have intensified their clampdown and adopted more revengeful measures against the steadfast people in the town including:

- Damaging food and medical supplies inside the houses they storm, especially flour, besides stealing light but valuable properties.

- Blocking water from reaching the towns of Bint Jbeil and Aita al-Shaab..

- Intensifying storming and arrest campaigns. Some people, including women and elderly, were arrested repeatedly in a bid to force them to cooperate with the occupation.

"According to the reports, agents Aql Hashem, Fawzi al-Saghir, and Ali Khalil Haidar - in cooperation with an "Israeli" occupation force - yesterday demolished the house of Adnan Bazzi after detaining his son Dani and taking him to al-Khiam detention center 15 days ago".

Amnesty International moves to free detainees in besieged Aita al-Shaab(2)
"Amnesty International has urged the enemy prime Minister Ehud Barak to immediately release five Lebanese citizens detained from the besieged town Aita al-haab.

"We would like to inform you that the "Israeli" occupation forces and its auxiliary militia has put the town of Aita Shaab under siege since July 15, 1999. The "Israeli" intelligence has ransacked the homes of the residents and horrified the citizens, especially that searching houses takes place during the night. At least 75 people has been interrogated since July 15, most of whom were juveniles, women and old people.

"The "Israeli" intelligence has confiscated mobile phones. "Israeli" forces has also threatened citizens of demolishing their houses as "Israeli" trucks were seen in the town.

"The towns siege has led to a shortage of nutritious matter. In addition, the "Israeli"-backed militia has cut electricity and water supplies to the town as a collective punishment. The citizens of the town are accused by "Israel" and its auxiliary militia of providing information to the Lebanese army and the resistance about the activities of the "Israeli" occupation forces.

"We were acknowledged that the following were taken to the Khiam camp: 1 - Ali Mohammed Khalil Srour (20 years old), university student. 2 - Samih Srour (42), a policeman. 3 - Abbas Samih Srour (16), student. 4 - Hassn Moussa Rida (15), student. 5 - Mohammed Ahmed Jamil (25), teacher. 6 - Mohamed Moussa Rida (27), worker. 7 - Mohammed Nehmeh Rida (15).

""Israel" is detaining another three citizens from Aita Shaab in the Khiam camp. They are the following: - Nehmeh Hussein Dakdouk, detained since 1992. - Mohammed Ahmed Haris, detained since 1997. - Mohammed Sayyed Mohammed, detained since 1998.

"Five Aita Shaab citizens are held in Ayalon prison inside "Israel" since April 15, 1987. Although they have finished their sentences 10 years ago, "Israel" has not released them yet as it regards them hostages for bargain. They are the following: - The three brothers: Abdel Hassan, Abbas and Ahmed Hassan Srour. - Yussef Yaqoub Srour. - Hussein Fahd Dakdouk.

"Such a siege of a town and its civilian residents is a grave breach of the Fourth Geneva Convention. Besides, the detention of juveniles in the Khiam camp and horrifying civilians violate international human rights conventions.

"We demand the United Nations and concerned international organizations to take action to free Aita Shaab and to put pressure on "Israel" until all detainees are released from the Khiam camp."

July: 130 "Israeli" aggressions; Resistance retaliates with 12 operations(3)
""Israeli" occupation forces staged 130 air, sea, and ground aggressions on Lebanese territories during July 1999. Air aggressions constituted 20.76% of the gross aggressions (10 raids).

"These assaults resulted in wounding two civilians, detaining 30 Lebanese people, expelling 19 people, damaging 15 houses, two cars and a mosque in the bombardment that targeted 28 villages and scores of fields and groves in villages adjacent to the occupied zone.

"On the other hand, the resistance retaliated with 112 operations against the occupation forces. The enemy acknowledged that one occupation soldier was killed and three others were wounded, while two militiamen were killed and three others were wounded during the month".

Military Operations(5)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) group of martyrs Bilal Mustafa Hemadi and Mohammad Hassan Jaber attacked, at 15:40, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 20:45, a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was traveling on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan. Shortly afterwards, the IR unit of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Hassan and Ahmad Trabolssi targeted it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite hits.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Ghassan Hassan Kobeissi and Hussein Mohammad Haidar attacked, at 23:00, an engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able score direct hits and prevent it from achieving its goals.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Issam Kamel Kansou and Bassam Akanan targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported.
