Daily Report
 
 
August Friday 13-1999:

Occupation acknowledges besieging towns in south Lebanon(1)
""Israeli" occupation army has acknowledged detaining a large number of people from the towns of Bint Jbeil and Aita al-Shaab during the siege it has been imposing on the towns.

"A spokesperson for the occupation army claimed that the detainees were "suspects" as "agents for Hizbullah" and that they have admitted to having supplied the party with information that it has used in launching attacks against the "Israeli" army and its militia.

"The spokesman added that weapons and explosives were found during the search the occupation had conducted in the towns.
"Meanwhile, the UN is exerting efforts to lift the siege which is imposed on the Lebanese towns in the area occupied by the "Israeli" forces.

"Lebanon's Representative at the UN Samir Mubarak said international officials have made contacts with the enemy government to put an end to the siege, release the detainees, and allow food and medical supplies to enter the besieged towns".

Monitoring Group blames "Israel"(2)
"The five-nation Monitoring Group convened in Naqoura to hear 11 complaints, seven filed by Lebanon and four by "Israel".
""Israel" was judged to have violated the April Understanding by wounding a civilian and damaging houses, a telephone station and the electricity network during an artillery bombardment of Mansouri and Majdal Zoun on Aug. 3. "Other "Israeli" violations included damaging houses in Yohmor with flechette tank rounds and rocket-propelled grenades on Aug. 7, damaging a house and a car in Baraasheit on Aug. 10 and damaging a house in Majdal Zoun in an helicopter attack on Aug. 11.

Regarding an "Israeli" complaint over the death of a civilian in a roadside bomb attack on Aug. 4, Lebanons delegate said that the victim had been delivering material to an "Israeli" outpost and therefore could not be considered a civilian target.

"Although the group agreed that the employment of civilians in military sites puts them at risk, the attack was automatically judged a violation because a civilian had died".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupying troops launched the following "Israeli" aggressions:
10:15, three tank rounds hit Arid El-Zennar area.
10:25, Hamra area was shelled by two 155mm missiles.
15:00, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by nine 155mm missiles.
Between 18:50 and 20:30, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by nineteen 155mm missiles.
20:05, two 155mm missiles hit the perimeters of Khorbet Selem.
20:15, the peripheries of Hamra area subjected to three 155mm missiles.
Between 21:10 and 23:35, Mount Sojod was targeted by five 120mm shells.
23:35, two 81mm rounds struck the perimeters of Loweizeh village, whereas the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Najah Mahdi Amhaz and Hussein Ali Krayem attacked, at 14:55, an "Israeli" force as it was beefing up Taibeh position. Using the adequate arms, the militants were able to score direct and certain hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Moussa Habib Fahs and Youssef Abdel-Karim Yassine targeted, at 19:50, the "Israeli" post of Izzeih striking its defenses and chalking up direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR men spotted, at 23:40, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Kassem Jamil Kaoun and Mohammad Atieh attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported.

ICRC ORGANIZES FAMILY VISITS TO KHIAM DETENTION CENTER(5)
"The International Committee of the Red Cross today (yesterday) took the families of six Lebanese men detained by "Israeli" occupation forces to the Khiam detention center. The ICRC organizes regular visits to the prison in occupied southern Lebanon, where "Israel" detains some 125 Lebanese without trial or charges. Another forty Lebanese are also detained inside the Jewish state. "Israel's" Supreme Court has justified holding some of the Lebanese as hostages to be used as bargaining chips. The follow-up committee for the release of detainees today appealed to the chairman of Amnesty International to pressure "Israel" to stop torturing Lebanese detainees. They also called on him to help lift "Israel's" month-long blockade on several villages in southern Lebanon".
