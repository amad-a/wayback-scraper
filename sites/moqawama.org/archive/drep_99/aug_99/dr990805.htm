Daily Report
 
 
August Thursday 5-1999:

Introduction
The "Israeli" occupation troops pounded heavily several areas north of the occupied zone with mortars and artillery, while the "Israeli" warplanes targeted the valleys and a road with air-to-surface missiles. The shells wounded seriously a Lebanese Army soldier and causing damage to several houses.

In response to these aggressions, the Islamic Resistance (IR) men staged a series of military operations at a number of the "Israeli" and Lahdi positions. The Lebanese police said that an "Israeli"-backed militiaman was killed by a roadside bomb detonated by the IR men.

"Israeli" targets Lebanese army and civilians in new violation
"Few hours after condemnation, the "Israeli" occupation forces reviolated the April Understanding by targeting Lebanese civilians in their villages in south Lebanon.

"The "Israeli" artillery breached the understanding when a bomb struck the main coastal road near al-Mansouri, wounding Lebanese soldier Faysal al-Shayeb, 20, who was rushed to the hospital for treatment.

"The "Israeli" bombs also struck the towns of al-Qlaileh al-Mansouri and Majdal Zoun where several houses got damaged.
"The houses belong to Samir Abu Khalil, Nabil Mhaidli, Ali Fayad, Ahmed Jaber, Rabih Khashab, Mohamad al-Dor, Hussein al-Dor, Awad Kassab, Badih Abu Khalil, Abdul Karim Hassan, Fouad Shams and Ibrahim Khalil Salman.

"In addition, several cars were damaged, among them the car of Badih Abu Khalil.

"This violation came shortly after the "Israeli" warning to the Lebanese army for taking a "neutral" position from what is going on in south Lebanon between the "Israeli" enemy and the Islamic Resistance to liberate the Lebanese land from the "Israeli" occupation.

"The "Israeli" occupation's warplanes this morning (Wednesday) raided the outskirts of Zibqeen and the hills between Zibqeen and Jibal al-Botom, launching 14 air-to-ground missiles that burned nearby orchards and fields, in a new violation of the April Understanging.

"Earlier, the "Israeli" artillery fiercely bombarded the outskirts of Majdal Zoun, Zibqeen, Bouyout al-Siyyed, al-Qlaileh, al-Mansouri, al-Chaitieh and al-Henneih,

"For their parts, the "Israeli" gunboats this morning opened their fire against the coastal road between Ras al-Ain and al-Qlaileh, while the "Israeli" warplanes intensively hovered over the Tyre district, staging mock raids.

"Moreover, the "Israeli" occupation forces closed the Hamra crossing, linking the occupied zone to the liberated areas"(1)

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Hassan and Ahmad Haroun attacked, at 00:30, enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Also, the IR unit of martyr Nasser Hassan Mansour targeted, at 3:00, "Israeli" military vehicles at the entrance of Radar post burning a number of them.

At the same time, the IR group of martyr Abdel-Hassan Izzeddine attacked Radar post with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying a number of its fortification.

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyr Hussein Abdallah Fawaz attacked Hardoun position, whereas the IR team of martyr Mohammad El-Zein rushed upon Shamaa outpost. In the meantime, the IR unit of martyr Ahmad Abdallah Khalifeh was attacking Bayada position.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyr Hassan Ali El-Sahli targeted, at 3:15, enemy groups near Hardoun outpost chalking up direct goals.

At the same time, the IR team of martyr Hassan Mahmoud Krayem reiterated targeting fiercely Radar post destroying its fortifications where several blazes were seen sparking for more than one hour.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Rida Khreis and Youssef Khalifeh attacked, at 6:30, Tair Harfa outpost with the proper weapons. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and certain hits.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Rida and Hussein Trabolssi targeted violently, at 7:45, Bayada post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking most of its defenses.

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Nidal El-Hoq and Raad Fares attacked the same post scoring direct goals.

Twenty minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Ali Chahla and Mohammad Ali Farhat fell upon Hardoun outpost with the machine guns chalking up direct and certain goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Youssef Mahmoud Olleik and Hussein Ali Abdallah attacked, at 13:00, a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Rshaf position with the adequate arms. The militants managed to score direct goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Abdel-Amir Fadel and Hussein Mosheimesh attacked, at 23:05, a Lahdi engineering force as it was reinforcing Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to score direct hits and prevent the enemy force from achieving its goals.

"Israeli" collaborator dead(4)
"The "Israeli" and collaborators Radio stations today (Wednesday) admitted that collaborator Ahmed Kamal Youssef died in a roadside bomb attack on the Bayada - Radar road inside the occupied zone in south Lebanon.

"The two stations said the dead militiamen was driving a truck full of cement inside an "Israeli" occupation outpost.

"The "Israeli" army, lately, build new ramparts and bomb shelters inside its outpost to avoid the Islamic Resistance's daily attacks against several "Israeli" occupation outpost".
