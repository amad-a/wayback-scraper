Daily Report
 
 
January Tuesday 19-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions:
Yesterday, the "Israeli" occupation troops launched the following aggressions:
00:55, two 120mm shells landed the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
1:20, the peripheries of Mlikh came under five 81mm rounds.
3:05, four 120mm shells slammed into Oqmata plain.
3:25, eight 120mm shells fell on the perimeters of Sojod village and four others on Oqmata plain.
3:40, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
Between 16:25 and 16:50, the peripheries of Loweizeh came under ten 81mm rounds and four 120mm shells.
16:50, Mount Sojod was targeted by two 120mm shells.
