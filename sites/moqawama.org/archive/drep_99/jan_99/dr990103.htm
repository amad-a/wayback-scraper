Daily Report
 
 
January Sunday 3-1999:
 
Introduction
Hizbullah warned the "Israeli" enemy Prime Minister against trying to boost his popularity before the elections by attacking Lebanon, saying the country has been the graveyard of many "Israeli" governments and that betting on Lebanon to win the elections will backfire.

However, more "Israelis" call for withdrawal from Lebanon saying that "Israel's" retaliation policy will be costly. A former senior officer of "Israeli" intelligence system said the occupation forces must withdraw immediately from South Lebanon, for the occupied border area does not provide "Israelis" with security.

Hizbullah Warns "Israel" (1)
"The Islamic resistance (IR) group of Hizbullah warned "Israeli" enemy's prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu Saturday against striking Lebanon to gain election votes, saying the country had been the "graveyard of many "Israeli" governments.''

"We warn Benjamin Netanyahu against using the south Lebanon card for electoral gains,'' Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, the leader of Hizbullah and the Islamic Resistance that is battling to oust ""Israel"" from occupied south Lebanon, told Reuters in Beirut.
""Israel"" is due to hold early elections on May 17 after Netanyahu's government failed to win broad support for his settlement moves with the Palestinians.

"The lives of people of the south should not become votes to stuff the ballot boxes. We want to remind Netanyahu that Lebanon was the graveyard of many "Israeli" governments and that betting on Lebanon to win the elections will backfire.''

"Sayyed Nasrallah was referring to the 17-day aggression launched by former "Israeli" prime minister Shimon Peres against Lebanon a few weeks before the 1996 election".

"The "Grapes of Wrath'' operation resulted in the deaths of 200 Lebanese people, mainly civilians, and Peres lost his bid for reelection after "Israeli" Arabs declined to vote for him in a close-run race."

"South Lebanon is the last active Arab-"Israeli" battle front. Twenty-four "Israeli" soldiers were killed there in resistance operations during 1998, prompting the "Israeli" entity to review its occupation of the area."

""Israel"" has occupied parts of the south for 20 years, including a 15 km (nine-mile) deep occupation zone it carved out in 1985.

Last week Hizbullah lobbed scores of Katyusha rockets across the border after a mother and her six children were slaughtered during an "Israeli" air raid around Baalbek, near Lebanon's eastern border with Syria.

""Israel's"" cabinet vowed Thursday to hit targets within Lebanon to avenge the rocket attacks, which wounded 16 "Israelis".

Sayyed Nasrallah said such threats would not spare the "Israeli" entity or halt the Islamic Resistance's war of attrition against the occupation.

"Words won't do them any good, they must take action. They must withdraw unconditionally, and if they really want to avoid the Katyushas, they should not attack civilians,'' his Eminence said.

"We only launch the Katyushas at occupied northern Palestine to revive the understanding which protects civilians.''

"Under the "April Understanding'' which ended the 1996 "Israeli" aggression, the resistance men and the "Israeli" occupation army are barred from striking civilian targets, but not military ones.

Despite the understanding, the "Israeli" aggressions claimed 28 Lebanese civilian lives last year"

Lebanon blasts "Israeli" attack threat: Katyusha rockets are always ready to defend civilians and protect infrastructure (2)
"Lebanon on Saturday strongly condemned the renewed "Israeli" threat to attack the country's infrastructure"

"The "Israeli" threat clearly showed its intention of aggression against Lebanese civilians," Lebanese Minister of Displaced Affairs and Information Anwar Khalil told Lebanon's Central News Agency.

""Israel's"" threat, which seemed like a respond to Katyusha attack on its north, was designated in fact to cover its criminal act against a Lebanese family, seven members of which were killed in "Israeli" air raids, he added.

"Last month, "Israeli" warplanes raided Lebanon's eastern Beqaa Valley, massacring a woman and her six children. ""Israel"" later claimed that was a "mistake" and made a public "apology" for the victims.

The next day, the Hizbullah resistance group slammed scores of Katyusha rockets into "Israeli" colonies in occupied northern Palestine in retaliation, wounding at least 16 "Israelis"".

"The development forced the "Israeli" security cabinet to review its policy on south Lebanon.
In its meeting last Thursday, the "Israeli" government decided to attack Lebanon's infrastructure when its north was hit again by Hizbullah's rocket attacks".

"Minister Khalil stressed that Lebanon's resistance movement is a legal right and will continue till the day all the country's occupied lands are liberated.

On the other hand, Lebanese parliamentarian and Hizbullah member Sayyed Ammar al-Musawi warned Friday that the Islamic Resistance men will attack ""Israel"" with Katyusha rockets if it strikes at Lebanon's infrastructure.

"Attacking the country's infrastructure is a violation of the April (1996) understanding, and we reserve the right to respond in an adequate manner," he said during an Iftar, the evening meal to break the daily Moslem fast during the holy month of Ramadan.

"The Katyusha rockets are always ready. They will defend civilians and protect the infrastructure," he said.

More "Israeli" calls for withdrawal from Lebanon (3)
"Enemy war minister Yitzhak Mordechai announced the formation of a council made up of "Israeli" military commanders and senior security officials; the aim of the council is to discuss the situation in Lebanon."

"The new council which is chaired by Mordechai will meet every week"

""Israeli" opposition Labor Party leaders said ""Israel's"" retaliation policy will be costly.

It is "a very stupid decision," said Labor Knesset member Yossi Beilin. "It means in 1999, more ("Israeli" occupation) soldiers will be killed in Lebanon, and that civilians on both sides will pay a very high price."

"Former "Israeli" prime minister Shimon Peres said only diplomatic solutions can resolve the current conflict in south Lebanon".

"Some see "Israeli" prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu's tough stance as political posturing before the May general elections".

"The political dimensions nowadays reflect the mood before the election period," "Israeli" military analyst Ze'ev Shif was quoted by US news network CNN as saying. "No matter what, if someone will hit us, we will respond much stronger. Everyone wants to show his muscles."

In another show of the "Israeli" command's not knowing what to do concerning Lebanon, "Israeli" chief of general staff Shaul Mofaz told reporters Friday that "Israel" has no interest in escalating tensions in south Lebanon, denying reports that the "Israeli" army will strike infrastructures in Lebanon.

Mofaz made the remarks just one day after the "Israeli" security cabinet's threats against Lebanon.
On the other hand, former senior officer of ""Israel's"" espionage apparatus Haim Yefrah said the occupation forces must withdraw immediately from south Lebanon, for the occupied border zone "does not provide "Israelis" with security".

"Yefrah, who resigned his post two days ago, said the border zone "was originally made to prevent Palestinians from crossing the border, but nowadays there are no such Palestinian attempts; there is one party: Hizbullah. And its declared objective is to achieve "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon."

"In an interview with "Israeli" radio, Yefrah said a lot of "Israeli" intelligence officers share his opinion".

Military Operations (5)
The IR squad of martyrs Hussein Mohanna and Moussa Yassine attacked, at 11:00, an enemy motorized patrol on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Ghassani and Ibrahim Moghnieh targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from supporting the attacked patrol. Also, direct and certain hits were scored, the militants reported.
