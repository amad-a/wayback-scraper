Introduction
The "Israeli" occupation troops pursued their aggressions against the Lebanese villages and towns. Meanwhile, the April Understanding Monitoring Group is expected to meet next week to look into three Lebanese complaints concerning the "Israeli" shelling of Sarba, Al-Sawaneh and Haddatha villages.

However, Irish PM, who started a three-day official visit to Lebanon on Sunday, made remarks in a tour of the village of Qana outside the "Israeli" occupied area in South Lebanon.

"Israeli" bombardment and raids, AUMG convenes next week
""Israeli" occupation forces at noon today (yesterday) bombarded the Mount al-Dahr area (western Beqaa) while enemy warplanes staged mock air raids over al-Bizzalieh (northern Beqaa). "Israeli" aircraft also flew over Baalbek breaking the sound barrier".

"In the south, "Israeli" warplanes flew at low altitude over the areas of Tyre, Bint Jbeil, and
Nabatieh.

Wednesday morning, "Israeli" artillery bombarded the eastern hilltops of upper Nabatieh and Kfarromman along with the vicinity of the deserted army barracks in Nabatieh".

Meanwhile, enemy forces raked the targeted areas with heavy and medium machine guns.
In the afternoon, the vicinity of the Hammad and Hamra farms, the Yohmor Shqief, and the Kfartibneet woods were bombarded from the enemy's outpost in Beaufort Castle".

"The occupation forces in Blatt, Hardoun and Bayyada Tuesday morning shelled the villages of Mansouri, Majdel Zoen, Byout al-Siyyad, al-Medfneh area, and Yater".

"Moreover, the Radar outpost raked the residential areas in the town of Mansouri, while "Israeli" shells landed in Wadi al-Izzieh and the outskirts of Zibqeen".

"On the other hand, the April Understanding Monitoring Group is to convene next week to discuss three Lebanese complaints over the occupation forces' bombardment of the towns of Sarba, al-Sawwaneh, and Haddatha. The "Israeli" aggressions caused damage in several houses" (1)

Irish PM visits site of "Israeli" massacre in Qana(3)
"Irish Prime Minister Bertie Ahern Monday said his country is committed to the peacekeeping mission in south Lebanon to help maintain stability and peace there".

"The prime minister, who started a three-day official visit to Lebanon on Sunday, made the remarks in a tour of the village of Qana outside the "Israeli" occupied border zone in south Lebanon".

"Over 100 Lebanese civilians were massacred in the village during the 1996 "Israeli" aggression on the country"

"Visibly moved, Ahern shook hands with Khairieh Borgi, a woman in her 70s who had one arm and one leg amputated after "Israeli" artillery pounded the U.N. center on April 18, 1996".

"Over 900 people had taken shelter there when "Israeli" artillery opened fire. A subsequent U.N. report cast doubt on the official "Israeli" explanation-- that its guns had fired accidentally. Amnesty International said the "Israeli" entity had deliberately targeted the area in south Lebanon".

"The elderly woman showed Ahern pictures of her dead sons and grandsons and described how she lost 19 members of her family".

"We have seen this over and over in Ireland; how brief moments can take away so many innocent lives," Ahern told Qana residents.

"He saw a wrecked building where dozens of children died in the "Israeli" massacre. The shells detonated as parents were preparing lunch and half opened tuna fish cans still lay on the floor".

"Israel" to probe "mishandling of soldiers' remains" (5)
"The "Israeli" army on Monday announced an inquiry into whether the remains of two soldiers should be exhumed for identification following the mishandling of a grisly body exchange with Lebanese resistance men", a reuters report said.

"Announcement of the investigation was the latest development in an affair that began with an "Israeli" botched commando raid in south Lebanon in 1997 in which 12 occupation soldiers were killed.

Distraught families of two of the dead have charged that the army added body parts returned in the swap to their sons' graves without consulting them and without properly establishing which parts belonged to which soldier", the report said.

"The "Israeli" army said the investigation commission, to be headed by a retired Supreme Court judge, would start work in the next few days and had 90 days to issue its "findings and recommendations.''

"The commission was asked to check the way the body parts brought from Lebanon were identified and the way the parts were buried,'' an army spokesman said.

"Members of the amphibious commando unit, known as Flotilla 13, landed on Lebanon's shore on the night of September 5, 1997, for an operation against resistance men in Insariyeh, north of Tyre".
"The 16-member squad ran into an ambush of the Islamic Hezbollah (Party of God) group. A helicopter rescue team retrieved 11 bodies, leaving behind the remains of officer Itamar Ilya, Reuters said.

"Last June, "Israel" exchanged the bodies of 40 Lebanese resistance men for Ilya's remains but the body bag that arrived from Lebanon contained the parts of more than one soldier, including five feet, the report added.

""Israeli" Army officials buried the extra parts in the graves of two other commandos killed in the raid -- Guy Golan and Raz Tabi."

"Parents of the two soldiers said the army informed them about the extra parts only after adding them to the graves. They are demanding that their sons be exhumed for DNA identification."
Religious law requires that Jews be buried intact when possible and that their remains should lie undisturbed.

"This second burial in the dead of night is unheard of. What happened to their respect for the dead? What happened to their respect for the family?'' Guy Golan's father, Yoram, told Army Radio last November.

"Israeli" army chief Shaul Mofaz later apologized for the way the parts were buried and said the parents should have been consulted.

"Raz Tabi's father, Nachshon, said the probe did not meet his demands. "I lost all trust in the army chief and the defense minister. No army commission is acceptable...I demand a criminal investigation,'' he told the "Israeli" army Radio".
