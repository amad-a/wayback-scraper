Daily Report
 
 
January Tuesday 5-1999:
 
SLA commander escapes assassination attempt (1)
"Joseph Karam, the notorious commander of the South Lebanon Army* in the Jezzine area, narrowly escaped assassination Tuesday when a roadside bomb exploded on a road outside the town as his convoy drove past".

"Security sources said Mr. Karam, nicknamed Allouche, suffered superficial shrapnel wounds and no one else in his entourage was hurt by the blast on the Ain Majdalain road".

"In a statement issued in Beirut, the Islamic Resistance claimed responsibility for the 4:30 P.M. ambush. It claimed Mr. Karams car was destroyed and several of his bodyguards were wounded.
Mr. Karam took over the command of the Jezzine area last year, but has remained a largely unpopular SLA officer, partly because he is not a native of the south, hailing from a family in northern Lebanon.

Last week, he failed in a bid to mediate between SLA commander Antoine Lahd and militiamen requesting reinforcements be sent to their outpost at Kfar Falous. The development prompted 17 militiamen to defect and seek refuge for several days with Internal Security Forces at the Safaray garrison."

"They have since returned to Anan, a village outside Jezzine that was vacated by the SLA after the defection".

Lebanon protested "Israeli" aggressions before monitoring group (2)
"Lebanon formally protested the wounding of the family in the Janta attack to the five-nation monitoring group which is scheduled to convene in Naqoura Thursday".

"The group, comprising delegates from Lebanon, Syria, France, "Israel" and the U.S., will also hear another Lebanese complaint over the destruction of six houses in Arnoun Dec. 28. "Israel" has lodged a complaint over allegations of the resistance staging an attack from populated areas of Majdal Zoun".

"President Emile Lahoud said Monday that Lebanon was contacting world powers to prevent an escalation of "Israeli" attacks".

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss said that he had contacted the embassies of countries with permanent seats on the UN Security Council to protest the "Israeli" air raids on Janta".

"So-called mistakes in which Lebanese civilians die apparently go unpunished by the "Israeli" military. The lieutenant-colonel who commanded the Dec. 22 air raid on Janta in which a mother and six of her children were killed also took part in Sundays strike against the same village, according to "Israeli" reports. Yediot Ahronot reported that the unidentified officer apparently took to heart his previous error and was permitted to participate in the second raid".

"Information Minister Anwar Khalil protested Monday a two-week-siege of Shebaa, in the eastern sector of the occupied zone. He said the village was in dire shortage of food.

Security sources said that since the siege began, 90 villagers had been detained and questioned by "Israeli" troops".

"Israeli" Aggressions (3)
The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions:
Between 4:00 and 4:25, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by thirteen 155mm missiles.
4:05, twelve 155mm missiles pounded the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village.
13:35, Qaissieh Valley came under five 155mm missiles.
16:30, sixty 81 and 120mm shells slammed the hilltops of Ain El-Tineh, south of Mashghara, the 1700th Hill, Zawarib area and Bayada heights.
21:00, Mount Sojod was bombarded by eleven 120mm shells and four 155mm missiles. At the same time, Mlita village was hit by five 155mm missiles and Tasseh Well by two others.
22:50, two 81mm rounds struck Ali Taher woodland, whereas the surroundings of Dabsheh position came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
23:20, Kfar Romman town subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Military Operations (4)
The IR squad of martyr Abdel-Aziz Kourani attacked, at 6:45, enemy movements in and around Radar post with the adequate arms scoring direct hits. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyr Mohammad Fayad was targeting Hardoun position with the suitable weapons. The position's main fortification was hit directly as the militants reported.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Abdel-Rassoul Baydoun and Rafik Zahwi targeted, at 13:15, an "Israeli" force of two military vehicles near Qabaa post with the rocket-propelled grenades. The force which was fortifying the post was hit directly and the militants were able to damage one of the vehicles.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Zein El-Abedeen Suleiman and Radwan El-Anqouni ambushed, at 16:30, the convoy of the 20th Brigade's commander known by Alloush as it was travelling on the road leading to Ain Majdaleen. The mujahedeen blew up a powerful explosive device destroying his car and inflicting a number of his bodyguards.

At 20:20, the IR squad of martyrs Mahmoud Hussein Salman and Ali Kawtharani attacked a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.
