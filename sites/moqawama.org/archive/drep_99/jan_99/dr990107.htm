Daily Report
 
 
January Thursday 7-1999:
 
New rebellion in ranks of militia
""Israeli" occupation forces have introduced reinforcements to the collaborators' outpost in Ain Majdaleen following a rebellion by 5 militiamen who withdrew from the outpost three days ago and went home", reports from the occupied zone said.

"The five militiamen complained that their command has abandoned them. They said they could not stand the fact that their lives are constantly threatened (due to resistance operations)"... (1)

"Meanwhile, the head of Israel's allied militia in the south, Antoine Lahd, was hospitalized in Israel with chest pains. But close aides of the ex-general who had been sentenced to death in Beirut for collaborating with the enemy, said has been quote steadily improving. His militia, which is equipped and financed by "Israel" has been crumbling and its members have been deserting their posts" (2)

"Israeli" Aggressions (3)
"The "Israeli" occupation forces proceeded with their aggressions against civilian areas. Enemy artillery today bombarded the Jib Swaid area with 155mm rounds and west of Yater village with eight others.

Moreover, "Israeli" warplanes staged more provocative flights and mock air raids in the south and broke the sound barrier over Beirut, while espionage MK drones flew over southern areas".

"In the afternoon, enemy aircraft flew over Sidon at low altitude".

"Enemy gunboats intercepted a boat in Lebanese regional waters off the al-Qlialeh shore.

Fishermen brothers Hussein and Ahmed Taha were held and questioned by the occupation forces for hours before being allowed to return to the Tyre port".

Lahoud stands firm on withdrawal conditions (4)
"President Emile Lahoud stated clearly Wednesday that neither Beirut nor Damascus would accept a partial withdrawal and that the ties between the two countries remained immutable".

"In his first official reception for the diplomatic community since becoming head of state, General Lahoud told ambassadors that Lebanon had always relied on the support of the international community in its struggle to oust Israel from the south".

We believe that a comprehensive and just peace will guarantee the security of everyone in the region, he said. Our concept of peace means the rejection of partial measures that lead only to an incomplete peace and which neither ensure the security of the region nor provide for a complete and clear withdrawal from the area.

General Lahoud insisted that the Lebanese-Syrian track was necessary to bring about a comprehensive regional peace.

Lebanons national interest requires the coordination of the Lebanese and Syrian tracks in the peace negotiations, whatever the circumstances or considerations may be, he said.

The president told the assorted diplomats that the resistance was a legitimate expression of the national cause, the withdrawal of the "Israeli" occupiers from its territories, and the end of the daily attacks against its people.

Military Operations (5)
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Abdallah Sawli and Hussein Mannaa attacked, at 14:15, an "Israeli" engineering force as it was fortifying Kassaret Al-Oroush. The mujahedeen who used the rocket-propelled grenades managed to chalk up direct goals.
