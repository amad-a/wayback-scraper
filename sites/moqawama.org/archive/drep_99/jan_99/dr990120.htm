Daily Report
 
 
January Wednesday 20-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions:
In its statement, the Islamic Resistance reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
5:55, two 81mm rounds fell on the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
10:20, the residential areas of Mansouri village exposed to heavy machine gun fire.
10:30, two 120mm shells struck the center of Mansouri whereas its peripheries subjected to one 105mm tank round. Meanwhile, the surroundings of Bayada outpost subjected to 12.7mm and Mag machine gun rounds.
10:45, three 120mm shells hit Mount Sojod.
