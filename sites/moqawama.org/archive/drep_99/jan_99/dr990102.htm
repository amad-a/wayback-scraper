Daily Report
 
 
January Saturday 2-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said the only solution to stop Katyusha rockets from falling in colonies in northern occupied Palestine is to strictly observe the April Understanding and not to target Lebanese civilians.

Meanwhile, the residents of Annan are still refusing to garrison the outpost of Roum and to allow the collaborators to build a new gate in their town as well.

Sayyed Nasrallah: threats are nothing new (1)
"Hezbollah Secretary general his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said the ""Israeli" threats to bombard civilian infrastructures in Lebanon are not new, nor will they undermine the resolve of the Islamic resistance to defend its people."

"The only solution to stop Katyusha rockets from falling on colonies in northern occupied Palestine is to strictly observe the April understanding and not to target Lebanese civilians, his Eminence said.

Commenting on the positions announced by the "Israeli" inner cabinet, the secretary general underlined that "As it performs its duty and natural right to defend its people, the Islamic Resistance vows to continue its Jihad operations against the occupation."

"The enemy has no choice other than an unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon", Sayyed Nasrallah said.

Collaborator militia's disintegration may force it to retreat into Jezzine (2)
"The command of the proxy militia is still unable to recruit militiamen to garrison the outpost of Roumfollowing the refusal by the town's militiamen to man the compound".

"Meanwhile, the residents of Annan are still refusing to allow the collaborators to build a new gate in their town".

"The proxy militia has established a new military outpost near the Dahr al-Ramleh outpost.
Reports from the occupied zone said the collaborators plan to draw a new deployment plan in the western sector of Jezzine".

"The militia may retreat once again towards the Dahr al-Ramleh outpost which observers classify as fallen at the military level", the reports added.

"Security sources did not rule out a retreat by the militia towards Jezzine "especially as this option is repeatedly reiterated in the ranks of the militia command and in Jezzinite circles, following the rebellions and defections in the militia's ranks besides the residents' rejection to let their sons join the militia".

AP reports about repercussions of resistance operations on "stupid" "Israeli" army (3)
" Hours after a recruit was killed by friendly fire, senior "Israeli" army officers announced their conclusion: his immediate commander was to blame".

"For an army that once prided itself on extended soul-searching each time one of its troops fell, the haste was astonishing for some -- but not surprising, given reports of the erosion of military values after 16 years in Lebanon".

"We have gone from being a small but clever army to a small but stupid army,'' 1st Sgt. Ohad Zach's mother, Naomi, said in remarks widely repeated in the media Thursday.

"On Wednesday afternoon, less than 18 hours after the shooting, Brig. Gen. Effi Eitam called a news conference."

"He explained the rear section of an ambush force opened fire on the forward section and concluded: "The main reason for this chain of professional mistakes was the (squad) commander.''

"Other soldiers in the unit, shocked at what they saw as Eitam's unseemly hastiness, asked their relatives to tell the media that the problem ran much deeper. Soldiers are barred from addressing the media directly".

"Efraim Nisim -- whose son, Guy, lost an eye -- said soldiers in the unit blamed the system more than they did the commander, 2nd Lt. Assaf Lapid."

"Nisim said the recruits had warned Lapid's superiors in the past that he had made errors -- but they had failed to heed their warnings".

"I heard from my son and his friends that they didn't trust Lapid, that they passed it on,'' he told Channel Two.

""Israeli" "doves" have long charged that the occupation of south Lebanon has led to a series of failures that have crippled morale and set up a hierarchy of blame".

"Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's spokesman said the friendly fire shooting was not representative."

"One soldier killed by friendly fire, if that was indeed the case, doesn't indicate anything except that in one incident it was a case of negligence,'' David Bar-Illan said.

"Lapid -- who Zach wounded in the hand in the shooting -- was willing to take responsibility, his father Herut said. But Lapid wanted accountability higher up".

"Our forces fired at each other, our children fired at each other, and someone, in order to cover his rear end, would put Assaf in jail,'' Herut Lapid told Channel One.

"Israeli" Aggressions (4)
The "Israeli" occupying troops pursued their aggressions and staged the following assaults:
00:05, Kfar Romman came under a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds, whereas several flare shells were fired overhead.
Between 3:40 and 6:40, Mount Sojod was bombarded by nineteen 120mm shells and five 81mm rounds.
11:50, the surroundings of Kassaret Al-Oroush compound subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
14:40, three 155mm missiles hit west of Yater village.
15:40, seventeen 155mm missiles pounded south of Jibal El-Botom village.
16:00, the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom came under five 155mm missiles.
16:45, Mount Sojod was bombarded by ten 120mm shells.
16:50, three 120mm shells landed the peripheries of Loweizeh village.

Military Operations (5)
The IR squad of martyrs Nizar Slim and Abdel-Aziz Kourani attacked, at 15:30, enemy movements inside Blat post with the appropriate arms scoring direct hits. At the same time, the group of martyrs Youssef Barakat and Khodor Khiami targeted Hardoun outpost with the adequate weapons chalking up direct goals.

The IR group of martyrs Mustafa Nazar and Rameh Mahdi targeted, at 19:15, motorized movements at Sojod outpost with the proper weapons chalking up certain goals.
