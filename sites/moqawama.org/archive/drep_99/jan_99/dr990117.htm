Daily Report
 
 
January Sunday 17-1999:

"Israeli" aggressions damaged two houses in Haddatha (1)
""Israeli" occupation forces proceeded with their daily aggressions and violations of the April Understanding against areas in the south and the western Beqaa".

"The occupation's artillery this morning bombarded residential areas in the town of Haddatha inflicting damage on two houses, belonging to Hussein Nasser and Ibrahim Nasser, and several cars".

"The bombardment also covered all the mid and western sectors' villages that are adjacent to the occupied zone, especially Yatar, Kafra, Harees, Aita al-Jabal, al-Mansouri, and Majdel Zoen."

"After 8:00 a.m. enemy warplanes staged two raids on Mleta Hilltop and Tair Harfa (western sector)".

"Moreover, "Israeli" shells slammed Zawtar al-Sharqieh and Zawtar al-Gharbieh, the outskirts of Yohmor, the Mrabaa area, the woods of Maifadoun whose schools were closed when some "Israeli" rounds hit the town's public school."

"Occupation forces also bombarded the deserted army barracks in Nabatieh, the Zahrani River banks, and the Iqlim al-Touffah Hilltops".

"In Hasbayya, an "Israeli" tank opened fire on a group of villagers. No casualties were reported."
"To the opposite of the Tyre port, "Israeli" gunboats intercepted a freighter laden with merchandise bound for the town. The ship was thoroughly searched for hours on end."

"Lebanon today (yesterday) filed two complaints with the April Understanding Monitoring Group over the occupation's bombardment of the towns of Sarba (Friday) and al-Sawwaneh (Thursday) which resulted in damaging houses and property".

"On the other hand, The Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Martyr Faraj Ballouq at 21:00 Friday attacked enemy movements in the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in al-Swaidaa with the appropriate weapons, scoring direct hits".

"Israeli" agent Lahd has not resigned (2)
""Israeli" television yesterday said head of the "Israeli" proxy militia Antoine Lahd who had resigned ten days ago backed up two days ago following  3meetings with an "Israeli" delegation from the northern command and the "Israeli" liaison unit".
 
"Lahd rendered his resignation to the Israelis to protest remarks made by a Likud Knesset member in which he insulted the chief of the collaborator militia".
 
"What is the "Israeli" army doing in south Lebanon other than protecting Lahd's commerce, MK Gideon Azra had said, accusing Lahd of failing to resolve his militia's problems and confronting Hizbullah".
 
"Lahd wanted a written apology for Azra's remarks,hreatening with resignation."
"Lebanese daily an-Nahar today said agent Lahd had left Marjayoun this week following a disagreement whose circumstances are not clear".

"It has been learnt that he had sacked one of his assistants outside Lebanon", the daily added.

Military Operations (4)
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Jawad Al-Horr attacked, at 21:10, a collaborator force as it was fortifying Sojod outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score certain goals. In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Ali Hussein Ghmaloush and Ali Khalil Yassine targeted "Israeli" movements near Kassaret Al-Oroush compound chalking up direct and certain goals.
