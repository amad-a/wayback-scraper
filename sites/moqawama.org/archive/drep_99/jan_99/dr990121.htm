Daily Report
 
 
January Thursday 21-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions:
In its communiqué, the Islamic Resistance mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:

00:55, Tasseh Well watercourse was bombarded by fifteen 81mm rounds.
1:05, one 120mm shell struck the perimeters of Sojod village.
2:10, Mount Sojod was targeted by six 120mm shells.
7:15, four 155mm missiles hit the area between Maifadoun and Zawtar Sharqieh villages.
20:50, Hamra area was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
22:05, one 120mm flare shell was fired over the area between Haris and Kafra villages.
