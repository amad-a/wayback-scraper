Daily Report
 
 
January Wednesday 6-1999:
 
Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants set off a roadside bomb near Beaufort Castle wounding seriously one "Israeli" soldier hours after the occupation troops stormed and searched the neighboring village of Arnoun.

Meanwhile, a letter smuggled from the notorious Khiam detention camp detailed the physical diseases suffered by many prisoners and requested that elderly and long-serving detainees are released.

"Israeli" soldier wounded by bomb near Beaufort Castle
The IR group of martyr Haitham Moghnieh targeted, at 00:45, an "Israeli" motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Ghanem and Nabil Akhdar targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct hits.

A group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades attacked, at 6:30, Haddatha compound with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. In the meantime, another group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades was targeting Baraasheit compound. The mujahedeen, who used the proper arms, were able to chalk up direct goals.

On the occasion of Badr Grand Battle, the IR militants penetrated all the enemy's military procedures when the Engineering Unit's group of martyr Zahi Haidar Ahmad detonated, at 14:40, a roadside bomb as an enemy inspection patrol was passing near Beaufort Castle.

Also, the same group set off, at 20:30, another powerful explosive device 150 meters only away from the entrance of Beaufort Castle. A command car was hit and burnt immediately whereas its passengers were either killed or injured. Several "Israeli" helicopters landed at the scene to evacuate the casualties (1).

In "Israel", Haaretz daily newspaper acknowledged that "an "Israeli" soldier was seriously wounded yesterday evening by a remote-control bomb set off by Lebanese guerrillas".

"The bomb was detonated beside a road near Beaufort, a Crusader castle where "Israel" maintains a base. The Hezbollah guerilla organization has claimed responsibility for the attack".
"The strategic Beaufort, which overlooks areas of South Lebanon and Northern "Israel", is located some four kilometers from the "Israeli"-Lebanese border".

"Guerrillas followed the bombing with rocket-propelled grenades and machine-gun fire"

"The bomb exploded in close proximity to a Hummer vehicle that headed a convoy".

"One of the soldiers in the Hummer was seriously wounded; however, because of the vehicle's heavy shielding, the other occupants were not injured".

"The wounded soldier, who suffered internal injuries in the blast, was rushed by helicopter to Haifa's Rambam Hospital and is currently in the intensive care unit; his condition is described as very serious" (2).

"Israeli" occupation launches more aggressions
""Israeli" warplanes at 10:30 and 14:50 today violated Lebanon's airspace staging a series of mock raids at a low altitude over the southern towns of Nabatieh and Tyre".

"The "Israeli" aggression took place as schoolchildren were returning home, creating a state of panic among them".

"The "Israeli" jets were met with anti-aircraft fire from Lebanese army positions".

"Meanwhile, the occupation's outpost in Ali al-Taher shelled the towns of Zawtar al-Sharqieh and Zawtar al-Gharbieh and Hima Arnoun with 81mm, 105mm, and 120mm rounds".

"At 15:00, occupation forces in Zaffateh and Beauforte Castle outposts bombarded Mazraat al-Hamra, Hima Arnoun and the outskirts of Yohmor and Beauforte Castle with heavy and phosphorous rounds, while enemy heavy machine guns raked the targeted areas".

"Moreover, a joint "Israeli"-collaborator force in the afternoon stormed the town of Arnoun and searched some of its houses" (3).

However, an IR statement reported the following "Israeli" aggressions (4)
4:15, the surroundings of Baraasheit compound exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
6:00, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds hit the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost.
6:40, four 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Baraasheit town.
6:55, the peripheries of Haddatha town subjected to one 120mm shell.
11:50, the surroundings of Houla post subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
14:35, two 155mm missiles fell on Hamra area.
20:55, four 120mm shells hit Mount Al-Rafih whereas Hamra area was targeted by three 155mm missiles.
21:30, the perimeters of Arnoun came under twelve 82mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds. Meanwhile, the "Israeli" helicopter gunships raked the surroundings of Beaufort Castle.

Letter smuggled from Khiam detention camp details neglect suffered by detainees (5)
"The inmates of Khiam have smuggled a letter to the Follow-up Committee for the Support of Lebanese detainees detailing the physical ailments suffered by many prisoners and requesting that elderly and long-serving detainees are released".

Our situation is becoming worse than ever, particularly on the health level ... Usually "Israelis" dont take detainees to Marjayoun hospital unless they are unconscious from pain and illness, the letter said.

"Several detainees were highlighted, including Nabil Asmar and Hussein Aqil who have lost 70 percent of their sight because of untreated cataracts".

"The letter added that Ibrahim Karnib is suffering from thalassemia, a genetic form of anemia, Marwan Kanaan has arteriosclerosis and Mohammed Qotbay, 64, is paralyzed and cannot stand because of rheumatism".

"The detainees asked the committee to press for the release of three children under 18 years old and a number of elderly inmates as well as Suleiman Ramadan and Ali Saghir, who have been detained for more than 13 years".

Monthly report on "Israeli" aggressions on Lebanon (Dec. 1998)
"There were 124 "Israeli" ground, air, and sea aggressions on more than 100 Lebanese areas and districts during December 1998".

"Air aggressions constituted 31.6% of the gross aggressions (32.9% in November).

In December "Israel" added a new hideous massacre to its history which is already full of crimes against Lebanese peaceful civilians".

"On Dec. 22 "Israeli" warplanes raided with six missiles an under-construction house at the entrance of the town of Janta (Beqaa). The raid annihilated a family of six children and their mother besides seriously wounding the father. The family members were preparing to break their fasting in the holy month of Ramadan when the massacre took place turning the bodies of Mohammed Amin Othman's family into charred remains and small pieces".

"The martyrs are Mohammed's wife Nadwa al-Sayyed, 35, his children Amina, 16, Amin, 14, Souad, 12, Abda, 10, Ali, 5, and Sobhi 18 months".

"The "Israeli" raid was condemned by all Lebanese political, religious and popular bodies and figures. Lebanon sent a letter of protest to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan in which Beirut rejected the "Israeli" "apology" for killing the victims. Lebanon also filed a complaint with the April Understanding Monitoring Group".

"Moreover, December witnessed "Israeli" provocative mock air raids which were carried out over the capital Beirut and other Lebanese cities, panicking civilians, especially in schools as some schoolchildren left their classes and returned home for fear of "Israeli" aggressions".

""Israeli" warplanes also raided the al-Mashaeb area (in the woods of the Jbal al-Botom town) on Dec. 3; the deserted mills near the Habboush River, Wadi Zillaya, Birkit al-Jabbour, Abu Rashed (western Beqaa) on Dec. 4; Jabal al-Rafieh Hilltops, Mazraat Oqmata on Dec. 12; a wadi between Zillaya and Qilia on Dec. 13; Mothallath al-Tamerieh, Wadi al-Molook, and wadi al-Qaissieh (Khorbet Selim) on Dec. 18; and al-Jabbour Hilltops and the woods of Abu Rashid (western Beqaa) on Dec. 22.

On the other hand, "Israeli" bombardment of the town of al-Tiri wounded a woman whom UNIFIL troops were able to pull back and hand over to the Red Cross".

"On Dec. 17 and "Israeli" commando unit killed a 17-year-old scout boy. Mohsen Abdel-Raoof Khalaf was martyred while performing his duty as a civil defense volunteer".

"Occupation forces' gunboats on Dec. 5 encircled Lebanese fishermen's gunboats off the shore between Rashiedieh, Qlaileh, and al-Mansouri (south of Tyre)".

"The "Israeli" gun boats showered the fishermen's boats with heavy machine gun fire wounding Malik Sikmani, 34, while another 11fishermen narrowly escaped death".

"In other "Israeli" practices inside the occupation zone, "Israeli" forces on Dec. 25 stormed into the town of Shibaa after blocking it off".

"Some of the town's residents were questioned by the occupation troops who also detained 16 people, including 5 Lahd militia members, under the pretext of looking for those who assassinated collaborator Ghassan Daher who was mysteriously killed earlier".

"Press reports said Daher was preparing to flee the occupied zone following the "Israeli" reports of an imminent "Israeli" withdrawal from the occupied zone".

"Other press reports said a UNIFIL officer stated that "Israeli" occupation forces and their proxy militia carried out an earlier campaign of detentions on Dec. 6 in the occupied town of al-Tiri, abducting ten residents, including seven women. The farmers who were on their way to their olive groves in Bait Leef were taken to the Saf al-Hawa detention center (Bint Jbeil)."

"Reports added that the collaborator militia has summoned Mahmoud Abdallah Shaito, 65, of al-Tiri, to Saf al-Hawa for questioning. The militia also detained three of its members for investigation after the clash between "Israeli" and militia patrols in al-Majiedieh".

"The collaborator militia also detained Youssef Ali Ahmed, 50, a taxi drivers of the town of al-Khiam, on Dec. 7, Raef Essa from the town of Ramia on Dec. 19, and Salam Abbas Swaid on Dec. 30 claiming that the latter cooperated with "enemy security bodies".

"On Dec. 27. an "Israeli" motorized force advanced to the eastern neighborhood of the town of Arnoun, destroyed seven houses, and demolished dozens of olive trees that surrounded the houses in which the occupation claimed to have found weapons and ammunition, an accusation flatly denied by the residents."

"The "Israeli" occupation forces on Dec. 3 expelled Sami Youssef Nammour, 28, through the Zimrayya crossing, claiming that he "cooperated with Lebanese security bodies". Nammour had been detained a week before he was expelled".

December witnessed a number of tours and celebrations made or attended by the enemy military commanders.

"On Dec. 12, a military meeting was held at the Marjeyoun barracks in the presence of "Israeli" army officers and collaborator militia commanders".

"The meeting aimed at discussing the situation in the occupied zone following the increasing resistance operations and the mounting "Israeli" losses, besides the question of cooperation between some militiamen, especially Maronites, with the resistance, a fact that has shocked the "Israeli" political leadership".

"A joint "Israeli"-Lahdi military delegation on Dec. 30 toured the Jezzine sector and inspected the outposts which the proxy militia had evacuated."

"The "Israeli" military delegation met the Lahd militia command in the sector.

On Dec. 31, commander of the northern area in the "Israeli" occupation army Gaby Askenazi visited the occupied zone and inspected the site of a clash between two Golani units in al-Taibeh.

A celebration has been held in the occupied zone in the presence of the liaison unit commander Gen. Erez Gerstein. The rally was not attended by the proxy militia's commander Antione Lahd who was reportedly spending the holidays with his family in France. However, reports affirmed that Lahd left the area for an unknown destination after the unrest in the ranks of his militia.

The collaborator militia carried out war games at the Majiedieh Institute for Training on Dec. 2 in the presence of Gerstein and Lahd".

"Joint "Israeli"-collaborator forces carried out two war games with live ammunition on Dec. 7 and Dec. 14 with the aim of intercepting any potential advancement at the Hasbayya axis.

The occupation and its collaborators also reinforced their positions at the Shraifeh hilltop, and closed the crossings of Kfarfalous, Zimrayya and Roum."

"These aggressions resulted in the martyrdom of eight civilian, one Islamic Resistance martyr, wounding seven civilians, detaining 33 people (including eight Lahd militiamen), expelling one resident, destroying seven houses and damaging ten others".

11 men were indicted on charges of collaborating with the "Israeli" enemy (7)
"11 men, including the commander of the South Lebanon Armys* force in a
village in the eastern sector of the occupied zone, and four other members of the militia, were indicted Tuesday on charges of collaborating with the Jewish state".

"According to the indictment, which was prepared by Chief Military Investigator Riad Talih, Mr. Deeb sent a woman identified as Iman Youssef Youssef to do the job, but she was arrested and revealed the plot to investigators".

"Ms. Youssefs name was not among the list of suspects charged in the case, who are all at large.
The suspects were also charged with smuggling "Israeli" goods into the occupation zone and making contacts with "Israeli" civilians and soldiers".

"Mr. Talih demanded the execution of all suspects and their trial before the Military Tribunal.
SLA commander Antoine Lahd was sentenced to death in 1996 in absentia by the Military Tribunal, having found him guilty of collaborating with "Israel" and masterminding the killing and detention of Lebanese citizens".

"The same court, which is presided over by Brigadier Hussein Shaheen, sentenced on Tuesday a man identified as Mohammed Attieh Raja to a year in prison for contacting "Israelis"".
