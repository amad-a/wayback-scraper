Daily Report
 
 
January Sunday 10-1999:
 
Occupation aggressions reach areas far from front lines; bombardment of power station
""Israeli" occupation's terrorist acts against liberated areas in the south and the western Beqaa continued yesterday. Enemy targeted areas that are far from front lines in Caza (district) of Sidon as "Israeli" shells hit and damaged a power station in Bisri at dawn today".

"A number of 155mm rounds landed in the vicinity of the power plant, one of them slammed the station itself".

"Moreover, occupation forces shelled the area between Roumeen and Houmeen (Sidon Caza) and the Arab al-Jal area. Several rounds also hit the Sarba-Roumeen highway. No casualties were reported.

These areas are relatively far from the front lines. This was the first time they are shelled in a long time".

""Israeli" artillery in Abu Qamha and al-Shraifeh pounded al-Daher Hilltop, Wadi Zellaya and Meimas. Tal al-Zaa'tar, the outskirts of Baraasheet, Aita al-Jabel and Tibneen also received their share of "Israeli" shelling emanating from the occupation's emplacement in Mhaibeeb. A house belonging to Lebanese citizen Najib Farhat was destroyed as a result of the enemy shelling on residential areas in Baraasheet.

Security bodies inspected the damaged house which may be the subject of an additional Lebanese complaint to be lodged with the April Understanding Monitoring Group".

"The panel will convene in Naqoura on Monday to examine 4 Lebanese complaints and six "Israeli" ones".

"Meanwhile, "Israeli" warplanes staged a number of over flights at low attitude over the locality of Nabatieh and Iqlim al-Touffah" (1)

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following aggressions (2):
00:15, two flare shells were fired over the area between Majdal Selem and Shaqra town.
2:05, several 81mm flare shells were unleashed over the area between Majdal Selem and Shaqra.
3:05, the area between Shaqra and Houla was lightened by a number of 81mm flash shells.
5:40, Mount Sojod was hit by three 120mm shells.
5:55, two 120mm shells struck Mount Al-Rafih.
6:45, Mount Zaatar was targeted by four 155mm missiles, whereas the perimeters of Haddatha town came under three others.
12:15, four 155mm missiles fell on the peripheries of Haddatha.
17:25, two rounds landed the southern perimeters of Roumeen village.

PM reiterates backing resistance and following up detainees' issue (3)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss today received a delegation from the Follow-up Committee for Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prisons (FCLD). The delegation handed over to the premier a memorandum including the names of detainees in "Israeli" prisons inside occupied Palestine and in the "Israeli"-run al-Khiam detention center".

"Al-Hoss stressed the importance of the detainees' issue".

"The detainees are resistance men. We will see what we can do to perform our duty, al-Hoss said.
The government will support the freed prisoners and take action to secure the release of those who are still in captivity, he added, underlining that the council of ministers will study the issue thoroughly".

He told the freed detainees "We back the resistance, and you are part of it, for its is a combination of detainees, freed men, martyrs, and steadfastness."

 Military Operations (4)
The IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mreish and Ali Abdel-Wahed attacked at midnight enemy motorized movements at Sojod outpost with the appropriate arms. The militants managed to score direct hits.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Ali Ahmad Nazzal and Ali Sharara targeted, at 6:30, enemy movements at Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to chalk up direct goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyr Malek Al-Mousawi targeted, at 10:35, Ahmadieh position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate goals.
