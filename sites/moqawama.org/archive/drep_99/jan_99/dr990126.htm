Daily Report
 
 
January Tuesday 26-1999:

Introduction
The "Israeli" proxy militia in South Lebanon has released sixteen detainees from the notorious Khiam detention center. Fourteen of the freed hostages returned home in the "Israeli" occupied area. Two of them were deported to the liberated areas.

Meanwhile, the April Understanding Monitoring Group (AUMG) affirmed, yesterday, that the "Israeli" shelling of the towns of Haddatha, Al-Sawaneh, Sarba and Zillaya, subjects of the Lebanese complaint, violated the April 1996 Understanding.

"Israeli" occupation freed 16 hostages form al-Khiam (1)
""Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborator militia this morning freed 16 hostages of the al-Khiam detention center".

"All of the released are from occupied zone villages. They are: Mohammed Hussein Nasser (Aita al-Shaeb), Marwan Kanaan (Shibaa), Mohammed Qassem Sha'ar (Hibareya), Hussein Hamoud (Beit Leef), Jamal Nibaa (Shibaa), Ali Hijazi (Aytaroun), Ahmed Mohammed al-Sheikh Ali (Khiam), Mousa Salim Atieh (Tair Harfa), Hasib Mohammed Ali Fakih (Aytaroun), Latif Youssef al-Hajj (Reesh), Jim Jean Haddad (Borj al-Molouk), Hussein Faraj (Bint Jbeil), Mohammed Ousaily (Bint Jbeil), Nabil al-Asmar (Al-Ediaseh), Kamal Habib Khraizat (Aytaroun), Mostafa Mohsen Sadaqa (al-Khiam).

The occupation forces expelled two of the ex-deatinees. Ali Ibrahim Hijazi and Mostafa Sadaqa were taken by the proxy militia to the Kfartibneet crossing and ordered to leave the occupied zone.
Hijazi who had spent 11 years in the notorious detention center immediately visited Hezbollah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah".

"After his arrival at the Rassoul al-Azzam hospital, Hijazi moved with his family and Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) MPs Abdallah Qassir and Nazih Mansour, besides Head of Hezbollah's Social Affairs Unit former MP Mohammed Berjawi, to the party's Secretariat HQ where they met Sayyed Nasrallah."

"International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) representative in Lebanon Henri Fournier said the release was part of the natural course adopted by ICRC which seeks to release all the sick detainees.

There are sick among the detainees, he said, adding that ICRC's efforts to free more prisoners will go on".

"Fournier briefed PM Salim al-Hoss on the situation of some detainees".
"For his part al-Hoss said the detainees' issue tops the priorities of his government.
The detainees' issue is being followed up with those concerned in ICRC to secure the release of all
detainees", the premier said.

"On the other hand, the Follow-up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons (FCLD) said it welcomes the release of any detainee but protested that the children and tens of sick, women and old detainees were not freed, "especially as some of those had their legs implied such as
Suleiman Ramadan."

"The step (release) is a mere attempt to calm down the international pressure campaign which has been mounting after the deterioration of some detainees' health conditions, FCLD said.
The alarming conditions of detainees has been unveiled in the letter which has been smuggled by some detainees", FCLD added.

AUMG: "Israel" violated the understanding (3)
"The April Understanding Monitoring Group (AUMG) today (yesterday) examined five complaints, four of them lodged by Lebanon over the "Israeli" occupation's bombardment of the towns of Haddatha, al-Sawwaneh, Sarba and Zillaya which resulted in damaging several houses and other properties".

"The "Israeli" occupation claimed that resistance men have fired on an occupation outpost from within the town of Haddatha".

"The "Israeli" enemy has deliberately included this claim in spite of its baselessness, in order to have one complaint in the face of the Lebanese complaints", Lebanese security sources said.
"Following the meeting, AUMG acknowledged the four Lebanese complaints, affirming that the "Israeli" bombardment violated the 1996 April Understanding."

"Concerning the complaints over the Haddatha shelling, the panel included in its statement the points of view of the Lebanese and "Israeli" sides."

Also, the IR group of martyr Hassan Haidous targeted, at 15:30, Blat post with the adequate arms. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported.
