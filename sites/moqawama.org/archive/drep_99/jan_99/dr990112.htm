Daily Report
 
 
January Tuesday 12-1999:
 
Introduction
In response to the news that "Israel's" Prime Minister may strike South Lebanon in a large-scale attack that could include Syrian targets, Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said the Islamic Resistance (IR) is ready for any confrontation.

Meanwhile, the Lebanese Prime Minister held US the responsibility for such an aggression and said only Washington can prevent an "Israeli" onslaught against Lebanon's infrastructure due to the influence it has on "Israel".

Nasrallah: Resistance ready to retaliate for "Israeli" aggressions (1)
 "Resistance is ready for any confrontation. It will make the "Israeli" enemy pay for its aggressions on our country, Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said Saturday".

"Speaking at an iftar held by the Woman Council of the Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance, his Eminence said "Those who think a settlement will solve our problem with the Zionists are either stupid or under a delusion."

"We must not rely on the UN security council or similar bodies, for the world would not have noticed the Janta massacre in which Zionists had killed a mother and her six children if there had been no shelling of "Israeli" colonies", he said.

"The US, which had earlier barred the security council from condemning the Qana massacre, would not let denouncing the Janta slaughter pass; however, we insist that a complaint must be filed with the security council to let the world know what is going on, even though we don't count on the security council", he added.

"We only count on and believe in the resistance option, for this is the only option to retaliate for the Zionist aggressions; we are ready to offer sacrifices, in this path, the secretary general said stressing that "Gone is the time when we used to be killed alone and displaced alone, gone is the time when threats are leveled at us".

"Sayyed Nasrallah stated that Resistance is ready to confront any "Israeli" aggression".

Resistance is ready for the confrontation. It will have the enemy pay for its aggressions against our country", he said.

"The enemy has tried all ways such as aggressions and wars to pressurize us in vain. The so many sessions by the enemy's inner cabinet are evidence to the "Israeli" quagmire, his Eminence added.
The resistance in Lebanon doesn't want anything for itself. On the contrary, it is ready to sacrifice everything to defend its people and country", the secretary general said.

He added, "In September 1993, Hizbullah carried its martyrs and avoided an internal confrontation to use all its strength against the Zionist enemy."

"In the past few years, some tried to evoke sectarian sentiments against the resistance by raising the Jezzine issue and other issues", he said.

"Sayyed Nasrallah said, "The resistance has proven to be very remote from sectarianism and confessionalism, and everybody found out that the path taken by the resistance is the right one for the liberation of Jezzine and all the occupied areas. Here is the Lahd militia being forced to evacuate several outposts in Tomat Neha and al-Mashnaqa. The Resistance's operations will force the Lahd militia and the Zionist army to withdraw from the occupied zone."

"Some used to accuse the resistance of being sectarian. Although this is not true, we established the (multi-faith) Lebanese Resistance Brigades which every' Lebanese can join", he said.

"The Hizbullah secretary general said, "Some have tried to raise fears of the fact that the resistance men follow religious doctrine. These young men's observance of religious teachings is a strength factor for the resistance; we need to distinguish between religious commitment and sectarian sentiments. A religious man confronts the unjust even if they are from his own sect. We are proud to be committed to our religious teachings", he added.

"The peril threatening Lebanon is the sectarian sentiment, not observing religious teachings, which when observed, lead to success in reaching supreme goals", he concluded.

Levanese PM held US the responsibility of any "Israeli strike on Lebanon(2)
"Prime Minister Salim Hoss expressed fears Monday of a "military aggression" against Lebanon ahead of "Israel's" national elections in May and said only the United States could rein in the Jewish state". "Only the United States is capable of preventing "Israeli" aggressions against Lebanese infrastructure, such as electricity, water and road networks," Hoss told AFP. ""Israeli" occupation of a part of Lebanon is one of the main issues worrying the "Israeli" society and one of the main issues at stake in the electoral campaign," he said. "This is why we do not exclude the chance of "Israel" carrying out an act of aggression against Lebanon." His remarks came after a recent escalation of "Israeli" -Hizbullah violence which has raised fears embattled "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu could use the southern Lebanon issue to boost his stature before the elections. Netanyahu is "killing innocent people, women and children for the sake of his electoral campaign," said former Lebanese foreign minister Fares Bweiz. The situation brings back horrific memories of April 1996, just before the Jewish state's last general elections, when then prime minister Shimon Peres waged a major two-week offensive in Lebanon. The 17-day "Grapes of Wrath" onslaught left 175 dead, most of them civilians. That operation was officially meant to "crush" Hizbullah, but it was clear that Peres wanted to prove to the "Israeli" electorate an intransigence in matters of security equal to that of Netanyahu, who defeated him". "Now the tables have turned and many fear a weakened Netanyahu -- who after the collapse of his ruling coalition was forced to call for May elections -- will unleash a tough military campaign on Lebanon".

"Israeli" surfers assault Hizbullah internet site (3)
" "Israeli" Internet surfers have been bombarding the web site of the Hizbullah, the main resistance group fighting "Israel's" occupation of south Lebanon, a Beirut daily said Monday. "Death to Hizbullah. We will kill you all," read an extract of an e-mail sent by an "Israeli" surfer on December 8, a day after a Hizbullah attack, the As-Safir newspaper reported. The number of e-mails and their tone, mostly threatening, varies according to the military situation in southern Lebanon, the paper said. "Please stop using explosive charges. Death is following us at every step that we make in southern Lebanon," said an e-mail signed by an ""Israeli" soldier in southern Lebanon" sent December 17, a day after a Hizbullah bombing in the area killed three soldiers and wounded four others. But there are also e-mails carrying hostile religious attacks: "The Jews are the rulers of the world. Death to our enemies ... we will liquidate all the Arabs," said one of the messages. A Hizbullah spokesman said the group received several messages from "Israel" nearly every day on its site (www.Hizbullah.org), mostly signed by "Israeli" soldiers. But it is a "one-way" conversation, as Hizbullah which does not recognize the "Zionist entity," refuses to answer any of the messages. "We only receive the messages, and we never answer. It helps us to have a feel of the "Israeli" public opinion and of the size of the opposition there to the occupation of southern Lebanon," said the Hizbullah spokesman".

Monitoring group discusses truce violations; "Israel" attacks infrastructure in east and south (4)
" The 1996 cease-fire monitoring group is at this hour discussing complaints filed by Lebanon and "Israel" over alleged truce violations. The committee will discuss a recent wave of "Israeli" attacks on civilians and infrastructure in Lebanon.

 " The international committee monitoring the cease-fire in south Lebanon is meeting to discuss eight Lebanese and five "Israeli" complaints of truce violations. The panel in its first meeting this year, convened at the United Nations headquarters in the coastal town of Naqoura on the "Israeli"-Lebanese border. Lebanon is accusing "Israel" of eight violations of the April 1996 cease-fire agreement not to attack civilians or launch assaults from civilian areas. Lebanon protested the expulsion of 25 villagers from occupied Shibaa, the demolition of scores of houses in the front-line village of Arnoun, the attack on the Bisri power station, shelling that damaged a house in Baraachite, and two civilian casualties during a raid on the Bekaa Valley last week. The committee will also discuss five complaints filed by "Israel", claiming the resistance of firing shells on northern "Israel", killing a civilian in the occupation zone and launching attacks from civilian regions. A source in Hizbollah had called on the truce panel to issue a clear condemnation against "Israeli" aggressions, adding the committee should distance itself from the policy of equating aggressors with victims. There has been concern in Lebanon over "Israel's" heightened aggressions in the past two weeks, coinciding with a tough electoral campaign in the Jewish state. "Israeli" artillery fire rocked several regions of Lebanon on Sunday, damaging infrastructure facilities as well as four houses and two cars. Some 40 shells fell on the eastern Bekaa Valley, cutting a major power line that supplies electricity to the area as well as the majority of villages in occupied south Lebanon".

"Police said that in Zilaya, along the border of the eastern part of the occupation zone, drinking-water pipelines were damaged along with three houses. Further west the "Israeli" shelling damaged two cars in Nabatiyeh as well as a house in the town of Arabsalim".

Enemy targets south with scores of shells; stages raids and mock raids
""Israeli" occupation forces launched more aggressions on liberated areas firing scores of heavy rounds while enemy warplanes violated the Lebanese airspace launching several raids and mock raids over Nabatieh and Iqlim al-Touffah".

"Occupation forces in the outpost of al-Shraifeh and Zaffateh today bombarded with a number of 155mm rounds the outskirts of Yohmor, Mazra'at al-Hamra, Hima Arnoun, the deserted army barracks in Nabatieh, the Litani River banks, and the outskirts of Zawtar al-Sharkieh".

"Moreover, the vicinity of the town of al-Sarai in Jezzine and the Hariri compounds (near the Kfarfalous crossing) were shelled by the proxy militia".

"Enemy warplanes staged seven raids, three of them within 15 minutes, on the Mazraat Sojod area and other areas in Iqlim al-Touffah".

""Israeli" aircraft fired air-to-ground missiles and was intercepted by anti-aircraft fire from the Lebanese army and the Islamic Resistance, barring it from achieving its goals".

"At night enemy warplanes flew at low altitude in the skies of Nabatieh and Iqlim al-Touffah staging mock air raids".

"Earlier today, the proxy militia's artillery in Jezzine outposts (at 15:30) bombarded the outskirts of Sfarieh, Joun (Iqlim al-Kharoub), and the Hariri compounds near the Kfarfalous outpost" (5)

Lahoud says expelled Lebanese will return to the zone (7)
" President Emile Lahoud was quoted by visiting deputies as saying contacts are underway to secure the return of 25 Lebanese, who were expelled by "Israeli" occupation forces from Shibaa in the south". "Lahoud said the step is significant because it manifests the government's support to those who remain steadfast under "Israeli" occupation.

 Military Operations (8)
