Introduction
Following a four-day meeting, the Monitoring Group endorsed the Lebanese complaint and ruled that demolishing actions against civilian property are in violation of the understanding.
In the meantime, the "Israeli" gunners shelled several liberated villages and towns in South Lebanon.
However, Lebanon's Army Leader General Michel Suleiman ordered his troops to maintain practical readiness to respond to "Israeli" aggressions.

Monitors finally decide that blowing up houses is wrong (1)
"After four days of intense debate, the Monitoring Group ruled Thursday that the demolition of civilian homes is contrary to the April Understanding and accused "Israel" of violating the agreement by destroying 23 houses in Arnoun".

"In a record session lasting 75 uninterrupted hours, the group, consisting of delegates from Lebanon, Syria, France, "Israel" and the U.S., assessed a total 16 complaints, 10 filed by Lebanon and six by "Israel". But it was the two Lebanese complaints over the demolition of buildings in Arnoun Dec. 27 and Jan. 7 that bogged down the proceedings"

"The Lebanese delegate, Colonel Maher Toufeili, was reportedly anxious that a failure to designate the demolitions a violation of the understanding would lead to further homes being destroyed in front-line villages"

"The "Israeli" delegate, Brigadier General Daniel Arditi, contended that the houses were used to store weapons and bomb-making equipment and that their demolition was therefore justified".

"But the group chose to honor a precedent set in February last year when "Israel" was judged to have violated the understanding by blowing up an uninhabited house on the outskirts of Nabatieh Faouqa".

Keeping in mind its previous decisions, the Monitoring Group concluded that demolition actions against civilian property are in violation of the understanding, the final statement said. The group added that this conclusion, however, does not invalidate the concerns and positions of the parties regarding the use of civilian property in military operations.

""Israel" was also blamed for six other violations of the understanding, including the wounding of six civilians in Janta during an air raid Jan. 3"

No blame was attached to the expulsion of 25 residents from Shebaa, as there are differing views on whether the expulsion of civilians falls within the purview of the understanding.

"Israeli" bombardment on Iqlim al-Touffah, terrorist practices in Shibaa
"The "Israeli" occupation's artillery in the outposts of al-Zaffateh and al-Aishieh today bombarded Mount al-Rafieh, Nabeh al-Tasseh, al-Lwaizeh woods, Sojod, Rihan, and Mazraat Oqmata".

""Israeli" warplanes flew over Nabatieh and Iqlim al-Touffah staging mock air raids.
In the occupied zone, occupation forces detained Rida Hassan al-Taweel from his hometown Kfarkilla. He was taken to al-Khiam detention center".

"Moreover, enemy forces' arbitrary practices against the residents of occupied Shibaa went on after the crime of expelling 25 people from the town last week".

"The occupation is now trying to sow seeds of unrest among the residents by spreading rumors about the killing of collaborator Ghassan Daher".

"There was a fierce argument between the town's imam Sheikh Ismail Dallah and the occupation's intelligence which the imam accused of killing Daher and trying to create unrest in the town.
Occupation forces has recently summoned a number of the town's influential figures, including Sheikh Dallah, to the "security center" in Hasbayya" (2)

Army ready to respond to aggressions, says chief  (4)
"Army chief Gen Michel Suleiman ordered his troops to maintain practical readiness to respond to "Israeli" aggressions".

"During a tour on army units in the south, Suleiman commended the armed forces' steadfastness and sacrifices at the front".

""Israeli" threats will not prevent us from practicing our legitimate right or defending our soil, said Suleiman who rejected any negotiations or security arrangements that ""Israel"" is seeking to get rid of the quagmire caused by its occupation in order to achieve electoral goals".

"Peace must be just and comprehensive through withdrawal from the south and the Golan Heights and solving the existing problems including the right of the Palestinian refugees to return to their homeland", Suleiman added.

"The army chief underlined his intention to stick to the method of his predecessor, president Emile Lahoud, in rebuilding the army and its defense and security performance, besides its role in defending the south and the western Beqaa".

"The general's tour included the southern area command at the Mohammed Zagheib barracks in Sidon and the bases along the confrontation lines with the "Israeli" enemy in East of Sidon and Iqlim al-Touffah"
