Daily Report
 
 
January Manday 25-1999:

Aggressions and Operations (1)
"A South Lebanon Army militiaman was wounded Saturday as Islamic Resistance (IR) bombarded several "Israeli" and SLA positions along the edge of the occupation zone in the western Bekaa.
Retaliatory "Israeli" shelling damaged a number of homes in villages facing the zone".

"The militiaman was wounded and four cars belonging to the SLA were reported destroyed during a mortar bombardment of a position at Ain Qenya. Posts at the Zimraya crossing point and Kawkaba were also targeted by the (IR)".

""Israeli" artillery pounded the area opposite with up to 100 shells, damaging 10 houses in Zillaya and electricity cables which disrupted power to the neighboring villages of Qleia, Dallafa and Yohmor.

In response to the damaged infrastructure, (IR) returned to the western Bekaa Sunday to attack the posts at Ain Qenya, Zimraya and Abu Qamhat".

"The resistance was active elsewhere over the weekend with (IR) attacking the Bir Kallab post at the same time as another squad ambushed an armored patrol with rockets and fired anti-aircraft missiles at helicopter gunships to prevent them from approaching the area".

"Two houses were damaged in Arab Salim in an "Israeli" artillery bombardment"
"The Lebanese Resistance Brigades attacked the positions at Haddatha and Rshaf early Sunday morning. No casualties were reported".

"The five-nation monitoring group is to convene Monday to hear three Lebanese complaints over damage to property in three villages. The damage to buildings and infrastructure in Arab Salim and Zillaya Saturday was expected to be added to the list of complaints".

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
The "Israeli" troops launched the following aggressions South Lebanon and Western Beqaa:
00:45, the surroundings of Haddatha compound subjected to one 120mm shell and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
2:15, one 155mm missile struck Zaatar ravine.
3:10, three 120mm shells hit the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
7:30, Haddatha forest was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
7:40, the peripheries of Haddatha town exposed to five 155mm missiles.
10:50, sixty 155mm missiles slammed into Litani River banks and the surroundings of Qilya village, whereas Bir Dahr area was bombarded by ten 105mm tank rounds.
14:05, Litani River watercourse was targeted by sixteen 155mm missiles.
18:20, Mount Sojod was shelled by five 81mm rounds.
18:55, five 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Touline village.
19:00, Qalawani area between Touline and Qalaweih villages was targeted by three 155mm missiles and the west of Baraasheit town by two others.
Between 21:30 and 22:15, Slouqi Valley came under five 155mm missiles, four 120mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds, whereas two flare shells were fired overhead.
23:10, five 155mm missiles slammed into the area between Maifadoun, Zawtar Gharbieh and Zawtar Sharqieh.

Military Operations (3)
The IR squad of martyrs Fadi Ballouq attacked, at 6:50, Kfar Houna position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct and definite goals.

At the same time, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted Haddatha compound with the suitable weapons scoring direct hits. Meanwhile, another group of LRB targeted Rshaf position with the proper arms chalking up direct goals.

Also, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) squad of martyr Hassan Hammoudi bombarded, at 9:30, Ain Qinya post with the rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and accurate hits.

Ten minutes later, the IR group of martyr Hassan Chokor targeted an enemy security parade at Zimraya position with the appropriate weapons. The militants managed to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Furthermore, the ARU squad of martyrs Khodor Obeid and Naameh Hossaiki bombarded, at 10:15, Abou Qamha emplacement with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. Several blazes had been erupted inside the emplacement as it was shelling the liberated areas of South Lebanon.

At 14:00 also, the IR gunners reiterated targeting Abou Qamha emplacement with the adequate arms scoring certain hits.
Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Saeid Mohammad Hachem and Mohammad Hussein Zeidan targeted, at 18:10, a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Several casualties were inflicted among its ranks, the mujahedeen reported.

At 21:20, and as an "Israeli" foot-patrol reached to an ambush site made by the IR militants near Hojeir Valley, the IR group of martyrs Ziad and Wafiq Zahwi fired medium and light machine gun rounds and hand grenades at the patrol inflicting a number of casualties. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Younes and Ali Mustafa Kahin fired from a close range at an Israeli force as it was trying to back up the attacked patrol inflicting several casualties among its soldiers. The clashes lasted for about one hour during which the "Israeli" soldiers were heard screaming loudly. Meanwhile, the ARU group of martyr Hassan Hammoudi was targeting the enemy reinforcement. At 22:15, the clashes renewed resulting in further "Israeli" casualties. The "Israeli" helicopter gunships interfered to support the attacked forces.

Finally, the IR squad of martyr Bassel Alaaeddine targeted, at 23:35, an "Israeli" force near Qantara position with the proper arms. Direct and definite goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.
