Daily Report
 
 
January Manday 18-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
Yesterday, the "Israeli" occupying forces staged the following aggression on Lebanon's areas:

At 4:40 and 5:05, Baraasheit town and the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal subjected to heavy machine gun fire.
Between 6:00 and 6:20, Mount Sojod was hit by three 155mm missiles and three 81mm rounds.
6:25, twelve 155mm missiles slammed into Qaissieh Valley.
6:35, west of Yater was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
6:45, four 155mm missiles struck Jib Soweid area.
7:45, the peripheries of Jmaijmeh village came under ten 155mm missiles.
8:00, the surroundings of Mashaaroun outpost subjected to an  intense heavy machine gun fire.
12:50, seven 155mm missiles pounded into the perimeters of Haddatha town whereas eight 81mm rounds struck west of Baraasheit compound.
18:35, Mazraat Hamra subjected to three 120mm shells.
20:40, five 120mm shells landed the perimeters of Mlikh village.
Between 2:45 and 23:05, Mount Sojod was targeted by seven 120mm rounds.
20:55, three 120mm rounds struck the peripheries of Loweizeh village.
21:00, Slouqi Valley subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
21:25, two 81mm rounds and seven grenades fell on the perimeters of Mlikh.
21:40, one 120mm shell hit the peripheries of Loweizeh village.
22:45, the southern perimeters of Shaqra came under three missiles, whereas the surroundings of Mashaaroun exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
22:55, Tasseh Well was targeted by two 120mm shells.
23:00, the surroundings of Taibeh outpost exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Military Operations
A squad of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked intensely, at 7:20, Mashaaroun outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate hits.

At 12:25 also, another group of LRB targeted Baraasheit compound with the adequate arms chalking up direct and certain hits. In the meantime, a third group of LRB attacked Rshaf position with the appropriate weapons scoring definite goals.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Hassan Ali Ayoub and Kassem Abdel-Hassan Mallah attacked, at 20:05, Sojod outpost with the suitable arms. The militants managed to chalk up certain and accurate hits. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Abbas Mohammad Hammoud and Mohammad Ali Najem attacked Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals (2)

"However, Amal claimed it wounded four "Israeli" soldiers Sunday when it detonated a roadside bomb as a 12-man patrol drove between the "Israeli" positions of Taybeh and Qantara in the central sector of the occupied zone".

"A statement by the group said one of its fighters was wounded during a clash with the "Israeli" back-up unit ­ which came to assist the "Israeli" patrol ­ and was able to score several casualties in its ranks.

The group said the clash took place soon after it had detonated the bomb at 1 A.M".
"Amal added that a commando managed to return to the site of the clash to pick up the wounded militiaman (3)
