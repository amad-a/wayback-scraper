Daily Report
 
 
January Sanday 24-1999:

Army Center hit, houses damaged in occupation bombardment on western Beqaa (1)
""Israeli" occupation forces today staged more aggressions on western Beqaa areas. The enemy artillery in Zoghle and al-Ahmadieh heavily and randomly bombarded populated neighborhoods in the town of Zellaya. Some houses were damaged along with a Lebanese army center in the shelling.
"Israeli" aggressions also covered the towns of al-Dallafeh and Qilia besides the roads leading to both towns".

"Furthermore, more than 100 155mm rounds hit the Litani River banks and the vicinity of the Libbaya village".

IR Retaliates for "Israeli" aggressions on western Beqaa, attacks occupation outposts in Ain Qinia, Zimrayya, al-Ahmadieh, and Baraasheet (2)
"In retaliation for the "Israeli" aggressions on villages and houses in Western Beqaa, the Islamic Resistance's (IR) squad of Martyr Naji Abu Dayya in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) at 13:30 Saturday attacked enemy movements at the occupation's outpost in al-Ahmadieh. The Mujahedeen used the appropriate weapons in the attack, scoring direct hits".

"At 13:45, IR's squad of Martyr Deeb Bahjeh attacked a collaborator militia force which was fortifying the outpost of Baraasheet. The Mujahedeen scored definite hits in the attack".
"Moreover, IR's squad of Martyrs Assad Allah Shamas and Ahmed Jaafar in ARU at 15:00 p.m. attacked the "Israeli" occupation's outposts in Ain Qinia and Zimrayya with mortars and rocket propelled grenades, scoring definite hits".

Annan recommends renewing UNIFIL mandate (3)
"U.N. Secretary-General Kofi Annan Friday recommended another six-month renewal of the U.N. Interim Force in Lebanon, where he said fighting had increased between "Israeli" occupation forces and their allies and Lebanese resistance".

"During the past six months, fighting in south Lebanon continued at an increased pace and the situation in the area remained volatile and dangerous with a heightened risk of escalation," Annan said in a written report to the Security Council.

"Regrettably, civilians were again killed and injured. As before, UNIFIL did its best to limit the conflict and to protect the inhabitants," he added.

He recommended that the council respond positively to a request from the Lebanese government
and extend UNIFIL's mandate for another six months, until July 31.

UNIFIL, comprising nearly 4,500 troops from eight countries, was established in 1978 to restore the Lebanese government's authority over the southern part of the country after an "Israeli" invasion.
The secretary-general said that "UNIFIL's contribution to stability and the protection it affords the population in the area remain important."

"Annan's recommendation is expected to be approved by the Security Council at the end of the month with the full text of the report being released afterward".

Fornier discussed status of al-Khiam detainees with agent Lahd (4)
"International Committee of the Red Cross' (ICRC) representative in Lebanon Henri Fornier, accompanied by ICRC representative in the occupied zone Bshara Hanna, yesterday met collaborator Antoine Lahd", ICRC sources in the occupied zone said.

"Discussions focused on the affairs of the al-Khiam detention center and the health situation of the detainees in the notorious camp", the sources added.

Chilean government Secretary calls for withdrawal of "Israeli" "cancer" from south (5)
"Secretary of the Chilean government Claudio Wehbe today visited the hometown of his forefathers in Nabatieh".

"Wehbe started his visit with meeting the town's municipal council and the town's governor Justice Mahmoud al-Mawla who welcomed the guest".

"Chile backs Lebanon in its call for implementing UN Security Council resolution 425 and the withdrawal of "the "Israeli" cancer" from the south", Wehbe said.
"Then Wehbe visited the mausoleum of late scientist Hassan Kamel al-Sabbah and the grave of his grandfather Youssef Wehbe in Hai al-Sarai in Nabatieh".
