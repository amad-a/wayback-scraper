Daily Report
 
 
January Wednesday 27-1999:
 
Introduction
Two "Israeli" occupation soldiers were wounded as the Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen rushed upon the so-called "Israeli" elite force of Egoz unit at Kfar Houna area. The "Israeli" unit was in its way to carry out new aggressions on the liberated area of South Lebanon. Mounting "Israeli" human losses and an increasingly popular occupation have intensified calls in "Israel" for a pullout from the south.

Military Operations (1)
The IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Zaatar and Zahi Haidar Ahmad ambushed, at 6:00, an "Israeli" commando unit as it was trying to advance to the perimeters of Kfar Houna on the purpose of carrying out certain aggressions. When the "Israeli" commando reached to the ambush site, the militants opened fire from a close range and inflicted a number of casualties among its ranks. There were four certain casualties whose screams could be clearly heard. Several "Israeli" helicopter gunships interfered to back up the ambushed unit, through showering the scene, and to evacuate the casualties.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Merhi and Mohammad Al-Mousawi attacked, at 10:30, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Moreover, the IR militants spotted, at 10:55, Ahmadieh position. Immediately afterwards, the squad of martyr Hussein Hakim targeted enemy movements with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals. 


The IR squad of martyrs Ali Ghamloush and Ahmad Salman attacked, at 17:00, enemy movements at Tohorra outpost with the adequate arms. Direct and definite goals were scored, the mujahedeen  reporeted.

Also, the IR men showered, at 10:00, the perimeters of Qalaweih village.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Nasser Hawi and Ahmad Dbouk staged, at 20:45, a 25-minute-attack at Salaah position. The militants, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, managed to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Quarter an hour later, the IR squad of martyrs Jamil Shahla and Assaad Tabaja targeted Qsair position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Ali Shqeir and Suleiman Yahia attacked, at 21:30, enemy movements at Hojeir Valley. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants chalked up direct and definite goals.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Hussein Abdallah and Mohammad Ali Itani attacked a motorized force as it was about entering Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score certain hits. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Hussein El-Atrash and Abdel-Majid Karaki targeted a collaborator engineering force as it was travelling on the road between Rihan and Sojod. The militants, who used the suitable arms, managed to chalk up direct goals.

Also, the IR men reiterated, at 22:05, targeting the remaining retreating force inflicting more casualties among its ranks. The Israeli helicopter gunships tried to back up the attacked force, but they were repulsed by the mujahedeen.

At 22:45, the IR group of martyr Ramzi Daifallah Abou Salim repeated attacking the Israeli force at Hojeir Valley with the proper weapons scoring definite goals.

For its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigade (LRB) attacked, at 18:00, Hardoun outpost with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals, while at the same time, another LRB group was targeting Tair Harfa position with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

Also, a third group of LRB staged, at 20:00, a swift attack on Qantara compound with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored and many casualties were inflicted, the fighters reported.
 

Army chief calls for constant caution and alert (2)
"Army Commander Gen Michel Suleiman called on his troops not to underestimate the "real dangers encircling us, especially as the enemy's ambitions in our soil and water are no secret to anyone, although the enemy is trying to conceal them while waiting for the appropriate time."

"Speaking at a graduation ceremony in Baalbek, Captain Mohammed Abbas who represented Suleiman called on the army troops to "maintain a permanent state of caution and readiness, based on our military creed which clearly identifies the enemy, "Israel"."

18:20, Haddatha woodland subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

23:00, four 155mm rounds slammed into the peripheries of Touline village.
23:05, five 155mm missiles landed the area called Houranieh beneath Ghandourieh village.

FTR condemned "Israeli" aggressions, demanded freeing detainees (4)
"Hizbullah's parliamentary bloc, Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR), today (yesterday) held its ordinary meeting which was chaired by the bloc's head MP Sayyed Ibrahim al-Sayyed and attended by all the bloc members".

"The bloc issued a communiqué in which it hailed "the Jihad of the Islamic Resistance's heroes who are confronting the "Israeli" repeated brutal aggressions, especially the latest botched infiltration attempts."

IR's Mujahedeen have been courageously intercepting the occupation's aggressions and protecting their people, the bloc said, condemning the "Israeli" aggressions."
