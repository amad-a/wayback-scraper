Daily Report
 
 
January Saturday 30-1999:

Bomb with Hebrew inscriptions explodes in Habboush(1)
"A roadside bomb was detonated near Nabatieh before dawn Friday. The explosion caused severe damage to the road and adjacent power cables. No casualties were reported, though", a security official said.

"The blast at al-Sit Zoubeidah Bridge on the Arab Salim-Habboush road took place around 2 A.M. and left a 50-centimeter-deep hole in the ground".

"Around dawn, soldiers and policemen arrived in the area to begin their investigation. They said the TNT explosive was camouflaged in fiberglass to resemble rock; its weight was estimated at 25 kilograms, according to the source".

"Since the camouflage replicated that of at least six other roadside bombs planted by "Israeli" occupation troops in recent years, the source said the explosive was almost certainly planted by them.

The source added: The explosive was probably detonated by remote control from the nearby Sweida post, about a kilometer away, which is manned by occupation troops.
"The explosion took place while an "Israeli" MK reconnaissance drone was flying over the area", the
source said.

"Israeli" Aggressions (2)
The "Israeli" occupation troops staged the following aggressions:

00:45, several flare shells were fired over Qantara position.
Between 1:00 and 4:00, Slouqi Valley was bombarded by four 120mm shells.
17:40, Beit Yahoun village subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
21:00, the perimeters of Nabatieh El-Fawqa came under several 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
21:20, one flare shell was fired over Ilman area.
22:55, two 155mm missiles struck Sojod village.
23:00, Shayar Sakhri near Soweida outpost was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
23:05, several 155mm missiles and 81mm rounds fell on the perimeters of Nabatieh El-Fawqa. At the same time, the west of Yater village was targeted by three 155mm missiles, whereas Habboush Bridge was hit by several others.
23:10, five 155mm missiles slammed into Srobbeen ravine.
23:15, six 155mm missiles pounded into the peripheries of Haddatha town, while at the same time, four 120mm shells hit Litani River watercourse.

Military Operations (3)
At 19:30, the IR squad of martyrs Mahmoud Khodor Baghdadi and Tahseen Ali Shraifeh attacked an enemy motorized patrol at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct goals.

For its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance brigades (LRB) attacked, at 12:40, enemy vehicles at Soweida outpost with the proper weapons chalking up direct goals.

Also, another squad of LRB attacked, at 22:45, Hemayed position of the collaborators' 80th Brigade chalking up direct hits. Meanwhile, a third group of the LRB targeted Rshaf position with adequate arms. Direct goals were hits, the fighters reported.
