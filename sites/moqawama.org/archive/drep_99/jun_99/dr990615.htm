Daily Report
 
 
June Tuesday 15-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese liberated areas as reported in a communiqué issued by the Islamic Resistance (IR):

At 00:05 and 00:09, the "Israeli" warplanes staged three sorties into Mlikh area.
00:12, the "Israeli" jets raided twice into Oqmata area firing eight air-to-ground rockets.
Between 4:05 and 4:25, Mount Zaatar was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
4:40, five 81mm rounds hit the peripheries of Aita El-Jabal.
4:45, Beit Yahoun forest was targeted by three 81mm rounds.
12:10, three 155mm missiles were fired at Mount Sojod.

Martyr(2)
The Islamic Resistance announced, proudly, the martyrdom of the martyr Imad Hussein Haidar Ahmad, born at Ras Osta-Jbeil in 1975, single, subjected to a number of military and cultural courses, participated with several military operations. He was martyred on 12-6-1999 in the course of the heroic operation carried out at Mount Abou Rkab.

Four Lebanese detainees released, suffering continues in Khiam prison(3)
"The collaborating militia Monday released four Lebanese detainees from the Khiam prison, they are: Fouad Abbas Morad from Aitaroun, Nimr Hussein Ghaddaf from Dibbeen, Fatima Abdul Nabi from Aita al-Shaeb and Abdul Massih Nimr from al-Qulaia".

"However, the suffering of some 120 Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators' prisons is still going on.

"Minors and women are among the detainees which are all sentenced without trials".
