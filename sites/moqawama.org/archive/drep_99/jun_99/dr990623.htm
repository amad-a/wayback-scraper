Daily Report
 
Jun Wednesday 23-1999:

Introduction
After a large number of the "Israeli" allied militiamen rejected to man it fearing of more attacks launched by the Islamic Resistance (IR), the collaborators resorted to destroying Beit Yahoun outpost.

Meanwhile, it was transpired that there are doubts that the electricity networks are a new "Israeli" artillery target for many villages' posts and transformers were hit recently in South Lebanon.

Elsewhere, a US researcher Magnus Ranstrop stressed that Hizbullah made huge achievements and profits by using the Internet network besides its field and psychological war.

Collaborator Lahd evacuated Beit Yahoun outpost(1)
"The Lahdi collaborating militia destroyed the Beit Yahoun outpost and leveled the ramparts but kept a nominal crossing point and transferred the real check point to Saf al-Hawa near the town of Bint Jbeil.

This move came after a large number of collaborators refused to man the outpost and some others deserted to liberated areas and surrendered to the Lebanese authorities or the Resistance, preferring to settle their problems with the Judiciary instead of serving the enemy or getting killed".

"The Beit Yahoun outpost was stormed by the Islamic Resistance a month ago. The Mujahedeen succeeded to capture a militiamen and an M113 armored personnel carrier, in addition to killing or injuring the manning force".

Electricity , New "Israeli" target(3)
"Many southern villages in Lebanon were blacked out when "Israeli" occupation's 120 and 155 mm rounds fell on electricity posts and transformers Sunday".

"Electricité du Liban (EDL) announced that most of the electricity networks were damaged in the targeted villages."

"EDL technicians and workers started repairing the damage yesterday at noon, after the situation calmed down, a source at EDL said.

"There are doubts the electricity networks are a new "Israeli" artillery target, especially that the Lebanese government is having difficulties in constructing and repairing electricity network", the source added.

Jewish-US researcher admit Hizbullah supremacy(4)
"US researcher Magnus Ranstrop stressed that Hizbullah made huge achievements and profits by using the Internet network besides its field and psychological war".

"Ranstrop, whose origin is Jewish, said that Hizbullah has four sites on the Internet displaying news in Arabic, English and Hebrew".

"The "Israelis" have failed to jam and close down these sites by sending tens of thousands of e-mails, Ranstrop stated, adding that Hizbullah was able to repel these attempts"

"Hizbullah, Ranstrop noted, is conducting the confrontations on both sides, militarily and psychologically, considering that videotaping the operations is the best proof that Hizbullah is bound to win both wars together".

"ISRAELI" OCCUPATION SOLDIER WOUNDED IN SOUTHERN Lebanon(5)
"An Israeli soldier was wounded when Hizbullah resistance guerrillas attacked the outpost of Bayada in south Lebanon. A pro-Israeli militia source said the soldier was moderately injured in the attack".

Military Operations(6)
The IR team of martyrs Rodwan Sami Rashid and Khalil Ibrahim Said targeted, at 00:10, an "Israeli" engineering force as it was fortifying Beit Yahoun outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain goals.
