Daily Report
 
 
June Sunday 6-1999:

Lahdi collaborators' clashes in occupied zone(1)
"Witnesses coming from the occupied zone in south Lebanon said Lahdi collaborators clashed among themselves near Khiam".

"The collaborators used light and medium arms in the clash that resulted in one death and several injuries in the collaborators' ranks".

"Witnesses added that the feuding militiamen were among the ones who withdrew from Jezzine, stating that the fight broke up over confiscating houses for the retreating collaborators.

"In the meantime, conflicts surfaced in the ranks of the collaborators who support the militias' top officer agent Antoine Lahd on the one hand and those who support the commander of the so-called western regiment agent Aqel Hashem, who is a likely candidate to replace Lahd.

"Reports said collaborators who support agent Hashem are storming houses in several villages in the Marjeyoun district and forcing the residents to sign a petition demanding that Hashem replace Lahd.

"Moreover, reports from the occupied zone stated that a fight in the town of Ain Qinya (Hasbaya district) between some residents and collaborators developed into fire exchange.

"On the other hand, the "Israeli" occupation forces prevented ten collaborating officers from fleeing the occupied zone via Naqoura port and returned them to the towns of Qliaa and Marjeyoun.

"Elsewhere, Father Boulos Khawand, the Head of the Mashmoushe Notre-Dame Monastery, said, so far, more than a hundred Lahdi militiamen have turned themselves in to the Lebanese army's checkpoints in Bisri, Jezzine and Kfar Falous".

"Father Khawand added that the collaborators' surrender process will be over by Tuesday".

Four Mothers demands a unilateral withdrawal from South Lebanon(2)
"On the 17th anniversary of "Israel's" invasion of Lebanon. "Four Mothers,'' a group that demands a unilateral withdrawal from southern Lebanon, marked the day by retracing the invasion route taken by the "Israeli" army in 1982".

"We want to remind people how long this war has gone on,'' rally organizer,'' Linda Ben-Zvi told Reuters.

"About 500 "Israelis", including parents of occupation soldiers killed by Resistance men in south Lebanon, joined a motorcade of buses and cars that drove from occupied Palestine's northern border to Tel Aviv to protest the continued "Israeli" occupation in the area".

"Ben-Zvi said "Four Mothers'' was in favor of a speedy withdrawal from Lebanon, with or without Syrian involvement".

"We would very much like to negotiate with Syria but we are afraid a protracted negotiation would give Syria the right to draw a timetable for us to get out of Lebanon,'' she said.

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The IR released a communiqué in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions which launched on Lebanon's towns and villages:
4:40, the surroundings of Shamaa and Mashaaroun position exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
5:10, Jib Soweid area between Mansouri and Majdal Zoun was shelled by ten 155mm missiles.
5:20, seven 155mm missiles slammed into the area between the villages of Zibqeen and Henneih, whereas the surroundings of Tair Harfa outpost came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 7:00 and 7:45, the peripheries of Jibaa town came under thirteen 155mm missiles.
7:05, three 155mm missiles were fired at Qaissieh Valley.
10:50, the "Israeli" warplanes fired two air-to-ground rockets into the perimeters of Jibaa.
15:05, six 81mm smoke rounds hit the surroundings of Qantara position.
16:10, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
Between 16:15 and 16:25, the perimeters of Haddatha came under five 155mm missiles, three 120mm shells and two 81mm rounds.
16:35, Aita El-Jabal subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
18:40, the surroundings of Taibeh position exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
19:25, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the outskirts of Haddatha.
19:35, Mount Sojod was bombarded by eleven 155mm missiles, whereas the surroundings of Sojod outpost subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
19:50, four 155mm missiles landed the outskirts of Jibaa town.
22:40, the surroundings of Houla position came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Military Operations(4)
The IR team of martyrs of Hussein Trabolssi and Hussein Hassan Hayek targeted, at 5:10, Radar post with the appropriate weapons striking a radar launching pad and causing damage to its fortifications.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Ali Rida attacked violently Tair Harfa position where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Also, the IR group of martyr Imad Al-Salman ambushed, at 6:30, an "Israeli" foot-squad near Mlikh village. The mujahedeen opened fire from their machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades from a close range inflicting several casualties.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Ali Hussein Shibli and Ali Moussa Abdel-Karim attacked, at 16:05, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Haddatha compound. The militants, who used the suitable arms, were able to chalk up direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Mahmoud Zaraket and Bassel Ala-eddine targeted, at 19:30, an "Israeli" collaborator patrol as its traveling on the road leading to Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Zoheir Kobeissi and Hussein Trabolssi attacked, at midnight exactly, enemy movements near Blat post with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
