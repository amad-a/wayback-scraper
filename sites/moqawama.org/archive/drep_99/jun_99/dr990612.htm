Daily Report
 
 
June Saturday 12-1999:

Dead "Israeli" officer was Egoz unit commander(1)
"Israeli" military sources admitted Friday that the dead "Israeli" officer, who was killed during the Oqmata confrontations with he Islamic Resistance (IR), was a squad commander in the Egoz elite unit named Roey Keller, adding that he died shortly after he was seriously wounded by the fire of Hizbullah's Mujahedeen".

""Israeli" media unveiled that it took the "Israeli" enemy several hours to evacuate the casualties from the scene at Oqmata, due to the heavy fire by Hizbullah's Mujahedeen.

UN imposed compensation on "Israel" for Qana massacre(2)
"The United Nations General Assembly Wednesday, endorsed a resolution demanding that "Israel" pay $1,2 millions in compensations for bombarding Qana during the April 1996 aggression".

"Shelling the UN base in Qana took the lives of 107 people".

"The US and "Israel" voted against the resolution while 42 countries abstained - among them all European countries - and 74 backed it".

"During the session, "Israeli" delegate at the UN, Dori Gold attacked the Lebanese government because it is not willing to discuss security arrangements with "Israel" allowing an "Israeli" pullout from Lebanon".

Sheikh Yazbek: Zionists dreaming of unrest after Jezzine Liberation(3)
"Representative of Imam Khamenei in Lebanon, Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek Friday, held the "Israeli" enemy and the collaborators responsible for the Sidon Palais de Justice crime that "was aimed at destabilizing the nation's security to create the image that Lebanon has neither security nor stability."

Messing with the country's security, Sheikh Yazbek added, targets to drag the nation back into destabilization, for the enemy is afraid of the Lebanese unity and harmony among the people, government and the Resistance in confronting it".

"The Zionists dreamt that unrest will prevail in Lebanon following Jezzine's liberation", Sheikh Yazbek concluded.

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The "Israeli" occupying forces staged the following aggressions on Lebanon's towns and villages:
2:20, Mount Sojod was targeted by five 120mm shells.
4:10, the surroundings of Qsair position exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
7:20, four 155mm missiles were fired at the west of Yater.
12:00, the road of Sawan area was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
19:25, four 122mm missiles landed the area between Houla and Shaqra.
Between 21:15 and 21:50, the perimeters of Majdal Selem came under seven 155mm missiles and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
21:45, Majdal Selem exposed to heavy machine gun fire damaging a motorcycle.
Between 21:50 and 22:20, the surroundings of Sojod outpost subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
22:15, Haddatha woodland exposed to three 81mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
22:15, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
22:35, each of the west of Yater and Jib Soweid area came under three 155mm missiles.
23:10, Mount Sojod was bombarded by five 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(5)
The IR team of martyr Hussein Ghassani targeted, at 1:00, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were scored, the militants reported.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 10:15, a Lahdi engineering force near Zimraya position. Shortly afterwards, the IR squad of martyr Ahmad Zaiter in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted it chalking up direct hits.

As an enemy motorized force was entering Sojod outpost, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Ahmad Trabolssi and Ghassan Ghanem attacked it, at 22:00, with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.
