Daily Report
 
 
June Wednesday 9-1999:

"Israel' weighed chance of militia collapse(1)
""Israel" made contingency plans last week for the collapse of its client militia in Lebanon, "Israeli" senior war officials said Monday".

"They said the plans were discussed at an urgent security cabinet meeting while the collaborating militia withdrew under fire from the Jezzine enclave in Lebanon".

"The officials, who spoke on condition they not be identified, said the security cabinet approved steps war minister Moshe Arens would take in case the situation deteriorated.

"In one scenario, the cabinet considered the possibility that militiamen would abandon their outposts en masse in the occupation zone.

"The officials did not say how "Israel" would respond to such a development. The war ministry declined official comment.

"The collaborating militia completed its withdrawal from Jezzine last Thursday and its 2,000 agents continue helping "Israel" patrol the occupied zone.

"In the meantime, "Israeli" enemy expects tougher times in the occupied zone regarding security.

A source of the so-called "Israeli" army northern command, however, said the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborating militia will continue fighting Hizbullah.

"The source denied that more than 200 militiamen deserted in Jezzine, stating that around ninety agents have moved to the occupied zone and some forty others will quit the militia.

"Nevertheless, the head of Mashmoushe Monastery Father Boulos Khawand said more than 180 of the 203 militiamen turned themselves in so far to the Lebanese army, pointing out that the surrender process will be completed by Tuesday evening or Wednesday morning at the most.

"In addition, the Lebanese Army's Intelligence Directorate referred 22 Lahdi collaborators, who had surrendered to the army, to Military Prosecutor General Nasri Lahoud".

"The directorate also referred Sharbel Khawand, brother of Father Boulos, to the same court on charge of collaborating with the "Israeli" enemy".

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)
In its statement, the IR mentioned the following aggressions which staged by the "Israeli" occupation forces:
00:05, the perimeters of Arab Salim came under several rounds and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
00:15, six 120mm shells hit the peripheries of Mlikh, whereas the surroundings of Bir Kallab exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
2:55, two 81mm rounds struck the area between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun towns.
15:00, Mount Abou Rkab was bombarded by twelve 120mm shells.
Between 15:30 and 16:35, the perimeters of Mlikh village came under six 120mm shells.
16:35, three 60mm rounds were fired at Aita El-Jabal.
17:50, four 155mm missiles slammed into the peripheries of Jibaa town.
18:50, one 105mm round landed near farmers at Qabrikha but they miraculously escaped unscathed.
21:10, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by four 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(3)
The IR squad of martyrs Habib Tarhini and Hussein Ballout attacked, at 6:00, Dabsheh outpost with the adequate arms striking its southern fortification and igniting a blaze inside it.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Ali Sharif Osman and Youssef Ali Nassereddine targeted, at 17:50, an "Israeli" infantry force near Mlikh village. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score direct hits.

Earlier, two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 5:30, the positions of Dabsheh and Soweida with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct and definite goals.
