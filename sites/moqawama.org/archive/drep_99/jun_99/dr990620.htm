Daily Report
 
Jun Sunday 20-1999:

Enemy Navy aggressed Tyre fishermen(1)
"The "Israeli" enemy Navy Saturday warned Lebanese fisherman in Tyre not to sail out with their fishing boats because they will be fired at without previous notice".

"The warning was swiftly carried out as the enemy's gunboats prevented the fisherman from sailing out to the Tyre water by shooting at them with heavy machine guns when some of them attempted to go beyond a one kilometer distance from seafront".

"Head of fishermen's syndicate in Tyre Mohammed Baowab called on the Lebanese government to act and make the necessary contacts to stop the enemy from carrying out its warning which violates all international customs and laws".

Sweden: Lahdi collaborators not welcomed(2)
"The Swedish Immigration Department announced that 40 Lahdi collaborators lately traveled from the occupied zone in Southern Lebanon and applied for political asylum in Sweden".

"Ulf Kloflet, an official at the Immigration Department said to the media that his country does not sympathize with those application, for the applicants do not face the death penalty in Lebanon".

"According to available information from Sweden, the law there does not prohibit handing over those collaborators to the Lebanese authority".

"The collaborating militiamen are arriving in Sweden by using Lebanese and "Israeli" passports with "special visit" visas stamped on them".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions as reported in a communiqué issued by the IR:

2:10, three 81mm rounds landed Slouqi Valley.
6:50, four 155mm missiles hit the perimeters of Touline village.
7:05, Mount Sojod was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
7:30, five 155mm missiles were fired at Qaissieh Valley.
Between 7:05 and 15:35, west of Yater village was bombarded by fourteen 155mm missiles.
10:15, twenty two 155mm and 160mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Henneih, Jibal El-Botom and Yater villages.
10:25, the area between Chaitieh and Zibqeen was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
16:00, the perimeters of Haddatha town subjected to one 81mm round, whereas the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
16:15, three 155mm missiles struck west of Haddatha town.
18:45, seven 155mm missiles landed the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom.
At 19:10 and 19:30, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the area between Jibal El-Botom and Chaitieh firing twelve air-to-ground rockets.
19:25, two 155mm missiles hit east of Mazraat Hamra.
21:00, the peripheries of Touline came under four 155mm missiles.
21:05, four 120mm shells landed Sawaneh village whereas several others were unleashed at the outskirts of Qabrikha.
Between 21:10 and 24:00, Slouqi Valley was bombarded by six 120mm shells, four 155mm missiles and four 81mm rounds.
Between 22:30 and 22:40, the perimeters of Qantara-Adsheit Al-Qsair came under six 120mm shells and one 81mm round.
24:00, one 81mm round hit the surroundings of Qantara position whereas a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the surroundings of Mashaaroun position.

Military Operations(4)
The IR militants observed, at 13:40, a Lahdi engineering force near Kfar Houna position. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Kassem Khreis and Rabih Khodor Maatouk attacked it with the rocket-propelled grenades barring it from achieving its goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Aiman Al-Baalbacki and Ibrahim Manaa attacked, at 15:55, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Haddatha position with the proper weapons chalking up direct hits.

Moreover, the IR men spotted, at 18:40, enemy movements near Blat post. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Trabolssi and Ali El-Assaad targeted them with the rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct goals.

Earlier, two units of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted, at 10:00, the positions of Blat and Tair Harfa with the suitable weapons scoring direct goals.
