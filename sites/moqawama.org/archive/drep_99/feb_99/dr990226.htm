Daily Report
February Friday 26-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Head of Politburo MP Mohammed Raad decried firmly the U.S. attitude, which reflects Washington's solidarity with the "Israeli" terrorism and provides political cover for "Israeli" aggression against Lebanon.

Meanwhile, Lebanon's complaint to the UN Security Council over the annexation of Arnoun by "Israeli" Forces remained on hold Thursday, but President Emile Lahoud said he is concerned with resolving the Arnoun crisis with all possible means to reach the desired result which is freeing the village.

Two "Israeli" soldiers wounded in a "military base explosion"
Once again, the Islamic Resistance (IR) men leveled, at 12:20, new blow breaking through all the enemy's tactics and steps. The militants had surprised the posts and patrols of the "Israeli" occupation troops deep inside the occupied zone and along with the sectors of Abbad, Qabaa, Markaba and Odaisseh reaching to the Lebanese-Palestinian border. The enemy suffered a state of confusion and embarrassment enforcing it to bring more military reinforcements into the scene coming from the positions of Meskaf-Am and Abbad so as to secure an artillery cover for its attacked forces.

Meanwhile, the IR militants was attacking Mashaaroun position and the HQ of the 70th Brigade Command. The fierce clashes lasted throughout the day. Also, the mujahedeen ambushed a common "Israeli"-Lahdi patrol inside Markaba town inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. The "Israeli" warplanes raided, in a clear indication of its failure, into the perimeters of Markaba firing several air-to-ground rockets. This is the first time in which the "Israeli" warplanes coerce to shell an occupied area adjacent to Palestine's border.

Furthermore, the "Israeli" helicopters staged several sorties and raked a vast areas of Markaba, Bani Hayan and Tallousa during the confrontations. All the IR teams returned safely to their bases after inflicting many casualties among the "Israeli" soldiers.

Earlier, the IR team of martyr Ali Al-Bostani targeted, at 9:05, Qantara compound with the rocket-propelled grenades striking a number of its fortifications.

At 11:30, also, the IR squad of martyr Said Chehab attacked the position of the "Israeli" 70th Brigade Command at Markaba with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying some of its defenses. The smoke columns were seen emanating from it.

At 13:00 later, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Aloueih and Bassem Kashakesh targeted Qantara compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyr Yehia Farran attacked, at 14:30, the "Israeli" post of Taibeh position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Nabil Akhdar and Ghassan Ghanem targeted, at 16:10, enemy force as it was beefing up Sojod outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Twenty minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Abdel-Wahed and Jihad Shbeeb attacked enemy force as it was fortifying Bir Kallab outpost, which was attacked the day before. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to score definite goals.

However, two "Israeli" soldiers were seriously wounded last night in an explosion at a military base in the center of occupied Palestine, "Israeli" media said.

The explosion was defined by "Israeli" military sources as a "work accident" rather than a "training accident."

An investigation was launched into the circumstances of the incident.

It's worth noting that the enemy always declares casualties attributed to various incidents following IR operations" (2)

Hizbullah denies fighters killed (3)
"Hizbullah Thursday denied "Israeli" claims that the occupation forces had killed four resistance fighters in retaliation for an ambush earlier this week in which three "Israeli" officers were slain.

In a series of clashes Wednesday against an "Israeli" position near Markaba in the central sector of the occupation zone, Hizbullah only lost contact with one resistance fighter, a spokesman from the group told The Daily Star".

"Speaking on condition of anonymity, he denied that there were any other losses among fighters who took part in the operation. But a Lebanese security source said two Hizbullah fighters had been wounded in the attack and that the group had managed to rescue them from the scene.

"The attack came two days after Hizbullah guerrillas killed three "Israeli" officers and wounded five soldiers in an ambush in the occupied zone early Tuesday morning. The dead, who included the commander of an elite paratroop reconnaissance unit, were the first "Israelis" killed this year in south Lebanon"

Syria hails Resistance operation in South Lebanon (4)
"Syrian newspapers Wednesday hailed the operation staged by the Islamic Resistance in South Lebanon Tuesday and pledged to exert every effort as to liberate occupied Arab territories.

''The operation comes to underline that no security or settlement will be established in the region as long as the "Israeli" occupation of Arab territories is standing,'' Syrian al-Baath paper said.

"Three "Israeli" "elite" officers were killed and another dozen were wounded when Islamic Resistance men surprised and clashed with "Israeli" occupation troops in the Western Beqaa.

""Israel" perhaps possesses a military force that can strike here and there, but the "Israeli" leadership, either on purpose or due to its ignorance, closes an eye on the fact that the region's peoples have the right and that they own the land that was occupied due to aggression,'' said al-Baath.

The daily stressed that the region's peoples will not keep silent vis-a-vis ""Israel's"" daily practices against peaceful inhabitants in South Lebanon and other occupied Arab lands.

"For its part, the Tishrin daily vowed that Syria would not submit to the occupation.

''"Israel" has nothing to do but to pullout it forces with no conditions from South Lebanon and the Golan Heights in addition to sticking to just and comprehensive peace,'' said Tishrin.

"Al-Thawra newspaper criticized ""Israel's"" annexation of the village of Arnoun in South Lebanon last week and said "Israelis" are wrong if they believe such acts would harvest any fruits.

The paper said "Israelis" are one thousand times wrong if they think that such practices will pass without any direct or indirect price on the long or short run.

"Resistance at home or abroad will remain legal and continuous protected by the international law and international legality as long as land is occupied and rights are usurped,'' the paper said.

Hizbullah denounced U.S. pressure on Lebanon concerning Arnoun's affair (5)
"Hizbullah condemned U.S. pressure on Lebanon to refrain from filing a complaint to the Security Council and urged the government to refuse to bow to it."

We firmly condemn the U.S. stance which reflects Washingtons solidarity with "Israeli" terrorism and provides political cover for "Israeli" aggression against Lebanon, said the head of Hizbullahs politburo, Nabatieh MP Mohammed Raad.

The U.S. position is aimed at preventing Lebanon from filing a complaint to the Security Council to avoid embarrassing Washington and forcing it to use its veto against any resolution in favor of Lebanon, he said.

He accused the United States of trying to gain time for "Israel" to make the annexation of Arnoun a
fait accompli.

U.S. pressure is aimed at turning the annexation of Arnoun into a fait accompli. The government should dismiss this pressure and insist on filing a complaint to the Security Council and should be aware of any attempt to turn the Monitoring Group into a political entity. We should make sure that this group sticks to its original mandate which is to ensure a mechanism to prevent "Israel" from attacking civilians, he said.

Cabinet assures following-up Arnoun crisis till the very end (6)
"Lebanons complaint to the UN Security Council over the annexation of Arnoun by "Israeli" forces remained on hold Thursday with official sources saying the government was concerned that such a motion would overshadow its quest for the implementation of Resolution 425".

The government cannot limit its action to the Arnoun issue and ignore its long-standing demand for unconditional "Israeli" withdrawal under Resolution 425. The issue of Arnoun is a part of a larger problem, a source told The Daily Star.

Lebanon isnt hesitating to file a complaint but is exercising caution, awaiting the results of consultations with Security Councils five permanent members. Thats why the government called on the Monitoring Group of the 1996 April Understanding to discuss the problem, because Lebanon is aware of the serious situation created by the annexation of Arnoun and the possible repercussions it could have in the Security Council, the source continued.

The source was referring to a threat by the United States to veto any new UN resolution that would be in favor of Lebanons position.

"The prime minister said that Lebanons ambassador to the UN, Samir Mubarak, had informed him of the results of the consultations he had with the members of the Security Council.

The results are not final. We are continuing our contacts in New York to achieve an international stance supporting Lebanons national interests, Mr. Hoss said.

Kesrouan MP Rushayd Khazen said President Emile Lahoud was adamant that the annexation of Arnoun be reversed and is awaiting the results of U.S. mediation.

General Lahoud has focused his efforts on the need to free Arnoun from "Israeli" occupation through all possible international means. Lebanon is following up international reactions to avoid the possibility of getting nowhere if the U.S. fails to secure a diplomatic solution, Mr. Khazen said.

This wait-and-see approach came as unwelcome news to Parliaments Defense and Security Committee which called Thursday for immediate action at the Security Council.

The committee considers that the annexation of Arnoun into the occupied zone creates a very serious precedent that could be repeated elsewhere.

Therefore the government should file an urgent complaint to the UN and call for an urgent meeting of the Security Council to condemn "Israel", regardless of whether any veto will be used against it, said the head of the committee, Western Bekaa MP Sami Khatib.

Defense Minister Ghazi Zeaiter represented the Cabinet during the committees meeting at Nijmeh Square and explained the governments position. The

the committee members argued that Monitoring Group was not the proper forum for discussion of problems related to Lebanese territorial sovereignty violated by the "Israeli" occupation.

The annexation of Arnoun into the occupied zone is not part of the groups mandate, which includes protecting civilians and monitoring fighting in the south, Mr. Khatib said.

By annexing Arnoun into the occupied zone, "Israel" changed the border of that zone and expanded it. Therefore, "Israel" could annex the villages of south Lebanon one after another to force us to accept the implementation of Resolution 425 under certain conditions that we have already refused.

Former Foreign Minister Fares Boueiz stressed the need to file a complaint to the UN after calling for an urgent meeting of Arab foreign ministers.

This is not in contradiction with U.S. efforts to reverse the annexation of Arnoun. If these efforts succeed, Lebanon could withdraw its complaint, Mr. Boueiz observed during a news conference.

Before filing the complaint, we should call for an urgent meeting of Arab foreign ministers to file another complaint in the name of Arab states. The Security Council is the first and only international authority in charge of keeping international security. By refraining from filing a complaint, we are relieving the council of its duties and responsibilities toward Lebanon.

Meanwhile, army commander General Michel Suleiman inspected military positions in Tyre and Nabatieh.

The annexation of Arnoun provides new evidence of "Israeli" aggression against Lebanese territory and sovereignty, he told the troops.

"Former Egyptian Defense Minister Colonel Amin Howeidi, who held the post in the 1960s, visited Arnoun to express his solidarity with the residents".

I do not represent the Egyptian government, I am here in my capacity as an Egyptian and Arab citizen, Colonel Howeidi told The Daily Star.

"Israel" has been living in a large ghetto over the last 50 years and it wants to make Arnoun a small ghetto and punish it because "Israel's" security is threatened by resistance attacks, he said. Dont wait for American intervention, just step foward and move the barbed wires, he said.

Cat and mouse in Lebanon (8)
A combination of political error and military weakness is causing the continuing casualties in Lebanon. The army is imprisoned by outmoded concepts and the politicians are doing all they can to avoid a settlement

"At the Systems Analysis Center of the IDF* General Staff Planning Division, life, like death, is a computer game. Software developed over the last decade allows unit and formation commanders, commanding generals and general staff chiefs - and if they want to, defense ministers and prime ministers - to move corps around and destroy them, courtesy of a standard, army-issue mouse. The developers of the software, which has also been purchased by the armies of Switzerland and Singapore, say that although the officers sit in front of the computer and are not in any physical danger, they still experience anxiety, surprise, resourcefulness and confusion.At the Systems Analysis Center, they are still wary of analyses that lead directly to political conclusions, although seemingly innocent research also tends to lead in that direction. For budgetary reasons, the IDF must keep maintenance costs down and maintain a ceiling on stocks of ammunition, spare parts and fuel (and also training exercises, which require engine, sea and flight hours).

"The IDF, for lack of choice, recognizes the weakness of standing power, and tries to make up for this weakness by honing its striking power. The clash of paratrooper reconnaissance troops with a small Hezbollah force this week demonstrated, once again, that our striking power is not infallible. One could easily imagine the public's response if the numbers had been reversed and three "Israelis" had defeated a much larger enemy force. In the 1950s, Unit 101 and the paratroopers were celebrated for achieving victories like these".

"Times have changed and our enemies are different, but the earth shakes when operations go wrong and casualties are suffered by elite frameworks like Sayeret Matkal, paratrooper reconnaissance troops, Flotilla 13, the Mossad and the Shin Bet. The naval commando has never really discovered what happened at Ansariya a year and a half ago. Without knowing what went wrong, it is difficult to fix things. Mossad agents are still locked up in Cyprus. The security guards at the "Israeli" consulate in Berlin bravely fought off the Kurdish mob that stormed the building, but not before some of them infiltrated the "holy of holies" - the code room. This time, the codes were safely locked away in a safe, but one cannot be sure that "Israel's" state secrets were not leaked to a certain Western country - one that is friendly toward us and angry with us by turns - after the discovery of a surveillance system installed at one of "Israel's" foreign embassies".

"Brigade commander Col. Gershon (Gerry) Yitzhak, who was authorized this week to speak to the media about paratrooper reconnaissance operations, will soon be appointed to another senior post, although his affiliation with airborne combat divisions will continue. Until then, the paratroopers and rest of the army have little choice but to go on playing cat and mouse in Lebanon."

"A fiasco that costs human lives is the joint product of political error and military weakness. "Israelis", who have no patience for a prolonged fight, are always crying for a cease-fire or an escalation that will end in a cease-fire. Without one or the other, the situation seems like an endless nightmare. If the IDF enjoys a technological advantage over its enemies, this does not seem to show itself on the battlefield in Lebanon (although without this advantage, the number of casualties would perhaps be higher). What is particularly astonishing is that when "Israel" initiates a strike in Lebanon, there is no on-the-spot intelligence, despite all the apparatus - carried, transported or airborne - which is available for this purpose and could be used to keep tabs on the situation every step of the way. The Hezbollah does not have such intelligence either, but it does have good instincts for analyzing the moves of the troops it encounters. The game is a dangerous one: a Hezbollah force sits in ambush for an "Israeli" force that sits in ambush for another Hezbollah force. Some day it may become known that "Israel" scored hundreds of successes, proclaimed or unproclaimed, for every failure that ends in military funerals. But that scorecard has no value because it does not advance a political settlement (which the politicians are doing all they can to evade)".

"Although the refusal to move forward with negotiations with Syria is the major reason why "Israeli" blood is being shed in Lebanon, the IDF senior command cannot evade responsibility for military failures. A recent issue of the IDF journal Ma'arakhot contains an article that is unusually frank for an officer in active service and an official army publication. Col. Yehuda Wagman, an instructor at the Command and General Staff College and the former head of the armored corps, writes about the "decline in the performance graph of the IDF since the Yom Kippur War," and a "chain of failures" which he attributes to "patterns of thought and action nourished by an anti-academic military ethos."

Wagman finds "serious flaws in an organization that perceives itself as a professional body," among them a lack of professional training and a mindset that prevents it from operating as an efficient war machine. Mistakes made in 1973 were repeated with even greater severity in 1982, and no lessons were learned because the army clung to the simplistic belief that the outcome of the Yom Kippur War was determined chiefly by the fact that "Israel" was surprised and did not have enough armored formations. The mistaken credo of IDF land forces (as opposed to the air force) was "If force doesn't work, apply more force."

"Until 1967," Wagman writes, "the average officer grew up in an atmosphere in which the importance of studying the military profession was scoffed at." The successes (or what were believed to be successes, thanks to propaganda and censorship) appeared to justify the system, "and improvisation and fighting experience were sanctified as the solution to every problem on the battlefield."

"The early founders of the state were a special breed, and that made up for their lack of military expertise. The fact that the standards of the other side were so low was also crucial in the series of successes "Israel" chalked up during the first two decades of the state. Over the next three decades, "Israel's" military failures were a product of "the unwillingness of the intellectual elite to join the career army, the continued disregard for professional military education and the steady improvement made by the Arab guerrilla and terrorist organizations." Military operations in Lebanon like operations "Accountability" and "Grapes of Wrath," says Wagman, have proven that the primitive "more force" credo is still alive and well in the IDF".

"In Lebanon, this plodding army, with its declining performance graph, now confronts an organization whose performance graph is soaring upward. "Hezbollah has never been so well off," states a report of the Foreign Ministry's Political Research Center. "It has expanded its social, economic and political activities all over Lebanon; the character of the new Lebanese administration and Syria's new operational strategies have increased support for the organization's resistance activities; it sees itself as winning in the battle against "Israel" and the public debate in "Israel" is growing. At the moment, say the researchers, Hezbollah believes that it is abiding by the "April understandings" (a tacit agreement reached between "Israel" and this organization in 1996) and working to prevent an undesirable escalation of tension".

The policy it plans to adopt after "Israel" withdraws from Lebanon is being kept intentionally vague, "although there are signs that the struggle will not end here, and will continue through other means."

During a visit of Hezbollah leaders to Iran last November, the organization stepped up its operations against the IDF and decided, after consultation with its Iranian hosts, against joining the Lebanese government .Not that the term "Lebanese government" means much when it comes to foreign affairs and defense issues. It might be more correct to talk about a regional council of southwest Syria - a body that is about as independent as the Upper Galilee regional council is in "Israel". Hezbollah will be faced with a real dilemma if "Israel" decides to withdraw in an orderly fashion, as part of an agreement with Syria on the Golan Heights. If that happens, the organization will have to resign itself to being dictated to by Syria - or confront it head on, but none of this seems likely in the near future.

Over a period of three years, ending this June, Benjamin Netanyahu has been responsible for making the above scenario a remote possibility. His refusal to renew talks with Syria at the point where they were broken off may be in line with international law, but the bloody game in Lebanon is played by different rules. Yitzhak Rabin's choice of the Palestinian channel over the Syrian channel prolonged the battle in the north. But Netanyahu made things even worse: He managed to dry up both channels.
In the five years since the Oslo accords, the IDF has lost 135 soldiers in Lebanon, not counting the 73 who died in the Yasur helicopter crash".
