Daily Report
 
 
February Tuesday 2-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah discussed the conditions of the detainees in Al-Khiam detention center, especially their health status, with the representative of the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) Henry Fournier.

Meanwhile, the monthly report concerning the "Israeli" aggressions on Lebanon during January 1999 showed that there were 117 "Israeli" ground, air and sea aggressions on more than 100 Lebanese areas.

On the other hand, the "Israeli" proxy militia have been launching a large-scale conscription campaign in the occupied region of South Lebanon.

In Cyprus, two "Israeli" spies were sentenced to three years in prison yesterday for approaching a prohibited area while a military operation was taking place.

Sayyed Nasrallah discussed Detainees' conditions with ICRC (1)
"Hizbullah Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah today (yesterday) hosted Representative of the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC), Henry Fournier, and his assistant in the presence of Hizbullah's Coordination and Liaison Unit official."

"The conditions of the detainees in al-Khiam prison, especially their health status and ICRC's role in easing their suffering were discussed during the meeting".

"Sayyed Nasrallah stressed the need by ICRC to take interest in the detainees' issue in order to draw the world's attention to the tragedies and injustices in the notorious detention center.
Discussion also touched on the detainees who had ended their "sentences" but are still imprisoned in jails inside occupied Palestine."

"ICRC representatives were asked to raise this issue in concerned circles and groups in the world.
The ICRC representative explained to the secretary general the circumstance which had led to the occupation forces' release of 16 prisoners last week".

"Moreover, talks covered the "Israeli" enemy's escalation, especially its aggressions and shelling of civilian areas, besides the enemy's expulsion of civilian residents from the occupied town of Shibaa and its demolition of houses in Arnoun and other towns".

Monthly report on "Israeli" occupation's aggressions on Lebanon in January 1999 (2)
"There were 117 "Israeli" ground, air, and sea aggressions on more than 100 Lebanese areas and districts during January 1999".

"Air aggressions constituted 34.9% of the gross aggressions (31.6% in December 1998).
During this Month, occupation forces staged another raid near the site of the massacre perpetrated by ""Israel"" last month in the town of Janta, ""Israel"" claimed that the slaughter which took the lives of a mother and her six children was the result of a "pilot's error" as he used old photos".

"This month's raid targeted the same area and hit once again civilian sites, wounding eight civilians. They are Hamda Shahadeh Ayoub, 65, Sarah Ahmed Ayoub, 5, Hamda Naef Haikal Ayoub, 67, Tohfa Ayoub, 40, Amal Ahmed Ayoub, 5, Shojoun Ahmed Ayoub, Zeinab Ayoub, 2, and Syrian citizen Jinan Hosam Bakier, 20".

""Israeli" warplanes also raided the antenna of the Radio of the Oppressed near the town of Baalbek.

Scores of schoolchildren narrowly escaped death as they were leaving school when the raids took place."

"On Jan. 11, 1999, occupation forces raided the Litani River banks, Mount Sojod woods and Mazraat Oqmata. On Jan. 13, they fired missiles on Wadi Zillaya, Tomat Neha, a Wadi between Jbal al-Botom, Yater, Lwaizeh Hilltops, and the woods of Mount al-Rafieh, and on Jan. 15, they raided the Jbal al-Botom town.

On the other hand, raking the town of Meimes by heavy machine-gun fire on Jan. 1, hit a BMW car. The driver, Imad Shamel Maddah, Dany Saeed Darbieh, and Hussan Noaman Abu Saeed were seriously injured when the car hit an olive tree and turned upside down. Eleven days later, Abu Saeed died of his wounds.

On Jan. 8, combing of the Markaba woods resulted in the Martyrdom of Mahmoud Sharif Atwi, 60. The occupation tried to acquit itself of the crime claiming that Atwi was martyred during exchanged shelling with the resistance. The martyr had 12 children, including three who had been expelled by the occupation forces for rejecting to cooperate with them.

"Israeli" gunboats, on Jan. 6, intercepted fishermen's boats near the Mansouri shore. Fishermen brothers Hussein and Ahmed Taher were abducted by the occupation forces. They were held, questioned and tortured for two hours.

"On Jan. 29, an "Israeli" bomb made to look like a rock exploded at the Habboush-Arab Salim road.

Concerning "Israeli" practices in the occupied zone, enemy forces on Jan, 7, expelled 25 people from the occupied town of Shibaa. The expelled, most of whom from the Nabha family, included elderly, children and women. "

The expulsion evoked condemnations and demands that "Israeli" violations of the April Understanding's spirit must be stopped.

"Moreover, occupation forces detained two men from the Nabha family on charges of assassinating agent Ghassan Daher. The "Israeli" step was viewed as an attempt to sow seeds of unrest among the town's residents in order to screen the suppressive measures committed against the Shibaa residents.
The occupation forces claimed that during the investigation with the two detainees, they confessed killing the agent. However, a Shibaa residents' delegation refuted the claims made by the occupation officers and their collaborators as "an attempt to create turmoil in the town".

The residents demanded to meet the detained brothers who foiled the "Israeli" plot that aimed to create rift between the families of Nabha and Daher. They told the delegation that the claims of the "Israeli" spy service were baseless, denying killing Daher."

"On Jan. 10, an "Israeli" occupation force stormed into Shibaa, although the "Israelis" had lifted the blockade which they imposed on the town for 2 weeks. The enemy troops detained Mahmoud Mostafa Dallah, 40, a shepherd. He was released later.

On Jan. 13, the occupation troops detained Rida Hassan al-Taweel of Kfarkila, and on Jan. 28, Farid Jerjes Karam. They were both taken to the notorious al-Khiam detention center.
On jan 7, an "Israeli" motorized force accompanied by 3 bulldozers, 30 infantry troops, and a number of "Israeli" intelligence elements razed 15 houses in the town of Arnoun.

""Israel"" acknowledged the act of demolition, but an "Israeli" spokesperson claimed that Lahd militiamen razed the houses "which had been used by groups that plant bombs against "Israeli" army and militia patrols."

"Enemy leaders conducted a few tours in the occupied zone. On Jan. 25, former "Israeli" war minister Yitzhak Mordechai, along with a number of "Israeli" senior officers, inspected the international borders with Lebanon. He met the chief of the proxy militia, Antoine Lahd, near the Motulleh colony.

Moreover, "Israeli" chief of staff Shaul Mofaz, accompanied by the "Israeli" liaison unit commander Erez Gorestein, toured the occupation's positions in Marjayoun".

"On Jan. 29, "Israeli" war minister Moshe Arens and his chief of staff met agent Antoine Lahd and military commanders in one of the "Israeli" outposts in occupied northern Palestine."

"On the other hand, occupation forces fortified a number of their outposts in Tallet Zoghle, Ain Qinia, Shwayya, and Mount al-Rihan. They also added another heavy artillery emplacement in the northern part of the al-Shraifeh Hilltop outpost. The occupation created a permanent center in the eastern part of the Kawkaba outpost, along with the proxy militia forces, to monitor individual movements, tap and jam communication as the "Israeli" installed on Jan. 26, a radar antenna linked with electronic devices and several sophisticated antennas."

"These aggressions resulted in the martyrdom of two civilians, wounding ten others, detaining 7, expelling 25 from the town of Shibaa, along with demolishing 15 houses, damaging 19 houses, 7 cars, a school in the town of Zillaya besides three power stations".

"Israel's" agents impose a conscription campaign

"Fathers are forced to join the militia in place of any son who is outside the occupied zone", the reports said.

"Moreover, "Israeli" occupation's spy service has been surveying the villages and residents of the occupied zone for over a week".

"Residents are forced to fill in forms including the names of the males in the family, the number of family members, those who are outside the zone, and the land owned by the family".

"Furthermore, reports said the occupation forces have been bulldozing a patch of land near the town of Yaroun. "Israeli" bulldozers and trucks were seen loaded with rocks and barbed wires".
"The trucks have been unloading unidentified material in the area, eye witnesses said.

On the other hand, the occupation forces today (yesterday) expelled Mrs Jihad Nassar from her hometown of Shibaa. Nassar, 40, was led to the Kfartibneet crossing, where she was ordered to leave the occupied zone and never to return" (3)

The impoverished residents are also concerned at the failing health of their cattle, and one villager is still awaiting $6,700 compensation for his pick-up truck which was destroyed in December by two "Israeli" Merkava tanks."

"Talloussa, perched above Wadi Salouqi in the central sector of the zone facing Majdal Silm, often finds itself in the middle of fierce fighting between the resistance and the occupation forces.
The villagers stressed that life is very difficult at the moment and they are living in fear, the UN source said.

Two "Israeli" agents sentenced to three years in prison in Cyprus (7)
"Two "Israeli" spies were sentenced to three years in prison in Cyprus on Monday for approaching a prohibited area while a military operation was taking place. Igal Damary, 49, and Udi Hargov, 37, who were arrested on November 9 and initially charged with spying, were also given six-month jail terms by Judge George Arestis for possession of illegal listening equipment. The sentences are to run concurrently. Damary and Hargov pleaded guilty on Friday to the charges of approaching a prohibited area and possession of banned listening devices. In exchange for the guilty pleas, state prosecutors agreed to drop the more serious charges of espionage and conspiracy to commit espionage, which carried a maximum sentence of 10 years in prison. They could have faced a maximum of six years in prison on the charges of approaching a prohibited area and one year for possessing illegal listening devices. Passing sentence, Judge Arestis said he could not accept the defense's explanation of why the two "Israelis" were on this Mediterranean island. Lawyers for Damary and Hargov said they had come to Cyprus as part of an anti-terrorist unit to collect information on possible terrorist targets."

"They said the pair had served as "electronic lookouts" for a secret meeting of "Israeli" intelligence informants who had information on possible terrorist attacks by international terrorist groups. Arestis said the defense argument might explain the scanners but did not explain why the pair had approached a military area while a highly classified National Guard operation was going on. "Even if we accept the defense's version of events it cannot be accepted as a mitigating factor because no explanation was given as to why they went to a prohibited area without permission from the interior ministry," he said. "Cyprus does not have the luxury to accept such activity," the judge said, adding, in a reference to Turkey, that "Cyprus is an occupied country fighting to survive against a superior power." The two "Israelis" showed no emotion when the verdict was read out in court at the end of the 40-minute sentencing hearing. Andis Triantisyllides, one of their lawyers, said they were considering filing an appeal but no final decision had been made. Cypriot Attorney General Alecos Markides called the plea bargain last week "a satisfactory result" in the case and that it had been taken in the public interest. "On the one hand it helped get a conviction and also it stopped military secrets being divulged in court," Markides said. He said the "Israeli" government, US senators and European officials all had extensive contacts with President Glafcos Clerides and Foreign Minister Ioannis Kasoulides to convince them the two "Israelis" were not acting against Cyprus. The plea bargain agreement followed a reported secret visit to Cyprus by "Israeli" attorney-general Eliyakim Rubinstein to argue that the two men were not spying on Cyprus or acting against Cypriot interests, The two suspected Mossad agents were arrested near the southern fishing of Zygi in possession of scanners, other sophisticated listening equipment and maps reportedly showing the locations of secret military installations. "Israel" had initially refused to recognize that the two were state agents, but issued statements saying they "were not acting against Cyprus or on behalf of Turkey," which maintains 30,000 troops in the north of the island".
