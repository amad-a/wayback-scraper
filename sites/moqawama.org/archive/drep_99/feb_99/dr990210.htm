Daily Report
 
 
February Wednesday 10-1999:

Hizbullah attack "Israeli" outpost
The Islamic Resistance (IR) group of martyrs Hussein Bachir and Khodor Chehadeh attacked, at 13:15, enemy movements inside Blat post with the adequate weapons. Direct hits were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Harmoush and Hussein Chalhoub targeted Tair Harfa position with the suitable arms chalking direct and certain goals.

Meanwhile, the IR group martyr of Hassan Hammoudi targeted intensely Radar post with the appropriate arms scoring direct hits

The IR squad of martyrs Moussa Adib and Hussein Borji targeted intensely, at 12:40, an "Israeli" force at the entrance of Blat post. The militants, who used the adequate arms, were able to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR group of Ali Hassan Daoun and Abdel-Hassan Fahs attacked, at 13:00, Izzieh post with the appropriate weapons striking, directly, its fortifications. The moke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Salman Krayem and Abdel-Hassan Qatbay attacked, at 16:00, an enemy force as it was beefing up Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct goals.

One hour later, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Mahmoud Fahs and Abdallah Soueli rushed upon an "Israeli" convoy of nine command cars and military vehicles at the entrance of the Israeli position of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and definite hits. Also, the columns of smoke were seen emanating from the post (1)

The Daily Star reported that "an "Israeli" soldier was wounded Tuesday when Hizbullah guerrillas attacked outposts in the western sector of the occupation zone".

"The soldier was said to be lightly hurt when the guerrillas fired mortar shells at the positions of Blat, Teir Harfa and the coastal radar station at Biyada".

"The resistance shelling followed a heavy exchange of fire Monday night when Hizbullah fired some 60 Katyusha rockets and mortar rounds at several "Israeli" posts along the border"

"Security sources revealed that an "Israeli" soldier was wounded Sunday night in the western sector when another soldier mistakenly fired his gun.

"Five "Israeli" soldiers have been wounded in the south since the beginning of the year. There have been no "Israeli" Army fatalities" (2)

13:25, Majdal Zoun village was targeted by two 120mm shells causing damage to the houses of Mohammad Ali Moselmani and Mohammad Merhi.

14:15, four 120mm shells slammed into the peripheries of Loweizeh.
Between 14:55 and 16:00, the perimeters of Majdal Selem town came under twelve 155mm missiles.
Between 20:10 and 20:35, the perimeters of Qalaweih village came under seven 155mm missiles and several 81mm rounds.
20:10, seven 120mm shells slammed into the peripheries of Qantara town.
21:10, eight 81mm rounds pounded into Hojeir Valley.
21:20, the surroundings of Mothallath position subjected to five 81mm rounds.
21:30, Mount Sojod was targeted by four 120mm shells.

Classes on heroic acts of Resistance (4)
"Minister of National Education, Youth and Sports issued a decree instructing Lebanese Universities schools, and vocational schools to dedicate classes to talk about the resistance, its heroic acts and the need to support it. The event which is scheduled for February 18 is part of the Resistance Week activities"

"For its part, the Cairo-based Arab Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance in Lebanon (ACSIR) announced a series of political and cultural activities that are to be held as part of marking the Resistance Week "to immortalize the meaning of Jihad which has been rooted by the struggle of the Mujahedeen in South Lebanon."

"ACSIR said it had organized during the first week of February the first of its symposiums to mark the event with the heading "Resistance: the present and the future."

"Prominent Islamic intellectual Dr. Mohammed Salim al-Awa and a group of scholars and thinkers took part in the symposium".

Children cling to life after "Israeli" assault (5)
"Zeinab Maaz, 8, was lying prone in her Geitawi Hospital bed swathed in gauze, only her eyes and mouth uncovered."
"Nearby, her brother Abbas, 2, his little face swollen, his body covered in bandages, fretted for his absent mother."
Zeinab and Abbas are two of the three children who were severely burned when "Israeli" soldiers bombarded Majdal Silm with phosphorous and shrapnel shells Saturday. The children are still in critical condition".

"Zeinab, Abbas, and their older brother Mohammed, 10, were admitted to the Center for Intensive Burns at Geitawi Hospital Saturday after Tibnine Hospital and Sidons Hammoud Hospital were unable to cope with the extent of their wounds".
"The children were visiting their grandmother, Fatima Maaz ­ who was also wounded in the attack ­ when a mortar shell ripped through the living room, showering phosphorous over the house and its occupants".

"The "Israeli"s" and their South Lebanon Army proxies regularly use shells filled with white phosphorous in the south. Although the weapons are designed to produce an immediate smoke screen to provide cover for troops, the "Israeli"s" frequently use them against personnel. The phosphorous ignites spontaneously on contact with air and is very difficult to douse".

"Mohammed, who was in the kitchen at the time of the attack and sustained the lightest wounds, was discharged from hospital Sunday. But Zeinab and Abbas remain there, struggling for their lives".

"Dr. Georges Ghanimeh, the burns specialist who is treating the children, told The Daily Star that their condition was grim. Theyre suffering from deep third-degree burns and their situation is critical, he said.

"Jamileh Maaz, the childrens mother, has not been allowed to be with them due to the risk of infection. I can only look at them through the window, even though the babys crying for me to be near him, she said.

"She said she had been kept in the dark about the condition of her children and all she can do is wait.

She had been tending cattle with her husband in fields near Majdal Silm when the mortar attack occurred. They learned of their childrens wounds later."

My husband and I came here by taxi yesterday after we found out where they had taken our children, she said.

"Mrs. Maaz is worried that her daughter may be scarred for life. Zeinab opened one eye and looked at me earlier, she said. But she cant speak, she can only murmur and nod. Shes in a lot of pain.

She also fears for her childrens eyesight and hearing. Phosphorous bombs burn, their smoke blinds and suffocates, said Mrs. Maaz who is well versed in the different types of shells used by the "Israeli"s" and the havoc they wreak".

"Majdal Silm is on the edge of the central sector of the occupation zone and the surroundings area is shelled by the "Israeli"s" on an almost daily basis. But Saturdays bombardment was the first time the center of the village had been hit since the April war in 1996"

"Like many others, Mrs. Maaz has adopted a fatalistic attitude to help her cope with living in a war zone. Despite the fact that three of her six children have been wounded and two of them lie battling against death, she refuses to succumb to self-pity"
Whatever happens to a person in life is written by God, she said. All Im asking for is that with Gods will, my children will get better.
