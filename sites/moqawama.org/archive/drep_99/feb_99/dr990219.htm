Daily Report
 
 
February Friday 19-1999:

Introduction
The "Israeli" occupation troops and their allied militia have expanded their occupation area in South Lebanon barricading the village of Arnoun. The "Israeli" enemy had enclosed the largely depopulated village with the barbed wire and razed olive groves. The residents said they were told that Arnoun was part of the so-called security zone which "Israel" occupies since 1985.

However, the Head of Hizbullah's Politburo MP Mohammad Raad said the measures in Arnoun, the bombs in civilian areas in addition to the bombardment of houses are part of the daily aggressions for which the resistance retaliates with real action.

Arnoun is annexed into occupied zone (1)
"A technical adjustment, not an expansion of the zone, was how the "Israeli" Army Thursday described the annexation of the village of Arnoun into the occupied border strip."

"But for the handful of residents who have refused to leave their homes, despite having lived with the burden of occupation for 17 years, this technical adjustment has sounded the death knell for Arnoun.

Surely, the few remaining residents now have no choice but to abandon the village.
Prime Minister Salim Hoss protested the appropriation of Arnoun in a letter to UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan".

It is ridiculous for "Israel" to claim that it wants to withdraw from Lebanon when it occupies a new village and annexes it to the zone it already occupies, Mr. Hoss said in the letter.

President Emile Lahoud told Richard Ehrdmann, the U.S. delegate and new chairman of the five-nation Monitoring Group, that Lebanon would be filing a complaint over the seizure of Arnoun, which he said was a new "Israeli" aggression against Lebanon.

It only took five hours for "Israeli" troops and South Lebanon Army militiamen to kill this front line village.

Between 7 P.M. and midnight Wednesday, earth barricades were bulldozed across the entrance of Arnoun, coils of barbed wire were strung up between the "Israeli" post at Beaufort Castle overlooking the village and Kfar Tibnit, 3 kilometers away. Land mines were planted along the length of the new border.

The 40 or so residents of Arnoun woke Thursday morning to find themselves trapped inside the zone, cut off from the rest of Lebanon by the barbed wire. Dangling from the wire were signs warning of landmines inscribed in Arabic, English and Hebrew. On the reverse was a message written in Arabic: Stop. The border is in front of you.

"The "Israelis" have instructed the residents of Arnoun that they can only leave the zone via the SLA* checkpoint at Kfar Tibnit. This means the villagers will have to walk the 3 kilometers to Kfar Tibnit along an overgrown track. The "Israelis" long ago banned all vehicles in Arnoun."

"It was, perhaps, inevitable that Arnouns tenuous grip on state-controlled Lebanon was going to end. The village lay in an uneasy no-mans land with the nearest army checkpoint 1.5 kilometers up the road toward Nabatieh. Half the village technically lay within the occupation zone while the other half was subject to repeated forays by "Israeli" soldiers and SLA militiamen".

"The departure of much of the population in November and the demolition of 22 houses in December and January signaled the end for Arnoun.

"There was nothing the handful of remaining residents could do Thursday except ponder their dismal prospects".

We thought they were just closing the road. Not this, said Khadija Khawaja, 40, indicating the barbed wire and bulldozed earth that separated her from reporters.

She said that the residents had relayed their plight to the International Committee of the Red Cross.
We dont want to live here any longer, not if the wire is going to stay in place, she said.
"As she spoke, four "Israeli" soldiers were visible standing on the ramparts of Beaufort Castle, watching the proceedings below".

"Mounir Tawbe, 6, scampered along the occupied side of the barbed wire fence, exploring the new limits of his world with a friend, Hussein Marouni".

"Moments later, Mounir scrambled down the unoccupied side of the freshly bulldozed earth barricade to join reporters, having crossed the minefield and squeezed through the barbed wire.

Undaunted by the scolding he received from reporters, Mounir said, The "Israelis" are liars. There are no land mines there.

"His sense of triumph undiminished, he returned through the wire to join his family".

Village is now a free-fire zone (2)
"The annexation of Arnoun into the occupation zone appears to be the culmination of an "Israeli" scheme to depopulate the village and turn it into a free-fire zone, or an area where anything that moves would come under fire".

"In November, most of the residents walked out of the village, declaring that conditions had grown intolerable. The "Israelis" further hastened the exodus by bulldozing six homes Dec. 29 and another 16 homes Jan. 7".

"The second round of demolitions came two days after an "Israeli" soldier was wounded in a roadside bomb attack. The "Israeli" Army justified the demolitions by claiming to have found bomb-making equipment in one of the homes"

"The demolitions were subsequently condemned by the five-nation Monitoring Group.
A security source said that, with the likely imminent departure of the few remaining residents, the "Israeli" Army had achieved its aim".

They dont have to bulldoze any more homes, they will simply treat the village as a free-fire zone. he said.

A new "Israeli" crime: boy sustains burns from explosion of "Israeli" booby-trapped object (3)
"The endless series of "Israeli" crimes against children and civilians harvested another victim today.
Hassan Talal Fneish, 14, today (yesterday) sustained burns in the face when an unidentified object exploded near his house in the town in the Maaroub".

"The boy had to be admitted to the UNIFIL hospital in Borj Qalawieh".

Resistance won't be dragged into reactions not on agenda, says Hizbullah (4)
("Head of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Mohammed said the recent "Israeli" measures were sparked by "Israeli" upcoming election, although "they unmask the aggressive nature of the "Israeli" enemy which can not be partner in any co-existence".

""Israeli" leadership wants to tell voters: "we are taking measures that may curb our losses", Raad said.

The measures in Arnoun, the bombs in civilian areas plus the bombardment of houses are part of the daily aggressions for which the resistance retaliates with real actions", he added.

"But the Hizbullah MP stressed that "the resistance will not be dragged into reactions that are not included in its agenda. Therefore, we can affirm that the enemy will not achieve its goals".

What took place in Arnoun is a blatant violation of the April Understanding. International groups and the UN Security Council must view these measures as ones creating strong tension in the region, if there is intention to safeguard a stability based on justice."

"Any escalation in Lebanon against the Zionist occupation is the legal right which we practice at every moment with our resistance, the Hizbullah MP said.

"The tension resulting from the operations which succeed in hitting important targets is also our right which we do not label as escalation" he concluded.
