AUMG discusses 4 complaints including annexation of Arnoun (3)
"The April Understanding Monitoring Group convened this morning at the UNIFIL HQ in Naqoura to examine 4 complains, 3 Lebanese including the recent "Israeli" aggression of annexing the town of Arnoun into the occupied zone".

"Lebanon also protested the "Israeli" deliberate bombardment of the town of Arabsalim (Iqlim al-Touffah) last Saturday which seriously injured Mrs. Sanaa Shibani".

"The third Lebanese complaint is over the "Israeli" shelling which damaged two houses in the town of Jarjou".

"The "Israeli" occupation claimed in its complaint that the resistance fired on occupation outposts from the woods of the Majdel Zoen village".

"Lebanese delegation's sources said it will seek to achieve a condemnation of ""Israel"" for its annexation of Arnoun and force it to liberate the town".

"If these efforts can not achieve the desired results, the Lebanese government has to move through diplomatic channels", the sources said.

"The "Israeli" complaint is baseless and unjustified. Similar complaints have been filed (by ""Israel"") earlier, but the group did not consider them after noting that the resistance does not carry out attacks randomly", the sources added.

Students protest annexation of Arnoun (4)
"Students from the American University of Beirut and the Lebanese Universitys campus in Sidon staged sit-ins Monday to protest the incorporation of Arnoun into the occupation zone last week.
At AUB, members of the University Student Faculty Committee (USFC), which organized the sit-in, asked students to sign a petition denouncing the "Israeli" take-over of the village".

Its a way of spreading awareness, said Dania Hashem a member of USFC and Hizbullah. People should know that "Israel" occupies 103 villages in the south and western Bekaa and that they have added one more to become 104.

"The small crowd sat on the ground by West Hall in front of a board with a map of Lebanon and pictures of the occupied village. Arnoun today, all of Lebanon tomorrow, said Yasmine Dada, an AUB student at the sit-in who is planning to gather donations to help refugees from the south. We are here to send a message to the rest of the world, if we dont protest, who will? she asked.

"Attendance at the Lebanese University in Sidon was considerably greater than at AUB, reflecting the citys proximity to the occupation".

"Some 300 students from different political affiliations stood behind barbed wire, with one protester holding aloft a metal land mine warning sign taken from outside the village.

Many students denounced the absence of any effective action from the international community, saying the resistance was the only way to free the south".

We have staged this sit-in to show our solidarity with our countrymen in Arnoun and all other occupied villages. We ask the UN to take action to return Arnoun to Lebanese sovereignty, said Samar Diab, a student.

Arnoun is a very dear part of our country and we will not leave it under "Israeli" occupation. The world is asleep and should wake up and see the crimes of "Israel" in Lebanon, said Raja Sinn.

How can the "Israeli" extremist Cabinet claim that it is willing to implement UN resolutions and then annex Arnoun into the occupied zone? Using roadside bombs and rockets is the only solution to force "Israel" to evacuate the south, Alaeddine Abu Husun said.

"Israeli" Army branches bicker over naval commando burials (6)
"New contradictions between branches of the IDF* came to light yesterday in the second day of discussions by the committee investigating the burial of two naval commandos killed in a Hizbullah ambush last year in South Lebanon. The navy and the IDF's human resources branch hurled recriminations and gave contradictory versions of the events".

"At the committee's previous meeting, three senior officers of the human resources branch had provided the same story. Major General Yehuda Segev, his predecessor, Major General Gideon Sheffer and the head of the casualties department, Brigadier General Drora Pe'er, concurred that human resources issued a directive to inform the families of Sergeant Major Raz Teby and Sergeant Major Guy Golan that parts of their sons' bodies had been returned to "Israel". However, they said, the navy did not carry out the order in time, and so the body parts were buried almost two weeks before the families were informed".

"Yesterday, however, senior naval officers testified to a contradictory version of events. Navy Commander Alex Tal and Colonel Nir Maor, who heads the naval personnel division, said the human resources branch issued instructions on the matter late, if at all. That division, they said, did not tell the navy the bodies were already buried when it instructed the navy to inform the families".

"The most embarrassing contradiction concerned Colonel Maor. On Friday, Major General Segev had told the committee a moving story, which he claimed he learned while investigating the
circumstances of the burial: Many years ago, he said, Maor's brother was killed in an accident during his military service. The body was badly mutilated, and Maor was called to identify it. The incident traumatized him so badly that he wanted to spare the families a similar trauma. He therefore decided not to inform them that the body parts were returned."

"In the beginning of his testimony yesterday, Maor asked to correct some details of the story. His brother, Major Tzur Maor, was not killed in an accident, but during the Lebanon War. The brother, the commander of an armored brigade, was killed after destroying seven Syrian tanks. He received a medal of honor for his service. His body was not mutilated. At the time, Nir Maor was in a
submarine several hundred meters away. Therefore, he was not called to identify the body, and so was not traumatized by it. In addition, he said, he made no decision against informing the families.

Maor said that the announcement was delayed by two days because he wanted to confirm some
details".

"Major General Tal's account contradicted Sheffer's testimony. "It has been said that I received an instruction or an order from Sheffer. I want to make it clear beyond a shadow of a doubt: I received no such instruction."

"Hearings on the petition submitted by the parents of the two commandos to reopen their sons' graves will be delayed until the committee completes its work in a time limit of 90 days, the High Court of Justice decided yesterday".

"The parents are demanding that DNA tests be conducted on the body parts. Also present at the hearing was a representative of the mother of Raz Teby, who is the only one of the bereaved parents who opposes reopening the graves".
