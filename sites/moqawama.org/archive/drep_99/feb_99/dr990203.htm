February Wednesday 3-1999:

Hizbullah: We'll keep attacking until "Israel" gets out of Lebanon (1)
(views in resistance)
" Sheikh Nabil Qawook, spokesman of Hizbullah in southern Lebanon, vowed that resistance attacks would continue with no let-up until "Israel" withdraws "without a single condition", local newspapers reported Tuesday. "The enemy (Israel) must be
made to pay for its occupation, not reap rewards from it," Qawook was quoted as saying by the daily An Nahar. Withdrawal offers made by "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his senior aides were not genuine. "They were made to shore up his sagging election fortunes," Qawook said. Lebanese Prime Minister Selim Hoss was meanwhile quoted in the local press as saying "Israel's" attempts to separate Lebanon's track from that of Syria in the U.S.-sponsored Middle East peace process were "doomed in advance". "We reject any unilateral negotiations of unilateral accords," he said. "We stick to our commitment to seek a comprehensive, just peace based on returning all occupied territories to their legitimate owners." He added that Lebanon is also committed to the resumption of peace negotiations from the point they stopped at when they were stalled in 1995".
