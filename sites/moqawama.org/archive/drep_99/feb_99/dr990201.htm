Daily Report
 
 
February Monday 1-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
In its communiqué, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions on the liberated Lebanese villages and towns:

00:15, four 155mm missiles struck Mount Sojod.
5:30, the perimeters of Zawtar Gharbieh and Zawtar Sharqieh came under eight 155mm missiles.
13:40, Hojeir Valley exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
14:15, three 81mm rounds landed Haddatha forest.
15:00, Litani River watercourse and the perimeters of Qilya and Zillaya came under sixty 155 and 160mm missiles.
16:20, two 81mm rounds hit the peripheries of Habboush town.
16:30, Mount Al-Rafih exposed to twelve 155mm missiles and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
Between 16:35 and 16:55, the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village came under thirty nine 155mm missiles.
16:35, three 120mm shells fell on Litani River watercourse.
16:45, Habboush town was shelled by several 155mm rounds.
17:50, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into Intara ravine between Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen villages.
21:45, the surroundings of Dabsheh outpost came under several 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
22:00, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the surroundings of Soweida outpost.
22:40, a number of 81mm shells fell on the area between Tohorra and Dabsheh outposts, whereas several 60mm rounds hit the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh El-Fawqa.
22:45, the peripheries of Kfar Romman town subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
Between 23:30 and 23:45, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by twenty seven 155mm missiles.

Military Operations (2)
At 13:45, the IR men spotted enemy movements at Ahmadieh position. Immediately afterwards, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit group of martyr Mohammad Bjeiji targeted them with the appropriate arms. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Five minutes later, the IR fighters observed enemy movements inside Zimraya position. Shortly afterwards, the ARU squad of martyr Abou Ali Shahla targeted the position with the adequate weapons chalking up accurate hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Fayad and Hassan Abou Raya attacked, at 16:15, enemy movements inside Soweida outpost with the suitable arms scoring direct and definite hits. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Tohini and Hassan Slim targeted enemy movements in and around Blat post. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyr Youssef Soueli reiterated, at 16:55, targeting Blat post with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits.

At 23:05, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Abdel-Hassan Qatbay and Soheil Mohammad Diab targeted enemy movements near Dabsheh outpost with the adequate arms. Direct and definite goals were scored the mujahedeen reported.
Five minutes later, the martyr Hadi Nassrallah Special Forces Brigade's group of martyr Mohammad Hazimeh detonated an explosive device at about one hundred meters away from the "Israeli" posts of Abbad and Dawawir. The explosion occurred as an Israeli foot-patrol was passing nearby.

"Israeli" farmers reap benefits of our soil (3)
"The prospects of Israel returning the soil it stole from Lebanon last October was always remote. But that likelihood has faded to near impossibility now that "Israeli" farmers have planted saplings in the stolen earth".

"The picture above shows part of the terraces constructed from the distinctive dark red soil of the fertile al-Marj Valley, just north of the border. In the foreground is the border and in the background lies the "Israeli" settlement of Kefar Giladi".

"Hundreds of tonnes of soil were stolen by "Israeli" businessmen last October and trucked 4 kilometers south of the border for the benefit of the farmers of northern Galilee. The terraces are clearly visible from the border road running between Kfar Kila and Adaisseh".

"The "Israeli" government initially denied soil was being stolen after The Daily Star first broke the story in October. The theft, however, provoked a diplomatic uproar, forcing Israel to admit soil was being taken illegally and to stop the plunder".

"The excavation site, 1 kilometer north of the border, was abandoned in November and is now filled with rainwater".
