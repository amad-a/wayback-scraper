Daily Report  
 
February Thursday 25-1999:

Introduction
In a press conference at a mountain cave less than five kilometers from the scene of Birkat Jabbour confrontations in which three "Israeli" officers were killed and seven soldiers were injured, the Islamic Resistance (IR) men who took part in these confrontations showed off a captured assault rifle, a radio, a black face mask and a blood-soaked "Israeli" officer shirt that had been torn by bullets. They used a military map to show how the mujahedeen ambushed the "Israelis".

However, Lebanon decided to hold further contacts with the UN Security Council member states and listen to their views pertaining to Arnoun affair before taking any further governmental moves.

IR displays weapons taken from occupation troops… (1)
"The Islamic Resistance last night held a press conference at a mountain cave less than 5 kilometers from the scene of the Birket Jabbour operation in which three occupation "elite" officers lost their lives Monday night".

"The five Resistance men who staged the ambush displayed a blood-stained shirt and M18 rifle equipped with a a night-vision device, a radio, a black mask and some bullet magazines which belonged to the killed "Israeli" officers"

"The ambush commander, in his mid-20s, had a bandage on his left cheek, covering a wound apparently suffered in the fighting by a small blister.

"He used a map to show how the Mujahedeen ambushed the "Israelis", but declined to answer questions on whether the Islamic Resistance had advance knowledge of the paratroopers' movements.

"We expect "Israel" to retaliate and we're ready for them. It's business as usual," said one of the unidentified fighters, clutching a balaclava in his hand.

Security sources remarked that the clash had occurred in a usually quiet sector of the zone and suggested that the "Israeli" commandos were after a specific target rather than on a routine search-and-destroy mission, the Lebanese Daily Star newspaper said.

Observers noted that given the general lack of hostilities in the area, Hezbollah's ambush was unlikely to have been staged by chance. Instead the guerrillas appeared to have had advance information of an "Israeli" unit heading north out of the zone, the daily added.

"The ambush was well-planned. The ambush squad would have remained in contact with the mortar teams to the rear to relay the map coordinates pinpointing the approaching patrol, allowing the mortars to be calibrated. It was a testimony to the accuracy of the Hezbollah mortar operators who were dropping rounds very close to their comrades once the shooting began", it concluded.

Birket Jabbour operation commended… (2)
"Head of the Higher Islamic Shiite Council Ayatollah Sheikh Mohammed Mehdi Shamseddeen hailed the latest Islamic Resistance operation and followed up the Arnoun crisis developments.

"Blessed are the honored hands that carried out the resistance operations during the last two days, especially the one that inflicted the most serious harm on the "Israeli" army," Ayatollah Shamseddeen said.

He reiterated that ""Israel" only understands the language of resistance; therefore, resistance operations must be intensified."

Meanwhile, Druze religious leader Sheikh Bahjat Ghaith also praised the heroic Islamic resistance operation.

"The principle of holding to the right and defending it remains the source of strength that gives the everlasting power to this courageous resistance which is dealing painful blows to the "Israeli" enemy and teaching it tough lessons," Ghaith said.

MP Najah Wakim sent a message of congratulation to the Islamic Resistance men who "scored a glorious point in the Lebanese history."

These men are the essence, and all the rest are mere details, Wakim said.

Hezbollah Deputy Secretary general Sheikh Naem Qassem received a call from former speaker Hussein al-Husseini who congratulated him on the courageous Islamic Resistance operation against the "Israeli" commandos.

He also assured his continuing support to the resistance against the "Israeli" enemy until liberation is achieved.

"The Lebanese Doctors' Association sent a letter to Hezbollah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah in which they congratulated the Mujahedeen on their qualitative achievement".

Lebanon delays the complaint to the UN Security Council… (3)
"Lebanon Wednesday put on hold a complaint to the UN Security Council over "Israel's" seizure of Arnoun. At a four-hour meeting, the Cabinet decided to postpone the adoption of a final stand until after Prime Minister Salim Hoss has assessed contacts under way with “all countries that wield influence with the Security Council.”

"The decision came despite heightened calls to take the case to New York after the failure of the five-nation Monitoring Group to offer a verdict.

"The government denied it had bowed to U.S. pressure when it reversed Tuesday’s decision to go to the Security Council, insisting this was still an option.

“What’s important is that these contacts take place so that when the case is presented to the Security Council, the resolutions will be the way Lebanon wants it to be and not diluted so as to alter the Lebanese position,” Information Minister Anwar Khalil told reporters after the Cabinet session.

President Emile Lahoud opened the session with a strong denunciation of "Israel's" annexation of "Arnoun, insisting that the government would not drop the case.

Mr. Hoss explained that if the Monitoring Group had been able to redress the situation, Lebanon would not even have contemplated going to the Security Council.

"Earlier Wednesday, however, U.S. Ambassador David Satterfield asserted that the Monitoring Group was still the best forum to discuss security concerns and “ease tension in the region.”

"Mr. Satterfield, who also discussed Arnoun with Speaker Nabih Berri, is scheduled to depart for Washington Thursday on a previously arranged trip to take part in an annual conference for U.S.
ambassadors in the Middle East. Diplomatic sources said that although Mr. Satterfield will be discussing Arnoun with the State Department, it was not the primary reason for his visit.

"General Lahoud also met the commander of Unifil, Major General Joiji Konrote, and Timur Goksel, political adviser to the peacekeepers.

"The president told them that he had originally favored taking the Arnoun case to the Monitoring Group so that Lebanon could not be blamed for sidelining the committee. Now that the Group had deferred adjudicating on Arnoun, it was necessary to raise the issue elsewhere, the president was quoted as saying.

"Mr. Hoss asked Lebanon’s UN ambassador, Samir Mubarak, to step up his consultations with the five permanent members of the Security Council and assess their backing for a Lebanese complaint".

No homecoming for Arnoun dead… (4)
"Since "Israel" cordoned off Arnoun into the occupation zone last week, the final resting place for deceased natives now depends on whether they still live inside or outside the village.

For Afaf Hamdan, 47, who died two days ago in Beirut, the residents were forced Wednesday to re-open an abandoned cemetery on the edge of the village for her funeral."

"Half the mourners, including Miss Hamdan’s sister, Zeinab, were unable to grieve beside the grave as they remain trapped inside the village behind coils of barbed wire and land mines. The existing cemetery is found in the middle of the village, now inaccessible to outsiders".

"Miss Hamdan, daughter of Arnoun’s Mukhtar, Afif Hamdan, died Tuesday at the Hayat Hospital in Beirut from a heart disorder. The woman’s relatives in Beirut transported her body to the outskirts of Arnoun".

"The Mukhtar, who was refused permission to leave the village earlier this week, was transported by the International Committee of the Red Cross via the Kfar Tibnit crossing so he could attend the ceremony".

"But Zeinab remained behind, crying in grief along with the other trapped residents.

Former residents flocked to the village to take part in the funeral while "Israeli" troops in Beaufort Castle sent two armored personnel carriers to monitor the proceedings 200 meters from the village entrance".

Military Operations… (7)
The IR team of martyr Mohammad Ali Assaf attacked, at 24:20, enemy movements in and around Hemayed position with the adequate arms scoring direct goals.

At 8:05, also, the IR squad of martyr Ali Soueli targeted the "Israeli" post of Qabaa with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militant managed to chalk up direct hits.

Moreover, the IR group of martyr Habib Tarhini attacked, at 10:50, enemy movements near Qabaa post with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyr Hussein Obeid targeted, at 11:15, an enemy force as it was beefing up Hemayed position with the proper weapons. The militants were able to chalk up direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Shamma and Hassan Obeid attacked, at 12:00, enemy movements at Mothallath position chalking up direct and definite goals.

However, the IR team of martyr Assaad Tabaja targeted, at 13:55, Mashaaroun outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades barring it from reinforcing the attacked force near Markaba village.

At 15:20, the IR squad of martyr Ali Hijazi attacked a common "Israeli"-Lahdi motorized force at Mashaaroun area with the suitable weapons chalking up direct and certain hits.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyr Nimr Ftouni was attacking the "Israeli" 70th Brigade Command at Qabaa position with the rocket-propelled grenades. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Ten minutes later, the IR team of martyr Hussein Ghassani targeted an "Israeli" agents engineering force as it was beefing up the outpost of Bir Kallab with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain goals.

At 21:00, the IR men spotted enemy movements near Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR team Abdel-Amir Fadel and Khalil Hammoud targeted them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen chalked up direct and certain hits.

Also, the IR group of martyr Hassan Shamma attacked at 23:45, enemy movements inside Rshaf position. The militants, who used the suitable weapons, were able to score direct hits.
