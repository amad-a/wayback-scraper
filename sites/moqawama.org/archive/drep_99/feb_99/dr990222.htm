Daily Report
 
 
February Monday 22-1999:

Introduction
The "Israeli" proxy militia acknowledged that one of its members was wounded as a result of an attack made by the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants on the collaborators' HQ in Jezzine Saturday.

Elsewhere, a woman was shot in her hand when the "Israeli" agents showered the northern outskirts of Arab Salim and Jarjoua towns Sunday morning.

Meanwhile, it was appeared that a former "Israeli" collaborator was blamed for having incited Friday's shooting in a store in the village of Qrayyeh near Sidon.

Hizbullah hits Jezzine and more to come (1)
"Hizbullah guerrillas stormed the South Lebanon Army* headquarters in Jezzine Saturday, wounding one militiaman in an attack that possibly signals the beginning of a new offensive in the district as speculation mounts that "Israel" is considering returning the area to the government".

The 12:30 A.M. assault on the barracks, situated in the center of the town, involved at least four guerrilla squads.

"According to a Hizbullah statement, once inside the barracks, the fighters destroyed some of the buildings and vehicles, including a tank and two armored personnel carriers, before planting the partys flag and retreating".

"Other squads pinned down reinforcements with simultaneous attacks on nearby SLA positions at Ain Majdelain, Kroum al-Arz, Ain Toghra and Bater".

One militiaman was reported wounded in the assault.

"The assault on the SLA headquarters coincides with a growing belief that "Israel" may soon hand back the Jezzine enclave to the government and withdraw to new frontline positions along the Litani river.

"The pullback would also reduce the frontline by around 40 kilometers, thus allowing the "Israelis" the freedom to reduce the number of troops serving in the zone".

"The abandoning of the Toumat Niha post last September as well as the desertion of 17 militiamen and the subsequent pullback from Safaray to Roum last month reflects the lack of will and the inability of the SLA to confront the resistance".

"This has been exacerbated by local resentment toward the SLA commander in Jezzine, Joseph Karam, popularly known as Allouche. He has filled his dwindling ranks with former Lebanese Forces militiamen from East Beirut and the north, from where Karam originally hails. Jezzines residents and militiamen have little empathy for Karams unsympathetic strongarm tactics, which have included tightening travel restrictions in and out of the area".

"Now that "Israeli" troops have almost completely dropped routine foot patrols to spend their entire time in their bunkers, military targets further south are becoming sparse. As such, Hizbullah may take advantage of the uncertain pre-election climate in "Israel" to step up the pressure on the softer SLA targets in Jezzine to further undermine the militias morale and hasten the return of the enclave to the state".

"Sheik Naim Qassem, Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, promised further headline-grabbing attacks, claiming Saturdays assault on the Jezzine barracks was the first practical response to the annexation of Arnoun.

Liquor store shooting blamed on an "Israeli" collaborator (2)
"Politicians and security forces Saturday blamed a former South Lebanon Army official for having incited Fridays shooting in a store in the village of Qrayyeh near Sidon that wounded four people.
The manhunt for the perpetrators stepped up over the weekend with troops establishing checkpoints and stopping cars in Qrayyeh and Sidon."

"The roads leading to the Ain al-Hilweh and Mieh Mieh Palestinian camps were filled with security personnel."

"A man entered a store selling alcohol in Qrayyeh Friday and fired several shots from a pistol before driving off on the back of a moped. Four men were wounded and one escaped unharmed. Qrayyeh residents and MPs staged a sit-in Saturday to protest at the attack against the predominantly Christian village".

"Security sources in Sidon said that Hanna Helayhel, a former South Lebanon Army militia official who lives in Jezzine, had been taking advantage of such incidents to stir up sectarian tension and incite further attacks. They added that the attack could be linked to a series of recent incidents in Sidon, including the assaults on stores selling alcohol, the killing of two members of the Internal Security Forces and the attempted murder of a Lebanese army soldier. The sources said that the same procedure and weapons were used during each attack".

"Elsewhere, Sana Mounif Shebani, 33, was shot in the hand when a machine gun in the Sojod post raked the northern outskirts of Arab Salim and Jarjoue Sunday morning".

"The wounding of Ms. Shebani is one of three complaints filed to the five-nation Monitoring Group which is to convene in Naqoura Monday. The other complaints concern the annexation of Arnoun and damage to houses in Arab Salim".

"Israeli" Aggressions (3)
The "Israeli" occupying troops launched the following aggressions on Lebanon's towns and villages:
7:15, four 120mm shells hit the perimeters of Jibaa town.
7:40, Mount Sojod was targeted by two 120mm shells.
7:55, the area between Zibqeen and Jibal El-Botom villages was shelled by seven 155mm missiles.
8:00, five 120mm shells fell on the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost and one another on Oqmata plain.
15:50, the peripheries of Jibaa came under eighteen 155 and 120mm missiles.
16:05, two 155mm missiles struck the perimeters of Kfar Fila.
16:25, the perimeters of Hamra area exposed to three 155mm missiles.

Military Operations (4)
The IR team of martyrs Mahmoud Baghdadi and Tahseen Sharifa attacked, at 7:00, Sojod outpost with the machine guns, mortars and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its fortifications. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Mustafa Haidar and Wa'el Darwish targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the adequate arms. Direct and certain goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Wehbi Abdallah Wehbi and Ali Mohammad Hussein Shrara attacked Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the appropriate weapons. The militants
managed to chalk up direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Mustafa Haidar and Mohammad Hemayed attacked, at 15:40, enemy squads at the Twentieh Brigade post of Ain Thaghra with the suitable weapons. The militants were able to score direct and definite goals.

For its part, a team of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted, at 7:15, Tair Harfa outpost with the proper arms chalking up direct hits.

Quarter an hour later, another team of LRB attacked Blat post with the adequate weapons scoring direct goals.

MK Bshara praises Hizbullah (5)
"Attorney-General Elyakim Rubinstein will investigate whether MK Azmi Bashara (Hadash) sh ould be prosecuted for the statements he made in support of Hizbullah".

"Bashara was talking over the weekend at the convention in which he was chosen as head of the Balad Party, which will run separately from Hadash. He also questioned the Zionist nature of the state.

He said Hizbullah is an Arab nationalist movement fighting bravely against occupation by "Israel". Bashara said Hizbullah had given "Israel" the choice of either reaching a just peace while withdrawing from south Lebanon and the Golan Heights - which he described as southern Syria - or not reaching peace at all."

"Even the Hebrew media have stopped calling Hizbullah 'terrorists' and now refers to 'Hizbullah fighters.' "

He said he was referring to Hizbullah "only in the context of a Lebanese national organization fighting against the occupation of its country - which is fair - with no mention of other aims which Hizbullah may or may not have."

"He also said there is nothing new in his comments on the state and he has made them in public before, including in the Knesset. Bashara said he works through democratic means to change the Zionist - not the Jewish - nature of the state".

"He said the mood in the country has been radicalized by the Right. "I welcome any debate about the nature and character of the state," he said. "My program is considered liberal democratic. If they want to go and complain to the Central Elections Committee, as [Eitan] threatens, concerning the legality of my party, I think the issue will become the legality of democracy in this country."

"He said that "I do not challenge the Jewish character of the state, because it reflects the character of the majority and I have no problem with that. My problem is the Zionist character, which claims to be the state of the Jews in the whole world... not the state of its citizens."

"Bashara said as a result, the whole country becomes subject to Zionist rationale which results in land expropriations to build up a demographic Jewish majority in places like Galilee and the Negev.

Another Balad official said it also means that "Arabs become second-class citizens and Jews coming from Brooklyn to "Israel" immediately get preferential rights over them."

Bashara said: "More importantly, you can't talk about the separation of state and religion because who is a Jew and Jewishness is also religiously defined, so if you want the separation of state and religion and if you want equality, the state must function as the state of its citizens. I don't think this will happen tomorrow or next year, but this could be the compass for the [direction] of the future. And I think more and more Jews also think the state should be the state of all its citizens."

He said nobody could consider himself liberal or democratic "and at the same time question my right to propound liberal democratic ideas."
