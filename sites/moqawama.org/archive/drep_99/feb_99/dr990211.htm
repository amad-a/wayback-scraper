Daily Report
 
 
February Thursday 11-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) released a communiqué in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:

5:55, the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
11:00, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the perimeters of Kfar Romman town sparking a blaze in a Van car related to the telephone company.
Between 12:50 and 13:05, the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village came under sixteen 155mm missiles.
12:55, the surroundings of Mohaibib and Mount-Jamaah exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
13:05, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by several 155mm missiles.
13:20, nine 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Khorbet Selem.
15:55, nine 120mm shells hit the peripheries of Loweizeh and seven others struck Mount Safi.
16:20, Mount Sojod was targeted by two 120mm shells.
16:40, seven 120mm shells pounded into the surroundings of Tasseh Well.
17:20, the perimeters of Touline village subjected to four 155mm missiles, whereas the surroundings of Taibeh compound exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
17:25, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the surroundings of Qabaa post.
Between 17:35 and 18:35, Mazraat Hamra was shelled by more than twelve 155mm missiles.
Between 20:05 and 21:50, Mount Sojod was bombarded by five 155mm missiles and three 120mm shells.
21:45, four 155mm missiles landed on Mlita Hill.
22:20, Hojeir and Slouqi valleys, the surroundings of Qantara position and the perimeters of Qabrikha village came under 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
22:25, the surroundings of Tohorra outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

"Israeli" soldier wounded in resistance attack (2)
"An "Israeli" soldier was slightly wounded when Hizbullah resistance fighters shelled an "Israeli" position overlooking the southern village of Arnoun. The soldier was the second "Israeli" wounded in occupied south Lebanon in as many days".

Lebanon Watch Australia tours officials in solidarity with south and prisoners (3)
"General Coordinator of Lebanon Watch Australia Khodor Saleh is scheduled to meet Lebanese officials, NGOs' representatives and families of detainees in "Israeli" occupation's prison".

"Lebanon Watch is a watchdog that takes interest in defending the rights of the Lebanese in South Lebanon and the Western Beqaa in the face of "Israeli" occupation's aggressions.

Saleh's visit aims to coordinate action and pressure in order to organize campaigns for supporting the steadfastness of southern residents and taking action to free the detainees in the occupation's prisons in al-Khiam and inside occupied Palestine".

"The Sydney-based group which was formed in 1996 seeks to make Australian and other institutions aware of the results of "Israeli" aggressions in occupied territories in Lebanon, Palestine and Syrian Golan Heights".

"It also demands implementing international conventions over protection of civilians during times of war and implementation of UN Security Council resolutions over "Israeli" withdrawal from occupied territories".

Kfarfalous crossing to be opened Saturday, collaborators' practices against residents (4)
"The "Israeli" proxy militia is still closing the eastern gate of the Kfarfalous-Jezzine crossing before cars. Only pedestrians can cross the gate which links liberated areas to those occupied by the "Israeli" enemy".

"Informed sources quoted Jezzine circles as saying the crossing will be reopened on Saturday.
This, the Jezzine circles said, was announced by the chief of the proxy militia agent Antoine Lahd who came to Jezzine yesterday afternoon following a series of complaints by the Jezzinites against the so-called chief of the 20th Brigade in the collaborator militia.

Jezzinites said agent Joseph Karam has been insulting residents violating houses under the pretext of looking for young man who had "fled conscription".

Agent Karam's elements last night detained three militiamen who would not man the Annan outpost.
The three militiamen returned to the town following the militia's retreat towards the outpost of Roum. "They are Habib Martinos, his son George, and Milad Iskandar. They were detained in Jezzine and taken to its barracks for questioning".
