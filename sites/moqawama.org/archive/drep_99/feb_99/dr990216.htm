Daily Report
 
 
February Tuesday 16-1999:

Introduction
The "Israeli" occupying troops, yesterday, pursued their aggressions on the Lebanese towns and villages.

In its retaliation, the Islamic Resistance (IR) launched a series of attacks on the positions of the "Israeli" enemy and its collaborators.

Meanwhile, reports said "Israel" has adopted new tactic which includes withdrawing the human element from direct combat with the resistance fighters.

Enemy withdraws human element from confrontation, replaces it with aircraft (1)
"Sources concerned with the resistance told al-Safir newspaper that the enemy's military commanders have resorted to a new tactic which they were forced to adopt".

"The new tactic which was put into effect before the resignation of former "Israeli" war minister Yitzhak Mordechai includes withdrawing the human element from direct combat with the resistance while assigning other occupation army divisions to engage in such confrontations, the air force in particular", the daily said.

"Al-Safir said "Occupation forces now never leave their outposts unless they are being replaced by other units. There are no more "Israeli" patrols to inspect the vicinities of outposts; convoy movements are null."

"The occupation army is now boosting the armor division, which now covers every moving body, human or vehicle."

Moreover, the enemy is permanently seeking to develop its technical capabilities to confront the road-side bomb weapons, the daily added.

"The sources told the daily that the "Israeli" army is training a special force, to engage in guerilla warfare, and developing its commando units".

"Moreover, the "Israeli" forces are trying to plant espionage rings in Lebanese territories', However, this specific issue is being watched closely by Lebanese officials".

"Surprising and tough blows are being dealt to these networks, some of which are already there, and others are under construction, while security activity in the ranks of the collaborators militia, which is living its last days, continues".

Collaborators stormed into Tair Harfa (2)
"The reports from the occupied zone said a proxy militia force, headed by the "security chief" in Tair Harfa, agent Akram Oulayan, stormed into houses owned by people who had been expelled by the occupation forces and their collaborators".

"The militiamen ransacked and stole the furniture in the houses of Said Assad Bayyad, Nazem Said Bayyad, and Ali Hassan, before occupying the houses themselves".

"The people of Tair Harfa issued a communiqué in which they condemned "the militia's arbitrary measures against the residents", urging the government to take action in this regard and raise the issue with human rights groups".

"Israeli" Aggressions (3)
In its communiqué, the IR mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions on South Lebanon and Western Beqaa:

10:20, three 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Baraasheit.
13:45, the surroundings of Houla post subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
14:50, the south of Jibal El-Botom village was bombarded by fourteen 155mm missiles.
15:50, the "Israeli" jets staged a sortie into Intara ravine between Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen villages.
16:00, Mount Abou Rashed was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
16:30, one tank round hit the area between Kfar Milki and Kfar Beet villages.
16:55, the surroundings of Sojod outpost subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:00, eight 120mm shells pounded into the peripheries of Baraasheit town.
Between 18:55 and 19:05, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by eight 155mm missiles.
20:35, three 160mm rounds fell on Mount Sojod.
23:05, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal village exposed to five 81mm rounds.

Military Operations (4)
The IR squad of martyrs Tahseen Sharifeh and Mustafa Haidar attacked, at 14:45, an "Israeli" force as it was fortifying Blat position. Several casualties were inflicted, the mujahedeen told.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Hemayed and Haitham Abou Daya targeted, at 20:20, a motorized "Israeli"-agent patrol as it was entering Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyr Hussein Bashir attacked, at 22:35, Haddatha compound with the appropriate weapons. In the meantime, the IR team of martyr Soheil Diab targeted Beit Yahoun outpost with the proper arms chalking up direct hits.
