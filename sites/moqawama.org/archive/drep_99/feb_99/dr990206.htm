February Saturday 6-1999:

Introduction
The bellicose threats fired at Lebanon by senior "Israeli" and U.S. officials has prompted a clear-cut response from Hizbullah and Lebanon's PM Salim Hoss. Representative of Imam Khamenei in Lebanon Hizbullah Consultative (Shura) Council Member H.E. Sheikh Mohammad Yazbek said the Resistance's arms are legitimate in the confrontation with the occupier as long as it is usurping our land. Mr. Hoss was quoted as saying the "Israeli" government is changing facts and misleading public opinion by holding Lebanon responsible for the escalation in South Lebanon, warning that the Jewish state is preparing a military offensive against Lebanon.

However, on the occasion of the week of the Islamic Resistance in Lebanon, Hizbullah's Central Press Unit inaugurated the Islamic Resistance's Fair 1999.

Meanwhile, the customs officials in South Lebanon have been clamping down on smuggling operations taking place in valleys just north of the area occupied by the "Israeli" occupation forces.

Sheikh Yazbek condemns Gold's threats and Indyk's comments (1)
"Representative of Imam Khamenei in Lebanon Hizbullah Shura Council Member his Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek said "the Resistance's arms are legitimate in the confrontation with the occupier as long as it is usurping our land. These arms will never be put down regardless of "the enemy's feeble pretexts."

"In the Friday Khotba he delivered in Baalbek today (yesterday), Yazbek replied to the usurping entity's representative at the UN who attacked the Lebanese government because it backs the resistance.

We want the whole world to understand that the resistance in Lebanon is the resistance of all the Lebanese against the invading occupiers; it is natural for the state to back it. It is unnatural for the state not to back it", Sheikh Yazbek said.
"Why doesn't the enemy leave our villages and land? Is Lebanon the occupier, or is it the Zionists? He asked."

His Eminence denounced the comments made by the US secretary of state's assistant Martin Indyk "who expressed his country's policy which is always biased in favor of the enemy and which justifies its occupation and aggression".
"The US cannot pressurize our resistance or drive us to concede our conviction, because what we are doing is an ethical, human, and Jihad duty".

Lebanon responds to "Israel's" accusations (2)
"A barrage of criticism and bellicose threats fired at Lebanon from senior "Israeli" and U.S. officials has led Prime Minister Salim Hoss to suspect that the Jewish state is preparing the ground before unleashing a military offensive against the south".
This campaign indicates that "Israel" is preparing something against Lebanon and that it is trying to justify it by distorting the
facts, Mr. Hoss said in a statement Friday.
"The premier was responding to a letter delivered Thursday to UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan by "Israel's" ambassador to
the international body, Dore Gold".

"In the letter, Mr. Gold blamed the Beirut government for the violence along the border and warned that Lebanon would be held accountable".

"Israel" reserves the right to take whatever measures may be necessary to assure its own self-defense, he said.
"The south has been quiet lately and there was no apparent reason for the timing or the pugnacious tone of the letter. But the implicit threat conveyed by the letter prompted Mr. Hoss to question the logic behind the "Israeli" claims".

Its most extraordinary that the aggressor should make accusations against the victims of aggression and blame them for acts of resistance which is a legitimate right of all peoples whose land is being occupied, he added.

"Mr. Hoss also lashed out at remarks made Thursday by U.S. Assistant Secretary of State Martin Indyk who openly
supported "Israel's" claim to be willing to abide by Resolution 425".

"In response to the U.S. officials comments, Mr. Hoss said that if "Israel" had genuinely accepted Resolution 425, why has it not already pulled out from south Lebanon?"

The truth is that "Israel" is still maneuvering and linking its withdrawal to the fulfillment of several conditions that Lebanon has rejected and will always reject because they are in total contradiction with the resolutions text and its purposes, he added.

Inauguration of IR Fair, Information Minister calls for embracing the resistance option without hesitation (3)
"On the occasion of the Week of the Islamic Resistance in Lebanon, Hizbullah's Central Press Unit inaugurated "the Islamic Resistance's Fair 1999" under the auspices of Information Minister Anwar al-Khalil at the Glass Hall (Ministry of Tourism)".
"The event was attended by MPs Mohammed Raad, Nazih Mansour, Ammar Musawi and Mohammed Fneish, besides representatives of Army and ISF commanders and political, social and media figures.

Minister al-Khalil spoke at the inauguration. He hailed the resistance and its martyrs underlining that backing the resistance and liberating the south and the Western Beqaa are priorities to the president and the government".

"He called on media to undertake their responsibilities when dealing with the Resistance, "for our strength and the strength of our resistance is based on our good use of this important sector."

We need to use our media capabilities, not to import material and positions that are made by someone else," al-Khalil said.
"The strength of the resistance is strength to the government which now adopts resistance as a first option to liberate the land and restore sovereignty, he said, calling for embracing this option without hesitation".

"With its credibility and sacrifices, the Resistance in the south has become an Arab source of hope whose news are enthusiastically anticipated by all of the Arab world, the information minister added.

The fair includes pictures and miniatures about the resistance, martyrs and the homeland, handwork by detainees in occupation prisoners, Martyrs' belongings, and models of "Israeli" weapons looted by IR during operations against the occupation".
"Throughout the Fair days, videotaped operations against the occupation forces will be displayed for visitors to see".
"Israeli" agents bid to abduct ISF member fails (4)
"Militiamen of the South Lebanon Army* attacked a police station in the Jezzine region overnight in a failed bid to abduct a
member of the Internal Security Forces whom they accuse of helping a relative defect from the SLA".

"The head of the police station, Sergeant Major George Qazzi, told The Daily Star in a telephone interview Friday that more
than 25 militiamen, led by the head of the 20th SLA regiment, Joseph Karam, known as Allouche, tried to storm the station overnight Thursday".

They laid siege to the station at a time when we had only three policemen and insulted me. Allouche asked me to hand him Adjudant Basil Lebbos, who is from the neighboring village of Ain al-Mir. He accused Adjudant Lebbos of helping his nephew, Elie Lebbos, defect from the SLA to the liberated territory, Sergeant Major Qazzi said.

But we refused and forbade the SLA gunmen from entering the station, despite his threats to storm it. They posted several armored vehicles around the station to try to frighten us and blocked us in for two hours.

Sergeant Major Qazzi said the force withdrew after failing to disarm the police.

Allouche approached me and ordered me to surrender our weapons, but I refused. When he found out that he could not do anything, he withdrew with his men, Sergeant Major Qazzi said.

Eight months ago, Allouche abducted Adjudant Lebbos after shooting him in the leg. But the policeman escaped".

Lebanon clamps down on smuggling operations from occupied zone  (5)
(characteristics)
"Customs officials in southern Lebanon have been clamping down on smuggling operations taking place in valleys just north of the zone "Israel" occupies in the south. The campaign coincided with reports that the head of the "Israeli" allied militia in the south, Antoine Lahd, has been building a fortune from such illegal activities".

"This was the catch - tobacco smuggled from the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon. Customs officials in Sidon on Tuesday night rounded up the goods and those smuggling the 900 cigarette packets on backs of donkeys through the Bisri-Meydan valley just north of the occupied Jezzine enclave. After shooting rounds, the smugglers managed to escape, but customs officials ceased the tobacco, with the help of the Lebanese Army intelligence. Customs officers in Sidon said the operation is part of a campaign to clamp down on smuggling originating from the occupied zone. This coincided with news reports in "Israel" unveiling the chief of the "Israeli" allied militia in occupied south Lebanon, Antoine Lahd, has profited handsomely from "Israel's" occupation of the area through a network of bribery, smuggling and crooked business dealings.

Reports first appeared in Western embassies that the "Israeli"-protected and commissioned Lahd has been imposing taxes on imports of stolen cars from Europe via the occupied port of Naqoura. Paris reports also said Lahd is making a daily profit of some 7,000 US dollars from imported gasoline to the occupied zone. Lahd has also been charging a 40,000 dollar tax per shipload of smuggled cigarettes through occupied Naqoura. Lahd has been condemned to death by Lebanese authorities for collaborating with the enemy, and aiding "Israel" in its occupation of Lebanese land. He heads the three thousand strong militia financed and trained by "Israel"".
