Daily Report
 
 
February Sunday 21-1999:

IR blows up Jezzine barracks, attacks surrounding outposts (1)
"As part of the Resistance Week activities, the Islamic Resistance (IR) heroes have been able to add a new qualitative achievement to their record which is full of victories, asserting that they possess the initiative and that they are able to penetrate the security and military measures of the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators".

"IR Mujahedeen today managed to run over the proxy militia's 20th battalion command HQ in Jezzine, killing or wounding the enemy troops who were in the compound."

"IR men also attacked the neighboring enemy outposts before returning safely to their bases.
The attack was launched at 00:30 (Saturday) as IR's Brigades of Sayyed Abbas al-Musawi penetrated into the barracks which includes five buildings. The compound was under the Mujahedeen's control for a while. They killed or wounded the enemy troops in them, raised resistance flags on the fortifications then dynamited the buildings".

"Moreover, the Mujahedeen destroyed the vehicles which were in the compound. These were a half-track tank, two APCs, a Toyota Land and a Dodge truck".

"In the meantime, other IR squads attacked the neighboring outposts of the occupation and its collaborators.

"Squad of Martyr Hussein Nasser attacked an enemy force in the Bater outpost, squad of Martyr Ahmed Khalifeh attacked the Kroum el-Arz outpost, squad of Martyr Mohammed Hamoud pounded the 20th battalion's HQ in Ain al-Toghra, squad of Martyr Karim Atwi targeted the artillery outpost, and squad of Martyr Hussein Shamas attacked the Ain Majdaleen outpost".

"The Mujahedeen used machine guns, mortars and rocket propelled grenades in the attacks, scoring definite direct hits".

Sheikh Qassem: Jezzine operation is just the beginning of retaliation for Arnoun occupation (2)
"The Islamic Resistance's operation in Jezzine this morning is the first practical retaliation for annexing the town of Arnoun into the occupied zone, Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem said.

"The operation expressed determination and firm resolution that resistance will continue as the only means for liberation, he added.

"Sheikh Qassem underlined that the Resistance will carry out more operations that will have impact on the occupation's course and deter it from waging more aggressions".

"The resistance men who are carrying out operations deep inside the occupied zone and near the border with occupied Palestine surely can cross the barbed wires and mine fields planted by the enemy around Arnoun, the deputy secretary general said in a radio interview".

"He said the US connives with the Zionist enemy in all its policies and acts and, therefore, cannot be relied on to deter the enemy's aggressions".

"Sheik Qassem said the American ambassador's call not to go beyond the monitoring group or raise the Arnoun issue at international forums "is evidence to the US desire not to allow escalation of pressure against the Zionist enemy."

Parties want complaint with Security Council to stop "Israeli" aggression (3)
"Lebanese parties, political forces and unions underlined that annexing the town of Arnoun into the occupied zone reflects the aggressive expansionist nature of the Zionist entity.

The occupation is trying to screen the quagmire it is confronting due to the resistance operations which are inflicting on its military, psychological and morale defeats, the parties said at a meeting they held today".

"They stressed the Lebanese popular and official invariable stand that resistance and the steadfastness of the people are the only ways to liberate the land and recover sovereignty.

The parties and forces urged the government to file a complaint with the UN Security Council and call on the Arab League and Arab diplomacy to take immediate action in order to secure an Arab stand to stop the "Israeli" aggression".

"The party representatives called for rallying and mobilizing political and popular forces to boost solidarity with the steadfast residents in occupied towns".

"Israeli" Aggressions (4)
The "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions:
2:00, a lot of rounds fell on the old Toumat post and the areas of Ain El-Khoukh and Shayar El-Batoun.
21:30, Mount Safi was bombarded by seven 155mm missiles.
21:40, three 155mm missiles struck the perimeters of Jibaa town.
