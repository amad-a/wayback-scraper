Daily Report
 
 
February Tuesday 9-1999:

Introduction
Yesterday, the Islamic Resistance (IR) men took revenge on the savage "Israeli" shelling of Majdal Selem and Haddatha town on Saturday and Sunday. More than twelve Lebanese civilians were wounded over the weekend when the "Israeli" shells struck the front line towns just north of the "Israeli" occupied area of South Lebanon. In its quick and powerful retaliation, the IR militants manage to breach all the military measures adopted by "Israel" and its agents around and in Sojod outpost. The "Israeli" allied militia acknowledged that five militiamen were injured and one armored personnel was destroyed. The IR men asserted that three collaborators were killed as seen by the naked eye.

IR storms into Sojod outpost and plants Hizbullah's flag
Once again, the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants made a qualitative achievement through storming into the formidable Sojod outpost following the intensive measures taken by the command of the "Israeli" occupation troops.

At 10:45, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Ghazi Al-Husseini and Ghassan Zaatar in the Special Forces of Martyr Hadi Nasrallah's Brigade penetrated into Sojod outpost and killed, certainly, three collaborators, whereas the IR group of martyr Ibrahim Dakdouk managed to inflict two "Israeli" agents and destroy the northern and western fortifications. The clashes lasted for about quarter an hour during which the mujahedeen combed the remaining fortifications. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Moussa Abou Taam and Hassan Malek Harb attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. In the meantime, the IR squad Mahmoud Baghdadi and Tahseen Sharifeh targeted an "Israeli"
force at Rihan hilltops preventing it from reinforcing the attacked position. Also, the militants were able to chalk up direct goals.

As an "Israeli" force was brought to Sojod outpost, the IR group of martyrs Mustafa Haidar and Wa'il Darwish, at 11:50, reiterated attacking it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades preventing the force from carrying out its goals. Also, direct hits were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Zahi Haidar Ahmad and Mustafa Chokair detonated, at 12:45, a roadside bomb as an enemy motorized patrol was in its way to back up Sojod outpost. An M-113 armored personnel carrier was destroyed and burned completely whereas another military vehicle was hit also. The enemy troops suffered hysterical state and were heard screaming loudly.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Fadi Al-Tawil and Hussein Merhi targeted, at 13:10, Sojod outpost with the suitable weapons chalking up direct and accurate goals (1)

For its part, The Daily Star covered the IR storming of Sojod outpost as follows:
"In fierce clashes that left five members of the South Lebanon Army* wounded, Hizbullah guerrillas Monday attacked and overran the hilltop Sojod outpost".

"Claiming a major accomplishment, Hizbullah said that a special forces squad at 10:45 A.M. assaulted the Sojod compound in the Iqlim al-Touffah overlooking the villages of Arab Salim and Jarjoue"
"At the same time, other teams advanced toward the nearby outposts at Bir Kallab and Rihan to prevent reinforcements from supporting the SLA in Sojod."

"The statement said that one squad was able to breach the defenses of Sojod and enter the post while a second team destroyed the northern and western fortifications. Two SLA militiamen were wounded in the gun battle".
"Bir Kallab came under assault from machine guns and anti-tank rockets from one Hizbullah team while another ambushed an "Israeli" patrol as it headed for Sojod", the statement said.

"Two hours after the fighting began, the guerrillas detonated a roadside bomb near Sojod as an SLA armored patrol passed by, wounding another three militiamen and destroying an armored personnel carrier".
"Hizbullah said that the successful outcome of the attack came despite intensive measures adopted by the "Israelis" to prevent the resistance from entering the post again.

"Last August, a Hizbullah guerrilla broke into the Sojod compound, stole some equipment, punched an "Israeli" soldier and departed. The incident embarrassed the "Israeli" army and was a powerful propaganda coup for Hizbullah".
"The attack on Sojod came two days after an SLA militiaman was killed and another wounded in a roadside bomb blast in
Houla".

"Monday night, the SLA position in Houla came under heavy mortar fire by the resistance. Some rounds were reported to have hit "Israeli" border posts. No casualties were immediately reported. Security sources believe that Hizbullah is in the process of stepping up operations after last months lull, with the SLA being the favored target over "Israeli" troops".

"Elsewhere, the Kfar Falous crossing linking the Jezzine enclave with Sidon is expected to re-open Tuesday morning having been closed since the end of December, security sources said. The road was closed after 17 militiamen deserted their posts in Anan Dec. 31, declaring they were no longer willing to serve in the SLA due to the threat of roadside bomb attacks perpetrated by Hizbullah. Faced with the shortage of manpower, the SLA command decided to redeploy its front line from
Safaray to Roum and close the Kfar Falous crossing".

"The pullback extended the no-mans land between the state and the militia from 3 kilometers to around 6 kilometers. A new gate has been installed in Roum which now marks the border of the SLA-controlled enclave" (2)

Monitoring Group to meet Thursday to discuss "Israeli" violations (3)
" The five nation cease-fire monitoring group is expected to meet on Thursday. They will discuss two complaints filed by Lebanon over "Israeli" shelling in south Lebanon. 12 Lebanese civilians were wounded in "Israeli" attacks over the weekend."

" Tensions rose in south Lebanon over the weekend. 12 Lebanese civilians were injured Saturday when "Israeli" shells hit the front line village of Majdel Selm just north of the zone "Israel" occupies. Eight of those injured were members of Ali Maaz's family. "Israeli" shelling also targeted the village of Hadatha. Several houses were damaged. Lebanon filed a complaint to the five nation cease-fire monitoring group. The group monitors a truce in the south that bans attacks on and from civilian areas but permits the Lebanese resistance to attack "Israeli" occupation troops and their local allies. Lebanon's Prime Minister Selim el-Hoss had denounced the "Israeli" shelling saying it was evidence of "Israel's" ongoing atrocities against the people of the south. Security sources said the "Israel" may be trying to escalate the situation".

"Also on Saturday, an "Israeli" allied militiaman was killed and another was wounded when resistance guerrillas of the Hizbullah movement detonated a roadside bomb in the occupied village of Houla. Meanwhile, two Irish peacekeepers serving under the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon, UNFIL were wounded Sunday when "Israeli" allied militiamen manning the outpost overlooking the village of Hadatha fired several mortar shells into the village. Mortars fell close to an Irish headquarters and according to UNIFIL the shelling could have been deliberate. UNIFIL launched an investigation and formally protested the shelling to the "Israeli" army. It was not the first time UN peacekeepers were targeted by "Israeli" shelling. They have been deployed in south Lebanon since 1978".
