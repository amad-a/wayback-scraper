Daily Report
 
 
February Thursday 18-1999:

Introduction
In an indication that fighting the "Israeli" enemy is strategic and comprehensive, the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants extended their efforts to include the economical area. Yesterday, the IR men confiscated "Israeli"-made dairy products from the village of Sreifa, where UNIFIL's Finish battalion is deployed.

Meanwhile, Lebanon accused 200 people of collaborating with "Israel's" intelligence apparatus, smuggling "Israeli" drugs and cigarettes across the Lebanese-Palestinian border.

However, the "Israeli" occupation forces escalated the military situation in Southern Lebanon and Western Beqaa damaging a house in Nabatieh city.

"Israeli" goods confiscated, Hizbullah wants stricter measures
(Israeli aggressions)
"Hizbullahs resistance efforts turned to confiscating "Israeli"-made dairy products Wednesday, after villagers were caught trying to make money by selling them in the south.

The party announced that they had seized four boxes containing a total of 120 bottles of milk and yogurt in the village of Srifa, east of Tyre and a few kilometers from the "Israeli"-occupied security zone in southern Lebanon."

"The plastic bottles with Hebrew writing on them were taken away from the villagers by local Hizbullah members, who took them to the groups office in Tyre, a spokesman for the group said.

The boxes will be handed to the Lebanese authorities, who will launch an investigation into the matter, he added.

Lebanon observes an Arab boycott of "Israeli" products.

"Hizbullahs leader in southern Lebanon, Sheik Nabil Qaouq, said: Smuggling, selling or using such goods, even if they are gifts, is religiously, nationally and morally forbidden"
Those who deal with these products are traitors to their nation, and we urge the concerned authorities to investigate the matter and adopt stricter measures to prevent such acts, he said.

We also call on our people to report the presence of any "Israeli" goods on our market, wherever they are, he added (1)

"UNIFIL spokesman Timur Goksel denied that international force troops have taken part in the smuggling operation"

"Goksel said some traders may have brought the goods through the Kfarkilla crossing, adding that a lot of "Israeli" goods pass through the crossing gates at the Lebanese Palestinian borders" (2)

"Israel" escalates situation in South
(Israeli aggressions)
 "Southerners running for their lives. "Israeli" gunners pounding civilian populated areas in the southern town of Nabatiyeh Fawqa. Quickly the entire region turned into a ghost town. "Israeli" shelling targeted the deserted army barracks in the market town of Nabatiyeh, just north of the occupation zone. Intense "Israeli" bombardment on the front-line village Kfar Tibneet, left the home of Hussein Ali Hayek destroyed. The ravines of Ali Taher, Shookeen, Mazraat Hamra, and the villages of Zawtar Sharakiyeh and Zawtar Gharbiyeh were not spared the "Israeli" attacks. For its part the "Israeli"-equipped and financed militia in occupied southern Lebanon, combed the front-line area of Hasbani-Zimraya in the Western Bekaa Valley. Heavy machine guns and grenades were used" (3)

In its communiqué, the IR mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions (4):
5:45, the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost subjected to heavy machine gun fire whereas several 81mm flare shells were fired overhead.
5:50, four 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village.
5:55, the surroundings of Sojod outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
Between 6:45 and 7:10, the peripheries of Loweizeh village came under three 155mm missiles and three 120mm shells.
6:45, four 120mm shells fell on the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost and three others on Mount Al-Rafih.
17:25, the hilltops of Rihayat and Jabbour came under five 155mm missiles.
200 accused of collaboration (5)

"Military Prosecutor Nasri Lahoud Wednesday accused 200 people of collaboration with "Israeli" intelligence, entering "Israel" and smuggling drugs and cigarettes across the southern border.

The group was uncovered after the arrest and interrogation of an officer of the South Lebanon Army earlier this month".

"The officer, Mohammed Khalil Nabaa ­ responsible for security in Shebaa in the occupied zones western sector ­ is among the accused. Nine others being held with him are Bassem Mohammed Ghader, Khaled Mohammed Asaad, Mohammed Taysir Qaadan and his brother Majed, Saeb Mohammed Rahil and his brother Khaled, Jamal Mohammed Rammal, police Sergeant Nabil Amin Ghannam and Omran Deeb Daafour."

"Majed Qaadan and Khaled Rahil were doing military service at the time. Together with Jihad Hussein Nassar and Ikram Ali Sheet, they face prison sentences and possible execution if convicted of spying for Mossad".

"Mr. Lahoud also indicted 188 people who are still at large.

All are accused of supplying Mossad with information on the Lebanese Army and resistance since 1992. Some are also believed to have sold drugs to "Israeli" servicemen in the occupied zone and smuggled European and U.S.-made cigarettes into Lebanon from "Israel"".

Military Operations (6)
At 6:15, the Islamic Resistance (IR) men staged a swift attack on Dabsheh outpost scoring direct and certain hits and destroying its western fortification.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Akram Khatoun and Ali Sbeiti targeted Beaufort Castle with the adequate weapons. Direct and definite goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported. Five minutes later, the IR militants targeted Dabsheh outpost with the suitable arms scoring definite hits.
