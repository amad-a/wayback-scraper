Daily Report
 
 
February Saturday 20-1999:

Introduction
Dozens of Arnoun population protested the Jewish state's actions at the edge of the village and tried to remove the barbed wires placed by the "Israeli" occupation troops.

On the other hand, "Israel" has admitted for the first time that the Insarieh fiasco in which twelve of the so-called "Israeli" elite troops were killed resulted from a security penetration in the interest of the Islamic Resistance (IR).

Massive sit-in in support of Arnounites; occupation forces bar residents from crossing barbed wires (1)
"Residents of Arnoun today (yesterday) tried to remove the barbed wires placed by the "Israeli" occupation forces.

Enemy troops in the Beaufort Castle outpost fired shots at the residents and a group of reporters who tried to enter the town which was annexed to the occupied zone Wednesday night".

"Minister of Public Works and Transportation Najib Miqati, Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc member MP Abdallah Qassir, MPs Yassin Jaber and Abdel-Latif al-Zein, Nabatieh Governor Mahmoud al-Mawla, besides a large number of party representatives headed for the southern town today (yesterday).

"Minister Miqati spoke to the residents from behind the barbed wires.

"The government is taking action to remove the occupation from the town and other southern villages and cities, Miqati told Arnounites".

"The people of Arnoun this morning staged a massive sit-in in the Husseineit of neighboring Kfartibneet"

"Minister Miqati took part in the sit-in along with a number of MPs and party representatives.
Speakers at the sit-in called on the international community to end the "Israeli" violation of Lebanon's sovereignty urging the resistance to step up its attacks in order to foil the enemy plans.

Retaliation for this aggression must take the form of more unity, said Hezbollah official in the south Sheikh Nabil Qawooq".

""Israeli" mounting aggressions and killing of children with booby-trapped toys will not give the enemy tranquility nor will it drive away the death coming from the resistance men's arms, he added.

Sheikh Qawooq stressed that the war against the enemy will continue".

It is a war fought by the resistance to achieve victory, he said.

"Residents of neighboring Yohmor also staged a sit-in in solidarity with the Arnounites. The occupation forces in the Beaufort Castle raked the town's vicinity with machine guns".

"Israel": Hizbullah knew about the Insariyeh botched raid (2)
""Israel" has acknowledged for the first time that the Insarieh fiasco in which 12 of the so-called "Israeli" elite troops were killed resulted from a security penetration in the interest of the Islamic Resistance, Lebanese daily al-Kifah al-Arabi said today (yesterday)".

"Hizbullah was in possession of some intelligence information on the botched aggression which took place 2 years ago", the daily added.

"An inquiry panel set up by "Israeli" chief of staff Shaul Mofaz said Hezbollah did have information about the September 5, 1997 raid, and had the time to set the ambush which took the lives of 12 "Israeli" troops".

"Earlier, the "Israeli" army had said that the navy unit was discovered after the troops landed on the Lebanese shore".

"The panel acknowledged that Hizbullah, as the party announced before, had known that the commando unit was about to stage the raid, without necessarily knowing the route which the troops would take".

"Israeli" aggressions (3)
The "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions:
1:40, Slouqi Valley was bombarded by eleven 120mm shells.
6:25, one tank round hit Mount Sojod.
6:30, Mount Sojod was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
6:40, four 155mm missiles struck the area between the villages of Zibqeen and Maallieh.
6:55, five 155mm missiles fell on the area between Siddiqeen and Kafra and three others on Hamra area.
11:10, an "Israeli" warboat was seen cruising off the seashore of Ras El-Ain.

Military Operations (4)
The (IR) team of martyrs Ali Assaad and Mustafa Nazar staged, at 6:00, a swift attack at Soweida outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western fortifications. Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Samer Bshara and Othaina Kansou targeted the "Israeli" post of Dabsheh with the peroper arms. Direct and definite goals were scored. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Shamma and Moussa Mohammad Abou Taam attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct and certain hits.
