Daily Report
 
 
February Sunday 14-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions (1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions (1):
00:45, one 120mm shell hit Slouqi Valley.
00:50, the peripheries of Houla village exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
1:15, one 155mm missile struck Mount Al-Rafih.
12:45, ten 81mm rounds pounded into the surroundings of the old Qantara compound.
13:40, the ravines adjacent to Qantara compound came under a number of 81mm rounds.
16:50, Oqmata plain was bombarded by twelve 120mm shells.
19:50, Slouqi valley subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
20:45, the surroundings of Salaah position came under two 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
23:45, four 155mm missiles hit Mount Sojod, whereas the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations (2)
The IR men spotted, at 16:45, an "Israeli" foot-force near Sojod village. Then, the group of martyrs Ghassan Ghanem and Mohammad Hassan attacked it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. Also, the attacked force was driven off by the militants. Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Assaf and Khodor Shehadeh was targeting Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket propelled grenades chalking up direct goals. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Bachir and Abdo Agha Zada targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and certain goals.


Footnotes:
