Daily Report
 
 
February Manday 15-1999:

Introduction
In a clear indication of its growing profile, Hizbullah held a rally at Unesco Palace in the heart of the capital Beirut to commemorate the martyrdom of Hizbullah's former Secretary-General H.E. Sayyed Abbas Al-Mousawi and Sheikh Ragheb Harb. In attended the rally were a lot of the Lebanese officials headed by PM Salim Al-Hoss.

Meanwhile, thousands of southern people escorted the child Ahmad Moqalled to his final resting place on what would have been his six birthday. Moqalled had died of his wounds when a cluster bomb, believed to date back to the 16-day "Israeli" aggression against Lebanon in April 1996, exploded. Moqalled's funeral was attended by dozens of students who carried wreaths.

PM makes clear strong support for resistance (1)
"Hizbullahs growing influence in the political arena was apparent Sunday when Prime Minister Salim Hoss headed a diverse list of officials attending a rally at Unesco Palace to commemorate the assassinations of Hizbullah leaders Sheik Ragheb Harb and Sayyed Abbas Musawi".

"The gathering was the first major Hizbullah occasion to be held outside Beiruts southern suburbs, the partys traditional stronghold. Hizbullah Secretary General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah paid tribute to the two leaders, addressing an audience of some 1,200 supporters and a crowd outside who watched the event on television".

With the martyrdom of these two great men, we now possess important elements of power. The most important of these is credibility, because these leaders were living the life of every other person who suffers because of the occupation, he said.

Mr. Hoss praised fighters killed in the battle against "Israel", stressing resistance was the only path to victory.

This (resistance) is the only language the cowardly enemy understands, the enemy that shields itself with dominant international forces to practice crimes against our country, people and land, he said.
The presence of Mr. Hoss highlighted the governments closer links and greater sympathy with Hizbullahs political aspirations. Although former Prime Minister Rafik Hariri paid lip service to resistance efforts, relations between the his government and the party were often strained.

Mr. Hoss described Hizbullah as the greatest resistance movement in the history of Lebanon. It has perfected the use of weapons and changed "Israel" from a feared force to one that is continuously afraid.

Also in attendance were representatives of the Syrian Baath Party, the Palestinian Islamic Jihad and the Iranian Embassy.

Nawwaf Musawi, a member of Hizbullahs politburo, said that holding the rally at Unesco Palace, rather than a location in the southern suburbs, indicated Hizbullahs growing profile.

There is no doubt that choosing a place in the heart of the capital is symbolic. It was also the largest
place we could find, he said.

With party supporters filling the hall in a matter of minutes, hundreds of others who arrived in buses arranged by the party sat outside watching the proceedings on a large television set. Hizbullah has moved from the level of popular support to that of an official and regional nature, Mr. Musawi said, stressing the diverse representation at the rally.

The head of the Maronite League made a point to be at the event, in addition to representatives from Palestine, Syria and Iran, he said.

Such developments mean more efficient coordination between Hizbullah and the Army and Hizbullah and security forces, said Mr. Musawi.

The fact that our president was an army commander makes our coordination with the Army better, he said.

He also noted that the prime ministers support for the resistance has a long history. Mr. Hoss was one of the first to present condolences when Sayyed Abbas Musawi was assassinated in 1992. Sayyed Musawi was killed on the outskirts of Jibsheet along with his wife and a son when his car was destroyed by an "Israeli" helicopter gunship.

Sheik Harb, one of the founders of the resistance, was shot and killed 15 years ago in Jibsheet while leaving a mosque. Sheik Nasrallah recalled that Hizbullahs first major use of Katyusha rockets against "Israel" came a day after Sayyed Musawis assassination.

During an incident of "Israeli" aggression against the villages of Kafra and Yater, Hizbullah fired hundreds of Katyushas on Zionist settlements in northern Palestine, initiating a new tactic of confrontation, he said.

Cluster bomb victim Ahmad buried on his sixth birthday (2)
"A boy who died Friday of wounds sustained when a cluster bomb exploded while he was on a picnic with his parents near Nabatieh was buried Saturday, on what would have been his sixth birthday.

Ahmed Raed Mouqalled and another child, Mahmoud Hussein Hamadi, aged two, were wounded when the cluster bomb, believed to date back to the 16-day "Israeli" onslaught against Lebanon in April 1996, exploded. Ahmad, whose stomach and right leg were torn apart, died of his wounds at al-Raai Hospital in Sidon"

"Army experts found two other cluster bombs in the same area Saturday and detonated them, police said. They also cordoned off the area pending a further search for unexploded devices. Cluster bombs are frequently used by "Israeli" troops in their attacks against Lebanese targets despite their ban in international covenants signed by the Jewish state".

"Mahmouds six-year-old brother, Ali, was with the two children at the time of the explosion but managed to escape unscathed because he ran as soon as he saw smoke emanate from the bomb just before it exploded".

"Despite being in a stable condition after undergoing surgery to remove shrapnel from his head and body, Mahmoud remains in danger, said intensive care doctor Bassema Ghader at al-Raai Hospital. He is still being given tranquilizers for his pain and may have to undergo further surgery, she added. At Ahmeds kindergarten classroom at the Evangelical School, lessons were suspended for a day allowing the slain boys classmates to attend his funeral. Pointing to Ahmeds picture on the wall, his teacher, Amal, said: I still cannot imagine that Thursday he was with us and today he is gone.
"Ahmeds funeral, which coincided with his sixth birthday, was attended by dozens of students who carried wreaths".

"His father Raed, the press attaché of the Shaqif social club near Nabatieh, collapsed behind the coffin and was heard asking: What am I supposed to tell your brother when I return home without you?

The funeral was attended by Bint Jbeil MP Ayoub Humayed and Nabatieh MPs Mohammed Raad and Abdel-Latif Zein, in addition to Nabatieh Governor Mahmoud Mawla and local political, community, military and religious leaders".

This crime is yet another indication of the cruelty perpetrated against our children by "Israel" and its ally, the United States, which supplies it with the latest and the most efficient killing equipment, said Sheik Abdel-Hussein Sadeq, the imam of Nabatieh, during the eulogy. Mr. Mawla later issued a memorandum reminding all residents of Nabatieh to avoid touching suspicious objects and to report them to the nearest security position.

"Israel" raids Jabal Botom (3)
""Israeli" warplanes Sunday raided wadis near Jabal Botom in the western sector".

"Two jets fired a pair of missiles into the area. No casualties were reported in the raid, which brought to 14 the number of "Israeli" air attacks on Lebanon since the beginning of the year".

""Israeli" artillery in Mohaibib and Taibe Saturday shelled the central sector around the villages of Majdal Silm, Qabrikha and Souwanneh. Shelling also reached Wadi Salouqi, Wadi Qaisieh, Wadi Hojeir and the Litani River".

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following aggressions (4):
10:50, three 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Borj Qalaweih.
11:15, the peripheries of Qalaweih came under nine 155mm missiles.
Between 14:20 and 14:30, the south of Jibal El-Botom village was shelled by twelve 155mm missiles.
14:30, Jib Soweid area was bombarded by twelve 155mm missiles.
15:10, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the village of Jibal El-Botom firing two air-to-surface rockets.
Between 17:50 and 18:00, each of Mount Sojod and the perimeters of Loweizeh came under fourteen 120mm shells.

TL cameraman arrested for collaborating (5)
"A Tele-Liban cameraman who disappeared two days ago near a Hizbullah stronghold in Beiruts southern suburbs has been arrested by authorities for allegedly dealing with "Israel", a security source said Sunday".

Mahmoud Jalloul is currently being questioned over allegations that he leaked information harmful to national security to "Israeli" and pro-"Israeli" agencies, a security source told The Daily Star. He could be part of the ring of collaborators that was cracked last week.

"Army intelligence announced Friday that it had arrested 20 members of a group suspected of spying on the armed forces and on resistance groups fighting "Israeli" troops and allied militiamen in the south.

Mr. Jalloul was reported to have been abducted near Bir al-Abed Thursday after a traffic argument with two men, but it now appears that he was ambushed by the resistance and turned over to the authorities. Elias Abu Rizk, the TL union head who had decried the kidnapping, gave no comment when contacted by The Daily Star".

Military Operations (6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) marked the Week of Islamic Resistance and the Anniversary of Martyrdom of Sayyed Abbas Al-Mousawi and Sheikh Ragheb Harb with a series of military operations.

At 10:20, the IR group of martyrs Ali and Mohammad Hassan blew up a roadside bomb as an "Israeli" agents patrol was passing on the road between Roum and Zahr Ramleh scoring direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Abdo Agha Zada and Soheil Diab attacked, at 14:00, Blat post with the suitable weapons. Accurate hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported. Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Mustafa Mozannar and Mohammad Hemayed targeted Tair Harfa post with the appropriate arms chalking up direct hits, whereas the IR group of martyrs Hassan Obeid and Hassan Shamma attacked Hardoun position with the adequate arms scoring accurate goals. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Moussa Abou Taam and Hassan Harb targeted Radar post with the proper weapons. The western fortification was hit definitely.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Soheil Diab and Mustafa Mozannar attacked, at 17:20, an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and definite goals.
