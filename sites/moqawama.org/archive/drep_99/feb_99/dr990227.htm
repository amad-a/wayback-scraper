Daily Report
February Saturday 27-1999:

Introduction
Two days after killing three "Israeli" officers and wounding seven soldiers in the Islamic Resistance (IR) ambush at Birkat Jabbour in Western Beqaa, the "Israeli" Army tried to boost its soldiers' morale through  broadcasting on all "Israeli" television channels Thursday night, a video footage from a unpiloted reconnaissance drone, showing the supposed final moments of IR man. Its attempt, however, goes to nowhere.  Friday night, the IR "living martyr" managed to withdraw miraculously from deep inside the occupied zone and contact hits command after a 48-hour breakoff.

On the other hand, hundreds of the Lebanese students stormed yesterday into the village of Arnoun, nine days after "Israeli" occupation troops and their proxy militia sealed it off from the rest of the country.

"Hadi al-Sayyed Hassan" returned safely (1)
"Resistance man "Hadi al-Sayyed Hassan" was able to contact his squad after a 48-hour breakoff.
Contact between the Islamic Resistance (IR) and the resistance man was lost following the Markaba operation in which IR clashed with "Israeli" occupation forces three kilometers away from occupied Palestine".

"After the Markaba confrontation, the IR squad was able to retreat except for one of the Mujahedeen who had chosen to be a martyr in order to cover the retreat of the other Mujahedeen, IR sources said.

The resistance man was able to hide in a deserted house for some time. The "Israelis" who spotted him in the hideout surrounded the house with a large number of infantry troops and two armored vehicles without daring to storm the house", the IR sources added.

"Thursday morning, the occupation forces fired a rocket propelled grenade on the house. But the resistance man, who was named "Hadi al-Sayyed Hassan" after the son of Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, Hadi, who was martyred during an operation against the occupation forces, was able to jump out of the house's porch".

"He resorted to nearby bushes, while confusion engulfed the "Israelis", but he was being watched by the "Israelis" who called in a helicopter to chase him"

"The IR sources affirmed that the enemy helicopter fired an air-to-ground missile at the resistance man but missed him. However, he sustained some scratches and bruises".

"He then started a miraculous withdrawal camouflaging himself with three branches, until he was able to escape the circle which the "Israelis" tried to besiege him inside".

"The resistance sources which would not reveal the "living martyr's" name said he is twenty years old, has a fiancée and his condition does not call for concern".

The "living martyr" talks about the confrontation (2)
"I reached a stage at which I thought I will die, but I had great faith in Allah. I did the impossible to fight death. It's not that I did not want to reach the afterlife, for this is our wish, all of us, but in order to deny the unjust Jews any tiny victory, the "living martyr" who was named Hadi al-Sayyed Hassan said.

The living martyr is in a Sidon hospital where he is being treated for scratches and bruises sustained during his confrontation with the enemy's military machinery".

"He was visited today (yesterday) by delegations and figures who congratulated him on his safe return.

He received Head of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad, Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) member MP Nazih Mansour, Hizbullah's official in the south Sheikh Nabil Qawooq and a group of IR martyrs' fathers".

"Hadi al-Sayyed Hassan" spoke bravely and proudly. He stressed that "The Berket Jabbour operation had a great impact on the Zionists' morale; therefore, they desperately needed to kill a resistance man to save face and boost their morale.

He underlined that his mission in Markaba was "excellently accomplished".

"I didn't know I was being filmed. Death was just there in front of me. I turned my face to the Qibla and trusted in Allah, for I had set out to be a martyr in Allah's sake, he said.

Zionist warplanes staged raids to kill me. One of the missiles exploded only 2 meters away from me.
Two trees blew up in front of me, but I was able to stand up with Allah's assistance, he added.
"Afterwards, two helicopters were after me. I overturned on the ground twice. I thought the warplanes would raid to kill me once again."

"The living martyr said "I hoped occupation troops would approach to pull me back, as they thought I was dead, but Allah -- praised be His name -- has planted panic in their hearts. I wanted them to approach, I was eager to kill one of them".

"Having returned safely, the living martyr affirmed that he will continue "in the Jihad path, in the ranks of the Islamic Resistance against the invading Zionists".

"Our action has no limits. It ends with victory or martyrdom, he said.
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah phoned the living martyr and congratulated him on his safe return".

Hizbullah fighter killed by "Israelis" appears on TV (3)
"Praising its troops for a job well done, the "Israeli" Army televised video footage from a unpiloted reconnaissance drone, showing the supposed final moments of a Hizbullah guerrilla.

"The dramatic film, which was broadcast on all "Israeli" television channels Thursday night, clearly portrayed the fleeing guerrilla, helicopter gunships, explosions and gunfire.

"The "Israeli" Army, releasing the video two days after three officers were killed and five soldiers wounded in a Hizbullah ambush, clearly hoped the footage would boost morale.

Imagine the red faces all around, then, when the killed guerrilla turned up alive and well in the Hammoud hospital in Sidon 24 hours later, with a few scratches on his face and arm.

"Clearly exhausted by his lengthy ordeal, the guerrilla, given the nom de guerre Hadi Sayyed Hassan, quietly recounted to The Daily Star his astonishing escape, which saw him chased by soldiers, tanks, helicopter gunships and even a missile-firing F-16 fighter-bomber.

"He and three colleagues embarked on a mission Wednesday, creeping into the central sector of the zone through Wadi Salouqi and heading for Markaba, 3 kilometers away.

The Markaba operation was not a routine mission since we had to cross lengthy distances and pass between "Israeli" positions. Simply getting so close to the Palestinian border is a victory in itself, he said.

But on the way back from the mission, the squad ran into an "Israeli" patrol.

We adopted a fire-and-withdraw tactic. I lay on the ground to cover the withdrawal of the guys using my Kalashnikov and hand grenades so that the "Israelis" couldnt advance, he said.

The fighters had inadvertently stumbled upon a unit of Golani Brigade troops trained to operate at close quarters against guerrillas. Having secured the withdrawal of his comrades, Hadi found himself cut off and took cover in an abandoned house in Markaba.

I took all the precautionary measures. Crouching under a staircase, I covered my head with my arms as I clutched onto my hand grenades, he said.

"Trapped by Merkava tanks, troops and with a helicopter gunship circling above, Hadi said that, at one point, he believed his only choice was to commit an act of martyrdom and kill the greatest number of Zionists".

I decided to face them and not surrender at all, he said.

Suddenly, Hadi said, a Merkava tank fired shells at the house followed by a long burst of machine gun fire.

I remained in my position, still clutching the hand grenades, waiting for a chance to throw them.

When I decided to stand up and fire at a soldier standing on the Merkava, a bulldozer approached in an attempt to destroy the house, he said.

Hadi jumped off the back porch and crouched down to assess the situation.
I realized that I was in the tanks sights there too. I fired several shots and ran as fast as I could, he said.

"The drone continued to track Hadi as he raced down a street and into fields surrounding the village.
As he approached Wadi Salouqi, with the edge of the zone tantalizingly close, Hadi took cover momentarily to regain his breath. But at that moment a missile fired from an F-16 warplane exploded beside him".

Trees around me flew and I couldnt hear anything. I buried myself in the soil, knowing that none of the "Israeli" soldiers would dare come close to me, he said.

The "Israelis", apparently assuming Hadi had been killed, gave up the chase.

Hadi, meanwhile, discovered that his walkie-talkie was still working. He contacted his comrades and was guided out of the zone.

"Hospital officials said that Hadis condition was stable. He lost a lot of blood, but three people donated blood to him and he is fine now, an official said.

Chaos, confusion, worry in enemy command following Berket Jabbour operation (4)
""Israeli" enemy's war minister Moshe Arens reiterated threatening Hizbullah and announced that the "Israeli" inner cabinet's decision "will be implemented, in reference to a new "Israeli" policy of
retaliation for resistance operations with bombarding Lebanese depth".

""Israeli" newspapers reflected the confusion of the enemy leadership following Monday's clash in Berket Jabbour which took the lives of three "Israeli" elite paratroopers and wounded seven others.
"Israeli" dailies quoted military officials who believe that the Islamic Resistance (IR) ambushed the occupation paratroopers; others said the clash was coincidental, ruling out that a double agent has informed Hizbullah about the "Israeli" force's movement".

"Other newspapers said the resistance squad stay at the operation site and its collection of loot indicate that the "Israeli" northern command officers were wrong in their assumption that Hizbullah fighters were surprised to see the "Israeli" force".

"For his part, the "Israeli" paratroopers' Brigade commander said his forces were unable to fire in a concentrated manner, and was unable to pinpoint the sources of fire which were targeting the "Israeli" troops".

"After the first phase of the clash, at which the "Israeli" paratroopers' unit commander was killed, the "Israeli" force did not return fire", he said.

Sheikh Qassem: IR had advance information of Berket Jabbour commandos (5)
"Hizbullah Deputy Secretary-General Sheikh Naem Qassem affirmed today (yesterday) that the Islamic Resistance (IR) had advance information of the "Israeli" commando unit that was ambushed last Monday at Berket Jabbour".

"Sheikh Qassem would not give a further details but said the IR Mujahedeen were prepared and had already some kind of a plan".

"The event was not an ordinary surprise or coincidence," he said.

He also noted that the outcome of the Berket Jabbour qualitative operation matched IR's continuous interest in observing the enemy's movements and tracing its soldiers in various outposts and places".

Students to the rescue (6)
"An army of outraged students ­ ignoring the likely presence of land mines and the threat of "Israeli" attack ­ stormed into Arnoun Friday in a protest action that seems to have convinced the occupation forces that they they had no option but to reverse last weeks annexation of village".

"Just when the governments diplomatic offensive appeared to be rapidly losing momentum and the five-nation Monitoring Group helpless in the face of the new "Israeli" agggression, more than 1,000 students journeyed to Arnoun from Beirut, pulled down the barbed wire fences with their bare hands and marched into the village to hug its residents. It was a scene of extraordinary emotion in the beleaguered hamlet".

"Reliable Western diplomatic sources confirmed for The Daily Star Friday night that the "Israelis" have no intention of replacing the barbed wire barricades cordoning off Arnoun and are content to leave the village as it is ­ a clear sign that the occupiers want to de-escalate tensions growing out of Arnouns siege and isolation".

"Events came to a head shortly after the students stormed Arnoun. President Emile Lahoud instructed Lebanons delegate to the Monitoring Group, Colonel Maher Toufeili, to inform the committees chairman that "Israel" would be held responsible for any harm that befell the students".

"Richard Ehrdmann, chairman and U.S. delegate to the group, relayed a message to the Lebanese that "Israel" would leave the village alone as long as the student demonstrations were halted. Lebanon responded that it would not yield to any "Israeli" conditions and that any future disputes of this nature be settled by the Monitoring Group".

"Speaker Nabih Berri said that the U.S. government had pressured "Israel" not to re-erect the barricades. But we cant trust the "Israelis" and we must continue to take care, he told The Daily Star.

Asked his reaction to the fact that it took hundreds of students to return the village to Lebanon rather than diplomatic initiatives, Mr. Berri said: If the people want something badly enough, then there is nothing anyone can do to stop them.

"The astonishing turn of events Friday began as hundreds of students flocked to Arnoun shortly after midday to hold a rally beside the barricades erected by "Israeli" forces nine days ago."

"As numbers grew, the students became more excited and began chanting slogans against the occupation, singing the national anthem and shaking the fence.

A few residents of Arnoun, on the other side of the fence, asked the students not to tear the fence down as they were concerned at "Israeli" reprisals".

"Zeinab Hamdan, sister of Afaf Hamdan, who was buried two days ago in a cemetery outside the village, told the crowd that she had been unable to attend the funeral because she was trapped inside Arnoun".

"Her lamentations spurred the crowd to begin ripping out the stakes holding the barbed wire.
At that point, the "Israeli" soldiers manning Beaufort castle on the hill above Arnoun, began firing
several bursts from a machine gun over the heads of the demonstrators.

Undeterred, the students continued ripping the fence down and then charged into the village through a field dotted with warnings of land mines".

"Two "Israeli" armored personnel carriers drove out of the castle and began descending
the road leading to the village. The students ignored the implied threat and continued to fan out around the village. One of them climbed an electricity pole and unfurled the Lebanese flag at the top.

The news that the village had been stormed spread like wildfire. Local imams called from the mosques of nearby villages for everyone to proceed to Arnoun. Hundreds answered the call".

"Some 200 meters of fencing were torn down and the swing gate in the center of the village, originally erected by the "Israelis" to demarcate the beginning of occupied Arnoun, was removed.

General Emile Lahoud himself acknowledged the bold move of Lebanese students and their courage in front of "Israeli" forces.

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss also praised the students who took part in this demonstration of pride and dignity that challenged the "Israeli" siege imposed on Arnoun.

This was an act of heroism in defending our national and legitimate rights and to reflect our attachment to our land, he said.

"Israeli" attacks increasingly target children (7)
" The annexation of Arnoun was only one of many illegal acts "Israel" committed in Lebanon. Children have increasingly become targets and victims in south Lebanon. Our next report is about their plight.

Report: Almost three years ago, the world was shocked by this site - a young girl lying dead in an ambulance that was taking her to safety. The ambulance was targeted by an "Israeli" air to ground missile. But attacks like this did not stop and over recent weeks, children have increasingly become targets of "Israeli" aggressions. So they decided to raise their voices, hoping that this time they will be heard. Children from across Lebanon took to the streets to ask the world for protection. Eight year old Zeinab and her two year old brother Abbas are still in hospital three weeks after "Israeli" forces who occupy land in south Lebanon shelled their village of Majdel Selm. They were severely burned when phosphorous and shrapnel shells landed in their living room. Doctors say they are still in critical condition. Not too long after that attack, one child was killed and another wounded when they stumbled on a cluster bomb planted by "Israeli" forces in the southern village of Nabatiyeh. Five year old Ahmed Mokalled died of his wounds. His father holds back his tears as he recalls the events of that fateful day. Raed Mouqalled, father said: "I took Ahmed and his friends on a picnic. They were playing. And suddenly we heard an explosion. Ahmed died and his friend was wounded". Cluster bombs are frequently used by "Israeli" troops in their attacks despite their ban in international conventions signed by "Israel". Ahmed's mother has still not come to terms with her son's death. His father, Raed said his wife has still not been able to go home - everything there serves as reminders of her youngest child. His toys still lie on his bed and his clothes still hang in the closet. Ahmed was a playful and loving kid and that is how his father remembers him. And memories are all he has left. Ahmed was buried on his sixth birthday. Ahmed was not a terrorist nor did he carry arms. He was a young boy who had a future ahead of him. He was not the first to be deprived of fulfilling his dreams. And he may not be the last".

Military Operations (9)
The Islamic Resistance team of martyr Mohammad Atwi attacked, at 00:45, enemy movements at Qabaa post with the adequate weapons chalking up direct goals.

At 20:20, the IR team of martyr Hassan Soueli attacked an "Israeli" infantry force near Taibeh post with the machine guns and hand grenades scoring direct hits.

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Soueli targeted Taibeh post as it interfered to back up the attacked force. The militants, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, chalked up direct hits and destroyed the fortification which overlooks the scene barring it from achieving its goals.

Also, the IR group of martyr Youssef Dia attacked, at 20:40, Qantara position with the rocket-propelled grenades destroying its main fortification.

Moreover, the IR anti-aircraft guns and rockets repelled, at 21:30, the "Israeli" warplanes as they were trying to support the attacked force. The clashes lasted for more than half an hour where various kinds of weapons were used. The "Israeli" enemy suffered a state of panic firing, randomly, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds at the houses of Qabrikha village, but no casualties were reported. In the meantime, the "Israeli" helicopters reiterated trying to interfere in the confrontations, but the IR anti-aircrafts drove them away.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Bachir and Abdo Agha Zada attacked, at 22:10, enemy movements inside Dabsheh outpost with the suitable arms. The militants were able to chalk up direct goals.

Later, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas and Amin Zahwi reiterated targeting enemy movements in and around Dabsheh outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

At 23:35, the IR mujahedeen scored a remarkable breakthrough following a series of heroic attacks near Taibeh area. So, the IR team of martyr Hassan Mreish in the Special Forces of Sayyed Hadi Nassrallah Brigade penetrated deeply inside the "Israeli"-occupied zone and managed to evacuate the bodies of the martyrs Hassan Mohieddine Hamzeh, Ihab Haidar Medlej and Rabih Ibrahim Marzouk from the scene. The three martyrs were martyred two days before in fierce confrontations against the "Israeli" soldiers near Taibeh village in the occupied zone.

In the meantime, another squads of IR were attacking the "Israeli" position of Taibeh and the Lahdi post of Qantara, while the IR anti-aircraft guns fired at the "Israeli" helicopters as they were trying to reinforce the enemy forces.

Also, the IR group of martyr Nidal El-Hoq targeted, at 23:40, the "Israeli" Command of the 70th Brigade with the rocket-propelled grenades, while at the same time, the IR team of martyr Mohammad Atwi attacked the Lahdi HQ of the 70th Brigade at Markaba area scoring direct hits.
