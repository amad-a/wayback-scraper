Daily Report
 
 
February Saturday 13-1999:

Introduction:
Two children were injured, yesterday, as seemingly cluster bomb, remnant of earlier ""Israeli"" aggressions, was exploded in Tool village.

Meanwhile, the April Understanding Monitoring Group has held "Israel" and its allied militia responsible for violating the understanding by firing phosphorous shells causing serious burns to three children and one teenager in the southern town of Majdal Selem.

However, The Lebanese Army's Intelligence Apparatus has unveiled a terror and espionage network working for the ""Israeli"" enemy. Most of the cell members have been put under arrest.

Two infants seriously wounded in an "Israeli" remnant bomb.
"An unidentified object, remnant of earlier ""Israeli"" aggressions, exploded at 14:20 today in the town of Tool (South) wounding two infants".

"Mahmoud Hussein Hmadeh, 2, and Ahmed Raad Moqalled, 5, were seriously wounded and taken to al-Rahi and Hammoud hospitals in Sidon".

"On the other hand, ""Israeli"" warplanes circled over southern areas continually all day long today.

""Israeli"" artillery at 16:30 bombarded Mazraat al-Hamra and the outskirts of Yohmor" (1)

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) issued a communiqué in which it reported that the "Israeli" occupying troops had staged the following aggressions (2):
1:50, the perimeters of Jibaa town came under seven 155mm missiles.
2:00, Salaah position showered its surroundings with a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds, whereas six flare shells were fired over Mothallath position.
7:25, Sojod outpost raked Mlita and Mount Sojod with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
9:40, Mount Sojod was targeted by four 120mm shells.
10:35, two 120mm shells struck south of Zibqeen village.
12:30, the surroundings of Houla position subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
13:25, Mazraat Hamra was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
15:10, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the surroundings of Dawawir post.
15:45 and at 16:20, the surroundings of Beit Yahoun position exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Monitoring Group says "Israel" violated the April Understanding (3)
"The five-nation Monitoring Group condemned "Israel" Friday, saying it violated the 1996 cease-fire agreement by targeting civilians".

"In a statement, the group deplored attacks by both sides, including two ""Israeli"" artillery attacks of the village of Majdal Silm in which six Lebanese civilians were wounded.

Four victims, three of them children, sustained serious burns from ""Israeli"" phosphorus shells.
Meeting Thursday and Friday at Unifil headquarters in Naqoura, the group ­ which comprises Lebanon, Syria, France, the United States and "Israel" ­ discussed six Lebanese complaints and seven filed by "Israel"".

"In addition to the two filed over the shelling of Majdal Silm, the Lebanese complaints included two protests concerning the shelling by ""Israeli"" forces and its allied militia ­ the South Lebanon Army ­ of the village of Majdal Zoun, one on an attack against Haddatha and another concerning the shelling of Kfar Roummane".

"The group acknowledged six ""Israeli"" complaints, including three over last months shelling of the village of Rihan in which a house was damaged and 50 goats killed."

"But a security source said the group did not accuse any party of shelling the ""Israeli"" settlement of Kiryat Shmona, because the "Israelis" did not present evidence to support their claims".

The Monitoring Group acknowledged that on Feb. 8, 1999, one anti-aircraft round impacted in Kiryat Shmona. The ""Israeli"" delegate stated that this was a violation of the understanding. The Lebanese delegate said that Lebanon had no information that such an attack had occurred on that date, the group said.

"The group urged all concerned parties to take urgent measures to ensure that military operations are carried out in strict conformity with the provisions of the understanding".

20 arrested on spying charges (4)
"Army intelligence has arrested 20 members of a group suspected of spying on the armed forces and on resistance guerrillas fighting the occupation by ""Israeli"" troops and allied militiamen in the south, the security agency said in a statement Friday"

"The group was uncovered following Thursdays arrest and interrogation of an officer of the pro-""Israeli"" South Lebanon Army militia, the statement said.

"The arrests were made during a campaign in all Lebanese regions, the statement added without giving further details".

"The officer, Mohammed Nabaa, was responsible for security in the village of Shebaa in the ""Israeli""-occupied zone. He was earlier said to have defected from the SLA and left the occupation zone with his family."

"Last year, the Military Tribunal handed down prison sentences to 75 people who were convicted of collaborating with "Israel"".

Military Operations (5)
The IR squad of martyrs Ali Khalil and Ahmad Hariri attacked, at 1:30, enemy motorized movements at Bir Kallab outpost. The militants who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalked up direct and accurate goals.

For its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted, at 7:30, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying its main fortification.

"However, Amal Movement announced that it had targeted at 13:15 (Friday) and ""Israeli"" patrol that was traveling along the Beaufort Castle outpost's road".
"At 13:20, another Amal squad targeted ""Israeli"" movements inside the outpost of Al-Izzieh (6)
