Daily Report
 
 
February Friday 12-1999:

Hoss doubts Netanyahus promises (1)
"Prime Minister Salim Hoss Thursday called "Israeli" Prime Minister Netanyahus promise to conclude a peace accord with Lebanon and Syria insincere and a maneuver in the run-up to "Israeli" elections.

No one can believe what Netanyahu says, especially in light of his violations to promises he has made, Mr. Hoss said.
"He was responding to Mr. Netanyahus promise Wednesday to deliver a peace agreement with Syria and Lebanon by next year."

Netanyahus remarks are not aimed at achieving a just and comprehensive peace, but at gaining votes in the battle for the prime ministers post, added Mr. Hoss.

"He said that as long as "Israel" did not unilaterally and unconditionally withdraw from Lebanon, discussion of a regional settlement was useless".

collaborators' official in Shibaa fled to liberated areas (2)
"Head of the intelligence body in the proxy militia in the Shibaa town fled to liberated areas today.

Security sources in Rashayya said Mohammed Nabaa was able to escape with his wife and six-year-old son. They had to cross a mountainous area".

"Nabaa gave himself up to Lebanese authorities".

"He replaced Ghassan Daher who had been assassinated about a month ago".

"Israeli" Aggressions (3)
In its communiqué, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:
00:40, the surroundings of Houla position subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
11:0, several 80mm shells fell on the area between Ilman and Mothallath.
11:10, the outskirts of Khorbet Selem exposed to three 155mm missiles.
11:15, each of Mazraat Hamra and the perimeters of Borj Qalaweih and Ghandourieh came under two 155mm missiles, whereas the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom village came under three others.
Between 12:35 and 12:55, fifteen 155mm missiles pounded into the perimeters of Jibaa town.
15:50, the "Israeli" warplanes fired one air-to-ground rocket at an area between Touline and Qalaweih.
16:45, the "Israeli" fighters staged two sorties into the peripheries of Touline village.
16:50, the warplanes reiterated targeting the area between Touline and Qalaweih with five air-to-ground rockets.
16:55, the "Israeli" jets launched on raid into the peripheries of Khorbet Selem town firing four air-to-ground.
17:30, three 155mm missiles struck Mazraat Hamra.

Military Operations (4)
The IR squad of martyrs Karim Atwi and Abbas Bahja attacked, at 12:15, the headquarters of the Twentieth Brigade Command at Jezzine area with the adequate weapons. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Ibrahim Hussein and Mohammad Hassan targeted, at 12:45, Ain Thaghra emplacement at Jezzine area with the appropriate arms. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct hits.
