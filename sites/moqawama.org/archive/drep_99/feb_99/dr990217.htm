Daily Report
 
 
February Wednesday 17-1999:

Introduction
A sixteen-year old Lebanese civilian sustained first degree burns when an explosive device went off near the village of Siddiqeen in South Lebanon.

Meanwhile, Hizbullah's Central Information Unit made a tour for about seventy representatives of local and foreign media at a remote front line location in Iqleem Al-Toffah.

However, unofficial Security sources revealed that the Islamic Resistance (IR) managed to uncover four people who have been directly collaborating with the "Israeli" enemy through a European security apparatus.

"Israeli" explosive injures young man in South Lebanon (1)
" A 16 year old Lebanese suffered from first degree burns when an explosive charge went off near the village of Sidikeen in south Lebanon. Mahmoud Balhas had picked up the device which looked like a remote control before it went off. Mahmoud is in hospital. His face, hands and legs were severely burned. The device was apparently placed by "Israeli" occupation troops".

"Israel" bombs Jabal Botom for third day (2)
"An "Israeli" warplane dropped a pair of bombs for the third day in a row into what must by now be a battered stretch of countryside near Jabal Botom in the western sector. As with similar raids on the area Sunday and Monday, there were no casualties".

"Further north, "Israeli" troops and two tanks from Beaufort Castle set up a road block on the outskirts of Arnoun between 3.30 P.M. and 5 P.M. Monday, checking all cars heading for the village and neighboring Yohmor".

However, the IR statement reported that the "Israeli" occupation troops staged the following aggressions (3):
6:00, Mount Sojod was shelled by five 120mm shells.
6:35, several 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Haddatha town.
6:50, Qaissieh Valley was targeted by four 155mm missiles, whereas Jib Soweid are was hit by four others.
7:00, five 120mm shells landed Mount Sojod.
7:30, four 120mm shells slammed into Haddatha woodland.
11:40, Litani River watercourse was bombarded by twenty 155mm missiles.
14:25, two 120mm shells struck Mount Sojod.
14:40, four 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Loweizeh and three others on Mount Safi.
Between 15:45 and 15:50, west of Yater village came under fourteen 155mm missiles.
16:00, the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh El-Fawqa was shelled by a number of 155mm missiles.
16:15, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the west of Yater.
17:55, three 155mm missiles struck the peripheries of Majdal Selem towns.
18:20, Masslakh quarter of Nabatieh city subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:30, five 155mm missiles fell on the south of Jibal El-Botom village and several 155 and 120mm shells on Mazraat Hamra. At the same time, the surroundings of Dabsheh and Beaufort Castle subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
18:35, Dabsheh outpost showered the adjacent Khanouq area with 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
18:45, the deserted Lebanese Army barracks of Nabatieh was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
18:55, three tank rounds struck Ghandour Palace at Nabatieh El-Fawqa.
19:00, Habboush River watercourse was shelled by several 155mm missiles.

Hizbullah offers rare glimpse of activities (4)
"Usually shrouded in secrecy, Hizbullahs military wing Tuesday allowed reporters a rare glimpse into the world of the resistance fighter, his weapons and tactics at a remote front line location in Iqlim al-Touffah".

"Some 70 representatives of local and foreign media were herded into coaches for the trip, organized by Hizbullahs Central Information Office, to the frontline village of Ain Boussoir, 6 kilometers north of Nabatieh."

"The reporters were led through olive groves just outside the village to an isolated area by camouflaged Hizbullah fighters who seemed to know the location of every stone and tree along the slippery and rocky path".

"The fighters showed their weary visitors to designated chairs facing a makeshift platform overlooking the villages of Ain Qana and Jibaa".

"Impatient photographers and cameramen were told when and of whom they could take pictures by the polite but security-conscious resistance officials."

"Press officer Atallah Hammoud introduced a local Hizbullah commander as Ammar.
Clad in a camouflage uniform, with a black keffiyeh around his neck and sporting sunglasses, Ammar said that the chosen area was secure enough for visitors but was nonetheless classified as a military zone."

"Indicating a map with a laser pointer, he said that his unit was responsible for targeting the South Lebanon Army militiamen based in Rihan.

Ammar informed the reporters that the strategic "Israeli" positions at Bir Kallab and Qassaret Aaroush in the northern sector of the occupation zone were protected by SLA outposts dotted along the frontline.

The SLA positions are used as barriers to protect the "Israeli" soldiers. Recently, the SLA positions have been rebuilt with reinforced cement roofs and equipped with 24-hour radar surveillance, he said.

Ammar added that several of the "Israeli" Armys state-of-the-art Merkava Mark III tanks had been deployed to sensitive military areas.

But despite all these reinforcements, resistance fighters have been able to break through them on numerous occasions, he said.

Ammar said that the number of SLA soldiers had halved to 900 from 1,800 in 1995, but he refused to specify the number of Hizbullah fighters.

Describing the effect of the psychological war against "Israel" through videotapes of Hizbullah operations, Ammar said each film was scrutinized by a special committee before broadcasting to
ensure no secret military tactics were included.

Listing the steps fighters go through before staging an operation, Ammar said that when an outpost or ambush was chosen, an operations committee picked the best way to target it and prepare a back-up plan.

We then pray and look forward to Gods blessing and head to our positions, he said.
Reporters were then shown three different weapons commonly used by the guerrillas, including a Katyusha rocket launcher, a Sagger anti-tank missile and a 122mm mortar.

One Hizbullah fighter, his face daubed with camouflage paint, said that the mortar was a favorite weapon for the resistance because it could be dismantled into three pieces and easily transported across difficult terrain.

We can fire around 15 shells a minute. We can also use fragmentation and phosphorous shells, smoke rounds and flares, the fighter said, as an "Israeli" pilotless reconnaissance plane could be heard buzzing above our position.

"A plan to allow journalists to witness an actual operation by Hizbullah was canceled at the last minute as Ammar received information that security conditions were unstable".

"As reporters mounted the two coaches rented by the party on the return trip, several Hizbullah members were praying under an oak tree."

"The press officer said that they were on their way to stage an operation".

Uncovering espionage ring working under European cover (5)
"Unofficial security sources disclosed that the Islamic Resistance was able to uncover four people who have been directly collaborating with the "Israeli" enemy through a European security apparatus.

The network worked in several Lebanese areas for years using extremely cautious methods to avoid being uncovered, the sources said, adding that the ring members traveled to European and Mideastern countries to deliver information and reports, besides receiving instructions from enemy intelligence officers".

"The spies focused mainly on gathering information about Hezbollah officials, the sources said.
The "Israeli" enemy had to resort to this method (using the cover of a European security body) after uncovering a large number of its networks, and its decreasing ability to directly recruit agents.

Moreover, "psychological perils" prevented many people from working with the enemy's spy service, so it resorted to "seduce some weak individuals to work with it under the cover of European security apparatuses", the sources added.

"Al-Safir newspaper said a Tele Liban cameraman, Mahmoud Jalloul, who is currently in the custody of the Lebanese army intelligence service is one of the network members".

"During investigation, Jalloul talked about the networks' methods, communication with other countries, and the types of activities he has been involved in".

TL cameraman confesses that he collaborated with "Israel" (6)
"A cameraman who was arrested by security forces last week has reportedly confessed to charges of collaborating with "Israel", a judicial source told The Daily Star Tuesday. The source said that Mahmoud Jalloul, a cameraman with state-run Tele-Liban, admitted supplying information to people in Europe who then redirected it to "Israel"".

"Mr. Jalloul and two spy rings recently arrested by the authorities on charges of collaborating with "Israel" will be referred to the military tribunal for trial once interrogation is complete"
"Mr. Jallouls wife reported her husband abducted after he was beaten up and taken away by two men near Bir al-Abed in Beiruts southern suburbs after a traffic disagreement Thursday. Security sources later said that Mr. Jalloul was arrested for allegedly leaking information to "Israeli" intelligence and pro-"Israeli" agents which jeopardized national security".

Beqaa marked memory of Sayyed Abbas Musawi (7)
"Beqaa and the town of Nabi Sheet today (yesterday) marked the anniversary of Sayyed Abbas al-Musawi's martyrdom.

"Former secretary general of Hezbollah, Sayyed al-Musawi, was martyred, along with his wife and toddler son Hussein, by an "Israeli" raid.

"Hezbollah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah attended the rally along with a large number of politicians and figures besides tens of thousands of people.

"Partial solutions and humiliating settlements will go to hell along with those who make them, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding that "I don't say this out of enthusiasm, but out of certainty".

We are confronting an enemy (the "Israelis") that can be defeated and humiliated. They can't sacrifice a lot for an illusion called "a national homeland for the Jews", he said .

"His Eminence called for creating a real resistance wherever there is occupation.

I am sure that these "Israelis" will rush into planes to leave (occupied Palestine), because they are seeking the life in this world, while we demand our right, he said.

"What we need in order to achieve victory are those people who are ready to be martyred, a little time, and a lot of faith, his Eminence concluded".

Children march demanding a UN condemnation of "Israeli" crimes (8)
"Hundreds of children today (yesterday) staged a silent march in Beirut on the martyrdom anniversary of toddler Hussein Abbas al-Musawi who was martyred along with his father, former Hezbollah Secretary General Sayyed Abbas al-Musawi, and his mother in an "Israeli" raid.

"The children marched to the UN HQ in Beir Hassan. They handed officials there a memorandum to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan demanding condemnation of the "Israeli" enemy's crimes against children and working on securing "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon."

"Head of Hezbollah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad said this annual gathering of Lebanese children is a live witness to the "Israeli" crimes and massacres against our children in the southern villages while the world turns a deaf ear to these aggressions".

"What has the world given to our childhood? Is it the Mother's Day gift of the Nabatieh bus massacre?", "How has the world protected out rights? Has it protected them with the missiles which blew up the Mansouri ambulance?", "Is it international law for our rights to be ravished?" read some of the signs which the children carried during the march".

Military Operations (9)
The IR men spotted, at 11:30, enemy movements inside Zimraya post. Shortly afterwards, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit's (ARU) group of martyr Nassar Nassar targeted the post with the proper weapons chalking up direct hits.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Moselmani and Youssef Krayem attacked, at 15:30, enemy force as it was beefing up Blat post. The militants, who used the adequate arms, managed to score direct and certain goals.

For its part, a team of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked at 6:15, enemy movements at Beaufort Castle scoring direct hits. Meanwhile, another team of LRB was targeting Mothallath position striking some of its defenses.
