Daily Report
 
 
February Friday 5-1999:

Introduction
"Israel" escalated the situation its South Lebanon, yesterday, as in warplanes raided into the outskirts of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri ravine. The "Israeli" artillery, also, shelled several villages and towns damaging a house at the town of Majdal Zoun.
Meanwhile, Col. Bashar El-Assad said that the Lebanese resistance is a national state which originated as a result of usurping Lebanese territories.

In "Israel", Chief of Staff Shawl Mofaz disclosed that there are officers in the "Israeli" Army who advocate a unilateral withdrawal from South Lebanon.

"Israel" steps up attacks in South
" "Israeli" warplanes raided an area near the village of Mansouri early this morning (yesterday). A house in Majdel Zoun was damaged in "Israeli" shelling."

"At 7:20, an "Israeli" fighter bomber raided southern Lebanon. The jet fired two missiles near the village of Mansouri east of the port city of Tyre and just north of the zone "Israel" occupies. A warplane also fired a missile near the village of Majdel Zoun. The house of Abdullah Kassab was damaged. Lebanon is expected to file a complaint to the monitoring group. The cease-fire bans attacks on and from civilian areas but permits the Lebanese resistance to attack "Israeli" occupation troops in the south. No casualties were inflicted in either raid" (1)

Bashar al-Assad: resistance has moved the Arabs' feelings and minds (3)
"The Lebanese resistance has moved the Arabs' feelings and minds and has pushed the enemy's society into turmoil, Col Bashar al-Assad said yesterday".

"He told the Lebanese newspaper al-Kifah al-Arabi the Resistance has made ""Israel"" torn between two decisions: either to stay in Lebanon and take more blows and continuous losses or to pull out unconditionally with all the implications of humiliation and acknowledgment of defeat within the second option" al-Assad said.

"He denied that the resistance is a Syrian bargaining chip".

"The Resistance is a national state which originated as a result of usurping Lebanese territory, he said.
When it defends this land, the resistance defends the honor of every Lebanese. And as this is an Arab land, it is defending the honor of every Arab", he added.

Military Operations (4)

At 7:15, the IR squad of martyrs Soheil Diab and Mustafa Mozannar attacked Kassaret Al-Oroush compound and an "Israeli" patrol on Rihan hilltops. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, inflicted a number of casualties among its ranks.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Hemayed and Haitham Abou Daya targeted, at 7:30, from a close range Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Several casualties were inflicted whereas the northern fortification was hit directly.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Sbeiti and Akram Khatoun targeted Sojod outpost with artillery shells chalking up direct hits. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Obeid and Hassan Shamma targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Akram Khatoun and Ali Hussein Shbeeb attacked, at 17:25, Tair Harfa position. Using the proper weapons, the militants managed to chalk up direct and accurate goals. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Haitham Fawzi Abou Daya and Mahmoud Khodor Baghdadi targeted Hardoun outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct goals.
Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Moghnieh and Mustafa Mohammad Mozannar attacked Blat post with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits.

For its part, Amal Movement targeted, at 6:30, the positions of Taibeh and Qantara with mortar shells and rocket-propelled grenades.

23:00
The IR group of martyrs Moussa Abou Taam and Hassan Malek Harb attacked enemy movements at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Fawaz and Jaafar Zwawi targeted a collaborator force inside Sojod outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to chalk up direct goals.

Enemy chief of staff opposes officers advocating withdrawal (5)
""Israeli" chief of staff Shawl Mofaz disclosed that there are officers in the "Israeli" army who advocate a unilateral withdrawal from south Lebanon".

"Mofaz said he opposes those officers adding that such a withdrawal "poses a bet and colossal perils that can not be expected under the current circumstances."

"A heated debated is under way in the "Israeli" political and military institutions besides the "Israeli" society over the usefulness of occupying south Lebanon in light of the huge losses inflicted on the occupation at the hands of the Islamic Resistance".
