Daily Report
 
 
February Monday 8-1999:

For the second day respectively, the "Israeli" gunners shelled the populated villages and towns of South Lebanon.Yesterday at:

6:55, Haddatha town came under six 81mm rounds and several 120mm shells causing damage to the houses of Moslem Jawad Fadel, Massoud Sabra, Mohammad Kassem Karnib and Hussein Karnib and inflicting the shops of Kassem Mohammad Nasser and Ali Dbouk. Meanwhile, the surroundings of Haddatha compound subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire .
Between 6:55 and 7:25, the perimeters of Haddatha town, Bayada area, came under thirty three 120 and 81mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
7:15, Mount Al-Rafih was bombarded by eight 120mm shells.
12:00, four 160mm missiles fell on Litani River watercourse and two others on the southern peripheries of Zillaya village.
Between 15:30 and 15:40, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by two 120mm rounds and more than five 155mm missiles.
15:30, the perimeters of Majdal Selem town exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire (1)

"Also, two Irish Unifil soldiers wounded Sunday in a mortar attack on Haddatha, a day after heavy shelling of Majdal Silm wounded 12 civilians, the highest number in a single incident in 18 months".

"The coincidence of two village centers being shelled on consecutive days has raised fears that the Israelis may have decided to
revert to the pre-April Understanding tactic of shelling villages indiscriminately in retaliation for resistance attacks".

I hope this is not the beginning of a change, said Timur Goksel, Unifils political advisor and spokesman. If this pattern continues, then its something to worry about.

"The two Irish peacekeepers were wounded when SLA* militiamen manning the outpost overlooking Haddatha in the western sector fired several mortar shells into the village, according to a security source".

"Nine rounds fell close to the Irish B company headquarters in the village, with one round striking the roof of the building beside a machine gun post"

"Corporal Noel Roche was injured in his right leg and hand and Private John Flaherty, who was inside the building at the time, was hit on the head and shoulder by falling masonry, the source said.

Both men were evacuated to Unifils hospital in Naqoura where they are recovering.

Thankfully, they are both out of danger, the source said.

"Irish peacekeepers have been wounded before by SLA militiamen in the Haddatha compound. Last July, an Irish soldier was
wounded in the arm when a mortar round fired by the SLA exploded beside his vehicle."

"Mr. Goksel said that Unifil had formally protested the shelling to the Israeli army".

There was no official response from them other than saying they would open an investigation, he said.

"The attack launched by the guerrillas on the Haddatha outpost was at least 700 meters from the Irish headquarters, according to the security source. If the SLA had intended to strike back at the source of fire, no shells should have hit the Irish position. Mr. Goksel suggested that the shelling could have been deliberate".

Having nine mortar rounds around the post is hard to write off as an accident, he said (2)

Military Operations (3)
A group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 7:00, Dabsheh outpost scoring direct hits, whereas another two groups of LRB targeted Soweida outpost with the appropriate arms striking its main fortification. At the same time, a fourth squad of LRB attacked Haddatha compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up definite hits.

Few minutes later, the LRB's Artillery Unit shelled the concerned compound, whereas another group bombarded Baraasheit compound with the proper arms. Direct hits were scored, the fighters reported.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance group of martyr Mohammad Mahmoud Fahs attacked, at 15:15, Mashaaroun outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.
