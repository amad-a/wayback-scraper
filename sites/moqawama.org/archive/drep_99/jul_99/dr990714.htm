Daily Report
 
 
July Wednesday 14-1999:

Introduction
The sources in the occupied zone in South Lebanon said that huge "Israeli" bulldozers worked on shoveling sand from the fields and orchards of the occupied Khiam area and filled large quantities in "Israeli" army trucks which transported it to the occupied northern Palestine.

Meanwhile, the search for a replacement of top "Israeli" collaborator Antoine Lahd is still on as words about the collapse of Lahd's health are spreading inside the occupied areas.

"ISRAEL" STEALING SOUTH LEBANON SOIL AGAIN, LEBANON SAYS(1)
"Lebanon today (yesterday) called on the United Nations to take action against "Israel" following reports that it is stealing fertile soil from the occupied village of Marjeyoun in south Lebanon. The Lebanese permanent delegation at the world body asked the UN to investigate the reports and halt the theft. This is not the first time "Israel" was accused of such an action. Last year, "Israel" admitted that civilian contractors from the northern Galilee systematically stole soil from the occupation zone".

Search for replacement of top collaborator still on(2)
"The "Israeli" occupation last night (Monday) deployed two Mirkava tanks to the occupied eastern sector in south Lebanon, while the enemy's bulldozers continued building and broadening roads in the area, especially from Zimraya to Ibl al-Saqi".

"In the meantime, the search for a replacement to "inherit" the post of top collaborator Antoine Lahd at the command of the so-called militia a south Lebanese army", is still on as words about the collapse of "Lahd's health are spreading inside the collaborators' circles.

Information said that collaborator Etien Sakr (Abu Arez) started his quest to replace agent Lahd by holding several meetings with other top collaborators, benefiting from his good ties with agents from the militia's security service and the support of the "Israeli" occupation's security services in occupied south Lebanon.

"Agent Abu Arez was quoted as saying that collaborator Lahd is suffering from Cardiac problems and undergoing medical treatment in Paris.

Therefore, collaborator Sakr continued, he (Lahd) might never come back and the area will not be left without a commander".

Lebanese detainees conditions worsening in "Israeli" prisons(3)
"The National committee of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" enemy prisons called on President Gen. Emile Lahoud, Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss, the nations of the world, the human rights organizations and Amnesty International to stand against the "Israeli" occupation's atrocities in the Khiam prison and the ones inside occupied Palestine".

"The committee said the detainees are suffering from extensive torture and many of them are complaining from several symptoms, especially the heroes of the latest Intifada inside the Khiam prison.

"The conditions of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prison are as follows: Riad Kalakesh: got paralyzed lately and suffering from severe pains in the vertebra and the basin.

"Hussein Aqil: completely lost his right eye sight and the left one is threatened.

Mustafa Arabieh: Ulcer infection and constant kidney pain attacks.

"Suleiman Ramadan: left leg amputated, presently inflected and psychological situation is very bad.
Mohammed Birzawee: very bad and difficult psychological situation due to severe torture.

Mohsen Qassam: chronic asthma without treatment.

"Amine al-Amine: lost all his left eye and the right one is very weak.

Mustafa Taobeh: suffering from cardiac problems and his blood pressure reaches 240, putting his life at constant risk.

"Adel Kalakesh: lost his hearing in the left ear and the right one is infected, in addition to a gigantic peptic ulcer."

"Khanjar Shouaib: in solitary confinement despite his suffering from a heart disease.

"Last week's announcement about a letter from the Khiam prison to Beirut prompted the "Israeli" occupation forces and the remains of the collaborators to storm the Khiam prison, the committee noted.

The "Israelis", the committee continued, started beating up and torturing the detainees to make them confess and tell the name of the person who wrote the letter. But when they all refused to tell on their friends, the occupation forces punished them all and deprived them of privileges that become possible by the efforts of the International Red Cross and their visits to the Khiam prison".

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions:
5:10, the outskirts of Haddatha came under nine 155mm missiles.
Between 10:30 and 17:05, Mount Sojod was bombarded by ten 120mm shells and eight 155mm missiles.
16:55, one 120mm shell hit Arid El-Zennar area.

Military Operations(5)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Nizar Kamel Jawad and Ali Reslan Chokair attacked, at 10:00, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct hits and prevent the force from achieving its goals.
