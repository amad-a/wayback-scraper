Daily Report
 
 
July Sunday 4-1999:
 
New "Israeli" pull out expected(1)
"Journalistic information in Beirut agreed that the next "Israeli" pull out will be to the lines of the so-called "Free Lebanon" state that was declared on March 19, 1979 by the collaborating militia's commander back then, Saad Haddad.

"This pull out is expected to start at the end of July or the beginning of August 1999.

Despite "Israeli" denial of such information, the media considered the Jezzine experience a signal for the expected "Israeli" move, especially after information about the collapse of the Lahdi militia and the desire of top collaborator Antoine Lahd to leave the occupied zone.

"Reports from the occupied zone said that collaborators close to the "Israeli" enemy are nowadays talking about the expected move in the open.

This new pullout, if carried out, will liberate the whole Hasbaya area, in addition to what is still under occupation in the Jezzine area and Western Beqaa.

Agent Lahd ready to leave for France(2)
"Top collaborator agent Antoine Lahd notified his "Israeli" superiors his final decision to give up his post as commander of the collaborating militia and join his family in France, reports from the occupied zone said.

"Lahd told the so-called northern commander in the "Israeli" army Gabi Ashkenazi that he is disgusted of the situation and will not reconsider his decision.

"The reports noted that agent Lahd felt that the "Israelis" are interfering with his affairs and militiamen, who turned against him and held him responsible for the breakdown of the militia and the Hizbullah easy infiltration of it".

"Moreover, Lahd told Ashkenazi that the circumstances have dramatically changed and that his officers are no longer concerned with the military or security affairs, all they care about is to collect money and make preparations to leave the occupied zone and nobody can change this fact".

"The report added that the "Israelis" are looking for a replacement to fill in for agent Lahd. They held several meetings at the Marjeyoun barracks to discuss the names competing for the post".

"Among these names are: Aql Hashem, Karamallah Saaed, Nabih Abu Rafeh and Colonel Issam Abu Jamra".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions:
00:10, the surroundings of Tohorra outpost subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
8:55, eight 81mm rounds slammed into the surroundings of Qsair village.
22:45, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the residential neighborhoods of Kfar Tibneet town.
22:55, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal village exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants spotted, at midnight exactly, military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Al-Borji and Haidar Salhab attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals.
Also, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Ali Ahmad and Yasser Nayef Waridan attacked, at 14:00, a Lahdi engineering force as it was building a road near Mount Abou Rkab. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to strike a bulldozer in which a blaze was sparked.
