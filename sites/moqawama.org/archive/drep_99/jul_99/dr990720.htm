Sayyed Nasrallah: Resistance won't stop until Liberation (1)
Hizbullah's Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah stressed on Sunday that the Zionist enemy will not disparage the Resistance's resolve and determination to proceed with the Jihad march, emphasizing "we are very determined to continue this path now more than ever."

During a commemoration for the Haidar Ahmed family Martyrs, his Eminence said the Resistance in the South and Western Beqaa became the only factor pressurizing, depleting and jeopardizing this (Israeli) enemy, beyond the limit of troubling the Israeli occupation army in Lebanon.

The enemy, his Eminence continued, admitted more than once and in different occasions the defeat against the Resistance, the incapability to confront it, the failure to infiltrate it or beat its will and determination.

This Resistance, with its Mujahedeen, its Martyrs' families and its supporters, is more than ever resolved to continue the Jihad and sacrifice's march, Sayyed Nasrallah stated, pointing out that the (Israeli) enemy understands that very well and knows that it is confronting a tireless and weary less Resistance that overcomes all the wounds and stabs.

As for the suggestions calling for a halt to the Resistance's work during the negotiations, his Eminence noted that these are Israeli ambitions we witnessed during the past years.

But after (Ehud) Barak's election the talk about stopping the Resistance's operations came back for the Israeli refuses to negotiate while under pressure and inside the exhausting circle, the Secretary- General argued.

The Resistance will proceed with fighting the (Israeli) occupation and is not concerned with the return of the parties to the negotiation table, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"What would the Resistance do if the (Israeli) enemy leaves the south and signs a peace treaty with Lebanon and Syria?", his Eminence said the answer to this questions will come at the right time.

Moreover, Sayyed Nasrallah stressed that the Resistance is a factor of strength for Lebanon and Syria. It is very normal for the Israeli enemy and its protector and supporter, the United states, to weaken Lebanon and Syria in order to make them go to the negotiation table, scared, shaky and standing on soft ground.

The US administration has never been a fair judge nor a just intermediate. It is a party, an opponent and an enemy bearing the responsibility of each and every drop of blood shed in Lebanon and occupied Palestine, his Eminence concluded.

LEBANESE JUDICIARY TO TRY SLA MEDIA EMPLOYEE  (3)
(dpa) - Military judge Mohammed Makki referred Paul Charbel, a 20-year-old TV presenter for the Israeli-backed militia Middle East TV (MET), to stand trial on charges of collaboration and working for Israeli media, justice ministry officials said Monday. The maximum jail term that Charbel could receive is one year, the officials said. The military judiciary has recently been examining cases against the employees of the militia's media institutions, namely the broadcasters and technicians of the Middle East Television station and two radio stations, Voice of the South and Voice of Hope, the sources said. Nevertheless one of the "legal obstacles" that could hinder the prosecution of militia media employees was that MET was owned by American missionaries. The trials of the remaining militia members, who opted to surrender to the Lebanese authorities after the proxy militia withdrew from the Christian town of Jezzine on June 3, were expected to commence this week, the sources said. The As Safir daily quoted on Monday unnamed judicial sources as saying, the Penal Court has already issued a decision whereby the cases against MET employees were to be reviewed in civilian courts and not military tribunals .Last Thursday, the Beirut military tribunal sentenced two militia members for jail terms ranging from six months to one year.
