Daily Report
 
 
July Monday 5-1999:

Sayyed Nassrallah answers to Satterfield(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah took U.S. Ambassador David Satterfield to task over remarks the diplomat made regarding the April Understanding. When the US ambassador speaks of the April Understanding, he intentionally precedes it with references to the Katyushas before mentioning the "Israeli" aggressions on civilians and infrastructure, Nasrallah said referring to Satterfields comments last week following meetings with Lahoud and Prime Minister Salim Hoss.

He does not respect the civilians of this country or its victims, Nasrallah added. Referring to the "Israeli" clampdown on Yohmor Friday, in which a seven-year-old girl was wounded by an exploding mortar round and residents attempting to leave the village were shot at, Nasrallah condemned the lack of response.

Where are Satterfields comments on that? Isnt this child a civilian? Was she armed? he asked. He who bets on Americans to protect a city must remember that the U.S. administration prevented the UN Security Council from condemning "Israel" for the Qana massacre, he added.

Qassem says "Israel" now planning another pullback(2)
"Sheikh Naim Qassem, Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, said Sunday he expects "Israel" to stage a partial withdrawal soon from the occupation zone to prevent further military casualties.

Qassem also said Israels militiamen have been passing intelligence to Hizbullah information on "Israeli" troop movements".

"Speaking at a religious ceremony along the airport road in Beirut, Qassem added his weight to the growing belief that the "Israelis" are planning to tighten their frontline by pulling out of the more isolated sectors of the occupation zone".

"The partial withdrawal will not come as part of a political stand but to reduce the blows the "Israeli" army is being dealt by the resistance such as in Jezzine and its vicinity, Qassem said.

"Many observers believe the "Israelis" will shortly redeploy from the northern sector to south of the Litani river gorge. The northern sector, which linked the "Israeli"-controlled areas along the border with the collaborators-controlled Jezzine district has become largely redundant following the retreat of the militia from the enclave last month.

Qassem said the collapse of the militia in Jezzine, which led to the pull out, reflected a wider disintegration of the Israeli proxy.

The "Israeli" Army may in the past have hidden politically and militarily behind the agents but today this militia has collapsed and no longer provides cover for them, he said. Furthermore, some of the security and political information on the enemys movements and those of its main leaders get to the resistance through these agents. The Israels militia has turned into an extra burden for the "Israelis".

Qassem said the "Israelis" have attempted to create friction between the resistance and the government by staging air raids against the infrastructure to cripple the economy.

But the positions taken by officials, with President Emile Lahoud at the head, offers us an extra boost. Thus all the plans of the enemy were paralyzed and ended with a far different result that what the "Israelis" wanted, he said.

"The Hizbullah official urged the government to answer the needs of the villagers of Kfar Houna, which now lies on the frontline following the Israeli agents withdrawal from Jezzine".

The residents of Kfar Houna are somewhat sorry that the government did not pay complete attention to all the villages that were liberated (in the Jezzine area), he said.

"The Christian and Shiite residents of Kfar Houna are concerned their village will bear the brunt of "Israeli" retaliation for resistance attacks in the area. Authorities have yet to establish a checkpoint just south of the village at the beginning of the occupation zone. Qassem called on the government to take care of all villages equally.

Man dies of injuries from "Israeli" attack(3)
A man who was among 36 wounded during the "Israeli" air strike on Baalback 10 days ago died of his wounds Sunday.

Abbas Toufeili, who underwent several operations during the past week, was taken to the American University Hospital suffering serious wounds in several parts of his body.

He raises to nine the number of civilians killed in the air raids. Another 64 were wounded.

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:
Between 4:50 and 8:55, Haddatha woodland was targeted by four 155mm missiles and a number of 81mm rounds.
4:55, the western perimeters of Baraasheit town exposed to heavy machine gun fire.
5:05, four 81mm rounds hit the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal whereas the surroundings of Haddatha compound subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 11:55 and 12:40, Jib Soweid area, east of Majdal Zoun village, was shelled by seven 15mm missiles.
17:15, Ali Taher forest was hit by a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Kiryat Shmona crisis center not secure against Katyushas(5)
"The Kiryat Shmona crisis center does not meet security standards and is not reinforced against Katyusha missiles, the city engineer said. Ha'aretz has learned that even though the Homefront Command complained about the situation four years ago, nothing had been done since then to correct the situation. The center, where the emergency teams convene during security crises, is on the first floor of the municipality building".

"Last week's criticism followed the recent missile attacks on the town, when two Katyushas hit the front of the building, killing the two residents Toni Zana and Shimon Elimelech. The engineer had made a similar complaint in 1993 when the structure first began to be employed as a crisis center, nevertheless, it continued to be used as such. A senior source at the municipality said yesterday, "I don't want to think about how many persons would have been killed if the missile had hit the roof while there were 40 people inside".
