Daily Report
 
 
July Friday 16-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said that Hizbullah would not recognize "Israel's" right to exist even if all Arab countries signed peace deals with the Jewish state.

Meanwhile, the four collaborating militiamen held by the Islamic Resistance (IR) willingly affirmed that they regret working with the "Israeli" occupying troops and that the collaborator Antoine Lahd used to deceive them by telling that he is fighting against the legalizing the Palestinians in Lebanon.

However, the "Israeli" soldiers expelled one woman and a family of a mother and her four children who arrived very tired at Kfar Tibneet crossing. The expelled civilians are from Shibaa town.

HIZBULLAH SAYS IT WILL NOT RECOGNIZE "Israel's" RIGHT TO EXIST(1)
"The deputy leader of Hizbullah, Sheikh Nahim Qassem, told Sky News television, Hizbullah would not recognize "Israel's" right to exist even if all Arab countries signed peace deals with the Jewish state. Sheikh Qassem added, the party will continue to reject "Israel" as long as it continues to occupy Palestinian land and Palestinian refugees remain scattered in different countries. Sheikh Qassem refused to say whether Hizbullah would stop fighting "Israel" if it pulled out from occupied southern Lebanon. He stressed that question would be answered after a full "Israeli" withdrawal. Hizbullah which leads the resistance war against "Israeli" occupation troops in southern Lebanon rejected "Israeli" demands for security assurances in return for a pullout".

Imprisoned collaborators:"We hope for fast exchange"(2)
"The four collaborating militiamen held by the Islamic Resistance willingly affirmed that they are very well treated and in good health.

"The prisoners said they regret working with the "Israeli" occupation forces and that collaborator Antoine Lahd used to deceive them by telling them he is fighting against "legalizing the Palestinians in Lebanon."

"The truth is that agent Lahd was helping the enemy to occupy the Lebanese land and kill its civilians they added.

"The Lebanese daily "al-Nahar" visited the imprisoned collaborators and said afterwards the prisoners requested from the International Red Cross to put pressure on the "Israelis" to make the exchange process with Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prisons successful.

"Prisoner Boutros Wehbe is receiving a good treatment and eats all kinds of food, sweets and drinks, in addition to reading newspapers and magazines, al-Nahar added.

The four imprisoned collaborators are: Boutros Wehbe, Mohammed Bassam, Najem Habib and Assad Turmos".

Occupation forces expelled a family from Shibaa(3)
"Khadijeh Saab and her four children this morning (Thursday) arrived very tired at the Kfar Tibneet crossing after spending the whole night under the mercy of the "Israeli" occupation soldiers.

"Khadijeh said the "Israeli" soldiers stormed her house yesterday afternoon and took her with the children to the collaborator's post in the area before driving all five to the crossing point and telling them they can not return to their hometown Shibaa in the occupied zone.

"Mrs Saab arrived in Shibaa two days ago to spend the summer vacation there with her four children, the eldest one is ten years old.

"In addition, the "Israeli" enemy expelled Nadia Abboud Zahra, also from Shibaa, when the "Israeli" soldiers drove her to the same crossing and told her she can not return to her town."

Three towns in occupied zone stormed by enemy soldiers(4)
"The "Israeli" occupation forces renewed their storming campaign which started four days ago after the Islamic Resistance attack against collaborator Ahmed Shebli Saleh who was seriously injured.

"Information from the occupied zone said the "Israeli" occupation and some collaborators last night stormed the occupied towns of Ramia, Odeissy and Aita al-Shaaeb.

"They arrested several civilians, took them to the Saf al-Hawa investigation center and detained them in very bad health conditions", the information added.

Enemy chief of staff admits weakness(5)
"The "Israeli" enemy chief of staff Shaool Mofaz, once again, admitted the weakness of the occupation army in the confrontation with the Islamic Resistance.

"We have to complete our mission in south Lebanon from a powerful stand not a weak one, Mofaz told his soldiers".

"As soon as a settlement is signed you will be out of Lebanon; you have to fulfill your duties in the best way you can", Mofaz concluded.

"Israeli" Aggressions(6)
The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions:

1:05, three 81mm rounds hit Mount Sojod.
4:30, Mount Zaatar was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
4:55, the perimeters of Aita El-Jabal came under three 81mm shells, two 60mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
5:05, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
Between 11:45 and 12:05, Mount Sojod was bombarded by eight 155mm missiles and four 120mm shells.
12:00, five 120mm shells struck the peripheries of Loweizeh village.
12:05, one 120mm shell hit Tasseh Well watercourse.
Between 14:55 and 15:30, the perimeters of Remadieh village came under thirty two 155mm missiles.
15:55, four 155mm missiles landed the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom village.
20:15, four 120mm shells hit the outskirts of Mansrouri.
Between 20:45 and 21:10, several 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Khorbet Selem town.
20:50, Qaissieh Valley was targeted by more than two 155mm missiles.
21:00, two 120mm shells landed Ain El-Tineh village.
21:10, three 155mm missiles fell on the west of Yater and several others on the perimeters of Ain El-Mallouleh village.

Military Operations(7)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Mohammad Ali Jaafar and Taleb Mohammad Ballouq attacked, at 16:50, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

IDF* rabbinate mixed up body parts(8)
"The IDF admitted yesterday that its rabbinate had mixed up the identities of the body parts of naval commandos Raz Tebi and Guy Golan, and consequently placed them in the wrong graves. The remains were returned to "Israel" by the Hizbullah some nine months after the commandos were killed in action in Lebanon".

"Tests conducted by pathologists from the Abu Kabir forensic institute on a body part exhumed from Guy Golan's grave determined conclusively that it belonged to Tebi and not to Golan. This body part was the only one not stored in formalin, which breaks down the chemical makeup of cells and severely damages DNA, making identification impossible.

"Head of the IDF's manpower branch, Major-General Yehuda Segev, visited the homes of the bereaved families yesterday evening to give them the news in person. Golan's body was exhumed last Thursday as the result of a High Court of Justice-brokered compromise between the parents of the two dead soldiers and the IDF. Tebi and Golan were killed on September 5, 1997 and buried in "Israel" two days later"

"On June 25 of last year, when "Israel" got the body of slain commando Itamar Ilia back from the Hezbollah, it was also given what were believed to be additional parts of the bodies of Tebi and Golan. IDF rabbis sorted the remains and without notifying the respective families opened the graves and added the parts".
