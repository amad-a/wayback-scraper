Daily Report
 
 
July Thursday 8-1999:

Introduction
Lebanon's Primer Salim Al-Hoss said the "Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak seems to be ignoring U.N Resolution 425 which calls for an unconditional "Israeli" retreat from South Lebanon.

Meanwhile, the news reported that the collaborating militia's officials are handing out questionnaires to militiamen asking them if they wish to leave the zone or stay in case a new withdrawal takes place.

Elsewhere, a fireman who was wounded during the latest "Israel's" air aggression on infrastructure facilities died Wednesday, bringing the total casualties of the aggression to ten.

Hoss wary of promises(1)
"Prime Minister Salim Hoss on Wednesday injected a note of criticism into the general optimistic mood sparked by the arrival of a new government in "Israel", accusing Ehud Barak of failing to commit himself to an unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon".

I dont want to be pessimistic when many are expressing optimism about the possibility of resuming the peace process in the region, Hoss told reporters. But I noted Baraks omission of Resolution 425, which calls for an unconditional "Israeli" pullout, when he talked about his intent to pull "Israeli" occupation forces out of Lebanon within a year.

"Hoss was referring to comments made by Barak in his inaugural speech as premier in which he promised to revive peace negotiations with Syria and withdraw troops from Lebanon.

Hoss said that Lebanon remained committed to Resolution 425 and rejects any term that "Israel" may link to a withdrawal.

"Also adopting a wary tone was Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah, a leading Shiite cleric, who said that there would be no genuine peace, just a settlement imposed on the Arab and Islamic world. He said the settlement would stem from U.S. pressure and Arab weakness.

Questionnaires for Lahdi collaborators as commander arrive in Paris(2)
"Top collaborator Antoine Lahd today (Wednesday) appeared in the French capital just hours after announcing his intention to resign as commander of the so-called "south Lebanese army".

"Reports from the occupied zone in south Lebanon said the collaborating militia's officials are handing out questionnaires to militiamen, asking them if they wish to leave the zone or stay in case a new withdrawal takes places".

"The questionnaires contains two options: are you ready to join the withdrawing forces? or you prefer to stay in your house and area".

"The aim behind this poll is to put a programmed plan for the next pull out to prevent the repetition of the Jezzine experience, when the collaborating militia withdrew to the occupied zone and left behind some of the undetermined militiamen who surrendered to the Lebanese government".

"Other militiamen returned to Jezzine after withdrawing to give themselves up to the Lebanese government because there was no plan to settle them in the occupied zone".

Fireman dies of wounds from "Israeli" blitz(3)
"A fireman who was wounded during "Israel's" air strikes on infrastructure facilities last month died Wednesday, bringing the total casualties of the attacks to 10".

"Mohammed Ahmad Baltaji died of severe burns and wounds he sustained while trying to put out a fire at the Jamhour power plant after it was attacked by "Israeli" fighter-bombers. Four other firemen were also killed in the attack".

"Baltaji is the latest fatality after a civilian who was wounded in the attack died in Baalback on Sunday.

Prayers will be held at the Martyrs Mosque in Shatila on Thursday. He will be buried at the Martyrs Cemetery".

Escape attempt from Khiam prison failed(4)
"A group of Lebanese detainees lately tried to escape from the Khiam prison by perforating a hole through the prison wall, the "Lebanese detainees follow-up Committee" said today (Wednesday).

At letter, arrived from inside the prison saying the leader of the group, Mehdi Ramadan, succeeded to hide a metal tube under his clothes while he was outside his jail cell to get some fresh air, the Committee stated".

"Once the tube got inside, the group started perforating the hole through the cell wall, the letter said.

It added that a "cell agent", a collaborator planted by the "Israelis" inside the prison, succeeded in infiltrating the group and knew about the escape attempt by gaining the confidence of a detainee.

The "cell agent" informed the prison officials against the group which all of its members were put in solitary confinement and tortured, especially Mehdi Ramadan who was subjected to a "special" torture session, the letter stated."

"Detainee Mehdi, the letter continued, is in a very bad health. His leg was amputated inside the prison.

It is worth noting that this attempt comes as the 14 of July nears, the National Day of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prison, whose number is 120, some of them are women and children".

"Israeli" Aggressions(5)
The statement issued by the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
4:30, three 155mm missiles hit Mount Sojod.
9:30, the perimeters of Loweizeh village came under five 155mm missiles.
22:00, the surroundings of Baraasheit compound subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets, whereas one flare shell was fired over Baraasheit town.
22:05, Slouqi Valley was targeted by 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire. whereas one 81mm flare shell was unleashed overhead.

Military Operations(6)
The IR team of martyrs Hassan Ibrahim Sbeiti and Mohammad Ali Safieddine attacked, at 8:30, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain hits.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Nayef Assaad and Mohammad Ali Soueidan targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Malek Ali Akhdar and Ali Hussein Ghamloush was attacking an "Israeli" enemy force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and definite goals.
