Daily Report
 
 
July Wednesday 7-1999:

"Israeli" occupation freed Tyre fishermen(1)
"The "Israeli" occupation forces this morning (Tuesday) freed the Lebanese fishermen abducted yesterday by the "Israeli" enemy gunboats, three kilometers south to the Tyre coast near Qlayleh.

After eight hours of abduction, during which the fishermen's boats were thoroughly searched, they returned to their homes".

"The fishermen are: Ali Atwi Muslmani, Ammar al-Zarzour, Sayyed Abu Jomaa, Jomaa Jomaa, Bilal Audi, Badih Audi and Badih Tusca"

"The "Israeli" occupation also freed Abbass Rashidi, a Khiam resident, after two weeks of abduction at the Khiam prison, because he refused to pay tributes for the collaborators".

"Israeli" aggressions(2)
The "Israeli" artillery proceeded with the shelling against Lebanese towns and villages on the front lines with the occupied zone in the south".

"Maifadoun, Shoukeen, Qaaqaiyat al-Jisr and western Zawtar in al-Nabatieh district where targeted this morning, in addition to the Iqleem al-Toffah hills".

The "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions:
00:15, two 155mm missiles hit south of Zibqeen village.
10:20, Jib Soweid area was targeted by three shells.
21:20, two shells hits Jib Soweid area.
Between 22:40 and 22:55, Mount Zaatar was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
23:10, eight 155mm missiles slammed into Hamra area.
23:30, several flare shells were fired over Houla area.

Collaborators burned orchards in Hasbaya(3)
The collaborating militia yesterday (Monday) set a huge fire in orchards and fields in the western vicinity of Mimes in the occupied zone in south Lebanon.

The area residents said the fire is the second of its kind in one week. It started when six collaborators in the Ain Qinya front line, fired phosphoric bullets two kilometers deep into the liberated areas, causing a huge fire in Grape vines and olive orchards.

Firefighters from Hasbaya worked very hard to control and put out the raging fire.

Security forces said the orchards belong to Mhanna Abu Hamdan, Youssef Abu Hamdan, Fayez Abu Hamdan, Mhanna Barkat and Hamza Alameh.

It was reported that the fire came as a response to the injury of an occupation soldier at the outpost of al-Radar two days ago.
