Sheikh Obeid 10th anniversary in captivity
The Follow-up Committee of Lebanese Detainees in Israeli enemy prison called for the unity of all national and public efforts to support the detainees' case and leave it out of the political, religious and sectarianism interests.

Marking the tenth anniversary of kidnapping Sheikh Abdel Karim Obeid and his two friends by the Israeli occupation army from their homes in Jibsheet. The committee issued a statement urging the Lebanese government to put the detainees issue, on the top of them Sheikh Obeids case, as a primary aim in the Lebanese foreign policy, and to double the diplomatic efforts to save them from the enemy's prisons.

Moreover, the committee condemned the international silence regarding the detainees' case, calling on the international community to act for the release of all Lebanese detainees in Israeli prison.

Preventing the International Red Cross from visiting Sheikh Obeid in his solitary cell and depriving him from receiving and sending letters to his family, is a crime against humanity and a violation of the Geneva Accord and the International Human Rights Declaration, the committee added.

Sheikh Obeid became the Imam of Jibsheet after the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators assassinated Sheikh Ragheb Harb in 1984.

Four years later, the Israeli commandos kidnapped Sheikh Obeid in July 1988 from his home with two of his friends.

All three are still in captivity in prisons inside occupied Palestine along with other sixty Lebanese nationals without trials.

Lebanese detainees in Israeli prisons very sick
Some 190 Lebanese nationals are now living in very bad conditions inside the Israeli enemy prisons. Among the detainees there are children, women and elderly.

Lately, the health of these detainees who most are held in solitary confinement is worsening, especially the elderly, women and children due to malnutrition, torture, bad ventilation and psychological terror.

In the Khiam prison alone, there are more than 130 detainees most of them have passed the age 50 At least 10 are facing death and they are:

- Ali Mohammed Ghanwi, 64, from Houla. He is suffering from cardiac and stomach pain. Four years in detention.

- Mohammed Salim Qatbay, 60, from Arnoun. Suffering from cardiac crisis and was rushed to the hospital 15 times. Three years in detention.

- Al-Abdeh Qassem Malkani, 70, from al-Qoussair. Suffering from a severe nervous breakdown. Three years in detention.

- Farid Georges Karam, 55, from al-Qlaiaa. Suffering from stomach infection. One year detention.

- Abdallah Mohamad Obeid, 56, from Um al-Tout. Suffering from back pain and not able to walk. Four months in detention.

- Hussein Owadah, 65, from al-Khiam. Suffering from cardiac problems. Two months in detention.

- Khalil Ibrahim Youness, 47, from Shiheen. Suffering from back pain and severe stomach pain. Four months in detention.

- Hussein Saleh Abu Saad, 54, from Shwayya. Suffering from a curve in the spinal cord and from pain in his legs and arms. Three months in detention.

- Kamal Wehbe Mounther, 50, from Ibl al-Saqi. Suffering from cardiac crisis and stomach pain. One month in detention.

- Mustafa Taobeh, 49, from Arnoun. Suffering from cardiac misfunction. Two years in detention.
Detainee Taobeh is the father of detained child Ali Taobeh who is at the Marjeyoun hospital.
The committee called on the UN Secretary General Cofi Anan and the international human rights organizations to work for the release of the sick and the elderly from the Israeli enemy prisons.

Israeli occupation expelled a family from occupied zone
The Israeli occupation forces today (Friday) expelled Shebaa resident Wafaa Salim Melhem and her two-year old daughter Nourham via the Zimraya crossing.

The expelled family arrived at the Lebanese army check point in Mimmess and were transported by the Lebanese Red Cross to a relative house in Beirut.

Information said the Israeli occupation are punishing the Shebaa residents for they refused to cooperate and collaborate with it.

Wafaa Melhem's husband Mustafa Qassem Nabaa refused to collaborate with the Israeli enemy despite promises of more money.

Israeli enemy allowed collaborators to use civilians as human shields
The Israeli occupation forces lately permitted their collaborators to use civilians in the occupied zone in south Lebanon as human shields to protect themselves.

Security sources said the collaborators consider the crossings from and to the occupied zone as "entrances to absolute death" and that the Resistance's Mujahedeen know very well how to avoid and preserve the civilians.

Previous experiences prooved that the Mujahedeen have a capability to accurately define and determine their targets and can wait for hours and days in order to detonate their bombs when the scene is free of civilians, the sources added.

During the past ten days, the Islamic Resistance's (IR) succeeded to infiltrate the Israeli occupation security measures in three different occasions.

The first attack seriously injured collaborator Ahmed Shebli Saleh, the second killed collaborator Kamil Badawi in Kfarhouna and the third bomb targeted a bus carrying Israeli soldiers near al-Bayyada.

These three operations prompted the Israeli occupation's command to intensify their patrols on the roads of the occupied zone.

OCCUPATION FORCES EXPEL WOMAN AND HER DAUGHTER FROM ZONE
Israeli allied militiamen today expelled Wafa Melhem and her two-year old daughter from the occupied village of Chebaa. Melhem's husband, a former Israeli-allied militiaman was expelled from Chebaa five months ago. Dozens of Chebaa residents have been driven out of the village since the beginning of the year.

LATEST DEVELOPMENTS IN SOUTH LEBANON
Israeli occupation forces pursued today their aggression against south Lebanon, whereby Israeli artillery pounded the outskirts of Majdel Selm, Khirbit Selm, Al-Sawaneh, Al-Jmaijmeh and Al-Qayssieh valley in the central sector.

In a statement issued by the Lebanese Resistance, Amal Movement, it declared that a unit of its men attacked with RPGs and machine-guns the Israeli position in Teebah Project, inflicting severe material damage.

On the other hand, Israeli occupation forces deported the citizen, Wafaa' Selim Milhem, and her two-year old daughter, from the village of Shebaa' via Zamrayya Crossing.

In another development, UNIFIL's Information Office organized a tour for the international and local visual medias in the UNIFIL's Finnish Contingent Center in Teebah, which was subjected to Israeli shelling last Monday night. The purpose of this media visit is to raise this issue locally and internationally.

It is to note that UN Secretary General, Kofi Annan, condemned this shelling on UNIFIL Center, and asked Israel for guarantees so as to avoid such incidents in the future.
