Daily Report  
July  Wednesday 28 ,1999

Introduction
The “Israeli” enemy gunners bombarded the residential neighborhoods of Baraasheit town violating the April Understanding as the shells damaged several houses .In this regard , the five-nation monitoring group accused “Israel” of breaking the Understanding by shelling the southern village of Yohmor on July 20 , damaging two houses and a car .
Meanwhile ,UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan asked the Security Council to renew the mandate of peace-keeping forcers in the south and hoped that areas under “Israeli” occupation will soon come under Beirut’s full control .

ISRAEL SHELLS VILLAGES DAMAGING TWO HOMES; INTERNATIONAL PANEL ACCUSES ISRAEL OF VIOLATING AGREEMENT  (1)
There was an upsurge in Israeli shelling in southern Lebanon today(yesterday). Occupation forces shelled the village of Baraashit, which borders the zone Israel occupies in the south, damaging two homes. Israel also attacked the outskirts of villages on the edge of the occupied zone, including Hadatha and Tibnine. Meanwhile, the international truce panel accused Israel of violating the 1996 understanding by shelling the southern village of Yohmor on July 20, damaging two homes and a car. The panel which includes delegates from the US, Israel, France, Syria and Lebanon urged Israel to avoid civilian targets. The three-year-old agreement bans attacks on and from civilian areas, but permits resistance operations against Israeli occupation troops in the south.

ANNAN WARNS OF VOLATILE SITUATION IN THE SOUTH BUT IS HOPEFUL OF ISRAELI PULLOUT (2)
UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan described the situation in southern Lebanon as volatile during the past six months, citing increased clashes between Lebanese resistance fighters and Israeli occupation troops. Annan who asked the Security Council to renew the mandate of peace-keeping forces in the south, said there is hope that areas under Israeli occupation will soon come under Beirut's full control.
In a written report to the United Nations Security Council, UN Chief Kofi Annan yesterday appealed to all parties to the conflict in southern Lebanon to respect the noncombatant status of civilians. He recommended another six-month renewal of the 4,500-strong UN Interim Force in Lebanon, Unifil, as requested by the Lebanese government. The force has been in place since 1978. It costs nearly $150 million a year to sustain. It is mandated with witnessing Israel's implementation of Security Council Resolution 425 which calls for an unconditional and immediate Israeli pullout from Lebanon. Annan warned that the situation during the past six months has been volatile adding civilians were again targeted despite UN peacekeepers efforts to limit the fighting and protect civilians . He strongly condemned an attack on a Unifil base which left one Irish soldier killed. The base was shelled by Israeli allied militiamen. Annan said recent hostilities reached their peak on June 24 "with Israeli air raids against civilian targets in Lebanon. Israeli attacks last months left eleven Lebanese civilians killed and damaged bridges and electricity networks. But Annan said there were "positive signs," noting that the town of Jezzine, previously occupied by Israeli allied militiamen came under the full control of the Lebanese government. Annan stressed there is new hope that the same will become possible soon for the part of Lebanon that is still under Israeli occupation. Commentators said he was referring to the stated intention of Israel's recently elected Prime Minister Ehud Barak to pull out all Israeli troops from southern Lebanon within a year.

“Israeli” Aggressions  (3)
The “Israeli” occupation forces staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese liberated areas :
5:35 the surroundings of Hemayed position came under five 81 mm. rounds and a barrage of 12.7 mm. heavy machine gun bullets , while  at the same time  , the surroundings of Jamousse position subjected to 12.7 mm. heavy machine gun fire .
5:45 four 155 mm. missiles hit Qaissieh Valley .
5:50 several shells landed the west of Baraasheit town damaging the house of Hussein Hennawi , whereas the outskirts of Aita-El-Jabal village came under a number of 81 mm. rounds and one 120 mm. shell .
5:55 the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to several 81 mm. rounds and a volley of 12.7 mm. heavy machine gun bullets .
Between 5:55 and 6:20 more than four 120 mm. shells struck the perimeters of Jibaa town .
Between 6:50 and 7:35 seventeen 120 mm. shells and 155 mm. missiles slammed into the villages of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri .
Between 7:15 and 7:45 mansouri ravine was shelled by eight 155 mm missiles
