Daily Report
 
 
July Saturday 17-1999:

"Israeli" occupation preparing for pullout. 48 collaborators resigned in July(1)
"The "Israeli" occupation forces lately increased its security and military patrols in the occupied eastern sector and Western Beqaa, making the situation look like a blockade in the towns of Hasbaya, Ain Qinya, Kawkaba, al-Hibarieh, Kfarshouba and Ibl al-Saqi, reports from the occupied zone in south Lebanon said.

"The information noted that this is an attempt by the enemy army to arrange and organize the area's affairs before the expected pullout, in order to prevent any obstacles from the collaborators or any clashes with the area's civilians, especially after the Druze religious leader's forbiddance of collaborating with the "Israeli" occupation".

"So far, the number of collaborators who resigned from the collaborating militia since the beginning of July 1999 reached 48 in the eastern sector, the reports added, pointing out that another 24 were sent off".

Military court's first verdict against collaborators(2)
"The Lebanese military court today (Friday) commenced the trials of collaborators who surrendered to the Lebanese authorities, following their militia's withdrawal from the Jezzine area.

"The first verdict came against collaborator Habib Nassib Harb who was sentenced to one year in prison".

"Most of these trials are expected to be completed before the Judiciary vacation next August.
On the other hand, the military court today convicted an espionage ring for the "Israeli" enemy, headed by agent Mohammed Khalil Ahmed Nabaa".

"The sentences varied between 3 months and 5 years in prison".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupation troops staged the following aggressions:
2:15, four 81mm rounds hit Oyoun Valley.
21:00, Hamra area was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
21:55, Mount Sojod was bombarded by six 155mm missiles.
