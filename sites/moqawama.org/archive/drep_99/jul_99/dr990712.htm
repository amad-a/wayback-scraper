Daily Report
 
 
July Munday 12-1999:

Sheikh Qassem: IR to break "Israeli" tyranny(1)
Deputy of Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem stressed that the Islamic Resistance (IR) will be the force to break the arrogance of "Israel".

During an educational ceremony in Beqaa, east Lebanon, his Eminence said the "Israelis" will be using the force to impose their conditions for "peace".

Ehud Barak, the enemy's Prime Minister, says the peace depends on the force and arrogance of the "Israeli" army, Shiekh Qassem stated.

The Resistance, his Eminence continued, is the opposing force that will break the tyranny of the "Israeli" army.

"Israeli" killed after tank overturns near Rihan(2)
The "Israeli" occupation forces claimed that an "Israeli" soldier was killed an another wounded Saturday when their Merkava tank overturned while approaching the Rihan outpost in the northern sector of the occupation zone.

The tank was returning from a routine night patrol when it tumbled off the mountain road, only 200 meters from the entrance to the "Israeli" outpost in Rihan.

The accident raises the "Israeli" death toll in the south this year to11, with another 36 soldiers wounded.

However, news agencies reported that the "Israeli" soldier was killed by gunfire near an occupation outpost in Al-Rihan. He was found dead after his tank overturned but the circumstances of the death are still unknown.

Building up a new road to prevent planting roadside bombs(3)
The "Israelis" have been busy constructing a new road below Beaufort Castle in a bid to prevent Hizbullah from planting roadside bombs. The new road runs close to the castle before joining the existing route at the beginning of the village. The original road has been widened and re-asphalted.

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) issued a communiqué in which it mentioned the following aggressions which launched by the "Israeli" occupying troops on the Lebanese liberated areas:
1:00, the surroundings of Tohorra outpost subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
20:30, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
21:10, nine 155mm missiles slammed into Jib Soweid area.
