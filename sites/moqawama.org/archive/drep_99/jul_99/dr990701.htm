Daily Report
 
 
July Thursday 1-1999:

Introduction
It seems that "Israel" had decided not to go too far in exploiting its barbaric aggression against Lebanon.

Former enemy Premier Minister Shimon Peres severely criticized the outgoing "Israeli" War Defense Minister Moshe Arens' statement to boycott the Monitoring Group meetings and ignore the provisions of the Understanding as "extreme and nonsensical".

For its part, Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Naem Qassem said that "Katyushas are ready to defend civilians and Infrastructure at the time, place and style the Resistance chooses to hurt the "Israeli" enemy.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" warplanes flew low in the airspace of Beirut breaking the sound barrier.

New "Israeli" PM expected to reinstate April pact(1)
"Ehud Barak, "Israel's" premier-elect, is expected to reinstate the April Understanding on taking office next week, thus overruling a decision by the outgoing government to ignore the provisions of the three-year-old agreement, a senior Labor party official said Wednesday".

"Former Premier Shimon Peres described the decision by outgoing "Israeli" Defense (War) Minister Moshe Arens to boycott the Monitoring Group meetings and ignore the provisions of the understanding as extreme and nonsensical.

In their last week in office ­ when they have no authority, when they lost the election ­ they throw a wrench in the works, Peres told "Israel" Radio.

Peres said that Barak, who was chief of staff when the understanding was signed in 1996, would almost certainly reverse the decision as soon as he takes office. Barak is close to reaching a deal which will enable him to form his coalition government before the July 8 deadline.

A Labor Party spokesman said that Barak would make a decision on the April Understanding only after he had formed his government.

" The unilateral abandonment of the agreement angered several senior "Israeli" Army officers, who described the decision to boycott sessions of the Monitoring Group as a scandal.

"The officers said on "Israel" Radio that the committee is the only forum where "Israeli", Syrian and Lebanese officers sit together on a regular basis along with U.S. and French representatives.

"The officers, who have always held the committee in high regard, described as unacceptable that "Israel" could lodge 11 complaints with the group and then refuse to attend the session.

"The public dissension of the "Israeli" officers typified the often acrimonious relationship between the military establishment and the government of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

"During Netanyahus three years in office, individuals in the "Israeli" Army repeatedly criticized government policy, including the continued occupation of south Lebanon".

Sheikh Qassem : Katyushas ready to defend(2)
"Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem said the Katyushas are ready to defend Lebanese civilians and Infrastructure".

"The time, place, procedure and pattern of the Katyusha defense will be decided by the Resistance to hurt the "Israeli" enemy and achieve the goal of balancing the terror with it, Sheikh Qassem stated.

"He added that ""Israel"" have failed to reach the four aims intended by last Thursday's aggression.

"These four aims, his Eminence continued, are : (1) to elevate the broken morale of the occupation army, (2) to benefit from the interim between the outgoing and forthcoming "Israeli" Cabinets to conduct an attempt to establish a new foundation for an aggression and for political stands that will be benefited from later, (3) to create a difference and a disagreement between the Lebanese state, the Resistance and the people on the basis of holding the Resistance responsible for the destruction of the Lebanese infrastructure, (4) to establish a new pattern which might help reaching a practical April "Understanding's amendment, as long as a theoretical one and a fundamental change are impossible to achieve".

"Israeli" warplanes broke sound barrier over Beirut(3)
""Israeli" warplanes at 10:30 this morning (Wednesday) swooped law over Beirut, breaking the sound barrier, while MK drones hovered over the Beqaa and the South".

"In front line villages the "Israeli" occupation's machine gun fire and artillery rounds kept on falling in the vicinities.

"In the meantime, Tyre fishermen tried to sail out, taking advantage of the relative calmness in the area, but "Israeli" gunboats intercepted them by machine gun fire and forced them to return to the Tyre port.

"The enemy, two weeks ago, imposed a curfew in Tyre, forbidding fishermen to sail far from the port".

However, the "Israeli" occupation forces staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese liberated area as reported in a statement issued by the Islamic Resistance (IR) (4):

New arrests in occupied zone(5)
"Lebanese security sources said the "Israeli" occupied forces yesterday (Tuesday) arrested Kfar Kila resident Hussien Awada, Abbas Hassan al-Rashidi, 50, from Markaba and Ibrahim Bahjat al- Basha, 22, from al- Khiam and imprisoned them at the Khiam prison".

"Mr Awada is very ill and undergoing a steady treatment, the sources stated, adding there is no reason for these arrests other than racketeering, after their relatives refused to pay tributes for the collaborators.

"It is likely that the collaborators have informed against the three detainees to the "Israeli" occupation forces and told them they are members of the Resistance or cooperating with it".
