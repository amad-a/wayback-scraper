Daily Report
 
 
July Saturday 10-1999:

Ashkenazi trying to "recycle" collaborating militia in Hasbaya (1)
"Following the series of defeats suffered by the collaborating militia, the "Israeli" officials are trying to revive this militia by refurbishing what is left of it, despite the deadlock they faced before, specifically in Jezzine".

"The so-called "Israeli" army northern commander Gabi Ashkenazi visited the Hasbaya area and denied the army's intention to withdraw from it, claiming that Hizbullah is not victorious on the ground but in the psychological and media war".

"Ashkenazi's attempt consisted of paying extra money and raising wages of militiamen, in addition to answering to their questions and again forcing civilians to be recruited to the collaborating militia.
"He is also trying to get them immigration visas to western countries or landing permits in occupied Palestine".

"Ashkenazi's visit to Hasbaya came after Nabih Abu Rafeh, the collaborating militia's official in the town, admitted the desertion of 85 collaborators from his "eastern regiment" due to the Fatwa that forbids the collaboration with " Israeli" occupation which was taken by the Druze religious leaders in al-Bayada meeting few months ago".

INTERNATIONAL PANEL TO RESUME MEETINGS(2)
""Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak said, "Israel" would take part in a meeting next week of the international group that monitors the south Lebanon April ceasefire understanding. The announcement reversed a decision made last week by the previous government to pull "Israel" out of the five-nation group. The committee was supposed to have met last week to discuss Lebanese and "Israeli" complaints following "Israel's" nighttime aerial blitz on Lebanon and Hizbullah's Katyusha rocket attacks on northern "Israel". "

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
In its communiqué, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
11:50, the perimeters of Kfar Romman town came under several 81mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
2:25, two 81mm flare shells were fired over the outskirts of Kfar Romman.
5:00, five 155mm missiles struck the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal village.
5:35, Mount Sojod was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
9:00, the peripheries of Baraasheit town exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
