Nassrallah: Resistance will continue regardless of resumption of peace negotiations(1)
Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah stressed that the "Israeli" enemy will not be able to harm the Islamic Resistance's determination and persistence of pursuing the track of Jihad.

Nassrallah added: "The Resistance had become the only element of exhausting and exerting pressure on "Israel"".

In response to the repeated "Israeli" and American demands of halting the IR military operations during the alleged peace negotiations, he affirmed that the Resistance is not concerned, at all, about the resumption the peace talks. As long as there is an occupation, the resistance activities will go on intensely. Also, he denied that the Resistance will curb its activities if peace negotiations resume. The Resistance will continue regardless of whether parties have resumed negotiations or not, adding that it has never waited for negotiations before.

"Nowadays, the Resistance is one, of if not the only the most significant element of force the Arab parties should invest it very well so as to be able to face the present challenges", he told.

The Secretary General refuted the political and press reports about the possibility of "Israel's" adoption of the same policy which adopted against Yugoslavia in Kosova.

Collaborators sentenced to jail(3)
"Cases involving 220 ex-militiamen who handed themselves in to the authorities after the SLAs* retreat from Jezzine last month are due to begin Wednesday in the Military Court".

"Habib Nassib Harb, who joined the SLA in 1990, was sentenced to one year and Denise Gerges Said, in the militia since 1989, was imprisoned for six months."

"The ex-militiamen will stand trial in groups of between seven and 10 in a six-member court presided over by Brigadier Maher Safieddine, the head of the Military Court. Twenty-seven ex-SLA members have been formally charged, with most trials expected to last no more than one day. Sentencing will occur immediately after".

"Only a handful were still members of the militia at the time of the Jezzine pullback".

"Their fate will be closely watched by militiamen in the occupation zone, whose morale has deteriorated in recent months with the growing likelihood of an "Israeli" withdrawal".

" The "Israelis" have begun a program to bolster the flagging spirit of their Lebanese allies by channeling extra funds into the occupation zone. They are planning to pump in $13 million this year, up from $8 million in 1998, to improve roads and facilities in the Bint Jbeil and Marjeyoun hospitals. The average monthly militia salary is to increase from $400 to $500".

Amnesty International: Fourth Geneva Convention meeting represents an abdication of responsibility (4)
Amnesty International is appalled by a 10 minute meeting of High Contracting Parties to the Geneva Conventions, convened yesterday in Geneva, which failed to consider enforcement measures to ensure that "Israeli" respect its obligations under the Fourth Geneva Convention.

"The High Contracting Parties have only met to decide to defer their responsibilities. It is a supreme irony that, on the 50th anniversary of the Geneva Conventions, a conference that was set up to bring back to the limelight the plight of the protected population in the Occupied Territories lasts only 10 minutes. Today marks a scandalously missed opportunity to reaffirm international humanitarian law".

Resolution ES-10-6, adopted by almost all members of the UN General Assembly on 8 February 1999, called for the convening of this meeting of High Contracting Parties to consider enforcement measures. At today's meeting the High Contracting Parties failed to demonstrate a serious commitment to ensure protection for people in the territories occupied by "Israel" in particular, and to ensure compliance with international humanitarian law in general.

""Israel", as a High Contracting Party, has been violating the Fourth Geneva Convention for more that 30 years, when it carries out willful killings extrajudicially, when it (tortures) or when it indiscriminately uses force", Amnesty International said. "The High Contracting Parties themselves however have violated their obligation enshrined in Article 1 of the Convention, to ensure respect for the Fourth Geneva Convention".

Instead of studying ways to enforce compliance with the obligations of the Convention, the meeting was a simple procedural one and was concluded after 10 minutes. Even worse, it set no date for a substantive discussion and decisions on enforcement measures, confining itself to the postponement of the meeting sine die. The meeting will be reconvened only in the light of the developments in the "humanitarian situation in the field".

If the resolution adopted by the General Assembly is not to remain simple lip service, it is imperative that the meeting of High Contracting Parties be reconvened before that end of the year, and that the question of enforcement measures be addressed in order to lead to concrete steps.
