Daily Report
 
 
July Tuesday 13-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants wounded, the "Israeli" senior agent Ahmad Shebli as a roadside bomb destroyed his car near the occupied town of Aita Al-Shaab in South Lebanon.

Meanwhile, the Follow-up Committee of Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons revealed that an Intifada took place inside the Khiam prison last Thursday in protest to the torture activities against the detainees.

However, two Lebanese civilians were arrested Sunday by the "Israeli" occupation forces and took to Khiam detention center.

Collaborator Shebli wounded by IR bomb(1)
"The Lahdi collaborating militia today (Monday) admitted that agent Ahmad Shebli was injured when a powerful roadside bomb planted by the Islamic Resistance (IR) destroyed his car near the occupied town of Aita al-Shaab in south Lebanon."

"Collaborator Shebli is one of the "Israeli" intelligence agents in charge of security in the eastern sector (Bint Jbeil district and surroundings) in the occupied zone.

"He conducted several missions for the "Israeli" intelligence, "Mossad", during which many Lebanese were killed.

"Agent Shebli, 50, has been collaborating with the "Israeli" occupation for 20 years and he is responsible for the imprisonment of many Lebanese who oppose this arrogant occupation".

"Lately, he was appointed as head of the "504 service" in charge of recruiting Lebanese agents for the "Israeli" enemy".

Intifada inside Khiam prison(2)
"The follow-up Committee of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prisons (Monday) revealed that an Intifada took place inside the Khiam prison last Thursday".

"It all started as the collaborators in charge of the prison tried to torture detainee Suleiman Ramadan, after his last week's escape attempt failed, prompting his inmates to go on hunger strike, the committee said.

"Some of the collaborators rejected the conduct of their colleagues who wanted to torture detainee Ramadan and decided to desert the prison, the Committee stated.

"At once, the Committee continued, the "Israeli" occupation forces stormed the prison and redistributed the detainees into solitary confinement, torturing them with electric shocks and beating.
"The outcome of the stand off was the injury of five detainees, Suleiman Ramadan, Mustafa Taobeh, Riad Kalakesh, Hussein Aqil and Ali Ghazi al-Saghir.

"Moreover, the Committee reported that the "Israeli" enemy threatened to keep the detainees in solitary confinement with small portions of food and water to halt the visits of the detainees' families, organized by the International Red Cross, if another escape attempt or Intifada take place".

"However, the follow-up Committee called on International organizations to interfere and save the 120 Lebanese detainees held without trials at the Khiam prison, where some of them have been there for more than 14 years and suffer from malnutrition due to the constant torture, starvation and thirst".

Two more Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prisons(3)
"Two Lebanese civilians yesterday (Sunday) joined the 120 unfortunate Lebanese detainees at the Khiam prison".

"Security sources said that collaborator Michael Farah (Abu Farah) stormed the house of Mahmoud Mehdi, 40, a teacher a the Naqoura public school, and took him to the Khiam prison".

"Hours earlier, civilian Samir Mehdi, also from al-Naqoura, was abducted and taken to the same prison.

"The Mehdis are imprisoned without being charged with any crime or violation.

"Al-Naqoura is a Lebanese town three Kilometers away from the Lebanese Palestinian border and 15 Kilometers south of Tyre".

Blast on eve of Monitoring Group session(4)
"The Monitoring Group convenes in Naqoura on Tuesday to hear a record 37 complaints, the first session to be held since Ehud Barak overturned a decision by the outgoing "Israeli" government to abandon the April Understanding".

"Lebanon has lodged 16 complaints and "Israel" has submitted the other 21, the largest number to be received by the group in its three years of existence. The majority of the protests stems from the violence last month".

"President Emile Lahoud has instructed the Lebanese delegation to press for a condemnation of "Israel's" air raids, in which 10 civilians died.

"A Western diplomatic source expected the meeting to be concluded swiftly, despite the number of complaints. They will be dealing with the actions of the previous government and I doubt they will want to waste time on this, he said.

"The delegates are expected to agree to group together the large number of complaints dealing with the June escalation and blame both sides for violating the agreement".

Military Operations(5)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Ibrahim Sabra and Jamil Ali Shahla targeted, at 18:50, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Nizar Kamel Jawad and Ali Reslan Chokair attacked, at 19:45, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.
