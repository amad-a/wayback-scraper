Daily Report
 
 
July Sunday 18-1999:

Sheikh Qassem: Resistance march won't stop(1)
"The Deputy of Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem yesterday (Friday) stressed that the Resistance's march to liberation will not stop until the land and people are free form the "Israeli" occupation".

"We in Hizbullah do not care about (Ehud) Barak's thoughts and ideas about an unjust settlement and we are not scared from the pressure which is aimed at depriving us from our rights, his Eminence said.

No matter how high the cost, we will proceed with confronting the "Israeli" occupation until we get our rights by ourselves, Sheikh Qassem stated.

"As for the Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prisons, his Eminence continued, we know well how to release the detainees and we know what to do in order to make them return to be with their families again."

"Israeli" occupation reinforcement in Hasbaya(2)
"The "Israeli" occupation army yesterday (Friday) deployed an armored force to the Hasbaya area in the eastern sector of the occupied zone in South Lebanon".

"Security reports said that 12 armored vehicles at dawn yesterday entered the area via the Dardara - Ibl al-Saqi - Souq al-Khan road.

"The armored force was deployed to the occupation outposts at the Kawkaba hill, Zoghleh and Wadi Abu Qamha amid strict security measures executed by the occupation forces, the reports stated, adding that no collaborators participated in this operation".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
In its statement, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:
3:00, four 120mm shells hit the perimeters of Jibaa town.
4:45, Haddatha woodland was bombarded by eighteen 81 and 120mm shells, whereas the surroundings of Haddatha compound subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 9:45 and 14:15, Litani River watercourse and the surroundings of Zillaya and Qilya villages came under about two hundred and twenty five 155, 160 and 175mm missiles.
13:35, the "Israeli" warplanes dropped four air-to-ground rockets into Litani River watercourse.
16:15, the "Israeli" fighter-bombers raided twice into Litani River watercourse firing four air-to-surface rockets.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Issa Mohammad Ismail and Mohammad Rida El-Horr attacked, at 2:00, an "Israeli" motorized force at Rihan hilltops. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score direct and certain goals.

Quarter an hour later, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Mustafa Haidar Ahmad and Ibrahim Ismail Alaoueih targeted an enemy motorized patrol at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

Also, the IR mujahedeen spotted, at 9:15, a joint "Israeli"-Lahdi force at the eastern hilltops of Qilya village. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Mahdi Shamas and Ahmad Al-Mousawi in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) attacked it with the adequate arms. Certain casualties were inflicted, the militants reported.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Hammoud and Karim Atwi targeted, at 12:05, an enemy force as it was trying to reinforce the besieged force east of Qilya village with the suitable weapons chalking up direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyr Mohammad Moussa attacked, at 13:50, a collaborator force as it was trying to back up the besieged force at eastern hilltops of Qilya barring it from achieving its goals.
