Israeli blokade of Aita Al-Shaab continues(1)
The Israeli  occupation forces and their collaborators have tightened the ten-day blockade of Aita Al-Shaab preventing its residents from going in and out of village.

The news reported that the Israeli military intelligence in the occupied zone, which is known by 504 apparatus, has been storming and ransacking its houses since three days.

 The coming people asserted that the Israeli agents headed by Sharbel Barakat and Ahmad Shibli Saleh and Ali Al-Daoud, had arrested so far more than twenty nationals, mostly are children and elderly, who were subjected to torture on the pretext of cooperation with the resistance men. The enemy had confiscated the cellular phones from the residents.

Also, the Israeli collaborators, headed by the agents Akram Olayan, Riad Rahal and Youssef Hachem have been storming, since three days, into the villages of Jibbain, Tair Harfa, Rshaf, and investigating and beating the inhabitants.

Collaborators` manever at Zimrage post(2)
In Eastern Sector, the news reported that the Israeli allied militiamen stationed at Zimraya post had carried out a live ammunition maneuver during which several shells landed near the villages of Meimess and Ain Fjour causing damage to the olive groves in the area

Awali bridge repairs started  (3).
The process of rebuilding the Awali Bridge began this morning (Saturday) by the Milad Yammeen company (Engineering Consulting Office).

The cost of the repairs is estimated at $295,000 donated by the Islamic Republic of Iran, Mr. Yameen said.

The Lebanese Ministry of Public works and the Iranian company "Shoussa", designated by the Iranian government, are supervising and controlling the process which will be completed in a 16 week period, the company stated.

The Awali bridge, linking south Lebanon to the rest of the country, was partly destroyed by the Israeli occupation warplanes during the latest Israeli aggression against the Lebanese infrastructure on the night of June 24,1999.
