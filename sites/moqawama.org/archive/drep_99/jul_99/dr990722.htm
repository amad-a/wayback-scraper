UN Chief condemned Israel for shelling UN base ... (1)
UN Secretary- General Kofi Annan condemned Tuesdays shelling of a Unifil position by an Israeli artillery unit, saying Finnish peacekeepers narrowly escaped with their lives. Finnish troops had a narrow escape when

five Israeli 155mm artillery rounds exploded close to their position near Wadi Hojeir in the central sector of the occupation zone. Two rounds directly hit the post, while three other shells fell between 4 and 25 meters from their position near Qantara, a Unifil spokesman said.

There were no casualties but two prefabricated huts were destroyed and a power line was cut. It was only because of the quick reaction of the Finnish troops manning the position and a great deal of luck that there were no casualties. The damage was considerable. the spokesman said.

The spokesman said the incident, which occurred despite Israeli assurances, is deeply unsettling.

The secretary-general condemns this attack against United Nations personnel and calls on the government of Israel to take all necessary measures to ensure Israel and its allies respect the noncombatant status of Unifil peacekeepers, a UN spokesman in New York said.

In May and June, Irish positions were targeted by the Israels militia and on May 21, an Irish soldier was killed by a mortar round and another was wounded.

A total of 227 Unifil peacekeepers have been killed since deploying in the south in 1978.

Collaborators at Kfarhouna outpost revolt ...(2)
Information from the occupied zone in south Lebanon today (Wednesday) revealed that the collaborating militiamen at the Kfarhouna occupation's outpost are refusing to conduct patrols and surveillance missions in the surroundings, for they fear the danger they are facing in the area due to the Islamic Resistance's (IR) operations and roadside bombs.

This rebellion comes following the death of collaborator Kamil Badawi in an IR roadside bomb attack on the Kfarhouna crossing road two days ago.

Several Israeli officers at the so-called liaison unit visited the Kfarhouna crossing yesterday, then held a meeting inside the occupation's command Headquarters to discuss the military situation in the area and the collaborators' rebellion, the information added.

Israeli occupation arrested several residents in Aita al-Shaeb ... (3)
Witnesses in the occupied zone in south Lebanon said the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators during the last two days stormed the occupied town of Aita al-Shaeb in the Bint Jbeil district and arrested several residents.

Among the arrested were Zahra Hassan Rida, 17, a school student, Ali Hassan Nasser, Mohammed Mossa Rida, Mohammed Ahmed Jamil and a resident from the Droubi family.

Trial of former Israel collaborator opens with conviction ... (6)
The trial of former members of the South Lebanon Army* opened at the Military Tribunal on Wednesday, with the court hearing four of the 200 scheduled cases.

The court, presided over by Brigadier Maher Safieddine, heard the charges against George Aoun, Aoun Aoun, Naji Asmar, Muhana Hanna and Tony Nasr who, having fled from authorities, is being tried in absentia.

The court sentenced Nasr to 10 years in prison with hard labor, citing his failure to show up in court as incriminating evidence, but he has the right to a retrial if he turns himself in.

The four in custody were among the 220 SLA militiamen and civilian employees who refused to leave the Jezzine enclave when the militia pulled out June 3. All SLA dissidents surrendered to the authorities and now face trial on charges of collaborating with Israel.

George Aouns trial was adjourned until Sept. 1 at the request of his lawyer, who said that he needed more time to prepare his defense.

Aoun Aoun said, I was a cook with the SLA and never carried a weapon. His lawyer demanded his acquittal, arguing that his client was not involved in any combat missions.

Asmar said, I was forced to spy on Hizbullah positions but I didnt hand any useful information over to the SLAs officers.

His lawyer demanded his acquittal, saying Asmars acts did not threaten national security.
Hanna said that she joined the SLAs Voice of the South radio station in 1992 and quit less than a year later. Her lawyer also demanded her acquittal.

Assistant Military Prosecutor Myassar Shokor called for the imprisonment of Aoun Aoun, Asmar and Hanna for at least one year.

The panel was scheduled to deliver verdicts and sentences by midnight in the cases of Aoun Aoun, Asmar and Hanna. The court will convene every Wednesday until all cases are heard.
