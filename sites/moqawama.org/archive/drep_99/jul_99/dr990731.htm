Daily Report  
July Saturday 31-1999:

Introduction
On the eve of Army Day, President Emile Lahoud "cautioned the armed forces to remain vigilant to face the challenges that may arise as "Israel" seeks to strengthen its bargaining position and paid tribute to the "heroic resistance that is a source of pride for us on the world scene".
Meanwhile, the so-called "Israeli" security service chief Ami Ayalon said that the detention of 19 Lebanese nationals in "Israeli" prisons as a bargain paper is a failure and does not benefit its entity in any way.
Elsewhere, Aita El-Shaab has been under an "Israeli" blockade for the past three weeks and no one is allowed to enter or leave the village. Its residents warned that the village may face starvation.

Lahoud tells army: ‘Stay on the alert’…(1)
"On the eve of Army Day, President Emile Lahoud said Friday that the Middle East was entering a critical phase with the imminent revival of the peace process, and cautioned the armed forces to remain vigilant to face the challenges that may arise as "Israel" seeks to strengthen its bargaining position".
“Your anniversary is not measured by years, but by achievements,” the ex-army commander said in his Army Day message. “In all your duties, you’ve remained true to your slogan: Dignity, sacrifice and loyalty.”
He said that whether in the confrontation with "Israel" or in enforcing internal law and order, the army has never wavered in its allegiance to the civil authorities and respect for the law.
“But now, in this era of critical regional developments, you’re urged to be exceptionally vigilant and prepared for a possible confrontation here or a security breach there,” Lahoud said. “The state of alert cannot be relaxed before all the ambiguities regarding the future are eliminated and the picture becomes clear.”
He paid tribute to the “heroic resistance that is a source of pride for us on the world scene,” and renewed his rejection of any "Israeli" preconditions for resuming the peace talks.
Had Lebanon bowed to "Israeli" pressures for security arrangements in return for a partial withdrawal from the south, Lahoud said, the country would have become hostage to "Israeli" demands, including the permanent settlement of Palestinian refugees here.
“We are for peace. But there will be no guarantees or arrangements outside the framework of a just and comprehensive peace that secures a complete "Israeli" withdrawal from the south, the Western Beqaa and the Golan. This could only be the result of simultaneous negotiations on the Lebanese and Syrian tracks to consolidate the unity of our stands, goals and fate.”
As the resumption of negotiations nears, the president said, “we must reiterate our positions and stress that the concept of preconditions is rejected because it is tantamount to surrendering before the negotiations.”

Enemy admits failure of detaining Lebanese as hostages…(2)
""Israeli" Security Service Chief Ami Ayalon said during a private annual conference for Prosecutor Generals that the detention of 19 Lebanese nationals, among them Sheikh Abdulkarim Obeid and Hajj Mustasfa al-Dirani, in "Israeli" prisons as a bargain paper is a failure and does not benefit its entity in any way".
"The "Israeli" media quoted Ayalon as saying that this bargain paper will not help in releasing missed "Israeli" soldiers in Lebanon.
"However, the "Israeli" Supreme Court had given the "Israeli" services permission to detain the Lebanese nationals as hostages without any trials and denied them the right to have defense attorneys, despite the bad health condition".

Villagers protest blockade of Aaita Shaab by "Israeli" forces…(3)
"As residents of Aaita Shaab rallied in Beirut on Friday to protest at the "Israeli" clampdown on their occupied village, Prime Minister Salim Hoss vowed to take action and called on Unifil to help end the two-week blockade.
"After a delegation from the village met Hoss at the Grand Serail, the premier said that the International Committee for the Red Cross would be asked to supply the residents with the necessary medical supplies.
"The "Israeli" and SLA* militia’s security wings have imposed travel restrictions for the past two weeks on the residents of Aaita Shaab, a village in the western sector of the occupation zone, 12 kilometers from Bint Jbeil.
"Residents of Aaita Shaab, who gathered outside the Escwa building in downtown Beirut on Friday to protest at the clampdown, handed Hoss a petition calling for international efforts to halt “the occupation forces from practicing their despotic activities in our town.”
“This siege has led to severe shortage of food supplies including children’s milk, and medicine … Dozens of houses were broken into and searched. The occupation forces open fire inside the houses and terrify children and elderly,” it said.
Ali Saleh, a resident of Aaita Shaab who now lives in Beirut, said that eight villagers had been taken to Khiam prison while several others had been questioned then released.
“The "Israelis" have imposed a curfew,” he said. “The villagers are not even allowed out of their houses.”
Saleh, who is planning a sit-in outside the Unifil headquarters in Tyre on Tuesday, added that the "Israeli" security forces had confiscated cellular phones in the village. “We have not been able to contact anyone for three days,” he said.
"Unifil’s acting spokesman, Daljeet Bagga, said that no official request from the government had been received as yet regarding the situation in Aaita Shaab. But even if such a request arrived, there was little the peacekeepers can do.
“The village is out of our area of operations,” Bagga said. He added that the personnel of the UN’s Observer Group Lebanon who patrol the zone would “observe and report” to Unifil. Otherwise “there’s not much we can do,” he said.
"The "Israeli" General Security Service, in coordination with its militia equivalent, clamped down on the village two weeks ago following the wounding of Ahmad Shibli, the local SLA security chief, in a roadside bomb blast.
"Tensions in the area were exacerbated by the detonation of a large roadside bomb nine days ago on the border at Ramieh, 3 kilometers from Aaita Shaab, as a bus full of "Israeli" soldiers crossed into Lebanon.
"The "Israelis" apparently placed ground radar in the wadis leading into the occupation zone between Beit Lif and Rshaf to detect guerrillas infiltrating the zone. But the devices failed to pick up any movements and the radar units were removed. The "Israelis" appear now to believe that Hizbullah has a contact living in or near Aaita Shaab who has been exploding the bombs for the resistance.
"The growing certainty of an imminent end to the occupation has persuaded disillusioned SLA militiamen and residents of the zone to cooperate with the resistance to guarantee freedom from prosecution, or worse, once the "Israelis" depart the south.
"This aid can extend from gathering intelligence on the troop movements to planting and detonating roadside bombs".

Expulsions continue in occupied zone…(4)
"The episodes of expulsions starred by the "Israeli" occupation forces are continuing against Lebanese civilians in the occupied zone in south Lebanon.
"The latest is the expulsion of Fariza Abdul Amir Serhan from its occupied villages of Kfar Kila last Tuesday.
"A statement issued by the villages residents said collaborator Khodor Hussein Joumaa took Fariza to the Kfar Tibneet crossing and informed her about the expulsion, depriving her from the right of calling her family which she financially supports since her father died, in addition to preventing her from collecting her personnel belongings.
"Fariza rejected the expulsion and stood all day at the crossing but without any response.
Kfar Kila residents called on officials and international organization to put pressure on the "Israeli" occupation in order to stop the expulsions and return Fariza to her home".

16:50, three tank rounds fell on Hojeir Valley, two on the perimeters of Touline and two others on the peripheries of Qantara town.

Military Operations…(6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) unit of martyrs Mohammad Ali Jaafar and Kassem Ali Jaber attacked, at 00:15, enemy movements near Beaufort Castle with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

Qlaiaa residents sat in against "Israeli" occupation…(7)
"Inhabitant of occupied al-Qlaiaa yesterday (Thursday) staged a sit in to protest the killing of Carlos Salameh, 20, and the injury of three others the day before yesterday.
"The residents today held a meeting to discuss ways of confronting the "Israeli" occupation's severe measures against their town and the neighboring towns occupied by the "Israeli" army who is giving a free choice for the soldiers to shoot at any thing moving when ever they want, without taking into consideration the people and the agricultural land".
