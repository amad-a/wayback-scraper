Daily Report
 
 
July Sunday 11-1999:

IR aborts "Israeli" act of terrorism(1)
"The Islamic Resistance's (IR) Mujahedeen at dawn (Saturday) aborted an "Israeli" act of terrorism against the liberated areas in south Lebanon".

"IR said in a statement the Mujahedeen defused several land mines planted by the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators on the road between Kfar Houna and the nearby crossing point.

"As volunteers at the International Red Cross were examining the road to see if they can go through the Kfar Houna crossing, they noticed the presence of land mines, the statement noted.

The volunteers then asked the Resistance men to prevent a disaster against civilians who travel on this road, the statement added.

"At once, an IR's engineering unit answered to the Red Cross call and defused the mines, after taking the necessary security precautions".

"In the morning IR's Mujahedeen, attacked the "Israeli" enemy forces at the outpost of Kassaret al-Oroush, scoring definite hits".

"In the meantime, another IR's squad pounded enemy movements in the vicinity of Al-Rihan hill top, preventing it from reinforcing the outpost of Kassaret al-Ouroush".

Collaborating officers afraid(2)
"Following the latest attempt by Gabi Ashkenazi, the so-called northern commander of the "Israeli" occupation army, to revive the collapsed collaborating militia, reports from the occupied zone said the collaborating officers demanded from Ashkenazi the immediate return of top collaborator Antoine Lahd from Paris to the zone or appoint a replacement".

"The report added that some speed boats' owners began making trips, organized by "Israeli" travel agencies, from al-Naqoura port in the occupied zone to Cyprus, transporting deserting collaborators who were promised with expensive immigration visas, and the pay is up front".

"Collaborating officers during the last few days bulldozed trees and rocks around their houses and reinforced security measures with what is left of the collaborating militia men".

Resistance Martyrs equal to army Martyrs(3)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss has informed the chairman of "Faithfulness to the Resistance Bloc" MP Sayyed Ibrahim Amine al-Sayyed the decision of equaling the sons of the Resistance Martyrs with the sons of the Lebanese army Martyrs".

"The decision says the benefits and privileges of the army Martyrs' sons will be from now on enjoyed by the Resistance Martyrs' sons, including hospitalization, education and a monthly income for the families of Martyrs who served for more than three years".

"Moreover the wounded of the Resistance were also equaled with the injured soldiers of the army".
