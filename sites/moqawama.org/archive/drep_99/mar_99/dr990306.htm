Daily Report
 
 
March Saturday 6-1999:

Introduction
The head of what so-called "Israel's" northern command has acknowledged that it is having a hard time locating the Islamic Resistance (IR) men. He added his voice to the number saying that the April 1996 Understanding must be subjected to amendment.

On the other hand, "Israel's" top military commander have joined the public debate over the advantages of a withdrawal from the "Israeli" occupied area of South Lebanon.

"ISRAEL" ADMITS DIFFICULTIES IN THE SOUTH AS EFFORTS TO AMEND APRIL TRUCE INTENSIFY (1)
"The top commander of "Israeli" occupation forces in southern Lebanon has admitted that his army was having trouble tracking down and battling Hizbollah resistance fighters. Gabi Ashkenazi also joined a chorus of recent "Israeli" complaints about the 1996 truce agreement. But Lebanon has made clear it will not accept any amendment to the US-brokered understanding".

" The "Israeli" army's northern commander acknowledged his forces are finding it difficult to locate Hizbullah resistance fighters in south Lebanon. Echoing earlier remarks by "Israeli" leaders, Gabi Ashkenazi claimed the resistance is operating from civilian areas, which "Israel" cannot target due to the 1996 cease-fire understanding, which is monitored by a five-nation panel. "Israel" has almost never abided by the agreement. "Israeli" officials have been hinting they are ready to scrap the deal which bans attacks to and from civilian areas. Since it went into effect three years ago, about a hundred Lebanese civilians have been killed in southern Lebanon. Not one "Israeli" civilian was killed. But "Israel", which is suffering mounting troop losses in occupied south Lebanon, is blaming its defeat on the cease-fire. Ashkenazi said if "Israel" embarks on a major operation against Lebanon, it would quote have to fix the understanding. War Minister Moshe Arens has been calling for an abrogation of the cease-fire to allow the pounding of civilian areas. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has ruled out scraping the deal for now, but he warned this week that "Israel" would respond to Hizbullah attacks, even if within the cease-fire understanding, with what he described as a "harsher" policy of reprisals. Prime Minister Selim Hoss said Lebanon remains committed to the 1996 deal, and stressed, Beirut will not accept any amendment to the agreement. Last week's resistance operation which killed "Israel's" highest ranking officer in Lebanon, Erez Gerstein, and four others, prompted waves of threats against Lebanon. US sources were today quoted by some Beirut dailies as saying Washington supports "Israel's" desire to amend the 1996 April cease-fire deal. But State Department officials were also quoted by other newspapers as saying the US has no reason to change the role of the panel group, adding if all parties to the conflict desire to expand the scope of the understanding, then that would quote be different".

"ISRAELI" MILITARY DEBATES WITHDRAWAL FROM Lebanon (2)
" Faced with mounting casualties, "Israel's" top military commanders have joined the public debate over the merits of a withdrawal from the occupied zone in southern Lebanon. "The time has come to stop mincing words: We have no business staying in Lebanon," a high-ranking officer told Haaretz newspaper Friday following the deaths of a general and five other soldiers in Lebanon in less than a week. "Hezbollah isn't a terrorist organization. It's a guerrilla organization. It's a national liberation organization," said the officer, who was not identified. "Show me a single case in history in which an army managed to occupy territory over time and defeat a guerrilla organization that benefited from massive support and political backing from the local population," he said. The senior army officer said "Israel" can withdraw from Lebanon just like the United States did from Vietnam, France did from Algeria, and Russia did from Afghanistan".

"Similar opinions have been expressed by "Israel's" former northern region commander, General Amiram Levin, now the number two man in the Mossad secret service, and by the former head of military intelligence, reserve General Haim Efrati. The current northern region commander, Major General Gabi Ashkenazi, admitted Friday in an interview with Maariv newspaper that the military was having problems tracking down and engaging Hizbullah fighters. "The truth must be told, we are finding it difficult to locate Hizbullah in built up areas, not only at the point of contact but in the rear," Ashkenazi said. But he opposed an unconditional withdrawal from southern Lebanon, where "Israel's" top commander inside the buffer zone, Brigadier General Erez Gerstein, was killed by a Hizbullah roadside bomb on Sunday. "A pullout without an agreement or disarmament of the Hizbullah would be too dangerous," he said. Amir Oren, a military expert who writes for Haaretz, said "Israel's" top brass are "as divided as the rest of (public) opinion on this issue. The Haaretz commentator added that "the military command feels that following the recent heavy losses in southern Lebanon, the issue of a withdrawal has taken a prominent role in the campaign for May elections for parliament and the post of prime minister. Both opposition Labor Party leader Ehud Barak and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu have held out the possibility of a withdrawal from the "Lebanese quagmire." Barak said that if he is elected prime minister, he will pull "Israeli" forces out of occupied southern Lebanon by June 2000. Neither Barak nor Netanyahu, however, has called for an unconditional withdrawal, which Labor Party official Yossi Beilin has called for from the left of the political spectrum while Foreign Minister Ariel Sharon made a similar appeal from the right".

I Sheikh Yazbek: Our Ummah's crying era now replaced by enemy's (3)
"Imam Khamenei's representative in Lebanon his Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek affirmed that it is the Resistance's duty to defend its people if the "Israeli" enemy breaches the April Understanding.

Sheikh Yazbek warned the "Israelis" of any military adventures, "for they will only harvest more disappointment and humiliation."

"During his Friday Khotba at Imam Ali (A.S.) Mosque in Baalbek, Sheikh Yazbek hailed the latest Islamic Resistance's operations especially the Marjeyoun - Hasbayya attack.

"Our Ummah's crying time is gone for good and is now replaced by the "Israeli" enemy's crying era, this equation is the result of IR's operations" Sheikh Yazbek said.

"The strongest and most influential voters in ""Israel"" today are the south and the western Beqaa, and that is why we hear these statements by the enemy leaders, he added.

"What concerns us is to see an "Israeli" humiliating unconditional withdrawal from our land, not what goes on inside the usurping entity", he concluded.

The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions on Lebanon's towns and villages:

11:35, the "Israeli" warplanes reiterated raiding into the same area and fired several air-to-surface rockets and timed bombs.

17:35, Mount Sojod was targeted by four 155mm missiles and four 120mm shells.
17:45, ten 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
17:50, the peripheries of Mlita village subjected to four 155mm missiles.
18:05, three 155mm missiles struck Sawan area.
18:10, Hojeir Valley was targeted by four 81mm rounds.
21:30, six 160mm missiles hit Mount Abou Rashed and Martyr Abou Saleh Hill.
Between 22:45 and 23:40, Mount Sojod was bombarded by four 155mm missiles and two 120mm shells.
23:10, Rihayat Hill was shelled by six 155mm missiles.

Military Operations (5)
The IR team of martyr Hussein Moghnieh attacked, at 17:20, enemy movements at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and definite goals. Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyr Adnan Hassan was targeting the same position with the suitable arms scoring direct hits.
At 18:00, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Assaf and Deep Bahja attacked enemy movements inside Mothallath-Qsair position with the adequate weapons chalking up direct goals.
