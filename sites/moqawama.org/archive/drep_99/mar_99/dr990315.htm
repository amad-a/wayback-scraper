Daily Report
 
 
March Monday 15-1999:

Students mark anniversary of the "Israeli" occupation at UN House(1)
 This isnt your land, so beat it and Lebanon isnt a pie. Stop slicing it up. With these simple yet emotive slogans, some 300 students marched Sunday to mark the anniversary of the 1978 "Israeli" invasion of the south.

"With yellow ribbons, the symbol of solidarity with people living under the occupation, pinned to their chests, students from some of Beiruts most prestigious private schools walked from the College Protestant Francais (CPF) to the United Nations House in downtown Beirut.

They submitted letters to UN representatives condemning the laxity in implementing Resolution 425 that leaves 11 percent of Lebanon occupied.

Despite their digital camcorders, cellular phones and designer sunglasses, the students were serious in their resentment of the 21-year old "Israeli" occupation.

We have a mistaken reputation that all we care about is partying, riding in fancy cars and checking out pretty girls, said Fadi Bizri, 18, a permanent member of Comite dAction, a student club at CPF.

We are living in a country fighting for a cause that should be won. Weve just started acting the way others before us should have and we sure feel that we will make an impact on everyone around us, he added.

"All students at the march seemed adamant about getting their message across to as many people as possible ­ particularly the young.

"Israel's" aggression should be common knowledge, said Lana Khalil, 17, of Fight "Israeli" State "Terrorism (FIST), a student committee at the American Community School. If we remain silent,
nothing will stop our aggressors from escalating their crimes, said Lana.

Her letter to the United Nations, which she read in English to the enthusiastic crowd blocking the streets across from UN House, was the story of Abu Ali, a man who lost his wife, children and land during the 1978 invasion.

"In her letter, which was greeted with loud applause, Lana said: If we want "Israeli" brutality to end, Lebanese people should unite and put behind them their sectarian bull ­ the actual word was longer and more colorful.

Students also submitted two other letters, in French and Arabic, to Nejib Friji, the information officer at Escwa.

Does the United Nations really exist? I dont think so ­ at least not when it comes to Resolution 425, said CPF student Nadim Deaibes.

Students shouted for an end to "Israel's" occupation, sang the national anthem and chanted revolutionary tunes. They distributed Lebanese flags to passers-by and collected donations for the International Committee of the Red Cross.

Why should I need a permit to visit my relatives and friends in my village and why should they be isolated from us by a stupid gate? asked 15-year old Nouhad Mouawad, a 10th grade student at ACS who is originally from Jezzine.

I know carrying banners and chanting on the streets wont liberate the occupied south, but it will show how the young can push for change. Every little bit helps, said Alfred Tarazi, 18, who came with some 10 other classmates from the Louis Wegmann school.

"Other students expressed regret that their peaceful protest could not be scheduled during a weekday, to get the message out to more of the public.

Its a shame we werent allowed to demonstrate except on a Sunday when theres no one on the streets to see us, said Tamim Bazzi, 16, head of the Student Representative Council at the International College. Demonstrations are allowed Sundays without a license from the Interior Ministry.

But at least we have the chance to send our message across to people around the world through the media, a luxury that students before us did not enjoy, said Tamim, a ninth grade student who grew up in France".

In the south, focus is on front-line villages(2)
"Lebanese across the country commemorated Sunday the 21st anniversary of "Israel's" first invasion
with demonstrations and declarations of support for those living under "Israeli" occupation.

In the early morning, hundreds flocked to Nabatieh and front-line villages, including Arnoun, to express their solidarity with residents. Roadblocks were erected in most villages in the south and in "Beirut where volunteers distributed pamphlets and yellow ribbons" March 14 is an event that chronicles "Israel's" brutality against children, the elderly and women, read one banner in Zahrani.

Wreaths were laid on the graves of victims of the Qana massacre while boy scouts holding lit candles led processions through the streets of the village. In Baalbek, residents burned the "Israeli" flag while in Hermel, cars equipped with loudspeakers toured the region playing national songs".

"Elsewhere in the Bekaa, Hizbullah set up points to collect donations and distribute yellow ribbons.

They also burned the "Israeli" flag at the entrance of Baalbek.

Military Operations(4)
On its first anniversary, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted, at 6:05, the "Israeli" post of Dabsheh outpost with the proper weapons hitting its defenses. At the same time, another team of LRB attacked Soweida outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.

Also, the LRB fighters made, at 7:45, a swift attack on Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking its fortifications. Meanwhile, another squad of LRB targeted Haddatha compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate goals. In the meantime, a team of LRB attacked on the old Beit Yahoun outpost with the suitable weapons. The fighters were able to score direct hits. Two "Israeli" helicopters gunships interfered to back up the attacked posts which came under another attacks of LRB fighters.

Moreover, a squad of LRB targeted, at 8:20, the positions of Blat and Tair Harfa scoring direct goals.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) men observed, at 9:15, enemy movements the old Beit Yahoun outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR squad of martyr Hassan Balhas in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted the outpost scoring accurate hits.

Three hours later, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Mohsen Haroun and Toni Abi Ghanem attacked enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Haidar Nayef Assaad and Sobhi Youssef Smadi targeted, at 12:30, an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to chalk up definite hits.

Quarter an hour later, the IR squad of martyrs Abdel-Karim Kansou and Salim Ahmad attacked Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking its southern fortification.
