Daily Report
 
 
March Sunday 21-1999:

Condensed "Israeli" aggressions against Arnoun(1)
""Israeli" occupation forces staged more aggressions against the town of Arnoun. A security source in Nabatieh announced that an "Israeli" bulldozer, reinforced by a motorized force, broadened the route from the Beaufort Castle to the gate which had been installed by the "Israeli" enemy at the town's
upper quarter".

"The "Israeli" bulldozer also leveled the debris of the Arnoun houses which had been destroyed by a collaborating force two days ago, in the eastern part of the town".

"The destroyed houses belonged to Abbas Alawieh, Qassem Marouni, Abdel-Hussein Qatbay, Muslim Ajami, Nimr Jabber, Ibrahim Hamdan and Yahia Muslim.

"In the meantime, an "Israeli" motorized force which was on the road to the Beaufort Castle fired 3
rounds on the vicinity of the castle.

"Earlier, Arnoun residents heard three explosions Friday night after two "Israeli" tanks arrived in the village from Beaufort Castle, according to a security source.

"The residents said the blasts, which occurred at 8.40 p.m., resembled the noise made Thursday when two homes were demolished by occupation forces.

"Elsewhere, a demonstration was staged in Nabatieh to protest the "Israeli" aggressions against the children of the south".

"Demonstrators crossed the city and then gathered in front of the Husseiniet where Sheikh Abdul Hussein Sadeq made a statement in which he denounced the "Israeli" aggressions and called on the UN along with the international community to pressure "Israel" to implement Resolution 425".

"Today (yesterday), "Israeli" occupation forces bombarded Wadi Zillaya, Maidoun and al-Qatrani Hilltops, while enemy helicopter gunships hovered at a low altitude over front lines in the Western Beqaa"

"On the other hand, witnesses from the occupied zone said "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators at the al-Hamra Bridge crossing barred occupied zone residents who wanted to travel to Mecca for Hajj from crossing to liberated areas".

"The occupation and its collaborators also confiscated an ambulance belonging to the town of Tair Harfa".

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas:
10:10, one 81mm round hit Mount Sojod.
17:55, Mount Sojod was targeted by two 81mm rounds.
22:35, several 155mm missiles struck Mount Al-Rafih.

Two pro-"Israeli" militiamen wounded in IR operation, aggressions continued on liberated areas(3)
"The "Israeli" proxy militia's radio today (yesterday) acknowledged that two collaborators were wounded Friday in a bomb explosion on the Ain Majdaleen road (Jezzine) in south Lebanon".

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) yesterday announced that a bomb was detonated at the entrance of the collaborators' Ain Majdaleen outpost".

"The attack raised to 11 the number of militiamen wounded in IR operations since the start of this year. One militiaman was killed during the same period".
