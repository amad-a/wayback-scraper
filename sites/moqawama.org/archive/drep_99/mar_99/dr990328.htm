Daily Report
 
 
March Sunday 28-1999:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
The IR released a statement in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
1:10, two 81mm rounds hit Slouqi Valley.
8:40, Haddatha woodland was targeted by three 60mm rounds.
Between 11:30 and 12:25, Mount Sojod was bombarded by thirteen 120mm shells and five 155mm missiles.
20:45, three 60mm rounds struck the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal village.
22:45, eight 120mm shells fell on Mount Sojod and three others on Tasseh Well.
22:50, two 120mm shells hit the outskirts of Zibqeen village.


Footnotes:
1- IR statement, 27-3-1999.
