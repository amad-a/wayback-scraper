Daily Report
 
 
March Sunday 14-1999:

Sayyed Nasrallah: Resistance ready for any possibility, Katyushas ready to protect civilians(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah today (yesterday) ruled out the possibility of an "Israeli" large-scale operation against Lebanon".

"Any aggression will be useless and without any clear objectives, the secretary general said at a ceremony to honor teachers".

"The "Israeli" enemy will proceed with a severe psychological warfare against Lebanon while looking for security operations against resistance centers and leaders, he added, stressing that "Resistance will always be ready and prepared for any possibility."

"Katyushas will always be ready to protect civilians. Targeting civilians is not acceptable, Sayyed Nasrallah said, commending the government's position on "Israeli"-US proposals to amend the April Understanding".

"On the other hand, his Eminence stressed the role of teachers in raising a resistance generation and creating a resistance culture in the face of the Zionist enemy".

President and Premier accuse "Israel" of stepping up tension(2)
"President Emile Lahoud Saturday accused "Israel" of creating tension with its military night flights over Lebanon, describing them as a violation of Lebanese sovereignty".

"The action illustrates "Israel's" aggressive policy aimed at creating tension, in particular after Lebanon refused to modify the 1996 understandings," he said in a statement carried by the official National News Agency".

""Israeli" war minister Moshe Arens said earlier this month that his entity might stop observing the understanding's conditions, which prevent targeting civilians".

"Lahoud's remarks echoed those of Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss, who criticized today (yesterday) the intensive "Israeli" flights over Beirut and other parts of Lebanon, saying they were intended to terrorize civilians.

"The heavy hovering of "Israeli" warplanes over Lebanon last night shows "Israel's" determination to violate Lebanon's sovereignty, scaring peaceful inhabitants, mainly women, old people and children," al-Hoss said in a statement".

"If Israel thinks it can intimidate us and make us relinquish our right to demand an unconditional "Israeli" retreat, it is dreaming, because our position is unshakable," he added.

Military Operations(4)
The IR team of martyrs Mustafa Mohammad Nazar and Wissam Ali Fsahi attacked, at 5:45, enemy movements inside Dabsheh outpost. Direct hits were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Mohannad Fouani and Habib Hijazi targeted, at 8:25, Taibeh position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. the smoke columns were seen emanating from it as well.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Mortada Rammal and Ismail Hamzeh attacked, at 11:30, the "Israeli" post of Radar as enemy movements were seen inside it. The militants, who used the proper arms, managed to chalk up direct hits.

In the meantime, the IR team of martyr Hachem Mohanna was targeting Hardoun outpost with the adequate weapons, whereas the IR group of martyr Moussa Adib was attacking Tair Harfa outpost with the appropriate arms.

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyr Fadl Soueidan attacked Blat post with the suitable weapons scoring direct and definite goals.

Ten minutes later, the IR team of martyr Hassan Hammoudi in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted Blat post with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Adnan Rida and Abdallah Kassem attacked, at 13:25, an enemy force as it was beefing up Taibeh position which damaged in another attack earlier morning. The mujahedeen, who used the adequate arms, were able to chalk up direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Ali Chrara and Wehbi Wehbi detonated, at 15:00, an explosive device inside the western fortification of Sojod outpost destroying it and inflicting all its defenders. At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Mannaa and Haidar Haidar targeted the same outpost with the proper weapons scoring direct goals.

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Nabil Akhdar and Hassan Obeid attacked an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct hits.

At 15:45, the IR men reiterated targeting Sojod outpost barring its member from evacuating the casualties.

One hour later, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Salman Krayem and Mohammad Qatbay attacked an enemy motorized patrol at the entrance of Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring certain hits.
