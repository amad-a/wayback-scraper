Daily Report
 
 
March Sunday 7-1999:
 
Hizbullah rejects any amendment of April Understanding(1)
"The heroic operation in Hasbayya in which the "Israeli" occupation forces' commander Erez Gerestein was killed was one of the most important operations staged by the Resistance, Hizbullah Secretary General said yesterday".

"Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah rejected any amendment of the April Understanding "for it would mean abolishing this understanding".

Commenting on the enemy's war minister Moshe Arens' statement that the understanding "ties the hands" of the occupation army, his Eminence said "this restriction is single; it prevents Israelis from targeting civilians."

"Arens wants to take revenge on civilians when the occupation forces are attacked, and this can not be accepted by anybody, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"There might be American attempts to amend the understanding, but they will certainly be met with Lebanese and Syrian rejection, because such an amendment will be in the interest of the Israelis alone, he added.

"The Secretary General underlined that the idea of "Israeli" military invincibility had been destroyed and blamed "Israel's" ego for not withdrawing from the south.

"It's difficult for the legendary army and the state which enjoys hegemony and superiority in the region to admit defeat especially since Lebanon is the weakest of Arab countries," he said in an interview with Associated Press.

His Eminence pledged that operation and military strikes against the occupation and its collaborators will continue.

"He ruled out dramatic developments or steps, as far as the settlement process or withdrawals are concerned, before the "Israeli" elections.

"Asked about Hizbullah's position in the event of an "Israeli" withdrawal, his Eminence said "We don't talk about this issue. Let the Israelis pull out from our land and then every development will be dealt with according to the circumstances."

"We, Arabs, need to do more and talk less; we in Hizbullah try to do this", Sayyed Nasrallah concluded.

"Israeli" colonel assures insecurity among enemy officers due to Hizbullah pursuit(2)
"As conflicting stands and statements by "Israeli" army's top officers about south Lebanon continued, an "Israeli" colonel assured that enemy army officers and troops feel insecure as they move in south Lebanon due to Hizbullah's taking after them".

""Israeli" army colonel Eyal Sheline, commander of an enemy unit in south Lebanon, urged his superior commanders to plan assassinations against Hizbullah leaders in retaliation for the current situation and for claiming responsibility for killing the "Israeli" occupation forces' commander Erez Gerestein.

"Israeli" television's military correspondent said that enemy army chief commander Shaoul Mofaz declined to comment on colonel Sheline words".

"The TV correspondent added that the "Israeli" army realizes that the cost of assassinating Hizbullah leaders could be very high".

"The time has come to stop mincing words: We have no business staying in Lebanon," a high-ranking officer told "Israeli" Ha'aretz newspaper Friday.

"Hizbullah isn't a terrorist organization. It's a guerrilla organization. It's a national liberation organization," said the officer, who was not identified.

"The current so-called northern region commander, Major General Gabi Ashkenazi, admitted Friday in an interview with Ma'ariv newspaper that the military was having problems tracking down and engaging the Islamic Resistance fighters".

"The truth must be told, we are finding it difficult to locate Hizbullah in built up areas, not only at the point of contact but in the rear," Ashkenazi said.

"Ashkenazi said army intelligence was notably having trouble identifying Hizbullah fighters".

"Israeli" Aggressions (3)

In its statement, the IR reported that the "Israeli" occupation troops staged the following aggressions:
Between 3:35 and 5:55, Mount Sojod was bombarded by five 155mm missiles and four 120mm shells.
4:10, four 155mm missiles struck Mlita Hill.
8:55, south of Jibal El-Botom village was targeted by four 155mm missiles.
10:35, twelve 155mm missiles slammed into the peripheries of Zibqeen and Henneih villages.
19:10, the perimeters of Yater village subjected to eleven 155mm missiles.
19:40, Litani River banks and the perimeters of Zawtar village exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
20:50, four 155mm missiles fell on the peripheries of Jibaa town and four 120mm shells on Mount Sojod.

Military Operation (4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyr Hussein Kamal Younes attacked, at 18:45, enemy movements at Hemayed position with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals. At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Jamal Ali Al-Shimali targeted enemy movements at Rshaf position with the suitable weapons. The militants managed to score accurate hits.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Moussa Fahs and Ahmad Monzer targeted, at 19:30, enemy movements at the post of Mothallath-Qsair with the appropriate arms. Direct hits were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Akram Sweid and Hussein Mistrah attacked Salaah position with the suitable weapons. The militants were able to score direct and accurate goals.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Zahreddine and Mustafa Chokair targeted, at 20:35, enemy engineering unit and motorized foot-patrol as they were beefing up Rihan compound. The militants, who used the adequate arms, were able to score direct and accurate hits and bar them from achieving their goals. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Moshawrab and Ali El-Chab attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.
Quarter an hour later, the IR team of martyrs Ali Chokair and Hussein Wehbi targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to score direct and accurate goals.

Earlier at 10:30, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked Tair Harfa compound with the proper weapons scoring direct hits. At the same time, another group of LRB targeted enemy movements near Hardoun outpost with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.
