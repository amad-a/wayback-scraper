Daily Report
 
 
March Wednesday 24-1999:
 
Introduction
The "Israeli" occupation forces pursued, for the fourth day, the siege of the occupied towns of Hasbaya, Ain-Qinia and Ibl El-Saqi preventing the residents from leaving the occupied zone.

For its part, Hizbullah said the blockade of the Hasbaya area in South Lebanon, in addition to storming into houses and detaining civilians, are aggressions that reveal the confusion and suspicion the "Israelis" are experiencing.

Hasbaya blockade enters fourth day; Bayyada Sheiks call for boycotting occupation(1)
(Israeli Aggressions)
"The "Israeli" blockade imposed on the occupied towns of Hasbaya, Ain-Qinia, and Ibl as-Saqi entered its fourth day. Occupation forces are barring 11,000 residents from leaving the occupied southern zone.

Moreover, "Israelis" detained, questioned, and threatened about 50 Lebanese, with some later released.

"The arbitrary measures have evoked angry reactions by residents and religious leaders. Druze sheikhs in al-Bayyada held a meeting at which they discussed the recent "Israeli" measures.

"The sheiks condemned the "Israeli" arbitrary acts and called for rejecting any form of dealing with the occupation, especially at the economic level.

"Al-Safir newspaper said the Khalwat al-Bayyada sheiks, with the religious and moral position they represent, have become engaged in a direct confrontation with the occupation.

Residents have been instructed, through religious channels, to boycott "Israeli" goods, reject any collaboration with the occupation, and adhere to the Lebanese legitimacy and institutions, on top of which President Emile Lahoud, the daily said.

"Al-Safir said the "Israeli" indiscriminate arrests in Hasbayya and neighboring villages aimed to intimidate residents as "Israeli" feared that the Druze sheikhs' movement may turn into a general anti-occupation uprising".

"During the Bayyada meeting, the sheikhs condemned the "Israeli" practices and urged national and international groups to end the towns' siege, stop the occupation's terrorist acts, and secure the release of civilian detainees".

"Moreover, reports from the occupied zone said the "Israeli" enemy at 17:00 yesterday leveled a house in the occupied town of Houla, without declaring the reasons for the terrorist act".

"A collaborating force protected the "Israeli" bulldozers during the demolition process".

Hizbullah: Hasbayya blockade reveals "Israeli" confusion and suspicion(2)
(Israeli Aggressions)
"Hizbullah today (yesterday) said the blockade of the Hasbayya area in south Lebanon, in addition to storming houses and detaining civilians, are aggressions that reveal the confusion and suspicion the "Israelis" are experiencing as well as the enemy's grudge against the civilians who refuse to co-exist with it and submit to its occupation".

"These spiteful "Israeli" aggressions proved to be useless in convincing honorable civilians to collaborate with the enemy or give up to its occupation and terrorism, a Hizbullah statement read.

Strongly condemning the aggressive "Israeli" measures, the statement affirmed Hizbullah's solidarity with the Hasbayya residents and praised the Druze religious leaders' stance at al-Bayyada meeting "which expressed a national commitment to the Resistance and confronting the occupation."

We are certain that the Zionist enemy will never be safe or stable in our land. It has only one option: an unconditional withdrawal from all the occupied zone, the statement read.

Senior "Israeli" officer: debate over withdrawal dismantled Lahd militia(3)
(military operations)
"The heated debate in "Israel" over withdrawal from south Lebanon has led to a state of disintegration in the ranks of the "Israeli" Lahd militia, senior "Israeli" officer has acknowledged".

""Israeli" army radio quoted the officer as saying that the losses inflicted on the "Israeli" army by resistance operations in south Lebanon has led to the desertion of a large number of militiamen.

A state of floundering and confusion is prevailing among the "Israeli" security bodies which control the militia", the officer said.

""Israeli" occupation forces have recently arrested three militia officers on charges of assisting resistance men in the occupied zone".

"ISRAELI" ALLIED MILITIAMAN DESERTS LEBANESE ARMY(4)
(military operations)
"An "Israeli" allied fighter has deserted from Israel's proxy militia in occupied southern Lebanon and surrendered to the Lebanese Army. Marwan Khalil Nabaa, from the village of Shebaa fled on foot through ravines to avoid occupation soldiers. He said his brother Mohamed, a militia intelligence officer, had deserted five months ago and disclosed information that led to the arrest of about 20 pro-"Israeli" agents operating inside Lebanon. A growing number of militiamen have fled the occupied zone in recent months".

"Israeli" Aggressions(5)
In its communiqué, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:
00:25, three 120mm flare shells were fired over the south of Zibqeen village.
1:05, three 120mm shells hit the perimeters of Yater village.
3:00, a series of explosive devices was detonated at Slouqi Valley.
5:00, Mount Sojod was bombarded by 120mm shells.
14:55, Kfour ravine was shelled by nine 155mm missiles.
Between 15:35 and 15:50, thirty 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village.
15:45, Jib Soweid area was targeted by four 155mm missiles.
Between 16:00 and 16:55, twenty one pounded into the area between the villages of Zibqeen and Henneih.
16:50, the peripheries of Chaitieh village came under eight rounds.

Military Operations(6)
The IR group of martyrs Ali Assi and Fadi Wehbi attacked, at 14:45, Dabsheh outpost with the proper weapons scoring direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR men observed, at 15:30, enemy movements inside Blat post. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ali and Hussein Moghnieh targeted them with the adequate arms. Direct hits were chalked up, the militants reported.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Ali Shour in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit bombarded intensely the concerned post. In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Zeidan and Abdel-Hassan Izzeddine targeted, after spotting enemy movements inside it, radar post with the suitable arms striking its western fortification.

Moreover, the IR men spotted, at 16:40, enemy movements near Tair Harfa position. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Mahmoud Jaber and Jawad Moghnieh targeted them with the appropriate weapons chalking up accurate hits.
