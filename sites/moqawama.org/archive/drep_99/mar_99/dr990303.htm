Daily Report
 
 
March Wednesday 3-1999:
 
Introduction
A day after "Israel" announced a new policy of harsher reprisals against the Islamic Resistance (IR), an "Israeli" war ministry official said the operation in Lebanon has ended.

Meanwhile, the heated debate over "Israel's" presence in South Lebanon has moved to center stage in the "Israeli" election campaign following a series of human losses at the hands of the IR heroes.

Yesterday Likud and Labor stepped up their political head-butting and both promise that "Israel" will be out of Lebanon within a year.

However, the cautious calm has reigned over the southern towns and villages but was breached by the "Israeli" occupation warplanes roaring in the sky of South and Western Beqaa.

Lebanon quagmire at center of "Israeli" election campaign
""Israel's"" costly occupation of south Lebanon moved to center stage of the entity's election campaign Tuesday with prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu scrambling to match rivals' promises to withdraw the occupation troops.

"With the "Israeli" public demanding answers following a spate of troop losses in Lebanon, including the slaying by Hizbullah fighters Sunday of ""Israel's"" top general in the occupied zone, Netanyahu and opposition Labor Party chief Ehud Barak each claimed that only he could end the conflict" (1)

Jerusalem Post reported that "with quiet restored to the northern border yesterday, the Lebanese problem moved into the political arena.

"Labor Party leader Ehud Barak, following up on comments he made Monday night, reiterated his commitment to have the "Israeli" Army out of Lebanon by June 2000. He also attacked Prime Minister Binyamin Netanyahu's approach to the problem, saying he is "running after the mosquitoes," instead of working to "dry up the swamp."

"Netanyahu is leading a government that is bogged down in every field. You need guts to move this issue, to start going forward and stop this incessant jabbering," he said.

Labor MK Yossi Beilin took the criticism further, saying that Netanyahu had proven once again that "he has no vision for solving the Lebanese problem... A vote for him is a vote for remaining in the bloody trap that is Lebanon. In contrast to the picture Netanyahu is trying to paint, the Lebanese question is a political one of the first order, and the elections will clearly show if the people want to give Barak the mandate to leave Lebanon, or give it to Netanyahu to stay there."

Barak said Labor's plan for Lebanon includes three points: renewing talks with Syria, changing tactics in south Lebanon, and coordinating with the international community for diplomatic support, and a possibility for stationing international forces in southern Lebanon".

"Netanyahu lashed back at Barak, arguing that he is trying to make electoral gain out of the sorry situation, and that he is the "last person in the world" to be giving advice on Lebanon, as it was he who had entangled "Israel" there in the first place".

"It was he who brought about the April 1996 understandings - bad arrangements which had the "Israeli" Army for the first time recognizing Hizbullah's right to hit at our soldiers and, at the same time, bound the "Israeli" Army arms and reined it its ability to hit back within the towns and villages," Netanyahu told Army Radio.

"Later on Channel 2, the prime minister called on Barak to meet with him and the relevant security officials to present his plans if, in the premier's words, they're serious plans and not just an election
gimmick".

"In response, Barak said the prime minister "knows my telephone number" and would agree to meet him if asked, "even though no one believes in him. He had three years to hold negotiations and improve the situation, but no one did anything." Barak also termed Netanyahu's offer as "television leadership"

Meretz leader Yossi Sarid also entered the fray, saying that the return of the Golan was the price "Israel" will have to pay for peace on the Lebanese border. "When Menachem Begin went to negotiate with Egypt, and announced ahead of time that he was willing to retreat from all of Sinai, people came to him with complaints. I didn't have any complaints. That was the obvious price we had to pay," said Sarid.

"The situation is the same with Lebanon. We could have an agreement within months, it does not have to be put on hold until we have a comprehensive peace with Syria. But, we need to come to terms with the fact that there is a real price to pay. The price is an "Israeli" withdrawal from the Golan Heights - with needed security arrangements".

Liat Collins adds:
"Tourism Minister Moshe Katsav said Barak is harming "Israel" by turning the Lebanon issue into election propaganda. Katsav, who is head of the Likud campaign headquarters, said Barak is trying to turn Lebanon into a political issue along party lines, even though all parties are interested in safely leaving south Lebanon. He said the cabinet had discussed the matter, together with members of the security establishment, and decisions would be taken not out of political considerations, but according to the diplomatic and security situation" (2)

The enemy has to choose between withdrawal or the Resistance's hammer, says Hizbullah (3)
"Hizbullah Secretary General's political Assistant Hajj Hussein Khalil said the "Israeli" enemy has only two options: either to withdraw unconditionally, defeated and humiliated, or to stay under the Islamic Resistance's hammer".

"In an interview with al-Noor Radio, Khalil stressed that the Resistance will force the enemy to leave Lebanon in humiliation.

"The enemy leaders started to seriously consider withdrawal after the resistance operations unveiled the falseness of the claims they made to world public opinion, plus uncovering the real "Israeli" army which used to be called the invincible legend", he said.

"Khalil said the whole Lebanese society has become a resistant society.
"Resistance has become a factor uniting the different classes and sects of the Lebanese people, he said.

He praised the firm official stand which is united with that of the army, the people and the Resistance.

This unity has deprived the "Israelis" of their pretexts and uncovered the truth concerning the occupation", he said.

At Martyr Mohgnieh's funeral, Sheikh Qawooq stresses: Resistance is determined to attack occupation commanders (4)
"Islamic Resistance supporters this morning escorted Martyr Hussein Abdallah Moghnieh to his final resting place.

"Moghnieh was martyred during confrontations with the "Israeli" occupation troops and their collaborators Sunday.

"The funeral procession set out from the Mount Amel Hospital in Tyre to the martyr's hometown of Tair Dibba.

"When the long motorcade which was preceeded by motorcycles reached the town, the martyr's coffin was carried by mourners to the cemetery.

"The resistance is determined to hunt the "Israeli" occupation's commanders and troops, Hizbullah's official in the south Sheikh Nabil Qawooq told mourners.

"The Resistance which is always fully ready will retaliate if "Israeli" aggressions crossed the limits. We will not remain silent if the enemy changes the equation imposed by the Resistance, he added.

"Sheikh Qawooq underlined that the enemy's threats to initiate a new aggressive policy will only end up in more losses in its ranks, adding that resistance against the occupation forces will continue.

"The Resistance will not allow the enemy to catch its breath and recover its strength, for it is determined to continue targeting Zionist commanders and troops, having earlier hunted Merkava tanks and stormed outposts, he said.

"They are doomed to die in ambushes and bombs as long as they occupy our land, he added.
Sheikh Ali Yassin led the prayer for the martyr's soul before the pure body was laid to rest at the town's cemetery".

"Martyr Adnan Mohammed Hassan is to be escorted to his final resting place tomorrow in his hometown of Bisarieh".

SYRIAN PRESS WARNS "ISRAEL" ITS STRONG-ARM TACTICS IN LEBANON WILL FAIL (5)
" Syrian newspapers hit out on Tuesday at "Israel's" "strong arm" policy of occupation in southern Lebanon, and warned the Jewish state against any attack on its northern neighbor. ""Israel" will be committing a serious new mistake if it attacks Lebanon ... It will achieve nothing, for Lebanon has always resisted all the previous savage attacks launched against it," wrote the ruling party newspaper Al-Baath. "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced Monday a "new policy" of
harsher reprisals for attacks by Lebanese guerrillas on "Israeli" forces in south Lebanon, following the killing of a top general and three other "Israelis" on Sunday. ""Israel" has been trapped ever since it occupied the border zone (of southern Lebanon) which has become a deadly quagmire destroying the Zionist theory of security based on expansion and occupation," the paper continued. "Israeli" policy in Lebanon has collapsed, said the government paper Tishrin, "since what "Israel" calls a security zone has become one of the least secure regions and a trap which is draining the energies of the "Israelis"." "Netanyahu's attempts to improve his position for the elections through military adventures will turn against him, as such adventures have already brought down three "Israeli" prime ministers before him," the paper said. "Last week's heroic actions in southern Lebanon deepened the crisis in "Israel" and showed that the policy of might and occupation will not solve the problems and that the only possible way is an immediate and unconditional withdrawal of Lebanon from southern Lebanon," said Tishrin.

MONITORING GROUP MEETS AS TENSIONS DEFUSE IN SOUTH Lebanon (6)
" The five-nation cease-fire-monitoring group, will hold an urgent meeting shortly in an attempt to defuse rising tensions in south Lebanon. Prime Minister Salim el-Hoss announced the meeting of the monitoring group had been brought forward from Wednesday. He said there has been an improvement in the situation on the ground and believed it is the start of de-escalation. Even though the group has no power to act, it has on previous occasions managed to contain the violence".

" The monitoring group was born after "Israel" launched a 17-day air, artillery and naval assault against Lebanon in April 1996. Its task is to monitor a cease-fire understanding between Lebanon and "Israel". That understanding bans attacks on and from civilian targets but allows the Lebanese resistance to launch attacks against "Israeli" occupation troops and their local allies in south Lebanon. The group which is made up of representatives from Lebanon, "Israel", France, Syria and the United States has held over 100 meetings since it was established. The panel rules on complaints filed by Lebanon and "Israel" over alleged truce violations. But it has no power to act. A year ago, the United States tried to expand the role of the group so that it could discuss security issues including UN resolution 425. But Lebanon and Syria rejected broadening its scope insisting that UN resolution 425 is clear cut and not negotiable. "Israel" has been trying to attach conditions to the resolution, conditions the Lebanese government categorically rejected. Last month, Washington tried to persuade Lebanon to allow the monitoring group to tackle "Israel's" annexation of the southern village of Arnoun. But the group was unable to reach a consensus forcing Lebanon to threaten to take the issue to the UN. But Lebanon's youth during a protest took down the barbed wire encircling the village of Arnoun".

Yes to unilateral withdrawal (9)                          By AVRAHAM SELA
"The vociferous public debate over Lebanon of the last two weeks has revealed two main developments. The first is the continuously descending threshold of "Israeli" society's
willingness to further tolerate the toll of continued military presence in south Lebanon. The second is the unmistakable awareness of the government that the growing public unrest can no longer be ignored".

"Indeed, the days when the IDF's* presence raised no questions, reflecting "consensus," are over. The deepening involvement of grass-roots groups in the debate and the ever-strengthening
voices in support of a unilateral withdrawal represent a widening consciousness of the undeniable failure of the "security zone" concept in terms of cost-benefit calculations.

"The public awakening came after years of denial and hibernation. It was the disaster of last year's helicopter tragedy, which claimed the lives of 73 soldiers, that gave birth to the "Four Mothers" organization. Similarly, the fall of 12 more in the abortive naval commando raid that September triggered the establishment of The Movement for Leaving Lebanon Peacefully, headed by MK Yossi Beilin. Yet for a society overwhelmed by issues of national security, peacemaking with the Palestinians, and economic depression, the attempts to pressure the decision makers "from below" remained ineffective".

"The recent toll of seven soldiers within two weeks brought the issue back to center stage, showing that despite long intervals between disasters, we have grown ever more impatient with the high military and political echelons. We are less and less tolerant of the explanations, apologies, and promises for reassessment and more creative tactics, all of which proved to
be empty words".

"To put it bluntly, "Israeli" decision-makers in this government, like their predecessors, prefer to preserve the security zone concept regardless of its undeniable failure rather than taking the risk of
post-withdrawal attacks on "Israel" from south Lebanon for which they may be held responsible".

"No doubt, the preferable policy is to reach agreement with Lebanon that would ensure "Israel's" security interests before returning to the international border. In the absence of such a possibility, however, what is the calculated risk of a unilateral withdrawal?"

"The question is no longer whether the IDF should pull out of Lebanon, but how to minimize the risks of the proposed alternatives. In other words, should "Israel" withdraw without full proof guarantees, and in their absence, how would Syria and Hizbullah, the main actors on the other side, respond.

"The opponents of a unilateral withdrawal argue that Syria would continue to encourage guerrilla attacks on "Israel", because it would be the only way to bring its government to resume the
negotiations over the Golan Heights. In this effort, they claim, Hizbullah would play a leading role because of its own interest in eliminating "Israel" and liberating Jerusalem."

"These arguments ignore Syria's interest in maintaining Lebanon's stability, continued economic prosperity, and rehabilitation following 15 years of civil war. That these developments have been in force since the early 1990s was due to Syria's efforts since the mid-1970s, during which it has
paid dearly in material and human losses. Syria would have to consider carefully the damage that "Israel" could cause to Lebanon as a Syrian asset, should there be repeated attacks on
"Israel's" northern territory from Lebanon".

""Israel's" presence on Lebanese soil makes it easy, even legitimate for Syria to encourage guerrilla attacks on the IDF and the South Lebanese Army**. But given Syria's interest in  advancing a settlement on the Golan, it would have to consider the impact of such attacks on the US administration, whose involvement would be necessary for Syria in any case. And  finally, Syrian President Hafez Assad has shown impressive prudence regarding violation of "Israel's" sovereign territory, even when proxies are involved".

"As to Hizbullah, the picture of a mercenary group presented by the opponents of a unilateral withdrawal is far from accurate.

Hizbullah is deeply rooted in the social and political reality of Lebanon, competing for power and access to resources like any other political movement. That Iran has been its patron and  financier, and Syria is allegedly supporting it militarily or approving its activities, does not mean that these two countries own it".

"Unlike the Palestinian guerrilla organizations that dominated south Lebanon and were expelled by "Israel" in the war of 1982, Hizbullah's intentions have never been to liberate Palestine, but to advance domestic social and political goals, in which the armed struggle against "Israel" has played a major role. Despite occasional announcements that it intends to fight "Israel" until Jerusalem is liberated, Hizbullah leaders have also said the opposite - that their war is not with "Israel" as such and that they would cease fighting once it fully withdraws from Lebanon".

Another striking piece of evidence is that in over 15 years of fighting, Hizbullah's guerrillas have never attempted to cross the border into "Israel", even though they have repeatedly demonstrated the permeability of the security zone by physically reaching it".

"Finally, unilateral withdrawal is not meant to create a rosy situation in which peace prevails in the North. Given that the "security zone" is unable to prevent Katyusha rockets from hitting "Israel", our northern border can be defended from inside "Israeli" territory. Our continued presence in Lebanon is a liability more than an asset, and it is about time to bring to an end the anomaly of protecting one's own territory by occupying a neighbor's."

The writer is a senior lecturer in the Department of International Relations at the Hebrew University and a former member of the "Israeli" delegation to the Nakoura talks.
