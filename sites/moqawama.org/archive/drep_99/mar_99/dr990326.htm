Daily Report
 
 
March Friday 26-1999:
 
Introduction
The "Israeli" allied militia is reported to be in state of heightened alert after several militiamen were arrested for allegedly collaborating with the resistance and Lebanese authorities.

Meanwhile, US ambassador to Lebanon has acknowledged at a private meeting that the Resistance in Lebanon has proven to be useful and created real problem in Israel.

Resistance has proven to be useful, but we won't admit this publicly, US ambassador(1)
"US ambassador to Lebanon David Satterfield has acknowledged at a private meeting that the Resistance in Lebanon has proven to be useful and created a real problem in "Israel".

Al-Safir newspaper said that during talks between him and a Lebanese politician, Satterfield said that all forces in "Israel" are now competing to offer an earlier withdrawal from Lebanon.

The US ambassador was told that ""Israeli"" leaders used to brag that they do not trust accords or papers, and that they have an army that provides their entity with security".

Satterfield said "We realize that Lebanon has become a problem for all forces in "Israel".

This means that you acknowledge that the Resistance has proven to be useful; and we are right in adopting and supporting it".

"The US ambassador shook his head and said, "I can only say that you are right."

Can you state such a view before the press? The Lebanese politician asked.

No, of course", Satterfield said.

"Earlier, during a dialogue between a Lebanese minister and American pressmen, the US journalists admitted that "Israel" has started to face a dilemma due to the heavy losses and pressures resulting
from Hizbullah's operations".

Al-Bayyada Sheikhs disown "Israeli" enemy's collaborators(2)
"Following their latest meeting at al-Bayyada in which they called on the Hasbayya residents to uprise against the "Israeli" occupation, the Hasbayya Druze Sheikhs disowned all "Israeli" agents, the collaborating militiamen and those who work in the "Israeli" entity".

"Hasbayya area residents reacted positively to the Sheikh's moves, while occupation forces threatened to detain or expel Druze religious leaders in addition to tightening the blockade imposed on Hasbayya.

Moreover, "Israeli" intelligence told its collaborators' chief in Hasbayya Alamedine Badawi to crackdown on residents to prevent potential movements after the Bayyada Sheikhs' call to boycott "Israeli" goods and those who collaborate with the occupation".

"For his part, President Emile Lahoud hailed the residents' brave stance and steadfastness against the "Israeli" occupation forces and praised the Bayyada Sheikhs' positions.

"What is taking place in the villages of Hasbaya, Ibl es-Saqi and Ain Qeny is no less than oppressive and a violation to international laws and customs and to the basic standards of human rights," he told the Cabinet yesterday."

"The President also pledged government support for the area. Prime Minister Salim Hoss spoke in support of the president's views".

"Lahoud also commended the Bayyada Sheikhs for clinging to their country and calling on civilians to confront the occupation and boycott its products".

"The Bayyada Sheikhs rejected an "Israeli" attempt to meet with a delegation of senior "Israeli" officers in the Khalawat. The "Israelis" wanted the Sheikhs to violate the disownment they had declared themselves.

Following the Sheikh's rejection to meet enemy officers, the "Israeli" intelligence tightened the siege around the town of Ibl al-Saqi and prevented residents from entering and exiting the town.

Enemy troops also set up barricades inside the town, stormed houses and abducted four civilians.

Reports from the occupied zone said "Israeli" occupation forces are firing shots randomly inside towns and villages to terrorize civilians who are currently living in a state of upheaval as the number of detainees is nearing sixty, including ten collaborating militiamen and officers".

"In the meantime, Chief collaborator in Jezzine agent Joseph Karam, heading a militia unit, stormed into the village of Sfaray and broke into the houses of Elias Hareem and Maroun Suleiman.

"The collaborators told Hareem and Suleiman that they must leave the area after accusing them of supplying the Lebanese government and the Resistance with information".

Confidence crisis between occupation and collaborators: intelligence nightmare renders "Israeli" sleepless (from al-Safir daily)(3)
"The "Israeli" officials are still expressing remarkable interest in the so-called "Syrian espionage network" in Hasbayya, while insisting - in contrast - that the issue is based on mere suspicions that some of those arrested have worked for Syrian intelligence".

"Israel" is trying to show that investigations with "security zone residents" are being conducted by the collaborator militia's intelligence service and that "no link has been proven to exist between the detainees and the explosion that killed Gen. Erez Gorestain and three other "Israelis" a month ago."

According to Ha'aretz, "Israeli" sources underline that none of the detainees has "served" as an officer in the collaborator militia's intelligence service.

"The issue has been exaggerated in news agencies' reports from Lebanon and in some "Israeli" media, the "Israeli" sources said, adding that the issue was a mere local espionage case the likes of which are unearthed in south Lebanon from time to time".

"The case has nothing to do with the killing of Gorestain and the detainees were not intelligence officers in SLA, the sources added, claiming that Adel Younes is not affiliated with the Lahd militia or its intelligence".

"In spite of this, according to Yedioth Ahronot, "Israeli" army has decided to "tighten supervision" over the Lahd militia to block any cooperation between militiamen and Hizbullah. A meeting between senior "Israeli" officers and militia officers took place last Monday to serve this goal.

These meetings are held on the grounds of what has been published over the arrest of 3 SLA security militiamen for suspicion that they had presented Hizbullah with information about "Israeli" army, the "Israeli" daily said.

"Yedioth said the northern command aims to study the general mood in the ranks of the Lahd militia over sympathy with "Israel".

""Israeli" army realizes that a lot of SLA men fear that if the "Israeli" army withdraws from the "security zone", they will be left without any support and will not be able to protect themselves, in the face of Hizbullah and Syria; therefore, they are, according to "Israeli" military estimations, thinking about the future and improving relations with Hizbullah", Yedioth Ahronot said.

The daily said the Lahd militia has recently tried not to comment on the arrest and searches conducted by the "Israeli" army but "senior "Israeli" officers fear more than ever to move in Lebanon, for they fear information about their movements reach Hizbullah."

"Although "Israeli" official sources try to dissociate the arrests from the killing of Gerestein, while trying to link these arrests with the desertion of a Lahd militia officer six months ago, the real campaign started effectively in the wake of Gerestein's killing, and the real aim of this campaign is to "uncover Lahd militiamen and security zone residents who cooperate with Hizbullah," according to "Israeli" daily Ma'ariv".

"Enemy war minister Moshe Arens' statement that claimed that arrests were not linked to the Lahd militia, was a last-ditch attempt to maintain "the last thread" which still links the low sides".
""Israelis", practically, no longer trust the Lahd militia. They, however, do not want to give up a fig leaf that covers nothing.

"Therefore, they insist that the news, which has come out from "Israel" itself in the first place, were "intended to sow consternation between the SLA and "Israeli" army and to feed the public debate in Israel" over "Israeli" occupation of south Lebanon".

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions:
00:20, several 155mm missiles hit the area between Maifadoun and Zawtar.
Between 00:25 and 1:05, Mazraat Hamra was shelled by fourteen 155mm missiles.
00:50, one 105mm tank round struck the surroundings of Soweida position.
Between 1:10 and 1:30, Sawan area was bombarded by twenty 155mm missiles.
1:25, five 155mm missiles fell on the area between Choukeen and Jibsheit villages.
13:15, Kfour ravine was targeted by four 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(5)
The IR squad of martyrs Ibrahim Chaheen and Ali Fadel attacked, at 00:15, enemy movements at Ali Taher position with the proper weapons scoring direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Zein Assi and Ahmad Abdallah targeted, at 1:05, enemy movements at Beaufort Castle with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain hits.
