Daily Report
 
 
March Tuesday 16-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah asserted that "the "Israeli" soldier is our direct enemy, but the money and political cover are ensured by the United States who have pledged to maintain "Israeli" supremacy over all the countries of the region".

Elsewhere, a member of the "Israeli" allied militia has deserted from his outpost and surrendered to the Lebanese Army, security sources said Monday. In the meantime, the "Israeli" occupation troops and their collaborators expelled a Lebanese policeman yesterday from the occupied zone.

Hizbullah says United States the "real enemy"(1)
"The American administration is our real enemy because it is the one that began the aggression against people in the (Middle East) region," Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said Monday.

"The successive American administrations were responsible every time a drop of blood was spilled in southern Lebanon, a house was destroyed there or a tree burnt by "Israeli" bombardment, Sayyed Nasrallah told the weekly magazine Al-Wasat.

"The "Israeli" soldier is our direct enemy, but the money and political cover are ensured by the United States who have pledged to maintain "Israeli" supremacy over all the countries of the region," he added.

The Secretary General also said that Katyusha rocket attacks on occupied northern Palestine should be carried out only by Hizbullah, although other groups have similar weapons.

"The participation of these groups in the armed struggle against the "Israeli" occupation is welcome, but we ask them to give us exclusivity for the use of Katyusha rockets because we have proven that this weapon is efficient in our hands," he said.

"When we fire these weapons, we claim them and we do not just fire two or three, but several rounds at a time," he said.

"Firing on occupied northern Palestine is a political decision, not a military one, because it is an arm used to prevent ""Israel"" from pursuing aggressions against Lebanese civilians," Sayyed Nasrallah added.

"We use this arm with precision so that it serves our purpose of dissuasion. It should not be used haphazardly because it will only serve to give "Israel" pretexts" to retaliate, he said.

Hizbullah fires Katyushas on occupied northern Palestine in retaliation for "Israeli" bombardments that target civilians in southern Lebanon.

Sayyed Nasrallah Sunday vowed that Hizbullah will continue its resistance movement till the day when all Lebanese territory is liberated from "Israeli" enemy.

At a mass rally in Beirut's southern suburbs on the occasion of the 21st anniversary of "Israeli" invasion of Lebanon, his Eminence warned ""Israel"" not to launch a large-scale offensive against Lebanon, saying any aggression will fail just like earlier "Israeli" aggressions.

"Thousands of people across Lebanon held various demonstrations against "Israeli" occupation on the same day".

LEBANESE BRIGADES TO UPGRADE ATTACKS AGAINST "ISRAELI" TROOPS IN SOUTH Lebanon(2)
" It was a year ago when the Lebanese Brigades of Resistance to "Israeli" Occupation launched their first attack against "Israeli" occupation troops".

" Fighters of the Lebanese Brigades of Resistance to "Israeli" Occupation have been undergoing training to launch attacks against "Israeli" occupation forces in south Lebanon. They carried out their first operation on March 14, 1998 to coincide with the 20th anniversary of "Israel's" first invasion of Lebanon. These fighters come from all regions and sects in Lebanon. That was the logic behind its creation, according to the Secretary General of the Hizbullah movement Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

The Hizbullah chief announced the birth of the organization in November 1997. It was created to give the opportunity to any Lebanese regardless of religion or political affiliation to participate in the resistance war against "Israel". Many Lebanese, Nasrallah said wished to fight "Israel" in a national rather than an Islamic framework. Officials refused to disclose the number of fighters but stressed they come from various religious backgrounds. For the time being, the group's attacks mainly target "Israeli" allied militiamen. They have still not attacked "Israeli" occupation troops inside the occupation zone in the south. They are still undergoing training for that. Members of the Brigades are given the same treatment as Hizbullah resistance fighters".

Collaborator turns himself in to army(3)
"Further evidence of the crumbling morale of the South Lebanon Army* militia emerged Monday with the defection of a militiaman from Ibl es-Saqi who turned himself over to the Lebanese Army after he escaped from the zone on foot".

"The deserter was identified as Khaled Munzer, whose brother Naji killed a Syrian SLA collaborator, Nidal Nasr, five months ago, according to security sources. Their parents were expelled from the zone after the incident".

"Mr. Munzer fled through wadis to avoid "Israeli" and SLA checkpoints and asked villagers to lead him to the Lebanese Army, who accepted his surrender Monday.

"The SLA have reportedly imposed travel restrictions on the residents of Ibl es-Saqi, preventing them from leaving the zone".

"The "Israeli"-funded militia faces an uncertain future, with growing expectations of an eventual "Israeli" withdrawal from the south. Several prominent militiamen, including SLA commander Antoine Lahd, have been sentenced to death in absentia by a Beirut court".

"ISRAEL" EXPELS POLICEMAN FROM ZONE(4)
""Israel" today (yesterday) expelled a Lebanese policeman from the occupation zone in south Lebanon accusing him of collaborating with the state's intelligence services. The policeman had been held for three months at the "Israeli" run detention center in the zone. Hatem Jabr Hadithi told reporters he was subjected to torture and maltreatment. He was forced to leave behind his wife and three children.

Military Operations(6)
The IR men spotted, at 7:30, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Majed Jaffal and Mohammad Hussein Kassem attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Borji and Hussein Chalhoub targeted, at 11:20, enemy movements at Blat post with the adequate arms. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported. In the meantime, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) was bombarding the concerned outpost hitting its eastern fortification. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Hachem Mohanna and Moussa Adib attacked Radar position. The mujahedeen, who used the appropriate weapons, scored direct and certain goals.

Ten minutes later, the IR team of martyr Mohammad El-Hadi reiterated targeting Radar post with the suitable arms striking its western fortification and destroying a radar installation.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyr Hussein Mohammad Nasser attacked, at 13:25, enemy movements inside Baraasheit compound. The militants managed to chalk up direct and definite goals.
Furthermore, the IR group of martyr Habib Ahmad targeted, at 14:00, enemy movements at Hemayed position with the proper arms scoring direct and certain hits.

WESTERN MEDIA NOT FAIR WHEN REPORTING ON SOUTH Lebanon(7)
"As part of events to coincide with the day of solidarity with south Lebanon and the western Bekaa, the Ministry of Information on Friday took foreign journalists to areas on the front-lines to highlight the conflict there. More on that tour and Lebanon's coverage in the international media in this report.

Report: "Israeli" troops have been occupying areas in south Lebanon since 1978. And over the past 21 years, villagers living on the front-lines have had to face daily hardships and constant shelling. The Ministry of Information took international journalists on a tour of these villages. The purpose,
organizers say, is to allow these reporters and photographers to experience first hand the situation on the ground. Lebanon's Speaker of Parliament, Nabih Berri said that (dubbing): "We all know that the killing of "Israeli" soldiers who are occupying our land makes headlines in the western media. But the death of Lebanese men, women and children in "Israeli" attacks is never covered. There is a clear and obvious bias". International and Arab journalists agree that the world only understands the story from the other side of the border. Stefano de Paolis, Italian news agency said that: the "Israelis" make more noise story coming from Tel Aviv and Jerusalem. Dr. Mohammed Saed Idriss, Khaleej newspaper said that: there is no good coverage for all that is happening here in south Lebanon.

United Nations peacekeepers who have been here since 1978 escorted the journalists to villages that lie underneath "Israeli" manned positions. The blue berets have over the years tried to provide southerners with protection but there is only so much they can do. This is a mass grave in the village of Qana. It was here where over 100 Lebanese men, women and children were killed. They were taking shelter in a UN base when "Israeli" gunners pounded it in April 1996".

"Timor Goksel, spokesman of UN peacekeepers in south, said that: "those based in Lebanon cover it quite well. They know what the story is they can ask. But the dangerous coverage about south Lebanon is by so called experts who don't know much about Lebanon and then make comments that is dangerous. They have preconceptions about what the story is. Lebanon has long argued that the world tends to forget that "Israel" is the occupier and attacks against its troops are legitimized under a cease-fire accord. The tour comes amid efforts by authorities to launch an international media campaign against "Israel". But organizers admit that will not be easy. In the interim, villagers were out in force hoping that their voices would reach the world. There is widespread belief in Lebanon that the international media does not cover properly the south Lebanon conflict. It is either ignored or reports tend to be biased toward "Israel". And that is why the Ministry of Information organized this tour hoping to raise awareness among international journalists about the conflict here, the suffering of southerners and "Israel's" 21 year old occupation of Lebanese territories".
