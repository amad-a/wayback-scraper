Daily Report
 
 
March Wednesday 31-1999:

"Israeli" helicopters wound four Lebanese soldiers(1)
""Israeli" helicopters fired on Lebanese troops in the southern Beqaa valley on Tuesday, wounding an officer and three other soldiers, a military source said.

"The soldiers were hit during an exchange of fire with "Israeli" helicopters in the Jinem area, southeast of Rashaya and just north of "Israel's" south Lebanon occupation zone, the source said.

Enemy helicopters in the afternoon staged several raids on the Jabal al-Wastani area near Mount Hermon and flew heavily over the areas of al-Kfair, Meimes, Ain Ata. They also raked these areas with heavy machine gun fire.

"Meanwhile, "Israeli" warplanes hovered over the Hasbayya District villages.

""Israeli" occupation's artillery sporadically bombarded the Jabal al-Wastani area and Wadi Jala'm.
In the south, enemy artillery pounded the outskirts of Haddatha, Wadi al-Kfoor, and Mount al-Rafieh with a number of heavy bombs.

""Israeli" enemy forces in Wadi Abu Qamha and Tallat A-Shrayqa at dawn today (yesterday) bombarded Mount al-Daher's Heights, Wadi Zillaya, Maydoun Hilltops and Birkat al-Jabbour with more than twenty 155mm rounds.

"Along with the bombardment, tens of flare shells were dropped over the western Beqaa front.
An "Israeli" collaborating militia unit widely rocketed the frontline of al-Hassbani, Zimrayya and Kawkaba with heavy and medium guns, with bullets reaching the perimeter of the Zimrayya crossing.
Moreover, "Israeli" arbitrary measures against civilians in the Hasbayya area are continuing,
preventing them from traveling inside the occupied zone".

"Today (yesterday), the collaborating militia released Fadi Shdeed and Kamal Ghaddar, residents of Ibl Assaqi, after detaining them at al-Khiam detention center for eight days.
Other Ibl Assaqi residents are still detained."

""Israeli" forces continued their aggressions against south Lebanon over the past two days, shelling the outskirts of Froun, al-Ghandourieh, Khorbet Selim, al-Sawwaneh, Wadi al-Hjeir and Wadi al-Slouqi in the mid-scetor with 120mm and 155mm rounds".

"Shelling also reached the vicinities of Eastern Zawtar, Western Zawtar, Mazraat al-Hamra, Litani River Banks and the abandoned army barracks in Upper Nabatieh. The neighboring fields were raked by heavy machine guns."

"Moreover, the outskirts of Arabsalim, Mleta, Lwaizeh, Oqmata area and Mount al-Rafieh in Iqlim al-Touffah were hit by heavy rounds from "Israeli" positions".

"For their part, "Israeli" helicopters raided the Kafra vicinity targeting areas with two air-to-ground missiles and heavy machine gun fire. No casualties were reported".

14:45, fifteen 175mm missiles slammed into Janaam Valley and Mount Al-Wastani.
Between 15:40 and 17:25, three 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Aita El-Jabal village.
16:55, the outskirts of Jibal El-Botom came under ten 155mm missiles.
17:25, several 155mm missiles hit the peripheries of Zibqeen.

18:55, the surroundings of Slouqi Valley came under six 120mm phosphorous shells.

20:20, two 155mm missiles hit Oqmata plain.
Between 20:25 and 20:50, the outskirts of Mlikh village subjected to two tank rounds and one 155mm missile.
Between 20:30 and 20:55, five 120mm shells slammed into Tasseh Well watercourse.
22:15, several 81mm flare shells were fired between the villages of Shaqra, Houla and Baraasheit.

Military Operations(3)
At 20:25, the IR team of martyrs Moussa Diab Yehia and Youssef Abdel-Hussein Badran attacked a Lahd's motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals. At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Moussa Nasralah and Abdallah Ahmad Rammal targeted an "Israeli" force at the area of Kassaret Al-Oroush. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct hits. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Hassan Ibrahim Rida and Salah Mohammad Ghandour was attacking Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket propelled grenades striking its main fortification.
