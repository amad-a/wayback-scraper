Daily Report
 
 
March Thursday 25-1999:
 
Introduction
The number of "Israeli" proxy militiamen deserting their ranks is increasing. On Tuesday, an intelligence officer in the militia had defected and surrendered to the Lebanese Army.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" bombers conducted two air raids into the area between the villages of Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen in the Western Sector. Also, a school bus loaded with children was hit by a shrapnel when the "Israeli" gunners targeted Tibbnine town with a number of shells.

CIA: "Israel" uncertain about agents' loyalty(1)
"The confrontation between the Bayyada Sheikhs on one hand and "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators on the other has intensified".

"The occupation and its agents have been imposing curfew in Hasbayya, Ainqinia, and Ibl as-Saqi. Several residents, including collaborator militiamen, have been detained by the occupation.

On the other hand, Lebanese security sources said occupation forces have released the agent officer in the Lahd militia Adel Younes (of Ainqinia) who had been detained 4 days ago".

"Reports from the occupied zone said a state of outrage is prevailing in the Hasbayya area with residents rejecting the practices of the occupation and its collaborators, while adhering to Lebanese institutions and government".

"The "Israeli" closure is taking its toll on the residents, although the occupation has allowed 6 trucks to pass through the Zimrayya crossing and transfer food into the besieged towns".

"In a related development, al-Kifah al-Arabi newspaper's correspondent in Washington said ""Israel" Wire" published that ""Israel"" had "detained three intelligence officers of the allied Southern Lebanese Army on Sunday, suspecting them of providing information pertaining to "Israeli" activities in southern Lebanon to Hizbullah according to foreign media reports."

"Adel Younes, a militia liaison officer with ""Israel's"" Mossad Intelligence Agency, was among those held on suspicion of helping Hizbullah", the "Israeli" news wire added.

"It was first reported that the persons under arrest may have assisted Hizbullah in the ambush of an "Israeli" convoy in the "security zone" in which Brig.-Gen. Erez Gerstein was killed, but "Israeli" Radio on Monday reported that it has already been established that this was not the case, it claimed.

An "Israeli" army spokesman added that the incident is one of the continued "psychological" warfare being waged against the "Israeli" army and its militia and the arrests are in no way connected to the incident in which Gerstein fell in combat".

"Al-Kifah al-Arabi quoted US intelligence sources as saying that "Israeli" forces have been staging successive arrest campaigns in the occupied zone in an atmosphere clearly showing that ""Israel"" suspects all the militiamen who are supposed to be loyal to it in that area, especially after the killing of Gerstein".

"On the other hand, Reuters quoted Lebanese security sources as saying on Tuesday that an intelligence officer in the pro-"Israeli" militia had defected and surrendered to the Lebanese army.

"Collaborator officials could not confirm Marwan Khalil Nabaa, described as a liaison officer with
""Israel's"" Mossad secret service, had quit the militia which helps "Israel".

"He left home on Monday evening and has yet to come back,'' Nabaa's family said.

Nabaa's reported defection follows what appears to be an "Israeli" crackdown on the militia. On Sunday, militia sources said ""Israel"" detained three militia intelligence officers on suspicion of aiding Hizbullah, Reuters said.

"Security sources said ""Israel"" had restricted access to the occupied southern village of Hasbayya and was questioning several militia officers stationed there", it added.

"Nabaa's brother Mohammed, who was also in militia intelligence, defected last month".

BUS LOADED WITH CHILDREN HIT IN "ISRAELI" SHELLING, NO INJURIES
"A school bus loaded with children was hit with shrapnel during shelling by "Israeli" troops in southern Lebanon on Wednesday, police said. No one was injured. The bus was hit while it was parked at the entrance to the Farah private school on the outskirts of the village of Tebbnine, east of the southern port city of Tyre, police said. "The children escaped miraculously from this aggression. They started screaming and running in panic to the school buildings," a police officer told AFP. A truce accord signed in 1996 after "Israel's" "Grapes of Wrath" offensive against anti-"Israeli" guerrillas in Lebanon prohibits attacks on civilians".

""Israeli" warplanes this morning raided areas in south Lebanon. Two fighter bombers fired two air to ground missiles near Jabal Boutom. There were no reports of casualties. The "Israeli" air force has been striking southern Lebanon nearly every day since a resistance attack killed "Israel's" top general last month"(2)

Lahoud condemns "Israeli" measures in zone(4)
"President Emile Lahoud decried moves by "Israeli" forces in the occupied zone and hailed the steadfastness of the areas residents Wednesday".

What is taking place in the villages of Hasbaya, Ibl El-Saqi and Ain Qeny is no less than oppressive and a violation to international laws and customs and to the basic standards of human rights, he told the Cabinet, according to information Minister Anwar Khalil.

"He also pledged government support for the area, Mr. Khalil said, adding that Prime Minister Salim Hoss spoke in support of the presidents views".

"Three Druze civilians were seized Wednesday amid a wave of arrests of suspects accused of links with Hizbullah. "Israeli" radio said Monday that between eight and 11 members from the "Israeli"-allied South Lebanon Army had been arrested since Friday".

"More than 40 members of the Druze community have also been interrogated over suspected links to Hizbullah".

"The SLA said "Israel" suspected them of providing information which helped Hizbullah kill one of "Israel's" top generals in the south and three other "Israelis" last month".

"Meanwhile, "Israeli" warplanes attacked a wadi near Jabal Botom and some open countryside near Nabatieh".
