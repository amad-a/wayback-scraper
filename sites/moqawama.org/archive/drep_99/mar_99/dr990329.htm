Daily Report
 
 
March Monday 29-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
The Israeli occupation troops staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese towns and villages:
1:25, six 155mm missiles slammed into Mazraat Hamra.
1:40, Qaissieh Valley was targeted by three 155mm missiles.
2:00, two 120mm shells fell on Slouqi Valley.
3:05, Qobaa position showered its surroundings with a barrage of machine gun rounds.
5:35, five 120mm shells pounded into Oqmata plain.
13:10, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by four 175mm missiles.
13:35, the perimeters of Arnoun town subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
20:15, the "Israeli" helicopter gunships fired two air-to-ground rockets into the ravine surroundings with Yater village.
21:30, Mount Al-Rafih was targeted by four 120mm shells.

Military Operations(2)
The IR squad of martyrs Mohammad and Ali Monif Ashmar attacked, at 1:20, the "Israeli" post of Taibeh with the proper arms chalking up direct and certain hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Rida Youssef Yassine and Mohammad Mahmoud Ismail targeted, at 12:30, Blat post with the appropriate weapons. Direct and definite goals were scored, the militants reported.

Ten minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Anis Ayoub and Hassan Kamel Tarhini reiterated targeting Blat position with the suitable arms. The militants managed to score definite goals.

Moreover, the IR men observed, at 15:20, enemy movements at Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ali Hussein Moghnieh and Ali Chour attacked them fiercely with the proper weapons. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.
