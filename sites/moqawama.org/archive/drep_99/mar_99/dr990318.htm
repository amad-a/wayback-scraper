Daily Report
 
 
March Thursday 18-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said "an amendment of the April Understanding will deprive the resistance of its right to use Katyushas which is definitely impossible.
In Jerusalem, the "Israeli" enemy's Prime Minister said that his war minister is looking into the possibility of some form of redeployment of forces in southern Lebanon.

Sayyed Nasrallah: AU amendment deprives Resistance of right to retaliate(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said "an amendment of the April understanding (AU) will deprive the Resistance of its right to use Katyushas," which is "definitely impossible."

"His Eminence ruled out an "Israeli" large-scale offensive against Lebanon. He also considered unlikely an "Israeli" pullout as a result of an understanding with Lebanon and Syria or with Lebanon
alone.

Sayyed Nasrallah stressed that there is no way to penetrate the united Lebanese stance which is reinforced and protected by Syria to confront "Israeli" aggressions".

"In an interview with the Syrian "Weekly Tishreen", Sayyed Nasrallah said that any "Israeli" military operation like the "Grape of Wrath" or "Accountability" operations will be fail to achieve its major goal, which is annihilating the Resistance's infrastructure.

"Any "Israeli" withdrawal from the south and Western Beqaa following an understanding with Lebanon and Syria is away far from becoming true, and its circumstances and conditions are not available", the secretary-general added.

Sayyed Nasrallah stated that a unilateral withdrawal is the policy of neither the "Likud" nor the "Labor".

 Civilian wounded in "Israeli" air raids
"An elderly civilian woman was wounded Tuesday night when "Israeli" warplanes staged several raids near villages facing the western sector of the occupation zone".

"Several bombs were dropped at 5 P.M. between Jabal Botom and Zibqine. The warplanes returned 20 minutes later and bombed the area surrounding Shaitieh, just north of Zibqine.

Wassila Khalil, 67, from Zibqine, was struck in the head by shrapnel when a bomb exploded beside her as she was working in a field. She was transferred to state hospital in Tyre where doctors said
her condition was stable. Eight houses in Zibqine were also damaged in the attack.

Army units and Hizbullah fighters fired anti-aircraft guns at the "Israeli" jets. The raids came shortly after the Blat and Teir Harfa outposts were shelled by the resistance."

"Another civilian, Youssef Alayan, was wounded Tuesday when the resistance fired rounds at the Biyada radar station"(2)

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Salah Fares and Jaafar Al-Ziwawi attacked, at 6:35, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western fortification.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Saad and Hussein Kassir targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score accurate hits.

Five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Ramadan and Adnan Qara Ali targeted an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. The mujahedeen, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, were able to chalk up direct goals.

Also, the IR team of martyr Allam Soweidan attacked, at 16:50, Radar post with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits.

Ten minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Jamal Ali Chehimi and Hussein Kamal Youness targeted enemy movements inside Blat position with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to
chalk up direct goals.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Ali Wahab and Ali Hassan Atwi attacked, at 17:45, Qantara compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits.

At 21:20, the IR squad of martyr Mohannad Ali Fouani targeted enemy movements inside Baraasheit compound with the suitable arms. The militants managed to chalk up direct hits.

Netanyahu: We're checking possible redeployment in Lebanon(5)
"Prime Minister Binyamin Netanyahu said yesterday that War Minister Moshe Arens is looking into the possibility of some form of redeployment of forces in southern Lebanon.

Netanyahu was addressing the Knesset Foreign Affairs and War Committee for the first time since the deaths of seven "Israelis" in clashes in the security zone, including Brig.-Gen. Erez Gerstein and "Israel" Radio reporter Ilan Roeh.

"According to an unofficial report, Netanyahu stressed that "Israel" cannot leave southern Lebanon without security arrangements which would stop Hizbullah from attacking major "Israeli" targets such as Haifa from the border".

"In a clear reference to statements by Labor Party leader Ehud Barak that he would pull out of Lebanon within a year if elected prime minister, Netanyahu said such declarations make it harder to reach a satisfactory security arrangement for the region".

"Netanyahu said Hizbullah does not want to fire Katyushas at the Galilee region because they know this would be met by a strong retaliation from "Israel"".

"He said "Israel" must demand a higher price for Hizbullah violations of the 1996 Grapes of Wrath understandings, because this is the only way to solve the situation. He did not elaborate".
