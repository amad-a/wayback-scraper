Daily Report
 
 
March Friday 19-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah said the Islamic Resistance (IR) has brought down the myth of the invincible army and puzzled the enemy generals and technological devices, calling for a comprehensive and uniting resistance in the Arab and Islamic world to recover the usurped land.

In a flagrant violation of the April Understanding, the "Israeli" occupation troops and their agents stormed into the town of Arnoun and forced the residents to gather at the town's square.

Hizbullah calls for an Arab comprehensive uniting resistance to liberate all of Palestine(1)
"The Islamic Resistance has not only changed the rules of the game, but concepts as well, Hezbollah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said.

"At a dinner party given to honor 175 thinkers and researchers participating in the Ninth Arab Nationalist Conference, his Eminence said, "The Islamic Resistance has brought down the myth of the invincible army and puzzled the enemy generals and technological devices."

Resistance in Lebanon has will, determination, and certainty that victory will come, before being a weapon and a bomb, he said.

"Our major cause is the liberation of Palestine from the (Mediterranean) Sea to the (Jordan) River, he added, calling for "a comprehensive and uniting resistance in our Arab and Islamic world to recover the usurped land."

"He noted that "Resistance in Lebanon has been able to unearth our strength elements and the enemy's weakness elements. It could also unite the Lebanese behind its option."

"He called on Arabs to follow the example of this Resistance.

"The "Israeli" enemy is unable to achieve any goal. If Zionists want to put an end to the Resistance, they will have to kill all the Lebanese, for all of Lebanon is Resistance, the secretary general said.

"We are fighting without thinking of what will become of us, will we become ministers, rulers, or end up in prison and be punished as terrorists for fighting the "Israeli" enemy."

Each of us is a project that moves to achieve victory and whose personal objective is to be martyred, he said. "Therefore we refrain from getting party or sectarian gains, because our major cause is the Resistance which is the pivot of our daily movement."

His Eminence stressed "We are an Islamic Arab Lebanese movement fighting the "Israeli" enemy which is occupying our land."

"We are eager to limit our fight, Jihad, arms, and experience against the "Israeli" enemy, his Eminence said, underlining that "we need to stay away from any internal conflict in any Arab or Islamic country.

"We need to dedicate our capabilities and energies against the "Israeli" enemy; the liberation battle is the priority. We need the uniting resistance whose spearhead is represented by martyrs and Resistance men, the secretary general concluded.

"Arab Nationalist Conference Secretary General Abdel-Hamid al-Mahri spoke at the dinner. He stressed that the only option for Arabs in the Resistance.

"What has been achieved by Lebanon's resistance has strengthened this view", al-Mahri said.

Collaborators destroy two homes in Arnoun
"South Lebanon Army ("Israel's proxy) militiamen marched into the unoccupied half of Arnoun Thursday and dynamited two houses after claiming to have found six roadside bombs stored in one of the buildings.

Prime Minister Salim Hoss condemned the demolitions and Lebanon filed a complaint with the five-nation Monitoring Group over the incident".

"A 20-man SLA (militia) patrol accompanied by two armored personnel carriers and a jeep descended the road between Beaufort Castle and Arnoun at 2.30 P.M. The militiamen warned the residents to stay away for their own safety because they were about to explode roadside bombs
that had been discovered in one of the houses".

"The villagers immediately moved to the entrance of the village and shortly afterward heard two massive explosions. Two homes, belonging to Ahmad Qotbay and Zeinab and Mariam Tawbe, were completely flattened while a third house, owned by Nimr and Mohammed Nayef Olleiq, was partially destroyed. The homes were abandoned in 1985".

"The SLA (collaboratos) incursion was the first serious incident to have occurred in the village since hundreds of students stormed Arnoun last month, one day after the "Israelis" cordoned off the village with barbed wire and land-mine warning signs".

"Mr. Hoss sharply criticized what he called the new "Israeli" piracy against Arnoun, which he said was an act that should be swiftly condemned by the Monitoring Group".

"Israel's" blowing up of homes in Arnoun is an insolent aggression, not only against Lebanon and a safe village, but also against the international will which welcomed a few days ago the liberation of
Arnoun from the "Israeli" occupation, he said in a statement"(2)
