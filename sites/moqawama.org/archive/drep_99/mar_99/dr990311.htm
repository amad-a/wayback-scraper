Daily Report
 
 
March Thursday 11-1999:

Hizbullah rejects modifying April 1996 Agreement(1)
"Hizbullah announced that modifying the April Understanding would effectively do away with the agreement and lead to an escalation of violence."

Theres no sense in amending the agreement. Either we have an accord to protect civilians on both
sides and prevent the firing of Katyushas against northern "Israel", or "Israeli" forces will attack Lebanese civilians and we will reply by attacking "Israeli" civilians, said Hizbullah Deputy Secretary-General Sheik Naim Qassem.

We will not stop our attacks against "Israeli" forces or even ease them. We will not stop using roadside bombs or staging ambushes against "Israeli" troops, he said. In the past, Lebanese officials refused a U.S. proposal to put an end to the use of roadside bombs because this is a weapon in the hands of the resistance.

Resistance victories between President and FTR(2)
"President Gen. Emile Lahoud today (yesterday) received a delegation from Hizbullah's parliamentary bloc, the Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR), presided by the bloc's head MP Sayyed Ibrahim Amin al-Sayyed.

The MPs reviewed with the president the developments in the south and other internal affairs".

"Talks touched on the Islamic Resistance's victories against the "Israeli" occupation forces and their field and political reflections, besides the importance of the Lebanese official and popular stand of backing the resistance", MP al-Sayyed said.

"The "Israeli" recent suspicious moves to amend the April understanding were also discussed", the Hizbullah MP added.

"He quoted the President as affirming "the firmness of the Lebanese position on backing the resistance, rejecting any security arrangements with the enemy, and the inseparability of the Lebanese and Syrian tracks."

AWC: Resistance embodies supreme meaning of freedom, dignity, and pride(3)
"Arab Work Conference (AWC) which is holding its 26th session in Cairo "hailed the Lebanese people and its brave resistance which has embodied the most supreme meanings of freedom, dignity and pride in confronting the "Israeli" occupation in south Lebanon and the western Beqaa".

"AWC condemned the practices of the occupation forces and their violations of work rights in occupied territories, calling on "Israel" to implement the related international resolutions and withdraw unconditionally".

"The conference called on Arab countries to promptly provide the Lebanese people with all types of support and assistance to enhance its steadfastness in the face of "Israeli" aggressions".

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Mohammad Ali Hadi attacked at midnight exactly enemy movements in and around Baraasheit compound with the proper weapons. Direct hits were chalked up, the militants reported.

At 6:00, the IR squad of martyr Mortada Ahmad Rammal targeted Beit Yahoun position with the rocket-propelled grenades hitting some of its fortifications. Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Adnan Moussa Rida and Anis Ayoub was attacking Baraasheit compound with the adequate arms.

The mujahedeen managed to score direct goals.

Half an hour later, the IR team of martyrs Habib Hijazi and Hassan Harb targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits. Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Nabil Akhdar and Rameh Mahdi targeted the same outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up certain goals. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Assaad Chabshoul and Hassan Hani Obeid targeted Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to score direct and accurate hits. In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Mustafa Nazar and Wehbi Wehbi targeted an "Israeli" motorized patrol at Rihan hilltops inflicting a number of casualties.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Najem and Mahmoud Khodor Baghdadi attacked, at 12:30, enemy movements at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

Two hours later, the IR men spotted enemy movements near Hemayed position. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyr Hachem Abdel-Menhem Mohanna targeted them with the appropriate weapons. The militants managed to score direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Khalifeh and Youssef Faqih targeted, at 16:20, enemy movements at Sojod outpost with the suitable weapons. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Fort its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 6:25, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its fortifications and chalking up direct and certain goals. At the same time, another group of LRB targeted the same outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. Certain hits were scored, the fighters reported.
