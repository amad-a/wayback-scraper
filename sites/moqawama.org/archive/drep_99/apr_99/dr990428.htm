Daily Report
 
 
April Wednesday 28-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance mujahedeen capped their recent successes Tuesday with a swift attack on Sojod outpost destroying its defenses and raising Hizbullahs flogs on it. The "Israeli" enemy army admitted that eight "Israeli" soldiers in addition to six collaborators were wounded.

While the IR mujahedeen were storming into Sojod outpost, other IR teams staged over two hours attacks on more than twenty "Israeli" and Lahdi positions.

In the meantime, the "Israeli" jets raided into several villages of Iqleem Al-Toffah. Two civilians were injured at Jarjoua town, thus, violating the April Understanding.

Resistance keeps "Israel" on its heels Mujahedeen wounded fourteen in blite on occupation forces(1)
On the occasion of Ashora - Karbala days, asserting the option of Jihad and resistance path, honoring sacred blood of our martyrs, underlining the futility of the "Israeli" military measures and carrying out the orders issued by Hizbullah Secretary General, the IR teams of April Martyrs in the special forces, at 16:00, stormed into Sojod outpost destroying its fortifications and hoisting the IR's flags on it. At the same time, the IR squads of martyrs Fadl Atwi, Khodor Khiami, Ahmad Cherri, Sami Melhem, Ali Madi, Hassan El-Amin, Ali Kassem, Youssef Bzeih, Moussa Salman, Ali Harmoush, Mohammad Faqih, Hussein Salman, Hussein Nahim and Hassan Salman and the teams of martyrs of Mansouri, Nabatieh, Jmeijmeh and Sohmor massacres staged attacks on the positions of Blat, Radar, Taibeh, Beaufort Castle, Rihan, Dabsheh, Tair Harfa, Mashaaroun, Qantara, Soweida, Bir Kallab, Kfar Houna, Ahmadieh, Dabboura, Kassaret Al-Oroush, Zaffateh, Shraifeh and Ain Taghra respectively.

The attack on Sojod outpost resulted in wounding eight "Israeli" soldiers and three collaborators as the "Israeli" Army acknowledged.

Earlier, the IR group of martyrs Bassel Alaeddine and Ali Mohammad Nasser attacked, at 1:05, the position of the 70th Lahdi Brigade Command (Qabaa) scoring direct and certain hits.

At 22:40 also, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Ghamloush and Majed Jaffal reiterated attacking Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite hits were scored, the militants reported.

For its part, two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 23:45, the positions of Salaah and Qsair with the adequate weapons. The fighters were able to chalk up direct goals.
 

Occupation renewed aggressions against south and breached the April Understanding(2)
""Israeli" occupation forces have once again breached the April Understanding by shelling residential areas in the towns of Jarjoua and Jibaa.

"In Jarjoua, "Israeli" rounds today (today) hit the house of Mohammed Darwish, injuring his wife Fatima Mouqaled, 40, and their son Hassan who is only 8 years old.

"In the meantime, the "Israeli" artillery intensively bombarded the outskirts of Arab Salim, Houmine, Habboush, Sitt Zbaideh Bridge, Zahrani River banks, Nabeh al-Tasseh banks, Loweizeh, Mlita, Mazraat Oqmata, the woods of Sojod and Rihan and Upper Nabatieh where rounds fell near the Saint Antonios Convent.

"The "Israeli" artillery in Haddatha Hill and Dhour Meis at 10:00 a.m. this morning bombarded the outskirts of Haddatha and Harris with some fifteen rounds.

"The bombardment was accompanied by intensive "Israeli" warplanes hovering over the areas of Tyre, Bint Jbeil, Nabatieh and Iqleem al-Toffah.

Earlier at 1:30 a.m. a 155mm round fell inside the backyard of a three story building owned by Mehdi Noureddine in the town of Khorbet Selem, but failed to explode. The building is inhabited by five families".

"In the meantime, the outskirts of Khorbet Selem, al-Sawaneh and Wadi al-Qaissieh were also shelled with "Israeli" 155mm rounds.

"At noon "Israeli" warplanes hovered over the areas of Iqleem al-Toffah at low altitude reaching the Sidon area and staging mock air raids.

At 16:00 the "Israeli" artillery renewed its intense bombardment targeting the Iqleem al-Toffah Heights from emplacements in Zaffateh and Tohorra inside the occupied zone.

"Hovering by "Israeli" warplanes was reported over the area.

In the mid sector, the towns of Sawaneh, Khorbet Selem Jmeijmeh and Wadi al-Qaissieh were also shelled by some twenty rounds.

"In addition, the outskirts of Shaqra, Wadi al-Slouqi, al-Hojeir, Qabrikha, Toleen, Borj Qalaweih and Majdal Selem.

"At 16:20, enemy warplanes raided the Mlita Hilltops in Iqleem al-Toffah firing four air-to-ground missiles.

"At 17:40, "Israeli" warplanes renewed raids on the area north of the town of Zibqeen dropping several missiles.

"The two raids coincided with intensive and severe bombardment on Iqleem al-Toffah, targeting the towns of Jarjoua and the Sojod Hills.

"Furthermore, "Israeli" warplanes which staged raids and mock raids all day long are still hovering over many southern areas, accompanied by MK drones, while "Israeli" helicopters flew over Iqleem al-Toffah.

Anti-aircraft guns of the Lebanese army and IR opened fire on the attacking "Israeli" planes and helicopters".
