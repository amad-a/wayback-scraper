Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 8-1999:

Introduction
The popular protest in Shibaa continued yesterday as residents staged a sit-in at the town's mosque in spite of the enemy's motorized patrols and the allied militia's arbitrary practices. The day before, an upheaval was erupted among Shibaa's population as the "Israeli" occupation troops and their collaborators detained the town's Imam Sheikh Ismail Dalleh who called for Jihad against the occupation forces. But the Shibaa people clashed with the collaborators and finally managed to bring him back to the town.

Hizbullah hailed the stand taken by "our people in Shibaa in the face of the "Israeli" occupation arbitrary practices".

The expulsion of 20 nationals from Shibaa prompted Prime Minister Salim Hoss to deliver urgent messages to the United Nations and the Monitoring Group to hasten an end to the deportations and return the evicted residents to their houses.

Shibaa uprising frees detained imam with bare hands(1)
"The occupied town of Shibaa yesterday was stage for a new uprising which was ignited when the "Israeli" occupation forces and their proxy militia detained the town's imam.

"Sheikh Ismail Dalleh called from the town's mosque for Jihad against the occupation forces.
"We are passing through a very difficult stage. The town will belong to us or to the "Israelis"," Dalleh said.

"We must confront this new attack which aims to expel us from our town, but we will remain here, clinging to Lebanese legitimacy at any cost, he added.

"Following this call, the town's imam was arrested by Lahdi militiamen and taken to the security headquarters in Zoghle.

When Dalleh was detained, a state of upheaval prevailed in Shibaa. Around 100 cars set out to the Zoghle HQ.

"The militiamen tried to keep the residents away. But the Shibaa people clashed with the collaborators who fired shots over the residents' heads to intimidate them.

Finally the residents were able to bring back the Sheikh to the town.

"The popular protest in Shibaa continued today (yesterday) as residents staged a sit-in at the town's mosque.

Hundreds of residents took part in the sit-in in spite of the enemy's motorized patrols and the proxy militia's terrorist practices".

"Reports from the occupied town said the protesters walked to the UNIFIL HQ in Shibaa. They handed officials there a memorandum demanding that international bodies take action to stop "Israeli" terrorist practices and pressure the "Israeli" entity to withdraw from Lebanon".

"Reports said preparations are being made to stage similar uprisings in Hasbayya and Ibl as-Saqi.
On the other hand, occupation forces lifted the blockade it had imposed on the town of Ibl as-Saqi three weeks ago".

"When it sealed off the town, the occupation forces subjected the residents to several types of provocative and terrorist practices".

"Yesterday morning, occupation allowed a number of civilian cars to travel through the Zimraya crossing.

"However, eye witnesses said the occupation army is still patrolling the town and closely observing the Lahdi militiamen in it.

"Due to the besiege, the town ran out of food, vegetables and fruit. The sick and university students were not allowed to leave the town.

"Occupation forces have subjected 80 residents to prolonged investigation and detained twelve citizens, six of them are still held in the notorious al-Khiam detention center.

"The town's residents thanked all those who had worked to lift the siege, "especially the honorable Bayyada Sheikhs, whose excommunication of those who collaborate with "Israel' has sped up lifting the siege."

"In the town of Hasbayya, Sami Sharaf was seriously wounded when a collaborator militiaman called Raja Abu Dehen shot him".

"Abu Dehen is one of the bodyguards of Alameddeen Badawi, the militia's security chief in the area.
Sharaf was taken to the Ain w'Zein hospital".

Hizbullah hails heroic stand of Shibaa people(2)
"Hizbullah today (yesterday) hailed the stand taken by "our people in Shibaa in the face of the "Israeli" occupation's arbitrary practices".

"The courageous popular move in Shibaa in response to the town imam's call and the confrontations that followed fall within the overall popular revival in the face of the occupation forces and their collaborators, a Hizbullah communiqué read.

"The broad state of resurrection created by the resistance amidst our people and nation has moved all the strength and capability sources besides spreading the spirit of sacrifice and giving among our people in the liberated areas and those occupied alike, it added.

"Hizbullah also noted "the integration between the resistance's sacrifices and the overall popular will which has placed the enemy before an ever-growing quagmire".

"The occupation will have only one option: to withdraw from our land with its tail between its legs without achieving any of its political objectives, expansionist claims, or security interests", the communiqué concluded.

LEBANON ACCUSES "ISRAEL' OF VIOLATING TRUCE AFTER IT EXPELLED 18 FROM SHEBAA(3)
" Lebanon has accused "Israel' of violating the April cease-fire understanding after it expelled 18 Lebanese from the occupied southern village of Shebaa. Lebanon has called on the five nation cease-fire monitoring group to condemn "Israel''s action and demand the residents return to their homes."

" A delegation from the occupied village of Shebaa visited Prime Minister Selim el-Hoss this morning to update him on the hardships faced by the residents. Lebanon has filed a complaint to the five nation cease-fire monitoring group accusing "Israel' of violating the truce by expelling 18 Lebanese from the village yesterday. The group is to meet in Naquora tomorrow. Hoss has called on the
monitoring group to condemn "Israel' for the action and demand the 18 are allowed to return home.
Hoss has contacted the American and French ambassadors to Lebanon. The prime minister also intends to send a complete file on the case to the United Nations since the village falls under the control of the UN peacekeeping force in south Lebanon. The 18 deportees were relatives of four "Israeli" allied militiamen who deserted from their ranks over the weekend. The villagers expelled were mainly women, children and the elderly. The delegation from Shebaa had requested the prime minister to set up a makeshift camp for the deportees in an area lying between "Israeli" occupied land and government held territory. The delegation said those expelled would stay there until they are allowed to return. Hoss has reportedly instructed concerned parties to take measures to alleviate the plight of the deportees. The delegation later held talks with the Mufti of the Republic Sheikh Mohamed Rashid Kabbani. House Speaker Nabih Berri meanwhile who sent a message to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan demanding immediate intervention to repatriate the deportees especially since the village lies within UN peacekeepers' area of operations".

MONITORING GROUP TO DISCUSS "ISRAELI" SHELLING OF SOUTH Lebanon(4)
" A five-nation monitoring group is due to meet Thursday to discuss "Israel''s recent artillery bombardment of two southern Lebanese villages in which eight civilians, including seven children, were wounded, government sources said Wednesday. Lebanon has lodged a complaint with the group charging that the bombardment of Habboush and Arab Selim last Sunday was "a flagrant violation" of the April 1996 understanding which prohibits attacks on civilians on both sides of the border. One of the shells fired from "Israeli" positions inside the occupied border zone exploded near a house in Habboush, wounding seven civilians. Another round of shells hit Arab Selim wounding a 13-year- old boy. "Israel' also lodged a complaint accusing Hizbullah resistance guerrillas of launching attacks against its troops inside the buffer zone from civilian areas. The monitoring group comprises members from France, "Israel', the United States, Syria and Lebanon".

Military Operations(6)
At 22:30, the IR mujahedeen observed enemy movements near Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyr Ismail Youness in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted them scoring direct hits.

At the same time, the IR men spotted enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost. Immediately afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Olayan and Hussein Salman attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Nazzal and Hassan Balhas targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.

For its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 22:00, Hemayed position with the adequate arms chalking up direct hits.

At the same time, another group of LRB targeted Rshaf position with the appropriate weapons. The fighters were able to score certain goals.
