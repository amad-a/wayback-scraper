Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 22-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Kassem blasted the April Understanding Monitoring Group's (AUMG) statement which contained a condemnation of Islamic Resistance's (IR) roadside bombs which killed "Israeli" occupation soldiers near to Beaufort Castle.

In this regards, President Emile Lahoud sought to dispel speculation that the unprecedented reference to "bilateral channels" in the statement, on Arnoun implied a change in the government's policy toward the peace process.

Also, the Lebanese parties and forces rejected considering the resistance's two operations near the Beaufort Castle as violation of the April Understanding.

Lahoud: No to any direct contact with the occupiers(1)
"President Emile Lahoud Wednesday sought to dispel speculation that the unprecedented reference to bilateral channels in the international Monitoring Groups statement on Arnoun implied a change in the governments policy toward the peace process.

"Tuesdays statement, in which Lebanon also accepted blame for an attack against "Israeli" forces from a populated area, had fueled interpretations about a possible departure from the erstwhile stated policies toward "Israel".

"What the Monitoring Groups statements failed to explain was that the proposed bilateral channels to resolve the plight of Arnoun in fact meant indirect negotiations through the United States and France, which are key participants in the panel, and not a direct dialogue.

Lebanon remains firmly committed to its position, which is clear and leaves no room for interpretation, General Lahoud told the Cabinet during its weekly session Wednesday.

Lebanon is committed to the unity of its people, to its army and to the resistance, he said. The joint peace track with Syria and the common destiny we share are stronger than any enticement or blackmail.

"The president added: Neither Arnoun nor any other inch of occupied territory will be turned into a buffer for protecting the occupiers. Efforts to lure Lebanon into dropping its opposition to any form of contact with the Zionist enemy are doomed to failure".

The president also reiterated that "Israel" was trying to use its occupation of Lebanon as a card in its forthcoming elections, an issue Beirut will resist.

The enemy is reeling from the successful blows of the resistance and the steadfastness of our army, and is seeking to turn the south into a ballot box for its elections, he said.

Our choice is unequivocal commitment to the legitimacy of our resistance to the occupation and the rejection of any invitation that would turn our armed forces into guards for the occupation ... As for the occupiers, they only have one choice ­ to withdraw.

Earlier Wednesday, General Lahoud discussed the issue of Arnoun with French Ambassador Daniel Jouanneau and said after his meeting that Lebanon insisted on recovering Arnoun.

Prime Minister Salim Hoss also rejected the interpretations of the five-nation Monitoring Groups statement.

The heads of the French and the American delegations will make the needed contacts between Lebanon and "Israel", he said, disclosing that he received a telephone call from Mr. Jouanneau about the Monitoring Groups statement".

"Sources close to the premier said that Paris has asserted that it was ready to play the needed role in helping Lebanon to recover Arnoun".

France said it was willing to make the needed contacts between the concerned parties to solve this issue, one source said.

"In its statement Tuesday, the five-nation group said that further contacts should take place between the parties and through bilateral channels.

"Hizbullah immediately reacted to the statement, accusing Lebanons delegate, Colonel Maher Toufeili, of being too lenient with "Israel"".

What happened that led the Lebanese delegate to agree to such wording in the statement? asked the head of Hizbullahs politburo, Nabatieh MP Mohammed Raad.

"Western Beqaa MP Sami Khatib, who met with Mr. Hoss Wednesday, said Lebanon would never accept any changes to the April Understanding.

Mr. Hoss explained to me the true interpretation of the groups statement and said that there was no mistake made by the Lebanese delegate. The premier insisted that Lebanon will not accept amending the April Understanding under any circumstances, he said.

"Government sources said that Lebanons accepting the principle that Arnoun should not have been used as a launching pad for military attacks against nearby Beaufort Castle was in compliance with Beiruts respect for the understanding, which rules out attacks from populated areas.

"But this position did not calm Hizbullah and the partys deputy secretary-general, Sheik Naim Qassem, who Wednesday reiterated the groups criticism of the government for agreeing to the statement".

The resistance has the right to use roadside bombs against "Israel". The "Israelis" have tried before to force Lebanon and the resistance to accept a freeze on using roadside bombs in return for stopping "Israeli" attacks against civilians, he said.

Sheikh Qassem blasts AUMG statement(2)
"Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem blasted the April Understanding Monitoring Group's (AUMG) statement which contained a condemnation of Islamic Resistance's (IR) roadside bombs which killed "Israeli" occupation soldiers and collaborating militiamen in the Arnoun area".

"His Eminence said he was surprised by the Lebanese delegation's stand which approved the statement.

Sheikh Qassem warned that the AUMG move could be one of the unacceptable steps towards security arrangements."

"Arnoun will remain a curse haunting the "Israelis", just like the south and western Beqaa. IR bombs and traps will always be hunting down the "Israeli" occupation forces until they understand that there is no room for them on our land and that there is nothing to keep us from pursuing them to kill their officers, soldiers and collaborators", Sheikh Qassem added.

Moreover, he hoped that the Lebanese officials will pay attention to the seriousness of what took place during the last AUMG meetings.

"We have, from the beginning, praised the government's performance, the way it deals with the Resistance and adopting it, at the official and popular levels, as means for liberation", his Eminence stated, calling on to the government to proceed with supporting the Resistance to confront the enemy, "because we all need unite and fully coordinate steps to be stronger while confronting the enemy."

Lebanese parties criticize AUMG communiqué(3)
"Lebanese parties and forces said they rejected considering the resistance's two operations near the Beaufort Castle as violation of the April Understanding, "for they had targeted the occupation forces at a military outpost (the Beaufort Castle) as has been acknowledged by the enemy media".

"The parties which held their periodical meeting today (yesterday) noted that the communiqué included contradictions".

"While the first paragraph claims that the side-bombs were detonated in Arnoun village on April 13, 1999, the second paragraph states that "Israeli" forces controlled the village on April 15, 1999, the parties' statement read.

How could the bombs be detonated in Arnoun prior to the time the occupation forces entered the village? the parties asked.

"They also noted that the AUMG communiqué did not condemn the occupation of Arnoun, but had suspicious and ambiguous wording that is in contrast with the Lebanese official stance.

Party representatives warned that the ""Israeli" enemy is trying, through imprisoning Arnoun, to amend the April Understanding."

"They hailed the resistance for the heroic operations it is carrying out against the "Israeli" occupation forces".

PSP against any "distortion" of April Understanding(4)
"Progressive Socialist Party rejected "any distortion of the April Understanding under the cover of bilateral channels or the like".

"A PSP communiqué said, "It is strange how the government is not able to realize these perils. It is also odd how the foreign policy, performance and unstable positions are committing these suspicious mistakes and then promptly clarifying them, only to repeat the same errors later."

"PSP expressed concern at "the objectives behind this policy" wondering whether "there are, within the cabinet, some US- NATO circles that want Lebanon to go back to earlier status."

"For his part, former foreign minister, MP Fares Boaez stressed that the Arnoun issue can only be solved through the Monitoring Group's mechanism without any amendment of the understanding.

"Boaez called on the cabinet to file a complaint with the UN Security Council "for Arnoun has been annexed to the occupied zone and the UN, mainly the Security Council, must undertake its international responsibility."

YOHMOR SHKEIF MAY BE "Israel's" NEXT TARGET AFTER ARNOUN(5)
"Lebanon has reaffirmed its right to resist "Israel's" occupation of south Lebanon. It has also categorically rejected "Israel's" demand to reach security arrangements in the southern village of Arnoun which was occupied on Thursday. Today (yesterday), five families abandoned their homes. They were the latest to leave the village. And this morning, residents were denied entry by "Israel's" local allies who are controlling the village and manning a checkpoint at its entrance. In Yohmor Shkief, which borders Arnoun, people are scared they may be "Israel's" next target".

" The southern village of Yohmor has never been so close to the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon. It is nestled between hills and valleys that have for long been occupied by "Israel". Yohmor is only a glance away from settlements inside "Israel". There is only one road linking the village to the rest of Lebanon. That road is no longer safe. "Israeli" forces and their local allies who recently occupied Yohmor's twin village, Arnoun, about two kilometers away, have built an encampment on the side of the road, sparking fear among Yohmor's residents, who say they could be "Israel's" next target for occupation or harassment. The village's local official, Hussein Al-Jaraf, is worried. He says he has been holding back seven families from leaving Yohmor. The men commute to neighboring towns for work, and students use the road which practically goes through Arnoun to get to their schools. Anyone traveling on that route could easily be snatched by occupation troops. Al-Jaraf also warns that "Israel" could attempt to blockade the village as it did three years ago. That he stresses would amount to a disaster, since Yohmor depends on neighboring Nabatieh for all its services and for purchasing food. Inhabitants say they starved during the last blockade. The only crop people can cultivate here is tobacco; "Israel" does not allow them to reach their farmland.

Seventy-year old Fatima, like others in Yohmor, is terrified. She describes her village as an island that is suffering. "Israel", she laments, is constantly watching over their activities. With most of Arnoun now deserted, she fears for her village's destiny. Upon hearing the news of Arnoun's occupation, Fatima said her family felt as if the home collapsed on their head. It is difficult to imagine that two hundred and thirty seven families live in Yohmor. The roads and alleys are empty. Most people feel safer inside their houses, especially since Arnoun was occupied. Life has been disrupted by developments down the road. Only one doctor treats patients at this clinic, and recently he has been reluctant to show up, fearing possible attacks on the road to Yohmor. A nurse is trying to help the local residents. But she is concerned over the increased number of sick people in the village since Arnoun's occupation. She believes they are experiencing psychological trauma. Students are showing up to this only school in the village, but many of their teachers, who commute from nearby places, have been refusing to make the now dangerous trip. There is a lot of silence in Yohmor, not many are willing to express their anxiety on camera, they fear retaliation by occupation forces. One man told us, "Israel's" local allies have lately been venturing out of the Beaufort Castle outpost. That is only exacerbating fear in an already traumatized village".

Smugglers busted in south(6)
"Customs agents seized cigarettes worth $7,200 being smuggled out of the occupied zone following a shootout near Sidon Wednesday, a security source said.

"Hassan Ajab, a resident of Mazraat al-Chouf, was arrested after being ambushed by agents who lay in wait overnight Tuesday in Wadi Bisri, a village bordering the zone".

"His attempts to resist by shooting at the agents failed when they fired back, forcing him to surrender. No casualties were reported.

"The agents confiscated 12 boxes of smuggled cigarettes, each containing 600 packets. They also took the two horses Mr. Ajab used to transport the goods. Police determined that the cigarettes had been smuggled through the port of Haifa, "Israel"".

 "Customs officials have been on the look-out for smugglers since the government announced its decision to raise taxes on imported cigarettes."

"Israeli" Aggressions(7)
The "Israeli" occupying forces staged the following aggressions on Lebanon's towns and villages:
Between 5:35 and 6:00, Mount Sojod was targeted by five 120mm shells.
Between 5:45 and 6:00, six 155mm missiles struck Mount Al-Rafih.
7:00, the perimeters of Jarjoua exposed to four 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(8)
The IR group of martyr Hussein Ibrahim Rida attacked, at 6:30, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying its western fortification. At the same time, the IR team of martyr Salah Mohammad Ghandour targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.
