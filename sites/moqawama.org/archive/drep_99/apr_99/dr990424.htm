Daily Report
 
 
April Saturday 24-1999:

IR wounds Top collaborators officer
"Israelis" fly Allouche across border for treatment
"The already demoralized South Lebanon Army* suffered another blow Friday when its notorious commander for the Jezzine area was wounded in a roadside bomb ambush that killed his driver.

"Joseph Karam, alias Allouche was in a two-car convoy on the Dar al-Ramleh Road east of Jezzine when resistance fighters detonated the device at 7:10 P.M. His driver, George Gerges, who went by the nickname of Makhoul, was killed instantly. Karam was riddled with shrapnel.

The SLA chief was flown to "Israel's" Rambam hospital".

"Another militiaman was wounded, though less seriously".

"Hizbullah claimed responsibility for the attack, disclosing that its secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, had personally ordered his fighters to liquidate senior collaborators".

"The attack dealt a severe blow to the SLA, which has been increasingly plagued by defections. It also shattered a two-month lull in the Jezzine area which had seen many of the towns natives who reside outside the occupied zone visiting, especially around Easter".

"Fridays attack was also a setback for "Israel's" attempts to reduce its own military presence in the occupied zone and use the SLA as a substitute.

"It was the third attempt to kill Allouche, who was put in charge of the so-called 20th regiment last August, replacing the dismissed Emile Nasr, who was accused of cowardice in the face of resistance attacks".

"He escaped unscathed in a September assault, and suffered a minor wound in January.

"Defections picked up in January, when 17 militiamen from the village of Sfaray refused to serve in the militias positions, despite threats by Allouche, forcing the SLA to evacuate seven villages east of Jezzine"(1)

Earlier, the IR team of martyr Hussein Ghassani attacked, at 6:10, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Meanwhile,, the IR squad of martyr Ghassan Ghanem was targeting Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking it from several sides(2)

"Israeli" occupation wounds three Lebanese civilians(3)
Yesterday, three civilians were wounded when "Israeli" forces shelled the village of Kfar Tibnit. A woman and two men were wounded when shells fell near a Lebanese Army checkpoint in the village.

"Khairiyeh Nahle, 60; Salim Younes, 70; and Mohammed Younes, 35, were taken to hospitals in Nabatieh. There were no reports of resistance attacks prior to the Kfar Tibnit attack".

"Security sources also told The Daily Star that "Israeli" forces were building a barrier on the main road in Arnoun to separate it from the rest of the country".

Theyve set up several half-meter-high cement blocks, a source said. And theyve taken over an area north of the public school, possibly to use it to check vehicles entering the occupied zone.

The "Israeli" Army has also built cement fortifications on the roof of Nasser Alawiyehs home near its new position inside Arnoun, the source added.

"Meanwhile, "Israeli" warplanes fired at least four missiles at the outskirts of Yater, east of Tyre, security sources said. The raid targeted a valley between the villages of Zibqine and Jabal al-Botom".
