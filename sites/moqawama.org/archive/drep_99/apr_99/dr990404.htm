Daily Report
 
 
April Sunday 4-1999:

Large changes in "Israeli" movements follow theft of Gerstein's office(1)
"The office of former "Israeli" liaison commander Gen Erez Gerstein in the Marjayoun barracks was stolen three weeks ago, a report from the occupied zone said.

"The well-organized and delicate theft of $1,300,000 along with special classified documents belonging to the liaison unit and the Lahd militia, plus "Israeli" occupation army's plans, was discovered by the liaison command 24 hours after it took place, the report which was published by as-Safir daily today added.

"It also said the "Israeli" command in the occupied zone and the so-called "Israeli" northern command accused a number of senior Lahd militia officers who work in the Marjayoun barracks in addition to an "Israeli" officer that used to be an assistant of Gerstein of involvement in the theft.

The former liaison unit commander, the highest ranking officer of the occupation forces, was killed in an Islamic Resistance operation in Markaba last February.

"An "Israeli" force from the northern command sealed off the Marjayoun barracks for around 48 hours. Those working in the barracks were meticulously searched, and around 80 militiamen were interrogated, including a senior officer whose last name is Rizq," the report said.

"A special inquiry panel, including officers from the northern area command and the enemy general staff was established to investigate the incident."

"The report stated that the long series of investigations ended up in arresting three militia officers of the town of Qliaa from the families of Mubarak and Sa'ayyed, in addition to an "Israeli" officer. They were taken to a prison inside occupied Palestine for more investigations.

The thieves accurately knew the safe that was inside Gerstein's office and the way to open it. They seized the opportunity of the state of anxiety and chaos that prevailed in the liaison unit's HQ following the killing of Gerstein and his assistants. They were able to finish their job accurately and secretly, the report said.

""Israeli" command fears that the stolen classified documents fall in the hands of Hezbollah; therefore, it has conducted alteration in the ranks of "Israeli" and Lahd militia officers, canceling many military missions inside and outside the zone," the report added.

"Moreover, the new liaison unit commander Elie Amitai, who was appointed a week ago, was assigned to choose a new command and conduct alteration in the ranks of the Lahdi officers who work under his command, besides putting new substitute plans for search operations, patrols, and confrontations in the occupied zone", the report concluded.

Sheikh Halawi stresses: agents' disowning sacred(2)
"MP Faysal al-Dawood quoted Sheikh Aref Halawi, the Druze spiritual leader, as saying that religious disowning of the "Israeli" occupation's agents is as sacred as prayer, adding that breaching the al-Bayyada Sheikhs' disowning is a breach of the Druze religion, and Arabic and Islamic values.

"The MP was speaking at a rally held in the town of Aiha (Rashaya District) in solidarity with the al-Bayyada Sheikhs. The ceremony was attended by Druze religious leaders besides political, social and educational figures".

"Al-Dawood also called for continuing the struggle until liberation is achieved. He urged those who collaborate with the enemy "to recover their Arab and national roots, come out of the disgrace quagmire, and repent before it is too late."

Monthly report on "Israeli" aggressions for March 1999(3)
"Following the successive defeats in the field at the hands of the resistance men in Berket Jabbour and Hasbayya and the liberation of the town of Arnoun by Lebanese students, the "Israeli" enemy has escalated its aggressions against Lebanese liberated areas and civilian residents. "Israeli" suppressive measures victimized several villages and towns in the occupied zone".

"After the failure of the "Israeli" maneuver to contain the Arnoun defeat and turn the town into a barricade in the face of Resistance men as a new established fact, the enemy entered the town and dynamited two of its houses".

"The occupation forces also intensified their arbitrary practices in the occupied zone, sealing off the towns of Hasbayya, Kawkaba, Ain Qinia, Ibl al-Saqi, and Aitaroun and imposing a curfew on Ibl al-Saqi, Ain Qinia and Hasbayya.

"These practices were confronted by a courageous decision taken by the Khalwat al-Bayyda Sheikhs who prohibited collaboration with the occupation, thus confusing the enemy and its collaborators who released some of the detainees arrested earlier.

"There were 122 "Israeli" air and ground aggressions in the month of March. Aerial aggressions constituted 25,4%, including 9% as mock air raids, and 7,4% hovering, in comparison with the 30,9% air aggressions in February 1999.

"Enemy warplanes staged 11 raids on areas in the south and the western Beqaa.

"On March 5, they rocketed the area between Jbal al-Botom and Zibqeen, injuring Loai Mohammed Bzei, 12, in Zibqeen. The boy panicked and fell off a wall.

The enemy raided the same area on March 15 and 17. It also fired missiles on the woods of Shaitieh town, Mrs. Wasila Shibli was wounded and some houses were damaged as a result of the aggression.

On March 19, "Israeli" aircraft attacked the Abu Rashed area, woods of al-Qatrani, al-Sraireh, and Berket Jabbour (western Beqaa)".

"Enemy warplanes on March 22 raided Mount al-Rafieh, the Sojod woods, Mleta (Iqlim al-Touffah), Mount Abu Rashed, and al-Qantrani (western Beqaa)".

"On March 24, enemy warplanes staged a series of raids on the mid and western sectors; the area east of Zawtar al-Gharbieh; the hilltops between al-Lwaizeh and Mleikh; and the area between Zibqeen and Jbal al-Botom.

"The most prominent was the enemy's raid on a Lebanese army checkpoint in Wadi Jahanam (Rashia) which injured one officer and three soldiers.

"On March 16 and 17, "Israeli" enemy bombarded the town of Haddatha with two 120mm rounds damaging a number of houses and a civilian car. The enemy artillery also fired three 120mm rounds on the village of Zillaya and damaged a number of houses.

Moreover, occupation forces targeted with shells the people of Mleikh as they were on their way to return to their hometown that has been deserted for years. The residents had to postpone the return.
"On the other hand, enemy forces sealed off the occupied town of Aitaroun, leaving only one of its entrances open. "Israeli" troops barred the residents from leaving the town.

Enemy also besieged the towns of Hasbayya, Ibl as-Saqi and Ain Qinia as a collective punishment for what the occupation army called the villages working with "enemy security parties".

"The blockade of Ibl as-Saqi has been going on for over eleven days now. Enemy forces have arrested 8 of the town's residents and barred the Bayyada Sheikhs from traveling through the Zimrayya crossing".

"The enemy questioned 40 residents in the area, and later released some of them, moving the rest to the al-Khiam detention center and "Israeli" prisons in occupied Palestine.

Occupation forces on March 14 detained Khalil Ibrahim Youness from Sheheen after storming the town.

"Furthermore, the enemy on March 8 expelled noncommissioned officer in Internal Security Forces Zaid Youssef Srairi after detaining him for 13 months. They also deported ISF corporal Hatem Jaber Hadaifah, 32, of Qinia on March 15. Eight days later "Israelis" expelled the family of Ghattas Swaidi of the Araya town (Jezzine District). On March 26 the enemy deported Youssef Melhem al-Khouri from the Ain Zhalta town".

Military Operations(5)
The IR team of martyrs Youssef Bzeih and Moussa Salman attacked, at 13:20, Shamaa position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and accurate goals. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Khodor Khiami and Ahmad Ossaili targeted Hardoun outpost with the appropriate arms scoring direct and definite hits.

However, a team of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) targeted, at 6:30, enemy movements at Qsair position with the proper arms. Definite goals were scored, the fighters reported.
