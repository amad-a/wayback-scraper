Daily Report
 
 
April Saturday 17-1999:

Introduction
Dozens of "Israeli" soldiers and collaborator militiamen backed by bulldozers, tractors and trucks worked throughout the night erecting barbed wires, digging trenches and placing obstacles on roads leading to the mostly-deserted village. A cameraman for a Gulf television station was wounded on Friday by a rubber bulled fired by "Israeli" occupation troops at a group of journalists who tried to approach the village. In the meantime, "Israel's" re-annexation of Arnoun provoked local and international criticism.

"ISRAEL" OCCUPIES ARNOUN(1)
" The southern village of Arnoun has been occupied by "Israeli" troops and their local allies. Lebanon has demanded the international community intervene to force "Israel" to end what it called its dangerous invasion of Arnoun. But "Israel" threatened severe consequences if any attempt is made to interfere with what it called its new policy in Lebanon. Hizbullah for its part warned the Jewish state it would reap the repercussions. This morning, "Israeli" forces shot and wounded a Lebanese cameraman who was among journalists taking footage at the entrance of the village".

" It was the second time in less than two months that many of the residents of the southern village of Arnoun had to pack their belongings and leave. "Israeli" troops and their local allies barricaded the village with barbed wire fences and earth embankments preventing cars from entering. The road leading to the center of Arnoun was also closed. "Israel" denied Lebanese charges that it annexed Arnoun and incorporated it into the zone it occupies in the south. But never before did pedestrians have to pass through a checkpoint manned by "Israeli" troops and their local allies - measures applied at other border crossings between the occupied zone and government held territory. Some 20 "Israeli" soldiers and members of their proxy militia are now deployed in the village and are controlling the movements of residents. Reporters converged at the village's entrance. And it was very clear that the "Israelis" and their allies did not want us there. They roared their tanks and smoke billowed from their checkpoint. Then using a megaphone they told us to leave. And suddenly, they started to fire smoke bombs and tear gas canisters in our direction. We were forced to get into our cars after choking from the gases that were emitted. Then they fired rubber bullets and a soundman working for Abu Dhabi television was shot in the back. Kassem Durgham was taken to hospital in the nearby village of Nabatiyeh were he underwent minor surgery. The plastic bullet was removed from his back. Kassem Durgham said that: we were standing near the checkpoint when they opened fire at us. If I die here not scared it is my land, we are not hurting them, they are hurting us, they are paying a high price for occupying our land. "Israel" is threatening the Lebanese that any attempt to counter what it called the security operation in Arnoun will be met with severe consequences. The warning appeared to prevent any attempt to liberate the village. Arnoun was sealed off last February but Lebanese demonstrators tore down the fence a week later. "Israel" said the village's seizure aimed at preventing Lebanese resistance guerrillas from using it to launch attacks. A few days ago, a Hizbullah resistance attack killed an "Israeli" soldier near Beaufort castle. That "Israeli" position overlooks the village. But the ambush did not violate a cease-fire understanding that permits resistance attacks against "Israeli" occupation troops. The annexation of the village did not come as a surprise. "Israel" had threatened through diplomatic channels that it would retake the village if Lebanon did not deploy army troops and prevent resistance attacks. Beirut had rejected the "Israeli" demand on the grounds that it could not provide an occupying force with security. Only weeks before "Israeli" elections, the Likud government is trying to show the electorate that it is doing something to ensure the security of its troops in south Lebanon. Also by annexing Arnoun, it is trying to set a precedent - by obtaining security guarantees from Lebanon in exchange for a withdrawal. But Lebanon has repeatedly said it would not reach any security arrangements with "Israel" nor will it negotiate a withdrawal. It wants "Israel" to pullback unconditionally in line with UN resolution 425.

Lebanon contacted the ambassadors of France and the United States in Beirut to protest against "Israel's" action and the Hizbullah resistance movement warned the Jewish state it would reap the consequences. And early in this afternoon, Israseli warplanes flew at low altitude over Beirut, breaking the sound barrier".

Iron-fist policy of renewed occupation signals "Israelis" intend to stay this time(2)
"The iron-fist policy of "Israel's" renewed occupation of Arnoun was dramatically demonstrated shortly after dawn Friday when South Lebanon Army* militiamen entrenched in a new outpost at the entrance to the village opened fire at reporters, wounding a television sound engineer.

"The presence of the bulldozed earth position manned by the SLA, along with an extensive network of barbed wire and trenches at the entrance of the village, indicates that, this time, the "Israelis" plan to keep Arnoun firmly under their control.

"Any notion that the incursion was a limited action was swiftly dispelled by the pugnacious tone of the "Israeli" Army, which warned against any interference with "Israel's" activities inside the village.

Any attempt to impede the security operation carried out by the "Israeli" Army and the South Lebanon Army in Arnoun, especially by endangering the lives of civilians, will be met with a very firm response, an "Israeli" Army spokesman said.

"Israel" calls on village residents and all Lebanese to follow these instructions to avoid putting the security of innocent people in danger, he said.

"The first reporters arrived shortly after dawn at the Lebanese Army checkpoint in Kfar Tibnit, 1.5 kilometers northwest of Arnoun, with vague reports that "Israeli" soldiers were still deployed at the entrance of the village further down the road.

"With the sun slowly climbing into a deep blue sky above Mount Hermon in the east, and warnings to be careful from the soldiers at the checkpoint, a convoy of reporters slowly drove up to Arnoun.

On descending the hill to the entrance of the village, it became clear that the "Israelis" have no intention of leaving the village soon.

"The road which had been constructed and asphalted with such haste following Arnouns liberation six weeks ago had been destroyed. At least two trenches, each a meter deep, had been dug across the road, severing at the same time the recently installed water pipes. Rows of barbed wire had been laid for a length of 150 meters across the front of the village and to a depth of about 30 meters.

"But most forbidding of all was the new outpost of bulldozed soil rammed up against the first house in the village. From behind the earthen ramparts, the silhouettes of SLA militiamen were clearly visible against the rising sun.

"Unlike "Israel's" last attempt to annex Arnoun, this time access to and from the village has been arranged via a narrow dirt path that winds through the barbed wire.

"A group of villagers huddled in the distance, not daring to venture en masse past the post.
But individuals were permitted to pass through.

"One young woman, clearly nervous and unwilling to give her name, carried her baby through the barricades. Everyone wants to leave, she said climbing into a taxi. Its impossible here now. Im leaving the country Monday.

"The militiamen, observing the proceedings, announced over a loudspeaker that the reporters had five minutes to vacate the area.

"More villagers passed through the fence, some with their belongings bundled together.
Said Alawieh, 100, bent double with age, staggered into view helped along by his wife, Kamleh, 70.

The couple struggled across the uneven path before being defeated by one of the trenches.

With the old man feebly trying to clamber up the other side, a reporter raised his hands in the air for the benefit of the watching militiamen and walked forward to help him.

In a tremulous voice, Mr. Alawieh said: We built Arnoun, we have always lived here, but now we are finished.

"His wife said that she was taking him to Nabatieh and that she would join her husband in two or three days.

"The five minutes were up, the SLA announced through the loudspeaker and hurled smoke grenades at the reporters. But the billowing clouds of smoke proved an attractive sight to capture on film and failed to disperse the journalists.

"A few minutes later, the militiaman barked a final warning: Go to your cars. Get out now.
A militiaman opened fire with his rifle and the reporters hurried for cover as bullets whipcracked inches away from them.

"Qassem Dergham, a sound engineer with Abu Dhabi television, was struck in the back with what was said to be a plastic bullet.

Mr. Dergham, who was shot twice during the civil war, was treated in a Nabatieh hospital for his wound, which was not said to be serious.

"The SLA announced that anyone approaching Arnoun from then on would be shot.

With tension remaining high, the Lebanese Army stopped cars from traveling down the road to Arnoun and beyond to Yohmor.

"With Arnoun annexed into the zone and the SLA controlling the road running past the village, Yohmor now faces an uncertain future. Unlike Arnoun, Yohmor has a population in the hundreds. Its residents, despite living on the front line, have, by and large, been spared the rigors shared by most villages facing the zone".

MP Raad: IR will retaliate for Arnoun occupation(3)
"The "Israeli" reoccupation of Arnoun and attempt to annex the town to the occupied zone is a real aggression and blatant breach of the April Understanding, the Head of Hizbullah's political council MP Hajj Mohammed Raad said.

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) will retaliate in accordance with a program and a timing set by IR alone, MP Raad stated, adding that IR's retaliation will be more painful and pressurizing.

He pointed out that this new aggression comes as "Israeli" prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu attempts to reinforce his election campaign".

"The "Israeli" occupation army is trying to adopt a new tactic through occupying Arnoun. However, the Mujahedeen have their own adequate tactics to confront each and every new measure taken by the enemy", Raad said.

Resistance will not stop, Hizbullah affirms(4)
"Commenting on the "Israeli" re-occupation of the town of Arnoun, Hizbullah stressed that "the Islamic Resistance is proceeding with its Jihad method of confronting the unjust occupation, armed first with faith in Allah and second with the wide popular embrace and the steadfast and resisting national official stand."

"Our constant response will be continued operations along the occupied territories of our dear country, in all outposts, without discrimination and whenever accurate field information for successful operations is available," a Hizbullah communiqué read.

"The party hailed the "steadfast official stand which refused to subdue to the occupation's desire and pressures, while scoring a wonderful patriotic position when the suspicious US mediation, which aimed to serve the Zionist enemy and its occupation, was rejected."

Hoss: Arnoun's occupation is a terrorist operation par excellence(5)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss noted that the "re-occupation of Arnoun is a terrorist operation par excellence aimed at the innocent residents of this peaceful town."

"We refuse this situation; and we are working once again to drive the "Israeli" occupation out of Arnoun with all possible means", al-Hoss said.

"The premier contacted the concerned countries, especially the US and France. He also dispatched a letter to the UN secretary General Kofi Annan and asked him to distribute it to the Security Council members states".

"Furthermore, Mr. Hoss charged Lebanese ambassador to Washington with contacting the US administration and discussing the issue with US officials in order to exert pressure on the "Israelis" to pull out of Arnoun".

"The Premier hoped that the "Israeli" escalation would be halted and that the "Israeli" entity would reverse its occupation as soon as possible.

"Commenting on the Qana massacre's third anniversary, the Premier said "Three years ago the civilized world was shocked by the "Israeli" brutality practiced against the peaceful town of Qana."

"More than one hundred children, women and elderly people were torn to pieces while hiding inside a UNIFIL position, for they thought that the United Nation's flag could protect them from "Israeli" barbarism, Mr. al-Hoss stated.

"However, al-Hoss added, ""Israel"" proved them wrong and provided a new evidence of its aggression and disrespect of the UN, the international community and the different international conventions and committees".

"Al-Hoss said the massacre's martyrs will always be torches that light the way for the Lebanese in their confrontation with the "Israeli" occupation.

"Their immaculate blood will always quench the thirst of spirits eager for liberation", the premier concluded.

TEHRAN CONDEMNS ARNOUN OCCUPATION(6)
"Iran meanwhile condemned the annexation of Arnoun. It said the move showed that "Israel" is the source of insecurity in the region. Tehran stressed the need for the international community to adopt more serious measures against Tel Aviv. Iran reaffirmed its support for the Lebanese resistance against "Israeli" occupation".

Villages annexation meets with protests except U.S(7)
"The United States stepped in quickly Friday to discourage Lebanon from taking the case of Arnouns re-occupation to the United Nations, while France decried the new aggression that has catapulted the south once again to the forefront of regional politics.

"President Emile Lahoud discussed the developments in the south with the army commander, General Michel Suleiman, while Prime Minister Salim Hoss protested the re-invasion of the village, calling it a terrorist aggression par excellence.

Diplomatic sources disclosed that the U.S. State Department had informed Lebanons Ambassador in Washington, Farid Abboud, that it would be better to refer the matter to the Monitoring Group of the 1996 April Understanding.

This was the answer that Mr. Abboud got from the State Department when he asked the U.S. administration for help in bringing "Israeli" forces to evacuate from Arnoun, a senior Foreign Ministry source told The Daily Star.

"In Washington, State Department spokesman James Rubin called on "Israel" and the resistance to avoid escalation or provocative acts, adding that the attack on Arnoun was precisely the kind of issue the Monitoring Group was set up to deal with.

Lebanon heeded the U.S. advice and filed a complaint to the group, requesting an urgent meeting of the panel".

"The Foreign Ministry source said that the annexation of Arnoun 56 days after it was liberated by students was a challenge to the United States and France which asked Lebanon two months ago not to file any complaint to the UN Security Council against "Israel"".

The meeting of the Monitoring Group on Monday will determine its ability to force "Israel" to withdraw from Arnoun, a source said.

"The Lebanese delegation to the panel filed two complaints over "Israel's" occupation of the southern village and the shooting of Qassem Dergham, a Lebanese sound engineer for Abu Dhabi television.
Lebanons mission at the United Nations asked UN chief Kofi Annan to help end the dangerous invasion".

Elsewhere, France expressed disappointment over "Israel's" occupation of the Arnoun.
Were disappointed at this fresh military operation which comes as talks on the Arnoun situation are continuing between various parties, said French Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Anne Gazeau-Secret.

We hope that all measures will be taken to ease the tension, she added.

Saudi Arabia condemned the act, calling on the international community to help stop "Israeli" acts of aggression, the official Saudi Press Agency reported.

"The agency quoted an official source as inviting the international community to pressure "Israel" into halting its persistent violations of south Lebanons sovereignty, set a limit to the Jewish states continuous arrogance and aggression, and end its cynical infractions, it added.

"Meanwhile, a stream of protests against the annexation of Arnoun flowed, with the leader of the Jamaa al-Islamiya in Lebanon calling on the government to expel U.S. Ambassador David Satterfield from Lebanon for his suspect activities and the problems he has created in the country.

Mr. Satterfield has used the problems from which several Lebanese regions are suffering to promote U.S. policy in the Middle East. He has become a messenger for "Israel" who informs the government that it should refrain from filing any complaint against "Israel" to the UN Security Council, Sheik Maher Hammoud said.

He informed the government of the "Israeli" intentions concerning Arnoun during a meeting that lasted only a few minutes without showing any respect of protocol, he said.

Solidarity sit-in with Shibaa residents calls on collaborators to repent(8)
"Al-Arqoub and Shibaa non-resident people staged a sit-in this morning at the Press Syndicate Headquarters in solidarity with their fellow countrymen who had been expelled from the town by the "Israeli" occupation forces".

"Many political figures spoke during the sit-in shedding light on the suffering of the occupied zone's residents, especially the ones in Shibaa".

"Speeches focused on the "Israeli" occupation's aggressive campaign against the area which is aimed at emptying it from its people, stressing that the ongoing residents' uprising expresses a state of maturity and the resolve of our people to fight the occupation".

"Moreover, participants called on collaborators to repent and to turns their guns towards the enemy soldiers chests."

"They also called for dismantling the collaborating militia and endorsing the draft law of pardoning collaborators who repent".

"The law was presented to the parliament by Hizbullah's Faithfulness to the Resistance parliamentary bloc in a bid to encourage militiamen to give up collaboration with the enemy".

If passed, the draft grants amnesty to deserters of the collaborating militia.

"We don't dissociate the situation in Arnoun and Shibaa from the US. We will not deal with the "Israeli" enemy or the US but with force," the participants concluded.
