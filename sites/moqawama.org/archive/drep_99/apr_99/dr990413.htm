Daily Report
 
 
April Tuesday 13-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants made a new qualitative accomplishment as an "Israeli" soldier was killed and several others wounded in a powerful roadside bomb near Beaufort Castle which overlooks the partially occupied village of Arnoun. The casualties raise the number of "Israeli" enemy soldiers perished in South Lebanon this year to eight.

Elsewhere, the "Israeli" occupying troops and their agents have deported three children from the occupied village of Shibaa in southern Lebanon.

Meanwhile, the first National Conference for Supporting Resistance and Enhancing Steadfastness (NCSR) was concluded yesterday at the UNESCO Hall in Beirut following two days of discussions.

IR kills at least one "Israeli" soldier and wounds others
In a new qualitative accomplishment, after the "Israeli" complicated precautionary measures and under the enemy's continued threatens, the IR mujahedeen carried out a new breakthrough and reached to 100 meters only from the Beaufort Castle. So, the IR group of martyrs Haitham Faqih and Hussein Ateih detonated, at 16:25, a powerful explosive device as an "Israeli" foot-patrol accompanied by a M-113 military vehicle inflicting a number of casualties among its rank. The "Israeli" soldiers, who suffered a panic state, were seen evacuating the casualties. Also, the "Israeli" gunners shelled intensely the scene. For its part, AFP asserted that one "Israeli" soldier was killed and others wounded.

Earlier, the IR squad of martyrs Haitham Mohammad Faqih and Moussa Diab Yehia attacked, at 16:10, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and certain hits. At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Abdallah Moghnieh and Adnan Mohammad Hassan targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals(1)

For its part, Jerusalem Post reported "St.-Sgt. Noam Barnea, 21, of Holon, was killed yesterday afternoon in a roadside bomb attack near Beaufort Castle in the security zone".

"Barnea, of the Combat Engineers Corps, was at the head of a foot patrol that had left the IDF* outpost at the Crusader fortress to carry out a routine search-and-sweep operation.

"The bomb was detonated alongside him. Military sources said the fact that the soldiers had maintained a safe distance from one another helped prevent further casualties.

"The bombing that killed Barnea occurred about one kilometer from the Beaufort Castle outpost not far from Arnoun village. The village was fenced off several weeks ago to prevent Hizbullah gunmen using deserted homes there as launching pads for attacks on troops.

"The action created an outcry in Lebanon and the fences were subsequently breached by Lebanese protesters during demonstrations at the site. The barricades around the village were not reerected.

"Aviv Bushinsky, spokesman for Prime Minister Binyamin Netanyahu, said the cabinet was meeting last night to discuss the government's strategy in Lebanon. Sources added that ministers were debating what retaliatory action to take.

"A senior military intelligence officer said yesterday, before the bombing, that Hizbullah had renewed its strategy, after a lull, of attacking IDF and SLA** soldiers. The officer was briefing the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee.

Another senior security source said last night that the IDF views the use of Arnoun by Hizbullah as a "very grave matter indeed."

"Barnea was also the first casualty since the bombing six weeks ago which claimed the lives of Brig.-Gen. Erez Gerstein, head of the Liaison Unit to southern Lebanon, his driver, his radio operator, and "Israel" Radio reporter Ilan Roeh.

"Hizbullah, it appeared, eased its operations somewhat after those deaths, apparently to avoid provoking widespread retaliation, and concentrated instead on long-range mortar attacks on IDF and SLA outposts."

"Barnea's death brought to eight the number of "Israelis" killed in south Lebanon so far this year.

Members of the Four Mothers movement, which is pressing for the IDF to be pulled out of Lebanon as quickly as possible, met with President Ezer Weizman at Beit Hanassi yesterday.

"Chairwoman Rahel Ben-Dor said a number of ideas were raised on ways to end the "tragic war." It was agreed beforehand not to release any more details of the talks, although the delegates said the atmosphere was very warm".

"Ben-Dor said the meeting with Weizman was one of several between movement activists and the country's leaders and politicians. Four Mothers has also asked for a meeting with Defense Minister Moshe Arens to clarify his recent statements about plans to reduce the number of IDF troops in the
security zone"(2)

"ISRAEL" EXPELS THREE CHILDREN FROM SHEBAA(3)
""Israeli" occupation forces have expelled three children from the occupied village of Shebaa in southern Lebanon. "Israel" has expelled some 25 people from Shebaa, since village residents started what has been dubbed as an uprising against occupation troops and their local allies".

" "Israeli" occupation forces expelled three children from this village. Shebaa lies inside the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon, close to the internationally-recognized border with "Israel".

Some three thousand residents remain in Shebaa, compared to more than 40,000 prior to "Israel's" occupation of southern Lebanon 21 years ago. The parents of the three children expelled, were also exiled from the zone last week. "Israel" has been expelling the families and relatives of its allied militiamen who defected to government-controlled territories recently. Some 25 residents have already been expelled. Shebaa residents say an uprising against "Israeli" occupation troops and their local allies is underway in the village. The elderly and spiritual figures in Shebaa have been calling on allied militiamen to defect from the "Israeli"-financed and controlled militia. So far 12 militiamen have deserted the militia this year. "Israeli" forces and their local allies have been storming and searching homes in the villages, and interrogating the remaining residents. They have also placed restrictions on their movement. The Imam of Shebaa today (yesterday) issued a statement reaffirming that village residents would continue their disobedience campaign and "uprising" against occupation troops. He had reiterated a call on village residents enlisted in the "Israeli"-allied militia to defect. He also stressed the village will reject any form of "normalization" with occupation troops. The militia today (yesterday) prevented the mufti of Hasbaya and Marjayoun, Sheikh Mustapha Ghader from entering Shebaa. He was banned from the zone eight years ago. Sheikh Ghader was sent by the Mufti of the Republic, Sheikh Mohammed Rashid Qabbani, to inspect the situation of villagers. Shebaa residents met UN peacekeepers in the village and asked them to stop occupation troops and their local allies
from entering Shebaa and harassing the residents".

CONFUSION OVER ARENS'S DECLARATION ON REDUCING SOUTH LEBANON TROOPS(4)
""Israel's" Defense (War) Minister Moshe Arens's declared intention to reduce the number of soldiers in occupied southern Lebanon is continuing to make waves. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu clarified that he was involved in fashioning the policy change, while the army continued to wonder what policy change everyone was talking about".

" Arens called for the reduction of "Israeli" forces in the occupation zone in south Lebanon to minimize the dangers they are exposed to. Arens also said he was looking at handing over "Israeli" posts in the zone to the "Israeli" allied militia. But he stressed his proposal was not a unilateral retreat.
"Israeli" President Ezer Weizman said that he has the impression that Arens was not in step with the army when announcing the change. For his part, the chief of staff Lieutenant General Shaul Mofaz was asked for his position. Mofaz said the army has over the past few months been assessing the situation in Lebanon. This he said has led them to the direction of decreasing the permanent presence in the outposts in order to reduce the exposure of soldiers and increase their mobile operations. The chief of staff avoided giving a direct answer to the question of whether the army has received a directive from the defense (war) minister regarding a cutback in troop deployment in Lebanon.

Senior army sources repeated yesterday that there has been no recent change in policy, but the army has already been reducing the number of soldiers positioned in Lebanese outposts for a number of months. Other measures aimed at reducing "Israeli" casualties include a sharp decrease in the number of "Israeli" convoys, handing over some outposts to their local allies and some supply routes to Lebanese contractors"

NCSR announces birth of National Committee for Supporting Resistance(5)
"The first National Conference for Supporting Resistance and Enhancing Steadfastness (NCSR) was concluded today (today) at the UNESCO Hall in Beirut following two days of discussions.

"The conference came out with a political paper which emphasized that the Resistance is a legitimate duty and that the Zionist entity is dangerous to the world and humanity.

"The "Israeli" entity poses a certain and continuos threat to Lebanon, which found the whole world inattentive to implement international resolutions until the Resistance revived the world's memory, the NCSR paper said.

"NCSR called on the Lebanese government to review the situation of its Resistance men and steadfast people in the occupied zone and to supply them with steadfastness means, especially in front-line areas, in addition to embracing the martyrs' families and taking action to free all detainees from enemy prisons".

"Moreover, the NCSR included eight essential working plans which will be implemented by the new National Committee for Supporting the Resistance that was established by the conference.

"The National committee includes representatives of: political parties, religious leaders, vocational, labor and artistic syndicates, organizations, cultural sectors, youth movements, writer's union, cultural clubs, women's organizations, the media and other public committees.

"Furthermore, the endorsed eight working plans to support the resistance are: the roles of expatriation, youth, media, state, economic support, Arabs, in addition to the legal and cultural roles".

"The NCSR recommendations were announced by the head of Hezbollah's political council, MP Hajj Mohammed Raad who commended president Emile Lahoud's sponsorship of the conference".
