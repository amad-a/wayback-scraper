Daily Report
 
 
April Friday 23-1999:

Introduction
Parliamentary Foreign Affaire Committee underlined that the roadside bombs detonated by the Islamic Resistance (IR) men against the "Israeli" soldiers near Beaufort Castle were not in violation of the April Understanding.

In this regard, the Chief of Lebanese Army General Michel Suleiman said that the Army will never be guard for the occupation.

Foreign Affairs Committee against Lebanese Army deployment in Arnoun(1)
"Parliaments Foreign Affairs Committee said Thursday that Lebanons position on "Israel" was clear and involved no changes".

Lebanese officials have clarified our position on "Israel" and said that Lebanon wont be dragged into any form of bilateral talks with "Israel", and that the Lebanese Army will not protect "Israeli" forces or prevent the resistance from staging attacks against them, said the committees chairman, Tyre MP Ali Khalil.

The committee also discussed the ambiguity in the Monitoring Groups statement concerning the condemnation of a resistance attack against "Israeli" forces in Arnoun, and considering it a violation of the April Understanding. This isnt true. The attack wasnt a violation of the agreement, he said.

Army chief stresses rejecting and resisting occupation(2)
""Israeli" intimidation with repeated aggressions and the annexation of Arnoun to the occupied zone will not force the (Lebanese) army to abandon its position of rejecting and resisting the occupation, nor will the enemy's enticement proposals over partial and unreal withdrawals, Army chief Gen Michel Suleiman said today (yesterday).

"The army will never be guard for the occupation, Gen. Suleiman said during a tour in which he inspected the army units deployed in Metn and Kesrwan".

"The "Israeli" enemy's occupation of more territories will never provide it with security, he said.

Coordination with the Syrian army is underway at defense and security levels in confronting the "Israeli" army".

Occupation detains two residents(3)
""Israeli" occupation forces stormed into the towns of Yareen and Merwaheen in the western sector of the occupied zone".

"The enemy troops abducted Abdallah Mohammed Obeid, 55, and Hassan Ali Saif, 27, and took them to the notorious al-Khiam detention center."

"Sources said Obeid had been wounded in different parts of his body when Saed Hadad's militiamen (who collaborated with "Israeli" occupation) fired shots at him in the year 1975."

"Occupation forces had also detained Hassan Saif's brother, Atef 20, ten days ago. He was also taken to al-Khiam prison".

A commemoration was held for the detainees(4)
"The friends and relatives of Lebanese held in "Israeli" prisons gathered Thursday to mark 20 years since Samir Kintar was put behind "Israeli" bars."

"To a large audience, Mr. Kintars brother Bassam read a letter from Nafha Prison written by his brother Samir, who is the longest-serving Arab detainee in an "Israeli" prison.

I was kept for days under the sun and nights standing up with my hands fastened to the wall, and my head was covered with a black bag that smelt putrid, the letter read.

I used to imagine approaching a stream of water, I was so thirsty. But I wasnt given water for days, Samir Kintar added.
He recalled that his "Israeli" jailers placed loudspeakers next to his head with noise so loud that it hurt his ears.

"The letter said when he was detained in 1979, he had seven bullets embedded in his body, all removed without anesthetic.

During the operation, his mouth was taped so that he could not disturb the "Israeli" doctors.

And every time I was taken to the infirmary for a check-up the doctor would prod my bullet wounds with his finger, putting me in great pain, he said.

At Unesco Palace in Beirut, where the commemorations were held, Post and Telecommunications Minister Issam Naaman called for international recognition of the poor conditions under which detainees are held.

"There are at least 150 Lebanese in Khiam detention center, which is run by the "Israeli"-backed South Lebanon Army, and in "Israeli" prisons.

"Mr. Naaman said on behalf of President Emile Lahoud that the UN should take action against the human rights violations. He asked the UN to form a committee to inspect the prisons in "Israel" and report on the detainees treatment.

"A UN committee should visit ailing prisoners to see if they were receiving adequate treatment and to find out the cause of detainee deaths. He specifically mentioned the detention of the very young and the elderly.

"Baalbek-Hermel MP Ammar Musawi, representing Hizbullahs secretary-general Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, said that the Lebanese media could do far more to support the detainees and the resistance.

Most media outlets cover the matter with simple reports that tend to be confined to special occasions like this one. The excuse they give is that they dont want to keep reminding people of the pain, said Mr. Musawi, who added that it was important not to forget.

For the first time, politicians are campaigning with proposals for a quick withdrawal from the south, the MP said.

"The head of the Follow-Up Committee for the Support of the Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons, Mohammed Safa, said the plight of those detained should be at the forefront of the political agenda for international diplomacy and foreign policy".

"He praised a Cabinet decision to print postal stamps carrying the names of the detainees.

"Reiterating his frequent call for government bodies to give jobs to newly released detainees, Mr. Safa thanked Ogero, the telephone repairs and maintenance body, for recruiting 75 men."

"But he reminded the Electricity and Water Resources Minister, Suleiman Traboulsi, that he had not yet fulfilled his promise to employee another 50 detainees at Electricite du Liban"

University students rebuffed at checkpoint(5)
"University students Thursday attempted to reclaim the southern village of Arnoun from "Israeli" occupation but were turned back by a Lebanese Army checkpoint to avoid a massacre.

The students action came amid continuing political efforts to solicit international pressure to force an "Israeli" withdrawal.

"Approaching in three cars and a van, the 50 students were intercepted at the last checkpoint outside Nabatieh and prevented from proceeding to Kfar Tibnit, the closest village to occupied Arnoun.

"Military sources informed The Daily Star that the students were not allowed to approach the village for their own safety.

This time, South Lebanon Army militiamen* occupy the village, but last time they were up in Beaufort Castle, far enough away to guarantee the safety of the civilians, said an army officer. Arnoun is now considered an SLA outpost, so if the students attempt to take it back, theyll be shot at. A massacre would ensue and the "Israeli" media would make a fuss about how the Lebanese are fighting one another in south Lebanon, he added.

"Carrying Lebanese flags and tents, the students tried to set up camp at the checkpoint but were again ordered out of the area.

"Last week, when Arnoun was overrun for the second time this year, a group of reporters approaching the village came under SLA fire. SLA officers vowed that they would shoot at anyone seen heading in their direction.

"The students said their aim was to encourage civilian resistance to "Israeli" occupation.

"Israeli" Aggressions(6)
The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions:
18:35, the surroundings of Sojod outpost exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
20:00, Hamra area was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
20:10, four 105mm rounds hit Mount Sojod.
20:40, Jabbour area was targeted by three 155mm missiles.
Between 22:45 and 22:55, Sawan area between Zawtar and Qaaqaiyat Al-Jisr towns was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
21:00, two 81mm flare shells were unleashed over the outskirts of Zibqeen village.
22:05, two 81mm rounds landed west of Baraasheit town.
22:40, Litani River watercourse was targeted by four 81mm rounds.
23:00, several flare shells were fired over Zawtar town.
23:15, two flares shells were unleashed over Litani River watercourse.
23:40, Slouqi Valley exposed to two 120mm shells, whereas one flare shell was fired over head.

Military Operations(7)
At 23:00, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked enemy movements near Salaah position with the adequate arms. The fighters chalked up direct and certain goals.
