Daily Report
 
 
April Monday 12-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
In its communiqué, the IR reported the following "Israeli" aggressions staged on the Lebanese areas:
1:55, three 155mm missiles struck the outskirts of Haddatha town.
2:15, the surroundings of the old Beit Yahoun outpost subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
Between 2:15 and 2:30, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal exposed to five 60mm shells, two 81mm missiles and volleys of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
2:30, two 81mm rounds hit the perimeters of Baraasheit town.
6:20, Mlita village was targeted by 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
6:35, eight 120mm shells and two 81mm rounds were fired at Mount Sojod.
11:55, the surroundings of Mashaaroun and the new Houla positions came under 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
12:25, four 155mm missiles were unleashed at the west of Yater village.
Between 12:25 and 17:30, the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom subjected to seven 155mm missiles.
The "Israeli" warplanes raided three times, at 17:50, 17:55 and 18:00, on the area between the villages of Zibqeen and Chaitieh firing more than ten air-to-surface rockets.
18:25, the "Israeli" jets launched a sortie into Mlita area.
18:45, one round hit Boslaya Hill near Kfar Milki village.
19:40, one 60mm shell struck Aita El-Jabal forest.
21:05, the outskirts of Haddatha town exposed to three 81mm rounds.

Military Operations(2)
The IR men spotted, at 17:40, enemy movements inside Blat post. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyr Hassan Hammoudi attacked them intensely scoring certain hits.
