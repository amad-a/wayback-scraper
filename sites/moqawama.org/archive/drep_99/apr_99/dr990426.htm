Daily Report
 
 
April Monday 26-1999:

"Israel's" militiamen
 Told not to travel after Allouche attack(1)
"Antoine Lahds militiamen, still reeling from a bomb attack that critically wounded their commander for the Jezzine area, had been ordered to stay in their positions to avoid resistance attacks, security sources said Sunday".

"The sources said Lahd visited Jezzine late Saturday in a secret trip to try and lift the low morale of his militia".

"The commander of the Jezzine area, Joseph Karam, known as Allouche, was seriously wounded in a roadside bomb attack Friday which killed his driver George Gerges".

"The Hizbullah attack took place as Karams two-car convoy was on Dar al-Ramleh Road east of Jezzine".

"Latest reports said Karam is in a coma at the Rambam hospital in "Israel".

Residents said the SLA* has imposed tight restrictions on the Roum-Kfar Falous crossing" "SLA members positioned at Roum gate questioned all passers-by and scrolled through mobile telephones writing down all numbers in the memory and told people not to bring their telephones on future visits".

"The timing of the attack was said to be in response to "Israels" re-annexation of Arnoun. The attempt on Karams life, the third since he assumed his duties last summer, was reportedly welcomed by many Jezzine residents who have suffered under his despotic and tyrannical control".

"Fridays attack was hailed by Hizbullah Secretary-General Sheik Hassan Nasrallah as a message to the "Israelis".

"Sayyed Nasrallah, who personally ordered the killing of senior SLA collaborators, said Sunday that "Israels" reliance on its agents by placing them at frontline positions to reduce its own presence was a mistake".

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)
In its statement, the IR reported the following "Israeli" aggressions on the Lebanese areas:
6:50, three 120mm shells hit Mount Sojod.
13:00, Kfour ravine was shelled by eight 155mm missiles.
16:55, the peripheries of Mansouri village exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
17:20, five 155mm missiles fell on Jib Soweid area.
18:05, the surroundings of Qobaa position subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
20:45, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the surroundings of Mashaaroun position.
21:10, two 120mm shells were unleashed at Mount Sojod.
21:15, the surroundings of Qoba position came under 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
21:40, six 155mm missiles fell on Jib Soweid area and two others on a ravine between Mansouri and Majdal Zoun villages.
