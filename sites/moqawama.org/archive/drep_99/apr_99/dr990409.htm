Daily Report
 
 
April Friday 9-1999:

Introduction
Two more members of the "Israeli" allied militia in Shibaa deserted Thursday, raising to six the number of collaborators to have fled the town in the past week.

In response to the nation wide outcry over the expulsion of 18 residents from Shibaa two days before, the European Union's Middle East envoy, Miguel Angel Moratinos, pledged that the EU would ask "Israel" to halt all deportations and allow the residents to return home.

Meanwhile, the five-nation Monitoring Group acknowledged the "Israeli" violations of the April
Understanding and called "Israel", to take more effective measures to avoid a repetition of such incidents".

Shibaa defies "Israeli" occupation(1)
 "Shibaa residents yesterday challenged the "Israeli" occupation, staging a sit-in at the village's Mosque without fearing the "Israeli" tanks that patrolled the town streets".

"The Shibaa men and women were protesting the "Israeli" occupation forces' expulsion of eighteen residents. Protesters threatened to confront the enemy in case it did not lift the siege imposed on their town.

"The sit-in was called for by Shibaa's Imam Sheikh Ismael Dalah who was detained for a few hours by the enemy and then freed by residents.

Intensive security measures imposed by the "Israeli" occupation and its agents accompanied the sit-in. Houses were ransacked and young men were summoned to Lahd's security headquarters in Tallet Zoghle where they were subjected to an investigation before they were released.

"The protesters dispatched a letter at the UNIFIL's command in which they requested from the UN Indian battalion positioned in Shibaa to fulfill its duties and protect villagers from "Israeli"
aggressions.

Shibaa falls within UNIFIL's mandate; the occupation forces have no direct jurisdiction over the village.

"The residents denounced the "Israeli" barbarism aimed at displacing them and called on the international groups to work on returning the deportees.

"They also called on the remaining Shibaa residents who are still collaborating with the enemy -- not more than ten people -- to regain their conscience, leave the collaborating militia and stand beside their fellow villagers in confronting the enemy.

In Beirut, a solidarity meeting with the residents of Shibaa was held at the Press Syndicate headquarters and was attended by parliamentary blocs' representatives and religious leaders who
addressed the crowds.

"Lebanese security forces today (yesterday) said two collaborating militia security officers in Shibaa Khalil Ahmed Nabaa and Fadi Khalil Nabaa surrendered to the Lebanese army at a barracks north of the occupied zone after they had walked 12 kilometers.

"Another four collaborators from Shibaa deserted and crossed to the liberated areas with their wives and children last Sunday.

Reports said "Israeli" occupation forces are intensively patrolling the vicinity of Shibaa, while imposing strict security measures and conducting scattered storming operations into the towns of Hasbayya, Ibl as-Saqi, Kfarhamam and Kfarshouba.

"In Jezzine security sources said the Lahd militia's checkpoint in the town of Roum prevented the priest of Jezzine and Kfarhouna Father Nicholas Darwish from entering Jezzine to participate in Easter prayers and services.

"Militiamen at the checkpoint told Father Darwish to return to where he came from (Saida).
Elsewhere, reports from Marjeyoun said the "Israeli" liaison unit's command held a meeting for army officers positioned in the occupied zone.

"They discussed the excommunication of collaborators which was issued by the Druze spiritual leader Sheikh Aref Halawi and its effect on future relations between the occupied zone's Druze and the "Israeli" occupation.

"The meeting also discussed the link between the excommunication and the defection of collaborating militiamen.

"Furthermore, another meeting was held between liaison unit officers and Lahd militia officers to discuss the same excommunication issue.

"Druze collaborating officers were also summoned to the liaison unit's headquarters for the same purpose, especially after a number of Lahd's Druze militiamen handed in mass resignations.

"After the excommunication announcement by Sheikh Halawi, an officer in the so-called civil administration in the occupied town of Ain Jarfa called Mhanna Mohammed Draibe rendered his resignation to the occupation forces.

"Mhanna was summoned to the "Israeli" occupation's headquarters in the town and questioned about the resignation.

"He said his move was in compliance with the excommunication. He was replaced by Anwar Mahmoud Draibeh at the "civil administration".

"Furthermore, a number of the occupied zone residents who work inside occupied Palestine refused to go to work".

"Handwritten leaflets were circulated among workers saying that the money which is paid to
Lebanese workers in return of services in occupied Palestine is ill-gotten and can not be used".

EU to ask ""Israel"" to stop South Lebanon expulsions(2)
"The European Union will ask ""Israel"" to stop expelling villagers from their homes inside the south Lebanon occupied zone, its envoy to the Middle East, Miguel Angel Moratinos, said on Thursday".

"We will contact "Israel" to stop any action of driving people away from their homes,'' Moratinos, who arrived in Lebanon from Syria, told reporters.

"He was referring to the expulsion of 18 people, including five children, from Shibaa village this week by the "Israeli" occupation forces and their militia".

"Speaking after a "useful and fruitful" meeting with President Gen. Emile Lahoud, Moratinos said, "The EU is diplomatically and politically ready to prevent any escalation in the south."

"The European envoy said the EU wants Lebanon to participate in the European-Mediterranean foreign ministers' conference".

"He also discussed the developments in the south with Speaker Nabih Berri and Prime Minister Salim al-Hoss".

Monitoring Group acknowledged "Israeli" violations of understanding (3)
"The five-nation Monitoring Group held one of its shortest meetings on record Thursday, taking only three-and-a-half hours to find "Israel" guilty of violating the April Understanding on two counts".

"The group met in Naqoura to hear four Lebanese complaints. An "Israeli" complaint over a roadside bomb attack near Beaufort Castle was withdrawn at the last minute".

""Israel" was blamed for wounding nine civilians in Habboush and Arab Salim Sunday, and damaging two houses in Yater Monday".

"As for the complaint concerning the expulsion of the Shebaa residents, the group said in a statement that there were differing views on whether this incident fell within the mandate of the April Understanding.

"The group noted that the issue has been raised in other channels and is being addressed by the appropriate authorities, the statement added.

Military Operations(5)
The IR team of martyrs Hassan Ghamloush and Rameh Mahdi attacked, at 12:45, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were chalked up. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Abdel-Amir Fadel and Khalil Hammoud targeted an "Israeli" force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct hits. At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Rabih Wehbi and Jihad Shbeeb attacked Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled greandes. The mujahedeen were able to score direct goals.

At 18:30, the IR team of martyrs Moussa Diab Yehia and Youssef Abdel-Hussein Badran attacked Hardoun outpost with the proper weapons. Accurate goals were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Moussa Nassrallah and Abdallah Ahmad Rammal targeted Tair Harfa position with the suitable arms. The militants were able strike its western fortification.
