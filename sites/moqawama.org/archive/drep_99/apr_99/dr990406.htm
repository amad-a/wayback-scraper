Daily Report
 
 
April Tuesday 6-1999:

Introduction
Lebanon has demanded an emergency meeting of the five-nation monitoring group after nine civilians, mostly children, were injured when the "Israeli" artillery shells struck the villages of Habboush and Arab Salim Sunday.

Hizbullah swiftly condemned the aggression as a blatant violation of the April Understanding which looks void and without any meaning in front of the children's wounds and screams.

Elsewhere, the collapsing morale of the "Israeli" proxy militia in the eastern sector spurred four more militiamen to flee the zone Sunday.

MONITORING GROUP TO MEET AFTER "ISRAELI" SHELLING WOUNDS NINE LEBANESE CIVILIANS(1)
" The five nation cease-fire monitoring group is to meet tomorrow (today) to discuss an "Israeli" violation of the truce understanding. Yesterday, nine Lebanese civilians were injured in "Israeli" shelling on the front-lines in south Lebanon. The Hizbullah resistance movement said "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has given the army a greenlight to attack Lebanese civilians".

" Two year old Fatima is in pain. She is the most recent victim of "Israeli" attacks on residential neighborhoods in southern Lebanon. Fatima and her family were visiting her grandpa, who just returned from the Hajj pilgrimage, when "Israeli" shells slammed into his house in the front-line village of Habboush. Fatima's uncle and her cousins were also injured. Seven children and two adults were wounded yesterday in two separate attacks on Habboush and Arab Saleem, which also lies just north of the zone "Israel" occupies in the south. Attacks on civilians are prohibited under the internationally-observed 1996 cease-fire understanding between Lebanon and "Israel". But occupation forces have kept up their aggressions against civilians, including children like Fatima, who is lying on an emergency room bed in the city of Sidon. Fatima has already undergone surgery in her stomach, to remove a shrapnel that tore through her intestines. In two days, doctors will operate on Fatima's brain to remove another shrapnel. Upon seeing her parents, Fatima starts crying. Her mother Iman is still shocked. She said that shells hit the entrance of the home, minutes after some fifteen family members and relatives entered the house, adding a massacre was averted. Iman only realized that her daughter was injured twenty minutes after she rushed outside the home and brought Fatima inside to safety. The childs' parents say Fatima keeps asking them whether she was hit because she had done something bad, but her father Ali explained to her that it was "Israeli" occupation troops who did a bad thing. The Hizbullah resistance movement's official in south Lebanon, Sheikh Nabeel Qawook, believes the attacks on Habboush and Arab Saleem were a message to Lebanon proving "Israel's" capabilities, and also, a move by "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, to gain more support before "Israeli" elections in May. Sheikh Qawook warned that "Israel" has been expanding its attacks on Lebanon recently, stressing it is the only way for "Israel" to vent its frustration following a series of military setbacks on the hands of the resistance.

Sheikh Qawook said yesterday's attacks were a quote scandalous violation of the cease-fire understanding. The Hizbullah official also told Future Television, Netanyahu is personally responsible for giving "Israel's" military establishment a free-hand to attack Lebanese civilians in the south. He said the shelling that injured Fatima and her relatives was not an accident, but was a result of a political decision by "Israel". Responding to a question on whether Hizbullah would shell northern "Israel" in retaliation for the attacks, Sheikh Qawook only answered the resistance has vowed to protect civilians and stressed Hizbullah would not allow "Israel" to change the "rules of the game", by turning south Lebanon and civilians into hostages in the hands of Netanyahu and his election campaign".

Mortar attack victim, 2, fights for her life(2)
"Two-year-old Fatima Halwani, the most seriously wounded in the "Israeli"-allied militia mortar attack on Habboush, was still fighting for her life Monday with doctors stabilizing her condition ahead of another surgery later this week to remove shrapnel lodged in her head".

"Fatima was playing with her friends in her grandfathers house when the mortar shell exploded in the courtyard. Shrapnel ripped her intestines and spleen and a small shard lodged in her head.

Despite the seriousness of the injuries, the doctors at the Hammoud hospital in Sidon saved her life by operating swiftly to contain the loss of blood incurred during the frantic ambulance journey from Habboush".

"In the operating room just before the effect of the anesthesia took hold, Fatima asked for the doll her grandfather had brought her as a gift from Mecca, where he had been on pilgrimage".

"She woke up in the intensive care unit screaming with pain at the tube which had been placed through her nose to extract intestinal secretions. The sight of her doll sharing her hospital bed was not enough to comfort her".

"Dr. Mohammed Yassine said that Fatimas condition was more or less stable, but that she would have to undergo another operation to remove the shrapnel in her head".

MORE DEFECTIONS FROM "ISRAELI" ALLIED MILITIA, RELIGIOUS DECREE ISSUE FORBIDDING DRUZE FROM COLLABORATING WITH "Israel"(3)
"There have been more defections in the crumbling "Israeli" allied-militia force in occupied southern Lebanon. Four deserters and their families surrendered to the Lebanese Army yesterday".

" Four deserters from the "Israeli"-allied militia sneaked out of the "Israeli"-occupied zone of southern Lebanon yesterday and handed themselves over to the Lebanese Army. Security sources said the four, three Druze and one Christian, all came from the largely Druze village of Shebaa. They were accompanied by their wives and children, making a total of 12 people. They said they had walked for 12 kilometers or eight miles through hills and valleys before they reached a Lebanese Army post outside the zone. They bring to six the number of militia deserters in the past month, all of them from the Druze-dominated eastern sector. The Druze member of parliament for Hasbaya-Rashaya, Faisal Daud, part of whose constituency is occupied by "Israel", appealed Saturday to Druze members of the militia to give themselves up to the Lebanese Army, promising that they would not be prosecuted.
He added that a highly respected Druze spiritual leader, Sheikh Aref Halawi, who is in his 90s, had issued a religious decree forbidding Druze from collaborating with "Israel". This religious decree has been praised by Lebanese officials and the Secretary General of the Hizbullah resistance movement Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah who said it was an important development in the resistance struggle. There are several hundred Druze serving in the 3,000-man militia, which includes collaborators of all faiths.

But lately there has been a wave of arrests of militiamen who "Israel" suspects of providing Hizbullah with information enabling them to kill "Israel's" top general in southern Lebanon, Erez Gerstein last February. A number of top collaborators with occupation forces and the "Israeli" Mossad secret service have also been defecting from the zone "Israel" occupies in Lebanon. Some have helped Lebanese security services in cracking down on "Israeli" agents operating in the country".

"ISRAELI" SOLDIER KILLS A FRIEND AND COMMITS SUICIDE ON LEBANESE BORDER(4)
" An "Israeli" army soldier shot a fellow soldier and then committed suicide after a dispute at an army post on the "Israeli"-Lebanese border, "Israeli" radio said. An army statement confirmed the deaths but refrained from giving details about the incident. "Two army soldiers, Sergeant Ronen Kimchi, 20, and Private Alexander Reznikov, 19, were killed and an officer was lightly wounded in the north of "Israel"," the statement said. "From the initial investigation it appears that a dispute arose between the two soldiers ... and one soldier fired from his weapon. Both soldiers died from their wounds," it added. Reznikov was a driver and Kimchi was a nurse. Radio "Israel" said they had argued over guard duty. "Israeli" troops occupy a border strip in south Lebanon. UN resolution 425 calls on "Israel" to withdraw its troops unconditionally. It has so far refused to do so".
