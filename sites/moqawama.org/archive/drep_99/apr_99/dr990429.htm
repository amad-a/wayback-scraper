Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 29-1999:

Introduction
The Lebanese government discussed the heightening tension in South Lebanon following the "Israel's" threats that it would wage military operations deep inside Lebanese if the government offers logistic support to the Islamic Resistance (IR) men. Premier Minister Salim Hoss said that "resisting occupation is a legitimate right for any people whose land is occupied. The resistance in Lebanon enjoys support from all Lebanese, from all regions and groups.

In another development, a woman claiming to be an "Israeli" national has been arrested in the southern port city of Tyre and was interrogated yesterday by the general prosecutor Adnan Addoum.

Premier retorts to "Israeli" threats: all Lebanon supports Resistance(1)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss retorted to "Israeli" threats that were launched yesterday by saying, "the solution is simple: to withdraw "Israeli" occupation forces from Lebanon."

"Following his meeting with President Gen Emile Lahoud last night, Mr. al-Hoss told al-Nahar newspaper that the "Israeli" occupation's soldiers are being killed on occupied Lebanese soil.

"It is a legitimate right for the Lebanese to defend themselves and their land against the continuous "Israeli" aggressions, the premier stated.

"The "Israeli" occupation is terrorism," Mr. Hoss continued, "and the killing of children in their homes is the terrorism."

"As for the Resistance, it is a natural result caused by the "Israeli" occupation and aggressions; the Resistance is also a legitimate right for any people whose land is occupied and that is being killed by an occupying army".

"Replying to the statement made by "Israeli" Army Intelligence chief Amos Malka, who said that the Lebanese government is legitimizing Hizbullah's operations against "Israel", PM al-Hoss stated: "we repeat once again that all of Lebanon supports resisting the "Israeli" occupation. Resistance is a legitimate right as long as occupation continues."

"The premier announced that Lebanon has filed a complaint with the April Understanding Monitoring Group over the "Israeli" bombardment yesterday which targeted populated areas in southern villages and wounded a mother and her child in the town of Jarjoua, besides damaging a number of houses".

Russian Ambassador rejects "Israeli" threats(2)
"Russian ambassador to Lebanon Oleg Peressypkine described the "Israeli" threats leveled at Lebanon as "unacceptable", saying "it is not in the interest of any party to escalate tension and military problems in the region."

"Mr. Peressypkine attributed the "Israeli" threats to the approaching "Israeli" elections.

"The ambassador was talking following his meeting with Foreign Ministry's Director-General Ambassador Zafer al-Hassan in which he presented the Russian proposal concerning Iraq".

LEBANON ARRESTS "ISRAELI" WOMAN - BEIRUT JUDGE(3)
" A woman arrested in the southern port city of Tyre said during questioning that she was an "Israeli" who had crossed the border to visit Lebanon, general prosecutor Adnan Addoum said on Wednesday. The woman, who was arrested on Tuesday by Lebanese general security near the security service offices in Tyre, said her name was Mary Weizman and she was 47 years old, Addoum told reporters at his office after questioning her for 15 minutes. He said the woman claimed to have come from Nazareth in northern "Israel" by herself to visit an old friend in Beirut and had thrown away her "Israeli" passport. There was no explanation how she could cross the official border between the countries, which is controlled by "Israeli" forces on both sides, and subsequent Lebanese army checkpoints. He said the woman would undergo further questioning on allegations she was working for "Israel's" secret service, Mossad. Lebanon is officially in a state of war with "Israel" which has occupied part of southern Lebanon for 21 years".

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
In its statement, the IR reported the following "Israeli" aggressions on the liberated areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa:

Between 00:10 and 00:25, Slouqi Valley was shelled by eight 81mm rounds.
00:30, five 155mm missiles struck Sawan area.
12:10, Aita El-Jabal forest subjected to five 120mm shells.
Between 12:45 and 17:10, Mount Sojod was bombarded by sixteen 120mm shells and seven 155mm missiles.
12:50, two 155mm missiles hit Tasseh Well watercourse.
14:55, eighteen 155mm missiles fell on the outskirts of Haddatha woodland and three others on the perimeters of Shamaa village.
15:20, the outskirts of Jibal El-Botom exposed to three 155mm missiles.
16:20, five 155mm missiles slammed into the east of Mazraat Hamra.
16:40, Loweizeh village was targeted by two rounds.
Between 17:00 and 17:35, the outskirts of Jibal El-Botom came under five 160mm missiles and three 120mm shells.
17:10, two 160mm missiles struck Intar ravine.

Military Operations(5)
The IR men spotted, at 00:10, an enemy motorized patrol as it was entering Sojod outpost. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyr Hussein Nasser attacked it with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.
