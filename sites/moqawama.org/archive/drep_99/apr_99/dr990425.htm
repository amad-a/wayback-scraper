Daily Report
 
 
April Sunday 25-1999:

Some details of IR's qualitative operation(1)
"Al-Nour Radio reported that before Jezzine chief collaborator Joseph Karam, alias Alloush, got into his car, ten of agent Lahd's militiamen set up a checkpoint on the road where the bomb was planted.

"Another militia patrol thoroughly searched the area without being able to detect the bomb.

Following these measures agent Karam rode in an armored Mercedes, and changed several cars before the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen detonated the bomb".

"The explosion tossed Karam's car more than fifty meters, al-Nour Radio added.

"Al-Manar Television this evening aired footage showing these details.

MP Raad: private company supplies occupation's outposts with food(2)
"Head of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad unveiled that an "Israeli" private company is supplying the "Israeli" occupation's outposts in the occupied zone with food".

"This measure was adopted by the "Israeli" army in order to keep the Islamic Resistance from spotting the enemy troops as they come inside and outside of the outposts, MP Raad said.

"Where is the enemy's combat spirit and ability to continue its occupation?" MP Raad wondered, saying "The defeated enemy attempts, from time to time, to lure us into traps, such as the Jezzine first and Arnoun first, in order to subdue us to the conditions it has set to pull out of Lebanon.

"MP Raad affirmed that the Resistance is cautious, alerted and always ready to counter these "Israeli" attempts which are backed by the US.

"Such maneuvers are always discovered and aborted due to the coordination between the Resistance and the officials, in addition to the popular embrace of the resistance", MP Raad concluded.

Military court sentenced eight collaborators to death(3)
"The Military Court presided over by Major-General Maher Safieddine sentenced, in absentia, eight of the collaborating militia's officers to death."

"Wahib Fahed Sadeq, Wassim Saleh Deeb, Houssam Mahmoud Outhman, Adel Hussein Wehbe, Nader Toufik Khaddaj, Robin Ibrahim Abboud, Karim Salim Farhat and Hussein Hassan Abdullatif al-Abdallah were convicted of crimes related to contacting the "Israeli" enemy and providing it with security information concerning the Resistance and both Lebanese and Syrian armies, in addition to entering the occupied territories in Palestine".

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The IR released a statement in which it mentioned the following aggressions which launched by "Israeli" occupation forces on Lebanon's areas:

8:00, Mount Sojod was bombarded by fifteen 120mm shells.
8:30, the "Israeli" jets raided twice on Mount Sojod.
8:35, the "Israeli" warplanes staged a sortie on Mlita area.
8:57, the "Israeli" bombers dropped missiles on the perimeters of Jibaa town.
9:15, the "Israeli" warplanes launched a sortie on the northeast of Jezzine town, but the IR mujahedeen fired one Sam-7 ground-to-air rocket.
Between 12:55 and 13:28, Mount Al-Rafih was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
Between 16:45 and 17:10, Haris village was targeted by six 81mm rounds causing damage to Nazih Nasser's house and Afif Jawad's car.
18:20, one 120mm shell hit the valley between Majdal Zoun and Mansouri.
20:45, two 81mm rounds struck west of Baraasheit town.
Between 21:00 and 21:35, Litani River watercourse exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
21:15, the surroundings of Taibeh position subjected to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
22:35, Habboush ravine came under a number of 155mm missiles.
23:55, the deserted Lebanese Army barracks came under fifteen 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(5)
The IR team of martyr Khodor Khiami attacked, at 6:00, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct hits.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyr Hassan El-Amin targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.

Furthermore, the IR men spotted, at 18:00, enemy movements inside Blat post. Immediately afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Ali Harmoush and Moussa Salman attacked them with the suitable arms scoring direct and definite hits.

Five minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Nahim and Hassan Balhas attacked Radar post chalking up direct goals. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyr Allam Soweidan was targeting Tair Harfa outpost with the appropriate weapons. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.
