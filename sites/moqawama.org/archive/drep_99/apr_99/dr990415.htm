Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 15-1999:
 
Hizbullah: Resistance is ready to confront any possible "Israeli" aggression(1)
"A Hizbullah senior official source told the Nidaa al-Watan newspaper chances are that "Israel" might stage a limited military offensive, but the "Israelis" are not able to pinpoint resistance targets".

"The "Israelis" want to achieve morale victories, but they are nowadays suffering from security weakness following the arrest of their spying rings and in light of the people's compassion that turned both the Lebanese government and people into an arena embracing the resistance", the source stated.

"As to the threat transferred to Speaker Nabih Berri by US ambassador to Lebanon David Satterfield, the source said, "Threats are not new to us and we don't deal with words, we deal with deeds."

We always expect an "Israeli" aggression at any moment. Therefore, we are ready to confront any "Israeli" move, the source said.

"If they think that direct messages and threats scare us, they are wrong, because they know the price of any offensive that they might launch", he concluded.

MONITORING GROUP MEETS(2)
"The five nation cease-fire monitoring group is meeting in Naquora to look into four complaints over alleged truce violations. Lebanon accused Israel of violating the April cease-fire understanding when its gunboats opened fire on fishermen, damaging their fishing boats off the coast of Tyre. The cease-fire bans attacks on civilian targets but permits the Lebanese resistance to carry out military operations against "Israeli" occupation troops and their local allies. Israel for its part filed three complaints. They are accusing the resistance of planting explosives in a civilian building, firing from a civilian area and damaging buildings in the occupation zone".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas:
9:45, four 155mm missiles struck Jib Soweid area.
11:55, three 120mm shells fell on Sojod village and two others on Mount Al-Rafih.
17:30, the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom subjected to two 155mm missiles.
17:45, Mount Sojod was targeted by four 120mm shells.
