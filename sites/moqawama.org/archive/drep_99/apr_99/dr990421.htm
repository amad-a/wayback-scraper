Daily Report
 
 
April Wednesday 21-1999:

Introduction
The ambiguous statement released by the Five-Nation Monitoring Group sparked considerable speculation and led the Lebanese government sources to clarify that "bilateral channels' does not mean holding direct contacts between Lebanon and "Israel". Also, the "Israeli" complaint over the roadside bombs was upheld despite the fact, that the charges exploded at least 100 meters from the "Israeli"-manned Beaufort Castle.

In this connection, Chief of Hizbullah's Politburo MP Mohammad Raad underlined that Beaufort Castle or any other area where "Israeli" enemy and its collaborators are deployed will be targets for the Resistance which will proceed with its operations until the enemy evacuates our land without any conditions.

Monitors avoid laying blame for Arnoun(1)
"The five-nation Monitoring Group avoided apportioning blame Tuesday for the annexation of Arnoun, urging instead that Lebanon and "Israel" to take concrete steps to restore calm in the area.

"The group recommended that further talks should take place between the parties and through bilateral channels. The phrase sparked considerable speculation and led government sources to deny that it meant direct contacts between Lebanon and "Israel".

"The five delegates to the group convened in Naqoura Monday to hear four complaints.

Lebanon filed three: The incorporation of Arnoun into the zone, the wounding of a journalist Friday outside the village, and the wounding of a civilian and damage to a school in Arab Salim Sunday.

"Israel" included a previously lodged complaint over a twin roadside bomb attack against a patrol of the South Lebanon Army* at Beaufort Castle".

"The annexation of the village dominated the proceedings.

Lebanons delegate, Colonel Maher Toufeili, argued that the incursion was unjustified and illegal. His "Israeli" counterpart, Brigadier Daniel Arditi, responded that the take-over was necessary to protect the soldiers manning Beaufort Castle above the village.

It appears that the group decided to refrain from pronouncing who was to blame in favor of further consultations".

The group noted the serious concerns surrounding this action and its potential for raising tensions, while acknowledging that there were differing views on whether this action fell within the purview of the April Understanding, the final statement said.

It added that it was in the interest of all parties to seek a solution. The issue should be further examined on an urgent basis, both by the parties and through bilateral channels, it said.

The goal of these concrete steps would be a return to the situation in Arnoun prior to April 15, to ensure respect for the provisions of the understanding concerning the non-use of civilian areas as launching sites for military activities, and to reduce sources of tension.

Government sources said that the statements reference to bilateral channels referred to mediation by the co-chairs of the Monitoring Group.

The contacts are to be made through the French and American delegates, the source said.

Nabatieh MP Mohammed Raad, a member of Hizbullahs Loyalty to the Resistance parliamentary bloc, criticized the findings of the group.

What happened that led the Lebanese delegate to agree to such wording in the statement? he asked. We protest this language and reject any changes in the terms of the understanding.

The "Israeli" complaint over the roadside bombs was upheld, despite the fact that the charges exploded at least 300 meters from a populated house.

He added that both sides had disagreed on whether the annexation of Arnoun fell within the scope of the understanding".

"As for the SLA outpost that has been constructed in the village, the source said that the presence of militiamen in the village will raise tension.

The group blamed "Israel" for violating the understanding by wounding the journalist and civilian, and described as grave the damage to a school in Arab Salim by shelling".

Resistance will continue on all areas occupied by "Israel", says Hizbullah MP(2)
"All the outposts of the ("Israeli") occupation and its collaborators in the occupied zone are and will always be legitimate targets for the Islamic Resistance, Chief of Hizbullah's political Council MP Mohammed Raad said today (yesterday).

At a press conference he held in the parliament, the Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc member said, "It is really strange how the Lebanese delegate to the Monitoring Group has agreed on the panel's statement."

"As resistance, it is our concern to ask, as long as the bombs, according to the group's statement, were detonated against "Israeli" forces, what where these forces doing in Arnoun?" Raad asked.

This means that it is not Arnoun which is meant, but the Beaufort Castle which is manned by occupation forces, he added.

"MP Raad underlined that Beaufort Castle or any other area where "Israeli" enemy and its collaborators are deployed will be targets for the Resistance which will proceed with its operations until the enemy evacuates our land without any conditions.

All sneaky attempts and suspicious statements will never change this fact, he said.

"The Resistance is committed to the April Understanding and it won't be dragged into the enemy's attempts to interpret the understanding according to its interests, he added.

"The Hizbullah MP noted that the AUMG communiqué stated that "there were differing views" on whether the occupation's annexation of Arnoun is in violation of the Understanding.

"This is really suspicious. I wonder how the Lebanese delegate agreed to have this communiqué issued with such errors and terminology, Raad said, adding that "Hizbullah has welcomed the President (Emile Lahoud) and PM's (Salim al-Hoss) remarks on the Resistance legitimate right and their considering the enemy's annexation of Arnoun to the occupied zone as a clear violation of the April Understanding."

"Then, how could the Lebanese delegate agree on such a text in the statement? MP Raad asked.

He also noted that AUMG communiqué calls for using bilateral (Lebanese - "Israeli") channels over Arnoun".

"Such an act will be meeting, although implicitly, the enemy's demand to amend some of the understanding's articles, including neutralizing some areas, such as Arnoun, he said.

"The Beaufort Castle was targeted (by the Resistance). The two bombs were planted by the Resistance men at the Castle's gate, as the Monitoring Group acknowledged, MP Raad said.

"If the Beaufort Castle is part of Arnoun, the Arnoun residents have no problem with the Resistance men. Moreover, Resistance men do not need the Arnoun passage to reach the Castle, for they can reach any "Israeli" outpost in the occupied zone whenever they want with means that do not breach the April Understanding, Raad said.

"As we state these facts, we stress our opposition to this terminology (used in the AUMG communiqué) and affirm that we will never accept any amendment of any article in the April Understanding, he said.

"Resistance will proceed with its operations in every Lebanese area in which there is direct or indirect "Israeli" occupation", the Hizbullah MP concluded.

Cabinet sources clarify the AUMG statement; Hoss: army won't be deployed in Arnoun(3)
"Lebanese Government sources clarified that the part in the April Understanding Monitoring Group's communiqué about "bilateral channels" does not mean holding direct contacts between Lebanon and ""Israel"".

"What has been agreed on during the AUMG meeting is that US and French delegates hold contacts with the other delegates to take concrete steps that would allow a return to the situation in Arnoun prior to April 15, 1999, the sources said.

"The formula which was reached by the panel points out that the Arnoun issue is to be tackled by the delegates, and dealing with the issue is still open through the contacts among these delegates.

Meanwhile, Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss said Lebanon has rejected to deploy the Lebanese army in Arnoun "because we do not want to place the army in direct confrontation with the occupation forces."

"Arnoun is adjacent to the Beaufort Castle. If the Lebanese army is deployed in the town, the army will be facing unguaranteed possibilities, the premier said.

Following a meeting with al-Hoss, MP Naseeb Lahoud ruled out the possibility of filing a complaint with the UN Security Council over the occupation of Arnoun."

"The Lebanese government, parliament and people all agree that "Israeli" threats will not change the policy of Lebanon which upholds its right to resist the occupation," MP Lahoud said.

Lebanon urges U.N. to force ""Israel"" to quit village(4)
"Lebanon has urged the Security Council to force ""Israel"" to evacuate a village that it included last week in its so-called "security zone" in south Lebanon".

"In a letter to Secretary General Kofi Annan circulated on Monday, Lebanese U.N. representative Samir Mubarak said ""Israel"" sent 50 men, preceded by a tank, an armored personnel carrier and a jeep, into the village of Arnoun on April 15".

"In a new attempt to include it into the occupied zone in the south, they separated Arnoun from the liberated area and proceeded to arrest residents and to prevent inhabitants from entering," he said.

"In bringing this dangerous development to the attention of the secretary-general, the members of the Security Council and the international community, the Lebanese government urges swift action to put an end to a situation that constitutes a flagrant violation of the Charter of the United Nations and the principles of international law," Mubarak added.

"The Lebanese government holds "Israel" responsible for all material and other damage that may be done to civilians by this action," he said.

"He also urged implementation of a 1978 Security Council resolution that called on ""Israel"" to withdraw from southern Lebanon".

At the same time, two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked the positions of Blat and Tair Harfa. The fighters managed to score direct and definite hits.
