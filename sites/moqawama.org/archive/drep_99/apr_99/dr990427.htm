Daily Report
 
 
April Tuesday 27-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah announced that "we will neither accept "Israel's" proposal to withdraw from South Lebanon nor relinquish our resistance whatever rewards are offered".

However, the Islamic Resistance (IR) displayed a footage showing how Joseph Karam's two-car convoy was destroyed by a roadside bomb on Dahr Ramleh road at Jezzine area. The attacked critically wounded Karam Known as Alloche and killed his driver. The reports from the occupied zone told that a state of confusion had prevailed in the militia's ranks following this attack. Also, mass resignations have been rendered to agent Lahd's command.

Hizbullah Secretary General's Speech on Ashora Day(1)
"Hizbullah has once again rejected conditional "Israeli" offers to withdraw from south Lebanon.

"All experiences have proved that the (armed) resistance is the only way ... to regain the land," Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah today (yesterday) told hundreds of thousands of mourners who gathered in Beirut's southern suburbs to mark Ashora Day.

""Israel" is a cancerous gland in this region ... this is why the only choice for our Arab and Islamic nation is that ""Israel"" should cease to exist," he said, the crowds raised their fists shouting "Death to "Israel"."

""Israel"" and the US represent the peak of corruption, injustice and arrogance in the region and the world, his Eminence added.

"All the measures taken by the Zionist enemy in the occupied zone will be useless; decreasing the number of occupation troops will not ease the bleeding of their officers and soldiers, nor will the increased violence in artillery and aerial bombardment curb the resolve of the Mujahedeen who are fighting with Imam Hussein's (A.S.) mind, spirit and resolve,"

"The enemy's attempt to place the collaborators' militia in front-line outposts will also fail, because the Zionist army has already tried this option and seen the outposts falling one after the other under the feet of the Islamic Resistance's Mujahedeen, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding that "These outposts will keep on falling and the militia won't be able to protect the enemy or its security."

"The Hizbullah Secretary General called on the collapsing militia to rebel and turn their arms against "Israeli" forces in occupied southern Lebanon.

"I call on all militia officers and members not only to surrender but also to carry out an uprising in the face of this enemy," Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"I call on them to turn their weapons on the chests of the occupation officers and soldiers in the occupied zone," he added.
"He warned the militiamen that if they "do not surrender, the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen will await them not only at crossroads, valleys and mountain tops, but also at the doors of their homes and bedrooms.

"On the Tenth of Moharram (Ashora Day) we reiterate our option in Lebanon, call on continuation of national - popular - official coherence in backing the resistance and not giving up our right to resist, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"All must be sure that the enemy will not settle for guarantees but will ask for more, he said.

On the Tenth of Moharram we reiterate our permanent readiness to face any possibility, especially as we are on the eve of the "Israeli" elections on May 17", he added.

"ISRAELI" ALLIED MILITIAMEN SUFFERING FROM LOW MORALE(2)
"The morale of the "Israeli" allied militia has reportedly reached its lowest ebb following the attack that targeted the commander of the occupied Jezzine enclave".

"Friday's attack critically injured Joseph Karam known as Allouche and killed his driver. His car was destroyed in the blast. Karam is reportedly in a coma in a hospital in "Israel". The Hizbullah movement has said it carried out the attack against Karam's two car convoy after studying and monitoring the movements of "Israeli" occupation forces and their local allies. Hizbullah said Karam had first rode in an armored Mercedes. He then changed his car several times before reaching the site where the bomb was set off. "Israeli" forces have opened an investigation into the attack and are questioning militia members whom they accuse of providing the resistance with intelligence information. The attack dealt a severe blow to the militia and has apparently disrupted plans by the "Israeli" army to reduce its presence in the occupation zone. Sources said members of the so-called 20th regiment deployed in the Jezzine enclave are being transferred to other areas of the occupation zone. "Israel" is replacing them with other militiamen. Karam heads that regiment. Over recent months the number of defections from the ranks of the militia has been increasing."

Confusion in militia ranks after IR's qualitative operation in Jezzine
"The Islamic Resistance's (IR) qualitative operation in which the collaborating militia's 20th Regiment Commander Joseph Karam (alias Alloush) was injured and his driver was killed two days ago in Jezzine is in the center of attention caused a state of confusion in the militia's ranks.

Reports from the occupied zone said that mass resignations have been rendered to agent Lahd's command."

"Security reports from the occupied zone stated that the "Israeli" occupation, following IR's qualitative operation, embarked upon reinforcing the 20th Regiment with new collaborating militiamen from Marjayoun, along with transferring some militiamen to other areas".

"The so-called "Israeli" liaison command in the occupied zone formed an investigation committee and subjected some of agent Karam's intimates and 20th Regiment's officers to questioning.

"The occupation considers that targeting its agent with such accuracy indicates that groups close to the collaborating militia are leaking information about "Israeli" and collaborating officers to IR.

"Israeli" liaison commander Major General Erez Gerstein was also killed in a qualitative IR operation last February".

"Meanwhile, the militia's radio threatened that residents in the occupied zone who throw any bags on the side roads will be severely punished"(3)

"On the other hand, the "Israeli" occupation prevented Father Athanasios Farhat, the Pastor of Ein Saadé, from entering his hometown Ibl Assaqi".

"Farhat wanted to head a mass in the town's church in commemoration of his brother Amine Farhat.

The "Israeli" occupation had prevented the Mufti of Marjayoun and Hasbaya Sheikh Mustafa Ghader from entering his hometown Shibaa".

"Marjayoun area residents and public figures considered the action as "Israeli" terrorism against civilians in the occupied zone"(4)

One "Israeli" soldier killed, two wounded in Resistance operation(5)
"Al-Quds-based Quds press said an "Israeli" occupation soldier was killed and two others were wounded in an attack against an "Israeli" force near the Lebanese-Palestinian border".

"The agency quoted an "Israeli" military spokesman as saying that Givati Brigade corporal Yakoub Ashkenazi, 20, was killed Sunday and two soldiers were wounded when an "Israeli" Jeep was attacked.

The "Israeli" vehicle was patrolling the Palestinian coast near the Lebanese border."

"The spokesman said a northern command investigation into the incident found out that the military Jeep has toppled as it was traveling speedily along the road."

"Israeli" Aggressions(6)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions on the Lebanese areas:
00:40, the perimeters of Haddatha town exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
00:45, one 81mm round hit the surroundings of Haddatha compound.
1:05, the outskirts of Haddatha came under four 81mm rounds.
1:10, four 120mm shells struck Haddatha forest.
6:10, the west of Yater village was shelled by seven 155mm missiles.
6:25, three 155mm missiles were fired at Jib Soweid area.
7:10, Litani River watercourse was targeted by four 155mm missiles.
14:15, Kfour ravine was bombarded by seven 155mm missiles.
16:05, the perimeters of Houla position subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations(7)
The IR team of martyr Mohammad Abbas Faqih attacked, at 00:30, an enemy engineering force as it was beefing up Haddatha compound. The militants, who used the appropriate weapons, managed to score direct hits.
