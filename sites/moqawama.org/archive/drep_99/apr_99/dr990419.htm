Daily Report
 
 
April Monday 19-1999:
 
Monitoring Group meets to discuss Arnoun the "Israeli" agents reinforce positions(1)
"A critical session of the five-nation Monitoring Group begins Monday to attempt to forge a resolution to the occupation of Arnoun by "Israel"".

"President Emile Lahoud told U.S. Ambassador David Satterfield that Lebanon rejected the incorporation of Arnoun into the occupation zone and declared it was a clear violation of the April Understanding.

The "Israelis" are seeking to play party politics with a Lebanese village and its inhabitants and sow discord between the Lebanese state and the resistance, General Lahoud said.

The president added that erecting barbed wire and earth barricades was an attempt to install a Berlin Wall in Lebanon. Prime Minister Salim Hoss told Mr. Satterfield that the move was totally unacceptable, adding that Lebanon would continue to lobby the UN Security Council for support to end the occupation of Arnoun. Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah vowed that the resistance would continue and that the annexation of Arnoun should lead to a large national, popular, Arab and Islamic rising.

"He added that the re-taking of the village came as no surprise given the brutal racist and aggressive nature of the "Israeli" enemy. Describing Mr. Satterfield as that insolent ambassador, Sayyed Nasrallah said that the U.S. had pressured the government since the liberation of Arnoun six weeks ago to deploy Lebanese troops in the village to protect the "Israeli" posts in Beaufort Castle and its surroundings.

"Lebanon has filed three complaints to the Monitoring Group which convenes at 10 A.M. Monday. The first complaint is over the annexation of Arnoun, the second for the wounding of a television sound engineer when the SLA* opened fire at reporters Friday. The third protest was lodged for the wounding of a civilian in shelling of Arab Salim Sunday.

"The U.S. has advised Lebanon to refer its displeasure over the annexation of Arnoun to the ceasefire group, rather than address the issue to the UN Security Council.

"However, given "Israel's" clear determination to hold onto Arnoun and its dislike for the strictures the April Understanding imposes on its troops, it is highly unlikely that the meeting will end with the departure of the SLA from the village. While the diplomatic arrangements continued over the weekend, the SLA militiamen now occupying Arnoun sought to consolidate their defenses.

"The SLA has taken over the house adjacent to its earthen outpost and installed machine gun posts both on the roof of the building and on the ramparts of the dugout.

"The owner of the house, Hassan Alawieh, was forced to remove his furniture and store it in a neighbors home. Mr. Alawieh then departed the village".

Kahalani says "Israel's" Army has no reason to tighten its hold on Arnoun(2)
"Internal Security Minister Avigdor Kahalani told "Israel" Radio yesterday that instead of tightening the hold on Arnoun on the edge of the security zone near the Beaufort Castle, the IDF** should be withdrawing from the area".

"I don't think the Hezbollah is in the village or is stockpiling munitions in Arnoun," Kahalani told the radio interviewer, adding that the village was not in the security zone".

Remembering those that died in Qana and their grieving families(3)
"Thousands gathered in Qana Sunday to commemorate the third anniversary of the massacre in which 107 civilians were killed during an "Israeli" bombardment of the Fijian UNIFIL headquarters.

Under a blazing sun, schoolchildren, scouts, local dignitaries, clerics, MPs and the merely curious converged for the annual tribute to the victims".

"Amid the tumult, sitting at one end of the cemetery, the survivors of the slaughter, some occasionally weeping, stared in silence at the marble tombs containing their loved ones.

"The faces of many of them have become familiar through the media: Saadallah Balhas, from Siddiqine, who lost his wife and nine children at Qana, from 30-year-old Ghaleb to four-month-old Hassan. In all, 32 members of his extended family perished.

The blast that killed his children also claimed his right eye and today he has a glass substitute. Fatmeh Borji from Rishknaniyeh, who like the Balhas clan, lost dozens of relatives in the shelling. Fatmeh Balhas, who was sitting in the Fijian officers mess with her husband Qassem Khalil, 28, her brother Abdel Karim, 16, her sons Hussein Khalil, 3, and Hassan, 2, and her 17-day-old child Mohammed. The shell that destroyed the flimsy building wiped out her entire family. She survived with a crippled arm.
Samira Hajj, who was in Beirut at the time of the bombardment, was also present Sunday to pay her respects. Samiras mother-in-law, Georgette, was the only Christian to die in the massacre. Samira prayed before a photograph of her dead relative and made the sign of the cross before joining the other women.

"The road from Tyre to Qana was festooned with commemorative banners, many written in English. "Israel" is damned forever, and U.S.A. is "Israel's" partner in bloodshed and massacres vied for space with a mathematical equation - Hizbullah style: Steadfastness + Katyusha = Victory.

"The cemetery filled with increasing numbers of mourners during the morning, with numerous delegations laying wreaths. One wreath came from the Fijian battalion. Several soldiers presently serving with UNIFIL were in Qana at the time of the massacre.

Lance Corporal Joe Thomson said the Fijians were moved on the anniversary of the slaughter.

Most of us have families and we feel tremendous sympathy for the people here, he said.

"As the commemoration attracted the mourners, so too did it draw the politicians.

Bint Jbeil MP Mohammed Fneish, a member of Hizbullah, said the victims of the massacre had inadvertently contributed to a victory by uniting the country behind the resistance.

It also contributed to a shield for civilians ? the April Understanding. "Israel" was forced to accept it after Qana, so the martyrs protected todays civilians from other massacres, he said.

Beside the cemetery, construction had halted briefly on a museum dedicated to the victims. The museum is expected to be completed by the end of the year and is one of several projects under consideration to boost awareness of what happened at Qana.

"Salah Salameh, Qanas mayor, had a slight dig at those officials eager to gain political mileage from being seen sympathizing with the grieving survivors"

Last night, I dreamt that I met the martyrs I knew, he began. They asked me, who is taking care of our children and those who were disabled? What happened to the money contributed for the benefit of our relatives? When I couldnt answer, they asked, what care was taken for our people in the new budget, what is being done to continue our memory. Again I could not answer.

"Israeli" attacks on Ashoura weekend(4)
"A civilian was wounded Sunday when the SLA shelled the village of Arab Salim shortly after their outpost at Soueidat came under assault.

The "Israeli" warplanes attack the wadi systems between Jabal Botom, Shaitieh and Zibqine".

"Jawad Musa, 35, whose left leg was amputated below the knee from an attack two years ago, suffered a fresh shrapnel wound in the upper leg during the early morning bombardment. Mr. Musa was taken to a hospital in Nabatieh for treatment."

"His house was also damaged in the shelling as was the house of his father, Mohammed, and that of his brother, Ali. Shelling also badly damaged the southern side of the village school.

"Israeli" planes Sunday fired up to 10 missiles on Zaafaran hill between Zibqine and Shaitieh, the blasts clearly audible in Tyre. Army anti-aircraft units returned fire at the warplanes but missed. Two SAM 7 rockets were reportedly launched at the planes, also unsuccessfully".

Military Operations(6)
The IR group of martyrs Rameh Mahdi and Ahmad Cherri targeted, at 6:15, Soweida outpost with the appropriate arms chalking up direct hits. Also, the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Twenty minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Adnan Khreis and Majed Jaffal attacked Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking its main fortifications.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Al-Shab and Mohammad Ramadan was attacking Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and definite goals.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Hussein Balhas and Hassan Ghamloush targeted, at 18:30, enemy movements inside Blat position with the proper weapons chalking up direct goals.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Youssef Rida Bzeih and Khodor Ibrahim Khiami attacked Radar-Bayada position with the appropriate arms. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported. Also, the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Moussa Fahs and Abdallah Rammal targeted, at 22:30, enemy movements inside Beaufort Castle. The militants managed to score direct goals.
