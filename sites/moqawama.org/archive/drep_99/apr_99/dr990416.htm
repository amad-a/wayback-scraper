Daily Report
 
 
April Friday 16-1999:
 
Introduction
Yesterday, the "Israeli" soldiers and their collaborators stormed into the village of Arnoun, on the edge of the central sector of the "Israeli"-occupied zone in South Lebanon. The "Israeli" troops fenced off the village, erected earthen barricades, destroyed the electricity pylons and prevented traffic from passing through.

Meanwhile, the Lebanese military intelligence dismantled an espionage ring of Lebanese agents working for "Israel". Sources said eight have been seized and a search continues for an unspecified number of other suspects.

"Israelis" invade Arnoun again to break Hizbullah blockade(1)
"Arnouns short-lived freedom came to an abrupt end Thursday night when "Israeli" troops backed by armored vehicles stormed the village and began erecting new fences and earthen barricades, destroying electricity pylons, and preventing traffic from passing through".

"With the Lebanese government rejecting "Israeli" demands to curb Hizbullah assaults on the troops manning Beaufort Castle, "Israel" ordered its largest incursion into the village since its liberation six weeks ago".

"However, it was still unclear late Friday night whether the incursion was a limited action in retaliation for the roadside bomb attacks earlier in the week ­ one of which killed an "Israeli" soldier ­ or a full-fledged bid to re-incorporate the village into the occupation zone".

"A senior government source said President Emile Lahoud and the government were monitoring the developments, adding that "Israel" would be held fully responsible for the consequences of this new aggression.

"An "Israeli" Army official claimed Hizbullah had imposed a blockade on the "Israeli" position in Beaufort Castle, which overlooks Arnoun".

"The foray came hours after the five-nation Monitoring Group decided to postpone until April 28 the discussion of an "Israeli" complaint over the planting of roadside bombs near Beaufort Castle. The "Israeli" incursion notwithstanding, the decision of the group appeared to suggest that Arnoun had been granted a two-week reprieve from the possibility of being re-annexed into the occupation zone".

"About 25 soldiers accompanied by two armored vehicles, a truck, a jeep and a civilian Mercedes approached the western edge of Arnoun at around 6.45 P.M. and set up a checkpoint at the entrance of the village".

"The "Israelis" stopped all vehicles traveling to and from the village and Yohmor, 2 kilometers further down the road. The only people allowed to pass through were a pregnant woman from Yohmor, Jawhar Azan, and her husband, Youssef Alleik. The couple continued to a hospital in Nabatieh.

"A earthen barricade was bulldozed across the road leading into the village and barbed wire was unfurled. Witnesses reported a tall security fence being erected at the entrance to the village.

Electricity pylons installed just a weeks ago were torn down, plunging the village into darkness.
"On hearing of the foray, Prime Minister Salim Hoss contacted U.S. Ambassador David Satterfield and French Ambassador Daniel Jouanneau to protest the "Israeli" action. He also charged the Lebanese delegation at the UN in New York to lobby UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan over the issue".

"Israeli" spy ring broken by the army(2)
"The army has cracked a spy ring of Lebanese agents working for "Israel", a communiqué from military intelligence said Thursday".

"The members of the ring were arrested after military intelligence lured an "Israeli" 504 unit intelligence agent out of the occupation zone", the statement said.

"The identity of those arrested was not released. Sources said eight have been seized and a search continues for an unspecified number of other suspects.

"The official statement said that based on the agents confessions, the army was able to track down on the rest of the ring and swiftly arrest many of them after raiding their houses.

"The army said that the arrest comes following the recent security penetrations of collaborators intelligence, which has led to the state of clear confusion the enemy and its agents are going through in the south and the western Bekaa.

Thirteen people were charged with spying and collaborating for "Israel" last month after they were arrested by the army".

"The group was uncovered after the arrest and interrogation of a collaborator officer, Mohammed Nabaa, an intelligence agent responsible for the Shebaa area in the eastern sector".

"The group was reportedly recruited by "Israel" to collect information about Lebanese and Syrian military forces, in addition to the Communist Party, Hizbullah, the PFLP and the DFLP".

Comedian exchanges laughter for tears at Qana anniversary(3)
"If the children of Qana were expecting to hear Ghawwars clogs rattling on the pavement and to laugh at the jokes of the folkloric characters creator, Doreid Lahham, they were in for a surprise".

"Syrias most famous comedian visited the southern village Thursday on the third anniversary of the April 18, 1996, massacre in his capacity as Unicef Childrens Ambassador".

"He wept as he listened to the suffering the children had gone through during and after the "Israeli" onslaught".

"After laying a wreath at the cemetery of the 107 civilians killed during the massacre, Mr. Lahham approached the children".

"He was shown one of the two buildings that took direct hits during the April aggression, killing almost everyone inside. The burnt out room has been kept as a memorial and has a sign outside it that says The Second Holocaust.

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions:
6:30, four "Israeli" gunboats were seen cruising off the seashore of Adloun town and heading for Tyre.
12:15, the surroundings of the new Houla position subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
17:25, Mount Sojod came under four 120mm shells.

Military Operations(5)
The IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Olayan and Ali Nazzal targeted, at 00:30, an enemy motorized engineering force as it was backing up Beaufort Castle with the adequate weapons scoring certain hits.
