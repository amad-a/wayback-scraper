Daily Report
 
 
April Tuesday 20-1999:

Introduction
As a result of the recent qualitative attacks staged by the Islamic Resistance (IR) men, the "Israeli" occupation forces clamped down the areas of Marjeyoun and Hasbaya. Security Sources described the measures as preventive and an attempt to halt military operations and the desertion of the collaborators to the liberated areas.

In the meantime, the Monitoring Group began yesterday morning a session to examine more "Israeli" violations of the April 1996 Understanding.

Elsewhere, the teachers and students at Arab Salim Public School staged a protest Monday to condemn ""Israel's" shelling of their school.

"Israel" forbids teenagers from serving in the Lebanese Army's conscription(1)
"The "Israeli" occupation took a series of military measures in the areas of Marjeyoun and Hasbaya. Security sources described the measures as preventive and an attempt to halt military operations and the desertion of agent Lahd's militiamen to liberated area".

"A 15-man patrol from the collaborating militia raked, for the first time, the mountain area north of Hasbaya, Hasbani area and the outskirts of the towns of Mimass and Ain Qana, the sources said.

An "Israeli" force advanced into the front-line of Janaam north of Shibaa and surveyed the Area for three hours, the sources added.

"Reports from Marjeyoun said the "Israeli" army is flying an espionage and surveillance balloon over the Marjeyoun barracks.
"Furthermore, the "Israeli" occupation issued a decree that forbids young men of 18 years of age and above from serving in the Lebanese army's conscription".

"The decree stressed that anyone who does will expose his family to severe punishment that extends to expulsion.

"The "Israeli" army decree obliges Lebanese youth in the occupied zone to join the collaborating militia instead.

"A list with the names of all young men, 18 and above, in the area was dispatched to every crossing point that links the occupied zone with the liberated areas to prevent them from leaving the occupied zone".

MONITORING GROUP DISCUSSES ""ISRAEL'S" OCCUPATION OF ARNOUN(2)
"The international panel monitoring the truce in south Lebanon is discussing Lebanese complaints over ""Israel's" occupation of the village of Arnoun and the wounding of two civilians. But observers believe that the group will be bogged down in heated debate over the issue of Arnoun's occupation".

" The cease-fire monitoring committee convened this morning at the UN headquarters in Naqoura. The Lebanese government has accused "Israel" of violating the terms of an April 1996 cease-fire agreement by occupying the southern village of Arnoun on Thursday. "Israeli" troops, backed by tanks and soldiers from the "Israeli"-allied militia had sealed all entrances to the village which lies just north of the zone "Israel" occupies in south Lebanon. They also set up a checkpoint at the entrance of the village controlling the movements of its residents. Yesterday, the "Israeli" allied militia expelled a family of four from Arnoun. They told the family that their house would be turned into a military post. Arnoun is now a part of the "Israeli" occupied zone in south Lebanon. Beirut also filed a second complaint after a Lebanese cameraman working for Abu Dhabi television Kassem Durgham was slightly wounded by a rubber bullet fired by "Israeli" troops at a group of journalists at Arnoun's entrance on Friday. The third Lebanese protest was lodged after a Lebanese civilian Jawad Moussa was wounded Sunday during an "Israeli" mortar bombardment of the village of Arab Salim located just outside the occupation zone. Jawad whose left leg was amputated below the knee from an attack two years ago suffered a fresh shrapnel wound in the upper leg during the bombardment. Shelling also badly damaged houses and the southern side of the village school. Today (yesterday), residents of Arab Salim staged a sit-in to denounce the "Israeli" aggressions. Sources expected the monitoring group to denounce "Israel" for violating the cease-fire by wounding civilians. But they expected heated discussions over the issue of Arnoun's occupation.

The truce committee, made up of representatives from France, "Israel", Lebanon, Syria and the United States, oversees the 1996 accord which commits the Jewish state and Lebanese resistance guerrillas not to target civilians or launch attacks from civilian areas. But it permits the resistance to launch military operations against "Israeli" occupation troops and their local allies.

The panel rules on complaints filed by Lebanon or "Israel" about truce violations, but has no power to act".

School protests "Israeli" attack(3)
"Teachers and students at the Arab Salim Public School in the south staged a protest Monday to condemn ""Israel's" shelling of their school."

"Dozens of students and teachers gathered in the school playground and held banners denouncing last weeks attack and calling for the support of the resistance in the face of ""Israel's" raids".

"The school suffered an estimated $3,000 damage Saturday during "Israeli" shelling on the village which hit the computer and lecture rooms, damaging equipment as well as sewage and water networks.

The army will remain a protector of the nation and a support for the brave resistance, one of the banners read.
"Officials from the region who participated in the event included Nabatieh Governor Mahmoud Mawla and the village Mayor Ismaeel Wehbi".

The residents of Arab Salim built this school with their efforts and offered it to the government. Theyre ready to rebuild it again so that it serves as an example for their steadfastness and commitment to this land, Mr. Mawla said.

"Israel" does not distinguish between a house or a school with its shells and wants to force us out of our school, 11-year-old Bilal Mohammed Zakar said.

BEILIN ACCUSES ARENS AND MOFAZ OF BLOCKING ARMY OFFICERS FROM EXPRESSING THEIR VIEWS ON SOUTH Lebanon(4)
""Israeli" Labor party deputy Yossi Beilin has accused Defense (War) Minister Moshe Arens and army chief-of-staff Shaul Mofaz of blocking top army officers from expressing their opposition to ""Israel's" occupation of southern Lebanon. Beilin said the men have prevented officers from reporting to the "Israeli" cabinet as well as parliament's defense committee "to avoid other opinions being heard". Beilin stressed that the officers in question were still serving in the force. The occupation of southern Lebanon has taken center stage in "Israeli" elections".

Military Operations(6)
The IR team of martyrs Moussa Diab Yehia and Abdel-Hassan Badran attacked, at 12:00, an "Israeli" infantry force near Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and certain hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyr Ibrahim Dakdouk targeted, at 17:00, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.

Half and hour later, the IR squad of martyr Karim Atwi targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Allam Soueidan and Yassine Ahmad Chehab attacked, at 23:10, enemy movements inside Blat post with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goals were scored.
