Daily Report
 
 
April Thursday 1-1999:
 
"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
The IR issued a statement in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
6:00, the perimeters of Baraasheit town subjected to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
7:10, Oqmata plain was targeted by six 120mm shells and one 155mm missile.
12:05, ten 155mm cluster missiles slammed into the northern peripheries of Zibqeen village.
12:20, thirteen 155mm missiles pounded into the outskirts of Jibal El-Botom village.
13:35, Hamra area was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
13:50, Litani River watercourse was bombarded by thirty four 155mm missiles.
13:55, the "Israeli" bombers dropped two air-to-ground rockets on Intari ravine between the villages of Jibal El-Botom and Yater.
Between 13:55 and 14:10, Sojod village exposed to six 155mm missiles, four 120mm shells and one 81mm round.
Between 13:55 and 15:55, Mount Sojod was bombarded by thirteen tank rounds, seven 155mm missiles, three 105mm shells and one 120mm round.
14:15, one 155mm missile struck the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom.
14:25, nine 155mm missiles fell on Litani River watercourse.
Between 14:45 and 15:05, Slouqi Valley was shelled by thirteen 155mm missiles, whereas the surroundings of Sojod outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
16:00, one 105mm round struck near a shepherd between Majdal Selem and Shaqra villages, but he miraculously escaped unscathed.
Between 18:55 and 23:10, the outskirts of Jibal El-Botom came under thirty one 15mm missiles.
19:05, the "Israeli" warplanes launched a sortie on Intara ravine near Jibal El-Botom firing ten air-to-ground rockets.
20:00, eight 155mm missiles fell on west of Yater village and five others on Hibbarieh in Hasbaya area.
23:10, Mazraat Hamra was shelled by five 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(2)
The IR team of martyrs Moussa Diab Yehia and Fadl Mohammad Atwi made, at 6:25, a swift attack on the Israeli outpost of Dabsheh striking its western fortification.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Mahmoud Kurani and Hassan Haidous targeted, at 12:00, enemy movements at Blat post with the adequate arms scoring accurate hits.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Sharif Akkash and Fadl Soweidan reiterated, at 13:20, targeting enemy movements at Blat post chalking up accurate goals. An Israeli bulldozer was defused also.

At 18:50, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Tehini and Hassan Balhas targeted an "Israel" Merkava tank at the entrance of Blat post with the appropriate arms striking it directly.
