Daily Report
 
 
April Monday 5-1999:

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
In its statement, the IR reported the following "Israeli" aggressions on the Lebanese towns and villages:
00:05, Blat post showered the adjacent ravines with a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
14:00, twenty five 160mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Qilya, Zellaya and Yohmor.
14:05, Litani River watercourse was bombarded by twenty five 155mm missiles.
Between 14:20 and 15:30, Mount Al-Rafih was shelled by seventeen 155mm missiles.
14:30, the "Israeli" bombers dropped twelve air-to-ground rockets into the area between Zellaya and Yohmor villages.
Between 15:10 and 15:20, the outskirts of Jibal El-Botom subjected to eighteen 155mm missiles.
At 15:35, 15:40 and 15:50, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the area between Zibqeen and Chaitieh villages.
15:50, the "Israeli" jets dropped two bombs into the area between the villages of Jibal El-Botom and Zibqeen.
15:40, one 81mm round landed near the houses of Habboush town inflicting the civilians: Mustafa Habib Hjeij, 6, Fatima Ali Halawani, 18, Zeinab Taleb Khdair, 7, Fatima Kassem Ayoub, 2, Moussa Ali Atriss, 40, and Latifeh Sheit, 60. At the same time, Habboush River watercourse exposed to a number of 81mm shells whereas the surroundings of Soweida outpost came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
16:00, Mazraat Hamra was bombarded by five 155mm missiles and five 120mm shells.
16:15, Arab Salim village was targeted by six 81mm rounds and several 105mm shells.

Military Operations(2)
The IR men observed, at 13:45, enemy movements inside Ahmadieh position. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyr Mohammad El-Mazbouh targeted the concerned position with the appropriate arms. Accurate hits were chalked up, the militants reported.

Twenty minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Salman and Moussa Yehia attacked enemy movements near Blat post with the proper weapons. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Soueli and Habib Tarhini made, at 16:10, a swift attack on the "Israeli" post of Dabsheh with the rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western fortifications. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it as well.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Chahrour and Ahmad El-Hariri detonated a roadside bomb as an "Israeli" foot-patrol was passing on the road leading to Beaufort Castle scoring direct goals.
