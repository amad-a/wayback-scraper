Daily Report
November Monday 22-1999:

Hizbullah pledges new tactics after pullout(1)
Hizbullahs struggle with Israel will not stop with the end of the occupation in the south, the head of the groups politburo vows.

Our struggle with Israel will continue as we see fit for the benefit of Lebanon and the liberation of Jerusalem and the Golan Heights, said Mohammed Raad, who is also a Nabatieh MP.

Speaking during a ceremony commemorating a Hizbullah martyr in the Nabatieh village of Kfar Sir, Raad said that Hizbullah would adopt new ways of fighting the Israeli enemy to pressure a withdrawal.

Speaker Nabih Berri questioned whether Israels claims regarding a withdrawal really meant peace, adding that they would not mean anything unless accompanied by security guarantees for Lebanon.

There are so many questions that come up with such an Israeli proposal, Berri told delegates from various groups at his home in Mseileh. We say that a withdrawal doesnt mean peace and a withdrawal doesnt mean that Lebanon will guard Israels border.

He accused Israel of seeking to separate the Lebanese and Syrian tracks of the peace process and to prevent Palestinian refugees from returning to their homeland.

Peace is a comprehensive issue that is directly linked to the nature of the Arab-Israeli struggle in the region, he said.

The real results of this struggle is the dispersion of Palestinians. Lebanon isnt a land without people so that it could act as a place for the settlement of another countrys people, he said.

On Saturday, Prime Minister Salim Hoss said he hoped that a withdrawal takes place through a peace settlement within the Lebanese-Syrian framework. In case of a resumption of the peace negotiations, what is stipulated by (UN Security Council) Resolution 425 will be our demand, the premier said.

Lahoud calls on nation to protect armed struggle against Israel(2)
President Emile Lahouds address to the nation on the eve of Independence Day signaled a call for political cooperation and an insistence that the armed struggle against Israel must fall under the rubric of domestic resistance groups.

The 14-minute speech, broadcast live on local television, was the presidents second major address to the country, coming four days prior to the anniversary of his much-celebrated inaugural address upon assuming office in November 1998.

Addressing the regional question, Lahoud described Israeli proposals for a unilateral withdrawal from the south outside the framework of a comprehensive peace deal as a security framework that will make Israel comfortable at our expense.

The president also reiterated his position that the government supported armed resistance against Israel by those working under the framework of the countrys resistance forces, and accused supporters of Palestinian President Yasser Arafat of sowing turmoil in Lebanon.

Any other arms, which seek to gain their legitimacy from those who have made peace with Israel at our expense  are the arms of sedition, and should be surrendered to the state, or to others waging the resistance struggle, he said.

Lahoud summed up by asserting his confidence that the country would overcome its current difficulties and challenges.

Seven people abducted by Israeli soldiers(3)
On Saturday seven people from the occupied village of Aita Shaab were reportedly abducted by Israeli soldiers and taken to the Khiam detention center, the Follow-Up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in Israeli Prisons said. The committee, which condemned the monstrous act, said that school teacher Ahmad Hussein Srour was among the detained.
