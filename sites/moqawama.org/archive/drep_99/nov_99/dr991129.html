Daily Report
November Monday 29-1999:

Israeli Aggressions:
The Israeli occupation troops staged the following aggressions:
00:45, the surroundings of Beaufort Castle exposed to 12.7 and 40mm heavy machine gun fire.
10:45, three 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Oqmata.
Between 10:45 and 14:30, eight 155mm missiles slammed into the outskirts of Mlikh village.
16:35, the surroundings of Rshaf position came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
22:00, Al-Jabbour area was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
22:20, six 81mm rounds struck west of Baraasheit, whereas the surroundings of Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun position exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
23:10, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets hit the surroundings of Sojod outpost.
23:15, Mount Sojod was bombarded by fifteen 81mm rounds and four 120mm shells.

Military Operations(2)
The IR team of martyrs Abbas Ahmad Zehri and Ali Najib Choaib attacked, at 1:05, Sojod outpost with the proper weapons. Direct and certain hits were scored.
