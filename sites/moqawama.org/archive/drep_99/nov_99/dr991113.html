Daily Report
 
November Saturday 13-1999:

Introduction
The Israeli occupation troops along with their allied militia launched the worst aggressions for several weeks. In all, the Israeli gunners fired more than 150 missiles at the areas of Iqleem Al-Toffah and western sector of the Israeli occupied zone. Also, the Israeli war jets fired around thirty air-to-ground rockets in eleven separate passes over the same area. Three civilians and a freelance photographer were wounded whereas a number of houses were damaged as well.

In retaliation for these ferocious Israeli aggressions and marking the Martyrs Day, the Islamic Resistance (IR) men attacked about twenty three outposts stretching from Kfar Houna in the northern sector to the Israeli-manned radar base in Bayada on the coast. The IR attacks resulted in wounding four collaborators as the Israeli proxy militia acknowledged.

Israeli aggressions reach a record high; 32 towns shelled and raided(1)
The Israeli occupation forces have gone too far in violating the April understanding, launching more aggressions on Lebanese liberated villages.

The Israeli bombs and air raid missiles hit most of the villages located to the north of the occupied zone between the Syrian borders and the Mediterranean sea.

Raids started at 6:45 a.m. as the occupation's warplanes fired a number of air-to-ground missiles on the outskirts of Jmeijmeh; five minutes later, another raid targeted wadi al-Qaissieh.

At 7:30, the Israeli warplanes staged a third raid on an area near the town of Khorbet Selem, another sortie targeted the woods of Chaitieh at 8:30.

At 12:30, the occupation's aircraft raided the Sojod woods; another raid, five minutes later, targeted the same wadi, while other warplanes staged two sorties on the Ain al-Smahieh area (between the towns of Zawtar al-Sharqieh and Choukeen).

The occupation's artillery bombarded 32 towns:
- (In western Beqaa): Zillaya, Qilya, Ain al-Tineh, Maidoun, and most of the area's hilltops.

- (In Nabatieh) Zawtar al-Sharqieh, Zawtar al-Gharbieh, Choukeen, Jarjoua, Yohmor, Arab Salim, Houmeen al-Fawqa, Qaaqaiyet al-Jisr, Loweizeh, Sojod woods, Mlita hilltops, al-Hamra Farmstead, al-Sawaneh, Habboush, besides the town of Nabatieh whose cemetery and city house were hit along with the houses of Hani Darweesh, Moussa Saliba, Ibrahim Fakhouri, Mohammed Abdo Bitar, Abu Qassem Lamah and Mohammed Youssef al-Shami.

A number of citizens were lightly wounded in the aggressions and treated in local dispensaries and hospitals.
In the middle sector, Israeli occupation's rounds hit Zibqeen, Majdal Zoun, al-Rmadieh, Jibal al-Botom, Yater, Haddatha, Rshaf, Baraasheit, Majdal Selem, Khorbet Selem, Siddiqeen, al-Henneih, al-Jmeijmeh and Kafra (a number of houses was damaged). Ali Moussa Yahia and his family narrowly escaped death when his house in Kafra was directly hit by an Israeli round.

Although the aggressions receded, the south and the western Beqaa remained cautious as a result of the large-scale escalation.

The atmospheres remained tense also as the enemy's warplanes and helicopters circled over the targeted areas, swooped low, and staged several mock air raids.

IR stages 24 operations and IJP3 other on occupation and militia outposts(3)
In the atmospheres of Martyr's Day and the blessed birth day of Imam Hussein (A.S.), the Islamic Resistance (IR) at 11:05 a.m. Friday launched a series of attacks and raids on a large number of the outposts and compounds of the Israeli occupation forces and their Lahdi militia along the front line.

IR men used various types of machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, clashing with the enemy troops manning the outposts and inflicting definite casualties in their ranks besides destroying the ramparts and fortifications of the targeted outposts.

The attacks were as follows:
- Squad of Prince of Martyrdom freedom fighters Ahmed Qassir attacked the Sojod outpost - clashed with the troops manning the outpost at close quarters for 15 minutes.

- Squad of Martyrs Haitham Dbouk and Abdallah Atwi attacked the Bir Kallab outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Asaad Berro and Ibrahim Daher attacked the Rihan outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Hassan Qassir and Abou Zeinab attacked enemy forces at al-Kobala Hilltop, Jabal al-Daboura  and Jabal Shmeis al-Arkoub.

- Squad of Martyrs Mostafa Mozanar and Mohammed Sawli attacked the Kfar Houna outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Mohammed Darwish and Ali Sleibi attacked the Kfar Houna outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Youssef Rihani and Hussein Qbeissi attacked the Soweida outpost, the Mujahedeen clashed with the enemy unit at close quarters.

- Squad of Martyrs Mahmoud Fayyad and Ghassan Zaatar attacked the Beaufort Castle outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Moussa Fahs and Mohammed Ramadan attacked the Dabsheh outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Issam Matar and Moussa Hamdar attacked the Taibeh outpost. The clashes went on for around 30 minutes.

- Squad of Martyrs Jaber and Abdallah Shaito attacked the Mashaaroun outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Ali Ouleiq and Mohammed Mrad attacked the Mohaisbat and Merj Houla outposts.

- Squad of Martyrs Ali al-Bostani and Mohammed Berro attacked the Baraasheit outpost at close quarters.

- Squad of Martyrs Hassan Balhas and Ibrahim Zaher attacked the collaborator's 80th regiment HQ in Jamoussa.

- Squad of Martyrs Mohammed Abbas and Ahmed Oseily attacked the Hemayed outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Ghassan Mortada and Ahmed Fouani attacked the Rshaf outpost.

- Squad of Martyrs Hassan Sherry and Hussein Tawil attacked the Radar outpost.
Other IR squads in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) pounded the enemy's artillery emplacements in Zaffateh, Shreiqeh and Abu Qamha in a concentrated and precise manner, preventing them from backing the targeted outposts.

As the enemy's warplanes tried to intervene, they were intercepted by IR's anti-aircraft units that barred them from achieving their goals.

The Israeli enemy's media and the radio of its militia acknowledged that four militiamen were wounded in IR's attacks saying that Hizbullah today (yesterday) staged the largest number of operations in a long time.

Earlier, IR's squad of Martyrs Farhan Ismail and Abbas Tleis at 00:30 Friday attacked Israeli movements in the vicinity of Chihine with rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Furthermore, IR's squad of Martyrs Abbass Mohammed Saleh and Ahmed al-Dimasi at 6:00 a.m. Friday attacked the Mashaaroun outpost with rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits, while squad of Martyrs Abdel-Latif al-Ameen and Mohammed Haballah attacked the Mohaisbat outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring definite hits.

For its part ,the Islamic Jihad in Palestine Movement (IJP) - squad of Martyr Mohammed Mahmoud Suleiman at 6:05 a.m. (Friday) attacked the Israeli occupation's outpost in Dabsheh with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, scoring definite hits, while members of the same squad attacked the Qalaah outpost with the appropriate weapons, scoring direct hits, an IJP communiqué read.

At 6:08 a.m., IJP's squad of Martyr Hassan Ghazi Abu Shamat attacked the Taibeh outpost with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades scoring definite hits.

In the meantime, men of the same squad attacked the Blat outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

Smoke billowed from the three outposts of Dabsheh, Qalaah and Taibeh, the communiqué added.

As we pledge to our proud people inside and outside Palestine, besides our good martyrs, to go ahead with confronting the Zionist enemy wherever it exists, we will perform Jihad to pave the way for the return of our proud people freely and in dignity to its usurped land, the communiqué concluded.

President Assad: Resistance may continue after Israeli unilateral withdrawal(4)
Syrian President Hafez al- Assad said the Resistance will continue following the Israeli occupation forces from south Lebanon.

Al-Hayat newspaper said al-Assad told French Foreign Minister Hubert Vedrine that Damascus will not prevent "Israel" from withdrawing but Resistance will stay in the face of the occupation.

The Israeli occupation's radio that rushed to air the news said the French diplomacy's sources in Damascus said the Syrian leadership believes that Barak is maneuvering and will, at the last moment, adjust his plans to withdraw the occupation army unilaterally from Lebanon.

Some media have been marketing the Israeli threat that told Vedrine that Barak is ready to launch a largescale aggression against Lebanon if "Israel" is attacked following the withdrawal.

As he concluded his visit to Damascus, Vedrine said the settlement process' parties are not yet ready to resume talks.
