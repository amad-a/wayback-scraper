Daily Report
 
 
November Wednesday 03-1999:
 
Introduction
The Israeli fighter-bombers raided into the ridges of Daidaba mountain in southeastern Lebanon, but no casualties were reported. Also, the Israeli warplanes broke the sound barrier over Beirut, the capital.

Meanwhile, it was reported the Israeli occupation forces employ wild boar to track down resistance fighters entering the occupied zone. The reports said that the pigs are equipped with collars containing an array of electronic equipment.

Occupation's warplanes renew attacks, throw suspicious objects(1)
Lebanese security forces have warned against suspicious objects that had been thrown by the Israeli occupation's warplanes last night and this morning in the areas adjacent to the front lines between the liberated areas and those occupied by the Israeli forces.

Such objects were discovered in the woods of Ain Bousoir, Jarjoua (Iqleem al-Toffah), and Wadi al-Qaissieh (mid sector).

Two of the occupation's warplanes at 11:25 raided the Daidaba Hilltops (western Beqaa) firing a number of air to ground missiles.

In Iqlim al-Tuffah, Monday night was one of Israeli raids and fear.

Ten raids were staged at the outskirts of Jbaa, Jarjou, woods of Sojod, Oqmata, Mount Abou Rkab.

The explosions of the enemy missiles caused damage in the neighboring villages and houses. One rocket that exploded at the entrance of Jibaa shattered the windows of tens of houses. No casualties were reported.

The enemy's artillery today (yesterday) used phosphorus incendiary bombs in shelling the Mazlam Wadi (west of Beit Leef - South of Yater, south-east Tyre).

For its part, the IR issued a statement in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions (2):
At 11:05 and 11:25, the Israeli war jets raided into Daidaba hilltops.
13:25, two 81mm rounds hit north of Haddatha compound.
15:00, the road of Jarjoua was targeted by a barrage of heavy machine gun bullets.
15:15, one 81mm round landed the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost, whereas a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck Arid El-Zennar area.
16:30, the surroundings of Rshaf post came under one 120mm shell and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
17:15, Jib Soweid area, east of Majdal Zoun, was shelled by four 155mm missiles were fired at the perimeters of Majdal Zoun.
19:15, the Israeli war jets staged a sortie into the peripheries of Jibaa.
19:25, two flare shells were unleashed over the seashore of Mansouri.
Between 20:20 and 20:30, the area between the villages of Zibqeen, Ramdieh and Jibal El-Botom was bombarded by twelve 155mm missiles.

Occupation officers check on disputing militia(3)
A number of the Israeli occupation officers checked the position's of the proxy militia in Hasbaya and held meetings with militia commanders and members amidst strict security measures that included closure of the Zimraya crossing to maintain the secrecy of the visit and its goals.

Reports said there was a clash inside the Zoghle outpost between some of the outpost's militiamen and newly-recruited Ayad al-Shufi over "regional belonging".

The occupation's officers took al-Shufi who had been detained in one of the Zoghleh outpost's room to al-Khiam detention center to investigate the reasons behind the clash, the reports added.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyr Mohammad targeted, at 14:10, a Lahdi engineering force as it was dismantling Kfar Houna crossing chalking up direct and accurate goals.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Ali Yassine and Riad Youssef Dimashq fell upon enemy movements inside Hemayed position. Using the proper weapons, the militants managed to score direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Nayef Izzeddine and Ahmad Habib Slim attacked violentely, at 17:10, Radar position with the adequate arms hitting the radars installing on the western fortification.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Hikmat Dbouk and Hassan Mohammad Jaghbir attacked, at 18:20, a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen told.

Two hours later, the IR team of martyrs Safa Naamat Baydoun and Younes Youssef El-Bazzal rushed upon Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct and definite hits.

Wild goose chase after Israels boar battalions(5)
Israels occupation of south Lebanon has generated unusual stories in the past but surely none more bizarre than a report that the Israeli Army employs wild boar to track down resistance fighters entering the zone.

The tale, carried by the respected London-based Foreign Report weekly, lends weight to the recent disclosure that the Israeli Army has been planting scare stories about Hizbullah and Iran in the foreign, Arabic and Israeli press to justify the continued occupation of the south.

Some imaginative frontline villagers have long suspected the Israelis of deliberately training wild boar to destroy their crops.

But even they doubt that Israeli troops have been scampering around the steep wadis on the edge of the zone to catch the pigs and deploy them against infiltrating guerrilla squads.

Foreign Report, admittedly with a raised eyebrow, quoted Israeli security sources as saying that captured pigs are being equipped with collars containing an array of electronic equipment.

The boar are then released back into the dense thickets along the edge of the occupation zone.

The Israelis are able to track the movements of the pigs electronically. The clincher, however, is the mini-microphone attached to the collar.

Wild boar, apparently, give a distinctive grunt when detecting the presence of humans. Thus, if Hizbullah guerrillas on an operation in the zone bump into a member of the boar battalion, the Israelis pick up the warning grunt on the microphone, check the location of the animal on the monitor and act accordingly.

In a setback to the normally well-informed Foreign Report, the publication was cited last week, along with Paris-based Al-Watan al-Arabi newspaper, as having been duped into running falsified stories by the Israeli Armys psychological warfare unit.

According to Israels Haaretz newspaper, the unit has planted stories in these publications, among others, to back military intelligence assessments that Iran is the greatest threat to Israel.

Attempting to prove the veracity of the wild boar story is a little more than a wild goose chase. Nonetheless, it neatly coincides with tales circulating in some southern villages of Hebrew-inscribed ear tags being found on wild boar killed by local farmers.

Ive heard about metal tags on their ears but nothing about collars, said Sabah Mansour, 34, in Qabrikha on the edge of the zones central sector.

Qabrikha lies beside Wadi Salouqi which is home to numerous wild boar that prey on the crops of local farmers.

A group of old men nod sagely at the mention of Israeli-trained boar destroying crops. The Israelis do this to harm our livelihoods. They leave the tags on the animals because they dont care if theyre recognized, said one man.

Although all had heard of the ear tags, none had seen them. As for the porcine patrols, it was Mohammed Darwish, who injected some reality into the yarn.

The pigs came from a farm in Israel, he said. Darwish, 70, recalled how dozens of boar had escaped from a farm in northern Galilee years ago and crossed the border into Lebanon. They bred with the local wild pig population and their offspring continue to roam the wadis of the south.

Maybe the original animals had metal tags but they must have all died by now, he said.

A reasonable explanation perhaps, but for one old man it was a little too convenient.

The Jews dont eat pork. So what were they doing rearing pigs? he asked pointedly. The plot thickens.
