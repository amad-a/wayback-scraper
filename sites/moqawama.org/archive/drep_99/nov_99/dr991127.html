Daily Report
November Saturday 27-1999:

Lahd threatened if Israel retreated unilaterally(1)
The meetings designed, to project the logistic plans for a partial withdrawal from certain areas in South Lebanon, have continued between the commands of the Israeli enemy and its proxy militia.

In this regard, a meeting, held at Marjeyoun barracks, included the so-called Commander of the Northern Region in Israeli Army Gabi Ashkinazi, Commander of Liaison Unit General Benni Ginz, Israeli agent Antoine Lahd and a number of other senior officers.

It was reported that Lahd had informed the Israeli command his objection to continue in his office in the case of an Israeli unilateral withdrawal. Also, he refused the positioning line of Saad Haddad expressing his fears that this project will made his militiamen as "scapegoats" and stressing that the Israeli retreat should be as a result of an agreement with Syria and Lebanon.
The sourceS reported that Ashkinazi tried to belittle the negative consequenceS of any potential retreat, saying that an accord will be concluded about this issue.

Israeli enemy practices its repressive actions(2)
The security forces reported that the Israeli intelligence soldiers had arrested the youngman Imad Saasouh from Hasbaya town and moved him to unknown destination.

Also, the occupation forces prevented the unarmed residents whose ages range between seventeen and thirty six-year old from leaving the Israeli occupied zone.

Israeli Aggressions(3)
The Israeli occupying troops launched the following aggressions:
17:25, a volley of heavy machine gun bullets struck the eastern perimeters of Kfar Tibneet town.
17:30, several Mirkava-tank rounds hit the house of Ahmad Nakhleh whose family were narrowly escaped death.
Between 19:30 and 22:55, Mount Sojod was bombarded by five 155m missiles and three 120mm shells.
20:25, two 120mm shells hit the perimeters of Loweizeh.
22:50, the hilltops of Ain El-Tineh and Abou Rashed were bombarded by fifteen 155 and 175mm missiles.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Ghassan Mohammad Saouli and Hassan Ali Ahmad attacked, at 12:30, enemy movements inside Jamoussa HQ of the 80th Brigade Command with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Zaatar and Sharif Akkash attacked, at 19:25, enemy military vehicles as they were entering Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and definite goals.

Moreover, the IR men spotted, at 22:30, a Lahdi engineering force as it fortifying Aramta hilltops. Then, the IR group of martyrs Taha Mortada Tahan and Issam Kamel Kansou attacked it with the rocket-propelled grenades barring it from achieving its goals.
