Daily Report
 
November Saturday 6-1999:

Introduction
An electricity network and several houses were damaged when an Israeli tank at Dabsheh outpost fired several rounds at Nabatieh El-Fawqa.

In the occupied Palestine, the Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak had reportedly endorsed a plan prepared by military commanders to withdraw from South Lebanon as a part of an agreement with Syria.

Israeli aggressions damage houses and grid(1)
An Israeli Merkava tank positioned in the occupation's outpost in Dabsheh (at around 6:00 a.m. Thursday) fired several 105mm rounds on the Manzle neighborhood and the vicinity of the Ghandour Palace in Upper Nabatieh.

One of the rounds hit a deserted house belonging to Moheiddeen Ali Moheiddeen, created a hole in its northern wall and shattered its windows.

Meanwhile, the Israelis' heavy machine-guns in the outposts of Ali al-Taher and Dabsheh raked the residential neighborhood in Upper Nabatieh. Israeli rounds hit the houses of Ghassan Assaad and Bassam Mohammed Mohsen. No casualties were reported.

The Israeli bombardment of the Manzle neighborhood damaged the district's grid. The residents called on the concerned officials and ICRC to help repair the grid and bring back the electricity.

The residents said several sniper shots were fired from the occupation's outposts against people who tried to repair the grid.

The town residents called for security forces who came to the Square and extracted a bomb with flechettes (anti-personnel bomb) that has not exploded.

The occupation artillery at 10:00 a.m. Friday bombarded the outskirts of Mlita, Daidaba, and the vicinity of the occupation's outpost in Sojod with 160 mm, 120 mm, and 105 rounds.

Moreover, the occupation's shelling of Majdal Selem this morning damaged the houses of Hussein Abed Ali Melhem, Abed Al-Aziz Hamzeh and Jawad Jaber.

ISRAELI REPORTS SAY MILITARY COMMANDERS PREPARED PLAN TO LEAVE SOUTH LEBANON(3)
Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak has reportedly endorsed a plan prepared by military commanders to withdraw from occupied southern Lebanon as part of an agreement with Syria. Israeli television gave a detailed account on the reported pullout plan. Report: Israeli Prime Minister Barak has reportedly endorsed an Israeli army plan which outlines a pullout strategy from occupied southern Lebanon in the realm of an agreement with Damascus. Israels Second Channel TV reported that the plan calls for hi-tech electronic equipment to be installed on the internationally-recognized border, with an electric fence to surround villages and towns in northern Israel. Barak has pledged to end 21 years of Israeli occupation of Lebanese territory by next July, with or without an agreement with Beirut and Damascus. The report has yet to be confirmed. The plan reportedly calls for the international panel monitoring a 1996 cease-fire understanding to execute a pullout plan. The Israeli military is also, the TV Channel claimed, preparing a pullout plan without an agreement with Syria and Lebanon.

Martyrs(4)
The IR announced, proudly, the martyrdom of Hussein Ali Mazloum and Ali Najib Medlej as they were confronting the Israeli enemy on Tuesday October 2,1999.

Hussein Ali Mazloum, born at Brital in 1971, married with four children, joined the IR in 1988, shared with several qualitative military operations and subjected to a number of military an cultural courses. He had gained several tributes from Hizbullahs Secretary General.

Ali Najib Medlej, born at Baalback in 1978, single, joined the IR in 1997 and participated with a number of military and cultural courses. He had obtained several tributes from Hizbullahs Secretary General.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Hassan El-Hoq and Fawzi Hassan Issa fell upon Radar post with the adequate arms chalking up direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Nabil Akhdar and Ali Moghnieh targeted, at 5:40, Dabsheh outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct and certain goals. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Nazar and Jawdat Olleik attacked, at 12:45, Beaufort Castle with the suitable arms. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Rifaat and Ali Olleik targeted, at 14:15, Dabsheh outpost with the proper weapons hitting its main fortification. The smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

Besides, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Harb and Soheil Diab attacked, at 19:20, a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.
