Daily Report
 
 
November Monday 1-1999:

Israeli Aggressions(1)
The Israeli occupation forces launched the following aggressions:
Between 5:30 and 5:40, the surroundings of Haddatha compound came under three 120mm shells and two 81mm rounds.
5:50, the surroundings of Baraasheit outpost exposed to two 81mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
17:30, the outskirts of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri came under 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
Between 18:35 and 19:00, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to two 120mm shells, two 81mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
20:15, five 120mm shells fell on the eastern perimeters of Dallafeh.
22:05, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal.

Military Operations(2)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Mohammad Mahdi El-Husseini and Hassan Adel El-Akhras attacked, at 15:30, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen were able to score direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Chaouki Ali El-Jawhar and Bilal Ahmad Taha fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.
