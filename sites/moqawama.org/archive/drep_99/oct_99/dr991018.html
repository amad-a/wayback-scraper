Daily Report





October Monday 18-1999:

Girl, 16, tortured by the Israeli agents(1)
A 16-year-old girl was rushed to hospital in Sidon on Sunday, one day after she was tortured by South Lebanon Army* militiamen.

Fatima Mahmoud Jaafar was beaten with rifle butts at the SLAs No. 17 detention center in Bint Jbeil.

Jaafar was abducted from her home in the occupied village of Ainata, Bint Jbeil, on Saturday and tortured for several hours, according to the Follow-Up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in Israeli Prisons. FCLD secretary-general Mohammed Safa told The Daily Star that Jaafar was beaten until she lost consciousness.

An International Committee for the Red Cross ambulance rushed Jaafar to the Sheaib Hospital in Sidon, Safa said, adding that the girls skull was fractured. She also experienced memory loss.

Jaafars relatives, who were waiting nervously at the hospital ward, refused to talk to the press. Several residents from the zone also refused to comment, fearing punishment from the SLA. Psychiatrist Hassan Toufeili, who examined Jaafar, said she arrived in a serious condition and had suffered a nervous breakdown.

He said that she had endured a similar experience a year ago, when she was also abducted and beaten by SLA men.

Prime Minister Salim Hoss office called the hospital on Sunday afternoon and informed them that the Health Ministry would pay her medical expenses.

Fatima is not the only member of the Jaafar family who has suffered from SLA abuse.

Friends close to the family said that her sister, Ghinwa, was released from Khiam on Sunday after eight days of detention. Ghinwa, 24, reportedly was hemorrhaging and needed immediate hospitalization. However, SLA militiamen have prevented Ghinwa from leaving Ainata.

The friends said two SLA militiamen, Jamal Khanafer and Ali Khalil, were responsible for the recent village abductions. Jaafars two brothers, Bilal, 19, and Ahmad, 20, were detained by the SLA two weeks ago and are still being held. Safa said eight other Ainata residents remain in detention.

Lebanese lose out in latest release deal(2)
Forty-one Lebanese detainees remain in Israeli prisons, a follow-up group announced on Sunday, in the wake of conflicting reports about the presence of Lebanese among those released as part of an Israeli-Palestinian agreement.

The Follow-Up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in Israeli Prisons said that 10 of the 151 prisoners released by Israel were Palestinians whose families live in refugee camps in Lebanon.

On Friday, Israel freed 151 Arab prisoners as part of the latest interim peace agreement it signed with the Palestinians. Among those freed were 109 Palestinians and 42 Arabs of other nationalities.

Committee members said in a written statement that while they were pleased that the detainees were released, they were also disappointed that the Palestinian Authority did not lobby for the release of Lebanese held by the Jewish state.

Some of these Lebanese detainees, most of whom were defenders of the Palestinian cause, have been imprisoned for more than 25 years, the statement said.

 In a letter sent to the Follow-Up Committee, Qantar said that the majority of the Arab detainees were liberated except for the Lebanese, including himself. He said that tensions were high in Israeli prisons and asked the Follow-up Committee to continue its support of them and denounce the Palestinian Authoritys neglect of Lebanese detainees.

Qantar confirmed that they are going to participate in the hunger strike on Oct. 28, on an international day calling for the closing of the Khiam detention center, located in occupied Lebanese territory.

Twelve Syrian detainees from the Golan Heights were also denied release, according to Syrian prisoner Shakib Abu Jabal, who was released on Friday after 12 years of detention.

The Follow-Up Committee promised Lebanese, Palestinian and other Arab detainees to strengthen international cooperation in a bid to close the Khiam facility, as well as other Israeli prisons.

15:20, five 155mm missiles struck Mount Sojod.
18:10, the surroundings of Sojod outpost came under six 60mm rounds.
21:20, Mount Zaatar was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
21:55, four 60mm rounds landed Mount Sojod.
23:45, the perimeters of Shaqra village exposed to four 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Ahmad Mohammad Hassan Kamal and Ali Ghaleb Ali Ahmad targeted, at 15:15, an Israeli infantry force at Rihan heights with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were scored, the mujahedeen told.

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Ayoub Nassereddine and Hussein Mohammad El-Tawil was attacking Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up certain hits.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Mahmoud Baghdadi and Sheikh Ibrahim Morad attacked, at 21:45, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.

However, Amal Movement targeted, between 20:50 and 23:25, Baraasheit and Rshaf outposts with several artillery shells.
