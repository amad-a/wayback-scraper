Daily Report




October Tuesday 26-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah has dismissed as media rhetoric the Israeli statements about a partial withdrawal from Hasbaya area.

In the occupied Palestine, a parliamentary lobby, including MKs from both left and right wings, has been set up to campaign for a unilateral withdrawal from Lebanon.

Talk about withdrawal "media rhetoric", Hizbullah SG(1)
Hizbullah Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah has dismissed as "media rhetoric" the Israeli statements about a partial withdrawal from Hasbaya.

Speaking at the Engineers' Eighth Conference held yesterday at the Jinaan Hall, Sayyed Nasrallah talked about the current situation in Lebanon and the region.

The enemy is still looking for ways -political and military- to get rid of the quagmire of its occupation in Lebanon, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding that the occupation command, in spite of the confusion it is confronting, is still examining the unilateral withdrawal option.

"We are now in a position at which the occupation cannot impose its conditions on us. It will get desperate in this regard. It is no longer enough to have the occupiers' withdraw unconditionally to relieve the occupation of its dilemma, the secretary-general told the rally.

"The occupation's field measures and attempts to limit casualties in its ranks, especially the air raids method, will fall, just like the earlier measures did. This method has not affected, and will not affect, the Resistance men's morale."

The battle with the Israeli occupation is open until it comes out of our land in humiliation, his Eminence concluded.

The Engineers' conference aimed also to honor Dr Ghassan Qansou, a famous Lebanese scholar. The ceremony was attended by Transportation Minister Najib Miqati, Engineers' Chief Samir Domet, and Vice-President of the Moslem Engineers' Association's Administrative Council.

 Israeli allied militia releases four from Khiam prison(2)
The collaborators released four detainees from Khiam on Monday, and promptly expelled two of them from the occupation zone.

Zaki Awada from the village of Khiam and Mounif Hammoud from Kfar Kila, were escorted by an Israeli agent vehicle toward the Kfar Tibnit crossing. Once they reached a Lebanese army checkpoint, Awada, 66, and Hammoud, 35, took another car toward Beirut, where they are to stay with relatives. The other two, Simon Saade and Ghassan Issa, were allowed to return to their villages of Marjeyoun and Maroun al-Ras respectively.

Speaking shortly after his release, a pale and weak Awada said he was detained for four months. My 27-year-old daughter Rana is still in Khiam. She was detained two months before I was. My wife is still in the village alone and now I cant stay there, said Awada, who almost collapsed in front of reporters and had to be held up by two men.

Awada, who said he has heart problems, said he did not know why he was detained by the collaborators. Hammoud, however, said it was the fourth time he had been detained in the Khiam prison in southern Lebanon. He was twice detained in 1985, once in 1991 and once in 1998.

During his last detention, Hammoud said he spent one year in Khiam.

They accused me of being a member of Hizbullah and of collaborating with the Lebanese security agencies, he said. Hammoud said he was forced to sign a release form and was told to leave the occupation zone.

Since 1985, more than 120 Lebanese have been detained at Khiam without trial or charges. The prison is staffed by the Israels militia and is under Israeli supervision.

Forty-two Lebanese are also held in prisons inside Israel. Some have been held for more than 10 years without trial. Half of them have had their administrative detention renewed every six months.

Human rights organizations, including Amnesty International and Human Rights Watch, repeatedly condemn cases of torture and ill-treatment of Lebanese detainees.

Israeli authorities say they are holding the Lebanese detainees as hostages to swap them for missing Israelis in Lebanon.

Israeli Aggressions(3)
The Israeli occupation troops staged the following aggressions against the Lebanese liberated areas:
1:10, six 81mm rounds struck Arid El-Zennar area, whereas the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine bun bullets.
Between 1:15 and 1:25, the perimeters of Jibaa town came under four 155mm missiles.
6:15, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds hit the surroundings of Salaah and Qsair positions.
6:45, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by six 155mm missiles, whereas the southern neighborhood of Khorbet Selem town was targeted by two others.
12:25, three 120mm shells landed Mount Bassil.
18:45, two flare shells were unleahsed over the seashore of Qlaileh.
21:45, an Israeli gunboat fired one flare shell over the coast of Mansouri.
22:55, four 155mm missiles landed the perimeters of Mlikh.
23:00, Mount Kabir near Siddiqeen village was bombarded by fourteen 155mm missiles.
23:30, three 120mm shells slammed into Salhani area.
23:55, three 155mm missiles hit Mount Zaatar.

Military Operations(4)
The IR team of martyrs Mohammad Khalil Abbas and Ghassan Hassan Zein targeted, at 22:30, Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals.

The fighters of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) bombarded, at 6:25, Mashaaroun outpost with the artillery mortars scoring direct hits.

At the same time, another group of the LRB attacked Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the fighters told.

For its part, Amal Movement fired three 107mm rocket-propelled grenades at Salaah position and two others at Qsair post.

Likud MK sets up Lebanon lobby(5)
A parliamentary lobby has been set up to campaign for a unilateral withdrawal from Lebanon, Likud MK Danny Naveh yesterday revealed at a meeting with members of the Four Mothers movement. The lobby will include MKs from both left and right, Naveh told Ha'aretz.

Naveh does not believe the withdrawal should be linked to any sort of political agreement with the Syrians. He opposes an Israeli withdrawal from the entire Golan Heights, but believes that creating a link between Lebanon and a political agreement will actually weaken Israels bargaining power in the negotiations.

Naveh said that the Likud government made a mistake by not pulling out of Lebanon, something that he had expressed in his capacity as government secretary. He explains the apparent contradiction between his hawkishness and his support for the withdrawal by saying that the matter is not an ideological one. He believes that the Israeli army can defend the northern border just as well from the international line.
