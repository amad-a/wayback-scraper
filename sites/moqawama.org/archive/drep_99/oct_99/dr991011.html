Daily Report




October Monday 11-1999:

Israel will pull out even with no agreement(1)
The July deadline for an Israeli troop withdrawal from the south appears to be hardening with Israels tourism minister stating on Sunday that a pull out would occur even if there was no agreement with Syria and Lebanon.

Amnon Shahak, a former army chief of staff, said that Israeli Premier Ehud Barak had made it clear that a withdrawal would occur next July.

Certainly, an agreement would be best. But if theres no deal, we have to (pull out) as quickly as possible, he said on Israel Radio.

In January 1998, during his tenure as chief of staff, Shahak famously said that the Israeli Army was prepared to remain in Lebanon for another 1,000 years if no security deal could be arranged with Syria.

But Shahak said that a unilateral withdrawal would not stop Israel from staging reprisal raids into Lebanon if cross-border attacks occurred.

The Israeli army has to be prepared not only for the defense of the towns in the north but also for responding, inside Lebanon, if hostile activities from Lebanese territory are directed at Israeli territory, Shahak said.

The Israeli Armys general staff held its first meeting Sunday to examine options for a withdrawal, either in line with an agreement or unilaterally.

A report on Israel Radio said that the army was looking at areas of deployment on the Israeli side of the border and would consider the possibility of relocating the families of some 2,000 members of the SLA* militia to Israel or other countries.

However, Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general Sheikh Nahim Qassem argued that the Israelis would not withdraw unilaterally.

There has not been a single Israeli sentence that gives a clear suggestion and confirmed commitment on a unilateral withdrawal.

When Barak suggested that Israel would withdrawal by July 2000, he said that with the hope of reaching an agreement. This is why it is still unlikely but no one will stop it in case it happens, he said at a seminar in Sidon on Saturday.

Israeli Aggressions(2)
The Israeli occupying troops staged the following aggressions:
6:35, the western perimeters of Baraasheit town exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
9:25, Mount Sojod was targeted by two 120mm shells.
10:20, five 155mm missiles fell on Qaissieh Valley.
10:25, Qaissieh Valley and the perimeters of Majdal Selem town came under six 155mm missiles.
13:45, the west of Yater was bombarded by seven 155mm missiles.

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Abdel-Mawla Hussein Ismail and Ali Wahab Hijazi attacked, at 9:50, enemy movements at Jamoussa position of Lahdi 80th Brigade Command with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Mshawarb and Hussein Zahreddine targeted, at 16:30, an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain hits.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Mohammad Chokair and Abdel-Menhem Qassir fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Berrou and Ali Mohammad Serhal rushed upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

Fifteen minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Monib Jalloul and Imad Ali Balhas attacked Kfar Houna position with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

For its part, the groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 6:30, the positions of Soweida, Zaffateh and Tohorra with the rocket-propelled grenades and mortar rounds scoring direct hits.

Three hours later, another group of the LRB rushed upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.
