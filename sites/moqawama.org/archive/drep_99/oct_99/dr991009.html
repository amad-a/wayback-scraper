Daily Report


October Saturday 9-1999:

Introduction
The "Israeli" enemy has unveiled that the so-called head of "Israel's" liaison unit in South Lebanon General Beni Gantz was among the "Israeli" patrol which came under the attack staged by the Islamic Resistance (IR) men at Rihan area in the Central Sector of the occupied zone. It is to be mentioned that his predecessor Erez Gerstain was killed in a similar attack by the IR last year in the occupied Hasbaya region.

However, Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said the "Israeli" enemy's idea to intensify aircraft aggressions was old, but the enemy wants to make it as if there were a new method.

"Israeli" general was targeted by IR operation Thursday(1)
"The "Israeli" enemy media unveiled that "Israeli" liaison unit commander Gen Beni Gantz was in the command convoy that was targeted by IR's ambush Thursday afternoon at the Rihan barracks's entrance (mid-sector).

"The enemy's television (Channel 1) said the "Israeli" general and those accompanying him were showered by "hell-like fire" that targeted the general's car and the road leading to the outpost.

"As the "Israeli" Television did not talk about Gen Gantz condition, it unveiled that the occupation's command is conducting investigations to find out whether the resistance had prior information on the movements of the general who - the television said - was inspecting the Rihan barracks position when he was taken aback and almost killed by the Resistance men".

Raids won't achieve goals, Resistance is proceeding, Hizbullah(2)
"Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem said the "Israeli" enemy's idea to intensify aircraft aggressions was old, but the enemy wants to portray it as if there were a new method.

"The enemy wants to achieve 3 goals:
1-To try to cut the roads used by the resistance men and to target their potential outposts in a bid to inflict casualties in their ranks.

2-To give a dose of morale to the "Israeli" army and the "Israeli" interior and make them feel that the occupation army is in control, as the talk of "Israeli" media is about the heaviness of fire. This may insinuate strength, control and holding the initiative on the grounds that a lot of noise may produce a certain impact. It also may ease the demands calling for measures as they (the occupation forces) are doing something new.

3- To cover up partial withdrawals and make them look as part and parcel of the military plan which has been escalated as the aircraft is bombarding areas on the one hand, while there are withdrawal tactics from some outposts on the other. This would make an integral picture: field measures without the other party winning while covering up the helplessness which has taken its toll on the "Israeli" army's morale plus silencing internal political demands for withdrawal.

Speaking in a press interview, Sheikh Qassem added, "We believe that the enemy inevitably thinks of different methods to stage its aggressions, but for us, as a resistance that inflicts field losses in the ranks of the enemy and defends its people, nothing has changed. Resistance intensifies or decreases according to field circumstances. In all cases, we deal with the outcome and confront it accordingly. We do not care that much about analysis and statements."

"The Islamic Resistance is proceeding with its plans to achieve its goals. Yesterday's achievement in Rihan reaffirm the strong resolve to prevent the enemy from achieving its goals and to inflict maximum casualties in the ranks of the occupiers", his Eminence concluded.

We will not stop Resistance, Hoss tells BBC(3)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss reiterated Lebanon's support for the Resistance at the political and immaterial levels as long as there is "Israeli" occupation on our land.

"In an interview with BBC, the premier denied that anyone has proposed stopping the Resistance as a precondition for the resumption of the settlement negotiations.

There isn't such a condition. And we do not accept it anyway, he said, adding, "as long as Lebanese territories are occupied, there will be resistance."

"Dr. al-Hoss stressed that it is not in the interest of Lebanon to stop the Resistance.

Asked why Lebanon is not giving clear position regarding the situation after the Zionist occupation's withdrawal, al-Hoss asked, "Is it reasonable for us to uncover all our security views before the negotiations? If we engage in these details, what will the negotiations tackle?

"The prime minister also stressed the inseparability of the Lebanese and Syrian tracks".

"ISRAEL" FIRES AT JOURNALISTS(4)
" Lebanese journalists were the latest target of "Israeli" gunners in southern Lebanon. Occupation forces this morning opened their sniper fire on a group of journalists that was inspecting the aftermath of overnight "Israeli" bombardment of the front-line village of Kfar Tibneet. Journalists scrambled to take cover and sniper fire rained down close to them. The car of a journalist was shot at. "Israel" last night pounded Kfar Tibneet with some 20 shells, damaging four homes and three cars, after it mounted tension in southern Lebanon this week, injuring three civilians".

WORLD DAY FOR THE CLOSURE OF KHYAM DETENTION CAMP: OCTOBER 28(5)
"Lebanon's education ministry has called on teachers and students to take part in a rally infront of UN House in Beirut on November 8 to express solidarity with Lebanese detained in "Israeli" detention camps. Mothers of Lebanese in "Israeli" camps have announced, they will go on a hunger strike on October 28 in front of the UN headquarters in Beirut. A human rights group called for that day to be a World Day for the closure of the Khiam camp in occupied southern Lebanon.

" Some 144 Lebanese, of all ages, men and women, are being held in this detention camp in occupied southern Lebanon, by "Israeli" occupation troops and their allied militia. Fourteen Lebanese have died under torture in Khiam. The rest have been held, some of them for 15 years, without trial or charge. The head of the committee for the support of detainees in Khiam, Mohammed Safa, has appealed to international human rights organizations and activists against torture and human rights abuses to join Lebanese in their protest against "Israel's" detention of innocent Lebanese civilians. He described Khiam as the worst and most isolated prison in the world. Safa appealed for worldwide support on October 28 to highlight the plight of Lebanese detainees and to pressure "Israel" to release them and close down the notorious detention camp. Thursday's Mothers, a group of relatives who also rally support for detainees, called for a hunger strike on the same day in front of UN headquarters in downtown Beirut as a sign of solidarity with the detainees. They issued a statement calling on supporters who could not join them to send messages of protest to the UN Middle East commission instead. Khiam is off limits to journalists and human rights organizations, many of which, including Amnesty International, have repeatedly criticized its conditions and the use of torture. It was also closed to relatives of detainees and to the International Committee of the Red Cross until 1995. The camp is run by "Israeli"-backed militiamen, under the guidance of "Israeli" occupation officers who carry out interrogations and order torture".
