Daily Report


October Tuesday 5-1999:

"Israeli" allied militia solicits a protection(1)
"Several senior South Lebanese Army* commanders have recently requested that "Israel" take steps to ensure that the SLA not be dismantled, in order to guarantee that they and their troops are not abandoned if and when the IDF** withdraws from southern Lebanon".

"The renewed demand has surprised the defense establishment. The current SLA policy has been to ensure that "Israel" will not abandon them to their fate, and that the well-being of the SLA and their families will be assured.

"Many of those serving in the SLA are former members of Lebanon's armed forces. Many of them have been court-martialed in abstentia and sentenced to long prison terms and even death. Their main concern until now has been to ensure that once the security zone returns to Lebanese control, their sentences will be annulled by some form of amnesty or pardon.

"SLA General Antoine Lahad said that the growing unrest and uneasiness is having an impact on SLA morale. "We are talking about people who have been fighting side-by-side with the "Israelis" for the past 23 years not because of anyone's nice eyes, but to protect their own homes, families and dignity. If they believe they are about to be sold down the river, they may become desperate, and I do not know how they will react under such circumstances".

Three Lebanese join the list of hostages in "Israeli" prisons(2)
"The number of Lebanese detained by the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators has risen as the proxy militia arrested three citizens in the occupied zone.

"The detainees Hussein Ahmed Semhat, 39, his wife Najwa Hussein Semhat, 36, and their son Ahmed, 15, are from Ainata. The militia took the father and the son to the notorious al-Khiam detention center; the fate of the wife is still unknown.

"Their detention came as the occupation and its collaborators stormed the town of Bint Jbeil following the killing of agent Fawzi al-Sagheer last week.".

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupation forces launched the following aggressions:
00:55, the surroundings of Soweida outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
1:15, two 155mm missiles fell on the outskirts of Aqaba.
5:00, Mount Abou Rkab was targeted by a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Hassan Khalil Cherri and Abdel-Melhem Qassir attacked fiercely, at 12:10, the "Israeli" post of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Berrou and Ali Mohammad Serhal rushed upon Salaah position with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Imad Ali Balhas and Hassan Youssef Balhas targeted, at 15:30, Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to hit a 12.7mm heavy machine gun and the northern fortification.
