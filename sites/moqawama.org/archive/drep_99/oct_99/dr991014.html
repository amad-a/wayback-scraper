Daily Report




October Thursday 14-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullahs Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said the party has lined up several options should Israel withdraw from South Lebanon, but that it was not ready, to divulge them yet.

However, the collaborator militias chief agent Antoine Lahd has broken silence and said that he is not a toy in the hands of the occupation adding that he may render his resignation if the Israeli Army proceeds with planning a withdrawal without taking into account the situation of the Lahd militiamen.

Meanwhile, Israel and its allied militia are reportedly preparing for a pullout from the occupied Hasbaya in the Eastern Sector of the occupied zone.

Hizbullah has options prepared for peace(1)
Hizbullahs deputy secretary general, Nahim Qassem Wednesday said the party has lined up several options should Israel withdraw from south Lebanon, but that it was not ready to divulge them yet.

The partys cadres have adopted several alternatives after studying this issue extensively, Qassem said during a televised interview. But unveiling them to the public, to the Israelis or to others would leave Hizbullah exposed.

He said that war strategies required tricking the enemy while concealing ones ideas and plans for the future. Qassem said that because Hizbullah was a dogmatic, political, social and cultural party that was present in Parliament and among the people, it would not be dismantled, even if a peace settlement is reached.

Hizbullah will remain after any settlement, Qassem said. Those who think Hizbullahs survival is threatened by to the possibility of a peace settlement are misled, as the future will prove.

He added that although Hizbullahs circumstances, and performance may weaken or strengthen, it would always remain a viable organization.

Hizbullah will deal with a settlement accordingly, if it happens, he said. But this does not mean that Hizbullah will be abolished or has become trapped because it is rooted in the people and the internal political, social, struggles and cultural spheres.

Although Hizbullahs priorities are centered on the Israeli occupation, Qassem said Hizbullah did not exist simply because of the Jewish state. Hizbullah was created in 1982, after the Israeli invasion of Lebanon.

Sick Lahd threatens to resign(2)
The collaborator militia's chief agent Antoine Lahd has broken the silence unveiling the proxy militia's state of collapse.

Yesterday, Lahd said he is not a toy in the hands of the occupation adding that he may render his resignation if the Israeli army proceeds with planning a withdrawal without taking into account the situation of the Lahd militiamen.

The enemy's television which relayed Lahd's comments said the collaborators' chief has been admitted to an Israeli hospital following the deterioration of his physical and psychological status.

Lahd wanted to meet the occupation's coordinator in Lebanon Uri Lubrani urgently to know the date of withdrawal and the occupation army's field plans.

The Israeli television said he wanted the Israeli leaders to know that he is extremely worried that the Jezzine experience be repeated when his militiamen were roasted by the Islamic Resistance which forced the collaborators to dynamite artillery, equipment and ammunition for not being able to pull them out.

It is clear now that a large portion of the Hasbaya district collaborators (40%) wants to stay in the area (due to the Jezzine experience); 35% of the collaborators said they wanted to immigrate to Europe or America, while 25% said they prefer to withdraw with the militia to the villages adjacent to the international border (Marjeyoun and Bint Jbeil), the area to which the occupation army will retreat from Hasbaya and Rihan after dismantling equipment and barracks.

This dismantling process is almost over, reports said, adding that the occupation is practically outside the areas in which its existence is restricted to ambushes and guarding points.

These measures are part Of the occupation's plan to achieve a quiet withdrawal that would spare the retreating troops and militiamen the fire of the resistance men.

Within this famework, political sources in Beirut said the Americans have raised this withdrawal with the Lebanese officials to know the measures that Lebanon will take.

The sources said the Lebanese officials are concerned with protecting their citizens and land, and "the Jezzine experience will be repeated in Hasbaya or other areas from which the occupation will pullout."

HASBAYA ALMOST FREE OF OCCUPATION FORCES: REPORT(3)
Israel and its allied militia are reportedly preparing for a pullout from occupied Hasbaya in the eastern sector of the occupied zone. One source claimed the region is almost free of Israeli forces. But the head of Israels allied militia, Antoine Lahd, warned Prime Minister Ehud Barak against what he said are preparations for a unilateral withdrawal from southern Lebanon.

There are conflicting reports emerging from occupied Hasbaya, an area in the eastern sector of the zone Israel has been occupying in southern Lebanon. A source inside the zone was quoted as saying Israel and its allied militia have pulled out almost all their forces and military equipment from the region. Another source was quoted as saying Israel has reinforced a major occupation encampment at Zimraya, at proximity from areas free of Israeli occupation. Israeli allied militiamen are reportedly fearful of major resistance attacks prior to their pullout. What was described as a reliable diplomatic source in Beirut today, was reported as saying, Lebanon has been quizzed on how it would react following an Israeli withdrawal from Hasbaya. A Lebanese official who was not named was quoted by the daily Safir as saying the United States discussed the issue with Lebanon. Beirut has reportedly made clear it has a vested interest in protecting its citizens and territory, but stressed the government will not be drawn into any unfavorable situation. Following a withdrawal, the official added, Hasbaya's status will be similar to that of Jezzine. The Lebanese Army has not moved into Jezzine following a June withdrawal from Israel, but police units have been deployed in the area. Occupation forces still in Hasbaya, according to sources quoted in Beirut, have reportedly been put on full alert, following news that quote armed groups have infiltrated the area. Talk of an Israeli withdrawal from Lebanon has not been well received by Antoine Lahd, the head of Israels proxy force in the south. He has been furious at remarks by officials close to Prime Minister Ehud Barak that Israel will withdraw unilaterally as early as next January, without an agreement with Beirut, if peace talks falter. Lahd told Israeli newspapers such statements could send his fighters right to Hizbullah resistance ranks. Israel has created Lahd's forces, which comprises some 5000 fighters, trained, financed, and controlled, by occupation forces. Yedioth Ahranot said Lahd has hinted he may resign, adding a crisis could occur in militia ranks if fighters believe Israel is about to withdraw from the south and quote abandon them. It quoted the head of the militia, who has been sentenced to death in Beirut for collaborating with Israel, as saying he is not a "pawn in Israels game". Israeli television reported last night, Lahd is in a hospital in the Jewish state, recuperating from physical psychological ailments. Late this afternoon, a Beirut radio station reported that the so-called head of militia security in the occupied Hasbaya, Alameddine Baddawi, has left the region for an undisclosed location. There has been no confirmation of the report.

Hizbullah: Israeli talk about new arms aims to justify current and future aggressions(4)
Hizbullah has reiterated denial of the report published by Israeli daily Yediot Ahronot concerning new arms received by the party.

The news is baseless; it is in the context of Israeli repeated lies that aim to justify the current and future aggressions staged by "Israel" against Lebanon, a senior Hizbullah source said.

The Hizbullah Secretary General's statement on new weapons in the possession of the party was not a new position, for the Resistance and the party have always affirmed the existence of these weapons that can be used at any time in retaliation for any Israeli aggression against Lebanon, the source added.

The atmospheres were raised by the Israeli daily's report in which it claimed that Hizbullah had received, through the Beirut Airport, arms shipments and that the Israeli officials are concerned about Hizbullah's capabilities.

Lebanese Defense Minister Ghazi Zaiter dismissed the reports as "baseless".

The Beirut Airport has never witnessed any arms movement processes, Zaiter said.

ISRAEL DESTROYS HOMES AND CARS IN SOUTH LEBANON
Israel intensified its attacks on civilian targets in southern Lebanon today (yesterday)). Israels artillery shelled an area stretching between Habboush and Arab Salim in Iqleem Toffah damaging two cars. Israeli shelling north of the western sector of the occupied zone also damaged five houses in Majdal Zoun(5)

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it mentioned the following Israeli aggressions (6):
At 00:05 and 2:35, Slouqi valley was targeted by 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
3:00, an Israeli war boat fired heavy machine gun bullets at the seashore between Simmahieh and Ras El-Ain areas.
6:30, the Israeli war jets raided into the perimeters of Froun village firing two air-to-grounds releasing anti-missile flares and dropped several flare shells over Hojeir area. Meanwhile, seven tank rounds struck the peripheries of Majdal Zoun.
6:35, Jib Soweid area was shelled by four missiles, whereas the perimeters of Majdal Zoun exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
6:40, five 155mm missiles landed Roweisset Tayaran area.
18:55, five 155mm missiles slammed into the west of Yater village.
Between 20:00 and 22:10, the Israeli fighter-bombers staged seven sorties into Litani River watercourse firing twenty air-to-ground missiles.
20:45, Mount Sojod was targeted by five 120mm shells.
22:00, the Israeli war jets raided into Yohmor village destroying the house of Abou Mohammad Assaad Abbas.
23:40, seven 155mm missiles slammed landed the peripheries of Jibaa.

Military Operations(7)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Hassan Hijazi and Abdel-Menhem Qassir targeted, at 8:00, enemy movements near Dabsheh outpost with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Imad and Hassan Balhas was attacking a Radar installation near Dabsheh outpost with the adequate arms. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Also, the IR men observed, at 19:45, enemy movements near Zimraya position. Shortly afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Faisal Melhem in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted them with the appropriate arms chalking up direct and accurate hits.

Furthermore, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Zahreddine and Mohammad Ali Darwish attacked, at 20:35, a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was entering Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Rifaat Olleik and Najwan Hemadeh targeted, at 23:15, an Israeli motorized force at Rihan hilltops with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct and certain goals.
