Daily Report




October Saturday 16-1999:

Girl wounded by Israeli aggressions: 4 raids, artillery bombardment(1)
A little girl was wounded in the Israeli raids that targeted Lebanese villages adjacent to the occupied zone.
Following a short halt, Israeli occupation's air force resumed launching aggressions against Lebanese areas. Enemy warplanes Thursday night raided the outskirts of Zibqeen firing two air-to-ground missiles.

Farah Youssef Bzeih was wounded when one of the missiles fired by the enemy aircraft exploded near her parents' house.
At around 19:00, Israeli warplanes raided the Sojod woods; another sortie at 19:20 targeted the Nabeh al-Tasseh gorge. Two air-to-ground missiles were fired in each of the two raids. No casualties were reported.

Today (yesterday), enemy aircraft at around 15:00 Friday raided the outskirts of Loweizeh (Iqleem al-Toffah) with a number of missiles.

The occupation's artillery all night long (Thursday) pounded the woods of Sojod-Rihan, al-Aishieh, al-Jarmaq and the foot of Mount Hermon.

For its part, the Israeli occupying troops staged the following Israeli aggressions (2):
6:30, the perimeters of Haddatha town came under ten 155mm missiles.
15:15, the Israeli war jets raided into the peripheries of Loweizeh village.

Military Operations
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Salah Fares and Abdallah Ismail attacked, at 17:45, an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were chalked up, the militants reported.

Meanwhile, the IR unit of martyrs Adnan Khreis and Mohammad Ali Nasser was targeting an enemy infantry force as it was moving at Rihan hilltops. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct and definite hits.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Salem Halawi and Hassan Haidar Chokair fell upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits(3)

For its part, the Lebanese National Resistance Front (LNRF) at dawn (Friday) carried out a complex qualitative operation against the outpost of the Israeli occupation and its collaborators in Shwayya (at the western foot of Mount Hermon), an LNRF communiqué read.

Squad of Arqoub Martyrs of Martyr George Nasrallah's Unit attacked the outpost and clashed with the troops manning it for 30 minutes with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, LNRF added.

It said that following the retreat of the first LNRF squad, another squad attacked the outpost with missiles scoring accurate hits.

"The confrontations intensified as the occupation forces and those of the proxy militia in the outposts of Ain Qinia and Zaghleh intervened. The occupation forces raked the near areas and lit up the Mount Hermon foot and the surrounding wadis with flare shells, the communiqué concluded, affirming that LNRF men returned to their bases safely(4)

Preparation for an Israeli withdrawal mounted(5)
A senior Israeli officer was quoted as saying Israel is building a new post for its allied militiamen at a cost of 100,000 US dollars. The unidentified official said the post is on the northern border of the eastern sector of the occupied zone. Israel is strengthening its allied militia as tension and uncertainty rises in the zone as a result of Prime Minister Ehud Barak's promise to withdraw Israeli troops from Lebanon by July 2000. Israeli officials said, the Jewish state increased the salaries of militiamen by 50 US dollars at the beginning of October. Militia men on the front line now receive 500 US dollars a month while others receive some 400 US dollars. Military officials in Israel were quoted as saying many of their proxy fighters who fear for their future if the Israeli army withdraws unilaterally are trying to get foreign passports and to seek asylum in Israel.

One militia officer warned, according to AFP, that if Israel does not secure the safety of the force, then fighters will join Hizbullah, which is the main group battling Israeli occupation. The militia, which was created by Israel, has some 2,500 men in its force. Israeli media reported last week that 500 men had already been offered asylum in Israel in the case of a unilateral withdrawal, but there has been no official confirmation of the report. Eyewitness reports of preparations for an Israeli withdrawal from parts of the occupied zone -- in particular Hasbaya, in the eastern sector -- have been mounting in the last three days.
