Daily Report


October Wednesday 20-1999:

Introduction
The aggressions practiced by the Israeli occupation forces against Lebanese villages and towns were renewed yesterday as a house was hit by two artillery rounds fired from the occupations outpost of Dabsheh on the residential areas in the town of Nabatieh El-Fawqa.

Meanwhile, Prime Minister Dr. Salim Al-Hoss reiterated the Lebanese official stand of moral and political backing for the Resistance as long as Israeli occupation exists.

ISRAELI SHELLING DAMAGES ONE HOUSE IN NABATIYEH FAWQA(1)
Israel today (yesterday) shelled the village of Nabatieh Fawqa in southern Lebanon damaging a home that belongs to Ismail Mohamed Shokor. Two shells landed in the house but no one was injured. The shelling violates a 1996 internationally-brokered understanding that bans attacks on civilian targets. Israeli artillery also opened fire on the outskirts of Nabatieh.

However, the Israeli occupation troops launched the following aggressions as an IR statement reported (2):
1:25, six 60mm rounds struck the perimeters of Shaqra village.
5:45, two missiles hit Nabatieh El-Fawqa causing damage to the house of Ismail Mohammad Chokr.
8:15, one 120mm shell fell on Aita El-Jabal village.
12:45, the surroundings of Taibeh outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

PM reiterates backing resistance against Israeli occupation(3)
Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss reiterated the Lebanese official stand of moral and political backing for the Resistance as long as Israeli occupation exists.

The premier told the Cairo based Arab Radio that there is nothing to prove that there will be a partial withdrawal from Hasbaya.

What is being circulated is press information that has no field evidence, he said.

The premier said that he had not received any indication of such plans. "There's nothing to suggest that the Israelis intend to withdraw from Hasbaya," he said.

Al-Hoss doubted that Israeli prime minister Ehud Barak would keep his promise to withdraw his occupation troops from the south by July 2000.

"The Israelis are counting on an agreement with Lebanon to follow their withdrawal. However, they can't expect an agreement when Lebanon refuses to make peace without Syria," he said.

During a graduation ceremony at the Lebanese University's Engineering Faculty, the PM said the Arab Israeli conflict will not be over with signing a "reconciliation", but will go on in a fiercer manner.

This means that every Arab must ready himself to be able to steadfast and win in a conflict that will not be over with treaties but will continue in various fiercer forms, the PM said.

Jaafar in stable condition; collaborators try to claim innocence by force!(4)
Collaborators Jamal Khanafar and Ali al-Khalil tried to claim that they had no role in torturing Fatima Mahmoud Jaafar, 16, and having her sent to the hospital almost dead.

Reports from the occupied town of Ainata said the two collaborators have written a list in which they claimed that the girl was not detained or tortured, but was hurt as she was moving in the hometown.

The two agents tried to force the town residents to sign the list. The residents did not cooperate in spite of the threats and intimidation attempts made by the agents.

Dr Hassan Tfaili who is treating Fatima said she is in stable condition but needs a long psychological treatment, just like all the freed detainees, due to the torture and suppression she had received in the detention center.

Fatima and her brother would not talk to the press for fear that the occupation and its militia take revenge against their mother and two brothers who are still held in the notorious detention center of al-Khiam.

Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss yesterday called the Shaib Hospital where Fatima is being treated and called on the Health Ministry to pay the treatment bills.

The premier also sent South Governor Faisal al-Saigh to visit Fatima and relay the support of the Lebanese state and government to her and to her hometown.

Reports on threat from Hizbullah planted by Israel(5)
A special Israeli army unit is playing up the threat from Iran and Hizbullah, planting reports in foreign newspapers which then get picked up by Israeli journalists, stoking the publics fears, according to the Haaretz daily.

Asked why the Israeli army was touting Hizbullah victories, a senior officer said: This way, we can explain to the public why the army needs to invest a lot in defending our soldiers in Lebanon.

Apparently, officers of the psychological warfare unit provide Israeli journalists with translated material from Arabic newspapers and pressure them to publish it.

One journalist was quoted by Haaretz as saying that a senior officer approached him, saying: Its important that you publish this item, and I tell you its true. I know, because I gave it to the Arabic paper myself.

He bragged that the unit gets items printed in the Paris-based Al-Watan Al-Arabi and the Foreign Report in London.

In July, Al-Watan Al-Arabi published a western intelligence report about a summit meeting that allegedly took place in Greece and included representatives from Hizbullah, Hamas, and Islamic Jihad. The assembly was reportedly headed by Irans spiritual leader, Ayatollah Ali Khameini.

According to the report, participants concocted a joint plan to derail the peace process and Israels withdrawal from Lebanon. The main elements of this story appeared several days ago in the Israeli press, spun as proof about Irans dangerous plans against Israel.

State Security cuts off phone link to Israel(6)
State Security agents have arrested several people for establishing telephone links with Israel. The calls were made from a booth at a telephone exchange in the northern Beirut suburb of Zalqa, a statement from the agency said.

The arrests were made on Oct. 13. The agents detained the owners of the exchange, Harout and Hovnan Demirdjian, who have confessed, and an unspecified number of people who had used the booth to call Israel.

The exchange has been closed and its equipment and records confiscated pending an investigation, the statement added. The security agents took the action on orders from Chief Military Prosecutor Nasri Lahoud, who was tipped off about the illegal activities.

Military Operations(7)
Two groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters attacked, at 6:00, Tohorra and Dabsheh outposts with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits in its fortifications. At Dabsheh outpost, the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.
