Daily Report



October Tuesday 19-1999:

Introduction
The Lebanese Army anti-aircraft gunners unleashed barrages of heavy machine gun bullets at an Israeli pilotless drone circling over Beirut. The MK flew for thirty minutes over the area between Aramoun and the southern suburbs through the Beirut Airport.

However, the Israeli enemy foreign Minister David Levy said following discussions with the Kenessets foreign affairs and defense committee that Israel will withdraw its troops from South Lebanon even without an agreement with Syria.

Meanwhile, the Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss dismissed reports on Monday of an imminent partial Israeli withdrawal from the South as media conjecture.

ISRAELI DRONE HOVERS ABOVE BEIRUT DRAWING ANTI-AIRCRAFT ARTILLERY(1)
Lebanese anti-aircraft guns opened fire early today (yesterday) at an Israeli pilotless reconnaissance drone which overflow the Beirut area. Guns fired twice at an unidentified object which turned out to be an Israeli spy plane flying at high altitude over the southern suburbs of Beirut. Meanwhile, Israeli sniper fire on the village of Yohmor-Shkeif on the edge of the zone Israel occupies in the south, damaged two homes. It also caused power outage in the village.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported in its statement the following Israeli aggressions (2):
1:45, the surroundings of Haddatha compound came under six 81mm rounds and five 155mm missiles.
Between 1:55 and 2:50, the perimeters of Haddatha exposed to five 155mm missiles and three 81mm rounds.
19:20, four 105mm shells fell on Mount Sojod whereas one 81mm round on the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal.
20:45, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
21:25, the surroundings of Haddatha compound came under two 81mm rounds.
22:25, two 81mm rounds were fired at the peripheries of Haddatha.
22:35, one 81mm round struck the western part of Srobbin.

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Salem Kamal Halawa and Mohammad Mahmoud Naameh targeted, at 20:00, enemy motorized movements inside Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were scored.

BARAK WANTS OUT OF SOUTH LEBANON (3)
Prime Minister Ehud Barak has also reiterated his commitment to pullout all occupation forces from Lebanon by next July.
 In a rare television interview, Israels Ehud Barak said he will withdraw occupation forces from Lebanon with an agreement by July 2000, in line with campaign pledges prior to his election in May. He turned away questions over what might happen if he is unable to forge a peace deal with Damascus, saying he would not deal with "speculations". Barak insisted that peace talks with Syria would re-start in the coming weeks or months. Press reports in Israel said Barak is now stepping up the war in southern Lebanon and his rhetoric as prospects for a resumption of peace talks faltered. Reports cited Barak's order to draw contingency plans for a pullout from the south, and intense bombardment of southern Lebanon. Observers said Barak aims to split the south Lebanon issue from Syrian-Israeli peace talks. He recently told the Jerusalem Post that a unilateral pullback from Lebanon would split the ranks of Syria and Lebanon. His designation of the occupied Golan as a top priority settlement development area was also seen as a quote new tactic. Leading commanders in Israels army last week warned that any withdrawal from the south without an agreement with Beirut and Damascus could lead to war. Barak last night insisted that peace with Syria will involve what he called painful compromises but did not elaborate, reiterating that the extent of an Israeli pullout from the occupied Golan Heights "will equal the depth of peace and security arrangements". Peace talks between Syria and Israel broke off in 1996, while negotiations with Lebanon came to a standstill in 1994. Damascus says former Israeli Prime Minister Yitzhak Rabin committed Israel to fully withdraw from the Golan in return for peace. Barak rejects such a commitment.

For its part, Israeli Foreign Minister David Levy said that Israel would withdraw its troops from Lebanon even without an agreement with Syria.

The desired option is to reach an arrangement that will allow for a withdrawal of our troops from Lebanon, Levy said. But we wont be prisoners or hostages to those who dont want an agreement. Israel will not stay in Lebanon forever(4)

Withdrawal dismissed as media conjecture(5)
Prime Minister Salim Hoss dismissed reports on Monday of an imminent partial Israeli withdrawal from the south as media conjecture.

The premier said that he had not received any indication of such plans. Theres nothing to suggest that the Israelis intend to withdraw from Hasbaya, he said.

Hoss also denied conflicting reports that the Israelis have stockpiled more equipment in the southern village. Weve received no information that would support this claim, he said.

He said that efforts to resume the peace process were underway. Both Syria and Lebanon insist on resuming the talks from the point at which they broke off in 1996. This is the main issue being debated, the premier said.

Hoss doubted that Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak would keep his promise to withdraw his troops from the south by July 2000.

The Israelis are counting on an agreement with Lebanon to follow their withdrawal. However, they cant expect an agreement when Lebanon refuses to make peace without Syria, he said.

Occupation extends detention of Obeid and Dirani(6)
As 21 Lebanese people remain detained administratively in the Israeli occupation forces' prisons for more than 10 years, an Israeli court yesterday extended the detention of his Eminence Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid and Hajj Mustafa Dirani who are held as "bargaining chips" to secure the release of enemy servicemen or their bodies.

A Tel Aviv court asked the Israeli so-called supreme court to take a decision in the case of 19 other Lebanese held in the occupation's prisons without trial or charges.

Bayada Sheikhs refuse to receive agent Tarif(7)
Bayada Sheikhs refused to host Israeli Mossad agent Moaffaq Tarif.
Lebanese MP Faisal al-Dawood who unveiled the incident said the Israeli enemy tried at this delicate stage to drive agent Tarif to meet the Bayada Sheikhs.

The step aims to serve a certain project, Dawood said, but added that the Sheikhs not only refused to receive Tarif and boycotted his visit, but also prevented him from entering the Khalwat Bayada Temple on the grounds of the excommunication from the Druze sect taken against all those who collaborate with the occupation.

MP al-Dawood hailed the Sheikhs' position stressing that they will always be landmarks when it comes to national and patriotic stands that embody Arab and Islamic history which is full of generous giving in defense of the land, the holy sites, and the causes of Arab and Islamic Nation.

TEENAGE GIRL IN STABLE CONDITION AFTER ISRAELI-BACKED MILITIA TORTURE ORDEAL(8)
The condition of a teenage Lebanese girl who underwent torture on the hands of Israeli-backed forces in occupied southern Lebanon is stable. But the sister of Fatima Mahmud Jaafar, who was also abducted and tortured by Israeli allied militiamen, is still in the occupied village of Ainata. Occupation forces would not allow her to leave for treatment in Sidon despite her life-threatening condition.

 The teenage Lebanese girl, Fatima, was admitted to hospital in "very serious condition" yesterday after being beaten by members of Israels allied militia. The head of the follow-up committee for the release of Lebanese detainees in Israeli detention camps, Mohamed Safa, said sixteen-year-old Fatima Mahmud Jaafar was beaten on the head with revolver butts in the militia's number 17 detention centre in occupied Bint Jbeil until she lost consciousness. The girl was taken by an ambulance of the International Committee for the Red Cross to the Shaib hospital in Sidon with a fractured skull and memory loss.

Hospital officials said her condition is "very serious". She is from the village of Ainata in the central sector of the zone Israel occupies. She was abducted on Saturday along with her brothers Bilal and Ahmed, who are still being held by Israels proxy militia. Fatima's sister, Ghinwa, was released Saturday after two weeks of detention. Her friends said she also suffers from serious ailments, and is hemorrhaging, but occupation forces would not let her leave Ainata. Eight other residents of Ainata remain in custody. Doctors said Fatima went through the same torture on the hands of Israeli-backed forces last year.

BEIRUT CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY DENIES HOLDING BUSINESS TALKS WITH ISRAELIS(9)
The head of Beirut's chamber of commerce and industry Adnan Kassar has denied a statement by US Commerce Secretary William Daley that Lebanese businessmen held talks in Cairo last week with their Israeli counterparts. Daley who is on a regional tour said delegates from the chambers of commerce of Lebanon, Israel, Morrocco, Tunisia, Egypt and the Palestinian Authority met in Cairo. He added quote "can you imagine hearing Lebanese and Israeli business people agreeing with one another on how to make the region more open for business?" Kassar said, a meeting of a council of Lebanese and Egyptian businessmen took place in Cairo recently but the chamber was not represented. He denied categorically that Lebanese members of the council would have had talks with Israeli businessmen.
