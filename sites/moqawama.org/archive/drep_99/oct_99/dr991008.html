Daily Report



October Friday 8-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants dealt another blow to the Israeli enemy and its collaborators in Rihan area penetrating the strict security measures adopted by the occupation troops in South Lebanon. Three Israeli soldiers were wounded as the IR men ambushed an armored convoy at the entrance of the Israeli-manned outpost of Rihan as a part of coordinated attack against the occupation force in the Northern Sector.

Meanwhile, the reports from Hasbaya area said that the Israeli occupations field command told the remaining collaborators that the withdrawal from this area is imminent.

THREE ISRAELI SOLDIERS INJURED IN RESISTANCE ATTACK(1)
Security sources in southern Lebanon said three Israeli soldiers were wounded in a Hizbullah ambush of an Israeli patrol near the village of Rihan on the edge of the central sector of the occupied zone. Hizbullah said it attacked barracks of the Israeli army and their proxy militia in Rihan and military posts in several other locations in the occupied zone. Israel only acknowledged the injury of two soldiers.

For its part, the Islamic resistance IR issued the following statement.

"Once again, within less than ten days, and following the blow dealt to the Zionist enemy and its collaborators in the Rihan area, and after the strict security measures adopted since then, IR has been able to penetrate these measures and assert that it possesses the field initiative in confronting the enemy which is hiding inside its fortified outpost.

"IR men today (yesterday) engaged in fierce clashes with the occupation forces in the Rihan area using various types of machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, while the Zionist enemy used its warplanes."

"IR's squad of Martyr commander Abu Ali Rida Yassen set up an ambush for a Zionist command convoy at the entrance of the Zionist barracks in Rihan.

"When the convoy reached the ambush site at 13:30, it was blitzed by IR men who used their machine guns and rocket propelled grenades inflicting definite casualties that were seen by the naked eye.

"Immediately, a Zionist infantry force came out of the barracks to back up the targeted convoy. Squad of Martyr Abbas Khreibani attacked the enemy force at close quarters inflicting several casualties in the enemy ranks and preventing the force from achieving its mission.

"In the meantime, squad of Martyr Hussein Trabulsi attacked the Lahdi militia's barracks in Rihan with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades. The attack resulted in burning a number of vehicles. Smoke was seen billowing from the barracks.

"Moreover, squad of Martyr Mustafa Haidar attacked a Lahdi force at the Hamra square (Rihan) scoring definite hits, while squad of Martyr Hussein Nasser attacked the Sojod outpost, squad of Martyr Ashraf Kheiredeen targeted the Bir Kallab outpost, and squad of Martyr Ali Yassin in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted the Zaffateh emplacement, scoring accurate and direct hits.

"As the enemy tried to bring in additional reinforcements, IR men attacked an Israeli infantry force in Kassaret al-Oroush and another force in the Rihan Hilltops with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades.

"The clashes went on for around an hour during which IR men inflicted definite casualties in the ranks of the two forces.

"At 14:30, the enemy involved its warplanes in the confrontations, but these intercepted by IR's ARU that used anti-aircraft missiles that forced the enemy warplanes to retreat"

Furthermore, the IR militants spotted, at 22:15, an Israeli motorized force at Kassaret al-Oroush compound. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Merhi and Mustafa Chokair attacked with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite this.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ibrahim Hussein Khalil and Hussein Ghassani fell upon enemy movements at Rihan hilltops. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals(2)

Occupation tells militia withdrawal is near(3)
Reports from the Hasbaya area (south east) said the Israeli occupation's field command told the remaining officials and members of the occupation's proxy militia that the withdrawal from this area is imminent.
Israeli television unveiled that Israeli chief of staff Shaul Mofaz and his officers have prepared a plan for withdrawal from Lebanon.
Mofaz said the plan includes a program related to the occupation's militia adding that the process is based on security details that have been set according to the Israeli government's political view.
Hizbullah official sources affirmed that the Israeli occupation's partial withdrawal is imminent, adding that the Islamic Resistance's men have spotted field arrangements which indicate that the pullout will include the areas of Kfar Houna and Jezzine.
Member of the enemy's general staff Ibraham Roshen acknowledged that it is impossible for the occupation army to achieve any victory in south Lebanon.
Roshen told the war committee of the enemy's Knesset that the war in Lebanon will not end, and it is impossible to win this war, for the regular army is fighting a war with tanks, planes, and artillery that are all useless in the face of fighters engaged in a guerilla war that can "turn your life to hell".

Ainata besieged, Hizbullah affirms: the arm of Resistance is long(4)
The remains of the proxy militia maintained the blockade the collaborators have been imposing on the town.
A statement by the town's residents disclosed that agents Ali Khalil Hmayyed and Jamil Khanafer handled the acts of suppression and detention that raised to 10 the number of the Ainata residents detained by the occupation and taken to the al-Khiam detention center within 4 days.

Among those detained are: Ali Khalil Khanafer (24), Sayyed Ibrahim Fadlallah (60), Bilal Mahmoud Jaafar (17), Ghinwa Mahmoud Jafaar (21), Hussein Mohammed Samhat (65), his wife Najwa Hussein Samhat (36), and their son Ahmed (15).

The residents called on the Lebanese government and the international human rights groups to intervene and unveil the fate of hundreds of detainees in Israeli prisons besides putting an end to the arbitrary practices of the occupation and its collaborators.

Hizbullah, in turn, talked about the enemy's practices.

"The Zionist occupation army and its collaborators are proceeding with their arbitrary measures against the residents of the occupied zone, the latest of these measures being the siege of Ainata and detaining a number of its steadfast residents, including elderly people, women and boys, besides storming houses, blocking movement inside and outside the town, and intimidating and threatening residents. These acts will have dire consequences on the besieged residents," a Hizbullah communiqué read.

"This Zionist terrorism against unarmed civilians comes as the Zionist entity is practicing daily aggressions of bombardment and air raids on the villages adjacent to the occupied zone, believing that this might protect it from the blows dealt by the Islamic Resistance men or rescue it from the quagmire of occupation.

"The enemy has, in a humiliated manner, acknowledged that it keeps it troops away from ground confrontations with IR men for fear of sustaining human losses, and that it is using instead its air force and suppressive measures against our people in the occupied zone.

"As we hail the Ainata people's steadfastness, clinging to their land, and rejecting the occupation and its collaborators, we stress that the Islamic Resistance that will always be the watchful eye safeguarding its people and land, will proceed with Jihad until the occupation is expelled and the land is liberated.

"IR will pursue the traitor collaborators who must learn the lesson from their predecessors, for the arm of the Resistance is long, and it knows how to reach them one by one in their houses outposts, cars, or wherever they may hide," the Hizbullah communiqué concluded.
