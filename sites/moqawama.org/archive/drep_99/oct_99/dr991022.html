Daily Report




October Friday 22-1999:

Introduction
The Israeli occupying troops hit the residential neighborhoods of the southern village of Baraasheit causing damage to about eighteen homes, but no casualties were reported.

However, Fatima Jaafar has calmed down as she became certain that she is no longer in the joint Israeli-Lahdi investigation center and recounted her ordeal at the hands of Israeli collaborators.

Eleven houses damaged by Israeli bombardment(1)
Israeli occupation forces have once again violated the April Understanding and disregarded the calls made by the understanding's panel to avoid civilians during military action.

The enemy this morning directly bombarded the town of Baraasheit, damaging the houses of Anees Mroueh, Abdel-Latif Mroueh, Sami Farhat, Kamal Taleb Farhat, Mahmoud Ali Ismail, Hafez Abdel-Hadi, Ahmed Zaki Farhat, Rida Sh'hab, Youssef Kamal Shehab, Hussein Adnan Farhat, Hussein Taleb Farhat and Mohammed Ali Mnassa.

The shelling also burned crops in a number of the town's fields.

Security sources said more than 30 mortar rounds hit the town's neighborhoods in the bombardment that soon extended to include the vicinity of Haddatha, Aita al-Jabal, and Jabal al-Zaatar.

The occupation's artillery at 11:00 a.m. pounded Iqleem al-Toffah Hilltops and the Sojod-Rihan Woods. Meanwhile, enemy warplanes heavily circled over the targeted areas and staged several mock air raids.

The Israeli occupation's espionage drones (pilotless planes) haunted the Beqaa skies for the second day in a row. Enemy bombers specifically flew over the mid-Beqaa and Baalbeck City all day long today (yesterday).

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions...(2):
Between 6:05 and 6:55, Baraasheit town and its perimeters came under more than twenty five 81 and 120 shells, four 122mm missiles and barrages of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets causing damage to the houses of Rida Chehab, Hassan Adnan Farhat, Hussein Taleb Farhat, Mohammad Ali Mnassa, Anis Mroueh, Mahmoud Ali Ismail, Hassan Kamal Chehab, Abdel-Latif Mroueh, Sami Farhat and Kamal Taleb Farhat.
6:05, the peripheries of Beit Yahoun town and the surroundings of Rshaf outpost exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
6:50, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the perimeters of Haddatha and Aita El-Jabal villages.
15:25, one 155mm missile hit the outskirts of Yater village.
Between 15:40 and 16:00, Raheb area was shelled by seven 155mm missiles.
16:15, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
17:25, six 155mm missiles slammed into Qaissieh Valley.
20:15, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck Nabatieh El-Fawqa.
21:50, the perimeters of Kfar Romman town and Ali Taher woodland exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Tension won't be over with partial withdrawal, Goksel(3)
United Nations Interim Forces in South Lebanon's (UNIFIL) Political Advisor Timur Goksel underlined that an Israeli partial withdrawal (from Hasbaya) will not put an end to tension in the occupied zone.

As long as there is Israeli occupation, there will be tension that moves from one area to another according to the occupation's movements, Goksel said.

Asked about a UNIFIL deployment in Hasbaya if a withdrawal takes place, Goksel said Hasbaya is outside UNIFIL's mandate.

LEBANESE CHURCH LEADER DOUBTS BARAK ON LEBANON PULLBACK(4)
The head of Lebanon's Maronite Church, Cardinal Nasrallah Sfeir, cast doubt Thursday on Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak's pledge to withdraw his forces from south Lebanon with or without an accord with Syria and Lebanon. "The declarations of Israels leadership are contradictory, they change from day to day," Sfeir told AFP, charging Israel with deliberately maintaining uncertainty. "One day they say they're leaving, the next they say they won't go if Lebanese attacks continue. The Israelis are practicing misinformation, as they have done for 20 years," he added. Sfeir, head of the largest Christian community in Lebanon, was expressing a skepticism that is widespread in the country over Israels intentions. Few believe that the Israelis will quit the 850 square kilometer (340 square miles) of southern Lebanon they have occupied since 1978.

Girl recounts ordeal at hands of Israeli agents(5)
 All I can remember is the awful pain from being hit in the head with rifle butts and then I woke up in the hospital, Fatima Jaafar told a news conference on Thursday.

From her hospital bed in the Shuaib Hospital in Sidon, Jaafar, 16, spoke in a low, weak voice to dozens of reporters.
The two Israeli militiamen inva-

ded our house and immediately began beating me on my head and face. They threatened to shoot me, she said.

On Saturday, Jaafar was alone in her home in the occupied village of Ainata, Bint Jbeil, when the Israeli-allied militiamen stormed in. Her mother was buying medicine for her brothers, Ahmad and Bilal, who had been detained in Khiam two weeks earlier.

On Sunday, Jaafar was taken to the hospital where she was treated for multiple fractures and a nervous breakdown. Prime Minister Salim Hoss ordered the Health Ministry to pay her medical expenses.

They ransacked the house and beat me again. Then one of them, Ali Khalil, dragged me by my hair and said he was going to kill me, just like he had killed many others, she recounted.

Jaafar said that she screamed and cried for mercy but her attackers did not stop.

When Jaafars mother returned, the two militiamen pointed their guns at her and ordered her to remain quiet.

Jaafar said that she does not want to return to her school in the village. She begged her parents to move outside the occupation zone so that we can live without being threatened every day.

Mohammed Safa, the secretary-general of the Follow-Up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in Israeli Prisons, visited Jaafar at the hospital and told reporters that more and more Lebanese, including four teenagers in Khiam, were suffering at the hands of the Israeli agents.

These crimes against humanity and childhood should be stopped. We hold Israel completely responsible for these violations, he said.

Safa urged concerned Lebanese to join him for a hunger strike next Thursday in front of the UN House.

Military Operations(6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Hicham Salameh and Abdel-Amir Fadel attacked, at 00:35, a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Aramta hilltops. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to score direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Hassan Sami Jaghbir and Hussein Salim Hachem attacked, at 6:40, Baraasheit compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its northern and eastern fortification.

Meanwhile, the IR unit of martyrs Abbas Mahmoud Bjeiji and Ali Hassan Saleh was targeting its western defense with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Accurate and direct hits were chalked up. In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Hassan Sailami and Hassan Hussein El-Hussein was attacking the aforesaid compound with the mortar rounds scoring definite and accurate hits.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Abdallah Mahmoud Atwi and Ali Qassem Ballouk rushed upon Haddatha outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up accurate goals.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, at 20:10, several 107mm rocket-propelled grenades at the outposts of Ali Taher and Borj.
