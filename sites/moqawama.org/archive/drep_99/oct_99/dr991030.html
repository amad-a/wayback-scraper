Daily Report
 
 

October Saturday 30-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen launched a series of military operations against the Israeli occupation troops and their proxy militiamen in the occupied zone of South Lebanon. The Israeli allied militia acknowledged that two of its members were killed, one of them was identified as the commander of Aramta outpost collaborator Kamel Al-Damouri, and others wounded.

Meanwhile, the Israeli fighter-bombers carried out several air raids into Iqleem Al-Toffah mountain ridge just north of the occupation zone. Three Lebanese civilians were wounded at Hamra bridge when a 120mm mortar round landed near the road that leads to the proxy militias crossing.

Two militiamen killed, others wounded in IR's operations(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of Martyrs Mohammed Ramadan and Ahmed Mshawrab at 10:30 Friday attacked the ramparts of the Sojod outpost with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, while enemy troops were moving inside the ramparts that were definitely hit.

Meanwhile, squad of Martyrs Hussein Qbaisse and Hussein Qansou pounded the Sojod outpost with rocket propelled grenades in a violent and concentrated manner, inflicting further casualties in the enemy ranks.

At 10:50, squad of Martyr's Ibrahim Zahr and Ghassan Mortada targeted the occupation's outpost in Hardoun with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits, while squad of Martyrs Hassan Abdallah and Mohammed Darweesh attacked the Bayada outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring definite hits.

The occupation's proxy militia acknowledged that one of its militiamen was killed in IR's qualitative operation at the Aramta-Rihan road.

A powerful bomb went off as an armored vehicle was traveling along the road, the collaborators' radio said.

Sources inside the occupied zone said an M-113 half-track completely burned down (along with those riding it) when the bomb exploded.

Collaborator Kamel al-Damouri, the commander of the Aramta outpost was identified as one of those killed in the operation.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 12:00, enemy movements near Jamoussa position. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ali Ibrahim Hassan and Salem Kamal Halawi targeted them with the proper arms scoring direct hits.

IR squad of Martyrs Hassan Hani Obeid and Hussein Ahmed Trabulsi at 14:15 attacked the Kfar Houna outpost from different sides with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades. IR men clashed with the enemy troops in the outpost, thus destroying a 12.7mm emplacement and killing or wounding its troops at the outpost's western rampart which was completely destroyed.

In the meantime, squad of Martyrs Zaher Hmadeh and Mohammed Radi al-Finn destroyed the outpost's eastern rampart, while squad of Martyrs Mohammed Abdallah Saoli and Mohsen Fneish pounded the outpost with rocket propelled grenades, destroying a vehicle that was inside the outpost.

When the Aramta outpost tried to help the targeted Kfar Houna outpost, squad of Martyrs Hussein Hodroj and Ali Taha attacked Aramta with the appropriate weapons scoring definite hits, while squad of Martyrs Mohammed Moneef Qashmar and Fadi al-Tawell of IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit retaliated and silenced the enemy's emplacements in the Lahdi Rihan outpost.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Chokair and Ali Khalil detonated, at 19:25, a powerful roadside bomb as a Lahdi motorized patrol was traveling on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan. One M-113 military vehicle was hit, whereas its occupants were inflicted.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Ibrahim Dakdouk and Khaled Ahmad Taher was targeting the remaining force with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades inflicting more casualties and forcing it to retreat to Rihan area.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Yehia Hammoud and Ali Midlej fell upon sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.

For its part, Amal Movement's Military Operations' Division announced that its squads launched two attacks, one on the Israeli occupation's outpost in Haddatha and the second targeted an Israeli patrol near the Rahrah Triangle.

Three civilians wounded in the Israeli aggressions
Three Lebanese civilians were wounded this morning at al-Hamra Bridge when a 120mm mortar round landed near the road that leads to the proxy militia's crossing.

A UNIFIL spokesman who announced the news said Ali Moussa Jamil, one of the wounded, is in serious condition. He was taken to UNIFIL's field hospital in Naqoura.

The men who hailed from occupied Alma al-Shaab and Aita al-Shaab were waiting at the crossing to be allowed to return to their homes in the occupied towns.

The crossing's militiamen immediately shut the crossing and expelled the citizens waiting to enter or leave the occupied zone.

On the other hand, the occuaption's warplanes at 15:00 raided the hilltops of Kfar Houna (near Jezzine). Another enemy raid followed on the Nabeh al-Tasseh gorge, firing a number of air-to-ground missiles. No casualties were reported in the raids.

Furthermore, the towns of Jarjoua and Houmine al-Fawqa, Maidoun, and Ain al-Tineh were targeted this morning by enemy machine-gun fire and artillery bombardment that extended later to include the Sojod and Oqmata woods besides the Nabeh al-Tasseh gorge(2)
