Daily Report



October Sunday 10-1999:

Occupation investigates how IR penetrated its measures in Rihan(1)
Seventy-two hours after the Islamic Resistance's qualitative operation in Rihan, reports talked about a state of hysteria that has hit the command of the Israeli occupation forces in Lebanon.

The enemy brass has been trying to uncover the circumstances of the operation and how IR was able to find out about the convoy, set up the ambush, and retreat from the operation site in spite of the air strikes that followed the ambush.

According to the enemy's television, the convoy included the top officers of the occupation's command in Lebanon: higher commander Beni Gantz, northern area (Golan, south Lebanon, and northern Palestine) commander Gabi Ashkenazi and six senior officers including two field intelligence officers.

The only question that worries the Israeli forces' command is: how did Hizbullah know about the Israeli commanders' tour? the Israeli television said, adding that according to the answer of this question the other questions on the ambush and the (IR men's) retreat can be answered.

Journalists have noted that Hizbullah has been successful in re-imposing its method on the Israeli occupation army which has been propagating a so-called victory over the Resistance through air warfare or "the eagle hunts the snake" as named by the occupation's command which aims, according to the enemy deputy war minister Ephraim Sneh, to curb the troops' losses by intensified air raids that bombard the Resistance's fire sources as fast as possible.

The enemy's television recalled IR's achievements with talking about the everlasting question "Did Hizbullah know", a question raised after every Israeli botched operation, in particular Insarieh (September 1998), killing of Erez Gerestein (Beni Gantz' predecessor), Berkat al-Jabbour and other Israeli operations that have been aborted by IR.

Dispute between occupation and collaborators over withdrawal from Hasbaya(2)
Successive meetings have been held recently between the Israeli occupation's command and the remains of its proxy militia in Hasbaya. Observers saw in these meetings the beginning of preparations for the anticipated pullout from Hasbaya.

Reports said a dispute has broken out between the occupation's command and the militia when the militiamen were informed about the criteria set by the occupation for deciding the militiamen who will be settled and accommodated inside occupied Palestine.

The occupation wants to sponsor the high-ranking officers and the security members of the militia remains while the militia officials want the occupation to absorb all those who want to withdraw, the reports said.

"The problem started with the withdrawal from Jezzine as the talk about pullout from Hasbaya surfaced," the reports added, noting that "a committee set by the occupation to discuss this point reached the decision that between 200 and 300 militiamen and their families can be taken; the militiamen want the figure to be 1200 militiamen."

Reports said the occupation has constructed a condominium for the militia's senior officers near the Mtullah outpost, hundreds of meters from the international borders.

The condominium can take 100 families. Each family will be using a 70-meter unit that is made of two rooms, a kitchen and a bathroom.

What the occupation did not tell the collaborators is that they will have to pay about $50,000 for each unit.

Hizbullah comments on the US state department's report: an attempt to screen facts(3)
"The US administration has always installed itself as prosecutor against the peoples and states that confront injustice, occupation and hegemony, one time in the name of human rights and ethnic minorities' religious rights, and one time by classifying organizations and states as terrorist or sponsoring terrorism," a Hizbullah communiqué read.

"The latest report (of the US state department on "terrorist organizations") is no exception of this policy, although the US itself needs to be acquitted of this charge, for it is, in the eyes of oppressed peoples, the state of terrorism and the sponsor of the state terrorism practiced by the Zionist entity.

"The peoples' memory is full of American terrorist practices in Vietnam, Iran, Lebanon, Somalia, and Iraq where children, women and the elderly are killed by the US state terrorism and US arms; a lot of peoples are still suffering from the US unjust policies.

"As for Hizbullah, it is clear that the US administration is trying to screen facts and create confusion by listing the names of eight organizations, some of them have never been heard of, and claiming that Hizbullah is known by these names, besides leveling baseless charges at Hizbullah and holding it responsible for certain acts in order to justify the US administration's claims and labeling Hizbullah as terrorist, although all these accusations are categorically baseless."

"Hizbullah has its parliamentary and popular presence; it has a clear identity which is its source of pride: the identity of resisting the occupation. Hizbullah works under one and only one name known by all the world: Hizbullah; its field of activity is restricted to its honorable resistance of the Zionist occupation in Lebanon.

"This resistance is overwhelmingly backed by the Lebanese and the Arabs. It is a right acknowledged by the international laws and conventions that the US is violating by backing the occupation and screening its official terrorism."

"It is sufficient for Hizbullah to have a popular credit and presence in the hearts of the peoples; it does not need an American certificate about its behavior and conduct, for the US has been used to fabricating and marketing groundless charges."

"Today, the whole world realizes that these accusations aim to distort the identity of peoples and their rights to confront state terrorism and resist the occupation represented in its most hideous types by the Zionist occupation."

Military Operations(5)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Hassan Ali Abdallah and Mohammad Ali Darwish attacked, at 17:15, an Israeli Infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush area with the machine guns and hand grenades. Certain casualties among the force were inflicted, the militants reported.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Hassan Mohammad Jaghbir and Mohammad Hussein Hammoud targeted the Israeli barracks of Rihan with the rocket-propelled grenades, whereas the IR squad of martyr Naameh Mohammad Hseiki fell upon the collaborator barracks of the 10th Brigade Command with the rocket-propelled grenades. The smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

Fifteen minutes later, an Israeli tank near Rihan tried to back up the attacked force, but the IR group of martyr Rifaat Ali Olleik targeted it with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 22:45, an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush area. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Nabil Mohammad Akhdar and Hussein Khalil Cherri rushed upon it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.
