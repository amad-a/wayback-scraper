Daily Report



October Sunday 24-1999:

Israeli Aggressions(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) issued a communiqué in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions:
1:00, three 120mm shells hit west of Baraasheit town.
Between 1:20 and 7:15, Mount Zaatar was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
Between 2:00 and 6:00, the surroundings of Haddatha compound came under seven 81mm rounds.
Between 2:10 and 2:30, the surroundings of Baraasheit outpost exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
2:10, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the surroundings of Haddatha compound.
5:15, the Israeli war jets raided into Mount Zaatar and the area between Haddatha and Aita El-Jabal firing two air-to-surface rockets into Jamaa area.
6:45, three 155mm missiles landed Mount Sojod.
6:50, the perimeters of Jibaa town came under two 155mm missiles and several 120mm shells.
7:10, six 120mm shells struck the peripheries of Aita El-Jabal.
8:50, two 155mm missiles were fired at the perimeters of Haddatha, whereas the perimeters of Haddatha and Haris exposed to heavy machine gun fire.
13:50, Qaissieh Valley was targeted by four 155mm missiles.
19:15, Habboush River watercourse came under five 81mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 19:15 and 19:50, Mount Sojod was bombarded by eight tank rounds and four 155mm missiles.
19:40, two 155mm missiles hit Mount Haddatha.
19:50, Oqmata plain was targeted by six 155mm missiles and heavy machine gun fire, whereas the surroundings of Dabsheh outpost came under four 60mm rounds and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
22:00, three 81mm rounds were unleashed at the surroundings of Dabsheh outpost.
22:30, several flare shells were fired over the seashore of Qlaileh.
23:50, the perimeters of Haris exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Military Operations(2)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Abdel-Mawla Hussein Ismail and Ali Wehbi Hijazi attacked fiercely, at 12:30, Tair Harfa outpost with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits.

Half an hour later, the IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Hekmat Dbouk and Hassan Mohammad Jaghbir rushed upon Chihine post with the appropriate weapons. Direct and definite goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Mahmoud Bjeiji and Ali Hassan Saleh targeted, at 19:10, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen told.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Adnan Farhat and Mohammad Hassan Hammoud attacked, at 20:00, enemy movements inside Hemayed position with the suitable arms chalking up direct hits.

Ten minutes later, the IR men spotted enemy military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ali Hassan El-Sailami and Hassan Hussein El-Husseini attacked them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Abdallah Mahmoud Atwi and Ali Qassem Ballouk bombarded the position with the rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western and northern fortification.
