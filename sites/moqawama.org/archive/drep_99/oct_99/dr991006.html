Daily Report
 
 
October Wednesday 6-1999:

Introduction
The "Israeli" war jets violated yesterday the April Understanding when they staged a sortie into a house at Qabrikha village with US-made Tow guided missiles. Another warplanes raided into Kfour ravine at Iqleem Al-Toffah area which is located on the edge of zone "Israel" occupies. The "Israeli" air raids resulted in wounding three Lebanese nationals.

However, the "Israeli" daily Maariv quoted a high ranking political source as saying the families of five hundred "Israeli" allied militiamen will live in "Israel" following a pullout which is expected to begin from Hasbaya region as of January.

Civilians wounded, houses damaged in "Israeli" violation of Understanding(1)
"Two "Israeli" helicopter gunships made a low-level dash across the central sector of the occupation zone on Tuesday morning to fire a guided missile into an abandoned home on the edge of Qabrikha Village".

"The suddenness of Tuesdays attack stunned villagers who, had been enjoying the rare pleasure of a peaceful morning. As they chatted or went about their daily chores, they were being scrutinized by a pilotless reconnaissance plane. The "Israeli" drone had been buzzing above the village all morning, its slender crucifix shape invisible against the deep blue sky. "Little stirred in the central sector, just a cloud of dust marking the passage of a tank between the "Israeli"-manned outpost at Taibeh and the new SLA position at Mheisbat. But at around 11.30am, the village was alerted to impending trouble by the throbbing beat of approaching helicopters. Moments later an explosion rocked the village.

"Two Apache gunships emerged from a backdrop of dun-colored hills. Shedding anti-missile flares in their wake, the helicopters wheeled and turned a few hundred meters east of the village. The crack of two outgoing mortar rounds fired by the resistance came from somewhere north of the village. One of the rounds exploded just short of the Mheisbat post and beneath the helicopters. The Apaches headed back toward "Israel".

Two cars, horns blaring, raced down the road. In the back seat of the rear car, a man had his face buried in his hands.

The helicopters fired a rocket into a house, said one man.

A crowd had gathered in the center of the village. Several children clutching schoolbooks milled around uncertainly. The rocket attack had brought an abrupt halt to classes in the village school. Grim-faced Hizbullah members cordoned off the targeted house while their comrades sifted through the rubble.

"The missile had struck the side of the building, knocking a gaping hole in an upper floor before punching through a wall into a neighbors garden. Fragments of the missile lay scattered in the garden, some of the components still too hot to touch. The building, whose owner lives in Beirut, was empty at the time of the attack, the villagers insisted.

We were all sitting together when we heard the helicopters then a loud explosion, said Khatiba Mansour, 17, who lives next to the targeted building. We didnt know what had happened ­ we were very scared.

Sitting on the floor in Khatibas house, a woman was sobbing. Her brother had been wounded in the blast. He was the man seen clutching his face in the back of the car as his friends took him to Tibnine Hospital. "The drone was still circling as "Israeli" artillery began pounding Wadi Qaissieh between Qabrikha and Khorbet Selem. The shells exploded in the wadi".

IR impact: "Israelis" change their tactic in South(3)
"As "Israeli" helicopters struck Qabrikha and warplanes staged raids elsewhere in the south on Tuesday, the "Israeli" Army confirmed that it was intentionally making greater use of its air power to reduce exposing ground forces to unnecessary dangers in the zone.

"Other than the helicopter attack on Qabrikha, "Israeli" warplanes fired missiles into Wadi Kfour near Nabatieh and twice struck at wadis near Jabal Botom facing the western sector of the zone.

"With talk of an "Israeli" withdrawal on everyones lips, "Israeli" Army officers were quoted on Tuesday as saying that air power had largely replaced troop activities on the ground.

During the recent period, in the wake of new instructions, soldiers are remaining more in outposts and carrying out routine activity. For a long while now we havent been sent to initiate activity, said an "Israeli" officer quoted in the Yediot Ahranot newspaper.

"The officers said that it was clear to everyone that the intention to take the "Israeli" Army out of Lebanon by July 2000 is the reason that activity on the ground has been reduced in order to prevent any unnecessary escalation".

HASBAYA PULLOUT TO START IN JANUARY: SOURCES(4)
""Israel's" daily Maariv today (yesterday) quoted a high ranking political source as saying the families of five hundred "Israeli" allied militiamen in southern Lebanon will live in "Israel" following a pullout. Security Sources in southern Lebanon told us "Israel" is getting ready to withdraw its troops from the Hasbaya region starting January. We visited villages in Hasbaya that are just outside the zone "Israel" occupies.

" Workers construct a 60,000 dollar toust complex... in the front-line region of Hasbaya... less than four kilometers from the zone "Israel" occupies in south Lebanon. Wahib Madi says inhabitants of this economically devastated region of eastern Lebanon need his project. "This project will not yield the same amount of money here as it would in any other region of Lebanon, but we need it," he says. What if "Israeli" occupation forces withdraw from Hasbaya? "I am not betting on a settlement, but of course I am optimistic, if they withdraw then the entire region would open up for business..." Hasbaya is rife with reports of an imminent "Israeli" withdrawal from this part of the occupation zone. Security sources tell us, "Israel" will pullout its forces and its allied militiamen from this entire region to a newly fortified outpost deep inside the occupied zone, before a complete and a final withdrawal from Lebanon is carried out. Informed sources here told us occupation forces will withdraw from the Hasbaya region starting January next year to Tallet Shreifi, near the central occupation town of Marjeyoun, some half a kilometer from occupied Khiam, close to the border with "Israel". All retreating militiamen and "Israeli" forces who are now based in this mansion would gather, sources added, at the newly expanded outpost of Tallet Shreifi, until "Israel" finally decides to end its 21-year occupation of southern Lebanon. Sources also said some 350 men from Hasbaya serve in "Israel's" proxy militia, adding 150 are ready to turn themselves in to the authorities. They claimed, about one hundred fighters will retreat with "Israeli" forces, some thirty will take refuge in "Israel" and the rest will travel abroad. The eastern sector of the occupied zone was the least volatile region in occupied southern Lebanon. It is strategically located on Mount Hermon on the other side of the Syrian Golan Heights, which is also under "Israeli" occupation. It is also close to the northern "Israeli" Gaillee region. Starting last year, Lebanese resistance fighters infiltrated the area, dealing "Israeli" forces and their trained militiamen severe blows. In February, a roadside bomb near this village of Kawakaba, killed "Israel's" liaison officer in the zone, Erez Gerstein, and two other "Israeli" soldiers. That was followed by a record number of desertions by militiamen. They flee occupation ranks through these luscious hills and valleys and defect to government-held territories. Many have been heeding calls by religious authorities in the region, who issued an edict forbidding any form of collaborating with "Israel". Even "Israel" acknowledges the collapse of its militia in this part of the zone. Behind these gates at the Zimraya crossing point which links Lebanese land under occupation to the rest of the country, the resistance, despite constant observation by forces encamped inside this post, have succeeded in placing a number of roadside blasts killing militiamen here. That has been putting a damp on the morale of the militia, which has helped "Israel" sustain its occupation. A number of senior "Israeli" allied officers, including some who served in this post at Ain Qinya, have been detained by "Israel". They were accused of aiding the Lebanese resistance. "Israel" raids these areas on regular basis now, especially Jabal al-Dahr, where an "Israeli" war jet recently destroyed what was reported to be a military radar it installed, as part of preparations for its withdrawal from Hasbaya. A sense of normalcy in eastern Lebanon ends here at Marj al-Zohour, a village overlooked by a number of occupation outposts. The local population suffers hardships from continued occupation. But villagers in Meimes suffer the most. Meimes is the closest village to areas under occupation, and large plots of farming land are under "Israeli" control. "Israelis" do not allow farmers to cultivate land here. Since the central town in the district is under occupation any legal paper work must be finalized inside the zone. That, according to Hassan Sakr a member of the Kfeir municipal council, makes life more difficult. "The Zimraya crossing point is usually closed during shelling or clashes. That hinders our work," Sakr said. Piles of cars waiting to enter government held territory at Zimraya. Their papers have to be carefully checked. When we approached to ask them about the situation inside the zone, they all claimed there was nothing wrong. We were told they won't risk their lives. But people living outside the zone under the protection of the Lebanese Army are optimistic. Abdel Ghani Abdel Ghani is from Kfar Shouba the first Lebanese village to be totally destroyed by "Israel" in 1970. He sought refuge here, and he believes he will return to his border village. Hopefully the pullout will soon start, we are now optimistic, our resistance heroes are dealing "Israel" painful blows, their militia is collapsing.. I will return home... "Israel" has reportedly informed its militia of the decision to withdraw from Hasbaya. If "Israel" withdraws its troops from Hasbaya soon, Madi will receive more clients in his restaurant and pool when it opens next summer... The hardships endured by people in Meimes and the rest of Hasbaya will also end".

Occupation's suppressive measures intensified(5)
"Israeli" occupation forces have intensified their suppressive measures against the residents of the zone "Israel" occupies in south Lebanon and its western Beqaa.

Reports from the occupied zone said the town of Ainata has been repeatedly stormed by the occupation forces and their collaborators.

"Yesterday Hussein Semhat, his wife, and their son were taken to the notorious al-Khiam detention center.
"The occupation today (yesterday) barred all the residents born in Ainata from leaving the occupied zone, the reports said, adding that similar measures have been imposed on the Bint Jbeil residents who are being harassed at the proxy militia's checkpoints while moving within the occupied zone.

"Moreover, the occupation forces prevented the Lebanese Internal Security Forces (ISF) personnel who live in the occupied zone from entering or leaving it.

"The occupation's militiamen said the measure, which also includes civil servants' families, is the result of a decision by the "Israeli" occupation army.

"On the other hand, the occupation's engineering forces razed the sides of the roads that link Hasbaya to Ain Qinya and Shwayya. The arbitrary measure caused heavy damage in the neighboring groves and their walls".

Earlier, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters targeted, at 5:45, enemy squad at Soweida outpost with the suitable arms scoring direct goals.

Also, the LRB fighters attacked, at 12:20, enemy movements at the "Israeli" post of Dabsheh with the proper weapons chalking up direct and accurate hits.
