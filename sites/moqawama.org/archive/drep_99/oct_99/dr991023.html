Daily Report



October Saturday 23-1999:

Another Israeli violation of April understanding(1)
Lebanese steadfast residents in Kfar Tibneet survived a violent night of Israeli bombardment that targeted their town. The occupation's artillery targeted the town's populated neighborhoods all night long.

The town residents woke up in the morning and came out of their shelters to find that most of the residential buildings were hit with bullets or fléchettes (small dart-shaped projectiles) that are spread by internationally-banned anti-personnel bombs. Four cars were also damaged in the bombardment and the raking conducted by the Israeli occupation forces.

In the morning, the occupation's artillery in the Zaffateh outpost bombarded the Iqleem al-Toffah Hilltops while the pilotless espionage MK drones heavily circled over the southern skies all night long and most of today (yesterday).

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) issued a communiqué in which it mentioned the following Israeli aggressions(2):
1:40, three 155mm missiles hit Mount Zaatar.
13:35, Mount Sojod was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
Between 18:10 and 19:05, four 120mm shells landed Ain area between the villages of Aita El-Jabal and Beit Yahoun.
18:45, Mount Zaatar between Tibnine and Baraasheit towns was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
Between 19:00 and 21:10, the perimeters of Haddatha came under five 81mm rounds and four 155mm missiles.
Between 19:20 and 19:55, Dallafeh area was targeted by four 120mm shells.
21:10, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to two 155mm missiles and one 81mm round.
21:45, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck Ali Taher forest.
21:55, three 105mm tank rounds were fired at Ali Taher woodland.
22:00, fifteen 81mm rounds slammed into the peripheries of the deserted Lebanese Army barracks at Nabatieh El-Fawqa.
23:35, the road linking between Henneih and Zibqeen villages was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
23:50, three 155mm missiles landed west of Yater village.

PRO-ISRAELI MILITIAMAN WOUNDED IN ROADSIDE BOMB ATTACK IN SOUTH LEBANON
A pro-Israeli militiaman was seriously wounded in a roadside bomb attack inside the zone Israel occupies in south Lebanon. Militia sources said the fighter was hurt in the Bir Kallab area. The militiaman the 59th to be wounded since January was taken by helicopter to Israel for treatment. Sixteen militiamen have been killed in the south so far this year. In Beirut, the Hizbullah resistance movement said its fighters had staged a series of attacks on the Bir Kallab area. Hizbullah is spearheading the guerrilla war to oust Israeli troops from southern Lebanon(3)

For its part, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Sami Jaafar and Hussein Salim Hachem targeted, at 12:40, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Twenty minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Ali Abdallah and Mohammad Ali Darwish rushed upon the same outpost with the various kinds of weapons. The militants managed to chalk up direct and certain goals(4)

LEBANON BANS FRENCH STAMP COMMEMORATING FRANCO-ISRAELI FRIENDSHIP(5)
 Lebanese postmen have been ordered not to deliver letters and parcels from France bearing a stamp issued to mark 50 years of Franco-Israeli friendship. The Lebanese security services issued a statement Friday telling the Libanpost company, which is responsible for mail distribution, to return to sender any post with the stamp, which shows the French and Israeli flags.

Anti-Israeli feeling runs high in Lebanon, whose southern border zone is occupied by Israeli troops. Beirut enforces the Arab boycott of Israel and any contact with "the Israeli enemy," direct or indirect, is forbidden and punishable by law.

The collaborators expelled a sergeant(6)
The Israeli allied militia expelled a sergeant in the Lebanese army from the occupation zone. The militia accused 35 year old Hani Yusuf Abu Dahan of collaborating with the Lebanese army. Abu Dahan told Lebanese police he was arrested two weeks ago at the hospital in Marjeyoun where he was visiting his wife. Abu Dahan, who comes from the occupied village of Hasbaya said the militia conducted him to the Zimraya crossing point that links the zone with the rest of Lebanon. He then drove his specially-adapted car on to the Lebanese army post, where the intelligence services took charge of him.
