Daily Report




October Wednesday 27-1999:

Introduction
Hours before the meeting of the April Understanding Monitoring Group to examine the Israeli occupations violations of the Understanding, the Israeli enemy once more deliberately breached the Understanding as its artillery pounded the villages adjacent to the occupied zone.

Meanwhile, Lebanon will mark the International Day to shut down the Israeli-run Khiam detention center in occupied South Lebanon on Thursday. Sit-ins, protest marches and hunger strikes are scheduled to take place. Days before, Israel has detained a number of Lebanese including a teenager.

FAMILY OF FOUR WOUNDED IN ISRAELI SHELLING IN SOUTH LEBANON
A Lebanese family of four, including an eight-year-old girl, were wounded this morning by Israeli artillery fire on south Lebanon. One of the injured is in serious condition.

A house in the southern village of Arab Salim was directly hit by an Israeli rocket this morning. The rocket fired from an Israeli manned position just above the village shattered the three storey house and wounded its occupants. Jawad Moussa, his wife Mona Hammoud, their eight year old daughter Afife and Moussa's mother Maryam were taken to hospital in the town of Nabatieh. Hammoud is in serious condition while the other family members suffered moderate injuries. According to his neighbors, it was the fourth time that Moussa had been wounded by Israeli fire. The morning shelling in an area just north of the zone Israeli occupies in the south also damaged homes, provoked panic in the village and forced school buses to return students home. Israeli shelling also targeted villages in the south facing the occupation zone(1)

For its part, the IR issued a communiqué in it reported the following Israeli aggressions (2):
1:45, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal village exposed to a barrage of light and middle machine gun bullets.

21:30, three 81mm rounds hit south of Baraasheit compound, whereas the surroundings of Haddatha position exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) men penetrated all the Israeli military and security measures taken around the Beaufort Castle and reached to about one hundred meters away from the post. The IR team of martyrs Youssef Rihani and Assaad Shabshoul detonated, at 16:20, a powerful explosive device as an Israeli foot patrol was on the road leading to the post inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Abbas and Moussa Shouman targeted the scene and Israeli reinforcements with the adequate weapons chalking up direct goals.

However, the group of martyrs Rajeh Eidi of the Islamic Jihad Movement in Palestine attacked, at 6:35, Soweida outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western and southern fortifications. At the same time, another group of the Movement targeted Tohorra outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring certain hits.

For its part, Amal Movement fired five artillery rounds at the Beaufort Castle.

Also, the IR mujahedeen observed, at 18:35, an Israeli engineering force beefing up Jamoussa position. Shortly afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Ali Khreibani and Izzeddine Ftouni fell upon it with the rocke-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Youssef Baddah and Hussein Younes targeted Jamoussa post of the 80th Command Brigade with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to score direct hits.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Marwan Faour and Adnan Hassan Izzeddine ambushed, at 19:30, a common Israeli-Lahdi infantry force near Haddatha compound targeting it with the machine guns and hand grenades scoring direct hits.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Yehia Abdel-Wahab Hammoud and Khaled Ahmad Daher targeted Jamoussa outpost with the proper weapons Chalking up direct goals, whereas the group of martyrs Ali Kamel Midlej and Hussein Mustafa Kobeissi attacked Haddatha compound with the suitable arms scoring direct hits.

Meanwhile, the IR team of martyrs Adel El-Akhrass and Ahmad Dbouk was attacking the targeted force with the appropriate weapons. Direct goals were scored, the militants reported.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Ismail Issa and Ali Ibrahim Hassan spotted, at 21:15, enemy movements at Jamoussa position. Then, the squad rushed upon them with the adequate arms chalking up direct hits.
 

ISRAEL STEPS UP DETENTIONS IN OCCUPIED SOUTH LEBANON(4)
 Reports from the occupation zone say that Israeli forces detained thirteen year old Ali Mohammed Abdel Hassan Srour who is from the village of Aita Shaab. Ali was reportedly taken to the Khiam detention center in occupied south Lebanon. Ali's parents and sisters were apprehended last month. Israeli forces also detained Najwa Semhat and her husband Ahmed from the village of Ainata. Najwa who is pregnant was reportedly bleeding as a result of torture at the hands of the Israelis and their local allies. International human rights groups like Amnesty International have documented torture in Khiam. In another development, Israeli forces Monday detained the parents of a teenage girl who hit the headlines after she was allegedly beaten up by members of the Israeli-allied militia. The committee for the support of Lebanese prisoners in Israeli jails said the Israeli intelligence services kidnapped the teacher 50 year old Mahmud Jaafar, from the school in Ainata and took him, with his wife Nahdiya, to a centre in the village of Bint Jbeil. It said the arrests came as a result of the media reaction to the case of sixteen year old Fatima Jaafar who was hospitalized in an unconscious condition on October 17, after being beaten in the head "in a centre controlled by Israeli intelligence and the militia." Committee head Mustafa Safa told AFP at the time that Fatima "was taken to the Shaib hospital in Sidon ... with a fractured skull and memory loss." A hospital official was quoted as saying the girl's condition was "very serious." Safa said Fatima had been arrested along with her brothers Bilal and Ahmed.

More than 120 Lebanese are detained without trial or charge, some since 1985, at the jail, which is manned by Israels proxy militia under Israeli supervision.

TWO JAILED FOR SPYING AGAINST ISRAEL(5)
 A former Israeli intelligence agent and a Lebanese policeman were sentenced to long jail terms Tuesday for spying against Israel by a court in Haifa, a judicial official said. The two, of southern Lebanese origin, whose identity was kept secret, were jailed for 18 years and 15 years respectively by the court in the northern Israeli city. The source said the first man, aged 52, who had been a longstanding senior official of Shin Beth, Israels domestic intelligence agency, in southern Lebanon, had been persuaded to turn his coat by the 47-year-old policeman. He had transmitted Israeli state secrets to Beirut. The two men were snatched from southern Lebanon by Israeli agents three years ago, and charged in August 1997 with spying against Israel, conspiracy and aiding the enemy in time of war.
