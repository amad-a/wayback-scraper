Daily Report




October Friday 15-1999:

Introduction
The news reported that the occupation needs some time to schedule the final withdrawal process and the new positioning points for fear that this withdraw may lead to a complete collapse of the collaborators militia which is currently with no command after agent Antoine Lahd was admitted to a hospital for treatment.

Meanwhile, the non-resident people of the occupied town of Ainata have proceeded with their action and meetings with political, religious and social figures in a bid to stop the Israeli occupations arbitrary measures against their town.

However, Amnesty International appealed to Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak to immediately release nine Lebanese, including two children, who have been held in a detention camp inside the occupied border zone of South Lebanon since October 7th.

Israeli withdrawal is eminent, but when(1)
The question has been taking a lot of the time of political, military, security and press circles. The step is nearly certain: The Israelis will pull out from Hasbaya and the rest of Jezzine, but when will the withdrawal take place?

On the ground, the picture is as follows: outposts that nearly have no equipment; only a few militiamen, rarely accompanied by Israeli troops, are in the occupied outposts, there are no vehicles or barracks except for a few guards at the entrances.

The occupation needs some time, according to Lebanese security sources, to schedule the final withdrawal process and the new positioning points for fear that this withdraw may lead to a complete collapse of the collaborators' militia which is currently with no command after agent Antoine Lahd was admitted to a hospital for treatment.

The occupation's command believes agent Karamallah Said (from Qlaile) would be a suitable successor for Lahd that would satisfy the militia's security body and the remains of its eastern and western regiments, the sources added, noting that conflicts between the military militiamen and the militia's security body are surfacing as the two parties compete to secure better positions in the withdrawal process, especially when it comes to obtaining visas and compensations.

The sources say the occupation army's withdrawal timetable has not been set yet although the decision has been taken and put into effect, but the confusions that govern the militia's position besides not being able to reach a new positioning line for the occupation army and its proxy militia imposed a longer delay, although field facts indicate that the details of the Jezzine area withdrawal are complete, but in Hasbaya are under construction.

The Lebanese sources said the occupation wants to prolong the militia's life as much as possible to use it in the settlement process negotiations.

ZIMRAYA BORDER-CROSSING CLOSED "FOREVER" AND MILITIAMEN INFORMED OF HASBAYA WITHDRAWAL(2)
Lebanon has denied receiving any official notification of an Israeli withdrawal from the eastern sector of the occupied zone. But Israels so-called northern command this afternoon reportedly informed Israeli allied militiamen that in the event it pulls out from Hasbaya it would only withdraw seventy out of the 350 militiamen who are originally from the region. Reports said that caused widespread anger and chaos in Hasbaya. Sources also said militia defections in the area have been at record highs this week. The Zimraya border crossing which links Hasbaya to the rest of Lebanon was shut down and one Beirut newspaper said it will never be reopened.

 Prime Minister Selim Hoss today (today) denied reports that the government has received official notification of Israeli plans to withdraw occupation forces from the Hasbaya region. There have been growing reports of Israeli pullbacks from the region which lies on the eastern sector of the zone Israel occupies in the south. Press reports in Beirut yesterday quoted official sources as saying Beirut has been informed of the expected Israeli pullout plan, and has been quizzed on how it would react. Hoss made clear that Hasbaya's fate would be similar to that of Jezzine, which was vacated by occupation troops in June. No Lebanese Army has deployed in the region, only police units patrol Jezzine. The prime minsiter also reiterated Lebanon's demand that Israel implements UN Security Council resolution 425 which calls for an unconditional and immediate withdrawal from Lebanon. Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak has pledged to withdraw occupation forces from southern Lebanon by July 2000, with or without an agreement with Syria and Lebanon. But that contradicts Israeli military officials who yesterday reiterated they would remain in Lebanon until security deals are concluded with Beirut. But there have been mounting reports of Israeli and Israeli allied militia troop movement in Hasbaya indicating a pullout is imminent. Sources quoted in the region had confirmed that Israel has already drafted plans for a military pullback to the main occupation town of Marjeyoun, deep inside the zone. The Zimraya border crossing which links occupied Hasbaya to the rest of Lebanon was shut down today (yesterday), and according to one Beirut press reports, for good. There was heavy machinegun fire around the border crossing today (today). Israeli jets also attacked the nearby Jabal Daher. Defections and resignations from the Israeli allied militia force reportedly picked up in Hasbaya this week. While speculation shrouds the Hasbaya pullout reports, radio stations in Beirut this afternoon claimed Israeli forces have abandoned the Tallat Soweida post which overlooks the villages of Arab Salim and Jarjoua in Iqleem al-Toffah. But the reports have not been confirmed.

Ainata people want siege off their town, prisoners released(3)
Non-resident people of the occupied town of Ainata have proceeded with their action and meetings with political, religious and social figures in a bid to stop the Israeli occupation's arbitrary measures against their town.

Thursday Mothers' sit-in was dedicated today (yesterday) to remind the world's public opinion of the nine detainees who had been arrested by the Israeli occupation and its collaborators recently.

The protesters presented the ICRC representative in Lebanon Henri Fournier with a memorandum containing the names and ages of the detainees.

The abduction of women, children and the elderly is an atrocious violation of international laws, the memorandum read, urging international human rights groups to take action and pressure the occupation to free the detainees.

Today's sit-in also witnessed first-hand reports told by the concerned people themselves about their experiences when the occupation and its collaborators stormed and ransacked houses, arrested civilians of all ages no matter what their health conditions were.

The most touching report was delivered by an old man who managed to leave the town after witnessing the storming of Ainata and detaining a number of its residents.

Amnesty appeals to Barak for detainees(4)
Amnesty International appealed Wednesday to Israeli prime minister Ehud Barak to immediately release nine Lebanese, including two children, who have been held in a detention camp inside the occupied border zone of south Lebanon since October 7th.

The appeal said the detainees were snatched from the village of Ainata inside the occupied zone and have been held at the al-Khiam jail, which is run by the Israeli occupation and its militia.

Amnesty International said it, "fears that the detainees could have been tortured  in the Khiam camp, which is the main detention and investigation center in the occupied area."

It recalled that a number of prisoners who were freed from the Khiam jail reported that they were tortured by electrical shocks and other means.

The Amnesty statement, released in Beirut by Lebanon's Follow-Up Committee for the Support of the Lebanese Detainees in Israeli Prisons, said Israel denies any responsibility for the Khiam jail and blames its militia, "but there is a clear link between the Israeli occupying forces and the arrests at Khiam camp."

It said the Israeli Army commander admitted last month to an Israeli court that the intelligence service trains Lahd militiamen on investigation and torture in the Khiam camp and that Israeli intelligence visited the prison three times during the past seven months.

Amnesty International demanded that "the detainees of the Ainata village are not subjected to any kind of torture or maltreatment in the camp" and "to release them immediately as long as no charges were pressed against them or being tried in line with international trial norms."

There are nearly 140 Lebanese, including a number of women and teenagers, still held at the detention camp, which was established in 1985 when "Israel" withdrew the bulk of its troops from Lebanon and established the so-called security zone.

Israeli Aggressions(5)
The Israeli occupying troops staged the following aggressions against the Lebanese areas:
Between 00:10 and 12:15, Mount Sojod was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
Between 7:00 and 7:15, the perimeters of Jibaa town came under seven 155mm missiles and six 120mm shells.
11:20, the surroundings of Baraasheit town exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 18:10 and 18:45, nine 155mm missiles slammed into the west of Jibal El-Botom village.
18:50, Mount Kabir, west of Siddiqeen village, was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
Between 19:00 and 19:40, the perimeters of Zibqeen came under twelve 155mm missiles.
19:05, the Israeli fighter-bombers raided in Tasseh Well watercourse.19:10, the Israeli warplanes launched a sorties into the north of Zibqeen.
19:20, the Israeli war jets dropped four air-to-ground rockets in Tasseh Well watercourse.

Military Operations(6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants observed, at 00:25, an Israeli motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Qassem Khreis and Ali Merhi rushed upon it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.

Also, the IR mujahedeen spotted, at 18:30, an Israeli infantry force near the perimeters of Chihine village. Then, the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Chalhoub and Ali Hassan rushed upon it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Ossaili and Ali Ahmad Merhi fell upon Blat post with the adequate weapons. Definite hits were chalked up, the militants told.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Hassan Hammoud and Mahmoud Mohammad Fayad attacked, at 20:55, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring certain hits.
