Daily Report




October Monday 25-1999:

Israel: Sky above Lebanon is dangerous for Israeli aircrafts(1)
Israeli Air Force Commander Eitan Ben-Eliahu said yesterday that the skies above Lebanon are becoming more dangerous for Israeli aircraft, but that the IAFwas improving its methods of evading enemy fire. Speaking at a press conference held at an air base in the center of the country, General Eitan Ben-Eliahu said that in Lebanon today, there are "more anti-aircraft weapons, more sights [for targeting aircraft], more attempts to hit planes. Against this we are attempting to avoid routine: changing [our] means, methods, [and] flying techniques. The Hizbullah, and others, are trying to disrupt and destroy, and we are improving our methods and means of defense, and are doing everything necessary in order to avoid being hit."

The Air Force chief's comments come two weeks after a Hizbullah announcement that it had acquired a "secret weapon" that would surprise Israel. The organization has apparently been trying to acquire modern anti-aircraft weapons.

Responding to a question on the Hizbullah's intentions of shooting down Israel Air Force planes or helicopters over Lebanon, Ben-Eliahu said, "We should all be glad that, until now, it [the IAF's air defense against anti-aircraft fire] has worked successfully. But these things aren't an insurance company. They are liable to change from day to day."

Ben-Eliahu said that the marked rise in the number of Israel Air Force attacks in Lebanon in recent months is the result of a number of causes, including the nature of Hizbullah activity.

Berri says peace hopes declining(2)
Speaker Nabih Berri said over the weekend that the prospects for a peace agreement are declining because of the Jewish states escalation of attacks.

Speaking during an official visit to Poland, Berri said he expected that peace negotiations would not begin until next years United States presidential elections have concluded. This delay isnt in the interest of peace, and we therefore should be careful, he said. Although were not pessimistic, were also not optimistic.

Israeli Aggressions(3)
The Israeli occupying forces launched the following aggressions against the Lebanese liberated areas:
Between 00:10 and 7:05, the perimeters of Haddatha town came under nineteen 155mm missiles.
Between 2:20 and 7:10, Mount Zaatar was shelled by nine 155mm missiles.
Between 5:25 and 5:40, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal village exposed to four 155mm missiles and two 120mm shells.
Between 9:20 and 9:40, five 155mm missiles struck Mount Sojod.
9:30, two 155mm missiles hit Mlita hilltops.
10:30, the Israeli warplanes raided into the peripheries of Jarjoua town.
18:45, Mount Abou Rkab was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
Between 23:25 and 23:40, thirteen 105mm missiles slammed into Kfour ravine.

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyr Mustafa Mozannar attacked, at 6:00, Kfar Houna position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were chalked up, the militants reported.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Qassem Ballouk and Mohammad Abdallah Sawli targeted, at 9:10, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR militants observed, at 20:25, an Israeli motorized force on Rihan heights.

Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Marwan Talal Faour and Salah Abdel-Hadi Ghassan attacked it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite hits.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Ali Ghaleb Ali Ahmad and Hassan Adel El-Akhrass targeted, at 23:15, Dabsheh outpost with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.
