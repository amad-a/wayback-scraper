Daily Report




October Friday 1-1999:

Introduction
The recent successful military operations staged by the Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen had left a direct impact in the ranks of the "Israeli" allied militiamen, especially those in the Eastern Sector, Hasbaya. The reports say that most of Hasbaya's collaborators have publicly expressed their desire to quit the militia.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" occupation troops continued their aggressions against Lebanon where their warplanes struck the south for the fifth straight day damaging several houses and setting off brush fires.

Will the Jezzine story be repeated in Hasbaya?(1)
"The impact of the Islamic Resistance's (IR) operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators are now taking their toll on the proxy militia's structure faster than ever.

"In the past, the Resistance's blows needed some time to be reflected on the situations of the occupation's collaborators in the form of resignations AND desertions from the occupied zone.

"The operations of Bait Yahoun (3 days ago) and Rshaf (yesterday) left a direct impact in the ranks of the militiamen, especially those in the eastern sector (Hasbaya).

"Press reports from Hasbaya said more than 350 of those who are still dealing with the occupation have publicly expressed their desire to quit the militia, while only 95 wanted to retreat with the remains of the militia in case the occupation pulled out from the area.

"The reports were based on a poll conducted by the occupation's field command in the sector which has given personal forms that were filled out by each collaborator about his history with the militia (name, date of birth, date of collaboration, training courses, residence, salary, and number of family members).

"The form ends up with a question on whether the collaborator wants to stay in the area after the withdrawal or to retreat with the occupation forces.

"According to the reports, 225 collaborators said they will stay and give themselves up to the Lebanese authorities, 45 wanted to leave the country, and 95 said they wanted to stay in the militia.

"The reports said these figures resulted from the success of what happened in Jezzine to those who surrendered to the Lebanese authorities. The 45 who wanted to travel fear tough trials as they had perpetrated crimes and dealt with the enemy's intelligence (the activity of most of them had been unveiled to the Lebanese army intelligence by repenting or prisoner militiamen).

"Those who wanted to retreat to Marjeyoun are married to women from that area and can live there and they are probably still hoping that the final withdrawal may include the fulfillment of the occupation's promise to provide a solution for the proxy militia".

"Israeli" aggressions: 2 raids and artillery bombardment(2)
""Israeli" occupation's warplanes at 9:25 Thursday raided Wadi al-Hijari (between the towns of Maifadoun and Zawtar al-Sharqieh).

"The enemy warplanes fired two air-to-ground missiles that exploded 100 meters away from the houses of Zawtar, inflicting on them various types of damage.

The houses belong to Mohammed Deeb Qassem, Amin Khalil Qassem and Mohammed Ameen Qassem.

"The enemy planes at 15:10 targeted the area between Sojod and Oqmata in Iqleem al-Toffah, firing two air-to-ground missiles which ignited fires that Civil Defence men were not able to put out.

Earlier, the "Israeli" artillery bombarded the Yohmor Plain, the Litani River gorge and the Hamra Farmstead with a number of 155mm rounds.

"At dawn, the occupation pounded the Zahrani River gorge, Mount al-Rafih (Iqleem al-Toffah), the outskirts of Yater and Jibal al-Botom with different types of rounds. The shelling went on sporadically until sunset.

"At 19:00 yesterday (Wednesday) "Israeli" warplanes raided the Froun area firing a number of missiles that set fires in neighboring groves.

"Lebanese security sources disclosed that the occupation has been deliberately using incendiary phosphorus bombs when shelling hilltops and wadis in the south and the western Beqaa.

"Such bombardment yesterday set fires that devoured thousands of square meters of planted fields and bushes. Civil Defense teams, in cooperation with residents, tried to extinguish the fires, but they were targeted by shots fired from the outposts of the occupation forces and their collaborators".

Military Operations(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen observed, at 22:50, enemy movements near Zimraya position. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Haidar Ismail and Nouh Hassan targeted them with the adequate arms scoring direct goals.

LEBANON ACCUSES "ISRAEL" OF INTRANSIGENCE(5)
"Lebanese parliament's foreign relations committee in Beirut today (yesterday) discussed "Israel's" latest attacks against Lebanon. Prime Minister Selim Hoss who headed the meeting in his capacity as foreign minister, updated deputies on his recent discussions in New York where he attended UN General Assembly meetings. He warned that "Israel" does not seek peace as manifested in its daily attacks on Lebanese towns and villages. Parliamentary sources said following the meeting in downtown Beirut, that despite efforts underway to kick-start peace negotiations with "Israel", both sides remain far apart. Hoss reportedly stressed, neither secret nor public peace negotiations are taking place with "Israel". He also said Lebanon will not enter any peace talks if there are plans to resettle Palestinian refugees here, that is out of the question", the premier added.

MOTHERS OF LEBANESE IN "ISRAELI" DETENTION CAMPS APPEAL TO U.N(6)
"The mothers of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" detention centers appealed Thursday to the United Nations to secure their release. Some 100 people took part in a sit-in outside the headquarters of the United Nations Interim Forces in South Lebanon (UNIFIL) in Tyre and handed over a letter for U.N. Secretary General Kofi Annan. The letter urged U.N. efforts to secure the release of the detainees in "Israeli" camps and in Khiam center, in occupied southern Lebanon. An estimated 140 Lebanese are detained at Khiam. Most are held without trial or charges for several years. One of the detainees is freelance journalist Cosette Ibrahim, 25. She was detained on September 2 by "Israeli" security agents at her village inside the occupied zone. Cosette was accused by "Israeli" authorities of writing reports about the occupied zone and submitting information to the Lebanese army related to the movement of "Israeli" troops. For the first time ever this week, "Israel" admitted that it has a direct role in the detention of Lebanese".
