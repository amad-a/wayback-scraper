Daily Report



October Monday 4-1999:

Lull in artillery fire over as "Israel" pounds south(1)
"With the Jewish holiday season ending over the weekend, "Israeli" artillery guns began to step up bombardments of the south after a month-long lull while warplanes continued launching air strikes in reprisal for individual resistance assaults.

"Warplanes fired missiles Sunday evening into an area near Jibaa opposite the northern sector shortly after Hizbullah fighters targeted the Bir Kallab outpost.

"Jets had earlier attacked an area just north of Yater facing the western sector of the occupation zone from where Amal guerrillas had launched a mortar attack on the Blat outpost.

The area between Yater and Kafra came under heavy bombardment at the same time. The area was also "heavily shelled Saturday following an assault on the Blat post.

"The high number of air raids throughout the south in recent days ­ 27 sorties in the past week alone ­ has come as pre-emptive and retaliatory artillery bombardments by the "Israelis" and the SLA* have significantly declined.

"The SLA militia ­ notoriously lax in taking precautions not to hit civilian areas with its mortar shells ­ has been ordered by the "Israelis" to leave any retaliation to the artillery guns ranged along the border.

"Although artillery shelling tends to be less accurate than pin-point air strikes, the "Israelis" with their 155mm guns are considerably more prudent than their militia allies.

Security sources said the "Israelis" are concerned that haphazard mortar shelling by the SLA could inflict civilian casualties which, in turn, could force Hizbullah to respond with a Katyusha attack on the settlements of northern Galilee.

"While the "Israelis" do not relish rocket attacks on Galilee at any time, the past few weeks have been particularly sensitive due to the Jewish holiday season. The holidays began with Rosh Hashanah, the Jewish new year, on Sept. 11, followed by Yom Kippur and Succot. The season ended over the weekend with Simhat Tohra.

"Security sources said the "Israeli" Army was anxious to prevent a potential barrage of criticism from northern Galilee residents if they had been forced to spend the holidays in their bomb shelters because of the threat of Katyusha attacks in retaliation for Lebanese civilian casualties caused by careless artillery fire.

"Security sources believe that pre-emptive shelling and retaliatory shelling will gradually return to normal levels in the coming days".

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)
In its communiqué, the IR reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
2:45, four 120mm shells struck the perimeters of Slouqi Valley.
6:20, Oqmata area was targeted by two 60mm rounds.
6:50, Mount Sojod was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
Between 7:10 and 7:30, twelve 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Jibaa town.
13:30, several 155mm missiles landed the outskirts of Haddatha town and three others on the outskirts of Haris village whereas the surroundings of Rshaf position exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
14:10, the "Israeli" warplanes fired two air-to-grounds rockets into the area between Kafra and Yater.
16:00, the west of Yater village was bombarded by seven 155mm missiles.
18:15, four 155mm missiles landed the perimeters of Jibaa town.
Between 8:20 and 20:45, Mount Sojod was shelled by seven 155mm missiles and five 60mm rounds.
18:46, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the peripheries of Jibaa.
20:40, the surroundings of Sojod outpost came under 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Military Operations(3)
Amal Movement fired three 81mm mortar rounds at the surroundings of Hemayed position.

The Islamic Resistance (IR) group of martyrs Ali Al-Bostani and Hassan Hijazi attacked, at 18:00, an "Israeli" infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct goals.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Abdel-Rida Morad and Fouad Abdel-Rassoul Baalbacki was targeting Bir Kallab outpost. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score certain hits.
