Daily Report
 
 

October Friday 29-1999:

Introduction
Hundreds of Lebanese people gathered at downtown Beirut to mark the International Day for the closure of Khiam detention center. Relatives and supporters of the Lebanese detainees staged a seven-hour hunger strike on Thursday to highlight the plight of their loves ones and press for the closure of the notorious prison.

The call was made by the Follow-up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in Israeli prisons which invited the Lebanese to take part in the Thursday Mothers sit-in and hunger strike in front of the ESCWA HQ in Beirut.

LEBANON MARKS INTERNATIONAL DAY TO CLOSE KHIAM DETENTION CENTER
Lebanon today (yesterday) marked what is being dubbed the World Day for the Closure of the Khiam detention center. A sit-in and hunger strike by relatives of Lebanese detained in the Israeli run jail in the occupied south was the latest bid to rally international support to their cause.

 Moussa Hussein Samhat is making one request to the world. He wants to know the fate of his 36 year old sister Najwa who along with her husband Ahmed Jaafar and her son Ahmed were detained by Israeli forces and their local militia allies in occupied south Lebanon. All he knows is what the International Committee of the Red Cross told him - they were taken to the Khiam detention center in occupied south Lebanon. Moussa was not the only one asking questions. More than 100 Lebanese protestors and human rights activists demonstrated outside the UN house in downtown Beirut demanding the release of detainees and the closure of Khiam.

(NS: Mohammed Safa, the head of Follow Up Committee for Detainees told Future Television: "28 October is the International Day for closing down Khiam. The reason is this camp is against all principles of human rights. Same time today (yesterday) we have sit in front of UN, the same thing is happening around the world in Canada, Washington, France, New York, Japan, Palestine, Israel)

Mothers of Lebanese prisoners in Israeli jails went on a hunger strike for six hours to protest their continuing detention. Some 160 are held - many of them women, children and the elderly. And the follow up committee handed the UN a memorandum addressed to the Secretary General of the world body Kofi Annan. The memorandum outlines the situation of the detainees and requests the world body to send an envoy to check the conditions at the detention center.

(NS: Safa saying: "we hope that this time the UN listens to our voices the voices of the mothers children at least if they cannot close the center then what is strange for 14 years the UN did not send anyone into Khiam)

Khiam was established in 1985. 10 years later, Israel granted the International Committee of the Red Cross access to visit Khiam. But the organization is not allowed to report its findings.

None of the detainees have access to lawyers. They are held without trial or charge, some of them for more than 15 years. They are held either because they refuse to cooperate with Israeli forces and their local allies or they are suspected of collaborating with the Lebanese resistance or army. According to the follow up committee for the support of detainees, 14 prisoners have died in detention. Maltreatment and torture have been documented by groups like Amnesty International.

Israel for years has maintained that the prison is administered by its local militia allies in south Lebanon. But not too long ago, it made an unprecedented admission. It said its security services interrogates, instructs and cooperates with its local militia allies in running Khiam. Despite this it continues to ignore calls by human rights groups to end abuses there. Zeina Khodr. Future Television. Beirut(1)

Outside UN House in downtown Beirut, up to 200 protesters carrying banners and pictures of loved ones gathered for the occasion, joined by MPs and officials.

Khiam currently holds about 150 detainees without trial.

It was set up by the Israeli occupation forces in 1985, and has been branded by human rights groups as a center of brutality, with the main methods of torture reportedly consisting of flogging, electrocution, and beatings.

According to reports by the committee, more than 2,000 Lebanese citizens have been held at the detention center since it became operational. The committee said there are currently at least 15 minors, nine elderly people, including a 72-year-old woman, 60 students and five women detained at Khiam.

Andree Lahoud, the presidents wife, arrived at the site in time to watch a re-enactment of a torture scene where an Israeli agent beat hooded prisoners on the head, bellowing at them to lower their heads.

Watching the scene was too heart-wrenching for one of the elderly mothers from Hasbaya, who started crying.

Our son, Rabah, was kidnapped at a checkpoint two years ago, explained the father. He was just 16 then.

Many of those detained at Khiam were kidnapped because they refused to collaborate with the Israelis or join their agents. Others, mostly the elderly, were detained as a means of pressuring their sons to join the collaborators.

Protesters also waved banners in support of Samir Qintar, who has been dubbed the doyen of detainees as he was the first to be captured by the Israelis 22 years ago. Friends of Cosette Ibrahim, the 25-year-old journalist kidnapped two months ago during a visit to her family in the south, came out in force.

Weve learned that she had been sent to the hospital because her pre-existing ulcer deteriora-

ted as a result of extreme torture conditions, said May Elian, one of Ibrahims college friends.

The Israelis are behaving just like the Nazis, she added. Theyre making me grow up on hatred, which I will feed my children on for 100 years to come.

British teacher Lynda Keen protested the double standards used by the West. The whole world condemned the taking of foreigners as hostages during the (civil) war, she said. But now, dozens of Lebanese are being kidnapped by Israelis and the world remains quiet.

Hazem Beblawi, the executive secretary of the Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia, stepped out of UN House to join the protesters and said that its important that people protesting human rights abuses choose UN House to do so.

He added that he would convey the plight of detainees to UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan, who had met with representatives of detainee support groups during his visit last year.

Safa, however, said that Annan had promised to send a representative to discuss the detainees plight. But we have yet to see anyone, he said.

In Israel, about 20 members of the Four Mothers group demonstrated in front of the defense ministry in Tel Aviv. Organizers said it was the first time that Israelis and Lebanese were demonstrating together.

Four Mothers was founded by the mothers of four Israeli soldiers serving in Lebanon.

The group calls for a unilateral Israeli withdrawal(2)

HUMAN RIGHTS WATCH SAYS ISRAEL SHOULD END TORTURE PRACTISES IN KHIAM(3)
The US-based Human Rights Watch group called on Israel to respect international humanitarian law in occupied southern Lebanon and immediately end torture practices in Khiam. The group said Israel is responsible for Khiam. The director of the Middle East and North Africa division of Human Rights Watch, Hanny Megally, said Khiam operates entirely outside the law, adding the international community should no longer accept that Israel has nothing to do with the prison. The organization said Israel bears responsibility for its own actions and the actions of its allied militia that affect civilians under international humanitarian law.

Military Operations(5)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants spotted, at 17:40, enemy movements inside Jamoussa post of the 80th Brigade Command. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Qassem Jaber and Hussein Fadlallah Chalhoub attacked them with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits.

Thirty minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Haidar and Issam Qansou targeted Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Zaatar and Mustafa Chokair set off, at 19:40, a powerful roadside bomb as a Lahdi motorized patrol of three military vehicles was traveling on the road linking between Rihan and Aramta destroying one vehicle and inflicting its occupants.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Hani Taha and Ashraf Kheireddine showered the remaining vehicles with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades suffering more casualties among the enemy ranks. The clashed lasted until 20:20.

As a Lahdi reinforcements came, at 20:50, to the scene, the IR team of martyrs Junblat Jaber and Abdallah Ismail fell upon them with the suitable arms. The militants managed to chalk up direct goals.

Thirty minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Hassan Hayek and Moussa Habib Fahs rushed upon Beaufort Castle with the appropriate weapons. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen told.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Salem Kamal Halawi and Mohammad Hassan Hammoud reiterated, at 21:45, targeting Beaufort Castle with the proper arms chalking up direct hits.
