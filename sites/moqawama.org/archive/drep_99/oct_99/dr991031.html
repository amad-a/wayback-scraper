Daily Report
 
 

October Sunday 31-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah asserted that we will keep on resisting until the Israeli occupation is out of our land without any conditions or accords.

However, another civilian was killed when a cluster bomb left over from earlier Israeli aggressions on Western Beqaa went off. The incident came only days after two little girls were killed and other children were wounded in a similar incident.

Hizbullah: we will resist until occupation is out(1)
We will keep on resisting until the (Israeli) occupation is out of our land without any conditions or accords, Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said.

He was speaking at the massive rally organized by the Islamic Jihad in Palestine at al-Yarmouk camp in Damascus to mark the fourth anniversary of Dr. Fathi al-Shqaqi's martyrdom (IJP's Former Secretary General).

Lebanon today, thanks to its brave resistance, is imposing its conditions on the arrogant Zionist entity, although Lebanon is the weakest among the countries surrounding the Israeli entity, the secretary general said.

Official Lebanon says that "Israels" ending its current quagmire in south Lebanon will only be through withdrawal from all of the Golan Heights (until the 4th of July line), while the Islamic Resistance says more than that, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

He disclosed that several international envoys have asked Hizbullah to promise to stop military activity after the occupation's withdrawal from south Lebanon, "in exchange for turning Hizbullah into a political party" and giving Lebanon a lot of economic grants.

IJP's Secretary general Dr. Ramadan Abdallah Shalah stressed the Palestinians' right to armed struggle through any territory in which there is resistance, in reference to two attacks staged on Israeli occupation forces in south Lebanon by IJP on Thursday.

Jihad will continue in Palestine until the occupation is removed, Shalah said.

Hamas Politburo Member Mossa Abu Marzoq and Iranian Ambassador in Damascus Hussein Sheikh al-Islam both delivered speeches at the rally. They stressed the importance of Palestine in Moslems' creed besides underlining the invariable position on the settlement process.

Civilian killed in cluster bomb explosion left by occupation(2)
Only days after two little girls were killed and other children were wounded in a similar incident, Kamel Moheiddeen Abdel-Al, 55, was killed today (yesterday) when a cluster bomb left over from earlier Israeli aggressions on western Beqaa went off.

Abdel -Al was working in one of his groves near the Qaroun Lake when the bomb exploded and took his life.
Resident of al-Qaroun town urged the Lebanese government and international groups to conduct comprehensive survey of the area lest their should be any mines or bombs left over from Israeli aggressions on the area.

Security forces sealed off the area and barred residents and journalists from nearing the groves for fear that other bombs may still be there.

Occupation releases one citizen only to detain another(3)
The Follow-up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in Israeli Prisons (FCLD) said the Israeli occupation forces have released Fahd Ali Saleh, a teacher at Aita al-Shaab High School following two months of detention at al-Khiam detention center.

The Israeli occupation army, however, immediately detained another man, FCLD added.

Yaqoub Srour, 50, was "charged" with rejecting the existence of the proxy militia and the Israeli occupation besides cooperation with the Lebanese authorities.

Ali Srour, Yaqoub's son, has been detained in Ayalon prison (inside occupied Palestine) for 13 years.

Israeli Aggressions(4)
The Israeli occupying troops staged the following aggressions:
Between 4:00 and 6:00, Mount Zaatar was shelled by six 155mm missiles.
6:05, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the surroundings of Beit Yahoun outpost.
Between 6:30 and 6:45, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to two 120mm shells and two 81mm rounds.
14:00, the west of Yater village was bombarded by seven 155mm missiles and three 120mm shells.
15:05, the surroundings of Baraasheit compound exposed to various kinds of machine gun fire.
17:30, the surroundings of Soweida outpost came under five 81mm rounds and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets, whereas four 81mm rounds hit Mount Al-Rafih.
At 18:20 and 18:30, the Israeli warplanes raided into Tasseh Well watercourse.
21:40, seven 81mm rounds and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Military Operations(5)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of martyrs Ahmad Ismail Ossaili and Ali Ibrahim Hassan attacked, at 22:25, the Israeli outpost of Dabsheh. The mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.
