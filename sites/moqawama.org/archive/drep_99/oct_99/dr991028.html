Daily Report



October Thursday 28-1999:

Introduction
A child was killed and four others were wounded by a cluster bomb that had been thrown by the Israeli occupation forces during the 1982 invasion. The bomb exploded as the children were playing around.

In the meantime, the Israeli occupying troops and their agents expelled a family of four members from the occupied zone for refusing to collaborate with them.

Israeli aggressions continue, enemy raids wadis
The Israeli occupation forces proceeded with their aggressions against Lebanese peaceful villages that are adjacent to the border zone occupied by the Israeli forces in south Lebanon and the western Beqaa.

Two Israeli warplanes at 7:30 a.m. Wednesday raided the wadis and hilltops between the towns of al-Rmadieh and Jibal al-Botom, firing two air-to-ground missiles. No casualties or damage were reported.

Prior to the raid, the occupation's artillery bombarded the targeted area with 155mm rounds. The shelling later covered the outskirts of Zibqeen, Habboush, Shokeen, Maifadoun, the deserted Lebanese army barracks in Nabatieh, the gorges of the Zahrani and Habboush rivers, the Arab Salim - Habboush Highway and Iqleem al-Toffah.

Earlier, the town of Upper Nabatieh this morning was the target of Israeli 12.7mm rounds that were aimed at the town's residential areas and hit some of the houses' walls.

The occupation's warplanes and helicopters circled heavily over southern areas. Enemy warplanes flew over the capital Beirut at 13:25(1)

In its communiqué, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mentioned the following Israeli aggressions (2):
Between 5:25 and 13:25, the perimeters of Haddatha town came under nineteen 155mm missiles and three 120mm shells.
5:40, five 155mm missiles hit the peripheries of Aita El-Jabal village.
5:45, Mount Zaatar was targeted by two 155mm missiles.
6:05, seven 81mm rounds slammed into the surroundings of Haddatha compound.
7:15, the area between Zibqeen and Chaitieh was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.
7:35, the Israeli warplanes dropped one air-to-ground rocket into the area between Zibqeen and Chaitieh.
13:50, Jib Soweid area was shelled by 155mm missiles.
17:45, two 81mm rounds fell on the perimeters of Mlikh village.

Child killed, 4 wounded in explosion of Israeli bomb(3)
Child Warda Mohammed al-Moussa, 4 years, was killed and four other children: her sister Basma, 10, Aiman Mahmoud al-Izzo, 4, his sister Hoda, 7, and Ahmed Omar al-Qario, 9, were wounded by a cluster bomb that had been thrown by the Israeli occupation forces during the 1982 invasion. The bomb went off as the children were playing.

According to the official security report, the children, of Syrian nationality, were playing yesterday morning near a factory at the Zahle-Bareleis road when one of them tripped over the bomb.

When the bomb went off, the children were close to one another. They were taken to hospitals in the area while security forces came to the area to conduct investigations.

Commander of the Shtura Gendarme Division Major Fadi Salman and military expert Sergeant Hosni Krayyem said the bomb had remained in the spot since the Israeli invasion in 1982.

They said the surveillance of the area where the explosion took place revealed that there were such enemy bombs in the area.
The whole area will be searched to make sure no such deadly bombs are left there, Salman told reporters.

A Lebanese engineer, Naji al-Hrawi, was killed 15 years ago in a similar explosion.

A number of Lebanese are killed or wounded every year by bombs left over from earlier Israeli aggressions, especially bombs like the one that exploded yesterday (between 5 and 10 cms) that can go off years after being thrown from a warplane or being fired by artillery.

Occupation expels family from zone(4)
The pro-Israeli militia expelled from the occupation zone on Tuesday another family that refused to collaborate with the occupation.

Jamil Ali Wansa, 42, his wife Majeda, 43, and their two children Mansour, 16, and Bushra, 9, were escorted by the collaborating militiamen to Kfar Tibneet, one of the five crossings that link the occupied zone with liberated areas.

The family, which came from the village of Dibbin near Marjeyoun, walked to a Lebanese Army checkpoint and then went to Tyre to stay with their relatives.

According to police, the Wansa family refused to collaborate with the Israeli occupation and would not allow their son to join its proxy militia. As a result, they were declared "unwanted people" inside the border enclave.

The family said the militia's so-called security official in the town Abbas Abu Abbas (alias al-Konti) came to the family house in Dibbin. When the wife refused to open the door as her husband was at work, the collaborator broke into the house, brutally beat the wife and told her that the family must leave the occupied zone, otherwise they will be taken to the al-Khiam detention center.

Already this year, some 50 people have been expelled from the occupied zone after refusing to collaborate with the occupation and its militia or being accused of cooperating with resistance forces.

Arrests in phone scam(5)
State Security said on Wednesday that two suspects ­ Rafik Jamil Naim and Wadih Beshara Ammar ­ had been arrested on charges of operating an illegal telephone network in the occupied zone.

It said in a statement that the exchange was supplied with 18 cellular lines and four land lines, which were all legal lines. Naim then illegally linked the exchange to Israels telephone network and another exchange he owned in Ain al-Roummaneh.

The scam, which began in April, was defrauding the Treasury of $3 million a month.

Military Operations(6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Jawdat and Rifaat Olleik attacked, at 6:45, the Israeli outpost of Dabsheh with the proper weapons scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Ashraf Kheireddine and Imad Haidar Ahmad detonated, at 17:50, as a group of Lahdi members were at the entrance of Aramta position inflicting a number of casualties.

For its part, the fighters of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 7:10, an Israeli squad at Blat post with the artillery shells chalking up direct goals.

However, Amal Movement fired, at 11:45, several 120mm shells at Mohaisbat position. Also, the Movement unleashed, at 12:40, a number of 107mm rocket-propelled grenades at Taibeh position.
