Daily Report


October Thursday 21-1999:

Introduction
The Lebanese fishermens boats yesterday came under fire from the Israeli occupation troops which proceeded with their aggressions against the Lebanese liberated areas.

Meanwhile, the Israeli enemy officials were quoted as saying a withdrawal from the occupied southern Lebanon will cost more than 200 million dollars, mainly for security matters.

However, the Israeli supreme court has postponed until unspecified date the consideration of a plea presented by the lawyer Steve Reesh on behalf of Lebanese prisoners Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid and Hajj Mostafa Dirani.

Enemy gunboats stage aggressions on fishermen(1)
The Israeli occupation's gunboats proceeded with firing shots at the Lebanese fishermen's boats.

Although the fishermen have been abiding by the occupation's instructions: to avoid sailing for more than one kilometer off the Lebanese shore, turning on the boats' lights, and more than one fisherman sails in the boat, the fishermen's boats at dawn today (yesterdaty) came under fire from Israeli gunboats.

The fishermen had to leave their nets at sea near the Mansouri shore and return to the town's port.

They called on the Lebanese cabinet to intervene and call on human rights groups to deter the occupation's threats which are preventing the fishermen from earning their bread.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) issued a statement in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions(2):
00:05, four 155mm missiles hit the perimeters of Haddatha town.
00:35, Mount Sojod was shelled by the perimeters of Jibaa town.
00:40, four 155mm missiles struck the peripheries of Jibaa town.
6:00, one 155mm missile landed the house of Hussein Youssef Saad at Aita El-Jabal village but it didnt explode.
12:00, Daidaba hilltops came under ten 160mm missiles.

ISRAEL CALCULATES COST OF LEBANON WITHDRAWAL(3)
Israeli press reports claimed an army withdrawal from occupied southern Lebanon will cost Israel some 234 million dollars.

Most of the money will reportedly be allocated for constructing shelters in Israeli towns and villages along the border with Lebanon. A new border fence with sophisticated electronic systems will also be built. Lebanon has also been calculating its own costs.

Lebanese hostages Sheikh Obeid and Hajj Dirani plea for release(4)
The supreme court in the Israeli entity has postponed until unspecified date the consideration of a plea presented by lawyer Steve Reesh on behalf of Lebanese prisoners Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid and Hajj Mostafa Dirani.

The attorney had demanded the release of the two prisoners due to "the violation of human rights represented by their detention, besides the fact that Israel will not benefit from their detention."

Sheikh Obeid has been imprisoned in the Israeli occupation's prisons in occupied Palestine for 11 years; Dirani has been in detention for six years.  They were abducted in two Israeli piracy operations from their homes in Jibsheit (south) and Ksarnaba (Beqaa).

The occupation's authorities are still holding the two hostages under the pretext of exchange of prisoners or the release of Israeli pilot Ron Arad whose plane was downed as he was launching aggressions on Lebanese populated villages.

DETAINEE MOTHERS TO HOLD HUNGER STRIKE(5)
The follow-up committee for the release of Lebanese detainees in Israeli detention camps today announced that the mothers of detainees will hold a hunger strike next Thursday. The committee invited Prime Minister Selim Hoss to take part in an international campaign that will be launched on that day. It aims to raise world-wide support for closing the Israeli-run Khiam detention center in occupied southern Lebanon. The committee said campaigns will be launched in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and France. Israel holds some 158 men, women and children in Khiam, where international human rights groups have documented torture and maltreatment.

Military Operations(6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) men spotted, at 00:00, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Aramta heights. Shortly afterwards, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad El-Husseini and Atef Mahdi Obeid targeted it with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits and preventing it from achieving its goals.
