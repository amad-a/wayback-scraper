Daily Report


December Friday 31-1999:

The Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen banged, once more, deep inside the "Israeli"-occupied zone breaking through all the "Israeli" security procedures. Yesterday, a martyrdom car bomber blew himself up as an "Israeli" Army convoy near Marjeyoun killing and wounding fifteen soldiers. The martyrdom-bomber exploded his car at 5:15pm as the "Israeli" convoy of four military vehicles was approaching the Dimashqieh junction on a hilltop between Qlayah and Marjeyoun. The attack, which is the first for almost four years, was timed to concide with Quds International Day. The security sources said that such an attack would have required a careful planning. It is worth noting that the last martyrdom bomb attack occurred in March 1996 when a resistance fighter blew himself up beside an "Israeli" patrol near Odaisseh village.

The IR released a communiqué in which it reported that on the occasion of  the martyrdom of Imam Ali Ben Abi Taleb (peace be upon him), on the eve of Quds International Day, announced by Imam Khomeini and on the beginning of Qadr Night, one of the IR heroic martyrdom-militants detonated his car, at 5:17pm, when an "Israeli" occupation forces' military convoy was passing by the crossroad of Qlayah, Dimshqieh and Marjeyoun.

The "Israeli" convoy, consisted of a Hummer all-train vehicle, a truck and two command cars, was hit directly killing and wounding fifteen "Israeli" troops among them was a senior officer.

Immediately after the martyrdom operation, the "Israeli" Army and its agents condoned the scene, whereas the "Israeli" helicopters and ambulances rushed to transfer the casualties.

The "Israeli" enemy moved the military truck, which was hit directly, from the scene at 19:20, i.e. following two hours of the operation.

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah will reveal the identity of the martyrdom-militant today during the ceremony of the Quds International Day which will be held at the Southern Suburb of Beirut.

Earlier, The IR militants spotted, at 13:20, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Hemayed position. Then, the IR team of martyrs Moussa Amin Shouman and Youssef Ali Ammar fell upon it with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

Five minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Khalil Haidar and Ali Mahmoud Horshi rushed upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled chalking direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Najib El-Rahman Mahmoud Tormos and Tony Khalil Abi Ghanem attacked Bir Kallab compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, whereas the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Hassan Ghaddar and Marwan Mohammad Kisserwani was falling upon an "Israeli" motorized force near Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Ali Qassem Yassine and Ayad Hussein Ghandour attacked violently and in a concentrated manner, at 17:00, Radar post with the suitable arms damaging one radar installation.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters launched, at 7:05, a swift attack against the "Israeli" post of Blat chalking up direct and certain hits.

In the meantime, another group of (LRB) targeted Tair Harfa outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits(1)

LEBANESE VOICE SOLIDARITY WITH DETAINEES IN "ISRAELI" CENTERS(2)
"A sit-in was held infront of the International Committee of the Red Cross building in Beirut, in protest over the continued detention of more than 120 Lebanese in the Khyam detention center and more than sixteen inside "Israel". Protestors marched in the Hamra district in solidarity with detainees, who have been held for years, including women and children, without charges. The head of the committee for the release of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" prisons called on the government to adopt a tough stance in negotiations with "Israel" when dealing with the issue of detainees. He warned against referring their ordeal to expert committees, saying the release of all detainees should constitute a priority. Safa welcomed the release of five Hizbullah fighters from "Israel" this week, saying it was the fruit of his groups' campaign, adding the German mediation helped ease international criticism against "Israel", stemming from the illegal detention of Lebanese. He also said negotiations should require the release of all those kidnapped from Lebanon, abducted in occupied southern Lebanon, missing in action, and should secure compensation for the families of detainees killed in "Israeli" prisons. No peace deal should be signed, Safa warned, before all these issues are solved. International human rights groups, including the London-based Amnesty International, have documented torture and maltreatment at the Khyam detention center in occupied southern Lebanon".
