Daily Report
 
 
December Monday 20-1999:

Resistance keeps "Israelis" on their toes(1)
"Despite "Israel's" belief that Syria is restraining resistance fighters in the south, attacks on "Israeli" outposts continued over the weekend as Hizbullah leaders reiterated their vow to continue to battle the occupation forces". "Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, Sheikh Nahim Qassem, listed three key components on Sunday to be adopted by the resistance for the duration of the peace process.

"The first was to continue battling "Israeli" forces in the south regardless of the progress or stalling of negotiations and regardless of whether "Israel" and America scream or not.

The second was to confirm Hizbullahs traditional silence on its post-peace intentions.

We shall retain this stand until the suitable moment because it brings power to the resistance, he said during an iftar in Shmustar.

The third was the belief that the current situation was imposed by arms and intimidation and is not a true peace.

"Hizbullah fighters continued launching attacks over the weekend, generally concentrating on "Israeli" targets.

"Late Friday night, the resistance detonated a roadside bomb beside an "Israeli" engineering patrol near the Blat outpost in the western sector. On Saturday, fighters from Hizbullah and the multi-faith Lebanese Resistance Brigades separately attacked the outposts of Qalaah, Bir Kallab, Mashaaroun and Qabaa". No casualties were reported.

"Israeli" agents ask Jewish state to grant them asylum(2)
"SLA* militiamen asked "Israel's" Supreme Court on Sunday to grant them refuge in "Israel" should Ehud Barak withdraw his troops from Lebanon as planned.

""Israeli" attorney Zvi Rish, who petitioned the court, said that up to 17,000 Lebanese may seek political asylum or even citizenship in "Israel" because they fear for their fate at the hands of Hizbullah after any withdrawal. The number includes SLA militiamen and their families who are growing more afraid as a withdrawal from the occupation zone becomes more likely, he said.

Theyll be slaughtered, Rish told Reuters. There is no doubt that the Hizbullah will slaughter them. However, Barak told SLA leader Antoine Lahd last week that "Israel" would guarantee the safety of the militiamen.

The security of the soldiers of the South Lebanon Army and the inhabitants of the security zone will be guaranteed in the framework of the agreements "Israel" intends to negotiate with Syria and Lebanon, an "Israeli" Army statement quoted Barak as saying. "Israeli" public radio said the domestic security service Shin Bet was preparing a rescue plan for SLA members and their families.

Yossi Peled, a former "Israeli" Army commander in the occupation zone, said that "Israel" was morally obligated to protect the militiamen and must go beyond mere statements in a peace agreement to guarantee security.

People will be hurt, Peled told "Israel" Radio. Families will be murdered. Thats the Lebanese mentality.

"Israeli" Aggressions(3)
The "Israeli" occupying troops launched the following aggressions:
12:15, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck west of Baraasheit town.
15:30, the surroundings of Sojod outpost exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine guns and several rounds.
15:40, Mount Sojod was targeted by four shells and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
23:30, the "Israeli" war jets raided into the area beneath to Bir Kallab outpost.
23:45, one 120mm shell hit Mount Sojod.

Military Operations(4)
The IR team of martyrs Hassan Abdel-Latif Jawad and Wissam Abdel-Hussein Zaiour attacked, at 15:20, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Talal Mohammad Al-Saghir and Hassan Mohammad El-Hajj Ali was targeting Bir Kallab outpost with the proper weapons chalking up direct goals.

For its part, Amal Movement fired nine 120mm shells at Taibeh compound.
