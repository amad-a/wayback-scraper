Daily Report
 
 
December Sunday 5-1999:

Israeli Aggressions :
In its communiqué, the IR reported the following Israeli aggressions:
5:25, nine various kinds of shells slammed into Mount Zaatar and the surroundings of Haddatha compound.
5:50, seven 120mm shells fell on the perimeters of Haddatha town and five 81mm rounds on the surroundings of Haddatha compound.
6:43, the Israeli warplanes raided into Ain Smahieh area between Maifadoun and Zawtar.
14:50, the towns Kfar Romman and Habboush came under a number of 81, 120 and 155mm missiles and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
15:10, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck Habboush.
Between 15:15 and 15:35, Mount Bassil was shelled by eight 155mm missiles.
15:55, three 120mm shells were fired at the outskirts of Chihine village.
16:20, the peripheries of Majdal Selem exposed to three 120mm shells, whereas the crossroad of Slouqi Valley came under two others.

Military Operations :
The IR team of martyrs Ali El-Khatib and Jihad Hijazi targeted, at 14:40, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Zehri and Karim Atwi was rushing upon an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to chalk up direct goals.

Twenty minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Ali Saad and Othaina Kansou targeted Zaffateh emplacement with the appropriate arms scoring direct hits.

Moreover, the IR mujahedeen spotted, at 18:45, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up the recently established Shayar Azour position. Immediately afterwards, the IR group of mart Lutfi Madi targeted it with the proper arms barring it from achieving its goal.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Abbas Hassan And Nabil Akhdar attacked, at 20:35, an Israeli armored vehicle at Rihan hilltops with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Kassem Kaoun and Hassan Harb fell upon an Israeli motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades.
 

However, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) detonated, at 14:30, an explosive device at the barricades of the Israeli occupations proxy militia outpost of Soweida scoring direct and certain hits.

In the meantime, another group of LRB targeted the same outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

Japan: Peace won't be at Lebanon's expense(3)
Japanese prime minister's special envoy for foreign affairs Nobaro Sashero announced that peace must not be at the expense of Lebanon or the cause of the Palestinian refugees.

Following his meeting with Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss, the Japanese diplomat said his country had proposed a 4-point plan on South Lebanon, reiterating Tokyo's support for the implementation of UN Security Council Resolution 425.

The peace process is a very important topic to all the world's nations including Japan, he said.

The Japanese envoy unveiled that he had handed invitations to visit Japan to President Gen Emile Lahoud and PM al-Hoss.
