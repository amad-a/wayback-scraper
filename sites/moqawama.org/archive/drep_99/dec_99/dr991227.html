Daily Report
 
 
December Monday 27-1999:
 
Five hostages were released by "Israeli" enemy
Yesterday, five Lebanese hostages were freed from the captivity of the "Israeli" enemy's prisons inside the occupied Palestine. The hostages have been held for years in so-called administrative detention. Hizbullah sources asserted that nothing was offered to "Israel" in return for the released hostages.

Reportedly, the operation was accomplished under the auspices of Germans. In the past, Germany was involved in negotiations for the exchange of hostages and bodies between "Israel" and Hizbullah.

The five boarded a Lufthansa plane for their trip to Beirut via Frankfurt. According to Hizbullah sources the prisoners are Ahmed Obeid, Hashem Fahs, Hussein Tlaiss, Ahmed Srour and Kamal Rizk. Obeid and Fahs were bodyguards of Sheikh Abdel Karim Obeid, a senior Hizbullah official captured by "Israel" during a raid in July 1989.

"Israel" has admitted to holding 19 Lebanese citizens, in addition to Obeid and Mustafa Dirani, another senior Hizbullah official, in "administrative detention" as bargaining chips for the release of Ron Arad.

War officials have faced with an awkward situation since the Tel Aviv District Court decided to call for the immediate release of the prisoners, who have been held without trial. About a year ago the captives appealed to an "Israeli" court, through their lawyer, Zvi Rish, seeking freedom. The State appealed the District Court's decision to the Supreme Court whose president, Judge Aharon Barak, promised to reach a decision in a number of weeks.
Attorney General Eliyakim Rubinstein has supported the release.

"Israeli" Aggressions(1)
In its statement, the IR reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:
12:25, three 81mm rounds hit the perimeters of Mlikh village.
15:00, Mount Sojod was targeted by one 120mm shell.
16:40, three 155mm missiles struck the peripheries of Loweizeh village.

Military Operations(2)
The IR team of martyrs Fadi Taissir Batsh and Hani Ali Taha attacked fiercely, at 6:40, the "Israeli" compound of Taibeh. The militants, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score certain hits.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 16:40, an "Israeli" motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Then, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Abdel-Latif El-Jawad and Ali Hussein Jradi rushed upon it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Mustafa Nasser and Shaalan Youssef Al-Mokdad was falling upon Sojod outpost with the proper weapons scoring direct goals.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Marwan Mohammad Kisserwani and Hussein Hassan Ghaddar targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct hits.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters staged a fifteen-minute attack, at 6:40, Soweida outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the fighters managed to score direct hits.

Half an hour later, the LRB fighters rushed upon an enemy squad at Beaufort Castle with the suitable arms chalking up direct goals.

Martyrs(3)
The IR announced, proudly, the martyrdom of Bilal Melhem El-Bazzal, born on 18-9-1973, married with three children, joined the IR ranks in 1989, shared with a number of cultural and military courses and gained a tribute from Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah. He was martyred on 25-12-1999 in the course of performing the heroic confrontation at Kfar Houna area.
