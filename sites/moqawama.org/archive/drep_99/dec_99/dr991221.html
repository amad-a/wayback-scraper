Daily Report
 
 
December Tuesday 21-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah asserted "the resistance's arms shall remain solid and sharp. The political developments in the world and region cannot intimidate the courage and determination of the resistance men".

Meanwhile, the school of Arab Salim town reopened yesterday and received its students following a three-day closure as a result of the "Israeli" artillery shelling which wounded twenty students.

However, the "Israeli" sources claimed that "Israel" will have to spend 240 million dollars to build new lines of defense after the pullout of its occupation troops from South Lebanon next year.

Hizbullah SG: Lebanon's interest in Resistance until the last occupation soldier is out(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah warned against "any lagging behind or fatigue before we harvest the fruit of liberation".

"Speaking at an Iftar held by the Women's Association in the Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance at the Shahed Complex Saturday, the Secretary General noted that "the major obsession for the "Israeli" occupation and the US is the Resistance."

"That is why they have been asking the Lebanese and the Syrians since the first negotiation rounds to drop the strength weapon and sit unarmed at the negotiating table, he said.

The Iftar was attended by Minister Hassan Shalaq'a wife who represented the president's wife, along with wives of ministers and MPs.

"It is in Lebanon's real and definite interest for the resistance to continue firing and conducting operations, inflicting on the enemy maximum casualties until the last Zionist soldier leaves south Lebanon," his Eminence said.

"On the Arab Salim crime, Sayyed Nasrallah stressed that the occupation's rushing to apologize for the crime has indications that the enemy fears that Katyusha rockets may rain down on northern occupied Palestine".

More conflicts among collaborators(2)
"Report from the occupied zone said agent Taleb Siblini, the western sector's security chief in the "Israeli" occupation's proxy militia, has obstructed construction activities along the borders between Lebanon and occupied Palestine.

"Siblini tried to create some riots along with a number of fellow collaborators in the area of Naqoura, and the villages of al-Jibbien, Tair Harfa, and Shamaa, the reports added, stating that the "Israeli" occupation forces had to control the Naqoura port to circumvent the riots while the collaborators in these villages refrained from mounting guard outside their villages.

"The reports said the "Israeli" intelligence has been intensifying its efforts to circumvent the conflicts between collaborators' chiefs which have led to first fights as was the case in the town of Ramia between followers of agents Ahmed Shibli Saleh and Ali Khaleel Hemayed, and several fights in other towns".

"ISRAEL" MULLS LEBANON WITHDRAWAL COSTS; "ISRAELI" ALLIED MILITIAMEN SEEK REFUGE(3)
"Official figures in "Israel" claim that "Israel" will have to spend 240 million dollars to build new lines of defense after the pullout of its occupation troops from southern Lebanon next year, in line with a pledge by Prime Minister Ehud Barak. Channel Two Television in "Israel" said the amount included the building of a 40-kilometer-long (25-mile-long) fence at the border, new military posts on the "Israeli" side of the border and the tearing down of posts in Lebanon. It also covers the warning and surveillance equipment. Barak has vowed to end "Israel's" 21-year-occupation of Lebanese territory by next July. The Yedioth Ahranot newspaper said "Israel's" defense ministry is to solicit bids in two weeks for the building of new defensive lines in preparation for the withdrawal. The work would include a new road network, strengthening the electrified fence along the border, fortifications, and trenches. The paper described the bid as the first concrete plan for an "Israeli" pullout from occupied southern Lebanon. The plan, called New Horizon, was modified, Yedioth reported, following the resumption of Syrian-"Israeli" talks last week. "Israeli" military chief of staff Shaul Mofaz approved modification to the withdrawal plan that had initially been drawn up. The newspaper said Barak will approve it next week. Barak's insistence on ending "Israel's" occupation and the latest declared plans by the "Israeli" defense ministry have sent "Israeli"-financed, backed, and trained militiamen to the Jewish state's supreme court, seeking asylum. "Israeli" attorney Zvi Rish, who petitioned the court, said up to 17,000 Lebanese could seek political asylum or even citizenship in "Israel" should Barak make good on his pullout promise. The number includes militiamen and their families who are growing more afraid as an "Israeli" withdrawal becomes more likely", Rish said.

CLASSES RESUME AT ARAB SALIM SCHOOL(4)
"The school of Arab Salim opened Monday for regular classes after a three-day closure following "Israeli" artillery shelling that wounded 20 pupils last week. School director Mohammed Abdullah told reporters that most of the teachers and pupils, some of whom were wounded in last Thursday's shelling, were present. Two "Israeli" shells slammed into the school, wounding 20 pupils, four of them seriously. The children, aged between 5 and 13, were attending classes at the school just north of the central sector of the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon. Abdullah said security precautions were being intensified and there were plans to build a shelter in the school. Arab Salim school is situated in the Iqleem al Toffah region, which is frequently the target of "Israeli" shelling. Abdullah said damage caused by the "Israeli" shelling last week had already been repaired. "The appearance at school of those young children, especially the ones who were wounded, proves that Lebanese children are defying the "Israeli" aggressions and that they are determined to continue their studies even under the shelling," Abdullah said. The "Israeli" army claimed it bombed the school by mistake and apologized. "Israel" bombed the school with internationally-banned cluster shells".

Military Operations(6)
The IR team of martyrs Ahmad Youssef Hammoud and Abdel-Hassan Berro targeted, at 12:30, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Zahreddine and Adnan Fadl Khreis was rushing upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up.

Also, the IR militants observed, at 18:40, a Lahdi engineering force, accompanied by a bulldozers as it was beefing up Hemayed position. Then, the IR squad of martyrs Abbas Khreibani and Ali Abdallah Krayem targeted it with the adequate weapons barring it from achieving its goals.

Forty minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Mustafa Kamel Nazar and Fouad Madani ambushed a Lahdi patrol as it was traveling on the road leading to Soweida outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to hit a military vehicle.

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Saad and Othaina Qansou was targeting the same position with the rocket-propelled grenades preventing it from backing the attacked force.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Daoud Fahs and Ali Moussa Nassour rushed upon Tohorra outpost scoring direct goals.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Qassem Ali Harb and Hassan Khalil Mansour targeted violently, at 20:00, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen mentioned.

Thirty minutes later, the IR militants spotted enemy military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Ahmad Youssef Hammoud and Abdel Hassan Berro fell upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled chalking up direct hits.

Also, the IR mujahedeen observed, at 20:35, enemy military vehicles moving inside Blat post. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Rida Jaafar Khalil and Ahmad Hassan Trad rushed upon them with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring accurate goals.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Farid Said Bazzi and Ali Hassan El-Refahi was attacking enemy motorized movements at Mount Bassil with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to score direct hits.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Nayef Ahmad Rammal and Rida Jaafar Khalil fell upon Sojod compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up direct and definite goals.
