Daily Report
 
 
December Monday 13-1999:

Israeli Aggressions(1)
The Israeli occupying forces launched the following aggressions:
Between 00:00 and 5:10, the outskirts of Haddatha town exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire, whereas three flare shells were unleashed over head. At the same time, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 00:00 and 5:20, the outskirts of Aita El-Jabal exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
5:25, two 155mm missiles hit the outskirts of Haris village.
13:20, the south of Yater village came under a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

Military Operations(2)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Hassan Daoud Fahs and Ali Moussa Nassar attacked, at 13:05, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting its eastern fortification.

Five minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Ahmad Hussein Taleb and Abbas Ali Karaki fell upon an Israeli infantry force at Rihan hilltops scoring direct and certain hits.

Also, the IR militants spotted, at 13:45, Lahdi movements at Hemayed position. Immediately afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Abdel-Rahman Ali Houmani and Hachem Fakhreddine rushed upon them with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct and definite goals.

Two hours later, the IR unit of martyrs Monzer Mohammad Monzer and Ahmad Ali Ossaili fell upon an Israeli infantry force at the outskirts of Aramta village. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct and certain hits.
