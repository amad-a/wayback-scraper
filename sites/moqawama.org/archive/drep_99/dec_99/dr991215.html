Daily Report
 
 
December Wednesday 15-1999:
 
"Israeli" jets blast Iqleem al-Toffah(1)
""Israeli" jets staged several air raids on Iqleem al-Toffah in the south on Tuesday, and a civilian was wounded in shelling, a security source reported.

"Two "Israeli" jets fired four missiles near Sojod in Iqleem al-Toffah and fired two more missiles 10 minutes later at a target in Oqmata, east of Sojod and 12 kilometers from Nabatieh.

" Elsewhere, a woman was reportedly wounded in "Israeli" machine-gun fire directed against Hay al-Maslakh in Nabatieh.

"Hanan Abdel-Wahhab Andris, 35, was taken to Nabatieh Hospital where she received treatment for bullet wounds to her leg.

"The incident occurred as "Israeli" forces and their agents manning the Dabsheh post were combing the area".

In its communiqué, the IR mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions (2):
Between 00:30 and 13:30, Mount Sojod was bombarded by nine 120mm shells and six 155mm missiles.
5:55, a volley of medium machine gun bullets struck the surroundings of Sojod outpost.
6:00, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
At 14:34 and 14:42, the "Israeli" war jets staged two sorties into Mount Sojod.
19:00, the surroundings of Ali Taher, Borj and Beaufort Castle exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
21:15, the perimeters of Baraasheit came under a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

"ISRAEL" KIDNAPS LEBANESE CITIZEN(3)
"The Follow-Up Committee for the release of Lebanese detainees in "Israeli" detention centers today (yesterday) said "Israeli" occupation forces and their local allies have kidnapped a 23-year-old Lebanese man from occupied southern Lebanon. The group said Youssef Ali Srour was taken from his home in occupied Aita Shaab, which has also been under an "Israeli" siege. Youssef was reportedly taken to the notorious Khiam detention centers, where his brothers Adnan and Ahmad are being detained as well.

"Israel" has detained hundreds of Lebanese in Khiam and in prisons inside "Israel" without charge in clear breach of international law. Scores of international human rights organizations have condemned maltreatment and systematic torture in Khiam".

Military Operations(4)
The IR team of martyrs Faraj Amin Ballouq and Mohammad Jaafar El-Mazbouh attacked, at 00:12, enemy movements inside Sojod outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct and certain hits.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters targeted, at 6:25, Blat post with the suitable weapons chalking up direct goals.

In the meantime, another group of (LRB) was rushing upon Tair Harfa outpost with the proper arms scoring direct hits.
