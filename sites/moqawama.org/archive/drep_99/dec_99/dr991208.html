Daily Report
 
 
December Wednesday 8-1999:

Introduction
The Israeli occupying troops escalated their aggressions against the populated areas in South Lebanon in another violation of April Understanding. A citrus farmer was killed and two workers were wounded by tank fire in the neighboring village of Mansouri. At Majdal Zoun town, also, a family had narrowly escaped unscathed when their house was badly damaged by the Israeli shelling.

Hizbullah asserted that targeting civilians should not change the rules of the game. We are committed to the maximum limits of protecting the people and civilian targets. The resistance considers it a religious and humanitarian duty not to allow any laxity, no matter what the circumstances are, it said.

LEBANESE CIVILIAN KILLED IN ISRAELI SHELLING(1)
Fifty-year old Ahmad Abdallah Dawood was found dead at dawn in a plantation field in the village of Mansouri, near the southern port city of Tyre, and close to the zone Israel occupies in the south. He was transported to the government hospital in the city. Two Syrian men, who were working for Dawood, suffered injuries and were taken to hospital for treatment. More than 25 Israeli shells fell overnight on Mansouri and the nearby village of Majdal Zoun. There had been no military activity in that region prior to the Israeli attack. Israeli gunners also attacked residential neighborhoods in the village of Majdal Zoun, destroying three houses. United Nations peace-keepers in the region said a family of six made a quote miraculous escape from death when they fled their house which was destroyed by Israel shelling.

The bombardment was in clear violation of the 1996 monitoring panel which observes a cease-fire aimed at protecting civilians. Hours after the attack, representatives of France, the United States, Israel, Lebanon and Syria met in the border town of Naqoura where they discussed six Lebanese complaints over previous Israeli shelling of civilian targets. Israel for its part has lodged nine complaints, over claims that Lebanese resistance fighters breached the cease-fire accord. Since the beginning of the year, 23 Lebanese civilians have died and 161 others have been injured in southern Lebanon. Israel has been occupying Lebanese territory in the south for the past 21 years. Prime Minister Ehud Barak has pledged to withdraw his forces from the south by July next year, as part of an agreement with Lebanon and Syria. He has remained vague on whether he would order a unilateral pullout from the south in the event frozen peace talks are not relunched.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it mentioned the following Israeli aggressions (2):
Between 6:15 and 13:10, the perimeters of Loweizeh village came under nine 155mm missiles.
6:20, Mount Sojod was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
Between 6:25 and 6:45, Majdal Zoun village was bombarded by twenty 81mm rounds and 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire causing damage to the houses of Rida Ali Salim Fayad and Hussein Mohammad Fayad, the car of Haidar Ahmad Salman and the tractor of Rida Ali Salman.
6:25, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the perimeters of Mansouri.
6:35, two 155mm missiles hit Jib Soweid area, east of Majdal Zoun.
13:05, the Israeli fighter-bombers dropped two air-to-ground rockets.
19:50, five 155mm missiles hit Daidaba hilltops.

Hizbullah: targeting civilians will not give enemy gains(3)
Commenting on the Israeli violation of the April Understanding in the Mansouri area that led to the martyrdom of a civilian and wounded others, Hizbullah's official in the south his Eminence Sheikh Nabil Qawooq said, "As political maneuvers and American tours continue, the enemy has intensified targeting civilians and southern villages in an indication of the political and military bankruptcy and in violation of the April Understanding's essence and content."

"Targeting civilians and the repeated violations of the April Understanding will not give the enemy any gains or changes in the situation or the rules of confrontation, nor will they screen the bitter defeat of the enemy," he said.

The Resistance is committed to the utmost to protecting its people and the civilian targets as a national and human duty that can not be ignored or be subject to maneuvering no matter what the political conditions or changes are, he added.

Military Operations(4)
In retaliation FOR the massacre committed by the Israeli occupation forces against Mansouri village and its population, the IR group of martyrs Ghassan Mohammad Zaatar and Haitham Afif Ghamloush ambushed, at 16:25, an Israeli infantry force at Radar position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants managed to inflict a number of casualties among its ranks.

In the meantime, the IR unit of martyrs Allam Soueidan and Ali Abdallah Bakri in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) rushed upon Radar position with the artillery shells. A radar installation was damaged whereas the blazes were seen enkindling the western fortification. Also, it was reported that several casualties were suffered.

Earlier, the IR team of martyrs Khalil Mohammad Madi and Ahmad Mahmoud Mansour attacked, at 12:35, an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Mohammad Harb and Majed Mohsen Kansou was rushing upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

For its part, the groups of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 6:30, the positions of Shamaa, Hardoun and Bayada with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying a 12.7mm heavy machine gun and scoring direct goals.

Fifteen minutes later, the artillery unit of the (LRB) bombarded the positions of Radar and Blat damaging a radar installation and scoring other goals.

Hizbullah discusses situation in besieged Ainata(5)
Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem hosted a delegation from the occupied town of Ainata. The delegation explained the suffering of the residents due to the arbitrary measures of the Israeli occupation forces besides the siege, harassment and detentions which included some women.

Sheikh Qassem underlined the need to unite all efforts to raise this humanitarian issue at all forums, especially the international bodies, in order to shed more light on the Israeli terrorism which is covered by the US.

The deputy secretary-general promised to raise the issue with Lebanese officials to take action at all levels.
