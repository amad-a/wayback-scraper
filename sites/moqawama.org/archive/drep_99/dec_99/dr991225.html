Hizbullah: agents have no religion, sect or confession(1)
"The Zionist enemy will get out of the south and the western Beqaa with or without an agreement. This fact is not out of love; it is a Zionist fate, Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said.

"When the pullout takes place, it will be one of the qualitative fruits of the Resistance, his Eminence said at an Iftar held by influential figures from the occupied zone at the Shahed Complex.

"The Iftar was attended by MPs Mohammed Fneish, Nazeeh Mansour, Ali Hassan Khalil, Lebanese army chief's representative Mohammed Abdallah, representative of General Security Mohammed Faqih, vice-president of the Lebanese Communist Party Saadallah Mazraani, and other figures from the occupied zone.

"This stage must witness qualitative Resistance operations. Whether the negotiations advance or not, the resistance will proceed as long as there is occupation, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding, "The Resistance's arms must be adhered to officially and popularly regardless of the outcome (of the negotiations).

"The Secretary General underlined that if the Zionist withdrawal takes place in a month, Lebanon will remain within the circle of Zionist ambitions, for the enemy will withdraw its military but will seek to stay in Lebanon at the security, economic and other levels.

He warned that the Zionist entity will work, in cooperation with the US, to create unrest in Lebanon
following the pullout.

"Let ""Israel"" pull out of our land and there won't be any internal problems, Sayyed Nasrallah said, recalling the lies made by the occupation that Hizbullah would harm the people of Jezzine.

The Secretary General called on the people of the occupied southern area to be aware of the enemy's plots, stressing the need for "confronting normalization with the enemy which is a peril that will face Lebanon and the region."

Finally, his Eminence underlined that the fate of the occupation's collaborators will be in prisons or graves.

"An agent has no religion, sect or confession. Therefore, these collaborators must face the severest sentences on charges of treason. Either they leave with the enemy or be tried and punished," his Eminence concluded.

"Following Sayyed Nasrallah's speech, Michel Abu Zeid delivered a speech on behalf of the occupied zone's influential figures".

LEBANON SAYS RESISTANCE ATTACKS TO CONTINUE UNTIL ALL LEBANESE TERRITORY INCLUDING 'SEVEN VILLAGES' ARE LIBERATED(2)
"The head of parliament's foreign relations committee, Deputy Ali Khalil, today (yesterday) denounced "Israel's" new reported annexation of Lebanese territory, saying such moves reveal "Israel's" "real intentions". Khalil confirmed that "Israel" has dispatched technical teams to southern Lebanon to plot a new border with Lebanon in a way "which does not reflect reality". DPA yesterday quoted UN sources as saying "Israeli" soldiers have fenced in and annexed the outskirts of four Lebanese villages on the border with "Israel" over the past few years, including areas in the villages of Odaisseh, Maroun Al Ras, southeast of Marjeyoun, and Alma Al Shaab. The "Israelis" claim the fences were erected for practical reasons and to apprehend infiltrators, the UN sources said. They added that the original fence demarcating the common border line is still in place and is monitored around the clock by the United Nation's 55-member Armistice Observer Group, who were deployed in 1948 to monitor the Lebanese-"Israeli" armistice pact. Lebanon will continue to launch resistance attacks in southern Lebanon, Khalil added, until "Israeli" occupation troops withdraw from all Lebanese territories, including the Shibaa farms and the seven villages, which lately raised controversy here. Prime Minister Salim Hoss yesterday denied reports that Beirut will drop its demands for seven villages which were annexed to Palestine in 1923 during the drawing of the border agreement of the French and the British. He was quoted by the Daily Star as saying the villages were part of Palestine when France and Britain carved up their protectorates in the Levant. But Hoss yesterday stressed that the return of the villages will be "a Lebanese demand that will be on the agenda of the future peace talks with "Israel"". Lebanon is expected to resume peace negotiations with "Israel" following the second round of talks between Syrian and "Israeli" officials in Washington on January 3".

Martyr Rihan laid to rest(3)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) Friday escorted Martyr Abbas Rihan to his final resting place in his hometown of Maifadoun.

"Rihan was martyred during confrontations with the "Israeli" occupation forces in the vicinity of the Beaufort Castle outpost on August 22, 1999.

"Mourners moved in cars from Sayyida Fatima's (A.S.) Complex in Sidon to the entrance of Maifadoun. Several Hundreds of people were waiting to carry the coffin in the town's streets amidst slogans insisting on the continuation of the Resistance until the occupation in removed.

Chief of Hizbullah's Political council MP Mohammed Raad, MP Abdallah Qassir, party officials, political, social, educational and religious figures took part in the funeral.

"These martyrs have proven to the Ummah that they are able no only to defeat the enemy at the military and morale level but also to inject defeat into the "Israeli" society as well, Qassir said in a speech he delivered during the funeral.

"This Resistance will continue as long as one Zionist is on our soil. Operations will go on until our people return to all the occupied villages and we recover all our detainees and prisoners, he added.

"The Martyr was then laid to rest at the town's cemetery".

IR stages nine operations against enemy outposts(4)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) Friday afternoon staged a series of attacks against the outposts of the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators.

Squad of Martyrs Ahmed Taleb and Abbas Karaki at 12:30 spotted and attacked enemy vehicle movements in the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in al-Qalaah.

In the meantime, squad of Martyrs Hassan Mansour and Ahmed Hammoud targeted the Jamouseh outpost, the command HQ of the proxy militia's 80th regiment, while squad of Martyrs Adnan Khreis and Qassem Khreis in IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted the Radar outpost and squad of Martyrs Talal al-Sagheer and Abdel-Hassan Berro pounded the enemy's outpost in Blat.

After about 15 minutes (at 12:45), squad of Martyrs Hassan Zahreddeen and Mohammed Salman attacked the occupation's outpost in Dabsheh.

IR men used machine guns and rocket propelled grenades in the attacks scoring definite hits in the targeted outposts.

Later in the afternoon, squad of Martyrs Nizar Awdi and Moussa Shuman at 13:45 Friday targeted the enemy's outpost in Sojod with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades, destroying the outpost's new northern rampart and inflicting definite casualties in the ranks of the enemy troops manning the outpost.
Also, the IR team of martyrs Youssef Ali Ammar and Ahmad Khalil Haidar targeted, at 17:00, enemy movements inside Blat post with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Najib El-Rahman Mahmoud Tormos and Mahmoud Ali Mahmoud was falling upon Radar position damaging its fortifications.

Ten minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Ammar Hassan Mohanna and Hicham Ali Salami rushed upon Tair Harfa compound with the suitable weapons causing damage to its fortifications.

LEBANESE FISHERMEN INTERROGATED BY "ISRAELI" NAVY(5)
"Two Lebanese fishermen were abducted by "Israeli" sailors and questioned for twelve hours. Lebanese police said Samih and Abbas Darmiss had just cast their nets two kilometers (one mile) off the coast of the southern city of Tyre when an "Israeli" patrol boat opened fire on their craft. They were ordered aboard the patrol boat and interrogated about Lebanese resistance activities in occupied southern Lebanon. After 12 hours, the fishermen were released and returned to Tyre. The "Israeli" navy periodically detains and questions Lebanese fishermen".
