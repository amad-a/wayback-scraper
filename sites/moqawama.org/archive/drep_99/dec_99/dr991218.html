Sayyed Nasrallah: aggression on Arab Salim deserves shelling colonies (1)
Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said, "What happened Thursday in Arabsaleem deserves bombardment of Zionist colonies in Northern Palestine by the Resistance."

Speaking at an Iftar held by "Imdad Imam Khomeini's Charity" in Kafaat (Southern Suburbs), Sayyed Nasrallah said, "The aggression that took place in Arabsaleem, and the harm inflicted on the town's schoolchildren is a blatant dangerous violation of the April Understanding."

Such incidents have been on the rise for the past few weeks. One civilian was killed in a grove weeks ago. This issue must be the concern of all the Lebanese. I, once again, call on the media to take interest and expose these issues, he said.

The Lebanese must know the details of what is going on in the south to be with the Resistance when it implements, on the ground, its decision to defend its people".

"What happened yesterday deserves bombardment of the Zionists' colonies in Northern Palestine. In our opinion it is worth this kind of reply and more, but we're going to settle for a clear and stern warning addressed to the enemy, for the Islamic Resistance can no longer take the Zionist challenge and violations," the secretary general said.

We reserve our right to retaliate as we say that our patience is running thin. If we launch a retaliation in the next days in the face of any violation or aggression against our people, we hope that everybody will understand that this is our right to defend our people, nothing more, his Eminence concluded.

Arabsaleem school will stay (2)
The Arabsaleem school is 1000 meters away from the Israeli occupation's outpost in Swaida.

The school's 2-story building can take 400 students of the elementary and intermediate stages.

The school's principal said the Lebanese army which is positioned in the school's vicinity had cooperated with the school administration to build protective walls in the playground and classrooms opposite to the Israeli outpost.

Afif Noureddeen said, "We have no choice other than staying and continuing to teach, which is our only objective."

Our children are from poor classes. We are not used to escaping or displacement. What we seek today is to have a bomb shelter within the school campus, Noureddeen said.

Geography teacher Mohammed Abdel-Latif Farhat who recited how the massacre took place ruled out closing the school.

The only option we have is to fortify the building and the classrooms, he said, adding, "True this may withhold sunlight, but that will be better than surrendering and fear."

French language teacher Laila al-Shami asked about the responsibility of the international groups that claim to protect children.

We will teach children even if under a tree. "Israel" will be defeated and the Lebanese will win eventually, she said.

Education Minister: aggressions won't defeat Resistance spirit (3)
Representing President Gen Emile Lahoud and Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss, Education Minister Mohammed Youssef Baydoun visited the children who were wounded in the Israeli aggression against the Arabsaleem school.

The minister said the wounded children enjoyed high morale.

One of them told me, "When I grow up I'll fight Israel", Baydoun said.

"With such a spirit, we can retaliate for the Israeli aggressions which if I described as low, I would be lenient."

"No matter what Israel does," the minister continued, "and no matter how far it goes with its aggressions, it will not be able to take out the resistance spirit from the souls of the Lebanese in general and the Southerners in particular."

"The Lebanese will keep on sacrificing their bloods until the soil is liberated."

He said this crime will not be the last, "but we are ready to take more and always be steadfast and resisting until we liberate our land and defeat the aggression."

Minister Baydoun concluded, "I see no difference between "Israel" and the collaborators' militia, for Israel uses this militia as a cover. Those who fired and hit (the school) are Zionist Israelis".

ELDERLY LEBANESE CIVILIAN DIES OF WOUNDS AFTER SHELLING (4)
A Lebanese woman has died of injuries she received during Wednesday's clashes in southern Lebanon. Seventy-year-old Turkieh Baydoun, was in her village of Beit Yahoon, inside the central sector of the zone Israel occupies, when she was hit by shells from ongoing clashes between occupation forces and Lebanese resistance fighters. So far this year 22 civilians have been killed and 187 wounded in southern Lebanon.

SOUTH LEBANESE SCHOOL A LONG WAY FROM WASHINGTON (5)
Ten-year-old Hanan Mussa learnt something at school Thursday that she will never forget for the rest of her life -- the real meaning of war in southern Lebanon. She is one of the 20 pupils of the school in the southern Lebanese village of Arab Salim, injured by shrapnel from Israeli shells that smashed into the two-story building. Peace talks may be underway between Syria and Israel, but Hanan said she doesn't know anything about them, whimpering as she held her hand to her injured eye. The school in Arab Salim, a village of 8,000 inhabitants, is a mere 600

meters (yards) away from the position of the Israeli-allied militia at Sueida on the edge of the Israeli-occupied zone of southern Lebanon. The headmaster, Afif Nureddin, recalled how his school, with its 500 pupils ranging from kindergarten to school leaving age, came under Israeli fire four months ago.

That time no-one was hurt. "Today, they wanted to shell the peace process," he said. Shrapnel from the two cluster shells which hit the school playground in the middle of the morning shattered the windows and walls of eight class rooms, including four belonging to the kindergarten, where the pupils, aged between four and 13, were working. The injured were taken to the nearby town of Nabatiyeh, where hospital sources said four were seriously hurt. Mohammad Farhat, one of the teachers, said panic broke out when a first shell exploded some distance away. "Just afterwards, a second shell hit the perimeter wall, and the children started rushing round in all directions, screaming," he said. "A few moments later, a tremendous explosion made the walls shake and filled the classrooms with black smoke. It was total chaos, and all we could hear was the children crying and howling." "There was a van standing in the playground, so I shoved five of the injured children into it and drove them to
Nabatiyeh," he said. There were splashes of blood on the floor and on the desks, and on the books and papers that lay scattered all over the place. "Shells were still raining down round the village, and it felt as if the school was going to collapse on top of us, so we just dragged out the injured," said teacher Mohammad Nureddin, explaining the long trails of blood down the corridors and stairs. "There is a faction in Israel which wants to assassinate (Israeli Prime Minister Ehud) Barak and maybe they are behind the shelling. They don't want peace, and they shell civilians to provoke a response against northern Israel in order to muddy the waters and get the talks suspended," he said. Habib Musa was standing at the door of his classroom when the blast from the first explosion blew him into the corridor. "Six pupils in that room were hurt, and three of them are in a serious condition," he said. At the An-Najda hospital in Nabatiyeh, Maryam Sharara held the hand of her four-year-old nephew Ali, who was given painkillers after shrapnel was removed from both his legs. "Why do they shell innocent schoolchildren?" she asked. Nur Hattab was in tears, as she hugged her son, Mohammad Nadar, 11, who had had an operation to remove shrapnel from his chest. A few beds further on, Marwa Hassan, 9, white bandages swathing her whole head, said the last thing she remembered was packing her things into her schoolbag after the first shell exploded. "I woke up in hospital," she said. The Israeli army apologized for the shelling, saying that it had been a "mistake.

Military Operation  (6)
The group of the martyrs Abdel Rahman Humani and Hachem Fakherddine in the Islamic Resistance attacked at 4:20 the Sujud post with machine guns and RPGs inflicting direct hits.

Also, the IR mujahedeen spotted, at 18:50, enemy military vehicles entering Sojod outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Ghassani and Said Farran targeted them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and definite goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Qassem Ali Harb and Hussein Mohammad El-Hajj attacked, at 20:10, enemy movements and around Beaufort Castle with the suitable arms scoring direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Jawad and Talal Al-Saghir attacked, at 23:30, enemy motorized movements near the Beaufort Castle with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals.

Fifteen minutes later, the IR team of martyrs Khalil Mohammad Madi and Mohammad Naameh Youssef detonated a powerful roadside bomb as an "Israeli" engineering patrol was advancing towards Mount Bassil. One military vehicle destroyed completely.
