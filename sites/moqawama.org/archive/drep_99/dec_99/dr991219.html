Daily Report
 
 
December Sunday 19-1999:

Introduction
Head of Hizbullah's Politburo MP Mohammad Raad vowed that the Resistance will always be embraced within the framework of a strategic confrontation of the perils of the Zionist project that will move from the domain of war to the domain of political and economic hegemony and the endless influence inflict.

Meanwhile, the five-nation monitoring group condemned the "Israeli" occupation forces' mortar attack that wounded twenty children in the school of Arab Salim town.

In this respect, hundreds of Lebanese children paraded outside the UN HQ in Beirut on Saturday to denounce a mortar bomb attack staged by the "Israeli" gunners.

Resistance is our strategy to confront the Zionist project, Hizbullah(1)
The Resistance will always be embraced within the framework of a strategic confrontation of the perils of the Zionist project that will move from the domain of war to the domain of political and economic hegemony and the endless influence conflict, Head of Hizbullah's political Council said.

Speaking at an Iftar held by the Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance in Nabatieh, MP Mohammed Raad "reassured" those who ask every day about the fate of the Resistance after the "Israeli" withdrawal from Lebanon stressing that "the Resistance which has stood by Syria's side to make a dignified political stand will not be abandoned by the Lebanese or the Syrians."

The negotiations, which have started under different headlines in tactic and form from the negotiations on other tracks, were an ("Israeli") conspicuous acknowledgment of defeat in Lebanon, Raad said.

What happened in Washington does not make peace in our region, but a settlement that is governed by the current balance of power. It will not be an eternal settlement if these negotiations were to bear fruit, he added.

The Hizbullah MP noted that the settlement "will not make us forget that the Zionists are our enemies who have usurped al-Quds (Jerusalem) and Palestine. Nor will it drive us to recognize the Zionist entity.
"In the afternoon, Raad issued the following statement:

"Agence France Presse quoted my words on the confrontation with the Zionist project after the settlement and tried to interpret these words, direct them and put a hot title for the story in a way that is not in line with what I said whatsoever.

"Therefore, I need to clarify that neither did I speak about Hizbullah's readiness to lay down its arms nor has the party changed its known position that any statements about the post-withdrawal and the post-settlement era is premature, as the party will announce its position in the proper time.

"Thus, we call on all the media, especially AFP, to be accurate in what they quote us as saying especially in order not to confuse the public opinion."

AUMG condemns "Israeli" aggression on Arab Salim school(2)
"The five-way group monitoring fighting in south Lebanon on Friday condemned the "Israeli" occupation forces' mortar attack that wounded 20 children in a school.

"The children, all less than 10 years old, were wounded on Thursday when "Israeli" mortars hit their school in the village of Arab Salim just north of the occupation zone.

"In view of the tragic consequences of this regrettable shelling, the Monitoring Group condemned the action as a violation of the (April) Understanding,'' the group said in a statement.

"The group was set up to monitor a U.S. sponsored understanding in 1996 to end a bloody "Israeli" blitz on Lebanon which killed more than 200 civilians, mostly women and children.

"The understanding banned attacks from and at civilian targets on both sides. It did not ban attacks against "Israeli" soldiers in the 15 km (nine miles) deep occupation zone.

"The Monitoring Group urged the "Israeli" occupation forces to "take more effective precautions on an urgent basis to ensure that under no circumstances will civilians be the target of attack, even if unintentional."

"The group which includes representatives from Lebanon, Syria, France, the United States and "Israel" convened upon a Lebanese urgent call to examine the "Israeli" massacre in the school.

In a bid to screen their hideous crime, the "Israelis" this morning filed a complaint claiming that the Resistance had fired mortars on the occupation forces from the town of Jarjoua.

"However, the heads of the Lebanese and Syrian delegations, Col. Maher Tfaili and Gen. Adnan Balloul refused the inclusion of the complaint in the urgent meeting".

Children protest "Israeli" bloody school attack(3)
"Hundreds of Lebanese children paraded outside the U.N. headquarters in Beirut on Saturday to denounce a mortar bomb attack on a school by the "Israeli" occupation forces which wounded 20 schoolchildren.

"The children, aged between five and 10, carried gruesome pictures of the wounded and signs decrying the indifference of the international community.

"Stop the "Israeli" terror!'' one placard read. "The massacre at Arab Salim is the other side of peace with Israel.''

"Twenty children were wounded on Thursday in Arab Salim village in south Lebanon when a mortar bomb fired by the "Israeli" occupation forces hit their school.

"The school lies just outside the zone occupied by "Israel" in southern Lebanon and the attack followed the first peace talks in almost four years between Syria and "Israel".

"The rally, organised by Hizbullah and Amal Movement, was held outside the headquarters of the U.N.'s Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia in central Beirut".

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The "Israeli" occupying troops launched the following aggressions:
7:00, two 81mm rounds hit west of Baraasheit town.
7:15, the surroundings of Mohaisbat position came under several 60 and 81mm rounds and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
20:30, the perimeters of Majdal Selem town exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Military Operations(5)
The IR team of martyrs Ahmad Mahmoud Mansour and Ali Mohammad Harb attacked violently, at 6:35, Mashaaroun outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and certain hits. The smoke columns were seen billowing out of its western fortification as well.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Mustafa Wehbi and Mahmoud Khodor Baghdadi targeted, at 8:20, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.

Moreover, the IR men spotted, at 23:00, enemy movements at Sojod outpost. Immediately afterwards, the IR group rushed upon them with the adequate arms scoring direct goals.

For its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 21:25, Qabaa compound with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, another group (LRB) fell upon Mashaaroun post with the appropriate arms chalking up direct goals.
