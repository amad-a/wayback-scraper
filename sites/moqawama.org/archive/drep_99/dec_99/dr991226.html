Daily Report
 
 
December Sunday 26-1999:
 
Hizbullah wants collaborators' severely punished: all our land will return(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah urged the Lebanese government to inflict the utmost punishment on those collaborating with the "Israeli" occupation following the enemy forces' withdrawal.

"The collaborators affiliated with Islamic sects, especially Shiaas, must get the severest punishment, Sayyed Nasrallah said at the Iftar held by the Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance (CSIR).
"The Secretary-General told the Lebanese government not to submit to pressures stressing the importance of the Resistance's arms in war and peace and in all calculations and domains.

"Lahd's militiamen must hear tough statements now if you don't want them to serve the occupation, he said addressing Lebanese officials.

"Those giving the militiamen hope are not the Zionists alone; in Lebanon, one way or another, intentionally or unintentionally, there has been political, press and judicial practices that gave Lahdis hope, he added.

"We were keen to make the Jezzine experience succeed "without even one person slapped in the face" as the president (Gen Emile) Lahoud said. We were all keen to abort any error that may happen as we realized the consequences of any such mistakes on the whole Lebanese situation."

"The judicial verdicts against the collaborators were lenient, he said, noting that the militiamen hail from all Lebanese sects: Moslem, Christian, Shia, Sunni, Druze, Maronite, Catholic, and Orthodox.

"Here, we are not talking about an issue related to one sect; it is an issue that affects a whole country, its dignity and future.

"All the Lahdis in the outposts along the front lines are criminals, killers, accomplices in every drop of blood shed in the south and the western Beqaa. Leniency towards these will encourage them to stay at the service of the enemy.

"Collaborators have no religion or sect; theirs is treason on which they must be punished.

"In the past, dismantling Lahd's militia required an amnesty law. It seems that dismantling this militia now needs a law to execute everyone who stays in this militia."

Regarding the negotiations Sayyed Nasrallah said, "In order to recover our land, we don't have to offer any prices or prizes to the "Israelis". We urge the government to abide by the parliamentary joint committees' recommendation, in particular demanding and recovering every Lebanese territory that was annexed by the "Israeli" enemy."

"Every Lebanese territory must return to Lebanon, every inch and every grain of sand, including the seven villages, the Shibaa Farmsteads and hundreds of kilometers on the Lebanese borders," the secretary-general concluded.

Military Operations(2)
The IR team of martyr Hussein Mazloum staged, at 13:30, a swift attack against Kfar Houna position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying three of its main fortifications and inflicting a number of casualties. Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyr Ali Midlej was falling upon Shayar Azour with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate hits.

At the same time, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit in the IR bombarded emplacements at Abou Qamha and Rihan barracks with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Kamel Abbas and Zaher Fakhri Hemade targeted, at 21:15, an "Israeli" motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct hits.

In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Ibrahim Ali Issa and Salem Mohammad Abboud was falling upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct hits.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ayad Hussein Ghandour and Ali Mahmoud Assi targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring definite goals.

"Israeli" radio last night acknowledged that an "Israeli" soldier was wounded in IR's attack on the Blat outpost.

The radio also admitted that a Lahdi militiaman was wounded in the Sojod outpost. The militiaman was taken to a hospital in occupied Palestine due to his serious injury.

PM: Lebanon rejects any "Israeli" attempts to adjust borders(3)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss described the "Israeli" step of demarcation the international borders with Lebanon and fencing deserted Lebanese villages as "a serious measure".

The premier commented on what the "Israeli" daily Ma'ariv has published in this regard by saying that "the information is being followed up by the Lebanese diplomacy to figure out the real "Israeli" official stand" reiterating Lebanon's clinging to the internationally-recognized borders.

Any "Israeli" attempt to have border adjustments is categorically rejected, al-Hoss said, adding, "Lebanon will not accept anything less than the content of UN Security Council's resolution 425."

The prime minister announced that Lebanon will not name the officials to take part in negotiations with the "Israeli" entity before the date for the talks is set after the second round of the Syrian-"Israeli" negotiations.

"Lebanon will not take the first step as it does not want to create any illusions or interpretations and to abort any maneuvers by "Israel", al-Hoss added.

"Israeli" Aggressions(4)
The "Israeli" occupying troops staged the following aggressions:
5:30, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by five 155mm missiles.
6:30, four 155mm missiles hit Mount Sojod.
7:05, two 155mm missiles fell on the perimeters of Loweizeh village and two others on the peripheries of Mlikh village.
8:00, the outskirts of Mlikh came under four 155mm missiles.
8:05, three 155mm missiles landed Mount Abou Rkab.
9:45, Jib Soweid area, east of Majdal Zoun town, was targeted by three 155mm missiles whereas the surroundings of Blat post exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
14:15, the "Israeli" fighter-bombers dropped two air-to-ground rockets into Hala Hill.
15:30, three 155mm missiles were fired at the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village.
15:55, Jib Soweid area was hit by three 155mm missiles.
21:30, six 155mm missiles struck the perimeters of Jibaa.
