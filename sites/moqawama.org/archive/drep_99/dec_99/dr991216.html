Daily Report
 
 
December Thursday 16-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen launched a series of military operations against the positions of the "Israeli" occupying troops and their collaborators in South Lebanon. Twenty two outposts came under attack, with IR fighters breaching the fortifications of the hill-top Sojod post, allowing a flag to be planted and part of the position blown up.

However, the "Israeli" occupation forces intensified their aggressions against the Lebanese liberated areas violating the April Understanding which bars targeting civilians. The news reported that two civilians were wounded and several houses were damaged.

IR stages 22 attacks on occupation and militia outposts(1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) Wednesday launched a string of attacks on the positions of the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators in south Lebanon.

"In the holy month of Ramadan, as part of its continuous method to proceed with resistance until all the soil is liberated from the impurity of the "Israeli" occupation and its collaborators, in spite of intensified precautions and measures adopted by the enemy command to protect the occupation's outposts especially the Sojod outpost in which the enemy's general staff has directly supervised the organization of the defensive plan set after IR men several times trod the outpost with their feet, moved inside the outpost, and shattered all the precautions and the military tactics that have been used to curb IR's penetrations, IR squads belonging to various units at 8:40 a.m. Thursday staged a massive attack that covered 20 positions held by the "Israeli" occupation army and its proxy militia.

Squad of Martyrs Ali Zahri and Jihad Hijazi of IR's Special Forces stormed the Sojod outpost after IR men opened a hole in its fortifications under a heavy artillery cover provided by IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit.

IR men raised the Resistance's flags on the outpost's rampart and on Prophet Sojod's Shrine following a clash with the enemy troops manning the outpost.

Afterwards, Squad of Martyrs Ali al-Khateeb and Ahmed Sbaiti of IR's Engineering Brigade penetrated the enemy's lines and planted inside the outpost's rampart large explosive charges that were detonated by the Resistance men destroying the ramparts completely.

In the meantime, other IR squads targeted the enemy command centers; squad of Martyr Adnan Hassan pounded the enemy's command HQ in Aishieh, squad of Martyr Ghassan Zaatar targeted the "Israeli" enemy's HQ in Rihan, squad of Martyr Ashraf Kheireddeen targeted the "Israeli" outpost in Dabsheh, squad of Martyr Mohammed Fayyad al-Qalaa outpost, squad of Martyr Moussa Qarout the Blat outpost, and squad of Martyr Hassan Salami the Radar outpost.

IR men used different types of weapons in the attacks scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, other IR squads staged concentrated attacks on the collaborators' command centers; squad of Martyr Haitham Ghamloush attacked al-Hardoun outpost, squad of Martyr Mostafa Shqier the Tair Harfa outpost, squad of Martyr Hussein Bourji the Baraasheit outpost, squad of Martyr Salaah Fares the Beit Yahoun outpost, squad of Martyr Ali Mazloum the Rshaf outpost, squad of Martyr Ali Medlej the Hemayed outpost, squad of Martyr Hussein Nasser the Salaah outpost, and squad of Martyr Qassem Yassin the Mohaisbat outpost.

Furthermore, IR squads pounded the enemy's artillery emplacements in Shraifeh, Aishieh, and Zaffateh, besides targeting "Israeli" motorized forces in Kassaret al-Oroush and the Rihan Hilltops.

IR men used different types of weapons in the attacks scoring definite hits.

The enemy's shock and helplessness in the face of IR men forced the occupation to involve its warplanes in the confrontations.

"Israeli" aircraft staged several raids that targeted Sojod, the Nabeh al-Tasseh gorge, and Mlita.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Ibrahim El-Saalouk and Rassil Ahmad Rahal attacked, at 20:25, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals.

Resistance committed to people's safety(2)
"Hizbullah's official in the South area his Eminence Sheikh Nabeel Qawooq commented on the "Israeli" enemy's violation of the April Understanding stating that "the enemy's escaping from the battlefield of confronting the Resistance men and targeting civilians and their properties to make up for its weakness or to pressure the Resistance to curb the Resistance's confrontation of the enemy only show the enemy's defeat."

"Besides, the enemy's violation of the April Understanding on the day the understanding's monitoring group is in session underlines the enemy's going too far in crime. This also jeopardizes the usefulness of this understanding and its essence."

The enemy's attempt to exploit the current developments in the region to pass its crimes will not benefit the enemy for the Resistance is committed to the utmost to its people's security regardless of all developments, Qawooq said.

"No one can provide security and protection to their troops through these aggressions, for the Resistance men insist on continuing their operations and achieving liberation through their arms", the Hizbullah official concluded.

FIGHTING ESCALATES IN SOUTHERN LEBANON; CIVILIANS INJURED AND HOMES DAMAGED(3)
"Hours before peace talks resumed between Syria and "Israel" in Washington, "Israeli" occupation forces stepped up their attacks on southern Lebanon. "Israeli" warplanes raided villages just outside the occupied zone, as heavy clashes erupted by "Israeli" troops and Hizbullah resistance fighters. Two civilians were wounded. A 70-year-old woman, Turkieh Beydoun, was injured near Beit Yahoun inside the region "Israel" occupies. Another Lebanese civilian, Ahmad Harkous, was wounded by shrapnel from an "Israeli" shell as he drove a long a road just north of the occupied zone, in Zawtar as-Sharqieh. Last night, an Ethiopian maid was wounded in the southern town of Nabatieh, after "Israeli" gunners targeted residential neighborhoods in Nabatieh. She was taken to a government hospital in the town. The casualties raised to 150 the number of civilians wounded in southern Lebanon this year. Twenty-three have been killed during the same period. In a clear breach to the 1996 cease-fire understanding, occupation forces pounded the village of Maifadoun, which is located outside the central sector of the occupied zone. Iqleem al-Toffah also came under heavy "Israel" shelling. The attacks coincided with a meeting of the international panel which monitors the truce agreement. Delegates from the US, France, "Israel", Syria and Lebanon, have been discussing eleven alleged truce violations: four filed by Beirut, and the rest by "Israel"".

19 Lebanese join hostage-prisoners in occupation's notorious prison(5)
"The Follow-up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" prisons (FCLD) announced that an intelligence force belonging to the "Israeli" occupation forces stormed the besieged town of Aita al-Shaab, ransacked several houses, abducted Youssef Ali Srour, Adnan Ali Srour, and Ahmed Ali Srour and took them to al-Khiam detention center.

"Following the sit-in staged by the town's people living in Beirut in front of the ESCWA HQ in Beirut, the collaborators' chief in the town, Ahmed Shibli, gathered those who are still residing in the town and told them to sign a communiqué denouncing the action taken by FCLD and the non-resident people, an FCLD communiqué read.

"The group said Shibli has threatened to tighten the siege and conduct more detentions if the residents do not sign the communiqué; however the residents refused to sign any statement unless the siege is lifted and the detainees are released.

Agent Shibli's response was the detention of the three Srour brothers.

"Elsewhere, reports from the occupied town of Rmeish quoted security sources as saying that the occupation forces had detained nine Lebanese civilians and took them to the Center 17 in Saff al-Hawa (Bint Jbeil) for questioning.

"Reports said the citizens, of different ages, were detained following their return from Beirut".
