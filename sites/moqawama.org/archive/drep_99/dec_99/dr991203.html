Daily Report
 
 
December Friday 3-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen launched a series of military attacks against several positions of the Israeli occupation forces and their agents. Israels radio admitted that three Israeli troops were wounded and taken to hospital for treatment.

 Meanwhile, the Israeli enemy gunners shelled the outskirts of Majdal Zoun, Mansouri and Baraasheit towns. The security sources in South were quoted as saying four Lebanese citizen were wounded ad the result of the Israeli shelling.

Three Israeli troops wounded in IR operations
Three Israeli soldiers were wounded on Thursday when Hizbullah fighters attacked the clifftop radar station at Bayada with mortars and rockets.

The casualties were the first suffered by Israel since the beginning of October.

An officer and a soldier were moderately wounded and another soldier was lightly hurt in the attack, according to an Israeli Army statement.

So far this year, 12 Israeli soldiers have been killed and 57 wounded in south Lebanon.

Three months ago, the Israeli Army adopted a strategy of reducing troop movements and instead relying more on surveillance technology to monitor the perimeter of the zone while responding to resistance attacks with air strikes.

The new tactic has had mixed results. While it has failed to reduce resistance attacks ­ October recorded the highest number of assaults against the occupation forces since 1982 ­ Israeli casualties have dropped. Given that a withdrawal from the south seems now to be only a matter of months away, keeping casualties to the minimum while not compromising the defense of Galilee is the primary concern of the Israeli military. Launching offensive operations against Hizbullah fighters and other resistance groups is no longer a priority.

Although Hizbullah is having no problem staging long-range mortar and rocket attacks, it is experiencing difficulties in finding occupation forces to kill now that almost all Israeli soldiers spend their tours of duty hidden beneath reinforced concrete that protect their outposts.

Even the SLA* militiamen, traditionally a softer target than their better equipped, trained and protected Israeli allies, have received morale-boosting defensive measures. The Israelis have improved the fortifications of the militias outposts, issued new equipment and, most importantly, given them a pay raise.

The SLA is painfully aware that the end is approaching. But given the lack of alternative employment in the impoverished zone, the lure of monthly salaries coupled with the advantage of family members augmenting their incomes by working in Israel will probably ensure that most continue serving until the Israelis leave.

With the Israeli Army in a defensive and fairly safe holding pattern in the south and the Jewish states air force gaining plenty of combat practice in the skies above, there is little public pressure on Barak to withdraw unilaterally anytime soon.

This does not alter the fact that in the event of peace talks with Syria failing to resume by July next year, Barak will have little choice but to honor his promise to his electorate and disengage the Israeli Army from south Lebanon, even without an agreement.

But if Hizbullah is able to break the status quo and suddenly inflict serious casualties among Israeli troops, the whole debate in Israel over a unilateral withdrawal will take on a new urgency. Barak may find that public pressure will not allow him the luxury of keeping the Israeli Army inside Lebanon until next July(1)

For its part, The IR team of martyrs Hussein Abdel-Latif Jawad and Wissam Abdel-Hussein Zayour attacked, at 6:15, the Israeli post of Dabsheh with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct and accurate hits. The smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Daoud Fahs and Talal Mohammad Hassan El-Saghir fell upon Beaufort Castle with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen told.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Jaafar Habib Harb and Hassan Mohammad El-Hajj Ali rushed upon Soweida outpost. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to hit its western and northern fortifications.

Quarter an hour later, the IR unit of martyrs Hassan Hallal and Ali Chehab staged a swift attack at the Israeli post of Radar causing damage to its equipments and western fortifications.

Meanwhile, the IR team of martyrs Youssef Baddah and Hussein Hammoud was targeting Hardoun outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct goals.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ghassan Zaatar and Hussein Ahmad Trabolssi rushed upon Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking its northern fortification.

For its part, Amal Movement shelled, at 5:45, Baraasheit compound with several artillery rounds(2)

Houses damaged in Israeli aggressions(3)
The Israeli occupation forces proceeded with staging aggressions against Lebanese areas.  The enemy this morning bombarded the outskirts of Habboush, Arab Salim, and the Zahrani River gorge with a number of 155mm rounds.
On the other hand, the occupation's warplanes flew over southern areas and the capital Beirut.

In the afternoon, the occupation forces violated the April Understanding in a series of aggressions that targeted the towns of Majdal Zoun and al-Mansouri.

The occupation's artillery positioned in the outposts of al-Hardoun and Shamaa bombarded the residential areas in the two towns damaging the houses of Hussein Matar and Ali Mohammed Salim Harb in al-Mansouri, while the houses of Mahmoud al-Hajj Abdo and Khalil Merhi were damaged in the town of Majdal Zoun besides a shop owned by Ali Jbeili.

Moreover, an Israeli round fell near Majdal Zoun's mosque.  Crops were also ruined in both towns.

At 15:50, enemy warplanes raided the area between Majdelzoen and al-Mansouri firing 4 air-to-ground missiles.

15:20, the residential areas of Mansouri village came under a number of 120mm shells causing damage to the houses of Hussein Matar, Ali Salim Harb and Mohammad Salim Harb in addition to Bashir Mdaihalis orchard.
15:46, the Israeli warplanes dropped four air-to-ground rockets into the area between Majdal Zoun and Mansouri.

Israels ALLIED MILITIA RELEASES FOUR LEBANESE DETAINEES(5)
Israels allied militia in the south released four Lebanese detainees from the notorious Khiam detention center, in occupied southern Lebanon. The four identified as Fadi Olayan, Abdel Amir Nura, Jean Halim Haws and Talih Abi Rashed, were allowed to return to their homes inside the area Israel occupies. About 140 Lebanese are being held, including women and children, in Khiam without trial or charge. Another forty-two are held in prisons inside Israel. Human rights groups, including Human Rights Watch, repeatedly condemn cases of torture inside Khiam.
