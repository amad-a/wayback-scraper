Daily Report
 
 
December Friday 10-1999:

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen launched a series of fierce military operations against the positions of the Israeli occupying troops and their allied militiamen. The security sources said that four Israeli soldiers were wounded during the attacks.

However, the Israeli occupation forces intensified their aggressions against the liberated areas of South Lebanon and Western Beqaa. The Israeli war jets raided into the outskirts of several villages in Western Sector of the Israeli-occupied zone. No casualties were reported but the farmers said that large tracts of their fields went up in flames.

Israeli air and ground aggressions(1)
As the US announced that negotiations between Syria and "Israel" will be resumed, the Israeli occupation forces intensified their aggressions against Lebanese territories.

Enemy aircraft Thursday staged 9 raids while its artillery pounded areas in all sectors adjacent to the occupied zone.

Israeli warplanes staged five raids between 8:15 and 9:10 a.m. targeting the outskirts of Qabrikha, al-Jmaijmeh, Mlikh and Oqmata.

The occupation's air force at 17:40 staged two sorties on Mlikh firing four air-to-ground missiles.
At 18:10, enemy gunship helicopters staged two raids on the outskirts of Loweizeh.

The enemy between 6:15 and 8:00 a.m. bombarded the outskirts of al-Jmaijmeh, Touleen, Qabrikha, and Wadi al-Qaissieh.

Meanwhile, enemy pilotless surveillance drones circled over the targeted areas.

In Nabatieh, the outskirts of Hamra Farmstead, Upper Nabatieh, Choukine, and the deserted army barracks in Nabatieh were sporadically shelled by 120mm and 155mm rounds.

For its part, the (IR) issued a communiqué in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions (2):
7:40, eleven 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Jmaijmeh village.
Between 7:55 and 13:05, the outskirts of Qabrikha village came under more seven 155mm missiles many of them were smoke ones.
8:10, the Israeli fighter-bombers dropped three air-to-ground rockets into the peripheries of Jmaijmeh.
8:40, the Israeli warplanes staged four sorties into the outskirts of Qabrikha.
Between 8:55 and 9:08, the Israeli war jets raided four times into the perimeters of Mlikh.
9:25, several 155mm missiles struck the peripheries of Jibaa town.
13:05, Slouqi Valley was shelled by a number of 155mm smoke missiles.
16:00, five 120mm shells were fired at the west of Baraasheit town.
16:15, Baraasheit town exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
17:20, Qaissieh Valley was targeted by a number of 155mm missiles.
17:25, the Israeli warplanes raided twice into the perimeters of Mlikh village.
17:35, three 155mm missiles were unleashed at Mount Sojod.
17:45, the perimeters of Mlikh came under five 155mm missiles.

18 IR operations against occupation forces
The Islamic Resistance (IR) engaged in fierce clashes with the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators using different types of weapons and inflicting human and material losses on the enemy.
At 6:10 a.m., IR's squad of Martyr Ali Tohme attacked a Lahdi foot patrol in the Rob Thlathine area. IR men used machine guns and hand grenades in the attack inflicting several casualties in the force's ranks.
IR's Martyr Toni Abi Ghanem's squad attacked a motorized enemy force in the vicinity of the some area with the appropriate weapons barring it from backing the targeted patrol.

One hour later, IR's squad of Martyr Ali Taha attacked an Israeli motorized force in the vicinity of the Mohaisbat outpost, scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, Martyr Mohammed Abdel-Monhem's squad pounded the Taibeh outpost scoring accurate hits.

At the same time, squad of Martyr Hassan Yassine blitzed an Israeli force in the vicinity of Rob Thlathine with rocket propelled grenades. IR men attacked the same force 30 minutes later.

Meanwhile, squad of Martyr Hussein Nasser attacked enemy movements near the Taibeh outpost.

At 8:10 a.m., squad of Martyr Wafiq Zahwi attacked another Israeli force near the Rob Thlathine outpost, while squad of Martyr Hassan Khalil Mansour targeted an Israeli motorized force at the Taibeh-Rob Thlathine road.

At 8:30 a.m., IR men clashed with an Israeli-Lahdi joint force in the Rob Thlatine area. Due to the ferocity of the clashes, the enemy had to involve its helicopters to back its forces.

Furthermore, squad of Martyr Hassan Mohammad Saoli at 9:45 targeted Israeli movements near Rob Thlathine with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

Moreover, IR at 10:35 a.m. Thursday spotted a joint Israeli-Lahdi force that was moving under protection from military vehicles in the Rob Thlathine area. Squad of Martyr Ali Hussein Mistrah in IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) targeted the enemy force with rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

At 11:00, the same squad targeted the enemy force with the appropriate weapons scoring definite hits.
IR's squad of Martyrs Wafiq Bahje and Marwan Kisrawani at 11:25 ambushed an Israeli infantry force at the Beaufort Castle outpost's gate.

When the enemy force reached the ambush site, IR men detonated a powerful bomb against the enemy troops. Definite casualties were scored.

Meanwhile, ARU's squad of Martyrs Sha'lan Youssef al-Moqdad and Haidar Nayef Asaad pounded and paralyzed the enemy's outpost in Qalaa, while squad of Martyrs Ali Mohammad Ashmar and Ibrahim al-Bazzal attacked the Israeli occupation's outpost in Dabsheh with the appropriate weapons scoring definite hits.

Moreover, squad of Martyrs Nazih Nassreddeen and Ahmed Youssef Hammoud at 12:10 targeted a motorized Lahdi force that was entering the enemy's outpost in Bait Yahoun.

IR men used rocket propelled grenades in the attack scoring definite hits and setting fires in the outpost.
In the meantime, squad of Martyrs Abdel-Mawla Ismail and Suheil Mohammad Yassin pounded the occupation's outpost in Baraasheit with rocket propelled grenades barring it from backing the targeted force. Definite hits were scored in the attack.

Security sources in the occupied zone said four Israeli troops were wounded in the clashes with IR men(3)
Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Adnan Mahmoud Zeineddine and Wissam Abdel-Hussein Zayour attacked, at 14:35, an Israeli motorized force inside Taibeh position with the rocket-propelled grenades.
At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Abdel-Karim Bakri and Ahmad Youssef Abdallah rushed upon an Israeli force near Rob-Thlathine village(4)
 

Agent Lahd resigned?(5)
The Israeli occupation's command in Lebanon denied that it had accepted agent Antoine Lahd's resignation from the command of the occupation's militia.

As the occupation's denial could not screen what is happening in the Lebanese areas occupied by the Israeli forces, comers from the zone said agent Aql Hashem, the most prominent figure to succeed Lahd, has become the actual chief of the militia, and his militiamen act as it if they had looted the militia's command,

"Lahd had rendered his resignation more than once to force the occupation's command not to pull out from South Lebanon without arranging the militiamen's status; however, Israeli prime minister Ehud Barak's setting a deadline for withdrawal without minding the fate of the militiamen made Lahd confirm his resignation and retreat from public life to self-imposed exile in Paris," reports from the occupied zone said, adding that Lahd aims to be in Paris in July 2000, and, consequently, not responsible for what will happen until then.

The reports affirmed that Lahd and some of the militia's senior officials have left the occupied zone and are now in occupied Palestine where they will be able to cash a $5 million cheque for their collaboration with the occupation before heading each to his exile.

Protesters demand freedom for detainees(6)
An organization supporting Lebanese detainees held in Israeli prisons organized a sit-in Thursday, one day ahead of the International Day for Human Rights.

Held at the International Red Cross Center in Nabatieh, protesters presented a memorandum to Naji Asaad, the International Red Cross director in Nabatieh.

After more than 50 years since the UN Declaration of Human Rights, Israeli forces are still exerting pressures on Lebanese civilians and detaining them in Khiam and other Israeli prisons, the statement issued by The National Committee for Lebanese Detainees in Israeli Prisons said.

There are 42 prisoners reportedly at Khiam, including two women from Arnoun who were recently incarcerated.

There are more than 160 Lebanese detainees reportedly held in Israeli prisons where they are allegedly subjected to physical and psychological torture.

Israel has also imprisoned children under the age of 15, female students and old people, the statement said.

For this reason, the International Committee for the Red Cross and Human Rights organizations pressure Israel to release children, women and sick people. We also demand many rights, including the admission of an international group of doctors from the Red Cross to consult detainees, more frequent parental visits in the Israeli prisons and improved conditions of visiting facilities.

The statement also called for the release of Lebanese detainees and the creation of an International Committee for the Red Cross in Israeli prisons and Khiam.
