Daily Report
 
 
December Monday 6-1999:

Israeli Aggressions(1)
The Israeli occupation troops launched the following aggressions:
6:15, seven 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Khorbet Selem town.
6:30, the Israeli war jets staged four sorties into the outskirts of Zawtar.
11:15, the surroundings of Sojod outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
12:25, three 155mm missiles fell on Mount Amoud between Khorbet Selem and Jmeijmeh and two others on Qaissieh Valley.
19:15, five rounds struck the perimeters of Loweizeh village.
20:00, Mount Abou Rkab was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
23:00, the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
23:05, the outskirts of Mlikh came under two 120mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine rounds.
23:45, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets hit the surroundings of Dabsheh outpost and the outskirts of Yohmor village.
Between 23:45 and 23:55, the outskirts of Arnoun town came under more than three 105mm tank rounds and 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Military Operations(2)
The IR mujahedeen observed, at 9:40, a Lahdi engineering force which was fortifying Atour area. Immediately afterwards, the IR team of martyr Ali Kassem rushed upon it with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Mistrah and Nasser Missmar targeted, at 11:25, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Majed Jaffaf and Tarek El-Saghir was attacking an Israeli infantry force near Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct goals.

Moreover, the IR unit of martyrs Hassan Mohammad Saouli and Hassan Abbas Yassine attacked, at 14:00, enemy movements at Baraasheit compound with the suitable weapons. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR unit of martyr Sami Melhem targeted, at 19:30, Kfar Houna position with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR men spotted, at 22:50, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost. Then, the IR team of martyrs Ali Kassem Qaoun and Ali Kamel Jahjah fell upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Haidar Nayef Assaad and Jaafar Afif Harb was targeting an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct and certain hits.

However, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 6:00, Mashaaroun compound with the artillery shells scoring direct hits.

Ten minutes later, another group of LRB targeted Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.
