Daily Report
 
 
December Thursday 9-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullahs Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah warned the Israeli occupying troops and their proxy militia who are besieging Aita El-Shaab town that they will face the wrath of the resistance.

The resistance militants will take their revenge on the heads of the agents who are laying siege to the town, he said.

However, the Follow-Up Committee for the Support of the Lebanese Detainees in Israeli prisons called on Israel to release seven women reportedly being tortured at the notorious Khiam detention center.

Hizbullah warns collaborators against besieging Aita al-Shaab(1)
Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah warned the collaborators who are besieging the occupied town of Aita al-Shaab that they will be punished, stressing that the continuation of besieging the town, assaulting residents, and detaining young men will force the Islamic Resistance to deal with the issue harshly.

Sayyed Nasrallah received at the party's Secretariat HQ a delegation from Aita al-Shaab. They explained to his Eminence the suffering that the town has been subjected to for five months.

The Resistance men will punish The collaborators' chiefs who are sealing off the town, he said, calling on the occupation's militia to stop harming the residents; "otherwise, they will be punished and be examples to all those who plan to besiege occupied towns."

"The pain and suffering will not be in vain; they are part of the state of Jihad, steadfastness and resistance besides being part of the weapons we have to confront the occupation."

The Secretary General commended the unity and solidarity of Aita al-Shaab residents with all their different political affiliations, noting that the united stand of the residents has great impact on the morale of the occupation troops and their collaborators, "especially as the enemy is betting on our disintegration and divisions.

There are good omens of victory and liberation of the occupied territories in the south and the western Beqaa, thanks to the Resistance, besides the steadfastness of the residents and the patience of the prisoners and detainees in the occupation's prisons.

Sheikh Ali Srour and Sheikh Hussein Srour spoke on behalf of the delegation praising the Resistance, "the hope for liberation and return".

On the other hand, Sayyed Nasrallah hosted Italian Ambassador to Lebanon Gioseppi Cassini who briefed his Eminence on the grants' program proposed by the Italian government to develop the southern occupied areas.

Talks also touched on the general situation and the recent developments in the region.

LEBANON CALLS ON ISRAEL TO RELEASE WOMEN DETAINEES IN KHIYAM DETENTION CAMP(2)
The Follow-up Committee for the Support of the Lebanese Detainees in Israeli detention camps called Wednesday on Israel to release the seven women detainees still held in a center inside the zone Israel occupies in southern Lebanon. A statement by the committee said the world should exert pressure on Israel to release those women " who suffer the worst kinds of torture in Khiam". " It is a prison which is against all international laws treaties," the statement added. The committee said 161 detainees, including the seven women, were currently being held hostage there without trial or charges. The committee said 42 Lebanese are being held in jails in Israel. Mohammed Safa head of the committee said "the main and only reason people are put in the Khiam jail is because they refused to cooperate with the Israeli forces" or to join the ranks of the Israeli allied militia. He added that some 14 Lebanese have died from torture in Khiam, which is jointly run by Israeli occupation forces and their allied local militia. The center is closed to journalists and human right organizations, many of which, including Amnesty International, have repeatedly criticized conditions and the use of torture. Safa added that the prisoners are subjected to electric shock on their nails, tongues and genitals. Khiam was set up in 1985 inside the border zone which Israel occupied in the same year.

ISRAEL ACCEPTS LAHD RESIGNATION: REPORT(3)
Security sources in southern Lebanon said Israel has accepted a recent resignation by the head of Israels allied militia in occupied southern Lebanon Antoine Lahd. Sources added Lahd resigned after failing to convince Israel not to withdraw from southern Lebanon, and leave him and his followers behind. Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak has set a July 2000 deadline to pullout occupation troops from the south. Lahd and top militiamen reportedly received a five million dollars compensation from Israels defense ministry, for their cooperation with occupation forces. Lahd will reportedly join his family in Paris. Sources said Akl Hashem, the deputy militia commander is now in charge of the crumbling force. But Israel and its allied militia have yet to confirm the report.

MONITORS ACCUSE ISRAEL OF BREACHING ACCORD(4)
The five-nation group monitoring a 1996 truce accord in south Lebanon held Israel and its allied militia responsible for killing a Lebanese civilian and injuring three others. Monitors from the United States, France, Israel, Lebanon, and Syria, also said three other civilians were injured inside the zone Israel occupies following shelling on the area. The cease-fire agreement bans attacks on and from civilian areas but permits resistance attacks against Israeli occupation troops.

Israeli Aggressions(5)
The Israeli occupying troops launched the following Israeli aggressions:
14:30, Mlita Hill exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
Between 14:30 and 16:20, Mount Sojod was bombarded by ten 120mm shells.
16:37, the Israeli war jets staged two sorties into Mount Sojod.

Military Operations(6)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Adnan Mahmoud Zeineddine and Jawdat Ali Olleik attacked, at 15:50, an Israeli motorized force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the adequate arms. Direct and certain hits were scored, the militants reported.

Ten minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Samer Hussein Ghizzawi and Adnan Hassan Farhat rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades destroying its western fortification.
