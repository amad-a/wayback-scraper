Daily Report
 
 
December Sunday 12-1999:

Introduction
Hizbullahs Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem asserted that the Islamic Resistance (IR) men will continue their operations against the Israeli occupation forces despite the imminent resumption of settlement talks between Syria and the Israeli entity.

However, the people of Aita El-Shaab town staged a sit-in front of the ESCWA HQ in Beirut on the occasion of the International Day for Human Rights. The town has been under the siege, which imposed by the Israeli occupying troops and their agents, for five months.

Hizbullah will not stop resistance, withdrawal result of our attacks(1)
The Islamic Resistance men will continue their operations against the Israeli occupation forces despite the imminent resumption of settlement talks between Syria and the Israeli entity, Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary-General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem said.

"Our attacks will continue until Israel withdraws completely from the occupied zone to the international border, even if there is also a resumption of talks on the Lebanese-Israeli track, Qassem said.

"I don't think that Lebanese and Syrian officials will ask Hizbullah to halt its attacks, which are not related to the negotiations in the Middle East, he told Agence France-Presse in an interview.

Any halt of resistance attacks will strengthen the Israeli position, he said, describing Israeli prime minister Ehud Barak's promise to withdraw from Lebanon by July 2000 as "pressurizing means through which Barak wants to silence his opposers and twist Syria's arm".

Sheikh Qassem stressed that the Israeli pullout will be the result of Hizbullah's strong blows and the heroism of its fighters, noting that any partial or staged withdrawal does not bind Hizbullah to stop its operations.

Hizbullah's deputy secretary-general said the solution which is currently negotiated is unjust because it deprives the Palestinian people of 8/9 of its soil and denies 4 million Palestinians the right to return to their homeland.

Aita Shaab people protest 5 months of siege and 40 detainees(2)
Five months after the siege which the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators have been imposing on the town of Aita al-Shaab and following the detention of 40 of the town's residents in the prisons of the occupation and its proxy militia, the town's non-resident people staged a sit-in in front of the ESCWA HQ in Beirut on the occasion of the International Day for Human Rights.

The participants in the sit-in included the Follow-up Committee for the Lebanese Detainees in Israeli prison (FCLD), the National Committee for Detainees (NCD), representatives of NGOs, MPs and clergymen.

Aita al-Shaab residents who had fled the Israeli occupation's arbitrary measures brought with them their women, children, and elderly.

His Eminence Sheikh Ali Srour spoke on behalf of the protesters; speeches were also delivered by his Eminence Sheikh Hussein Srour, FCLD Secretary General Mohammed Safa, NCD Press official Jamal Mahroum, MP Marwan Fares and Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc member MP Nazih
Mansour who stressed that all the Lebanese people backs the Aita al-Shaab residents.

The protesters addressed a memorandum to UN Secretary General Kofi Anan stressing that the Lebanese hostages in al-Khiam detention center and other Israeli prisons along with the residents of the besieged villages and the members of the expelled families all appeal to the whole world on the International Day for Human Rights and voice their plight that is the result of the Israeli violations of human rights in Israeli detention camps.

The memorandum urged the world to take action and shut down the notorious Khiam prison, free all the detainees in Israeli prisons, lift the siege imposed on Lebanese villages by the Israeli occupation forces and their collaborators and return the expelled to their homes.

Israeli Aggressions(3)
In its statement, the (IR) mentioned the following Israeli aggressions:
4:40, the perimeters of Habboush town exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
14:55, the southern peripheries of Baraasheit town came under three 81mm rounds and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
15:05, the Israeli warplanes raided into Mount Sojod.
Between 15:05 and 17:40, the outskirts of Loweizeh village came under three 155mm missiles and three 81mm rounds.
15:25, three 81mm rounds hit Aita El-Jabal woodland.
17:45, Mount Sojod was shelled by three 155mm missiles.
20:00, three 155mm missiles were fired at Jib Soweid area, east of Majdal Zoun village.
21:05, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck east of Yater village.
21:35, five 155mm missiles slammed into the outskirts of Jibaa town.
Between 21:35 and 22:55, Slouqi Valley was targeted by seven 81mm rounds.
22:05, two 81mm rounds targeted the perimeters of Majdal Selem town.
23:45, seven 81mm rounds fell on the outskirts of Haddatha town and five 155mm missiles on the perimeters of Khorbet Selem town.

IR stages 9 operations against occupation forces
The (IR) Saturday morning staged a series of operations against the outposts of the occupation forces and their collaborators.

IR's squad of Martyrs Wissam Zaiour and Talal al-Sagheer at 6:30 a.m. targeted the occupation's outpost in Soweida, while squad of Martyrs Mohammed Jaber and Youssef al-Rihani attacked al-Tohorra outpost, and squad of Martyrs Moussa Fahs and Hassan Saoli of IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) pounded occupation's outpost in Dabsheh.

IR men used machine guns and rocket propelled in the three attacks scoring definite hits in the targeted outposts.

Furthermore, squad of Martyrs Fadi Batsh and Hussein Mhanna at 7:55 a.m. attacked an M-113 troop carrier at the al-Qawzah outpost's gate with rocket propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.

Moreover, squad of Martyrs Suheil Yassine and Hassan Krayyem at 14:00 attacked an Israeli infantry force near the Rihan Hilltops with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades.

When the enemy's outpost in Aramta tried to back up the targeted force, it came under fire from ARU's squad of Martyrs Ali Hmade and Ali Gharieb that used the appropriate weapons and scored hits.

In the meantime, squad of Martyrs Moussa Ahmed and Hassan Salameh attacked the occupation's outpost in Bir Kallab with machine guns scoring direct hits(4)

Also, the IR group of martyrs Kassem Nahim and Adnan Hassan Farhat attacked, at 20:00, Tair Harfa outpost with the adequate arms chalking up direct and certain goals(5)
