Daily Report



December Thursday 30-1999:

"Israeli" bombs target civilians, kill goats(1)
"A set of connected explosive devices planted by the "Israeli" enemy to the east of the town of Majdal Selem went off near a goat herd belonging to Ahmed Naef al-Dorr.

Fifty goats were killed by the explosions that spared al-Dorr who suffered a great loss due to the aggression.

"The explosions' site is overlooked by the "Israeli" occupation's outposts in Tair Harfa and al-Hardoun. The site is also 300 meters away from UNIFIL's Nepalese battalion's base.

"The artillery of the "Israeli" occupation forces this morning bombarded the southern neighborhood in the town of Arab Salim and the Zahrani River gorge.

Several 155mm rounds landed in the targeted areas. No casualties were reported.

"The "Israeli" occupation's warplanes in the morning flew over the city of Baalback (Beqaa) breaking the sound barrier.

"In the afternoon, the "Israeli" aggressions intensified as the occupation's warplanes raided the outskirts of Jibaa firing two air-to-ground missiles while the enemy's artillery bombarded the woods of Sojod and Oqmata with a number of 155 mm rounds.

"At night, the shelling extended and reached the outskirts of Yater, Zibqeen, al-Chaitieh, and Wadi al-Qaissieh". No casualties were reported.

In its communiqué, the IR mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions (2):
Between 5:05 and 5:40, thirteen 81mm rounds slammed into the surroundings of Haddatha compound.
5:40, Haddatha forest was targeted by four 120mm shells.
5:45, the surroundings of Baraasheit compound came under twelve 52mm rounds.
Between 5:45 and 6:00, the surroundings of Beit Yahoun outpost exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets and one 120mm shell.
Between 13:10 and 15:30, Qaissieh Valley was bombarded by sixteen 155mm missiles and four 105mm rounds.
13:20, an explosive device planted by the "Israeli" occupying troops east of Majdal Zoun village was detonated perishing seventy sheep owned by the citizen Ahmad Nayef El-Dorr who was narrowly escaped death.
14:25, the surroundings of Salaah and Qssair exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.
14:40, Mount Sojod was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
14:45, the perimeters of Jibaa town came under eighteen 155mm missiles.
14:55, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the peripheries of Jibaa.
Between 16:10 and 16:20, the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village came under fifty one 155mm missiles.

Recovered martyrs' remains laid to rest(3)
"Supporters of the Islamic Resistance (IR) took part in the funeral procession of returning martyrs Moussa Mahmoud Ahmed Qarout and Hassan Ali Salame who were martyred during confrontations with the "Israeli" occupation forces in Rob Thlathine on December 9, 1999.

"Hizbullah recovered the martyrs' remains yesterday through the International Committee of the Red Cross.

"Hizbullah's Political Council Chief MP Mohammed Raad, former MPs Ali Ammar and Mohammed Birjawi took part in the funeral along with Ulama and a large crowd of people who chanted slogans condemning "Israel" and stressing the option of Jihad and Resistance against the "Israeli" occupation until full liberation is achieved.

"The body of Martyr Qarout was taken to the Shahedain Cemetery where his Eminence Sheikh Yousef Qarout led the prayer of the dead on the martyr's body before he was laid to rest.

"Martyr Salame was taken to the south in a motorcade. He was laid to rest in his hometown of Qaaqayeit al-Jisr".

No room for bargain or discussion when it comes to resistance, Hizbullah(4)
"There was no bargain or possibility to discuss the Resistance's operations in exchange for recovering the martyrs' remains or the return of the five detainees, Hizbullah's Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc member Dr Hussein al-Hajj Hassan said.

"This has never happened in the past, nor will it happen in the future, he added.

"Speaking at an Iftar held by the Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance (CSIR) in the Beqaa town of Riaq, Hajj Hassan called for the continuation of resistance, firmness, and steadfastness more than ever, "because the "Israeli" occupation wants to achieve through the negotiations and the settlement process what it was not able to get through occupation and aggression."

"The Hizbullah MP noted the occupation's suspicious proposals concerning the violation of Lebanese skies following the settlement process, border demarcation, and water sharing, calling for caution and awareness of the enemy's proposals and ill intentions.

"He reiterated Hizbullah's stand of rejecting to recognize the entity of the Zionist occupation neither now nor in the future".

IR wounded three "Israeli" collaborators(5)
The IR's squad of Martyrs Jawdat Ouleiq and Samer Ghizzawi at 14:00 attacked the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Kfar Houna with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades hitting the outpost's western and southern ramparts, a 12.7 mm machine-gun and a Mag machine-gun.

Meanwhile, squad of Martyrs Ahmed Farhat and Ali Ghrayyeb attacked the occupation's outpost in Sheer Azour with rocket propelled grenades, and squad of Martyrs Monzer Mohammed Monzer and Ahmed Asal staged a violent concentrated attack on the Aramta outpost scoring definite hits.

When the Lahdi militiamen in the Rihan barracks tried to intervene and back the Aramta outpost, it came under fire from the squad of Martyrs Adnan Melhem and Othman Faqeeh that used the appropriate weapons and barred the militiamen from performing their mission.

Furthermore, IR's squad of Martyrs Faraj Ballouq and Mohammed al-Mazbouh at 14:15 attacked an "Israeli" force near the Rihan Hilltops with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades.

In the meantime, squad of Martyrs Hassan al-Sa'louk and Hussein Kazma attacked the Bir Kallab outpost scoring direct hits, while squad of Martyrs Hashem Fakhreddeen and Naef Rammal blitzed the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Sojod in a violent and concentrated manner scoring definite hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Hassan Trad and Farid Said Bazzi attacked, at 16:00, Blat post with the rocket-propelled grenades striking some of its fortifications, whereas the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

The security sources in the "Israeli"-occupied zone stressed that three of "Israeli" allied militiamen were injured on the hands of IR militants yesterday.
