Daily Report
 
 
December Thursday 23-1999:
 
Introduction
Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said that the US-"Israeli" project for Lebanon following the withdrawal is one of unrest between the Lebanese. They have already started preparing for the unrest project.

Meanwhile, Hizbullah recovered the bodies of four martyrs who had been in the battlefield since August 1999. A group of the International Committee for the Red Cross and UNIFIL brought the bodies to the city of Tyre.

In a dangerous development, the "Israeli" occupying troops and their agents are trying to bury the roadside bombs which had been planted by Resistance to hunt their soldiers and members.

Hizbullah: US-"Israeli" post-withdrawal project one of unrest(1)
At the annual Iftar held by Hizbullah's Educational Mobilization Unit, Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah blasted the American Ambassador in Lebanon David Satterfield whom he described as the "owl-ambassador that represents the US in Lebanon who has come to give us lessons and talk about 'your internal conflicts, and your history that is full of wars and clashes.. and what will you do after the "Israeli" withdrawal."

"We tell him: 'The wars between the American south and north are still being used as material for American movies. Your country, American ambassador, is the one full of wars and internal conflicts; we don't need anyone to give us lessons," he added.

Sayyed Nasrallah stressed, "No one is allowed to say such words in Lebanon, the country of civilization, history and culture. Why is the American ambassador allowed to speak in this manner? The question is: What has he given you to take anything in exchange?"

"He wants to play games on us following the "Israeli" withdrawal, (stating that) 'there is no problem with the Zionist project, so the only thing the Lebanese have to mind is their internal problems.' I say, "the post settlement perils are great. The term 'to solve the internal problems' is very sweet. (but) This means that following the "Israeli" pullout from South Lebanon, the Americans will come to provoke each Lebanese group against the other.

"The US-"Israeli" project for Lebanon following the withdrawal is one of unrest between the Lebanese. The head of this unrest is this little clerk in the American embassy whose name is Satterfield. They have already started preparing for the unrest project.

"Why are his words met with silence? This is encouraging him, and he starts touring schools and universities; by the way, why don't you appoint him as a university professor?

"Why doesn't anyone answer him and tell him 'you are interfering in Lebanese internal affairs.' Satterfield says, 'the Lebanese people respect Hizbullah because it is fighting "Israel", and you consider it a resistance party;' (Well, we tell him we really appreciate that) but according to American standards, Hizbullah is a terrorist party.'

"We tell him we have not killed Red Indians, nor have we usurped someone else's land, committed massacres, or adopted racial discrimination.

"The US is a usurping entity, just like ""Israel"", for the American people came from European countries, occupied the New World and killed the Red Indians.

"How can the US ambassador be allowed to say that the Resistance is terrorism while all of Lebanon, the government and the people, claims to back and take pride in the Resistance?

"We want an explanation from the Foreign Ministry and the government and we wish that MPs will ask a question to the Foreign Ministry that supports the Resistance and recognizes its right to liberate the land.
"How is the US ambassador allowed to use such terms in Lebanese schools and universities? Is the country out of control?

Hizbullah recovers martyrs' bodies(2)
"Hizbullah today recovered the bodies of four martyrs who had been in the battlefield since August 1999.
"The recovery followed search that went on Wednesday morning by ICRC teams in cooperation with UNIFIL troops at the areas of confrontation with the "Israeli" enemy in Wadi al-Slouqi.

The 5-hour search in which a UNIFIL helicopter took part ended up with finding the bodies of Martyr Hussein Mahmoud Qabalan (from Meis al-Jabal), Martyr Ahmed Abdel-Hussein Foani (Houla) and Martyr Radwan Moheiddeen Dogha (Riaq, Beqaa).

"The three were martyred during the heroic confrontations against the "Israeli" occupation forces on August 17, 1999. The enemy then acknowledged the killing of two of its troops and the injury of seven others.

"More search was conducted in the afternoon by ICRC that was able to withdraw the body of a fourth Resistance man: Martyr Youssef Ahmed Baddah from Bait Leef who had been martyred during the confrontations with the occupation forces in the town of Shamaa on November 24, 1997.

"The Martyrs' coffins that were wrapped with Hizbullah's yellow flags reached the Mount Amel Hospital at 15:15. They were received by a number of Hizbullah officials, including south area official Sheikh Nabeel Qawooq, journalists and ICRC representatives.

Qawooq underlined that recovering the martyrs' bodies was the result of continuous efforts made by Hizbullah and ICRC along the past few months "away from any political development, and within a humanitarian framework".

"Recovering our martyrs enhances our hope to proceed with the course of resistance to liberate all our land, and to keep Hizbullah as the quagmire to ""Israel"", Qawooq said.

We will not spare any chance to attack the enemy that will have no room for reassurance within any framework during the negotiations, he added.

"ICRC's search will continue in the next few days to find the bodies of martyrs Hussein Jameel Hammoud (of Bait Leef) and Youssef Abbas Rihan (from Maifadoun)".

Bury bombs instead of defusing them, occupation(3)
"In order not to be the last soldier killed in Lebanon" a slogan repeated by the "Israeli" occupation troops and their proxy militiamen. But this slogan has turned from mere words to a real practice during combat.

"The recent development in this regard is burying the roadside bombs which had been planted by Resistance men to hunt the occupation troops.

One of the examples was what happened at the western entrance of the town of Hasbaya near the site where two bombs were discovered on Monday.

"The "Israeli" explosives' expert said he could not dismantle the bomb. He ordered that the bomb be buried instead of detonating it. A bulldozer performed the mission.

"Such acts will pose great danger on the residents in the future, for these bombs can go off due to oxidization and corrosion"

Hoss: Beirut will demand 7 villages(4)
"Prime Minister Salim Hoss released a statement on Wednesday denying that the government intends to drop a demand for the return of the seven villages during future peace talks with "Israel". The premiers statement reads as follows:

The seven villages do not fall within the scope of Resolution 425, as they were not part of the area occupied by "Israel" in 1978. Although Lebanese, the seven villages were incorporated into Palestine during the French Mandate period, when Lebanons southern frontiers were drawn by the French and the British. The recovery of the seven villages, however, remains a Lebanese demand. It would be one of several other issues to be tackled in the prospective peace talks apart from Lebanons insistence on a full implementation of Resolution 425.

"The premier declined a request by The Daily Star for further clarification.

"Hoss was responding to an article published in The Daily Star on Wednesday in which he said that it was unlikely that Beirut would pursue the demand in the negotiations with "Israel" because the villages were never part of the Lebanese state".

BARAK RECEIVES LEBANON PULLOUT PLAN: REPORT(5)
""Israel's" daily Maariv today (yesterday) reported that the "Israeli" army has presented Prime Minister Ehud Barak with a plan for a troop withdrawal from occupied southern Lebanon. The plan, code-named "New Horizon" and formulated on orders from Barak, envisages an "Israeli" pullout from the south by July 2000 as part of an agreement with Lebanon and Syria. But the "Israeli" army, Maariv said, wants to begin implementing the plan as early as the beginning of next year, owing to the need to being construction work and technological-logistical projects along the internationally-recognized border. The plan presented to Barak deals with several issues related to a withdrawal from Lebanon, including intelligence, technology, logistics and manpower. An increasingly effective Lebanese resistance in southern Lebanon, especially Hizbullah, has prompted growing calls in "Israel" for a Lebanon pullout. Barak also holds the defense portfolio. "Israel" created an occupation zone in southern Lebanon in 1985, following a widescale invasion of Lebanon, including Beirut. But "Israel" has been occupying Lebanese territory since 1978, despite a UN Security Council Resolution (425) calling for the immediate and unconditional pullout of occupation forces from the south".
