Daily Report
 
 
December Tuesday 7-1999:

Introduction :

Hizbullah SG: Resistance made the occupation's quagmire(1)
"We are in the middle of a violent conflict with an enemy that not only occupies our land and desecrates our soil but also has a dangerous project to dominate and control this region, enslave its peoples, and steal its resources," Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said.

Speaking at a family reconciliation under his auspices in the town of Nabi Sheeth, Sayyed Nasrallah stressed, "The Resistance has made the occupation's quagmire; the quagmire that the enemy wanted us to live."

The Israeli does not know what to do; it is stumbling over its proposals for withdrawal. We hear the enemy prime minister launching a different statement everyday: one time he promises to pull out without an agreement, other times he says there needs to be an agreement. Other statements are made about staying in Lebanon, the Secretary General said.

We can defeat the enemy and boost the honor, dignity, and pride of this people that is making miracles. No one should underestimate the strength of this people, his Eminence concluded.

Occupation creates intelligence to observe proxy militia(2)
Reports from the occupied zone said the Israeli occupation army has begun forming a special intelligence unit whose task is to closely observe the Lahdi militiamen and follow their contacts inside and outside the occupied zone.

Within this framework, a series of meetings were held at the Bint Jbeil barracks and attended by Israeli intelligence officers and senior collaborators in Lahd's militia.

During the meeting, it was decided that a specialized intelligence body will be established to observe the Lahd militiamen. It will include elements from different sects in the occupied zone. These elements' task is to watch all the Lahdi militiamen and report their movements and contacts.

These elements have already started counting the Lahdi militiamen, those of them who have mobile phones, their phone numbers, and lists of their relatives living outside the occupied zone.

The new measures attempt to curb the mounting desertions from the occupation's proxy militia to the liberated areas besides the likelihood of Lahd militiamen dealing with the Lebanese security bodies amidst continuous talk about Israeli withdrawal from Lebanon early next summer.

Any withdrawal will be victory for Lebanon and the Resistance, says premier(3)
Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss said any withdrawal by the Israeli occupation army from Lebanese territories it occupies in south Lebanon and the west Beqaa will be victory for Lebanon and the resistance.

The premier told UPI there were no new developments regarding the settlement process in the region.

US secretary of state Madeleine Albright will not visit Lebanon as part of her regional tour, according to the American statement, al-Hoss said.

Lebanon hopes and wishes a withdrawal will take place following a comprehensive settlement accord within the framework of the inseparable Lebanese and Syrian tracks, for then the withdrawal would be regulated, which is in everyone's interest, he added.

"If there is no agreement on a comprehensive settlement with "Israel" before that, we consider any withdrawal a victory for Lebanon and the Resistance."

The PM said, "We do not consider a withdrawal without accord as peace, for this (peace) has certain conditions that are not met with a non-agreement withdrawal, including the refugees issue and their right to return to their homeland; the water which is being stolen by Israel from Lebanon; the issue of the Shibaa Farmstead area and the seven villages; and, least but not last, the issue of the Lebanese detainees in Israeli prisons".

Dr. al-Hoss underlined that Lebanon will not sign a peace treaty without Syria.

He said the issue of the resistance following the withdrawal is not currently discussed.

When asked about the Israeli occupation's threat to strike Lebanese infrastructures if "Israel" is attacked following a unilateral withdrawal, the premier said "these are hypothetical questions".

Occupation stages more aggressions on liberated areas(4)
The Israeli occupation's warplanes staged four raids that targeted, between 6:30 and 7:05 a.m. Sunday, the Hamra Farmstead and Ain al-Smahieh in the Nabatieh district with four air-to-ground missiles.

Before and after the raids, the occupation's artillery in the Farmsteads of Tamra, al-Mansouri, and the Beaufort Castle targeted, besides Hamra and Ain al-Smahieh, the Hima Farmstead, Woods of Zawtar 
al-Sharqieh, the Plain of Yohmor al-Shqief, and Wadi Abu Salim.

Saturday night, the enemy violently pounded and raked with heavy and medium machine guns the outskirts of Habboush, Kfar Romman, Arab Salim, Houmeen al-Fawqa, the Zahrani River gorge, the vicinity of the Habboush-Arab Salim Highway.

The aggressions also covered Mount al-Rafih Hilltops, the woods of Sojod, Rihan, Loweizeh, Oqmata Farmstead, and Nabeh al-Tasseh gorge (in Iqleem al-Toffah).

Over one hundred mortar, field and tank rounds slammed the targeted areas.

The aggressions on the vicinity of the Arab Salim town damaged a water reservoir belonging to Hassan Fahs.

For its part, the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following Israeli aggressions (5):
00:05, the deserted Lebanese Army barracks was bombarded by ten 60mm rounds, whereas Ali Taher woodland exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
12:08, the Israeli war jets staged a sortie into Sojod village.
18:45, one 120mm shell landed the peripheries of Loweizeh.
20:05, Mansouri area was bombarded by thirteen 105mm tank rounds, whereas the seashore of Mansouri-Bayada was targeted by two others.
20:30, four 105mm rounds were fired at the outskirts of Henneih village.
23:10, the perimeters of Yohmor village exposed to five 120mm shells and a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
23:30, Mount Zaatar was shelled by four 155mm missiles.
23:50, four 120mm shells fell on the perimeters of Baraasheit.

Military Operations(6)
The IR team of martyr Monir Rizk attacked, at 16:30, a Lahdi engineering force as it was building road leading to Khazen area. Using the appropriate weapons, the militants managed to bar it from achieving its goals.

Also, the IR men spotted, at 19:50, a Lahdi motorized patrol as it was traveling on the road linking between Rihan and Sojod. Shortly afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Kassem Ahmad Nahim and Kassem Zeinelabideen Yassine targeted it with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.
