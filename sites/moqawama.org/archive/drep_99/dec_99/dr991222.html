Daily Report
 
 
December Wednesday 22-1999:
 
Hizbullah stresses: Resistance will proceed(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah reiterated the party's "determination to continue with the resistance with all firmness and strength, without any hesitation until the last occupation soldier is out of our land, no matter what the political developments are," stressing that "the strength of the Lebanese and Arab stand stems from the continuation of this Resistance."

"As he noted the "delicacy, seriousness, and sensitivity of this stage that requires the will of the strong firm men," Sayyed Nasrallah said, "If we want to recover our Lebanese territories with minimum losses, we must maintain the national, official and popular coherence behind the resistance option."

"The resistance's weapons shall remain solid and sharp. The resistance will not weaken, withdraw or hesitate and the political developments in the world and the region cannot intimidate the courage and determination of the resistance fighters," Nasrallah said during a religious ceremony on Monday to mark the passage of a week since the martyrdom of Sheikh Moussa Mahmoud Ahmed, the hero of the Rob Thlathine confrontations against the "Israeli" occupation forces.

The ceremony, at Shahed Educational Complex in Beirut, was attended by Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc MPs, and political and social figures.

"If the enemy troops are fighting with the spirit of the one who does not want to be the last to be killed in this war, The Islamic Resistance men are fighting and competing to reach martyrdom in this war, the secretary-general said.

"He called for postponing the discussion and not rushing to talk about the post-withdrawal era, "for this is better and is a strength element to Lebanon," underlining the importance and need for coherence and unity of Lebanon's stand at this sensitive stage.

"The problem of America and "Israel" is not only with political Islam. They are seeking to make us abandon our religion and our prayer and disregard our history and ethics", his Eminence concluded.

Lebanon wants occupation to compensate for stolen antiques(2)
"Speaker Nabih Berri underlined that Lebanon will demand that "Israel" compensate for all the losses inflicted on Lebanon, including the "Israeli" entity's exploitation of Lebanese water.

Lebanon will be invited to the negotiating table when the Syrian-"Israeli" talks reach a certain level, Berri said.

"Speaking as he met the parliament's media correspondents, the speaker added, "There will be something like a conspiracy against Lebanon, if it joins the negotiations before Syria obtains an "Israeli" pledge to return to the June 4, 1967 border line."

"Lebanon will be the one to decide to join the negotiations following the second round, Berri said quoting Syrian President Hafez al-Asad as saying that he does not accept an "Israeli" withdrawal from the Golan Heights without a pullout from south Lebanon.

"At the beginning of the meeting, the speaker raised the issue of "Israel's" theft of Lebanese antiques.
He recalled that he had talked about "Israel's" looting of Lebanese antiques in a speech he had delivered in the southern town of Tair Dibba.
