Daily Report

September Sunday 26-1999:

"Remaining militiamen" storm al-Jibbein, attack residents(1)
Report from the South Lebanon area occupied by the Israeli forces said agent Taleb Siblini (of the occupation's proxy militia) stormed the town of al-Jibbein.

Accompanied by his bodyguards, collaborator Siblini ransacked houses and attacked residents, the reports added.
On the other hand, the occupation forces and their militiamen have once again detained Fahd Ali Saleh who is a Math teacher at the Aita al-Shaab public school.

Saleh had been detained earlier and taken to the al-Khiam detention center. He was released when his health condition deteriorated and admitted to hospital for treatment.

As he got better, the occupation forces retook him to the notorious prison, although he has not recovered completely yet.
The Follow-up Committee for Lebanese Detainees in Israeli prisons (FCLD) announced that the occupation forces have once again detained Ali Mohammed Jamil, 14, and took him to al-Khiam camp only two weeks after his release.

FCLD called for taking action to secure the release of Jamil and prisoner Moussa Khalil Ghoson who is dying in one of the occupation zone's hospitals due to his acute asthma and his inhaling smoke when a deliberate fire broke out in the prison earlier this week.

Ghoson who used to weigh 85 kgms (around 160 pounds) when he was detained by the Israeli occupation forces three years ago, now weighs only 50 kgms (around 90 pounds).

Israeli Aggressions(2)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) released a communiqué in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions:
1:50, the surroundings of Haddatha outpost subjected to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.
11:50, two 155mm missiles hit Qaissieh Valley.
15:30, the area between the villages of Majdal Selem and Sawaneh was bombarded by eight 155mm missiles.
15:35, nine 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Majdal Selem town.
Between 17:10 and 21:40, the peripheries of Jibaa town exposed to seven 155mm missiles.
18:30, four 155mm missiles struck the perimeters of Jarjoua town.
21:25, Mount Sojod was shelled by four tank rounds.

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Suleiman Yehia and Ali Chokair attacked fiercely, at 11:30, the Israeli post of Taibeh where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of its western fortification.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Abdel-Karim Qansou and Ziad Mohammad Zahwi targeted Qobaa position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyr Nabil Akhdar attacked, at 16:15, an Israeli infantry force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to chalk up direct and certain goals.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Mahmoud Hachem and Hussein Abdel-Aziz Sammour was attacking Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The western and southern fortifications were hit directly, the mujahedeen told.

Meanwhile, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Ali Mahdi and Hassan Haidous rushed upon Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades striking its northern fortification and preventing it from achieving its goals.
