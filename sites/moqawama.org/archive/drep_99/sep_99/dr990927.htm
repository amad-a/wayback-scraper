Daily Report

September Monday 27-1999:

H.E. Qassem: Resistance will continue exerting pressure on Israel (1):
Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, Sheikh Nahim Qassem, declared on Sunday that Israels repeated vows to withdraw from the south were designed to place Lebanon and Syria under pressure.

They talk about withdrawal daily to exert more pressure and we will talk about the resistance daily to exert more pressure. They launch the words and we launch the bullets, he said at a religious ceremony in Bir Hassan in the southern suburbs of Beirut.

Qassem said that Hizbullahs leaders had decided the future of the party years ago in the event of an Israeli withdrawal.
Hizbullahs silence about the aftermath of a withdrawal is because we do not want any information to be released that could serve the enemy. We want them to remain confused and not knowing what to do. How we shall continue the struggle against the Israeli enemy and how we would deal with the changes that will occur is a secret, he said.

Israeli Aggressions(2)
The Israeli occupation forces staged the following aggressions:
1:05, five 155mm missiles hit the perimeters of Mlikh village.
16:20, the west of Baraasheit town was targeted by five rounds.

Military Operations(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Mahmoud Kourani and Hassan Mohammad Abdel-Jalil attacked, at 16:20, Sojod outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades hitting its western fortification.

Furthermore, the IR men observed, at 20:35, enemy motorized movements at the entrance of Bir Kallab outpost. Shortly afterwards, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Abdel-Amir Khashab and Ali Abdel-Halim Shraim fell upon them with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Nidal Ali El-Hoq and Tarek Jaafar El-Saghir targeted and Israeli force at Kassaret Al-Oroush compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite hits were scored, the militants reported.
