Introduction

The Islamic Resistance (IR) militants staged a large number of military operations against the positions of the "Israeli" occupying troops and their collaborators along the frontline of the "Israeli" occupied zone. One of these attacks concentrated on Bayada outpost which was destroyed completely after the IR men overran it and detonated powerful explosive devices. The IR attacks resulted in wounding four "Israeli" soldiers and three collaborators.

Meanwhile, Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah blasted the international envoys who had appealed for clemency for the occupation's militiamen.

Lebanese civilians wounded in occupation's aggressions on south
The "Israeli" occupation forces' inability to confront the Islamic Resistance men made the enemy troops direct their artillery and warplanes against innocent civilians in villages.

The occupation's violations of the April Understanding on Thursday wounded eight Lebanese civilians and a UNIFIL soldier.

In the port city of Tyre, four civilians -- a girl, a woman and her children aged four and six -- were wounded in heavy shelling from the occupied zone and air raids on the area, which faces the western sector of the occupation zone.

More than 100 mortar bombs hit the outskirts of villages in the area.

A spokesman for the United Nations force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) said a Fijian peacekeeper was wounded when mortar bombs fired from the "Israeli" zone hit his position.

The UNIFIL spokesman said the occupation's proxy militia warned peacekeepers not to use a coastal route to the zone.

"The militia told us they will shoot at any U.N. vehicle that might pass through Hamra crossing point," he said.

The occupation's shells that rained down on civilians in the town of al-Mansouri at 6:30 a.m. Thursday wounded Alia Mohammed Darweesh, 34, her two children Ziad Sawan, 4, and Samar Sawan, 6, Diba Sawan, 35, and her child Ali, 4, and Ibrahim Shehab, 25.

In Zibqeen the enemy's rounds wounded Zeinab Hassan Barakat, 15, and Haroun Iskandar. Ahmed Shehab was wounded in the town of Haddatha by the occupation's shelling.

The occupation's warplanes and artillery staged aggressions that went on all day long on the areas adjacent to the occupied zone.

The enemy's warplanes staged a series of raids while its artillery pounded the western, middle, and eastern sectors. Aggressions concentrated especially on Iqleem al-Toffah and the Western Beqaa.

ANF delegation visits Hizbullah(5)
Hizbullah Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah blasted the international envoys who had appealed for clemency for the occupation's militiamen.

"Foreign emissaries who proposed they (the occupation's militiamen) be granted amnesty are vilifying Lebanon and conspiring against it," his Eminence told a 19-man delegation from the Arab National Forum, an independent think-tank.

Sayyed Nasrallah was referring to statements made by Terje Roed-Larsen, special envoy to UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan, and Peter Hain, British Minister of State for Foreign Affairs. Both called on the Lebanese government to be lenient on the militiamen once "Israeli" occupation soldiers vacate the south.

"At this stage, we should not be easy on collaborators lest they turn from being an "Israeli" problem to an "Israeli" land mine implanted on Lebanese territory," the secretary-general said.

He also praised residents in the occupation zone and the sectors straddling the "Israeli"-occupied strip for aiding Hizbullah's resistance fighters mount their operations against "Israeli" troops.

"We consider that Hizbullah has been the vanguard in scoring this victory, but others have lent us a hand," Sayyed Nasrallah said. "Residents in the occupied strip have long cooperated with us. Most of them were honest people who showered our fighters with the best aid there is."

Touching on the recent Palestinian uprising against "Israeli" occupation troops in Palestinian self-ruled areas, the secretary-general urged the Palestinians to step up their Intifada and emulate Hizbullah's military victory over "Israel".

"We call on you to escalate your uprising  and benefit from the great Lebanese victory," he said. He was referring to the recent showdown between stone-throwing Palestinians and "Israeli" occupation soldiers which left three Palestinians dead on Tuesday.

"I assure the people of the new uprising that they're capable of imposing their demands, including the release of Palestinian detainees in "Israeli" prisons," he said.

Plans for post-pullout security being drafted(7)

"The government on Thursday sought to allay fears of an upheaval in the south, with Interior Minister Michel Murr pledging that security authorities would ensure the wellbeing of pro-"Israeli" militiamen who surrender and protect civilians.
Speaking after presiding over a meeting by the Central Security Council at the Interior Ministry in Sanayeh, Murr said the government is not concerned with the future of Israeli-proxy militiamen.
However, the council gave orders to military intelligence to seek those who desert the militia and surrender to one party or another. The dissidents should be registered with the intelligence group before they re-enter the mainstream, he said.
The dissidents may be infiltrating the liberated areas to carry out actions that may be harmful to security, he added. They should be monitored by military intelligence and be tried.
The dissidents usually surrender to Hizbullah or the Amal movement. On Thursday, the militiaman Haidar Mohammed Haidar, a 25-year-old native of Taibeh, turned himself in to Amal, a day after three of his comrades handed themselves over to Hizbullah and three others surrendered to Amal.
The CSC, which groups top judicial and security officials, will meet once every two weeks, instead of once a month, to devise plans to face any security developments that may result from the withdrawal, Murr said.
Participants in the session included Defense Minister Ghazi Zeaiter, State Prosecutor Adnan Addoum, Military Prosecutor Nasri Lahoud as well as the heads of Surete Generale, Internal Security Forces, State Security and Military intelligence, respectively Generals Jamil Sayyed, Abdel-Karim Ibrahim, Edward Mansour and Raymond Azar.
Three top Syrian officers ­ including Colonel Rustom Ghazale, the intelligence chief for the Beirut area ­ also attended.
The level and broad spectrum of participation underlined the importance of the effort, coming after a barrage of accusations that the government was ill-prepared to cope with any possible post-withdrawal surprises.
The participants looked into reports by security agencies about the situation in the country. The reports will be used in drafting the plans, Murr said, indicating that concrete measures are yet to be decided.
The CSC also looked into a complaint by President Emile Lahoud about the fact that there are more than 60,000 weapon permits ­ a number which is too high and a situation thats threatening security, Murr said.
Based on orders from the president, whose view is backed by Premier Salim Hoss and Defense Minister Ghazi Zeaiter, I will study means to limit the number of permits, he added.

Four Lebanese released from notorious Khiam jail(8)

" Four Lebanese held without trial for more than a year in the notorious Khiam jail in "Israeli"-occupied southern Lebanon were freed Thursday, "Israel's" allied militia said.

Atef Seif, Kamal Munzer, Sadeq Nasrallah and Mahmud al-Qaiss had returned to their villages in the occupied zone after being detained between 14 and 18 months, the militia said.

According to the figures of a Lebanese prisoners' rights committee 143 Lebanese are still held in Khiam, some of whom have been there for nearly 15 years. Most are accused of collaborating with anti-"Israeli" guerrilla groups.

"Israel's" supreme court Wednesday ordered the government to settle the fate of Khiam's inmates before "Israel" withdraws from the region in July.

Human rights groups, including the London-based Amnesty International and the Washington-based Human Rights Watch, have repeatedly denounced cases of torture and ill-treatment of the detainees".

"Israel" plans to bomb and destroy Beaufort Castle: reports(10)

""Israel" may be getting ready to blow up one of the most significant historic sites in southern Lebanon- the Beaufort Castle. Officials in the south are appealing to the UNESCO organization to help save one of the country's landmark archeological sites.

The "Israeli" occupation troops have turned the crusader-era fortress into a war icon and one of their main military compounds in the region. It overlooks areas as far as Beirut, Syria's occupied Golan Heights, cities inside "Israel", and the Jordan Valley. Reports surfacing in "Israel" now say occupation troops plan to destroy the historic site, which is located near Nabatieh and dates more than 850 years back. "Israel" has been occupying the fortress in clear breach of the international Hague Treaty, which explicitly prohibits nations from using archeological and historic sites for military purposes. Ali Badawi, who is in charge of south Lebanon antiquities, said he will soon submit a report to the Paris-based UNESCO, warning that "Israel" may be getting ready to blow up the entire Castle before it pulls out from Lebanon in July. Badawi was quoted as saying "Israel" will be held responsible for all its illegal activities at Beaufort. He stressed, "Israeli" archeologists may have already exploited the area, by excavating artifacts, as they did in other parts of southern Lebanon. The facade of the fortress has already sustained heavy damages. "Israel" rained down hundreds of shells on Beaufort when it invaded Lebanon in 1982. It has since, been under "Israeli" occupation. Experts were quoted as saying "Israel" has replaced historic walls and defenses with bunkers, adding if Beaufort is spared following "Israel's" pullout, it will require millions of dollars to reconstruct the Crusader-era castle".


Annan may visit to break logjam(11)

"UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan is expected to visit Lebanon in the next 10 days to kick off a regional tour that will focus on the planned "Israeli" withdrawal, government sources disclosed on Thursday.
The news came as a closed-door Security Council briefing by UN Middle East envoy Terje Roed-Larsen on the pullout was postponed until next week. A written report by Annan on the results of Larsens recent trip to the Middle East has also been delayed a few days, according to sources at the Foreign Ministry.
Another government source said that Lebanons ambassador to the United Nations, Salim Tadmuri, had received word that Annan would likely visit Lebanon, as well as Syria and "Israel", before the end of the month.
The trip would likely take place after Annans meeting with "Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak in New York next week to discuss, among other topics, Beiruts call for "Israel's" withdrawal from the Shibaa Farms.
Premier Salim Hoss has officially invited Annan, but no answer has been received. Annan could be waiting to meet Barak before confirming his visit, the government source said.
According to a diplomatic source in Beirut, Larsens Security Council briefing and Annans report were delayed on a US request after Speaker Nabih Berri showed on Wednesday a map prepared by the US Department of Defense showing the Shibaa Farms inside Lebanon.
The US felt that it couldnt ignore a map produced by one of its departments, the source said. It wants to study the matter.
Lebanese officials affirmed that Beirut would not be responsible for the consequences of a pullout that fails to include these regions and warned that resistance attacks would continue.
Annan, who will discuss this withdrawal with Lebanese authorities, will most likely ask that a settlement between the various parties on Shibaa be agreed until after the withdrawal.
However, according to the government source, Lebanese authorities will continue to reject such a proposal. The Shibaa Farms are on Lebanese territory and must therefore be treated as such, the source added.
He also noted that the mounting controversy could lead to one of two developments: an escalation of violence in the south or a rapid relaunching of the peace talks between "Israel" and Syria.
 Also on Thursday, Syria told the European Union that "Israel" must leave the Shibaa Farms as part of its planned withdrawal, a Syrian Foreign Ministry spokesman said.
He said that Syrian Foreign Minister Farouk al-Sharaa also told EU Middle East envoy Miguel Angel Moratinos that "Israel" should leave Lebanon without conditions, in compliance with Resolution 425.
Mr. Sharaa stressed the necessity of implementing Resolution 425 fully without any conditions, in a way that leads to the full withdrawal of the "Israeli" forces from all the Lebanese lands including Shibaa, the Syrian spokesman said.
