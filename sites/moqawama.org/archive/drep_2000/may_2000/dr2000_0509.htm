Daily Report

May Tuesday 09-2000:


Introduction

After semi-daily Islamic Resistance's (IR) attacks against it, the "Israeli" occupying troops and their collaborators abandoned Shayar Azour outpost in Rihan sector.

The evacuation processes carried out by the "Israeli" occupation forces from a number of outposts in the Lebanese territories were reflected in a confusion by the commanders of the occupation's proxy militia.

Meanwhile, "Israel's" Army had stepped up its preparations for the withdrawal which may be happened before 7 July as pledged by the "Israeli" enemy's Prime Minister Ehud Barak.


Occupation and militia evacuate another outpost in Rihan(1)
Residents of al-Rihan area woke up at 2:00 a.m. Monday because of blasts coming from the occupation's outpost in Shayar Azoue. They soon discovered that the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators were dynamiting the outpost before evacuating it.

In the morning, a spokesman for the occupation's proxy militia said the withdrawal was part of the anticipated pullout by the occupation forces in July.

What the spokesman did not say is that the outpost has been subjected to the Islamic Resistance's (IR) "outpost nibbling policy", the most recent victims of this policy was the Aramta outpost which was stormed by IR men by the end of last month.

Shayar Azour was the target of semi-daily attacks. The last of the militiamen killed in the outpost fell in an IR operation yesterday (Sunday) in which machine guns and rocket propelled grenades were used.

The militia's spokesman also did not mention that evacuating Shayar Azour will expose the occupation's outposts in Bir Kallab, Rihan and other outposts that the occupation command will find itself obliged to evacuate soon due to IR's continuous operations.


Militia officials confused, threaten war and unrest(2)

The evacuation processes carried out by the "Israeli" occupation forces from a number of outposts in the Lebanese territories were reflected in a confusion by the commanders of the occupation's proxy militia.

Although the "Israeli" occupation's media had said that the "Israeli" army command decided to take back the heavy weaponry it has given to its collaborators, the head of the "Israeli"-run militia Antoine Lahd on Monday threatened a war with Lebanese authorities after ""Israel"" ends its 22-year occupation.

Lahd, who was sentenced to death in absentia by a Lebanese court, said the alternative to more violence in the south was a blanket amnesty for those who collaborated with ""Israel"" over the years.

Claiming to be spokesman for the occupied zone residents in a statement directed at Lebanese President Emile Lahoud, Lahd said the "population" of "Israeli"-occupied south Lebanon had either to emigrate, submit to Lebanese laws as collaborators or fight when the "Israelis" left.

Lahd said the majority preferred the last option: "To carry arms to defend themselves from emigration, prison, humiliation and reproach."

Lahd warned that a Lebanese army attack on his militia after a withdrawal could restore the religious divisions of the civil war.

"If confrontation is restricted to the population and what is called 'the Islamic resistance', the problem would not be so great," he said.

"But if the Lebanese army were to take the side of this so-called resistance, I am certain that national unity would be most seriously disrupted," said the 72-year-old ally of ""Israel"".

Lahd, whose family lives in France, called for Lebanon to grant an amnesty for all crimes committed by his militiamen during the period when the Lebanese government did not control the occupied zone.


Early withdrawal on the cards

""Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak said on Monday that "Israel" may withdraw from south Lebanon ahead of its self-imposed July 7 deadline.
He told "Israeli" Army Radio that preparations were underway for the pullback but no date has been set to complete the mission.
When asked if the withdrawal could take place earlier than the target date, Barak said: By July 7 means not after and doesnt rule out having it before. ,(3)
"The leading Hearts newspaper reported Monday that the army had stepped up its preparations for the withdrawal, which the government announced in March would take place by July, the first anniversary of Barak's taking office.

It said the army was "readying itself for the possibility that the government will move up the day for the pullout."

But Ha`aretz said Barak was under heavy US pressure not to advance the withdrawal to give Washington leverage in its efforts to renew the Syrian track of the peace talks, which collapsed in January over the fate of the occupied Golan Heights plateau.

Preparations are already underway along the northern border, with the army building fortifications and preparing to evacuate border posts in occupied south Lebanon.

Reports of a hastened pullback follow a flare-up of cross-border violence last week, including rockets fired by Hizbullah, the main group fighting "Israel's" occupation, into northern "Israel".

"We intend leaving south Lebanon in an orderly manner and not abruptly," army chief of operations Major-General Giora Eiland told reporters Friday, the day after Hizbullah fired salvoes of rockets on northern "Israel", killing one soldier and lightly wounding dozens more people.

"If we will be called upon to leave earlier than intended, we will be able to," he said. (4)


"Israel" starts works on Lebanon border fence(5)

" "Israeli" army soldiers began Monday maintenance works on electronic devices on the fence along the border with Lebanon, an AFP correspondent on the scene reported.

Army teams were deployed in small groups with pick-up trucks to carry out works on the electronic devices meant to prevent guerrilla infiltration attempts, ahead of "Israel's" planned pullout from southern Lebanon by July 7, the correspondent said.

On April 30, the public "Israel" Radio said construction of a new fence began on "Israeli" soil near Metulla in the Galilee panhandle. It will replace 45 kilometres (27 miles) - out of a total borderline of 150 kilometers - which was put up on Lebanese soil "for operational reasons".

In northern "Israel", authorities have recently started building bunkers, widening roads, laying communications and erecting steel nets designed to catch incoming mortar shells and missiles.

"Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak's government has committed itself to quitting south Lebanon by July, with or without a peace accord with Syria, the major power broker in Lebanon".


Hoss praises Arab League's call for freezing normalization(6)

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss welcomed the Arab Leagues call for a freeze in normalizing relations with "Israel", saying it was another sign that Arabs fully supported Lebanon.
Id like to thank our Arab brethren for their support, he said in a statement. The call reiterated a similar position held by Arab foreign ministers in Beirut in March.
In an emergency meeting on Saturday held after "Israeli" warplanes bombed two power stations, the Leagues permanent representatives urged Arab states that have relations with "Israel" in the framework of the peace process to review these ties after the aggression.
They also called for a halt to all Arab participation in the multilateral negotiations until there is substantial progress on all tracks of the peace process.


Syria, Egypt demand full "Israeli" pullback(7)
"Egypt expressed concern after a visit by Syrian President Hafez Assad on Monday that "Israel" would not leave all of south Lebanon by July in a planned withdrawal.
There are fears of how complete the "Israeli" withdrawal will be, Foreign Minister Amr Moussa told a news conference with his Syrian counterpart, Farouk al-Sharaa.
As Damascus and Cairo were coordinating their positions, "Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak also found himself squeezed between territorial demands from the Palestinians and resistance to land concessions from his closest political partners.
"Assad held talks with Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak before leaving without speaking to reporters. The first thing to be done is to realize a complete withdrawal from Lebanese territory, said Moussa.
The withdrawal should be complete on land, sea and air and an honest implementation of Resolutions 425 and 426, Moussa said, referring to UN Security Council resolutions calling for an "Israel" withdrawal from Lebanon".

PM tells expatriates, "We count on you abroad"(8)

"Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss explained to a group of Lebanese expatriates in Australia the Lebanese daily suffering due to the "Israeli" aggressions against the people and their properties, especially the enemy's attacks against Lebanese infrastructures.

Al-Hoss urged the delegation to intensify the action taken by the Lebanese community and the contacts with Australian officials to relay a clear image of the "Israeli" aggressions and the heavy losses inflicted on the power stations in the recent "Israeli" aggression on May 4.

Lebanon will not abandon one span of its territories, especially the Shibaa Farms. It will resist with all forms to liberate its soil from "Israeli" occupation, he said.

The premier commended the action taken by the Lebanese communities around the world to support Lebanon.

This (action) is a huge support, at this stage, and a way to reinforce the Lebanese stand to liberate its occupied soil at the international forums, he said.


Families of helicopter disaster planning to release secret report(9)

" The families of the 73 soldiers, who perished in the mid-air crash of two military helicopters over northern "Israel", are planning to use the Internet to release classified portions of the state report on the crash.

On February 4, 1997, two helicopters carrying troops to the security zone of southern Lebanon collided in mid air over the northern community of Shar Yashuv.

One of the parents whose son perished in the accident stated that in light of the harsh statements made by former OC Northern Commander Maj.-Gen. (Res.) Amiram Levine, he plans to release the entire classified portions of the report on the Internet.

Many parents of the fallen insist the air force continues to interfere in the releasing of the real truth concerning the accident.

Levine stated that on the day of the crash, he was visiting a bereaved family in Jerusalem and recalls having looked up  seeing the harsh weather conditions. He called the operations officer of the Northern Command, asking if the choppers had taken off, expressing his concerns over the inclement weather conditions. He was told that the helicopters had taken off.

Levine also revealed that following his arrival at the scene of the crash, he received orders to release information gradually due to the magnitude of the tragedy but as rumors began to circulate, he made the decision to disregard the directive and announced what had really occurred on national television, resulting in his taking it on the head, as he put it.

The Office of the Israeli Army`s Spokesman reported the events as depicted by Levine are unknown to officials, adding that one hour and 55 minutes following the mid-air crash, the spokesman officially released the announcement of the tragedy.

Family members have been considerably less than satisfied with the official investigation and the resulting measures taken against senior air force and other commanders. Many now feel the release of additional information may increase public awareness and lead to demands for a more comprehensive investigation.

Military operations(11)

The Islamic Resistance (IR) and the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) proceeded with dealing blows to the outposts of the "Israeli" occupation forces and the remains of agent Antoine Lahd's militia.

IR's squad of martyrs Hassan Mahdi Alaeddeen and Ahmed Ali Shahla at 8:00 a.m. Monday attacked the enemy's outpost in Houla with direct rocket propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Ali Hussein Shamas and Hassan Mahdi Alaeddine targeted, at 13:00, Radar post with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Rida Yassine and Ali Mahdi El-Hajj Hassan attacked from a close range, at 15:45, enemy movements inside Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct goals.

Half an hour later, the IR group of martyrs Imad Mohammad Qansou and Hussein Wajih Mshaik rushed upon Dohour El-Kassara compound with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.

Furthermore, the IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Mohammad Abdallah and Hassan Ali Chokr attacked, at 16:45, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

Quarter an hour later, the IR team of martyrs Hisham Qassem Haidar Ahmad and Hussein Hadi Mortada fell upon the "Israeli" outpost of Dabsheh with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Zeid Jamil El-Mousawi and Haidar Hassan El-Mousawi reiterated targeting, at 17:05, Dohour El-Kassara compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to score direct hits.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Adel Hussein El-Mousawi and Malek Ali El-Mousawi was rushing upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Moreover, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Ismail Qamar and Mahmoud Dib Chokair targeted, at 21:00, a Lahdi motorized force as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score definite goals.

Quarter and hour later, the IR team of martyrs Ghaleb Riad Mazloum and Ali Qassem El-Anqouni fell upon enemy movements inside Tair Harfa position. Using the adequate arms, the militants were able to chalk up direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Amir Qassem Rizk and Ahmad Hussein Serhan attacked, at 21:45, enemy military vehicles inside Bir Kallab outpost. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score direct goals.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Samir Sobhi El-Harakeh and Mohammad Hassan Jaber rushed upon enemy motorized movements inside Dohour El-Kassara compound. Using the appropriate weapons, the militants were able to chalk up direct hits.

In its turn, LRB squads Monday morning staged three attacks against the occupation's outposts.

An LRB squad at 6:00 a.m. targeted the al-Hardoun outpost with direct rocket propelled grenades while a second squad targeted the Shamaa outpost and a third, at 6:10 a.m., pounded the Blat outpost with rocket propelled grenades.

Definite hits were scored in the three LRB attacks.
