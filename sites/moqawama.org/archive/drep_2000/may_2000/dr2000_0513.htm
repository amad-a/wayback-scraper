This request is wrong because the occupation's withdrawal is no favor from anyone, for the occupation does not get out with any means other than the military operations, he said, adding, "the withdrawal must be unconditional."

Such a truce is fraught with perils, and probably allows for the establishment of a new "security strip", dismantling equipment and rearranging other things, he said.

The super powers are now seeking to secure a political cover for the pullout in order to ease the Zionist entity's military defeat, he added.

"Sheikh Qassem said it is likely that the UN Security Council would issue all that may assist the occupation to withdraw within this framework".

Lebanon calls for "Israeli" pullout from the farms of Shibaa, via UN(5)

" Lebanon said Friday "Israel's" withdrawal from southern Lebanese territory held since 1978 must also include territory it captured in 1967.

The pullout must include the farms of Shibaa, said Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss in a memorandum to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan, saying if that area is not included the pullout would be "incomplete."

The area was first occupied by "Israel" in the 1967 six-day war, and Lebanon contends it must be viewed as part of the occupied area from which "Israel's" withdrawal was stipulated in the 1978 UN security council resolution 425.

In a five-page letter issued to the press with eight main points, Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss said "it is clear, irrefutable and unequivocal that the farms of Shibaa are Lebanese."

"An "Israeli" pullout from these farms is an integral part of the application of (UN Security Council) resolution 425, if not it would incomplete," he said.

"It would merely constitute a redployment, with all the consequences that implies and not a total pullout from Lebanese territory as set out in resolution 425," Hoss added.

He included seven separate documents in the memorandum to back up claims to the 14 hamlets, at the western flanks of Mount Hermon.

The documents include photocopies of maps and property deeds dating back to 1942, a map of the Shibaa hamlets drawn up by the Lebanese government in 1966 and a construction permit issued by Lebanese authorities in 1929 to a resident of the Shibaa farms.

The memorandum recalls that "Israel" first occupied the territory, known as the "farms of Shibaa," in the June 1967 Arab-"Israeli" war and pursued such occupation of the hamlets in April 1989.

"The farms of Shibaa are Lebanese, at the Lebanese-Syrian borders and are not at the heart of a border conflict between Lebanon and Syria. Thus, no authority can look into it," it said.

It said a letter was addressed in 1946 by the Lebanese government to Syria to state that part of the Shibaa hamlets were included by mistake within Syrian territory by France, the mandatory power which drafted both countries' borders in 1923.

The letter said the Syrian government admitted in a memorandum dated September 29, 1946, that the "error was purely technical and it was not meant to alter the borders or include the named farms within Syria."

"The Shibaa farms on the Lebanese-Syrian border went under "Israeli" control when the Jewish state occupied the Syrian Golan Heights in 1967.

Most importantly, the Shibaa plateau, 1,500 metres (5,000 feet) up on Mount Hermon, is a vital source of water, with the Wazzani river flowing over the Golan into the Sea of Galilee.

""Israel" has also set up ski resorts, tourist villages and spy stations on the plateau".


Hoss pledges security after withdrawal
No settlement if Palestinians are not allowed to return(6)

Security after the withdrawal is a government responsibility, the same as it is in other Lebanese regions. The residents of the occupied zone are Lebanese first and always, Hoss said in an interview with Saudi Arabias Al-Medina newspaper.
He said residents were entitled to their rights under the government, but that they had a duty toward it as well.
Were in a state of law and institutions, and not in a state in which the law of the jungle prevails, he said. Hoss said that if some of the poor souls of the zone had sold themselves to the enemy, this did not mean that everyone favored the occupation.
He predicted that Lebanon would have a prosperous future in political and security terms if "Israel's" withdrawal was complete and abided by United Nations Resolution 425. He said the country must remain wary of "Israel's" intentions, as Lebanon was used to treachery and backstabbing by the Jewish state.
But the prime minister dispelled fears of internal sectarian strife and said that such talk was part of a campaign launched by "Israel".
Its as if the objective of this campaign is to say that the "Israeli" occupation prevented sectarian divisions from taking place in Lebanon. But reality is very different, he said. Hoss added that Lebanon insisted that Palestinian refugees in Lebanon be allowed to return to their homeland.
He also said Lebanon did not expect a settlement with "Israel" if the agreement did not include a fair solution for the Palestinians that would rule out the possibility of settling them in Lebanon. The refugee camps situation following the withdrawal will be discussed with the Palestinian authorities so that no side will use Palestinians to strike at Lebanons security, he said.


As preparations for withdrawal continue, Mofaz reiterates enemy's inability to pay the price of occupation(7)

"The chief of staff of the "Israeli" occupation army, Shaul Mofaz, acknowledged that the reason behind the withdrawal from south Lebanon is ""Israel's"" inability to pay the price inflicted by Hizbullah on the occupation troops.

There is no doubt that there has been erosion in the "Israeli" society and in its ability to fight and take casualties, Mofaz told the "Israeli" daily Ma'ariv.

The "Israeli" newspaper published a poll stating that 63% of the "Israelis" back an "Israeli" unilateral withdrawal from Lebanon, while 29% oppose such a pullout and 8% had no say.

According to Ma'ariv, the percentage in last March was 68% for and 24% against, that is the rate has decreased by 5% in less than a month due to the "settlers fear that the army will not be able to protect them", and the stalemate of the settlement process without reaching an accord with Lebanon.

On the other hand, reports said the occupation army has built a road that links between the Zimraya crossing and the Hasbani bridge.

"The occupation army is using giant helicopters to move the needed equipment and technicians from and into Lebanon in order to hasten the withdrawal preparations", the reports added.

Top Arab comedian Adel Imam pays homage to Hizbullah(9)

"One of the most popular Arab actors, Egypt's Adel Imam, has given a ringing endorsement to the Lebanese resistance group, Hizbullah, calling it the "most honorable" movement in the Arab world in an interview published in a Jordanian daily Thursday. "I salute Hizbullah," he told the independent Al-Arab al-Yawm, saying it was legitimate for the group to use "all means to recover this territory from ("Israeli") occupation." Hizbullah is "the most honorable example now existing in the Arab world," he added. Imam has previously shown his solidarity with Lebanese groups resisting the "Israeli" occupation in Lebanon with a visit to the southern village of Qana where more than 100 Lebanese civilians were killed in an "Israeli" attack on a UN post in April 1996. Imam was recently named a UN goodwill ambassador for Arab refugee affairs".
