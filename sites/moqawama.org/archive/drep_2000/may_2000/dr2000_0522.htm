Daily Report

May Monday 22-2000:


Introduction

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah announced that the Shibaa Farms now fall within the scope of resistance operations like other occupied territory and vowed further attacks in the area.

Meanwhile, the increasing military pressure made by the Islamic Resistance (IR) against the "Israeli" occupation troops lead the "Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak to acknowledge openly that "heavy Hizbullah fire is dictating an early withdrawal and it will be possible to withdraw in one night one June 1".

However, thousands of villagers accompanied by Hizbullah flooded into five villages in the central sector of the occupied after the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators gave up a number of outposts overlooking the freed villages.

Elsewhere, dozens of the "Israeli" agents deserted yesterday and surrendered themselves to the IR mujahedeen.


Nasrallah warns that Shibaa Farms have entered the circle of fire(1)
"The debate over the sovereignty of the Shibaa Farms took a new twist on Sunday when Hizbullah staged a rare attack against an "Israeli" outpost on the edge of the disputed area in the eastern sector of the occupation zone.
The increase in fighting in the south led "Israel" television to report that "Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak had ordered the military to be ready for a final pullout by June 1.
Heavy Hizbullah fire is dictating an early withdrawal  it will be possible to withdraw in one night on June 1, the report said Sunday night.
 Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah announced that the Shibaa Farms now fall within the scope of resistance operations like other occupied territory and vowed further attacks in the area.
The Shibaa Farms has this morning entered the circle of the Islamic resistance fire, Nasrallah said at a commemoration for the sixth anniversary of the kidnapping of Mustafa Dirani, the head of the Believers Resistance. Dirani was snatched by helicopter-borne "Israeli" commandos from his home in Qasr Naba in the Beqaa and has been held in "Israel" since.
At 6.30am, mortar shells exploded around the remote post located on Jabal Rouss, 2 kilometers southeast of Kfar Shouba. The attack came as part of a series of operations against positions in the eastern sector including Ain Qinya, Abu Qamha and Zimraya.
Nasrallah laid out further conditions for "Israel's" pullout. He hinted that resistance attacks could continue unless all detainees were released. We have the right to do anything to recover and liberate the detainees in Khiam and Zionist jails, he said. Even if the enemy leaves Shibaa Farms, but Abu Ali Dirani and Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid are not released, we wont consider this a full withdrawal from Lebanon.
Nasrallah also had stern words for the Israeli agents, giving them the stark choice of surrendering to the authorities or departing with the "Israelis". Any collaborators who continues to man the occupation zone will be killed, he declared.
If you remain in the occupied zone and dont turn yourselves over to the judiciary or leave with the "Israelis", were coming to you with weapons because its not acceptable that "Israel" leave us land mines and traps in the region which we liberated with blood. These are "Israelis" and not Lebanese, he said, referring to the collaborators".


Barak: Heavy Hizbullah fire dictates an early withdrawal(2)

""Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak instructed the army Sunday to be prepared for a pullout from south Lebanon by June 1 in light of the Hizbullahs intensive attacks in south Lebanon, "Israeli" public television said.

"The prime minister instructed the army to be prepared to pull out of Lebanon by June 1," the public television station announced.

"Heavy Hizbullah fire is dictating an early withdrawal ... it will be possible to withdraw in one night by June 1," it added.

The television said that the "Israeli" soldiers are left with the bare minimum in order to allow for a pullout overnight.

"All the soldiers will have only the necessary personal fighting gear, from this weekend there will be no more offices, no more kitchens, the soldiers will start eating food rations in preparation for an early withdrawal."

The "Israeli" government had decided to withdraw its troops from a zone it has been occupying for 22 years by July 7, but since the militant Hizbullah movement stepped up its attacks on the withdrawing troops, there have been discussions of an earlier withdrawal.

Barak cancelled his trip to Washington where he was scheduled to meet US President Bill Clinton Tuesday due to the Hizbullah pressure in Lebanon and Intifada in the Palestinian territories.

"Israel" was alarmed Sunday by the presence of Hizbullah guerrillas in the Taibeh post only four kilometers north of the border with Lebanon. The post was evacuated by "Israel's" proxy militia, which fears for its fate in light of the withdrawal".


Lebanese civilians flood villages as "Israelis", proxies leave...(3)

" More than a thousand villagers flooded into southern Lebanon on Sunday, triumphantly returning to their homes for the first time since "Israel" took the area in 1978.

The villagers returned to five villages in the central sector of the "Israeli"-occupied zone after the departure of the the pro-"Israeli" militia that had taken over some posts after "Israeli" withdrawals.

The Lebanese civilians were accompanied by unarmed members of the militant group Hizbullah, who waved flags and gave the crowd victory signs, AFP correspondents on the scene said.

Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss issued a statement Sunday praising the villagers and telling them Beirut was ready to "welcome back the villages to its bosom, and is ready to tend their wounds and alleviate the suffering arising from the yoke of occupation."

"I pay homage to the high national spirit, which the Lebanese have shown in their attachment to their land, and in their spirit of initiative, despite all the barbaric acts of "Israeli" occupation of which they have been victims," he said.

The first villagers to return said they had been attending a funeral in Ghandourieh, just outside the occupied zone, when they learned the Israeli-proxy militia had evacuated its positions around Qantara.

The former inhabitants of other villages quickly heard from Hizbullah what was going on, and joined the "freedom march."

Finnish UN peacekeeping troops in the sector briefly tried to stop the villagers from entering, but were obliged to give way under the flood of people, said Hizbullah MP Nazih Mansur who was present.

The villagers bumped on down the shattered roads through Wadi al-Hujair, a valley that saw violent shelling just two days ago, to get to Adsheit al-Qsair, Alman and Deir Syrian, villages the militia also left Sunday.

They then went on to Taibeh, without even knowing whether the village, which the "Israelis" left Wednesday, had also been abandoned by the Israeli agents.

" The few villagers who had remained greeted the returnees by showering them with rice and flowers.

The collaborators also left Sunday a coastal position some 14 kilometers (eight miles) south of Tyre, near the "gate" of Hamra, which opens the western part of the occupied zone to the rest of Lebanon, an AFP correspondent saw.

"Israel", which has pledged to withdraw its forces from the whole of southern Lebanon by July, started its pullback on May 9.

The militia has taken over some of the "Israeli" positions, but has also withdrawn from seven villages it held.

The militia, whose fate after the "Israeli" withdrawal is uncertain, has seen 36 defections since May 15, according to Hizbullah and Amal. But Lebanese security services put the number at about 80, saying many defected directly to the authorities rather than to the guerrillas".


Militia defections accelerate in Lebanon as "Israeli", its militia pullout continues(4)

" Thirty-five more members of the pro-"Israeli" militia deserted Sunday to give themselves up to the Hizbullah,

These latest mass desertions round of a week of upheaval that has seen a total of 71 militiaman turn themselves in to Hizbullah, the Amal militia, or villagers in southern Lebanon.

However, Lebanese security services put the figure much higher, saying before this latest mass defection, around 80 militiamen deserted in the past week.

Most of the latest deserters simply stayed in the five villages evacuated by the militia, which were quickly taken back by their former inhabitants, as more than a thousand returned to their homes accompanied by unarmed members of Hizbullah.

Earlier Sunday, eight collaborators handed themselves over to them, six to Hizbullah, two to Amal, those groups reported.

Hizbullah said in a statement that the desertions show the decline of "the agents paid off by "Israel.
