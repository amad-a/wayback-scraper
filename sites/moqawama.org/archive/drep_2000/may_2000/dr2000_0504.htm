Daily Report

May Thursday 04-2000:


Introduction

As the ""Israeli"" officials were talking about withdrawal and enemy Prime Minister Ehud Barak said his forces must pull out from every Lebanese span, fourteen Lebanese civilians, including five children, were wounded when the ""Israeli"" war jets raided into a house at the middle of Habboush town.

In the meantime, the Lebanese government asserted that the resistance will continue as long as one single centimeter of Lebanese soil remains under occupation.

However, the highest ranking officer ever to defect from "Israel's" allied militia Emile Nasr has surrendered a few days ago to the Lebanese security forces.


Fourteen Lebanese civilians wounded in "Israeli" air raid on southern Lebanon

" The wounding of 14 Lebanese civilians Wednesday in an "Israeli" air raid aimed at the home of a leader of Amal movement sparked a wave of tit-for-tat attacks between "Israel" and Lebanese guerrillas.

"In the initial incident "Israeli" planes carried out four raids on the villages of Arab Salim and Habboush, firing a total of eight air-to-ground missiles, a Lebanese police spokesman said.

Police said two elderly women, including the mother of Abbas Hallal, the Amal official in Habboush, an 18-month old baby and his 40-year old mother were hurt by shrapnel for a missile which also caused considerable damage to their two-storey house.

"Six people -- a mother and her five children aged from five to 13 -- in a car driving near the house were also injured, the police added.

"Four more people walking past the house were also hurt.

As for Hallal himself, he suffered bruising when he fell down the stairs trying to get out of the building.

All the casualties, whose injuries were described as moderate, were taken to hospital in the town of Nabatieh.

"Other missiles exploded about 15 metres (yards) from a school bus on the road linking Habboush to Arab Salim, north of the central sector of the occupied zone, the police said, but there were no injuries".

Ninety minutes later two houses in the village of Khorbet Selem north of the occupied zone were damaged by a missile fired from an ""Israeli"" helicopter, Lebanese police said, adding that "by a miracle" there were no casualties.

"A civilian was injured Tuesday when his village came under fire, prompting Hizbullah to respond by firing mortar bombs at an "Israeli" position.

Since the beginning of the year four civilians have been killed in "Israeli" shelling of southern Lebanon and 85 injured despite an April 1996 agreement between the belligerents to spare civilians"(1)

Lahoud slams heinous crime(3)

"President Emile Lahoud denounced the Jewish states air raid on civilians in Habboush on Wednesday as a heinous crime for which there is no excuse.
This crime will surely prove to the public that "Israel" does not seek peace and security, Lahoud said in a statement shortly after the 12 civilians were wounded in an "Israeli" air raid on the Iqleem al-Toffah village.

Beirut warns "Israel" to get out of Shebaa

Resistance will continue if one single cm remains under occupation(4)

"The UNs special envoy to the Middle East flew into Beirut on Wednesday to a stern warning from the Lebanese government that the resistance in the south will not cease if "Israel" does not relinquish the Shibaa Farms in its planned troop withdrawal.
The toughening of the governments stance coincided with a new escalation in violence in the occupation zone, where 12 civilians, including a two-month-old baby, were wounded in an ""Israeli"" air raid on Habboush, Iqleem al-Toffah.
Our stand is clear and unequivocal. The resistance will continue as long as one single centimeter of Lebanese soil remains under occupation, Information Minister Anwar Khalil said. He was responding to a reporters question on Beiruts stand if the Shibaa stretch were not incorporated in "Israel's" withdrawal plans.
Khalil briefed reporters after the weekly Cabinet session, a portion of which was devoted to expectations of the visit by the UN envoy, Terje Roed-Larsen. He said that President Emile Lahoud and Prime Minister Salim Hoss would be together when they received Larsen in Baabda on Thursday to underscore the united stand of the leadership vis-a-vis the impending ""Israeli"" withdrawal.
Larsen arrived late on Wednesday after talks in "Israel" earlier in the week.
Once again this country has a rendez-vous with history, Larsen said in a statement on arrival. I believe it is no exaggeration to say that the eyes of the world are again on Lebanon. Only now, the world sees hope where for so many years there has been suffering and tragedy.
He made no direct reference to the controversy over the Shibaa Farms. But he stressed that Resolutions 425 and 426 were unambiguous and that there can be no compromise on a full ""Israeli"" withdrawal, which must be confirmed by the UN.
During his talks in "Israel", Larsen said, he received pledges that "Israel" would strictly comply with the requirements set out by the Security Council.
He added, however, that the UN had no mandate to rule on border matters in dispute between member states. Demarcating borders is a matter for the states, he said.
The role of the United Nations is to confirm "Israel's" withdrawal from all Lebanese territory to the internationally recognized boundaries, he said. To do this, we are working with the parties to identify that line for the purpose of determining whether a full withdrawal has occurred.
Larsen will hear an unwavering, straightforward and positive Lebanese view that is based on Lebanons known and unwavering national principles, Hoss told reporters earlier on Wednesday.
Lebanon wants to liberate its occupied territories and restore its sovereignty there. It will not give up one centimeter of these territories, especially the Shibaa Farms, the premier added, referring to the Lebanese area "Israel" seized during the 1967 war. The highly fertile farms are located on the Lebanese side of Mount Hermon, Syrias highest mountain, which is under ""Israeli"" occupation. "Israel" argues that the Shibaa Farms belong to Syria, and that a withdrawal from the area would be considered in peace talks with Damascus if and when they resume.
Speaker Nabih Berri also upheld the importance of the area in remarks on Wednesday.
The Shibaa Farms are indisputably Lebanese. We have documents and maps that prove this. Lebanon will never bow to "Israel's" claims that the area is part of Syria, he said. This is a firm stance that I will express in any contacts with international officials.
"Issam Khalife, a history professor at the Lebanese University, said that "Israel's" claims that the Shibaa Farms belong to Syria are baseless. Speaking to the Voice of Lebanon, Khalife added: The area has been part of Lebanon, at least since Lebanon and Syria delineated their common border in 1951. The criterion used in demarcating the border was the direction of water streams. All streams heading west were considered part of Lebanon.
According to Khalife, "Israel's" early-warning station on Mount Hermon stretches into part of the Shibaa Farms. Also, displaced residents  still have their ownership deeds of the farms. The deeds were issued by Sidons Real Estate Department.
The historian argued that "Israel" wants to keep the area because of its rich underground water that supplies Lake Tiberias (in northeastern "Israel") through the Hasbani and Wazzani rivers. Also, "Israel" has built recreational facilities in the area.
"Berri said last month that the skiing resorts in the Shibaa Farms raise millions of dollars for "Israel", while vineyards and wine factories operated by ""Israeli"" settlers also help its economy".


Highest ever officer to defect from "Israel's" allied militia surrenders to security forces(5)

"The highest ranking officer ever to defect from "Israel's" allied militia has surrendered to Lebanese security forces. Militia sources said Emile Nasr, who commanded "Israel's" backed forces in Jezzine until they retreated from the area last June, slipped out of the occupied zone and surrendered a few days ago. The defection came as "Israel" is about to end its 22-year occupation, leaving the fate of the ""Israeli""-trained and paid force uncertain. "Israel" has said it will shelter some militia members who want to leave Lebanon. The militia has suffered heavy casualties this year at the hands of the Lebanese resistance. A growing number of proxy fighters have been giving themselves up to the Beirut authorities. But the pro-"Israeli" "Guardian of the Cedar" group vowed it will continue to fight in south Lebanon following "Israel's" withdrawal.


Agent Etian Saqr extorts occupation, threatens to stay after withdrawal(6)

"As the date for the "Israeli" occupation forces' withdrawal from south Lebanon is drawing near while the status of the Lahd militiamen is unclear amidst growing tension and concern, the oldest but least important collaborator, Etian Saqr, tried to reassure the occupation's agents and issued a statement in which he thought he could extort the occupation's command.

"The soldiers and residents of the (occupied) zone have decided to stay in their villages and not leave them after "Israel" withdraws from south Lebanon, Saqr said at a press conference in occupied al-Quds (Jerusalem).

"They will defend themselves against any side that plans to harm them, said the president of the so-called "cedar guards' party" at the conference that was attended by some opposition members of the ""Israeli"" Knesset.

"The occupation's Knesset on Tuesday postponed for the third time a discussion on the militia's future".


Military Operations(7)

The Islamic Resistance (IR) and the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) proceeded with dealing blows to the outposts of the ""Israeli"" occupation forces and the remains of agent Antoine Lahd's militia.

In retaliation "Israel's" going too far in targeting our civilians in Habboush town, the IR team of martyrs Moussa Mohammad Boussi and Hasan Sareddine Hijazi attacked in a violent and concentrated manner, at 19:10, the border post of Dahr El-Jamal scoring direct hits.

Earlier, An LRB squad at 5:30 a.m. Wednesday attacked the enemy's outpost in Haddatha with machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades.

In the meantime another LRB squad targeted the same outpost with artillery rounds scoring hits in the outpost's fortifications and the enemy troops manning the outpost.

For its part, Amal Movement shelled, at 15:55, Taibeh position.
