Daily Report

May Monday 08-2000:


Introduction

In the framework of chasing the "Israeli" enemy and its collaborators, the Islamic Resistance (IR) proceeded with its blows yesterday killing one "Israeli" agent as its militants attacked Rihan position on the edge of central sector of the "Israeli"-occupied zone.

Meanwhile, the settlers of the "Israeli" colonies in the occupied northern Palestine set tyres ablaze and blocked streets protesting the "Israeli" Army's inadequate response for Hizbullah's painful attacks, as well as the cost to local business and industry, poorly stocked bomb shelters and poor conditions.

Elsewhere, the news today reported that the "Israeli"-proxy militia has abandoned Shayar Azour outpost at the edge of the "Israeli"-occupied area in southern Lebanon after being hit dozens of times by the IR men recently.


Pro-"Israeli" militia member killed in Hizbullah attack in southern Lebanon

" A member of the militia allied with "Israel", was killed Sunday in an attack by Hizbullah in the "Israeli"-occupied zone of southern Lebanon, according to a hospital.

The militia member had been taken by helicopter from his position in Rihan, on the edge of the central sector of the "Israeli" zone, to the Safed Hospital in northern "Israel".

"The fatality brings to 21 the number of "Israeli-allied militiamen members killed since the beginning of the year, according to a count by AFP. Forty other militiamen have been hurt in attacks, mostly by Hizbullah, which is supported by Beirut, Damascus and Tehran"(1)

For its part, the IR team of martyrs Fadi Hassan Al-Tawil and Hamdan Hassan Hamdan attacked, at 13:35, Lahdi infantry members as they were positioning at the rampart of Berkat Dajaj post. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to inflict a number of casualties among the enemy ranks.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Hamieh Mustafa and Ahmad Abdel-Latif was rushing upon Dohour El-Kassara compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Anwar Ali El-Mir and Ali Daher Ghazzawi targeted Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Ismail Ismail and Eid Mohammad Hachem fell upon Berkat Dajaj compound as a 113-military vehicle was entering it in order to evacuate the casualties. The militants, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, barred it from achieving its goal.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Ali Saadallah Itani and Mohammad Abbas Bahmad attacked, at 18:05, a Lahdi force near Qabaa position of the 70th Brigade Command with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Five minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Fadi Hassan Melhem and Mohammad Ali Ghazi El-Husseini rushed upon Mashaaroun compound from different sides chalking up direct goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Ibrahim Ali Hassan and Hussein Hassan Hakim staged, at 18:15, a close range attack against a Lahdi motorized force near Mohaisbat position. Direct hits were scored, the militants told.

Meanwhile, the IR unit of martyrs Abdallah Youssef Safwan and Youssef Majed Amhaz was falling upon Mohaisbat position barring it from supporting the attacked force. Also, the IR anti-aircraft guns fired at the "Israeli" helicopter gunships.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Zaydan and Jaafar Taan Dib attacked, at 22:10, enemy movements at Afandi area with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Hassan Daoun and Abdel-Rahman Khalil Sherri targeted Shayar Azour compound. The mujahedeen, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to score direct hits.

For its part, Amal Movement shelled Taibeh outpost with four 107mm rocket-propelled grenades(2)


"Israeli" allied militia abandons outpost in south Lebanon(3)

" "Israel's" allied militia abandoned Monday an outpost at the edge of the "Israeli"-occupied border zone in southern Lebanon, a militia source said.

The militiamen dismantled and then blew up their position in Shayyar Azour in the central sector of the border zone, it said.

The militia had already given up a position at Aaramta, also in the central sector, which has been deserted since Hizbullah guerrillas seized the post on April 28, planted explosives and detonated them after withdrawing, killing three militiamen.

About four months ago, the "Israeli"-proxy militia also quit the Sojod outpost in the central sector of the border area.

"Israel" has also begun to clear some of its outposts in south Lebanon in preparation for its full withdrawal from the region by July after two decades of bloody occupation.


"Israel's" Army hastens plans for pullout(4)

" The "Israeli" Army sharply stepped up its preparations this week ahead of its withdrawal from southern Lebanon, with the army readying itself for the possibility that the government will move up the date for the pullout, now set for July 7.
"In General Staff discussions held over the past few days, the various "Israeli" Army branches involved were instructed to accelerate their preparations for a unilateral withdrawal. This involves building fortifications along the border and preparing to evacuate outposts in southern Lebanon.
Heavy equipment has already begun to be removed from the outposts, though no weapons have as yet been taken out.
The new haste is the result of the events of the past two weeks. The Hizbullah-driven escalation, particularly the two volleys of Katyusha rockets the guerrillas fired into northern "Israel" last week, exposed "Israel's" difficulty in creating a new deterrent balance in the north as long as the Army remains in the security zone and its possibilities of acting are limited.
In this situation, compounded by rising doubts about the continued functioning of the "Israeli"-controlled militia, the assessment in the Army is that the withdrawal may in fact take place earlier than originally planned.
Deputy Chief of Staff Uzi Dayan hinted at this possibility yesterday when he told Channel One, "The "Israeli" Army operates at the directive of the political level. The political level says, 'No later than the beginning of July.' In a follow-up discussion I held this morning ,  I emphasized that we have to be ready no later than the beginning of July, and possibly also earlier."
Prime Minister Ehud Barak has not yet made a decision on this question. He is under heavy American pressure not to advance the withdrawal to give Washington leverage in its efforts to renew the Syrian track. The United Nations would also apparently like "Israel" to stick to the original schedule so that it will have more time to organize the expansion of UNIFIL, the multinational force in southern Lebanon.
Nevertheless, senior officers, particularly in Northern Command, are urging a withdrawal now.
They say the border fortifications will not be completed by July in any event, and that every additional day of the Army's presence in Lebanon only aggravates the situation".


Syria says it will cooperate with UN on "Israeli" withdrawal: envoy(5)

" Syria is committed to cooperating with the UN forces tasked with helping restore peace and security in southern Lebanon when "Israeli" troops withdraw by July, the UN special envoy said here Sunday.

"Foreign Minister (Faruq) al-Shara assured me of Syria's support for the United Nations," envoy Terje Roed-Larsen told reporters after meeting the Syrian minister.

Shara himself said that "Syria supports the role of the United Nations for the implementation of the two resolutions and all other related resolutions of the Security Council," according to the official news agency SANA.

Resolutions 425 and 426 call for an immediate and unconditional "Israeli" withdrawal from Lebanon.

Shara also said he recognized "the need for the United Nations to fulfill its responsibilities."

Roed-Larsen praised the May 4 Palmyra declaration from Shara and his Egyptian and Saudi counterparts, particularly the "call of the three ministers for the United Nations, through its UNIFIL (Interim Forces In Lebanon), to 'be allowed .. to carry out the security tasks with which it is charged' under Resolutions 425 and 426."

"The kind of unconditional support for the UN expressed in the Palmyra statement provides us with the backing that we will need to shoulder our heavy responsibilities" towards Lebanon in line with these resolutions, he said.

He said full cooperation was needed from all parties concerned, and praised Lebanese President Emile Lahoud's commitment to the security and safety of UN troops operating in the country.

That document also expressed support for Syria's insistence on a total withdrawal by "Israel" from the occupied Golan Heights to the positions existing before the  June 1967 war.

It blamed ""Israeli" stubbornness" for the hold-up in Syrian-"Israeli" talks, frozen since January.

UNIFIL has been in southern Lebanon since 1978, with the stated goal of assuring "Israel's" pullout, re-establishing international peace and security and helping Beirut regain sovereignty over its territory.

The UN force is made up of 4,500 troops from nine countries -- Fiji, Finland, France, Ghana, India, Ireland, Italy, Nepal and Poland.

"The UN envoy's tour of the region has taken him to "Israel" and Lebanon. After Damascus he goes to the Jordanian capital, Amman".


Arabs urge UN to stop "Israeli" attacks in Lebanon(6)

" Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss on Sunday thanked the Arab League for supporting Lebanon after "Israeli" raids on the country.

"This position is in accordance with the position adopted in the special session of the council March 11 in Beirut, showing solidarity with Lebanon when faced with "Israeli" aggression," Hoss told reporters. "We express our deep thanks to our Arab brothers."

Arab League head Esmat Abdel Meguid sent a message Sunday to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan calling for the immediate application of UN Security Council Resolutions 425 and 426 of 1978, which call for "Israel's" withdrawal from southern Lebanon.

The Council of the Arab League met Saturday in Cairo and called for a freeze in normalization efforts with "Israel" after the Jewish state's "aggressions," reiterating the position it adopted in March after "Israeli" air raids February 7 and 8 on Lebanese civilian installations.

"Israeli" Prime Minister Ehud Barak pledged last month to pull "Israeli" troops out of Lebanon by July and has made it clear the withdrawal will go ahead regardless of whether an agreement is reached with Syria .

""Israel" launched the overnight raids Thursday after attacks by the militant Islamist group Hizbullah into "Israel", themselves a riposte to "Israeli" attacks on Lebanese civilians".


Arab Americans strongly condemn "Israeli" attacks on Lebanon(7)

" The American-Arab Anti-Discrimination Committee (ADC) issued the following statement last week regarding "Israel's" retaliatory attacks in southern Lebanon.

The American-Arab Anti-Discrimination Committee (ADC) strongly condemns the "Israeli" bombing of civilian areas in Lebanon over the past three days which have killed at least two civilians, injured many more and once again plunged much of Beirut into darkness. Tensions increased after two "Israeli" attacks on Lebanese civilians on Wednesday, May 3. "Israeli" forces shelled the southern Lebanese village of Al Katrani killing two civilian women, 82-year-old Almaza Muzhir, and her 40-year-old daughter Ilham Tawil. In a separate attack, "Israeli" warplanes bombed the village of Habboush, wounding fourteen Lebanese civilians, including five children. The injured included two elderly women, an 18-month old baby and his 40-year old mother, and a mother and her five children aged from five to 13.

After Lebanese resistance forces retaliated against targets in northern "Israel", "Israeli" warplanes once again bombed Lebanese power stations in Beirut. In spite of its announcement that it intends to withdraw from most of occupied Lebanon in June, "Israel" continues to attack civilians and fuel dangerous escalations of tension. The US State Department has called for maximum restraint on all sides and has condemned attacks on civilian targets. ADC notes, however, that the State Department was silent when only Lebanese civilians were targeted on Wednesday and commented on the situation only after "Israeli" civilians were also drawn into the conflict.

Yet again, "Israel" has chosen to respond to attack and murder Lebanese civilians in support of its brutal occupation of southern Lebanon and then claim retaliation when bombing more civilian targets, including power stations. ADC urges the press not to simply repeat this official "Israeli" line, and to acknowledge in its reports that the current fighting began with "Israel's" attacks on Lebanese civilians on Wednesday.

""Israel's" campaign to continuously attack and destroy the Lebanese power grid, which it has attacked at least three times in the past year is a cruel attempt to prevent the Lebanese people from rebuilding their infrastructure and economy after years of devastating civil war. "Israel" has occupied southern Lebanon since 1978, when the UN Security Council, including the United States, unanimously demanded in Resolution 425 that "Israel" withdraw its troops from Lebanon forthwith and without condition. ADC President Hala Maksoud said In order to have any credibility, our government must also adopt a single standard whereby it is equally outraged at attacks on Lebanese civilians as it is by attacks on "Israelis".

"Israelis" burn tyres in protest at army restraint after Hizbullah attacks(9)

" Hundreds of residents of the northern "Israeli" town of Kiryat Shmona set tyres ablaze and blocked streets urging the government to do more to protect them against Hizbullah rocket attacks.

"Lebanon must burn," shouted demonstrators, many complaining at what they said was the "Israeli" army's inadequate response to the rocket salvos on Thursday and Friday that killed one soldier and injured around 30 other people.

Kiryat Shmona, the largest of the communities on the border with Lebanon, has for years borne the brunt of attacks by Hizbullah fighting "Israel's" occupation of its northern neighbour.

The Jewish state pounded civilian targets in Lebanon in pre-dawn strikes on Friday after a first volley of Hizbullah rockets on northern "Israel" but held back from further retaliatory raids after a second day of rocket fire.

"Israel" plans to withdraw from south Lebanon, where it occupies a self-declared "security zone," by July 7.

Deputy War Minister Ephraim Sneh, who visited Kiryat Shmona Sunday, vowed that "Israeli" will retaliate to attacks after its withdrawal "with greater force and more quickly."

Prime Minister Ehud Barak plans to present his cabinet with a 450-million-dollar plan to strengthen security and the economy in northern "Israel".

""Israeli" tourism officials estimate that the most recent Hizbullah attacks on the border communities, the fiercest since June, cost the region around six million dollars in lost tourism income".
