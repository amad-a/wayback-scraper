Daily Report

May Monday 15-2000:


Introduction

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah stressed that "the Islamic Resistance (IR) will continue its operations on a daily basis so that "Israelis" will never consider returning to our land", warning the "Israeli" enemy government against attacking Lebanese civilians.

Meanwhile, Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said that Hizbullah will pardon any "Israeli" agent who kills an "Israeli" or collaborator officer and who escapes from the "Israeli"-occupied zone.

However, Prime Minister Salim Hoss reiterated that "Israel's" failure to withdraw from Shibaa Farms would be considered a re-deployment and not a withdrawal.

 

Nasrallah: Resistance will continu its operations...(1)

"Hizbullahs secretary-general, Sheikh Hassan Nasrallah, said on Sunday that the Israeli-allied militiamen must not be granted amnesty while they continued to attack Lebanese civilians.
The door for amnesty must be locked in the face of collaborators who must be tried and given the harshest punishment, Nasrallah told more than 3,000 party supporters in Bednayel, Baalback.
Nasrallah warned that granting amnesty to the Israeli-proxy militia would allow "Israel" to create intelligence networks that would create sectarian strife. He warned against attempts to protect the militia or cover for members after the withdrawal. The militiamen have to either leave with the Jews, turn themselves in or be killed, he said.
Nasrallah said resistance fighters would continue with their operations until the end of the withdrawal process and until UNIFIL deployed in the occupied zone.
The Islamic resistance will continue its operations on a daily basis ... so that "Israelis" will never consider returning to our land, he said.
Nasrallah also warned the "Israeli" government against attacking Lebanese civilians.
If Barak does not want Katyushas to fall on the heads of the settlers, he must avoid attacking civilians and civilian facilities and stop Lahds agents from attacking them, he said.

Kill an "Israeli" and go free, offer to Israeli agents
Hizbullah will plead for soldiers who turn on their masters(2)

"Hizbullah promised an amnesty on Sunday for any the Israeli Army militiamen who kill an "Israeli" soldier or an agent officer and flee the occupied zone.
The call, which came as the group launched Katyushas at an "Israeli" border position following the wounding of a Lebanese civilian, was made by Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, Sheikh Nahim Qassem.
"He pledged to spare the militiamen from Hizbullah revenge or retribution if they followed his proposal. Any agent who kills an "Israeli" (soldier) or his agent boss and then flees to the liberated areas will be considered to have repented, and we will demand amnesty for him, said Qassem. We will do our best to save him from the punishment of prison or death facing him.
Qassem was speaking during the one-week commemoration of the death of Hizbullah martyr Jalal Rammal in Doueir, Nabatieh.
We want to give the collaborators a final chance. It is an opportunity, should they decide to take advantage of it, he said.
Qassem described the militias recent withdrawal from key positions such as Aramta and Shiyar Azour, at the edge of the zone as a defeat and not a redeployment as described by the militia.
They (collaborators) are not withdrawing because of military tactics like they claim but they are withdrawing because they are defeated, Qassem said.
He said the party would protect occupied zone residents of various religions, adding that it did not need the sponsorship of the United Nations or human-rights activists to do so".

Playing mind games(3)

"As any efficient guerrilla organization knows, achieving ones goal requires the ability to mount a deft propaganda campaign to sap the morale of the enemy as well as deadly prowess on the battlefield.
Part of Hizbullahs success in ousting the "Israelis" from Lebanon, empty-handed after 22 years of occupation, has been its ability skilfully to marry these two requirements.
While Hizbullah fighters have been chipping at the resolve of "Israel's" occupation forces with regular hit-and-run attacks, the partys propaganda machine has waged a more subtle battle to keep the "Israeli" government off balance.
An example of this is Hizbullahs refusal to divulge its plans toward "Israel" following the withdrawal of troops from Lebanon.
"Hizbullahs latest clever psychological ploy, delivered with perfect timing by Sheikh Nahim Qassem, the partys deputy secretary-general, was the offer of amnesty for any Israeli-allied militiaman who kills an "Israeli" soldier or an agent official and then escapes from the zone.
With this public offer, Qassem has struck at the heart of "Israeli" fears over the disintegrating the militia. "Israel's" military has long been concerned at the prospect of frightened militiamen turning their guns on their "Israeli" masters in the final days of occupation in a bid to curry favor with Hizbullah.
Qassem has astutely taken advantage of these fears. How many militiamen actually take up his offer is perhaps not as important as the psychological impact it will have on "Israeli" troops inside the zone, counting the days to a final pullout.
"The "Israeli" government is still in a quandary over how to handle the fate of their Lebanese allies. Qassems offer will add little encouragement to "Israeli" hopes for a smooth departure from Lebanon".


Hoss takes Shibaa Farm message to UN Security Council members(4)

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss on Saturday reiterated that "Israel's" failure to withdraw from the Shibaa Farms would be considered a re-deployment and not a withdrawal, despite comments by "Israeli" Foreign Minister David Levy that his country would not relinquish the area.
However, Hizbullahs Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said on Sunday that the party was committed to fight until the last inch of the country is liberated. The government says that the Shibaa Farms are Lebanese and this is what concerns us. We arent concerned with the views of either "Israel" or the Security Council, said Nasrallah during a speech in the village of Bednayel in Baalback.
On Saturday, Hoss telephoned Syrian Foreign Minister Farouk al-Sharaa to discuss the details of the memorandum, sent the day before to the UN, that contains documents proving that the Shibaa Farms are Lebanese.
There is no room for argument that the Shibaa Farms are Lebanese and the documents we sent to UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan prove that, Hoss said as he left the Grand Serail. The Premier said an "Israeli" withdrawal that fails to include the Shibaa Farms would mean that "Israel" has not implemented UN Security Council Resolution 425 and consequently, it will be considered a re-deployment and not a withdrawal as "Israel" claims.
He added that Lebanon demands a complete and unilateral "Israeli" withdrawal as stipulated by Resolution 425, including the Shibaa Farms. Hoss also telephoned Esmat Abdel-Meguid, the Arab Leagues Secretary-General, to inform him of details of the memorandum.
The Premier said Lebanons delegate at the Arab League would deliver a copy of the memorandum to Abdel-Meguid to be officially documented by the League.
He also asked Abdel-Meguid to make copies of the memorandum and distribute them among other Arab delegates who would, in turn, present them to their governments.
In addition, Hoss called for a meeting on Monday with the ambassadors of countries that are permanent members of the UN Security Council to discuss developments.
The meeting between Hoss and the ambassadors of the US, Russia, China, France and the UK comes shortly before a Security Council meeting expected to look into a report prepared by Annan on the "Israeli" withdrawal.
"On Sunday, Hoss said Lebanon had still not received an answer from Annan concerning an invitation to Lebanon to discuss the countrys position regarding the "Israeli" withdrawal".


Four Lebanese hurt in "Israeli" air raid, shelling(5)

" Four Lebanese were wounded Monday in "Israeli" air raids and shelling in southern Lebanon, police said.

Najib Mouneim, 26, and Ali Hussein Farhat, 32, were casualties of an "Israeli" air attack as they tilled their fields near Wadi Sohmor in the western Beqaa plain.

Mouneim was buried under earth thrown up by the explosion of a missile and was taken unconscious to hospital, while his companion was wounded by splinters in the legs.

"Israeli" warplanes also attacked a valley between the villages of Mlikh and Aaramta, just outside the central sector of "Israel"-occupied zone of southern Lebanon, police said.

In an earlier incident a Lebanese army sergeant on leave and his wife were injured when their house was hit by a shell while they were still in bed. The shell, fired from the occupied zone, fell on the village of Kfar Tebnite.

Artillery from the occupied zone also fired around 60 shells on Loweizeh and the areas around seven villages near the occupied zone.

"Six civilians have been killed and 86 wounded since the beginning of the year, despite arrangements made in April 1996, sponsored by France and the United States, under which the opposing sides committed themselves to avoiding civilians on either side".

AI calls for torturers to be tried(7)

"A visiting delegation from Amnesty International and a local activist group called on Sunday for the prosecution of all "Israeli" soldiers and their militiamen who tortured Lebanese prisoners.
Delegates from the London-based group and the Follow-up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons made a list of recommendations during a meeting in Beirut on the issue of Lebanese prisoners and "Israel's" withdrawal from the south.
The organizations seven-point plan of action included a call for prosecution of those who tortured detainees and the release of all of "Israel's" Lebanese hostages prior to the pullout.
They also called on all newly released prisoners and those still in detention to demand reparations from "Israel's" government.
All freed prisoners were urged to give graphic statements of their torture before international forums to inform global public opinion about "Israeli" human-rights abuses.
The organizations also demanded that "Israel" permanently shut down Khiam and free all prisoners held as bargaining chips, namely prominent leaders Mustafa Dirani and Abdel-Karim Obeid.
They also demanded that a rehabilitation center be set up in Khiam to treat victims of torture.
"Delegates from Amnesty International, which is soon due to open a regional office in Beirut, also met Hizbullah official and former MP Mohammed Birjawi on Saturday, when Birjawi called on the group to force "Israel" to account for Lebanese citizens missing since the 1978 "Israeli" invasion".
