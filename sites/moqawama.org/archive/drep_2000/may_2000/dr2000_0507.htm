Daily Report

May Sunday 07-2000:

Israeli Aggressions(1)

The Islamic Resistance (IR) issued a communique in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions:

4:55, the surroundings of Shayar Azour and Mothallath positions exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

23:25, the areas of Naameh Hill, Bir Dahr and Daidaba heights and the 910th hilltop came under a number of 160mm missiles.

23:55, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets hit west of Baraasheit town.


Military Operations(2)

The IR team of martyr Hussein Mahdi Mohammad Ali attacked enemy movements near Ahmadieh position with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct goals.
