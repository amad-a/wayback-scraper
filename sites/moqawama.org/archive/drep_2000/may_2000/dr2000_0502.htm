Daily Report

May Tuesday 02-2000:


"Israeli" Aggressions(1)

The IR released a statement in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:

00:05, four 155mm missiles landed the perimeters of Jibaa town.

Between 00:55 and 1:05, the surroundings of Bayada post exposed to a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets whereas two flare shells were fired overhead.

1:35, a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds hit the surroundings of Beit Yahoun outpost.

2:10, Slouqi Valley was targeted by three tank rounds.

3:05, several 120mm shells were unleashed at Haddatha woodland.

5:30, six 120mm shells slammed into the surroundings of Bir Kallab outpost.

Between 6:05 and 23:15, Sawan area was bombarded by fifteen 155mm missiles.

6:35, four missiles landed the peripheries of Shaqra town.

6:45, the south of Baraasheit town was pounded by eight 155mm missiles and eight 120mm shells.

Between 14:25 and 14:40, the west of Yater village was shelled by ten 155mm missiles.

15:35, the perimeters of Majdal Zoun came under three 155mm missiles.

Between 17:30 and 17:50, the surroundings of Mothallath position exposed to heavy machine gun fire.

17:35, four 155mm missiles fell on Mount Sojod and four others on the peripheries of Jibaa town.

17:40, a barrage of heavy machine gun bullets struck Arid El-Zennar area.

17:50, several 155mm missiles hit Mount Al-Rafih, Tasseh Well watercourse and the northern peripheries of Arab Salim town.

Between 17:50 and 18:25, Arid El-Zennar area was bombarded by twenty seven 120mm shells.

Between 17:55 and 19:00, Tasseh Well watercourse was pounded by fourteen 155mm smoke missiles and four 120mm shells.

18:00, Qaissieh Valley was shelled by four 155mm missiles.

18:10, one 155mm missiles landed the perimeters of Majdal Selem town.

18:25, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into Mount Sojod.

Between 18:50 and 21:00, Mount Abou Rkab was pounded by nine 155mm missiles and eight 120mm shells.

20:20, four 155mm missiles hit the peripheries of Arab Salim town, whereas Habboush River watercourse was shelled by a number of 155mm missiles. Also, the surroundings of Shayar Azour compound exposed to heavy machine gun fire.

22:00, an "Israeli" gun boat showered the seashore of Qlaileh and fired several flare shells overhead.

Between 22:05 and 22:40, the outskirts of Habboush town came under twelve 155mm missiles.

23:15, the area between Zawtar and Mazraat Hamra was targeted by four 155mm missiles.

23:25, three 120mm flare shells were unleashed over Kounine area.


Military Operations(2)

The IR team of martyrs Ali Toufic Khalil and Mohammad Abbas Zahwi attacked, at 6:30, Jabal El-Jamaah position with the proper arms scoring direct hits.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Amthal Hakim and Abbas Hussein Taleb staged a swift and violent attack against Baraasheit compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. During the close range and ten-minute clashes, the militants chalked up direct goals. The same compound also was targeted by the group of martyrs Abdel-Hassan Mohammad Izzeddine and Ahmad Mahmoud Mahdi. The mujahedeen, who used the adequate arms, were able to score certain hits and inflict more casualties.

Meanwhile, the IR unit of martyrs Samir Mohammad Khalaf and Majed Al-Zein fell upon Haddatha compound with the appropriate weapons chalking up direct and definite goals.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Mohammad Abdel-Rahim Hamdoun and Hassan Ali Ayoub targeted in a violent & concentrated manner, at 17:20, Bir Kallab outpost striking its western fortification and inflicting a number of casualties.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Hilal Ali Nasreddine and Samih Mohammad Wehbi was falling upon Dohour El-Kassara compound with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.

Forty five minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Ali Deeb and Abbas Ali El-Shab rushed upon Mashaaroun position with the suitable weapons chalking up direct goals.

Furthermore, the IR unit of martyrs Khalil Ibrahim Yassine and Mahmoud Salman targeted, at 18:30, a Lahdi force as it was entering Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Abdel-Mir Raqqa and Youssef Kourani attacked, at 21:30, Dohour El-Kassara compound with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the militants told.
