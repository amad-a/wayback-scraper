Daily Report

February Saturday 05-2000:


Introduction

Following the successive defeats which suffered the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators at the hands of the Islamic Resistance (IR) men, the "Israeli" enemy tried to revive its collapsed morale by launching air aggression against one of the IR leaders who survived with God's help and kindness, scoring by that a new defeat for the enemy.

Meanwhile, the IR has achieved a new victory in its long path of sacred struggle against the "Israeli" occupying troops and their allied militia. Sojod compound, the most heavily hit outpost in the occupation zone, was abandoned and blown up. Security sources reported the move came after the "Israeli" agents refused to continue serving in the post.

However, the "Israeli" occupation forces added another to the scores of violations of the April Understanding they perpetrated during the past week. Seven civilians among them a mother and her two children were wounded by "Israeli" helicopter raids on the road linking between the towns of Maaroub and Barish.


IR Cadre survives helicopter attempt on his life(1)
Following the successive defeats that were inflicted on the "Israeli" occupation army and its collaborators at the hands of the Islamic Resistance (IR) men, following the flood of threats made by the enemy leaders against IR, and in an attempt to distract attention from the brilliance of IR men's blows, the enemy tried to recover its collapsed morale through an air aggression against one of IR's cadres who, thanks to Allah, survived the attack and the enemy recorded another fiasco, an IR communiqué read.

"At 10:40 a.m. Friday, the first missiles launched by the "Israeli" helicopters could not hit the car of one of IR's cadres at the Maaroub-Barish Highway."

"He managed to leave the car before the second round of missiles hit it along with the nearby houses injuring a number of civilians," the communiqué added.

"IR considers what happened today (yesterday) a blatant violation of the April Understanding that will not go without retaliation whose form and timing will be chosen by IR that will put an end to this Zionist going-too-far (aggressions)."

"As it affirms that its cadres and men, who are engaged in daily confrontations with the enemy, expect to be martyred in order to achieve victory and expel the occupation, IR is determined to go along the path of Jihad and Resistance. It will not escape offering sacrifices in this path," IR said.

Let the enemy know that its methods and aggressions will not spare it or its collaborators as they both will face inevitable death until they are forced to retreat in defeat and humiliation, IR concluded.


 "Israel's" ALLIED MILITIA WITHDRAWS FROM SOUJOD(2)
""Israel's" allied militia has quit the strategic mountain-top military outpost of Sojod, which had come under heavy attacks from Hizbullah. The resistance group hailed the retreat as victory.

Another "Israeli"-backed militia retreat. This time from the strategic Sojod military outpost which overlooks the Iqleem al-Toffah region, where some of the most intense clashes between Hizbullah resistance fighters and occupation forces take place. Villagers in the region welcomed the pullout, saying they can now access farmland they had been banned from cultivating. A number of villages facing the post including Jarjoua, are now considered safer. Hizbullah said the withdrawal effectively liberated Lebanese territory, and will improve the resistance's ability to operate in the occupied zone. "Israel's" proxy force confirmed the withdrawal and said it would move to two positions in Rihan and Kassaret al-Oroush, where they would have better protection. The new encampments are only three kilometers away from Sojod. The "Israeli" army broke the news of the relocation and said the decision had been prompted by "operations considerations". The head of the "Israeli" governments' political and security department, Danny Yatom, said the withdrawal should not be interpreted as a unilateral withdrawal. "Israeli" planes blasted the outpost overnight and fired at nearby ravines and valleys. Military sources in Jerusalem were quoted as saying Hizbullah fighters had recently stepped up attacks on Sojod, among the farthest posts from "Israel's" border. Resistance fighters had on a number of occasions taken over the post for a few hours. "Israel" claimed Sojod's military importance had decreased after the militia's June retreat from Jezzine, a southern enclave that had been under occupation since 1985. Militiamen also retreated from another strategic post in Kfar Houna recently. It overlooked Jezzine. The Sojod pullout coincided with mounting militia and "Israeli" losses in southern Lebanon. This week, Hizbullah staged some of its most successfully attacks on occupation forces in the south, killing the militia's second-in-command, Aql Hachem, and three "Israeli" troops, triggering "Israeli" pledges of revenge. Prime Minister Ehud Barak has pledged to withdraw "Israeli" forces from Lebanon by July, within the framework of an agreement with Damascus and Beirut".


SEVEN CIVILIANS INJURED IN "ISRAELI" SHELLING(3)
"Seven civilians among them a mother and her two children were today wounded by "Israeli" helicopter raids near the southern port city of Tyre. Lamia Ezzedine, her two children, Nour and Mohammed, along with her sister Mounya, were injured when an "Israeli" missile exploded next to their car near the village of Barish. Four houses in the village were damaged by shrapnel. "Israeli" helicopters had hovered for more than an hour over Tyre, sparking panic in the city. Occupation troops also intensified shelling of areas north of the occupied zone. The villages of Mansouri, and Majdal Zoun near Tyre were rocked by "Israeli" gunfire. Warplanes also shelled Iqleem al-Toffah. "Israel" escalated its attacks against civilian targets in the south this week, drawing threats from Hizbullah that it would respond by launching Katyusha rocket attacks against northern "Israel". Meanwhile, the resistance announced its fighters today (yesterday) pounded "Israeli" positions in occupied Arnoun scoring direct hits".

Military Operations(5)

In a new victory achieved by the Islamic Resistance (IR) against the ""Israeli"" enemy and its proxy militia, the IR succeeded, as it had promised before and due to its successive and successful blows, to liberate Sojod compound.

Considered as one of the most significant positions overlooking Iqleem Al-Toffah area, the mujahedeen had managed many times to tread it with their feet and plant IR's flags on its "fortified" ramparts. Also, the IR men were able to disgrace the ""Israeli"" Army's elite units when an IR militant managed to walk through its bunkers, fist fight with one ""Israeli"" unit soldier leaving him injured and return safely to his base.

Today on Friday ‎4‎ ‎February‎ 2000, the ""Israeli"" collaborators pulled out of Sojod outpost after evacuating the military vehicles and destroying its fortifications and installations.

The concerned outpost witnessed a long tight blockade imposed by the IR which has a high profile of ambushes and attacks against it in which a great number of ""Israeli"" soldiers and their allied militiamen were killed or wounded. The last ambush made by IR occurred on yesterday night as a Lahdi patrol was traveling on the road linking between Sojod and Rihan destroying two military vehicles and inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.

Later on, more detailed information will be released by the IR.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Wa'il Anani and Taleb Harb attacked, at 6:45, the major fortification of Izzeih position scoring direct and certain hits.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Jaber and Farouq Ismail was rushing upon its other fortifications with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and definite goals were chalked up, the militants told.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Fayad and Moussa Fahs fell upon the same position with the suitable weapons where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

Meanwhile, the IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Rida and Ali Rida Yassine targeted Beaufort Castle scoring direct goals.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Hassan Mohieddine Hamzeh and Ihab Hassan Medlej attacked, at 17:10, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking some of its ramparts.

Moreover, the IR squad of martyrs Rabih Ibrahim Marzouq and Hassan Malek Harb attacked, at 20:30, motorized movements near Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

For its part, Amal Movement staged, at 18:50, an attack against Ali Taher outpost.


Proxy militia's internal conflicts renewed(6)
"Security reports from the occupied zone affirmed the divisions in the ranks of the "Israeli" occupation's proxy militia as clashes took place between the militiamen.

"The new reports said the chief of the so-called "coast security" in the militia, Taleb Siblini, held a meeting with the militia's security officials of the western sector villages.

"Following the meeting, Siblini barred the eastern sector's official from entering the areas he controls besides refusing to obey the orders coming from agent Karamallah Saed.

"A clash occurred in the town of Naqoura between the militiamen of Siblini and Saed, the reports said.

Agent Siblini has seized the Naqoura port, the collaborators' outposts in the western sector, and the Hamra crossing, they added.

"Siblini believes he has the right to replace agent Aql Hachem in the militia's western Brigade command, security sources said.

"A lot of militiamen have refused to man the collaborators' front-line outposts and resorted to Siblini to protect themselves from being arrested by Kramallah Saed's militiamen", the sources added.


LEBANON CALLS FOR FULL "ISRAELI" WITHDRAWAL FROM Lebanon(7)
"Prime Minister Salim Hoss welcomed the militia retreat from Sojod, saying Lebanon is looking forward to "Israel's" pullout from all occupied territories. Hoss also unveiled that Beirut has yet to receive a response from UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan concerning reports that "Israel" carved a new border with Lebanon. The prime minister said Lebanon's defense ministry has evidence that "Israeli" experts in fact plotted a new border fence. A number of newspapers recently reported that "Israel" has re-drawn the frontier allowing it to keep a number of strategic hills following a possible withdrawal from Lebanon".
