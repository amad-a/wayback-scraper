Daily Report

February Thursday 03-2000:

 

Introduction
The Islamic Resistance (IR) men proceeded with their powerful blows against the "Israeli" occupying troops and their agents. One "Israeli" allied militiaman was killed and three others wounded as the IR mujahedeen detonated a big explosive device on a track leading to Baraasheit compound.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" war jets staged a series of sorties against several areas in South Lebanon, flew low over the main southern coastal towns and broke the sound barrier over Beirut at noon.


IR kills one "Israeli" agents and wounded three others(1)
The IR team of martyrs Mohammad El-Hadi and Abbas Khreibani ambushed, at 19:15, a Lahdi patrol as it was traveling on the road leading to Baraasheit compound. As the patrol arrived at the determined spot, the IR team detonated a powerful roadside bomb inflicting a number of its casualties. Immediately, the "Israeli"-proxy militia admitted that one of its members was killed and three others wounded as a result of the blast. The casualties raise to five the number of militiamen killed so far this year with another eight wounded.

Earlier, the Islamic Resistance (IR) Wednesday afternoon staged a series of operations against outposts and forces belonging to the "Israeli" occupation forces and their proxy militia scoring definite hits.

Squad of Martyrs Youssef Faqih and Hassan Shamaa at 15:50 blitzed the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in al-Qsair with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Meanwhile, IR's squad of Martyrs Salaah Shaito and Deeb Bahjeh pounded the Salaah outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring definite hits.

Ten minutes later, as an "Israeli" Merkava tank positioned in the vicinity of the Salaah tried to back the targeted outpost, it came under fire from IR's squad of Martyrs Fadel Atwi and Mohannad Foani that used the appropriate rocket propelled grenades and barred it from accomplishing its task.

IR at 16:10 spotted enemy vehicles moving inside the Mohaisbat outpost. Squad of Martyrs Ahmed Sherry and Mostafa Salman of IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) immediately pounded the outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits. Smoke billowed from the outpost.

Ten minutes later, squad of Martyrs Hassan Obeid and Akram Khatoun pounded the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in al-Taibeh with rocket propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.

At 16:30, an "Israeli" Merkava tank in al-Taibeh tried to advance to the operation site. When the enemy tank reached the Mohaisbat area (Ras al-Birkeh), it came under fire from IR's squad of Martyrs Abdo Agha Zada and Suheil Diab which used the appropriate rocket propelled grenades and barred the tank from accomplishing its task.


Mofaz tries to boost his troops' morale: ""Israel" will retaliate"(2)
"The "Israeli" occupation army's chief of staff Shaul Mofaz tried to play down the statements made by his prime minister Ehud Barak who ruled out escalating the situation and staging aggressions against Lebanon in retaliation for killing agent Aql Hachem and the three "Israeli" soldiers in al-Izzieh outpost.

"Mofaz said the "Israeli" security cabinet has endorsed the "Israeli" army's recommendations pertaining to the situation in South Lebanon.

"In an attempt to boost the morale of the "Israeli" troops, Mofaz, who was visiting the family of one of the occupation soldiers killed in al-Izzieh, said ""Israel" will choose the appropriate time and place to retaliate," claiming that there is no tension between the army and the government in the "Israeli" entity adding that what has been published in this regard do not reflect the truth".


"ISRAEL" ATTACKS VILLAGES CLOSE TO TYRE(3)
""Israeli" warplanes today (yesterday) launched a series of air raids against south Lebanon and flew at low altitude over the main coastal towns. "Israeli" helicopters and navy boats pounded the outskirts of villages close to Tyre as the international panel monitoring a 1996 truce agreement met to discuss 29 complaints filed by Lebanon and "Israel" over alleged cease-fire violations.

" "Israeli" warplanes intensified raids on southern Lebanon. The jets fired at least three missiles on an area near Kafra outside the occupied zone and struck hills in the Iqleem al-Toffah mountain ridge. "Israeli" warplanes also raided an area between jabal al-Botom and Zibqeen, north of the zone "Israel" occupies. For the first time since 1996, "Israeli" warplanes raided a valley between the villages of Jwayya and Aiteit near Tyre. "Israeli" helicopters hovered over a number of villages near the port city. "Israel" escalated its attacks on the south this week, and the resistance launched a series of successful operations killing three "Israeli" occupation soldiers. "Israel" reacted angrily to their killing, accusing Damascus of being behind the upsurge of resistance attacks. But Syria rejected "Israeli" accusations saying resistance attacks in southern Lebanon were caused by "Israel's" continued occupation of Lebanese territory and its attacks against the country, not by instructions from Damascus. The Al-Baath daily said there is no link between the breakdown of Syrian-"Israeli" negotiations and Hizbullah resistance attacks in southern Lebanon. Syria which has been calling on "Israel" to withdraw fully from the occupied Golan Heights before peace talks can resume in the US, had accused "Israel" of trying to make peace in the US but of causing war in south Lebanon. "Israeli" Foreign Minister David Levy called on Syria to quote rein in Hizbullah. Levy warned that "Israel" is ready to hit back and painfully in response to resistance attacks in occupied southern Lebanon. "Israel's" losses in the south over the weekend and this week, which included its top allied militia military commander, Aql Hachem, had angered "Israeli" officials, who threatened to suspend peace talks with Damascus if resistance activities in the south continue. But Prime Minister Ehud Barak, who met to "Israeli" allied militia leaders yesterday, retreated and said peace talks and developments in southern Lebanon should not be linked. Barak who has pledged to end "Israel's" 21-year occupation of southern Lebanon by July vowed to avenge the losses. Washington, which mediated the faltering Syrian-"Israeli" talks, has appealed to Lebanon, Syria and "Israel" to show restraint. During a Knesset session, where legislators discussed the situation in the south last night, "Israeli" defense minister Ephraim Sneh said peace talks with Syria should not resume as long as "Israeli" occupation troops in Lebanon are being attacked. Member of Knesset Uzi Landau said "Israel" should have pulled out of Lebanon last June without an agreement, following former defense minister Moshe Arens's blitz against the country's civilian and economic infrastructure. Any unilateral pullout now, he added, would amount to an "escape".
