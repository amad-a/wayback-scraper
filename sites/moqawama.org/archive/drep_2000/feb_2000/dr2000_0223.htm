Daily Report

February Wednesday 23-2000:


Introduction

Kuwait's Foreign Minister Sheikh Sabah Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah has expressed his country's full support for Lebanon and pledged political and financial assistance to help reconstruct a part of the power stations destroyed by the "Israeli" air aggressions on Feb. 8.

Meanwhile, France announced that intense contacts are underway to convene a meeting of the April truce monitoring group in Southern Lebanon.

However, the "Israeli" occupation forces staged more aggressions on Lebanese civilians and towns wounding two fishermen fishing off the seashore of Tyre.


Summit hopes grow as Arabs close ranks
Kuwaiti offer could forge common front against enemy(1)
Kuwait on Tuesday declared support for convening an Arab summit to shore up support for Lebanon, even if it meant the Kuwaitis would have to sit face-to-face with their long-time foe, Saddam Hussein of Iraq.
The unexpected announcement by Kuwaiti Foreign Minister Sheikh Sabah al-Ahmad Al-Sabah during a brief visit to Beirut could help bring together regional powers after a decade of division.
Sheikh Sabahs visit coincided with reports that diplomatic efforts, spearheaded by Egypt, were under way to move the venue of an Arab League foreign ministers meeting from Cairo to Beirut in another show of support for Lebanon. A senior Arab diplomatic source said there was almost unanimous agreement on holding a March 14 meeting in Beirut.
For his part, Sheikh Sabah did not mention the possibility of moving the ministerial meeting to Beirut, but suggested that participants could devote one of the two days planned for the forum to send a clear message to "Israel" and the United States that there was a united Arab stand, which would have a great impact on the main sponsor of the peace process, I mean the United States.
Kuwait is ready to attend an Arab summit, even if Iraq was invited, he said, emphasizing how far his country would go to support Lebanon at this critical phase.
Kuwaits emir, Sheikh Jaber al-Ahmad Al-Sabah, delegated me to visit Lebanon to convey our countrys unwavering political and financial support, he said on arrival at the airport.
Sabah arrived from Cairo, where Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak briefed him on his own surprise visit to Beirut on Saturday.
Prime Minister Salim Hoss, who serves as foreign minister, greeted Sheikh Sabah at the airport and escorted him to the Grand Serail for a meeting. The Kuwaiti royal later visited President Emile Lahoud at Baabda.
Farouk Kaddoumi, head of the Palestine Liberation Organizations political department, also arrived in Beirut on Tuesday for talks with Hoss.
The premier conferred by telephone on Tuesday with Syrian Foreign Minister Farouk al-Sharaa, who underscored the importance of a concerted demonstration of Arab public outrage to counter "Israel's" attacks and threats. Hoss and Sharaa agreed that such concerted Arab denunciation of "Israel's" aggression on Lebanon has long been overdue.
In Algiers, Arab League parliamentarians wrapped up their three-day conference with a strongly-worded condemnation of "Israel", while encouraging member states to transform their verbal support for Lebanon into tangible material aid by the end of the year.
We ask that each Arab country commit itself to establishing a development project in south Lebanon and the Western Beqaa to support the steadfastness of the Lebanese people and their remaining on its land, the final statement added.
Speaker Nabih Berri, addressing the Algiers gathering, took a number of Arab countries to task for normalizing their relations with "Israel".
"Israel" is ... normalizing its relations with some states so that it can step up its aggression against some of your brethren, Berri said.
Berri also said he did not intend to accuse you or doubt the positions of Arab leaders who have expressed ... their support for Lebanese, Syrian and Palestinian brethren, but cautioned leaders not to let mere reactions at the moment of "Israeli" crimes govern your policies.
Challenging Arab states, Berri emphasized the importance of financial aid, saying that despite a post-Taif agreement by Arab states to contribute $2 billion to Lebanons reconstruction, the money had yet to be paid, while nothing had happened with regard to a decision to create a joint Arab fund for aid to Lebanon.
Saudi Arabia and Kuwait contributed about one-third of the amount Berri mentioned, while efforts to secure the rest stalled during the past decade.
Berri said that while European governments were racing to establish (development) projects in the south, Western Beqaa and even the border zone occupied by "Israel", where are the projects of the Arabs, or some of the Arabs, and where is their support?


APRIL TRUCE GROUP EXPECTED TO MEET SOON
"France's Ambassador to Lebanon Phillipe Le courtier said intentense contacts are underway to convene a meeting of the April truce monitoring group in southern Lebanon. Press reports in Beirut expected the April cease-fire panel to convene a long overdue meeting this week. But the Jewish state has yet to indicate it will take part in any talks aimed at discussing "Israel's" blitz on Lebanese civilian installations earlier this month.

" The new French Ambassador to "Israel", Jacques Huntzinger, said in "Israel", Paris and Washington are working hard to bring "Israel" back to the five nation truce panel in southern Lebanon. The panel includes delegates from the USA, France, "Israel", Syria and Lebanon. Huntzinger said French Prime Minister Lionel Jospin's visit to "Israel" tomorrow which will coincide with US envoy Dennis Ross's presence in the Jewish state, will help quote rebuild the monitoring group for the next period. Jospin is heading a high-profile team for talks with top officials in "Israel". Huntzinger stressed his country does not play a major role in peace-making, but is ready to take part in security arrangements following a possible "Israeli" peace deal with Syria and Lebanon. "Israel" walked out of the panel meetings earlier this month after one of its occupation troops was killed in southern Lebanon. It has so far not indicated when it will return to the panel meetings. Foreign ministry sources in "Israel" said Jospin may be carrying some new ideas for a cease-fire in southern Lebanon, or may offer to mediate between the Lebanese and "Israeli" governments. The 1996 accord bans attacks on and from civilian targets but permits resistance operations against "Israeli" occupation forces. "Israeli" officials had said they wanted to change the rules of the panel, to forbid any resistance attacks on occupation troops in the south. Beirut and Hizbullah have categorically rejected the "Israeli" attempt. Syrian Foreign Minister Farouk Sharaa was quoted by Beirut press as expecting the April group to convene a meeting in the next few days. Officials will discuss complaints filed by Lebanon and "Israel". They are expected to condemn "Israeli" attacks on Lebanese civilian infrastructure on February 8. Clashes in southern Lebanon and "Israeli" raids on Lebanon have traditionally subsided following the convention of the April monitoring group".


Sobhani: Iranian support of Lebanon will increase(3)
The Iranian Ambassador to Lebanon said on Tuesday that the reformists sweeping victory in this weeks Iranian parliamentary elections would not affect his countrys relationship with Hizbullah but could benefit Lebanon.
Hizbullah is a Lebanese party. The change of people in leadership positions in Iran does not affect our countrys support of Lebanon or Hizbullah, Mohammad Ali Sobhani said during a meeting with Melhem Karam, the head of the Journalists Union.
Sobhani maintained that Iran gives unanimous support to Lebanon and to all of the countrys different groups. If any change occurred, he argued that it would be positive.
We can even say that the Iranian support of Lebanon will increase in the next stage, in line with the policies of Iranian President Mohammad Khatami, who has said that he likes Lebanon and has expressed a desire to visit, the ambassador said.
Sobhani earlier paid a visit to President Emile Lahoud, during which the ambassador handed Lahoud a letter from Khatami.
The Iranian president reportedly expressed his countrys support for Lebanon, as well Irans readiness to help rebuild the power stations that were destroyed by "Israeli" air strikes earlier this month.


"Israeli" navy pounds coast near Tyre: police(4)
" The "Israeli" navy pounded the coast near the southern Lebanese city of Tyre at dawn Tuesday, preventing hundreds of Lebanese fishermen from putting to sea, police said.

The navy shelled the coast for an hour from 5:00 a.m. (03:00 GMT) while four of its gunboats patrolled Lebanese waters.

Chairman of the Tyre fishermen's association Khalili Taha blasted the "Israeli" shelling for "hitting the earnings of thousands of families in the fishing business".

In a press statement, he also called on the Lebanese government "to act rapidly to end the blatant violations of the April 1996 (truce) arrangements", designed to protect civilians on both sides of the "Israel"-Lebanon border.

"Israeli" artillery fired at the village of Shaqra from the zone occupied by the Jewish state in south Lebanon Tuesday, damaging three houses and a vehicle belonging to the municipality, the police said.

Meanwhile, "Israeli" airforce jets broke the sound barrier above the eastern Lebanese town of Baalback.

It was the first time "Israeli" jets had been in the area since the attacks of February 7 and 8 which destroyed three power relay stations, one of which was in Baalback.

11:15, several 120mm shells, including two smoke ones, struck the perimeters of Qabrikha and Bani Hayan villages.

13:05, Dallafeh crossroad was bombarded by ten 160mm missiles.

13:30, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets hit the surroundings of Qssair position.

20:45, a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds struck Kfar Romman town.


Military Operations(6)

The IR team of martyrs Wissam Ali Fsahi and Tahseen Ali Sharifeh attacked, at 11:10, Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades. Certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen reported.

Twenty five minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Hani Obeid and Abdallah Qassem Yassine rushed upon enemy movements inside the 80th Brigade HQ of Jamoussa with the adequate arms scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Ahmad Baalbacki and Ali Habib Jaber fell upon Haddatha compound with the proper weapons chalking up direct goals.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Mohammad Dib Al-Dirani and Ismail Ahmad Ismail targeted, at 15:40, an "Israeli" motorized force near Taibeh position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct hits.
