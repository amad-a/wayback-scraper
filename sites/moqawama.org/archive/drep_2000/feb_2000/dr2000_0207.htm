Daily Report

February Monday 07-2000:


Introduction

The series of successful and powerful attacks staged by the Islamic Resistance (IR) men continued against the "Israeli" occupation forces on weekend. One "Israeli" soldier, at least, was perished and seven others wounded, four seriously, as a powerful explosive device was detonated by the IR men when a nine-man patrol passed by about only dozens meters from the "Israeli"-manned post of Blat and 600 meters from the Lebanese-Palestinian borders. The fatal attack forced the "Israeli" Army to open an investigation that will seek to answer many puzzled questions, including why the device, which was detected by the dog, was detonated.


Hizbullah adds more "Israelis" to casualty list
One "Israeli" soldier killed and seven wounded

Proceeding with its program of extracting security and stability among the "Israeli" enemy soldiers and in retaliation for the recent "Israeli" aggressions against our civilians, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen penetrated all the enemys sophisticated weapons and measures. The IR Special Forces managed to infiltrate deep inside the "Israel"-occupied area, no more 600 meters away from the border line with the occupied Palestine, and ambushed an "Israeli" foot-patrol of fifteen soldiers as they were inspecting the area around the "Israeli" manned Blat position. As the patrol arrived at the determined spot, very close to the position, the IR militants detonated, at 12:30, a powerful explosive device inflicting all the "Israeli" soldiers where their screamings were obviously heard. When an "Israeli" force tried to evacuate the casualties, another team of IR men attacked it with the adequate arms inflicting more casualties among the enemy ranks.

Also the IR squad of martyrs Younes Mahdi El-Husseini and Ibrahim Abdallah Nehme targeted, at 20:45, Soweida outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRR) fighters attacked, at 27:30, the "Israeli" post of Qabaa striking its fortifications.

In the meantime, another group of (LRB) rushed upon Mohaisbat position with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct goals were chalked up, the fighters told(1)

For its part, The Daily Star reported that the deadly bomb attack came a day after "Israel" Radio quoted senior officials in Jerusalem as saying that "Israel" was suspending all informal contacts with Syria until the escalation in the south is contained.
The ambush occurred at 12.10pm on a track leading to the "Israeli"-manned Blat outpost, 750 meters north of the border.
Hizbullah said that one of its special forces units penetrated the zone to within 600 meters of the border to plant a powerful bomb. The bomb was detonated when a nine-man patrol passed by. The "Israeli" Army said that three soldiers were wounded in the blast. One soldier was killed and another four were wounded when Hizbullah fighters attacked them with mortars and Sagger anti-tank missiles while the casualties were being evacuated, the army added.

So far this year, five "Israeli" soldiers have been killed and 15 wounded. All but one of the casualties occurred in the past 12 days. To put the statistics into context and to highlight the intensity of Hizbullahs present campaign, between Aug. 17 last year and Jan. 25, no "Israeli" soldiers were killed in fighting in the south and only two were wounded.
A Western military source said that the "Israelis" would have to respond to the latest ambush but ruled out air attacks against Lebanons infrastructure. The "Israeli" Armys prestige is at stake, the source said. The pressure to respond is tremendous. But theyll confine themselves to military targets only.
It was widely reported last week that "Israeli" Army commanders had recommended that Barak order air strikes on bridges and power stations in Lebanon in an attempt to curb Hizbullah. But the source denied the reports, suggesting that the military officials had called for restricting retaliation to guerrilla targets.
The "Israelis" know that Hizbullah will fire Katyusha rockets into "Israel" if Lebanons infrastructure is targeted, the source said. We can expect more concentrated attacks on (resistance) people and the buildings they use(2)

However, Ha'aretz "Israeli" newspaper said: " The incident occurred yesterday around noon when a squad from the Givati infantry brigade left the Karkoun (Blat) outpost and set out on a combat mission. About 200 meters from the outpost, the patrol's tracking dog touched off a powerful bomb that exploded about 50 meters from the soldiers, wounding four of them lightly.
As their comrades began to treat them, the squad came under heavy mortar fire. IDF* officers in the sector said Hizbullah identified the force and directed accurate fire, using mortars and Katyusha rockets, at the soldiers from the village of Zikin, a few kilometers away.
Staff Sergeant Gefen, who was the paramedic of the force, was killed and three soldiers were seriously hurt.
"There was massive, accurate firing," the commander of the Galilee Division, Brigadier General Moshe Kaplinsky, said afterward in a briefing to reporters. He said the bomb had probably been placed at the site within the past month.
"The incident is part of incessant fighting that goes on in the sector," Kaplinsky said.
The evacuation of the soldiers to the international border, a distance of about 700 meters, took about 90 minutes because of the heavy exchange of fire in the region. The soldiers were brought out The soldiers were placed on stretchers on the road near Moshav Zarit, but again their evacuation to a hospital was delayed because the heavy fire prevented a rescue helicopter from landing in the area.
At this stage, cameramen from Channel One and Channel Two arrived on the scene and took close-ups of the wounded soldiers - close-ups that they later screened on their news broadcasts. This prompted an unusual statement by the IDF spokesman, who expressed outrage at the television stations' "insensitivity."
"There is no journalistic achievement here - only a lack of consideration for the soldiers and their families," the spokesman stated.
Asked about Hizbullah's ability to plant bombs so close to an "Israeli" outpost, a senior officer said: "We never pretended to seal the security zone against infiltration by hostile forces. Even with all our efforts, there are breaches.
"In this case, Hizbullah was able to plant the bomb. Just last week they hid a bomb in the same sector, but it was found and neutralized."
Army sources noted that Hizbullah planned the ambush well and, based on the assumption that the detonation of the roadside bomb would cause several casualties, "the terrorists located themselves in good strategic positions, observed the events and opened fire very accurately."
Northern Command held a situation appraisal yesterday in the wake of the recent string of events.
The assessment reached was that in light of its recent successes, Hizbullah will step up its activity in all sectors of southern Lebanon...(3)

OC Command to investigate where did Sundays events go wrong?(4)
"IDF OC Northern Commander Major-General Gabi Ashkenazi on Sunday evening promised the investigation into Sundays roadside bomb attack which claimed the life of Givati Brigade soldier Sergeant Yedidya Geffen, 20, and left seven soldiers wounded, wound be investigated to the most minute detail.

Brig.-Gen. Moshe Kaplinski, Commander of Galilee Forces, stated the explosive device that was detonated on Sunday afternoon was one of the many deployed by Hizbullah over the past month.

Ashkenazi will have to explain how an explosion, which injured three soldiers moderately-to-light, concluded with one fatality and four soldiers seriously injured.

According to Kaplinski, the investigation into Sundays roadside explosive device is just in the preliminary stages and there are many questions to be answered. The senior commander added that the trained K9 operating with the unit did its job, but was unable to explain why the roadside device that was detected did detonate. The investigation will seek to answer many questions, including why the device, which was detected by the dog, was detonated, injuring three soldiers moderately.

After the injured force pulled back about 100 meters (yards) from the Karkum (Blat) outpost, mortars were fired at the rescue team, killing Sergeant Geffen and injuring four soldiers seriously.

The injured were then evacuated to the border area, and treated on the patrol road while the force waited for choppers to evacuate the injured. Physicians and medics worked to stabilize the wounded, with the first helicopter transporting the four seriously wounded to Haifas Rambam Hospital Trauma unit and the remainder, listed in moderate condition, taken by a second helicopter to a hospital in Nahariya.

Deputy Medical Director of Rambam, Dr. Tzvi Ben-Yishai, reported the injured sustained shrapnel wounds to their abdomens, thoracic areas, arms, hands and more, including serious damage to internal organs. One soldier is listed in very serious condition.

Among the officers arriving at Rambam was IDF OC Southern Command, Maj.-Gen. Yom Tov Samiya, who is also in charge of the Givati Brigade.

In a related matter, Sergeant Ron Ben-Shmuel, who was gravely injured in the security zone on January 31, is reported to still be in very critical condition at Rambam Hospital, having not shown any signs of improvement following several surgical procedures. He remains on life-support equipment in a comatose state. In that attack, five soldiers were wounded and three were killed.

Funeral services for Sergeant Yedidya Geffen, 20, from Jerusalem, are to be announced.


Lebanese Army on alert after "Israel" threatens retaliation(5)
"The Lebanese Army went on its highest level of alert Sunday after "Israel" vowed to retaliate for the killing of one of its soldier and the wounding of seven others in a combined roadside bomb and rocket ambush beside the Blat outpost in the western sector of the occupation zone.
"A security source announced on Sunday night that Lebanese Army units had been placed on the highest levels of alert throughout the country in anticipation of any "Israeli" aggression.

Four Mothers call for immediate troop withdrawal from security zone(6)
" The Four Mothers organization, lobbying for an immediate unilateral "Israeli" troop withdrawal from the security zone of southern Lebanon, on Sunday evening called upon Prime Minsiter Ehud Barak to order an evacuation of forces from southern Lebanon immediately and unconditionally.

"The leaders of the national lobby stated that five soldiers have been killed in the first five weeks of 2000 and others are seriously wounded, with some fighting for their lives. The Four Mothers stated they are unwilling to have IDF forces remain in the security zone until the prime ministers July 2000 deadline, adding that nothing is gained by such a move and the cost in the number of lives continues to climb".

Sunday Times: Close Dimona nuclear facility(8)
"According to a Sunday Times report, quoting Professor Uzi Eben, a former senior official in Israels nuclear program, Eben is calling for the immediate closure of the facility which poses serious dangers and is not safe, and may lead to another Chernobyl. Eben is calling for a start of the process which would lead to the shutting of the facility at the very least.
The Times quotes Israeli scientists calling for the shutting of the facility which they deem unsafe, to prevent A second Chernobyl.
The daily Yediot Ahronot reports that experts have pointed out the Dimona facility began operating in 1960, and the nuclear plant is now approaching 40 years of operation. Yediot adds that experts report that facilities as old as Dimona are being shut around the world due to the dangers they pose to the area residents and environment.
Eben, quoted by Yediot, stated that the operations at the facility have contributed to its aging process, adding that although it is not comparable with the Chernobyl facility prior to the meltdown, it does pose a significant threat to the area and must be shut to avert a catastrophe.
Among problems, which may be encountered according to Eben, is leakage of radioactive water used to cool the facility, difficulty in controlling the facility. The Times adds that in addition to Eben, another prominent member of the scientific community who remains anonymous added his name to those calling for the shutting of the facility, explaining that the prolonged exposure of the structure to the radiation has rendered it prone to a disaster and has compromised it integrity.
