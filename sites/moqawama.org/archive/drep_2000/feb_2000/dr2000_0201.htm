Daily Report

February Tuesday 01-2000:


Introduction

The Islamic Resistance (IR) men continued, for the second day respectively, their brilliant and successful military operations against the "Israeli" occupation troops and their collaborators. In a new outcome of its sacred struggle, the IR slaeid one senior "Israeli" officer and two soldiers and wounded four others, two seriously, in a significant attack deep inside the zone "Israel" occupies in southern Lebanon.

The attack came less than twenty four hours after IR killed the top military and intelligence commander of "Israel's" allied militia Aql Hachem. "Israeli" enemy officials were quoted by the daily Yedioth Ahronot newspaper as saying Hizbullah has been using new and more advanced roadside bombs and expected high-profile resistance operations in South Lebanon.


IR Kills three "Israeli" soldiers and wounds four others(1)
In relation for the "Israeli" Armys going too far in its air and ground aggression against our honored people, asserting on its path of sacred struggle and in spite of the continuous threatens made by the "Israeli" enemy leaders, the Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Mohammad Jaber and Farouq Ismail launched at 11:15 today, a swift attack against the "Israeli" post of Izzeih. As a number of "Israeli" soldiers gathered inside its main fortification, the mujahedeen showered them with their guns and rocket-propelled grenades killing and wounding all the existing soldiers.

At the same time, several squads of the IR targeted the Beaufort Castle and Dabsheh Position. Five minutes later, the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) in the IR bombarded intensely Izzeih position barring the enemy from evacuating its casualties and inflicting the more among its ranks, whereas the smoke columns were seen billowing out of the targeted position.

For its part, AP Agency reported that one "Israeli" officer and two soldiers were killed instantly whereas four soldiers were injured. In this connection, "Israeli"-based western diplomats asserted that five soldiers, not three, were killed.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Adnan Mohammad Hassan and Hussein Abdallah Moghnieh attacked, at 22:10, an enemy engineering force as it was fortifying Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.

Earlier, the IR men spotted, at 1:25, an "Israeli" engineering force as it was beefing up the same compound. Immediately afterwards, the IR squad of martyrs Taha Ahmad Hussein and Hussein Ali Hodroj rushed upon it with the proper weapons chalking up direct and certain hits and barring the force from achieving its goals.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Wehbi and Hassan Shouman targeted, at 8:30, Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades hitting Mag machine gun and some of its ramparts.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters attacked, at 1:30, enemy movements at Dabsheh outpost inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks. Meanwhile, another group of (LRB) fell upon Beaufort Castle with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

However, Amal Movement shelled Taibeh outpost with two 107mm rocket-propelled grenades and Mohaisbat position with four 81mm rounds.


Hizbullah chalks up 3 more "Israeli" troops
Barak threatens end to talks unless Syrians restrain pace of attacks in the south(2)
"Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak declared on Monday that the peace talks with Syria will be suspended unless Damascus halts Hizbullahs attacks on "Israeli" forces in the south.
The stern ultimatum came hours after Hizbullah fighters killed three "Israeli" soldiers, including an officer, and wounded four others, one seriously, in an ambush beside the Izzieh outpost in the central sector.
"Israel" will not be able to negotiate peace as long as the Syrians dont restrain the Hizbullah from acting against the "Israeli" Army in the security zone, Barak said in a statement following an urgent session of his security Cabinet on Monday night.
The absence of a climate of peace will make peace talks with Syria difficult, the statement added.
The fatal ambush came less than 24 hours after Hizbullah guerrillas blew up Aql Hachem, a senior "Israeli" allied militia commander, outside his house in Dibel. Last Tuesday, an "Israeli" soldier was killed by an anti-tank missile at the radar station in Bayada.
Our boys are killed with their (the Syrians) permission. This pattern is unacceptable, said Deputy Defense Minister Ephraim Sneh.
The ministers attending the Cabinet meeting were given a series of retaliatory options by senior military personnel, including the chief of staff, General Shaul Mofaz. Among the options was targeting Lebanons infrastructure in a series of raids similar to the air strikes last June in which power stations and bridges were destroyed.
However, as in similar situations in the past, "Israel's" military options are limited. Hizbullahs ambush fell within the terms of the April Understanding, the loose set of rules that governs the conflict in the south and is designed to protect civilians.
In such circumstances, the option of destroying bridges or power stations in Lebanon is an unacceptable response. Retaliation is theoretically reserved by the "Israelis" for Hizbullahs cross-border Katyusha rocket attacks into northern Galilee.
Barak described the violence in the south as a grave intensification  that "Israel" will not allow to continue. We have known in the past and we still know to strike at the place and time of our choosing.
Baraks bellicose language paraphrased that of his predecessor, Benjamin Netanyahu, who frequently vowed revenge for military setbacks in the occupation zone or cross-border rocket attacks. Yet Netanyahu failed to fulfill his threats until last June when, with only 10 days left in office, he ordered the air raids against infrastructure targets.
The problem that Netanyahu faced in the past in finding an appropriate and justifiable military response is the same facing Barak today: the lack of credible Hizbullah targets.
The secrecy that cloaks Hizbullahs movements presents the "Israeli" Army with few, if any, available targets. This has long been one of the most frustrating elements of the conflict for the "Israelis".
In the absence of the military option, Barak appears to have played the diplomatic card.
A Western observer predicted that Syria would not yield to Baraks ultimatum.
I dont believe hes serious, the observer said. Barak knows that Syria wont go for it, especially as he has made the announcement publicly rather than through private channels.
The observer said that if Barak intends to stand by his ultimatum, then the peace talks are over.
Syrian-"Israeli" peace talks have remained frozen since a second round in the US which ended on Jan. 10 with no tangible results. The third round, scheduled for Jan. 19, was postponed indefinitely.
It is believed that before peace talks restarted between Syria and "Israel" in December, Barak made no serious attempt to press Damascus for quiet in the south as a condition for the talks to resume.
During earlier peace talks between the two sides, Hizbullah continued to attack "Israeli" forces in south Lebanon. "Israel's" seven-day air and artillery blitz in July 1993 came while "Israel" and Syria were still negotiating.
Communications Minister Benjamin bin Eliezer, who is close to Barak and took part in the Cabinet meeting, said he did not think suspending talks would be helpful. I doubt that suspending talks would make much of an impression on the Syrians or bring about changes on the ground in southern Lebanon, he told Channel One television.
The deadly ambush was a significant achievement for Hizbullah, which in the space of a week has reversed several months of declining fortunes.
Observers noted that the ambush occurred in an area not usually prone to resistance assaults. This could explain why the "Israeli" patrol was in an exposed position given that soldiers are now rarely seen in the open in broad daylight. The ambush also suggests that Hizbullah had acquired good intelligence to set up the assault at the opportune moment.
Shortly before the ambush, Hizbullah fighters mounted a concerted effort to overrun the Sojod outpost in the northern sector. The fighters came close to scaling the ramparts, forcing the "Israelis" to drop artillery rounds within 100 meters of the post, security sources said.
Observers believe that Syria has raised no objections to the Hizbullah attacks, in which
the resistance is inflicting mounting casualties against "Israeli" forces, so long as they do not spill over into northern "Israel", thereby jeopardizing the peace talks.


Deadly ambush catches enemy patrol by surprise in safest part of the zone(3)
Mondays deadly ambush staged by Hizbullah guerrillas occurred beside a position that "Israeli" officers long considered one of the safest postings in south Lebanon.
The Izzieh outpost, located on a conical hill above the village of Deir Mimes, is very rarely attacked, even by long-range mortar fire. Traditionally, the posts safety and its proximity to the border with "Israel", 2 kilometers away, made the post a popular viewing point for "Israeli" officials visiting the zone.
From accounts by Hizbullah and local residents as well as the geography of the area, it would appear that the two resistance squads that staged the ambush were hidden in dense undergrowth on a ridge just east of the village of Yohmor overlooking the 200-meter deep Litani River Gorge. The Izzieh outpost lies on the other side of the Litani in clear view of the guerrillas, 1 kilometer away.
The ambush was carefully coordinated. From the footage of the ambush broadcast by Al-Manar television, the "Israeli" patrol was walking near the Izzieh position at 11.15am when Hizbullah unleashed at least three Sagger anti-tank rockets at the soldiers.
Moments later, Hizbullah mortar teams began pounding the Izzieh post, as well as the "Israeli"-manned Beaufort Castle, 1 kilometer north, and the Dabsheh outpost overlooking Nabatieh, 8 kilometers north.
Residents in Yohmor said they heard three anti-tank missiles being fired by Hizbullah and the sound of the explosions seconds later.
The "Israelis" began shelling the area around Yohmor in an effort to hit the resistance squads as they stole away from the scene of the ambush.
Three 155mm shells exploded in a row less than 10 meters from Rabia Allaqs house.
When I heard the shells, I began screaming to my neighbor for help, she said. I thought my house would fall on my head. It was very frightening.
Another resident of Yohmor, Linda Qassem, 26, fainted when she heard the explosions as the ambush was launched. She was admitted to hospital in nearby Nabatieh before being sent home. Ive always been afraid of shelling, she said quietly, this time more than usual.
Several phosphorous rounds were fired at the outskirts of the village and "Israeli" warplanes raided the outskirts of Khorbet Selem and Shoukine opposite the central sector, but no casualties were reported. At least one building was damaged in an air raid on Ain Bousoir in Iqleem al-Toffah.
Allaq said that she rejoiced at the news that three "Israeli" soldiers had been killed.
After all the frightening things that the "Israeli" put us through every day, she said, it was right that the resistance was able to kill some "Israeli" soldiers.
Wajih Barakat, a shopkeeper in the village, said he feared the "Israeli" proxy militiamen occupying neighboring Arnoun would blockade the single road leading out of Yohmor.
They might put up a checkpoint at Arnoun and stop us from leaving, he said.


Qassem called on "Israeli" agents not to follow the footsteps of Hachem(4)
""Israel" has strongly denounced the assassination of its allied militia's second in command. Top "Israeli" officials have vowed revenge.

"Hizbullah's assassination of Aql Hachem was videotaped. The resistance group released a video which showed Hachem chatting with two other people in the backyard of a house. He then leaves his companion and moves to a place where a bomb goes off and he disappears. The Hizbullah resistance roadside bomb took place in the village of Dibel, some 30 kilometers southwest of Marjeyoun, the main town in the zone "Israel" occupies, and a few kilometers north of the "Israeli" border. Hachem who was about fifty, was the second most senior officer in "Israel's" proxy militia after its commander, Antoine Lahd. His name was mentioned as a likely successor to the 71-year-old Lahd. A visibly shaken Lahd described Hachem's killing as quote "an act of cowardice", adding all military operations stem from political decisions. Hizbullah supporters celebrated Hachem's assassination- the most spectacular resistance attack in southern Lebanon since the group killed an "Israeli" occupation officer in a roadside bomb last February, by handing out sweets to passers-by on Beirut and south Lebanon roads. Celebrations were accompanied by a stern warning from Hizbullah's deputy secretary-general Sheikh Nahim Qassem, who called on militiamen not to follow the footsteps of Hachem, whose death Qassem said will have a negative impact "Israel's" administration of the occupied zone. Qassem pledged that resistance attacks will continue unabated regardless to Middle East peace negotiations, until Lebanese territory is liberated from "Israeli" occupation".


The wounded soldiers from Hizbullah attack on Monday(5)
At 12:30pm Monday, an "Israel" Air Force helicopter landed on the heliport of Rambam Hospital in Haifa, bringing the wounded from Mondays Hizbullah attack in southern Lebanon to the trauma center.

One soldier died en route to the hospital. Ron Ben-Shmuel, whose injuries are described as very critical, continues to fight for his life at this time. He sustained head injuries and has undergone emergency neurosurgery. Following the surgery, his condition is listed as very critical and unstable.

Sergeant Rafi Azulai, from Jerusalem, is described in moderate condition with injuries to his eyes. Physicians are working to save his sight, at least in one eye. He sustained shrapnel injuries to his face, hands, legs and jaw.

Private Hillel Porat, from Tel Aviv, sustained shrapnel injuries and does not remember anything after he heard the explosion. He is complaining of hearing difficulties which at this early stage, remains unknown if it will be permanent. He too sustained shrapnel injuries to his ear, legs and hands and is listed in moderate condition.

The fourth injured soldier, Private Guy Shaked, from Ranaana, is also listed in moderate condition, sustained shrapnel injuries over his body.

None of the injured is aware of the deaths of the officer or fellow combatants.

Dr. Tzvi Ben-Yishai, the deputy director of Rambam, reports the wounded, with the exception of Ben-Shmuel, are in moderate and stable condition.

The names of the IDF soldiers, who fell in the line of duty in the security zone of southern Lebanon on Monday morning are;

1. Major Tidhar Templehoff, 23, Netanya

2. Sergeant Lior Niv, 21, Tel Aviv

3. Sergeant Tzahi Malka, 21, Metulla


Resistance picks up praise for elimination of top "Israeli" allied militiaman(6)
Political and religious leaders praised the dramatic increase in resistance victories in the south on Monday, calling Hizbullahs killing of a "Israeli" proxy militia commander an incentive for the collaborators to quit the militia.
Speaker Nabih Berri said the operation that killed a top "Israeli" agents commander, Aql Hachem, on Sunday was one of the highest-level resistance attacks.
He also seized the opportunity to call on collaborators to turn themselves over to Lebanese authorities.
This is a chance for all those Lebanese people to repent and surrender to the state, he said.
Sheikh Mohammed Mehdi Shamseddine, head of the Higher Shiite Council, called on the agents to draw a lesson from Hachems killing  and turn themselves in before its too late.
Shamseddines deputy, Sheikh Abdel-Amir Qabalan, insisted that the resistance operations were the only road to peace, arguing that talks could not lead to just and comprehensive peace.
The operations are the right of the resistance and part of its drive to defend the land, he said.
Zahle MP Shawki Fakhoury played down fears of a large-scale "Israeli" response to Hachems killing, saying the resistance had the right to use all means at its disposal.


Militia weightless after killing of Hachem(7)
The "Israeli" occupation forces buried their agent Aql Hachem as the occupation's proxy militia is living difficult times following the killing of the chief collaborator by the Islamic Resistance men.

Reports from the occupied zone said a state of shock and fear prevailed Sunday and Monday in the ranks of all the occupation's agents in the zone's towns and villages.

Hachem's killing increased the state of deterioration and weakness which the militia has been living for months; this has made the militia's commanders pant to get asylum abroad while leaving the small collaborators to face their fate, the reports said.

The state of fear in the collaborators' ranks made the militiamen close all the crossings that link the occupied zone with the liberated areas "until further notice".

The reports added that the proxy militia has forced the occupied zone's residents to close their shops to show mourning for the killing of agent Aql Hachem. The militiamen intimidated the shop owners to observe the "mourning".


Hoss asks UN chief to stop "Israel's" redrawing of border
Prime minister says activities constitute an aggression on Lebanons sovereignty(8)
Prime Minister Salim Hoss asked United Nations Secretary-General Kofi Annan on Monday to halt "Israel's" reported changing of its border with Lebanon.
Speaking to reporters from his office in the Grand Serail, Hoss said that he sent Annan a message asking him to interfere to stop "Israel's" carving up of Lebanese land and redrawing of borders in the south.
I drew the secretary-generals attention to the dangerous "Israeli" activities that constitute an aggression on the internationally recognized borders and Lebanons sovereignty, said Hoss.
He added that he also asked Annan to distribute copies of the message to the chairman and members of the UN Security Council and see that it receive the designation of an official UN document.
The army and the Defense Ministry, moving on orders from Hoss last week, are still investigating media reports that "Israel" was moving the border markers, which are under its control due to its occupation of the south.
According to a source at the Foreign Ministry, which is headed by Hoss, the message may not reach Annan before Tuesday because Lebanons mission to the UN needs time to obtain an appointment with the UN secretary-general.
