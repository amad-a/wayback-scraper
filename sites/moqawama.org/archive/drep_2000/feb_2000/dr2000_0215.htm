Daily Report

February Tuesday 15-2000:


Introduction

Following the statement of the US Assistant Secretary of state Edward Walker who admitted that last weeks Israeli air aggressions on
Lebanese power stations had violated the April Understanding, the US Ambassador to Lebanon David Satterfield acknowledged that the Israeli attacks on Lebanons power plants breached the April accord.

Meanwhile, the Islamic Resistances (IR) killing of seven Israeli occupation soldiers in the past three weeks in South Lebanon have rekindled public pressure for a quick withdrawal from the south.


Washington wants "strict implementation" of 1996 accord; Satterfield says Israeli attacks violated truce understanding(1)
The US Ambassador to Lebanon David Satterfield said efforts are underway to ensure strict implementation of the 1996 April truce agreement in southern Lebanon. Satterfield who held talks with senior Lebanese officials acknowledged that Israeli attacks on Lebanese power stations last week breached the April accord. House Speaker Nabih Berri told Satterfield Washington did not do enough to prevent last week's Israeli blitz.

The US Ambassador to Lebanon David Satterfield discussed the latest developments in southern Lebanon, which witnessed an upsurge in clashes last week, with President Emile Lahoud. Satterfield and Prime Minister Salim Hoss also discussed a letter the premier sent to Secretary of State Madeleine Albright, outlining Beirut's stance regarding the latest Israeli blitz against the country. Beirut had criticized Washington's justification of the Israeli attacks. Albright last week accused Hizbullah resistance fighters of breaching the April accord which bans attacks on and from civilian targets. But Lebanese officials stressed Hizbullah was acting within the framework of the understanding. (Satterfield saying: "We made clear once more that we are working now to solidify the April understandings to ensure that they are applied by all sides the strictest possible manner. We expressed our concern for the possibility of harm to civilians and civilian infrastructure. I will note that the April understandings are very clear: that there are to be no attacks on civilian infrastructure including power facilities"). House Speaker Nabih Berri informed Satterfield, the Lebanese resistance will continue to wage attacks against Israeli forces in the south until the occupation ceases. Berri reportedly told the US envoy, Washington could have exerted more efforts to avert an Israeli attack on Lebanon's power stations. The House Speaker also called for a resumption of the peace process, and for Israels withdrawal from occupied Syrian and Lebanese territories. Satterfield for his part stressed, Washington is working hard to avoid a repeat of last week's hostilities.


Barak faces mounting government and Knesset calls for early Lebanon pullout(2)
Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak is to face three no-confidence motions in the Knesset sponsored by Arab parliamentary blocs, in protest over Israeli air raids on Lebanon last week. Arab Israeli MPs said the attacks violated international law. Barak is also coming under mounting pressure from his cabinet to pullout occupation forces from Lebanon ahead of a July deadline.

Israeli officials said the Jewish state could decide in two months on a unilateral pullout from occupied south Lebanon. Government officials said Prime Minister Ehud Barak told a cabinet session last night he will open the question of a withdrawal from to full debate in late April or early May. Officials said Barak is sticking by his July target date for the completion of a pullout despite mounting calls for an early withdrawal from the Israeli public. The Hizbullah resistance's killing of seven Israeli occupation soldiers in the past three weeks in southern Lebanon have rekindled public pressure for a quick withdrawal from the south. A poll by the Israeli daily Maariv newspaper found that half of Barak's cabinet also backed an early pullout. But the prime minister called on his ministers to unite ranks, warning them of a quote heavy price still to pay both in southern Lebanon and possibly in Israel over the next few months. Israel last week stepped up its attacks against Lebanon, destroying three power stations and injuring 20 civilians. Barak, who had secured cabinet approval for the blitz, warned ministers that an early pullout will look like a defeat, adding he prefers to withdraw occupation forces as part of an accord with Beirut and Damascus. He has remained vague on what he would do if no accord is hammered. Cabinet minister Haim Ramon expected stalled talks with Syria to resume within weeks despite the escalation of fighting in southern Lebanon. He also claimed the effective resistance attacks against occupation troops were aimed at pressuring Israel to accept Damascus's conditions for a peace deal. Syrian-Israeli peace talks broke down last month after Israel refused to commit to a full withdrawal from the occupied Golan Heights. Beirut is awaiting for progress on the Syrian track before it joins peace-making.


Minister Zaiter: We can live without electricity, but not without dignity(3)
Lebanon can live without electricity, but the Lebanese can never live without dignity and pride, Defense Minister Ghazi Zaiter said.

The US biased position only increases the firmness and strength of the Lebanese in the confrontation against the Israeli occupation until our soil is liberated, Zaiter said in a statement.

The minister underlined that the "Israeli threats and aggressions will not make us give up our attitude in the confrontation against this enemy."

We will always cling to the confrontation on the basis of national unity along with coordination, cooperation, solidarity and unity with the brother state of Syria.


Syria: policy of killing and destruction will have dire consequences on Israel(4)

Syria underlined that the Israeli occupation entity's proceeding with the policy of killing and destruction will not achieve anything whatsoever for "Israel".

This policy will have very dire consequences on "Israel", al-Baath daily said.

The Syrian newspaper said, "What happened in Lebanon affirms that the policy of unjust force and aggression does not solve problems or provide security for "Israel". The only way to achieve this is withdrawal from the Golan Heights and south Lebanon besides putting an end to the policy of threat and aggression."

"Israel" is wrong if it thinks that Syria is in a weak position or that advanced pressures will be useful in negotiations with Syria or affect the Lebanese to go to the negotiating table without Syria or without coordination with Syria, al-Baath concluded.


Israeli air raids on Lebanon a "criminal act": Mubarak(5)
Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak said Israels recent air strikes on Lebanon amounted to a criminal act and stirred Arab hatred of the Jewish state. Mubarak told Egypt's national news agency the world must act immediately to stop Israels occupation of Arab land, its destruction of Lebanese facilities and the killing of civilians. The Egyptian President also called for a halt to fighting in southern Lebanon and the resumption of Mideast peace negotiations. Officials in Cairo last week condemned the massive Israeli raids on power stations and civilian targets.


Israeli Aggressions(6)

The Israeli occupation forces launched the following aggressions:

14:45, four 155mm missiles hit Mazraat Hamra.

15:03, The Israeli war jets raided into Mount Safi between 23:15 and 23:30, Habboush River watercourse was bombarded by fifteen 155mm missiles and a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

23:30, the area of tobacco company at Nabatieh exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

23:35, Kfar Romman town was targeted by a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.


Military operations(7)

The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) fighters attacked, at 23:20, enemy squad at Dabsheh outpost scoring direct hits and inflicting a number of casualties among the enemy ranks.

At the same time, another group of (LRB) rushed upon the posts of Tohorra and Soweida with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct goals.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, at 23:20, two 81mm rounds at Tohorra outpost.
