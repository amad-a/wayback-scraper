Daily Report

February Tuesday 08-2000:


Introduction

The "Israeli" enemy army staged early Tuesday morning an aerial aggression against Lebanon striking power stations of Jamhour in Beirut, Deir Nabouh at North and Baalback in Beqaa causing widespread blackouts. About eighteen Lebanese civilians were injured and admitted to hospital for treatment.

For its part, the IR warned that the "Israeli" settlers should pay the price of the "Israeli" Premier's oppressive policies and that it keeps for the right to retaliate at the suitable time which may be shortly.

However, Lebanon's Prime Minister Salim Al-Hoss asserted that the resistance has never been the cause of violence. The resistance is a result of the occupation.


"Israeli" aircraft attack Lebanon
The Jewish state retaliates as pressure for action mounts on Barak(1)
""Israeli" aircraft knocked out three power stations in Lebanon. Two large bangs were heard across the capital as Beirut plunged into darkness. Three hours earlier the "Israeli" planes destroyed electrical distribution stations in Jamhour. Lebanese Army anti-aircraft batteries reportedly fired at the planes.
Earlier raids on the Baalback power station in the East of the country resulted in at least 16 people being wounded, in eastern Lebanon, Lebanese security sources said.
At least 12 civilians were admitted to hospital for treatment, the sources said.
In the north of Lebanon "Israeli" jets struck a power plant in Deir Nabouh, 13 kilometers north of the port city of Tripoli.
The final count of civilian casualty is not yet known.
The "Israeli" army on Monday evening urged the 300,000 inhabitants of the towns and villages close to the border to take refuge in shelters for fear of missile attacks.
Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss said on Tuesday that "Israeli" air strikes would not end Lebanese determination to end "Israeli" occupation. Hoss made the statement shortly after the "Israeli" jets conducted the air strikes. "If "Israel" is attacking Lebanon to make them accept the occupation then they will be disappointed," Hoss said. He stressed that "Lebanon relies on resolutions of the United Nations which are usually violated by "Israel"."
Earlier, Prime Minister Ehud Barak visited the northern "Israeli" town of Safed, where he met representatives of the local authorities to warn them that people might have to go into the shelters.
Earlier on Monday, Prime Minister Hoss called on the United States to exert pressure on "Israel" to prevent a retaliatory offensive in Lebanon. . Hoss said that the only way of halting the cycle of violence was for "Israel" to withdraw from the south, in accordance with UN Resolution 425.
The resistance has never been the cause (of violence), he said. The resistance is a result of the occupation. Therefore, it is natural for the resistance to continue as long as the occupation persists.
The clamor in "Israel" for a military response grew on Monday after domestic television broadcast graphic footage of casualties being evacuated from the Blat outpost.
Pictures from Hell, screamed the headline in the Maariv newspaper. "The Lebanese valley of the dead penetrated the living room of Mr. "Israel" after 18 years of avoidance" it said, adding that a comparison with television broadcasts during the Vietnam war were unavoidable.
Barak appears to understand that ""Israeli" public opinion will not tolerate the current situation, even if it means hurting the chances for an agreement with Syria" he wrote.


IR: Retaliation is coming(2)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) released today dawn a firm communiqué in which it said: the "Israeli" aggression last night against Lebanon will not shield, at all, the "Israeli" soldiers, positioned at the occupied zone, from the powerful strikes of the (IR) militants. Whatever the "Israeli" reactions over killing or wounding any of the "Israeli" soldiers were, they will be certain targets for the mujahedeen's roadside bombs, ambushes and rockets.

The "Israeli" brutal aggression, however, is flagrant infraction of the April Understanding. While we believe that the "Israeli" settlers, now hiding in their bomb shelters in the northern colonies, should pay the price of their Premier's criminal policies on which they are agreed to, we reserve for ourselves the right to retaliate at the appropriate time which may be very near in future. The "Israeli" savage enemy will recognize well that it will not be able to impose any new condition or equation in the battlefields.

Earlier, the IR announced that the team of martyrs Jamal Chihimi and Ali Mustafa Fadel set off, at 21:00, a big explosive device as an "Israeli" military vehicle was traveling on the road of Qabaa-Dawawir destroying and inflicting all of its occupants.

For its part, Amal Movement shelled between 11:40 and 17:45, the positions of Qssair, Mohaisbat, Baraasheit, Taibeh and Hardoun.


"Israel" evaluates situation in southern Lebanon(3)
"Prime Minister Ehud Barak met with area leaders during the evening, having decided to personally visit the area prior to the start of the "Israeli" offensive. The prime minister flew by helicopter to the Northern Command Headquarters in Safed, where he conferred with the leadership of the frontline communities".

"300,000 residents of northern "Israel" spent the night in bomb shelters and on Tuesday morning, are being ordered to remain in the shelters. Later in the morning, officials of the Northern Command are expected to announce plans for the day. It remains unknown if residents will be permitted to exit the shelters or be ordered to remain inside in anticipation of retaliatory attacks for "Israel's" air strike against power stations in Lebanon early Tuesday.

"It remains unknown if northern area schools and businesses will operate on Tuesday. Military commanders are expected to announce a decision in the coming hours.


Syria warns "Israel" against attacking Lebanon(4)
"Official press in Syria warned "Israel" that any retaliation against Hizbullah for mounting losses in occupied southern Lebanon would be counterproductive, and would only drag "Israel" into the quagmire. The daily Baath said "Israeli" attacks on Lebanon had previously failed to weaken resistance fighters and the strong ties between Damascus and Beirut. The paper warned Prime Minister Ehud Barak that Hizbullah attacks continued after a major bombing campaign launched against Lebanese infrastructure by his predecessor Benjamin Netanyahu last June. Official press called on "Israel" to withdraw from all occupied Lebanese and Syrian territories as part of peace agreements with Beirut and Damascus".


IR announces martyrdom of Freedom Fighter Ahmed al-Ash'hab(5)
"The Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announced the martyrdom of Resistance Man Ahmed Moussa al-Ash'hab (Abu Moussa).

"Martyr al-Ash'hab, 27, married, had a daughter, joined IR in 1998, participated in several specialized military courses, injured several times during confrontations with the "Israeli" occupation forces, wounded during the "Israeli" July 1993 aggression against Lebanon, took part in many operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, obtained medal from Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

He was martyred on Saturday, February 5, 2000 of wounds sustained during the recent operations in the western Beqaa.

"IR supporters took part in the motorcade funeral that set out from the Dar al-Hikme Hospital in Baalback. The procession moved to the martyr's hometown of Sirrien al-Gharbieh.

Representative of Imam Khamenei in Lebanon his Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek who delivered a speech at the funeral said, "The Resistance has avenged its people when it executed agent Aql Hachem and killed the occupation troops in Izzieh, proceeding in the march of Jihad, nonchalant at the growing occupation's might and its sophisticated arsenal."

"Disappointment is chasing this enemy, what happened today (Sunday) in Blat establishes the state of fear and panic that the enemy is living in all its outposts", he said.


Occupation holds intensified meetings, Barak threatens to retaliate(6)
"The "Israeli" occupation's military and security command has intensified its meetings since Sunday evening to discuss the measures to be taken in order to confront the mounting Islamic Resistance's (IR) activity which took the lives of five occupation troops and wounded eleven others within a week, besides killing agent Aql Hachem, who has an "Israeli" nationality, and another agent plus wounding one of the collaborators.

"It was announced that the occupation's prime minister Ehud Barak today chaired a meeting by the enemy's security cabinet.

"The meeting aimed to discuss the means to retaliate for IR's operations.

Barak has threatened in Amman that he would retaliate violently as "the situation has become unbearable".

"The enemy premier denied that had adopted self-restraint towards Hizbullah, but said his decisions are dictated by the need for maintaining the safety of the colonizers in occupied northern Palestine".
