Daily Report

February Wednesday 02-2000:


Introduction

Hizbullah responded with defiance to the "Israeli" threats to revenge the killing of the occupation militia's second-in-command agent Aql Hachem. "The Resistance holds the initiative. It is the master of decision on political, military and field levels, "Hajj Hussein Al-Khalil, Political Assistant to Hizbullah's Secretary General said.

In this regard, "Israeli" enemy Prime Minister Ehud Barak backtracked on an earlier threat to avenge for the deadly ambush in which three "Israeli" soldiers were killed and four others wounded and killing the "Israeli" agent Hachem.

Meanwhile, Lebanon's President Gen. Emile Lahoud responded to Barak's threats, saying that "they do not frighten us", asserting that "the Resistance's youth represent our resolve to liberate the South and Western Beqaa".

However, the Maronite Patriarch came under heavy fire from Christian and Muslim leaders on Tuesday for sending a senior prelate to attend the funeral of Aql Hachem who killed at the hands of Islamic Resistance (IR) men.


Hizbullah affirms that it holds the initiative(1)
Hizbullah responded with defiance on Monday to the "Israeli" threats to revenge the assassination of the occupation militia's second-in-command agent Aql Hachem.

We are not surprised with the "Israeli" threats which have been repeated many times. The battlefield shows who holds the initiative, Hajj Hussein al-Khalil, Political Assistant to Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah told Reuters.

"The Resistance holds the initiative. It is the master of decision on political, military, and field levels".

Al-Khalil said Hachem's killing showed Hizbullah was strong enough to move freely inside the zone occupied by the "Israeli" entity.

"Killing the traitor proved we have infiltrated the militia and we are able to breach all "Israeli" barriers," Khalil said, "Our Youth are moving inside the occupation zone much easier than moving outside the occupation zone."

On the political level it is a painful blow to the Zionist political ambition in Lebanon. Hachem was a major symbol of the Zionist plans in Lebanon, Khalil said.

Khalil said Hachem's death raised fresh doubts about the future of the occupation's proxy militia and said that would put the "Israeli" army in greater danger.

The "Israeli" army used the proxy militia members as sandbags. The collapse of this militia will force the "Israeli" army to back it up or to take on the direct confrontation with the Resistance. In both cases its losses will increase, Khalil said.

He denied any connection between Hachem's assassination and the Syrian-"Israeli" settlement talks.

"It is part and parcel of the Resistance's strategy and program," Khalil said. "Resistance operations have nothing to do with the settlement negotiations in any way. They are closely connected to the battlefield".


"Resistance is the only option, we'll be steadfast regardless of sacrifices," President(2)
President Gen Emile Lahoud responded to the threats made by the "Israeli" occupation's prime minister Ehud Barak and some of his ministers.

We heard ""Israel's"" threats, and they do not frighten us. This is something we have grown used to. It is our estimation that ""Israel"" may strike; but this is the tax that we have to pay to achieve liberation, the president said.

The Resistance's youth represent our resolve to liberate the south and the western Beqaa as they have recovered Lebanon's consideration, enhanced its strength and impunity, and raised high the head of Arabs through their sacrifices and courage besides offering the most precious thing they have, he added.

There is no option other than this one: the clear direction which the Resistance's youth have drawn. As they have proven their superiority in achieving goals, we all need to be steadfast regardless of the sacrifices, Lahoud concluded.


Barak backtracks on threat to suspend talks with Syria(3)
"Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak backed down Tuesday on an earlier threat to suspend peace talks with Syria unless Damascus restrained Hizbullah from attacking "Israeli" forces in the south. But he promised that "Israel" would react strongly to the killing of three soldiers on Monday in a Hizbullah ambush.
We will respond in the time and the way that we choose and we will keep inflicting major blows on Hizbullah, he said at Metulla on the border with Lebanon. I dont think that we should at this juncture relate it in any direct way to the negotiations with Syria.
Hours after the fatal ambush on Monday, Barak delivered a strongly-worded statement in which he said peace talks would not continue unless Syria curbed Hizbullah.
But on Tuesday, the "Israeli" premiers tone had softened considerably, simply noting that Syria could do more to restrain the resistance and expressing his hope that Damascus recognized common interests with "Israel" in keeping the area calm.
Barak toured the border region as a morale-boosting gesture to "Israeli" troops and their allied militiamen. He hugged collaborators commander Antoine Lahd, saying that the leader was a brother in arms.
"Israel" warned that Syria should not allow the situation to deteriorate in south Lebanon if it wished to continue talking peace with "Israel".
One makes a mistake if one thinks that "Israel" will be forced by any way other than talks to do what runs contrary to its interest, "Israeli" Foreign Minister David Levy said after a one-day Middle East peace meeting in Moscow. Whoever plays with fire can also get burned. I hope that wisdom will guide Syria to return to the negotiations.
Syrian-"Israeli" peace talks have remained frozen since a second round in the US which ended on Jan. 10 with no tangible progress made. The third round, scheduled for Jan. 19, was postponed indefinitely.
Peace is achieved when responsible people sit around the table without tension, without a threat, without ultimatums and without violence, Levy said.
Despite calls by the "Israeli" military to respond swiftly and harshly to the deadly ambush and the killing on Sunday of Aql Hachem, a senior Israeli proxy commander, the "Israeli" government has opted for restraint.
The decision has frustrated "Israeli" army chiefs who fear that the militarys power of deterrence will be undermined by turning the other cheek.
I dont think its acceptable that the Syrians permit and encourage Hizbullah to act against the army, "Israel's" chief of staff, General Shaul Mofaz said. I think we have to react with force in a determined and unequivocal manner.
Hizbullah mounted several attacks on Tuesday, eliciting two air raids. A Katyusha rocket attack against the "Israeli"-manned Blat outpost prompted "Israeli" warplanes to drop bombs in two sorties into a wadi between Jabal Botom and Zibqeen. No casualties were reported. The Shear Azour and Kassaret Al-Oroush posts in the northern sector were attacked as was the Mashaaroun position in the central sector.
Hizbullah claimed to have detonated a roadside bomb in Houla beside a Lahdi patrol. 

Meretz and Hadash call for immediate unilateral withdrawal from Lebanon(4)
"The left-wing Meretz Party and the Arab Hadash Party are calling upon Prime Minster Ehud Barak to order an immediate unilateral withdrawal from southern Lebanon to prevent the spilling of more blood. The statement from the parties comes a day following the killing of three Israeli soldiers in the security zone and the wounding of four others, one very critically.

The parties called upon the prime minister not to wait until the July 2000 deadline, explaining that there was not logic to waiting which would result in additional deaths.

"Likud opposition MK Silvan Shalom called upon the prime minister to strike Syrian economic interests in Lebanon in retaliation for the killing of the soldiers on Monday. Shalom stated that a troop withdrawal from Lebanon was the correct step but a unilateral withdrawal would only compromise the security of northern communities and members of the "Israel"-allied Southern Lebanese Army".


"ISRAEL" RESPONSIBLE FOR UPSURE OF FIGHTING IN THE SOUTH: DAMASCUS(5)
"Official press in Damascus today (yesterday) said "Israel's" continued bombing of southern Lebanon made a mockery of its calls for peace. Syria also blamed "Israel" for the escalation of tension in the south. The daily Tishreen said "Israeli" warplanes conducted indiscriminate attacks against Lebanese civilians while "Israeli" and Syrian officials were holding peace talks in the US last month. Official press also stressed that Lebanon's national resistance is fighting on Lebanese land occupied by "Israel". Tishreen called on "Israel" to make the required steps to achieve peace with Beirut and Damascus".


Sfeir distances himself from Hachem eulogy(6)
The Maronite Patriarch came under heavy fire from Christian and Muslim leaders on Tuesday for sending a senior prelate to attend the funeral of Aql Hachem, a senior Israeli collaborators commander who was killed in a Hizbullah bombing.
Cardinal Nasrallah Butros Sfeir, who sought to disassociate himself from the incident, also made a rare trip to Baabda Palace on Tuesday to hold talks with President Emile Lahoud. The reason for the meeting remains unclear.
Government officials and political and religious leaders called on the patriarch to clarify the motives behind sending Tyre Maronite Archbishop Maroun Sader, to attend the funeral service, which was held in the occupied zone on Monday.
Sader presided over the funeral service for Hachem, a Maronite who headed intelligence operations for the "Israeli"-backed SLA. He was killed on Sunday, when a Hizbullah bomb exploded at his farm in Saders hometown of Dibel.
I am here to preside over the services on behalf of Cardinal Sfeir, who asked me to convey his fatherly condolences to the family of the deceased and to express to the residents of the area his feelings of solidarity with them, he said at the ceremony.
Lebanon has suffered for the last 25 years from the problems of the Middle East and has not enjoyed security, stability and peace. I do not want to get into details  but the deceased was a true believer, a good father and a loving human being. His blood purified the soil of this region, Sader added.
According to the head of the Press Federation, Mohammed Baalbaki, who visited the patriarch on Tuesday, Sfeir denied that Sader spoke on his behalf. Baalbaki said he visited Sfeir because it was my duty to clarify this matter.
The cardinal told me that he asked Monsignor Sader to pay condolences in his capacity as the Archbishop of Tyre, Baalbaki said. He did not ask him to eulogize Hachem on his behalf.
According to Baalbaki, Sfeir also said that he has voiced support for the resistance movement several times and does not see the need to reiterate this support.
Sfeir is scheduled to preside over the monthly meeting of the Council of Maronite Bishops on Wednesday and, at its conclusion, the council is expected to comment on the matter.
Information Minister Anwar Khalil said on Tuesday that he had asked Sfeir for the transcript of Saders sermon.
I hope the incident does not carry political implications. But I will not comment further before I receive the transcript, he told Hizbullahs Al-Manar Television. I will also bring up the matter at the Cabinet meeting on Friday.
Leading Shiite cleric Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah lambasted Sfeir, saying: I do not understand why a religious leader sent a representative to the funeral of a collaborator, whose killing was described by "Israel" as a major blow to itself and its proxy militia.
He added in a statement: I also do not understand why the leader praised the collaborator with terms that he never used to praise any martyr of the resistance movement. The religious leader in question should have shared the joy expressed by other Lebanese for the killing of the murderous collaborator.
Fadlallah said that the incident has embarrassed the religious leader and no future justifications can change that.
For his part, Baabda MP Elie Hobeika, a Maronite, said that Saders presence at Hachems funeral infringed on national principles. I hope that the move was a mistake that will soon be corrected, not an expression of a certain viewpoint.
Jean Ghanem, another Maronite Baabda MP and Hobeikas political ally, also denounced the incident, saying it cannot be justified by religious and humanitarian considerations because its political aspect was the most obvious.
Saders comments also angered Sheikh Maher Hammoud, head of the Sidon-based Gathering of Muslim Ulema.
The participation of a representative of Cardinal Sfeir  amounts to a national scandal. It reminds us of the civil war, during which many collaborators enjoyed the backing of their religious leaders, he said.
I ask the patriarch to clarify this tremendous mistake that contradicts the nationwide support for the resistance movement and violates Lebanese law, which stipulates the death penalty for collaborators, he added in a statement.
Sheikh Afif Nabulsi, head of the Committee of Jabal Amel Ulema, a grouping of pro-Hizbullah clerics, said that Hachem, was not a true Christian and did not deserve praise at his funeral.


Military Operations(7)

The IR team of martyrs Eid Sobhi El-Khansa and Ahmad Ali El-Hajj Hassan detonated, at 9:50, a roadside bomb as a Lahdi motorized patrol was traveling on the road linking between Houla and Markaba villages. The mujahedeen managed to inflict several casualties among its ranks.

Also, the IR militants targeted, at 12:00, an enemy engineering force as it was fortifying Shayar Azour position. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct hits.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Wa'il Anani and Habib Sharif Tarhini was rushing upon an "Israeli" motorized patrol near Blat post. The mujahedeen, who used the adequate arms, managed to chalk up direct goals.

Ten minutes later, the IR group of martyrs Haitham Mohammad Faqih and Hassan Aqil was falling upon Mashaaroun compound with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct hits were scored, the militants reported.
