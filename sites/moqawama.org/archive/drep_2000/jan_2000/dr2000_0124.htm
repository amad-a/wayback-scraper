Daily Report

January Monday 24-2000:


One Lebanese civilian wounded
The IR issued a statement in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:

Between 5:15 and 16:20, Mount Sojod was bombarded by thirty two 120mm shells and twenty one 155mm missiles.

7:45, eight 155mm slammed into the perimeters of Yater village.

10:50, the peripheries of Majdal Zoun and Mansouri villages exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

11:00, two 155mm missiles hit Jib Soweid area east of Majdal Zoun.

15:30, the peripheries of Baraasheit town exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets…(1)

For its part The Daily Star reported that one Lebanese civilian was hit by sniper fire from a position manned by the "Israeli" allied militia inside the occupied zone. 40-year-old Mohammad Daher was struck in the leg at Kfar Romman at the edge of the zone. He was treated at a hospital in Nabatieh...(2)


Military Operation…(3)

The IR team of martyrs Youssef Abdallah Saouli and Mustafa Ali Hassan attacked, at 12:00, enemy movements at Sojod outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain hits were scored, the mujahedeen told.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Nimr Nahim and Fadi Melhem targeted, at 17:45, enemy motorized movements near Qabaa position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to chalk up direct and definite goals.

For its part, Amal Movement staged, at 11:05, an attack against Baraasheit compound.

 

Footnotes:
