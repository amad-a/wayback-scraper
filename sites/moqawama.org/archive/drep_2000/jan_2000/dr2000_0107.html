Daily Report

January Friday 07-2000:

Occupation seals off 7 villages, expels resident(1)
"Israeli" occupation forces have expelled Talieh Hassan Abu Rashed, 30, from his hometown of al-Fardees to the liberated areas.


A car belonging to the occupation's militia "security" unit in the town took Abu Rashed to the crossing gate of Zimraya Wednesday afternoon.


The militiamen told him never to return to the occupied zone.


Abu Rashed had been detained for two years in al-Khiam detention center. He was released recently.

In the meantime, the towns of Bint Jbeil, Houla, Ainata, Aita al-Shaab, Aitaroun, Dibbeen, Maroun al-Rass are still being subjected to the most hideous types of harassment and arbitrary measures.


A statement by the National Committee for Developing the Occupied Zone called on the Lebanese government and the international foundations to interfere and press the "Israeli" occupation to lift the siege imposed on the towns, release the detainees and provide residents with food and medicine.


Meis al-Jabal condemn collaborators(2)
Reports from the occupied zone said the collaborators in the town of Meis al-Jabal have perpetrated arbitrary measures against the town residents who issued a statement condemning the occupation's militia.


- Agent Zuheir Shqeir, in coordination with his brother Dr. Suheil Shqeir, has stolen large amounts of medications that were sent by the Lebanese government to the town's dispensary.


Shqeir has also been barring the residents from leaving the town besides ransacking houses and forcing residents to pay coercive payments.


- Collaborator Ezzat al-Ghadban went recently to the Meis al-Jabal High School and insulted the school principal for not giving Ghadban's son a success certificate.


"In the face of the collaborators' arbitrary and criminal acts," the communiqué concluded, "we must speak up and expose these agents before all the Lebanese and the Lebanese government to make sure that they will have no room amongst the town's residents who will never forget their criminal acts."


Maroun al-Rass fears enemy annexation of territories(3)
The administrative committee of the Maroun al-Rass Development Committee (MRDC) condemned the "Israeli" enemy's seizure of Lebanese territories including some belonging to the town of Maroun al-Rass.


Following its meeting Wednesday, the committee said, "Following the repeated Zionist statements on appropriating Lebanese territories, including some of the territories belonging to Maroun al-Rass, MRDC sees it is a duty to confront such Zionist expansionist statements. It also condemns the recent statements made recently by some of the "Israeli" enemy's officials on demarcation of the northern borders of occupied Palestine, which requires, according to the enemy's statements, seizing Lebanese territories, including around 600 square kilometers from Maroun al-Rass".


"It is worth mentioning that the Zionist colony of Avivin had been established on territories belonging to Maroun al-Rass. Therefore, we stress and inform the Lebanese state and the concerned parties that we will never give up one inch of our precious land. We insist on liberating all of our occupied land, and we put ourselves at the service of the Resistance until full liberation is achieved," the communiqué concluded.
