Daily Report

January Monday 17-2000:

IR captured an "Israeli" agent(1)

"On Sunday, the Islamic Resistance (IR) captured an "Israeli" collaborator from inside the occupied zone.
Hizbullahs Al-Manar Television said that while a group of our strugglers were infiltrating Houla in the occupied border zone they managed to capture an "Israeli" allied militiaman.
"The report named the militiaman as Firas Mohammed Fares who was taken to areas outside the border enclave. ­ With agencies


Civilian dies in shelling as Israelis target resistance(2)
"One civilian was killed and another was seriously wounded on Sunday after an "Israeli" outpost mistook the pair for resistance fighters and began shelling the area.
"The attack took place near the abandoned Kfar Houna checkpoint linking the Israeli-occupied zone with the Jezzine area, police said. The Kfar Houna post was abandoned by "Israels" South Lebanon Army militia ally on Thursday.
"The two youths had parked their car in the area and were walking when the shelling began, police said.
"One of the youths was killed and his body was still at the site by nightfall due to the worsening security conditions in the area.
The other youth, who was seriously wounded, was taken to a hospital in the area.
The International Committee of the Red Cross was asked on Sunday night to retrieve the body, police said.
"Police said "Israeli" warplanes later struck the area between Kfar Houna, Sraireh and Rihan with two air-to-ground missiles".
