Daily Report


January Wednesday 26-2000:


Introduction

The Islamic Resistance (IR) scored yesterday a qualitative victory when the mujahedeen staged a series of military operations against a lot of enemy positions in the occupied zone. The IR successful attacks resulted in killing one "Israeli" soldier, wounding three collaborators and capturing two of them.

Meanwhile, the "Israeli" war jets raided into the perimeters of Jibaa town dropping several bombs on the building located 100 meters from an army checkpoint. Two women who live in vicinity were injured and moved to the hospital for treatment.


ISRAELI SOLDIER KILLED AND THREE MILITIAMEN INJURES IN RESISTANCE ATTACKS
"An Israeli occupation soldier was killed in a hail of shells fired at an Israeli position in southern Lebanon by the Hizbullah resistance movement. Three Israeli allied militiamen were also injured in resistance attacks on occupation outposts in the south.

"Fighting flared in southern Lebanon today (yesterday). Hizbullah resistance fighters attacked the Israeli occupation post of Radar with some sixteen shells, killing one Israeli soldier. It was the first death of an Israeli soldier in occupied southern Lebanon this year and the first since before peace talks resumed on December 15 between "Israel" and Syria. Earlier, Israeli allied militia confirmed that three of its fighters were wounded in a Hizbullah attack on its position of Baraasheit, in the central sector of the occupied zone. It was confirmed by Hizbullah, which said it also attacked the neighboring position of Haddatha, causing casualties. Hizbullah said the commander of the Baraasheit post, Kamal Jaafar, was seriously wounded. Yesterday, two militiamen were wounded during a resistance attack inside the zone"…(1)

For its part, the IR announced that the team of martyrs Mohammad Abbas Zahwi and Said Assaad Mossawi attacked, at 6:30, the northern fortification of Baraasheit compound, whereas the IR squad of martyrs Hani Mohammad Hijazi and Rashid Ali Moselmani targeted from close range its eastern rampart with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Hussein Youssef Baz and Jihad Ali Sayyed rushed upon the same compound with the adequate weapons scoring direct hits where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Jaafar Abdel-Hassan Alameh and Mohammad Abdel-Monhem Monhem fell upon Haddatha compound with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the militants told.

In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Mohammad Mortada and Hassan Ibrahim Qassem rushed upon a Lahdi force as it was trying to back up Haddatha compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Ibrahim Hassan and Hassan Ahmad Saad attacked, at 11:50, the "Israeli" post of Radar with the rocket-propelled grenades inflicting a number of casualties among its members and destroying its main fortification, where the soldiers' screamings were heard obviously.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Ali Hassan El-Ammar and Ahmad Mohammad El-Boun rushed upon the same post with the appropriate arms scoring direct goals, whereas the IR unit of martyrs Mahmoud Khaled Al-Merhbi and Hussein Riad Ballout fell upon Bayada post with the suitable weapons. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct hits.

In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Mustafa Al-Jouni and Hassan Kamel Al-Zein targeted Hardoun outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring definite hits.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Jamil Chahla and Fadi Hemadeh rushed upon Qssair position with the rocket-propelled grenades. Direct and certain goals were chalked up, the mujahedeen reported.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Jihad Shbib and Issa Qtaish attacked, at 12:40, an "Israeli" force as it was fortifying Taibeh position with the rocket-propelled grenades where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

For its part, a group of the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 20:00, Haddatha compound with the adequate arms scoring direct and certain hits.

Meanwhile, another group of (LRB) was targeting Hemayed position with the proper weapons chalking up definite goals…(2)


"Israeli" aggressions wounded two civilians:
"The "Israeli" jets destroyed an empty three-floor building south of Jibaa in Iqleem al-Toffah.
"The jets dropped several bombs on the building located 100 meters from an army checkpoint in the area, flattening it. Few dared to approach the building as "Israeli" jets continued to fly at a low altitude over the area.
"Two women who live in the vicinity were lightly wounded. Maymana Safa, 67, and Khadija Haidar,

23, were treated at the South Hospital in Nabatieh"…(3)
"The "Israeli" shelling on Baraasheit early today (yesterday) damaged six houses, in clear violation of a 1996 internationally-monitored cease-fire agreement, which bans attacks on and from civilian areas. Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak has pledged to withdraw occupation forces from southern Lebanon by July, but he has remained vague on whether he would order a pull out if a peace agreement with Beirut and Damascus is not reached. Lebanon has been demanding the implementation of the 1978 UN Security Resolution 425, calling for an immediate and unconditional Israeli withdrawal from the south. Late this afternoon, security sources in the south said Israeli forces have expelled two families from the occupied zone. "Israel" has been coming under mounting international criticism for its systematic expulsion of Lebanese civilians from the occupied zone"…(4)

For its part, the IR released a communiqué in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:

00:45, three 155mm missiles fell on Salhani area and two 160mm missiles on the perimeters of Yater village.

Between 6:40 and 13:55, Baraasheit town and its peripheries came under several barrages of 12.7mm heavy machine and Lancher gun bullets causing damage to the houses of Ali Fouad Khalaf, Hussein El-Hadi, Ibrahim Chehab, Salman Jaghbir, Ali El-Hadi and Mohammd Shammout.

6:45, the area between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun towns was bombarded by fifteen 120mm shells.

Between 12:15 and 18:10, Mount Sojod was shelled by six 155mm missiles.

12:50, ten 155mm missiles slammed into Qaissieh Valley.

15:55, the "Israeli" warplanes raided into the perimeters of Jibaa town destroying a three-storey building about 100 meters only from a Lebanese Army checkpoint. The "Israeli" sortie inflicted the civilians Maimana Safa, 50, and Khadijeh Haidar 30.

19:00, several 81mm flare shells were fired over Sojod compound.

19:30, ten 120mm shells landed Dallafeh area…(5)


"ISRAEL" GETTING READY TO ABANDON SOJOD FORTIFICATION: SOURCES…(6)
"Security sources in the south were quoted as saying "Israeli" occupation troops and their allied militia are getting ready to vacate the Sojod outpost in the central sector of the occupied zone. Sources said "Israeli" allied militiamen have been refusing to man the position, which has become a target of regular resistance attacks. Sources added, all attempts to secure the outpost against resistance operations have failed. They expected troops to abandon the fortification in the next few days. Occupation forces earlier this month withdrew from the Kfar Houna post on the edge of the Jezzine panhandle, after militiamen refused to man the fortification, and following a series of successful resistance attacks".


"Israeli" detained six citizens ... (7)
"The "Israeli" forces seized six civilians from the village of Naqoura and detained them at the Khiam prison. Witnesses said Israelis imposed a siege on the village, where UNIFIL headquarters are located, and banned people from leaving or entering.
"UNIFIL is still trying to secure the release of a Lebanese cook at the peacekeepers’ headquarters who was arrested by the collaborators over the weekend.
"The agency’s liaison branch is holding “high level contacts” to free Hussein Atwi, 36, who was grabbed from his home in Naqoura and taken to Khiam on Saturday.
Atwi is one of 150 Lebanese employed by the peacekeepers.

The "Israeli" agents deported four-member family…(8)  
- A Lebanese family was deported from the Israeli-occupied zone by collaborators through the Bater-Jezzine crossing on Tuesday.
Wissam Zalloum Joudieh and her three children, aged 2, 8 and 11, were evicted from their home in the town of Mari in Hasbaya.
"All were said to have been deported in reprisal for the escape of Zalloum’s husband, Wassim Joudieh, to Lebanese-held territory five months ago".
