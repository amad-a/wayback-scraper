Hizbullah warns against sectarianism: US pressure aims to weaken us…(1)

”Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem warned against "the temporary glamor of sectarianism and the tools of destruction under the headings of achieving rights and gains."

"It is also wrong to adopt positions without making calculations, for one cannot take standings without noting the services that the “Israeli” enemy will get from them," Sheikh Qassem said at the anniversary of the martyrs of the Arki town.

In the massive rally organized by the Islamic Resistance on the occasion, Sheikh Qassem said, "There is difference between the faith in which we believe as guide and instructor on the one hand, and sectarianism which is used by some as barricades to achieve gains and satisfy tribe spirit, on the other."

"Sectarianism brings down the homeland, whereas having relations with Allah builds the country. Sectarianism may build temporary apparent strength for those who use it as a barricade, but carries within itself all the essentials of enmity, destruction, treason, unrest and humiliation."

The deputy secretary-general asked, "What do the US and '“Israel”' want? And why are they exerting pressures on Lebanon, once in the name of the donor states and at other time demanding Lebanese army deployment in the south to protect '“Israel’s”' security, and other times through attempts to create internal confusion and unrest or unreal meaningless battles as far as timing, content and reasons (of these battles) are concerned."

"The US wants to take from us our strength and deny us the means of victory and defense. It wants to bring down Lebanon's potentials to demand its rights and to argue as an equal opponent in the face of its enemies".

"Lebanon is strong by Syria and Syria is strong by Lebanon. If they separate at this stage, they will both be weakened," he concluded.

French ambassador wants all Lebanese hostages released…(2)

French ambassador to Lebanon Philippe Le Courtier called on the “Israeli” entity to set free all the Lebanese hostages held in “Israeli” prisons.

Le Courtier said Paris is playing a role in this regard.

The envoy made the statement during a tour in the southern town of Nabatieh were he inspected the municipality HQ, a commercial fair, the province's serail, and a French school in neighboring Habboush.

Amnesty wants Khiam jailers to ber punished fairly…(3)

An Amnesty International delegation to South Lebanon said on Tuesday that former “Israeli”-allied militiamen who workd in the notorious Khiam detention center should not be pardoned but should receive fair trials.
The delegation’s report on human rights in the South was completed following its visit to the region after the “Israeli” withdrawal on May 24.
The human-rights group said militiamen who worked in Khiam should be punished for violent acts perpetrated against detainees. Former detainees testified that they were victims of psychological and physical torture during their stay in the notorious facility.

Lebanese man files lawsuit against “Israel” for killing his daughter…(4)

“A Lebanese man has filed a lawsuit against “Israel” after his daughter was killed when the “Israeli” army opened fire on civilians during its May pullout from southern Lebanon. Services were held for thirty-one year old Raymonda Rizk in her hometown Marjayoun in the south late yesterday. Her body was repatriated from “Israel” by the International Committee of the Red Cross. Raymonda's entire family had retreated with “Israeli” troops when they ended their 22-year occupation of south Lebanon. Her father, Raymond, was a former driver with “Israel’s” now defunct proxy militia. He filed the lawsuit against the “Israeli” army in the Jewish state. The entire family accompanied Raymonda's body back to Lebanon. She was treated in a Haifa hospital where she died. Raymonda's husband, who also fought alongside “Israeli” troops in south Lebanon, has been seeking political asylum in Switzerland. Hundreds of militiamen and their families have been returning to Lebanon, complaining of discrimination and poor living conditions in the Jewish state. Some six thousand fled to “Israel” with occupation troops. Beirut has urged the militiamen to return and face fair trials. Lebanese who collaborated with “Israel” and fought alongside occupation troops have been receiving sentences ranging from a few weeks in prison to the death sentence in absentia, which could be appealed.
