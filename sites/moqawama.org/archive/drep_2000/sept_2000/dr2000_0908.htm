“Israel” violates Lebanon's frontier at Abbad hill...(1)

““Israel” has again violated Lebanon's border at Abbad Hill. Residents in southern Lebanon said “Israeli” troops have crossed into Lebanon and encroached on thousands of square meters of Lebanese land along the frontier. 

Residents of the south Lebanon border village of Houla have accused “Israeli” troops of encroaching on some four thousand square meters of Lebanese territory at Abbad Hill. They said “Israel” built a new road in Abbad next to the Manara settlement just inside Lebanon. Troops crossed over the so-called Blue Line that was demarcated by the UN to certify “Israel’s” pullout from Lebanon. Residents accused UN peacekeepers of not stopping the encroachment. In July, UN peacekeepers deployed in the area and throughout the zone “Israel” occupied for twenty-two years. Some one thousand army troops and Internal Security Forces have also been stationed in the region. Ghanaian soldiers were deployed in the Abbad Hill flashpoint, where “Israeli” troops regularly cross into Lebanon and set up posts in clear violation of UN resolutions. Both Lebanon and “Israel” claim a shrine at Sheikh Abbad Hill is a holy site. Lebanon says it is the grave of a sheikh while “Israel” maintains a rabbi is buried there. The territory was divided between the two countries when “Israel” withdrew from south Lebanon in May. Most of it is now inside Lebanon. UN troops keep a presence there in a bid to keep the peace. The area has become the scene of regular standoffs between “Israeli” soldiers and Lebanese civilians. It took UN experts weeks to verify “Israel’s” withdrawal to the Blue Line. But “Israeli” troops on many occasions cross the frontier undeterred by the international force. 


Lebanese military delegation tours border areas...(2)

The first time since the retreat of the Zionist enemy from south Lebanon and the Western Beqaa, a Lebanese military delegation on Thursday toured a number of border spots.

The delegation, chaired by Gen Emad Anqa, noted some remarks pertaining to “Israeli” encroachments into Lebanese territories, such as “Israeli” bulldozers dumping rubble behind the Blue Line.

The army officers registered notes in the vicinity of the Abbad shrine.


Tribunal sentences 26th collaborators group...(3)

The Military Tribunal issued prison sentences on Thursday ranging from three weeks to two years against 10 former “Israeli”-proxy harges of collaboration with “Israel”.
Among those tried were Ali Rida and Youssef Hashem, who were sentenced in absentia to 15 years of hard labor. Defendants were also fined sums ranging from LL400,000 to LL1 million.
Hashem is the brother of the Aql Hashem, the militia’s mander who was killed by Hizbullah on Jan. 30.
The court acquitted Diana Nasser, who worked for the militia’s radio station, on charges of entering “Israel”.
This was the 26th batch of collaborators to appear before the court.
