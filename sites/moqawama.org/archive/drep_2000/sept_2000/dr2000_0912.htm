Hizbullah: It is the program of the cabinet that counts…(1)

Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah underlined that the program of the upcoming government is more important to the party than the person of its head.

"The question of the next government is not that of a person; it's that of a team," Sayyed Nasrallah said during his first visit in eight years to his home village of Bazourieh, near Tyre.

"It's not a matter of this or that person inspiring confidence … it's a question of the government's plan regarding domestic and regional policies," he said. "They usually produce ministerial statements in the form of slogans. But programs must be presented to the people."

The rally in Bazourieh's soccer field was held to commemorate the third anniversary of the martyrdom of Sayyed Nasrallah's 19-year-old son Hadi, his eldest, and Haitham Moghnieh and Ali Kawtharani, two Hizbullah resistance men, in a clash with “Israeli” occupation troops near the Sojod outpost above Arab Salim in the northern sector of the former occupation zone.

Addressing thousands of Hizbullah supporters, Amal officials and local Muslim and Christian clerics, Sayyed Nasrallah also said Lebanon must work on regaining the Shibaa Farms and securing the release of Lebanese detainees held in the “Israeli” entity.

The Shibaa Farms, which “Israel” says lie inside Syria, were captured by the occupation's entity during the 1967 war.

Lebanon also demands the return of 20 detainees held by “Israel”, some of whom have been held without trial for more than two decades.

"Will the new government send the army to police “Israel’s” border or will it maintain the army's role as defender of national security?" Sayyed Nasrallah asked. "The new government should make clear its program regarding Lebanon's ties with Syria and US plots for the Middle East," he added.

 

Iran to begin delivering generators to Lebanon next week…(2)

“ Iran will next week deliver the first of two mobile generators built specially for Lebanon after “Israeli” warplanes bombed its power stations, an Iranian official said Monday.

The second of the generators, which cost a total of nearly two million dollars to construct, will be delivered in October, said the official, cited by the state IRNA news agency.

Iran, Egypt and a number of other Arab and European countries have sent technicians, equipment and financial aid to help Lebanon repair its power grid after “Israeli” air-raids in February and the previous June.

 

Hoss packs his bags for UN…(3)

Prime Minister Salim Hoss leaves for New York on Tuesday to head Lebanon’s delegation to the 55th session of the UN General Assembly.
He is due to address the General Assembly on Thursday in a speech which is expected to focus on Lebanon’s reservations regarding a number of border points set up in the wake of the “Israeli” withdrawal from south Lebanon.
The premier is also expected to raise Lebanon’s demand to restore its sovereignty over the Shebaa Farms, a point contested by “Israel”.
Other key points include Lebanon’s refusal to resettle Palestinian refugees on its territory and its call on the international community to recognize the refugees’ right to return to their homeland.
Hoss is also expected to urge the international community to exert pressure on “Israel” to release all Lebanese detainees still held in its jails.
The prime minister’s talks with other leaders on the sidelines of the General Assembly session are also expected to cover the need to convene a promised conference of donor countries in Beirut during the first week of October.
