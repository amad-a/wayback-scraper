Introduction

Nasrallah: no time for sectarian rhetoric…(1)

“Israel” shouldn’t feel secure

Hizbullah’s secretary-general urged some leaders on Monday to refrain from sectarian slogans in their calls for pardons for Samir Geagea and Michel Aoun.
Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said the two men’s cases should be decided by the judiciary.
“There is a judicial aspect regarding the return of General Aoun and the freeing of Geagea,” he told a delegation from the Journalists’ Union led by the group’s president, Melhem Karam. “Politicians cannot deal with this. They should deal with the political aspect only.”
“Even on the political level,” he said, “the two issues are being handled in an unhealthy atmosphere that does not pave the way for results aspired for by some leaders.”
Geagea, former leader of the banned Lebanese Forces is serving four life sentences for masterminding the assassination of three rival leaders and planning to kill a fourth.
Aoun, a former army commander, has been living in exile in Paris since 1991, a year after his ousting by a joint Lebanese-Syrian military operation.
The judiciary has also investigated alleged embezzlement of public funds by Aoun, but no legal action has been taken against him.
“Those who advocate the release of Geagea and the return of Aoun say things that shouldn’t be said, like hostile words against Syria. These attitudes threaten Lebanon,” Nasrallah said. “They’re also hostile to the resistance movement and support “Israel’s” call for the army’s deployment in the South or for the disarmament of resistance fighters.”
Nasrallah denied his group’s responsibility for stone-throwing incidents at “Israeli” soldiers across the Fatima Gate on the international border in the South. “Stone-throwers are not instigated by anyone. Can anybody say that all stone-throwers are supporters of Hizbullah? The incidents are an expression of people’s feelings and a form of objection to the “Israelis”,” he said.
“The incidents sum up the meanings of Lebanon’s victory and the enemy’s defeat,” he added. “Hizbullah encourages these incidents, which we consider to be cultural, not political. Stone-throwing will not liberate the Shebaa Farms, rectify the Blue Line or free Lebanese detainees in “Israel”. But it carries a moral and cultural value.”
Lebanon has reservations concerning “Israel’s” continued military presence in the Shebaa Farms. Beirut also argues that the Blue Line, drawn by the United Nations to verify “Israel’s” pullout, does not coincide with the international border.
Lebanon also calls for the release of 19 Lebanese held in “Israel” as bargaining chips in exchange for the fate of several “Israeli” soldiers who went missing in action in Lebanon.
“If the army deploys to the border, it’ll be protecting “Israel”, not Lebanon,” said Nasrallah. “It’s not in Lebanon’s interest that “Israel” feel secure as long as it continues to threaten our country, occupy parts of it and hold detainees.”
Regarding a decision by donor countries to postpone a conference aimed at raising funds for the government’s plan to rebuild the South, the Hizbullah leader said: “Since the beginning, holding the conference was dependent on subjecting Lebanon to “Israel’s” conditions, including the disarmament of the resistance movement and the deployment of the army.”
The conference was postponed until a new government is formed after the new Parliament convenes on Oct. 17.
Nasrallah said that discussing the formation of a new government “should not be restricted to who will head it. I don’t say this to support or oppose any potential premier or because I want a share for Hizbullah. I say it because Lebanon needs to be saved from several threats, including those posed by “Israel” or those that will be posed if the situation in the South goes to explosive extremes.
“The new government should deal with the economic crisis and the political situation resulting from the elections. They could’ve threatened the situation if security were not under control or if the Syrian troops withdrew,” he said.
“The new government should have a program. It should not give false hope to the people,” he warned. “Hizbullah’s confidence is precious and will not be given to any government if we’re not satisfied with its goals. If the premier-to-be says: ‘I am the program and the government,’ we will not accept that.”

Sheikh Qassem: confrontation with occupation not over yet

"The confrontation with the Zionist enemy is not over," Hizbullah Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Naem Qassem said, citing the occupation of the Shebaa Farms and three other Lebanese areas, besides the Lebanese detainees in “Israeli” prisons and the complications of the Palestinian cause.

Speaking at a rally to commemorate the martyrs of the Qabriekha Town, Sheikh Qassem said, "“Israel” wants five things from Lebanon:

-To hold its tongue regarding the Shibaa farms and the three other occupied spots,
-To forget the issue of the Lebanese detainees and prisoners in “Israeli” prisons,
-To disarm the Resistance so that "“Israel”" feel relieved of a force that poses a threat against it,
-The Lebanese army has to be completely in charge of border security so that it preserves and be responsible for “Israeli” security,
-To weaken the Syrian presence in Lebanon in order to undermine Damascus' ability to recover the Golan Heights and support Lebanon and the Palestinian cause…(2)

Elsewhere, Qassem, said “those who rely on donor countries rely on a mirage.
“The United States is used to making promises and pledging financial aid then procrastinating for purposes of political blackmail,” he said.
Speaking during a ceremony held at a school in Nabatieh to honor its excellent students, he warned that Washington “plans to take from Lebanon the maximum it can give, and give it back the minimum it can.”
The United States “promised to give Lebanon financial aid once the Western hostage crisis was over, but the promises were not kept after they were freed … It should not be allowed to achieve for “Israel” what “Israel” failed to achieve in war”…(3)

MP claims further violations…(4)

“Lebanon claimed Monday that up to 60 square kilometers of Lebanese territory have been annexed by the “Israeli” Army along the border between Shebaa and Kfar Shuba.
The “Israelis” were also accused of violating the UN-delineated Blue Line in the area and constructing a military defense network on Lebanese soil.
Information Minister Anwar Khalil said that the government had learned the “Israelis” have encroached upon an area between 1 and 3 kilometers deep and 20 kilometers long. “They’re working on redrawing the border without referring to the international authorities,” Khalil claimed in a speech in Hasbaya.
““Israel” is working on an imaginary line completely different from the UN’s Blue line and is turning this imaginary line into what looks like a new defense plan through building reinforcements and military positions,” added Khalil, who is an MP for the qada of Marjayoun-Hasbaya, which incorporates Shebaa and Kfar Shuba.
“This new aggression should be immediately dealt with by the United Nations without any delay. We’ll raise this issue at the highest levels, both locally and internationally,” he said.
Khalil could not be reached for additional comment.
The disputed stretch of border claimed by Khalil appears to be part of the Shebaa Farms occupied by “Israel” since the 1967 invasion of the Golan Heights.
The residents of Kfar Shuba also claim tracts of land under “Israeli” control opposite their village. Residents of both Shebaa and Kfar Shuba entered the occupied area on Sunday for the first time since 1967 and held a demonstration.
Prime Minister Salim Hoss chaired a meeting on Monday to prepare a petition against “Israel” that will be sent to the International Court of Justice in The Hague. Lebanon wants the court to make “Israel” pay reparations for damages sustained during the occupation.

Lebanese PM blames US for delay in donors conference…(6)

“ The United States, seeking to punish Lebanon, is responsible for the indefinite delay in a donors conference for the zone formerly occupied by “Israel”, Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss said in remarks published Monday.

The prime minister, returning from New York on Sunday, had announced that the south Lebanon donors conference, scheduled for October 9, had been delayed indefinitely, without giving reasons.

"The postponement in the conference is American punishment against Lebanon because of its national positions concerning the liberation of the south from the “Israeli” occupiers," Hoss told the London-based Arabic newspaper Asharq al-Awsat.

Asked if a June telephone conversation between President Emile Lahoud and US Secretary of State Madeleine Albright was the cause of the delay, Hoss said: "You've put your finger on it."

Lahoud said after the conversation in June that he had rejected Albright's urging that the Lebanese army deploy up to the Lebanese-”Israeli” border following the end of the “Israeli” army's 22-year occupation.

Lahoud voiced defiance after the announcement that the conference had been postponed, saying Lebanon "will not accept that any condition be imposed for coming to its help and will not make any concession at the expense of its dignity and its sovereignty."

Hoss told Asharq al-Awsat that US-Lebanese tensions were damaged anew last week, when Albright refused to receive him on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly.

A preliminary donors meeting was held July 27, with 38 ambassadors stationed in Beirut and representatives from 10 international finance groups.

Hoss has said Lebanon needs 1.3 billion dollars to develop the former “Israeli” zone, 260 million of it urgently.

Fresh “Israeli” violations of Lebanese airspace…(7)

The warplanes of the “Israeli” occupation forces have once again violated the Lebanese airspace and sovereignty.

The enemy's aircraft at 11:10 a.m. Monday flew over the town of Yaroun towards Lebanese territories.

Earlier, an “Israeli” training plane violated the Lebanese airspace at 9:45 a.m. hovering over the towns of Malkieh and Salhieh.

On the other hand, the UN Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) troops set up nets on the barricades of al-Abbad post to bar Lebanese citizens from reaching it and getting close to the troops of the Zionist enemy.

Houneen people celebrate partial liberation…(8)

People of the occupied seven villages celebrated the liberation of a part of the Houneen Town.  They gathered at noon on Sunday on the liberated part of the village to demand the complete liberation of the town along with the six others that are still under “Israeli” occupation.

MP Mohammed Berjawi delivered a speech at the rally on behalf of the Houneen people.

Amal MP Ali Khalil underlined that the alliance between Hizbullah and Amal is the fruit of the will and sincerity of the people.

For his part, President of Hizbullah's Political Council MP Mohammed Raad stressed that "the Resistance alone can re-delineate the border correctly."

Raad condemned the demarcation that took place previously in a random and demagogic manner.

All speakers agreed that people of Houneen and the other six villages, who currently have Palestinian refugee cards, must be given Lebanese nationality.
