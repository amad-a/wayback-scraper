September Monday 25-2000:

“Israelis” fired gunshots over Lebanese heads…(1)
An “Israeli” soldier fired gunshots over the heads of a Lebanese woman and her toddler Sunday as they descended from the Lebanese side of the Sheikh Abbad Hill in the South.
Khadijeh Qanso Wazna, 23, and her 2-year-old son were leaving the hill when seven shots were fired above their heads.
Reporters on the scene said an “Israeli” soldier fired the shots.
Elsewhere in the South, “Israeli” soldiers fired warning shots into the air Sunday to disperse a convoy of 50 Lebanese cars protesting Israeli occupation of the Shebaa Farms, an “Israeli” military spokesman said.
The convoy entered an area in the farms annexed by Israel during its occupation and that was returned to Lebanese jurisdiction after the “Israeli” withdrawal in May. The group entered the region through a gate in the old “Israeli” security fence near Kfar Shouba but was forced to turn back after warning shots were fired from an “Israeli” barricade.
Two weeks ago, 200 Lebanese crossed the line to demand the return of the farms to Lebanese sovereignty.
Also on Sunday, “Israeli” soldiers reinforced an observation post at the Fatma Gate to protect themselves from rocks and bottles being thrown from the Lebanese side.
”Israel” erected a 4-meter iron fence at the Fatma Gate border post in front of three watch stations that were fortified with reinforced concrete.
The soldiers have fired back against Lebanese who hurl stones at them several times, wounding about 15 people over the four months.
In the past week, “Israelis” have also started to throw stones. A Lebanese woman sustained a minor injury on Saturday after she was reportedly hit on the head by a stone hurled by an “Israeli” soldier.
