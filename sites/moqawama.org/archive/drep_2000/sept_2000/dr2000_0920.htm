Introduction

Hizbullah’s Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah reiterated the party’s position that the resistance will continue as long as “Israel” occupies Lebanese territory like the Shebaa Farms and continues to hold Lebanese hostages.

In the meantime, the mayor of Nabatieh Fawqa Hussein Berjawi who scuffled with the “Israeli” soldiers was praised by President Emile Lahoud for his “exemplary national stand”. He quoted the president as saying that “Israel” would never consider to re-occupy our land as long as there are people like you”.

However, hundreds of Lebanese who fled into “Israel” after it ended its 22-year occupation of South Lebanon in May are soon to return home for feeling unhappy, isolated, homesick and idle.

 

Nasrallah warns against sectarianism

Resistance will continue as long as Israel occupies territories and hols hostages…(1)

“Hizbullah’s secretary-general warned against the dangers of further fueling sectarianism in on Tuesday, noting how quickly the Lebanese have forgotten them in the past.
“We not only forget but also lie to ourselves, repeatedly saying that we suffered from the war of others on our land” Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said. “Why do we all of a sudden want to move backward?”
The Hizbullah leader was addressing hundreds of women at UNESCO Palace who were celebrating the birthday of Fatima Al-Zahra, the daughter of the Prophet Mohammed, which is also the day Iranian Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini deemed Women’s Day.
Nasrallah said the country was in dire need of people ready to work for the benefit of their fellow citizens, whether Muslim or Christian.
“The fight for a stronger national identity is no less dangerous than the resistance struggle against the enemy,” he declared. “It’s even harder.”
He also criticized claims that the resistance no longer has a role in the wake of the “Israeli” withdrawal in May.
“Do you want to tell me that if “Israeli” soldiers attacked, there would be an order for the Lebanese Army to respond?” he asked. “No. Having the army at the border is only for the protection of the enemy.”
Nasrallah reiterated the party’s position that the resistance will continue as long as “Israel” occupies “Lebanese territory” like the Shebaa Farms and continues to hold Lebanese detainees.
“I also tell you that the detainees will not be freed through negotiations,” he said. “The enemy has no understanding of humanity.”

 

Mukhtar praised for Abbad scuffle…(2)

“A mukhtar from Nabatieh Fawqa who was visiting Sheikh Abbad hill and scuffled with “Israeli” soldiers was praised on Tuesday by President Emile Lahoud for his “exemplary national stand.”
Hussein Berjawi, 42, the mukhtar of Midan, a district of Nabatieh Fawqa, visited Sheikh Abbad Hill on Tuesday with his family to look at his original village of Hounine, one of the Seven Villages now located inside “Israel”.
Berjawi said that he was pointing out houses in Margaliot, an “Israeli” settlement located on the eastern slope of Abbad hill.
One of the soldiers cursed Berjawi and told him to return to the other side of the line. Berjawi refused and the soldier hit him with the butt of his rifle. According to the mukhtar, four other “Israeli” soldiers stepped in and pointed their rifles at him as he grappled with the first soldier.
Ghanaian UNIFIL soldiers then intervened and broke up the scuffle, asking the “Israeli” soldiers to back off.
Berjawi, who owns a lingerie shop in Nabatieh, said that Lahoud had congratulated him for his “brave stand.” He quoted the president as saying that ““Israel” would never consider to re-occupy our land as long as there are people like you.”
The telephone call from Lahoud to Berjawi was confirmed by the presidential palace. In turn, Berjawi heaped praise on Lahoud.
“It’s not unusual that the president of this country is so concerned with his people, especially the southerners, having stood by them and helped them defeat their enemies and liberate their land during his term,” Berjawi said.

”Israeli” Army: Hezbollah may renew hostilities against “Israel”…(3)

”Military Intelligence chief Major General Amos Malka warned yesterday that Hezbollah may resume hostilities against “Israel”, including attacks and attempts to kidnap “Israeli” soldiers and civilians along the Lebanese border.
Speaking at a session of the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense (War) Committee, Malka said the organization was disappointed by the results of the recent Lebanese elections, in which the position of President Emile Lahoud was weakened. He said Hezbollah officials had begun speaking publicly of renewing operations against “Israel”.
"We are witness to worsening rhetoric against “Israel” from Hezbollah leaders," Malka said.
In Malka's view, Hezbollah may resume hostile operations against “Israel” in the Har Dov area. Hezbollah leaders maintain that the dispute over marking the international border around Har Dov has not been resolved, and that “Israel” is still occupying Lebanese soil in the area.
From Hezbollah's standpoint, launching attacks and firing on “Israelis” near Har Dov would be relatively easy, he continued, noting that the area was military in character and that the border dispute lent Hezbollah "legitimization" for hostile acts against “Israeli” Army soldiers.
"The escalation in the remarks of the organization's leaders also expresses their desire to free Mustafa Dirani and Sheikh Obeid from prison," giving rise to concerns that Hezbollah will try to kidnap “Israeli” soldiers or civilians for use as bargaining chips to press for the jailed militants' freedom.
He said Palestinian groups in the territories, such as Hamas and the Islamic Jihad, are also threatening to abduct troops to bargain for the release of security prisoners. There are indications that kidnap attempts could be carried out during the holiday period.
Malka also said that despite taking heavy blows over the past two years from broad-based Shin Bet preventative operations, Hamas still has activists in the field.

 

Hundreds of south Lebanese in “Israel” to return home soon: army…(4)

“ Hundreds of Lebanese who fled into “Israel” after it ended its 22-year occupation of south Lebanon in May are soon to return home, an “Israeli” army official said on Tuesday.

"Hundreds of south Lebanese presented requests to return to Lebanon and they were granted," an army official involved with caring for the south Lebanese in “Israel” told AFP.

The official, who asked not to be identified, said the south Lebanese would cross “Israel’s” northern border soon, but did not provide precise details as to where or when.

Some 4,700 of the roughly 6,500 mostly-Christian Lebanese who entered “Israel” during its pullout still remain in “Israel”, according to army officials who have been caring for them and placing them in special schools.

The south Lebanese, the majority of whom worked for “Israel’s” proxy militia, feared they would face criminal charges or violence for having collaborated with “Israel” during its occupation.

Many of the south Lebanese, however, have been unhappy in “Israel”, feeling isolated, homesick and idle.

Most of the refugees have moved into Jewish communities as Palestinians have refused to live with them.

Twenty-four Lebanese returned to Lebanon on September 12. Three “Israeli”-allied militiamen among them were immediately detained by Lebanese police. Five other men in the group were kept for questioning while the women and children were allowed to return to their homes in the villages of Qlayaa and Rmeich, in the former “Israeli”-occupied border zone in south Lebanon.

More than 2,200 Lebanese are in the process of being tried before the military court in Beirut, or have already been convicted, for collaboration with “Israel” or simply having "contact with the “Israeli” enemy."
