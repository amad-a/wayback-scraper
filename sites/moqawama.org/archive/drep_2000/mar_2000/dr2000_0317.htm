Daily Report

March Friday 17-2000:

Israeli aggressions: (1)

The Islamic Resistance (IR) released a statement in which it reported the following Israeli aggressions:

1:05, the Israeli war jets raided into Qaisseih Valley.

6:45, two missiles hit Tasseh Well watercourse.

8:45, one 120mm shell landed Beit Yahoun outpost.

16:40, the Israeli fighter-bombers dropped several air-to-surface rockets into Mount-Abou Rashed.

17:15, the Israeli warplanes fired a number of air-to-ground rockets into the area between Jabbour and Abou Rashed.

17:25, the Israeli war jets staged a sortie into the area between Jabbour and Abou Rashed.

18:20, two Israeli war boats were seen cruising off the seashore of Rashideih.

22:30, the surroundings of Haddatha compound exposed to a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

22:40, the outskirts of Habboush came under three 120mm shells.
