Daily Report

March Wednesday 22-2000:

Operations:

09:00 the Islamic Resistance attacked the Bir Kalab post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits.

09:35 the Lebanese Resistance Brigade attacked the Blat post using suitable weapons, while at the same time another group attacked the Teir Harfa post with suitable weapons.

11:35 Amal attacked the Hadatha post.

12:05 the Islamic Resistance executed the Lahd traitor Hussein Ahmed Shahla by infiltrating into the occupied town of Markaba and firing at him from a range of 4 meters with an automatic machine gun, his death was confirmed when the Mujahid came closer and fired at him from a range of two meters making sure that he was dead.

Aggressions:

07:45 six 120mm shells landed on Mount Sujud.

09:40 four 155mm shells landed on Mount Al-Kabir.

09:45 three 155mm shells landed in the area between Zibkeen and Al-Heniyeh while another four landed on Jib Swied.

09:55 fifteen 155mm shells landed in the area between Zibkeen and Al-Heniyeh.

11:40 the Qaisiyeh valley was subject to bombing with 155mm shells.

11:50 six 155mm shells landed in the Qaisiyeh valley.

12:15 four smoke bombs landed in the town of Hadatha.

12:20 three 60mm shells landed inside the town near the houses of Aoun Saulli and Nabil Duher, no one was injured.

12:20 fifteen 81mm shells and three smoke bombs landed in the Sluki valley.

12:25 twelve 155mm shells landed in the Qaisiyeh valley. 

12:30 nine 81mm shells landed in the Sluki valley.

12:32 two 120mm shells landed in the Hadatha forest.

12:33 three 81mm shells landed in Bikat Risha in Majdal Silim.

12:35 one 81mm shell landed in the town of Hadatha near the house of Hussein Aoun Sauli.

12:35 six 155mm shells landed on the outskirts of Al-Jumeyjmeh.

12:40 seven shells landed in the town of Qabrikha and two 155mm shells landed in the Sluki valley.

13:00 five 122mm shells landed in the Qaisiyeh valley.

13:25 fifteen 160mm and 120mm shells landed in Bir Al-Daher while four 105mm shells landed in the same place.

22:25 combing with 12.7mm caliber machine gun at the outskirts of western Barasheet as well as the bombing of one 81mm shell at southern Barasheet.
