Daily Report

March Saturday 25-2000:


"Israel" informs collaborators about withdrawal from more outposts(1)

Press reports quoted sources of the "Israeli" occupation's militia as saying that the occupation forces had informed the collaborators that they (the "Israelis") are about to withdraw from the Rihan barracks and the joint "Israeli" -Lahdi outposts of Rihan and Aramta.

It was also reported that some "Israeli" hospitals have implicitly indicated to some Lebanese workers who are less than 40 years old that they may be dismissed by the end of March.


"Israeli" Aggressions(2)

In its statement, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:

Between 15:20 and 15:50, Jib Soweid area, east of Majdal Zoun village, was bombarded by ten 155mm missiles.

16:30, two 160mm missiles hit Mansouri ravine.

15:50, three 120mm shells struck the area between Mansouri and Majdal Zoun.

Between 20:05 and 20:40, eleven 120mm shells slammed into the west of Baraasheit town.

20:05, the perimeters of Mlikh village came under four 81mm rounds, whereas the surroundings of Kassara position exposed to a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

Footnotes:
1- Al-Manar TV, 24-3-2000.
2- IR statement, 24-3-2000.
