Daily Report

March Saturday 18-2000:

Military Operations: (1)

The Islamic Resistance (IR) team of martyrs Mohannad Ali Fouani and Hussein Hassan Nasser attacked, at 9:25, Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking its western and eastern portifications.

 

Israeli aggressions: (2)

In its statement, the IR mentioned the following Israeli aggressions:

At 5:05, and 10:10, four 155mm missiles hit Arid El-Zennar area.

8:45, Qaisseih Valley was shelled by four 155mm missiles.

16:00, a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck Mount Bassil.

19:00, the surroundings of Haddatha and Baraasheit compounds exposed to 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.
