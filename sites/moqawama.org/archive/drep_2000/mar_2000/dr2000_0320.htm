Daily Report

March Monday 20-2000:

 

Operations:

14:20 The Islamic Resistance attacked an engineering Lahd force at the post of Al-Kasara with suitable weapons.

At the same time another group attacked the Bir Kalab post with automatic machine gun and RPGs inflicting direct hits.

15:15 The Islamic Resistance attacked enemy movements within the vicinity of the Hasdoun post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits.

17:10 The Islamic Resistance attacked once more the post of Bir Kalab with RPGs inflicting direct hits.

20:10 Amal fired five 120mm shells at the Salaa post.

 

Aggressions:

04:00 Three 155mm shells landed between the villages of Bani Hayan and Al-Muhaisbat.

04:30 the vicinity of the Hadatha post was combed using 12.7mm caliber machine gun.

07:15 four 120mm and one 155mm shells landed on Mount Sujud.

08:10 four 155mm shells landed in Teir Harma.

08:16 five 155m shells landed on the outskirts of the town of Al-Louzeh.

10:25 sporadic firing in the vicinity of the Aramta post.

11:00 sporadic firing in the vicinity of the Aramta post.

12:20 five 155m shells landed on the outskirts of Kfarhouna.

14:32 one 155mm shell landed on the outskirts of Jbaa and one shell on Mount Sujud as well as three 120mm shells which landed on the outskirts of Kfarhouna.

14:36 seven 155mm shells landed on the outskirts of Al-Louzeh.

14:45 three 120mm shells landed on Arid Al-Zinar.

14:50 three 120mm shells landed on Mount Abu Rkab.

14:55 ten 81mm shells landed on the outskirts of Majdal Zoun.

14:55 three 105mm shells landed on Al-Tala Al-Sakhriya.

14:55 two 120mm shells landed on the vicinity of Sujud.

15:00 five 155mm shells landed on Jib Sweid as well as five 155mm shells in the area that lies between Zibkeen and Al-Heniyeh.

15:15 six 120mm shells landed in the vicinity of Sujud.

15:20 combing with 12.7mm caliber machine gun on Al-Areed Al-Zitar as well as combing with same caliber on the vicinity of Dhour Al-Kasarah.

17:20 seven 155mm shells landed on the outskirts of Jbaa.

20:05 bombing with 155mm shells on Mazraat Al-Hamra and Wadi Al-Kfour.

20:10 the Al-Suwan area was bombed with four 155mm shells.

20:10 three shells landed in Wadi Al-Kfour.
