Daily Report

March Thursday 30-2000:

FTR visits Lahoud, commends president's positions (1)
President Gen Emile Lahoud on Wednesday met a delegation from Hezbollah's Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc at the Baabda Palace.

The delegation included the bloc's head, MP Sayyed Ibrahim Ameen as-Sayyed, and MP Mohammed Raad, the head of Hezbollah's Political Council.

The FTR members commended Lahoud's "patriotic and brave positions which have integrated with the Resistance to form, both, strength for Lebanon in the face of the Zionist threats, plots, and maneuvers."

Furthermore, Lahoud met, in other meetings, FTR members MP Dr Hussein al-Hajj Hassan and MP Ibrahim Bayan besides Information Minister Anwar al-Khalil.  

Hoss: Withdrawal will be victory for resistance.(2)

Prime Minister Salim Hoss said :
 Lebanon insists on Israels implementation of UN Security Council Resolution 425 without any conditions. If Israel decides to keep some positions inside Lebanon, as some Israeli reports have indicated, Lebanon would consider the pullout a redeployment, not an implementation of Resolution 425.
Hoss affirmed that the resistance will continue as long as there is a centimeter of Lebanese territory still under occupation. But if Israel actually implements Resolution 425, it will be a clear victory for the resistance movement, since a full withdrawal has always been a Lebanese demand.

Lebanese woman seriously hurt in Israeli shelling.
A Lebanese woman was seriously injured Wednesday when Israeli shells hit her village on the edge of the Israeli-occupied border strip, Lebanese police said.

Yusra Farhat, 33, was hit by shrapnel from a shell fired from the occupied zone that fell near her house in Baraashit. She was taken to hospital in the port city of Tyre, the police said.

A total of three shells fell on the village and its surroundings.

Lebanese police reported, also, a raid at around 6:50 p.m. (1550 GMT) by Israeli warplanes which fired two missiles at the valley between the villages of Habush and Arab Selim, adding that about 30 shells landed around the villages, damaging two houses but without causing casualties. (3)

Military Operations: (5)
The IR team of martyrs Mahmoud Mohammd Jaber and Ahmad Ibrahim Shamas attacked, at 00:45, enemy movements inside Haddatha compound with the proper weapons. Direct hits were scored, the mujahedeen told.

Meanwhile, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Anis Ayoub and Hassan Kamel Tarhini was rushing upon Rshaf position. The militants, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, managed to chalk up direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Ali Khalil and Assaad Ibrahim Wanssa fell upon Hemayed post with the appropriate arms scoring direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Ahmad Daoud Hammoud and Adnan Sucrates targeted, at 13:15, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Shayar Azour position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to chalk up certain hits.

Moreover, the IR team of martyrs Youssef Abdel-Hafiz Hamdan and Ali Kourani attacked, at 16:45, a Lahdi engineering force inside Baraasheit compound with the suitable arms chalking up direct goals.

Furthermore, the IR squad of martyrs Hassan Abdallah Harb and Abdallah Ahmad Khalifeh detonated, at 18:00, a powerful roadsibe bomb as a Lahdi foot-patrol was moving on the road linking between Tohorra position and Jarmaq area inflicting a number of casualties among its ranks.

Quarter an hour later, the IR group of martyrs Youssef Abdallah Nasrallah and Khalil Mohammad Jradi rushed upon ad Israeli agents infantry force as it was backing up the attacked patrol on the road of Tohorra position. Using the adequate arms, the militants managed to inflict several casualties among its ranks. Over and above, the IR team of martyrs Haitham mohammad Faqih and Fadl Mahmoud Atwi in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded fiercely, at 19:10, the border post of Abbad with the rockets scoring direct hits.

For its part, the fighters Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 23:55, enemy squad at Dabsheh outpost with the appropriate weapons. The fighters were able to score accurate and direct hits.

However, Amal Movement fired four 81mm rounds at Mohaisbat position.
