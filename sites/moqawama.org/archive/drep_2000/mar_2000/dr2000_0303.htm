Hizbullah says February- "Israel's" "black month" in southern Lebanon(1)
"Hizbullah has described February as "Israel's" "black month" in occupied southern Lebanon. The resistance launched some of its most effective operations during that period.

' Hizbullah has released data on its operations against "Israeli" occupation forces in southern Lebanon, and on "Israeli" attacks during the month of February. "Israel" launched more than 720 air and artillery attacks, injuring 31 civilians, destroying three power stations, 63 homes, 22 shops and 13 civilian vehicles. Four civilians were detained by "Israel" and three "Israeli" allied militiamen defected to government-held territory. Among the most successful resistance operations last month, was the attack on the Blat outpost, in which two occupation troops were killed and ten were injured. Television footage of the injured "Israeli" soldiers being hauled to hospitals caused an uproar in the Jewish state and mounted pressure on the government to pullout from southern Lebanon. Effective resistance attacks on the Sojod outpost in the Iqleem al-Toffah mountain ridge led "Israel" and its allied militia to withdraw from the strategic encampment to areas deep inside the occupied zone. The resistance launched more than 120 operations last month, in which at least three "Israeli" soldiers and two "Israeli" allied militiamen were killed. Only one resistance fighter, Ahmad Moussa Ashab was killed during the same period.


Abdullah offers firm support for resistance(2)
Saudi Arabias crown prince capped the outpouring of Arab sympathy for Lebanon on Thursday, offering firm support for the resistance and warning that "Israel's" bellicosity would not alter Arab conditions for peace.
Prince Abdullah bin Abdel-Aziz, in Beirut on the final stop of a three-nation Arab tour, brought along a powerful 60-member entourage to highlight the kingdoms solidarity and a multimillion-dollar cash injection to buttress the reconstruction and development effort.
Lebanon is an important and integral part of the Arab world that has offered many sacrifices and patiently dealt with the many crises it has faced, Saudi Arabias future king declared after talks with President Emile Lahoud. We have come here to comfort our brethren, and to see what we can offer to bolster their resistance so that no aggression may shake their resolve.
Abdullah arrived from Damascus, where during talks with President Hafez Assad the two leaders declared unequivocal support for the Lebanese resistance in its quest to liberate the south and the western Beqaa.
The national resistance is aimed at eliminating oppression and reversing injustice. Its a legitimate right that no one can deprive us of, Abdullah reiterated in Beirut.
Legitimate rights are a fateful goal on which we will not compromise, he said, according to the official Saudi Press Agency.
Taking an indirect swipe at the United States ­ which has accused Hizbullah of seeking to derail the peace process in its attacks against "Israeli" forces in the south ­ Abdullah said: No power, regardless of how important its role is, can force others to abandon their legitimate rights.
I tell the "Israelis" that its high time to reactivate the peace process and steer it out of the dark tunnel, away from the language of guns. We are a nation that does not beg for its rights.


Houses damaged by occupation bombardment(3)

"Four houses were damaged when the "Israeli" occupation's artillery emplacements in al-Hardoun at dawn (Thursday) bombarded the residential areas in the town of al-Mansouri.

The houses, near the town's square, belong to Nasser Ali Zeid, Habeeb Mohammed Yassin Khashab, Mohammed Qassem Suleiman Harb, and Jaafar Yassin Khashab.

Two cars, belonging to Jaafar Khashab and Hussein Mheidli, were also damaged by the shelling. Moreover, the enemy bombarded Wadi al-Mansouri, Hai al-Mshah (Majdal Zoun) and the Jib Soweida area.

At 9:15 a.m., the occupation's outpost in Dhaira shelled the outskirts of Yater, Jibal El-Botom, and Siddiqeen with around 20 155mm rounds.

Earlier, the enemy's outpost in Baraasheit raked after Wednesday midnight the towns' residential areas with heavy machine guns.

The "Israeli" bombardment of the town of Habboush and its vicinity Wednesday night damaged the house of Jamil al-Hariri".

Syria rejects any change in south Lebanon fighting rules(5)
"Syrian Foreign Minister Faruq al-Shara ruled out on Thursday any changes in the April 1996 south Lebanon rules of engagement agreed on by "Israel" and Lebanon.

"Syria is committed to the April accords, refuses any change in their clauses and wishes to see the panel resume its work as soon as possible," an official source quoted Shara as telling Richard Erdman, the US representative to the international panel monitoring the agreement.

The panel also includes representatives from France, "Israel", Lebanon and Syria.

Erdman and the French representative on the panel, Laurent Rapin, were in Syria Thursday in an attempt to organize a new panel meeting.

"Israel" walked out of the panel's February 11 meeting when Hizbullah guerrillas killed an "Israeli" soldier in southern Lebanon.

Under the accord, the two sides undertook to refrain from targeting civilians or carrying out attacks from civilian areas.

Erdman told reporters: "We come here as part of ongoing US-French efforts to confirm the commitment of all the parties to the April understanding and to reach the earliest possible reconvening of the work of our monitoring group."

He said he would also hold talks with the other ("Israeli" and Lebanese) parties from the panel, which the United States and France co-chair, before drawing conclusions.

Rapin said after the meeting with Shara: "We had a very positive meeting." "We are going tomorrow to Beirut ... and we will be in "Israel" on Sunday."

"Israel" has hinted that it wished to change the 1996 rules of engagement.

The Hebrew state occupies a "security zone" in south Lebanon with the declared aim of protecting its northern territory from cross-border attack.

" Hizbullah carries out most of the anti-"Israeli" attacks in southern Lebanon".


Collaborators' rebellion after Ain Qinya operation(6)
The painful blow dealt by the Islamic Resistance (IR) men on Wednesday to the "Israeli" occupation's proxy militia has mounted the state of collapse and loss in the ranks of the militiamen.

Reports from the occupied zone said ambivalent sentiments prevail among the militiamen, especially as they see scores of militia officials prepare to leave the country leaving them to face the unknown.

The reports talked about rebellion in the ranks of the militiamen who refused to obey military orders.

Several ordinary militia patrols did not come out from the outposts during the past few days due to the fear by the collaborators of IR operations, the reports said.


Hizbullah delegation visits UAE Embassy(7)
"A delegation from Hizbullah's Foreign Relations' Unit headed by its official Hajj Youssef Merhi, visited the embassy of the united Arab Emirates (UAE) in Beirut.

The delegation discussed with UAE Chargé d'Affaires Saed Hamdan al-Naqabi the recent local and regional developments in light of the "Israeli" aggressions on Lebanon's infrastructures.

The phase between now and July is a serious one that is open to all possibilities, Merhi said, adding that the Islamic Resistance's achievements belong to the Arab and Islamic Ummah.

The chargé d'affaires stressed his country's support to Lebanon, its people and resistance, in the battle against the "Israeli" occupation in the south, besides the UAE readiness to provide all types of backing.

Naqabi commended the sacrifices offered by the Resistance and the Lebanese people to liberate the soil from the "Israeli" occupation".


"Israeli" Army has prepared an emergency plan for pullout(8)
"Six heavy "Israeli" mortar bombs slammed into the village of Mansouri south of Tyre, destroying four houses and two vehicles this morning. "Israel" also rained down more than thirty shells on nearby villages. Hizbullah for its part said it attacked occupation outposts in Radar, Hardoun, and Bayada, in the western sector of the occupied zone. Meanwhile, "Israeli" television said the army has prepared an emergency plan for withdrawing from occupied southern Lebanon should there be a mass defection by members of its allied militia. The plan provides for the evacuation of "Israeli" soldiers posted in Lebanon within several days should militiamen defect in droves to Hizbullah. Military officials fear the militia may collapse ahead of Prime Minister Ehud Barak's promised pullout from Lebanon by July. The force's morale has also been crumbling, after resistance fighters killed five militiamen deep inside the zone yesterday".


IR and LRB attack occupation and collaborators' outposts(9)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) squad of Martyrs Adnan Rida and Khalil Jradi Thursday morning attacked the "Israeli" occupation's outpost of Radar with rocket propelled grenades in a violent and concentrated manner scoring accurate hits, while squad of Martyrs Muneer Abde-Rahman and Ali Saqr of IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit targeted the same outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits in its ramparts and fortifications.

In the meantime, IR's squad of Martyrs Ali Baydoun and Youssef Hamdan blitzed the occupation's outpost in al-Hardoun scoring accurate hits while squad of Martyrs Ali Korani and Fadl Swaidan staged a similar attack on the enemy's outpost in Bayada.

Furthermore, IR's squad of Martyrs Hussein Farhat and Hussein Youness at 12:15 targeted enemy movements inside the Baraasheit outpost with the appropriate weapons scoring accurate hits.

Meanwhile, squad of Martyrs Ismail Rida and Jamal Sh'heimi attacked the Haddatha outpost with machine-guns and rocket propelled scoring direct hits.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) Thursday morning attacked the occupation's outpost in Blat with rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits.


Petition against paratrooper sergeant for refusing to fight Hizbullah(10)
"IDF* Captain Daniel Ben-Gavriel petitioned the High Court of Justice this week with a request to instruct the Chief of Staff and the military prosecutor to bring to trial a paratrooper, Sergeant Ofer Sharon, for disgraceful behavior in not charging Hizbullah during a battle in which three officers of the paratrooper commando unit were killed.

The battle took place in February 1999 between the paratrooper commandos and the Hizbullah. The three officers killed were the commander of the unit, Major Eitan Balahsan, engineering unit officer Major Liraz Tito, and Lieutenant David Granit.

According to Ben-Gavriel, during the battle, Sharon lay on the ground nearby, and did nothing. He had a machine gun on his back, the heaviest weapon the unit had with them. Lieutenant Granit called the machine gunner to join the force, but he didn't.

Sharon stated after the battle that he was afraid to get up. He said that he knew that getting up would mean dying in a meaningless battle. He said that he continued giving covering fire until additional soldiers arrived. He sent the soldiers to join the battle that he was too afraid to join. Finally, he says, he did get up and slid down toward the battle, between the rocks.

According to Ben-Gavriel, Sharon did not follow orders, and stayed behind cover. He stated that if Sharon had charged, firing the machine gun, the battle could have had an entirely different outcome.

Ben-Gavriel also quotes in his petition a statement made by an unidentified army officer, who claims that the fact that Ofer Sharon was not brought to military trial makes it difficult to educate and train soldiers toward military values of completing a task and aiming for victory in battle, which causes harm to IDF combat soldiers".
