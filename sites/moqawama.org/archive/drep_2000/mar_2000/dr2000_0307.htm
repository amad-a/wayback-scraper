Daily Report

March Tuesday 07-2000:

IR attacks occupation in Taibe and Beir Kallab
The Islamic Resistance (IR) - squad of Martyrs Ramzi Abu Salim and Hashem Mhanna at 21:15 Sunday blitzed an Israeli force inside the occupation's outpost in al-Taibe with rocket propelled grenades and missiles scoring accurate hits, an IR communiqué read.
In the meantime, IR's squad of Martyrs Hussein Jawad and Hussein Haidar targeted enemy movements inside the occupation's outpost in Beir Kallab with the appropriate weapons scoring direct hits, the communiqué added. 

LRB targets collaborators in Rshaf
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) Monday morning detonated an explosive device in the northern rampart of the occupation's outpost in Rshaf after LRB men had spotted collaborators' movements inside the rampart.
Definite casualties were inflicted in the ranks of the collaborators, an LRB communiqué said.

Occupation violates understanding: raids wound civilian damage houses
The "Israeli" occupation forces violated the April Understanding once again while they proceeded with their air aggressions for the fifth consecutive day.
The enemy's warplanes targeted the town of Korbetselim with 4 air-to-ground missiles wounding Rabieh Tamer, 20, besides damaging a number of the town's houses.


MP Musawi: withdrawal decision a maneuver to pressurize Lebanon and Syria
The "Israeli" occupation's delaying of the withdrawal timetable till July is an attempt to use the pullout issue in exerting more pressure on Lebanon and Syria, Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc number MP Sayyed Ammar al-Musawi said today.
The Hezbollah deputy said the communications' minister in Barak's government has requested that her son be exempted from serving in south Lebanon for fear that he may be killed at the hands of the Islamic Resistance men.
Musawi who discussed the "Israeli" decision with Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss said the occupation's government is trying to establish on the withdrawal idea a base to set out in political maneuvers to pressurize Lebanon and Syria.

LEBANESE CIVILIAN INJURED IN ISRAELI SHELLING
"Israeli" warplanes raided south Lebanon for the fourth consecutive day, wounding a civilian. Twenty-year old Rabih Tamer was injured when "Israeli" jets hit the outskirts of Kherbet Selm village east of Tyre, and outside the zone "Israel" occupies. The raids came amid heightened tension in south Lebanon, after "Israel" stepped up its attacks on civilian targets. On Saturday an "Israeli" occupation soldier was seriously wounded by Hizbollah resistance fighters inside the zone.

HIZBOLLAH DECLARES VICTORY IN ITS RESISTANCE WAR OVER "ISRAELI" IN SOUTHERN LEBANON
Hizbollah has declared victory in its resistance war to oust "Israeli" occupation forces from southern Lebanon. A statement issued by the resistance movement said the smallest Arab country has succeeded in "chasing away" "Israel". The Jewish state yesterday made official its pledge to withdraw occupation forces from southern Lebanon by July. Hizbollah warned "Israel" against making good on its threats to attack Lebanon, saying the resistance movement will not remain idle in the face of any new "Israeli" aggressions.

"Israeli" snipers fire on siege villagers
Information Minister Anwar Khalil on Monday called on international organizations to exert pressure on "Israel", saying the Jewish state has been holding a village at the occupation zones border under siege.
Khalil, also a Marjayoun-Hasbaya MP, said residents of the village of Ain Tinta in Hasbaya were being targeted by "Israeli" snipers every time they attempt to leave their village.
The "Israeli" occupation forces continue to increase their aggression on our people  The residents of Ain Tinta are unable to leave their village and go to their work as a result of the "Israeli" siege, Khalil said.
