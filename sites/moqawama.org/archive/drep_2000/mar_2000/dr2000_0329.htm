Daily Report

March Wednesday 29-2000:

Two Lebanese children wounded after Israel bombards village: police
Two Lebanese children were wounded Tuesday when Israel pounded a southern Lebanon village with mortar fire shortly after an Israeli position came under attack, police said.

Rima Darwish, 14, was hit by flying glass in the face and head and her nine-year-old brother Ahmad was also wounded after a 155mm Israeli shell landed several meters (yards) from their house in Majdal Zun, they said.

The two were taken to hospital for treatment.

Police said earlier that about a dozen shells fired from inside the Israeli-occupied border zone fell around Majdal Zun and Mansuri, along the edge of the zone's western sector.

Israeli warplanes also fired nine missiles on a valley in between the two villages, they said.

Since the beginning of the year three Lebanese civilians have been killed and 55 wounded in the region, according to an AFP tally. (1)

In its communiqué the Islamic Resistance (IR) reported the following Israeli aggressions:

10:40, three 155mm missiles hit west of Yater village.

13:07, the Israeli fighter-bombers dropped eight air-to-surface rockets into Slouqi valley.

13:35, the Israeli war jets raided into the perimeters of Majdal Zoun town firing eight air-to-ground rockets.

13:40, the Israeli warplanes fired eight air-to-surface missiles into Mansouri ravine.

Between 14:15 and 14:45, the peripheries of Shaqra town came under eight 155mm missiles and four 120mm shells.

14:45, two 120mm shells landed west of Baraasheit compound.

Between 17:15 and 19:50, fourteen 81mm rounds and five 155mm missiles slammed into the perimeters of Majdal Zoun wounding the child Ahmad Ali Darwish,6, and the teenager Rima Ali Darwish.(2)

Lebanon can be steadfast with the resistance will, president (3)
We have been able to present an ideal model of struggling till results are achieved, President Gen. Emile Lahoud said.

The president was quoted by visitors as underlining Lebanon's ability to be steadfast in the face of the Israeli intransigence following the Geneva summit.

During another meeting with the representative of the International Committee for the Red Cross (ICRC), Henri Fournier, Gen. Lahoud said the continued detention of Lebanese prisoners in Israeli detention centers mounts the Lebanese outrage at the Israeli enemy and increases the will to resist.

The president condemned the recent Israeli aggression which targeted a Lebanese Red Cross center in Nabatieh.

This act is not strange as far as "Israel" is concerned, for it has been detaining freedom fighter Mustfa al-Dirani whom it had abducted from his bedroom to practice against him the most hideous acts to torture, and it bars international groups from meeting him, Lahoud added.

Israeli preparations for complete pullout from Jezzine (4)
The Israeli occupation forces are preparing for withdrawal from the Aramta-Rihan area.

Reports said the occupation troops have been dismantling facilities and moving vehicles from the area besides dismantling the military facilities in the nearby barracks of al-Aishie.

Lebanese security sources said observation reports stated that Zionist military trucks were seen over the last two days transporting military devices and equipment from the Rihan area to the Lebanese-Palestinian borders amidst strict security measures.

With the completion of this Israeli pullout, the Jezzine area will be completely liberated following the occupation's retreat from the Jezzine town, Kfarhouna and Sojod.

Military Operations (5)
The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) attacked, at 10:30, Blat post with the adequate arms scoring direct hits where the smoke columns were seen emanating out of it.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, at 19:05, seven 120mm shells at Rshaf outpost.
