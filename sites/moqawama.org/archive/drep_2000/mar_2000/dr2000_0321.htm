Daily Report

March Tuesday 21-2000:


Operations

05:30 Amal fired a number of shells towards western Barasheet.
06:15 the Islamic Resistance attacked the Sweida post with RPGs and machine gun fire at the posts bunkers inflicting direct hits, at the same time another group attacked the Kfar Ruman post inflicting direct hits.
11:45 The Islamic Resistance attacked the Bir Kalab post inflicting direct hits.
14:20 the Islamic Resistance attacked a Lahd engineering force at the Shayar Azour post inflicting direct hits.
21:50 the Islamic Resistance attacked enemy movements within the Bir Kalab post with suitable weapons inflicting direct hits.

 

Aggressions :

04:30 three 155mm shells landed on Mount Sujud.
04:40 four 155mm shells landed on the outskirts of Jbaa.
05:30 combing with 12.7mm caliber machine guns at the vicinity of the Hadatha post and at the outskirts of the town of Hadatha.
05:35 five 120mm shells landed on western Barasheet.
05:35 bombing with 155mm shells on southern Barasheet.
06:30 bombing with 81mm shells with combing using 12.7mm caliber machine gun on the town of Kfar Ruman, the combing resulted in the damage of the houses belonging to Abbas Al-Juni, Hassan Jamil Daher and Ali Saleh.
06:55 the town of Habush was bombed using 81mm shells one of which landing on the house that belongs to Hussein Hallal.
07:05 one 155mm shell landed on Mount Sujud while two others landed on Nabaa Al-Taseh.
08:30 three 81mm shells landed on Mount Sujud.
08:40 three 120mm shells landed on Mount Sujud.
08:55 eight 155mm shells landed on Mount Sujud.
09:05 five 155mm shells landed on the Plain of Okmata.
11:55 the outskirts of the town of Jbaa was bombed with 155mm shells.
12:35 two 120mm shells landed on Ared Al-Zinar while two others landed on the plain of Okmata.
12:45 one 120mm shell landed on the plain of Okmata and a 155mm  shell landed on the outskirts of Louezeh.
16:10 three 155mm shells landed in Jib Swied to the North of Majdal Zoun.
17:50 fifteen 81mm shells landed on the outslirts of Majdal Zoun.
18:00 twdo 105mm shells landed in eastern Majdal Zoun.
19:05 combing on the outskirts of the Hadatha post with 12.7mm caliber machine gun as well as the town of Hadatha.
19:25 the town of Jbaa was bombed with 155mm shells.
21:05 combing on western Barasheet.
22:10 combing on the Ali Taher forest and the abandoned army barracks.
22:53 Israeli war planes raided the river bed of Nabaa Al-Taseh.
23:20 Israeli war planes raided the area of Hafour Dareya firing a number of air to ground missiles.
