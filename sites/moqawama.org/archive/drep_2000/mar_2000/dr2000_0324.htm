Daily Report

March Friday 24-2000:

 

Collaborator killed, 2 wounded in fresh IR blow (1)
The Islamic Resistance (IR) proceeded with dealing blows to the Lahdi militia killing one of the Israeli occupation militia members and wounding two others, according to enemy acknowledgment.

IR's squad of Martyrs Abbas Khreibani and Ali Krayyem at 9:30 a.m. Thursday spotted a number of the occupation's militiamen.

IR men targeted the collaborators with rocket propelled grenades; they were killed or wounded.

Security sources in the occupied zone said collaborator Ahmed Ali Nasrallah was killed and two others were wounded.

Another two agents were wounded yesterday when IR detonated a bomb at the Kfartibneet crossing.

So far this year, 15 militiamen have been killed in the south and another 23 wounded. Nine collaborators have been killed this month alone.
Furthermore, IR's squad of Martyr Habib Hijazi and Fadi Ezzeddeen, Thursday morning hit the major western rampart of the occupation's outpost in Mashaaroun. Smoke billowed from the rampart.

The Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) Wednesday night attacked the occupation's outposts in Baraasheet and Haddatha.

For its part, Amal Movement attached , at 16:20 and 16:25, the position of Mohaisbat and Taibeh respectively.

 

Annan puts men on Dirani abuse case (2)
UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan has instructed one of his top aides to investigate the Israeli imprisonment of resistance leader Mustafa Dirani, Prime Minister Salim Hosss office announced Thursday.
Diranis lawyer, Tzvi Rish, filed a $1.5-million lawsuit against Israel on March 13, saying Dirani was systematically beaten and raped by his Israeli jailors. A political-media storm ensued, with leaders and human-rights organizations demanding access to the prisoner.
Hoss contacted Annan on March 14 to draw attention to the case, according to the statement from his office.
Mr. Hoss received today a message from Mr. Annan saying that UN Human Rights Commissioner Mary Robinson is following up the matter closely, the office said in a statement.
Robinson has instructed Nigel Rodley, the UNs special rapporteur on torture, to appeal to the Israeli government to put an end to the prisoners plight, the statement said.
She will later carry out other measures, Hosss office said.
On Wednesday, an International Committee of the Red Cross spokesman said that his group, which also received a complaint from Hoss, hoped to gain access to Dirani as soon as possible.
Dirani has been held without trial in Israel since he was kidnapped in Lebanon in 1994. Israel seized him seeking information on Israeli airman Ron Arad, who disappeared in 1986.

 

Occupation bombards Yatar, damages houses
The Israeli occupation forces Thursday moved their aggressions and violations of the April Understanding to the town of Yatar.

The town's residential areas were bombarded with a number of heavy rounds that damaged its electricity grid and the houses of Wehbe Swaidan (the house was directly hit with an artillery round), Ameen Balaghy, Ali Qdouh, Mohammed Swaidan and Hussein Karim.

The enemy's shells that were fired from the occupation's outposts in Hmayyed, Qawzah, and al-Z'haire also targeted Yatar's Highway and the outskirts of Kafra.(3)
