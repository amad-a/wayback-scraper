Hizbullah urges jihad in Palestine…(1)

Hizbullah called Friday on Arab leaders at their Cairo summit to supply arms to the Palestinians and open up borders with “Israel” for those willing to join the “holy war” against the Jewish state.
“Arab leaders must adopt practical measures to protect the intifada and extend their help to Palestinian people by opening the borders to those striving for resistance and jihad, and supply the Palestinian people with weapons to defend itself,” a statement from the group said.
Hizbullah said the armed struggle was the choice of all Arabs, adding that “Israel” only understands “the language of power and war.”
“Arab leaders must come up with stands and decisions that match the aspirations of their people, who see the Palestinian intifada as the only option for the Arab nation and its only way to liberate the land,” it said.
They added that this option “has proved its efficacy and success when Lebanese and the resistance resorted to it and managed to thwart the occupation and liberate the land without making any concessions.”
The group also called for severing all relations with “Israel” and halting all attempts at normalization.
“The intifada needs decisive measures that will end all relations with “Israel”, including all diplomatic, economic, and media ties, as well as stopping all kinds of normalization,” it added.
Hizbullah called on Arab leaders “to re-evaluate their priorities and consider Palestine as their central cause and the essence of the conflict with the Zionist entity.”

The sources were reported as saying Clinton made the proposal to Barak on the sidelines of the US-sponsored, multinational, Sharm el-Sheikh summit in Egypt earlier this week. But the proposal was reportedly met with fierce opposition from Likud leader Ariel Sharon, who may end up joining a coalition government with politically embattled Barak. Hizbullah said he traveled from Brussels on a forged "foreign" passport. Hizbullah warned against such a move. “Israeli” security sources were quoted by the Haaretz newspaper as saying Damascus has been giving tacit support for the capture of “Israeli” soldiers and attacks on Lebanon's Shibaa Farms, which remain under “Israeli” occupation, despite the Jewish state's pullout from the south in May. Security sources also told Haaretz Hizbullah has moved shipments of Katyusha rockets to the border with “Israel” in preparation for any “Israeli” attack on Lebanon…(3)

Hizbullah says captured “Israeli” was agent in failed Swiss wire-tapping…(4)

“ The “Israeli” "spy" captured by Lebanon's Hizbullah group was a Mossad intelligence agent involved in an aborted wire-tapping operation in Switzerland in 1998, an Arab newspaper reported Friday.

Elhanan Tannenbaum tried almost "three years ago in Switzerland together with four other Mossad agents to tap the telephone of a Lebanese suspected of financing Hizbullah", said the London-based Asharq Al-Awsat.

A Swiss court in July handed a Mossad agent, who was not named, a 12-month suspended sentence for trying to bug the telephone of a Swiss man of Lebanese origin. Another four “Israelis” were arrested but released by police.

"The commander of the operation was (former) Mossad chief Dany Yatom who was sacked because of this failure," said Asharq Al-Awsat.

Hizbullah has said the “Israeli” businessman, an air force reserve colonel, was a Mossad spy caught in a sting after travelling to Lebanon in a bid to infiltrate the group.

His capture was announced on October 15, bringing to four the number of “Israelis” held by Hizbullah, which aims to trade them for Lebanese and other Arab prisoners held in the Jewish state.

“Israel” says Tannenbaum, 54, was employed in Switzerland by a consultancy linked to “Israeli” electronics and arms firms.

An “Israeli” court has banned publication of information on Tannenbaum.

Support Hizbullah’s actions, demand prisoners’ families…(5)

The Follow-Up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in “Israeli” Prisons reaffirmed its support for Hizbullah’s capture of three “Israeli” soldiers two weeks ago and called for worldwide backing to free 19 Lebanese prisoners still held by “Israel”.
The committee held a press conference on Friday at its office in Corniche Mazraa to announce its support for the latest “phase in the campaign” for the release of the 19 Lebanese detainees in “Israel”.
“It’s a big step in our campaign, but it can only be considered a step. Now, more than ever, we must increase efforts and create a worldwide people’s campaign,” said Mohammed Safa, a committee spokesman.
he has organized demonstrations, hunger strikes, international publicity trips and  campaigned on the internet for almost ten years.
Although the committee will not actively work with Hizbullah during any possible prisoner swap negotiations, it will play the role of “the hidden soldier” and provide information on the detainees.
Safa called on the public to support Hizbullah’s “operation” since, instead of exerting pressure on “Israel”, Hizbullah has been put on the defensive. He therefore called for a worldwide campaign to release the Lebanese and Arab detainees, one that would compare to
the campaign demanding the release of three “Israeli” captives.
“Why is it that the world is racing to come to “Israel’s” side when it has ignored the cries of mothers calling for the release of their loved ones for over 20 years,” he said. “What about people like Samir Qantar who has been detained for 22 years?”
Safa described the detainees in “Israeli” prisons as turning into “ashes.” They are forbidden from meeting with the International Committee of the Red Cross or receiving correspondence from their families.
Safa explained that the “heroic” capture of the soldiers in the Shibaa Farms was a result of “Israel’s” refusal to address the detainee issue, and described the action as a legal right and a part of efforts to resist occupation.
The committee has long chastised the government for considering UN Resolution 425 implemented. Insisting that diplomatic efforts be stepped up to lobby for the detainees’ release and the liberation of the Shibaa Farms.
Safa also called for the creation of a ministerial committee in the new government to follow-up the issue.
But he said that even if a prisoner exchange succeeds,
the “struggle will continue.”
The committee has prioritized making the Jewish state accountable for its crimes and rehabilitating former detainees.
Safa announced that a symbolic court organized by the National Egyptian Organization for Human Rights will be held next Thursday to judge “Israel’s” war crimes.
He also highlighted committee support for the new intifada  and called for an Arab economic embargo on “Israel” and for breaking all ties with the Jewish state.

“Israelis” fire on ex-“Israeli”-proxy militia base…(6)

“Israeli” helicopters and artillery pounded a border area near Kfar Shouba in south Lebanon Friday night, police reported.
A helicopter gunship fired several missiles on the Majidiyeh barracks, a former “Israeli”-proxy militia training camp in the western sector of the former occupation zone, they said.
Moments later, an “Israeli” Merkava tank in nearby Abbassiyeh fired at least 12 shells on Majidiyeh, they said.
It was not known whether anyone was in the barracks, evacuated a few days ago by Hizbullah, which had taken over the installation after the “Israeli” pullout from south Lebanon in May.
UNIFIL and the “Israeli” military reported gunfire, but it was not immediately clear whether there had been any shots fired from the Lebanese side.
Majidiyeh is adjacent to the disputed Shibaa Farms and near where  Hizbullah captured three “Israeli” soldiers on Oct. 7.
“Israel” captured the Shibaa Farms from Syria, which backs Lebanese claims to sovereignty over the area. “Israel” says the area’s fate can only be decided through a peace treaty with Damascus. ­

“Israeli” farmer scares off Lebanese journalists with machine gun…(7)

“ A group of journalists, including an AFP correspondent, was ordered Friday to get away from Lebanon's border with “Israel” by an “Israeli” farmer threatening them with a machine gun.

"We were taking pictures at the border when an “Israeli” farmer got out of his car and threatened us with his machine gun, saying: 'Get out of here,'" said AFP correspondent in southern Lebanon Ali Diya.

"We left the area, while the armed farmer hid behind rocks while still pointing his machine gun at us," he said.

The incident took place on the border between the Lebanese villages of Aadayseh and Kfar Kila, facing the “Israeli” settlement of Misgav Am.

Many farmers in north “Israel” have been seen carrying machine guns while working the fields following the recent capture of four “Israeli” officers by the Hizbullah, which wants to swap them for Lebanese detained in “Israel”.

Farmers and other agricultural workers in northern “Israel” previously only carried guns.

Workers continued Friday to build large walls and monitoring outposts near settlements, including Misgav Am, to protect them and nearby fields close to the borders.

Calls for Cairo summit to cut off all ties with “Israel”…(8)

Lebanese politicians and religious leaders on Friday called on Arab leaders to sever all ties with “Israel” and support the Palestinian intifada during their two-day Arab summit in Cairo this weekend.
Senior Shiite cleric Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah said Arab leaders must agree to boycott “Israel” politically and economically and reject any pressure from the United States.
“Arab leaders must not bend to pressures by the US, which controls most Arab regimes, but must respond to the call of their people who want them to boycott “Israel” politically and economically,” Fadlallah said in his Friday sermon at a Beirut mosque.
“We mustn’t be content with demonstrations and protest statements. We must move through with boycotts and confrontation within a comprehensive and well-devised plan,” he added.
Progressive Socialist Party President Walid Jumblatt said that Arab leaders must open their borders and allow their people to join Palestinians in their confrontations with “Israelis”.
“The Arab summit will be useless unless it leads to borders being opened for the popular armed action that proved its efficiency in south Lebanon and was capable of defeating the “Israeli” military machine,” he said.
Jumblatt praised the “Arab solidarity” expressed in protests throughout the region in a show of unity despite pressure by certain Arab governments.
“Demonstrations in all Arab countries proved that Arab unity exists beyond borders and above security and intelligence systems and obstacles,” he said.
“The Arab people will not accept this alien entity ­ “Israel” ­ which was planted by colonialism in the heart of the Arab and Islamic world,” Jumblatt said.
Arab leftist and Islamic groups also urged Arab leaders to sever diplomatic relations with “Israel” and use their “oil weapon” to support the Palestinian uprising during a meeting with Palestinians.
“We call on Arab countries to cut diplomatic ties with the Zionist enemy, withdraw their ambassadors, close embassies as well as trade and representative offices and halt normalization with the Jewish enemy,” a statement by the group said.
The meeting of leftists and Islamists urged Arabs “to devise a comprehensive confrontation plan by taking into account any of the options that could be adopted, including political and military ones, and the option of using the oil weapon.”
It called on oil-rich Islamic states to allocate some of their oil revenues to support the Palestinian uprising.
For his part, Tyre MP Abdullah Qasir said that “no Arab would disagree over the need to sever all political and economic ties with “Israel” and stop the normalization process.”
The Amal Movement issued a statement asking the leaders at the Cairo summit to stop normalization with “Israel”. It said Arabs were now more than ever required to support the Palestinian intifada.
A statement issued by Lebanese writers and journalists also called on summit participants to stop all normalization with “Israel”, boycott American and European products and hold the United Nations responsible for being “biased” in its decisions.
“Lebanese intellectuals and writers appeal to all of their Arab counterparts, artists and journalists to support Palestinians and expose practices by the terrorist Zionists and all who support them,” said Amin Mustafa, the group’s representative.
Tyre MP Ali Khalil said it was the Arabs’ duty to support the intifada and that the current crisis called for a “unified Arab stand that will enable us to resist “Israel’s” aggressions.”
Akkar MP Issam Fares said he hoped the Arab summit would result in a unified and firm Arab stance.
“Arabs must pressure “Israel” and force it to abide by international resolutions that guarantee Arab rights, namely the right of a Palestinian state,” he said.

Protesters call on leaders to ‘isolate “Israel”’…(9)

About 100 Palestinian demonstrators accused the United Nations on Friday of pro-”Israeli” bias and called on delegations to the Arab summit in Cairo to isolate the Jewish state.
At Ain Hilweh, the largest in Lebanon, protesters lashed out at the United Nations by burning UN, US and “Israeli” flags.
Carrying a Kalashnikov rifle, 75-year-old Hajj Mohammed Izdahmad stepped on the UN flag, saying, “We will fight “Israel” and return home victorious.”
Abed Maqdah, secretary of the camp's civic committee, denounced UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan as an “agent in the service of the Jews.”
The civic committee also issued a statement calling for the Arab summit to show “once and for all a clear and courageous position in favor of the Palestinian people.”
Delegates attending this weekend’s meeting, to be held in Cairo, were urged to break off diplomatic relations with “Israel” and end the normalization of relations with the Jewish state.
In Bourj Shemali on the outskirts of Tyre, refugees called for the summit to support the creation of a Palestinian state, with Al-Quds as its capital.
More than 2,500 Palestinians and Lebanese demonstrated in Haret Naameh in the Chouf and condemned the “Israeli” bloodshed in the occupied territories.
The protest drew members of the Progressive Socialist Party, the Syrian Social Nationalist Party and Ahmad Jibril’s Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-General Command, which has a strong presence in Naameh.
Demonstrators, wearing the Palestinian chuffed and carrying banners and symbolic caskets, condemned “Israel” and the United States for the recent violence in the territories.
PFLP-GC spokesman Abu Rushdi urged all supporters of the Palestinian cause “to open the borders and authorize demonstrations supporting our brothers in Palestine.”
A two-day summit, which starts on Saturday, is expected to come up with a unified Arab position concerning the “Israeli”-Palestinian clashes in the West Bank and the Gaza Strip.
In Beirut, a delegation of 200 prominent Palestinians and Lebanese delivered a letter to Arab League offices calling for Arab leaders to “break all links with “Israel”.”
It called for a rejection of “American pressures” and for a “response to the aspirations of the Arab people and to bring an end to the aggression organized against our people by the “Israelis”.”
In Tripoli, the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine organized a demonstration in front of the United Nation Relief and Works Agency’s headquarters. Protesters included representatives of Palestinian civil, social and educational committees, in addition to local political figures.
Palestinians carried banners calling for the right to return and rejecting the permanent settlement of the refugees in Lebanon.
They also read a letter addressed to the Arab League that urged Arab governments to cut their diplomatic, economic and political ties with “Israel”.
The letter criticized the Egyptian summit, saying it served American and “Israeli” interests and aimed at covering up atrocities by “Israeli” Prime Minister Ehud Barak and his coalition government.
Demonstrations were also held in the Nahr al-Bared refugee camp near Tripoli. Hundreds of people took to the streets of the camp following Friday prayers.

“Israeli” troops kill seven Palestinians in the occupied territories…(10)

There has been a dangerous escalation in “Israeli”-Palestinian clashes. “Israeli” troops have opened live rounds on protestors killing at least seven, two of them young boys. All but one of the killings occurred a short while ago, after fighting in Gaza and the West Bank raged. The 48-hour deadline for a truce had run its course and went into borrowed time. But the Jewish state has stepped up its war rhetoric. Prime Minister Ehud Barak warned of harsh measures if the fighting did not end. 

“  A 14-year-old Palestinian boy was shot dead by “Israeli” troops in the northern West Bank town of Selfit. He was hit by “Israeli” soldiers in the heart by a live round at an “Israeli” army checkpoint at the western entrance of the Palestinian-controlled town. The incident brings to more than 110 the number of Palestinians killed in a three-week wave of bloodshed across the Palestinian territories and “Israel”. Tens of thousands of Palestinians earlier took to the streets around the West Bank and Gaza Strip to protest against “Israel” and to bury a man killed by Jewish settlers and “Israeli” soldiers last night.  The latest deaths occurred when a Palestinian was shot dead and 14 others wounded by a group of Jewish settlers hiking in the village of Askar near the West Bank town of Nablus, which has been a flashpoint during the uprising.   One settler was killed in the exchange of fire. “Israeli” helicopter gunships fired on the area as they came to the settlers' aid. “Israeli” troops flooded occupied East Al-Quds, where minor skirmishes took place between “Israeli” troops and Palestinian youths after soldiers banned young men from entering the Al-Aqsa Mosque for Friday prayers. In Bethlehem, some 10,000 people turned out for the funeral of one of two members of Palestinian President Yasser Arafat's personal bodyguards killed in a blast yesterday. Security officials said the explosion was caused by gas canisters, but crowds insisted “Israel” was behind the incident. Tensions have been simmering only hours before the passing of a deadline on implementation of a US-brokered ceasefire deal agreed in Sharm el-Sheikh, Egypt.   While there was no written agreement at the Sharm el-Sheikh emergency summit in Egypt brokered by Clinton, a 48-hour end of hostilities was announced. This was later extended to Friday afternoon. However, an “Israeli” government spokesman Friday called the deadline "flexible".   UN Secretary General Kofi Annan, who spent 11 days in the Middle East during which he attended the Sharm summit, said the remaining hours before the deadline will be a crucial test of the truce. “Israel” had eased some of its blockade of the Palestinian territories, allowing flights to resume from the international airport in the Gaza Strip and opening a crossing point for freight from the West Bank.   “Israeli” Prime Minister Ehud Barak claimed the latest fighting was a "flagrant violation" by the Palestinians of the truce. But the Palestinian leadership, which met in Gaza under Arafat, accused “Israel” of not respecting the Sharm accord, in light of quote continued aggressions by “Israeli” forces and settlers. It urged intervention by the UN, Arab states, Annan, and the European Union. US National Security Adviser Sandy Berger warned the crisis is now the "most difficult and dangerous problem in the world". It is likely to get worse if Barak goes ahead with a plan to seal off Palestinian areas permanently.  Government sources in “Israel” said Barak and his military aides are discussing proposals for "unilateral separation" from the Palestinians.

Arab Foreign Ministers adopted a draft resolution calling for a halt to normalization with “Israel”…(12)
Arab Foreign Ministers, who held a preparatory meeting in Cairo before the Arab Summit, adopted a draft resolution which calls for the suspension of all sorts of normalization  relations with the Zionist Entity until the settlement process witness a tangible advance.

The Arab Foreign Ministers also agreed to adopt a draft resolution which states that an Arab Summit should be annually held in March.

The two draft resolutions will only enter into action if Arab leaders to meet in a 2-day Summit on Saturday and Sunday will give their accord.

The meeting also culminated in a strong-worded statement in which they demanded "“Israel”" indemnities for the martyrs and victims of the intifadah, and the formation of an investigation committee under the United Nations supervision.

Egyptian Foreign Minister Amr Moussa said, in a press conference, that the meeting's final statement was agreed upon by all the participants and that it deals with the ongoing situation and the deterioration of the "Peace" process, refusing to go into more details.

The Palestinian intifadah encouraged the Jordanian Syndicate Committee opposing normalization relations with "“Israel” to threaten to publish the names of those having links with the Zionist Entity.

This step comes while the Jordanian street is witnessing more and more demonstrations against the Sharm al-Sheikh Summit and in support of the intifadah.

“Israel” will not cooperate with UN inquiry into clashes with Palestinians; UN commission accuses “Israel” of "war crimes"…(14)

“Israel” vowed not to cooperate with a United Nations inquiry into “Israel’s” human rights violations. The UN Human Rights Commission yesterday adopted a resolution condemning the Jewish state of war crimes and crimes against humanity during the latest clashes with Palestinian demonstrators in the occupied territories. The resolution called for an independent human rights inquiry into the bloodshed. The draft condemned “Israeli” hawk Ariel Sharon's provocative visit to the al-Aqsa mosque compound, Islam's third holiest site, that triggered the deadly clashes. The vote was delayed for several hours as a compromise was sought by European countries. The US and European states voted against the resolution. The emergency session, called to condemn “Israel’s” brutal crackdown on Palestinians, is only the fifth in the Commission's history. The resolution requests an urgent visit by UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Mary Robinson to the region. “Israel” condemned the resolution as one-sided and inflammatory. 

“Israel” and us to vote against general assembly resolution condemning “Israel’s” excessive use of force against Palestinians…(15)

In New York the 189-member General Assembly passed a draft resolution condemning “Israel’s” excessive use of force against Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza Strip. The United States and “Israel” plan to vote against the resolution, claiming the text is one-sided. The European Union is trying to work out a text that waters down criticism against “Israel” ahead of its debate later tonight. UN chief Kofi Annan voiced support for Palestinians seeking UN action, despite efforts by Washington to keep the Mideast off the world body's agenda. Palestinian Representative Nasser al-Kidwa called for the special session after the American representative threatened to veto any resolution in the Security Council criticizing “Israel’s” use of excessive force against Palestinian protestors. Assembly resolutions are non-binding while many measures adopted by the fifteen-member Security Council are mandatory. The Security Council had passed a resolution condemning excessive use of force against Palestinians without naming “Israel”. The current draft before the General Assembly explicitly names “Israel” as an occupying force
