Lebanese civilian injured by “Israeli” landmine…(1)

“ A Lebanese civilian was seriously injured Friday when he stepped on a mine left from the “Israeli” occupation of southern Lebanon, police said.

The device went off when Hussam Mohammad Tabbaja, 26, stepped on it as he walked along the road leading to the border-region village of Hounine.

Tabbaja was taken to hospital, where he is in danger of needing to have both of his feet amputated, hospital officials said.

The number of accidents involving landmines and other booby traps in southern and eastern Lebanon has increased alarmingly since “Israeli” troops withdrew from the region on May 24 after a 22-year occupation.

Since then, nine civilians have been killed and 53 injured by exploding mines and booby-trapped objects.

The United Nations Interim Forces in Lebanon estimates that “Israel” left behind 130,000 mines in southern Lebanon.

“Israeli” jets stage mock air raids over south Lebanon

“ “Israeli” warplanes carried out mock air raids over this main port city and other regions of southern Lebanon Friday, AFP correspondents said.

At least four fighter-bombers dived low twice in a row over Tyre, breaking the sound barrier with loud booms and releasing flares in the sky, they said.

The jets also staged mock raids and provoked supersonic booms over the southern market town of Nabatiyeh and over Ramia, a border village from which Lebanese and Palestinians hurl projectiles at “Israeli” soldiers on the other side of the fence, they said.

“Israeli” planes also swooped over the coastal city of Sidon and the nearby mountainous region of Iqlim al-Tuffah..

No incident preceded the “Israeli” mock raids, which seemed to be a pre-emptive warning measure.

On Tuesday, “Israeli” aviation last broke the sound barrier across many regions in southern Lebanon before doing the same over the main northern coastal city of Tripoli.

And on Sunday, the warplanes caused supersonic booms over Beirut and in southern Lebanon.

Thirty-one Palestinians wounded in clashes with “Israeli” troops…(3)

At least 49 Palestinians were wounded by “Israeli” fire in scattered clashes in the West Bank and Gaza Strip, the day after “Israel’s” bombing raids on the Palestinian territories. “Israeli” tank crews fired machineguns in a clash that erupted during a rally in Ramallah, the West Bank town which “Israeli” helicopters rocketed yesterday. There was also fighting in Behtlehem, Hebron, and Jenin, as thousands of Palestinians took to the streets to denounce “Israeli” raids. In Al-Quds, “Israeli” police and Palestinians briefly clashed at the entrance of the Old City. 

“Israeli” police clashed with Palestinians at the entrance to Al-Quds's Old City amid heightened security following a major “Israeli” onslaught on the West Bank and Gaza Strip that lasted until late night hours. Clashes were sparked today when an “Israeli” policeman hit a young Palestinian who was protesting against a ban on all male worshippers below the age of 45 from attending Friday prayers in the al-Aqsa mosque. Youths denied access to the mosque prayed on the street. “Israel” kept the Palestinians territories sealed, while soldiers checked papers for everyone entering. The situation had deteriorated sharply when “Israel” rocketed Palestinian towns and villages in Gaza And the West Bank, following the killing of two of its soldiers by an angry crowd in Ramallah. At least 36 Palestinians were injured in the “Israeli” offensive. Helicopter gunships had also pounded Palestinian leader Yasser Arafat's headquarters in Gaza. The uprising continued overnight. Exchanges of fire in the West Bank town of Hebron were reported. “Israeli” helicopters fired rockets and guns on several towns in the West bank. Four rockets slammed into a Palestinian police academy in Jericho. There were no reports of injuries. “Israeli” helicopters also pounded Nablus and Hebron with machine gunfire, hours after the Jewish state announced an end to the military campaign, which it described as a symbolic operation. Jewish settlers joined in the attacks. They went on a rampage near Hebron, shooting and wounding three Palestinians. One Palestinian was shot in the head by a mob of settlers, who also set fire to Palestinian owned vehicles. One “Israeli” citizen was injured by stones in Qalqilya and one “Israeli” border guard was slightly injured in the Gaza Strip. Also in Gaza, thousands of Palestinians marched through the streets protesting the raids and calling for resistance against “Israel”. Bloodshed over the past two weeks was triggered by “Israeli” hawk Ariel Sharon's visit to the al-Aqsa mosque compound. 

Lebanon's Hoss says “Israel” behaving like "gangsters" with Palestinians…(4)

“ Lebanese Prime Minister Salim Hoss denounced “Israel” Friday for behaving like "gangsters" toward the Palestinians who, he said, have the right to their own country, with Al-Quds as its capital.

"“Israel” has showed again that there is no limit to its savageness. As usual, “Israel” does not behave like a state but like gangsters," Hoss said in a statement.

"World capitals denounce terrorism and turn a blind eye on the terrorism carried out by “Israel” against the people whose rights it had violated and refuses to return any of its rights," he said.

Hoss said "it is time that the international community realizes that the Palestinian people have human rights in their homeland."

"They (Palestinians) have the right for self-determination, to have a country with its capital Al-Quds on their land and that displaced refugees have the right to return home," he said.

"The issue in occupied Palestine is not a security problem as some activists working to calm the situation want to portray it. It is the issue of a people's right for life," he added.

On Thursday, “Israel” carried out air, sea and ground attacks against Palestinian targets, in a step closer to all-out war, and decided to set up an emergency government with hardliners after three “Israelis”, including at least two soldiers, were lynched.

 Islamic Jihad Movement in Palestine issued a statement in which it demands the Palestinian Authority to arm all the Palestinian people and to announce a large-scale armed struggle against the occupiers. Arab street demands new holy war…(6)

“Morocco recalled its diplomatic envoy from Tel Aviv on Friday and Qatar came under pressure to sever its low-level ties with “Israel” as angry crowds across the Arab world took to the streets, demanding open borders for a holy war against the Jewish state.
The mounting outrage over “Israel’s” onslaught against the Palestinians clashed with the Western world’s sympathies for “Israel”, dimming the prospects of a coordinated international effort to spare the Middle East another full-blown war.
Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Syria spearheaded regional diplomatic efforts to head off a spread of violence to other countries, and governments found themselves caught between inclinations to moderation and calls in the streets for a jihad, or holy war.
“Following the latest events in the Palestinian territories, Morocco has recalled its diplomatic representative in Tel Aviv, Talal Ghoufrani, for consultation, pending  assessment of the dangerous situation in the region,” an official announcement said in Rabat.
Egyptian Foreign Minister Amr Moussa flew to Saudi Arabia as the streets of Cairo filled with demonstrators calling for war against “Israel” ­ the first time such hard-line slogans have been raised in Egypt since the 1979 Camp David peace treaty.
Moussa was joined in Riyadh by his Syrian counterpart, Farouk al-Sharaa and they headed straight to a closed-door meeting with Saudi Arabia’s foreign minister Prince Saud al-Faisal.
Thousands of protesters rushed out of Friday prayers at Cairo’s Al-Azhar Mosque to march in the streets to denounce “Israel’s” air, land and sea attacks against Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza which followed the killing of two “Israeli” soldiers in Ramallah on Thursday.
“Where is the Egyptian Army?” they shouted as they burned the “Israeli” and US flags. “Jihad, jihad, allow us to go to jihad,” they cried repeatedly.
In one incident, protesters who ran into one a side street started throwing stones at police when they saw one of their number getting arrested. Police released the man and the stoning stopped.
Last week, Egyptian riot police blocked all doors of the historic Al-Azhar and banned protesters from marching in the street. But shocking television scenes of “Israeli” attacks and the public rage it has drawn apparently convinced security bodies in Egypt and elsewhere in the Arab world to allow the masses to vent their frustrations.
The mood in Jordan, which made peace with “Israel” in 1994, was similar, and security forces displayed marked tolerance.
More than 3,000 people, including Islamist and opposition leaders, marched to the prime minister’s office, demanding the ouster of “Israel’s” ambassador to Amman. They tried to march on the heavily fortified “Israeli” Embassy but were dispersed by police who threatened them with clubs. But there was no repeat of the violence of the previous Friday, when police used tear gas.
The leader of the Islamic Action Front, Hammam Saeed, called for jihad in a sermon. “O Abdullah, O Abu Hussein, open up those bridges for us to launch a jihad,” he said in an appeal to Jordan’s monarch.
Protesters in Oman, which severed its commercial links with “Israel” on Thursday after “Israel” escalated its assaults on the Palestinians, demanded that other Arab states follow suit.
“It is an unforgivable sin for Muslims to stand and watch the Palestinian massacre at the
hands of “Israel”,” one cleric told worshippers during a sermon in Muscat. “A few million “Israelis” cannot stand a chance against 1 billion Muslims when united.”
Demonstrators called for Qatar to follow in Oman’s footsteps. “If Oman has done it than Qatar must do it,” they shouted.
Omani Minister of State for Foreign Affairs Youssef bin Alawi bin Abdullah said: “What is happening now has nothing to do with peace. It is a case of “Israeli” military pressure on Arabs to surrender.”
In Syria,  police used tear gas to disperse some 2,000 angry demonstrators who tried to reach the US Embassy in Damascus, but allowed protests elsewhere in the capital. The demonstrators implored presidents Bashar Assad and Emile Lahoud to open the borders for attacks on the Jewish state, and  burned US and “Israeli” flags.  They also set alight an effigy of Likud leader Ariel Sharon, whose visit to Al-Haram Al-Sharif ­ known to Jews as the Temple Mount ­ on Sept. 28 sparked the violence.
Damascus Radio warned that Barak’s proposal to form a unity government with the hard-line Likud “will produce no result other than a hardening in the Arab position.”
It also criticized Washington for “not being up to the responsibilities demanded of it in the peace process, because of its flagrant alignment with “Israel”.”
Iran said Arabs had a duty to sever all links to “Israel”.
“The Prophet said no one is a true Muslim if he ignores a call for help. The oppressed Palestinians are at this very moment crying out for help from Muslims,” Ayatollah Ahmad Jannati told worshippers during Friday prayers at Tehran University. “The least that is expected of Islamic countries is to cut relations with “Israel”.”
Iran’s Foreign Ministry urged Islamic countries “to give wide support to Palestinians to help them achieve their rights.”
Ministry spokesman Hamid Reza Asefi said Tehran “expects international bodies to take serious measures to prevent widespread attacks by the Zionist military against innocent Palestinian civilians.”
Libyan leader Moammar Gadhafi said he doubted that Arab leaders would meet the aspirations of the masses at next week’s summit in Cairo.
“I’m not convinced of the usefulness of a summit,” he said in Riyadh, his first official trip to the kingdom in 20 years.
Cyprus, which has often been struck by ricochets from the Arab-”Israeli” conflict, tightened security around the “Israeli” Embassy and other diplomatic missions in Nicosia. It also offered to host a proposed “Israeli”-Palestinian summit sponsored by Egypt and the US.


Arabs ‘must unite’ against “Israel”…(7)

Calls on the Arabs to use the oil weapon and economic clout to counter “Israeli” attacks on Palestinians resounded across Lebanon on Friday, along with a denunciation of all ties and negotiations with “Israel” and demands for a boycott on American goods.
In a statement, the Islamic Tawhid Movement urged Arab and Muslim states to resort to the “oil weapon, this being capable of humiliating the American administration and compelling it to relinquish its bias in favor of the Zionist enemy.”
It said the region “will never experience security and stability as long as the enemy lurks in the midst of our nation, backed by various methods of American arrogance.”
It added that because of this, a “top priority for the upcoming Arab summit should be to adopt resolutions meeting the wishes of the proud people, the least of which is shutting down Zionist diplomatic and economic missions.”
The vice-chairman of the Higher Shiite Council, Sheikh Abdel-Amir Qabalan, strongly condemned what he described as criminal “Israeli” attacks on Palestinians, and called on the Arabs to unite.
He said the Arabs possessed “pressuring economic power which could be exploited in the battle of liberation, defending holy places, and forcing the international community to denounce “Israel”.”
Qabalan said the Arab region had “awoken from its deep slumber, thanks to the heroic resistance demonstrated by the Lebanese people, culminating in the victory scored against the “Israeli” enemy and liberation of the land.”
This is also manifested, he said, in the “struggle waged by the Palestinian people, their resistance and intifada in defense of the holy sites, and Arab dignity.”
He urged Arabs to persist in their demonstrations of support in every Arab and Muslim capital “or wherever they may be.” Qabalan also called for “severing ties established by certain Arabs with the Zionist entity and stopping all forms of normalization.”
He criticized UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan for “adopting a stand contrary to fact by announcing that the resistance operation in the Lebanese Shibaa Farms” ­ culminating in the capture of three “Israeli” soldiers ­ “was a violation of Resolution 425.”
Qabalan added: “Such a stand by the head of the highest international organization defending “Israel”, the aggressor state, calls for surprise … having forgotten that it was “Israel” which has not implemented UN Resolutions ever since these were adopted.”
He pressed Annan to “go back on his stand and tell the truth as it is, without bias in favor of “Israel”, the aggressor.”
The Amal Movement, meanwhile, urged the international community and the UN Security Council to adopt “immediate measures to stop the barbaric “Israeli” onslaught and aggressive operations against unarmed civilians.” It also called for the formation of an international court to “bring “Israel” and its rulers to justice for committing war crimes and massacres ever since the usurping entity was created.”
Amal called for a united Arab stand, immediately stopping all sorts of normalization with “Israel” and holding an Arab summit to lay the groundwork for “a strategy of resistance that will put an end to the Zionist enemy’s arrogance.”

Muslim protesters burn “Israeli” flags as they clash with police…(9)

 “In London, about 300 Muslims scuffled with police outside the Central Mosque in Regent's Park during a demonstration in which “Israeli” and American flags were burnt.

The protest was organised by the Al-Muhajiroun group, whose leader, Syrian-born Omar Bakri Mohammed, said he knew which "freedom fighters" were involved in the Yemeni attacks, and that others would follow.

He told the crowd: "All “Israeli” targets are legitimate for you. All “Israelis” must be destroyed. The American and British governments are acting to destroy Islam."

After the bombing of the British embassy in Yemen security around potential Jewish and “Israeli” targets in the UK was tightened. Police stepped up surveillance of Middle Eastern suspects in cities across Britain. Police and volunteers have increased patrols at synagogues and other Jewish community centres.

Amnesty concerned by “Israeli” attacks on Palestinians…(10)

International rights group Amnesty International has expressed grave concern for the safety of Palestinian civilians following “Israeli” attacks on the occupied territories. Amnesty said it received reports of civilian casualties and called for the UN to establish an independent international investigation into all allegations of serious human rights violations taking place in the region. Another report published by three European human rights groups accused “Israel” of using disproportionate force against civilians. It blasted “Israel” for resorting to live fire rather than conventional anti-riot equipment, which the report said resulted in widespread death and injuries among Palestinians. It charged “Israel” with using illegal dum-dum bullets. The report, compiled by a four-man investigative mission, which toured the West Bank, Gaza Strip and Arab towns in “Israel”, said the Jewish state used explosive bullets against peaceful protestors and local residents. The report blamed “Israel” for terrorizing civilians, attacking ambulances and peaceful individuals. The mission said Palestinians resorted to mainly stone-throwing and limited use of Molotov cocktails and firearms.
