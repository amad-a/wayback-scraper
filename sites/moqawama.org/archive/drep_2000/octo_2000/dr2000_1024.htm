 Thurs2ay 29-2000

 

“Israelis” trespass to erect fence…(1)

About 10 “Israeli” workers entered Lebanon on Monday to put up a fence along the 1-kilometer cement border divider from Manara settlement to Hounin Hill north of Abbad.
Several “Israeli” soldiers were deployed along the divider and patroled from Hamames Hill to the Fatima Gate-Kfar Kila road on the “Israeli” side. Two Ghanaian UNIFIL soldiers watched the “Israeli” works without interfering.
When asked about the “Israeli” trespassing, one UN officer told The Daily Star that the “Israeli” workers would not stay more than a few minutes.
Four Hizbullah members observed the developments from their position facing Abbad.
A 3-meter space was left open in the fence. It is believed that an iron gate, seen on the “Israeli” side near Hounin Hill, will be added to complete the fence.
A few Lebanese exchanged insults with the “Israeli” soldiers in Abbad while a UNIFIL helicopter conducted its daily patrol in the area.

Death of three Palestinians injured by “Israeli” gunfire as “Israel” deploys troops to seal off Arab village…(2)

“Israel” has closed Gaza's international airport for the second time since the start of the uprising almost four weeks ago. The “Israeli” military also threatened to surround the West Bank town of Beit Jala near Al-Quds after “Israeli” helicopters pounded the area last night.  Tensions have been simmering in the region following the death of another two Palestinian teenagers who were injured by “Israeli” gunfire.  A short while ago, Palestine Television reported that a fifty-three-year old Palestinian woman was martyred by “Israeli” troops in the West Bank town of Tulkarm. Officials said the woman was shot while picking olives. She was the first woman to be martyred by “Israelis” in 25 days of Intifada. Newsweek magazine said the “Israeli” army drafted a plan to reoccupy large parts of Palestinian-controlled land if the Middle East peace process collapses. President Yasser Arafat warned current clashes and tensions could unleash a war which no one can stop. 

“ “Israel” has deployed troops seal off the Palestinian area of Beit Jala near the West Bank town Bethlehem. Palestinian civilians were warned by “Israeli” military officials to evacuate their homes. “Israeli” helicopter gunships had pounded rockets at the Palestinian-controlled village overnight in the fiercest fighting around Al-Quds since Palestinian territories and Arab parts of “Israel” were plunged into bloodshed more than three weeks ago. A factory and three buildings were destroyed. Telephone and electricity lines were also hit, sinking the village and Bethlehem into darkness. Ten Palestinians were wounded in the attack. Hundreds of Palestinian civilians from Beit Jalla and the nearby Aida refugee camp managed to flee the besieged area. “Israeli” military officials claimed shots were fired from the area at a Jewish settlement in Gilo. Officials warned they have the green light to react with "all means." [Lieutenant Commander Raanan Gissin, “Israeli” (War) Force spokesman, "If anyone in the Palestinian Authority believes, or thinks, that he is going to change Al-Quds into Beirut, then we have some surprises for him because Beit Jalla and Beit Sahour and other places will turn into Beirut faster than Al-Quds. Jewish setters from Gilo cheered each attack on the Palestinian towns. In Gaza, Palestinians continued to bury their dead. In an outpouring of grief and anger, Palestinians buried 15-year-old Salah Nijem who died overnight. “Israeli” soldiers shot Nijem in the heart with a live bullet during clashes near the “Israeli” settlement of Kfar Darom. The Palestinian death toll continues to rise. Two Palestinian youths hit by “Israeli” gunfire last week today died of their injuries. “Israeli” troops also killed four Palestinians, including a 14-year-old in the West Bank and Gaza Strip yesterday. “Israeli” gunfire did not spare journalists. A reporter from the French weekly Paris Match, who was seriously wounded by “Israeli” gunfire in Ramallah, was evacuated for treatment in Paris. “Israeli” troops shot Jacques-Marie Bourget in the left lung as he was covering clashes. 

Iranian Parliament called on Arab countries to cut their relations with “Israel”…(3)

The Parliament of the Islamic Republic of Iran  called on the Islamic and Arab countries to cut their relations with "“Israel”" in protest against the “Israeli” repression of the Palestinian people.

The parliament stressed that the only way the Palestinians can obtain their rights is through the intifada.  The Iranian deputies also urged the world community to hold a conference in defense of the intifada.  Iran, which holds the periodical presidency of the Islamic Organization Conference had called for an urgent meeting of the foreign ministers of the Organization to discuss the latest developments in Palestinian territories.
