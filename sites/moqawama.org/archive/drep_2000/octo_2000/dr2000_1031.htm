Imam Khamenei urges muslims to support the Palestinian struggle…(1)

The Leader of the Islamic Nation Imam Sayyed Ali Khamenei called on the Islamic world to support the Palestinians, and not leave them combat the enemy alone. In a sitting with the participants in the 17th International contest on Recitation Memorization and Interpretation of the Holy Quraan held in Tehran, Sayyed Ali Khamenei said that the Palestinian people are fighting the enemy in the name of Islam and the Holy Quran, and this plants fear and horror in the enemy's heart.And since the Quran wasn't at the core of their struggle in the last 50 years, they failed to achieve something.Imam Khamenei asserted that the track chosen by the Palestinian fighters will surely lead to victory. Palestinians, in this manner, are using a shortcut to recuperate their rights,said Khamenei.

 

Cairo and Amman should expel “Israeli” ambassadors: Hizbullah…(2)

“Hizbullah called on Egyptians and Jordanians Monday to pressure their governments to expel the “Israeli” ambassadors in their countries.

"The Egyptian people, in all sectors, are called upon to put pressure on the government until it has broken relations with “Israel” and expelled the “Israeli” ambassador" in Cairo, said Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, according to local press reports.

"The Jordanian people must also assume their responsibility if they want to deliver their support to the Palestinian people," engaged in a bloody revolt against the “Israeli” army in east Al-Quds, West Bank and Gaza Strip, he added.

According to chief, "the breaking of relations between Egypt, which has a special place in the Arab world, and “Israel” will have more weight than any other breaking (of relations)."

If Egypt broke its ties with “Israel” it would "have a greater impact than dozens of bombs dropped on the “Israelis”," he said.

Egypt and Jordan are the only two Arab countries to have signed peace treaties with “Israel”. Cairo signed its accord in 1979, followed by Amman in 1994.

The Arab summit held October 21-22 in Cairo in solidarity with the Palestinians did not call on either country to end the relations.

Nasrallah also said Qatar should close the “Israeli” trade office in Doha or be barred from assuming the presidency of the Organisation of the Islamic Conference later this month.

Qatar's Foreign Minister Sheikh Hamad bin Jasem el-Thani said recently that the country would study a possible closing of the “Israeli” office.

Oman, the only other Gulf Arab country with an “Israeli” trade post, announced October 12 that it was closing down the office in Muscat and its reciprocal office in Tel Aviv in light of the Palestinian uprising.

The Palestinian uprising against “Israel” which began on September 28 has claimed the lives of more than 150 people and injured well over 4,000 others, the vast majority of them Arabs.

Morocco and Tunisia also announced this month that they would close down their interests sections in Tel Aviv and “Israeli” offices in Rabat and Tunis.

The only Arab country with full diplomatic relations with “Israel” apart from Egypt and Jordan is Mauritania.

 

“Israelis” will shoot first, ask questions later
Jewish state warns UNIFIL of hair trigger on border…(3)

““Israel” has informed UNIFIL that it will open fire at any “suspicious movement” on the Lebanese side of the border, a decision certain to increase tensions.
“They excluded UNIFIL and Lebanese security forces,” a UNIFIL officer said. “Anyone else wandering along the border will have to be careful.”
Previously, “Israeli” troops were under instructions to open fire only if their lives were directly threatened. The new shoot-to-kill policy has been matched in recent days by a barrage of warnings against Syria to curb cross-border attacks. The increasingly harsh tone of the military ­ toward the Palestinians as well as Syria and Lebanon ­ is being interpreted as preparing the “Israeli” public for the possibility of a second front being opened along the Lebanese-”Israeli” border.
“Israel’s” saber-rattling comes amid indications that Hizbullah is conducting a substantial re-armament, fueling concern that events in the South may run out of control. Security sources have confirmed to The Daily Star that Hizbullah is receiving substantial numbers of mortars and Katyusha rockets which are being dispersed throughout the South. Some of the weapons are being stored in former “Israeli” Army and its militia outposts.
“This situation can get out of hand very quickly. That’s why it’s very dangerous to mess around on the border,” a senior security source said.
“Israel’s” deputy defense (war) minister, Ephraim Sneh, claimed on Monday that Syria has given Hizbullah the green light to carry out cross-border attacks, and warned of retaliation.
“I think that (Syrian President) Bashar Assad is aware of the differences in strength between “Israel” and Syria,” Sneh told “Israel’s” Army Radio. “If there is a renewal of terrorist activity, we will stop it immediately and through more severe actions.”
“Israel” has been unhappy with the security situation along the border for months. In particular, “Israel” has lodged numerous protests over stone-throwing at the Fatima Gate in Kfar Kila and elsewhere along the border.
Although stone-throwing has decreased lately, the Palestinian intifada has fueled more serious incidents: Hizbullah’s capture of three “Israeli” soldiers on Oct. 7; the failed cross-border incursion near Majidieh by Palestinian militants on Oct. 20; last week’s shooting at Marwahine; and a second cross-border shooting claimed by the “Israelis” on Sunday in the same area ­ of which UNIFIL and Lebanese security sources have no knowledge.
The decision to refrain from retaliating for these incidents has angered the “Israeli” Army, which fears its deterrent value is being undermined. Amir Oren wrote in the Haaretz newspaper that Hizbullah’s actions along the border were causing “the blood of senior … officers to boil.”
“Israel’s” chief of staff, Lieutenant General Shaul Mofaz, was quoted as telling his officers during a tour of the border on Sunday that the military “must hit” Hizbullah’s secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, “and his cohorts.”
“The “Israelis” are creating the atmosphere first,” the security source said. “They’re preparing the country for violence.”
The source added that if “Israel” is intent on launching a punitive military action in Lebanon “any excuse will do.”
“Israeli” military intelligence has repeatedly stated that Hizbullah and Palestinian groups are preparing to launch attacks into “Israel” from the border.
But security officials consider a coordinated guerrilla campaign unlikely. Hizbullah is not expected to initiate conflict along the border.
Instead, it is the random “lone wolf” incident ­ such as the ill-conceived Palestinian cross-border raid and the hunter firing his shotgun at an “Israel” patrol ­ that is giving cause for concern.
If “Israel” seizes upon another “lone wolf” operation to justify a military strike, Hizbullah will almost certainly reply in kind. The stockpiling of weapons suggests that the resistance is anticipating such an outcome.

 

Germany is for a mediation role for “Israeli”-Lebanese prisoner swapping…(4)

German chancellor Gerhard Shroedor left Beirut for Amman, his third stop after Lebanon and Egypt in a six-stage tour of the Middle East.

During his breif visit to Beirut Shroeder met Speaker Nabih Berri, who expressed Germany's willingness to lead negotiations for a prisoner swap, but did not want to discuss it publicly.

Hizbullah captured three “Israeli” soldiers on October 7, as they patrolled occupied Shebaa Farms. Later on, an “Israeli” colonel working for the Mossad was also captured by Hizbullah, which is demanding the release of all Lebanese prisoners held in “Israeli” prisons.

Berri pointed out that his talks with Shroeder tackled economic and political issues.He reiterated to Shroader Lebanon's stand which considers the implementation of Resolution 425 incomplete.Shroeder supported the Lebaense position, regarding the “Israeli” occupation of Shebaa Farms.

 

“Israeli” soldier killed in occupied east Al-Quds; seven Palestinians wounded by “Israeli” gunfire…(5)

One of two “Israeli” security guards shot at an “Israeli” government building in occupied East Al-Quds has died of his injuries. Witnesses were quoted as saying a youth, probably a Palestinian, shot the two men at close range. In a statement faxed to AFP in Beirut, an underground group claimed responsibility for the shooting. “Israeli” troops had stepped up attacks on the Palestinian territories. Three Palestinians were injured when “Israeli” forces fired shells and rockets on Rafah, a town in the southern Gaza Strip, although the Jewish state denied bombing the region. One “Israeli” soldier was earlier injured in Rafah by a roadside bomb. Four Palestinians were wounded in the town when “Israeli” soldiers opened fire on demonstrators. The Union of Palestinian Medical Relief committees has accused the “Israeli” army of quote assassinating Palestinian demonstrators by deliberately shooting them in the head. 

Palestinians prepared to bury their dead. In the Gaza Strip, thousands attended two separate funerals of Palestinians shot dead by “Israeli” soldiers. Five Palestinians were killed by “Israeli” troops on Sunday, including a 14-year-old boy. At least eighty were wounded, some by “Israeli” tank machine-gun blasts. Another Palestinian youth was found dead last night. He was killed along with his brother in the West Bank, raising the Palestinian death toll to at least 151. Meanwhile, a young Jewish settler was found killed on the edge of Al-Quds. The circumstances of his death remain unknown. Fighting again erupted today. One “Israeli” policeman is in serious condition after being shot in occupied East Al-Quds. Another policeman has died of his injuries. An “Israeli” army officer was also lightly wounded in Rafah when a bomb detonated near a military convoy escorting Jewish settlers. A group calling itself the Martyr Omar al-Mukhtar claimed responsibility for the attack, prompting warnings from  “Israel’s” army chief of staff Shaul Mofaz that his soldiers would take what he described as the "initiative" against Palestinian demonstrators. Deadly clashes gripping the occupied territories and Arab towns in “Israel” have pitted Palestinian demonstrators armed with little more than stones and Molotov cocktails-on the rare occasion firearms- against “Israeli” tanks bristling with machineguns, automatic weapons and helicopters. Clashes erupted when “Israeli” hawk Ariel Sharon visited the al-Aqsa mosque compound, Islam's third holiest site about a month ago. Palestinian President Yasser Arafat has vowed the intifada or uprising will continue against “Israeli” repression and occupation until Palestinians regain sovereignty over occupied East Al-Quds and create a capital for a future state in the city. 

The Intifadah heavy on “Israeli” economy…(6)

The intifadah's massive impact on the expected growth average of the “Israeli” economy is becoming quite clear.

“Israeli” Finance Minister Ibraham Shohat said that his ministry diminished its expectations with respect to the growth average for next year from 5% to 4.5%, taking into consideration the Palestinian Intifadah's impact on Tourism and construction sectors. Shohat called for a financial policy of abstinence to preserve this average until year 2003.
