Islamic Jihad Group announced its responsibility for the first martyrdom operation in the recent intifadah…(1)

A Palestinian executed Thursday the first martyrdom operation against “Israeli” forces, since the beginning of the intifadah nearly a month ago.

The Islamic Jihad Movement announced it was responsible for the operation, and said in a statement that the "Quds Brigade", the military arm of the movement, carried out the attack.

The statement said that the bomber is Martyr Nabil al-Arair, who blew up himself at an “Israeli” military post, close to Ksovin Jewish settlement in Gaza Strip, said the Islamic Jihad.

"Several “Israeli” soldiers were wounded in the attack, which will not be the last," said the Islamic Jihad.

Frightened by the attack, the “Israeli” occupation forces said that a young Palestinian bicycle rider blew up himself in front of the military post.

“Israeli” General Yom Tof Samia, army commander of the South, clarified that an “Israeli” soldier was lightly wounded in the attack.

Fearing more such attacks, “Israeli” authorities strengthened the closure of the West Bank and Gaza Strip, accusing the Palestinian Authority of having given the green light for the operation.

"We think these are the people who were released by the Palestinian authorities only a couple of weeks ago," “Israeli” army spokesman Yarder Vatikay said..

“Israel” calls up reserves after martyrdom bombing…(2)

The month-long uprising that has killed 120 Palestinians and eight “Israelis” in the West Bank and Gaza took an ominous turn yesterday when a24-year-old Arab blew himself up at an “Israeli” checkpoint near a Jewish settlement in the Gaza Strip. One soldier was slightly wounded.

The apparent martyrdom bombing reinforced increasingly strident “Israeli” warnings of such attacks, inside “Israel” as well as in the occupied territories. Armed police and troops were patrolling the main streets of Jewish towns last night. In Al-Quds they were checking the papers of Arabs from the eastern side of the city who were still going to work for “Israeli” employers. For the first time since the Intifada erupted on 28 September, “Israel” is calling up reserve army units to take up the strain.

The Gaza bomber, identified as Nabil al-Arair, died after detonating an explosive-laden bicycle. Relatives confirmed that he was a member of the militant Islamic Jihad group, which yesterday marked the fifth anniversary of the assassination of its leader, Fathi Shqaqi, by “Israeli” agents in Malta. "We are very proud of what he has done," said a cousin, Abdel-Rahim al-Arair.

Both Jihad and the larger Hamas movement have vowed to continue their "armed struggle" for a Muslim state in the whole of “Israel” and the Palestinian territories.

 

A Jordanian soldier shot dead two-”Israeli” soldiers…(3)

A Jordanian source revealed to the al-Sharq Newspaper that a Jordanian soldier, called Maher Rasmi al-Bourini, killed two “Israeli” soldiers while he was patrolling the Jordanian-Palestinian border. The source said that the Jordanian soldier opened fire after the “Israeli” soldiers on the other side of the border opened fire at him.

The Jordanian authorities first thought the incident was that of a Palestinian fighter infiltrating into "“Israel”" across the Jordanian border.  The details of the incident was only known after the Jordanian soldier was captured at his home and interrogated..

 

Hamas condemns U.S. attempts to abort the intifadah and abait the Palestinian authority…(4)

The American administration warned the Palestinian Authority against getting involved in any cooperation with  groups opposing the "peace" process.

The U.S. is trying to get the President of the Palestinian Authority Yasser Arafat and the “Israeli” PM Ehud Barak to travel to Washington, to put an end to the intifadah. Nevertheless, the U.S. president Bill Clinton insinuated that Arafat constitutes the major obstacle to resuming the "peace" process. Reports said that Clinton will meet both Barak and Arafat at the beginning of November.

Hamas Movement condemned, in a statement, the resuming of meetings between Palestinian security chiefs and “Israeli” generals under the auspices of U.S. intelligence generals.  Hamas considered that these meetings neglected the  martyrs and wounded of the intifadah. Hamas also condemned Clinton for trying to abort the intifadah and push the Palestinian Authority to yield to Barak's conditions.

In addition, Hamas called for the pursuing of the Intifada and its escalation using all possible means.

"Tomorrow (Friday) should be a day of anger and revenge", said Hamas movement.

 

More confrontations in occupied Palestine and a 16-year old Palestininian was buried…(5)

Clashes in Palestinian territories erupted again, after “Israeli” tanks hit Wednesday night the  village of Beit Jala in West Bank. An inhabited house was shelled.

The Palestinians answered by opening fire on an “Israeli” bus inside the Jilo colony built on Palestinian land, which was seized by “Israeli” authorities in 1972.

Moreover, the occupation army shelled the village of Beit Sahour in the West Bank. Gunfire was traded between Palestinians and “Israeli” soldiers positioned in their military post. In Ramallah, the funeral, for a 16 year-old martyr shot by the “Israeli” soldiers in Erez crossing, turned into clashes.

Meanwhile, Aqsa Martyrs Group announced in a statement sent to al-Manar T.V. that it targeted two “Israeli” posts in Nablus and two military patrols in Ramallah.
