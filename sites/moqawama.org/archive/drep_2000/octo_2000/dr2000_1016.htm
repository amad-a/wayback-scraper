Hizbullah captures “Israeli” colonel…(1)

Nasrallah: It is our second gift to Arab hostages & Palestinians Intifada

Hizbullah tightened the screws on “Israel” on Sunday a day before a key summit in Egypt by announcing that the party had captured an “Israeli” Army colonel in a “complex security operation.”
“With God’s help, I’m honored to inform you gladly that the Islamic Resistance performed a complex security operation, capturing an “Israeli” colonel who works for one of the “Israeli” security agencies,” Nasrallah told a stunned audience during an urgent joint session of the Arab Nationalist and Islamic Nationalist Conferences held at the Carlton Hotel.
“Israel” Radio later identified the abducted man as Elhanan Tennenbaum, a 54-year-old reserve air-force colonel involved in “Israel’s” arms industry. He was reportedly seized in Switzerland.
Nasrallah said the latest Hizbullah accomplishment was “our second gift to the people, the martyrs and the wounded of the intifada and to every detainee in enemy prisons and their families who are waiting to meet their loved ones.”
Neither Nasrallah nor any other Hizbullah official would give further details. The resistance leader promised to announce “the appropriate details at the appropriate time.”
“Let’s leave the “Israelis” in confusion,” he said. “We’ll leave them to search for him, for his name, for his tale.”
The crowd of more than 180 participants at the conference rose in applause, some chanting: “Allahu Akbar.”
Prime Minister Salim Hoss, who was at the conference, seemed taken aback by the news.
Nasrallah extended his sympathies to Hoss, saying: “God help the premier today in dealing with the many telephone calls he will get from (US Secretary of State Madeleine) Albright.”
While the conference ­ which was organized to support the intifada in the Palestinian territories ­ continued, Hoss and Nasrallah, refused to make any comment as they left the meeting.
Responding to initial “Israeli” confusion over the identity of the kidnapped officer, Nawaf Musawi, a member of Hizbullah’s politburo, said “Israel” was known to “give different misleading versions when it doesn’t know what is really happening.”
“As for us, Hizbullah has enough credibility that allows people to believe what we say,” Musawi told The Daily Star.
Asked about the possibility of more tension near the border, Musawi warned “Israel” against staging an attack.
“Any attack will be reciprocated harshly and comprehensively. The “Israelis” should think 100 times before they make a mistake of this sort,” he said.
Jordanian opposition official Laith Shbeilat said that once again, Hizbullah proved that it could conquer the enemy.
“This capture of an “Israeli” colonel,” he said, “is a true gift of support to the Palestinians.”

 

Hizbullah’s latest intelligence coup…(2)
Several hours of confusion in "Israel”

Hizbullah appears to have scored an elaborate intelligence coup by netting a reservist colonel in the “Israeli” Air Force who works for an electronics firm supplying the military with sensitive equipment.
In response to Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah’s announcement on Sunday that another “Israeli” soldier had been captured by the party, “Israel’s” Defense (War) Ministry confirmed that an “Israeli” businessman, an officer in the air-force reserve, has been abducted abroad.
“He had gone abroad on his own initiative for personal reasons,” the ministry said, apparently trying to downplay speculation that the colonel was an intelligence agent.
“It’s a serious act, contrary to all international law,” the ministry added.
According to “Israel” Radio, the abducted man is a 54-year-old reserve air-force colonel named Elhanan Tennenbaum employed by a consultancy linked to two of “Israel’s” biggest electronic and arms companies, Tadiran and Rafael.
He was seized in the Swiss city of Lausanne, the radio said.
“Israel’s” Channel Two Television quoted an unidentified “Israeli” official as saying the man was a businessman kidnapped by partners in a business deal in Europe, and that he was transferred to Hizbullah.
Hizbullah is believed to have cells active throughout Europe and North and South America, primarily charged with raising and channeling funds to Lebanon as well as carrying out intelligence-gathering operations and weapons purchases.
In the absence of additional information from Hizbullah, the circumstances surrounding the abduction remain unclear.
A Lebanese military source said the kidnapping occurred a week ago, allowing time for Hizbullah activists to smuggle the “Israeli” to Lebanon before Nasrallah made his announcement.
Hizbullah fighters operating along the border on Saturday were given advance warning that an important announcement was imminent, the source said. The fighters were instructed to be on their guard against retaliatory cross-border operations by “Israeli” commandos.
There were several hours of confusion in “Israel” before confirmation that a reservist colonel was missing. Initial speculation suggested that the colonel was possibly an “Israeli” Muslim or Druze and had tured himself in to Hizbullah. However, the colonel’s alleged name, Elhanan Tennenbaum, would appear to suggest that he is Jewish.
If the “Israeli” was in fact kidnapped and smuggled to Lebanon, Hizbullah has increased the pressure on “Israel” ­ either to grant more concessions to Hizbullah in exchange for the hostages or to consider a military response.
Neither option is attractive to “Israel”. Releasing possibly hundreds of Arab prisoners would send all the wrong signals to the Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza. The Palestinians already hold Hizbullah in high regard and listen devotedly to every word Nasrallah says.
Yet a military response would ultimately be self-defeating.
Hizbullah will not release the hostages until their demands are fulfilled, regardless of threats. If “Israel” attacks infrastructure targets, Hizbullah will retaliate with rocket attacks into Galilee. Targeting Syrian military positions in Lebanon, as “Israel” has threatened, would be equally futile and would bring the region closer to war.
Hizbullah would appear to be in a strong tactical position. Not only has it acquired four valuable “Israeli” hostages ­ the party may have also tapped into a useful source of intelligence data. Tennenbaum worked with two of “Israel’s” key military suppliers.
Tadiran manufactures tactical communications systems in use with defense forces worldwide including the US Army.
The state-owned Rafael armaments company specializes in missile development as well as communications. It developed the NT Dandy, a long-range anti-tank missile which was test-fired in south Lebanon on several occasions during the occupation.

 

Hizbullah revels in “Israeli” colonel capturing 
Conlonel was sent to discover where abouts of three captured “Israeli” soldiers

It started at 10am yesterday when Sayed Hassan Nasrallah, the black-turbaned chairman of Hizbullah, appeared on the movement's Lebanese television station "Manar" (Lighthouse) to claim the capture of a fourth “Israeli”, a colonel no less, who was seized in what Nasrallah described as "a complicated security operation".

He was self-confident, almost gleeful, talking to an audience which stood to applaud his announcement. The newly-imprisoned “Israeli” was, he said, "an “Israeli” army officer". By dusk, the rival television station of parliament speaker Nabih Berri was giving the name of Hizbullah's latest prisoner: Elhanan Tannenbaum, 54, a Swiss businessman and reservist airforce colonel, captured in Lausanne, Switzerland, whose son had "lost contact" with him a week ago.

From that moment, the dark and sinister intelligence world of the Middle East spawned a dozen stories. Hizbullah had captured a Mossad agent in Beirut. He was a plain-clothes “Israeli” army colonel captured in the south Lebanese village of Kfar Shouba. He was an Arabic-speaking “Israeli” Jew – or an Arab “Israeli” – who had entered Beirut as a tourist. He was – this from an “Israeli” source – a former officer in “Israel’s” now defunct South Lebanon Army (SLA)* militia who may have been "arrested" by the Hizbullah while visiting "friends" in Lebanon. SLA intelligence officers have been given “Israeli” passports.

Lebanese intelligence – usually reliable in these circumstances – said that the "colonel" was an “Israeli” officer sent to Beirut to discover the whereabouts of the three “Israeli” soldiers captured during a Hizbullah military attack on the Shibaa farms area of the Lebanese-”Israeli” border nine days ago; he was the advance guard of an “Israeli” commando unit which would rescue the three “Israelis” from an underground prison.

In any event, the capture of a fourth “Israeli” was bound to increase two things: the domestic stature of the Hizbullah and the tension accompanying today's Palestinian-”Israeli”-American summit at the Egyptian resort town of Sharm el-Sheikh. If the story was correct – and Hizbullah has an unhappy habit of telling the truth on such occasions – the “Israelis” were trying to grab back their soldiers in advance of any negotiations for a prisoner exchange.

The Hizbullah are demanding the release of 19 Lebanese prisoners held as hostages in “Israel” – including Sheikh Abdul-Karim Obeid and Mustapha Dirani, both Hizbullah supporters – in return for the three soldiers (two Jews and a Druze) who surrendered to their guerrillas after a firefight of 15 minutes just over a week ago. Lebanese army armoured vehicles line the Beirut seafront each night to fight off an “Israeli” commando rescue raid. The Hizbullah knows very well that “Israel” would put agents into Lebanon in advance of any such operation.

At this point, questions – rather than facts – dominate the story. Are the three “Israeli” soldiers still alive? Are they held in Beirut or in southern Lebanon? Does the addition of a fourth prisoner – always supposing Nasrallah's statement is true – mean there would be a "dual" exchange?

The three soldiers were taken from Shibaa in a series of vehicles nine days ago. But UN peace-keeping troops who found the first vehicle, a black Range Rover, discovered blood in two areas of the the car. Was one of the soldiers wounded? Or two? The Range Rover, in the Hizbullah's desperation to flee the area of the battle, collided with a wall and the prisoners were moved to a Nissan which then broke down. But the Hizbullah had more than 12 vehicles near the border, and the three “Israeli” soldiers were taken in a third car deep into Lebanon.

When Kofi Annan, the UN Secretary General, arrived in Beirut last Thursday, he refused to meet Sayed Nasrallah unless the Hizbullah gave the International Red Cross or the UN access to the prisoners. Henri Fournier, the senior Red Cross delegate in Beirut, confirms that no such offer has been received. But by that time, the hostage-taking – as usual in Beirut – had become a bazaar.

Not only did the Hizbullah want the 19 prisoners – four of whom are believed to have asked to stay in “Israel” – but the Syrians were said to be demanding the return of a Syrian Druze air force pilot who defected to “Israel” (along with his Mig jet) 10 years ago. Mr Annan confirmed to me on a visit to the Iranian embassy in Beirut that the Iranian Foreign Minister, Kamal Kharrazi, had asked him to seek the release of four Iranians – two diplomats, a Revolutionary Guard commander and a journalist, who were seized by “Israel’s” Lebanese Phalangist militia allies at a road-block near the town of Jbeil (Byblos) in 1982. Pity, therefore, the poor UN Secretary General.

But the Lebanese prisoners are alive and, visited briefly by the Red Cross in “Israel”, well. Were the “Israelis” planning a sea-borne raid to grab their three soldiers before negotiations began for a swap? In the 1970s, the “Israelis” made frequent raids on Beirut. One was led by a young “Israeli” army officer named Ehud Barak.

 

Arab officials denounce summit…(4)
Nasrallah: Summit will not prevent Palestine from being a scene of bullets, bombs, knives & martyrdom-attacks

Participants at an urgent meeting in support of Palestinians warned on Sunday that the US-sponsored summit in Egypt on Monday would only serve to “abort” the newly rising intifada in Palestine.
“The General Secretariat of Arab parties warns of the aims of such a summit and urges the Palestinian Authority to hold on to the choice of the Palestinian people to resist the occupation,” said a final communique issued following the one-day conference.
More than 200 Arab participants from both the Arab Nationalist Conference and the Islamic Nationalist Conference joined forces on the eve of the Palestinian-”Israeli” summit in the Egyptian resort of Sharm al-Sheikh.
The Beirut meeting, which took place at the Carlton Hotel, was named after Mohammed al-Durra, the 12-year-old Palestinian boy who was shot and killed by “Israeli” soldiers in Ramallah on Sept. 30.
On hand were officials and activists from throughout the Arab world, including officials of Islamic Jihad and Hamas, and Prime Minister Salim Hoss and Hizbullah leader Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah. The participants spent the day discussing a plan aimed at mobilizing support behind the Palestinian people resisting “Israeli” occupation.
Conference participants are expected to present a memorandum of demands to officials who will participate in the Oct. 21 Arab summit in Cairo.
Nasrallah, who gave a speech after his surprise announcement of the party’s capture of an “Israeli” colonel, described the rising intifada in Palestine as a “significant and exceptional historic stage.”
“The people of our nation and the sacrificing people of Palestine observe this action with increasing hope. The big question here and now is if this huge meeting can live up to such a historic development and constitute a basis for a real metamorphosis,” Nasrallah said.
The Hizbullah leader harshly criticized the Sharm al-Sheikh summit, where President Clinton, Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak, Palestinian President Yasser Arafat, and “Israeli” Prime Minister Ehud Barak are expected to meet.
“I tell you, whatever the results of the Sharm al-Sheikh summit, it will not revive the dead. It will not progress from a settlement to normalization to acceptance of cohabitating with this racist terrorist presence called “Israel”,” Nasrallah said.
“Yes, this summit may stop the Palestinians from throwing stones but it will not prevent the land of Palestine from being a scene of bullets, bombs, knives, and martyrdom-attacks,” he added.
Nasrallah urged all Arabs to put aside their political and religious differences and join efforts to start a struggle against “Israel”.
“We have to admit today that our true leaders who are making our contemporary history are those young men who ousted the occupiers from Lebanon and who caused a stir among the world’s rulers when they courageously rushed to the streets and faced bullets with their bare chests,” he said.
Jordanian opposition official Laith Shbeilat said that Arab leaders would miss another historic chance to gain more ground on “Israel”.
Shbeilat also warned against wasting the effort expressed in the united front embodied at Sunday’s conference, saying that suggestions and proposals should be made tangible.
“These proposals will mean nothing if they are not linked to the Arab people who are hoping for a mobilization of efforts,” he said. “The struggling Palestinian people don’t need words, they need true solidarity.”

 

Hamas leader: I pray to God for the destruction of all enemies…(5)

Sheikh Ahmed Yassin, the 64-year-old spiritual leader of the Hamas movement, yesterday said he "prayed to God every hour of every day for the destruction of every “Israeli”", and vowed that the 18-day-old conflict would continue.

When it was put to him that nearly 90 per cent of those killed since 28 September had been Palestinian, the sheikh said: "It is not always that way. There have been attacks where many “Israelis” and no Palestinians have died. The struggle will continue by every means until “Israel” ceases its occupation of our land. This has made our youth more prepared for sacrifices and death. They are the righteous ones."

Sheikh Yassin's fiery words starkly demonstrate the dilemma for Yasser Arafat. Whatever Mr Arafat does, Hamas stands to benefit. If he is seen to compromise, it will anger those who have been confronting “Israeli” tanks and helicopter gunships with stones, but if the intifada, or uprising, is allowed to continue, Hamas will use it to promote its Islamic aims. "Hamas knows Arafat is weak, and that he can't fight on two fronts," said a Palestinian source. "On the other hand, Hamas doesn't want to be held responsible for killing peace."

Speaking in a house off an alleyway in Sabra, a shabby area of Gaza City, Sheikh Yassin. "All Palestinians are united, and all are determined to continue the intifada," he said. "To speak of a cease-fire is to ask the victim to stop defending himself."

The sheikh, crippled and wheelchair-bound since his youth, continued in his breathy, high-pitched whisper: "I don't fear death. We are seeking martyrdom. Hamas does not mount suicide operations, they are martyrdom operations. If the “Israelis” fear such operations, they are criminals, because criminals fear justice."

The Hamas leader exuded certainty. "If you ask me how I see “Israelis”, it is purely as enemies," he said.

 

Druze call on ‘brothers’ in “Israel” to mutiny…(6)

Druze politicians meeting at the Mukhtara residence of Chouf MP Walid Jumblatt on Sunday called on their co-religionists in “Israel” to rebel against the Jewish state.
“The MPs of the Druze community in Lebanon appeal to young men of our Arab Islamic sect in occupied Palestine, and urge you to stage a new revolt, similar to the one your ancestors staged in the 1950s,” a statement by the group said.
In addition to Jumblatt, the group included a number of Druze MPs, including Faisal Daoud, Akram Chehayeb, Ayman Shuqair, Marwan Hamade, Ghazi Aridi, and Sharif Fayyad, the secretary-general of the Progressive Socialist Party.
The legislature’s other two Druze MPs, Anwar Khalil and Talal Arslan, were not invited to the gathering, according to sources close to the meeting.
The participants called on “Israeli” Druze to “participate effectively in the heroic Palestinian intifada against the “Israeli” occupation and its agents,” urging them to “fiercely defend your Muslim and Arab identity, as your brothers in the occupied Golan do.
“We call on young Druze men in Palestine to reject compulsory military service, whatever the means of terrorism exerted by the Zionist enemy against you,” it added.
Unlike other “Israeli”-Arabs, Druze are the only Arabs to serve in the “Israeli” Army.
“We urge you to commit mutiny and desert the occupation army immediately ­ even if you will be arrested or tortured to death like many of your ancestors.
“Our call today is the call
of truth, history and duty. Doubtless to say that you are worthy of this call,” the statement concluded.
Meanwhile, senior Druze clerics meeting separately in Jahilieh on Sunday urged the Druze community in occupied Palestine “to adopt the same stand … in Lebanon and Syria, and which befits the history of this sect and the historic struggles of its leaders to protect the Arabs’ rights.”
