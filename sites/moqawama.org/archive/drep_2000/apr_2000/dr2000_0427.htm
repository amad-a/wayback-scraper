Daily Report

April Thursday 27-2000:


Introduction

Syria's Foreign Minister Faruq Al-Sharaa said that the deployment of a UN force was "highly sensitive and hypothetical. "We do not know why such a force will be necessary, nor what its nature or objectives will be. Will it be a deterrent force, and if so, deterring who?"

Meanwhile, Lebanon's Primer Salim Al-Hoss invited the people "to be joyful and feel pride at the historic change awaiting Lebanon".

Elsewhere, "Israel" has shelled the front-line town of Majdal Zoun in South Lebanon damaging two houses. Also, the "Israeli" war jets fired a number of air-to-ground missiles into Abou Rashed heights.


Sharaa unsure about UN force(1)

"Syrian Foreign Minister Farouk al-Sharaa cast doubt on Wednesday on the ability of the United Nations to maintain security in south Lebanon, saying a deployment of international forces after the impending "Israeli" withdrawal was highly sensitive and hypothetical.
"Addressing journalists at the Syrian Embassy in Paris, Sharaa said: We dont know why such a force will be necessary, nor what its nature or objectives will be. Will it be a deterrent force, and if so, deterring who?
He warned France against participating in a force which might not be strong enough to hold the balance between "Israel" and its potential adversaries.
Sharaa accused "Israel" of using plans to withdraw from the south as a setup for taking revenge on its Arab neighbors.
"Israel" has something else in mind that it will do after this withdrawal, he said. In return for this unconditional withdrawal, it will try to take revenge on Lebanon, Syria and the resistance, but I think they will fail.
Sharaa, who on Tuesday met French President Jacques Chirac and Foreign Minister Hubert Vedrine, also said: We in Syria, as in Lebanon, feel that this is a victory and we have the right to say it. Were not saying it to play a game.

Hoss: We are about historic change(2)

"Prime Minister Salim Al-Hoss said that it was "legitimate to remain on one's guard given the perfidy of "Israeli" policy," but said it was "inconceivable to wallow in unjustifiable pessimism and transform our victory against "Israel" into a defeat and its defeat into a victory."

"We invite people to be joyful and feel pride at the historic change awaiting Lebanon, and we say they should feel confident about their fate," he said.

"Lebanon "has a date with a return to stability, security and prosperity, God willing," he added.


"Israel's" warplanes raided into Western Beqaa

""Israeli" warplanes fired four missiles at 8am on Wednesday during two raids on Jabal Abu Rashed in the Western Beqaa, a Lebanese security official said, asserting that there were no resistance attacks prior to the raids.
Lebanese troops in the region confronted the jets with heavy anti-aircraft fire. An hour earlier, "Israeli" warplanes carried out two raids in the same region, firing four rockets, according to the source.
Also on Wednesday, Mohammed Sabbagh, 17, was seriously wounded in an area east of the southern port city of Tyre, when he stepped on a land mine while working in his field, police said.
They added that it is believed the mine was planted by "Israeli" troops to target resistance fighters(3)

Occupation considers forming post-withdrawal substitutes for Lahd militia(5)

"Lebanese political and security sources unveiled that the "Israeli" occupation is seriously considering other substitutes foragent Antoine Lahd's militia in case the "Israeli" unilateral pullout takes place without "Israel's" being able to increase the number of the UNIFIL troops.

Within this context, a considerable number of members of the banned "Lebanese Forces" militia has moved to the occupied border zone recently.

"The sources said the new comers started inspecting the situation, organizing their ranks, and calling on members and cadres who are abroad to come to the zone.

Reports from the occupied zone said new faces are now seen in the zone and talking about the formation of a "national guard" to defend the villages and towns from which the "Israeli" occupation will withdraw.

"The sources said the situation will be similar to the one ""Israel"" created in 1978 when it said it wanted to implement resolution 425 but kept the "national guard" in the zone it had invaded in what was known as Saad Haddad's state".


Military Operations(6)

The IR team of martyrs Fadl Mahmoud Atwi and Hussein Mahmoud Salman staged, at 5:45, a swift attack against Baraasheit compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct and accurate hits.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Mohammad Ali Slim and Hachem Ali Ballout was rushing upon Haddatha compound with the adequate arms chalking up direct goals.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Rodwan Najib Abdallah and Nimr Mohammad El-Youssef fell upon the same compound striking its main fortification.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Hussein Mohsen Nasser and Jihad Hussein Abou Harb targeted, at 11:15, a Lahdi engineering force as it was reinforcing Dohour El-Kassara position. The militants, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, barred the enemy force from accomplishing its goals.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Ali Safieddine and Ali Hussein Soueidan rushed upon a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Berkat Dajaj position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to prevent it from completing its task.

Moreover, the IR group of martyrs Assadallah Morched Shamas and Hussein Ali Aoudeh targeting, at 13:50, Dohour El-Kassara position scoring direct hits.

Also, IR's squad of martyrs Majed Ghanem and Allam Swaidan at 14:30 attacked a Zionist force in the vicinity of the occupation's outpost of al-Taibeh with rocket propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

Ten minutes later, squad of martyrs Basel Alaeddeen abed Ahmed Dbouq targeted a Lahdi infantry force that was moving near the barricade of the Houla outpost.

IR men used rocket propelled grenades in the attack scoring accurate hits.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Mohammad Moussa and Abdel-Hussein Hassan Fahs attacked, at 19:30, a Lahdi engineering force as it was beefing up Hemayed position with the proper arms chalking up direct goals.

For its part, Amal Movement shelled Rshaf position with four 120mm missiles.
