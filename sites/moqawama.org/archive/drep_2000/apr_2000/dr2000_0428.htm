Daily Report

April Friday 28-2000:


Introduction

Hizbullah Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said that ""Israel" remembered UN Resolution 425 when it needed to cover up its defeat and that its withdrawal from Lebanon has been motivated by is inability to deal with the resistance".

In the meantime, Prime Minister Salim Al-Hoss said that the deployment of an international force in South Lebanon following the withdrawal of the "Israeli" occupation troops depends on the approval of the Lebanese government.

Elsewhere, the former French Foreign Minister Herve de Charette charged "Israel" with planning the massacre of more than 100 civilians sheltered in a United Nations compound in Qana in 1996.


Hizbullah says "Israel" to withdraw entirely from Lebanon to escape from

IR(1)

" Hizbullah chief Hassan Nasrallah said "Israel" wants to withdraw from the entire zone it has been occupying in Lebanon in order to escape from the Islamic Resistance (IR) attacks.

"The planned pull out was imposed on "Israel" by the Lebanese resistance," he said in an interview published Thursday in the independent Jordanian newspaper Al-Arab Al-Yawm.

""Israel" intends to withdraw completely from Lebanese territory so as not to provide an excuse for military actions" against it by Hizbullah.

""Israel" is seriously considering not remaining on one inch of our land," proving that it "has been liberated by force and by the resistance," he said and advised Palestinians to follow that the IR's example.

""Israel" remembered UN Resolution 425 when it needed to cover up its defeat and that its withdrawal from Lebanon has been motivated by its inability to deal with the resistance," he added.

The Security Council resolution, approved in 1978, called for an immediate and unconditional "Israeli" withdrawal from Lebanon.

"Israel" occupies a "buffer zone" in 10 percent of Lebanese territory. But anti-"Israeli" attacks in south Lebanon have changed the public mood in the Jewish state and its government has pledged to withdraw from Lebanon by July 7.


PM: UN deployment depends on Lebanon's approval(2)
"Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss said Thursday the deployment of an international force in southern Lebanon after the "Israeli" occupation forces' withdrawal remained pending the approval of the Beirut government.

"Everything that concerns the international forces depends on the approval of the Lebanese government," said al-Hoss.

"Al-Hoss said Lebanon "does not accept any security arrangements as a condition for an "Israeli" withdrawal and in any case, we will not be border guards for the "Israelis"."

"Lebanon calls on "Israel" to withdraw without conditions and completely from Lebanese territories, up to the internationally recognized border lines drawn in 1923," he said in a statement.

"If the pullout does not respect this line, it means that "Israel" did not withdraw in line with the implementation of UN Security Council Resolution 425 and that its pullout will be in this case a redeployment."


De Charette: Qana was not a mistake(3)

"Former French Foreign Minister Herve de Charette charged "Israel" on Thursday with planning the massacre of more than 100 civilians sheltered in a United Nations compound in Qana in 1996.
"Israel" knew what it was doing. It wasnt a mistake, as "Israel" contends, de Charette said as he laid a wreath on the tombs of the slain civilians.
De Charette, who arrived on a private four-day visit to Beirut on Tuesday at the invitation of Sidon MP Bahiya Hariri, said the Qana massacre was an indelible event etched in his memory.
It is very crucial that the world never forget this massacre so that it wont be repeated ever again, he said.

Detainees plight nearing end(4)

"Mohammad Safa, the head of the Follow-up Committee for the Support of Lebanese Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons, said on Thursday that an "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon would spell an end to the detention of prisoners held in "Israel".
The detention of Lebanese prisoners in Khiam and "Israeli" prisons is nearing an end in the wake of "Israeli" intentions of withdrawing from south Lebanon, Safa said in a statement released after visiting Henri Fournier, the head of the International Committee of the Red Cross. The pullout is also a good omen for prisoners in Khiam.
Safa is lobbying for the release of the remaining Lebanese prisoners in "Israeli" jails following the freeing of 13 Lebanese detainees last week in a landmark "Israeli" Supreme Court decision.
The Follow-up Committee had lambasted the "Israeli" government for withholding two prominent leaders, Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid and Hajj Mustafa Dirani, as bargaining chips while releasing the 13.
Safa called on the ICRC and other human-rights groups to press for the release of the remaining prisoners and to investigate the fate of those believed to be in "Israeli" jails.
He urged the ICRC to intensify efforts to wrest control of Khiam detention center from "Israel's" proxy militia before and after an "Israeli" withdrawal takes place.
Safa also said that a delegation from London-based Amnesty International, which recently opened an office in Beirut, will visit Lebanon on May 4 to map out a course of action to have the remaining prisoners released".


Another IR man joins martyrs' convoy(5)

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announced to the Islamic Ummah and the Lebanese people the martyrdom of one of its men.

"Martyr Hassanto Rizq (Hassan al-Sheikh), 24, born in Mashghara (Western Beqaa), married, joined IR in 1993, participated in several military and educational courses, took part in several operations against the "Israeli" occupation forces, had encomium from Hizbullah's Secretary General, was martyred "Thursday morning of wounds sustained while intercepting the enemy's warplanes in the Western Beqaa Hilltops".

The IR released a communiqué in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:

5:55, the perimeters of Jibal El-Botom village came under two 155mm missiles.

6:00, six 155mm missiles struck Jib Soweid area to the east of Majdal Zoun village.

6:15, the peripheries of Jibaa town exposed to four 155mm missiles.

Between 6:35 and 6:45, three 155mm missiles hit Kfour ravine.


Kibbutz Manara residents fear the tourist industry is coming to an end for them(7)

" Deputy Minister of Defense Ephraim Sneh will on Sunday tour northern frontline communities to get a first-hand look at the situation and meet with community leaders.

The leaders of many of the frontline communities are angered and afraid that the new northern border fence under construction will be on the border of their communities, placing them on the border and therefore, prone to Hizbullah attacks. Leaders of Kibbutz Manara stated if the government proceeds with the plans for the unilateral troop withdrawal from the security zone, the tourism industry in their community would cease to exist, with tourists being too afraid to visit the area following the troop withdrawal.

Resident of Moshav Margaliyot stated they do not understand what the government leaders are expecting  adding that perhaps they are waiting for a serious incident to occur before they acknowledge the perilous dangers that the frontline community residents will face daily following the troop withdrawal.

During his visit to the north, Sneh will meet with the head of the Metulla Council head, Kobi Katz, who is demanding additional steps be taken to ensure the security of area residents.

Margaliyot Secretary Eitan Davidi expressed his opposition to plans that following the withdrawal, "Israeli" Army soldiers will pass through his community on their way to and from the newly located border outposts. "Davidi insists that the residents were promised that the community would remain demilitarized and now, have learned that the contrary is true".
