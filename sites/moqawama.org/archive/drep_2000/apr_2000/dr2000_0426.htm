Daily Report

April Wednesday 26-2000:


Introduction

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah reiterated that any withdrawal by the "Israeli" occupation forces must end the problems it had created. "If the withdrawal happens, it will be a tough defeat for the Zionist enemy and a victory for Lebanese and the resistance", he added.

However, a Lebanese civilian was seriously wounded when Shaqra village came under a barrage of heavy machine gun bullets. Elsewhere, three children aged between 6 and 10, who had gone to stay with their relatives in the "Israeli" occupied zone, were beaten and expelled by "Israel's" proxy militia.


Nasrallah calls for the withdrawal of "Israeli" allied militiamen alongside occupation forces(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah has warned against any presence of "Israeli" allied militiamen in southern Lebanon following "Israel's" planned pullout. Nasrallah said the resistance group holds "Israel" responsible for any militia activity, adding they will remain a target even after "Israel" withdraws. Nasrallah said the militia constitutes an occupation force, since it was created by "Israel" and is being financed, controlled and trained by the Jewish state. Militiamen face prosecution in Beirut for collaborating with "Israel". The Hizbullah leader, whose group spearheads resistance operations in the south, called for the withdrawal of militiamen from the south alongside "Israelis". Nasrallah also vowed to keep up attacks on "Israeli" forces in occupied south Lebanon even as they retreat".


Leaders hail impending withdrawal
Lahoud says Palestinian issue remains a timebomb(2)

The president and the prime minister hailed the impending "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon as a cause for celebration in remarks released on Tuesday.
While Prime Minister Salim Hoss stressed that there was no need for excessive pessimism over the prospects of post-withdrawal conflict, President Emile Lahoud reiterated that without the return of Palestinian refugees to their homes, Lebanons stability would remain threatened.
It will be a historic moment when the last soldier of the "Israeli" occupation troops leaves Lebanon according to UN Security Council Resolution 425, Hoss said in a statement.
Lebanon has been calling for the implementation of the resolution since it was passed 22 years ago. Its inconceivable that it does not welcome the resolutions implementation with happiness, he added.
The premier said that "Israel" decided to implement the resolution as a result of the sacrifices of Lebanons people and resistance movement.
He called on all citizens to share my hope that the pullout will be a prelude for real relief and an incentive for renewing our confidence in our homeland and ourselves and in our success in achieving this unequivocal victory.
Hoss warned against pessimistic views that exceed the limits of legitimate caution about "Israel's" intentions, saying that such views threaten to turn our victory into an "Israeli" victory.
Hoss was referring to recent statements from various officials, suggesting that an "Israeli" withdrawal, without a comprehensive peace agreement, would destabilize the country. He added that the withdrawal is a spectacular leap forward on all aspects and should not be treated any other away.
Addressing the Lebanese community in Saudi Arabia during his trip last week, Lahoud rejected "Israeli" threats to flatten Lebanon in the event of border attacks after a pullout.
If the enemy is not capable of protecting its border, how can we do so? I ask this question after hearing "Israeli" threats to launch retaliatory attacks to any cross-border incident, Lahoud said. Tele-Liban aired the full footage of his exchange with the community members on Tuesday.
Cross-border incidents happen anywhere in the world. India and Pakistan complain about incidents on their common border.
The two countries have nuclear weapons but they dont use them in retaliation for such incidents, he added during the meeting in Saudi Arabia. I say if they leave Lebanon, we will celebrate. But they dont have the right to impose conditions on us.
Lahoud elaborated on his earlier comments that Lebanon could not stop Palestinians from attempting cross-border attacks.
When the "Israelis" invaded Lebanon in 1982, they entered the Sabra and Shatila refugee camps. We all remember the massacres that took place there, he said. Are we supposed to enter the camps and allow similar incidents to take place? Can the United Nations do the same thing?
He said that if Palestinian refugees in Lebanon were not given the right to return home, they will become a timebomb. He also accused Palestinian militants of killing four judges in Sidon in June and instigating a Sunni fundamentalist rebellion in Dinnieh in January.
If the same Palestinians, who killed the judges and hid in the camps making it impossible for us to arrest them, tried to stage cross-border attacks, will "Israel" destroy Lebanon? he asked.
Lahoud blamed former governments secret diplomacy with "Israel", which, he claimed, weakened Lebanon and encouraged "Israel" to attempt to impose its conditions on us.


Lebanese civilian seriously hurt by "Israeli" fire

"A Lebanese civilian was seriously hurt Tuesday when his village close to the "Israel"-occupied border strip came under heavy artillery fire, Lebanese police said.

Mohammed Darwish Aqiqi was hit by a bullet in the stomach when several parts of the village of Shaqra was raked by fire from the "Israeli" position of Hula inside the occupied zone, they said.

Ten shells fired from Hula also fell on the village, causing serious damage to property, they added.

Since the beginning of the year, four civilians have been killed and 71 injured in violence in southern Lebanon, despite an agreement reached between the belligerents in 1996 to avoid targeting civilians.

In another incident a Lebanese goatherd was wounded in the explosion of an "Israeli" cluster bomb, dating probably from the 1982 invasion, police said.

Said Khalil Aql, 65, received multiple injuries in the blast as he was guarding his flock near Libbaya, in the southern part of the eastern Syrian-controlled Beqaa plain.

"Israel" first invaded its northern neighbor in 1978 in an offensive against Palestinian guerrillas before launching a disastrous full-scale invasion in 1982. In 1985 it pulled back to a self-declared "security zone" in south Lebanon with the aim of protecting its northern border from possible attack by Lebanese guerrillas(3)

Three Lebanese children expelled from "Israeli"-occupied zone(5)

" Three children aged between six and 10, who had gone to stay with relatives in the "Israeli"-occupied zone of southern Lebanon, were beaten and expelled by "Israel's" proxy militia, security services said Tuesday.

Khalil, 10, Khodr, 8, and Aala, 6, had been taken by an aunt to spend their Easter holidays in Shibaa village where they used to live before being expelled, with 23 other members of the family, last July.

The family was expelled because the boys' father, Mohammed Khalil Ibrahim Nabaa, was suspected of involvement in the assassination of an "Israeli" agent security officer, Ghassan Daher.

As soon as news of their arrival spread in the village Saturday, the militiamen ordered family members to lock the children up at home and bring them to intelligence headquarters the following day, relatives said.

"Khalil was struck violently across the face, and all three were aggressively questioned by the collaborators, who then took them to Kfar Houna, one of the points linking the occupied zone with the rest of Lebanon, where they put them in a hired vehicle which drove them to the first Lebanese Army road block".


Pro-"Israeli" militia arrest three deserters in south Lebanon(6)

"Three members of "Israel's" proxy militia who deserted a month ago, have been arrested and imprisoned in the Khiam detention camp in the "Israeli"-occupied zone of southern Lebanon, security forces said here Tuesday.

"The three, all members of the Nabaa family, come from the village of Shibaa, in the eastern part of the zone, where they were arrested last week, the forces said.

Two of them had been militiamen, while the other belonged to the "civil administration" set up by the "Israeli" army in the zone.

"Cases of desertion from the militia have increased since "Israel" announced it would withdraw from the occupied zone by July. However, most deserters cross into government-held territory and give themselves up to the Lebanese army, or to guerrilla groups such as Hizbullah or Amal, which spearhead resistance to the "Israeli" presence in Lebanon.

"Some 150 Lebanese are currently held without trial in Khiam, which has been repeatedly denounced by human rights groups for ill-treatment and torture".


IR and LRB attack occupation outposts(7)

The Islamic Resistance (IR) proceeded with retaliating for the aggressions of the "Israeli" occupation forces and their proxy militia against Lebanese civilians.

IR's squad of Martyrs Qassem al-Taweel and Fadi Mohammad Ghazal at 14:40 Tuesday managed to reach an area very close to the occupation's new outpost in Houla.

As a number of Lahdi collaborators were moving between the outpost's ramparts, IR men peppered them with their machine-gun fire and rocket propelled grenades inflicting definite casualties in the ranks of the enemy.

Moreover, the resistance men destroyed the outpost's major rampart and a 12.7mm machine gun that was inside the rampart.

Earlier at 5:50, IR's squad of Martyrs Hassan Abbas al-Hajj and Qassem Mohammad Ali Rammal, Tuesday morning, pounded the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Shamaa with heavy artillery rounds in a violent and concentrated manner scoring accurate hits in the outpost's ramparts and fortifications.

In the meantime, IR's squad of Martyrs Ahmed Hachem al-Musawi and Ali Mohammad Ali Sleem attacked the occupation's outpost in Tair Harfa with the appropriate weapons.

After six o'clock, IR's squad of Martyrs Ali Moghnieh and Basheer Ahmad Zgheib targeted the enemy's outpost in Blat with rocket propelled grenades scoring definite hits.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades at 7:45 a.m. attacked enemy forces in the occupation's outpost in Soweida with rocket propelled grenades scoring definite hits in the outpost's fortifications.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Sobhi Ali Chokr and Fouad Ali Zoaiter in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded, at 18:00, the border post of Ramieh with the artillery shells scoring direct and certain goals.

At the same time, the IR group of martyrs Ali Suleiman and Hassan Mohammad Moussa rushed upon Bir Kallab outpost with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and definite goals.

Meanwhile, the IR unit of martyrs Adnan Sucrates and Abdallah Ahmad Khalifeh was falling upon Dohour El-Kassara position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. Definite goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, between 11:05 and 12:17, several rocket-propelled grenades at Taibeh position. Also, it unleashed, at 14:15, a number of 120mm shells at Baraasheit compound.
