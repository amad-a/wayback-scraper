Daily Report

April Friday 21-2000:

 

Introduction

The Supreme Leader of Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyed Ali Khamenei appreciated the resistance of the Lebanese people to the Zionist regime as a unique example in the Arab world. "As long as the threat of "Israel" remains, the necessity for resistance is felt", the Supreme Leader said.

Meanwhile, the Islamic Resistance (IR) mujahedeen launched a series of military attacks against a number of the "Israeli" enemy and its agents positions. The IR attacks resulted in killing at least one "Israeli" collaborator and wounding two others alongside two "Israeli" soldiers as well.


President concludes visit to Iran(1)

"Leader of the Islamic revolution Ayatollah Sayyed Ali Khamenei on Thursday appreciated the resistance of the Lebanese people to the Zionist regime as a unique example in the Arab world.

In a meeting with President Emile Lahoud, the leader said the Islamic resistance movement has made Muslims proud of Lebanon.

As long as the threat of "Israel" remains, the necessity for resistance is felt, the supreme leader said.

The leader referred to Irans anti-Zionist stand and said Iran will always remain with Lebanon in campaign against the Zionist regime.

The leader said the usurpers of Palestine are tricksters and will do what they can to sow discord in Lebanon.

President Lahoud briefed the leader on the withdrawal of the Zionist forces from southern Lebanon and said his visit to Iran was aimed at thanking the Iranian nation for their support to Lebanon in its campaign against the Zionist occupation.

Lahoud here Wednesday evening appreciated Irans support for Lebanons resistance to the Zionist regime and stressed that the support resulted in withdrawal of the "Israeli" forces.

Lahoud, who is currently here at the head of a high-ranking delegation, made the remark in a meeting with his Iranian counterpart president Mohammed Khatami.

Referring to rich Iranian culture, civilization and history, he called for further expansion of Tehran-Beirut relations in all areas.

As to coexistence of various cultures and religions in Lebanon, President Khatami said that such variety has led to the countrys growth and development.

Pointing to the resistance of the Lebanese nation to the Zionist regimes expansionist policies, he stressed that the nation is representative of resistance and glory of the Muslim and Arab nations in today world.

On withdrawal of the "Israeli" forces from Lebanese territory, President Khatami said that the resistance of the oppressed Lebanese nation has been the major cause of such a move by the Zionists.

Turning to Tehran-Beirut friendly relations, he voiced the countrys interest in strengthening political, economic, social, scientific and cultural ties with Lebanon.

The Islamic republic of Iran is interested in regional peace and stability, the Iranian president said adding that no peace will be durable unless it is based on justice and restoration of rights of all regional nations.

He expressed the hope that all occupied lands will be liberated and all homeless Palestinians will return to their homeland peacefully.

The Lebanese delegation arrived in Tehran Wednesday afternoon and was greeted at the Mehrabad International Airport by the minister of housing and urban development, Ali Abdolalizadeh.

Later on, president Khatami officially welcomed president Lahoud at the Sadabad palace.
At the end of the visit, Iran and Lebanon released a joint statement here Thursday.

In a copy of the statement which was provided to IRNA, the two countries condemned "Israel's" occupation of southern Lebanon and stressed confronting the aggressors until the emancipation of the Lebanese territories.

They also lauded the Lebanese resistance, and said that the announced "Israeli" withdrawal from southern Lebanon is victory for the Arab and Moslem world.

The statement also referred to the Lebanese presidents appreciation of Irans role in supporting the Lebanese resistance, and stressing the effectiveness of the Iranian policy.

The presidents of Iran and Lebanon also expressed satisfaction on progress achieved in the bilateral ties in all areas, and voiced support for expansion of relations especially in economic and cultural areas.

The statement further refers to the need for a stable and lasting peace in the region and draws attention to the danger posed by the "Israel's" nuclear arsenal for the regional countries. It also calls for pursuing policies to protect the Middle East from nuclear weapons and weapons of mass destruction.

Lahoud voiced support for Irans policy of détente, confidence building measures and bolstering of relations with the regional Arab countries.

The statement says that the Lebanese president has extended an invitation to his Iranian counterpart to visit Lebanon in an opportune time and President Khatami has welcomed the invitation with the date to be mutually agreed upon in the future.

The Lebanese president ended his two-day official trip to Tehran Thursday afternoon. President Khatami saw him off at Tehran Mehrabad airport.

Lebanese couple expelled from homes in "Israeli"-occupied zone(4)

" A Lebanese man and his pregnant wife have been expelled from the "Israeli"-occupied zone of southern Lebanon, police announced here Thursday.

Saleh Sheet, 27, a primary school teacher in the village of Kfar Kila close to the border with "Israel", told AFP that members of "Israel's" proxy militia burst into his classroom on Tuesday and ordered him out of of the zone immediately.

"They didn't give any reason why I had been declared persona non grata, and accompanied me to the Kfar Tibnite gate," one of the five crossing points into government-held territory, he said.

He said he had not said or done anything that would explain such an action.

His wife Malak Jomaa, who is six months pregnant, was expelled 24 hours later by the militia men who conducted her to Kfar Tibnite.

The US-based human rights organisation Human Rights Watch in November described the forced expulsion of Lebanese civilians from their villages in the occupied zone as "war crimes" and a violation of the Geneva conventions protecting civilians in conflict zones.

It said "hundreds if not thousands of Lebanese civilians have been ordered to leave their homes and villages without notice and with no means of appeal" since 1985.


Egyptian, Lebanese leaders want "complete" "Israeli" withdrawal(5)

" Presidents Emile Lahoud of Lebanon and Hosni Mubarak of Egypt urged "Israel" Thursday to withdraw its air and naval forces from Lebanon in addition to its ground forces.

"The two heads of state stressed the need that the "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon be a complete withdrawal covering the ground, air and sea," Egyptian Information Minister Safwat al-Sherif told reporters.

"One must respect Lebanese territorial waters, air space and Lebanese sovereignty over all Lebanese territory," the minister quoted Mubarak and Lahoud as saying.

The two presidents, meeting in Cairo, were discussing "Israel's" plan to withdraw from its "buffer zone" in south Lebanon in July, after 22 years of occupation.

But the two leaders implied they were concerned "Israeli" gunboats would still sail off the Lebanese coast and "Israeli" warplanes would still fly over its territory.

Mubarak and Lahoud also said it was necessary to "respect international borders, whether it concerned Lebanon or Syria," Sherif added.

Lahoud, who flew here from Tehran, was greeted by Mubarak and a guard of honor at the airport before the two went to a presidential palace in northern Cairo for face-to-face talks.

Their delegations joined them later.

During his two-day visit to Egypt, Lahoud will also meet with other Egyptian officials as well as Arab League Secretary General Esmat Abdel Meguid on Friday.

Mubarak made a surprise visit to Beirut on February 19, the first by an Egyptian head of state, to show solidarity with Lebanon following massive "Israeli" retaliatory raids on the country's electrical infrastructure.

The raids were in reprisal for the killing of a number of "Israeli" troops by Syrian-backed Hizbullah guerrillas in Lebanon.

"Israel" denounced Mubarak's visit as giving support to Hizbullah, which is responsible for most of the attacks on "Israeli" occupation forces in south Lebanon.
