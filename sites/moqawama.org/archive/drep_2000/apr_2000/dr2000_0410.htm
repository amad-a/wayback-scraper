Daily Report

April Monday 10-2000:


Qassem: "Israeli" agents would be punished(1)

Nasrallahs deputy, Sheikh Nahim Qassem, warned on Sunday that all the "Israeli"-allied militia would be punished, regardless of their religion, but added that other residents of the occupied zone must not be subjected to the same fate since they did not directly support the occupation. The resisting residents of the occupied zone are our people and friends, whether Muslims or Christians and deserve to be given prizes because they have shouldered the burden of the occupation most of all, he said.
Qassem praised the memorandum of President Emile Lahoud to the UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan, arguing that the document also expressed Hizbullahs views.
This memorandum aims at diverting the "Israeli" conflict to the Zionist interior rather than the "Israelis" turning their conflict toward us, he said.

"Israeli" Aggressions(2)

The IR released a statement in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:

00:30, one tank round landed Mount Bassil.

00:45, six 155mm missiles were unleashed at Sawan area.

13:25, the west of Baraasheit town exposed to a hail of 12.7mm and Lancher machine gun bullets.

Between 14:05 and 15:45, Kfar Romman town and its perimeters came under several 81mm, 120mm and 155mm missiles and barrages of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets damaging the houses of Ghazi Mokdad, Bilal Hariri, Mohammad Abdel-Rida Daher and Jawad and a Van-car related to Zakariah Aqil.

14:10, a number of 155mm missiles struck the outskirts of Habboush town.

14:15, the peripheries of Arab-Salim town came under fifteen 81mm rounds, ten 155mm missiles and three 105mm tank shells inflicting the nationals Ibrahim Farhat, 50, Abbas Nazar, 50, Ali Moussa, 36, and the Lebanese Army Sergeant Najor Abbas Abdel-Sater and damaging the town's old mosque and several cars and houses. At the same time, Mount Zaatar was shelled by two 155mm missiles.

Between 14:50 and 15:05, the outskirts of Haris exposed to three 120mm shells, two 81mm rounds and 12.7mm heavy machine gun fire.

Between 14:50 and 15:05, thirteen 155mm missiles and a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck Haddatha town and its peripheries.

15:00, five 155mm missiles were fired at Kfour ravine.

15:00, a barrage of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets hit the peripheries of Baraasheit town.

Between 15:05 and 15:30, west of Baraasheit town was targeted by three 81mm rounds and two 120mm shells.

16:00, two 120mm shells landed the area between Baraasheit and Beit Yahoun.

17:00, six 155mm missiles fell on Qaissieh Valley and several others on Slouqi Valley.

17:05, west of Yater village was bombarded by twelve 155mm missiles.

17:25, the south of Jibal El-Botom was pounded by ten 155mm missiles.

Between 17:30 and 17:50, ten 155mm missiles slammed into the west of Siddiqeen village.

19:07, the Israeli fighter-bombers dropped two air-to-ground rockets into Qaissieh Valley.


Military Operations(3)

In the framework of chasing the enemy soldiers and penetrating through the military measures taken around their outposts and fortifications, the IR team of martyrs Abbas Saleh and Sami Salman Melhem attacked, at 6:05, the Israeli outpost of Dabsheh with the heavy artillery shells destroying its western fortification, whereas the blaze columns were seen emanating out of it.

In the meantime, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Hussein Moghnieh and Ali Harb was rushing upon the same outpost with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits, whereas the IR group of martyrs Mohammad Balhas and Ghassan Khalifeh fell upon Beaufort Castle with the rocket-propelled grenades. The militants managed to chalk up direct and certain goals.

Also, the IR unit of martyrs Youssef Badran and Abdallah Rammal ambushed, at 14:15, an enemy patrol as it was traveling on the road leading to Tohorra outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen were able to destroy a military command car and inflicting all of its occupants.

At the same time, the IR team of martyrs Yassine Chehab and Ossama Hammoudi targeted Soweida outpost barring it from the intervention.

Thirty minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Hussein Mortada and Ammar Haidar Bitar staged a swift attack at Rshaf position. The mujahedeen, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, were able to hit a Lahdi engineering force as it was reinforcing the position scoring direct goals.

In response to the enemy's going too far in targeting the secured villages and wounding civilians at Arab-Salim, however, the IR's Artillery Reinforcement Unit (ARU) bombarded, at 16:50, the posts of Berkat Risha, Dahr El-Jamal, Dawawir and Rmeish with the rockets and heavy artillery shells. The militants managed to chalk up direct hits warning the enemy that they will not sit on their hands in the case of more aggressions.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, at 13:25, several shells at Baraasheit compound.
