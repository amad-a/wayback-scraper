MARTYRS 

Name: Mohamed Hani Akhdar

DOB: 1980

Place of birth: Jibsheet.

Marital status: Single.

The martyr joined the Islamic Resistance in 1999. He completed a number of cultural and  military training courses. Took part in a number of operations and is he also a member of the martyr group.

He achieved martyrdom on the 21st of ‎April‎,‎‎ 2000, while fulfilling his duties in facing Israeli warplanes.
