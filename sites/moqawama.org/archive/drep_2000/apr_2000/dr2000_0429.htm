Daily Report

April Saturday 29-2000:


Introduction

In one of the most courageous attacks ever mounted by the Islamic Resistance (IR), four "Israeli"-proxy militiamen were killed and ten others wounded when the IR militants blew up Aramta outpost in the northern sector of the occupation zone. Two "Israeli" soldiers were injured, also, in a series of attacks against the enemy positions along the front-line.

In its statement, the IR vowed that the shattered remains of Aramta outpost "will be the image of all "Israeli"-allied militia posts in the near future".

In the meantime, Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said that the collaborators had three options, "either to turn themselves in to the judiciary, get killed or escape from Lebanon".


Occupation acknowledges: 4 Lahdis killed, collaborators and occupation troops wounded

"The "Israeli" occupation's media acknowledged that four members of agent Antoine Lahd's militia were killed and ten others were wounded in the resistance's attacks on Friday.

Security sources said five collaborators were killed in the resistance's operations all in the barracks of the occupation and its collaborators in Aramta.

The sources unveiled the names of those killed. They are Issam al-Isaimi, Rafiq Abu Hamad, Bassam Zuieher, Issam Abu Shahla, and Ziad Raidan.

Ten collaborators and two "Israeli" soldiers were taken to the Rambam hospital in Haifa due to their serious injuries, the sources added.

Until noon (Friday), the occupation and its collaborators were talking about removing the debris of the Aramta outpost as at least three collaborators are still under the debris of the three-story building which was leveled to the ground"(1)

AFP mentioned that Hizbullah launched a mass bombardment with rockets and mortars on "Israeli" army and allied militia positions at dawn Friday, police said.

Hizbullah, the main group fighting "Israel's" occupation of southern Lebanon, pounded 26 redoubts across the occupied zone with some 180 mortar bombs and Katyusha rockets.

At the same time, fighters with light arms and anti-tank rockets attacked four positions on the edge of the central sector of the zone, Aramta, Baraasheit, Shqaif al-Namel and al-Ghozlan.

At Aramta Hizbullah said its fighters killed or wounded all the occupants and blew up the fortifications before withdrawing unharmed(2)

However, the Islamic Resistance (IR) released a communiqué in which it reported that "in the framework of continuing the promised liberation mission and eliminating the Zionist occupation troops and their collaborators crouched on our sacred soil, the hero militants blew up completely another enemy position indicating the image of the dreadful fate of the remaining "Israeli" agents' posts in the near future.

Early morning of Friday (28-4-2000), common groups of the IR and Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) overrun, at 6:00, the Lahdi position of Aramta and attacked another 24 enemy posts along the front-line in order to paralyze them.

The mujahedeen were able to control entirely Aramta position as a special Lahdi company, forged  to act as direct backing up force, was inside it inflicting all its members. Then, the IR squads of Engineering Brigade intruded the position and detonated a number of powerful and huge explosive devices destroying it completely.

At the same time, the IR staged simultaneously a lot of attacks as follows:

-            The team of martyrs Haidar Mazloum and Assadallah Shamas targeted Bir Kallab outpost where the militants managed to destroy a number of its fortifications.

-            The squad of martyrs Hassan Mildan and Hassan Mazloum clashed, from a close range, with Mothallath position striking its ramparts and barring its members from the intervention.

-            The group of martyrs Hussein Aoudeh and Aqil Ayoub targeted Shayar Azour compound with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades striking the fortifications which are overlooking the scene. The militants managed to prevent it from backing the attacked positions.

-            The team of martyrs Ali Shaaban and Fadlallah Ibrahim attacked the Lahdi barracks of Rihan which is the HQ of the 10th Brigade Command.

-            The squad of martyrs Mohieddine Najjar and Ibrahim Sabra targeted the "Israeli" Command of the 10th barracks of Rihan.

-            The group of martyrs Ali El-Ammar and Abbas Abdallah bombarded Kassaret Al-Oroush compound whereas the team of martyrs Moussa Diab and Yassine Chehab targeted Berkat Dajaj post scoring accurate hits.

-            The squads of martyrs Youssef Badran, Ali Nasrallah, Abdallah Rammal and Hassan Rida in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit pounded the enemy emplacements in Shraifeh, Abou Qamha, Dallafeh and Aishieh paralyzing them entirely.

-            The group of martyr Sami Melhem and Ali Madi rushed upon Baraasheit compound from several sides hitting its ramparts.

-            The team of martyrs Ali Qassem and Mahmoud Harmoush fell upon Soweida outpost with various kinds of weapons chalking up accurate goals.

-            The squad of martyrs Mohammad Faqih and Hassan Ghamloush attacked the posts of Radar and Blat scoring certain hits.

-            The group of martyrs Haitham Faqih and Rameh Mahdi targeted Jamoussa position of the 80th Brigade Command whereas the team of martyrs Hassan El-Fayrouzi and Ali Shamas staged an attack against Rshaf outpost.

-            The squad of martyrs Abdallah Khalifeh and Sobhi Chokr launched an attack against Haddatha compound.

-            The group of martyrs Hachem Ballouq and Ali Melhem fell upon Hemayed position.

-            The team of martyrs Ali Amhaz and Mahdi Zohaiter rushed upon Houla post.

-            The squad of martyrs Zein El-Abidine Suleiman and Issa-El-Hoqq attacked Taibeh compound.

-            The group of martyrs Ali Qansou and Ali El-Hoqq targeted Jabal El-Jamaah position.

-            The team of martyrs Ibrahim Khiami and Fadl Atwi targeted Dabsheh outpost.

-            The squad of martyrs Youssef Bzeih and Ahmad Olayan attacked Beaufort Castle, whereas the group of martyrs Hassanto Rizk and Ibrahim El-Hajj targeted the positions of Ain Qiniah and Ahmadieh preventing them from the intervention.

Later on, at 8:45, the IR team of martyrs Taha Tahan and Jaafar Alameh rushed upon the enemy reinforcements as they were brought to the destroyed Aramta position. Using the rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.

Also, the IR squad of martyrs Hussein Ali Hodroj and Ossama El-Husseini targeted the destroyed Aramta position as the Lahdi militiamen were lifting the rubbles. The militants, who used the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, were able to chalk up direct goals.

Moreover, the IR unit of martyrs Ali and Moussa Ahmad Salman targeted, at 20:00, an observation tower of Shamaa post with the rocket-propelled grenades striking it directly and destroying a radar installation.

Furthermore, and in retaliation of the "Israeli" shelling of Majdal Zoun village, the IR team of martyrs Ahmad Ali Chohaib and Ali Ahmad Zaarour in the Artillery Reinforcement Unit bombarded, at 21:45, Tair Harfa with the artillery shells chalking up direct goals(3)


Qassem: "Israeli" agents will never find a hideout after today(4)

"Hizbullahs deputy secretary-general, Sheikh Nahim Qassem, on Friday said that the "Israeli"-proxy militiamen had three options, either to turn themselves in to the judiciary, get killed or escape from Lebanon...
Many of you may not witness the Zionist withdrawal because you will be dead under the ground and Aramta is the proof, he said addressing the militiamen. He added that the collaborators will never find a hideout after today.
"The attack against Aramta was a good example of the high level of coordination Hizbullah employs on the battlefield.
Hizbullah has concentrated its efforts in recent weeks on the cluster of positions in the northern sector. "In light of Fridays assault on Aramta, these attacks appear to have been a deliberate probing of defenses prior to launching a major assault".


Occupation forces fire 400 shells on front-line villages(3)

"The "Israeli" occupation forces on Friday turned the villages and towns adjacent to the occupied zone into a real battlefield with hysteric aggressions in which the enemy's field artillery, mortars, tanks, warplanes and helicopters took part.

"As the occupation's air force staged several raids on Iqleem al-Toffah, the artillery pounded the Western Beqaa Hilltops, Hasbaya District, and several areas in the western sector with more than 400 rounds. "The "Israeli" aggressions damaged houses along with water pipes and electricity grids in the targeted areas".


Hoss: pullout will bring foreign investment(4)

Prime Minister Salim Hoss said on Friday that an "Israeli" withdrawal from the south would spur badly-needed foreign investment to help Lebanon out of its recession.
For the first time, "Israel" is being forced to give up Arab territory, he said. Our victory over "Israeli" occupation will be the beginning of our victory over the economic crisis.
What has hindered and still hinders investment is the lack of stability, which is caused by continuing "Israeli" aggression against Lebanon.
Lebanon is mentioned in the media only when it is subject to "Israeli" bombardment which, in the eyes of foreign investors, makes Lebanon unstable and unsafe. This is of course untrue, he said. Hoss went on to say he expected the country to attract investors after withdrawal.

"Israeli" Arabs warn against granting refuge to "Israeli" allied militiamen(5)

""Israeli" Arab legislators and officials are demanding that "Israel" deny refuge to its south Lebanon allied militia and their families after the July pullout. MP Mohammed Barakeh said lawmakers and officials have met in a northern Arab "Israeli" village to plan tactics to oppose a government decision to settle militiamen and their families. Barakeh said they will not host quote traitors, adding the fighters should stand trial in Lebanon. The lawmaker urged the one million Arabs in "Israel" not to help the militiamen, saying they will spoil his people. Militiamen who are controlled, financed, and trained by "Israel", face military trials in Beirut for collaborating with the Jewish state and helping "Israel" wage its war against Lebanon. The militia has asked "Israel's" highest court to grant its fighters refuge following the planned withdrawal of occupation forces from the south. But Prime Minister Ehud Barak has said a solution for most of the militiamen would be found in Lebanon".


IR announces martyrdom of one of its men(6)

"The Islamic Resistance (IR) proudly announces to its honorable people the martyrdom of Fouad Abbas Shehab (Abu Ali) who was martyred during the heroic confrontations fought by IR against the "Israeli" occupation forces and their collaborators on Friday, April 28, 2000, an IR communiqué read.

"Martyr Shehab, 32, married, joined IR in 1989, participated in several military training courses and qualitative jihad operations including storming the outposts of Soweida and Ilman al-Shoumarieh," the communiqué added .

"He was wounded several times during confrontations with the Zionist enemy and its collaborators, received encomium from Hizbullah's secretary general, was martyred during the confrontations fought by IR against the outposts of the Zionist enemy in al-Ahmadieh and Ain Qinya in the Western Beqaa".


Pullout in sight, but villagers have had enough and flee(7)

i.a. *

"The occupation of south Lebanon may only last another few weeks but the residents of the frontline village of Majdal Zoun have had enough.
After years of enduring almost daily artillery bombardments and air raids in and around the dusty hilltop village, almost the entire population evacuated their homes last week and are now living in Tyre, 15 kilometers to the north.
The final straw came 11 days ago when a shell fired from an "Israeli" Merkava tank struck a house in the center of the village, killing a Syrian worker and wounding three others. The tank, located in the village of Shahine inside the occupation zone, fired three rounds. Two exploded harmlessly in tobacco fields. The third exploded inside the house, less than 100 meters from a position manned by Nepalese UNIFIL troops.
It was the latest in a series of assaults against the village ­ a mix of indiscriminate shelling from the "Israeli"-allied militiamen and deliberate air strikes by "Israeli" helicopter gunships.
The people here are terrified, they are very afraid, said Nawaf Harmoush, the director of Majdal Zoun school. Our neighbors are the enemy and they are firing into our homes.
The Syrian worker was killed the day school closed for the Easter holidays. Harmoush said that school reopens on Tuesday. I dont know if any of the children will show up now, he said.
Fatima Dour, who lives next to the building hit by the Merkava tank, said that most of the 2,000 villagers fled after the Syrian worker was killed. Less than 40 people remain.
Dour said that some residents returned two days ago, but only to collect some extra clothes and belongings. They have rented houses in Tyre or Bourj Shemali or are staying with relatives, Dour said, adding that she refused to leave her home.
On March 28, two children, aged 17 and six, were wounded when mortar rounds exploded in the heart of the village. They were fired from the Lahdi post in the village of Shemaa, one kilometer southeast of Majdal Zoun.
On March 8, Two "Israeli" helicopter gunships hovered above Tair Harfa, two kilometers south of Majdal Zoun, and fired a pair of TOW missiles into an abandoned house, again in the middle of the village. The house was destroyed. The fragments of the missiles remain uncollected amongst the rubble.
Deeb Dour, 70, whose house lies beside the targeted building, fled with his family when he heard the approaching helicopters. The missile struck his neighbors house when the family were barely 10 meters away.
Our fear is mixed with a sense of hope that this agony will soon end when the "Israelis" leave Lebanon, Harmoush said.
