Daily Report

April Saturday 15-2000:

 

Occupation postpones release of 13 detainees(1)
"The "Israeli" enemy's prisons service spokeswoman Orit Messer-Harel on Friday said the Lebanese detainees who will be freed next week from the Ayalon prison will be moved to an "Israeli" prison at the border with Lebanon.

"She said the measure is prelude to handing the 13 detainees to the International Committee for the Red Cross (ICRC) that will transport them to the Lebanese territories.

The occupation's entity postponed the release which had been scheduled for Friday to next week following a request from the Zionist army.

"The postponement is due to technical reasons, said Messer-Harel without giving details.

Today the enemy's radio announced the names of the detainees that are to freed. They are Hussein Baheej Ahmed, Ahmed Mohammed Taleb, Hussein Ahmed Rmaiti, Hussein Fahd Daqdouq, Hassan Sadreddeen Hijazi, Bilal Abdel-Hassan Dakroub, Abdel-Hassan Hassan Srour, Yousef Yaqoub Srour, Abbas Hassan Srour, Ali Hussein Ammar, Ahmed Mohsen Ammar, Mohammed Abdel-Hadi Yassin, and Ahmed Baheej Jalloul.

Likud Knesset member Dani Naveh asked the court to reconsider the decision to release the Lebanese detainees.

"He presented the "Israeli" cabinet's legal advisor with a request in this regard on behalf of the families of the Zionist soldiers who have gone missing in Lebanon.

"Naveh said he is considering that a law hindering the release be endorsed by the Knesset".
