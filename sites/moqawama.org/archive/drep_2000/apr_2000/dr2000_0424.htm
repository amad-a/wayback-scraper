Daily Report

April Monday 24-2000:


"Israeli" Aggressions(1)

The IR released a statement in which it mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:

00:15 one 81mm and four 155mm shells landed along the Litani river.

00:40 two 120mm shells landed on the outskirts of Oqmata.

05:30 combing with 12.7mm caliber machine gun from the Aramta post on its outskirts.

Combing with 12.7mm caliber machine gun on the outskirts of Haddatha.

21:20 combing with 12.7mm machine gun on Al-Ras Al-Sharki.

Footnotes:

1- IR statement, 23-4-2000.
