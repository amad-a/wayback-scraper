Daily Report

April Tuesday 11-2000:


Introduction

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said that there could be no comprehensive Arab-"Israeli" peace as long as the Jewish stated existed. ""Israel" is a terrorist and racist state that does not obey any logic but that of force", he said.

Meanwhile, the head of Hizbullah's parliamentary bloc stressed that "even if "Israel" withdraws from South Lebanon and Western Beqaa, we will continue to bear responsibility to liberate Quds and Palestine".


Hizbullah: solution means return of Jews to where they came from(1)
"Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah underlined that comprehensive peace in our region requires the return of the Jews to where they came from for the Zionist project which has created a state more than 50 years ago through usurping land, killing, and displacement. "Israel" will not stop seeking to achieve its expansionist goals in the region and if it could dominate, it would master the regional policy, economy, water and ties with the world.

Speaking at Hizbullah's central rally to mark Ashura, Sayyed Nasrallah said, "Why do we want to run away from the fact that there is a Zionist project that came to this region 50 years ago, created a state which is this projects' military, political and security base".

"This project wants to dominate this region and be the gate between the world and this region," he said.

"If this project manages to dominate (the region), a day will come, for instance, when "Israel", not the US, will tell OPEC how much oil it can produce and at what price it must sell it, a day will come when "Israel" will not discuss with us the water issue but will determine our share of water, and a day will come when "Israel" will interfere even in our international affairs and tell us with whom we can have ties and with whom we cannot."

"Some people are looking for solutions to reach a stable and secure situation, I say to them you are dreaming; if you want a word for the sake of truth, history and Allah, without deceit, distortion, and without beating around the bush: the reason behind the great catastrophes in this region is a "state called "Israel"; as long as a state called "Israeli" is in existence, these catastrophes and pains will continue," he added.


Sayyed: We will continue the responsibility of resistance(2)

"Baalback-Hermel MP Ibrahim Amin Sayyed, the head of Hizbullahs parliamentary bloc, said that the responsibility of resistance to occupation should not cease after an "Israeli" withdrawal from south Lebanon, but should continue until all of Palestine is liberated.
Even if "Israel" withdraws from south Lebanon and the Western Beqaa, we will continue to bear responsibility to liberate Quds and Palestine, he said at a gathering marking Ashoura in Amsheet, north of Beirut.
How we will continue the struggle is quite another matter, he added, in line with Hizbullahs tight-lipped policy over whether it intends to continue its military attacks after an "Israeli" withdrawal from the south.
Sayyed noted that an "Israeli" withdrawal would be just the first but not the last stage of victory over the Zionist and American enemies.
He also said that an "Israeli" withdrawal should not entail the deployment of a new multinational force other than UNIFIL.
We will not talk about the creation of a new multinational force because Lebanon has not been officially asked to answer such demands. At any rate, the deployment of extra peacekeepers to protect "Israel's" northern flank will not resolve contentious issues, Sayyed said.
Lebanon has already announced it will never be a police guard for "Israel" or strike a separate deal with "Israel" behind Syrias back.
Sayyed also accused the UN of sanctioning decades of "Israeli" terrorism.
All the crimes committed by "Israel" over the past century were executed under UN and American cover, he charged. The "Israeli" government has only now resorted to the UN to discuss its withdrawal plans from Lebanon in a bid to screen its crimes and the financial and human losses it inflicted on Lebanon.

Fadlallah: "Israel" will never be a legitimate entity(3)

Instead of worrying whether "Israel" is going to withdraw with or without a peace deal, we should instead focus on one single notion: "Israel" will never be a legitimate entity, even if the whole world recognizes its existence, said Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah in a ceremony commemorating Ashoura in Beiruts Haret Hreik suburb.
At this current stage of the political game, we should stop scurrying after defeatist ideas and refrain from wondering whether the door for negotiations is still open and whether the US-Zionist summit could succeed, Fadlallah added, referring to this weeks talks between President Clinton and "Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak.
He further charged that merely considering "Israel's" peace plans was the same as accepting "Israel's" seizure of Palestine.
The issue here is not whether "Israel" pulls out its troops a few meters here or there. We should not be discussing these issues as if "Israel" has the right to occupy Palestine and set up a state over captured land, he said.


"Israeli" Aggressions(4)

The IR issued a communiqué in which it reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:

3:15, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the surroundings of Baraasheit compound.

5:50, Mount Zaatar was shelled by four 155mm missiles.

Between 6:45 and 7:00, the west of Baraasheit compound was bombarded by twenty 155mm missiles and a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

7:20, the area between the villages of Haddatha and Aita El-Jabal was pounded by seventeen 155mm missiles and six 120mm shells.

14:55, six 155mm missiles landed the perimeters of Haddatha.

Between 14:55 and 15:30, the road of Kafra-Haris came under three 120mm shells and two 155mm missiles damaging the houses of Youssef Jawad and Mofid El-Faqih.

16:55, the surroundings of Houla lawn exposed to a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets.

20:05, one 155mm missile was unleashed at the west of Yater village.

20:10, three 155mm missiles fell on the peripheries of Jibal El-Botom.

22:45, a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds hit the surroundings of Beit Yahoun outpost.


Military Operations(5)

The IR team of martyrs Ahmad Daoud Hammoud and Ali Salman attacked, at 14:45, a Lahdi engineering force as its beefing up Rshaf post. Using the adequate arms, the mujahedeen managed to score direct hits.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, at 6:45, seven 120mm shells.
