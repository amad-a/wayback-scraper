Daily Report

April Saturday 08-2000:


Another "Israeli" agent militiaman surrenders to Hizbullah(1)

"An "Israeli" allied militiaman surrendered to Hizbullah on Friday, the second to do so in just 24 hours.
Helmi Yasser Awad, who served for18 months at the Lahdi post of Houla in the occupied zone, turned himself over to residents of a village at the edge of the zone early on Friday and later surrendered to Hizbullah.
Awad is the fourth militiaman to desert this month.
"Desertions among the "Israeli" proxy militia have increased since "Israel's" announcement that it would withdraw from the south by July.
According to Lebanese security services, between 2,400 and 2,500 men are left in the militia ranks today, compared to some 2,800 a year ago.
A report published in "Israel's" Yediot Ahronot newspaper on Thursday said "Israel" would pay the militia $175 million in compensation after it ends its 22-year occupation of the south.
The Lebanese government has said it will prosecute all the militiamen and treat them as collaborators.
"Lahd, along with other militia officials, has been sentenced to death in absentia for high treason by a Lebanese court".


"Israeli" Aggressions(2)

In its statement, the IR mentioned the following "Israeli" aggressions:

12:45, three 120mm shells hit Mount Sojod, whereas a volley of 12.7mm heavy machine gun bullets struck the perimeters of Loweizeh village.

13:05, seven 155mm missiles slammed into the peripheries of Loweizeh.

21:30, Mount Kabir, west of Siddiqeen village, was bombarded by eleven 155mm missiles.

Between 21:35 and 22:00, the perimeters of Yater village came under seventeen 155mm missiles and six 107mm shells.

21:55, the road of Sawan area was shelled by five 155mm missiles.


IR and LRB operations against occupation forces(3)
The Islamic Resistance (IR)-squad of Martyrs Abdallah Rammal and Shawqi Baydoun at 12:40 Friday attacked enemy forces in the "Israeli" occupation's outpost in Dhour al-Kassara with machine guns and rocket propelled grenades in a violent and concentrated manner scoring direct hits.

In the meantime, squad of Martyrs Ali Qansou and Usama al-Khalil staged a similar attack on the occupation's outpost in Beir Kallab.

Also, the IR team of martyrs Mahdi Ali Zoaiter and Zein El-Abidine Mohammad Suleiman attacked, at 17:50, Berkat Dajaj position with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

For its part, the Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) at 8:00 a.m. Friday attacked the occupation's outpost in al-Qalaah with rocket propelled grenades scoring accurate hits.

Another LRB squad, meanwhile, pounded the Dabsheh outpost with the appropriate weapons barring it from backing al-Qalaah and scoring direct hits in its fortifications.
