Daily Report

April Wednesday 12-2000:

Introduction

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah stressed that "people should be aware of the value of the victory that has been achieved because it's no small or simple thing… we've paid a high price".

Meanwhile, Prime Minister Salim Hoss dismissed "Israel's" ambiguous stance regarding the Lebanese-"Israeli" border noting that there is only one internationally recognized boundary to which the "Israelis" must withdraw.

However, another "Israeli" agent turned himself over to Hizbullah bringing the number of collaborator deserters to seven in two weeks.

Jewish state admits defeat, says Nasrallah…(1)

"Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah expressed his “surprise” on Tuesday at doubts that "Israel's" decision to withdraw from the south resulted from a defeat when the "Israeli" media was admitting as such.
“People should be aware of the value of the victory that has been achieved because it’s no small or simple thing … we’ve paid a high price,” he said during a Koranic session in Baalback on the occasion of Ashoura.
Nasrallah touched on the debate in "Israel" between those demanding withdrawal to the international border and the military, which wants to keep positions within the occupation zone.
“Those who want a unilateral withdrawal are only concerned with not giving Hizbullah a pretext for continuing its armed attacks,” he said. “They’re also not convinced by claims by "Israel's" military leaders that keeping these positions is necessary to maintain the security.”
Commenting on "Israeli" Premier Ehud Barak’s visit to Washington on Tuesday, Nasrallah said: “Barak’s visit to Washington is not a picnic but an attempt to search for a formula to get out of Lebanon so that "Israel" won’t have to face the same experience it did when it withdrew from Jezzine.”
"Two "Israeli"-allied militiamen were killed and several others wounded as the militia retreated from Jezzine last June".

Hoss: ‘There is only one border’…(2)

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss dismissed "Israel's" ambiguous stance regarding the Lebanese-"Israeli" border on Tuesday, noting there is only one internationally recognized boundary ­ to which the "Israelis" must withdraw.
"Hoss said that the "Israelis" speak of four different borders, any of which the "Israeli" Army might deploy behind. These borders, Hoss said, are the original 1923 frontier drawn up by the British and French mandate authorities when the states of Lebanon and Palestine were created; the 1949 Armistice Demarcation Line; the 1978 line; and the existing boundary.
“These words are baseless,” Hoss said. “The 1923 and 1949 borders are the same with no difference at all. The so-called ‘1978’ border does not exist. The situation at the time on the border was loose and "Israel" used to enter Lebanese territory whenever and however it pleased.”
When the armistice line was established in 1949, the UN decreed that the line follow exactly the 1923 frontier. This is the “internationally recognized” border that is referred to in Resolution 425.
The border in place today differs from the 1923/1949 line because the "Israelis" pushed the boundary fence up to 500 meters into Lebanon in five separate places from the early 1980s. The incursion zones allow the "Israelis" to dominate the high ground and serve as buffers to settlements that abut the border.
"Hoss said that "Israel's" references to multiple borders were designed to muddle the issue".

Khalil warns: withdrawal is an "Israeli" plot…(3)

"Tyre MP Ali Khalil, the chairman of Parliament’s Foreign Affairs Committee, said on Tuesday that "Israel's" claims that it will withdraw from Lebanon were mere attempts to redeploy in the occupation zone and urge a reconsideration of UNIFIL’s role.
“This requires the approval of the Lebanese government, which demands that it receives an official and written plan,” he said.
Khalil reiterated Lebanon’s refusal to give "Israel" security guarantees, which he said would only come in the context of a just and comprehensive peace agreement.
"Khalil also reiterated that President Emile Lahoud’s memorandum to United Nations Secretary-General Kofi Annan reflected the official and popular Lebanese position".

New "Israeli" proxy militia defection…(4)

"Another "Israeli"-allied militiaman turned himself over to Hizbullah, bringing the number of collaborator deserters to seven in two weeks, a statement from the group said.
"Ali Hassan Abu Taan, 21, from Taibeh, abandoned his post at Mohaisbat in the central sector and surrendered to the resistance group".

Safa: A lobby for the release of detainees…(5)

"The follow up committee for the release of detainees said a serious international campaign is underway to help free Lebanese held by "Israeli" occupation troops.

" At a news conference in Beirut, the head of the follow up committee for the release of Lebanese detainees, Mohammed Safa, said numerous governments and human rights groups are currently lobbying "Israel" to release more than 125 Lebanese it holds without trial or charge at the Khiam detention center. Safa who updated reporters on his group's recent participation at the 56th human rights committee meeting in Geneva, said French officials pledged to lobby for the release of detainees held by "Israel". Safa said a number of workshops and seminars were held to unveil "Israeli" atrocities against Lebanese civilians it continues to detain".

More "Israeli" violations of understanding

"The "Israeli" occupation forces have once again violated the April Understanding. The enemy's warplanes at noon on Tuesday staged three raids on Mount al-Kabir (near the Rmadieh town, Tyre).

The first raid was at 11:45, the second at 11:55 and the third at 12:10.

"The ten air-to-ground missiles which were fired by the occupation's air force caused heavy damage. No casualties were reported as the residents had fled the area when around 25 155mm artillery rounds landed in the area prior to the air aggressions.

"In the morning, the occupation's warplanes staged a raid on the area between Khorbet Selem and al-Sawaneh. The raid was followed by a series of mock air raids over the areas of Nabatieh and Iqleem al-Toffah"...(6)

Military Operations…(8)

The IR team of martyrs Ali Dakhlallah Madi and Ali Mohammad Qassem attacked, at 15:15, enemy movements inside Baraasheit compound with the appropriate arms scoring direct and accurate hits.

Fifteen minutes later, the IR squad of martyrs Ali Ahmad Nazzal and Hassan Ali Al-Faitrouni targeted Bir Kallab outpost. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the mujahedeen managed to chalk up direct and definite goals.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Ali Issa Shamas and Issa Mohammad El-Hoq was rushing upon Dohour El-Kassara position with the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades. The militants were able to score direct and certain hits.

At the same time, the IR unit of martyrs Ali Hussein El-Hoq and Hassan Mohammad Moussa attacked Berkat Dajaj post with the rocket-propelled grenades. Definite goals were chalked up, the militants reported.

In the meantime, the IR team of martyrs Rameh Hussein Mahdi and Ahmad Youssef Amhaz was falling upon Shayar Azour position. The mujahedeen, who used the rocket-propelled grenades, managed to score certain goals.

For its part, the fighters of Lebanese Resistance Brigades (LRB) staged, at 10:50, a rocket attack against the "Israeli" post of Blat scoring direct hits.

At the same time, another group of LRB attacked Tair Harfa outpost with the artillery shells. The fighters chalked up definite goals where the smoke columns were seen billowing out of it.

However, Amal Movement fired, at 13:55, two missiles at Haddatha compound.
