Daily Report

April Friday 07-2000:

Introduction

Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said that "we will not agree to a single inch of Lebanese land remaining in the hands of the "Israeli" enemy after it withdraws humiliated".

Meanwhile, Prime Minister Salim Hoss said that as long as the plight of the Palestinian refugees was unresolved, "Israel" would be held responsible for security breaches in South Lebanon following a troop withdrawal.

However, the "Israeli" daily Maariv newspaper reported that the majority of "Israeli" army officers oppose a unilateral withdrawal from Lebanon.


"Israel" must not keep "one inch" of Lebanese territory: Hizbullah(1)

" "Israel" must withdraw from Lebanon without keeping "one inch" of its territory, a top Hizbullah official said Thursday, adding the group will announce later how it will act after the "Israeli" pullback.

Hizbullah "will not agree to a single inch of Lebanese land remaining in the hands of the "Israeli" enemy after it withdraws humiliated, and with no booty to show for it," said Sheikh Naim Qassem, Hizbullah's second-in-command in a speech marking the beginning of the Islamic new year.

"We cannot accept such an enemy ... blackmailing us, or allowing its attacks and occupation to receive compensation," he said .

"It is only afterwards that we shall speak of what we will do and the detailed positions we will adopt towards other crises provoked by this enemy" in the Middle East, he added, in a reference to the issues of the Palestinians and Jerusalem.

"Hizbullah spearheads effort to force "Israeli" troops out of Lebanon.

"The "Israeli" army put forward a plan last month -- rejected by Prime Minister Ehud Barak -- which involved keeping small enclaves inside the frontier strip it currently occupies in south Lebanon.

"Israel" has pledged to withdraw its troops from Lebanon by the end of July".


PM hammers away at right of return for Palestinians(2)

"Prime Minister Salim Hoss said Thursday that as long as the plight of the Palestinian refugees was unresolved, "Israel" would be held responsible for any security breaches in the south following a troop withdrawal.
"Israel", said Hoss, must take responsibility for security breaches along the border as long as it prevents finding solutions for such explosive issues as the Palestinian refugees.
The premier made the statement after a meeting with Terje Roed-Larsen, the UN Middle East peace coordinator.
"Hoss confirmed Lebanons refusal to take responsibility for protecting the security of the southern borders and consequently our rejection of "Israel's" threat to Lebanon of painful blows in the event that any incident takes place there.

"Israel" proceeds with disintegrating militia, endorses compensations(3)
"There has been a further indication that the "Israeli" occupation's command is proceeding with its project to liquidate the Lahdi militia although the militia's chief Antoine Lahd had said he intended to stay in the Lebanese occupied zone after the withdrawal.

"The "Israeli" daily Yediot Ahronot Thursday said ""Israel"" will give $175 m in compensations to around 2500 militiamen and another 500 collaborators in the occupation's so-called civil administrations.

"The newspaper said another unspecified sum of money will be allocated to some 600 militiamen's families that are going to leave south Lebanon.

"The compensation budget was endorsed during a meeting with the occupation's coordinator Uri Lubrani. The compensation will raise to the double the cost of the Zionist evacuation which was previously estimated at $375 m".


"Israel" military officers criticize withdrawal(4)

"The Maariv newspaper reported Thursday that most army officials are opposed to Barak's plan to end the 22-year occupation of what it calls a "security zone" without first having in hand a peace accord with Syria.

"It said army brass were privately describing the plan as a "trap" and that any hope of having a UN force in the region would at best provide "a false sense of security."


UNIFIL takes its case to NY
Peacekeepers to warn Annan about perils of partial pullout(5)

"UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan will be advised by senior UNIFIL personnel next week that the failure of "Israel" to stage a full withdrawal to the international border and the continued existence of the "Israeli" proxy militia will almost certainly prolong the violence and undermine the capabilities of the peacekeeping force.
UNIFIL commander General Kofi Obeng and his special adviser, Timur Goksel, are traveling to New York on Sunday to hold consultations with Annan.
Well brief the UN on the latest developments and the impact on our contingency planning, Goksel said, adding that he and Obeng would be in New York for at least a week.
"Judging from recent statements by "Israeli" officials, if the option of a unilateral withdrawal is chosen ­ as appears increasingly likely given the deadlock in direct peace talks between "Israel" and Syria ­ the "Israeli" Army will not retire to the international border but to the so-called Purple Line.
The Purple Line represents the de-facto military border where the boundary fence has been pushed northward and westward to capture the high ground or to protect "Israeli" settlements that abut the frontier. "Israeli" troops would retain four outposts located on these parcels of Lebanese land and another three that straddle the border.
However, UNIFIL is not expected to deploy in areas vacated by the "Israeli" Army if the withdrawal is considered incomplete. The UN considers the international border as following the 1949 Armistice Demarcation Line, which in turn follows the 1923 mandate boundary. A partial withdrawal will be interpreted by Hizbullah as a mere redeployment and invite continued resistance attacks.
Four of the countries comprising UNIFIL ­ France, Italy, Ireland and Finland ­ have made it clear that they will not contribute troops if the "Israelis" fail to stage a full withdrawal.
Nobody is thinking of a peace-making mission, said a Western diplomat. Can you imagine European troops trying to enforce peace when caught between the "Israeli" Army with all its sophisticated military hardware and Hizbullahs extremely effective guerrillas?
The situation could be further imperiled if the "Israeli"-allied militia remains behind as a military force, as threatened on Monday by militia commander Antoine Lahd.
Lahd said he would continue to control the zone and prevent the state from re-asserting its authority unless the militiamen were given amnesty. Such comments, said Goksel, certainly complicate matters.

IR destroys more enemy ramparts(7)
In a new qualitative victory by the Islamic Resistance (IR) and in spite of the precautionary measures adopted by the enemy command to protect their outpost from the resistance men's missiles, IR managed on Thursday to destroy the major rampart of al-Dabsheh outpost.

The IR squad of martyrs Ali Hussein Mortada & Ali Ghorr El-Zayoun at 6:05 a.m. staged a concentrated violent attack with heavy artillery rounds on the fortifications of al-Dabsheh outpost. The fortifications were completely destroyed.

Afterwards, the IR unit of martyrs Ammar Haidar Bitar & Said Hussein Zehri targeted the outpost's major rampart which was accurately hit an completely destroyed as well.

At the same time, the IR squad of martyrs Mohammad Hussein Haidar & Jihad Ali Anani meanwhile, pounded the same outpost scoring more direct hits.

Moreover, IR group of martyrs Rameh Hussein Mahdi & Ahmad Ali Shreim targeted the Beaufort outpost with the appropriate rocket propelled grenades thus paralyzing the outpost and barring it from interference.

IR men used machine guns and rocket propelled grenades in the attack scoring accurate hits and barring the enemy force from accomplishing its task.

Earlier, the IR team of martyrs Qassem Mohammad Rammal and Fadl Daoud Ibrahim attacked, at 00:15, a Lahdi engineering force as it was fortifying Kassaret Al-Oroush compound. Using the machine guns and rocket-propelled grenades, the militants were able to score direct and certain hits.

Moreover, the IR unit of martyrs Naji Fawzi Abou Daya and Youssef Abdel-Hassan Badran attacked, at 14:35, enemy movements inside Haddatha compound. The mujahedeen, who used the proper weapons, managed to chalk up direct goals.

Meanwhile, the IR group of martyrs Ali Abbas El-Aouta and Hassan Ibrahim Rida was rushing upon a Lahdi engineering force as it was reinforcing Baraasheit compound. The militants, who used the adequate arms, were able to score direct hits.

Furthermore, the IR team of martyrs Hussein Nimr Nahim and Hussein Mohammad Al-Salman targeted violently, at 19:35, the "Israeli" post of Taibeh with the rocket-propelled grenades chalking up direct and definite goals.

Also, the IR group of martyrs Toufic Ali Hassoun and Ali Abdel-Wahab Amhaz targeted, at 20:45, the Lahdi post of Qobaa with the rocket-propelled grenades scoring direct hits.

For its part, Amal Movement fired, at 6:30, three 107mm rocket-propelled grenades at Taibeh position. Fifteen minutes later, it unleashed four 81mm rounds at Mohaisbat position.

Also, Amal Movement attacked, at 20:15 and 21:15, Baraasheit and Rshaf compounds respectively firing a number of missiles.
