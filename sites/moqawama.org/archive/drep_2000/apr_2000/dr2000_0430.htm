Daily Report

April Sunday 30-2000:


Introduction

On the sideline of the Nationalist-Islamic Conference (NIC), Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah hosted several Arab and Lebanese delegations which expressed their support to the Resistance and the importance of this "unique experience" in resurrecting the Islamic and Arab peoples.

However, Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem warned that the IR's patience will be exhausted if the Zionist enemy exceeds the limits.


Hizbullah SG hosts Arab Lebanese delegations(1)

The activity of Hizbullah's Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah on Friday and Saturday was full with a series of meetings with Arab an Lebanese delegations that came to Hizbullah's Secretariat HQ to discuss the conditions of Arabs and Moslems and their central cause: occupied Palestine.

On the sideline of the Nationalist-Islamic Conference (NIC), the Hizbullah Secretariat was visited by: - the Yemeni delegation including: NIC's General Coordinator Mohammad Abdel-Malek al-Mutawakel, the Secretary-General of the Nasserite Popular Unionist Organization Abdel-Malek Khelaf, al-Haq (Right) Party's Head of the Political Department Abdel-Karim al-Khiwani and Assistant Secretary-General Mohammad al-Maqaleh, and Yemeni Socialist Baath Party's Command Council Member Jarallah Omar.

The Moroccan delegation: President of the Civilized Substitute Sheikh Ibrahim Kamal, his Deputy Dr Mustafa al-Mo'tasem, Moroccan Parliament Member Mustafa al-Rmaid, Justice and Charity Command Council Members Abdel-Wahid Mutawakel and Fat'halla Erslan, and President of al-Ummah Movement Mohammad Al-Mardani.

His Eminence discussed with the Yemeni and Moroccans the Resistance affairs and the importance of this "unique experience" in resurrecting the Islamic and Arab peoples.

His Eminence also hosted delegations from the Arab Socialist Baath Party, the Syrian Socialist Nationalist Party, and the Islamic al-Tawheed Party, besides MP Hussein Yateem, and former MP Mohammad Qabbani who discussed with the secretary-general the activities of the Permanent Conference for Resisting the Zionist Cultural Invasion.


Hizbullah: our patience is running thin(2)

Hizbullah's Deputy Secretary General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem warned that the Islamic Resistance's patience will be exhausted if the Zionist enemy exceeds the limits.

The resistance has adopted new methods to achieve the maximum losses in the ranks of the enemy, Qassem told Radio Monte Carlo.

The enemy's fortifications and military-security plans are not as tight as they may appear, he said.

Qassem pointed out that the Resistance's recent operations, especially the one carried out by Martyrdom Freedom Fighter Ammar Hammoud, prove that the Resistance executes operations to liberate the land, and not political operations.

When the enemy notices that its measures have not prevented the Resistance from carrying out its Jihad operations, it tries to express its anger in a violent manner as a reaction to its helplessness as far as protecting its troops in concerned, so it bombards civilians and tries to hold the Resistance responsible for this, the Deputy Secretary General said.

We are committed to the April Understanding which we consider as a base. The one violating this understanding repeatedly is the "Israeli" enemy. We are patient because the interest necessitates this; however, if the enemy exceeds the limits, we will not be able to be patient, he concluded.


Occupation and collaborators abduct Lebanese citizen(3)

Comers from the occupied zone said the intelligence bodies of the "Israeli" occupation and its proxy militia have detained Khodor Nasser, 42, from his hometown of Yaroun and took him to the Khiam detention center after he had been questioned and tortured at the Center 17 in Bint Jbeil for four days.

Reports said Nasser, a taxi driver, had refused to cooperate with the collaborators and transport them from one outpost to another in his cab.


"Israeli" Aggressions(4)

In its communiqué, the IR reported the following "Israeli" aggressions:

15:10, the perimeters of Majdal Selem exposed to heavy machine gun fire.

15:15, the peripheries of Mansouri village came under ten 155mm missiles and three tank rounds.

18:30, two 81mm flare rounds were fired over Sojod compound.

21:05, an intense volley of heavy machine gun bullets struck Jarjoua town.

22:20, the road of Houla village was targeted by a hail of 12.7mm heavy machine gun rounds.

22:50, one 120mm shell landed the outskirts of Haris village.
