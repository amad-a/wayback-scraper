Lebanon asks UN to prevent “Israel” violating its border…(1)

“ Lebanon called on UN peacekeepers Tuesday to check reports of “Israeli” violations of its territory, and to prevent them if they are taking place.

The Lebanese authorities contacted the command of the UN Interim Forces in Lebanon (UNIFIL), asking them "to act to prevent encroachments by “Israel” which is carrying out work on Lebanese territory, namely dumping sand or flattening embankments," an official said.

"Such acts, if confirmed, amount to provocations and a violation of Lebanese sovereignty," he said.

An AFP correspondent saw two “Israeli” excavators levelling an embankment in the region of Aabbad Tuesday, continuing work they had started Monday.

An “Israeli” patrol, with sophisticated binoculars, also entered a few meters (yards) into Lebanese territory, but withdrew when a UNIFIL patrol arrived, witnesses told AFP.

However, an “Israeli” military spokesman denied that “Israeli” excavators had crossed the border to carry out work on the Lebanese side Monday.

At no time had the line drawn by the United Nations been crossed, he said.

According to local belief, Aabbad houses the tomb of a Muslim holy man, Sheikh al-Aabbad, who died 500 years ago. The “Israelis” also regard the place as a shrine, saying it is the burial place of a fifth century rabbi.

The incident at Aabbad was the second reported violation of the withdrawal drawn up by UN and Lebanese experts after “Israel” abandoned its 22-year occupation of southern Lebanon on May 24.

On Friday, an “Israeli” army excavator went through a metal barrier at Marjeyoun Valley, across the border from the “Israeli” town of Metulla, and dumped several loads of sand stretching six meters (yards) wide and several hundred meters long, according to an AFP correspondent.

The sand allows “Israel” to spot footprints and vehicle tracks on the Lebanese side of the border, experts say.


Border prayer: those who fled to the occupation's entity want to return…(2)

Agence France Presse said twenty Lebanese including women and children who had fled to the entity of the “Israeli” occupation following the withdrawal of the enemy forces from South Lebanon gathered on Tuesday for prayer at the Lebanese-Palestinian border.

"We have come to ask the Virgin, on her day, to ease our pains," one of the Lebanese told the reporters at the Lebanese side.

The young man who was a member of the “Israeli” occupation's proxy militia asked, "What are the crimes that our women and children committed to lead miserable lives as refugees from one country to another?"

We want to return to Lebanon even if they are going to throw us in prison, he said.
