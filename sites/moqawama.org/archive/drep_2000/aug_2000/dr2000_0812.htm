Introduction

Hizbullah’s Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nahim Qassem said that the firing of two “Israeli” shells into Lebanese territory showed “continuing “Israeli” aggression” asserting that the Islamic Resistance (IR) militants were “fully mobilized” to reply when it judged the time right.

However, the “Israeli” occupation army violated, for the second time within less than 24 hours, the Lebanese territories before the very eyes of the UN Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) troops and reporters.

Elsewhere, the “Israeli” army believes that Hizbullah could take up its weapons again at any time, a senior official said on Friday.

Hizbullah says its ready to answer “Israeli” attacks at right moment…(1)

“Referring to the two “Israeli” shells which were fired onto Lebanese territory, Hizbullah said Friday the incident showed “continuing “Israeli” aggression,” adding that its fighters were “fully mobilized” to reply when it judged the time right.
“The Islamic resistance is fully mobilized to fulfill its duty at the opportune place and time,” Sheikh Naim Qassem, Hizbullah’s deputy secretary-general, said.
“We will not be provoked by “Israel’s” actions, but we will define the adequate means of ending its aggression against Lebanon,” he added.
“Israel” fired two shells into Lebanon on Thursday during a military training exercise close to the Lebanese border in the Golan Heights.

Qassem added that “the “Israelis” must take responsibility for any future escalation.”
“They know that the price to pay will be very high. They must do their calculations and judge what is best for them, as we on our side will do what we consider sufficient,” he said.
”Qassem described the stone throwing as “a way for the Lebanese to express their rejection of the occupation, a form of freedom.”
In a statement released on Friday, Qassem said “Israel”, which “has not succeeded in getting guarantees from the Lebanese state despite pressure, particularly from the Americans, is trying to snatch up guarantees when and where it can.”
“We refuse to give “Israel” guarantees under any form whatsoever, whether they are about the whole border or certain gates, whether they have an overall security and political nature or whether they have a limited security nature," he said.

“Israel” carries out work inside Lebanese border…(2)

“ An “Israeli” army excavator dumped several loads of sand across the border into Lebanon Friday, guarded by “Israeli” troops, an AFP correspondent saw.

The sand, spread over a strip six meters (yards) wide and several hundred meters (yards) long, will show up any footprints or vehicle tracks on the Lebanese side, experts say.

The excavator went through the metal barrier put up by the “Israelis” in the Marjeyoun valley, opposite the “Israeli” town of Metulla, and crossed repeatedly back and forth, protected by an “Israeli” patrol.

The incident followed a report by Lebanese police Thursday that two “Israeli” artillery shells fired during military maneuvers exploded in an uninhabited area of southern Lebanon, but caused no casualties or property damage.

The incident occurred during three hours of live firing exercises by the “Israeli” army in the farms of Shebaa, located at the convergence of the Lebanese, “Israeli” and Syrian borders and controlled by “Israel” since 1967.

Beirut refused to give the green light to the deployment of UN forces in the border area until August 4, because of what the Lebanese said were continuing “Israeli” violations of the withdrawal line drawn up in conjunction with UN experts.

“Israeli” army sees Lebanese Hizbullah possibly taking up arms again…(3)

“ The “Israeli” army believes Hizbullah movement, which fought to oust “Israeli” troops from Lebanon, could take up its weapons again at any time, a senior official said on Friday.

"Our assessment is that it is only a matter of time until they pick up the rifle again, for any excuses they can find ... I hope they don't find any on the border," said army spokesman Ron Kitrey.

"There is no question in our minds that they are organised on the military side," Kitrey told foreign journalists.

He said that, after Hizbullah's proclaimed victory in ending the “Israeli” occupation of south Lebanon in May, the movement's future strategy could depend on its performance in upcoming parliamentary elections in Lebanon.

"Will a victory in the polls give them a reason to turn more to the political side in influencing Lebanon or will it blow up, and they become more extreme and more demanding in the situation in the Middle East, in the battle of Jerusalem for example," he said.

Hizbullah leader Sheikh Hassan Nasrallah has said that continued armed resistance against “Israel” remains a "sacred duty" and has repeatedly urged the Palestinians to use force to recover occupied land.

Kitrey also called on the Lebanese army and the UN peacekeepers who recently deployed in south Lebanon to do more to end the cross-border violence that has flared in recent weeks, warning that the situation could easily escalate.

He complained that the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon had moved into the region "too slowly and too separate in their deployment."

"We do expect them not only to be there ... but to take actual action in preventing any kind of potential fuse exploding," he said.

Security on Lebanon-”Israel” border is not up to UN peacekeepers: spokesman…(4)

“ Security at the Lebanese border with “Israel” can only be enforced with the cooperation of the Lebanese authorities, and is not the responsibility of the UN peacekeepers, the spokesman for the UN Interim Force in Lebanon told AFP Friday.

"There is nowhere in the UN resolution that says UNIFIL is responsible as a border force," said Timur Goksel. "We can't be responsible for the border of ... a sovereign nation."

“Israeli” Deputy Defence Minister Epraim Sneh said Tuesday that “Israel” planned to complain to UN Secretary General Kofi Annan that peacekeepers were shirking their duties by failing to prevent Lebanese civilians throwing rocks and Molotov cocktails at “Israeli” troops on the other side of the border at the Fatima Gate, a former crossing point between the two countries.

"They do not implement their mission of restoring security and order to this area. This is the reason they are deployed there, they shouldn't just be flying their flag," Sneh said after a tour of the border area.

Goksel said UNIFIL was "aware of what is happening at the Fatima Gate," and said they discussed the incidents with the Lebanese authorities "almost on a daily basis," but said UNIFIL alone could not handle it.

"What you have there is a law and order problem to handle. We must have the national authority with us," he said.

"First of all it has to be a decision by the Lebanese government. We shall certainly support them and do our best and take our part in it," he added.

On Sunday, “Israeli” soldiers responded with live gunfire to the throwing of a Molotov cocktail from near the Fatima Gate. Four Lebanese, including two journalists, were wounded. An “Israeli” soldier was slightly injured in a stone throwing incident Friday.

Four hundred UNIFIL troops have so far installed 17 military posts in the former “Israeli”-occupied zone. But none are directly on the border and the nearest one to the Fatima Gate is 1,500 metres (yards) away.

A joint force of 500 Lebanese troops and 500 police started deploying in southern Lebanon early Wednesday.

UN Security Council resolution 425 which established UNIFIL in 1978 described its role as "confirming the withdrawal of “Israeli” forces, restoring international peace and security, and assisting the government of Lebanon in ensuring the return of its effective authority in the area."

UNIFIL had not been able to start fulfilling its mandate until “Israel” ended its 22 year occupation of southern Lebanon on May 24.

PM met Gen Hoteit, discussed compensation file…(5)

Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss on Friday hosted the head of Lebanon's border inspection team Brig Gen Ameen Hoteit and his team.

Al-Hoss congratulated the Lebanese team on their serious action to remove the “Israeli” occupation's border violations, praising their persistence and experience which made the mission possible and successful "in recovering the Lebanese right and rejection of “Israeli” maneuvers."

The premier expressed satisfaction with the results achieved by the Lebanese team over two months of persistent work.

Dr al-Hoss also followed up the issue of the compensations that Lebanon is to demand from the “Israeli” occupation's entity for the latter's occupation and aggressions in Lebanon.

The prime minister met for this purpose the chief of Lebanon's Bar Association Michel Elian who put the bar's capabilities at the service of the Lebanese state.

Hajj issues arrest warrants for collaborators…(6)

“Examining Military Magistrate Abdullah Hajj interrogated seven former militiamen in the now-defunct militia on Friday and issued warrants for their arrest.
More than 2,000 “Israeli”-allied militiamen have been interrogated and 950 sentenced to prison terms since the trials began after the “Israeli” withdrawal on May 24.
