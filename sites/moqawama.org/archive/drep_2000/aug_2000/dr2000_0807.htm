Hizbullah slams US and UN pressure for border settlement pressure(1)

The United States and the United Nations exerted huge pressure on Lebanon to certify that the Israeli withdrawal from the South was complete, according to Hizbullahs secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.
Lebanon is subject to pressures from the UN Security Council, the UN secretary-general, and from the United States and the hag who runs its State Department, he said in a ceremony held in Mashghara, Western Bekaa, on Saturday to commemorate Hizbullahs martyrs.
They threatened Lebanon with embargo and to cut off aid, but it was Lebanon in the end that decided when UN peacekeepers would be deployed in the South, he added.
Nasrallah also said Lebanon refused to play the role of border guard for Israel, which he justified as the reason behind Lebanons refusal to send a 1,000-strong joint force of police and soldiers until the peacekeepers assumed their new positions.
In his speech, which was made following the deployment of peacekeepers, Nasrallah also said that continued armed resistance against Israel remains a sacred duty.
As long as one centimeter of Lebanese territory remains occupied, as long as there is a single Lebanese held in Zionist prisons, and as long as Israel threatens our country, armed resistance remains a sacred duty, he said.
Although Lebanon has now accepted that Israel has withdrawn along the Blue Line drawn by the UN, it still has four outstanding border disputes with Israel. One is the Shebaa farms, on the foothills of Mount Hermon, while the three others are differences between the Blue Line and the actual border.
Israel still holds Lebanese nationals, many of them without trial. While Israel has admitted locking up 13 men, Lebanon puts the number at 20.
Nasrallah lashed out at some Lebanese who called for deploying the army in the South before the withdrawal was officially verified, calling them traitors with links to (US Ambassador David) Satterfield.
Responding to the US labeling of Hizbullah as a terrorist organization for threatening to send US diplomats back home in coffins should Washington move its embassy from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem, Nasrallah said
Since the second half of the 20th century, honest defenders of their homelands have been described as terrorists, while those who obey US orders are identified as civilized. We reject this civilization, he added, calling on all Arabs to mobilize against Washingtons plans regarding its embassy in Israel.


Murr: joint force is ready to roll(2)

Interior Minister Michel Murr said on Saturday said the joint force of army and Internal Security Forces would consist of 500 soldiers and 500 policemen who will deploy in liberated areas to maintain internal security based on a Cabinet decree as soon as the UN will officially inform us that it has completed its deployment.
He was speaking after a meeting with senior army and ISF officers to discuss preparations. Murr added that nothing is delaying the deployment of the joint force. He added that all officers would continue to discuss the preparations in a joint meeting to distribute mission and roles in preparation to discover the region and decide where to locate the headquarters and its points.
According to the Cabinet decree, the ISFs Brigadier Nazih Abi Nader has been appointed commander of the joint force with two senior officers drawn from the army and the ISF as his aides. An operations room will be set up in the Interior Ministry and the force will have direct access to Murr.
The mission of the force is to preserve security and order in liberated areas by conducting patrols and setting up mobile checkpoints in these areas, the decree said.
The forces headquarters will be in Marjayoun and a subsidiary branch will be established in Bint Jbeil. Both operation rooms will be equipped with communications and the telephone numbers will be distributed to the public.
The force is to be equipped with vehicles including armoured personnel carriers for patrols, checkpoints and for quick intervention when necessary, the decree said.
The force and UNIFIL will coordinate through the armys liaison bureau for military matters and through UNIFILs liaison officer for other matters.


Teen wounded by Israelis(3)

Israeli soldiers on Sunday wounded a teenager who was hurling rocks at an observation post beside the Fatima gate in Kfar Kila. Ali Hayek was taken to hospital in Nabatieh after he was shot in the foot.
The Israeli Army said Hayek was wounded by a rock and not a bullet during disturbances at the gate where Lebanese citizens threw rocks, metal bars and bottles at Israeli soldiers and farmers working on the far side of the border fence.
Soldiers fired warning shots into the air twice and then, when they shot at the ground, a rock that was struck splintered and hit him, the army said.
But a doctor at Nabatieh hospital said Hayek was wounded by a bullet that ricocheted off the ground and into his foot. His condition is stable and the wound wasnt grave, he said.
Several Lebanese and Palestinian refugees have been wounded at Fatima Gate since the withdrawal.


Resistance is the natural course to confront occupation, Sayyed Nasrallah(4)
The Islamic Resistance's success in expelling the Zionist enemy from Lebanon was not an extraordinary phenomenon, but a divine law and course, Hizbullah Secretary general his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said, calling on the Palestinian people to learn the lesson from the Islamic Resistance in Lebanon.

Speaking to a 100-member delegation from the Damascus University's deans, directors, and professors (headed by the Damascus University President, Dr Hani Mortada, and including its Secretary General Abde-Razzaq Sheikh Issa, President of the Lebanese University Dr Assaad Diab, and Head of Hizbullah's Educational Mobilization Unit (HEMU) Dr Bilal Nheim), Sayyed Nasrallah reviewed the 18-year-old experience of the Islamic Resistance in confronting the Zionist occupiers, explaining the factors of strength and weakness in the Zionist society and how the Resistance used them.

"This (usage) led to weaknesses and shaking in the enemy's society following every defeat inflicted on the occupation army in South Lebanon," Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding, "the logic of resistance is the natural course to confront the occupation.  The Resistance phenomenon needs to be studied, benefited from, and circulated in the Arab world."

The secretary-general unveiled that universities are conducting studies - in the interest of certain sides - on the intellectual, ideological and creed backgrounds of Hizbullah and the Islamic Resistance besides the reasons of their formation, the elements of their strength and weakness, with the aim of confronting, besieging, and barring this phenomenon from spreading out.

The Palestinian people can defeat the Zionists.  Based on my experience by the Resistance men's side, I tell you we can recover al-Quds (Jerusalem). What we are invited to take part in today is the honor of participating in this forthcoming and inevitable victory, he said.

Study the Resistance's phenomenon in Lebanon very well, reconsider reading the enemy and its society.  If the Palestinian people listens to the advice we are offering them publicly due to our 18 years of experience in jihad and martyrdom, I am confident that they can expel the Zionist invaders from Palestine with a price much less than the one paid in Lebanon, he added.

Sayyed Nasrallah commended Syria's role in defending the Resistance in the face of the US and western pressures that aimed to stop and disarm the resistance.

The gathering had been opened with a speech by Dr Mortada who thanked Hizbullah for the invitation.

This visit will be inscribed in our hearts and minds forever, for we will never forget this overwhelming sense of joy as we visited the liberated areas, Mortada said.

Arabs can never recover their rights with anything other than force, he said, congratulating the Lebanese people, Hizbullah, and the Islamic Resistance on this great victory over the Zionist occupier.

At the end of the gathering, Dr Mortada presented Sayyed Nasrallah with a memorial shield in appreciation of the great role he played in achieving the historic victory over the Zionist enemy.


Collaborators in occupied Palestine: Israelis treat us as if we were dogs(5)

The collaborators who had dealt with the Israeli occupation forces are still experiencing the humiliation of being traitors to their country.

In an interview with the Israeli television, one of the collaborators who have fled to occupied Palestine said "Israel has been treating us as if we were less than dogs."

He said the Zionists have not provided them with apartments so they are still staying at cheap hotels and they feel that everything given to them is a handout as if they were beggars.

As he wept, the collaborator told the interviewer that they suffered shortages in food and clothing "and not being able to act freely in order not to annoy the Jews."

He described the lives of the collaborators as humiliating and full of insults.

I have dealt with the Israelis for 25 years, and now I can't stand being alive, he said.
