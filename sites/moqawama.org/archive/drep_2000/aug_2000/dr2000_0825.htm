“Israeli” patrol violates frontier…(1)

A squad of “Israeli” soldiers crossed the border into Lebanon south of Aitaroun Thursday, in the latest violation of the United Nations-delineated Blue Line.
A UNIFIL officer confirmed that a patrol of 20 soldiers crossed into Lebanon for a distance of 30 meters at Boundary Pillar 23, 3 kilometers southeast of Aitaroun.
The officer said that this was the second time “Israeli” troops had entered Lebanon at this point in the past 10 days. The soldiers spent “less than five minutes” on Lebanese soil before retiring back across the border.
The incursion was witnessed by Irish UNIFIL peacekeepers who have an observation post nearby, as well as by Hizbullah fighters also monitoring the border.
Hizbullah also alerted UNIFIL to the violation, security sources said. There was no explanation for the violation. The “Israelis” are building a new outpost at BP23.


Lebanese military court tries 81 more collaborators with “Israel”…(2)

“ Beirut's military court has issued verdicts for 81 collaborators with “Israel” since Monday, and found only four of them innocent, a judicial source said Thursday.

Two Iraqis, an Indian man and his Lebanese wife were found not guilty Thursday, while 17 Lebanese were convicted of membership in “Israel’s” ally. Their sentences ranged from one to four years in prison.

Earlier in the week, 60 other Lebanese were convicted of travelling to “Israel” and sentenced to prison terms of three weeks to one month and various fines of up to a million pounds (730 dollars).

The trials of more than 2,200 Lebanese accused of cooperating with the Jewish state began June 5, less than two weeks after “Israel” ended its 22-year military presence in south Lebanon. So far 1,097 people have been tried.
