Introduction

Hizbullah on Tuesday denounced statements by an American official branding the resistance as terrorist and accused the US of sponsoring terrorism. Moving the US Embassy from Tel Aviv to Al-Quds would be an aggression against all Arabs and an expression of the official terrorist policy that the United states sponsors and fully supports Hizbullah said.

Meanwhile, Interior Minister Michel Murr said an Israeli minefield has held up deployment of the Unifil soldiers and Lebanese troops in southern Lebanon. Reports agreed that a full deployment of UN soldiers should take place by Wednesday.


Hizbullah: US decision is a policy of terrorism against Palestinians and Arabs

Hizbullah commented on the statement issued by the US state department on the standing of Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah on moving the US embassy to occupied al-Quds (Jerusalem).

"As we renew our condemnation of the decision to move the US embassy to al-Quds, we stress the right of our Palestinian people to use all available means to defend its holy sites, and we support its Mujahedeen to confront the imminent historic peril," a Hizbullah communiqué read.

"We call on the Arab and Islamic peoples to take a firm stand in the face of the Zionist arrogance and American insolence in order to defend al-Quds which is the central cause of Arabs and Moslems."

The outrageous decision to move the US embassy to occupied al-Quds is an expression of the official terrorism policy which is sponsored and backed by the US, the Hizbullah communiqué said.

"No matter how much it attempts to wrap its aggression with slogans that our Ummah could not care less about, the US will not be able to distort Hizbullah's glamorous image, Jihad and historic achievements, the latest of which being the expulsion of the Zionist occupation from Lebanon and the resulting Arab embrace and international support to Lebanon's right to resistance.  This has outraged the US administration which came up with a statement against the Lebanese people and its Resistance, against Palestine and all the Arab peoples," the Hizbullah communiqué concluded(1)

Also on Tuesday, Nasrallah received Ramadan Abdullah Shalah, secretary-general of the Islamic Jihad in Palestine Movement, who said that Palestinians would sooner or later discover that the peace process was nothing but a delusion and will resort again to resistance, the only effective means to retrieving what is theirs(2)


Lebanon: army's task after liberation equals its pre-liberation role(3)

Lebanon has reiterated that what the country achieved would not have been possible if it were not for the unity of stand by the Lebanese army, the Resistance, the steadfastness of the Lebanese, and the Syrian support.

"The tasks that the army shoulders after the liberation equal the value of liberation itself," President Gen Emile Lahoud told a delegation from the Lebanese army that paid him a visit on Army Day.

The withdrawal of the Israeli occupation does not mean the achievement of just comprehensive peace which requires giving the Palestinian refugees the right of return besides withdrawal from the Golan Heights until the 4th of June, 1967 line, Lahoud said.

The president recalled the Israeli occupation's previous conditions that no more than 500 or 1000 troops can be sent to the south, "but now "Israel" wants the whole Lebanese army deployed at the borders."

The occupation's demand stems from its eagerness to maintain its security, not out of care for our interests, he said.

Lebanon will not take any steps to protect the occupation or secure its rest, Lahoud stressed, commending the internal stability in Lebanon.

The president recalled that the history of the occupation in Lebanon has been one of creating unrest among the Lebanese besides directly killing some of them.

The form of the recent pullout aimed to have the Lebanese fight one another; however, the awareness of the Lebanese foiled the occupation's plots, Lahoud concluded.


Israeli mines hamper UNIFIL deployment in south Lebanon: press(4)

 An Israeli minefield has held up deployment of the United Nations peacekeeping force and Lebanese troops in southern Lebanon, Interior Minister Michel Murr said Tuesday.

Speaking after a meeting with Prime Minister Salim Hoss, Murr said the minefield, left over after the Israeli occupation of the south which ended May 24, should be cleared within 48 hours.

Murr said the mines were delaying the deployment both of the UN Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) and a joint contingent of 1,000 Lebanese police and troops in the area.

A UNIFIL officer told AFP Ukrainian specialist troops would take some time to clear the 200 square metre minefield round the village of Yarun in the central sector of the border area.

The officer added that UNIFIL had put to the Lebanese authorities a plan for a complete deployment in the border zone and was awaiting Beirut's green light.

The situation was being discussed Tuesday by Lebanese President Emile Lahoud and Prime Minister Hoss, a government source said, adding that approval was expected.

Some 200 men of UNIFIL have so far taken over six positions along the border in the zone occupied by Israeli troops until May 24. Complete deployment would involve moving into some 20 further positions.

The deployment of UNIFIL troops in the zone had been delayed several times because of new Israeli encroachments found during border inspections.

Lebanon says it will not station its police and troops up to the border with Israel, with which it has yet to sign a peace treaty.


Lebanese civilian wounded by landmine left by Israeli occupation(5)

i.a.
A Lebanese civilian was injured Monday after stepping on a landmine left from the Israeli occupation of southern Lebanon, Lebanese police said.

Hassan Ismail, 19, was wounded in the leg. He was taken to hospital for treatment, the police added.

The Israeli occupation troops withdrew from south Lebanon on May 24 after a 22-year occupation.

Since then, six civilians have been killed and 50 injured by exploding mines. The United Nations Interim Forces in Lebanon (UNIFIL) estimates that Israel left behind 130,000 mines in southern Lebanon.


Army commander calls on troops to remain vigilant(6)

On the occasion of Army Day in Lebanon Army Commander General Michel Sleiman praised government troops and the resistance for ousting Israel from southern Lebanon. Sleiman urged the army to remain cautious in the face of Israeli plots, warning Israels pullout from Lebanon will not bring peace as long as the Jewish state fails to withdraw from all occupied Arab territories and allow Palestinian refugees to return home. Sleiman also said the Army should not interfere in upcoming legislative elections. 


Members of former pro-Israeli militia return to Lebanon with their families(7)

 Three former members of the former Israeli-allied militia who fled with retreating Israeli forces on May 24 returned to Lebanon with six members of their families Tuesday, police said here.

The nine returnees, all members of the Druze community, are from the town of Hasbaya, in the former Israeli-occupied border zone. They crossed the frontier at the western border post of Ras Naqoura and surrendered to police.

The three former militiamen were arrested, but the three women and three children with them were allowed to return home, the police said.

Of the 5,500 or so Lebanese who fled to Israel in the wake of the withdrawal, 309 have now returned. Some have complained of the poor reception they were given in Israel, while others say they do not want to remain in exile forever, even if they have to serve prison terms in Lebanon on their return.

More than 2,200 Lebanese are facing charges of collaborating with Israel.

The Beirut military court has already issued 911 sentences ranging between three weeks and 15 years imprisonment. One person has received a life sentence in absentia, while dozens have been acquitted.
