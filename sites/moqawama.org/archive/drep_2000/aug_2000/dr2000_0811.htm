Hizbullah SG received prisoners' relatives: serious action to free them(1)

The families of Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid, Hajj Mustafa Dirani, Samir Qintar, Anwar Yassin, Jawad Qasfi, Fadi al-Jazzar, Hassan al-Anqouni, Mohammed Bdair, Ali Balhas, Mohammed al-Birzawi, Yahia Skaf, Youssef Wazni and Mustafa Hammoud explained to Sayyed Nasrallah their loved ones' suffering in the enemy's detention centers.

The secretary-general underlined that this issue is the "responsibility of all: the state, the resistance, the people, and the NGOs that need to maintain this cause alive."


Lebanon consults with Iran over Quds and region

Iranian diplomat holds resistance as example for others to follow

President Gen Emile Lahoud said Wednesday that Lebanon wants to have permanent consultations with Iran, during a meeting with an Iranian envoy bringing a message from President Mohammed Khatami.

Gen Lahoud and Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Mohammed Sadr discussed "bilateral ties, the developments in the region and the need to step up efforts so that the region's peoples regain their rights," according to the state news service NNA.

The Islamic Republic of Iran, which holds the rotating presidency of the Organization of the Islamic Conference (OIC), has sent envoys to several Arab and Islamic capitals to discuss the status of al-Quds (Jerusalem), the major stumbling block in US-brokered negotiations between the Israeli entity and the Palestinians(2)

Lebanons resistance against Israel should serve as an example for other Muslims, who must follow the same line of action against the Jewish state, said Sadr on Thursday.
The heroic resistance and its great victories can be an example to be followed by other Muslims to act because peace negotiations proved that Israel is winning one concession after another from Palestinians, said the Iranian official after meeting Prime Minister Salim Hoss.
The prime minister and Sadr also talked about recent peace process developments ­ specifically, Jerusalem, and US and Israeli pressures on Palestinians to make more concessions.
Sadr also toured liberated villages, including Khiam, where he visited the abandoned prison that was run by the Israeli-proxy militia. He was greeted by Hizbullahs official in the South, Sheikh Nabil Qaouk.
The deputy foreign minister, who was accompanied by several Iranian diplomats, also visited the Fatima Gate and witnessed rocks being thrown at an Israeli border patrol(3)

Sadr met Tuesday with Syrian President Bashar al-Assad before traveling to Lebanon, where he also met with Parliament Speaker Nabih Berri and Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss.

The Iranian official held also a meeting with Hizbullah Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah and other Hizbullah officials.

Sadr congratulated Hizbullah on the victories achieved in south Lebanon and said the dignity and sovereignty of Lebanon are the outcome of the alertness of its people and resistance against the bullying acts of the Zionist regime.

The two sides discussed the current situation in the region and the attempts to encircle Palestine by the US and "Israel".

Sayyed Nasrallah thanked Iran for its support to the Lebanese resistance and condemned any compromise on the Palestinian issue. He said the only way to liberate the Holy Quds is to follow the lead of the resistance in south Lebanon.

Sadr also met and conferred separately with the president of Lebanon's Shia Supreme Council Mohammed Mehdi Shamseddin and Ayatollah Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah. He discussed with them the Muslim world problems, especially the issue of Palestine and Holy Quds(4).


Two Israeli shells hit Lebanese territory during maneuvers; no victims(5)

 Two Israeli artillery shells fired during military maneuvers Thursday exploded in an uninhabited area of southern Lebanon, but caused no casualties or property damage, police said.

The incident occurred during three hours of live firing exercises by the Israeli army in the farms of Shebaa, located at the convergence of the Lebanese, Israeli and Syrian borders and controlled by Israel since 1967.

It was the first such incident since Israeli troops withdrew from Lebanon in May, with the shells hitting 100 meters (yards) inside Lebanon.

It also occurred only a day after a mixed force of 1,000 Lebanese soldiers and police entered the former occupied zone to take up positions for the first time since the Israeli withdrawal.


Hoss to air border grievances at UN(6)

Prime Minister Salim Hoss plans to represent Lebanon at the opening of the 55th session of the UN General Assembly in New York next month, a Foreign Ministry source said on Thursday.
In his address, Hoss, who doubles as the foreign minister, will highlight Lebanons reservations about the Blue Line, the source said.
The Blue Line was drawn by UN experts as a reference for verifying the Israeli pullout. Lebanon says it violates Lebanese territory in three places, including a large area to the east of the Israeli border town of Misgav Am.
The source said the premier will also call for Israels withdrawal from the Shebaa Farms, an area seized by Israel during its 1967 war with Egypt, Syria and Jordan.
Another item on Hoss UN agenda is the conference of donor countries, due to take place in October.
The conference will focus on financial aid for Lebanon to rebuild and develop newly liberated areas.
Mr. Hoss will also call for the repatriation of Palestinian refugees (currently residing) in Lebanon, the source said, adding that the premier planned to hold several side meetings during his visit, scheduled between Sept. 12 and 16.
Hoss, whose term as prime minister expires on Oct. 20, will be back on time to take part in President Emile Lahouds consultations with MPs to form a new government.

Netherlands says no to Israeli request to host militiamen(8)

The Dutch government Wednesday evening rejected a request made by the Israeli government to receive former members of the Israeli occupation's proxy militia, calling on the Israeli entity to shoulder its responsibility by itself in this issue.

"The UN higher commission stated that there is no need to house former militiamen in another country as they have obtained a one-year license to stay in Israel, and the Israeli authorities have promised to grant them citizenship," the Netherlands foreign ministry said in a statement.

"Israel must shoulder its responsibility pertaining to the Israeli-allied who had worked actually for years in its interest," the statement added.


Court sentences 28 collaborators(9)

A Beirut military court sentenced 28 people on Wednesday, after convicting them of collaborating with Israel.
Two of the men, Fadi Asaad Qassem and Ali Mahmoud Olleiq, will serve hard labor terms of six and seven years respectively.
Both were Israeli-allied informers posing as inmates in Khiam prison. Six others were convicted in absentia and received sentences of 15 years of hard labor, although they are eligible for retrial if they surrender or are arrested. The men are believed to be among the 6,000 Lebanese who fled to Israel before the Jewish state concluded its withdrawal on May 24.
The rest of the defendants received prison sentences ranging between three weeks and two years, with additional fines of up to LL2 million. The court also banned the convicts from returning to their home areas for up to two years after serving their sentences.
Brigadier Maher Safieddine, presiding over the court, dropped charges against two other suspects, ruling that their acts lay outside the statute of limitations.
This latest batch of defendants was the 21st since the court began examining collaboration cases on June 5. The majority of the accused were members of the defunct militia. The total number of convicted collaborators now stands at 975.
