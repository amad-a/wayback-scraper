Hezbollah plans to free all Lebanese prisoners held by “Israel” at any cost(1) {Hostages}

Hezbollah said Saturday it is determined to free the 19 Lebanese still held inside “Israel”, who include key members of the movement, whatever the cost.

"We are committed to have our 19 brothers still held in “Israel” set free, whatever the price we have to pay," Hezbollah leader Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah told followers at a meeting in the town of Khiam on the Lebanese-”Israeli” border.

"“Israel” and the entire world should know that the liberation of our land from the “Israeli” occupier will only be complete after we have secured the return of our brothers, who include Sheikh Obeid, Mustafa Dirani (both leading Hezbollah figures), Samir Qantar and Anwat Dirani (from left-wing parties)," Nasrallah said.

He was addressing thousands of supporters about 100 metres (110 yards) from the former Khiam prison, which was taken down after the May 24 withdrawal of the “Israeli” army from southern Lebanon.

Alongside the seats used by a hundred or so former detainees of the “Israeli” prison at Khiam, 19 empty chairs had been set aside for those still held in “Israeli” prisons.

"Soon these chairs will be filled by our freed brothers," said Sayyed Nasrallah, prompting an enthusiastic response from the crowd.

In mid-July a Tel Aviv court ordered a three-month extension of the administrative detention of Obeid and Mustafa Dirani, who were kidnapped 11 and six years ago, respectively, by the “Israeli” army in Lebanon.

The other detainees have been sentenced to jail terms running to as long as life imprisonment, but a number of them who have already served their sentences have had their detention arbitrarily extended every six months for years.

The “Israeli” government has said it was prepared to exchange the detainees for “Israeli” soldiers who have disappeared in Lebanon, but the “Israeli” supreme court ruled the procedure to be illegal.

 

Hezbollah: Neither Arafat nor anyone else has the right to concede al-Quds or Palestine(2) {Views in Resistance}

Hezbollah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah noted that airing footage containing the “Israeli” nuclear plant in Demona (the Negev Desert) "was not an ordinary incident, but aims to remind Arabs and Palestinians that not all calculations must be made with Lebanon as the model"

Speaking at the rally of Victory and Faithfulness to the Martyrs in the Beqaa town of al-Ayn, his Eminence commented on the statement made by US secretary of state Madeleine Albright who claimed that Yasser Arafat had Arab mandate.

Neither Arafat nor anyone else has the right to concede al-Quds (Jerusalem) or Palestine, or to give up one span of Arab territories to the Zionists, he said.

The secretary-general talked about the press reports stating that a number of Zionist academics are looking for an island near Europe in which they want to establish their new community.  The reports were followed by a poll that included more than 2000 Jews, 36% of whom agreed to the academics' proposal.

This is just the beginning. It means that “Israel” is on the road to the end and elimination, he added.

"Only through Jihad can the Palestinian people prove to the Zionists that they have come to the wrong place and that their coming to Palestine was a political project that has nothing to do with religion which has been used as cover for this project."

What happened in America or Australia cannot take place in Lebanon, Palestine, or the Arab region, for we are no Red Indians, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

The Zionists will leave this land sooner or later, and those who take the path of settlement will find nothing other than the quagmire, whereas the path of resistance has clear results: victory, dignity, pride and honor. This is what we are inviting the peoples of the region to adopt, he added.

 

Raad makes first visit to Ain al-Hilweh by a party official (3) {Views in Resistance}

Nabatieh MP Mohammed Raad, head of Hizbullah’s politburo, made the first-ever visit by a party official to Sidon’s Ain al-Hilweh Palestinian refugee camp on Saturday.
 “The Palestinian issue will remain at the heart of the Arab-”Israeli” struggle, and we should not let it go at all,” Raad said.
Palestinian sources said that while Raad’s visit might appear to be part of an election campaign, it was mainly devoted to affirming Hizbullah’s support for Palestinian factions opposed to peace treaties with “Israel”.
“Hizbullah doesn’t need 1,000 votes in Ain al-Hilweh. It has formed a coalition in the South,” a source said.
Raad was received by Yasser Arafat’s Fatah officials as well as members of Syrian-based groupings opposed to Palestinian peace deals with “Israel”.
The naturalized citizens of Houla gathered at their quarter and for the first time brandished banners of Hizbullah and posters of its secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah. As soon as Raad arrived, he was showered by rice, rose petals and rose water by the naturalized refugees.
On hand to greet Raad was Abed Maqdah, secretary of the Syrian-backed Popular Committees, which oppose peace deals with “Israel”. “As we enter the camp we can’t but recall the blood spilled by the Palestinian resistance fighters,” said Raad.
“Hizbullah will always stand by the Palestinians until they get their rights,” he added.

 

Inquiry finds dereliction of duty in infiltration of Lebanese(4)

The inquiry by the Northern Command into the infiltration of two Druse from Lebanon last week found serious shortcomings in the actions of soldiers and their commander. These included soldiers at an observation post who did not spot the two crossing the border and the defective response of troops at a nearby outpost who should have captured them.

These failures were compounded, the inquiry found, when the two were even given a lift from the border into Kiryat Shmona by an “Israeli” Army tracker, after he and the sentry at the outpost they had approached assumed they were “Israeli” civilians working on construction of the security fence.

The two men from the Druse township of Hatzbiyeh in the eastern sector of south Lebanon crossed the border near Kibbutz Ma'ayan Baruch last Wednesday and spent nearly an hour inside the country before the alarm was raised.

The men were eventually arrested by police near Amiad junction south of Rosh Pina - some 50 kilometers from where they had crossed into “Israel”.

It later transpired that they had worked with “Israel's” defunct ally, and claimed they were seeking asylum after fleeing from Hizbullah.

They had taken a taxi from Kiryat Shmona with the intention of travelling to the Druse village of Usifay on Mount Carmel, south of Haifa.

The infiltration caused a major security alert throughout the North until the two were caught and it became clear they were not on a terrorist mission but wanted to live in “Israel”.

Nevertheless, the incident sent shock waves through the security establishment because of the ease with which they crossed the border, without arousing any suspicion.

OC Northern Command Maj.-Gen. Gaby Ashkenazi, who on Friday concluded the investigation into the incident, decided as a result that a paratroop company commander, with the rank of captain, would not continue in his post.

The inquiry found that the officer, who was in charge of the region where the infiltration occurred, had allowed alertness and readiness of the soldiers under his command to lapse, in contradiction of regulations for guarding the border.

Ashkenazi also decided that disciplinary action be taken against soldiers from an anti-aircraft unit, who were on observation duty and did not spot the infiltrators, as well as against the sentry from the outpost, for dereliction of duty.

The tracker is to face disciplinary proceedings for unsuitable behavior. The findings of the inquiry have been distributed to all commanders in region with instructions to reiterate regulations among officers and soldiers.
