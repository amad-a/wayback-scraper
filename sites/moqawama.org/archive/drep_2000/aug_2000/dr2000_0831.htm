Hizbullah, Former US ambassador question warning on Lebanon travel…(1)

Hizbullah MP Abdullah Qasir said on Wednesday that the US travel warning was a means "to put pressure on Lebanon" so that "it gives security guarantees to the “Israelis” at the border" in light of "Israel's" May withdrawal from south Lebanon.

The US move is tied to “Israeli” policy in the region, the Hizbullah MP added.

Meanwhile, A former US ambassador to Lebanon he did not understand Washington's travel warning for the country.

John Kelly, who spent some 10 years as US ambassador during Lebanon's civil war, said he was surprised at the warning by the State Department, where he once directed Near Eastern affairs.

"I understand that my former employer, the State Department, issued a warning. I suspect that was not wise timing on their part," Kelly said.

"I feel safe in Lebanon," he added.

Kelly is making a private visit to Lebanon, where he will attend an administrative meeting of the American University of Beirut.

The US State Department issued the warning Monday, pointing to "continued instability" in the southern suburbs of Beirut, parts of the Beqaa Valley, Palestinian refugee camps and at the Lebanese-Palestinian border.

"The Department of State warns US citizens of the risks of travel to Lebanon and recommends that Americans exercise caution while traveling there," said the statement, which was not in response to any specific incident.

Lebanese Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss said Tuesday the warning was unjustified and asked Washington to withdraw it.

“Israelis” shoot two youths on border…(2)

Two teenagers were wounded Wednesday when “Israeli” soldiers opened fire on stone-throwers at the Fatima Gate in Kfar Kila.
Mohammed Nazih Baddah, 18, and Ali Fakhreddine, 17, sustained moderate injuries to the ankles when “Israeli” soldiers fired their rifles at the ground where teenagers stood hurling stones across the border. Baddah was rushed to the South Hospital and Fakhreddine to Najdah’s hospital in Nabatieh.
Fakhreddine said that an “Israeli” soldier had provoked the teenagers by insulting them.
The casualties raise to 18 the number of civilians wounded since the “Israelis” withdrew from the South in May.


Series of “Israeli” violations: air and ground violations, Hizbullah is observing…(3)

The “Israeli” occupation's warplanes swooped low over Nabatieh and Iqlim at-Tuffah breaking the sound barrier.

The occupation scored another violation of the Blue Line in the Kfarshouba area. An enemy
bulldozer escorted by a military motorized patrol bulldozed the area 500 meters to the north of Tallet al-Somaqa.

Tallet al-Somaqa is originally a violation of the Blue Line. Lebanon has expressed its reservation concerning this violation.

Hizbullah commented on the latest “Israeli” violations.

The Zionist enemy is trying to exploit the Lebanese preoccupation by the elections to pass its various violations, targeting civilians at the Fatima Gate and attempting to change the border in Kfarshouba and elsewhere, Hizbullah official in the South Sheikh Nabil Qawooq said.

The Islamic Resistance (IR) believes that no internal engagement could distract the eyes of its Mujahedeen one inch from the borders, he stressed.

IR affirms that it is observing very closely the enemy's movement and violations which cannot be allowed to continue, he added.
