Hizbullah Secretary General discusses general situation with President Assad…(1)

Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah on Monday met Syrian President Dr. Bashar al-Assad in Damascus.

During the meeting which was also attended by the secretary-general's Political Assistant Hajj Hussein al-Khalil, the latest developments in Lebanon and the region were reviewed besides discussing the future potentials at the local and regional levels in light of the situation in the south following the liberation.

It was stressed that all possible efforts to fortify the Lebanese internal arena must be exerted along with working to revive Lebanon at all levels in order to complete the great victories achieved by the Lebanese people and its courageous Resistance.

The two parties also examined ways to enable Syria to assist in the revival of Lebanon and improving the development and social situation besides easing the plight of the dispossessed areas in Lebanon, especially in the Baalbek-Hermel area, which had a great role in the process of liberating Lebanon from “Israeli” occupation.


“Israeli” civilians stone Lebanese at Fatima gate…(2)

Low-level violence broke out again at the Fatima Gate border flash point in south Lebanon where “Israelis” from Netanya hurled stones over the fence at Lebanese civilians. Settlers from Netanya said they visited the border fence for the sole purpose of stoning Lebanese. “Israeli” troops dispersed the group, after Lebanese retaliated by throwing small rocks across the border. Fatima Gate has become the scene of almost daily cross-border violence since the Jewish state's withdrawal from Lebanon in May after twenty-two years of occupation. A number of civilians, including Jordanian tourists, have been injured by “Israeli” gunfire at Fatima gate. 

18 more collaborators arrested…(3)

Military Examining Magistrate Rashid Mezher interrogated 18 former members of the “Israeli”-proxy militia on Monday and issued official warrants for their arrest.
Another batch of militiamen trials are due to begin on Tuesday.
Approximately 2,200 people are currently being investigated or tried for collaborating with “Israel”. More than 1,000 have been convicted and sentenced for working with a wartime enemy since the “Israeli” occupation ended in late May.
