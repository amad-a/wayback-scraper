Mine clearance at Yaroun paves way to deploy 5,000 UNIFIL troops(1)

UN deminers cleared 130 mines from round Yaroun on Wednesday, paving the way for a Lebanese team to inspect the border area, UN sources said.
Brigadier General Amin Hoteit led the inspection team after the mines were removed by Ukrainian troops from UNIFIL. Hoteit refused to talk to journalists afterward. Lebanese security sources said a statement would be issued in Beirut, announcing the result of his inspection, with a UNIFIL deployment expected very soon. A total of 115 anti-personnel mines and 15 anti-tank mines left behind by Israeli troops  were removed in a two-day operation.
The presence of the mines has been holding up deployment of UN forces in the area.
In Beirut, Bernard Miyet, the UN Undersecretary General for Peacekeeping Operations, said that UNIFIL peacekeepers were likely to deploy throughout the South very shortly.
Miyet, speaking after a meeting with Prime Minister Salim Hoss, added that Lebanons own troops should also be deploying very speedily, although Hoss had not set an exact time or day.
But he told me that the Lebanese were ready today so long as the conditions attached to the blue line have been checked and it is up to the Lebanese authorities to let us know, he said.
Interior Minister Michel Murr told AFP Tuesday that the full deployment of some 5,000 men from UNIFIL will take place as soon as an Israeli minefield is cleared up close to Yaroun and will probably occur in the next 48 hours.
As soon as Lebanon gives the green light, it should take a few hours for the UN troops to advance and take up position near the Israeli border, a source close to UNIFIL said.


Lebanon: Security forces ready for deployment(2)

Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss underlined the continued cooperation between Lebanon and the UN in order to make it possible for the UN Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) to implement its mandate in line with UN Security Council's resolutions 425 and 426.

As he hosted UN Deputy Secretary-General for peacekeeping affairs Bernard Millet, who was accompanied by the UNIFIL's commander, al-Hoss said the Lebanese security forces are ready to deploy in the liberated areas along with the UNIFIL troops.

An inspection on the ground is taking place today (Wednesday) which will allow a quick redeployment of the UNIFIL troops, Millet said, adding that this deployment is correlated to the agreement with the Lebanese authorities.
