Hizbullah sets up observation posts on Lebanon's border with Israel(1)

 Hizbullah has set up some 20 observation posts on Lebanon's border with Israel, an AFP correspondent observed Thursday, as it tries to step up control over border traffic.

Hizbullah, which spearheaded Lebanon's armed resistance to Israels 22-year occupation, has put up posts along the barbed wire stretching from the coastal town of Naqoura to the outskirts of Kfar Shouba on the western flank of Mount Hermon, near Syria, the correspondent saw.

The group, according to a Lebanese security official, is looking to control entries and departures at Naqoura, the UN-operated border post that is the only remaining crossing between Lebanon and Israel.

Hizbullah wants both to stop any potential Israeli infiltrators from entering Lebanon and block anyone who collaborated with Israeli occupation from leaving, the official said.

"Hizbullah is apparently looking to avoid any possible movement at the border," the official said.

The Hizbullah fighters, organized in small groups of three or four and armed with assault rifles, set themselves up at former points of passage between the two countries or in posts left behind by the Israelis.

The Hizbullah members patrol the area with motorcycles and automobiles and keep an eye on anyone looking to cross at the border posts, which have been closed since the end of Israels withdrawal May 24.

In Naqoura, Hizbullah set itself up in two former seaside resorts held by Israels proxy militia in the area.

Hizbullah launched the measures the day after five Lebanese left the former occupied zone for Israel, the first known to leave since Israels withdrawal, when 6,000 former militia members and their family members headed south.


Lahoud: Detention of Obeid & Dirani is a sign of continued aggression(2)

 Lebanese President Emile Lahoud called Israels decision on Wednesday to renew the detention of resistance leaders Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid and Mustafa Dirani a sign of continued aggression against Lebanon.
The president made the comments in a meeting with former Khiam detainee Ghassan Said and a martyrs family at Baabda.
The deployment of all efforts is required in order to gain their freedom and that of all Lebanese hostages in Israeli prisons, said Lahoud.
An Israeli judge decided on Wednesday to keep the two men in custody for at least another three months because they remained a threat to security.
The two men were abducted by Israeli commandos from Lebanon ­ Obeid in 1989 and Dirani in 1994.


Occupation changes landmarks of one encroachment in Marjeyoun(3)

Agence France Presse said more than ten trucks belonging to the Israeli occupation forces emptied on Thursday their loads of earth dirt and other material on Lebanese territories at one of the points of the Israeli encroachments about 100 meters away from the barbed wires of the Lebanese-Palestinian borders.

A source said an Israeli bulldozer waited for the trucks at the Marjeyoun Plain 100 meters from the barb wired fence and tiled the loads of the trucks which came through a large hole in the fence opposite Metulla on the Palestinian side.

Although the aim or the reason behind what has been going on were not unveiled, the process continued for two consecutive days.

It was reported, however, that the occupation is trying to benefit from the remaining time in order to turn the area into "a buffer zone" and impose the current deployment on the Lebanese side.


UN envoy arrives in Lebanon to study elimination of Israeli border violations(4)

 UN envoy Terje Roed-Larsen arrived here Thursday to continue pursuing the elimination of Israeli violations of the Lebanese border, UN spokesman Nagib Friji said.

Speaking in Amman earlier Thursday, Roed-Larsen said there were still nine Israeli encroachments, which he hoped would be eliminated by the end of the month.

Israel ended its 22-year occupation of south Lebanon on May 24, but disputes over the border have prevented UN peacekeepers from being deployed in the area.

Roed-Larsen, who traveled to Lebanon from Israel, went to the headquarters in Naqoura of the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) to meet with its commander, General Seth Kofi Obeng, Friji said.

He was also to meet with Lebanese authorities in Beirut later Thursday to discuss with them the eventual deployment of UNIFIL troops along the border between Israel and Lebanon.


Occupation's gunboats assault Lebanese fishermen(5)

The gunboats of the Israeli occupation army has staged more aggressions on the fishermen of the Tyre area.

The enemy's vessels Thursday morning fired heavy machine-gun fire rounds on the fishermen's boats although they were sailing near the Lebanese shore of Naqoura.

This Israeli violation follows another one that took place on Wednesday when the Israeli occupation's warplanes broke the sound barrier over the town of Tyre.


Military court sentences 46 Israeli collaborators(6)

A Beirut military court Wednesday sentenced another 46 Lebanese to prison terms ranging from three weeks to 15 years for collaborating with Israel during its occupation of southern Lebanon.

Of the sentences issued, 13 were in absentia, judicial sources said.

Ten of those sentenced were forbidden by the court from entering the former occupied zone for periods ranging between one year and five years after finishing their prison terms.

The 46 were accused of joining the now-disbanded Israeli-allied militia, entering the enemy state of  Israel and collaborating with it.

The latest verdicts bring to 796 the number of Lebanese sentenced since June 5, when the court began holding marathon sessions to wade through the 2,200 people who had betrayed their country and collaborated with the Israeli enemy during its 22-year occupation of southern Lebanon, which ended May 24.


Push for compensation(7)

Foreign diplomats encouraged Lebanon on Thursday to push for compensation from Israel, saying leaders should not have backed down on damages that could rake in $40 billion.
A diplomatic source expressed surprised that Lebanese leaders did not bring up Israeli compensation for war damages in recent talks with the United Nations.
Lebanon conducted a successful diplomatic campaign following the liberation of the South and is about to succeed in its campaign to force Israel to put an end to its border violations, he said.
But this doesnt necessarily mean that it has to freeze the issue of compensation owed by Israel for the destruction caused by its aggressions, he added.
Other foreign diplomats supported the statement on Thursday and suggested that the issue be swiftly raised with the UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan.
Another diplomat estimated the compensation at $40 billion, or twice Lebanons debt and 40 times more than Lebanon hoped to muster at its planned foreign countries donor conference.
He also proposed setting up of a permanent committee that would include international law experts and diplomats to follow up on the matter.
