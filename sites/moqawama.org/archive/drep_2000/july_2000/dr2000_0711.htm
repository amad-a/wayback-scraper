Mine in ex-militia outpost wounds 3 in southern Lebanon(1)

Three Lebanese were seriously wounded in a land-mine explosion at a former Israeli-allied militia outpost in southern Lebanon on Monday, police said.
Abdel-Hussein Fayyad Naji, 75, and his grandsons Abdel-Hussein, 20, and Hamza, 17, set off the mine as they wandered at sundown around a hilltop outpost just outside the village of Haddatha in the hills southeast of Tyre and about 10 kilometers north of Israels border.
The victims were taken by villagers to a hospital in Tyre where the old mans leg was amputated and the two youths received treatment.
Mondays incident brought to 13 the number of people wounded in explosions from land mines or leftover shells since Israeli forces withdrew from south Lebanon in late May. Four Lebanese have been killed.
Lebanese media have warned about the dangers of land mines in the South, particularly along the border which separated Israeli-occupied land from the rest of the country for 18 years.
UN peacekeeping officials have said that once they deploy troops, UN engineers will help the army clear mines.


Iranian president praises the Resistance and stressed it should benefit from the national and international backing(2)

The Iranian News Agency reported that the Iranian president Al-Sayyed Mohammed Khatami praised the Lebanese Resistance upon receiving on Saturday Hizbullah's general secretary Al-Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah.

According to the Presidential Office's Information Department, President Khatami in the meeting referred to the great victory gained by the Lebanese over the Zionist regime of Israel in Southern Lebanon and said this outstanding triumph was achieved due to the Lebanese deep trust in Almighty God and the support given to the Resistance Movement by the Arab and Muslim World.

The survival of the Zionist regime relies on the usurpation and violation, said the President adding this is the first time the Israeli regime has ignominiously retreated from the Occupied Lands without being granted any concessions. This is a big victory for the Arab and Islamic world, he remarked.

The Chief Executive stated that the Hizbullah Resistance Movement is an independent current that makes its decisions based on the national expediencies adding the Resistance Movement should benefit of the national and international backing it enjoys now to reinforce the resistance movement structures.

Sayyed Nasrallah, on his part, offered his gratitude to the "spiritual support" for he Resistance during their struggle against the Zionist system.

He said, "Hizbullah's victory over the Israelis was both a moral and military triumph."


President Lahd: we are responsible for our citizens; danger comes from Israel(3)

Lebanese President Emile Lahoud affirmed that the Lebanese state is responsible for the security of its citizens and shall work to guarantee their security in the liberated zones, as well as, other areas, in the manner the state finds adequate.

Lahoud, who was speaking to a delegation of the American Organization "Task Force for Lebanon", said that the real source of danger for the citizens is "Israel" which is still occupying the Golan Heights; prevents the Palestinian refugees from returning to their real land and by this "Israel" is blocking the realization of a comprehensive and just peace.

The Lebanese president discussed the developments in the region with the president of the organization Najat Fares and the executive-director George Codty, who congratulated the president for the liberation "task force for Lebanon" organization's efforts in helping Lebanon.


Hoss gives donors a date to meet for South fund(4)

Prime Minister Salim Hoss said on Monday that the government would this week begin handing out invitations for donor countries to attend a July 27 meeting to raise funds for rehabilitating the South.
Hoss also said that within the next two days, the government would wrap up a plan for development projects in the South and western Bekaa. The plan, to be written in English, will be submitted to participants at the donor meeting, which will be followed in September or October by a meeting for development projects all over Lebanon.
A number of ministers for development are expected to attend the donor meeting.
Hoss also chaired a meeting of the ministerial committee responsible for overseeing development projects in the liberated zone, to put the final touches on preparations for the July gathering, which was announced on Saturday by Economy Minister Nasser Saidi.
Interior Minister Michel Murr, the head of the five-member committee, will finalize a report in the next 48 hours on the urgent needs of the liberated zone. The overall cost of the development projects is yet to be specified.
The meeting was attended by Saidi, Central Bank Governor Riad Salameh, the Finance Ministrys Director-General Alan Bifani, the head of the Council for Development and Reconstruction Mahmoud Othman, and the head of the World Banks Lebanon office, Hari Prasad.
Hoss held a separate meeting with Saidi, who announced that donor meeting participants will tour the South on July 28.
In three days, the CDR will finish outlining a five-year development plan, which includes projects on the Litani River.


Tribunal tries 40 more collaborators(5)

The trial of 40 people charged with collaborating with Israel opened at a military court on Monday. Most suspects were members of Israels now-defunct proxy militia.

With Brigadier Maher Safieddine presiding, the tribunal questioned suspects, who argued that they worked with the militia or in Israel because they had no other way to secure their livelihoods.
After listening to closing arguments by military prosecutors and defense lawyers, the court adjourned the trials, which were expected to resume around midnight to hand down the verdicts and sentences.
Since it opened on June 5, convening three times a week, the court has handed down 691 verdicts. Some 2,200 people have been accused of collaborating with Israel.
