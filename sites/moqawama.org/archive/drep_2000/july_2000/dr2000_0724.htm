Hizbullah SG offered condolences in Bleeda and Mais el-Jabal(1)

Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah on Saturday visited the houses of Martyrs Ali Zeeb Haidar (in Bleeda) and Qassem Ammar (in Mais el-Jabal) offering condolences on the martyrdom of the two Hizbullah men.

His Eminence denounced the incident that took place in the town of Markaba and took the lives of the two martyrs, but underlined the need to work for unity and disregarding all things that may create unrest.

Those who perpetrated the hideous crime are the ones to be blamed.  Responsibility and its consequences are restricted to them alone, he said.

The leadership of Hizbullah and Amal agree that the utmost punishment must be inflicted on those criminals, he said.

He called on the residents of the area to be aware and careful in the face of those who seek to sow dissension among the brothers in the same ranks and try to create unrest and problems.

UNIFIL decision could be today
Troops deployment hinges on solving last Israeli border violations(2)

The government and UN Middle East envoy Terje Roed-Larsen are to hold a decisive meeting on Monday, which, depending on the outcome, could signal the immediate deployment of UNIFIL peacekeepers into the former occupation zone.
The stumbling block, however, is the remaining Israeli violations along the border. The UN has acknowledged two border infringements: one near Rmaish and the other on Sheikh Abbad hill near Houla.
But Premier Salim Hoss told The Daily Star there were more than two violations of the so-called Blue Line of withdrawal and that UNIFIL would not deploy to the border until they had been cleared up.
Larsen is expected to relay to the government an Israeli pledge to resolve all violations by next Monday, the last day of UNIFILs present mandate.
Hoss said that the government was abiding by UN Resolution 426, which, he said, first calls for a full withdrawal of Israeli forces from Lebanese territory. Deploying UNIFIL into the former occupation zone comes afterward, he said.
In his report on the status of UNIFIL, UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan has recommended extending the peacekeepers mandate for six months on the understanding that the force will be enabled to deploy and function fully throughout its area of operation, and that the Lebanese authorities will strengthen their own presence in the area.
Hoss said there was no conflict between the government and the UN over the deployment of UNIFIL and Lebanese security personnel. The UN is supposed to help us see Israel out of Lebanon and help ensure Israeli violations are rectified, he said.
The government has said that 1,000 troops and Internal Security Forces personnel are on stand-by to move into the border district in conjunction with UNIFIL peacekeepers as soon as the Israeli violations have ended.
The difference in the number of violations voiced by the government and the UN would be discussed with Larsen during Mondays meeting at Baabda palace, Hoss said. The premier dismissed the possibility that UNIFILs mandate may not be renewed if there is continued delay in deploying UNIFIL troops.
I cant imagine that after 22 years sitting here in the middle of attacks and Israeli aggressions that UNIFILs mandate will not be extended, he said.
Annans report noted that stability in the south had greatly improved since Israel withdrew on May 24, but the situation in the Israel-Lebanon sector falls well short of peace, and the potential for serious incidents still exists. Annan said there were few flash points except for the former Fatima gate border crossing in Kfar Kila which has become a focal point of anti-Israel protests and stone throwing.
He said it was fortunate that large-scale violence didn't break out after the withdrawal and when Hizbullah guerrillas moved into the zone.
I note, in particular, that the fighters of the Lebanese resistance conducted themselves in a controlled manner that deserves to be acknowledged; there were no acts of vengeance as they reclaimed the area vacated by the Israeli forces, Annan said.


Lebanon still demands land on which occupation has built 85 houses(3)

As Lebanon regained 40,000 square meters (430,400 square feet) of territory that had been usurped by the Israeli entity at its creation in 1948 and built on it part of the Manara colony, the Lebanese team of border verification recorded remarks on two other violations.

Meanwhile, it was not clear what is the fate of 85 residential units established by the Israeli entity in the Mesgav Am colony on Lebanese territories?

Lebanon had recorded its reservation on these units in June and demanded that they be removed since they constitute a violation of the Lebanese border without being noted by the "Blue Line".

Lebanese and United Nations experts have ended a mission to certify that "Israel" has rectified encroachments into southern Lebanese territory, the head of the Lebanese team told AFP Saturday.

"Field verification works have been completed yesterday (Friday) at 6:00 p.m. (1500 GMT)," Lieutenant-Colonel Amin Hoteit said.

"We are now preparing a report that Lebanese authorities will discuss on Monday with UN special envoy Terje Roed-Larsen," he added.

Roed-Larsen, who has been shuttling around the region for the border certification works, is due to arrive in Beirut on Sunday after meeting with Syrian Foreign Minister Faruq al-Shara in Damascus.

The UN official was scheduled to start "decisive" talks with President Gen Emile Lahoud, Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss and other senior Lebanese officials on Monday.

Hoteit did not wish to discuss details of the field verification operation that started on Monday or disclose the extent of land recovered by Lebanon.

UN spokesman Fred Eckhard said late Friday from New York that there were still two "technical" encroachments by "Israel" into southern Lebanon which the Jewish entity agreed to correct by the end of the month.

Prime Minister al-Hoss told the An-Nahar newspaper on Saturday that he hoped "Israel" would rectify the violations before the meeting with Roed-Larsen on Monday.

The elimination of Israeli border encroachments into Lebanon would allow the Lebanese army and the UN interim forces in south Lebanon (UNIFIL) to deploy in the border zone of southern Lebanon occupied by "Israel" for 22 years until May 24.

Lebanon has ruled out the deployment of UN peacekeepers into southern Lebanon until all Israeli encroachments, some confirmed by the United Nations, are ended. 


Lebanese civilian severely injured by Israeli landmine(4)

A Lebanese peasant was severely injured Saturday after stepping on a landmine left from the Israeli occupation of southern Lebanon, security sources said.

Mahmud al-Hajj, 58, was working in field in the village of Zellaya, in the Western Beqaa region of southeast Lebanon, when the device went off, they said.

He suffered severe injuries and was immediately taken to a nearby hospital, they added.

Zellaya is on the periphery of the former Israeli-occupied border zone in southern Lebanon that remains peppered with thousands of landmines.

The number of accidents involving landmines in southern Lebanon has increased alarmingly since Israeli troops withdrew from the region on May 24 after a 22-year occupation.

Since then, six civilians have been killed and 50 injured by exploding mines.

The United Nations Interim Forces in Lebanon (UNIFIL) estimates that "Israel" left behind 130,000 mines in southern Lebanon.
