Tehran will back Beirut on South…(1)

Hizbullah: Resistance is needed even if one meter of land is occupied

Iran’s ambassador to Lebanon, Mohammad Ali Subhani, said on Thursday that his country supportes any stand the government takes regarding the proposed army deployment in the South.
Subhani, who met with Deputy Premier Issam Fares, said the situation was “very calm” in the liberated areas and that “Israel” should “not be granted the security it demands.”
Elsewhere, Nabatieh MP Mohammed Fneish said “Israel” only wanted the army deployed “to confirm that it had completely pulled out and that resistance was no longer justified.” The Hizbullah official added that in the event of the army’s deployment in the South, the Jewish state would be able to retain the Shibaa Farms, while local efforts to secure the release of detainees held by “Israel” would no longer benefit from any “means of pressure.”
Fneish made the comments while attending an iftar in Nabatieh organized by the Islamic Resistance Support Committee. Afterward, he expressed surprise that some parties were demanding an end to the resistance and the resumption of negotiations with “Israel” to liberate the Shibaa Farms. He said that resistance is “needed and justified even if one square meter of land is occupied.”

 

The Occupation reinforces its military presence along the border

“Israeli” occupation forces brought about heavy artilleries (155 mm and 175 mm) in addition to a make shift surgery room unto the ski center in the Sheikh Mountain on the Lebanese-Syrian-Palestinian border.

The military roads in Bab al-Hawa-Ramta in Lebanese occupied Shibaa Farms witnessed an exceptional movement of “Israeli” patrols. Newspaper sources mentioned that the occupation forces carried on constructing huge cement rooms on the Eastern part of Abbad Shrine under strict “Israeli” soldiers protection.

Moreover, an alarm apparatus was installed in the “Israeli” outpost northern Fatima Gate along the border with Lebanon.

 

Ministers to meet to support intifada…(3)

Foreign Minister Mahmoud Hammoud said Thursday that a follow-up committee of Arab foreign ministers would meet in Damascus during the first half of December to discuss the Palestinian uprising.
The meeting will be the second of its type since October’s Arab summit in Cairo discussed ways of supporting the Palestinian intifada against “Israeli” occupation.
Hammoud told reporters that foreign ministers from Lebanon, Syria, Egypt, Tunisia, Jordan, Saudi Arabia, and the Palestinian Authority would meet in Damascus from Dec. 10-12. Arab League Secretary-General Esmat Abdel-Meguid will also take part. “Discussions will focus on ways to back the al-Aqsa intifada and fund the situation in occupied territories and the Arab countries,” Hammoud said.
The foreign ministers held their first meeting on the sidelines of a summit of the Organization of the Islamic Conference, held in Qatar in November. After Damascus, the ministers are expected to convene later in Tunisia.

 

EU ‘quiet’ on “Israeli” rights abuses…(4)

Official sources expressed surprise Thursday over what they called the European Union’s silence on human-rights violations in the occupied Palestinian territories. Sources said the EU had lately been taking a neutral stand also regarding “Israel’s” economic siege of Palestine.
A government source who spoke on condition of anonymity complained that “there has been no denunciation of the Jewish state’s practices or even an expression of regret.”

 

“Israeli” political arena witnesses a fierce battle…(5)

After the intifadah shortened the political life of Barak, who himself on Wednesday surprised the opposition in the Knesset by calling for early elections, “Israeli” parties began campaigning for the electoral race.

The right-wing opposition Likud party leader Ariel Sharon is trying his best to confront Barak by minimizing the period until which early elections are to be held.

Sharon also wants to push the Knesset to pass laws reducing any “Israeli” cabinet's authority concerning  “Israeli” withdrawal from occupied Palestinian land.

The Likud leader's efforts came as it was thought that Barak was trying to reach an agreement with the Palestinian Authority and use the agreement as a credit in his favor in the coming elections.

Meanwhile, polls carried out by “Israeli” daily Yediot Ahronot  two days after the Knesset voted for early elections showed that former “Israeli” Prime Minister Beyamin Natanyahu has a lead of 14 point over PM Ehud Barak.

As to the Labor Party, differences arose between PM Ehud Barak and Shimon Perez, who announced that he shall not participate in the party's central committee meeting on Thursday because it is too early to hold it.

The 1800 party delegates were planned to hold a meeting Thursday in Tel Aviv to decide on the date of the Labor Party's initial elections in which the labor candidate for the premiership is to be named.

 

More than 287 Palestinian Martyrs in two- month-old Intifadah…(6)

Two Palestinians were martyred on Thursday. The first was shot dead by “Israeli” soldiers during clashes in the West Bank town of Beit Lahm. Three other Palestinians were wounded. The other was killed by an “Israeli” settler who ran over the Palestinian with his car close to Hussan village in the West Bank.

Moreover, thousands joined in a funeral march on Thursday for two Palestinian youths who passed away Wednesday from earlier wounds.

Most of the deaths these during the past two months of Intifadah (uprising) against “Israeli” occupation have been young men or teenagers.

 Gaza’s Shifa hospital official said Mohammed al-Mashharawi, 14, died in hospital from gunshot wounds in the head sustained on Saturday. Ashraf al-Basous, 20, another Palestinian from Gaza, died at a hospital in Amman in Jordan from gunshot wounds in the chest, the hospital and family members said.

The hospital said both were shot by “Israeli” soldiers during clashes with stone throwing Palestinian demonstrators at the Karni crossing point.Much of the cofrontations has taken place in the largely Palestinian ruled Gaza Strip, where thousands of Jews live in settlements, illegal under international law, scattered among about a million Palestinians.

The death toll of Palestinians in two months of intifadah rose to about 287 martyrs.

The “Israeli” Army said there had been a heavy gun battle near the Neve Dekalim Jewish settlement in the southern Gaza Strip and Palestinian gunmen had fired at military posts on the border with Egypt and at a convoy of settlers’ cars.

It said its troops had also engaged in fire fights with Palestinian gunmen in the divided West Bank town of Khalil (Hebron) and near Palestinian-ruled Ramallah.

Following a meeting in NewYork between Palestinian Authority Media Minister Yasser Abd Rabbo and U.S. Senator George Mitchel, an official Palestinian source said that the international fact-finding commission, headed by Mitchel, will travel to the Palestinian territories on December 11.

The fact-finding commission was formed upon the demand of the Palestinian Authority during Sharm al-Sheikh summit in Cairo.

The five-members commission's duty is to find the party responsible for the clashes in the Palestinian territories and propose solutions to halt confrontations.

Five Palestinian families asked in a letter of theirs to the head of the fact-finding commission that the commission investigate into the barbaric and international  “Israeli” army operations against Palestinians.

In addition, countries supporting Palestinians proposed to the U.N. security council to issue a resolution calling for the deployment of UN observers in the Palestinian Authority territories.  But the United Sates is sure to veto such a resolution if “Israel” refuses it.
