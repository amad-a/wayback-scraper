Hizbullah plans to drive hard bargain for “Israeli” captives

Nasrallah: Prisoner exchange to cover all Arab detainees in “Israel”…(1)

Hizbullah leader Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah warned “Israel” Wednesday that the party wanted Arab prisoners in addition to Lebanese for the German-mediated swap operation.
“Hizbullah’s demands in the swap operation include all Arab detainees in “Israeli” prisons, the bodies of those who died, information about those missing since 1982, and also missing Palestinians in the occupied territories. They also include maps of “Israeli” mines in the South,” Nasrallah said at an iftar in Expo Beirut.
Nasrallah’s warnings are an indication that Hizbullah intends to make the most out of the swap deal. They came after a report issued in Al-Mustaqbal newspaper Wednesday that the first stage of the exchange would probably take place “soon.”
Although the paper’s report said there would be two stages in the swap operation, it did not mention the demand for releasing non-Lebanese prisoners.
The demand for the release of Arab detainees and information on the missing were among the conditions Nasrallah announced following Hizbullah’s capture of the “Israeli” soldiers on Oct. 7.
However, the demand for maps of “Israeli” mines in the South is a new demand.“We’re now within the framework of negotiations with the German mediation,” he said, “and we’ll see what the responses are and how issues will develop.”
Seeking to tone down the information published in Al-Mustaqbal, Nasrallah called reports that the swap operation would take place before or after Eid al-Fitr “baseless.”
“I mean, if the Germans came to us tonight and they said that the “Israelis” agreed to our demands, the operation would take place tomorrow, inshallah. “It could also not happen,” he added.
Nasrallah attributed his insistence on Hizbullah’s demands to “humanitarian and ethical considerations.”
“Such considerations require us not to be in a hurry because it will be at the detainees’ expense,” he said, “and this is not at all acceptable.”
He also reiterated that any information on the “Israeli” soldiers would not be revealed without “a price.”
The newspaper’s report said that a German envoy in Beirut had reached a “draft proposal” for a two-stage operation to swap the four “Israelis” Hizbullah captured for 19 Lebanese detainees in “Israel”. According to the report, the first stage would involve Hizbullah releasing the three “Israeli” soldiers in return for 14 Lebanese prisoners.
It said the freed Lebanese must include Samir Qantar, the longest-serving detainee, Sheikh Abdel-Karim Obeid, and Mustafa Dirani. “Israeli” captured Hizbullah’s Obeid in 1989, and Dirani, a former Amal Movement security chief, in 1994, to hold as bargaining chips for Ron Arad.
Al-Mustaqbal said the first exchange was expected to take place soon. The daily said the second phase of the exchange, in which “Israel” would swap the remaining five Lebanese for an “Israeli” reserve colonel in military intelligence, might take longer.
The report claimed that all the “Israeli” captives were alive but that the health of some was not very good.
Monday, a security source said the negotiations had made significant progress and that a deal could be reached this month.
“Israeli” Chief of Staff Shaul Mofaz confirmed on Tuesday that Germany was mediating the negotiations but played down speculation that a swap would happen soon. However, “Israeli” Deputy Defense (War) Minister Ephraim Sneha said “Israel” was willing to trade Obeid and Dirani for captive “Israelis”.

Government rips “Israel”…(2)

The Cabinet accused “Israel” on Wednesday of obstructing peace and threatening Lebanese and Syrian security, and complained about attempts to “modify” the Blue Line in the South.
Information Minister Ghazi Aridi said the government sought strong cooperation with the UN but said certain “markings” on the Blue Line were being subject to tampering.
On Sunday, UNIFIL spokesman Timur Goksel denied that the UN had or would modify the line.

Lebanese women associations support intifadah…(3)

Under the tittle of "Women's assembly in support of the Palestinian woman and child", Lebanese civil and humanitarian societies, parties,  and associations held a massive sit-in in front of the United Nations Middle East  ESQWA headquarters in Beirut.

Posters condemning the “Israeli” barbaric aggressions against Palestinian children, youth and women were raised.

Head of Hizbullah's women societies issued a statement in the name of the assembly, in which she urged U.N. Secretary-General Kofi Annan to take the required steps to put an end to the “Israeli” ongoing barbaric massacres and aggressions against the Palestinians.

Five Palestinian martyred by “Israeli” Occupation…(4)

“Israeli” occupation forces escalated its aggressions against Palestinians and shot dead four Palestinian policemen and wounded tens when “Israeli” tanks Wednesday tried to storm into the Palestinian refugee camp of Khan Younis in Gaza Strip.

One of the fiercest clashes in 11 weeks of the intifadah took place near the camp. As many as 1,000 Palestinian gunmen joined Palestinian police in the night-long gun battle against the “Israeli” troops, which shelled the refugee camp. The Palestinians were able to push away the enemy forces.

Thousands of Palestinians later attended funerals for the four martyrs. Mourners, flying Palestinian and Hizbullah flags, shouted:" Millions of martyrs are marching to al-Quds."

Later on Wednesday morning, “Israeli” soldiers assassinated an activist of Hamas group in the West Bank city of al-Khalil (Hebron).

Hamas said the martyr, Abbas al-Awawi, 26, was hit by four bullets to the chest while he stood in front of his home. He died on the spot.

The attack on Awawi, is part of the continuing plan carried out by the “Israeli” army to eliminate activists of Hamas, the Islamic Jihad, and Fatah Movement.

Awawi had been jailed several times by both the Palestinians and “Israelis” for his alleged role in armed attacks against the “Israeli” occupation.

Moreover, witnesses reported the “Israeli” army to have demolished Wednesday at dawn four Palestinian houses in the city of Deir al-Balah near the “Israeli” settlement of Kfar Darom, in the Gaza Strip.

"“Israeli” bulldozers under the heavy protection of “Israeli” military forces destroyed at dawn four Palestinian houses without previous alert," said the witnesses.

They added," A two-story building owned by Youssef Bashir family from Deir al-Balah was completely destroyed, and three other houses belonging to the family Al-Tawwash was partially demolished."

American churches delegation criticizes “Israeli” aggressions…(5)

A delegation of church leaders, representing the various American churches criticized "“Israel’s” excessive use of force against the Palestinians", and called for peace in the holy land.

The delegation which is on a visit to occupied Palestine asked the U.S. government to stop the sales of attack helicopters to “Israel” and to restrain “Israel” by carrying out an investigation on its use of force against civilian targets.

The American church leaders in a statement of theirs described what they have seen such as the damages caused by U.S.A made bombs on Palestinian houses and ambulances, in addition to the “Israeli” settlements which suffocates and segregates the Palestinian people from each other.

Cardinal Vincent Forner, said that the U.S. legislators should see by themselves the situation. The delegation demanded from “Israel” to withdraw from the territories it occupied in 1967.
