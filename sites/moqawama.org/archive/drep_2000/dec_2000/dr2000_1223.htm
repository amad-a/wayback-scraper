Sayyed Nasrallah tells Palestinians on Quds International Day to count on intifadah…(1)

Quds (Al-Quds) International Day was marked in Lebanon Friday by hundred thousands of Lebanese and Palestinian refugees in various marches organized by Hizbullah in Beirut, Baalabak and Tyre.

Along Martyr Hadi Nasrallah's Road, Hizbullah Secretary-General's son, thousands of Hizbullah members and supporters marched through southern Beirut.

The Hizbullah members, wearing black combat gear and headbands emblazoned with Hizbullah's motto, stamped over American and “Israeli” flags while chanting: "Quds we are on our way" and "Zionists, Hizbullah will return".

The Lebanese President was represented by Social Affairs Minister Asaad Diyyab and representatives of Palestinian and Lebanese parties and forces were present on the occasion.

Hizbullah Secrtary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah urged Palestinians to go on with their intifadah, saying "you have a historic obligation to continue this intifadah."

Nasrallah told Palestinians not to bank on “Israeli” Prime Minister Ehud Barak.

"Barak is a butcher, he has killed and wounded more Palestinians than his challenger in the premiership election Ariel Sharon did," he said.

Nasrallah also told the Palestinians not to count on the new U.S. President George Bush. "The new U.S. president will be what "“Israel”" wants him to be. Negotiations will not bring back what is rightfully yours. Only your efforts will."

"The negotiations going on in Washington calls for concern, for what happened to the first intifadah of the Palestinian people should not be repeated," maintained Nasrallah.

He, moreover, called on the Palestinians especially Fatah cadres to pressure their leaders to maintain the intifadah (uprising) and not squander what was realized until now.

Hizbullah Secretary-General also urged Palestinians not to make a comparison count of their dead with that of the “Israelis”, saying that the Lebanese model proved that the option of resistance to confront "“Israel”" and that of the intifadah is the right way to reach the target.

In addition, Nasrallah listed the consequences of the intifadah until now.

Among the outcomes is that the blood of children and youth was able to rouse the Arab and Islamic Nation as it was never before. It imposed on a number of Arab leaders to cut ties with "“Israel”"; through martyrdom it stopped the normalization process with the “Israeli” enemy; "caused the downfall of (the “Israeli”) government"; and deepened the conflict within the “Israeli” society and institutions, said Nasrallah.

Hizbullah Secretary-General added that the Palestinian uprising "confirmed that the truth which the resistance in Lebanon proved i.e. that "“Israel’s”" army is not a legend and it can be defeated ... and that it (intifadah) was able to impose on the “Israeli” government to meditate on giving in concessions and reconsider its settlement policy .. and it united, as never before, the Palestinians."

Speaking to the thousands - strong crowd was also Ramadan Shallah, Secretary-General of the Islamic Jihad Movement in Palestine.

He asserted the Palestinians holding to the option of intifadah and resistance until all the aspirations of the Palestinian people become a reality.

 

Nasrallah cheers on intifada
Hizbullah leader marks Al-Quds Day with call to uproot “Israeli” ‘cancer’…(2)

Hizbullah’s secretary-general urged Palestinians on Friday to continue the intifada, describing it as “the only and right choice to realize their objectives.”
During during Friday’s Al-Quds Day celebrations, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said that the option of resistance has worked in Lebanon and would eventually work for the Palestinians.
Nasrallah was addressing tens of thousands of party members and supporters gathered along Beirut’s Hadi Nasrallah boulevard to watch a military parade organized by Hizbullah. The crowd stood on balconies and rooftops covered by huge posters depicting Hizbullah ’s fighters and the late Iranian leader, Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini.
Some 4,000 fighters participated in the nearly two-hour parade, which displayed 38 brigades named after the victims of massacres committed by “Israel”, such as the Deir Yassin massacre in Palestine.
Nasrallah warned that “Israeli” would try all means of halting the uprising, with peace talks in Washington now under way.
Nasrallah said Palestinians today had a tremendous opportunity for success because the whole world was watching and supporting them, urging them not to allow their uprising to end up like the 1987-1993 intifada.
Calling “Israel” a “cancer that needs to be removed at its roots,” Nasrallah praised this intifada for uniting the Arabs, forcing their leaders to meet, and coercing states to sever their ties with “Israel”.
“The uprising, like the resistance in Lebanon, has proved that the “Israeli” Army is not legendary and not invincible,” Nasrallah said. He said the “Israelis” were defeated in Lebanon because they were not only faced with an armed resistance, but with a strong people and a strong political leadership in Lebanon and Syria, both endorsed by Iran.
 Regarding the settlement of Palestinian refugees in Arab countries, Nasrallah said that no “honorable Palestinian would accept money in return for his right to go back to his homeland.” He also said the Arabs should not place their hopes on change brought about by new “Israeli” or American administrations. “They’re all alike, and we have to put our destiny in our own hands.”
After a display of posters
of the party’s leading figures and flags, including the Iranian flag, there was a march by
two brigades of well-trained children brandishing toy guns and ammunition.
In the final section of the parade, fighters from a special unit performed military exercises, including a risky performance by 7-year-old Jamil Sayyed, who rapelled from a 10-story building.
The fighters also renewed their oath of honor to Khomeini and to their party.
Speaking during the celebration, Palestinian Islamic Jihad representative Ramadan Abdullah Shalah praised Hizbullah’s support for the Palestinians and their intifada. Shalah said the Palestinians would also refuse settlement and would not abide by any settlement reached by the Palestinian Authority with “Israel”.

 

Hizbullah youths hurl stones at “Israeli” border point on Al-Quds Day…(3)

“ Dozens of Lebanese Hizbullah youths hurled Friday stones on an “Israeli” border point to mark the annual Al-Quds Day in support of the Palestinian struggle against the Jewish state.

About 150 members of the al-Mahdi scouts, a youth organization of Hizbullah, gathered at Fatima Gate at Lebanon's border with “Israel”, an AFP correspondent witnessed.

The scouts, accompanied by a music band, waved Hizbullah, Lebanese and Palestinian flags while shouting anti-”Israeli” slogans, he said.

The youths threw stones onto the “Israeli” side of the border as groups of them torched US and “Israeli” flags, he said.

Hizbullah has planned several rallies across the coutry to mark Al-Quds Day, first conceived after the 1979 Islamic revolution by the late founder of Islamic Iran, Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini. It is observed each year on the last Friday of the holy Muslim month of Ramadan.

 

New warning about mines…(4)

The army’s Directorate of Orientation warned the public once again on Friday about the danger of mines planted by “Israeli” forces prior to their withdrawal from the South.
The new warning took the form of a series of instructions on how to avoid the dangerous devices. The warning was re-issued because many people are expected to use the holidays to visit their homes and family in liberated areas. Such areas are considered particularly risky.
Germany has agreed to donate nearly $60,000 worth of de-mining equipment for use in the South. An embassy press release said the move completes “a German contribution of $100,000 to the UN Mine Action Services.”

 

Iranian crowds flock to annual anti-“Israel” protest…(5)

Iran marked its annual Al-Qods Day protest against “Israel” on Friday, with demonstrators inflamed by “Israel’s” killing of hundreds of Palestinians. Tens of thousands, including parents with small children and clerics, flocked through the streets of central Tehran, en route to the city's main Friday prayers venue. "Death to “Israel”, Death to the USA," chanted the marchers. Others called for direct resort to armed struggle with the Jewish state, which Iran does not recognize. "Hizbullah fights and “Israel” trembles," roared the crowd, referring to the resistance group. Many carried portraits of Iran's supreme leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. Children clutched balloons emblazoned "Death to “Israel”", while organized groups marched with Palestinian headscarves covering their faces.     Foreign Minister Kamal Kharrazi and veteran revolutionary cleric Ali Akbar Mohtashemi were among the dignitaries in the crowd. President Mohammad Khatami arrived in a presidential car to attend the prayer sermons that capped a morning of protests. State media said similar marches were held in cities across Iran. "“Israel” is a strong military base for the global arrogant powers in this region," former president Akbar Hashemi Rafsanjani said in his prayer sermon in Tehran. "It has been created to keep the region's Arab countries under control." Rafsanjani, echoing Iran's long-standing opposition to the U.S.-led peace process, said “Israel” had no intention of ever compromising with the Palestinians.  He cited what he said were "official" maps of a planned Greater “Israel”, encompassing huge swathes of Arab land -- from Saudi Arabia to Lebanon, from the Tigris to the Nile rivers. But he also had tough words for Iran's fellow Muslim countries, saying the world's Islamic community had done too little for the Palestinian cause. Rafsanjani, now head of an influential state body advising Khamenei, said Muslim nations had pledged $1 billion to aid the Palestinians in their latest uprising against “Israel” but had so far paid out only $31 million.  "Isn't this shameful? Is this all the money that you have to offer? What about all the money you gave Iraq in the years it was fighting us?" he asked, in a reference to Arab support for Baghdad during its eight-year war with Iran. "How much money are you spending on the Americans you have brought into the region, who are charging you by the hour? "It is not a big expectation to say oil wells should be closed for a few days, or that they should reduce their relations with their masters for a while," he added.  Before the prayer sermons, held on the campus of Tehran University, demonstrators in funeral shrouds proclaimed their readiness to die for the Palestinian cause. Special collection points were set up along the seven designated march routes to solicit public donations in support of the 12-week Palestinian uprising, or intifada. Qods Day, inaugurated by the late revolutionary leader Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini, is held each year on the last Friday of the Muslim holy fasting month of Ramadan.

 

Quds Day in Occupied Palestine: 3 Palestinian martyrs, 1 “Israeli” killed in operation…(6)

On Quds Day, a Palestinian intifadah fighter carried out a martyrdom operation, killing a zionist settler and wounding 11 others. He blew himself up in a cafe near the settlement of Mehola in the West Bank.

Three Palestinians were also martyred by “Israeli” occupation soldiers on Friday.

The Palestinian youth, Arafat Meshael, was shot in the head during clashes near the divided West Bank city of al-Khalil (Hebron), witnesses said.

The “Israeli” army also shot dead 52 - year old Salama al Sawarka and a third Palestinian while working at his farm near al-Khalil.

Stone-throwing clashes erupted in al-Quds where “Israeli” police only allowed 45,000 worshipper to perform Friday prayers.

The Islamic Jihad and Hamas Movement marked Quds International Day by organizing massive celebrations in Gaza and Beit Lahm. The Palestinian crowds shouted "Allahu Akbar" upon hearing Hizbullah Secretry-General's speech which was directly broadcasted from Beirut.
