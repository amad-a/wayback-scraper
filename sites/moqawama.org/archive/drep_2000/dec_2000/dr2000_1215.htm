Palestinians pin hopes on Hizbullah prisoner swap…(1)

“Palestinians with family members in “Israeli” jails said on Thursday they are hoping for their release in any prisoner swap between “Israel” and Hizbullah resistance fighters. "That would be the only way I could see my son free before I die," said 70-year-old Nadia, the mother of Abdel-Hadi Ghuneim, who was convicted of an anti-”Israeli” attack in 1989. He has so far served 11 years of his 2,064-year sentence. “Israel” on Tuesday confirmed reports it was in contact with Hizbullah to swap three soldiers captured in October and a fourth, a reserve colonel, for the return of some Arabs it has been detaining in its jails. Hizbullah Secretary General Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah said in Beirut on Wednesday the resistance group insisted that “Israel” free all Arab captives. "We consider ourselves concerned with every detainee jailed by this enemy, whether he is Lebanese, Syrian, Palestinian or Jordanian," Nasrallah said. A Palestinian official said Palestinians had provided Nasrallah with a list of detainees in “Israeli” jails, upon his request, after Hizbullah captured the soldiers. "I urge Sheikh Nasrallah to fulfil his promise," said Nadia, speaking in her reception room where a photograph of her jailed son was hung on a wall. Hisham Abdel-Razek, Palestinian minister for prisoners' affairs, said “Israel” is holding 2,200 Palestinians as "hostages" in violation of peace accords. Six hundred were detained during the 11-week uprising against “Israeli” occupation, he said. “Israel” has freed at least 10,000 Palestinians from prisons since the 1993 Oslo peace accord. Abdel-Razak questioned the possibility of further “Israeli” releases, referring to its closures of Palestinian-ruled areas. "Three million people in Gaza and the West Bank have been locked into a big prison by “Israel”, therefore, I doubt they can do it." Palestinians have been waving the yellow flags of the Lebanese group in the protests in Gaza and the West Bank. "Hizbullah knows how to bring those arrogant people to their knees. They only give up when they feel the pain," said Khaled, a 47-year-old store owner and relative of a Palestinian serving a life sentence in an “Israeli” jail.

Lebanon court sentences 44 for collaboration with “Israel”…(2)

Beirut's military court handed down prison sentences overnight to 44 Lebanese for entering the "enemy state" of “Israel” and for collaboration with that country, judicial sources said Thursday.

They were sentenced to jail terms ranging between three weeks and three years.

They were charged with entering enemy territory without permission when they lived in once “Israeli”-occupied southern Lebanon, enrolment in the now-disbanded “Israeli”-allied South Lebanon Army and collaboration with the SLA militia.

One person was found not guilty for the same charges and another had charges dropped because he had collaborated with Lebanese security services during the “Israeli” occupation that ended in May, they said.

In total, 1,860 sentences have been handed down since the June 5 start of the trial of some 2,200 Lebanese accused of collaborating with “Israel” before its May 24 withdrawal from Lebanon after 22 years of occupation.

Among them, 11 people have been sentenced to death in absentia and three others also still at large have received life sentences.

Charges have included collaboration with “Israel”, entering “Israel” or enrolment in the SLA militia.

Hizbullah, which spearheaded the anti-”Israeli” resistance, has frequently criticised the lightness of the sentences.

Hamas activist assassinated by “Israeli” occupation…(3)

“Israeli” occupation forces went on in its assassinations of intifadah cadres. On Thursday “Israeli” troops shot dead a member of Izzeddine al-Kassam brigade, Hamas military arm.

Ismail Haniyah, director of the office of Hamas founder Sheikh Ahmad Yassin, said troops at a check-point riddled Martyr Hani Abu Bakra with bullets as he sat at the wheel of a vehicle doing his job as a taxi driver.

The toll of Palestinian intifadah activists eliminated by “Israeli” commando units mounted to four martyrs in only four days.

Earlier, a Zionist settler was wounded when Palestinian gunmen opened fire at his vehicle near the village of Beit Awwa west of al-Khalil (Hebron) town in the West Bank.

Intifadah fighters also opened fire on an “Israeli” army vehicle on the road between al-Quds (Jerusalem) and Gosh Letsion Settlement.

Occupation troops later opened fire on the nearby Palestinian town al-Khodr.
