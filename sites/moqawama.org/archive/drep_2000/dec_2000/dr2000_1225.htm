 Thurs2ay 29-2000

 

Lahoud praises Nasrallah for Resistance sacrifices…(1)

President of Lebanon Emile Lahoud received a phone call from Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah who wished Lahoud a merry Christmas and New Year .

Nasrallah expressed the Resistance's esteem for the support it was given during its struggle against the “Israeli” occupation.

The Lebanese president responded by thanking Sayyed Nasrallah for his season greetings and praised what the Resistance's did for the liberation of south Lebanon from the “Israeli” occupation.

Lahoud especially lauded Hizbullah's Secretary-General for his sacrifices saying that Nasrallah gave through his person and the Resistance's approach a model to be imitated in confronting the occupation. Lahoud also wished Nasrallah a happy Eid al-Fitr.

“Israeli” occupation buried toxic waste in Lebanese territory…(2)

“Israeli” occupation forces buried Thursday and Friday barrels containing toxic waste in the farm of Debbeen , in the Lebanese occupied Shibaa farms.

Security reports said “Israeli” bulldozers dug several big holes and emptied 20 loads of barrels containing toxic waste.

Earlier, “Israeli” army was also caught burying toxic substances in the occupied Syrian Golan Heights.

On the otherhand , the Lebanese expert team responsible for the drawing of the borderline with occupied Palestine , inspected the area where the “Israeli” forces violated the blue-line drawn by the U.N south of al-khiyam valley.

At that location, the “Israeli” occupation army installed an iron fence on Lebanese soil.

The Lebanese expert team prepared a report on the violation to be later discussed with the UNIFIL , United Nations Interim Forces deployed in south Lebanon.

"Israel" reopens Lebanese collaborators Radio…(3)

 A radio station under the name " the Mediterranean Sea Coast" is broadcasting from “Israel” on sound wave 756 and its transmission is intermittently received in south Lebanon , a Lebanese security source said.

The voices of the station's announcers are the same as those who used to be heard previously from the collaborator Entoine Lahd's station, the source asserted.

Lahd was the head of the so-called former Southern Lebanese Army, a militia which served “Israeli” forces during its occupation of southern Lebanon.

Palestinians offer more intifadah martyrs…(4)

While negotiations between “Israeli” and Palestinian negotiators entered its last day in Washington, two Palestinians died of earlier wounds and hundreds of Palestinians buried their martyrs.

On Saturday, civil emergency worker Nabil Abu Awn, 28, died from a bullet wound to the head fired by Isareli troops near the Jewish settlement of Netzarin in the Gaza strip on Wednesday, Palestinian hospital officials said.

Another Palestinian youth, Abdallah Kennine also passed away from earlier wounds.

Moreover, the “Israeli” Radio said “Israeli” army outposts were attacked by Palestinian fighters. “Israeli” settlements were also subject to gun fire.

An “Israeli” soldier was wounded in occupied al-Quds (Jerusalem) when Palestinian youths hurled at him a firework.
