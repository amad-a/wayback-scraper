Belin: "Nasrallah swapping conditions harsh and preliminary"(1)

Yussi Belin, “Israeli” Minister of Justice, described Hezbollah's conditions for swapping “Israeli” soldiers with Lebanese and Arab detainees as "unrealistic".

"Secretary General of Hezbollah Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah makes the negotiations very difficult because we do not really know the conditions of our soldiers," said Belin following a meeting with families of the “Israeli” soldiers captured by Hezbollah from occupied Lebanese Shebaa Farms in October.

Nevertheless, Belin indicated that Sayyed Nasrallah's conditions were "initial" in the negotiations which lately started.

He said, "It is clear that  Nasrallah demands in his initial stand for everything. But for us to reach an agreement negotiations should go on."

Hezbollah's Secretary-General Wednesday made public the party's conditions for the exchange operation to take place.

"Hezbollah's demands in the swap operation include all Arab detainees in “Israeli” prisons, the bodies of those who died, information about those missing since 1982, and also missing Palestinians in the occupied territories. They also include maps of “Israeli” mine in South Lebanon, "maintained Sayyed Nasrallah.

 Lebanese Army experts tour on the Blue Line region(2)

A team of experts from the Lebanese Army, charged of monitoring the international frontiers, toured on the Blue Line area, at the flash-point of Fatima. The team conducted a survey in a point located between the two occupation posts of Rwaise and Rwaiset el Sammaka.

The team’s sources asserted that the dispute is not over two or even four hundred meters but over all the territories under the “Israeli” occupation, in Kfarshouba heights and Shebaa farms.

 Russia may help with mine-clearing in South (3)

Russian Ambassador Boris Polotin said Friday that his country was considering “suitable ways” of participating in the removal of thousands of land mines planted by “Israel” during its 22-year occupation of the South and the Western Bekaa.
“There are different kinds of participation and we’re considering the proper ways to do so,” he told reporters after meeting with Foreign Minister Mahmoud Hammoud for a review of bilateral relations.
Polotin said his country was following up the demining issue raised during a visit to Beirut on Wednesday by Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Alexander Sultanov.
Some reports said Russia was willing to help Lebanon remove the mines either directly or in conjunction with a third country.

Protesters in North support intifada (4)

About 10,000 Christians and Muslims from all over the North staged a demonstration in Tripoli on Friday to support the Palestinian uprising in the occupied territories.
Tripoli MPs Ahmad Hbous and Jean Obeid, Dinnieh MP Jihad Samad, Minyeh MP Ahmad Fatfat, Mufti of Tripoli Sheikh Taha Sabounji, the Orthodox Archbishop of Tripoli and Koura Elias Qorban, and several of the area’s officials were among the participants.
Protesters began the march at the Mansouri mosque after Friday prayers and headed toward the Rashid Karami Fairgrounds.
“Palestine is ours and Jerusalem is ours,” said Qorban, “we salute the Resurrection Church and the Al-Aqsa Mosque.”
He said he was surprised the United Nations did not intervene to stop the bloodshed in Palestine and urged Arab and Islamic countries to support the intifada with all “financial and moral” means.
Sabounji praised the Palestinian people’s resistance, seeing it as the only way to regain their rights. He called for “national unity” and avoiding internal discord. He also said there should be a “promotion” of relations with Syria to reinforce support for the intifada, stressing Syria’s sacrifices for the country and Arab causes.
The demonstrators chanted: “No to the instigators of internal strife, yes to the liberation of the Shebaa Farms” and “Cooperation between Presidents Lahoud and Bashar Assad reinforces national security.”
They also carried banners reading “Palestine only gave birth to two children: Baby Jesus and the intifada’s child” and “Arabs! Unite … victory is on the way.”
The protesters held up pictures of the Al-Aqsa Mosque compound and raised Islamic flags and portraits of Syrian President Bashar Assad.

Hizbullah prepares for Al-Quds Day (5)

More than 3,000 Hizbullah fighters trained in Sidon in preparation for the Al-Quds Day parade next Friday.
Iran’s Ayatollah Ali Khamenei set the date, the final Friday of Ramadan, for Muslims throughout the world to join protests against the occupation of Palestine.
The training included marching and taking an oath, under the supervision of Hizbullah’s commander in the South, Nabil Qaouk, and recruitment officials.
“Death to “Israel”, Al-Quds we are coming,” the trainer shouted through his microphone, as well as, “Ayatollah Khomeini said “Israel” should be destroyed.”
Students of all ages returning from school watched the training parade. Some marched alongside, brandishing plastic guns.

 Six Palestinians Martyrs on 3rd Friday of Holy Ramadan(6)

Tension was high on the third Friday of holy Ramadan. “Israeli” troops shot dead three Palestinians, who are Fatah members, near the West Bank village of Burin. Fatah, Arafat's PLO faction, said the three martyrs, Saad al-Kharouf, Mohammed Ramadan, and Abd al-Munem, were assassinated by “Israeli” soldiers, vowing to revenge their death.

17-year-old Mohammed Dawoud was also martyred by an “Israeli” life bullet which pierced his chest while in stone-throwing clashes against the occupation in the village of Hares near Nablus.

“Israeli” forces opened fire at a Palestinian police checkpoint near the West Bank town of Ramallah, killing a Palestinian policeman, the Palestinian National Security Forces said.

A sixth Palestinian was killed at the Erez crossing point between the Zionist Entity and Gaza strip.

Following Friday prayers at al-Aqsa mosque in al-Quds, where forty thousand Palestinians participated, clashes erupted between stone-throwing Palestinians and “Israeli” police who fired rubber-coated metal bullets and stun grenades.

Several ambulances rushed wounded from a narrow alley next to the mosque complex. Two “Israeli” policemen were also wounded, reported “Israeli” sources.

The Zionist Occupation authorities like every Friday heavily encircled al-Quds and prevented Palestinians, holding “Israeli” nationalities, below the age of 35 from entering the holy mosque while allowing only two thousand West Bank and Gaza Strip residents from participating in the Friday prayers at the mosque on condition of exceeding 45 years of age. However, only 200 of them were able to join in the Friday prayers.

Moreover , “Israeli” occupation troops during their deployment in the holy city intentionally provocated the Palestinians.
