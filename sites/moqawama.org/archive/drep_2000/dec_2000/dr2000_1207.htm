Lahoud warns Lebanon has taken "precautions" against “Israeli” aggression…(1)

Lebanese President Emile Lahoud has said Lebanon is prepared for eventual “Israeli” attacks upon its soil, and reiterated his refusal to deploy Lebanese troops along the “Israeli” border.

"We follow nearly all of the “Israeli” government's statements and take precautions to face “Israel’s” eventual attacks, of which it is capable," Lahoud was quoted as saying in press reports Wednesday.

On Friday, “Israel” threatened to target Syrian forces in Lebanon if Hizbullah continued its raids upon “Israeli” troops in the Shibaa farms.

Lahoud, speaking to visiting heads of Arab news agencies, dismissed the notion that Lebanon's military had any responsibility to protect “Israel’s” borders, especially from Hizbullah guerillas.

"It is out of the question, at the moment, to deploy our army along the border, in direct contact with the enemy, while we remain in a state of war with “Israel”, who has repeatedly attacked us by land, sea and air," Lahoud said.

On November 9, despite UN requests, Lahoud announced his refusal to deploy troops along the border as "definitive" and not up for discussion.

 

Nothing official on Shibaa’ from UN…(2)

Foreign Minister Mahmoud Hammoud said on Wednesday that a new proposal to deploy UN peacekeepers, based in either Syria or south Lebanon, to the Shibaa Farms has not been made to Lebanon by the United Nations.
“Such a proposal must be made to Lebanon by the UN and this has not taken place yet. When it is received, we shall study it carefully and consider how it came about and within what framework,” he said.
“Then we will look into its dimensions and aims, and in light of all this, a decision will be taken,” Hammoud added.
The minister was commenting on recent media reports that “Israel” is pressing for a deployment of the peacekeepers and the matter is being considered by the UN.
A senior UN source said Tuesday that the proposal was part of a “multi-channel approach” to pressure Lebanon and Syria to halt Hizbullah attacks and deploy Lebanese troops on the border with “Israel”.
According to the source, “Israel” suggested deploying UNIFIL troops or soldiers belonging to the UN Disengagement Observer Force (UNDOF), which has monitored the cease-fire on the “Israeli”-occupied Golan Heights since 1974.
The US has “taken an interest” in the idea, the source said.

The occupation schemes to divide Gaza strip…(3)

Military operations against the “Israeli” occupation and Jewish settlers in the West Bank and Gaza strip continued. Several shooting incidents were reported in different locations including Gilo Settlement and the village of Laban in Ramallah and Ariha.

Nightly gun battles raged on in the West Bank and Gaza late on Tuesday capping a day in which “Israeli” soldiers shot dead two Palestinians who took part in rock-throwing protests.

The deaths raised to 297 the number of Palestinians killed in the two-month-old uprising against “Israeli” occupation.

According to Palestinian sources more than 10,000 people have been injured since September 29.

“Israeli” army used its tanks and helicopter gunships to hit residential Palestinian areas in Beit Jala, injuring a number of civilians.

Moreover, “Israeli” settlers in al-Khalil (Hebron) caused damage to communication networks which provide services for Palestinian hospitals and public services.

“Israeli” soldiers and Palestinian gunmen have exchanged fire in Gaza, where an “Israeli” military check point has severely curtailed the movement of Palestinians between north and the south of the Palestinian territory.

This “Israeli” army act comes in the scope of military schemes to divide the Gaza strip into three parts in the aim of weakening the intifadah.

"“Israel”" says the measures are needed for security but Palestinians believe them to be a form of collective punishment.

"We absolutely condemn this division between the two sides of the Palestinian territory (of Gaza), “ Ahmed Jadallah, a resident southern Gaza waiting in a long line of cars at an “Israeli” checkpoint said.

Palestinians say the “Israeli” curbs on Palestinian movement in the territories make life unbearable for hundreds of thousands with no justification and constitute discrimination and grave human rights violation.  According to Palestinian sources the curbs were also placed on medical teams and ambulances.
