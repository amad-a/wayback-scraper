"Israeli" Reserve Officers Support Pull-Out
 
 Nicholas Blanford
Daily Star staff - 24/2/1998.

A group of 1,000 senior reservist officers in the "Israeli" army yesterday called for a unilateral withdrawal from the south, a move that is likely to increase the heated debate.

"If we don't do something positive this situation could last for years," said reserve Brig Gen Shaul Givoli, director of the non-partisan Council for Peace and Security.

"It's acceptable to pay the price if there is a light at the end of the tunnel, but if the situation is not changing it's just not worthwhile," he added.

A statement released by the council said that the "Israeli" army is strong enough to defend northern "Israel" from within the country's own borders. It added that a withdrawal would force the Lebanese government to maintain order in the south.

An increasing number of senior "Israeli" officers have begun openly questioning the value of maintaining the occupation.

Last November, after three months of taking heavy casualties, several anonymous officers quoted in the "Israeli" press began supporting a withdrawal, the first time the "Israeli" army had voiced such sentiments.

"Israel's" northern commander, Maj Gen Amiram Levine, also suggested a withdrawal, but only after a massive bombing campaign against Lebanon's infrastructure.
