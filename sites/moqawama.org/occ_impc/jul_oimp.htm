Occupation Impacts
 
   July Thursday 9-1998:

Repercussions of revealing the two "Israeli" terrorist network (1)
The success of the Lebanese Security forces in disclosing the two "Israeli"-connected spy and sabotage rings raised a pleasure among the political and popular circles. However, "Israel" tried to disregard the issued in order to limit its repercussions inside the "Israeli" society.

Nevertheless, the criticisms made by the former Head of General Security Service (GSS) and now MK Gidon Ezra, formed as an "Israeli" clear acknowledgement with its involvement. "It is unbelievable that such a big "Israeli" spy network to be revealed for this shows a failure in the planning and execution as well", he vowed.

Moreover, Ezra had suspected in the loyalty of Lahd's militiamen pointing out that one of them had blown up an "Israeli" building near Metulla settlement few months ago. He demanded the "Israeli" intelligence systems to be very aware of their activities and movements, adding, "the espionage actions against Lebanon shouldn't be stopped. Certainly, "Israel" is supposed to plant another spy rings".

It is not worthy that the spy ring is composed of 77 suspects, 17 of whom were held prisoners. As to the sabotage network, it includes eighteen of the now dissolved Lebanese Forces Militia, 11 of whom had been apprehended.

Today, the Military Investigation Magistrate Riad Talih will begin questioning the suspects in the presence of their lawyers. It is expected that it will take two days for Talih to make his decisions of either releasing those innocent and or continuing the detention of those suspected.

As-Safir news reported that Farah M., one of the suspects and who is on the run now, was in permanent connection with "Israel" and its surrogates. Also, the arrested Nader Nasri Daou, in cooperation with the fugitives Elie A. and Aiman, was trading in arms and narcotics sent from the "Israeli"-occupied enclave by members of Guards of Cedars Party headed by Itian Sakr (domiciled at Sabah village, Jezzine District, and judged in absentia with hard labor).

Also, it was appeared that the network was preparing the body trap cars at Daou's smothery shop in Kasslik. The litigation included the lawyer Elias Kh. who knew the ring's activities and nevertheless, he didn't inform the security competent authorities. It is worthmentioning that the military court had sentenced previously Elias Kh. one year imprisonment with a charge of "collaborating with the "Israeli" intelligence and facilitating land properties sales for the 'Israelis'".

Lahoud revealed that the network is involved in bombing the Syrian bus in central Damascus that killed 11 people and wounded forty two.

The prosecution came according to the Articles of Punishment Law no 335 (composing associations with the aim of committing serious crimes), 201/549 (premeditated murder attempt), 278 (contacting the spies) and 398 (not informing the authorities), the two articles of Law dated 11-1-1958 under no 5 and 6 (sentenced to death), the Article 128 of the new Law of Narcotics and the Article 72 of Law of Arms.

The arrested are: Bassam Antoine Harb, in provisional retirement Captain Kamil Mohib Yard, George Tanios El-Helow, Wadih Adib El-Khoury, Fares Hanna Rahmeh, Elie Akl Rahmeh (corporal policeman), Toni Toufic Rahmeh, George Sami El-Barakshi, Kayed Kahttar Eid, Nabil Jerjis Khattar and Nader Nasri Daou.

Also, Lahoud sued seven others who are on the run now, among them Abdo Antoine Sawaya, Naamatallah Mosallem and Khalil Fakhr El-Naddaf now domiciled in Australia.

"The abovementioned defendants are members of the disbanded Lebanese Forces Militia. They established an association for the purpose of carrying out terrorist actions and trying to assassinate leading politicians. Also, they were in their way to prepare, in coordination with the Australia-based LF's Office another sabotage operations", the indictment said.

"The terrorist activities they implemented are: bombing the Social Syrian Nationalis Party's centers of Jdeidet El-Matn and Bekfaya, blowing up a bus inside Syria, firing at a Syrian van killing its driver, detonating Charl El-Helow Taxi Stand, setting off a roadside bomb near squads of Syrian workers at Intelias, Sad El-Boushrieh and Nabaah areas. The latest terrorist operation failed as the bomb exploded while its holders Naameh Ziadeh and George Dib were transferring it at Daoura area", the indictment reported.

It should be noted that investigation over Daoura's blast on 19-6-1998 led the network to be disclosed.

Hizbullah's Secretary General H.E. Hassan Nassrallah (2) maintained "the 'Israeli' agent is neither a Moslem nor a Christian and even nor a Lebanese citizen, but an element of treason and should be punished whatever his sect is". He demanded the government to be tough with the network members.

As-Safir's Chief-in-editor (3)
Talal Salman, As-Safir's Chief-in-editor, considered that the judicial authorities have the final word over the sabotage ring. The significant issue, in this regard, is "the public psychological climate" by which the Lebanese received the news over the success of the security systems in revealing the ring and in obtaining detailed confessions that may clarify the secrets of some blasts which look like mass murder (bombing at Barmaki-Damascus). Also, these secrets may highlighted the bombings that aimed at raising sectarian conflicts or striking the Lebanese-Syrian relations".

All Lebanese, of different political currents, regarded the matter as a security accomplishment. None of the parties or sectarian authorities viewed it as political or confessional nature-like revenge, he added.

Salman mentioned: Its deepest real meaning is that the Lebanese body had confirmed its impunity in the face of Israels sabotage operations. The allegation that Israel has a party or parties or friendly confession in Lebanon is no longer exist.

Israel had become again out of Lebanon where should be always there in its capacity as the enemy, he said.

Salman concluded: All the Lebanese have the right to sue the collaborators of the only enemy.

   July Monday 20 -1998:

Expulsion (1)
The "Israeli" occupation troops expelled this morning a man held in Khiam detention camp for six months and who was subjected to torture, security sources said. They said Khozai Jamil Barakat, 45, was driven to the government held territory through Zimraya crossing point in the Eastern Sector of South Lebanon.

Accused by helping Hizbullah fighters, Barakat was questioned by "Israel's" military intelligence apparatus. Barakat is now in hospital after suffering serious injuries as a result of torture at the hands of "Israeli" soldiers and their surrogates. He was held prisoner from his house in Hibbarieh last February. Barakat is an employee at the water company and has eleven children.

It should be noted here, that since "Israel" established the Khiam concentration camp, fifty Lebanese detainees have died as a result of torture. Moreover, "Israel" still holds about 130 Lebanese in Khiam and nearly 50 others in its prisons inside "Israel" breaching thus the international law and human rights standards.

Detention (2)
Civilians coming from the "Israeli" occupied enclave reported that "Israeli" troops had arrested two civilians - Imad Ahmad Soweid from the village of Debbine and Ahmad Youssef Al-Raghda from Khiam - and imprisoned them at Khiam detention center.

Closing a Crossing (3)
The "Israelis" hept the Kfar Tibbnit crossing closed for the second straight day. Security sources said the crossing was closed because (the "Israeli"-allied) "South Lebanon Army" militiamen refused to serve at the position, as it is regularly targeted by the resistance.

   July Wednesday 22-1998:

Expulsion (1)
The "Israeli" troops deported the citizen Abdel-Karim Noura, 46, taxi driver, from his village Debbine to outside of the occupied enclave via Kfar Tibbnit crossing point. The causes of detention remained unknown, while his family member are still in the village.

Noura's relatives mentioned that he had been under house arrest in Debbine after he had been interrogated four months ago. So, he preferred to be out of the occupied enclave.

However, the security sources reported that the national Imad Ahmad Soweid was released after he had been taken to Khiam detention camp ten days before.

   July Friday 24-1998:

Three civilians martyred and one wounded in Arnoun Blast (1)
A delegation from the International Committee of the Red Cross was awaiting permission from the Israelis last night to enter the village of Arnoun to retrieve the remains of at least one man who was killed in an unclaimed bomb blast late on Friday.

Hussein Mehdi Attieh, 19, was blown to pieces in the explosion which reportedly occurred beside a house owned by Ali Tawbeh in the occupied half of the village. The house was empty at the time.
"Israeli" troops investigated the scene of the blast on Saturday morning and took a wallet belonging to Attieh.

Initial reports said that, other than Attieh, Ali Olleik and his cousin Wissam Olleik, were also killed. But the Olleik's later arrived unhurt in Nabatieh.

An Internal Security Forces patrol was shot at by "Israeli" troops as it neared the site of the bombing on Saturday morning.

The circumstances of the blast were not immediately clear. Hizbullah said it had no information over the incident.

Tellingly, an "Israeli" pilotless reconnaissance plane was flying overhead at the time of the explosion. The drones can detonate roadside bombs by remote control, suggesting that the explosive device could have been planted by the "Israelis".

The ICRC requested permission from the Israelis to gather Attieh's  remains, which lie scattered over a field beside the Tawbeh house. The "Israelis" have so far denied permission. Around ten "Israeli" soldiers entered Arnoun yesterday accompanied by two tanks to conduct house-to-house searches and interrogate residents.

Eight residents have been detained by the "Israelis", including a 12-year-old boy, according to security sources.

Arnoun, lying 5km southeast of Nabatieh, lies in an uneasy no-man's land beneath the "Israeli" position in Beaufort castle. The population, most of whom have fled, is regularly harassed by Israeli patrols from the castle.
