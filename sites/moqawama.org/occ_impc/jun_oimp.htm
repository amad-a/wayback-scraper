Occupation Impacts
 
June Thursday 4-1998:

Mothers of the Israeli Soldiers (1):
On the sixth anniversary of invading Lebanon by the Israeli troops, the mothers of the Israeli soldiers serving in South Lebanon marched in a demonstration before the Defense Ministry building in Tel Aviv and acclaimed with the retreat from Lebanon.

The demonstrators, belonged to the Group of Peace Bells, dressed T-shirts upon which there were Hebrew writings.

Stop the war was one these writings. The demonstrators, also, bore models of peace dove.

The mothers had found that the war in Lebanon is fruitless, for it mounts the Israeli human losses.

3- Al-Anwar, 5-6-1998, p:1.
