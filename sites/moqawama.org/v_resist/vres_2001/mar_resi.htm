March Friday 02-2001:

Sayyed Nasrallah Warns “Israel” from Attacking Lebanon…(1)

Hizbullah Secretary General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said the “Israeli” enemy will pay a high price for any new aggression on Lebanon.

The Executive Bureau of the Syrian National Student Union invited Sayyed Nasrallah to give a speech in Damascus on the 38th anniversary of the March 8 revolution. A large number of Syrian political personalities were present.

Hizbullah Secretary-General said, " “Israel” gambled that after its withdrawal from south Lebanon trouble will start between the state and the resistance. But there was always a side which put down their bets and contradicts their calculations, because the (Lebanese) state is other than the state they (“Israelis”) know and the resistance is different from the resistance they perceive. Syria played a major role in consolidating this new reality."

"In contrast to what some are writing, there is no conflict between the logic of the state and that of the resistance," added Nasrallah.

Sayyed Nasrallah also said the “Israelis” bet for years that late Syrian President Hafez Assad will yield to the pressures put on him but he proved to be firm and so is the situation after the death of Asad.

" (Outgoing “Israeli” Premier Ehud) Barak said: We shall leave Lebanon, and we tell Lebanon and its resistance not to try us. Some got afraid and others increased their belief in the weakness of this enemy. So we tested the “Israelis” in the Shibaa Farms and we captured three soldiers, and we tried them by capturing the “Israeli” intelligence colonel. Later on, we again tried them in a second and third operation in the Shibaa Farms and they still say do not try us," said Nasrallah.

He added, " Attacking Lebanon these days differs from the past, for the resistance, people and state (of Lebanon) varies. The resistance has different capabilities now and any aggression on Lebanon will be heavily paid by the “Israelis”. They are aware of that."

Sayyed Nasrallah said that since the “Israeli” withdrawal from south Lebanon there is a balance of terror which prevents the “Israelis” even of thinking about attacking Lebanon.

" I say without exaggeration and based on a detailed reading of the “Israeli” status-quo and that of the region, and in consideration of the structure of the “Israeli” society and that of its security and military institutions, I tell the great soul of late President Hafez Assad that if the crusaders remained in our region for about 200 years then those Zionists - you may start recording from now- may leave, by God's will, from our land in 20 years to the utmost," maintained Hizbullah Secretary-General.

Nasrallah Vows to Send 1 Million Jews Fleeing under Missile Fire if Sharon Attacks…(2)

Hizbullah leader Hassan Nasrallah has vowed to send one million Jewish settlers fleeing northern “Israel” under a rain of missile fire if Ariel Sharon launches a large-scale military onslaught against Lebanon, a Beirut newspaper reported Thursday.
The report in As-Safir coincided with a new threat served by “Israel’s” chief of staff Lt. Gen. Shaul Mofaz to "strike back hard" for any further Hizbullah attack on his troops in the farms of Shibaa.
"One million Jewish settlers will have to flee the area if the orders go out to the resistance to open fire," Nasrallah was quoted as telling Premier Hariri during a meeting they held in the aftermath of a Hizbullah rocket ambush that killed on “Israeli” soldier and wounded two.
"“Israel” is aware of this, and that's what's holding the Jewish State from unleashing a major offensive," said Nasrallah.
Mofaz made his threat at a meeting Wednesday in Jerusalem with representatives of the Jewish Agency, the semi-official organization responsible for the emigration of Jews to “Israel” from around the world.
"We simply cannot restrain ourselves from responding to Hizbullah attacks forever. Patience has a limit," said Mofaz. "If there is a new operation we will hit back hard."

March Saturday 03-2001:

Hizbullah’s ‘center of operations’ ? as alleged by “Israel” ? currently inhabited by Bedouin workers…(1)

The news that the former “Israeli”-allied camp in Majidieh is regarded by “Israel” as Hizbullah’s “center of operations” has come as a surprise to the 20 Bedouins who having been living there for the past month.
“There are no Hizbullah here. There’s no one here but us,” said a puzzled Omar Harati, surveying the deserted former SLA barracks.
But, according to the “Israeli” Army, the sprawling complex is home to “dozens of Hizbullah terrorists” who do “various jobs at the base,” as reported this week by “Israel’s” Yediot Ahronot daily.
It said that the Hizbullah fighters deploy different types of anti-tank weapons in the vicinity of Majidieh as well as conduct intelligence gathering operations.
“Israel’s” Radio has also described Majidieh as Hizbullah’s “headquarters.”
Yediot noted that the missile attack against the “Israeli” Army convoy two weeks ago, in which a soldier was killed and two others wounded, was launched from Majidieh.
It is true that the road where the “Israeli” convoy was attacked is clearly visible from the tree-lined lane that leads to Majidieh. The vehicle, a ‘humvee,’ was hit just before reaching an “Israeli” Army outpost at Tallet Ghazale, also visible from Majidieh.
The Hizbullah squad probably launched the missile, believed to have been a wire-guided Sagger, from near its old observation post at the junction of the lane and a main road which terminates at an Indian UNIFIL position on the UN-delineated Blue Line, 300 meters to the south.
The distance between the former post and the “Israeli” convoy is a little over 2 kilometers, just within range of a Sagger. The location beside a main road would have enabled the Hizbullah team to escape by vehicle immediately after the attack, evading “Israeli” retaliation.
Two gaping holes in the road, a few hundred meters north of the entrance to Majidieh, testify to the “Israeli” attempts to hit the fleeing Hizbullah attackers.
Since Hizbullah resumed operations against the “Israeli” Army last October, none of its fighters would be foolish enough to live in an isolated cluster of buildings located only 1 kilometer from the Blue Line and the “Israeli” Army.
Hizbullah fighters spent the summer months in Majidieh after it was vacated by the SLA during the May withdrawal.
But Hizbullah left Majidieh along with numerous other border posts following the capture of three “Israeli” soldiers from the Shibaa Farms on Oct. 7.
Instead, a group of Bedouin from Qabb Elias in the Bekaa valley  have settled in the former barracks to work for the Arslan family, which owns the Majidieh estate.
Majidieh is named after Majid Arslan, one of the figures of Lebanon’s independence and a long-serving politician. His country estate at the foot of the Shibaa Farms hills was taken over in 1977 by the militia of Saad Haddad, the founder of what was to become the South Lebanon Army.
Harati said that the Bedouin grew vegetables ? “not drugs,” he insisted ? in the fertile soil around Majidieh.
Asked if he was concerned for his safety living in such an exposed location beside the Shibaa Farms, Harati said there was no reason for fear.
“There are no Hizbullah here, there are no soldiers here, just us workers. Why should anyone cause us trouble?” he asked.
Harati said that Indian UNIFIL soldiers patrolled Majidieh by vehicle six times a day.
“We are protected by the UN,” he said. “The UN have told the “Israelis” that we are here.”
The “Israeli” Army is in a good position to know exactly who is living in Majidieh. Other than the regular overflights by unmanned reconnaissance planes and warplanes, Majidieh can be observed by three “Israeli” military outposts, Abbassieh, Tallet Ghazale and Ramta.

March Wednasday 21-2001:

Hezbullah Secretary-General Says “Israelis” Considering Withdrawal From Shibaa Farms

Hezbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said the “Israelis” began seriously discussing withdrawing from the occupied Lebanese Shibaa Farms, asserting that their presence in this area is costing them more and more, including the loss of lives.

Speaking during a ceremony organized by the Department of Women's Activities in the Islamic Resistance Support Foundation in honor of women who supported the Resistance morally and financially, Sayyed Nasrallah responded to the “Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon's threats saying that since the liberation of the South on 25 May, northern occupied Palestine is within the resistance's grip.

He said if the “Israelis” plan to attack Lebanon, then they would go through lots of considerations, fearing  the response of Hezbullah.

"Those believing that the issue with the Zionist Entity has ended with the withdrawal of the occupation forces from a large part of our land is wrong and wrongly perceives the local and regional circumstances," added Nasrallah…(1)

Hizbullah’s secretary-general  said that even if “Israel” pulls out from the Shibaa Farms, the resistance will keep its weapons to prevent future attacks.
“The resistance ­ its weapons, Katyusha rockets and fighters ­ will remain on the border until further notice because this serves Lebanese interests and security. “I want to tell you now and later that the country needs resistance.”
He also denied claims that his group’s continued presence on the border served the interests of Syria. “The presence of the resistance on the border serves the Lebanese national interests, first and foremost,” he asserted.
Nasrallah was referring to calls made by foreign countries and much of the country’s Christian population for the deployment of the army in the South ­ a move which the government has so far refused.
The Hizbullah leader said no one could guarantee that “Israel” would not attack the country “in a month or two” ­ a reference to recent “Israeli” threats to bomb a local pumping station being erected along the Hasbani River…(2)

Lebanon's Hariri: Hizbullah has right to attack “Israel”…(3)

Even though “Israel” pulled its forces out of his country, Lebanese Prime Minister Rafiq Hariri said Lebanon has the right to fight for the Shaba Farms.
"The Lebanese government is fully convinced that the Shaba Farms area is a Lebanese territory and must be liberated by all means available," Hariri said in an interview with the London-based Jane's Defense Weekly to be published today.
"One way to regain Shaba Farms is through diplomatic contacts, while another way is through armed resistance. Only circumstances determine which course we should follow," Hariri was quoted as saying.
Hariri also gave Hizbullah a free hand in striking at “Israel”.
"Things are not as they used to be in the 1980s. Wars in that era did not solve the problems, but made things worse," said Hariri. "Now if [the “Israelis”] wage a war, Hizbullah will escalate its attacks."
Still, Hariri says a political settlement is possible with Prime Minister Ariel Sharon "if the new “Israeli” government has the true desire to do so." But he quickly added that statements by Sharon and other “Israeli” officials "lead to a pessimistic conclusion about peace prospects in the region."
Hariri blamed a lack of peace with “Israel” on the Jewish state.
"The peace process has not reached its objective because the “Israelis” are not yet ready for peace... and we cannot wait," says Hariri. "When “Israel” is ready [for peace], we'll be ready."
As to the possibility of “Israel” launching air strikes against Syrian military bases in Lebanon or Lebanese infrastructure in retaliation for future attacks by Hizbullah, Hariri said: "If “Israel” carries out military operations against Lebanon, it will only complicate the situation because these operations will increase the scale of violence. The solution cannot be reached through violence but only by resorting to political dialogue."
However, Hariri denied reports of differences between the government and Hizbullah on the productivity of continued guerrilla operations in the south, and declined to directly address the issue of the Lebanese Armed Forces disarming the guerrillas and deploying to fully secure the entire border with “Israel”.
"There are no problems between Hizbullah and the Lebanese government. When “Israeli” forces withdraw from all of south Lebanon, Hizbullah guerrillas will disarm themselves and weapons will naturally go to the Lebanese authorities," Hariri said.

March Tuesday 27-2001:

Nasrallah refuses to confirm deny possession of Iranian missiles…(1)

Hizbullah’s secretary-general, Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, refused on Monday to either confirm or deny reports that the party has acquired new longer-range missiles from Iran.
In an interview with “Israel’s” Maariv newspaper Friday, “Israeli” Premier Ariel Sharon accused Syria of helping Iran arm Hizbullah in a massive airlift of rockets, arms and ammunition.
“We are not concerned to either confirm or deny such reports. Sharon assumes that we in Lebanon will be worried when he says that we have rockets. Instead, we would be happy if the rockets he talked about were in our possession,” Nasrallah told a rally in a suburb south of Beirut late Sunday.
Up until “Israel’s” withdrawal last May, Hizbullah had occasionally fired into “Israel” 122mm Katyusha rockets with a range of around 20 kilometers. The weapon in question, the 240mm version of the Katyusha, reportedly has a range of 70 kilometers.
Dismissing Sharon’s claim as an “old boring tune,” Nasrallah said: “I want to tell Sharon and all (members) of the enemy government that we will do what is our right and certainly, what is our duty. It is our right to defend our country and defend every drop of blood and every drop of water and every iota of soil.
“It is our right to be strong to repulse the enemy,” he said. “Can you imagine that “Israel”, which possesses nuclear weapons and arms of mass destruction, is protesting against the resistance on charges that the resistance possesses a rocket with a 70-kilometer range?”
Reports that Hizbullah has acquired longer-range rockets have been routinely aired since June 1996. In February last year, “Israeli” security sources were quoted as saying that Hizbullah had been given 240mm Fajr rockets with a 70-kilometer range, which were being guarded in the Bekaa by a unit of Iranian Revolutionary Guards.
Hizbullah is not thought to have fired any such rockets into “Israel” during the years of occupation and the reports that it possesses Fajr rockets have remained unconfirmed.
Hizbullah leaders have repeatedly denied receiving military aid from Iran, insisting that Tehran’s support is political and moral and that they buy their weapons on the global market.
The guerrillas have not fired into “Israel” since last May.
“Sharon is not worried about Kiryat Shmona or Nahariya,” Nasrallah said in reference to “Israeli” towns near the border, “but he is worried about Haifa and Tiberias and about the whole of occupied northern Palestine.”
