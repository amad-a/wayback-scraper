Views in the Resistance

  September Saturday 4 -1999:

Qassem: Resistance aborted Israeli June aggression (1)
Hizbullahs Deputy Secretary General H.E. Sheikh Nehim Qassem asserted that the Islamic Resistance (IR) September retaliation for the Israeli June aggression and the massacre against Libbaya village had corrected, once again, the compass direction towards the April Understanding which protects the civilians.

The IR escorted, yesterday, the martyr Hassan Qassem Aqil to his final resting place where he was buried at Rawdat Shahidain cemetery.

Our people and nation as will as the whole world had felt, during the last two days, that the mujahedeen had achieved a victory when the IR responded to the last Israeli aggression against Libbaya population, Qassem told.

He added that the IR engaged itself in protecting the civilians whatever the sacrifices and difficulties were. We call on our people not to be deceived that the enemy needs a pretext to launch aggression reminding that the pretenses of the Israeli July 1993 and April 1996 were weak and baseless.

Qassem concluded that the IR will proceed with its track until an unconditional liberation is achieved engage itself in protecting the civilians and infrastructure facilities and that the resistance activities are not subjected to the alleged negotiations and political considerations.

  September Tuesday 7 -1999:

Raad: IR had a special type of retaliation in store(1)
"Nabatieh MP Mohammed Raad, who also heads Hizbullahs politburo, said Monday that the resistance had a special type of retaliation in store if "Israel" targets Lebanons infrastructure again.

In case the enemy stages new infrastructure assaults, our retaliation will be different, Raad was quoted as saying by the Hizbullah-run Al-Nour radio. He did not specify what form the response would take, merely adding that Hizbullah was ready for this response.

Raad said that Hizbullah had expected "Israel" to bomb Lebanons infrastructure in response to last weeks Katyusha attack on northern Galilee. The attack, in which around 25 Katyusha rockets and mortar rounds were fired, was a reprisal for an earlier "Israeli" bombardment in which two civilians were killed.

But the enemy denied the Katyusha rockets landed on its settlements so as not to have to come up with a response, Raad said.

1- The Daily Star, 7-9-1999. 
  September Saturday 11 -1999:

Hizbullahs secretary general: we are not easily intimidated and the Resistance is ready to respond(1)
"Hizbullahs secretary general Sayyed Hassan Nassrallah stressed that the latest Israeli aggression on the northern Beqaa was a message that implied that the Zionist hand is long and can reach the depth of the Lebanese territory. He also added that the Islamic Resistance would do its duty in facing this message.

"The secretary general also stressed that the only escape for Israel is to withdraw, because the resistances operation hurts the enemy while Israel is not ready to commit the same sacrifices as the Islamic Resistances sacrifices.

"The president of the writers union Mr. Mohammad Baalbaki following his visit with Sayyed Nassrallah quoted these statements.

"The secretary general also received Khaled Jamal Abdul Nasser who was accompanied by the MPs Najah Wakim, Mustafa Saad and Dr. Bshar Kuwatly. Abdel Nasser praised the great role that the resistance was offering. He also said that I expressed to Sayyed Nassrallah, the interconnection of the Egyptian people with all the operations of the Resistance that are executed by Hizbullah against the Zionist enemy. And we aspire that this role reaches the entire Arab nation. He also quoted his father by saying that our struggle with the Zionist enemy is a struggle of existence and not just a border struggle".

1- Al-Manar TV, 10-9-1999.
