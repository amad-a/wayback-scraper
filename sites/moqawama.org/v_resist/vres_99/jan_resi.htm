Views in the Resistance

  January Monday 4 -1999:
 
A frustrating year for occupation troops
"A spate of activity, both military and political, in the last few weeks of 1998 has cast the debate over the future of south Lebanon wide open".

"Rising "Israeli" military casualties in the south, growing opposition within the Jewish state to the occupation, a security Cabinet divided over the best way to respond to Hizbullah and ­ most worrisome of all ­ a looming general election have ensured a very uncertain start to the new year".

"The present climate contrasts with the beginning of 1998, when the "Israeli" government offered a political solution that was perhaps the first serious attempt to extricate the troops from the blood-soaked wadis and hills of the south".

"Israeli" (War) Defense Minister Yitzhak Mordechai dropped a bombshell in a Jan. 3 interview with Paris-based Al-Watan Al-Arabi newspaper by stating that "Israel" was ready to comply with UN Security Council Resolution 425. It was the first time that a senior "Israeli" official had recognized 425, which was adopted following Israels 1978 invasion".

"But this apparent magnanimous gesture was tempered by "Israel" stating that Resolution 425 would be fulfilled only if Lebanon agreed to deploy the Army in the former occupation zone, dismantle the resistance, absorb members of the SLA into the Army, and maintain security in the south through or with Unifil".

"Lebanon and Syria rejected the proposal outright, declaring that Resolution 425 was unconditional. In the face of blanket opposition from Beirut and Damascus there was little "Israel" could do, although it tried hard to garner the support of the U.S., France and the UN".

"Washington was cautious in its response to the "Israeli" move. Privately, U.S. diplomats expressed doubts as to "Israels" sincerity, noting that the original offer had been made through the media rather than through normal channels. There was little UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan could do without the support of Washington, although it is that clear he would like to see 425 fulfilled, be it conditionally or unconditionally".

"France and the European Union sided ­ at least publicly ­ with Lebanon and Damascus".

"The "Israeli" Cabinet, amid much fanfare, formally adopted as policy the conditional acceptance of 425 April 1. Since then little more has been heard on the subject".

"Meanwhile, the fighting continued in the south unabated, with 1,302 attacks recorded against the occupation forces, the highest number since 1985."

"Hizbullah made greater use of their deadly roadside bombs, which claimed 16 of the 23 "Israeli" soldiers to die in the zone during the year".

"The multi-faith Lebanese Resistance Brigades, the brainchild of Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, launched their first operation in February, attacking the Haddatha post in the western sector. The Brigades, which are trained and led by seasoned Hizbullah fighters, staged attacks through out the year but their effectiveness remains limited".

"The "Israeli" Army adopted a defensive policy in 1998 after the offensive tactics of 1997 helped fuel the largest annual fatality rate in the south since 1985, with 39 killed. Foot patrols were reduced and millions of dollars were spent reinforcing the fortifications of their hilltop bunkers".

"With fewer open targets to strike, Hizbullah increasingly took to long-range mortar attacks, with negligible results against "Israeli" troops sitting in their underground bunkers. But the new "Israeli" tactics allowed Hizbullah greater freedom to roam the zone, with fatal consequences in November when seven "Israeli" soldiers were killed in the space of 10 days. All seven were killed in roadside bomb attacks, two of them in almost exactly the same place and all three very close to the "Israeli" border.

"Hizbullah launched two mass Katyusha strikes into northern "Israel" during the year, both in response to civilian casualties caused by "Israeli" attacks".

"The first attack Aug. 25 provided a crucial test for the "Israeli" government. The "Israelis" had to ask themselves whether they were willing to escalate the conflict by responding heavily or whether discretion was the more prudent option".

"The "Israelis" chose discretion and gave Hizbullah a significant psychological victory. In the past, anyone who launched rockets into "Israel" could count on a counter-strike  nowadays Hizbullah has the last word. The primitive Katyusha rocket has become, in the Israeli-Lebanese arena, a strategic weapon in Lebanese hands, wrote Zvi Barel, a commentator for "Israels" Haaretz newspaper".

"The second attack came Dec. 22 and was in response to the killing of seven members of a family in an air raid on the Bekaa. Again "Israeli" ministers strutted up and down the length of the border making all manner of bellicose threats. But, as in August, the "Israelis" had little choice but to back down".

"Israels" military knows very well that if civilian targets are attacked in Lebanon, Hizbullah would immediately retaliate with Katyusha attacks on Galilee thus, for Israel, defeating the point of the occupation in the first place. Clearly, the "Israeli" Army is itching to hit Hizbullah directly but it cannot find the targets. Such is the dilemma facing the "Israelis".

"Israels" frustration was indicated by the decision to hit the radio antenna in Janta in December in which the seven civilians were killed. One might be justified in asking how bombing the antenna of a minor radio station over 80 kilometers from the "Israeli" border serves to protect the population of northern Galilee. Especially as the bungled raid led the residents of Kiryat Shimona to spend the night in their bomb shelters".

"As if the tit-for-tat escalations were not enough, "Israels" general election scheduled for May 17 has cast a very unpredictable element into the debate. For the time being, the Israeli government is taking the advice of its military to maintain the occupation at its present level. Partial or full withdrawals have been ruled out as have serious escalatory measures".

"But this could change either way at a moments notice. Netanyahu has reportedly lent tacit backing for a staged pullout as advocated by Foreign Minister Ariel Sharon, which would apparently include the return of two villages on the Golan Heights to Syria".

"But he has also warned that further Katyusha strikes into "Israel" will meet with severe reprisals. Hizbullah, in turn, has warned "Israel" that Lebanese civilian casualties will prompt Katyusha attacks".

"With typical morbid timing, five civilians were wounded Sunday in an air raid on the radio antenna in Janta, a copycat attack that led to the last cross-border rocket strike just 13 days ago".

"Unifils political adviser and spokesman, Timur Goksel, acknowledges that the "Israelis" have finally begun a genuine debate over how to leave the south. Weve started the inevitable process of leading to a withdrawal, he said. Were not seeing the light at the end of the tunnel but we are seeing the tunnel.

  January Thursday 7 -1999:

Lahoud stands firm on withdrawal conditions (1)
"President Emile Lahoud stated clearly Wednesday that neither Beirut nor Damascus would accept a partial withdrawal and that the ties between the two countries remained immutable".

"In his first official reception for the diplomatic community since becoming head of state, General Lahoud told ambassadors that Lebanon had always relied on the support of the international community in its struggle to oust Israel from the south".

We believe that a comprehensive and just peace will guarantee the security of everyone in the region, he said. Our concept of peace means the rejection of partial measures that lead only to an incomplete peace and which neither ensure the security of the region nor provide for a complete and clear withdrawal from the area.

General Lahoud insisted that the Lebanese-Syrian track was necessary to bring about a comprehensive regional peace.

Lebanons national interest requires the coordination of the Lebanese and Syrian tracks in the peace negotiations, whatever the circumstances or considerations may be, he said.

The president told the assorted diplomats that the resistance was a legitimate expression of the national cause, the withdrawal of the "Israeli" occupiers from its territories, and the end of the daily attacks against its people.

  January Tuesday 12 -1999:

Nasrallah: Resistance ready to retaliate for "Israeli" aggressions (1)
 "Resistance is ready for any confrontation. It will make the "Israeli" enemy pay for its aggressions on our country, Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said Saturday".

"Speaking at an iftar held by the Woman Council of the Committee for Supporting the Islamic Resistance, his Eminence said "Those who think a settlement will solve our problem with the Zionists are either stupid or under a delusion."

"We must not rely on the UN security council or similar bodies, for the world would not have noticed the Janta massacre in which Zionists had killed a mother and her six children if there had been no shelling of "Israeli" colonies", he said.

"The US, which had earlier barred the security council from condemning the Qana massacre, would not let denouncing the Janta slaughter pass; however, we insist that a complaint must be filed with the security council to let the world know what is going on, even though we don't count on the security council", he added.

"We only count on and believe in the resistance option, for this is the only option to retaliate for the Zionist aggressions; we are ready to offer sacrifices, in this path, the secretary general said stressing that "Gone is the time when we used to be killed alone and displaced alone, gone is the time when threats are leveled at us".

"Sayyed Nasrallah stated that Resistance is ready to confront any "Israeli" aggression".

Resistance is ready for the confrontation. It will have the enemy pay for its aggressions against our country", he said.

"The enemy has tried all ways such as aggressions and wars to pressurize us in vain. The so many sessions by the enemy's inner cabinet are evidence to the "Israeli" quagmire, his Eminence added.
The resistance in Lebanon doesn't want anything for itself. On the contrary, it is ready to sacrifice everything to defend its people and country", the secretary general said.

He added, "In September 1993, Hizbullah carried its martyrs and avoided an internal confrontation to use all its strength against the Zionist enemy."

"In the past few years, some tried to evoke sectarian sentiments against the resistance by raising the Jezzine issue and other issues", he said.

"Sayyed Nasrallah said, "The resistance has proven to be very remote from sectarianism and confessionalism, and everybody found out that the path taken by the resistance is the right one for the liberation of Jezzine and all the occupied areas. Here is the Lahd militia being forced to evacuate several outposts in Tomat Neha and al-Mashnaqa. The Resistance's operations will force the Lahd militia and the Zionist army to withdraw from the occupied zone."

"Some used to accuse the resistance of being sectarian. Although this is not true, we established the (multi-faith) Lebanese Resistance Brigades which every' Lebanese can join", he said.

"The Hizbullah secretary general said, "Some have tried to raise fears of the fact that the resistance men follow religious doctrine. These young men's observance of religious teachings is a strength factor for the resistance; we need to distinguish between religious commitment and sectarian sentiments. A religious man confronts the unjust even if they are from his own sect. We are proud to be committed to our religious teachings", he added.

"The peril threatening Lebanon is the sectarian sentiment, not observing religious teachings, which when observed, lead to success in reaching supreme goals", he concluded.

  January Thursday 14 -1999:

Sayyed Nasrallah: Resistance will continue attacking occupation (1)
"The Zionist enemy will withdraw from the occupied zone sooner or later due to the victories achieved by the resistance last year, Hezbollah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said at an iftar yesterday".

"All "Israelis" agree that the occupation forces must come out of Lebanon without linking such a withdrawal to a "peace accord", his Eminence said noting that some "Israelis" want an unconditional withdrawal while others want that within the framework of security conditions "which we reject, for no one accepts the country's army to become border guards for the occupation."

His Eminence cited the partial withdrawals which were the result of resistance operations and the lack of men ready to die while defending these outposts in the militia ranks.

"Regardless of the threats made or practiced by the enemy against the resistance, the government or the people, the resistance will proceed with its war of attrition against the enemy to inflict more losses in its ranks. This is the only way to force the enemy to withdraw and to recover our land", Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"More martyrs in our ranks will only increase our determination to continue resistance, he added noting that the "so many sessions of the Zionist inner cabinet indicate the magnitude of the quagmire in which the enemy is stumbling without finding a solution."

"The Hezbollah secretary general urged the government to fulfill the promises it has made to the south and the resistance".

"The government has to make us -- along with the south residents -- feel that this issue is a priority on its agenda in spite of its indulgence in administrative reform and internal problems", he said.
"He urged the cabinet to file a complaint with the UN security council to protest the "Israeli" aggressions "in order to uncover those who claim to be neutral while sponsoring the so-called peace in the region."

In order to respond to the enemy's threats and along with the important projects, the government must allocate money to boost the steadfastness of the south and the occupied zone residents, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"He also called on the people of the occupied zone to unite warning against the enemy's attempts to create unrest among Lebanese".

"It is a national duty to exert more pressures on the collaborators' militia to dismantle it for good, he said, stressing the importance of the bill presented by Hezbollah's MPs on pardoning the collaborators who desert the militia immediately".

"Concerning the "Israeli" threats and what has been conveyed by the Americans in this regard, his Eminence said these threats may be part of the US press campaign against Syria to serve "Israel" and its interests".

"An "Israeli" aggression against Lebanon is not an easy option for the enemy. Atmospheres today differ significantly from those in 1993 and 1996, he said noting the mounting strength and presence of the resistance and the backing it enjoys from other Lebanese surrounding it".

"We cannot but wonder what new things can the "Israeli" get from another onslaught, he asked."
"The secretary general was speaking at an iftar organized by the occupied zone committees. The iftar was attended by Ahmed Tabara who represented PM Salim al-Hoss, MP Mohammed Fneish and president of the Antoinite Maronite Monasteries Youhana Selim, President of the General Labor Confederation Elias Abu Rizq, south Governor Faisal al-Sayegh, along with delegations from al-Arqoub and Jezzine".

  January Saturday 16 -1999:

Hizbullah marked al-Quds Day in Beirut and areas; Nasrallah; killing occupation troops will continue regardless of results (1)
"In an act of faithfulness to al-Quds (Jerusalem) and affirmation of the holy city's historic identity in the face of Judaization and occupation, Hizbullah marked al-Quds Day with a massive rally at Martyr Hadi Nasrallah's Boulevard in Beirut".

"The ceremony was attended by Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, Faithfulness ot the Resistance bloc MPs, Hizbullah leadership, MP Jamil Shammas, Iranian chargé d'affaires Mohammed Irani, Sayyed Ali Fadlallah (representing his father Ayatollah Sayyed Mohammed Hussein Fadlallah), representative of Druze religious leader Sheikh Bahjat Ghaith, president of the General Labor Confederation Elias Abu Rizq, Representative of Army Chief Col. Haidar Safa, Representative of State Security Directorate Col. Dakroub, Representative of Internal Security Maj. Mashmoushi, Representative of Army Intelligence Maj. Adel Qansou besides representatives of the Syrian Social Nationalist Party, Arab Socialist Party, Workers' League, Amal Movement, and Palestinian Groups".

"The rally began with reciting verses of the Holy Quran, then a symbolic parade by resistance brigade and detachments"

"Hizbullah's Secretary General replied to calls for "not giving the enemy justifications for escalation" saying "We are not giving justifications to anyone. We are fighting invading occupying troops. We kill their soldiers and officers, destroy their vehicles. Is this a justification to anyone?"

"The war of attrition against the enemy will continue ... until we force it to withdraw without any conditions," the Hizbullah leader said.

"We will never give up this resistance and this method of operations (against the "Israeli" occupation) no matter what threats are made, he said in reference to "Israeli" threats to launch attacks against Lebanese civilians targets".

"Our aim is to liberate the land, and the rhythm of our attacks depends solely on the circumstances on the ground," Sayyed Nasrallah added.

"As long as Lebanese civilians are being targeted, we will practice our right to confront without fearing the current intimidation", he added.

"He said people must not be frightened with an "Israeli" large-scale aggression "in the same time, we must not relax; we need to be careful all the time."

"The secretary general criticized the ongoing debate over "Israeli" elections concerning the winning of one person or another".

"This is absurd. There is no difference between "Israeli" officials", he said.

"Those competing to head the enemy's government are the same bunch of terrorists, killers and racists who killed our people in 1993 and 1996,"," Sayyed Nasrallah told thousands of supporters at the rally.

"Those Arab leaders who are staking their bets on the fall of ("Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin) Netanyahu are making themselves ridiculous and underestimate the intelligence of their peoples," his Eminence said of the elections for the "Israeli" parliament and prime ministership on May 17.

The secretary general condemned the settlement talks with the "Israeli" entity and said liberating al-Quds, occupied by ""Israel"" since 1967, "is a duty". He denounced Yasser Arafat for his troubled attempts to negotiate with ""Israel"".

"Peace talks are a losing option," he said. "The only option is jihad (holy struggle) and resistance."
"The cause of al-Quds belongs to all the Arabs and all Moslems," he said. "Its fate cannot be decided by (Yasser) Arafat or anyone else claiming to represent the Palestinian people. No one can sell it, betray it or abandon it. That is the will of Allah."

"Yes, people will pray in al-Quds ... but not under the Star of David," he said.

"Marchers trampled on U.S. and "Israeli" flags before prayers on the last Friday of the holy month of Ramadan"

"In Beqaa, Hizbullah marked the event with a rally near Imam Ali's (A.S.) Mosque in Baalbek. The ceremony was attended by political, religious, and party figures along with massive crowd of people.

Several hundred Hizbullah fighters paraded in front of thousands of supporters who burnt "Israeli" and US flags".

"A similar rally was held in the Husseiniet of Yohmor (western Beqaa)".

"In the south, a rally was held at Imam Sadiq's (A.S.) club in Tyre in the presence of Faithfulness to the Resistance (FTR) bloc member Mohammed Fneish and a group of Ulama, political figures, and party representatives."

"The enemy's policy of threats, siege of towns and dynamiting houses will not change the resistance's movement and the mounting operations against occupation forces", Fneish said.

"Moreover, Palestinians in Lebanon marked al-Quds Day in al-Buss camp (Tyre) and in other camps in Lebanon."
