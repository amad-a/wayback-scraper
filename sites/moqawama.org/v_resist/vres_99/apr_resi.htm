Views in the Resistance

  April Wednesday 7 -1999:

NATIONAL CONGRESS TO BE HELD ON RESISTANCE AGAINST "ISRAELI" OCCUPATION(1)
"A National Congress will be held on the 10th of this month to support the Lebanese resistance and the steadfastness of southerners in the face of "Israeli" occupation. This was announced by representatives of political parties and national groups this morning. The three day Congress will be held at the UNESCO palace. It will be opened by President Emile Lahoud. The Congress' aim is to form a committee that will lay the groundwork for future action. The objective is to back the ongoing resistance struggle against the "Israeli" occupation of south Lebanon".

1- Future TV, 6-4-1999. 

  April Sunday 11 -1999:

NCSR inaugurated(1)
"The first National Conference for Supporting the Resistance and Enhancing Popular Steadfastness (NCSR) against the "Israeli" occupation was inaugurated today (on Saturday) under the auspices of President Gen Emile Lahoud who was represented by Information Minister Anwar al-Khalil"
"A large number of ministers and MPs attended the inauguration, in addition to religious, diplomatic, political and union figures".

"Speaking on behalf of the president, Minister al-Khalil said supporting the Resistance and enhancing steadfastness top the government's priorities."

"Resistance is a legitimate right and duty until liberation of occupied land is achieved, al-Khalil added, stressing the correlation of the Lebanese and Syrian tracks".

"Moreover, al-Khalil noted that the issue of the detainees and prisoners in the occupation's jails will stay at the center of the government's concerns until these prisoners are freed.

The government is processing the necessary measures to treat the Resistance Martyrs' sons as equals to the Lebanese Army Martyrs' sons, he said.

"Iranian Chargé d'affaires, who spoke during the inauguration, praised the Resistance Mujahedeen who are fighting the "Israeli" enemy, saying that the Lebanese people's embrace of the Resistance is a model for a victorious society".

"For his part, the Ba'ath regional command member Toufiq Salha commended the outstanding Resistance against the "Israeli" occupation in the south and Western Beqaa.

"The Resistance has made South Lebanon a graveyard for the "Israeli" invaders, he said.

"Former Yemeni President Ali Nasser Mohammed said the phenomenon of the Resistance against the "Israeli" occupation in the south is the most nobel Arabic phenomenon".

"Mr. Nasser Mohammed underlined the support of the Yemeni people to the Lebanese people in their Resistance against the occupation."

"The conference is expected to be concluded by announcing the birth of a national committee to support the Resistance and enhance steadfastness".
 

1- Al-Manar TV, 10-4-1999. 
  April Tuesday 13 -1999:

NCSR announces birth of National Committee for Supporting Resistance(1)
"The first National Conference for Supporting Resistance and Enhancing Steadfastness (NCSR) was concluded today (today) at the UNESCO Hall in Beirut following two days of discussions.

"The conference came out with a political paper which emphasized that the Resistance is a legitimate duty and that the Zionist entity is dangerous to the world and humanity.

"The "Israeli" entity poses a certain and continuos threat to Lebanon, which found the whole world inattentive to implement international resolutions until the Resistance revived the world's memory, the NCSR paper said.

"NCSR called on the Lebanese government to review the situation of its Resistance men and steadfast people in the occupied zone and to supply them with steadfastness means, especially in front-line areas, in addition to embracing the martyrs' families and taking action to free all detainees from enemy prisons".

"Moreover, the NCSR included eight essential working plans which will be implemented by the new National Committee for Supporting the Resistance that was established by the conference.

"The National committee includes representatives of: political parties, religious leaders, vocational, labor and artistic syndicates, organizations, cultural sectors, youth movements, writer's union, cultural clubs, women's organizations, the media and other public committees.

"Furthermore, the endorsed eight working plans to support the resistance are: the roles of expatriation, youth, media, state, economic support, Arabs, in addition to the legal and cultural roles".

"The NCSR recommendations were announced by the head of Hezbollah's political council, MP Hajj Mohammed Raad who commended president Emile Lahoud's sponsorship of the conference".

1- Al-Manar TV. 12-4-1999. 

  April Wednesday 21 -1999:

Resistance will continue on all areas occupied by "Israel", says Hizbullah MP(1)
"All the outposts of the ("Israeli") occupation and its collaborators in the occupied zone are and will always be legitimate targets for the Islamic Resistance, Chief of Hizbullah's political Council MP Mohammed Raad said today (yesterday).

At a press conference he held in the parliament, the Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc member said, "It is really strange how the Lebanese delegate to the Monitoring Group has agreed on the panel's statement."

"As resistance, it is our concern to ask, as long as the bombs, according to the group's statement, were detonated against "Israeli" forces, what where these forces doing in Arnoun?" Raad asked.

This means that it is not Arnoun which is meant, but the Beaufort Castle which is manned by occupation forces, he added.

"MP Raad underlined that Beaufort Castle or any other area where "Israeli" enemy and its collaborators are deployed will be targets for the Resistance which will proceed with its operations until the enemy evacuates our land without any conditions.

All sneaky attempts and suspicious statements will never change this fact, he said.

"The Resistance is committed to the April Understanding and it won't be dragged into the enemy's attempts to interpret the understanding according to its interests, he added.

"The Hizbullah MP noted that the AUMG communiqué stated that "there were differing views" on whether the occupation's annexation of Arnoun is in violation of the Understanding.

"This is really suspicious. I wonder how the Lebanese delegate agreed to have this communiqué issued with such errors and terminology, Raad said, adding that "Hizbullah has welcomed the President (Emile Lahoud) and PM's (Salim al-Hoss) remarks on the Resistance legitimate right and their considering the enemy's annexation of Arnoun to the occupied zone as a clear violation of the April Understanding."

"Then, how could the Lebanese delegate agree on such a text in the statement? MP Raad asked.

He also noted that AUMG communiqué calls for using bilateral (Lebanese - "Israeli") channels over Arnoun".

"Such an act will be meeting, although implicitly, the enemy's demand to amend some of the understanding's articles, including neutralizing some areas, such as Arnoun, he said.

"The Beaufort Castle was targeted (by the Resistance). The two bombs were planted by the Resistance men at the Castle's gate, as the Monitoring Group acknowledged, MP Raad said.

"If the Beaufort Castle is part of Arnoun, the Arnoun residents have no problem with the Resistance men. Moreover, Resistance men do not need the Arnoun passage to reach the Castle, for they can reach any "Israeli" outpost in the occupied zone whenever they want with means that do not breach the April Understanding, Raad said.

"As we state these facts, we stress our opposition to this terminology (used in the AUMG communiqué) and affirm that we will never accept any amendment of any article in the April Understanding, he said.

"Resistance will proceed with its operations in every Lebanese area in which there is direct or indirect "Israeli" occupation", the Hizbullah MP concluded.

  April Tuesday 27 -1999:

Hizbullah Secretary General's Speech on Ashora Day(1)
"Hizbullah has once again rejected conditional "Israeli" offers to withdraw from south Lebanon.

"All experiences have proved that the (armed) resistance is the only way ... to regain the land," Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah today (yesterday) told hundreds of thousands of mourners who gathered in Beirut's southern suburbs to mark Ashora Day.

""Israel" is a cancerous gland in this region ... this is why the only choice for our Arab and Islamic nation is that ""Israel"" should cease to exist," he said, the crowds raised their fists shouting "Death to "Israel"."

""Israel"" and the US represent the peak of corruption, injustice and arrogance in the region and the world, his Eminence added.

"All the measures taken by the Zionist enemy in the occupied zone will be useless; decreasing the number of occupation troops will not ease the bleeding of their officers and soldiers, nor will the increased violence in artillery and aerial bombardment curb the resolve of the Mujahedeen who are fighting with Imam Hussein's (A.S.) mind, spirit and resolve,"

"The enemy's attempt to place the collaborators' militia in front-line outposts will also fail, because the Zionist army has already tried this option and seen the outposts falling one after the other under the feet of the Islamic Resistance's Mujahedeen, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding that "These outposts will keep on falling and the militia won't be able to protect the enemy or its security."

"The Hizbullah Secretary General called on the collapsing militia to rebel and turn their arms against "Israeli" forces in occupied southern Lebanon.

"I call on all militia officers and members not only to surrender but also to carry out an uprising in the face of this enemy," Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"I call on them to turn their weapons on the chests of the occupation officers and soldiers in the occupied zone," he added.
"He warned the militiamen that if they "do not surrender, the Islamic Resistance Mujahedeen will await them not only at crossroads, valleys and mountain tops, but also at the doors of their homes and bedrooms.

"On the Tenth of Moharram (Ashora Day) we reiterate our option in Lebanon, call on continuation of national - popular - official coherence in backing the resistance and not giving up our right to resist, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

"All must be sure that the enemy will not settle for guarantees but will ask for more, he said.

On the Tenth of Moharram we reiterate our permanent readiness to face any possibility, especially as we are on the eve of the "Israeli" elections on May 17", he added.

  April Thursday 29 -1999:

Premier retorts to "Israeli" threats: all Lebanon supports Resistance(1)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss retorted to "Israeli" threats that were launched yesterday by saying, "the solution is simple: to withdraw "Israeli" occupation forces from Lebanon."

"Following his meeting with President Gen Emile Lahoud last night, Mr. al-Hoss told al-Nahar newspaper that the "Israeli" occupation's soldiers are being killed on occupied Lebanese soil.

"It is a legitimate right for the Lebanese to defend themselves and their land against the continuous "Israeli" aggressions, the premier stated.

"The "Israeli" occupation is terrorism," Mr. Hoss continued, "and the killing of children in their homes is the terrorism."

"As for the Resistance, it is a natural result caused by the "Israeli" occupation and aggressions; the Resistance is also a legitimate right for any people whose land is occupied and that is being killed by an occupying army".

"Replying to the statement made by "Israeli" Army Intelligence chief Amos Malka, who said that the Lebanese government is legitimizing Hizbullah's operations against "Israel", PM al-Hoss stated: "we repeat once again that all of Lebanon supports resisting the "Israeli" occupation. Resistance is a legitimate right as long as occupation continues."

"The premier announced that Lebanon has filed a complaint with the April Understanding Monitoring Group over the "Israeli" bombardment yesterday which targeted populated areas in southern villages and wounded a mother and her child in the town of Jarjoua, besides damaging a number of houses".

1- Al-Manar TV, 28-4-1999.
