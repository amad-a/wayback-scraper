Views in the Resistance

October Saturday 3 -1999:

Confusion of the enemy is real, Resistance has the initiative, Hizbullah(1)
"The intensive air raids carried out by the "Israeli" occupation's aircraft attempt to imply that the enemy possesses the initiative in the south and the Western Beqaa front, Hizbullah Deputy Secretary-General his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem said.

"In a press interview, Sheikh Qassem said, "The succession of the Resistance's operations and the losses they are inflicting in the ranks of the occupation and its collaborators refute this claim."

"The enemy's talk about a change in the Resistance's tactic is a positive remark about the Resistance's efficiency, the Deputy Secretary-General said.

"Speaking about the "Israeli" confusion about a potential withdrawal from the south and the Western Beqaa, Qassem said the contrast (in positions) between the office of the enemy's prime minister and other "Israeli" decision-makers is real and not role-playing, and "is due to the Resistance's ability to impose a situation on the ground."

PM: Resistance will continue until liberation(2)
"Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss today (yesterday) reiterated backing the Resistance against the "Israeli" occupation forces and Lebanon's refusal to discuss stopping it at any time or place.

"There is a Lebanese consensus on backing the Resistance. The government supports it politically and diplomatically, for we believe the resistance is a legitimate right for any people whose land is under occupation, the premier told the Hungarian Television.

"Lebanon will not do anything that would stop the resistance as long as there is occupation, al-Hoss said, adding, "for the first time, Israel has recognized (the UN Security Council's) Resolution 425, even if verbally, and Ehud Barak has been calling for withdrawal from the south and the Western Beqaa within a year. This would not be the case if it were not for the resistance."

"The prime minister affirmed that "Israel is the terrorist. What it is exercising against Lebanon is none other than terrorism, he said, asking, "What has the occupation army been doing on our soil for 21 years? And why are these daily aggressions that cause large losses in lives and property?"

"Israel" is a terrorist state. What the resistance is doing is heroic and aims to achieve liberation, al-Hoss said while expressing his pessimism regarding the resumption of the settlement process in the region "due to the "Israeli" intransigence and Barak's Nos."

Lebanon will not be part of any negotiations or sign any accord without Syria due to the inseparability of the Lebanese and Syrian tracks, a fact that strengthens both Lebanon and Syria", he concluded.

October Saturday 9 -1999:

We will not stop Resistance, Hoss tells BBC(1)
"Prime Minister Dr. Salim al-Hoss reiterated Lebanon's support for the Resistance at the political and immaterial levels as long as there is "Israeli" occupation on our land.

"In an interview with BBC, the premier denied that anyone has proposed stopping the Resistance as a precondition for the resumption of the settlement negotiations.

There isn't such a condition. And we do not accept it anyway, he said, adding, "as long as Lebanese territories are occupied, there will be resistance."

"Dr. al-Hoss stressed that it is not in the interest of Lebanon to stop the Resistance.

Asked why Lebanon is not giving clear position regarding the situation after the Zionist occupation's withdrawal, al-Hoss asked, "Is it reasonable for us to uncover all our security views before the negotiations? If we engage in these details, what will the negotiations tackle?

"The prime minister also stressed the inseparability of the Lebanese and Syrian tracks".

October Thusday 14 -1999:

Hizbullah has options prepared for peace(1)
Hizbullahs deputy secretary general, Nahim Qassem Wednesday said the party has lined up several options should Israel withdraw from south Lebanon, but that it was not ready to divulge them yet.

The partys cadres have adopted several alternatives after studying this issue extensively, Qassem said during a televised interview. But unveiling them to the public, to the Israelis or to others would leave Hizbullah exposed.

He said that war strategies required tricking the enemy while concealing ones ideas and plans for the future. Qassem said that because Hizbullah was a dogmatic, political, social and cultural party that was present in Parliament and among the people, it would not be dismantled, even if a peace settlement is reached.

Hizbullah will remain after any settlement, Qassem said. Those who think Hizbullahs survival is threatened by to the possibility of a peace settlement are misled, as the future will prove.

He added that although Hizbullahs circumstances, and performance may weaken or strengthen, it would always remain a viable organization.

Hizbullah will deal with a settlement accordingly, if it happens, he said. But this does not mean that Hizbullah will be abolished or has become trapped because it is rooted in the people and the internal political, social, struggles and cultural spheres.

Although Hizbullahs priorities are centered on the Israeli occupation, Qassem said Hizbullah did not exist simply because of the Jewish state. Hizbullah was created in 1982, after the Israeli invasion of Lebanon.

October Wednesday 20 -1999:

PM reiterates backing resistance against Israeli occupation(1)
Prime Minister Dr Salim al-Hoss reiterated the Lebanese official stand of moral and political backing for the Resistance as long as Israeli occupation exists.

The premier told the Cairo based Arab Radio that there is nothing to prove that there will be a partial withdrawal from Hasbaya.

What is being circulated is press information that has no field evidence, he said.

The premier said that he had not received any indication of such plans. "There's nothing to suggest that the Israelis intend to withdraw from Hasbaya," he said.

Al-Hoss doubted that Israeli prime minister Ehud Barak would keep his promise to withdraw his occupation troops from the south by July 2000.

"The Israelis are counting on an agreement with Lebanon to follow their withdrawal. However, they can't expect an agreement when Lebanon refuses to make peace without Syria," he said.

During a graduation ceremony at the Lebanese University's Engineering Faculty, the PM said the Arab Israeli conflict will not be over with signing a "reconciliation", but will go on in a fiercer manner.

This means that every Arab must ready himself to be able to steadfast and win in a conflict that will not be over with treaties but will continue in various fiercer forms, the PM said.

October Tuesday 26 -1999:

Talk about withdrawal "media rhetoric", Hizbullah SG(1)
Hizbullah Secretary-General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah has dismissed as "media rhetoric" the Israeli statements about a partial withdrawal from Hasbaya.

Speaking at the Engineers' Eighth Conference held yesterday at the Jinaan Hall, Sayyed Nasrallah talked about the current situation in Lebanon and the region.

The enemy is still looking for ways -political and military- to get rid of the quagmire of its occupation in Lebanon, Sayyed Nasrallah said, adding that the occupation command, in spite of the confusion it is confronting, is still examining the unilateral withdrawal option.

"We are now in a position at which the occupation cannot impose its conditions on us. It will get desperate in this regard. It is no longer enough to have the occupiers' withdraw unconditionally to relieve the occupation of its dilemma, the secretary-general told the rally.

"The occupation's field measures and attempts to limit casualties in its ranks, especially the air raids method, will fall, just like the earlier measures did. This method has not affected, and will not affect, the Resistance men's morale."

The battle with the Israeli occupation is open until it comes out of our land in humiliation, his Eminence concluded.

The Engineers' conference aimed also to honor Dr Ghassan Qansou, a famous Lebanese scholar. The ceremony was attended by Transportation Minister Najib Miqati, Engineers' Chief Samir Domet, and Vice-President of the Moslem Engineers' Association's Administrative Council.

October Sunday 31 -1999:

Hizbullah: we will resist until occupation is out(1)
We will keep on resisting until the (Israeli) occupation is out of our land without any conditions or accords, Hizbullah Secretary General his Eminence Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said.

He was speaking at the massive rally organized by the Islamic Jihad in Palestine at al-Yarmouk camp in Damascus to mark the fourth anniversary of Dr. Fathi al-Shqaqi's martyrdom (IJP's Former Secretary General).

Lebanon today, thanks to its brave resistance, is imposing its conditions on the arrogant Zionist entity, although Lebanon is the weakest among the countries surrounding the Israeli entity, the secretary general said.

Official Lebanon says that "Israels" ending its current quagmire in south Lebanon will only be through withdrawal from all of the Golan Heights (until the 4th of July line), while the Islamic Resistance says more than that, Sayyed Nasrallah said.

He disclosed that several international envoys have asked Hizbullah to promise to stop military activity after the occupation's withdrawal from south Lebanon, "in exchange for turning Hizbullah into a political party" and giving Lebanon a lot of economic grants.

IJP's Secretary general Dr. Ramadan Abdallah Shalah stressed the Palestinians' right to armed struggle through any territory in which there is resistance, in reference to two attacks staged on Israeli occupation forces in south Lebanon by IJP on Thursday.

Jihad will continue in Palestine until the occupation is removed, Shalah said.

Hamas Politburo Member Mossa Abu Marzoq and Iranian Ambassador in Damascus Hussein Sheikh al-Islam both delivered speeches at the rally. They stressed the importance of Palestine in Moslems' creed besides underlining the invariable position on the settlement process.
