Fight your own battles

    "So the "Israeli" government likes to think that having its lapdog militia leave Jezzine with its tail between its legs constitutes a test of an eventual "Israeli" pullout from the entire occupation zone. This scales new heights on Mount Arrogance, a peak with which the "Israelis" are so familiar that they should seriously consider annexing it".
"The inference, of course, is that "Israel" will attempt to portray continued resistance attacks on occupation forces after the evacuation of Jezzine as a portent of what would follow a general withdrawal. The scenario would then be used to buttress the "Israeli" demand for security guarantees ahead of any such pullback".
"But the entire test-case argument rests on a foundation of insolence and contradiction, for the one has nothing to do with the other: The occupation zone is Lebanese soil, not "Israeli", and so long as "Israeli" troops and their proxies remain there, they should expect no quarter. As for what would follow a full-fledged withdrawal, the occupiers will have to wait for answers until they return to their own side of the border".
"The Jewish state is using the ever-present threat posed by its strategic military superiority in a cynical effort to cow Beirut and Damascus into ending the activities of the resistance, whose tactical superiority seems to increase with each passing day".
"Specifically, the "Israelis" have found no way to deal effectively with Hizbullah.
Their intelligence on Sayyed Hassan Nasrallahs group is increasingly murky and their willingness to come out of their bunkers and face the guerrillas on the ground is evaporating".
"Even the high-tech warplanes supplied to them by Washington are of little use these days beyond the infliction of new tragedies on civilians, the impotent destruction of rocks and shrubs in Iqleem al-Toffah, and the rattling of windows in Beirut".
"The "Israelis" are therefore trying to go around what they have consistently failed to go through. Faced with such an effective and implacable foe, "Israel" ­ and the United States ­ would like nothing better than to have others neutralize Hizbullah. That desire is understandable, but given what the "Israelis" have done to this country for over two decades, any expectation that Beirut will be in a hurry to end "Israeli" suffering is wishful thinking".
"But ludicrous demands that others must make concessions to preserve "Israeli" security are nothing new. Accordingly, it is incumbent on all Arab countries ­ including and perhaps especially those who have already made peace with "Israel" ­ to join their voices to Lebanons and make it unmistakably clear that the ball remains very much in the "Israeli" court".

1- The Daily Star, 2-6-1999.
