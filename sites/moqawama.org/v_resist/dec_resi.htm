Views in the Resistance

  December Friday 4 -1998:

Hizbullah is to continue fighting "Israeli" occupation (1)
"Hizbollah fighters have devoted their lives to the liberation of southern Lebanon from "Israel's" occupation. Professionally trained and committed, they are engaged in a war to defend Lebanese territory".

"These are the fighters who have proved to be a match for the "Israeli" military. The success of Hizbollah units like this is forcing "Israel" to consider ending its twenty year occupation of south Lebanon. "Israel" is aided by its allied militia in patrolling the zone and maintaining the occupation. Hizbollah is very secretive- these pictures were taken on condition of anonymity. They hide out in the mountains of south Lebanon close to "Israeli" positions. One Hizbollah officer explains why they have been so successful. It come from hard work strong beliefs and determination we will have victory or martyrdom. Hizbollah has its own TV station, filming its operations against the "Israelis" who denounce its precise and deadly resistance attacks as quote terrorism. This roadside bomb on November 16th killed three "Israeli" soldiers. Hizbollah has killed four other since then. Sheikh Naim Qassem is Hizbollah's deputy secretary general. He stressed they are legitimate fighters against an illegal occupation. Sheikh Qassem said force not diplomacy works with "Israel". The Lebanese government does not control Hizbollah but supports resistance against "Israeli" occupation. The Lebanese have spent vast sums rebuilding Beirut that was ravaged by a long war that ended in 1990.

Now some "Israeli" ministers want air strikes against Beirut's infrastructure every time an "Israeli" soldier is killed by Hizbollah. That was denounced by Lebanese officials who do not trust "Israel"...

In South Lebanon the Hizbollah fighters are on alert, they have warned "Israeli" they will retaliate if it attacks civilian targets. Hizbollah says it has plenty more volunteers, but believes the "Israelis" are losing the heart to fight on. These men are highly trained and highly organized. What they are doing here is sending shockwaves right into the heart of "Israel" and they vow to succeed in driving "Israeli" occupation forces".

Bibi asks Lebanon for a tiny "favor"! Weeping "Israeli" soldiers want to go home to their moms ...(2)
"Arabs must feel "sorry" for "Israeli" Prime Minister Benjamin (Bibi) Netanyahu. He has a "little" problem that he attempted to resolve on his own but failed. He is appealing for Arab assistance! He is urging the Arabs to show what Arabs are famous for, "generosity"-- King Hussein and "King" Arafat's style. In their dealings, the two Arab leaders have granted Bibi all the demands and concessions he wished for. As a matter of fact, Arafat recently was very proud to go on record to have given even more! This time, Bibi is not asking Lebanon for much, only a "tiny favor". The man is not "selfish or greedy"!

Bibi wants a helping hand from the Lebanese Army to disarm Hezbollah and force their guerillas out of a 9-mile deep southern Lebanon zone occupied by "Israeli" troops! He wants the Lebanese government to make it safe and easy for "Israeli" occupational forces to move around unhindered in a friendly and hospitable environment on Lebanese soil in violation of U.N. Resolution 425, which demands their immediate and unconditional withdrawal! "Poor" Bibi is having trouble sleeping. He is plagued by terrifying nightmares, even when he is awake! Since he took office, Bibi has not seen a pleasant day nor heard of any good news coming from the northern front! The eyewitness stories of Hezbollah resistance fighters laying mines under "Israeli" tanks and attacking their heavily armed occupants with almost barren hands, are unimaginable but true and terrifying! Several months ago, he saw a video of a lone Hezbollah guerilla fighter attacking an "Israeli" army post, forcing its soldiers to flee with their casualties and raising the Hezbollah flag before retreating back to his base-- a scene that Rambo would not dare imitating. Assaulting "Israeli" soldiers in their armored vehicles and fortified positions at any hour has become a daily and ordinary affair. In a two weeks period, seven "Israeli" soldiers were shipped back to the "Jewish" settler state in wooden boxes".

"Bibi Netanyahu has not delivered as he promised! He was able to defeat Shimon Peres in the last "Israeli" election after he assured the voters he would be tougher on Arabs, who, as he described, only understand the language of force. His opponent, who had ordered the rocket and artillery attack against Lebanese civilians taking refuge in a U.N. shelter in Qana that resulted in the massacre of 108 women and children, was mocked and labeled as a pussycat lacking the courage and the determination to strike back. Bibi had made a solemn pledge that, if elected, he would exterminate Hezbollah "terrorists" and their supporters, and guarantee the safety of "Israeli" occupational troops against attacks carried out by Muslim "savages". His constituents were made to believe that the military service in Lebanon would become an enjoyable vacation".

"Bibi really tried hard but fortunately or unfortunately, things have not gone the way he planned or wished! He attempted to do whatever he believed to be "right"! He subjected defenseless Lebanese towns and villages in the south to an average of one retaliatory "Israeli" air raid and indiscriminate bombing every three days. Two thousand pounds "smart" bombs raining down on Arab homes, schools, hospitals and houses of worship are donated by uncle Sam and cost "Israel" nothing! To his surprise, "Israeli" troops equipped with the most sophisticated war machinery ever invented by man and supported by the "Israeli" Navy and Air Force have turned out to be no match for Muslim fighters armed with outdated weaponry that they transport on their backs. "Israeli" critics ought to be fair! Why should Bibi be at fault and blamed?"

"It is true that "Israel" inflicted nearly three times as many losses on Hezbollah guerrillas as it sustained. However, Hezbollah men are quite happy and satisfied. No one ever heard them complain. These "fanatic" young men are convinced that that is a small price to pay for freedom. What troubles Bibi the most is the fact that, unlike their "Israeli" adversaries who were pictured crying to return home and be sheltered by their moms, these "strange" Muslim fighters seek martyrdom. They yearn for a better life after death, where, as they expect, will be lasting peace and tranquility."

"Bibi is actually no fool but realistic. He acknowledges that the presence of "Israeli" troops in Lebanon has been a mistake, and he is willing to disengage as fast as Hezbollah will allow. Regardless of his rhetoric, which is designed for public consumption, Bibi is prepared to tuck his tail between his legs and crawl out with head down and hands up as long as Lebanon ensures the security of northern "Israel". A recent opinion poll found support for a unilateral withdrawal had more than doubled over the past eight months to 40 percent. Even "Israeli" Foreign Minister Ariel Sharon, a security hawk who became a national "hero" after he spearheaded "Israel's" 1982 invasion of Lebanon and ordered the massacre of thousands of Palestinian men, women and children in Sabra and Shatela refugee camps in Beirut, has now become one of the strongest advocates of a one-sided pullback".

"Southern Lebanon has become a deathtrap and Bibi wants a way out. Hunting "Israeli" soldiers like rabbits has become Hezbollah fighters' favorite game. What alarms Bibi is the fact that the technical and executive capabilities of the young members of the Hezbollah resistance fighters in combat have reached a point where they can overcome "Israeli" military and technological superiority and preparedness".

"Lebanon's new president, former army commander General Emile Lahoud, had assured Bibi that the "Israeli" leader would not be "disappointed". Lebanon understands Bibi's dilemma and is always ready to "cooperate". Mr. Lahoud stated quite clearly and specifically at his inauguration that an "Israeli" withdrawal from Lebanon must also be accompanied by a withdrawal from the occupied Syrian Golan Heights"

"It is doubtful that Bibi gained a useful lesson from the Lebanon question! It is unrealistic to assume that Bibi is prepared to accept the fact that Syria and Lebanon are part of one Arab homeland and that the Syrians and Lebanese belong to one Arab nation, which, regardless of the setbacks it experienced, will eventually overcome. It is naïve to allege that Bibi will admit that the holy olive trees he uprooted in occupied Arab land are being replaced by new, young and healthy trees that are growing strong and will eventually give a blessed oil in abundance!"

"Certainly however, Bibi and his people ought to know that, when a people insist on the liberation of their lands from foreign invaders, they actually affirm their unquestionable birth given rights. They ought to comprehend that Arabs and Muslims are not asking Bibi or anyone else for a "favor"!"
 
  

By Ali Baghdadi  
(Arab Journal, Chicago)

Yearender: Hizbullah attacks force Israel to rethink Lebanon strategy (1)
During 1998 Hezbollah resistance guerrillas launched a growing number of attacks against Israeli troops and their allied militia in southern Lebanon, putting unprecedented pressure on the government of Benjamin Netanyahu to withdraw. Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, the Hezbollah secretary general, said his group's resistance activities inside the occupied border zone reached up to 1,200 operations during 1998. Independent sources confirmed that Hezbollah launched an average of three attacks a day during 1998. Nasrallah claims some 90 Israeli soldiers were killed and more than 200 injured while 37 Hezbollah guerrillas were killed in the operations. However, Israel has confirmed the death of only 23 soldiers and said 105 others were wounded in various operations carried out by Hizbullah. Nassrallah said the pressure exerted by Hezbollah attacks had helped oust the Labor government of Shimon Peres. "And this year we hope to contribute to the fall of the government of Benjamin Netanyahu," said one Hezbollah source. The Israeli government currently favors a withdrawal from southern Lebanon in return for security guarantees. Netanyahu says he will not end Israels 20-year occupation of southern Lebanon without guarantees from the Lebanese government for the security of its allied militia as well as its northern territory.

But Lebanon and Syria have rejected the demand and said Israel should withdraw unconditionally from southern Lebanon. They say pullback should be in accordance with United Nations Security Council resolution 425, which calls for an immediate and unconditional withdrawal. Over the last year there have been repeated calls in Israel for a withdrawal from southern Lebanon with different scenarios being leaked to the press. But the Netanyahu government remains reluctant to withdraw. "The Israelis are stuck in a quagmire ... Either they leave without conditions or we will keep killing them," said one Hezbollah source. "We have nothing to lose ... Our guerrillas have vowed to die for the liberation of our land ... It is up to the Israelis to decide whether they want to stay and die or leave," the sources said. A UN spokesman in southern Lebanon told the daily As Safir that "the most important change in the year 1998 is that the discussions inside Israel regarding a withdrawal from southern Lebanon are not only concentrating on the issue of withdrawal but it is also focusing on ways to implement it". The intensity of the Hezbollah attacks against Israeli troops and the militia inside the occupation zone have prompted both forces to make drastic changes in their tactics. On December 28 the pro-Israeli militia announced a redeployment of its forces in south Lebanon, which Lebanese officials said was prompted by a "rebellion in its ranks". "Their rebellion was prompted by mounting casualties in their ranks in recent weeks," the officials said. Lebanese security sources insisted that the militia's evacuation of two outposts in Mashnaqa and Annan followed the refusal of around 30 militiamen to continue to garrison the positions if they were not reinforced. The militia has already pulled out this year from one strategic position in Toumat Niha, which is located to the south of the Christian town of Jezzine. In addition, two months ago, Raja Ward, a militia intelligence officer handed himself over to the Lebanese authorities. His defection led to the arrest of a Lebanese network working for the Israeli intelligence in eastern and southern Lebanon. The big question remains, will 1999 brings about an end to the 20- year Israeli occupation in south Lebanon?
