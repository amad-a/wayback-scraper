Between Diplomacy and Resistance
 
 
Ambassador
Fouad Al-Turk
 

 Who said the resistance may be only by weapon?

Who said it is by men only? (Although they are our immortal martyrs).

It is above all an act of faith with all its rites and obligations.

It is a right word and an open-minded culture to reach to every human conscience.

It is a unity of the people with all its sectors, parties and sects. The people who backs, shares with and sacrifices for the sake of resistance.

It is something of mobilizing its society for the practice and theory to be as one.

It is the way to achieve the final goal? Liberating every inch of the Lebanese territories from the Israeli occupation forces.

The act of resistance includes all these elements. Also, it is something cannot be defined and restricted by works of one lecture or symposium. For, how can we siege a giant inside a very tiny box.

There are other aspects of the resistance action Aspects that did not put into practice until now and have an effectiveness more than that it can be obtained by the act of an armed resistance.
So, each of us can be a fighter from his own position:

One can resist by pushing his sons to sacrifice by their souls, saying the right word which provokes the people to wake up from its dogmatic lethargy and deepens the national conscienceness, paying money as a contribution, offering the advantages of his own career and job, praying to God so as to back up the right and destroy the wrong, exploiting his own position in order to lobby the policy-makers, giving aids and healing wounds by physicians and nurses at the battlefields and  in the back lines, making laws for the purpose that the right and justice have the upper hand over the wrong and injustice, following the ways of diplomacy which is the central point of this speech.
Dear friends,

The diplomatic resistance should be taken over by the competent officials either directly by making connections, meetings and negotiations or indirectly by the Lebanese diplomatic missions and consulates estimated one hundred these days. Liberating the occupied land is a pivotal point and has a primacy.

Israel is already occupying a part of our national land in South Lebanon and Western Beqaa. However, the international and regional organizations had condemned this occupation and passed a lot of resolutions that call on maintaining Lebanons sovereignty and independence. Also, the United Nations had, previously, confessed through its several resolutions with the right to resist the occupation forces and restore the territories. Furthermore, the whole world had acknowledged with our right and known who is the aggressor. Nevertheless, the right of power still has the upper hand over the power of right.

The role of diplomacy is to work heavily, from within the stated international regulations, mores and conventions, with the governments and states that have diplomatic missions in our country in addition to making necessary contacts and negotiations in order to persuade those unconvinced countries, yet, with our sacred right.

Also, the diplomacy actions represent in dealing with the peoples through:

    1 - Lectures, festivals and speeches.
    2 - Distributing the documents and publications.
    3 - TV films.
    4 - Press and intellectual conferences and symposiums.
    5 - Mobilizing the Lebanese Diaspora to be reunified and tied with Lebanon on one hand, and to form a link between Lebanon and the countries in which they live on the other hand. So, it is very necessary to make use of their great political, economical, information and cultural positions and capabilities for the sake of Lebanon and its great causes: liberating the occupied lands in South and Western Beqaa.
    6 - Establishing a friendship associations with Lebanon.
    In this connection, it is worth noting that enhancing the Lebanese embassies abroad with qualified staff is very essential for these embassies to perform its duties.
But the government had adopted an unuseful spreading policy, for it set up more than fifteen new embassies instead of supporting those found in the effective countries which still have no information, economic or cultural attache`, but also without a complete administrative personnel, necessary logistic equipments or computers. It is worth mentioning that these requirements have a significant role in obtaining information and preparing files necessary for diplomatic communications, addressing the local public opinion and thus for getting its support.

So, the coordination between the ministries of foreign affairs, expatriates and information is inevitable.

Dear friends,

Yes, the diplomacy has central and substantial role concerning the liberation of the land and rescuing the people from the yoke of occupation forces.

The right is clear and the goal is noble and thus, our cause is fair and defendable. All what we need are the ways and means. So, let us work together as a one hand, either within our country or abroad, to achieve the high national goal.

We are confronting an enemy worldly-wise in war and maneuvering affairs and well-informed about the prevarications and equivocations of the negotiations.

So, it goes without saying that the international community had been well acquainted concerning the diplomatic channels and ways adopted by the enemy as a pretext to perform its political and military goals.

Therefore, the diplomacy for the enemy is a main element of its projects and plans. And so must be for us. While exploiting the military means, the resistance has to make use of the diplomacy which must be a principal component of its activities.

So, let us take it as a sharp sword. For a diplomatic action taken in a negotiation process may save a life of a fighter entrenched at the frontline facing the enemy.

Only by the resistance, in all its forms, we can liberate our homeland Lebanon..
