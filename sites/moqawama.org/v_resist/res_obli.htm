Resistance is a Legitimate and National Obligation
 
A Speech by Ambassador
Halim Abu-Ezziddin
 
In the name of God most gracious most merciful.

Members of the Committee for the Resistance Support, Mr. President of Mufti Hassan Khaled Association, friends and colleagues the ambassadors, ladies and gentleman,
 I was a young junior student at University. In those days when we say University we meant the American University of Beirut (AUB) because there were only two universities, this one, and the University of St. Joseph known as the Jesuits. Despite my young age I attended a lecture for a well known American professor and an orientalist who lived in Lebanon. This professor was even born in Lebanon, under the name of William Van Dyck, his father who also lived in Lebanon founded the Faculty of Medicine at the AUB, he set standards in the Arabic language and wrote poems. His father also had national opinions in this country. I heard professor Van Dyck say things difficult for me to understand at that time, due to my young age, he stood on the stage at West Hall and repeated a holy speech: " Whom so ever witnesses a misdeed should correct it with his hands, if he is not able, then with his mouth, if he is not able, then with his heart, and that is the least that one can do". That speech was too hard for the young man to understand at that time, but those wise words stayed in his mind until he grabbed their meaning with age, for resistance is the most important thing in life. Resistance is religion, it is faith, resistance is sacrifice, it is putting things in their right place. All religions called for resistance and the prophet Mohammed  said so too. Jesus before him talked about it, the whole of Christianity is based on sacrifice. The Christians call Jesus  Al- Fadi (the one who sacrifices). Therefore, to sacrifice oneself is the essence of faith.

Resistance gentlemen, is self defense of  dignity, of values, of humanity and land. Laws in all countries of the World recognizes and supports the presence of resistance. What is self defense? Self defense is resistance. It is resistance against the aggressor. In Lebanese law for example, article number 513 regarding the law of punishment states that: The following acts are considered self defense:

    1-The act of defending ones home or money or other people or their wealth against those who use violence.
    2-An act committed against someone entered or tries to enter at night into ones home is a legitimate self defense act in the Lebanese law.
In the UN, section 9 of article number 51 of chapter 7 states: There is nothing in this convention which weakens or deprives the natural right of countries, groups or solitary actions in their self defense against a military aggression on a member of the UN, until the Security Council takes the appropriate steps to settle international peace and security. The 8th article of the Convention on Common Defense and Cooperation between Arab Countries states that: The countries consider any assault on any one country or several ones or its forces an assault on them all, therefore, these countries according to the legitimacy of self defense of its entity separately or in groups will give themselves the right to defend themselves  against the aggressing country in all the means they consider right including the use of military force in order to implement article number 6 of the Arab League and article number 51 of the UN . Therefore the Lebanese, Arab and the UN laws all encourage self defense actions. Resistance Is self defense.

Diplomacy comes in second place. In the holy speech resistance is the arm and diplomacy is the tongue. Diplomacy is there to defend people but according to Resistance, diplomacy is only present when the Resistance ceases to act or when signals of sincere peaceful steps come into action. In all cases Resistance remains the basis for defending ones country and preserving its dignity.

President Assad stated in one of his famous speeches three years ago that the Palestinian Fidaiin are the most honorable persons on earth. This statement is a fact because a Fidaii offers himself for the defense of his land and does not rely only on speeches. As we have mentioned Christianity is based on sacrifice. Jesus came down to earth to sacrifice himself for human sins. The Arab prophet also preached sacrifice and to use the hand before the tongue.

The resistance is doing the job of countries specially the Lebanese resistance. The Lebanese resistance in the war front has taken up the role of the government, the army and of the Lebanese people. We all dear brothers and sisters owe a lot to the men of the resistance who sacrifice themselves for the sake of the existence of this country.

My dear diplomatic colleagues they will talk to you about diplomacy regarding this matter, no, but this is a critical stage which is followed by diplomacy. The United States diplomacy controls the world, why? Because it the strongest country in the world. So diplomacy depends on strength, why? The word of the US is listened to more than the Lebanese word because the US is a strong country which is able to implement its policies with the use of force we have witnessed what has happened to Iraq lately. Then strength and resistance are the basis where as diplomacy comes in to build this basis.

For this I as a diplomat bow my head to the resistance and I say that the resistance is the basis while diplomacy is only the branch. Resistance is the arm, and diplomacy is the tongue. Assalamu alaikum
