January Saturday 01 -2000:

HIZBULLAH SAYS IT WILL KEEP ATTACKING "ISRAEL" DESPITE NEGOTIATIONS(1)
"Lebanon's Hizbullah movement warned in remarks published Friday that it will keep attacking "Israeli" troops in south Lebanon. "Hizbullah operations will not stop before or after the negotiations", set to continue Monday in the United States, Hizbullah press officer Muaffaq Jamal said, quoted by the press. The attacks on the "Israelis" "aren't linked to specific political developments but are part of a policy of hostility toward the "Israeli" enemy which will continue until it withdraws completely from our territory," he said. The daily As-Safir said Friday that the car bombing was "a message from the resistance to "Israel" saying that the military operations will continue despite that country's pressure for them to stop." The An-Nahar newspaper said Hizbullah planned operations during the negotiations, while Ad-Diyar said "the attack confirms the resistance's intentions not to respect any truce during the talks."

Metropolitan Hashem commends Resistance, Sheikh Yazbek calls for adhering to Christ's teachings(2)
"On the occasion of the birth of the Prophet of Mercy and the Apostle of Humanity Jesus the son of Mary (A. S.), Hizbullah and the Municipality of Iyat held a "Friendly Meeting" at the St. George Church in the town.

"The celebration was attended by Imam Khamenei's Representative in Lebanon his Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek, Dair al-Ahmar Maronite Pastor Metropolitan Monjed al-Hashem, Municipal Council members and residents.

"Sheikh Yazbek stressed the need for adhering to the teachings of Master Christ (A. S.) that are stated in the Holy Quran and the Holy Gospel.

"The Lebanese society has turned into a united brotherly resistance society; national melting has been achieved among its sons because this society has abided by these teachings that were sent to all humankind without discrimination, Yazbek said.

"The Islamic Resistance men have defeated the ("Israeli") enemy, which is usurping our soil and rights, with the teachings of the Holy Prophet Mohammed (A. S.) and the commandments of Prophet Jesus (A. S.), for both stood against injustice and assisted the oppressed, he added.

In turn, Metropolitan Hashem blessed the meeting "which shows the deep sincere brotherly relations between the Christians and their brother Moslems, for these ties are based on love, tolerance and coexistence, especially in the Baalback-Hirmel area."

Al-Hashem commended the role of the Resistance in the south and the western Beqaa.

The Resistance has made Lebanon proud at international and regional forums, he said, wishing that "next year will see Lebanon liberated from the "Israeli" occupation and recovering its role with the united efforts of its faithful sons."

January Monday 03 -2000:

Nasrallah: "Israeli" will leave our land humiliated(1)
Hizbullah named the martyr as Ammar Hussein Hammoud, 20, from Debbine in the occupation zone.
In a speech to mark Jerusalem Day, Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah vowed that the "Israelis" would have a bloody retreat from the south, with more martyrdom attacks planned.

Jerusalem Day, which was initiated by the late Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini, was held on Hadi Nasrallah Avenue in Beiruts southern suburbs.

The avenue was named after the Hizbullah chiefs eldest son, who was killed in a resistance attack in September 1997.

Addressing thousands of supporters, Nasrallah advised the faithful to: be patient.

Resist, he said. You will see that the destiny of the "Israelis" in Lebanon is to leave our land humiliated, in a rout, under fire, covered with bits of flesh from our martyrs like Ammar and his brothers who will, God willing, follow him.

He said that Hammoud was avenging the killing of one of his five brothers a year ago in a clash with "Israeli" troops.

Hizbullah claimed that seven "Israeli" soldiers were killed when Hammouds car exploded on the edge of Qlayaa and denied that Lebanese civilians had been wounded.

Regarding the Middle East peace talks, which will be resumed Monday on the Syrian track, Nasrallah said that "Israel" would always be the enemy, even if it makes peace with Syria and Lebanon.

Were saying it loudly: we will never recognize the existence of a state, a village or a hamlet calling itself "Israel" even if all the Arabs recognize it, Nasrallah stressed ahead of "Israeli"-Syrian talks on Monday.

Concerning the peace process in general and whatever its results, we believe that a just peace cannot be reached in the region because from the first, the basis of it has been unjust, since it gives part of Palestine to the Jewish usurpers, he said.

If Lebanon and Syria recover their land one day, it will be thanks to their political resistance. But no compromise can be just that gives the land of Palestine to the Zionists, loses Jerusalem and disperses the Palestinian people, he added.

The Egyptian and Jordanian people still consider "Israel" an enemy in spite of their peace accords, he said. The circumstances that imposed the peace process are not eternal and sooner or later these circumstances will change.

Referring to an "Israeli" gesture last week in freeing five jailed Hizbullah fighters and allowing the group to recover the bodies of seven more from southern Lebanon, Nasrallah said that political and humanitarian affairs must not be confused.

January Wednesday 19 -2000:

Sraireh residents thank state and resistance, call for new communications networks(1)
Sraireh residents expressed their joy at the removal of the Israeli occupation off their hometown.

In a statement distributed in Beirut, the town's residents said, "The Sraireh residents who have just come out of the sufferings created by the occupation that had perched on their chests for over 17 years are glad to hail the bloods of the martyrs that have watered the hilltops of Sraireh from the Litani River gorge till Mount Abu Rashed."

The communiqué thanked the Lebanese army, President Emile Lahoud, Speaker Nabih Berri, PM Dr Salim al-Hoss and the National and Islamic Resistance, on top of which Hezbollah "whose strikes succeeded in thwarting the enemy and its agents and forcing them to retreat."

They requested that the government execute a series of infrastructure projects in their newly liberated village asking for new roads and electricity and telephone networks so they can be reconnected to other parts of the country.

"We also hope that the army and other security forces will open the Sraireh-Mashghara road, which connects Jezzine to the western Beqaa," the residents said.

Sraireh, a small village that lies in the northern sector of the Israeli-occupied zone in the south, was liberated when the Israeli occupation's proxy militia abandoned its positions in Kfar Houna last week.

Some of the residents remained steadfast in the town in spite of the shelling, displacement and intimidation practiced by the Israeli occupation forces and its proxy militia.

January Saturday 22 -2000:

Nasrallah vows to fight normalization(1)
Hizbullah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah vowed on Friday to oppose the normalization of relations between "Israel" and Arab countries and called on the Palestinians to wage war against the Jewish state.
We have to make our nation know that normalization is the gate to Zionist economic, political, cultural and military hegemony over the whole region, he said.
Fighting normalization is essential to warding off the new wave of Zionist aggression, he added during the inauguration of the Third Islamic National Conference at the Carlton Hotel in Beirut.
He called on the Palestinians not to abandon the armed struggle.
The liberation of Palestine is the central goal of Arabs and Muslims, Nasrallah said,even if there is a settlement between Yasser Arafat and the "Israelis".
