September Tuesday 05 -2000:


Israel revisits claims of super Katyusha(1)

An Israeli general claimed on Monday that Hizbullah has long-range rockets capable of hitting the coastal city of Haifa and suggested that retaliation for any cross-border attack from Lebanon would extend to Syrian forces.
Fired from the border, rockets with a range of 100 kilometers could hit several main Israeli cities, including Haifa, Tiberias, Netanya and Herzliya, which is just north of Tel Aviv.
Even without launching a rocket attack, guerrillas could throw hand grenades at the yellow buses carrying Israeli schoolchildren or fire rifles at Israeli homes, he added.
They have the capability of starting an attack in a matter of seconds, Ashkenazi said.
The Israeli general warned that if the guerrillas attack, Israel would hit back not only at those who pull the trigger.
We will deal also with those who allow it, he said, a clear reference to Syria, which supported resistance efforts to oust Israel from its occupation zone in the South.
Since at least 1996, Israel has regularly claimed that Hizbullah is equipped with long-range rockets. Initially, the Israeli Army said that the resistance group possessed longer-range versions of the 122mm Katyusha rocket presently in Hizbullahs armory. The larger version travels twice the distance of the standard rocket, the Israeli Army says.
Since the beginning of the year, Israel has been claiming that Hizbullah has acquired Iranian Fajar rockets with a range of at least 70 kilometers.
Ashkenazi complained that Hizbullah was behind the daily bouts of stone-throwing across the border, particularly at the Fatima Gate in Kfar Kila.
They bring stones to the border in trucks and children to the border in buses to throw the stones, he claimed.
He condemned UNIFIL for failing to stop the incidents of stone-throwing, arguing that the peacekeepers mandate requires the force to maintain calm along the frontier.
Though his soldiers are under orders to show restraint, he said, he sees this as a temporary situation, not to be tolerated for long.
Ashkenazi also speculated that Hizbullah might launch attacks across the border if a large-scale confrontation between Israel and the Palestinians broke out.
As head of Israels northern military command, Ashkenazi played an important role in the occupation of south Lebanon and was responsible for overseeing the troop withdrawal.
According to Israeli press reports, senior officers in the northern command and the Israeli Armys general staff were furious at Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Baraks decision to unilaterally withdraw from south Lebanon. ­

September Monday 11 -2000:

Jezzine village pays tribute to soldier killed by Israelis(1)

A memorial commemorating an army captain killed by Israeli forces three years ago was unveiled in the village of Mashmoushe, Jezzine on Sunday.
The Mashmoushe Municipality organized the ceremony, which saw more than 300 people in attendance, including Defense Minister Ghazi Zeaiter on behalf of President Emile Lahoud, Labor Minister Michel Musa, a native of the town, and a host of officials and local dignitaries.
The South wouldnt have been liberated if it werent for heroes like you in the army and the honorable resistance, Colonel Hassan Ayoub read in his eulogy of Jawad Azar, who was 28 at the time of his death.
Azar and five other soldiers in his unit were killed on Sept. 2, 1997, when Israeli helicopters opened fire on their armored personnel carrier in Arab Salim, Iqlim al-Touffah.
The unit was firing back at the helicopters, which were sweeping the area hours after Hizbullah leader Sayyed Hassan Nasrallahs son Hadi was killed along with other resistance fighters during an operation in Rihan.
Zeaiter described Azar as an example for every Lebanese soldier and young man, adding that the army and the resistance have always been together on the front line. The army will remain a guarantee for the liberation, security and stability, and will face any conflict of security that may occur under any pretext or slogan, he said.
Zeaiter added that the army would not tolerate any threats against peoples security or stability and would continue to safeguard the liberation. He also said Israels withdrawal was not proof of peace, which he said required recovering the Golan Heights and a just solution for Palestinian refugees.
Azars father Fawzi said his son and others who were killed that night taught everyone a lesson in bravery and martyrdom.
The officials then unveiled the memorial, a bronze statue of Azar standing with a pistol in his hand. The 2.5-meter high statue was made by artist Andre Nammour at a cost of $15,000, which was donated by Mashmoushes residents.

September Friday 11 -2000:

Hizbullah: our arms are aimed at occupation, not to be used domestically(1)

Resistance is the only solution for Palestine, al-Quds (Jerusalem), all the occupied Arab territories, for the Shebaa Farms, Lebanon, the prisoners and for every confrontation with the Zionist enemy, Hizbullah Deputy Secretary general his Eminence Sheikh Nahim Qassem said on Wednesday.

When we talk about resistance in Lebanon, we mean the symbol for any act that leads to recovering rights, he told thousands of supporters during a Hizbullah rally at the party's Shura Square to mark the anniversary of the September 13 massacre martyrs.

"Resistance is to say no, to prepare yourself and build military capabilities; Resistance is not to give up one grain of sand, it is not to sign a surrender deed, it is to abandon all options of concession and surrender and be patient when it comes to international influence."

The rally was attended by Hizbullah's Faithfulness to the Resistance bloc members, party leaders, political, religious and social figures besides the martyrs' families.

We say to our people in Palestine: rights cannot be recovered and the Israeli project cannot be confronted with means other than the armed resistance that aims to liberate all the land, Sheikh Qassem said.

Regarding the September 13 incident, his Eminence said, "They wanted to bring down Hizbullah in the Lebanese interior by dragging it into an unrest with the Lebanese army to put an end to the Resistance through changing the direction of our compass and altering the target of our rifle away from liberating the land."

"That's why we said the Resistance's arms aim at the occupation; these are not weapons to be used domestically, to settle accounts or compete with anyone."

"Our arms aim at the occupation, even if we suffer or feel the pain, even if some acted with us improperly. We have been strong with our patient. Had one bullet been shot, we would have been weak and these achievements would not have been made," he said.

Nine Hizbullah supporters, men and women, were martyred on September 13, 1993 when Lebanese troops fired at the demonstration they were staging to protest the signing of the Oslo accord between Yasser Arafat and the Israeli occupation's authorities.

"Achievements were not made on May 24, 2000, they built up with many sacrifices since 1982, and the September 13 martyrs were partners in this outcome," Sheikh Qassem concluded.

September Wednesday 20 -2000:

Nasrallah warns against sectarianism

Resistance will continue as long as Israel occupies territories and hols hostages(1)

Hizbullahs secretary-general warned against the dangers of further fueling sectarianism in on Tuesday, noting how quickly the Lebanese have forgotten them in the past.
We not only forget but also lie to ourselves, repeatedly saying that we suffered from the war of others on our land Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah said. Why do we all of a sudden want to move backward?
The Hizbullah leader was addressing hundreds of women at UNESCO Palace who were celebrating the birthday of Fatima Al-Zahra, the daughter of the Prophet Mohammed, which is also the day Iranian Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini deemed Womens Day.
Nasrallah said the country was in dire need of people ready to work for the benefit of their fellow citizens, whether Muslim or Christian.
The fight for a stronger national identity is no less dangerous than the resistance struggle against the enemy, he declared. Its even harder.
He also criticized claims that the resistance no longer has a role in the wake of the Israeli withdrawal in May.
Do you want to tell me that if Israeli soldiers attacked, there would be an order for the Lebanese Army to respond? he asked. No. Having the army at the border is only for the protection of the enemy.
Nasrallah reiterated the partys position that the resistance will continue as long as Israel occupies Lebanese territory like the Shebaa Farms and continues to hold Lebanese detainees.
I also tell you that the detainees will not be freed through negotiations, he said. The enemy has no understanding of humanity.

Israeli Army: Hezbollah may renew hostilities against Israel(2)

Military Intelligence chief Major General Amos Malka warned yesterday that Hezbollah may resume hostilities against Israel, including attacks and attempts to kidnap Israeli soldiers and civilians along the Lebanese border.
Speaking at a session of the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense (War) Committee, Malka said the organization was disappointed by the results of the recent Lebanese elections, in which the position of President Emile Lahoud was weakened. He said Hezbollah officials had begun speaking publicly of renewing operations against Israel.
"We are witness to worsening rhetoric against Israel from Hezbollah leaders," Malka said.
In Malka's view, Hezbollah may resume hostile operations against Israel in the Har Dov area. Hezbollah leaders maintain that the dispute over marking the international border around Har Dov has not been resolved, and that Israel is still occupying Lebanese soil in the area.
From Hezbollah's standpoint, launching attacks and firing on Israelis near Har Dov would be relatively easy, he continued, noting that the area was military in character and that the border dispute lent Hezbollah "legitimization" for hostile acts against Israeli Army soldiers.
"The escalation in the remarks of the organization's leaders also expresses their desire to free Mustafa Dirani and Sheikh Obeid from prison," giving rise to concerns that Hezbollah will try to kidnap Israeli soldiers or civilians for use as bargaining chips to press for the jailed militants' freedom.
He said Palestinian groups in the territories, such as Hamas and the Islamic Jihad, are also threatening to abduct troops to bargain for the release of security prisoners. There are indications that kidnap attempts could be carried out during the holiday period.
Malka also said that despite taking heavy blows over the past two years from broad-based Shin Bet preventative operations, Hamas still has activists in the field.

Hizbullah: position on coming cabinet depends on program(1)

Hizbullah Shura Council member, Representative of Imam Khamenei in Lebanon, his Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Yazbek stressed that the party's position on the coming cabinet depends on its political program, especially regarding the resolution of the dispossession problem in Baalbek-Hermel, taking interest in the recently-liberated areas, following up the issue of the Resistance against the Israeli occupation which still occupies a dear part of our land, and tackling the issue of the detainees in the enemy's prisons.

Speaking at a celebration to mark the birthday of Lady Fatima al-Zahraa (A.S.) in Imam Khomenei's Husseineit in Baalbek, his Eminence warned those who label the Resistance as a "militia" against falling into the trap set by the Zionist enemy which demands that the Resistance be disarmed, "for the resistance's weapons keep the enemy in constant concern."
