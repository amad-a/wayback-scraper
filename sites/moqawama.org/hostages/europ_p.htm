The European Parliaments Resolution
Regarding The Detainee Suha Bishara
 

The resolution concerning the detention of Suha Bishara in the South of Lebanon.

The European Parlaiment after taking into consideration the resolution which was passed on 25/5/1989 for the release of Suha Bishara who was detained in the South of Lebanon.

And based on article no. five of the Geneva convention regarding the rights of civilians under foreign occupation:

    A - Where as on the 7 Nov 1989 the Lebanese student Suha Bishara was detained at the age of 21 years by the Southern Lebanese Army, after trying to assassinate the commander of the said Army.

    B - And with Suha Bishara taken to "Israel" for questioning before moving her to Al-Khiam prison.

    C - 9 years have passed on her detention without a trail or an accusation, and without enabling her to be assisted by lawyers.

    D - And refusing the International Red Cross from entering Al-Khiam prison since 10th of Sept.1997.

    E - And various measures were taken by a large number of human organizations, such as, The Federal International for human Rights - Amnesty International - The International Organization Against Torture - The International Democratic Lawyers Union for the sake of freeing Suha Bishara.
    Therefore:
     

      1 - Condemns the detention of Suha Bishara who has been detained since 1988, and calls a for at least to allow her to have a fair trail based on International Laws and freeing her.

      2 - Allowing her lawyers and family to visit her.

      3 - Ask the International Red Cross committee to enter Al-Khiam camp.

      4 - Ask the Parlaiment to take political and Diplomatic steps to enforce the resolution.

      5 - Ask for the resolution to be submitted to the President of the Parlaiment and to the country members and the Israeli Government.
       

      23/10/1997
