Israel prolongs detention of Hizbullah leaders
 
An Israeli court decided on Monday to extend the detention by six months of two leaders of the Lebanese Hizbullah resistance movement held for years in Israeli jails. The court made the ruling against Sheikh Abdel Karim Obeid and Mustapha Dirani on the recommendations of Defense Minister Yitzhak Mordechai, judicial officials said. Obeid was kidnapped by Israeli commandos in 1989 while Dirani was seized in a similar operation in 1994. About 150 Lebanese have been held for several years without charge or trial in prisons in Israel and its occupied zone in south Lebanon. Hizbullah spearheads resistance attacks against Israel and the latter's allied militia aimed at ousting it from a zone they occupy in southern Lebanon.
