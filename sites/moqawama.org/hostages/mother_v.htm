Mothers Voice Frustrations
at Second Sit-In
 

Frustration with the government, the media and support groups erupted into bitter reproach yesterday at a demonstration to publicize the plight of detainee to Israeli prisons.

At the second weekly Mothers Thursday the wife of detainees Nazih Mustafa, 33 , complain about the governments lack of concern while another woman expressed doubt the media would cover the event in detail.

Using Israels campaign to find missing airman Ron Arad as a yardstick, Sahar Zeaiter accused the government of not making enough effort to secure the release of detainees.
Since our officials do not have detained sons or relatives, they simply dont care about dedicating some of their time and effort to save our men who suffer daily from Israeli cruelty and Israeli treatment, she said.

Zeaiter said Israel succeeded in gaining international attention and sympathy when one of its soldiers was captured . When Ron Arad went missing or when some soldiers remains were held with their enemy, the whole world felt sorry for Israel, our eternal aggressor. But our husbands and brothers are held in Israels horror camps and not one Lebanese official bothers to demand their release, she said.

Calling upon officials to take action, Zeaiter vowed that the Mothers Thursday would knock on each and every officials door, and if they do not respond to our demands we will force them to.

Expressing the frustration of trying to gain support, a woman attempting to calm Zeaiter told her that the media would probably not cover the event anyway.

Safa called upon those attending to arrive in large numbers at next weeks sit-in, because Che` Guevaras son, Camilo, had been officially invited and is expected to attend.
 

 
The Daily Star
 17/10/1997
