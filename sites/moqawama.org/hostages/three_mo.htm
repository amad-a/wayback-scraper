List of Detainees During Three Months
 

The National Association of Detainees in "Israeli" Prisons issued a list of the civilians taken by the "Israeli" occupation forces to unknown destination. It announced that it sent out the concerned list to Amnesty International and Human rights Watchdog and its representatives in several European and American capitals.

The list included the following detainees:

    - On 2/10/1997: Kassem Latif Assaf, Mohammad Salim Qatbay, Abbas Mohammad Atieh and Mustafa Jawad Tawbeh and his son, 14.
    - On 10/10/1997: Taisir Dabajah, Naimeh Faisal Jaber, Khaled Idriss, Bassam Ali Hamadeh, Bilal Ibrahim Ashqar and Bilal Kayed Farah (Bent Jbeil).
    - On 13/10/1997: Zeinab Nasser (Bent Jbeil).
    - On 14/10/1997: Mazen Abdallah, 13, (Bent Jbeil).
    - On 25/11/1997: Almaza Ibrahim (Odaisseh) and Younes Chalhoub (Qilyah).
    - On 9/12/1997: Abdel-Nasser Skaineh (Blat), Ibrahim Iskandar, Bouloss Iskandar and Ivon Soweidi (Marjeyoun).
On 25/12/1997: Marwan Kenaan, his wife Hoda Markeiz and their babies, Nassar Hussein Nassar, Khalil El-Hachem and Mohammad El-Hachem (Shebaa).
 
As-Safir
