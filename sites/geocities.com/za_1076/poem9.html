Today's The Day
 By: Manal

Today's the day where everything
between you and I may change.
We've been planning for this for a little while
But everything will change.

I may not know what you look like
Or how you go about your day
But I do know you're a good person
Just by the things they say.

When I do meet you my dear
I won't know what to say
I'll turn red of shyness
But I know you'll look my way

I'll look at your face, your eyes,
Your presence, and everything you possess
I've only imagined what you may look like
Now my imagination can rest.

You'll tell me, nice to meet you
I will say the same
We'll immediately start speaking
And I'll be glad you came

True, I heard of you months ago
Thinking you're the average guy
To my great surprise I was wrong
About that I can't lie

You shocked me with your sensitivity,
your character, and your sole
You made me realize that I was empty
And you were there to fill that hole

I love your kindness, your soft words,
And your sweet voice
I love the way you think, the way you speak,
You are a great choice

You are a true gentleman In each and every way
You understand where I'm coming from
And always assure me everything will be O.K.

Our parents will speak of marriage
And what kind of husband you can be
They will ask you grueling questions
But you have to be strong, you see....

They'll want personal information
From the present and the past
They ask with good intentions though
For a marriage has to last

You may feel uncomfortable
But it's only for one night
You'll answer the questions confidently
In you they'll see the light.

They'll realize you are a good man
One that can take good care of their daughter
One to love and treat her with great respect
And give Man-made miracles to her

We have so much in common
I hope our relationship can last
We can make happy memories
And everything will move real fast

For if everything goes well,
Our relationship will take a turn
We will move up to a different level
And we'll have plenty of things to learn.

But if things don't turn out well,
You'll always know how I feel
This relationship we had
will be a memorable one
One that was very real.

But don't go being sad
Or upset about the end
You can always find someone else
And your broken heart, She will mend.

It was nice sharing some time with you
Whatever the conclusion may be
I wish you all the happiness life can give you
And remind you, you always had a friend in me.
