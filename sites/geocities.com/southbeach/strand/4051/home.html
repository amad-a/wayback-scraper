Map of Attil and surrounding area

 

Attil is a town in the West Bank (Palestine). It is situated 9km  north of Tulkarm and is about 90 km north of Jerusalem. The total population of Attil is not accurately known, but it is estimated to be around 18.000, 8.000 live in Attil and 10.000 live outside mostly in Jordan. A formal result of a recent census is not yet available.
