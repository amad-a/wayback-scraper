~ * BISM ALLAH AL-RAHMAN AL-RAHIM * ~

_______________________________________

We glorify Allah and believe in Him. All praise is due to Allah, Whose Favours are the sole cause of the accomplishment of all good deeds and blessings and salutations upon Allah's Last Messenger, the Most Noble Messenger, the most exalted of His entire creation, The Holy Prophet Mohammad (s).

The Last Messenger Mohammad (s) said: "I am the most exalted of all the children of Adam but I don't boast of it."

May the blessings of Allah be upon him, his Ahlul-Bayt, his Companions, and his followers until the day of resurrection.

O' Allah! Send down Your Mercy upon our Master Muhammad al-Mustafa (s).

_______________________________________

He is the master of all the Messengers, and the last one of the prophets, intercessor of the Sinners, a Refuge of the Homeless, and he is a Mercy of all the Worlds.

He is Nur (Light) from the Nur (Light) of Allah!

He is Sun of the Arifeen, and is the Light of Guidance for the Saliheen, and he is the Guide of all Jinn and Mankind.

He is our Master, Abu al-Qasim, Muhammad bin Abdullah.

He is the Father in-law of Imam Ali (Allah's Lion)

He is the Father of Fatimah al-Zahra (the best lady).

He is the Grandfather of Hassan and Hussain (the masters of paradise's youths).

_______________________________________

O' Messenger of Allah (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Most exalted by Allah (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Best of all the creations of Allah (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Beloved of Allah (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Imam of all Messengers (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Leader of the prophets! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Last of all the Messengers (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Messenger of the Lord of All the worlds (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' The Apostle of Allah! May salutations be upon you.

O' Muhammad! May salutations be upon you.

O' Ahmad! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-Mustafa! May salutations be upon you.

O' karim (noble)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Nur (light)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Ni'mah (Allah's favor)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Rahmah (mercy)! May salutations be upon you.

O' 'Abd (servant)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-Muzammil (enshrouded)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-Mudaththir (enwrapped)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-Nadhir al-Mubin (the clear warner)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Ra'uf Rahim (kindly and compassionate)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Shahid (witness)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the bringer of good tidings! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Warner (s)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Da'i (summoner)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Mundhir (warner)! May salutations be upon you.

O' 'Abd Allah (God's servant)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Mudhakkir (reminder)! May salutations be upon you.

O' Taha! May salutations be upon you.

O' Yasin! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-Mahi (the effacer)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-Hashir (the gatherer)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-'Aqib (the last to follow)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-khatim (the seal)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-ghayth (the succorer)! May salutations be upon you.

O' al-mutawakkil (he who trusts in Allah)! May salutations be upon you.

O' the Messenger of Allah! May salutations be upon you, and upon your chaste and pious family members.

O' the Messenger of Allah! May salutations be upon you, and upon your faithful wives who are the mothers of the believers.

O' the Messenger of allah! May salutations be upon you and your faithful Companions.

O' the Messenger of Allah! May salutations be upon you and upon all the Prophets and Messengers of Allah and upon all the righteous servants of Allah.

O' the Messenger of Allah! May Allah give you the best reward on your behalf, such reward which was never given to any messenger on behalf of his people. May Allah shower His Blessings upon you whenever people may talk about you and even when negligent people neglect your remembrance. May Allah shower His Blessings upon you among all the ancient people and among all the modern people; such choicest, perfect and pure blessings which never before were showered by Allah upon any of His servants. May Allah shower blessings upon you in the similar way as He guided us from the wrong path and removed our blindness because of your auspiciousness.

_______________________________________

I bear witness that their is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that you are His servant and Messenger, trustworthy and the Best of all His creation. I bear witness that you have conveyed the message of Allah and discharged the duty of the trust; and advised the Ummah (Community) and struggled in the path of Allah with right endeavor.

_______________________________________

Allah! Bestow Your choicest Blessings upon the Holy and Last Messenger beyond the expectations of those who expect for them.
