Never Show Off
What You do For God

One of the major threatening issues which every worshipping man may he confronted with is "showing off" his actions. This would not only invalidate worship but also creates a distance between man and his Creator, instead of bringing them closer. Technically speaking this is a type of hypocricy or religious pretense which refers to the presentation of oneself as a faithful worshipper at prayer in order to elevate one's position within the community, for materialistic reasons. In other words, this type of showing off can be defined as presenting a false pretention in order to make people believe one is religious. Many Qur'anic verses and narrations warn Muslims of the dangerous consequences of showing off. The Qur'an states: So woe to the praying ones' who are unmindful of theft prayers, who do good to be seen. (107:4-6) Muslims themselves must make an indepth study and analyze the points mentioned in these verses. Here we will only briefiy discuss the tone of the verses and their interpretation which clearly indicates that the prayer which has lost its purity by showing it off is not considered a prayer and that such a person is not regarded as a faithful worshipper. Furthermore, such prayer and such praying men are so despicable to God that He states: "So woe to the praying ones."

Prayer is not fulfilled when man pretends to worship God but his mind and heart are occupied with materialistic affairs. A person must he corrupt to use prayer as a means to achieve a position in the people's view and reach his degraded material intentions. These praying men are unaware of the reality of prayer and its spirit which is ascending towards Allah. God mentions them in the Our'an as: "Who are unmindful oftheir prayers." Prayer is making a journey through stages of monotheism and servitude and ascending towards Him. Prayer is breaking relations with all and turning towards Him. Then how could such goals be achieved by showing off one's prayers? In Chapter Kahf, verse 110 God says: "Therefore whoever hopes to meet his Lord, should do good deeds, and not join anyone in the service of his Lord." (18:110) This statement "Therefore who-ever hopes to meet his Lord" is a special interpretation. The statement addresses those who bear the memory of their original homeland and the sacred existence of God and hope to ascend to that point and reach Him. The verse continues to say that these people must conduct good deeds in order to become praise-worthy enough to reach Him and must seek no one else except Him.

To show off is a kind of polytheism in worship. Any other intention in worship besides ascending towards God would create an obstacle in the movement towards Allah and the honest worshipper must be purified from these impurifications.

Various Forms of Show Off Showing off has various forms, some of which are mentioned here:

1. When intention of a worshipper pertains to materialistic intentions instead of approaching Almighty God, and only pretends to be religious in order to achieve some materialistic goals. This is the worst form of showing off. It is obvious that not only the prayer of such a person is invalidated as he has not followed God's teachings nor fulfilled his duties but he has committed a grave sin. The status of such a worshipper is worse than that of one who does not perform prayer at all. For in addition to invalidating his prayer, this worshipper has mocked God and acted hypocritically in front of the people. Each of these particularly the former is considered as a grievous sin.

2. The main purpose of the worshipper is a materialistic goal and pretends to be a religious person in order to reach his worldly goals. Nevertheless he seeks some spiritual aims with his prayers. This type of worshipper probably does not perform his prayers if none is present to see him. The mere ordainance of God does not induce him to perform his prayers. In other words he worships God only when he is seen by someone. This form of hypocricy is similar to the previous one.

3. When one worships God with both intentions: To approach God and obey His commands, and to reach other goals as to pretend to he religious and praised by the people and seeking position within one's community through this means. The two intentions in this case are completely equal, i.e., had he not the intention to approach God he would not have performed the worship merely for worldly inclinations and similarly had he not the worldly inclinations he would not have worshipped God. Although this form of show off is different from the two previously mentioned forms, it also invalidates a person's prayer. This type of worship will not ascend towards God, and such a worshipper has not fulfilled his duty nor obeyed God's commands nor approached God. The Prophet (s) said: "Almighty Allah states I despise the worship and deeds of those who perform it for others and other intentions except Me." I am the most needless of any Partner and I leave all actions for the partner which has been sought besides Me. (Jame-ul-Sa'adat, vol 3, p.370). It is also narrated from the Prophet (s) as saying: 'God does not accept any deed or worship within which showing off is hidden."(Jame-ul-Sa'adaA vol Z p.370). The Prophet (s) also stated that: "The least bit of hypocricy is polytheism."

4. When one worships God in order to approach Him whether people see him or are informed of his worship or not. But since he is entangled with materialistic desires such a worshipper becomes happy when people are informed of his religious deeds. He performs his prayers with more attention towards God and in a higher spiritual state in the presence of people. But when he is alone or when none is present his worship is not performed with the same spiritual fervor. This is also one form of hypocricy. Many faithful men are affected by this form of hypocricy. They must endeavor severly in order to be saved and purified from such hypocricy. Because even if the majority of faithful worshippers worship God in order to approach Him and obey His commands, not for materialistic intentions, they are entangled with ambitiousness and like to be praised. It is very hard to be saved and purified from these wicked ideas. The faithful are not immune from these ideas when they worship. They may worship God with more joy and in a higher spiritual state in the presence of others in order to be praised and loved by the faithful, but they lack that spiritual state when they worship alone. It is obvious that such a spiritual state is not achieved only for Him for if that was the case, it would be so every time of worship particularly when alone. In fact this spiritual state is reached due to one's materialistic desires.

5. When the major intention of one is to approach God and obey His ordainances, but reveals his deeds to the people, either implicitly or explicitly, after the performance of his worship in order to reach worldly goals. This form of show off also creates obstacles in the movement of the faithful towards God and draws one towards ostentation. Muslims must always remember that worship must only be performed for God and should one perform it for other intentions his worship will become invalid and worthless. The philosophy behind worship is to free oneself from all strains and restrictions except God. To show off means the enslavement of these restrictions and contradicts the philosophy of worship. Therefore, if we intend to be real Muslims we must endeavour to save and purify our-selves from all manifestations of hypocricy and ostentation.
