Seven Questions

A man said to Imam Ali (a): I have seven questions to ask you.

The questions and the answers of our beloved Imam were as follows:

Q1.    What is bigger than the universe ?
A1.    Accusation of an innocent person is bigger than the universe

Q2.    What is wider than the earth ?
A2.    Justice is wider than the earth

Q3.    What is hotter than fire ?
A3.    Convetting is hotter than fire

Q4.    What is colder than ice ?
A4.    Anticipating help from a stingy person is colder than ice

Q5.    What is richer tha ocean ?
A5.    The mind of a contended person is richer than the ocean

Q6.    What is harder than rock ?
A6.    The heart of an unbeliever is harder than a rock

Q7.    What is weaker than orphan ?
A7.    The back-biter is weaker than orphan
