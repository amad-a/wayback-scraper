The Biggest Martyr's Wedding
In Silat Daher

Tariq Ismail Al-Hantouli of Silat Daher
became restless with gathering
olives for his family. When two trees
were left to harvest, he turned to his brothers and said, "yallah, who loves me,
will follow me".

The brothers went off to throw stones at the Israeli soldiers and
in a short time, Tariq was seriously
wounded in the head..

There are three roads out of Silat Daher.
One went to the Israeli Settlement, the
other went to Jenin and the third was
a winding road that went through
several villages before it reached
Tol-Karem.  The road to Tol-Karem
was the only choice to get Tarik to
the hospital.

Tariq Ismail Al-Hantouli, age 18, died
at the hospital in Tol-Karem of his head wounds.

Tariq was taken to Silat Daher and  
back to his family and to his home.
He was followed to the tomb by
his village and many others from the
surrounding area. Thousands
came to the Martyr Wedding of
Tariq Ismail Al-Hantouli, who believed
Palestinians should Fight on their
feet rather than die on their knees to
the Israeli occupation of Palestine.

This page is in honor of him.





Most importantly, I want to

the man in my life. This page is
a tribute of my love and devotion to him.

OH MY FATHER, I AM YUSUF

written by Mahmoud Darwish

Oh my father, I am Yusuf
Oh father, my brothers neither love me nor
want me in their midst .

They assault me and cast stones and words
at me. They want me to die so they can
eulogize me. They closed the door of your house
and left me outside.
They expelled me from the field

Oh my father, they poisoned my grapes
They destroyed my toys.
When the gentle wind played with my hair,
they were jealous

They flamed up with rage against me and you
What did I deprive them of, Oh my father?
The butterflies stopped on my shoulder
The bird hovered over my hand
What have I done, Oh my father?
Why me?

You named me Yusuf and they threw me into
the well. They accused the wolf
The wolf is more merciful than my brothers

Oh, my father
Did I wrong anyone when I said that
I saw eleven stars and the sun and the moon
Saw them kneeling before me ?
