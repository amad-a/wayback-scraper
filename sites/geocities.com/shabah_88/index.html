Books

This is a selection of books that cover various subjects related to the Arab world including ancient & modern history, politics, Western view, culture, novels, stories, cookbooks .....
The comments here are extracts from reviews and comments from editors and readers.
Click on the book title for full details and reviews.

New books:

The Enemy Has a Face: The Seeds of Peace Experience By John & Michael Walach
A superb book describing one of the most important experiments of people-to-people reconciliation involving Arabs and Israelis.

Water in the Middle East : A Geography of Peace By Hussein Amery & Aaron Wolf
Addressing water needs from a geographical perspective, the contributors to this book analyze and assess the impact of scarce water resources in the Jordan River basin countries and territories.

In the House of the Law : Gender and Islamic Law By Judith E. Tucker
"An original, valuable, and important study. . . . This book will quickly establish itself as a key work in the field of Middle East women's studies."

The Islamic World in Ascendancy : From the Arab Conquests to the Seige in Vienna By Martin Sicker
Sicker examines the thousand-year ascendancy of Islam from the Arab conquests to the zenith of Ottoman expansionism.

Peoples and Cultures of the Middle East By Daniel G. Bates, Amal Rassam
This book provides readers with a topical, social-anthropological introduction to the Middle East.

The Globalization of Business and the Middle East : Opportunities and Constraints By Masoud Kavoossi
This book examines the development of the region's business environment and the conditions that uniquely impact its growth.


Recommended reading:

Empires of the Sand : The Struggle for Mastery in the Middle East, 1789-1923 by Efraim Karsh
Empires of the Sand offers a bold and comprehensive reinterpretation of the struggle for mastery in the Middle East during the long nineteenth century (1789-1923).

Dream Palace of the Arabs : A Generation's Odyssey by Fuad Ajami
...the present book is in some sense an attempt to make amends, to set the record straight, to do justice to the richness, complexity and diversity of 20th-century Arab culture and to the courage and honesty of many individual Arab writers.

A History of the Modern Middle East by William Cleveland
This comprehensive work provides an analysis of the last two centuries of Middle Eastern history, from the rise of the Safavids and Ottomans to the recent Gulf War.

The Middle East : A Brief History of the Last 2,000 Years by Bernard Lewis
An authoritative, accessibly written overview of the history of the Middle East illuminates the region's traditions of government, economic systems, customs, and culture, as well as the religion of Islam and its legal system.

The Complete Idiot's Guide Middle East Conflict by Mitchell Bard
A comprehensive guide to understanding Middle East conflict. Don't be misled by the title. This book is a penetrating analysis of the history of the conflicts in the region and the prospects for peace.

Understanding Arabs : A Guide for Westerners by Margaret Nydell
Understanding Arabs provides an objective examination of Arab values, beliefs, and perceptions and compares and contrasts them with those of Westerners.

The Arabs : A Short History by Philip Htiti
An ideal introduction to the Arabs history and culture.

A History of the Arab Peoples by Albert Hourani
Encyclopedic and panoramic in its scope, this fascinating work chronicles the rich spiritual, political, and cultural institutions of Arab history through 13 centuries.

The Blood of Abraham : Insights into the Middle East by Jimmy Carter
The history of each of the nations that comprise the Middle East, the reasons for their different goals, and the nature of their prime concerns

Waging Peace : Israel and the Arabs at the End of the Century by Itamar Rabinovich
The former Israeli ambassador to the United States, surveys the history of Israeli-Arab relations from the founding of Israel in 1948 up through the tentative peace process of the 1990s.

Seven Pillars of Wisdom : A Triumph by T. E. Lawrence
Not only a consummate military history, but also a colorful epic and a lyrical exploration of the mind of a great man who helped shape the Middle East as it exists today.

The Adventures of Ibn Battuta : A Muslim Traveller of the 14th Century by Ross Dunn
An excellent synoptic introduction to the Muslim world in the Middle Ages.

The Arabian Nights by Husain Haddawy
Readers will discover in each story a world of high flamboyance and startling beauty, humor and magic, and lessons of loyalty and love's endurance.

Muqaddimah by Ibn Khaldun
Describes the stages that every civilization passes from the turmoil of the inception of political entities, through the stability of the "middle period," to the "senility" and decline.

The Space Between Our Footsteps : Poems and Paintings from the Middle East by Naomi Shihab Nye
In an unparalleled collection, honored anthologist Naomi Shihab Nye brings together the work of over 120 poets and artists from 19 countries in the Middle East.

Arabian Nights and Days by Najib Mahfouz
These utterly delightful tales begin where "A Thousand and One Nights" left off.

Serpent of the Nile : Women and Dance in the Arab World by Wendy Bounaventura
All sorts of information about the background of Middle Eastern dance.

The Music of the Arabs by Habib Touma
An overview of Arabic music through history to contemporary production.

A Book of Middle Eastern Food by Claudia Roden
More than 500 recipes from the subtle, spicy, varied cuisines of the Middle East, ranging from inexpensive but tasty peasant fare to elaborate banquet dishes.

From the Lands of Figs and Olives : Over 300 Delicious & Unusual Recipes from the Middle East by Habeeb Salloum
Over 300 recipes from countries throughout the Middle East and North Africa provide a strong representation of cultural influences and unusual dishes.
