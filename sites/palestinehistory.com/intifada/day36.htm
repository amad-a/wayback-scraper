Day 36 : 02.11.2000

Demonstrations continued in West Bank and Gaza Strip despite the understandings reached by the Israeli officials and the PNA.

Khalid Muhammed al-Khatib (17 years old ), was shot dead by Israeli soldiers at the village of Hizma during a protest march , when Israeli occupation soldiers opened live fire on the unarmed demonstrators . A second Palestinian , Yaser Ismael Halayka, was also shot dead by Israeli troops near the village of Khader. A Third Palestinian from Gaza also died of wounds sustained during confrontations with the Israeli soldiers yesterday.

An Israeli soldiers shot a 4 year old Palestinian child by a rubber-coated steel bullet in his right eye, plucking it out and causing irreparable damage to his face , near Bethlehm.

A car exploded in the Yaffa street in West Jerusalem killing two persons ( a man and a woman) and injuring nine others. Israel police described the explosion as " suicidal bomb attack ".

Three Israeli soldiers were killed and four wounded in two separate clashes. The first clash took place near Jericho, where an Israeli soldiers was killed by Palestinian gunfire. A more serious clash took place at the village of al Khader near Bethlehem where Palestinian resisters shot dead two Israeli army officer and injured four, one seriously.
