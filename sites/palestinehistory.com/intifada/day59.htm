Day 59 : Saturday 25.11.2000

Four Palestinians were killed, including a child during heavy bombardment of Palestinian residential areas in the West Bank and Gaza Strip.

Samir Alaraj (13 years old) was killed when Israeli tanks stationed outside the Jewish settlement block of Gush Katif fired several volleys of machinegun rounds on the streets of Khan Younis, killing the boy immediately and injuring as many as 35 civilians. In Jenin, Amjad Azmi Abbadi (20 years old), was killed when Israeli occupation troops opened fired on a peaceful march outside the city. Abdul Munim Izzedin from village of Arraba near Jenin, and Fuad Adnan Dweikat from Balata refugee camp near Nablus, were also killed.

The Israeli occupation authorities have again imposed curfew on Hebron less than 24 hours after it was lifted. Hebron has been effectively under a suffocating curfew since the Palestinian uprising broke out on 28 September, apparently in order to enable the nearly 250 Jewish settlers to carry out their bloody rampage against Palestinians undisturbed.

The Israeli army destroyed hundreds of dunums of verdant Arab fields in the Hebron area.
