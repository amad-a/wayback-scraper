Palestine Red Crescent Society

The Palestine Red Crescent Society (PRCS) is a national humanitarian society that provides a wide range of health, social and other services to the Palestinian People. PRCS was officially established on 26 December 1968. In 1969 the Palestinian National Council gave the Society the mandate and responsibility for providing health and social services to the Palestinian people inside and outside Palestine.
