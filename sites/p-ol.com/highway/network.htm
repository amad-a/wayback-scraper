Full Internet

Full Internet gives our users access to the World Wide Web, ftp, telnet, usenet news, International Relay Chat (Freetel), and archie. World Wide Web is probably the best known feature of the Internet. Full Internet for businesses is available through four different ways: Dedicated Phone Lines, Leased Lines, Frame Relay, and VSAT.

Dedicated Phone Lines

All Point to Point Protocol Accounts offer fast, unrestricted, and full Internet access features including; e-mail, shell, usenet conference groups, world wide web, archie, ftp, telnet, International relay chat, wais and wois. To maintain this ratio and to keep optimum service; we contiuously improve and upgrade our lines and hardware. Our network is protected from power failure, power spikes, and blackouts by using UPS and electricity generators.

Frame Relay

Frame Relay is a high speed, statistically-multiplexed interface standard for connection to Palestine Online. It uses virtual circuit functionality for bandwidth-on-demand connectivity.

Frame Relay is a packet-mode service that allows data to be sent in individual-address 'frames," consisiting of anything from a few, to several thousand characters of data

By reducing the amount of processing required to switch frames between network nodes, Frame Relay can eliminate most of the processing requuirements of a packet-based switching protocol.

Frame Relay can help your organization in searching for a cost-effective, dedicated, high-speed Internet access system. Frame Relay is priced at a flat monthly rate, regardless of your location.

Frame Relay service provides a complete range of solutions to connect your business directly to Palestine Online's fault-tolerant clear channel network. The Frame Relay circuits are easy to upgrade; there is no need to replace csu/dsu or router when you switch to a higher speed. This makes Frame Relay the perfect answer for businesses who plan to grow with the Internet.

Frame Relay is a proven technology and is universally accepted as a telecom transmission standard. Frame Relay architecture is based on the efficient switching of data packets over a shared digital network, ensuring optimal use of your bandwidth.

Leased Lines

Leased Line service provides your organization dedicated, point-to-point circuits to connect you directly to Palestine Online's fault-tolerant, high speed Internet network.

Leased Lines is primarily for those people who are searching for dedicated, high-bandwidth connectivity that will support active networks, Web servers, large data and file transfers, and mutlimedia applications.

Because there are some businesses that don't require the full bandwidth at all times, Palestine Online service allows their customers to pay only for the actual bandwidth used during the month. International leased line, wireless, and satellite connections are also available.
