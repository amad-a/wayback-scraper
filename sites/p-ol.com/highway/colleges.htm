Colleges of Palestine:

Ibrahimieh Community College

Ibrahimieh Community College is a private non-profit institute of higher learning located in Jerusalem. The College is accredited by the Palestinian Council of Higher Education, the Jordanian Council of Higher Education, and is also associated in some of its programs with the University of Franche-Comte, France.

College of Islamic Sciences

College of Islamic Sciences is located in Jerusalem. There are a total of 89 students enrolled, 67% of which are female. The college provides the following programs: Academics and Social Work.

Al Ummah College-Jerusalem

Al Ummah College is located in Jerusalem. There are 336 student enrolled, 92% of which are female. The college offers the following programs: Academic, Administration and Finance, and Computer.

Kalandia Community College (UNRWA)

Kalandia Community College has an enrollemnet of 38 male students. All 38 students are enrolled in the Engineering program that the college offers.

Women's Community College (UNRWA) 

The Women's Community College is located in Ramallah and has an enrollment of 212 students. The programs that are offered at WCC are ones in the fields of Academics, Engineering, Para Medicine, Administration and Finance, Computers, and Social Work.

Men's Community College (UNRWA)

The Men's Community College in Ramallah has an enrollment of 118 students, and the MCC offers programs in the fields of Administration and Finance and Computers.

Palestine Technical College

Palestine Technical College is located in Ramallah and there are approximately 100 female students enrolled. The college offers both an Academic and an Education Program.

College of Engineering and Technology

The College of Engineering is located in Hebron and has an enrollment of 535 students, 30% of which are female. The college offers programs in the fields of Engineering, Computers, and the Applied Arts.

Palestine Technical College (Khoduree)

The Palestine Technical College is located in Tul Karim. There are 130 students enrolled in the college, and 22% of them are female. The college offers programs in the fiels of Academic, Education, Engineering, Administrave and Financial, Computer, and the Fine Arts.

College of Islamic Sciences

The College of Islamic Sciences is located in Qalqilya. There are approximately 120 students enrolled, 79% of which are female. The college offers programs in the fields of Academics, Education, and Social Work.

An Najah Community College

Al Najah Community College is located in Nablus. There are 680 students enrolled, of which 51% of them are female. The college offers programs in the fields of Academics, Education , Engineering, Para Medicine, Administration and Finance, and Computers.

Al Rawdah College

Al Rawdah College is located in Nablus. There are 251 enrolled students, 47% of which are female. The College offers programs in the fields of Academics, Education, Para Medicine, Adminstration and Finance, and Computers.

Palestine Technical College (ARROUB)

The Palestine Technical Collegehas 267 enrolled students, 61% of which are female. The College offers the programs in the following fields: Academics, Education, Social Work, Applied Arts, Computer, and Administration and Finance.

College for Sciences and Technology

The College for Sciences and Technology is located in Khan Younis. There are 403 students enrolled, 33% of which are female. The College offers programs in the fields of Engineering, Computers, Administration and Finance, and Para Medicine.

Gaza Training College (UNRWA)

The Gaza Training College, as the name indicates, is located in Gaza. There are 156 enrolled students, 30% of which are female. The College offers programs in the fields of Engineering, Para Medicine, and Administration and Finance.

Anglic First Paptist College

The Anglic First Paptist College is located in Gaza. There are only 14 erolled students, four of which are female. The only program that this college offers is an Academic program.

Palesitne Technical College

The Palestine Technical College is located in Deir El Balah. There are 125 students enrolled, 28% of which are female. The college offers programs in the fields of Academics, Administration and Finance, Computer, the Applied Arts, and Social Work.
