Archeological Sites

For centuries, archeology has intrigued the greatest of minds. Oliver Wendell Holmes Jr said "When I want to understand what is happening today or try to decide what will happen tomorrow, I look back." This is what propels most archeologists in the first place: the desire to learn about the past and to discover some facts about the present. 

The Middle East is one of the most historical sites in the world...and Palestine is in the center of it all. Some of the oldest relics have been found in Palestine. Some remnants date back to ancient civilization. Palestine Online, with the help of the Palestinian Association for Cultural Exchange (PACE), has five marvelous sites for you to investigate. Take your time and look through the wonderous historical sites.
