Dial-in Access

All Point to Point Protocol Accounts offer fast, unrestricted, and full Internet access features including; e-mail, shell, usenet conference groups, world wide web, archie, ftp, telnet, International relay chat, wais and wois. This service provides access to the internet via modems, with local phone calls within the (02) Jerusalem and (06) Jenin phone calling areas. Internet access software, both shareware (Palestine Online Suite) and commercial (Netscape Personal Edition), are provided.

All modems used on our incoming telephone lines are 33,600 USRobotics V34 everything. Our modem-to-user ratio is within the international average range (1:10). To maintain this ratio and to keep optimum service; we contiuously improve and upgrade our lines and hardware. Our network is protected from power failure, power spikes.

Dial-in access accounts are available as an individual and/or dedicated phone lines for network connections.
