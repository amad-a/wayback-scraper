Universities of Palestine:

Al Azhar University in Gaza


Al-Azhar University is located in Gaza. There are approximately 7,040 students enrolled, 38% of which are female. There are seven different faculties, and these are: the Faculties of Arts, Education, Sharia'h, Business and Economics, Agriculture, Science and Technology, and Pharmacy. There are 145 employed professors, and the ratio of students to teachers is 49:1.

Alquds University in Jerusalem

Alquds University is the only Arab University in Jerusalem. Since its foundation in 1984, six different faculties have been established. These include: the Faculties of Science and Technology, Medicine, Allied Health Professions, Jurisprudence, Law, and the Hind Al-Hussaeini Faculty of the Arts. There are over 2300 students enrolled, 55% of which are females. Scholarships are available and are based on need and merit.

Arab American University in Jenin

The Arab American University is a new private university that is being established in Palestine in the city of Jenin with full affiliation with the California State University system. The location of the University makes it accessible to students from all over Palestine (including pre 1948 Palestinians). The University expects to accept its first group of students in the academic year 1997/1998. The University has the commitments of a number of Palestinian scholars to join its faculty, the names of those scholars will be published in the near future.

Bethlehem University in Bethlehem

Bethlehem University is a private co-educational institution open to students of all faiths and is sponsored by the Vatican to serve the higher educational needs of Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza. It was founded in 1973 with the administrative cooperation of the De La Salle Brothers (FSC). Students and graduates of Bethlehem University are accepted in Arab, European, and American Universities.

Birzeit University in Birzeit

Birzeit University in an independent Palestinian institution situated 26 km north of Jerusalem, in the Israel-occupied West Bank. Founded as a school in 1924, Birzeit began its university program in 1972 and is currently introducing a master degree program.

Education College Gaza

The Education college is also located in Gaza. This school specializes in Education. This is the only faculty offered at the Education College. There are 493 students, 55% of which are female. There are 30 employed teachers , and a ratio of students to teachers of 16:1.

The Hebron Polytechnic College

The Hebron Polytech College is specialized school of Engineering. This is the only faculty that is offered. There are only 390 student, 17% of which are female. With such a small enrollment, there are only 40 professors, and a student to teacher ratio of 10:1.

The Hebron University

The Hebron University has a current enrollment of about 1,630 students, 50% of which are female. There are five faculties, and these are: The Faculties of Art, Sharia'h, Business nd Economics, Agriculture, and Science and Technology. There are 65 employed professors, and the student to teacher ratio is 25:1.

Islamic University in Gaza

The Islamic University is located in Gaza. There are over 5,700 students, 34% of which are female. There are nine different faculties and these are : The Faculties of Business and Economics, Osol Eddin, Sharia'h, Education, Arts, Medical Profession, Science and Technology, Engineering, and Graduate Studies. There are 162 professors and the student to teahcer ratio is 35:1.

Jerusalem Open University in Jerusalem

Jerusalem Open University is located in Jerusalem. There are 5,517 students enrolled, 45% of which are female. There are six different faculties, and these are the Faculties of Education, Business and Economics, Agriculture, Graduate Studies, Social Development, and Computer Science. There are only 40 professors so the student toteacher ratio is 141:1. Jerusalem Open University also offers classes in both Ramallah and the Bireh.

An Najah National University

Najah Naitonal University is one of the biggest in Palestine. There are over 6,825 students enrolled, 40% of which are female. This university offers ten different Faculties, and these are The Faculties of Science and Technology, Agriculture, Business and Economics, Sharia'h, Education, Arts, Engineering, Pharmacy, Law, and Graduate Studies. There is a teaching staff of approximately 265, and the student to teacher ratio is 24:1.
