Information on exhibits by the following artist may be found in the Palestinian visual artists section: Abed Abdi (10/96), Nabil Anani (12/96), and Khaled Hourani (5/97). In 1997, the Sakakini also hosted a March exhibit of modern Russian art: works by Dudnig, Timur, etc. from a private Palestinian collection, & in July an exhibit of original works by Victor Vasarely.   In 1998, the Sakakini hosted a February exhibit by Taysir Sharaf, and a May exhibit of Nakba photographs from the UNRWA archives.
