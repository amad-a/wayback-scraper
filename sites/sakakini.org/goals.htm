Goals, Status & Finances

By the combination of its unique aesthetic setting, and its mission goals, the KSCC hopes to contribute to a renaissance of Palestinian arts & culture. The KSCC's mission is articulated around the following goals:

* Fostering arts appreciation,

 * Acquainting the artistic community and the general public with new art forms or innovations,

* Strengthening public awareness of the intellectual, artistic and cultural Palestinian heritage,

* Facilitating cultural exchanges, and

 * Spreading Palestinian arts & culture to the outside world.
 

Since its opening in May 1996, the Sakakini functioned  as a branch of the Palestinian Ministry of Culture, albeit with programmatic independence. Within a year of its opening, consultations assessing the Sakakinis work recommended that in order for it to better fulfill its mission and serve the local community, the Sakakini should change its status to a non governmental organization (NGO).

For that purpose, a founding board made up of local artists and philanthropists was convened in July 1997,  among its tasks was the drafting of  bylaws and helping in  fundraising. In May 1998, the Sakakini obtained its NGO registration from the Palestinian Ministry of the Interior, and a tax exempt status granted to non-profit organizations from the Ministry of Finance. In August 1998, an agreement was signed with the Ministry of Culture, whereby the Sakakinis building remains the property of the Ministry, while its premises are used rent free by the new Sakakini-NGO. The first general assembly meeting is due to be convened in September.

FINANCES:

Current sources of revenue are: donations, project-specific grants, members dues, partial coverage of overhead costs by the Carmel literary quarterly, and ticket sales from events held at the Centre.

The Sakakinis first funding priority after its change of status is to establish an endowment fund to enable it to pursue its activities in financial security, and constitute a funding base for future cultural support initiatives in Palestine.
