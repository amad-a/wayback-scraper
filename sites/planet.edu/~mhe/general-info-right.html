General Information

The higher education sector is composed of eight universities and a Polytechnic (which grant Bachelor’s degree and higher), and 16 community colleges (which offer 2-year diplomas, generally in technical and vocational fields). The universities are comprised of the following main faculties: arts, sciences, commerce and economics, engineering, agriculture, law, pharmacy, medicine, medical professions, nursing, education and hotel management.

 

Universities:

 

The universities are young; all were established during the seventies. Student enrollment for 1999/2000 puts the total figure at about 60,000 students, practically all of whom are undergraduates. Enrollment in graduate programs (which offer Master’s degrees and higher) total 2386 student

 

These institutions are distributed geographically as follows: two in Gaza, AL- Azhar and Islamic; and six in the West Bank; An-Najah in Nablus; Birzeit University near Ramallah; Bethlehem university in Beithlehem; Hebron and the Polytechnic in Hebron; AL-Quds University in Jerusalem; and Al-Quds open university with several centers in Gaza and the West Bank.

 

The sector of Higher Education was managed by the Council of Higher Education  from 1978-1994. However, with the establishment of the Ministry of Education and Higher Education in 1994, the management responsibility for community colleges was transferred from the Council to the Ministry’s Directorate of Technical Education and Community Colleges.  In mid-1996, with the setting-up of the Ministry of Higher Education, the entire sector of post-secondary education came under its responsibility. On the other hand, as non-governmental organizations, individual universities are administered, generally, by a Board of Trustees (as the main policy making body), and by a President (as the main executive responsible for day-to-day management).

 

Community and technical colleges

 

Post -secondary technical education is offered by community colleges, which aim at meeting the development of middle level human resources.  Study duration is 2 years leading to diploma in vocational, technical or academic qualification. There are 16 community colleges in the West Bank and Gaza, five of which are “governmental colleges“. The total number of community college students is 5,286, distributed among 44 disciplines.

 

Community colleges may be classified under three categories:

 

- Technical Community Colleges: offer courses aimed at preparing  technicians;

- Academic Community Colleges: offer courses aimed at preparing teachers

   training;

- Hybrid Community Colleges: combine both categories.
