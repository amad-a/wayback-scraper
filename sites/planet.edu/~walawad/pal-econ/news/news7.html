THE JERUSALEM INDEX

The Jerusalem Index rose by 3.69 points. PADICO shares were the most traded shares making 87% of the total traded shares on the PSE. Over the last two days of trading PADICO shares rose by 8.89%. Paltel rose by 0.34%, PRICO by 3.3%, and National Insurance Co. by 2.04%.
