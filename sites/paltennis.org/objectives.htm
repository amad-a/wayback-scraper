Objectives
  1. Promote Tennis and encourage competition.
  2. Promote Palestinian talents and potential in Tennis
  3. Encourage schools to adopt Tennis as a primary sport.
  4. Develop Tennis as a serious sport in Palestine.
  5. Organize Tournaments.
  6. Participate in International, Asian and Arab tournaments.
  7. Coordinate with other International and Arab Associations.
  8. Organize training courses for players, trainers and referees.
  9. Establish Tennis training centers all over Palestine.
