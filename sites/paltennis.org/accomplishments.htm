Accomplishments

  1. Organized the 1995, 1996, 1997 championships.

  2. Organized training courses for youth aged 8-11.
  3. Participated in the International Tennis Tournament in Baghdad and Beirut in 1997.
  4. Acquired formal certification from the Arab Tennis Association, ATA.
  5. Negotiated with the World Tennis Association for possible certification.
  6. Negotiated with the Asian Tennis Federation and was accepted as an observer member in their 1998 annual meeting in Bangkok.
  7. Conducted studies to renovate existing courts and build new ones.
  8. Acquired ATA’s approval to organize Bethlehem 2000 tournament championship.

  9. The first Palestinian Sports Association to have a presence on the Internet. Our Website is:

www.paltennis.org
