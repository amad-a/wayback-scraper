Welcome

We, at the Palestinian Tennis Association , would like to extend our sincere and warm thanks and gratitude for your keen interest and support of our Association. This web site is the first of its kind in Palestine and possibly in the Arab World.

Promoting Tennis in Palestine, not only enhances sports, but promotes young Palestinian talents. Encourages youth to develop more positive attitudes and teaches them to properly and professionally interact with others. This, we believe, is crucial for promoting and enhancing cross cultural exchange and mutual understanding among nations, which is a key ingredient to an ever lasting Peace in the Middle East.

The Palestinian Tennis Association have been working diligently in the past few months to enhance and promote Tennis. We have been working on a number of activities and projects, which will help make Tennis a prominent and a popular sport in Palestine.
