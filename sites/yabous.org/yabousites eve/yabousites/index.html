The Concert Series, Seasons or Yabousites evenings: Musical, dance or theatrical evenings performed by local high caliber artists are organized periodically to expose the Palestinian cultural heritage.

We are planning to host a weekly performance in Jerusalem, at the YMCA, Lutheran Church and other venues. Groups are invited from Palestine and inside the green line in order to revive the cultural life in Jerusalem.  Some of these concerts are organized in cooperation with the National Conservatory of Music.
