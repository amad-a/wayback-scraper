Administrative detainees transferred to interrogation

  • Muhammad Sarahni who was arrested on October 10 1996 and was transferred to interrogation a few days before his release on May 1 1999.

  • Bajis Nakhli who was arrested on march 5 1999 and was transferred to interrogation before his release on September 1 1999.

  • Yusif Allaham who was arrested on September 16 1998 and was transferred to interrogation before his release on February 25 1999.

  • Ibrahim Shawar who was arrested on August 15 1998 and received an extended sentence of administrative detention for four months after his appeal was dismissed. He was transferred to Al Muscobiyeh for interrogation on September 15 1999 where he received an indictment bill.

  • Jamil Tarwa who was arrested on December 21 1998 and was transferred to interrogation after serving his sentence in June 1999.

  • Muhanad Masalmi who was transferred to interrogation on February 6 1999 when he was supposed to be freed on February 8 1999.

  • Imad Ilaruj was transferred to interrogation from administrative detention on March 3 1999.

  • Muawya Qa'dan was transferred to interrogation from administrative detention on February 25 1999.

Cases of detainees who completed prison sentences and were then transferred to administrative detention in 1999

  • Imad Ilqawasmi received a five-month administrative detention sentence after finishing his five years sentence in January 1999. His appeal was dismissed and was told to take his case to the High Court.

  • Mahmoud Hamdi Shabani from Hebron was transferred to administrative detention after finishing his sentence of 5.3 years on September 16 1999. He was arrested on June 17 1994.

  • Samir Masalmi from Bet Awwa was transferred to administrative detention two weeks before the end of his sentence on December 20 1999 for three months after spending five years in prison.
