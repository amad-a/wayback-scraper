I. Introduction

Article 31 of the lower division of the regulations of prisoners' treatment under the heading "control and punishment" states:

"the physical punishment of placement in a dark cell or any other rough or inhumane or insulting method is totally prohibited as a preventive punishment."

One of the dangerous violations of human rights committed by Israel against detainees is solitary confinement in harsh conditions that cause physical and psychological damage. The detainees in solitary confinement are separated from their surroundings in a way that exceeds the limits and violates the rights given in the international and local rules and regulations.

Solitary confinement is a vengeful punishment, in particular because its period is not limited and can reach years. This procedure requires placing a prisoner in a cell alone; the cell does not have humane living conditions. These are some of the cases of long years of solitary confinement:

  • Mahmoud Mahajni's spent more than seven years in solitary confinement. He was released on September 9 1999.

  • Najib Ide'es' case has been in solitary confinement for five years.

  • Ahmad Shukri has been in solitary confinement for four years.

There are many other cases of more than two years in solitary confinement such as the case of Mahmoud Atwan, Majid Abu Qtaish, Muhammad Taqatqa and Hasan Salami. The number of prisoners in solitary confinement totaled more than 60 for this year, distributed amongst Nafha, Ber Alsabi'a and Asqalan prisons, in addition to a number of detainees who spend shorter periods of time in solitary confinement. Prisoners can end up in solitary for problems that occur at any time in the different prisons. The prisons' administration takes several procedures of punishment against prisoners in solitary confinement such as depriving them from having a recess, or families' visits or from buying things from the prison canteen, etc.

ADDAMEER followed up this issue through legal procedures. It sent a letter to the prisons' department requesting it to end solitary confinement of prisoners, especially those who spent more than eight months in solitary confinement such as Mahmoud Atun, Muhammad Taqatqa, Abdulnasser Isa and Ataya Jaber. A request for limiting the period of solitary confinement was also made. The Association has not received a response from the prisons' department, which requires taking the case to the Israeli High Court of Justice. The Association also conducts visits to those held in solitary confinement in order to assess their situation and conduct legal follow up of their cases.

Many prisoners were held in solitary confinement for security reasons as claimed by prison's administration. When the intelligence services finish interrogating a prisoner, he is sent to solitary confinement. Such cases include the case of Abdulnasser Isa, who spent 50 days in interrogation in Petakh Tikva prison, then was placed in solitary confinement in Bir Alsabi'a prison, as well as Mahmoud Atun who spent thirty days in interrogation.

The prisons' department has allocated special sections for solitary confinement in different prisons and in 1999 the main section for solitary confinement was in Bir Alsabi'a prison, in addition to solitary confinement cells allocated in prisons where prisoners are held from time to time. A new section for solitary confinement was opened in October 1999 at Telmond prison called "Hadarim'. It consists of 40 rooms that can accommodate 80 prisoners, with each room for two prisoners. The area of each room is 6 square meters and has a window, a table, two beds, two large closets and a television rack on the wall. The prisoners in this section suffer from many problems such as restricted family visits, which take place with a barrier of a glass window, with talking done via a telephone. This method was rejected by the prisoners who went on strike, refusing families' visits from the date of transferring them in the second half of October 1999. When prisoners enter this section, all things brought from Asqalan prison would be confiscated and returned very slowly.

After an attempt made by one of the prisoners at Asqalan prison to stab a soldier on October 17 1999, the administration held all prisoners in the room in solitary confinement. The whole section was deprived from visits for three months, electrical equipment was taken away and the recess period was reduced from 4 hours to one hour.

Bir Alsabi'a prison has two solitary confinement sections, including:

  1. Section (22): 25 prisoners. Some chose to be held in solitary confinement and others were held their by the intelligence services. The living conditions there are very poor because the cells are rotten, dirty and pungent, which makes one feel that he is living underground. The section includes common criminals, who bother the Palestinian prisoners of security reasons. Many Palestinian detainees requested to be transferred to another place but in vain.

  2. Section (9): the main section for holding security prisoners. It has, until the time of this report, eight prisoners who have been in solitary confinement for periods of six months to three years. The prisoners are subjected to many kinds of punishments mainly prevention of family visits.

    Six detainees were deprived from families' visits for more than a year, such as Abdulnasser Isa, who has been held in solitary confinement since June 1998. He was transferred to Petakh Tikva interrogation center at the beginning of 1999 where he spent fifty days. He requested the prison administration, on many occasions, to let him out of solitary confinement but his request was rejected. The Association requested the prison's administration to let him out of solitary confinement but the request was also rejected. The prisoners in solitary confinement asked the prison's administration to allow them to pray together during Ramadan, but their request was rejected.
