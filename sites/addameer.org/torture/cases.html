Some prisoners cases _________________



Walid Musa Hamid Hussein

He was arrested on February 4 1999 and was allowed to be visited on February 19 1999 after several attempts, including an appeal to the Israeli High Court against preventing visits and use of torture. Walid gave a deposition under oath before the lawyer saying that " I was arrested on February 4 1999 and was immediately taken to Al Muscobiyeh prison where I was interrogated for four continuos hours. Then I was forced to sit on a tiny chair with a sack on my head and loud music playing in the background for almost 12 hours. In the first eight days, this method was used continuously. I was allowed to take a one-hour rest after the first four days then another one-hour rest after another four days. I was not allowed to wash during the first week. An interrogator named 'Adnan' hit me in the face two or three times with his fist during the interrogation. Each round of interrogation went on for 8-12 hours for 12 days. In one of the interrogation rounds I was forced to squat for six hours and I was not allowed to stand up for even one minute. The interrogator used to push the cuffs on my legs with his leg and another interrogator would step on my chest and back against the chair. They also used abusive words against my family and me".

Bassam Abdulrahim Hamid

He is from Silwad, Ramallah and was arrested on March 18 1999. He was visited on April 2 1999 for the first time and on May 9 1999 for the second time. After an appeal at the Israeli High Court, the Association's lawyer was prohibited from visiting him on March 24, April 6, April 12, May 3 and May 6 1999. The reasons given for not allowing a visit were either placing the detainee under interrogation or a court order not allowing visits. He was left in the collaborators' room for many days. In his deposition about torture he said " I was arrested on March 18 1999 from work at 08:05. I was taken to Al Muscobiyeh prison on the same day for interrogation. The interrogation took two continuous hours then I was made to sit on a tiny chair with my hands tied behind my back and my feet tied against a chair. They covered my head with a sack and placed me in a freezing cold place. This went on continuously for the first five days and on Saturday I was allowed a two-hour rest. On the first day, the interrogator hit me with his fingers on my head and pressed his fingers against my neck. Then I was forced to squat for two continuous hours in front of the chair where he sat and pushed my legs until they reached the floor and pushed my head upwards. I was shaken violently twice on the first day of interrogation. I was allowed to wash but without changing my underwear. The food was bad and the last meal was served at four o'clock in the afternoon."

Fawzi Ayid Jabra

He is from Ein Yabroud, Ramallah and was arrested on June 28 1999 upon an administrative arrest warrant for three months. The ruling was appealed on July 29 1999 but he was taken to Al Muscobiyeh for interrogation on July 25 1999. The Association appealed before the court in an attempt to visit him and was granted a visit on August 8 1999. Fawzi said that he was subjected to torture such as sitting on a tiny chair with his hands and feet tied. He suffered a severe headache and only received headache tablets (Acamol brand). The interrogation rounds went on for many hours, sometimes from 09:30 to 05:00 o'clock on the next day. Fawzi's wife was brought for interrogation in an attempt to exert pressure on him.

Yassin Bazar

He was arrested on November 22 1999 in investigating a case he received a sentence for more than a year. He said in his deposition before ADDAMEER that: "in the first week of interrogation they -the intelligence officers- pushed me to collaborate with them. The interrogation sessions went on for more than six hours at a time, during which I would be tied against a chair in the interrogation room. The excuse for this was protecting the interrogator, though before the Israeli High Court ruling detainees were not tied during interrogation but they were kept tied with their head covered with a sack for long periods of time awaiting the next round of interrogation. From November 29 1999 the interrogation was continuous for two days and I was tied against a chair and my hands were swollen. The interrogator threatened to keep me in that situation for a week. Then I was put in solitary confinement in a small stinking and rotten prison cell. Therefore, I went on a hunger strike for two days until I was taken out of it."

Mansor Mahmoud Alshahatit

who was arrested on November 15 1999, said that: "I was not allowed to sleep for 36 hours at the beginning of interrogation. It went on for the whole day -from morning to ten o'clock in the evening- during the first week. I was tied against a chair…" Mansor was beaten by soldiers when he was arrested.

Rami Abu Hlal

He was arrested on December 20 1999 said: " after being arrested and at six o'clock in the morning I was tied against a chair with my hands cuffed behind my back for almost thirteen hours everyday. I was allowed to wash only once with cold water since there is no hot water. The food is very bad and for two days I only ate bread."
