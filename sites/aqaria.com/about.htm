INTRODUCTION

ABOUT AQARIA
Real Estate is now one of the major sectors attracting investors in Palestine. This is due to the obvious shortage in housing complexes and other buildings. This increasing demand resulted from the natural population growth, the needs of the returnees for suitable housing and the rising expectations of an eventual peace settlement for the whole region

Palestine Development & Investment Co. (WWW.PADICO.COM) was quick to appreciate the extent of the possibilities in this sector, and therefore established the Palestine Real Estate Investment Co. in 1994. AqariA is capitalized at $ 22 million of which Padico’s share is 47%. It has offices in Amman, Jordan, Ramallah – Elbireh and is headquartered in Remal Gaza

The Palestine Real Estate Investment Company, Aqaria, is considered one of the leading investment companies contributing to the rebuilding of Palestine, the promising land of the Future. The company’s present priority is the planning and the construction of well designed housing and commercial complexes, department stores, malls and central parking facilities thus providing high quality modern homes and comprehensive community services using local materials and expertise at reasonable prices. Our policy is to endow each project with a uniquely distinguished style.

The company is capitalized at $ 22 million and has offices in Amman, Jordan, ElBireh/Ramallah, and is headquartered in Al Remal – Gaza.
COMPANY
ACTIVITIES

Since its establishment in 1994, Aqaria has embarked in the following lines of action:

  • To survey and study the real estate market in the West Bank and Gaza through specialized consultants ( local and foreign ).
  • To explore with the Palestinian National Authority and municipal Councils the possible development, through joint investments, of suitable property owned by these authorities.
  • To locate and acquire suitable land plots for the purpose of future development, resale, and / or for building the company’s own housing or commercial complexes.
