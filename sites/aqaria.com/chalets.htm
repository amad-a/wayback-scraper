THE CHALETS


What better place to relish the clear and serene Gaza seashore and its moderate climate, than to enjoy it from your own private chalet, with a grand marina a few steps away, and a clean beach suited to a luxurious hotel, these chalets can only bring joy to oneself. Moreover, the chalets, which are accessed through a walk through tunnel come fully furnished, in a style appropriate to a resort close to home and close to heart.
