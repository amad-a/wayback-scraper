Reviving its reputation as a cultural and entertainment center, Ramallah boasts many historical buildings now serving as museums, restaurants, and galleries.

In addition to its famous summer festivals, the Ramallah cultural calendar is filled year round with theater productions, concerts folklore troupes performances art exhibitions. Respected High schools and Universities continue to draw students from all across Palestine.

Ramallah is also a home to national institutions, private enterprises and international agencies engaged in building the new Palestine, such as the Ministries of Education, Culture, Agriculture, Industry and Civil Affairs the new stock exchange and major banks.
