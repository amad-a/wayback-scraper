INTRODUCTION

The best of El Bireh/Ramallah translated into gracious community living all year long.

From its 12 dunum hilltop site in the sought-after forests of Irgal, AL MASAYEF STEPS captures sweeping green panoramas, abundant sunshine and fresh breezes. Designed to harmonize with the hillside, the nineteen residences of the lower tiers of our new community simultaneously recreate the terraced rhythms of traditional Palestinian villages and secure each family’s privacy.

Following through the heart of our village are the magnificent old style steps, uniting the lower tierce with the hilltop. These graceful steps encourage pleasurable social leisure moments both planned and spontaneous.

In El Bireh

Central Parking Station Project

This multi function building was realized through a joint investment between Aqaria and the El Bireh Municipality on a B.O.T. basis.

The building includes a modern Mall at the ground floor, while the top floor will be reserved for office space and multi-purpose halls. The remaining floors will include Bus and Taxicab terminals providing the passengers to and from El Bireh/Ramallah district with a safe, comfortable and pleasant journey. Moreover, there will also be two floors for private car parking in the building.
