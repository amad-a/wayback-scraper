Al Mashtal Neighborhood -- GAZA

This project comprises the development and sale of 279 dunum site located on the sea shore, North of Gaza.
The first phase of the project is is expected to be carried out during the period from 1997-2002.

Al NADA NEIGHBORHOOD PROJECT:

This scheme includes the development of 151,000 sqm. of built area of residential units on a 150 dunum site owned by the PNA. The built up area is estimates to compromise 1450 apartments.


AL MASAYEF STEPS

This project comprises the development of 111apartments spreading on a hilltop exceeding12 dunum in Masayef suburb, El Bireh-Ramallah.
The project will be completed in four stages over a three year period.

PADICO HOUSE, NABLUS:

Situated in down town Ramallah, this building was designed to comprise two commercial floors and eight office floors, as well as three parking floors. The total area of the building is about 16000 sqm.

THE DEVELOPMENT OF LAND IN NORTH OF NABLUS.

In order to provide to those interested in acquiring a piece of land in a suitable site at a reasonable price, the company purchased a piece of land of around 50 dunums in Naquoura area ( 8 kilometers to the North West of Nablus).
The land will be divided into plots of 500 to 850 sqm each most of which will be sold and the remaining will be used for the erection of residential complexes in the future.
