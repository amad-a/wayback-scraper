Al Mashtal Neighborhood

Welcome to Al Mashtal neighborhood, Gaza’s largest and most prestigious integrated housing complex, the all new mini- city vows to thrust a new standard of living in the promising land of Gaza. This project was the fruit of a long planning process, a process which took everything into consideration, from the excellent choice of site to the attention to the smallest details, and the use of the best building materials today’s technology can offer. In every way Al Mashtal project is a prelude for the future of Palestine.

B1 / B2 / B3 / B4 / B5 / S Building / Terraced Building / Chalets
Al Mashtal complex was designed to be built from the ground up, on a solid and modern infrastructure, thus delivering a new mini city of exemplary built quality and luxury; it is the complex that Gaza now deserves.

Housing complexes, shopping centers, private schools, Mosque, Hotel, Pedestrian promenade and a marina are only few of the facilities that this leading project will comprise.

Our engineers, fulfilled two major criteria in their design, mainly combining luxury and efficiency without one sacrificing the other.
