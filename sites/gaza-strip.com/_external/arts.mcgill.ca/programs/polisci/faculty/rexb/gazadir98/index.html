Forward

This directory has been designed and developed in an effort to support Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) presently operating in the Gaza Strip. It is an updated and revised version of the Directory of Non-Governmental Organizations in the Gaza Strip, produced by UNSCO in February 1997.

The number of NGOs operating in Gaza has more than doubled since the first directory and now numbers some 250. All sectors of the development effort are included - testimony to the vital role of participation and building civil society. Together with the United Nations family and the donor community, NGOs will continue to assist the Palestinian people in developing the West Bank and Gaza Strip.

UNSCO hopes that this directory will prove useful for Palestinian and international institutions, as well as the donor community, in their efforts to plan and improve coordination. UNSCO is currently working on a Directory for the West Bank with the intention of providing a comprehensive overview of the work and scope of NGOs there. In this way, UNSCO hopes to continue the flow of information among all partners in the development effort.

- Chinmaya R. Gharekhan
United Nations Special Coordinator in the Occupied Territories
July 1998

 

Introduction

NGOs are classified under the following sectors:

  • Agriculture and Environment
  • Culture and Sports
  • Democracy and Human Rights
  • Economic Development
  • Education and Training
  • Health and Rehabilitation
  • Social Services
  • Women and Children

Within each sector, NGOs are listed alphabetically. It should be noted that information about the NGOs contained in this directory are from the NGOs themselves; UNSCO staff are not responsible for the content provided.
