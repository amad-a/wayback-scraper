PAYING TRIBUTE TO BU FOUNDERS

The Congregation of Eastern Churches and the Institute of Christian Brothers
Since the founding of Bethlehem University in 1973 many organizations, foundations, NGO's and individuals have supported the University. However, the two Founders of the University - the Congregation of Eastern Churches and the Institute of the Brothers of the Christian Schools - carry the responsibility for its operation and development.

The Congregation, under the direction of the newly appointed Prefect, Cardinal Ignace Mousa I Daoud, assists in the financial support for the operation and development of the University. The Congregation has been the University's most consistent and generous donor. Since the beginning, the Brothers have been the chief administrators of the University's daily operations. During this time 48 Brothers have served Bethlehem University. The Institute of the Brothers is now under the leadership of the newly elected Superior General, Brother Alvaro Rodriguez. Presently, 13 Brothers serve as full time administrators and teachers.

IN MEMORIAM
We pay tribute to the late Monsignor Raouf Najjar, PhD Former President of Bethlehem University (1989-1998), who passed away on January 26, 2001 after attending the International Regents' Meeting in Rome. He was member of the University's Board of Regents. Monsignor Raouf was active in his priestly ministry. He founded the Catholic Press in Amman, Jordan and the The Voice of the Holy Land magazine in 1968. In 1980 he was acting administrator for the Latin Vicariate in Jordan and from 1989-1995, he was representative of the Apostolic Delegation in Jordan. A funeral Mass was held at the University Chapel on February 2, during which Professor Manuel Hassassian gave a short eulogy. Funeral Masses were also offered for him in Nazareth, his birthplace, and in Amman where he spent the active years of his ministry.
