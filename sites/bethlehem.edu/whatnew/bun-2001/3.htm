Moving Information Technology Ahead On Campus
Many activities are taking place on campus to enhance awareness and the efficient use of Information Technology tools. Mr. Tarik Daoud, of the Computer Center, established a new Intranet web site to make university documents and various department service forms available electronically. Donations from the Mary P. Dolciani Halloran Foundation enabled BU to acquire higher speed connection to the Internet to better serve more than 200 computers connected to the Internet.. Generous donations from the Lynch Foundation enabled the Computer Center to enhance the IT services being provided to students and faculty. A new Information Technology Newsletter is issued each semester to update faculty and staff on the latest IT news.

Early Childhood Festival: "New Dawn"
The Education Faculty and students held a festival for children from May 17-20 in the BU Social Cultural Center. Participants were K-5th grade children from schools in the Bethlehem area accompanied by parents, educators and community leaders. Activities consisted of sessions on the rights of the child, a panel discussion for educators on children's expression through drawing, plays for parents and educators about the effect of the current situation on a child's behavior, storytelling, and competitive sports. The four-day activity was sponsored by Ireland Aid and Missionary Childhood whose invaluable help made possible the successful hosting of this event and the preparation of materials used for the workshop.

 

A Course in New Media


Photo shows students who participated in the course with their Finnish teachers (right, front row) Nina Ruhkala; (2nd from right, 2nd row), Jussi Arvio, and Qustandi Shomali, PhD (3rd from right, 2nd row).		 A course in New Media was organized by the Communication and Media Center of Diaconia Polytechnic (Finland) in cooperation with Bethlehem University and Radio Bethlehem 2000. The six-week course was organized in September and October 2000 for students taking the Journalism Minor program at Bethlehem University. The students learned how to manipulate pictures in digital format and design Internet pages.
