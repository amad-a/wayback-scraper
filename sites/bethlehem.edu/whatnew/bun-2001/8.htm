Br. The Road of "Forty Pillars": From Bethlehem University to the Church of the Nativity in Manger Square
Seventeen artists of six nationalities (from France, Germany, Morocco, Japan, Italy and England) sculpted blocs of monolithic stone 3 to 3.5 tons each, and 250 cm. high which were extracted from Bethlehem. The pillars have been placed on an itinerary inside the old town of Bethlehem which starts on the road from Bethlehem University to the Church of the Nativity. These pillars were presented at Strasbourg in Place de Chateaux in December 1999. Fourteen pillars were sent to Bethlehem and three were left in Strasbourg to show the symbolic relationship between Bethlehem and Strasbourg. Photos below show 3 of the 14 pillars in three different landmarks dubbed metaphorically as "The Road of Forty Pillars". For more information, see web site <www.bethlehem-city.org>
