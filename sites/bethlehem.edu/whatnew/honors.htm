Awards of Excellence
May 3, 2001

The Awards of Excellence Ceremony was held at Bethlehem University in honor of 367 students.  It is interesting to note that 22% of those who received highest grades were females.

In his opening remarks, Brother Neil Kieffe, FSC, Vice President for Academic Affairs stated that this is a very special day; the third time that the Award of Excellence Ceremony is being held, but this year the political circumstances are difficult, so he congratulated the students on being able to excel despite the shooting and bombardment.  He announced that pictures of the honored students will be posted in two locations on campus; thus emphasizing the importance of academic excellence at Bethlehem University.

Brother Neil also acknowledged the financial contribution of donors who are making education possible at this University.  He acknowledged with appreciation the financial help and all other aspects of assistance B.U. is receiving from its friends.

He also noted the special donor who granted J.D. 1 per credit hour to every student, at an approximate total of J.D. 30 per student.  An anonymous donor contributed J.D. 60,000 in order to help reduce the cost of education.

This year, the Fareedeh Aby Dayyeh-Al Budeiri Nursing Scholarship was awarded to Mr. Mohammad Khleif.  On behalf of Mr. Jamil Al Budeiri, Mr. Hani Abyu Dayyeh offered the Scholarship, and stated reading from a text written by Mr. Budeiri:

A long time ago, I studied at the Arab College in Jerusalem, and I learnt to start my address with a positive note, but this time because of difficult circumstances I have to say that I am unable to be with you.  He stated that the Scholarship is not about money but rather about accomplishment, and he expressed hope that God will lead the students into becoming leaders who promote a free and democratic Palestine.  Mr. Al Budeiri added that his mother was a pioneer in her accomplishments; as a Palestinian Arab, and as a woman who traveled to Beirut in 1915 to study there.

At the end of the Awards of Excellence Ceremony, the audience was entertained to a musical presentation by the University Choir led by Sister Patricia Crockford.
