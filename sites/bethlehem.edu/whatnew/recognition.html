De La Salle Day

On May 15, Bethlehem University celebrated De La Salle Day.  The Christian Brothers, along with the faculty and staff came together in a Day of worship, celebration, and thank-giving for the many blessing which Bethlehem University brought to the City of Bethlehem.

 

Particularly special this year was the 50th anniversary of the brotherhood of Brother Cyril Litecky, Assistant to the Vice Chancellor; Brother Myron Collins, Chemistry Professor; along with the 25th anniversary of the Brother Thomas Houde, Drama Professor.

Following the mass, everybody got together in a ceremony to honor faculty and staff for their many years of service at the University.  34 Members of the faculty and staff were honored during De La Salle Day.

 

Nasra Dahdal, honored by Brother Vincent Malham, President / Vice Chancellor, for her 25 years of service at Bethlehem University.

Afterwards, everybody had lunch in the cafeteria.  All had a great time!!
