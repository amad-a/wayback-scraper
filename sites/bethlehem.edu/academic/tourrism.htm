INSTITUTE OF HOTEL MANAGEMENT AND TOURISM

DIPLOMA TOURISM MANAGEMENT

 

Bethlehem University is participating in a cooperative educational venture , the Tourism for Peace Program, with several other institutions of higher education around the Mediterranean Basin.

This program is under the umbrella of UNESCO's University of Culture and Tourism for Peace. The participating institutions are: Bethlehem University (Palestine); Ben Gurion University (Israel); the Institute International de Formation au Tourism (Marseille, France); the ecole de Management (Nice, France); and the Institute des Hautes studes de Management (Morocco).

The fundamental principal behind Bethlehem University's Tourism for Peace program is to offer integrated training courses in the professions of tourism which will give each student the necessary basic knowledge and skills in: physical layout and organization; facilitation requirements; and management.

Tourism for Peace Program is a three year program of studies and internships. For the first two years, the teaching aspect lasts for six months followed by an internship period of two to four months. These internships must take place in tourism related industries outside the country of the student. During the final year, students will follow courses for four months. Then for six months they will be required to create, develop and organized a tourism-related project - such as to design, develop, manage and produce a musical festival or a folklore festival.

Another project could be the planning and management of the physical layout of an ecomuseum or the site of a landmark and the conditions of access to that landmark. Students will be required to solve all the related issues, including the actual financing of the project.


What makes this program unique is that all participating institutions are connected via satellite interactive video-conferencing. The satellite communications system used in the Tourism for Peace Program is EUTELSAT with headquarters in Paris, France. Approximately half the courses are taught through interactive video-conferencing with each institution being responsible for two or three courses.

Description of the Satellite Transmission System between Participants in the Tourism for Peace Program.

Students and instructors in each institution are able to see and communicate with each other in real time when the classes are in session. This method gives the students the opportunity to see, react to, and communicate with students, professors, and professionals of other countries.

Each institution is able to have experts and professionals in the tourism industry "come to" their institutions (via interactive video-conferencing) as instructors for their students. The remainder of the courses are taught on campus by professors of Bethlehem University.

Upon successful completion of the program students will receive diplomas or degrees from their own institutions as well as an international degree from the University of Culture and Tourism for Peace with the endorsement of UNESCO and the WTO (World Tourism Organization).
