THE AUDIOVISUAL CENTER

INTRODUCTION :

The Audiovisual Center, established in 1978, is a special facility for the faculty, staff and students of Bethlehem University. The Center is located in the basement of Bethlehem University library. It offers a wide range of audiovisual, production and support services to the academic community. The collection consists primarily of nonbook materials and equipments to support the instructional programs.

COLLECTIONS :

A.V. Materials :

The Audiovisual Center collects the following materials:

  • Audiotapes;
  • CD-ROMs;
  • Filmstrips;
  • Maps;
  • Microfiches;
  • Microfilms;
  • Motion pictures;
  • Multimedia kits;
  • Overhead transparencies;
  • Phonograph records;
  • Reel-to-reel tapes;
  • Slides;
  • Videocassettes.

The A.V. materials can be identified, retrieved, and accessed by using:

  1. The A.V. card catalog : It lists materials held by the AVC and is divided into two sections : author/title and subject. It is located in the basement, near the Special Collections Room.
  2. Listing of video titles : This list is arranged alphabetically by subject matter.

A.V. SERVICES :

The A.V.C. provides a variety of services:

Apple Macintosh Computer :

The Center has an Apple Macintosh SE and laser printer. Faculty and Staff members can make use of this equipment to produce curriculum - related materials. A fee is charged to the department for printouts. Reservations are required in advance.

Reserve shelf :

Faculty place listening or viewing assignments for their classes. These materials are kept in the A.V.C. and are arranged by course number.

Graphic Design & Photographic Services :

The A.V.C. has the basic facilities to produce some graphic materials, including overhead transparencies and visuals. A limited range of photographic work is also carried out by the Center. Standard services include general photography and slide production for teaching.

Instructional & Reference Services :

Orientation tours, instructional sessions and workshops are conducted by the A.V.C. for new faculty and students. A.V.C staff members also assist users in finding information and materials.

Playback services :

The A.V.C. provides equipment for playback of many different audiovisual formats, including CD-ROMs, videocassettes, slides, filmstrips, audiotapes, pictures, records and transparencies.

A playback room (L3) is available and equipped to display a variety of nonprint formats. It accomodates 55 people. This room may be reserved by faculty members and students when a media program is an integral part of a class presentation.

Recording/Duplication Services :

The A.V.C. may record special lectures in audio or video format. Duplication service provides single or multiple copies of pre-recorded programs. These services are restricted to faculty members only.

CD-ROM Service :

The A.V.C. is equipped with a CD-ROM player and a small collection of CD-ROM titles are available for teachers and students.

BORROWING PRIVILEGES :

Faculty, staff and students may borrow audiovisual materials and equipment and use them either in the A.V.C. or on campus. Reservations for equipment and playback room (L3) should be made 3 days in advance of the date of use. They may be made by filling out the A.V. reservation form available in the A.V.C.

LOAN PERIODS :

Students can borrow certain pieces of equipment / A.V. materials only for the day with the presentation of a valid library card. All items must be used in Room L3, in the A.V.C or on campus. They should be returned not later than 3:30 p.m.

A.V.C. HOURS :

Monday - Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
