DEPARTMENT OF THE HUMANITIES

The Department of the Humanities offers eight programs which are available to students in all Faculties. Courses constitute a major part of the liberal arts component of the Bethlehem University education.

The Department does not offer degrees. It offers general electives and faculty-required courses.
