Mission

Profile

Dr. Marwan Kishek is a self-taught Artist. Trained as a Civil Engineer, he pursued higher education and obtained his Ph.D. from Cambridge University, England. A former University Professor and now a Consultant Engineer, he regularly practices oil painting in his spare time. His paintings speak for themselves.
