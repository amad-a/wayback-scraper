Palestinian IT Firms Mobilized for GITEX 2001

 

Ramallah, Palestine - The Palestinian Information Technology Association for Companies (PITA) will sponsor the Palestinian Pavilion @ GITEX 2001 (at Hall 8). PITA’s presence at GITEX will highlight the notable growth and momentum of the Palestinian IT sector, and showcase the vast number of new IT business opportunities in Palestine for the international investor.  The following members of PITA will be exhibit at GITEX 2001 (alphabetically):

 

Arab Technology System (www.ats-pal.com)

Founded in 1994, ATS is the largest software house in Palestine, with a strong presence in the public and private sectors.  Principal products/services include: Oracle Applications (Financial), Jdeveloper, JFP, Designer, ORACLE-8i.  ATS is an Oracle certified VAD, Oracle Approved Education Center, a CISCO Reseller, and Novell Business Partner.  ATS will exhibit a Municipality Management System; University Admission & Registration System; Archiving System (DACS); Health Care System; Human Resources Management System; Billing System and ERP System.
