Palestinian IT Firms Mobilized for GITEX 2001

 

 

Ramallah, Palestine – The Palestinian Information Technology Association for Companies (PITA) announced today its sponsorship of the Palestinian Pavilion @ GITEX 2001.  GITEX 2001 is the premier Middle East IT trade exposition to be held October 14-18 this year in Dubai.  PITA’s presence at GITEX will highlight the notable growth and momentum of the Palestinian IT sector, and showcase the various new IT business opportunities in Palestine for the international investor.

 

PITA’s Chairman, Ibrahim Barham, states, “A defining characteristic of the IT industry worldwide, and particularly here in Palestine, is its ability to look beyond current realities and to chart a course towards the future.  As one of Palestine’s pioneer industry trade associations, PITA represents Palestinian firms from every corner of the IT spectrum.   Our members understand the critical importance of establishing external market linkages at this crossroads of development.  GITEX 2001 will provide our members direct access to new markets, while at the same time opening doors for international firms willing to explore the many possibilities of Palestine.  No other Palestinian economic sector is as uniquely poised for growth as information technology.  At GITEX, Palestine’s IT sector will firmly establish itself as one of the international IT community’s new business arenas.”

 

The Palestinian Pavilion @ GITEX 2001 will highlight a number of bold new infrastructure, human resource and technology initiatives currently underway in Palestine.

 

GITEX is the largest and most successful event of its kind in the Middle East and  it has firmly established itself as the premier exhibition for computing and communication systems and applications dedicated to the IT industry and the entire business environment.

 

PITA is a membership-based private sector organization representing IT companies in Palestine. It was established in 1998, and is registered with the Palestinian Ministry of Economy & Trade. PITA’s membership comprises software, hardware, systems integrators, telecommunication, office equipment and consulting companies. Its headquarters is located in Ramallah, Palestine.  Contact PITA by telephone at 970-2-296-1988, by fax at 970-2-296-4121 or by email at info@pita-palestine.org.
