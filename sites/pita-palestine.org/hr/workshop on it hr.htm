Workshop on IT HR
23rd May 2001


A presentation by Qais Saleh of MCS and moderated by Sam Bahour and Marwan Tarazi was made to make an initial introduction of the HR IT Framework Study that was recently completed. The main points that were raised and discussed are:

  • No definitions of IT professionals formal qualifications

 

  • 30% of the Palestinian population are in the school system at any one time, hence adjustment to the education system is most important for greatest impact

 

  • Palestinian economy lacks the critical mass of IT professionals to have any meaningful IT development. This is a reality that we need to address and not cover with meaningless bean counting.

 

  • No "pipeline" between public and private sector, thus the education sector was chosen to be the focal point for reform, in order words creating a pipeline that can produce qualified graduates for the market

 

  • PITA can have input in the development of IT industry in many ways, first step is to put a private sector position paper and instigate policy makers toward the creation of a multi-ministerial commission that would include PITA with the goal of being the central focal point for all levels of education with regards to IT issues, curriculum, facilities, programs, etc.

 

  • Also PITA can work through technical forums such as the tri-party committee of Education, Higher Education and Labour ministries on vocational training

 

The private (PITA) sector position paper should include

 

  • Education is a high priority especially in a resistance economy

 

  • The formation of national body for the formulation and implementation of a
    national plan on IT and on which the private sector should participate

 

  • The cooperation of private, public and NGO sectors in short term IT
    programmes to upgrade graduates. This is not to skip by the current workforce considering the lack of a national strategy or government policy on IT

 

  • Raising IT awareness and its importance through out the Palestinian
    society. This will requires the cooperation of all segments of the community

 

  • Creation of the right environment for IT through the cooperation between
    government, Educational and training institutions and the private sector

 

  • Creating a thriving local IT labour pool is to a large degree the government responsibility
