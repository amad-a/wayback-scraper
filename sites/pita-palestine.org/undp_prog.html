Graduates employment program in the IT Industry
 

 
           Supported by : UNDP Programme of Assistance to the Palestinian People (PAPP) 
   As part of PITA commitment to the development of the IT sector in Palestine and making IT the pillar of the Palestinian economy, PITA in cooperation with the UNDP, invite the Palestinian private businesses in West Bank and Gaza, Jerusalem included to apply for fund to employ recent graduate (Year 2000 graduate and afterward) in the IT sector. The objectives of this program are as follows:

To increase the number of professionals employed in the IT industry and related support services within the West Bank and Gaza this will help IT companies overcome present economic problems specially small companies which are most affected by the Israeli blockage of the Palestinian territories.

To target new markets for the Palestinian IT industry through entry of new computer programmers and the development of sector marketing skills.

Increasing the number of IT professionals in Palestine as a long term objective, this project is a small step in this direction

To manage this project, please fill the application form (two different forms 1 for members and other is for non-members) and fax it to PITA ASAP, We will accept email attachments of the filled forms as long as it to be mailed from the company official email account and PITA staff will make a confirmation telephone call on receiving the digital form.

As a leader of IT sector, PITA offers this opportunity for both members and non-members but a fee of $50 for members and $100 for non-members will be charged for each successful application.


Initially PITA will accept no more than two applications from any one company.

The scope of operation and the general guide lines will be as follows:

Stage 1

a) A PITA committee will process all the applications and decide on all applications.


b) Successful applications will be notified and will be invited to sign contracts with PITA. The contract will have the following:

(1) Application forms as filled by the company

(2) Period of training

(3) Monthly payments to the graduate of $ 300 maximum , the training company commitment of minimum monthly salary of $600 inclusive of the fund contribution

(4) Any special condition and requirements which will include regular reporting to PITA

Stage 2

If companies require special skills which are obtained through specialized training and certification from outside training institute, the following procedure will be adopted:

a) All companies entering the first stage of funding will be notified about this 2nd stage funding.

b) All trainees after the second month of training will have access to funding for enrolling into a specialized training institute acceptable to PITA.

c) The companies can apply for up to $1000 support to wards the fees.

d) On PITA acceptance of supporting paying fees to any graduate, the company applying for this support must guarantee one year employment for the graduate after completing the course.


All applications will be dealt with on the bases of first come first serve while the fund lasts
