COMDEX 2002 Jeddah    

A pavilion but no delegation

After several months of hard preparations for Comdex Jeddah 2002, which took place on 1-4 April 2002, PITA delegation was not able to participate, a delegation consisting of at least 40 persons, among them the representatives of 8 exhibiting companies could not leave to Jeddah due to the Israeli last incursion of Ramallah.

The participation was planned to introduce the Palestinian IT capabilities & skills and to promote the Palestinian innovative software products which are tailored for the Arabic users.

Despite the absence of the Palestinian Delegation, brochures and promotional material were distributed by the only person who could make it to Jeddah; Mr. Sadeq Tawfiq from Ellam Tam, PITA contracted Media and PR Company.
