Gitex Dubai 2002

People and Potential

 

 Photo: PITA and Paltrade representatives honor Mr. Ibrahim Al-Hashimi, Acting Director for the DWTC continuous support.

After several months of hard preparations for Gitex Dubai 2002, PITA’s delegation consisting of at least 50 persons participated in this important regional business gathering.  Eight exhibiting companies as well as 6 visiting companies made an outstanding representation of the Palestinian ICT sector.  In addition, the official representation of the Palestinian minister of post & telecom has added value to the Palestinian presence in Gitex. 

The event was jointly organized and represented by PITA & Paltrade through a signed cooperation agreement that was aimed to facilitate the Palestinian participation on over a 100 SQM Palestinian Pavilion.  The Executive Director thanked DWTC in a letter on behalf of PITA BoD and members for donating the space free of charge to organize the Palestinian Pavilion.  In addition, Ellam Tam media and PR company – PITA contractors- and Al-Nasher Advertising Agency have played a major role in attracting attention to the Palestinian delegation and organized several important meetings with key figures in the ICT business area. 

By showing a high standard professional business attitude, the Palestinian companies have successfully managed to facilitate their previously developed marketing tools to attract business opportunities and create contracts and long term international business relations.

Palestine@Gulf,

More Success 2 Come

 Even in these troubled times, the Palestinian IT sector continues to survive and expand its market presence which was demonstrated by the official launching of PITA office, Palestine@Gulf at Dubai Internet City (DIC) which proved another outstanding success.

 The official opening was conducted on 13/Oct/2002 at the Dubai Internet City Pavilion at Gitex as an official event for DIC with the attendance of the Palestinian Minister of Telecommunication, the DIC CEO, Islamic Development Bank (IDB) Representative and PITA Chairman. The press conference was attended by many local & international media representatives and occupied the highlight spot of the 1st day of many Gitex activities. Special recognition was given to those who supported this office to become a reality.

 The Office is now setup and operational, fully equipped and ready for the new Regional Business Development Manager to begin the work, this initiative has been strategically supported by DAI/MAP program and Paltrade. MAP as partners has given financial and technical support to the establishment of the office.

The PR & networking plan for the next 6 months is now underway to ensure proper positioning of the Office and PITA in the Gulf and to facilitate the image building of the Palestinian ICT sector in that region.  As a result, the Islamic Development Bank has announced its support to the Palestine@Gulf office with more than $45,000.

As a result of successful launch, PITA is getting increasing inquiries from companies who are interested to join P@G and benefit from its services.  Some P@G member companies recently started operations in the next period will concentrate on building the Office systems, service programs and promotional tools and leveraging the existing network for marketing of the Palestinian ICT products.

GITEX Dubai 2002 (In the News) Exhibitors Activities

“Gitex was Palestinian this year” Gitex Dubai 2002 showed a solid and fruitful participation for Palestinian ICT companies that have demonstrated high quality products and services for regional & international markets. These Products and Services offered by pioneer Palestinian companies such as Castle Est. Company, Bisan Systems Ltd., Jaffa.Net Computer Systems, Sidata Information and Communication Systems, Asal Technologies, Siemens Information and Communication Technologies Ltd., Borders for Integrated Systems & Telecom, and Arab Technology System (ATS) were presented to private & public institutions in a well prepared marketing and technical layout.

The wide range of appealing products that varied from custom made applications to professional & specialized software and hardware received high attention from the business community and translated into solid distribution & business agreements.  The main issue highlighted and focused on was showing the competitive advantage of the Palestinian products in the region.  In addition, interests for outsourcing software development projects were expressed by many Gulf ICT companies.

The exhibitors expressed their satisfaction of the organization of their participation and the outcomes of this important event.  They also showed high attention & interest to participate in such important events in the future to maintain their presence in the external markets.  

 It is apparent that the developed opportunities will flourish in the future reflecting high standard business transactions and contracts for the benefit of the Palestinian ICT sector.  The burden now falls on the Palestinian ICT companies to continue to develop local products and skills in order to maintain the acquired success and attend even better results for their interests.

Q&A with PITA’s Executive Director

 PITA Newsletter has met PITA Executive Director, Dr. Mashhour Abudaka after his return from Dubai after participating  in Gitex Dubai 2002.

 Q1: How do you evaluate PITA’s participation in Gitex this year compared with year 2001?

A1: It was good in a sense it was productive and we in PITA managed to convey our message that we are still alive and innovative. Gitex press paid attention to us and we were the stars of all national pavilions. Last year was a learning experience for PITA and for the exhibitors, we have avoided many of last year mistakes, this year we were more focused and to a certain degree knew what we want.

 Q2: What mistakes you have avoided this year?

A2: As PITA, the organization was more developed; the Pavilion was ready on time. Our message to press was unified and gave more attention this year to our members’ interest. 

 Q3: You mean it was trouble free exhibition?

A3: No, we will never get this in a situation like ours. The curfews, the closures the siege, crossing the borders to Jordan and Egypt has contributed to our planning process. We can not plan for few hours let alone to plan for this kind of exhibition and when we had 60 members delegation. We plan for crises all the time.

Q4: What about the companies’ achievements?

A4: PITA does not get involved in the business deals that our members conduct, however when talking to representatives of the exhibiting companies during and after the exhibition, in general they are all happy.

 Q5: Can you put a value on deals made during Gitex?

A5: No, unfortunately companies are not frank when it comes to this. Some even refuse to make any statement in this regard. This depresses me because we can not quantify our success.

 Q6: What are you going to do about those who are not taking Gitex seriously?

A6: Since Gitex was and may will be heavily subsidized in the future by us, PITA will consider not to make participation in this important event open for any business who want to exhibit. We may have to work on mechanism to dictate where our subsidy is best invested. 

 Q7: What is the next marketing activity PITA is planning?

A7: We are working hard to enter the Saudi market, its capitalization worth 70% of all the Arab Gulf market. We must be in this market and our members wanted to be in the Saudi market. There are contacts with various economic entities in Saudi Arabia; I think we will be able to organize a trade mission soon as well as participating in next Gitex in Riyadh or Comdex Jeddah. Comdex has offered 100 sqm free for PITA to off set last year failed mission, PITA is considering both exhibitions and will only participate in the more feasible one for its members. 

 Q8: It looks like that Gitex 2002 is a success for PITA, How did you manage to make as such and what it takes?

A8: obviously a great deal of hard work not only by PITA staff but range of others, PITA BoD was involved all the way, we also have built a network of partners and we made it that our concerns are theirs too. Paltrade was the main partner and made concrete efforts by staff and Board to facilitate successful exhibition. DAI team worked hard before and during the exhibition to help staff and exhibiting companies prepare for the events. The Palestinian expatriate community in Dubai was at hand and provided all the support we needed to build business networks. Our subcontractors, Al-Nashir and Ellam Tam have professionally worked hard and around the clock.  Finally I must add here the Dubai World Trade Centre staff and DIC staff have helped us tremendously to make our work efforts succeed.
