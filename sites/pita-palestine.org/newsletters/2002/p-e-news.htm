In the news

PITA office is blockaded by Israeli Tanks

Since early December 2001 we did not have access to our offices in Ersal Street Ramallah, this due to Israeli blockade and the permanent presence of Israeli tanks and soldiers in and around the building which houses our offices.

Recently two of the occupants of the building, namely the Ministry of Ex-detainees and Global Management consultant, were able to gain entry to their offices in the building. Both have reported  broken doors and windows, ransacking of office, files, materials and furniture and most worrying to us is the theft of office hardware, such as PC, laptops, telephone and fax equipment … etc

Ten days ago PITA went to the DCO offices in Ramallah (the Palestinian-Israeli liaison office) to obtain a permission to enter our offices but so far we are unsuccessful.

We still not sure what will be left in the office, as it has been reported to us that the door of our office in broken and is wide open.

However, PITA operation has continued as normal as it can be possible, most files are stored in soft forms and retrived from other location but others such as financial accounting files are not accessible.

Seminar program, IT specialist training program, student training (See training programs in this Issue) $140K Training programs from the UNDP As part of its program for the development of IT human resources, PITA has signed two memorandums of understanding (MoU) with the UNDP to organized training programs for IT graduates. The first is to retrain employees of IT companies in Gaza. Five courses are offered which are on Cisco, Linux I, Linux II and A+( General Technician ). 60 employees in Gaza are to benefit from the above courses. Training has already started early this month.

The second program runs in all of the PNA territories including Jerusalem, through which IT and IT related companies (members and non-members) are encouraged to employ recent graduates in IT disciplines. PITA will meet half the salary of $300 for each graduate up to 3 months and renewable to further 3 months. If businesses see that the graduate requires further formal training off the job, then PITA will grant up to $1000 towards this formal training in exchange the company offers this graduate employment for one year.

Application forms have already in process, the first week of February the first group is hoped to start their on job training. Its hoped that 70 un-employed graduates will benefit from this program.
