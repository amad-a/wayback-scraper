   DAI / MAP     

     Market Access Program

     The Market Access Program (MAP) is a project funded by the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) and implemented by Development Alternatives Inc (DAI). 

The objective of MAP is to increase the competitiveness of the Palestinian private sector in order to expand economic growth and increase market share, sales, exports, and employment opportunities.

To accomplish this objective, MAP will provide technical assistance to strengthen the capacity of business organizations to furnish a range of business development and policy advocacy services to their members.  

MAP’s mission is to build new Palestinian models of business leadership and organizations that:

·        Advocate business-enabling policies and mechanisms through public and private sector cooperation;

·        Develop competitive products and services to increase market access for Palestinian business; and,

·        Create a market place through associations for business services to improve business performance

UNDP|PAPP   United Nation Development Programme | (Programme of Assistence to the Palestinian People)

      In December 1978 the General Assembly of the United Nations called upon the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) to undertake development activities on behalf of the Palestinian people. As a result, the UNDP Programme of Assistance to the Palestinian People (UNDP/PAPP) became operational. From modest early beginnings, UNDP/PAPP is now a leading development organization and has gained considerable experience through its two decades of efforts to assist the Palestinian people through periods of occupation, civil unrest, and the establishment of the Palestinian Authority. It currently has over $145 million in ongoing projects active in every part of the West Bank and Gaza Strip and a staff of 130, principally Palestinian employees.
