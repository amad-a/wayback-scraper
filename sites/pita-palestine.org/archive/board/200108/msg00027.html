> Hi Ibrahim,
>
> Thank you for your prompt reply. Following are quick answers to your two
> queries:
>
> 'Leased/used from PalTel only" means that ISP's who wish to work with,
> partner with PalTel should only use PalTel provided facilities. Meaning
> Leased lines, dial-up facilities, Internet Access etc. Meaning they should
> not use Israeli, or any other Telco facilities. That includes but is not
> limited to Internet Access via Satellites.
>
> PalTel does not intend to provide Internet Access Services to End Users of
> ISP's that cooperate/ work with/Partner with PalTel. That should and will
be
> the ISP's business to deal with their End Users. PalTel's Customers shall
be
> only the ISP's. Internet End Users in turn will deal with their respective
> ISP's.
>
>
> Ibrahim, I really would like to start the process of face to face
meetings,
> to discuss and dialogue such queries and a myriad of others. Clarify
issues,
> elaborate on others etc. That will be a lot faster.

> start.

11th.
