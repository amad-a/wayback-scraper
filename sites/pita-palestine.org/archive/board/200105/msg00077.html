PITA also recommends that ISPs adopt the following strategy:
  • To work with conjection with Paltel through various promotions, advertisemens, price reductions and so on in bringing the Internet subscribers in a time frame to be agreed upon with Paltel in excess of 30K.

  • ISPs do not compete in IP telephony with Paltel.

  • Paltel Infrastructure to be used exclusively. Including using Paltel as the
  • Int'l Gateways for ANY ISP services, on condition that Paltel reduce prices and increase technical support standards to comparable level with existing providers. This should be done on a phase out period. PITA also recommend that
      1. Both parties agree to this startegy

      2. Paltel and ISPs put their acceptance to this policy in writing and in public statement.

      3. Paltel and ISPs submit a plan that clearly specify the time table for enforcing the strategy and methods used to excute and enforce the strateg.
