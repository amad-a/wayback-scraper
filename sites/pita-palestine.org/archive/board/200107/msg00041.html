Dear Jameel

I agree with you about our partnership with the industrial association, but
we have to make it officially through a general assembly meeting in the near
future. And about the meeting itself, I can see the following results:

1- The great and respectful response from our members to all the non
valuable discussion from the minister and his colleagues, and I am thinking
of sending a big thanks to all the members for such response.
2- We should insist that PITA is an independent association, and works hard
to have good relations with the other private sector associations.
3- To be always diplomatic enough to protect our members interest, which
means to be neutral in our relations with the ministers, but when it comes
to protect our interests, we should be very clear such as what we were
yesterday and with Mr. Sha'ath last week and with Mr. Falouji before.
4- Our expected meeting with Mr. Masri should be very solid and to the point
where we could achieve our intersects.
