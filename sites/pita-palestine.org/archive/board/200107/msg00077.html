Ibrahim,

Thank you for your correspondence and your kind words.

I included your attached document as a refresher and a recap. Also to put
this subject into context.

I attached as per your request a Draft Working Paper outlining the framework
that I discussed with a number of your colleagues.

I believe we are all in synch and on the same page in terms of our common
objectives and how to get there.

There remains a few topics that require a dialogue, preferably face to face,
to iron out the details of our common understandings and basis for
cooperation. These include:

- Mechanisms/Processes for tracking our programs, achievements and targets.
- Agree on joint Public Awareness, Public Relations and Advertising
Programs.
- Incentives/Rewards for the better achievers. Call it Volume
Discounts....etc...together I am sure we can be very creative.

I look forward to the opportunity to having such a dialogue to finalize
matters to our mutual, and the End User's, benefits.

Ibrahim, please advise your colleagues about this correspondence and let's
try to expedite events and get the ball rolling. There are many challenges
and opportunities facing us all that are just waiting to be addressed.

My best Regards,

Abdel Rahman Omar
PalTel EVP for Tech Affairs.
