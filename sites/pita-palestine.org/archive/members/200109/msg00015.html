Subject:  Call for Proposals: infoDev Country Gateway Grants


In September 2000, infoDev announced a Call for Proposals for grants to assist groups in planning for the creation of Country Gateways in partnership with the Development Gateway. infoDev allocated $1.8 million for this program in FY 01
(July 2000-June 2001). By the end of April 2001, infoDev had received 71 proposals from 51 countries, and had approved funding for 32 proposals.

infoDev is pleased to announce a new Call for Proposals for Planning and Implementation Grants to assist groups for the creation of Country Gateways.

For both Planning and Implementation Grants, proponents should demonstrate the active involvement of stakeholders from civil society, government, and the private sector. They should link the activity clearly to development priorities and issues within the country and indicate how the activity will contribute to development.

For more information visit:
http://www.infodev.org/gateway/
