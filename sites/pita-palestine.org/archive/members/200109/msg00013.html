                   WHERE THE GLOBAL I.T. INDUSTRY MEETS

GITEX - THE LARGEST I.T. EXHIBITION IN THE MIDDLE EAST AND ONE OF THE TOP
FIVE IN THE WORLD NOW COMES TO HYDERABAD, INDIA.

Dubai  World  Trade  Centre L.L.C., the leading exhibition organiser in the
Middle  East,  under  the  patronage  of Government of Dubai (Department of
Economic  Development)  and  in  association  with the Government of Andhra
Pradesh,  announces the inaugural GITEX HYDERABAD EXHIBITION AND CONFERENCE
2002 to be held at HYDERABAD, INDIA from 7th - 9th January 2002.

Established as the premier exhibition for the Information and Communication
Technology  industry, GITEX Dubai, is the largest and most successful event
of its kind in the Middle East. Due to its immense success, the GITEX brand
expanded into wider regional markets in 1999, and is now staging successful
exhibitions  in  Cairo,  Egypt  and Beirut, Lebanon.  From 2002, GITEX will
bring  its  unique  and  proven  format to Hyderabad, India at the Infocity
venue in Hyderabad.

GITEX - the Middle East's premier IT exhibitions

·   GITEX Dubai - October 14 -18, 2001, October 13 ? 17, 2002
·   GITEX Cairo - April 3 - 6, 2002
·   GITEX Riyadh ? 21 - 25 April, 2002
·   Termium GITEX Beirut - September 18 - 23, 2001

Dubai World Trade Centre and the Government of Andhra Pradesh are committed
to  making  GITEX  Hyderabad  a resounding success.  The size of the Indian
market  and  the  Andhra  Pradesh  Government's keen interest in developing
economic growth through Information and Communication Technology means that
GITEX Hyderabad will be a truly world class IT Show.

It   is   universally  acknowledged  that  India  enjoys  a  unique  global
comparative   advantage  as  a  prime  location  for  the  information  and
communication  technology industry.  The primary strength of the country in
this  sector  is its resource base of highly cost-effective and technically
skilled  manpower.   India  has  enjoyed a 64% export growth rate and 34.7%
domestic growth rate in 2000 ? 2001.

Furthermore,  Andhra  Pradesh  (and  Hyderabad  in particular) has become a
preferred  destination  for  the  global  ICT  industry.   State statistics
confirm  that over 26% of Indian IT professionals in the USA originate from
Andhra  Pradesh,  and  the  state  aims  to  capture about 30% of the $ 1.3
trillion market for ICT enabled services in India by 2008.


Dubai  and  Hyderabad  have  a  great  deal in common as far as new economy
initiatives  are concerned.  The Government of Dubai has developed exciting
initiatives  including  Dubai  Internet City, Dubai Ideas Oasis plus a wide
ranging  number  of e-government strategies, which has seen Dubai emerge as
the  IT hub of the region. Hyderabad has similar initiatives in the form of
Hi-Tec  City,  a  number  of  dedicated  software  parks and a programme of
e-government activity.

GITEX  Hyderabad will play a key role in enhancing the interactions between
various  ICT  companies and professionals from around the world, signifying
the  beginning  of  a seamless flow of ideas, initiatives and professionals
between Hyderabad and the global ICT community.

opportunities unlimited @ Hyderabad

Andhra  Pradesh is moving rapidly from tradition to modernity.  To attain a
position   of  leadership  and  excellence  in  the  information  age,  the
Government  identified  several drivers, such as people, infrastructure and
lifestyle.  In each of these areas, the public private partnership model is
being  used  to  synergise  the strengths of the Government and the private
sector  to  attain  the  best  results.  The presence of  1,200 global I.T.
giants  including  Microsoft  and Oracle amongst many other in Hyderabad is
testimony to this.

The Andhra Pradesh Government, as a result, has created a business-friendly
environment in the state, backed by a series of IT Incentives policies that
offer many incentives to software companies, including land at no cost.

The  State  also  reviewed  the  major  Acts and Rules that regulate the IT
industry,  and  in  most  cases,  has  either  done  away  with  procedures
completely or has introduced a self-certification process.

Low-cost real estate and strong political will make Andhra Pradesh the most
preferred destination for IT majors from around the world.

An infrastructure designed for business success

Having  recognised  that a significant pre-requisite in fostering IT growth
is  dedication  to building the required infrastructure, the Andhra Pradesh
Government  planned  meticulously in this area.  This devotion to planning,
followed  by a single-minded, dedicated approach to implementing a suitable
infrastructure, means that Andhra Pradesh is now eminently placed as one of
world's foremost IT locations.
