Hi Mahhour,
I am just wondering why nothing else can be done at this time. HP is a
member of PITA and should immediately be asked to provide an
explanation.
This is a real test for PITA. Is PITA going to act when needed against
practices that hinder the development of the sector. OR is PITA going to
be another 3rd world country organization that provides lip services
only while its members (HP and others) are working against the benefits
of the sector which they claim they would like to develop for their own
good.
I  warn, that if this issue goes unchecked then all other international
companies will refrain from even looking at Palestine as we will all be
"labeled".
Please remember that this issue comes about when all donor countries
(and even Arab countries) are restricting their help to us on the basis
that we are corrupt. This case shows that international companies are
adding to the corruption. I propose that we send an urgent message to
the American consulate (commercial section) and inform them of the issue
and ask them to provide clear directions to American companies to work
in Palestine in accordance to a clear code of business ethics. We should
also forward this letter to all other international and Arab commercial
sections in Palestine.  BUT most importantly, HP as a member should be
asked to clear its position or be expelled from PITA in a way that
clears our reputation. What would be the position of Paltel, which we
all fight its monopoly ( as an impediment to the development of the
sector)?  If they see a member of PITA engaged in such practices? DO we
owe Patel an apology?

Guys, we need to act fast.

The above is only a personal opinion.

Tareq
