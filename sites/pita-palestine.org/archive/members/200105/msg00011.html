Dear All,

Outsourcing could be a way out for the Palestinian IT sector especially
during these rough times. However, and out of personal experience, there is
an important pre-requisite for outsourcing services that American and
European firms require, and this is mainly certification - for Systems and
Software Engineering.
Qualifying for certification requires hard work and may take a long time. I
suggest that we start thinking along these lines to be effective in this
endeavor. I have more information about this subject should anyone require
it.
