Dear Valuable Member;

As part of its services for year 2001, PITA announces the Business Planning training courses:

"Designing Roadmaps for Success"

The overall goal is to support the Palestinian ICT sector by helping companies to address key business challenges.

The training course will address the need and fundamentals of Business Planning with in-depth concentration on Financial & Marketing Management. The course will have case studies and also the option to have a per-company private consultation with the consultant. The company may choose take advantage of the private meeting in any way.

The course will be delivered by PITA in association with DAI over a two-day period.

Dates: Thu 11 Jan 2001 & Sat 13 Jan 2001

Time: 9 Am to 4 PM

Place: Al-Bireh Municipality

Audience: Top Management

Cost [Material Included]:

PITA Members: $80 per person

Non-PITA Members: $140 per person

 

Main Lecturer:

Dr. Bernard O'Sullivan:

Bernard O' Sullivan has worked on business planning and analysis in Europe, Asia and the Middle East. In Asia he worked for HSBC and Bank of China as well as various venture capitalist funds. Recently he has been working in Palestine examining investment and joint venture opportunities on behalf of European companies.
