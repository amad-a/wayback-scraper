Program

PITA Technical Consultancy

Time Frame

Year 2003

Target Group
  • Up to 10 ICT companies.

Description

The main goal of this Technical Consultancy program is to elevate IT companies’ technical expertise, and improve their software development, programming, and management processes,  through a direct in-house consultation provided by an experienced software developer and team leader. 

 

The program is also designed to respond to specific software development issues at IT companies. Those can be defined as a result of the assessment reports or based on PITA’s consultancy analysis to the company needs.

Methodology

· Interested PITA member companies to register for the program with PITA, whereas PITA will authorize the commencement of work with the consultant.

· PITA issues the letter of acceptance,

· The company issues the letter of commencement of work,

· The Scope of Work (SOW) and schedule to be finalized with PITA consultant

· Contracts are signed between the Companies and PITA.

· At the end of each assignment the company is requested to fill out an evaluation form, and the consultant will provide a summary report of the performed tasks, and recommend additional assignments.

Cost & Payment

• PITA member companies pay a reduced rate at ($35/day of 8 hrs) which is subsidized by more than %50 of actual cost.

• Non members rate at ($60/day of 8 hrs) payment are made directly to PITA.

          • The mentioned subsidy will be covered by the DAI/MAP as part of their contribution to the program.
