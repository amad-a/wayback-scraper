Time Frame

Valid until 31/Dec/2003

Description

As part of the Palestinian IT Association (PITA) support to its members and its effort to deliver competitive services to enable market access,  MAP/DAI has been working with PITA on several programs concerning increasing the technical and managerial capacity of the Palestinian ICT companies.

In this program MAP/DAI will help companies to determine their needs and help avail needed resources to implement respective operational improvement programs that are customized for each company.

Attached is a descriptive narrative about the different areas of technical assistance programs that can be offered through this LOA.

Methodology

1.   Interested companies are to register at PITA and pay the registration fee. 

2.   PITA will authorize the commencement of work with MAP.

3.   MAP will conduct a company visit to assess needs.

4.   MAP shall sign individual Letters of Agreements and/or Letter of Engagements with participating companies specifying Scope of Work (SOW), Level of Effort (LOE) needed, Deliverables, terms and conditions, estimated costs and financial subsidy.

Cost & Payment
  • $200 Members (Advance Payment)
  • $300 Non-Members (Advance Payment)
