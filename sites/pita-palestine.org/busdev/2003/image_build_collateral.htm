Program

Image Building / Marketing Collateral Program

Time Frame

Valid until 1 November 2003

Target Group
  • Up to 7 ICT companies.

Description

The Image Building / Marketing Collateral program is designed to assist ICT companies in preparing the content of their marketing and media kits including corporate profile, product profiles and data sheets, stationary, brochures, promotional items, catalogues, multimedia presentations, themes, slogans, taglines, logos, and Web site.  The program will also assist the companies by supervising the design and production of their promotional materials, in addition to providing  financial assistance for the cost of the promotional material.  The program also offers the companies a wide range of services to prepare for exhibitions and trade missions and other marketing related activities.

Methodology
  • Registration for the program with PITA using the attached form.
  • MAP will conduct a company visit to assess needs and a finalized Scope of Work (SOW) with deliverables and estimate of implementation cost.   
  • Scope of Work, deliverables and Program Cost approved by the company.
  • Company payment of set program fees.   
  • PITA will authorize the commencement of work with MAP.
  • Commencement of work after Letter of Agreement (LOA) is signed with MAP. 

Cost & Payment

  • $1,400 Members (Advance Payment)

  • $1,960 Non-Members (Advance Payment)
