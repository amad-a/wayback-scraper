Program Company Specific Development Programs
Time Frame Until 30/Apr/2002
Target Group
  • Up to 5  ICT companies.
Description

MAP will consider supporting company specific programs of business planning, marketing and acquiring specialized technical assistance as case by case in light of the company needs and plans.

This program is targeting ICT companies that are mature and ready to penetrate new markets.

 
Methodology

  • Interested companies (Companies) to register for the program with PITA and PITA will authorize the commencement of work with MAP.

  • MAP IT Sector Manager will evaluate the eligibility of registering companies. This will be based on company history, products, plans, feasibility, etc.

  • A SOW/Action plan will be finalized with MAP assistance.

  • Implementation of action plan is done in coordination, supervision and assistance of MAP.

 
Cost & Payment

MAP will reimburse the Companies with 30% (and up to $3,600) of the cost according to the agreed upon plan.

PITA member Companies will pay a registration fee of $200.

 
To Apply
  1. Attached Application Form should be filled & emailed to PITA at (pita@pita-palestine.org).
  2. Advanced payment of $200 with the submission of the Application.
  3. Company Profile filled at PITA website and for MAP.
