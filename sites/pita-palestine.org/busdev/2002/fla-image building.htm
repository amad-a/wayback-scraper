Market Access Program

Firm Level Assistance

 Image Building, PR & Marketing
Program Image Building, PR & Marketing

6
Time Frame

Until 15/10/2002
Target Group
  • Up to 10 ICT companies.
Description

This Program aims at improving the image of ICT companies and also address their specific market needs, including but not limited to the following:

  • Assess Companies current marketing and PR status based on field visits to the companies,

  • Assist Companies in preparing the content of their marketing and PR kits including company and product profiles, brochures, catalogues, themes, logos, website, etc,

  • Assist Companies in supervising the design and production of their promotional material,

  • Financial Assistance towards the cost of the promotional material,

  • Assist Companies to prepare for exhibitions and trade missions, and 

  • Prepare marketing plans and other marketing related activity requested by companies.
Methodology

  • Registration for the program with PITA. PITA will authorize the commencement of work with MAP

  • Company Visit to assess needs and a finalized Scope of Work (SOW) with number of days (Level of Effort - LOE) and deliverables

  • Commencement of work after company approval to the (SOW) and a Letter of  Agreement (LOA) is signed with MAP.

  • For the printing promotional material, the Companies will send out a tender for at least 3 companies to be evaluated with MAP.
Deliverables

  • Companies new images and market positioning

  • Marketing and promotional material

  • Marketing plans and strategies
The Service Provider This program will be executed by MAP contractors. 
Cost & Payment

LEVEL OF EFFORT:

  • The estimated Level of Effort is an average of 4 days per company. The cost per day is $175.

  • Companies will pay 40% of the LOE (estimated at $280) as a fee to PITA. 

  • Non-PITA members will pay 60% of LOE (estimated at $420).

PRINTING MATERIAL:

  • MAP will support with 40% (and up to $1,500) of the cost of producing promotional material for companies that are participating in international exhibitions for the first time or introducing new products. Otherwise, MAP will support with only up to $1,000  (40% of total cost).

To Apply:
  1. Attached Application Form should be filled & emailed to PITA at (pita@pita-palestine.org).
  2. Advanced payment of $250 with the submission of the Application.
  3. Company Profile filled at PITA website and for MAP.
