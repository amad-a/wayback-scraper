Palestine Net 
Palestine-Net is an independent, private Palestinian effort to collect and disseminate info about Palestine (the Holy Land). Palestine-Net's web site  is updated regularly from Palestine. You can read Palestinian news, listen to national songs, view pictures, learn about history, geography, education, business, tourism, culture or politics of Palestine. This site also has a review of Internet cafe's in Palestine and a Palestine Web Index/Directory.
