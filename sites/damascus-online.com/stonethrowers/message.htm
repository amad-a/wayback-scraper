A MESSAGE FROM THE PROGRAMMER

                                                      Cartoon by Baha Boukari, Palestine

Hi dear friends,

1. Why I programmed this game

Everytime I watch the suffering of our brothers and sisters in Palestine, I have a very strong desire to pick up a stone and throw it at the Israeli killers. So I programmed this game to have my dream come -let's say- elecronically true!! But I wish that you all remember that the real world which really matters.

2. Some notes about the game

It took me 4 days (and nights) to make this game. I decided to make it so simple 'casue I wanted it to be out as soon as possible. I made the resolution 320x240x8 and did use small and simple sound fx 'casue I wanted it to be small (399Kb) so that it can be downloaded quickly. I have the 640x480x16 version (8MB) plus sound fx and midi on a CD-ROM (at home!).

3. What is next?

I have been working on a video game called 'the Arabic Dream' which should look likd TombRaider (yeah.. it's so complex!) but without a producer (yup, I am looking for one!). The work might take so long (besides, I have to study, I am a student, you know!).

4. Thank you for reading this!

I hope you liked the game. I would be very glad to hear from you, so send me your feedback by signing the guest book and tell me your opinion about this game. This will encourage me to make more games...

Thanks again.

Next year in Jerusalem!

Chechine_Wolf (Mohammad Mouaffak Hamza)

Damascus, November 22, 2000
