Match for Maha! This page is dedicated to providing information regarding the quest to find a bone marrow match for our friend Maha Khalaf. You are encouraged to send this link to anyone and everyone you know who may be willing to help. As many of you know, Maha has been undergoing treatment for T-Cell Lymphoma for the past 10 months. Her next stage of treatment is to undergo a bone marrow transplant. The purpose of a bone marrow transplant is to give high does chemotherapy to eliminate cancer cells from the body. In doing this, the patient's immune system is damaged through the destruction of healthy cells along with the cancer cells. After treatment, the patient is infused with the bone marrow of a donor and if sucessful, a new healthy immune system is created. Finding a bone marrow match is a large task. The chances of finding a match between two unrelated people is anywhere from 1 in 100 to 1 in 2 million depending on the genetic code of each patient. THIS IS WHY WE NEED YOUR HELP!! The testing process is very simple. You will be asked to donate a small amount of blood, which is then tested to identify your tissue type. Once your tissue has been typed you will be added to a confidential national registry that doctors: search to find matches for sick patients. Maha:s best chances of finding a match are within the Palestinian community but everyone should be tested. Even if you don:t match Maha, you may get the opportunity to save someone else:s life! Once you are listed on the national registry, your results will be available to Maha:s doctor. If you are a match, for Maha or anyone else, you will be notified and called in for additional blood testing to determine if the match is close enough for a transplant to take place. Once you are identified as a match you are given the choice whether or not you wish to donate some of your bone marrow. If you do decide to become a donor, the marrow is collected from the back of the pelvic bones during a procedure which takes place under anesthesia and causes some discomfort for a few days. The National Marrow Donor Program and/or the recipients insurance covers the donor:s expenses. According to most donors, the few days of soreness they felt were a small price to pay for the chance to save someone:s life. Someone out there is a match for Maha and it may be you! Please take a few minutes of your time to find out if you can help her or any one of the millions of people affected by leukemia, lymphoma and other cancers. For more information on the donation process please visit http//:www.marrow.org If you are a member of Maha's family there are specific places and times that have been set up for you to donate in the San Francisco/Bay Area, and Houston area. Please see the sections below for information on where to go to be tested. If you live in areas other than these and would like to be tested please go here.
