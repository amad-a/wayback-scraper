Dear Friends, I am writing to draw your attention to the Al-Nakba site on the Internet http://www.alnakba.org/. This site is dedicated to the commemoration of the 50 years of dispossession. It is produced by The Khalil Sakakini Cultural Cente in the West Bank. It has a listing of destroyed villages, chronology of events, survivors testimonies, a photo gallery, bibliography and provides related links. It is extremely well done! I strongly urge you to visit the site. I think it is the kind of site that is consistent with the content of your excellent homepage and I would suggest that you make a link to it. The email address to contact is sakakini@sakakini.org. I also think it would be great if visitors to your page were encouraged to sign the Guest Book at the Al-Nakba site. It seems to me appropriate for our people in Palestine to know they are not on their own. So far, ADC National (under the "Palestine 50 Years of Dispossession Link), ADC University of Michigan, ADC New Orleans, ADC NYC and The American Committee on Jerusalem (Front Page) have made links to the site. In addition, a number of Arab and non-Arab personal homepages have made links. Ray Hanania, award-winning Palestinian author, has also written article and provided a link from his homepage. Other Chapters and organizations have been contacted with information on the site and with a request that they link to it and sign the guest book. Reminders about the 15 May 50 year commemoration rallies have been sent out with these messages. Please help us in this effort by informing your organization and contacting organizations you are familiar with. There is no time like the present!!! I look forward to your comments. Thank you and Keep up the good work! Zahi Damuni, Ph.D. ADC-Central PA Vice President
