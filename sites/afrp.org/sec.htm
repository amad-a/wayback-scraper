The American Federation of Ramallah, Palestine is accepting applications for a full-time Secretary/Administrative Assistant in its National Headquarters located in Westland, MI.

This would be an excellent and exciting opportunity for a stay-at-home mother or for a evening college student who desires to become more involved in day-to-day operations of grassroots organizing and fostering cultural awareness through community involvement. Responsibilities include, but not limited to:

  • Answer telephones - must have excellent telephone and interpersonal skills.

  • Word processing - must have proficient knowledge of Microsoft Word and prepare Federation correspondence, as well as brochures and other promotional materials.

  • Database management proficiency – must have experience using MS Excel and Access.

  • Mail and information dissemination – will be primarily responsible for processing correspondence received by the Federation and routing it to the appropriate committee for action.

  • Work closely with the Federation's Executive Director and President to implement projects (i.e., mailings, telephone campaigns, press contacts, grassroots organizing, etc.).
