Mid Year Meeting 2001

This meeting is one of the most important events that the Federation holds annually.

Please make every effort to attend this meeting.


The American Federation of Ramallah Palestine will hold its
2001 Mid-Year meeting in Detroit, MI on March 16th and 17th.


Hotel reservations can be made at the Livonia Courtyard
Marriott before February 28th by calling 1-800-321-2211.
Rooms are booked under the Federation name and rates are as
follows: $59.00 for a room w/ one king size bed and
$69.00 for a room w/ two double beds.
