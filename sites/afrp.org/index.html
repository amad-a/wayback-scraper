Ahlan wa Sahlan! Welcome!

With common roots in Ramallah, a town on the West Bank of the Jordan River, the members of AFRP have been living in the United States for over 80 years. We formed the AFRP, the largest organization of American Arabs in the U.S., to promote social interaction among our members and to cherish our common heritage. We welcome you to visit our web pages and learn more about the American Arabs from Ramallah, Palestine.
