Palestinian National Authority / The World Bank

Recruitment under the Palestinian Expatriate Professional Program (PEPP)

The objective of the World Bank-assisted Palestinian Expatriate Professional
Program (PEPP) is to recruit qualified expatriate Palestinian professionals for
senior management and technical specialist positions in selected Palestinian
government ministries and agencies for an extended period lasting up to two
years. Compensation will be internationally competitive. Arthur Andersen, one
of the world's largest multi-disciplinary professional services firms, is
working on behalf of the Palestinian National Authority (PNA) to identify and
recruit Palestinian expatriates for the following positions.
