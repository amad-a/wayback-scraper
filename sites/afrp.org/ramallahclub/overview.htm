Hotel Description:
  • As an "Official" hotel of Walt Disney World™, on site Disney, this world-famous resort offers a friendly atmosphere and exceptional facilities. Set amid 23 acres of beautifully landscaped paradise, the 814 guest rooms and suites offer a comfortable retreat for both the young and the young-at-heart. Enjoy complimentary and continuous transportation to the MAGIC KINGDOM Park, EPCOT, Disney-MGM studios and Disney's largest Theme Park yet, Disney's Animal Kingdom! And it's just within footsteps of Downtown Disney featuring The Downtown Disney Marketplace, Pleasure Island, and Disney's West Side. The Hilton in the WALT DISNEY WORLD™ Resort offers guests the exclusive DISNEY Surprise mornings. This exciting program allows our guests admission to a selected WALT DISNEY WORLD™ Theme park one hour prior to opening when using the provided Surprise Mornings Shuttle service. Take a refreshing dip in either of our two sparkling, heated swimming pools. Or, enjoy an invigorating workout at the complete health club including Nautilus equipment and sauna. Golfers of every caliber will find a challenge at our five Walt Disney World championship courses or the 9-hole executive course. Hilton guests receive preferred tee times and complimentary transportation. With nine restaurants and lounges to choose from, Hilton guests will find the cuisine and atmosphere to suit every mood and appetite.
