2000 Federation Essay Contest

For the past 4 years the Federation has successfully sponsored an Essay Contest for our youth. The essays received during the 4 years have been very impressive. It is programs like these for our youth that keeps them aware of our Clubs, Federation and their heritage. We encourage our youth to participate in the 5th annual Federation sponsored Essay Contest. We are anticipating more participation this year to exceed the successes of the previous 4 years.

Following are the rules and regulations for the 1999-2000 Essay Contest. If you have any question, please contact the Federation office or Maher Ajluni, at (248) 363-9256.

Rules and Regulations

  • 500 words or less, double spaced
  • Deadline for submission postmarked by June 1, 2000
  • Three age categories (birthday must be before November 1) 12 to 14, 15 to 17, 18 to 22.
  • Please submit all essays to:
AFRP Essay Contest
American Federation of Ramallah, Palestine
27484 Ann Arbor Trail
Westland, MI 48185

Awards:

  • Each age category will be awarded a $500 scholarship for the winner (total of 3)
  • In addition, a plaque will be given to the winner (total of 3)
  • A certificate plus $50 will be given to the next 4 highest scoring essays in each category (total 12).
  • Winning essays will be published in the Hathihe Ramallah Magazine.

Suggested Topics:

This year we will have 3 topics, one for each age group.

Ages 12-14

What do your friends admire about the Arabic Culture?

Ages 15-17

What would you like to see planned at the Annual Ramallah Convention?

Ages 18-22

Discuss the media roll in your perception of today's society?

Good Luck
