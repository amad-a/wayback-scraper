International Activists Arrested for Blocking Destruction of Palestinian Roads by Israeli Military

- Urgent Appeal for Equipment Donations

- Training in Jerusalem:

every Monday and Thursday

 

Friends,

On the verge of Palestine's Freedom Summer campaign, the Israeli military has again taken its forces into West Bank towns and has unleashed its military might on Palestinian cities. We fear that foreign civilians coming to join Palestinians in nonviolent, direct-action resistance to the Israeli occupation will again find themselves acting as human shields - trying to get in the way of excessive force being used by the Israeli military against Palestinian civilians, and providing humanitarian assistance to civilians when Israel's policies and military operations deny Palestinians food and medical care.

It is urgent that we get internationals in Palestine by the hundreds and thousands! If you can and are still interested in joining us for Freedom Summer, please see our website - www.palsolidarity.org for more information on registering and coming.

Along with sending groups of trained foreign civilians into West Bank / Gaza cities, towns and villages, we'd like to also send in at least one digital camera and one laptop computer into each area so that Palestinians and internationals can work together on documenting the horrors of occupation, things the mass media outlets almost never catch.

Unfortunately we're quite shy of the equipment we had hoped to have: 20 digital video cameras and 20 laptop computers. Therefore we are making a direct appeal to our supporters for help obtaining this equipment. If you can help us to set up this mechanism of documenting and reporting Israeli military and settler atrocities by donating the needed equipment, please contact us at ISMdonations@hotmail.com for more information on getting it to us.

Thank you,
