- Urgent Appeal for Equipment Donations

Last Training Day Changed to Friday 13 Aug.

 

Gaza:

For the last three days we have been in the Gaza strip. This means we are no longer in the middle of a military operation but we are in one of the world's largest prisons. 1.2 million people live in this prison in a strip about 6 miles wide and about 25 miles long. Of that land about 1/3 is taken by 3-5 thousand Israeli settlers, leaving 2/3 for the 1.2 million Palestinians. I do not have much time but I will give you some highlights.

We saw the checkpoint in the middle area of the Gaza strip two days ago. When we got there there were hundreds of cars sitting there in the extreme heat just waiting. The checkpoint had been closed all day, no one had gotten through. Our contact here is convinced that the only reason they opened the checkpoint is because the soldiers saw us from the US and Canada approaching. These checkpoints have absolutely nothing to do with security; they are merely to harass the population. When they opened it up they did not even do so much as check anyone's id, much less search any cars. If there were actually some kind of security risk would they have just let all these people pass through. It's really sick.

Yesterday we met with mayors from two of the municipalities in the area Nuseirat (where we are staying) and Boreig. These and the other areas of the middle area of the Gaza strip are extremely short of water. (If people are not aware, a lot of the reason for why Israel occupies Palestinian land, the Golan Heights and formerly southern Lebanon is to steal the water from these areas. They do that in Gaza as well often selling the water under illegal settlements back to the Palestinians whose water it rightfully is in the first place.) Aside from the water under the settlements there are two wells here outside of the Netzarim settlement. One of them is not working but it is close to the fence around the settlement. Every time Palestinian workers have attempted to go to the well to fix it and make it operational again they have been shot at and had to flee. This has happened even when they have gotten permission from the Israelis. We are working to see if there is any way we can help. If the workers are willing we told them we will accompany them to the well to make sure they are able to fix it. We spoke with soldiers at the settlement who basically gave us bureaucratic bullshit. We would like to go even without permission but need to see if the Palestinians are willing to do this.

On the way back from the settlement we saw an army bulldozer destroying an orchard with orange trees and olive trees. This has been happening a lot here in Gaza in the last two weeks (it happens all the time all over Palestine but has apparently been particularly bad). Even knowing that it happens and seeing someone actually sitting in a machine and destroying a farmer's crops for no reason is so disgusting. These particular fields had to be at least a kilometer and a half from the settlement. There is no reason.

The situation here is horrible. I urge everyone to do whatever they can to support the Palestinian people against the fascist policies of Israel. People are dying every day, crops are being destroyed every day, homes are being destroyed every day, people are being rounded up without charges every day. One of the Jews in our group has said that if you stand by and do nothing it would be like standing by and doing nothing knowing that the holocaust was going on. I find it hard to disagree with her. I also find it hard to disagree with the comparison of Israel to Nazi Germany, straight down to writing things on men's arms and using dogs to round people up. Please do what you can, contact your representatives in Washington and tell them that as a taxpayer you are absolutely disgusted by what is happening with US support here. Speak out against Bush's imperialist agenda in attempting to tell the Palestinians who there leaders should be. If the US decides for the Palestinians then there is no independence (then again the people of the US don't even get to decide who there leaders are anymore as we saw with Bush himself). Work for justice. The US must end all aid to Israel if it truly supports democracy and justice around the world (I'm under no illusions that it does but perhaps the people can force it to.)
