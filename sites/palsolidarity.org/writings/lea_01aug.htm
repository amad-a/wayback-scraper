- Urgent Appeal for Equipment Donations

 

We talked with several young people who had broken curfew in Ramallah during our curfew breaking walk on July 31- most of the kids we talked to were boys about 10 to 16 years old but a few couldn't have been more than five and some were young girls.

We asked them what they wanted the world to know about them. One boy said he wanted the world to know that the Palestinian people are oppressed; another says the Palestinians are steadfast.

They said they defy the curfew for freedom, for taking their right to freedom. Another said: If we live our whole lives under full invasion and occupation we will have to continue to struggle for our freedom for our whole lives. They are killing us. They say that we are the terrorists, but they are the ones that are terrorists they are killing us.

Another conversation with an older Palestinian man in old Ramallah - on the streets after the army threw tear-gas into an open-air basement apartment where several old people and children lived. The same night as a bombing at Hebrew University in Jerusalem. The old man said: The people that are committing acts of violence--both the soldiers and the people who did the bomb today--are hurting us all. I am afraid for the future. After a few months I think it will be worse. The army will not be good in this area. Our leaders don't do something good. What the army did today was very bad, but also, what our people did was very bad at the university. As a human being, I don't accept these acts of violence. Americans and Europeans need to push - they need to do something real.
