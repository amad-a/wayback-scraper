Hello all,

The first thing I wanted to say is that I mentioned the rounding up of men at the Al-Amari refugee camp near Ramallah in my last report. That night after I sent that message we got new information about another incident in the camp. At noon on that day, 6/30 soldiers entered the home of a family in the camp. They forced everyone in the family into one room and proceeded to watch television for several hours. Given the timing they were most likely watching the final game of the World Cup though this is just an assumption on my part. At about 4:00 pm they received orders to leave. In their haste they bumped an 18-month-old girl, Munia Adel Rehan into the stairwell. She fell down three flights of stairs.

The family followed the soldiers out into the street begging them for help with their injured daughter. The soldiers ignored both Munia and
the family. The family then called an ambulance. The Red Crescent ambulance arrived within 10 minutes but the soldiers would not let it reach Munia for another half hour. Members of the International Solidarity Movement were at the hospital where she was finally taken and took much of this testimony from Munia's mother. They said she finally arrived at the hospital at 7:00 pm. She had a large laceration visible at her hairline and she was very lethargic and sleepy. Though they were still waiting for test results the doctors were very concerned that she might have permanent brain damage.

What the fuck did an 18-month-old girl do to deserve this kind of treatment?

Stories from Ramallah

I wanted to give you all a few of the stories and quotes from Palestinians in and around Ramallah since these are never reported in the media.

These are some quotes from a worker in the hospital we stayed in for three nights in Ramallah. I will not give his name because he is afraid the Israelis want to arrest him.

"What we want exactly, we want to live in peace"

"This intifada does not come from Arafat or Hamas, it comes from all of us, we all have intifada in our hearts"

"9 of my friends have been killed"

"I will stay here and I will not leave and I think the same is true of the Jews. We must make peace together but without Sharon"

"We are human beings, not animals"

“Tell your people the truth. We don't want you to lie. In my country I cannot move."

"I have not visited my family in 3 years, it is 10 minutes away by car"

"We will not let anyone take this country from us. The only way this will happen is if all Palestinians are killed."

I thought these quotes from a much larger conversation were all incredible. And I feel like it is extremely important to relay the words of the Palestinians in their own words.

I also wanted to relay the stories of a few Palestinian we met in the rehabilitation center in Ramallah.

Mahmoud:

He was working his land near Nablus in November. At that time settlers had already uprooted over 240 of his olive trees. 4 settlers came and shot at him from far away. One of the bullets hit the main nerve in his leg and severed it. He still walks with Crutches.

Arkham (age 23):

He lived in the Jenin refugee camp. When the Israelis attacked in March one of his friends was shot and was lying in the street. He and another friend got a car and drove to their injured friend. They picked him up and put him in the car but as they tried to get away they were fired at from behind. His friend was hit in the shoulder. He was hit in the back of the neck and is now a total paraplegic. Both of his other friends were arrested and remain somewhere in the Israeli prison system despite their injuries though he does not know where they are. He will never use his legs again.

Hussam (age 13):

He lived in the village Beta near Nablus. He was standing at the gates of his village watching 3 Israeli soldiers far away on a hilltop. They suddenly opened fire on him, he thought for target practice. Obviously a 13-year-old boy with no weapon presented no danger to the soldiers. He was hit with 5 bullets. Proof that they were extremely far away was that the two that hit him in the arm did not even penetrate all the way through, they stayed in his arm. One hit him in the spine. He will also be paralyzed for the rest of his life. The main thing he wanted to tell us was "tell the truth about what is happening here" I only hope that I can do that in some small way.

I will collect more stories and send them. I feel it is extremely important to do this because so few people are doing it. Israeli dead and wounded always have names in US media, Palestinians are almost always just numbers.
