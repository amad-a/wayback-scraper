- Urgent Appeal for Equipment Donations

 

Hi Family and friends,

Qalqilya is a good thing. Everyone on the street and at the Red Crescent is very smiley and friendly, and our work is necessary and I feel like it's doable.

Ronise decided to take a few days off to recuperate from her work. It's exhausting to lipread all the time, and only Jennie was interpreting for her. Brave and beautiful and sparky and heartful Ronise is the first deaf ISMer. She is not only resting in Al Ram, but editing the footage she shot in Deheishe. She'll rejoin future ISM actions soon.

Right now there are 12 people in the group here. I am so very impressed with the group members' heart and initiative. People are responsible and eager to work, and also eager to do work that supports the group. This dynamic makes me believe in the ISM -- it really can work! For me it's a transition into working by consensus, but really it makes me feel like I'm living in the utopian part of my favorite book (Marge Piercy's Woman on the Edge of Time). It does feel futuristic. Unfortunately several of the members are leaving this weekend. I hope more people come to replace them.

I am comfortable with the ISMers, but it's also not really stepping outside my comfort zone to spend time with the Palestinians we're working with. I feel welcomed here, and useful.

Qalqilya is more conservative than some places, and my tsnius (modesty of dress) and negiyah (no cross-gender touching) training from childhood comes in handy. I keep covered up, and don't make eye contact with the men, mostly. The women are so beautiful here, and the kids, of course, are adorable. Especially the mischevious one who hit me in the leg with a pebble this morning when I was lost on the way from the apartment we're staying in (donated) to the Red Crescent. Through the magic of cellphones I was soon found.

This morning 4 of us went with the beautiful and fierce Souhad and the affable Hassam across the settler road (guarded) to visit the town of Hable. On the way there we talked to a farmer (on the Qalqilya side) and walked around his fields and orchards. While we were walking in his orchard near the road, the soldiers fired rubber bullets at us, so we turned tail and ran. Ester (cool Spanish ISMer) was hit in the shoulder, is fine, will have a bruise.

We then crossed the road through negotiating with the soldiers. We approached from far away holding our passports high, and they were really dumb with us, Where are you from, Do you know anybody in New Jersey, etc. The choicest part was when they said, Why do you want to go to Hable? It's not really a friendly, safe place. I recommend other towns in Israel, etc. Really, not as safe/friendly as here, where you just shot at us? Ester was biting her tongue.

So they let us pass, including the 2 Palestinians who were waiting far back, and we toured Souhad's father's farm. The person running it is a cousin of hers from Qalqilya who hasn't been home to see his family for 3 months. The farm is dying, because he can't sell to Israelis anymore, and the cost of transporting produce to other parts of the West Bank has skyrocketed. He gets harassed by the soldiers daily because the farm is close to the road. When the wall along the Green Line is built, the small piece of land between it and the settler road will be squeezed and probably razed, "for security purposes." The farm is so beautiful, with its fields and hards, a viney gazebo, and clear views of the town of Hable and the so-close settlement neighbor. How much more beautiful it must have been before, when there was enough income from the produce, and no settlement or wall or soldiers or Caterpillar driving through.

Tonight is my first ambulance shift, from 10 to 8am tomo morning. From
the looks of it, it will consist of a lot of sitting around in the Red Crescent, shooting the breeze with the drivers, with some rides thrown in. I am excited to do some work, finally. Tomorrow I think I will do checkpoint watch in the afternoon, not sure yet.

I miss you, and feel your support. Give me a call if you have the funds to talk for a few minutes: 972.67.338.935.

Everybody takre and be well.

love,k
