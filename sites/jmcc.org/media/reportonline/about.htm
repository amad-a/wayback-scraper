ABOUT PR

Palestine Report Online is a project of the Jerusalem Media and Communications Center in Jerusalem. It is a continuation of the print Palestine Report, which was established nearly ten years ago as a means of informing English-speakers about Palestinians and their daily lives in the context of the ongoing Israeli-Palestinian conflict.

The Jerusalem Media and Communications Center (JMCC) was started in 1988, in the early months of the Palestinian uprising, by a group of Palestinian journalists and researchers seeking to provide information on events in the Occupied Palestinian Territory. The Palestine Report has been one medium for its message.

Palestine Report Online is the JMCC's most recent effort to fulfill the needs of writers and policy-makers in the aftermath of the 1993 Oslo Accords. As Palestine's only independent news digest, it answers a need both inside and outside the West Bank and Gaza for succinct and current news in English. The Report's readers have always found its political focus and translations from the Arabic press an invaluable barometer of local trends. Palestine Report Online's additional attention to quality reporting on internal Palestinian concerns sets it apart from any other news outlet.


Our Staff

Ghassan Khatib, Publisher ghassan@jmcc.org

Mr. Khatib is a well-known Palestinian political analyst. Educated in the United Kingdom, he has lectured on history and philosophy at Birzeit University in the West Bank. A member of the Palestinian team at the Madrid peace talks, he is a regular source for international news media on the changing conditions and political climate in Palestine.

Joharah Baker, Editor-in-Chief joharah@jmcc.org

Ms. Baker translates and edits reports for area Palestinian organizations and international NGOs such as USAID and the World Bank. She also does simultaneous interpretation on a freelance basis.

Charmaine Seitz, Managing Editor cseitz@jmcc.org

Ms. Seitz writes frequently on both Israeli and Palestinian politics and society. Her work has been published in The Economist, In These Times, Washington Report on Middle East Affairs, as well as other magazines and journals.

Hadeel Wahdan, Journalist hwahdan@jmcc.org

Ms. Wahdan has been trained by MBC satellite television, works part-time with BBC Radio Arabic Service and is a recent graduate from Birzeit University's journalism program.
