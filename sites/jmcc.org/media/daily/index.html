Daily Press Summary

Our Press Summary is sent on a daily basis to journalists, radio and TV networks, press agencies, international institutions, and diplomatic missions, by facsimile/e-mail. The Daily Press Summary provides plus translations from local news and analyses from the Palestinian territory Palestinian dailies, summaries of their editorials, and a diary of major events in the occupied territories.
