This Week in Palestine

 A monthly publication to serve as a guide for international visitors and tourists, listing events in Palestine.

"This Week in Palestine" features a current listing of cultural and art exhibitions, music concerts, festivals, religious events and celebrations, tourist sites and services, as well as list of hotels, restaurants, theaters, museums, transportation, consular offices, emergency numbers, and other vital information necessary for any visitor to Palestine.
