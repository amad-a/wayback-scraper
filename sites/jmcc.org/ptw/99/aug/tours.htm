This Week in Palestine - Guided Tours
Issue no. 14  - August 1999

Guided Tours (01 August  - 15 August)

The Palestinian Association For Cultural Exchange (PACE) hosts a number of guided bus tours all over Palestine (all trips begin and end in Ramallah) tel: (02-2958825 / 2986854)
  • Sunday 01 August: The Refugee Camps of Ramallah
    • Highlights: Half a day visit (13:00 - 17:00) to the refugee camps in the Ramallah area including Jalazone, Am'ari, Qadura, and Qalandia. Cost: NIS 60
  • Friday 06 August: Jerusalem
    • Highlights: Mt. of Olives, Walls and Gates of Jerusalem, Haram al Sharif, the Wailing Wall, Via Dolorosa, the Holy Sepulchre. Cost: NIS 120
  • Friday 13 August: Bethlehem and Vicinity
    • Highlights: Monasteries of St. Saba and Ibn Ubeid, Shepherd's Fields, Herodion, Church of the Nativity, the Milk Grotto, Bethlehem Museum, Solomon's Pools, and Murad's Fortress. Cost: NIS 120
Alternative Tours: regular tours to the West Bank
Leaving from the Jerusalem Hotel, Jerusalem
Minimum number of participants is 5.
For inquiries call (052) 864205 Or the Jerusalem hotel at (02)6277216
 
  • Jerusalem: 3 hrs
    • Walking tour through the four quarters of the Old City. See the sights and learn about the situation of the Palestinians in the city. Leaves daily at 10:00 Price: NIS 40
    N.B. Tours focusing on the Israeli settlement policy in and around Jerusalem can be arranged upon request.
  • Bethlehem: 3 hrs
    • Visit the old city market, Church of the Nativity and the Milk Grotto. Leaves several days a week at 10:00 or 15:00 Price: NIS 40 rs.
  • Hebron: 6 hrs
    • Visit the Ibrahimi Mosque, the Arab market & the old city as well as glass and pottery factories. Leaves Sundays and Wednesdays at 10:00 Price: NIS 60
  • Nablus, Ramallah and surroundings: 1 day
    • See the old city of Nablus (Al-Kasabah) and Joseph's Tomb. Explore the Turkish Bath and visit traditional soap factories. Lastly tour the village of Jifna and visit a refugee camp. Leaves Tuesdays at 10:00 (trips to both cities can be arranged separately) Price: NIS 90
  • Refugee camp: 3 hrs
    • A visit to a West bank refugee camp: meet with families and learn about the camp life and background of the Palestinian refugees. Leaves Mondays and Thursdays at 15:00 Price: NIS 35
  • Jericho and Environs: 1/2 day
    • Enjoy Tel Jericho, Nabi Musa, Hishams' Palace, Mount of Temptation and St. George's Monastery in the Wadi Qelt. Leaves Mondays and Thursdays at 9:30 Price: NIS 60
  • Gaza (via Eretz checkpoint): 1 day
    • See Gaza City including a visit to a Palestinian NGO. Learn about Israeli settlements and the problem they cause. Visit refugee camps and Al- Omari Mosque. Leaves Wednesdays and Saturdays at 8:00 Price: NIS 130
  • Other Trips
    • Nazareth and Tiberias: 1 day
    Masada and the Dead Sea (sunrise tour): 11 hrs.
    Please ask for additional Dea Sea daytime trips
