The Popular Art Centre - El Bireh
organizes
The Palestine International Festival for Music and Dance 1999
28 June - 5 July
Birzeit University Old and New Campuses
The largest annual event organized by the Popular Art Center, the Palestine International Festival was first organized in 1993 and continues to be the most popular event in Palestine.
In addition to the regular program of music and dance performances from all over the world performed in open air, the festival also features a modern dance program to be performed at Kamal Nasser Hall at the Birzeit University Campus, the first such program in the country.
