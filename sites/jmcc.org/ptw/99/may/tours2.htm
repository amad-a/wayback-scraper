This Week in Palestine - Guided Tours
Issue no. 9  - May 1999

Guided Tours (15 May - 31 May)

The Palestinian Association For Cultural Exchange (PACE) hosts a number of guided bus tours all over Palestine (all trips begin and end in Ramallah) tel: (02-2958825 / 2986854)
  • Sunday 16/5: Bethlehem and Vicinity Highlights: Monasteries of St. Saba and Ibn Ubaid, Shepherd's fields, Herodion, Church of the Nativity, Milk Grotto, Bethlehem Museum, Solomon's Pools and Murad's Fortress. Cost: NIS 120.
  • Friday 21/05: Nablus and Vicinity Highlights: Khan al Lubban, Huwara, Jacob's Well, Tel Balata (Shechem), roman Nablus, The Old City, the Oriental market, the Grand Mosque, the Turkish bath, soap factory, Kunafa workshop, and Al Qasabah museum, Mt. Gerzim and Sebastiah (Samaria). Cost: NIS 120.
  • Sunday 23/05: Jericho and Vicinity Highlights: Good Samaritan Inn, Nabi Musa, St. Goerge's Monastery, Tulul Abu Al Alayeq (Herod's Winter Palace), Tel Jericho, Monastery of Temptation, Hisham's Palace, Khirbet Qumran, and the Dead Sea. Cost: NIS 130.
  • Thursday 27/05: Turkish Bath Highlights: Dinner with Nabulsi sweets followed by a performance of oriental music. Bath optional. Departure from Ramallah at 4:00 pm and return expected at 11:00 pm. Cost: NIS 110.
  • Friday 28/05: Jenin and Tulkarem Highlights: Bal'amah tunnel, Burqin Church, Jenin, Ta'annek, Um al Rihan, Tulkarem, Kur, Tel Dot. Cost: NIS 130.
  • Saturday 29/05: Musical Evening in Ramallah Highlights: Live Arabic music and dinner in one of the restaurants in Ramallah. The evening starts at 8:00 pm and goes on until midnight. Cost: NIS 100.
Alternative Tours: regular tours to the West Bank
Leaving from the Jerusalem Hotel, Jerusalem
Minimum number of participants is 5.
For inquiries call (052) 864205 Or the Jerusalem hotel at (02)6277216
 
  • Jerusalem: 3 hrs
    • Walking tour through the old city of Jeruaslem. Visit all the religious and touristic sights and learn about the situation of the Palestinians in the city. Leaves 10:00 am. Price: NIS 35
  • Hebron: 1 day
    • Guided tour to the historical and religious sites of Hebron and the Ibrahimi Mosque, Arab market, glass and pottery factories. Leaves Sun. And Wed. 10:00 am. Price: NIS 60
  • Ramallah and Nablus: 1 day
    • Visit the city of Ramallah, including Birzeit university and the traditional village of Jifna. Tour the city of Nablus in the northern West Bank and visit its sites including the old city, Joseph's tomb, and the Turkish bath. Leaves Tues. 10:00 am. Price: NIS 90
  • Bethlehem: 3 - 4 hours
    • Guided tour to the sites of the birthplace of Jesus, the city of Bethlehem. Visit the old city market, the Church of Nativity and the Milk Grotto and learn more about the political situation in the Palestinian Territories. Leaves several days a week. 10:00 am or 30:00 pm. Price NIS 35
  • Jericho: 1/2 day
    • Visit the city of Jericho including st. George's monastery, Wadi Qilt, Tel Jericho, the Mount of temptation, Hisham's palace and enjoy the orchards of the oldest city in the world. Leaves Monday and Thursday 9:30 am. Price: NIS 50
  • Refugee camp: 1/2 day
    • For those who would like to know more about the Palestinian cause and the situation of the Palestinian people. This trip takes you to meet with families and learn about the camp life. Leaves Mon. and Thurs. 3:00 pm Price: NIS 35
  • Gaza Strip: Full day guided tour to Gaza via
    • Eretz checkpoint including: Gaza city and the Gaza sea shore, Israeli settlements, refugee camp, and other sites. Leaves Wed. and Sat. at 8:00 am. Price: NIS 130.
