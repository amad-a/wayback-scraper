OCCASIONAL DOCUMENT SERIES

JMCC's occasional document series reprints in both English and Arabic resolutions, agreements, correspondence and other documents related to the peace process and Palestinian legislation, to encourage discussion and debate in the Palestinian community on the major issues facing us today, Palestinian autonomy and the interim period in the West Bank, Gaza Strip and East Jerusalem. By publishing these documents and expanding dialogue and discussion, we aim to encourage a more democratic atmosphere and participatory decision-making. The majority of documents are translated from the original English into Arabic. Some are official translations, while others have been translated by the JMCC.
