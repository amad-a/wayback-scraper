Oasis Casino marking the completion of the first stage

A Press Conference marking the completion of the first stage of Oasis tourist resort, near Jericho will take place on Monday, September 14th at 11:00 AM at Oasis Casino with the participation of:

  • Dr. Leo Wallner - Chairman and President, Casinos Austria
  • Mr. Alexander Tucek - Director General, Oasis
  • Mr. Paul Herzfeld - Chief Executive Officer, Casinos Austria
  • Dr. Joseph Schwrzecker - Member, the Austrian Unions Bank.
  • Member of the Board of Management, The Austrian Union Bank.
  • Mr. Peter Schachinger - Interior Director, Oasis

Itinerary:

10:00 - 11:00 AM Photographers and TV crews are invited to take photographs and film inside the Casino

11:00 - 12:00 AM Press Conference

12:00 - 13:00 PM Interviews

Please note that you have to bring your Press Cards with you for security reasons.
