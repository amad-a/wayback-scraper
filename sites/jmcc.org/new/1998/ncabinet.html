Changes to the Palestinian Cabinet, announced by President Yasser Arafat Wednesday, August 5, 1998

 

New Cabinet appointments:

  • Hikmat Zeid - Minister of Agriculture, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, from Jenin.
  • Rafiq Al-Natsheh - Minister of Labour, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, From Hebron.
  • Sa'edi Al-Krunz - Minister of Industry, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, From Gaza
  • Yousef Abu Safieh - Minister of Environment, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, from Gaza.
  • Nabil 'Amr - Minister of Parliamant Affairs, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, From Hebron.
  • Hisham Abdul Raziq - Minister of State of Prisoners Affairs, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, from Gaza.
  • Munther Saleh - Minister of Higher Education, independent, from Nablus.
  • Salah al-Ta'mari - Minister of State, Portfolio to be announced later, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, from Bethlehem.
  • Ziad Abu Zayad - Minister of State, Portfolio to be announced later, Legislative Council Member, affiliated to Fateh, From Jerusalem.
  • Hassan Asfour - Minister of State, Legislative Council Member, independent, from Gaza.

Ministers who lost their portfolio and appointed as minister of state:

  • Talal Sider, Hamas, from Hebron
  • Abdul Jawad Saleh, Legislative Council member, independent, from al-Bireh.
  • Bashir Bargouti, Palestinian People's Party, from Ramallah.

Ministers with portfolio change:

  • Hanan Ashrawi, formerly minister of Higher Education, now Minister of Tourism and Antiquities (She replaced late Elias Freij).

Vacant portfolios:

  • Ministry of Education
  • Ministry of Waqf and Religious Affairs
  • Ministry of Youths and Sports
  • Ministry of Interior

Ministers excluded from the new cabinet:

  • Yasser Amr, formerly Minister of Education.
