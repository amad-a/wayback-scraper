Tentative Schedule of President Clinton's visit to Israel and Palestinian territories:

JMCC learned from US officials that the tentative agenda of President Clinton visit to the region will be as follows:

Saturday 12, Dec. 1998

  • Clinton will arrive to Ben Gurion Airport at 11:30 at night.
  • The US delegation is estimated at 1000 persons of Congress members and businessmen and will include 300 persons of various US media staffs.
  • Israel Foreign Minister Ariel Sharon and his wife will receive Clinton, his wife and daughter at the airport .

Sunday 13, Dec.

  • In the morning, Clinton will have a reception with Israeli President Eizar Wizeman and Prime Minister Netanyahu.
  • Later, he will have a reception and meeting with Netanyahu at the PM office.
  • At noon, President Clinton will visit the grave of the late Israeli Prime Minister Yitshak Rabin.
  • Later, he will have launch with Wizeman.
  • Then, President Clinton will address a speech to the Israeli people.
  • Afterwards he will have dinner with Netanyahu in the Hilton Hotel in West Jerusalem.

Monday 14, Dec.

  • In the morning, he will land at Gaza Airport.
  • Later, he will meet with President Arafat in Arafat’s office.
  • Afterwards, he will have lunch with President Arafat.
  • In the evening, he will deliver a speech before the Palestinian National Council in Rashad Shawa Center.
  • Later, he will have a summit with President Arafat and Benyamin Netanyahu at Erez or in Askelon.
  • Departure to Jerusalem.

Tuesday 15, Dec.

  • In the morning he will have a meeting with Leader of the Israeli Opposition Ehud Barak

    • Later, he will fly to Bethlehem where he will visit the Church of Nativity and light the Christmas tree in Manger Square.
