AL-AQSA INTIFADA UPDATE:

Settlers kill an 18-month old girl from Talfeet in Nablus


The girl Sara Abdul Atheem Abdul Haq, 18 months old, died Sunday October 1st by the bullets of settlers. Sara was in the car with her father upon their return from Nablus to Talfeet. Nasim Musallam, a relative of the baby martyr, said the new crime occurred yesterday evening south east of Nablus while citizen Abdul Atheem was with Sara and his niece Rima, 21, coming back in their car when settlers in a vehicle opened fire at the Palestinian car; Sara received a bullet in the head.
