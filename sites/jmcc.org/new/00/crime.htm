A murder crime in cold blood
A child dies in the arms of his wounded father








 In Gaza near Netsarim settlement TV screens documented a murder crime in cold blood, a child Rami Muhammad Al-Dareh, 12 years old dies in the arms of his wounded father. The crime shocked the whole world as it showed the atrocity of an occupation that does not know mercy or respond to an appeal by a frightened wounded child and his helpless father who had to watch his son die in his arms.

The media were watching and have no other choice but to watch a young child and his father are besieged opposite to a wall and nothing to protect them from the heavy bullet shooting but only a small cement block on the road.

The scene of the boy martyrdom and the severely wounded father in Natsarim area in Gaza Strip during the bloody clashes which took place on Saturday September 30 were broadcast by the French TV  Channel 2.. Mohamnmed al-Durra, a father of seven children, was coming back from the car market accompanied by his son towards al-Briej. They were stuck in that area which witnessed violent clashes. To avoid the shooting, they sought refuge behind a cement barrier. The shot shows that the child Muhammad and his father are unarmed and barehanded, lying on the ground behind a cement block and screaming seeking for help, and the father was calling on the Israeli soldiers to cease fire; he tired to protect his son with his body but the bullets shot the young child in the chest, leaving the father severely wounded and unconscious.

It is unbelievable, all the world watched the execution live. The crime lasted about 45 minutes while no Palestinian was able to come close to them because of the heavy fire. Bassam Bulbeisi, the driver of an ambulance at the site, who tried to go and evacuate them was also shot dead a few minutes later. What kind of a human being can continue shooting until he kills the boy. What can Israel say? Can it claim it is self-defense?!

Later on, the Israeli army spokesperson justifies the incident that thousands of demonstrators were attacking the Israeli soldiers and they were firing against them. But if a small kid watch the TV screen of the scene he will realize that they were insisting to kill them.

Finally, Nabil Abu Rdeineh - Arafat President advisor said that the TV shot affirm that the child is innocent and killed in cold blood and all of the world was witness on that crime.
