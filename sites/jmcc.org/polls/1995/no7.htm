Methodology

A random sample of 1,397 Palestinians, over the age of 18 years, were interviewed face-to-face throughout the West Bank and Gaza Strip on 15 & 16 June 1995. The interviews were conducted on randomly selected homes, and the subjects inside each home were also selected randomly according to Kish tables. The interviews were conducted in 52 sampling points chosen randomly according to population.

In the West Bank, 892 people were surveyed from the following areas: Bethlehem: Deiheishe Refugee Camp, Handaza, Jerusalem: Jabel Mukabber, Sour Baher. Hebron: Halhoul, Hebron, Tarqoumya, Yatta, Bani Na'im, Jenin: Jenin, Qabatya, Ijjeh, Sansour, Toubas. Qalqilia: Qalqilia. Nablus: Nablus, Salem, Abwein, Kufr Ni'meh, Nur Shams RC and Qafin. Ramallah: Ramallah, Single. Jericho: Jericho.

In the Gaza Strip, 5.5 people were surveyed from: Gaza, Shate' RC, Nusseirtat, Rafah, Jabalya, Deir al-Balah, Rimal, Asqoula, Beit Lahia.

The major of error is plus or minus 3% with a confidence level of 95%.

Sample Distribution

  • 63.8% of the respondents were from the West Bank (including East Jerusalem)
  • 36.2% from the Gaza Strip
  • 41.2% said they live in villages
  • 15.0% in refugee camps
  • 43.8% in towns/cities
  • 45.7% were male
  • 54.7% were female
  • 65.7% were married
  • 27.0% were single
  • 4.0% were widowed
  • 1.7% were divorced
  • 6.1% No answer
  • The average of age of the respondents was 34 years.

Occupation respondents

  • Students 7.4%
  • Waged labourers 12.2%
  • Housewives 35.7%
  • Employees eg. secretaries/teachers/nurses/municipal employees 5.2%
  • Businessmen/private business 4.9%
  • Farmers/fishermen 1.3%
  • Craftsmen 8.1%
  • Professionals - eg. doctors/lawyers/pharmacists/engineers 9.7%
  • Unemployed 7.9%
  • Retired 2.1%
  • Other/No answer 5.5%
