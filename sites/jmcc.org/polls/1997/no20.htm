Introduction
  • 91% of Palestinians believe that the US role in the peace process is extremely biased in favor of Israel. In addition, only 0.8% think the US role is biased towards the Palestinians, while 0.4% think it plays a neutral role.
  • In general, opposition to the peace process has increased from the period of April to May 1997 from 23% to 26%, whereas the support of the peace process has declined, the same period from 73% to 69%. Moreover, only 12.5% of Palestinians support the Oslo track whereas 55% support it with caution.
  • The period between April 3 and May 15, 1997 shows a remarkable change in peopl's attitude towards the role of the Palestinian Authority. In comparison with the results of April 97, May's results show a sharp decrease in the percentage of people who think that the PA's role is good by 12% (79.6% in last month to 67.6% in May). Also, the percentage of people who think that the PA's role is bad, has remarkably increased by 12% (16% to 27.9%).
  • In opinions of people towards the role of Palestinian Legislative Council (PLC), reuslts show a decline of 6.5% (53.8% in April to 47.3% in May) of the number of people who think PLC's role is good. Also, results show a sharp decline in people's attitude towards the performance of Arafat. A comparison with the poll conducted in April, shows a decrease in Arafat's "good" performance from 67.8% to 53.7% (a decrease by 14%).
  • There is also further decline in people's trust in Palestinian leaders. Arafat's approval rating fell from 43.4% to 39.8%. In addition, the number of Palestinians who don't trust any Palestinian figure has increased from 18% to 25.5%. Moreover, trust in political or religious factions has also declined. Fateh's percentage delined from 38.5% to 37.9%. Support for Hamas and other Islamic factions delined from 10.6% and 5% to 10.3% and 4.2% respectively.
  • A comparison with the April survey shows that the number of Palestinians who believe that corruption in the Palestinian Authority is widespread increased from 34.8% in last month to 38.8% in May. Moreover, the number of people who think that corruption hardly exists in the PA decreased from 11.8% to 8.7%.
Methodology

A random sample of 1,185 Palestinians, over the age of 18 years, were interviewed throughout the West Bank and Gaza Strip on 3 and 4 April 1997. The interviews were conducted on randomly selected homes, and the subjects inside each home were also selected randomly according to Kish tables. The interviews were conducted in 60 sampling points chosen randomly according to population.

In the West Bank, 750 people were surveyed from the following areas: Jerusalem: Old city, a-Ram, Dhahya, Silwan, Wadi al-Joz, Shiekh Jarrah, Ras al-Amoud. Bethlehem: Bethlehem, Beit Jala, Beit Shahour, khader, Dihisheh RC. Hebron: Hebron, al-Aroub RC, Bani Na'im, Al-Dharieh, Rarqoumia, Doura.. Jenin: Jenin, Jenin RC, Toubas, Zababdeh, Yamoun, Kufr Dan, Ya'bad, Oabatya, al-Shuhad'a. Tulkarem & Qalqilia: Qalqilia, Tulkarem, Tuklarem RC, Ateel, Allar, Azoun.. Nablus: Nablu, Beit Fouriq, Balata RC, Salfit, Burqa, Madama, Asira al-Shamalieh. Ramallah: Ramallah, Al-Bireh, Deir Dibwan, Beitounia, Ein 'Ariq, Jalazon RC, Birzeit., Jericho: Jericho.

In the Gaza Strip, 435 people were surveyed from: Gaza, Naser, Al-Darj, al-Tufah, Sheikh Radwan, Khan Younis RC, Sabra, Magahzi RC, Khan Younis, Rafah RC, Beita, Rimal, Khan Younis al-Balad, Nuseirat, Rafah/ Tal al-Sultan, Brazil, Deir Al-Balah, Jabalia al-Balad, al-Shija'ieh, Southern Rimal, Sheikh Agaleen, Shaboura, Beit Hanoun, Beit Lahya, Breij RC Shati RC, Jabalia RC..

The major of error is plus or minus 3% with a confidence level of 95%.
