Methodology

A random sample of 1,178 people over the age of 18 were interviewed fac-to face throughout the West Bank and Gaza Strip on 19 and 20 August 1996. The interviews were conducted in randomly selected homes, and the subjects inside each home were also selected randomly according to Kish tables. The interviews were conducted in 60 sampling points chosen randomly according to population.

In the West Bank, 737 people were surveyed from the following areas: Bethlehem, Beit Jala, Beit Sahour, Hebron; Jerusalem: Jerusalem, Shufat, Jenin, Jenin Refugee Camp, Nablus, Ramallah, al-Bireh, Tulkarem, Qalqilia, Salfit, and Jericho.

In the Gaza Strip, 441 people were surveyed from: Gaza City, Deir al-Balah, Deir al-Balah RC, Nusseirat RC, Rafah, Jabalya, Jabalya a-Nazala, Jabalya RC, Khan Younis, Khan Younis RC, Rafah, Shati RC, Burij RC, Maghazi RC and 'Abasan.

The margin of error is 3 percent, with a confidence level of 95 percent.
