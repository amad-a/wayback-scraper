Palestine Online is a curated archive of websites created by Palestinians or about Palestine in the late 90s to early-mid 2000s, sourced from the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine. The project traces how the world wide web has played a role in Palestinian self advocacy and culture preservation, and is an attempt to create a free, interactive archive that revitalizes and makes newly visible the digital contributions and culture of oppressed peoples.
