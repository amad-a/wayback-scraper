Water & Waste Water Department
Director: Eng. Hazem Tarazi


The department is divided into three sections:

First: Waste Water & Storm Water Section
   Deputy Director: Eng. Hussain Abu-Zaid
   a- Networks
   b- Maintenance
   c- Treatment plant

Second: Water Section
   Deputy Director: Eng. Hamdan Zeyarah

 
Third: Planning and Coordination Section
   Deputy Director: Eng. Majed Zimmo

 

Responsibilities:

  • To supervise water projects like water networks and wells starting with planning and designing and ending by completion.
  • To supervise the sewage drainage projects, networks, and pumping plants.
  • To work the sewage water treatment plant and reuse the water produced for agricultural and organic fertilization purposes and refilling the ground tanks.
  • To coordinate and plan between this department and other municipal departments, as well as foreign bodies which prepare and implement different projects.
  • To conduct preventive and routine maintenance works for all sections of the department, and maintain all pieces of equipment needed for production in the sections of water and sewage drainage.
