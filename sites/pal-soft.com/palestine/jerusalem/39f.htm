Day Thirty Nine 

Day 39 : Sunday 05.11.2000

Huge  marches  were organized in four Palestinian towns in the West Bank to
protest the criminal Israeli bombing of Palestinian towns.

At  least  two Palestinians were killed and over a hundred other protesters
were  injured  by  Israeli  occupation soldiers bullets .

Marwan  Khalil  AL-Ghamri  (28 years old), was killed when Israeli soldiers
opened  fire  on  Palestinian  protesters near the Mintar junction near the
city  of  Gaza.  Maher  al Sa’eidi (16 year old), was killed by the Israeli
near  the  boarders of the Gaza Strip and Israel. Over 50 Palestinians were
reported injured in Gaza, seven sustaining serious wounds.

A Jewish settler who found himself in the midst of an Arab town in the West
Bank was escorted by Palestinian youths to the nearest Israeli army
roadblock.
