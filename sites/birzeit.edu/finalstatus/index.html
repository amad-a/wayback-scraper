A look at the Israeli-Palestinian peace process

There are a number of key issues that must be addressed in the final status talks between Israel and the Palestinian Authority, including closure and freedom of movement, economics, Jerusalem, refugees, settlements &: land, and water. Although construction of this website has now begun, funding for full-time workers is being sought through our Palestine Archive proposal in order to do the issues justice. "Final Status issues" is a website from the Birzeit University webteam.
