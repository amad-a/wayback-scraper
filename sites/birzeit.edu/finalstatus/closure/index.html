Closure & the freedom of movement

"Everyone has the right to freedom of movement and residence within the borders of each State."
Article 13.1, Universal Declaration of Human Rights
Freedom of movement is one of the key issues that must be addressed in the final status talks between Israel and the Palestinian Authority. This site will include a variety of material related to this issue.
