 Birzeit University Campus

The construction of Birzeit University's current campus began in 1977. The campus rests on approximately 200 acres of land, upon a hilltop that provides a vast panoramic view of the surrounding topography. The Campus is located on the outskirts of the West Bank town of Birzeit, about 20 kilometers north of Jerusalem. The masonry building and flourishing green landscaping make the campus quite unique in its beauty. Birzeit's campus is a reflection of the motivation and educational aspirations of the Arab and international communities. Built entirely from donations and sponsorship from Palestine, the Arab world and abroad, the campus was itself a success story in the face of military occupation. The currently existing and planned University buildings are:
- Science Building, built in 1980, houses the science departments, labs and several lecture halls.
- Umar Al Aqad Engineering Building, built in 1984, houses the engineering departments, workshops, laboratories as well some Faculty of Arts departments.
- Yusuf Ahmed Alghanem Library, built in 1985, is the main library on campus, and has a large collection of about 120,000 volumes including books, periodicals and reference materials in English and Arabic, as well as online services. The University also has branch libraries that focus on specific areas of knowledge and are located in the various institutes and departments.
- Administration Building, built in 1985, houses all the central administration offices.
- Kamal Nasir Auditorium, built in 1985, hosts a range of activities including lectures, conferences, and artistic and theatrical performances. The hall accommodates 288 people and is available to the University community and local individuals and institutions.
- Commerce and Economics Building, built in 1993, houses the departments of the Faculty as well as several Faculty of Arts departments.
- Institute of Law Building, built in 1998, houses a greatly expanded library and documentation center, space for research, teaching seminar rooms and classrooms, a conference hall and offices for administrative staff.
- Azeez Shaheen Medical Clinic, built in 1998, offers free basic medical services to the Birzeit University community.
- Diana Tamari Sabbagh Center for Fine Arts, Applied Arts and National Heritage built in 1999, houses Al-Qumariah Art Gallery.
- Sheikh Rashid Bin Said Al Maktoum Building, built in 1999, houses the University's student and faculty main cafeterias as well as the Media Institute, PAS, and other University institutes.
- Graduate Studies Building is under construction and scheduled for completion in 2001.
- Physical Education Building will be completed in 2001 and will augment the University's existing outdoor basketball, soccer and volleyball courts with an indoor gymnasium and other indoor facilities.
