 Birzeit University - Facts and Figures

The University Student Body
The number of students at Birzeit University reached 5447 during the first semester of the 2000/2001 academic year with female students representing 47% of the student body. The student body is a reflection of the community, representative of different geographical locations, and cultural, political and economic groups within the society.
