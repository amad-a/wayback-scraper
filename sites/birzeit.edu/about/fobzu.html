 Friends of Birzeit University

Friends of Birzeit association was formed over the years to provide moral and financial support and public relations to the University. Currently there are three branch offices for Friends of Birzeit: one in Ramallah, one in London in the United Kingdom and one in Michigan in the United States. In both the UK and the USA, the Friends of Birzeit associations have been able to obtain tax-deduction status for the University.
