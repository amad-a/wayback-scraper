Jerusalem Bloc Cartoons

Below is a selection of Jerusalem Bloc cartoons. The Jerusalem Bloc is considered the main rival to the Islamic bloc (the majority in the Student Council has alternated between these two blocs over the past 5 years) and naturally their cartoons focus on the Islamic Bloc. The cartoons represent the Islamic bloc as not having achieved anything for students and being more concerned with opposing the Jerusalem Bloc. The cartoons also represent the Jerusalem Bloc as the "middle road" and the voice of pragmatism. Their campaign has also focused on the traditional role of Fatah in the Palestinian struggle.
