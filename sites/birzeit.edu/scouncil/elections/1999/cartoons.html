Student Bloc Cartoons

One of the traditions of the Student Elections at Birzeit is the production of cartoons by each of the blocs. These cartoons often attempt to represent other blocs in a humourous or negative fashion as a way to get the message across in a more direct fashion than a leaflet. Below you will find links to the blocs which have produced cartoons so far, we have translated the captions of the cartoons into English.
