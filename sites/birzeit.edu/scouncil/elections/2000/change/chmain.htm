Change Bloc Leaflet 1

Election Program of the Change Bloc

  • Working to enhance the situation of students council as a unique umbrella to represent the students' will of the large base regardless of their belongings.
  • Working to disallow the monopolization of the student council committees from any bloc.
  • The fight to stop the policy of rates increasing and fine paying that makes education a heavy burden that many can't afford.
  • Continuing work, among the unified student movement to refuse and stop the parallel education system that class injustice and commercial investment in studying seats.
  • Work on finding constitutional formula to embody all university student from MA program in the university life and finding representation for them in the students council that fits with their presence and activities at the university.
  • Work within the unified students' movement to continue the initiative of pioneer work in different homeland issues on the base of the general will of the Palestinian nation away from any party dictation or authority.
  • Work to activate the role of students in the most essential issues… prisoners' issue, confiscation of land, making round roads and settlements…
  • Working to improve services that the university affords like the Cafeteria and the bookstore… and so on by enforcing less prices and higher quality.
  • Working to achieve social development through various activities that make wider opportunity for every student to participate more in the university life.
  • Working to achieve academic development through making workshops, exhibitions and seminars, and urging other blocks to concentrate more on this side.
  • Working on deepening the concentration on creative and artistic capabilities and taking care of them.
