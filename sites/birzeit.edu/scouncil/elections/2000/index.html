"The educational mission of Birzeit University includes promoting respect for democratic values, tolerence of diversity, and cooperation. Every year, the 51-seat student council parliament is chosen in open, democratic elections.

"The Birzeit University Student Elections are regarded by many as a barometer of Palestinian political opinion. This year, seven blocs are running, the highest number since the 1995 elections.

"In continuing the tradition of the last 4 years - Birzeit University's Elections WebTeam aims to provide daily coverage of election activities and statements of all blocs in a non-partisan and equal manner.
