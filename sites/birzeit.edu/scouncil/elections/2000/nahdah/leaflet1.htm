Nahdah Bloc Leaflet 1

These words were originally written by the martyr Khalil Al-Sharif from the Islamic bloc at Birzeit University who was previously the coordinator of AlShabibah Bloc in "The students sound" magazine. It is a letter from prison to those outside.

The Head of the Spear
The head of the spear is the first line of fighting with the enemy .. It's the first thing that makes the clash .. It carries the burden of the clash .. and at the same time it pays the price. Conversation takes place about lots of matters and it's among the matters that cannot be forgotten, and our head of spear was special and a model to others. And in our case also the rule is applied "In the game of benefit and self-interest the head of the spear is always out of the game."

"Martyrs without execution" is the most suitable sentences for their situation, despite that their situation doesn't need explanation since it speaks for it self, it also shouts loudly, but many don't hear them, since they are not experienced to hearing sounds, and these sounds are not known for them, since they are used to hearing classic music sometimes and at other times Madonna. If it's not music then it's the language of numbers and accounts.. but the sound of pain, the sound of disgrace, the sound of suffering, is difficult for them to understand, since they are the letter that this sound carries, and thus it's difficult to transfer it to them and translate the letter for them.

The letter says: "our brothers" sorry, we interfere in your affairs.. we cut your break time but you must know we are still here, we are still present in what you call "the past" … we are still present and from that long past until your future we will call you.. not because we think talking to you will change your situation but because the words of God "Remind if remember helps" The beginning of the story you know gentlemen since you were watching our movements… but we remained doing our job and lightning the castles of our homeland until the time came, we dreamed with the wanted hope, to return to our lovers again, and we saw you smiling… laughing… signing and promising… but nothing of this reaches us except some of the TV scenes that we were seeing on TV, and the view became clearer until the saying was applied to us "We dreamed of the mermaid … then the born baby was a monkey"

And the place of hope was far away from homeland and the migration was again taking us far from our home to our home… the borders increase… the walls increase… the thorny wires increase, and our future is placed in a corner in a prison cell.. Here is an Egyptian soldier that spent more than 20 years and another Iraqi soldier that has finished his judgment, those who still have the ability to speak spend most of their time in silence and their furthest hopes are to see a picture of their relatives, and sing together… "Longing for my mother's bread"

This is the story our gentlemen… It may seem boring for some or routine for others or it's not the suitable time for such issues, since we know you are busy with elections but this is life… Wind doesn't always go as we wish, and if the wind goes in the opposite direction this is our fate. At last we say… Homeland has a right that we must all satisfy and we have a right that they neglected. We are satisfied from the love of homeland and the coldness of prison cell.

An Nahdah bloc
