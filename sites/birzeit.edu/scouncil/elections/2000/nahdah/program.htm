An Nahdah Program

"Work so Allah, his messenger, and the believers will recognize your work"

Union
In order to assure the pioneering role of student council and to develop the student movement, we bring to you our union program. The main base out of which we come is fulfilling our responsibilities and the need to be honest in implementing this program hoping that Allah will help us to meet our duties and responsibilities.
General Aims
:
  • Work on keeping Birzeit open for all the Palestinian students and not allow the economic situation to become an obstacle in the face of students who want to continue their education.
  • Keeping the student council, as it was in the previous years, part of all student activities and procedures.
  • Work on representing students in Department councils to keep them in touch with academic issues.
  • Continue work on the project of Student Councils Union in all the Palestinian educational institutions in order to meet the requirements of union issues.
  • Continue supporting the Poor Student Fund and create sustainable and renewable funding resources for it.
  • Protect the right of free opinion and protect the university from the threatening corruption
  • Strengthening relationships with all those who look for the benefit of the country and its universities.
Public Relations Committee:
  • Support the Poor Student Fund through publishing the Alive Memory Agenda
  • Strengthening relations with all Palestinian student gatherings
  • Held "The Second Prisoners Week"
  • Organize an orientation day for students
  • Work on publishing an informative newsletter by the Student Council
Cultural Committee:
  • Protect the Palestinian identity and culture and fight the cultural and thought invasion that the student community faces.
  • Run a "Palestinian Cultural Week"
  • Organize lectures and conferences about the important issues.
  • Organize poetry and cultural contests
Specialization Committee
  • Reopen the Student Library and develop photocopying and computer services
  • Activate the colleges clubs and work on representing the students in the departments councils
  • Organize specialized activities such as technology and computer and specialized books exhibitions.
  • Intensive follow on of students academic problems and solving them
Financial Committee:
  • Organize projects to support the Poor Student Fund
  • Work on decreasing and abolishing some financial fines
  • Prepare the part-time work project of the students in coordination with the interested institutions
  • Raise the salaries of the student work hours in the university facilities
Social Committee:
    • Helping new students to become involved in the university atmosphere and decrease spaces between the different groups
    • Strengthening brotherhood and collegial relationships between students through joint activities between departments and faculties
    • Develop services in the student hostels and follow up on the problems
    • Care about handicapped students and support them in solving their problems
    Cafeteria Committee:
    • Develop student council cafeteria
    • Organize a "consumer week" in coordination with the financial committee
    • Observe the services and costs in the central cafeteria to prevent increases
    Health and Environment Committee
    • Organize a medical week accompanied with newsletters and lectures to spread health awareness among students
    • Develop Health Insurance Program
    Athletic Committee
    • Provide athletic resources
    • Organize athletic races that include various athletic activities on the university level as well as on the level of the rest of Palestinian universities
    Cooperation Work Committee:
    • Activate the role of student cooperation works in serving the Palestinian community
    • Participate in planting trees especially in lands threatened by settlements
    • Organize cooperation work trips during the various seasons
    Art Committee
    • Develop art talents through specialized courses
    • Reinvigorate Palestinian Heritage
    • Organize plays

    This is our program and these are our aims, we'll not stop unless we achieve all of it… We pray for God to help us in achieving it

    An Nahda Bloc
