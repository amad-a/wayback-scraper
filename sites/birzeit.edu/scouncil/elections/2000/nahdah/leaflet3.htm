Nahdah Bloc Leaflet 3

He Remained silent for ages and when spoke, he expressed disbelief in God

Brothers and sisters… Sons of Birzeit University
The Palestinian people in particular, and the Arab and Islamic people, in general are facing a difficult time. During this time, they lost their way and mistaken direction and accepted for themselves what they rejected in the previous years. They left leading the world and returned as followers. Some started to look for the way by adopting thoughts from here and there - eastern or western thoughts. Say from east, where this nation was and how great it was there during his life there or after he was defeated. Independent or following others. And now he follows others in a blind form. He follows from failure to lead others.

These days pass while the nation lost its self motivation and some thought that they will take it by applying thoughts that became nowadays in need to support these thoughts to overcome the troubles that they created for man. They created it under a colored material cover that was given several names. It was called knowledge once and other names that are free as the wolf was free from Joseph's blood.

Brothers and Sisters Finally the awaited messenger of this age appeared on Birzeit University, the messenger of deliverance, liberation, and democracy. Messenger of the revolution against traditions and habits. The messenger who will break the "habits and traditions" appeared. He appeared carrying his "program document", or "the message of breaking traditions and habits". He carries words of destruction double words of construction. He brought these words to us after a long period promising prosperity and deliverance. He brought them after a long absence that made us say "each late, will carry the good". And when he came, he was like a mirage that the thirst thinks water till he arrives on it to find it nothing. He carried these words to us while he promised us to be Abraham who breaks the idols with his axe, but he struck the axe on his own people's head

Brothers and Sisters After years and years, the Democratic Pole came out to us with his program that has no "program". It contains only flexible words to throw dust in the eyes to prevent seeing the clear fact. To prevent observing the loss of the way and the absence of the core in their program…

Empty from everything with the exception of the destructive words and not constructive ones. It contains only attachment to the visions of solution that depends on hopes and thoughts that support us from here or there. Who gave them the authority to decide that what they apply is the truth and not anything else. Did not they apply in their previous time a system and then asked later on support to get rid of the evils that it caused the world? So how are they able to decide what suits this nation and benefit it and what does not? Who told them that what suits a particular culture is suitable for another one? Or did they think that the Palestinian community is distinguished from other communities with marginal differences that have no role in forming it with a manner different from other communities?

We, in the Islamic Bloc, considered what is called the Democratic Pole and its program document as a reforming of thoughts that became part of the recorded history. All its attempts to jump over the facts through repeating expired thoughts by trying to reform it hoping to give life back to bodies that are living their final moments.
