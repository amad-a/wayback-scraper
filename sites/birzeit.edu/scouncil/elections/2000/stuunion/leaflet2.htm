Student Union Leaflet 2

Union and Social Program
  1. Establishing a special committee to deal with the problem of students' residence: We'll initiate to establish this committee from the owners of the estates and the students living in them and the student council. The aim of this committee is to assure that the students renting these estates and buildings get all the rights through forming the minimum facilities and set of laws that protect them. And it will form a clear address to which new students can go to find locations in the area.
  2. Increase the opening hours of the library and provide the latest publications to provide a developed educational level We'll work to reach an agreement that makes the library open until the end of the day at least. We'll also work on a cooperation project between the library and the other libraries, Arab and international publishing companies to provide the university with the latest publications.
  3. Forming a student committee to form laws about using the facilities of the university is a legal right. It should be mentioned that the administration of the student building cooperated well with the student movement during the time of the sit in; that was held to protest against the student arrests. But the issue is more practical and core than that. From the beginning it should have been invested for the benefit of student council.
  4. let us work together to use the achievements of the student movement by forming an agreement between the blocs and a joint agenda. We do not want to suggest deleting elections. We are not suggesting an unrealistic program. All we want is to unify the student movement in an organized form away from reactions and to form a code of honor. Out of this we can achieve a joint program that activates the role of the student movement in the face of the surrounding dangers.
