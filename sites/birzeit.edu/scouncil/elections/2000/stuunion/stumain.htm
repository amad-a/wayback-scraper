Students Union - Organizational Platform

The Students' Union Bloc is a student political and social organization that works in Palestinian schools, institutes and universities.

Student Union Banner from first day
The Bloc works in the student community as a part of the Palestinian Student Movement aiming to guarantee better educational circumstances. It seeks to provide equal educational opportunities regardless of gender, social, religious or other ideology. At the same time, it works to achieve an academic level that is able to prepare the students to face life confidently. The Bloc considers raising students' awareness of social and national issues one of its priorities. The Bloc adopts the ideology of the Palestinian Popular Party (PPP) guided by the Arabic and global progressive science and heritage alongside the values of freedom, justice, and equality. In its practical and theoretical practice, the bloc adopts the national Palestinian values in general and the Student Movement values in particular. In addition to that, it adopts all the progressive characteristics in the Arab global heritage.

Our Views

Academic
As a student union bloc in the Palestinian Educational Institutions, achieving a better educational level is its main aim. We believe that the student who is not committed to his studies, will not be committed to his homeland and people. As a result of this belief, the Bloc conducts various activities to develope the students' academic level through providing academic guidance and support. The bloc also works on increasing and developing opportunities to benefit the university itself. The bloc looks for an academic system that is more connected to the practical life and more effective. A system that supports students' knowledge and develops their capabilities.

Union
We give a great deal of attention to working with the Student Union, considering this important to achieve our aims. We consider contributions from the students in creating the regulations and the systems related to their life, as a continual union request. At the same time, we consider it important to be active in the student council and the other student bodies. Additionally, we emphasize the importance of coordinating between the student union bodies and the Employees Union to achieve a unified union that is able to achieve more in order to make Birzeit a place of justice and democracy.

Student Union Alliance
In order to neutralize disagreement upon points, and looking for points of agreement between all the democratic progressive persons in the homeland, we work on achieving the widest possible union and student alliance; an alliance that contains all the democratic student bodies and independent democratic individuals. At the same time, we want this alliance to be a middle way between the stream that represses in order to ensure unity, and the stream that remains inflexible in its beliefs. We seek the middle way, between the politically repressive stream and the inflexible stream. We want this stream to be embody differences of ideas and invest in efforts to protect the right of being different as well as efforts to achieve joint aims. We seek a democratic alliance that is far from bias and individuality in order to create a new alliance of liberal realistic responsibility. A alliance that allows progress on all levels to reach a responsible student movement that is self-ware.

Cultural and Thought Movement

Because the university stage is an important one in forming the student personality and discovering his talents for its richness with experiences that have wide-ranging affects; and because it is the main place that works on alerting thought and culture, the bloc works on increasing students' awareness to benefit from the variety of thought and political ideologies during the university time. The aim of all this is to develop the students in particular and the Palestinian community in general. The bloc considers working for a university life that develops culture and thought; and develops the student character in a form that suits his talents, one of its main aims. At the same time, it works on creating an open and renewable cultural philosophy.

Socially

We believe strongly in social justice and we are aware of the fact that discrimination aims to shake the national unification and block the liberal thought streams. For that we work on increasing the awareness of social justice and democracy concepts. Alongside that, we work on protecting human rights and the interests of all the social classes and guarantee the highest equality in rights and duties. Related to our awareness of women's role in the society and giving attention to the despotism that she faces, we realize that it is our duty to fight for a more just social system. A system under which women is given equal opportunities as men and they take their role in decision making.

National Liberation Struggle

We agree that the current stage of our nation's life is a liberation national stage, for that our bloc works on assuring our national demands such as:
  • Liberating the Palestinian land from the Israeli occupation
  • Palestinian self determination
  • Complete National Independence
  • Return of all the refugees
  • Establishing the Palestinian state with supremacy and Jerusalem as its capital

At the time we ask for applying the international resolutions that demand that Israel withdraw from the land occupied in 1967 and the return of the refugees. We give attention to the historical despotism that our people faced as a result of the 1948 occupation of the Palestinian land; and the negative effect of all that on the memory of the Palestinians.

The Israeli dealing with the Oslo process shows a determination to avoid withdrawal from the majority of the occupied Palestinian land and to dedicate its occupation to this land. At the same time, it wants to limit the Palestinians to a limited self-ruling. This policy shows, alongside what is mentioned before, an attempt to destroy the basis of a Palestinian independent state. All that shows the importance of reinforcing the Palestinian struggle to achieve its central aim, which is getting rid of occupation.

The Palestinian National Authority

The PNA, regardless of its limited authorities, is the first national authority on the Palestinian land. It forms the core of the Palestinian State, for that building the PNA institutions is an important opportunity for the Palestinians. Here there is a need to distinguish between the authority with its institutions that meet the interests of the entire nation and the government that leads this authority. The bloc's position towards the previous mentioned government depends actually on its commitment to the legal national Palestinian aspirations. It depends also on the work of the government to achieve the Palestinians interests to lead a dignified life and health services and social welfare and equal opportunities. Our position depends also on government respect of democratic principles and human rights and the application of these principles.

Membership:

Membership Conditions:

The member in Progressive Student Union Bloc is a student of Birzeit University and is committed to the following:

  • Approve the program of the bloc and its organizational platform
  • Participates in the activities and the meetings of the bloc
  • Pays the symbolic monthly participation
  • Not belonging to another bloc

Affiliation to the bloc:

  • The student applies with a written questionnaire and should have a recommendation from a member of the bloc
  • Becomes a member after the approval of the bloc's local body and he is granted a membership identity card
  • Membership ends in a case of resignation or a immoral or criminal action
  • In exceptional cases, the local body of the bloc has the right to block the membership of the member conditioned by the approval of two thirds of the conference members.

Rights and Duties

A) Rights
  • Participating, in a democratic manner and according to the bloc's organizational platform, in forming the policy and designing the aims of the bloc. The member has the right to criticize the bodies of the bloc through the working organizational authorities. He also has the right to make any complaints and to participate in discussing these issues.
  • Accessing the reports and information about the work of the bloc through the formal authorities.
  • The member has the right to express his individual right about any issue whenever and wherever but without pointing that this is the formal position of the bloc.
  • The member has the right to nominate and vote in all the bloc bodies.
  • The member has the right to leave the bloc whenever he likes but he should tell the bloc about that. His return to the bloc needs the approval of the local body of the bloc or the approval of two thirds of the conference members.
  • The bloc is committed to protect its members and save them if they were attacked as a result to their activities and missions on behalf of the bloc.
B) Duties
  • Commitment to the policies, program, and organizational platform of the bloc.
  • Strengthening the students' relations with the bloc and fighting for its interests. In addition to that, listening to the bloc's opinions and clarifying the policies of the bloc and its aims alongside working with it and direct its policies.
  • Working on widening the base of the bloc through gathering new members
  • Caring about developing the self in the academic, cultural, and political aspects.
  • Having high moral characteristics.

The Structure of the Bloc

A) Organizational Principles
Democracy defines the relationships inside the bloc, as well as a commitment to the decisions of the elected bodies and applying the decisions of the majority with respect to the minorities right to express its opinion yet commitment to the decision of the majority. All this is achieved through balanced discussion, being tolerant of different opinions and the expression of these opinions.

Following are some organizational principles:

  • The bloc depends on wide democratic representation in forming its bodies and depends on democratic elections in secret voting. The bloc applies the principle of renewal and continuity
  • The bloc applies the principle of open and free discussion related to all its activities and the work of its bodies and members.
  • The bloc depends on the principle of distribution of responsibility and the specialized actions of the bodies and members.
  • Decisions are made in a democratic manner through the approval of the majority. The minority has the right to fight for their opinion, yet it has to be committed to the majority's trend.
  • Exchanging the reports among the different bodies is considered one of the main aims for the work of the bloc.

B) the structure of the Bloc:
Following is the structure of the Student Union Bloc:
Bloc Executive Conference: it forms the members of the active committees inside the bloc, these committees are:

  1. Secretary Committee (the secretary of the bloc)
  2. Cultural Committee
  3. Financial Committee
  4. Media and Public Relations Committee
  5. Internal coordinating Committee
  6. It is possible to have two additional committees:
    • Specialization Committee
    • Social Committee

The local Body of the Bloc:
Consists of:

  • the secretaries of the previous committees
  • The secretary of the bloc
  • Public Conference (consisted of all the bloc members)
Bloc Executive Conference
The representatives of the conference are considered to be the leadership of student activities inside the bloc. The conference meets monthly and it supervises the implementation of the activities outline for the semester through the elected committees. The conference discusses the reports of the local body and the secretary. The conference passes its recommendations to the local body. The decisions of the conference are considered obligatory to all the members of the bloc.
Bloc Local Body:
The local body is the execution body of the conference. It is considered the daily leading cell of the bloc. This body meets at least once a week and follows the student activities and statements, in addition to coordinating the work with the rest of student blocs in the university. The local body also supervises the implementation of the conference decisions. This body consists of the committees' secretaries in addition to the bloc secretary. Each committee hands the secretary and the conference a monthly report.
Bloc Secretary:
The secretary is responsible for running the activities of the bloc in the duration between the meetings of the local body and ensures implementing its decisions. The secretary leads the meetings of the local body and he prepares the reports about implementing the decisions and the activities. He coordinates the local body meeting agenda and represents the bloc in the campus especially with the student affairs department, other student blocs, and outside the university. The secretary is not allowed to nominate himself more than twice. He has to be at least for one year in the university and have experience of student movement activities at the time he nominates himself for the first time.
General Conference:
Considered the highest body in the bloc and its decisions are obligatory for all the members. It elects the committees' secretaries, executive conference members, and the bloc secretary. The conference is consisted of all the bloc members who have the right of applying, nominating and electing the membership. It forms the organizational systems and modifies them and develops them. The conference meets at least once each semester or by a decision of the executive conference or by two thirds of the public conference. It is authorized to elect the bloc nominations to the general student council elections.

Notes:

  1. The general board of the bloc (general conference), is considered the highest authority in the bloc. It is responsible for making the critical decisions such as alliances with other blocs and the approval of the principles and the organizational platform
  2. The executive conference has the right to establish, when there is a need, permanent or temporary committees to help in achieving the works of the bloc in particular fields. Such an action is conditioned on not opposing the general structure of the bloc.
  3. The executive conference is consisted of 21 members elected directly by the student base and includes the bloc secretary and the committees' secretaries. Electing the executive conference members and the bloc secretary and the local committees' secretaries takes place each year.
