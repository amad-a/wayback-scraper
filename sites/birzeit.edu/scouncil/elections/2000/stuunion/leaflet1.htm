Student Union Leaflet 1

Here, we suggest issues that we'll work to embody during the coming year. We'll work on them alongside the rest of our union and social ideas that form our tendency and as stated in our organizational platform.

Academic Program

  1. No for deleting the Palestinian memory… Yes to return back the course the "Palestinian Question" as a university requirement: This is a required course in the cultural studies. The course was dropped just when we pass through this critical stage of the policy of abandonment by the PNA
  2. Reserving the academic staff makes graduation quicker and eliminates the pressure in the following year. Many students face the lack of particular courses in some semesters. For that reason they are forced to delay their graduation for a semester or two. Others leave the university until these courses are offered once again.
  3. Adopt New Specialization (Law for example) in the BA program to provide larger academic choices. Opening new doors with new majors encourages a larger number of students to choose Birzeit. In addition to that it attracts different academic qualified cadre instead of immigration abroad. We think that law is the most important major that should be applied as a BA degree
