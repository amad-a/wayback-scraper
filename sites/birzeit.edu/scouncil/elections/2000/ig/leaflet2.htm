Letter from the fighter Etiaf Alayan to Birzeit university women students

My sisters:
By the name of God
All wars with most God enemies has calmed, and this war was coming to you. Yes, to you, you are a difficult war… Satan has grouped for it lots of soldiers from humans… This war seems to be merciful but it's torture, seems to be nice sentences for freedom and Independence and development but it's for taking you from your roots and cutting all the relations of love between you and your family to be as God said "Like a malicious word like a malicious tree taken from above earth and doesn't have end in earth"

When you talk they want to shut you saying that hearing your voice is not allowed in Islam They think continuing your study and education is development even if you are clever, Development means for them to get out of your Islamic clothes and Islamic morals that God described as "The clothes of devoutness is better" to satisfy hungry eyes. They don't think your travel with a male relative development because that protects you from suspicion. They want you to travel alone to give chance to wolfs to get you alone. They don't you to send for one from your family and one from you husband's family in case of trouble, but they want you to go to police and get your husband to prison and to bargain for your honor as happened many times.

They want you to be independent and no to follow anyone to make their job in tearing you easy, that means tearing your family and your community… because you are the support of this community.

The Quran came down when a girl asked the prophet to protect her house, the story says that the husband of Kawlah Bint Tha'labah divorced her so she went to the prophet asking him to tell her what to do, so he told her that the divorce is finished but she remained arguing with him, telling him that they have children, if she takes them with her they will be hungry and if she gave them to her husband they will get lost, this woman made clear with cleverness the responsibility and cooperation between the couple, what will happen if one left this responsibility, and the Quran came down that the husband must pay money to return to his wife and this is not divorce.

You sister, in this time, can you stand in front on this danger. Go forward my sister and don't be afraid to be blamed so that God will help you to repair what many couldn't repair.

Your sister, Om hamdy
