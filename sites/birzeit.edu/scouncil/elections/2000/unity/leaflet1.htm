Unity Bloc (Al Wehdeh) Leaflet 1

Our colleagues at Birzeit University
Greetings to you who remain steadfast in this firm castle

Another year has passed and the Student movement is busy with fights between each other, not worrying about student's issues and troubles to the extent that it can't continue in such a situation. To protect Birzeit university and to continue in its role in protecting students interests we need to unify all students and blocs with a program that reflects students interests and protects their rights with clear objectives.

The economic situation our people endure is getting worse while the University administration is going on with a policy that doesn't benefit the university nor its students but benefits individuals, as if the university is converting into an investing company.

The hourly rate doesn't suit most students, support is neglected, and introduction of the parallel education program only gains profits for the university. The university is accepting a large number of MA students for the reason of solving the financial problems of the university, also the housing rates are sometimes more than it is in richest countries of the world.

On the other hand we see that the students movement is silent on the privatization policy of the university to protect its benefits and not taking responsibility of protecting the needy students by employing them in these services or protecting them from their costly rates that exceeds their capabilities.

The Al Wehdeh bloc at Birzeit University is asking all students to stand beside it in its students program and is telling them: "Hand by Hand with the students program and a clear motivation toward wider participation from the student council."

Towards an effective and unified students movement
AlWehdeh Bloc
Birzeit University
21/4/2000
