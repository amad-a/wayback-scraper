Unity Bloc Platform

Unity Bloc

The Unity Bloc believes in the right of all to participate in making decisions on the base of equal opportunities depending on the social justice. And proceeding from the responsibility of the community and its institutions towards the individual. The program of Unity Bloc depends on the belief that the united struggle is the main element of success. And that the role of the individual effects the individual and the group which creates from the individuals the true leadership of the community. The Bloc opens its doors for all students to achieve the participation of the widest available part of students in implementing the following program:

Union Program

Academic

:
  • The right of all students in studying and majoring and transferring between faculties. In addition to that providing a studying atmosphere in which the problem of overcrowding in the sections is solved where the number of students in sections should not exceed 30.
  • Decrease the costs of credit hours because of the difficult economic situation that our people face. Also return back to the classification system depending on social survey by student council, student affaires department, and employees union
  • Fighting the privatization of the university and find work opportunities for students in coordination with the national institutions. In addition to that activating the poor student fund through participation of the university staff and the national capital and social institutions
  • Establishing a committee from the student blocs, student affairs department to protect the students from exploitation by real estate owners
  • Establishing a support and guidance committee to help the students in the difficult courses through organizing lectures and support courses through the contribution of teachers and the brilliant students. The committee works also on introducing students to the laws of the university and guides them to choose the majors according to their desires and the community needs.
  • Reserve training opportunities to the students during their study period
  • Struggle to decrease prices of books and establishing a specialized library that provides the BA and Master students with the needed books and other materials.
  • Struggle in the General Union of the Palestinian students to provide the scholarships for the poor and outstanding students
  • Support the right of Gaza students to permanent education in the West Bank and give attention to this case in the registration office.
  • Reinforce the role of student council in forming the policy of registration and admission and fighting the personal and geographical discrimination so as to make the academic dimension the main measurement.
  • Provide a Master program as the rest of the universities in the world and provide a specialized library for such a program
  • Reject the exploitatitive phenomena that the university applies through the parallel education system.

Second: Community- Democracy Field:

We invite for the following in order to participate in building a civil democratic society based on multiplicity, respect other opinion, organization freedom:
  1. reinforce the democratic dialogue as a method in establishing the relationships between individuals and groups alongside rejecting violence
  2. reinforce the democratic education and held lectures and workshops about this issue
  3. organize and embody the program of the open day about student problems in cooperation with all the university community parts
  4. Prohibit the interfere of the security bodies in the university issues. Prohibit arresting students and prohibit the work of students in the security bodies. This work contradicts the independence of the university and its sanctity and democracy
  5. Respect the political multiplicity and thought and rights freedom and meetings and press freedom
  6. Establish a national observing body to form a reference point.
  7. Release all the arrested students in the PNA prisons and prohibit political arrests

We, in the Union Bloc, think that the student movement in Birzeit in particular and in the country able to protect the achievements on the way to embody the legal national rights and in building a civil society through the following:

  1. Develop the unity and organization of the student movement through forming developed forms able to reinforce the role of the student movement in the society.
  2. Strengthening the communication between the student movement and the various national and community problems of the community.
  3. Re appreciate many fields in which the role of student movement deteriorated especially the voluntary works and the Summer local and international camps, Palestine week, and the art festivals that should be open for all students to participate in.
  4. Reinforce the collective work spirit and the joint work between the students and the society.
  5. Strengthening gender relationships on the base of respect, justice, quality, and participating in making decisions and reinforce self confidence and collective will
  6. Strengthening relationships with Palestinians inside 1948 borders through trips and visits to the Palestinian cities and villages there and by held joined works and exchange on a joined struggle base.

Political Program

Our people's issue is the issue of the nation that was uprooted from its land and was moved to be refugees in the nearby countries. Palestine was, and still, an Arabic land on which the Palestinians lived and formed their identity as a part of the Arab nation. It is the issue of all Palestinians in West Bank, Gaza, Jerusalem, 1948 lands, and the Diaspora. It is a unified nation that fights for its legal rights that were admitted by the international resolutions. Peace is not possible without the legal Palestinian rights through practicing his supremacy on his land and establishing his independence on the base of right of return and self-determination and establishing its independence state with Jerusalem its capital. A state in which the human freedom and rights are protected alongside protecting dignity and the state is built on the base of justice, equality and the supremacy of law.

The political agreements (Oslo and the following agreements), that were signed by one part of PLO in an illegal manner in the behalf of PLO, do not represent the minimum of the Palestinian interests and legal rights. They lead to political and social division and produced parasitic classes and an authority that the space between it and people increases day after day. An authority that dedicates corruption and repression of general freedom. It leads our people and rights towards a dark tunnel and creates new obstacles in the face of embodying the national project. Continuing negotiations with Barak government, while the continual of settling and forcing the Jewish characteristics on Jerusalem alongside its aggressive policy against our people, will create new obstacles in the face of reforming the national position. At the same time it blocks the resistance choice and advocating the people to press on the enemy government till it admit our rights of independence and return.

Announcing the supremacy of the Palestinian State on all the land occupied in the year 1967 and reserving the elements of this state forms a national request. And it forms a political and practical response to the Israeli government and its exceeding of all the agreements regardless of how much these agreements are injustice.

We, in the United Bloc, in Birzeit University announce the following, following from the previous positions:

  1. Condemn the negligence of the national chart and the free deleting of some items in it according to the Israeli and American requests. The deleting that was applied before solving all the main elements that form the Palestinian Question and reaching a justice resolving of these elements, that meets the legal Palestinian national rights.
  2. Stopping the current negotiations with Israel till Israel is committed to the following:
    • Stop settlment construction that increases daily
    • Release all Arab and Palestinian prisoners from the enemy prisons
    • Stop the procedures of giving Jerusalem Jewish characteristics and isolating it from the rest of homeland.
    • Stop the aggressive policy that is represented in arresting, destroying houses, stealing the Palestinian fortunes, and other procedures.

The method of recapturing the national rights is through the return to PLO on new basis and democracy and electing new national council (free democratic elections). A council that represents all the Palestinians and forming a national political reference to supervise the final status negotiations. And referendum all our people about the results of these negotiations. The negotiations should depend on the resolutions of our national council especially the independence declaration round and not admitting the American role in the area. The American role aims to create a political, economic, and secure Regional regime that reinforces its control of people and resources in the area in the frame of globalization that America heads and fights for everywhere. For that, applying the National Unity and the connection between all negotiations channels, as a pressing tool, and allowing struggle, forms an entrance for a more balanced political reconciliation.

Union Work and Economy:

  • Fight to protect the right of establishing unions to all the classes and unifying these unions to fight for the interests and rights of people
  • Establishing active institutions to face unemployment and the covered unemployment.
  • Increase the budgets assigned for education, health, industry, agriculture, environment, and developing the infrastructure instead of stealing money and distribute it on a limited group to corrupt people.
  • Stop applying the economic agreements as a step to liberate the national economy
  • Fight the administratitive, financial and legal corruption

These are the main elements of our political and union struggle program and it is your program as well and the voice of your conscience. Through your efforts and determination, hope, will, success, and achievements will be made.
