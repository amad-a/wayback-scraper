The Democratic Pole Election Platform

The democratic Pole is a union, cultural, and political body aims to gather the students with national and democratic beliefs. Students who belong to the homeland and people issues; issues such as; human rights, social justice, dignity, development, and revolution thoughts in a manner that allows expressing opinions and held dialogue and initiate. At the same time, it adopts the students' cultural, union, and human issues.

Membership in the Pole is based on an individual decision with protecting the right of belonging to other bodies outside the university unless there is no contradiction with the main principles and the program document of the Pole. The Pole is guided by the changing cultural identity and reproducing of this identity with attention to the local characteristics with its contradictions and moving. The Pole considers all what is positive in the Arab and international heritage one of the basis on which it is built.

The Pole works to reform student awareness in a scientific and modern manner. It directs this awareness towards facing the racist occupation and criticizing the traditional structure represented in dominating and monopolizing the facts, ignoring other opinions, corruption and the absolute ruling. The Pole is a branch of the National Democratic Palestinian Project.

Background

The student movement had a significant role during Intifada and the other struggle years. The results of Oslo agreements and the followed agreements created new situation on land that blocked implementing the National Project and affected the rest of the Palestinian gatherings. As we noticed, the student movement was one of the parts affected by the case of dividing and retreat that led to decrease and delay its role. For that we see a need to reform its themes and structure to retake its role.

No doubt there are many changes in the Palestinian situation during the previous years. The main change is the marginalizing and emptying of the PLO. And the establishment of a Palestinian authority with control but not with supremacy on a small portion of the West Bank and Gaza Strip. Consequently, the importance of the civil society cores increased.

One of the main causes of the Pole establishing was the need of the student movement in particular, and our people in general, for the third stream that is between the authority stream and the Islamic stream. The Pole aims to raise once again the role of the student movement in the new phase.

The previous situation raised the need to experience the endless new manner. This experience is able to go on and renew with the renew of the thoughts and individuals in a cumulative manner that attempts to add qualified experience to the philosophy of the Palestinian struggle.

The reconciliation with Israel created a change in the locations of the main trends in Palestine (Right, Islamic, and Left) connected with the globalization and the American domination that has alliance with Israel and its reconciliation project.

It was not coincidence that the start was from student movement for the experiences, characteristics, and cadres it has. That might be a launch of a new trend in Palestine.

For four years, the actions among this experience and the results showed that it is possible to fight against the blocs in the face of its success. Fearing that the Pole will abolish any activity or body is not supported by the four years experience. At the time there is a need to protect the presence of the Pole, there is a need to protect the presence of its structure units with no effect of these units right to remain or withdraw. Belonging is elective, with our wishes to those who withdrawn and we give our hand to them to cooperate in order to protect the Pole's body and go ahead with it.

The Democratic Pole does not see the current political Palestinian parties as the correct tool to make the change. At the same time, the Pole does not deny its existence within the absence of the alternative that the Pole tries to contribute in forming a model of it and create it on the land. The Pole did not, and will never, aim to compete on the political leadership. It aims to push the political crisis parties, through its programs, to change through the involvement of the trends that believe in this choice. We see the "Political Islam" as the mean to lag the society as it is experienced in several Arab countries. Here we mention that religion is a central issue in our suggestion; we see it as an individual or group right and to believe in the legacy of this belief. For that we see the importance of secularism "applying science to identify the moral and behavior values. Internal Structure

The Pole consists of three bodies:
  1. Public Board
  2. Conference
  3. Administration office (Committees Secretary)

Public Board

  1. consisted of the base of the Pole
  2. considered the highest legislation body in the Pole
  3. meets minimum once each semester by the invitation of the Committees secretary. 50%+1 of the conference members have the right to invite for an exceptional meeting.
  4. The meeting agenda is prepared by the committees secretary
  5. The public board meets by the majority of 51% of the members and if it is not possible, the meeting is delayed two days and the committees secretary should announce the new and final date for the members.
  6. The meeting is administrated by the secretary or who takes his place
  7. At the beginning of each meet, the members have the right to discuss the agenda and suggest adding or deleting items from the agenda.

Authorities of the general Board

  1. Elects the conference members, committee secretaries, and the representatives of the Pole in to the student council elections
  2. The majority of the general board have the right to invite it for meeting to distrust the conference or the committees secretary or the secretary or to modify the program document of the Pole
  3. The secretary and the committees' secretary portray their report to the general board.
  4. Has the right to decide in the critical and main issues inside the Pole and the opinion of the majority should be the director of the Pole tendency

Conference

  1. The second authorized body after the general board and it aims to widen participating in making decisions on the first hand, and to create an observer of the work of the Pole bodies, on the other hand. These bodies include the secretary and the committees' secretary. It also aims to enable gaining and exchanging the experiences about the issues related to the work of the Pole and decreases the space between the Pole bodies.
  2. Conference members are elected directly by the general board.
  • Conditions of the conference members:
    • No academic probation
    • At least a member for one semester in the Pole
    • Paid his monthly and yearly participation
    • Apply for one of the cadre courses that the Pole organizes
  • The conference held his periodical meeting monthly
  • The conference elects in the first meeting the vise of the secretary and the conference coordinator
  • The Pole secretary and the committees' secretary have the right to invite for exceptional meetings of the conference. At the same time, two thirds of the conference members have the right to invite for such a meeting through a petition signed by them and presented to the committees' secretary to invite for the meeting.
  • The conference coordinator through coordinating with the secretary designs the meeting agenda.
  • Legal quorum of the meetings is 51% of the conference members. If the quorum is not complete, the meeting waits for 15 minutes, and if it remained not complete, invite for a meeting after two days and it is legal by any number attends.
  • All the conference members have equal rights through the meeting and no body has characteristics more than others.
  • If the conference member absented two meetings of the four semester meetings, loses his membership in the conference and in the body in which he works. The committees' secretary asks the conference to freeze the member of the secretary who absents the meetings.
  • The Pole members have the right to attend the conference meetings as listeners.
  • The conference has its supremacy and it has the right to take decisions about the issues of the Pole. In a case that a decision might affect the ideology of the Pole, the conference role is to recommend freezing the implementing of an item through the approval of two thirds of the members. And the conference in this case is responsible for its decision in front of the general board meeting that has the final decision to implement or freeze it.
  • The members are committed to the agenda according to the meeting mechanism and each member has the right to suggest anything to the agenda. Such suggestions should be presented to the conference coordinator at least a week before.
  • The conference elects the preparing committee for the next elections in the Pole
  • The conference meetings are head by the conference coordinator
  • The conference duration is a study year that starts in the first semester and ends in the following first semester in the second year.
  • Each member in the student council conference is a member in the Pole conference
  • Designs the general policy of the Pole that does not contradict the main aims of the Pole and it prepares the coming program document for the coming phase
  • Designs the semester participation of the Pole
  • Takes the law decisions against those who exceed the Pole organizational platform.
  • Authorized to elect the Pole representatives on the local and international levels in coordination with the secretary and the committees' secretary
  • The simple majority (50% +1) of the attendance takes decisions. If votes are equal, the decision is given to the side in which the secretary votes. But the decision of ending the Pole or modifying any item in its organizational platform are taken by the majority of two thirds.
  • If any member in the committees secretary resigned, the conference elects an alternative member that has to be a member in the conference
  • The conference has the right to question the Pole bodies if they do not fulfill their duties or if they exceeded the general lines of the Pole policies. Such questioning takes place in its periodical meetings.
  • The conference elects its coordinator and the coordinating committee.

    Administrating Office (Committees' Secretary)

    1. It is the highest body in the Pole and leads it through its round. It is consisted of the secretary, vise secretary, and seven elected members each one is a head of a committee in the Pole
    2. Follow on and implement the semester activities plan of the Pole
    3. The committee's secretary round is one year; starts in the first semester and ends in the first semester of the following year.
    4. Implements the daily missions and leads the activities of the bloc in the field.
    5. His periodical meeting is held weekly
    6. As a body, it questions the members of the committees' secretary if they exceed the organizational platform of the Pole or if they do not fulfill their duties or if they absent the meetings.

        Election Mechanism:

        Free elections method is applied in the Pole elections either in electing the leading bodies or electing the Pole representatives in the student council elections. The measurement in choosing is the ability and activity and the program that the nominate presents in a public meeting to introduce the nominated to the base. This mechanism is considered, as a new method of evaluating and questioning that will be applied by the end of the body duration. Evaluation takes place in open meeting where questioning and rewarding depends on the achievements of the body.

        Electing the Interior Leadership: Takes place by the end of the second semester. In this election, the base chooses:

        • Pole Conference Members
        • Committees' Secretary. Elections for each committee takes place separately
        • Pole Secretary

        Electing Pole's representatives to the student council elections: Takes place around the middle of the second semester. The members chose 51 nominations who are nominated to the student council elections Following this process, the 51 nominated members elect the members of the student council secretary and the members of the conference depending on the votes' results.

        Membership: The democratic Pole is a model aims to arrive with its members to a case of activating their energies. For that, the member is the core of the interests and the main base of building. The Pole aims to create the suitable atmosphere for the individual to express his talents in order to develop the society. There is an emphasis on the relationship between the individual and the collective in a form that does not delete the presence of the individual in the collective atmosphere.

        Depending on that, it is clear that the Pole does not aim to delete the community diversity based on gender, religion, or thought. On the contrary, the Pole works on encouraging the multiplicity and diversity in order to achieve a case where it is possible to get the maximum benefit of the individual and the and the groups. Depending on that, the rights and duties of membership inside the Pole are derived. Rights are not based on historical heritage or a particular achievement. Such a method carries us to a phase where the member can not ask for his rights before fulfilling his duties and presenting achievements.         Disciplinary Procedures

        These procedures are implemented against those who exceed the regulations of the Pole by the majority of 50%+1 of the conference members. The procedures are: Probation. First warning. Second warning. Freeze membership. Dismiss by the approval of two thirds of the conference members.

        General commands

        1. deleting or modifying any item in this organizational platform requires a decision proposal by the conference and the approval of the conference by the majority of 51% of the general board
        2. the Pole conference has the right of freezing implementing any item if that supports the interests of the Pole. Such action requires the approval of two thirds of the conference members and it is responsible for its decision in front of the general board in the first meet. The general board has the authority of approving or disapproving any decision and the conference has to accept the general board decision regardless of its tendency.
        3. If there is a difference in explaining any item, the conference is the authorized body to explain and he is questioned in front of the general board and this explanation is obligatory for all the Pole members.
        4. The member has the right to apply for the secretary position only twice
        5. The committees' secretaries and the conference members are allowed to absent only two frequented meetings without accepted excuses.
        6. The organizational order inside the Pole starts with the general board as the highest body then the conference and the committees' secretary
