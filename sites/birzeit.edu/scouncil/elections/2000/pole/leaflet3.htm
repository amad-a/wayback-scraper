Democratic Pole Leaflet 3

The Crisis and the Pole as Alternative

The elements of the Palestinian crisis increase daily and they touch the simple details of our daily life. It is obvious that no single day passes without an accident or quarrel. A simple survey of these year events shows that the elements of the Palestinian community are being destroyed. This is not strange, what is strange is to find that the ruling authority, either the part that is in charge or the opposite, is a part of the crisis. In addition to its inability to provide political and social resolutions for the crisis, it works by itself on increasing the crisis and playing the role of a participant in the crisis. It plays this role while it should have worked on presenting the tools and suggestions that facilitate the community life. A community without a leading and thinking leadership that guides it, become a place of conflict and divide between two parts, benefiting part and exploitation part. The presence of occupation and the circumstances resulting from that, increase the complicity of the crisis and makes solving it semi impossible. In addition to that, the occupation is a main cause of blocking the civil community advance. Alongside that is the limiting of work to political aspects in a manner that it became impossible to analyze the data objectively because the nature of conflict did not remain simple. The conflict did not remain only Palestinian- Israeli conflict. Multiplicity of groups arose in relation to this conflict. There is a group that sees Israel as an alliance who is able to protect its interests and support it in the political and economic interests that contradict with the community interests. There are some Palestinians who think that the resolution is to be Israeli and take the Israeli nationality. Other think that the conflict is still there in its classic manner of the previous thinking. Others think that the conflict is religious or national and other multiple characteristics. We are not here to defeat these opinions and divide it into two groups; friends or enemies. The real danger that should be mentioned is what is indicated from all these opinions is the process of monopolization of thought. We suffer from a lack of community. In addition to that, the advocacy resulting from these opinions deletes the existence of the community and makes it a group of tribes that has no collective vision or unifying elements. Before we are an election bloc, we are people living in this country and what happens to the country happens to its people. We think that the issue is not who will win the student council or even the legislative council. The issue is that when we will be mature enough, groups or individuals, and became able to meet our national responsibilities. This is the base out of which we come and consider ourselves a step in the alternative choice that is built on the following:

  • Betrayal is not an option: All thoughts and opinions should live together and accept each other and accept free opinions because the real division is between benefiting group and fighters group
  • Unifying the national work does not mean deleting its multiplicity and differences; it means creating a frame that allows multiplicity and difference through interacting and dialogue that does not aim to assure the individuality but to improve the collective existence circumstances. For that, we in the democratic Pole state that we have number of opinions equal to the number of individuals. And we look to present a political and cultural community model supports institutions and the community to form the alternative away from bias and unilaterally. Regardless of the differences in our thoughts, we are all with the Democratic Pole that works for the national interests.
  • Political belonging is a popular tool that organizes the national work to achieve the maximum aims with the least loss. Any tendency that becomes a goal in itself is no longer a positive element in the popular struggle. On the contrary, the current political bodies consider their existence as a goal and they protect that through wasting the community capabilities by internal fighting like what happened in Gazan Universities.
  • The educated are responsible for working on analyzing the phenomena and suggesting the alternatives for peoples' situation. Why we have to read about post modernism before we get our rights of freedom and bread?
  • The institutions and people have to guarantee the freedom space and security for the educated to avoid making him feel alien from his society and the only available choice for him is immigration. A country does not control the world through their local intellectual resources but through the resources they purchase. If we are not able to buy minds, let us at least protect the minds we have and so this country will not be "a country that the best choices in it are either immigration or crime."
  • The Press has a main role in forming the community awareness. We should ask our media to stop being a tool for the authority and people should commit to protect the owners of the free pens and raise their voice and protect their freedom.
  • Create bridges between all the all the community classes and create bridges to connect Palestine with the external world and its revolution groups and with any one whom is still with human struggle to give the Palestinian issue its importance and being the core.

Finally we reach with you to our final stage, the stage of free opinions as a measurement and condition to the community development and let our campaign start! Each has the right to say what he likes, and this is what we want to say:

  • The Pole exists as a case that was not created by an individual or body and none will delete its existence because it still dreams and develop
  • We will not justify our existence by the negativity of others, but by the program and the alternatives that we have and still has a power to effect anyone who is still interested in working for the benefit of the country
Together and hand by hand to make Birzeit a model for the Palestinian community. And let elections be free, democratic and honest
