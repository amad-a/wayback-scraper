Democratic Pole Leaflet 2

For a better situation!

Colleagues:
Although the student movement works on several political, social, and cultural levels, it is firstly responsible for student circumstances and needs and the circumstances of the university. We announce, with no hesitation, that the student movement passes a period of retreat in general, and on the union rights level in particular. The movement must recapture its active role through unifying its work on these levels and avoid the unilateral interests that do not help to overcome the obstacles that it should face.

Following is a summary of our union program:

  • Fees Issue: This is an issue that needs unifying all the efforts to face it. The university and the high education council should realize that solving the financial problem will not be through raising fees that might lead to depriving us from continuing education and the deprivation of poor students from this right. At the same time we mention the unilateral action that is far from the unified student movement when the Islamic Bloc formed, during the latest fees' crisis, what was called the union of student councils to solve the problem and it ignored the other student blocs
  • Parallel Education: We criticize the university policy relating to this issue because it blocks creativity and makes education a commercial venture. If it is implemented to solve the financial problem, why do we see the continual meetings of the employees union and the continual raising of fees???
  • We will work for the free choice of the student between the exam and the research; and decrease the pressure in the sections; and facilitating transferring between colleges and activating the "add and drop" mechanism.
  • Privatization of the university facilities: We strongly reject this policy because it ignores the students' and employees' interests. For that a portion of the fee that the university receives from renting these facilities should be transferred to the Poor student Fund till the problem is solved.
  • Issues of Violence Inside the Campus: We ask the university administration and the students to take the needed procedures to fight this phenomena. And here we ask why no procedures were taken against many unilateral actions that took place inside the campus
  • General Union of Palestinian Students Council: Let us work on preparing for democratic elections of the union and activate it.
  • High Education Council: Let us work together to press on this institution to contribute in solving the financial problem of the Palestinian universities. Rather than the Authority investing large amounts of money in the security bodies, it should transfer these amounts to the education field for its importance and necessity to the community.
  • University Library: Let us work to make the work of the library till 10:00 PM to facilitate research processes and give attention to the Master degree students.
  • Health Insurance: We will work together to press on the administration to make the health insurance cover all the treatment needs of the student.
  • Classifications: Let us work to make the classifications each semester and not only each year and they should include the graduating students as well
  • Immigration of the Academic Staff: This issue is the result of the administration policy in ignoring this cadre in forming syllabuses and the process of not paying them their salaries and pressing on them increasing number of courses.
  • Internet Services: Let us work to reserve this service for all students with a symbolic semester fee. And reserve locations for that in the student building and for the students with creative art and singing… etc. talents and skills.

Dear Students:
Our union issues are many and become difficult day after day. Promises to solve them are useless if they are not dealt with as they effect all of us. You are responsible to give this attention. We should examine the issues and ask why do they increase? Who is the one who did not work fully? And why did the student council became opposite to what we want and aim?

The Democratic Pole
Birzeit University
