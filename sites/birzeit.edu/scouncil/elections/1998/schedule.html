Election 98: The Schedule

    Key Election Dates

  • Monday, 23 March, 9:00a.m.: Nominations open.
  • Tuesday, 24 March, 3:00p.m.: Nominations close.
  • Wednesday, 25 March, 11:00a.m.: Deadline to for nominees to withdraw from the election.
  • Thursday, 26 March: Official announcement of nominees.
  • Saturday, 28 March: Election campaign begins.
  • Monday, 30 March, 4:00p.m.: Campaign concludes.
  • Tuesday, 31 March, 2:00p.m.: Debates begin. Classes are cancelled.
  • Wednesday, 1 April, 8:30-4:00p.m.: Polls open for voting.
  • Wednesday, 1 April, 5:00p.m.: Ballot count.
  • Wednesday, 1 April, 8:00p.m.: Unofficial results posted.
  • Thursday, 2 April : Official election results are announced.


Translation of the election committiee official guidelines for the 98/99 elections.
