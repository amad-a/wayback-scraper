Our Future Program

   

Cultural Issues:   

To hold a series of lectures dealing with social and cultural 
issues that concern a students looking for knowledge and  
beliefs.  
To issue a university newsletter .  
Social Issues:  
To start a liaison program with other univiersities.  
To arrange activities and volunteer work in order to  
deepen students' social conciousness.  
Sports Issues: 
To organize sports activities involving maximum
participation of university students and employees.  
Academic Issues: 
To work on making research the main teaching tool 
and the  basis on which the student is evaluated.  
To arrange forums between faculty representatives and 
student councilors on one side and students on the other to 
discuss the academic regulations and student issues.  
To create training programs and internships to further
student development.  
Art Issues: 
To encourage students with talent and support them by  
developing programs, 
exhibitions and competitions.
