Greetings Brothers and Sisters
The crisis facing the Palestinian political establishment is reflected in the student movement making it weak and almost impotent and preventing it from being able to act, behave, or make independent descisions. We believe in the need to face these problems and to change our reality for the better. We hope to encourage individuals take on responsibility for their homeland and their nation and to employ their cultural strengths and intellectual abilities.
As students representing the youth of Palestine, we have the power to change the future of our nation. We have the power to build and to reconstruct. This is not accomplished by following descisions that were made in our absence, but by freedom of thought and by making independent and courageous descisions.
We see that we as students can only act in one of the following ways:
  1. To be followers and let others make the decisions for us,
  2. To stand aside and watch, unable to find a party that represents our beliefs,
C. To create a movement inside the university and based on the student reality which reflects our thoughts and beliefs.
