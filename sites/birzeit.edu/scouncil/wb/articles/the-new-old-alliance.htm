 The New-Old Alliance between the Rulers (of Turkey and Jordan) and the Jews

On the 6th of September 1998 Mesut Yilmaz, Prime Minister of Turkey, visited Jordan on his way to Palestine. Before his visit Netanyahu, the Prime Minister of the Jewish enemy, made a statement in an article in the Turkish newspaper ‘Millyet’. He stated  “We are working to transform the Israeli Turkish co-operation in the Middle East into a regional security  order….”. He also stated “I discussed the matter with  Jordanian Crown Prince Hasan.”  expressing his hope that Jordan will participate in this regional security order.

 In Amman, during the visit of Yilmaz, Crown Prince Hasan and the Jordanian officials tried to conceal this initiative and to mislead the local and the international public opinion. Their statements were deceptive and contradictory and this revealed to the political observer that Jordan had taken many steps towards this regional alliance. After Crown Prince Hasan had said “Some try to portray this as an axis or alliance . . . they are wrong and their views are baseless” and after he had announced that he doesn't believe in alliances and axes, he said “ Jordan calls for the establishment of  a regional order that includes all the states”. He inquired “Why is it possible to develop an arrangement in the Korean Peninsula to address their common security issues while this region remains ignored when talking  about the development of dialogue between institutions about regional issues?”. Fayez at-Tarawna, the Prime Minister of Jordan, said “Jordan supports the establishment of a security order which includes all the states where all the issues are discussed, rather than through bi-lateral relations only”. In answering a question about Jordanian - Turkish military co-operation at-Tarawna said, “ This military co-operation is one aspect of the co-operation between the two friendly countries in various fields”. The Foreign Minister of Jordan, when asked about Netanyahu’s statements regarding the alliance said “…the sides give their statements and take their positions according to particular interests and we in Jordan have our national considerations…”. By this statement he did not negate the statement of Netanyahu. Rather he re-affirmed the existence of an alliance  with many sides, and he justified his reluctance to answer explicitly  by the national considerations of Jordan. A Turkish source revealed that Ankara invited Amman to participate in the Turkish- Israeli manoeuvres planned to be carried out in November of this year. Netanyahu also invited both Jordan and Egypt to participate in the manoeuvres which Turkey would execute with both America and Israel.

The current international standing which is shaped largely by the superiority and arrogance of the United States of America has forced the international struggle over interests and sphere's of influence to proceed covertly in fear of the American beast which seeks to dominate alone. This international situation is the one which makes the statements of the three sides of the alliance, Turkey, Jordan and Israel appear unclear  and contradictory. Crown Prince Hasan doesn't believe in alliances yet he wants to enter into a regional security order. Netanyahu invites Egypt to attend the manoeuvres with Jordan, yet Egypt announced its refusal and apprehension of this alliance and Yilmaz assured Syria and others that these agreements are bi-lateral and for the purpose of protecting peace and supporting the peace process.

The sides of the alliance are careful in concealing the role of Jordan in it because of the importance of Jordan and because of the severe conflict over it for it is the wide open gate of Israel to the Arab countries in order to achieve the expansionary ambitions of Jews and their economic interests. This is one reason. Another reason is that Jordan separates the puppets of America in Syria from those in Saudi Arabia. Netanyahu gave a statement to the same Turkish newspaper ‘Millyet’ “We (Turkey and Israel) are surrounded by radical regimes which are developing ballistic missiles with unconventional warheads”. When Netanyahu says Israel in this context he means Jordan with it because Jordan is the security depth of Israel and the first line of defence for it.

The sides of the tripartite alliance, despite their denial of the existence of it in the media, are proceeding to strengthen it with the support  of Britain. This has forced the puppets of America in the region to reveal their opposition to and apprehension of this alliance which they did in the Latakia meeting in which the states of the Damascus resolution, including Egypt and Syria, participated. This pushed Egypt, Syria and Lebanon to attack the visit of Yilmaz to Jordan and Palestine. Lebanon described this alliance as a military grip against the Arab states which did not sign peace treaties with Israel.
