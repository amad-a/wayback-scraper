The Islamic Bloc

Birzeit University

The Islamic Bloc in Birzeit University aims to build a generation  of students, whom believe in Allah (God), and whom take part in building their society. So that such students carry Islam in their harts, brains, and morals, to help their people and nation, and fight ignorance, tyranny, and occupation.

The Islamic Bloc: it is a group of students (males and females) that is gathered by the bound of Islam, and Islamic beliefs.

Its members: Any student (male or female), who believes in Islamic, and applies it in his (or her) life, and does the best to introduce Islam to others in the best way.

Its Principles: The Koran, and Sunna (the profit's tradition) are our guidelines, and from Islamic intellectuals and thinkers we get help.

Its goals: It aims to rise the intellectual, cultural, & educational level of the students, and help them to solve their daily life problems and studying problems.
