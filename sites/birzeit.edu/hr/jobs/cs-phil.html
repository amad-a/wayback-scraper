Faculty of Arts: Vacancy Announcement
Department of Philosophy and Cultural Studies


July 5, 2001

The Faculty of Arts announces three vacancies for three faculty members in the Department of Philosophy and Cultural Studies starting on the first semester of the academic year 2001/2002 (September 2001).
Candidates are expected to be specialized in philosophy, or political science, or other fields in the social sciences or humanities, and to have interests in interdisciplinary studies.

Previous teaching experience, research, and experience in teaching at the graduate level will be an asset.
Birzeit University encourages women and young scholars to apply.
Letters of interest and CV can be sent by fax or email before 21 July 2001.
