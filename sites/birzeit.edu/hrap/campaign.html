ACADEMIC FREEDOM FIRST!

As part of an overwhelmingly successful international day in solidarity with Gaza students and academics, Israeli officials in The Hague noted that the Israeli policy is being reconsidered and in Washington, DC, noted that discussions are taking place. International involvement can keep the discussions and reconsiderations happening! The ACADEMIC FREEDOM FIRST—International Campaign for Gaza Students WEBSITE invites visitors from all over the world to join efforts to end the ongoing harassment of students from Gaza by the Israeli authorities. The website contains background information about Israeli harassment of university students from Gaza who wish to study at universities in the West Bank.

The site includes five sections:

  • Why Do Gaza Students Need Your Help?
    An overview of the major points of the campaign.
  • Understanding the Issue
    A full background briefing on the issue with maps and charts.
  • Portrait and Testimony
    A portrait gallery including testimonies from several students. Warning: this is image intensive.
  • Campaign Platform
    A statement of the campaign's platform.
  • Action Page
    An invitation for visitors to participate in the campaign and suggestions for action.
