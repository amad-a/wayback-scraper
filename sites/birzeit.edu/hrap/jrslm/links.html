LAND CONFISCATION & SETTLEMENTS

  • The Applied Research Institute of Jerusalem (ARIJ) has produced and maintains an excellent on-line presentation about the "reconstruction" of Jerusalem. The presentation entitled, "The Status of Jerusalem Reconstructed: Israel's Unilateral Actions Determine the Future of Jerusalem" include outstanding aerial photographs, maps, and concise explanation of the development of each settlement and the overall campaign against Arab Jerusalem.

  • The Foundation for Middle East Peace publishes a bimonthly newsletter, Report on Israeli Settlements in the Occupied Territories. The Settlement Report provides up-to-date information on Israel's settlement policies as well as the ongoing negotations with the Palestinians over the future of the West Bank, Gaza Strip, and Jerusalem.
