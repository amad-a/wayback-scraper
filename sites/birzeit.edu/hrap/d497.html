    MORE INFORMATION ABOUT PNA INTERROGATION
    On any given day about 10 Birzeit University students can be found in prison as a result of an order issued by an Israeli military officer. The order does not contain charges or explanations. It does not have to be ruled upon in a court. Israeli military law permits a military commander to imprison a Palestinian for a renewable period of up to 12 months for reasons of protecting public security.

    This procedure has been come to be called "administrative" detention (AD), as the detainee is detained by an administrative procedure without court involvement. Such a policy stands in direct violation of internationally accepted standards articulated in Article 10 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and Article 9 of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights.
