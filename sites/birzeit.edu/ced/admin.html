Administration and Operational Structure

As an extension program of Birzeit University, CED enjoys a considerable degree of autonomy in managing its financial and administrative affairs. The University, as the supervising body, approves CED’s policies and oversees their implementation. CED functions in accordance with the University commitment to excellence, quality and financial integrity.

CED Financial Sources

CED operations are supported through several sources, including fees paid directly by local private and public sector institutions for commissioned services; and grants and donations from local and international institutions.
