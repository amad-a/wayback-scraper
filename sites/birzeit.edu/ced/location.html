Location and Facilities



The premises of the Continuing Education Department of Birzeit University are located in the center of the West Bank town of Ramallah. The building which houses the department was built in the early 1920’s, originally as a summer home for the then Jordanian King Abdullah.



The CED premises in Ramallah as well as the Birzeit University campus together, offer a wide variety of training sites including seminar rooms, workshops, laboratories, and a fully-equipped computer laboratory. CED cooperates with other institutions to secure training facilities in the West Bank and Gaza for training activities outside the Ramallah area.
