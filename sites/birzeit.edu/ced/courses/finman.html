FinancialManagement

Public Sector

Credit Management

This course was offered to senior and mid-level financial managers from both the private and public sector. The course introduced various financial computer software vital to processing loans efficiently and enhanced knowledge of financial management concepts.
Contact Hours: 40

Financial Analysis and Reporting

This course focused on concepts of financial management which relate to Palestinian NGOs and public sector organizations. It aimed to develop the capabilities of accountants in financial analysis, managerial accounting and financial reporting.
Contact Hours: 40

Financial Planning and Budget Analysis

This course was offered to senior and mid-level managers, in both the public and private sector and aimed at enhancing the skills of participants in preparing financial plans and analysing and identifying various types of budgets.
Contact Hours: 30
