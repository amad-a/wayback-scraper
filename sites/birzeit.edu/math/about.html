About Mathematics Department

Mathematics Dept. at BZU is as old as the university itself. Math Dept. plays a fundamental role in almost every colleges in Birzeit University, especially to science, engineering and commerce students.
As a matter of fact, to those the department offers service courses amounting to over sixty percent of its academic load in terms of credit hours in any given semester.
In 1994, the Department of Mathematics had become the Department of Mathematics & Computer Science since it introduce the Computer Science program, then the department of Computer Science was born in Jan 1999.
In Addition to that, the department offers a strong program leading to a first degree in Mathematics along several tracks.
Graduates of that program find jobs in teaching Mathematics at the local high schools or at local companies, or going abroad to pursue the graduate studies.
Mathematics Dept. is continuing to develop itself, So it introduced a new tracks as Minor in Statistics, Mathematical Economics which are very important tracks in satisfying our community needs, more over it developed the programs which are leading to B.Sc. in Mathematics & Mathematical Economics.
This service has been put together with the hope that it will introduce the department(Staff, Programs, Courses offering, ..etc) to the prospective student, the visitor and anybody or institution that has an interest to know.
