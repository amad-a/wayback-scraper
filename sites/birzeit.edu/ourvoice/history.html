HISTORY
[About the Background Image]
October 1999
  • A new article by Professor Roger Heacock entitled Between Systems and Salami: US Policy Towards the Palestinians and the Middle East in the Late Twentieth Century. The article traces the successive policies of US administrations towards the Middle East and proposes that sovereign states tend to range in their foreign policies between two strong alternative poles, and that this pendulum-like movement often occurs between successive administrations. Added 9/10/99.


January 1999
  • We publish the introduction of a book by the Birzeit University Centre for Research and Documentation on the destroyed Palestinian village Abu Shusha.. This article examines the history of the village and the massacre that took place there based on oral history interviews with survivors and other historical records. More information can be found here about other publications of the Centre - and how you can help us! Added 14/1/99.


December 1998
  • Roger Heacock's speech from the recent landscape conference at Birzeit University in which he examines differing views of landscape both historically and in Palestine. Landscapes of glory and Resignation. Added 6/12/98.


November 1998
  • Dr. Sharif Kanaana examines the different interpretations and the role that the Deir Yassin massacre played in the expulsion of the Palestinians in 1948, in his article Reinterpreting Deir Yassin. Added 24/11/98.


October 1998
  • Khalil Athamina, from the Mediterranean Studies Department, explores the significance of the colour black in the flags of early Islam in his article The Black Banners and the Socio-Political Significance of Flags and Slogans in Medieval Islam. Added 10/10/98.

  • Sharif Kanaana, from the Department of Anthropology and Sociology at Birzeit University, examines the Patterns of Palestinian Exodus in 1948. In this important article, Dr. Kanaana takes a critical look at the "new historians" such as Benny Morris, and argues that their writing is still trapped in the framework of Zionism. Added 8/10/98.

September 1998
  • Salim Tamari, from the Department of Sociology at Birzeit University, writes about the role of Shopkeepers, Peddlers and the Urban Resistance in the Palestinian Uprising. He traces the participation and development of struggle by the Palestinian merchant class during the Intifada. Added 28/9/98

  • Roger Heacock, Associate Professor of European History and Coordinator of the Mediterranean Studies Unit at Birzeit University, discusses the evolution of Palestinian nationalism and its relationship to international events and institutions in his article, Saying No: The role of rejection in national identity formation - the case of Palestine.Added 28/9/98
