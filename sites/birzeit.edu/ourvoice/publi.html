Palestinian Journal for Historical Studies. This journal, published by the Palestinian Society for Historical Studies in cooperation with Al Aswar-Acre, is a bi-lingual research journal. The first issue contains an article by Roger Heacock from Birzeit University, entitled Saying No: The Role of Rejection in National Identity Formation - the Case of Palestine. The contents of the first issue are as follows:
  • The Educational thought of Al Harith Al Muhasabi Dr. Ahmad Fahim Jaber (arabic)
  • The Andalusian Change of Attitude towards the Murabitoun Dr. Hisham Aburmailih (arabic)
  • A Comparative Study between Crusader and Zionist Colonization Dr. Said al Bishawi (arabic)
  • The Economic Life of Palestinian Fellaheen during the British Mandate (1922-1936) Dr. Mahmoud Hizmawi (arabic)
  • Forced Expulsion of the Palestrinian People during the British Mandate Dr. Tayseer Jibara (arabic)
  • The Role of Rejection in National Identity Formation - the case of Palestine Professor Roger Heacock (english)

For more information about the journal or subscription information, write to Dr. Hammad Hussein, Editor of the Palestinian Journal for Historical Studies at Hhussein@arts.birzeit.edu .
