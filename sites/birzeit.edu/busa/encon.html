Department Entry Conditions


In order for a student to be accepted by the department for major in Business Administration he must full fill the following conditions:-

1- A minimum overall average of 70% in the following courses:-

  1. BUSA130
  2. BUSA230
  3. MATH (235)

2- Department's approval.


Acceptance depends on the department's capacity of students which is decided by the Business department council, and by the sequence of the overall averages of the applicants in the courses listed above.
