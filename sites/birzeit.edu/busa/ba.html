Business Administration Major Requirements

I-Major requirements

Those students majoring in Business Administration must complete the following:
A. 35 credit hours of Business Administrations courses in addition to the following core Business courses.
  • Busa 234
  • Busa 237
  • Busa 330
  • Busa 340
  • Busa 341
  • Busa 438
B. A minimum of 6 credit hours of accounting courses in addition to the principle courses.
C. A minimum of 6 credit hours of economic courses in addition to the principle courses.

II-Minor requirements

Students minoring in Business Administration have to complete 18 credit hours of Business Administration courses in addition to the principle Business Courses.

Grade Point Average:

To graduate, students are required to have a 70% average in their major.
