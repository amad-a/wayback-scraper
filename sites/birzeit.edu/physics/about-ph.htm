ABOUT THE PHYSICS DEPARTMENT
  AT BIRZEIT UNIVERSITY

    The Physics Department at Birzeit University is as old as the university itself. Although physics is such an esoteric field, yet a basic knowledge of its fundamentals is, without doubt, indispensable for any science and engineering student. Thus, our department has an important role to play in the careers of over one half of the student population of the University. To those, the department offers service courses amounting to over fifty percent of its academic load in terms of credit hours in any given semester.

In addition to that, the department offers a strong program leading to a first (undergraduate) degree in physics along several tracks. Graduates of that program find jobs typically in teaching physics and mathematics at the local high schools or go abroad in pursuit of graduate studies.

This service has been put together with the hope that it will introduce the department ( staff, programs, course offerings ,... etc.) to the prospective student, the visitor and anybody or institution the has an interest to know.
