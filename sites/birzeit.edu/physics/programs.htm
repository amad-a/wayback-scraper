The Department of Physics offers programs leading to a Bachelor's degree in pure physics, a group major in physics and another field chosen by the student and the department, as well as a minor in physics.

Students should satisfy the following requirements to get accepted into the department:

  • Pass Physics 111, 112, 131, and 132 with a cumulative average of 70%.
  • Pass Mathematics 131 and 132 with a cumulative average of 65%.
  • Department approval.

    SPECIFIC TRACK REQUIREMENTS:


    1) Pure Physics ( 69 credits):

  • Complete a minimum of fifty seven credits including Physics: 211, 212, 231, 232, 331, 332, 333, 334, 335, 336, 337, 411, 430, 431, 432, 433, 436, and twelve credits from the following physics courses: 233, 234, 235, 338, 339, 434, 435, 437, 438, whereby these electives should include at least three credits from each of the following groups: (233,234,235) and (339,338) and (434,435,437,438).

  • Complete the following mathematics courses: 231,331, and 332.

  • Complete computer science 234.




    GROUP MAJOR( 42 credits):


  • Complete a minimum of thirty three credits including Physics: 211, 212, 231, 232, 331, 333, 335, 336, 411, 433, 436, and six credits from 233, 234, 235, 337, 338, 339, 430.

  • Complete the following mathematics courses: 231 and 331.

  • Complete computer science 234.

    The group major can consist of physics and any one of the following minors: Computer Science, Mathematics, Education.


    MINOR ( 26 credits):

  • Complete twenty six hours including physics 211,212,231,232,331,333,335, and nine credits from other physics courses offered by the department.
