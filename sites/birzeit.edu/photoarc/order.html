HOW TO ORDER PHOTOGRAPHS FROM THIS ARCHIVE

  1. Click on the button reading CONTACT PHOTO ARCHIVE on the top of any page to open up a pre-addressed e-mail message.
  2. Quote the PHOTO ORDER NUMBER/s on the index page. For photos from other parts of the website, cite the location with a brief description.
  3. Specify which size you would like the photo/s to be: 13x18cm or 15x21cm.
  4. Send a US$ cheque by airmail to Photographic Department, Public Relations Office, Birzeit University, P.O. Box 14, Birzeit West Bank, Palestine. Prices listed below. NB: Photos will NOT be dispatched until your cheque is cleared. For photos from other parts of the website, await confimation of availablility before dispatching your cheque.
  5. Organisations and print publications may request invoices, at the discretion of the Public Relations Office, for a speedy delivery.
