SPECIAL REPORTS

RELATED ARTICLES

  • "Insects don't have borders", Birzeit English Newsletter No.26, August 1995 - a look at the trials and tribulations of environmental health outreach workers.
  • Focus for action: the permanent struggle for academic freedom, Birzeit English Newsletter No.26, August 1995 - A look back at headlines in the university newsletter over the years, commenting on the never-ceasing need to address issues of academic freedom.
  • New report on Gazan students shows continuing violations of academic freedoms, Birzeit English Newsletter No.26, August 1995 - A review of the report Making Education Illegal - Students from the Gaza Strip: Israeli Restrictions and International Reactions from the newsletter staff.
  • University erects campus memorial to student martyrs, Birzeit English Newsletter No.26, August 1995 - 11 students names are engraved on the new memorial, errected in summer 1995.
  • New publications, Birzeit English Newsletter No.26, August 1995 - Details of Collaborators in the occupied territories: human rights violations and abuses by Yizhar Be'er and Birzeit's research centre's Dr. Saleh Abdul-Jawad.
  • Letter from the President, Birzeit English Newsletter No.25, June 1994 - Dr. Hanna Nasir comments on the university community's attitude towards the Hebron massacre.
  • The two worlds of Birzeit University, Birzeit English Newsletter No.25, June 1994 - An editorial looks at the academic and attendence experiences of a Gazan student at Birzeit University.
  • In Memorium: Rami Ghazzawi, Basima Shehadeh, Birzeit English Newsletter No.25, June 1994 - Two relatives of Birzeit faculty members died at the result of Israeli violence in a three-week period at the end of 1993. This article recalls the circumstances and the feeling of the university community.
