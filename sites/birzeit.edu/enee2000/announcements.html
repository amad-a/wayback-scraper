The Computer Committee announced a computer course for Delphi programming language a week ago. The truth is that the committee faced a lot of obstacles in creating such a course almost for nothing. We demanded a fast response for our preposition in order to organize the course outline and class dates. We hope that you "students" understand our conditions and be patient since the course will be started sooner than what you imagine.

The Computer Committee announces "The Computer Technology Week" which is to start at the beginning of marsh. This active week will include seminars about many fresh computers related topics and developments presented by specialists, hardware exhibition and computer peripherals that concern engineers in general. The Computer Committee with supervision of the EE department prepares this project.

The Sports Committee announces the creation of Sport teams that represent the department and play on behalf of the EE students. Names will be announced by Yazeed Abu Khader and will be added to this web site.

The General Affairs Committee announces a Poetry Seminar lead by the Poets of the Engineering Faculty.

It starts at 15:00 tile 16:00 on Monday 19.2.2000.
