Welcome to the personal web site of Enee2000, the electrical engineering club at Birzeit University. This is the first web site ever built for the club.

WE HOPE YOU ENJOY OUR WORK

The Site is constructed for many important reasons and purposes, that all dwell into the benefit of the student of the electrical engineering department in Birzeit University. It includes announcements, projects in progress, suggested projects and many important web resources that specially concerns the EE students and all visitors in general.

Its also contains personal home pages of each of the club members and each committee by it self.

Special Thanks for the computer center for it's helpful contribution to the club. According to our will, the computer center provided a full access to the University personal server in order to launch this site.
