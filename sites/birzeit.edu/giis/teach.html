The Master’s program in international studies for students possessing the BA degree. This program is interdisciplinary, and includes courses in international politics, international history, international economics and international law.

Students meeting at a staff member's house

The best experts in each discipline are on the faculty of the GIIS as permanent or part-time members of its faculty. They include:

  • Ibrahim Abu-Lughod (international politics, international law and organization)
  • Ali Jarbawi (political science)
  • Hisham Ahmad (international relations theory)
  • Roger Heacock (international history)
  • Riad Musa (international economics)
  • Musa Budeiry (international politics)
  • Helga Baumgarten (international politics and history)
  • Ziyad Abu Amru (Middle Eastern politics)
  • Yusuf Nasir (international economics)
  • Ghaith al-Omary (international law).
  • Ms. Najwa Siksek, Executive Secretary
  • Ms. Amal Jadou, Academic Assistant
