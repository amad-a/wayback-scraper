The Fourth Palestinian Studies Conference
"Prospects for the Palestinian State and Civil Society"

May 11th and 12th, 2000

Aim: The conference aims to identify the problems that face Palestinians in general and the Palestinian National Authority in particular and propose solutions for these problems.

Registration: 8-9
