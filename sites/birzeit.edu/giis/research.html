In studying for their degree, the students, under the supervision of their professors, carry out research projects, which for some of them culminate in a Master’s thesis. Great importance is given to research by the students, in terms of its quality and pertinence to the international system, as well as the country’s role in it as well as that of the region as a whole. The thesis advisor of the student becomes his or her guide through the literature and to the available sources, domestically and internationally.
