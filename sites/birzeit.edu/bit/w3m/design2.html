Web & Media Course: Promotion, Security and WYSIWYG

  • Promotion:
    • Know and target your audience
    • Know your competitors (mostly commercial)
    • Provide high quality content
    • Design the site to attract target (easy download, navigation ...)
    • Announce the site in search engines and directories
    • Make the site search engines friendly
    • Use banner exchanges, web rings, associations
    • Link to others and have them return the favor
    • Announce on Forums, newsgroups, chat places etc.
    • Use mail lists (caution!)
    • Use broadcast systems (tell a friend)
    • Host free, useful and promotional material
    • Create loyal audience and use word of mouth (answer your e-mail, be professional, update the site regularly ...)
    • Use free/paid submit-it sites and services or pay for ads on major or related sites
    • http://www.searchenginewatch.com/
  • Security:
    • Social engineering
    • Security holes in applications
    • User responsibility
    • Hacks
    • DoS, Mail bombs, pings
    • Annoying communications
