E-Publishing Course

Final Assignment: The Action Plan

Modules : one , two , three and four

Module leaders: Abed Khooli and Anna Seif

Brief

Your final assignment has been devised to be a test of all you have learnt on the e-publishing course in terms of technical , language sensitivity and editorial aspects. It is hoped that you will utilize the skills that you have developed to produce a document that will be of real use to you in your work place.

You are to submit a detailed action plan for your institutions ‘website and related issues in your role of information provider. The action plan should address specific problems you have identified in your problem statement ( assignment 2 for module three) and be realistic and pragmatic. Your action plan will be presented during a reception to be held in mid January although you should submit for assessment before this.

 

Task One

You should write a detailed and comprehensive action plan which addresses key problems identified in your problem statement.

Your plan should include the following :

information and management structure

available resources

a mission statement

audience profile

task plan

solutions to specific problems ( technical and content)

a blue print of a future site

 

Note: The action plan should be submitted to Abed and Anna by 10th January 2001

Task Two

You will be required to present your action plan at a presentation in mid January 2001.

Assessment

This assignment will be assessed jointly by Abed Khooli and Anna Seif and the assessment will form part of your final evaluation.

Note

You are encouraged to liaise with Abed and Anna while you formulate your plan and discuss any difficulties you have.

Good Luck
