Birzeit University is a non-profit institution. The charge to students is kept at the minimum consistent with high quality of instruction and adequate facilities. Approximately one-quarter of university budget comes from student fees, with the rest coming in the form of donations from individuals, alumni and organizations based in the Arab world or abroad.

All fees listed below are valid for the year 2000/2001 and given in Jordanian Dinars (JD) per credit hour.
Note: 1 JD is equal to about U$1.4 (as of March 2, 2001)
