Course Catalog 		Academic Programs (Undergraduate Programs)

Most academic departments offer a Bachelor's Degree in either a single major or in a major combined with a minor.
However, certain departments do not grant a degree, but offer courses that may be used as electives. The following are the specializations (a Bachelor's Degree) offered in each faculty:

Major fields in the Faculty of Arts are:
- Arabic Language and Literature
- English Language and Literature
- Geography
- History
- Media/Journalism
- Media/Radio Broadcasting
- Psychology
- Sociology & Anthropology
The Department of Education and Psychology offers a Teaching Certificate which qualifies teachers at the high school level as well as a Teacher's Diploma for teachers in service. A Diploma in Translation is offered by the Department of Languages and Translation.

Major fields in the Faculty of Science are:
- Biology and Biochemistry
- Chemistry
- Computer Science
- Mathematics
- Physics

The Faculty of Commerce and Economics offers majors in:
- Accounting
- Business Administration
- Economics
- Finance

The Faculty of Engineering offers majors in:
- Architectural Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering

Minor fields are offered in several other fields:
- Archaeology
- Biochemistry
- French
- Philosophy
- Political Science
- Statistics
- Women Studies
