Listening to Birzeit Radio is very simple. On the hardware side, you need a computer and an internet connection (which we assume you have since you are reading this), speakers and a soundcard.

You also need some software called the Real Audio Player. It comes in a free version which can be downloaded from the Real Networks Page.There are several Real Players, but the latest one at the time of writing is the G2 Player.

Real Audio uses streaming technology, which means you don't need to download the whole file before you start listening, you can listen live across your internet connection. The G2 player also enables you to watch streaming Real Video.

Once you have downloaded the software and installed it on your system (a straightforward process), you can listen to Birzeit Radio. Catch us live on Saturdays GMT (follow this link), or listen to an archived show here.
