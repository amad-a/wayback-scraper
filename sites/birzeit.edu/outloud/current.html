June 1st, if you have the Real Player installed, tune in by clicking on one of the links below. If you don't have the Real Player installed, click here for more information.

  • English Show (Streaming Real Audio File) or shift-click here to download (3.8 Mb) to your hard disk
  • Arabic Show (Streaming Real Audio File) or shift-click here to download (3.4 Mb) to your hard disk
  • Bizeit Student Compilation (Streaming Real Audio File) or shift-click here to download (3.4 Mb) to your hard disk

Information about playing Real Audio: Sound files are available in RealAudio streaming format at 16Kbps, just click on either "English show" or "Arabic show". If you experience network congestion shift-click in Netscape for the PC on the word "download" or, on a Mac, right mouse click and choose "Save this link as" to download. Then use the file:open command in RealPlayer to play from your hard disk.
