Imad Ghneim at work
We record OUTLOUD in the studio of the Birzeit University Media Center on a 12-channel mixer. Our Sound Engineer is Imad Ghneim (pictured on right) from the Media Center. Some interviews and events are recorded remotely on a DAT recorder and then mixed in the studio.

The Arabic and English shows are recorded separately as .DAT files. We then convert them to .wav files at a frequency of 44.1 Khz (CD quality) and finally encode them as Real Media files using the G2 Real Encoder which is available from the Real Networks site.
