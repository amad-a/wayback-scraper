Birzeit University's online radio station - OUTLOUD - was launched on October 17, 1998. The radio station broadcasts a half-hour show every two weeks. Each show is available in both Arabic or English.

OUTLOUD is the first internet radio station in Palestine and we are open to your suggestions. If there is something you want to know about Palestine, music you would like to hear, or someone you would like us to interview, then send the webmaster a note and we'll try and organize a show on your topic.

The shows are presented by Birzeit University media studies students as well as the Birzeit University Web team, with assistance from the Finnish Government. You can find out how we do this here.

Listening to Birzeit Radio is very simple - you need a Real Audio Player, a computer, sound card and speakers. For more information, visit our how do I listen page?
