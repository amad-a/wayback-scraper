Department Programmes & Course Requirements

    The Department of Math. and Comp. Science offers program study leading to Bachelor's degrees with majors in mathematics, Computer Science, double majors in Mathematics and Physics or Chemistry. It also offers minors in Computer Science and Mathematics.

The specific requirements Courses are as follows:

  • Major in Mathematic:

    • Finishing 44 cumulative credits in mathematics including math231 , math241, math331, math333, math334 , math335 , math337 , math411, math431 and math432 . And success in Phys211 and phys231 and one of the physics 242 , 331 or 333.

  • Minor Mathematics:

    • Finishing the following courses in mathematics math241 , math246 or (math231 and math331), math332 , math333 , math335 , math337.

  • Major in Computer Science:

    • Finishing 44 commutative credits in Computer Science including comp232 , comp233, comp235, comp242 , comp333 , comp334, comp337 , comp338 , comp431 , comp411 and comp433. And including 12 commutative credits from the following comp332, comp335 , comp336 , comp432, comp435 , comp436 , comp437 , comp438. And finishing math236 , math241 , math246 , math436, phys211 , phys231.

  • Minor in Computer Science:

    • Finishing 19 cumulative credits including comp232 , comp235 , comp242 , comp334 , and 6 cumulative credits of the elective courses.
