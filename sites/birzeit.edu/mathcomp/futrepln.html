Future Planning for Math & Comp Sci Dept

    In cooperation with the economic dept., a new program titled "Applied Mathematics For Economics" is under study. This program will be supported by the french government where some french faculty are expected to visit our department in the near future, and vice virsa some of our faculty will be visiting french universities where the program mentioned above has been implemented.
