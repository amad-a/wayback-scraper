About Mathematics & Computer Science Department

    at Birzeit University

    Math & Comp Sci Dept. at BZU is as old as the university itself. Math & Comp Sci Dept. plays a fundamental role in almost every colleges in Birzeit University, especially to science, engineering and commerce students.
    As a matter of fact, to those the department offers service courses amounting to over sixty percent of its academic load in terms of credit hours in any given semester.
    Two years ago, the Department of Mathematics had become the Department of Mathematics & Computer Science.
    In Addition to that, the department offers a strong program leading to a first degree in Mathematics & Computer Science along several tracks.
    Graduates of that program find jobs in teaching Mathematics at the local high schools or at local computer companies, or going abroad to pursue the graduate studies.
    Math & Comp Sci Dept. is continuing to develop itself through the planning to introduce a new tracks in the future as Statistics, Mathematical Economics which are very important tracks in satisfying our community needs, more over it develop the current programs which are leading to B.Sc. in Math & Comp Sci..
    This service has been put together with the hope that it will introduce the department(Staff, Programs, Courses offering, ..etc) to the prospective student, the visitor and anybody or institution that has an interest to know.
