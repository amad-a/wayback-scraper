Call For Papers

A person wishing to present a paper in the conference may send an abstract together with the registration form to the organizing committee. Material may be submitted via e-mail or regular mail. Papers reporting original and unpublished research results and expository papers are solicited. Accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings. More details about this will be provided during the conference.
