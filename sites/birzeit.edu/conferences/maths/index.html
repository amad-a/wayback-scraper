First Announcement And Call For Papers

THE SECOND PALESTINIAN INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON MATHEMATICS

August 19-22, 1998
Birzeit, Palestine
The Palestinian Society for Mathematical Sciences (PSMS)  and the Department of Mathematics at Birzeit University  (BZU) are organizing the 2nd Palestinian International Conference on Mathematics to be held August 19-22, 1998  at BZU.
  

Conference Topics:

  • Applied Mathematics.
  • Pure Mathematics.
  • Statistics.
  • Mathematics Education and Curriculum Developments.

Call For Papers:

A person wishing to present a paper in the conference may send a full length paper or  a single page  abstract together with the attached registration form to the organizing committee no later than June 1,1998. You can either  e-mail or use  the regular mail to send the abstract and the registration form. Papers reporting original and unpublished research results are solicited. Accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings. More details about this will be provided during the conference.

Click here to fill the registration form through the browser, Otherwise Click here and print it to fill it manually and send it by regular mail.

Conference Site:

The conference will be held at BZU located in the town of Birzeit, which is about 20 miles north of Jerusalem,  and about 4 miles north of Ramallah. BZU has one of the most modern and beautiful campuses in the region. The department of mathematics has spacious class rooms and an auditorium with capacity  of around 350. It has various computer labs.  The department offers fax, e-mail and secretarial support. Food  facilities are available on campus. In addition, local arrangements have  been made  for a  reception and banquet at the beginning and the end of the conference. Lodging facilities are available in Ramallah, which is about 4 miles from the BZU campus. Arrangements have been made to give special rates for the participants with the following hotels:
Ramallah Grand Palace Hotel (972-2-9986194) $100 for single rooms (SR)  and $117 for double rooms (DR).
City Inn Hotel (972-2-9959191) $50 (SR) and $70 (DR) .
Al Hajal  Hotel (972-2-9987858) $45 (SR) and $55 (DR).
Miami Hotel (972-2-9956808) $35 (SR) and $45 (DR).

Transportation will be  available from and to the conference.  Public transportation is available at a reasonable rate.  The local arrangement committee will make  arrangements  with the hotels  to provide transportation  to the BZU campus. In addition, car rental is available through most American car rental companies' offices in Jerusalem. Special tours have been prepared for participants to Jericho and the Dead Sea.
  

Registration fee:

The registration fee is $100.00 for non members and free for students. This fee includes the conference program, abstract, refreshments, session attendance, banquet, social events, and membership into the PSMS.
Method of payment: checks or money orders payable to Birzeit University.
