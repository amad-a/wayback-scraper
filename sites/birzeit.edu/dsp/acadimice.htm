Academic Programme

 Because of its belief in the importance of the academic dimension of development, the DSP cooperates with Palestinian universities and with academic centers to integrate and improve the curriculum relating to development studies in their educational programs. The DSP is also currently designing an M.A Program in Development Studies for the benefit of academics and practitioners in universities and in the government and non-government community.

Including Human Development in University Courses

 The DSP proposed to Palestinian universities and other local institutions of higher education the idea of developing courses related to, or specialized in, development. The Programme contacted the Chairpersons of the departments of Sociology, Economics, Education, Women’s Studies, Social Development, and Family Development, among others, and explained to them the above idea. Copies of DSP publications were suggested as sources of information, additonal reading material, or part of the curriculum. Copies were also presented to university libraries for the benefit of students and researchers. The DSP will continue to provide such publications to all those interested.

Dissertation Series

The Development Studies Program has started to publish M.A and Ph.D. theses relating to development issues.
