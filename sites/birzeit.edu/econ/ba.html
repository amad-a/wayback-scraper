Bachelor Degree in Economics

The Economics Department presents an academic program to achieve a Bachelor degree in economics; this program includes theoretical economic bases and their different applications. The department also offers a program for a minor in economics.

Junior Faculty of Commerce students usually study, in their first and second semesters, six courses provided by the faculty - two courses from the economics department (Economics (130), Economics(230)) and another two courses from the Business Department (Introduction to Business Admenistration (130), Principles of Adminestration(230)), and two courses from the Accounting Department (Financial Accounting I (231),Financial Accounting II (232)). These courses are prerequisites for any major in the faculty.
