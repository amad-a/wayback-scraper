ROOTS

Palestine needs for all Palestinains around the world to come back to Palestine and rediscover culture

For a variety of reasons, mostly political, Palestine has experienced high rates of migration. Many have moved to other countries in the Middle East, others have made lives in Western nations such as; United States, Honduras, Germany etc. These Palestinians in their new lives have had children who only know their parent's homeland through stories and old pictures. The Alternative Tourism Group feels that is important for both, the sons and daughters of these Palestinians as well as for Palestine itself for these second generation Palestinians to become aquatinted with the land of their parents. Thus we have developed a program for them to visit Palestine and get a first hand account of life inside this beautiful country.

The Roots program does not intends to leave out any Palestinan thus we have developed three programs


Roots College Students Tour
Roots High School Student Tour
Roots Fesival for workers for Palestinian Issues

Stories are an excellent way to discover your roots. We at the Alternative Tourism Group will begin collecting stories about Palestine and Palestinain related events. We will post these stories on our Palestinain Stories page for all to read. In order for this to work we need the help of all those who have Palestinain experiences. Please tell your story and do not let our history die.
