Welcome to the Alternative Tourism Group Home Page

Located In Beit-Sahour West Bank

The Alternative Tourism Group specialises in organising and supervising trips to Palestine. We take you deep into this unique land far from the well-trodden tourist trails and long queues to see the famous landmarks.

Traveling with the ATG is intended to give insight into how people in the Holy Land think, work and live. If you wish, we can arrange meetings with scientific, poltical, religious and social organisations, with both Palestinian and Israeli backgrounds. With complete freedom to openly exhange views on the subjects of your choice.

At the ATG we are very flexible to the tourist needs we offer at variety of tours Click on the trip of your choice to find out more
