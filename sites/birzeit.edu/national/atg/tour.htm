Tourist Trips

A tourist trip the holy land for many is a Christian pilgramage. Tourists with the ATG have the unique chance to go beyond Israel and visit the Holy Land. These Pilgrimages usually take one to two days and tourists can visit such things as: The Good Samaritan Inn, St. George and John of Coziba (an old desert monastery), Qumran, the Dead Sea, Ramallah, Nablus, Sabastia, Bethlehem, the Church of the Nativity,Shepards field, the Abraham Mosque and Hebron.
