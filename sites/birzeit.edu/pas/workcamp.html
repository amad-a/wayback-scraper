Workcamps for International Students

Each year, Birzeit University organizes two fortnight-long Summer workcamps which give international students the opportunity to work alongside Palestinian students on community-orientated projects. PAS students often join the camps, which take students all over the West Bank and Gaza Strip, working on projects for schools, municipalities, special needs centers and more.
