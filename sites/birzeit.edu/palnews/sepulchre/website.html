DESIGN OF THE COMMEMORATIVE WEBSITE


The design of this website was based on the colours someone sees when looking up at the inside of the Dome in the early evening.


Problems with printing out the website may exist, the result of bugs in most WWW browsers, including all versions of Netscape, for which this site is enhanced for. Simply select colours in your [PREFERENCES] menu and choose whatever the default option is.
