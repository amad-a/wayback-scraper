This archive contains university press releases in English, issued by the Public Relations Office and other departments in 1996. Other years can be found via the main index. To be added to our e-mailing list, to receive these releases for yourself, your institution or organisation, or for more information, please contact us at pr@admin.birzeit.edu. An additional feature of the on-line website press releases will be links to relevant sections on our website and relevant e-mail addresses.
