MARHABA: THE PALESTINIAN INTERNET W HITEPAGES

The use of computer technology in Palestine is spreading fast among institutions, companies, and individuals: Almost all Palestinian universities already operate computer networks with e-mail. Moreover, many governmental, non-governmental, and private institutions also have access to e-mail. There are four service providers in the West Bank providing access to e-mail, the Internet, and the World Wide Web. On the other hand, the number of Palestinians and Palestinian organizations outside of Palestine with access to e-mail is far higher.

An essential part of the process of the advancement of our people, obtaining our inalienable human rights, and the establishment of the Palestinian State is to combine all the efforts and expertise of Palestinians all over the world. With communication via e-mail so accessible and widespread these days, it would be useful to create a database aimed at facilitating communication among Palestinians. Birzeit University, Palnet (the largest Palestinian commercial Internet Service Provider), and Baraka (Palestinian NGO network) are the sponsors of this project. Information about Palestinians with e-mail will be made available on-line for everybody to use. Details of the profession, company/organization, city of residence, and Palestinian city of origin are to be also accessible.

One of the aims of this project will be coordinating the existing mechanisms and creating new and innovative ones to form a cohesive body of Palestinian individuals united in their goals of achieving a Palestinian state and of improving the living conditions of all Palestinians. We will ask Palestinians how they could help the Palestinian cause: by offering their expertise to other Palestinian individuals or institutions, by investing in projects in Palestine, by joining in letter writing campaigns intended to support the Palestinian cause, or by contributing through other means. Moreover, Palestinians in the Diaspora from the same village, city, or area of origin would be able to know each other and strengthen their bonds to Palestine. Eventually they will join forces to contribute for the betterment of their immediate Palestine community inside or outside of Palestine.

This project will be constantly updated and revised. To ensure the success of this project we need to hear from all Palestinians with e-mail, and for them to also spread the word about this very important project. The sooner we get together, the sooner we will realize our goals.
