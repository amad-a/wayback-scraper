Welcome to the Department of Chemistry at Birzeit University. The Department Department offers a four-year program of study leading to a Bachelor's Degree in one of the following areas : Chemistry as a single major, Chemistry major with Biochemistry minor, and finally, Chemistry major with concentration on applied chemistry.
