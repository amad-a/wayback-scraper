Study Visits to Germany

for the year 2001

 

The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) offers scholarships for study or research visits of 1-3 months to professors at Palestinian universities (Ph.D.-holders), who have plans for a clearly defined study or research project in cooperation with a German host institution or professional colleague. More than one invitation within the space of three years is not possible, i.e. after one study-visit there must be three years without study-visit, until you can reapply. Preference is given to academics who have not had the opportunity of an extended study visit to the Federal Republic of Germany during the past three years.

DAAD hopes that these scholarships will provide Palestinian academics with an opportunity to do research in cooperation with a German partner in the proper academic setting. At the same time, DAAD strongly encourages you to use this study-visit to establish actual research and academic relations with your host-professor and host-institution. Do remember that you can always extend an invitation to German professors to come and teach for some time (between one to four months) at your university in Palestine. DAAD will provide the German colleagues with scholarships for such a purpose.

The monthly allowance for a study visit amounts to between DM 3.200, -- and DM 3.700,--, according to the academic status of the applicant. (Additional funds for accompanying dependents are not available. Also, applicants are in general not encouraged to travel with their family.) DAAD will also supply travel-money for the trip to and from Germany.

Applications should be made on the blue forms and must include all documents requested therein. They should present as clear a picture as possible of the person and the qualifications of the academic. Precise details and reasons for the project should be provided. Applicants must provide proof of arrangements made with the inviting German academic concerning the study visit.

 

Requirements:

1. Research or Study Project (2 to 3 pages) including bibliography.

2. Invitation by one or more German professors and/or institutions to implement above research project.

3. C.V.

4. List of publications

5. Travel Plan (Tel Aviv or Amman, provisional date of travel).

6. Provisional itinerary inside Germany, in case you plan to visit different universities

All applications must be submitted in triplicate, i.e. three separate packages containing complete applications.

Only complete applications can be processed. Incomplete applications will be rejected.

All applications must be submitted to the DAAD Information Office in East-Jerusalem,

by Wednesday, October 18, 2000. Please, do keep this deadline.

 

No exceptions can be granted.

The DAAD head office in Germany will decide on the scholarships. Results will be communicated to you, as soon as they have arrived here.

As soon as you have been awarded the study visit, you should apply for your travel-papers. Visa procedures under the Schengen-Agreement take not less than three weeks.

Applications must be presented in person at the Embassy (you must submit an application, two passport-photos, all your DAAD-Scholarship-documents as well as an insurance for the time of your trip, and of course your Laissez-Passer for Jerusalemites or your Palestinian Passport in the case of Westbank and Gaza Strip) or at the Representative Office in Ramallah resp. Gaza. So plan your trip to Tel Aviv resp. Ramallah or Gaza immediately.

Once the application has been submitted in person, any other person can go to the Embassy to collect the travel-papers plus visa.

DAAD, unfortunately, is in no position to act as an intermediary. However, in cases of emergency, we shall try to give all help possible, esp. via the German Representative Office in Ramallah.

DAAD will give all professors who have been awarded a study-visit a specified sum as travel-money so that you can make your own travel-arrangements.

Because of the difficulties in finding adequate and affordable living-quarters in Germany, all applicants are strongly advised to travel without family. DAAD cannot administer any help in finding housing in Germany. Your hosts in Germany might, in some cases, be able to provide a guest-apartment, especially if they are informed well in advance! Put differently, as soon as you have received your invitation, do start to make the necessary preparations to find housing in Germany, either through your hosts or with the help of colleagues who visited Germany before or with the help of friends in Germany.

 

Please, make sure you have read this information-sheet carefully. If you have any other questions, please, do not hesitate to contact DAAD Information Office in East-Jerusalem for further information.
