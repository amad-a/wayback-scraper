Ph.D.- Scholarships to Germany

for the year 2001

Eligible:

Every year at least five Ph.D.- Scholarships to Germany are offered to Palestinian Graduates with a M.A./M.Sc. from any university. These graduates should have scored excellent results during their studies and plan to embark on an academic career. Nomination by a university will be considered positively, as this scholarship-program is designed as a faculty-building program. However, academic excellence is the decisive criteria for the selection-process. Maximum age at the time of application is 31. This year only those born in 1969 or after can apply.

 

Requirements:

        1. Full Curriculum Vitae.

2. Detailed and precise description of research project as well as information on any previous research project (for ex. résumé of Masters Thesis) between 3 to 5 pages including bibliography.

3. At least two letters of reference from your academic teachers at the Masters level.

4. Letter of acceptance as a Ph.D.-student by a German professor, who has approved your research project.

5. All academic certificates as officially authenticated copies in English translation

a) Tawjihe.

b) B.A./B.Sc. with full transcripts.

c) M.A./M.Sc. with full transcripts (at the time of application, candidates should have finished their studies and obtained their degrees!).

6. Three recent photographs to be attached to the application forms (glued, not stapled!).

7. List of publications (if any).

8. Copy of your passport.

Applications must be submitted in triplicate, i.e. three separate complete packages must be submitted with a complete application in each package (please, do not staple your application papers!).

Application - Deadline: Wednesday, September 27, 2000.

Please, use the green application-papers: One-Year Scholarship for Postgraduate studies. Scholarships are awarded on an annual basis, i.e. they will be renewed annually upon successful completion of one year of studies.

 

Only complete applications will be accepted and processed.

All applicants will be invited to an interview (conducted in English) by a bi-national Palestinian-German Committee which will make a preliminary choice of ten candidates in order of preference. This preliminary decision will be communicated to all candidates by October 30, 2000. Check our web site in October under "News" for detailed information about the interviews!

All applicants whose papers are not sent to Germany are asked to pick up their application papers at the place where they officially submitted them (i.e. your respective university, the German Representative Office in Gaza, or DAAD-Office in Jerusalem).

The final decision will be made by a German Academic Committee at the DAAD Head Office in Bonn, by the end of February 2001.

We are sorry that we cannot return application-papers that have been sent to Germany!

Final results will be available by March 2001.

The interviews will be held

-at Birzeit University on Friday, October 20, 2000. (For last-minute detailed information, call Dr. Helga Baumgarten at Birzeit University, Tel. 298 29 91 or

298 21 73)

- at Bethlehem University on Saturday, October 21, 2000. (For last-minute detailed information, call. Dr. Sami el-Yousef at Bethlehem University, Tel.274 12 41)

- at the German Representative Office, Gaza Branch (al-Rimal) on Sunday, October 15, 2000. (For last-minute detailed information call Mrs. Anke Abu-Sitteh, Tel. 07-282 55 84)

The candidates chosen will travel to Germany by September 30, 2001,

starting with a 6-month language course at one of the Goethe-Institutes in Germany. Only after successful completion of the language-course will students take up their Ph.D.-studies at their respective university.

 

Amount of scholarship:

During language-course: tuition and fees as well as room and board are taken care of by DAAD. All scholarship-holders will receive a monthly pocket money for additional personal expenses. During period of study: at the beginning a monthly stipend of DM 1.350,00. Only after official registration at your university as a Ph.D.-student will you receive the final scholarship of appr. DM 1.650,00. In addition, you receive a book-allowance every semester. Also, all DAAD-students are fully insured in Germany. Finally, you receive a plane-ticket from Palestine to Germany and a return-ticket upon completion of your Ph.D.
