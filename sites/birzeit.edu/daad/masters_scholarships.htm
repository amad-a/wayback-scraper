Masters' Scholarships at Jordanian Universities

for the year 2001

 

Eligible:

Palestinian Graduates (Sciences and Engineering) who graduated in 1999 or 2000 and have an accumulative average of at least very good (80% and above). They should envisage an academic career and be supported in this by their respective universities. (This program is designed as a faculty-building program. After having obtained a Master’s-Degree in Jordan, an application can be submitted for a Ph.D.-program in Germany). Maximum age at the time of application: 26 years.

Requirements:

        1. Curriculum Vitae

2. Certified copies (do not submit originals!) of the Tawjihe-Certificate, of the Bachelor’s-Degree and of the complete transcript (all in English).

3. Recommendation by the Head of your Department plus additional recommendations (as detailed and specific as possible) by other academic teachers.

4. Three recent photos.

5. Choice of one of the Jordanian universities (candidates should make sure that their desired specialization is available at the respective university. In general, our students study at the University of Jordan in Amman.

Only in special cases - i.e. if the field of specialization is not available in Amman - will they register at Yarmouk University or at JUST in Irbid). Special note for graduates in nursing: requirement for enrollment in any Masters Program is two years of professional experience after your graduation!

6. Copy of your passport.

7. Applications must be submitted in triplicate (i.e. three separate complete packages with a complete application in each package).

 

Application-Deadline: September 27, 2000.

 

Please, use the red application-papers (In-Country-Scholarships for Postgraduate Studies). Only complete applications will be accepted and processed.

 

All applicants will be invited to an interview (which will be held in English, not in Arabic!) by a bi-national Palestinian-German Committee, which will make a preliminary choice of five candidates plus five reserve-candidates. (Please check our website in October for detailed information about the interviews. Make sure when your personal interview is scheduled and do confirm your attendance with the DAAD Office in Jerusalem). The interviews will be held:

-at Birzeit University on Friday, October 20, 2000. (For detailed information call Dr.Helga Baumgarten at Birzeit University, Tel. 298 2991 or 298 2173).

-at Bethlehem University on Saturday, October 21, 2000. (For detailed information call Dr. Sami el-Yousef at Bethlehem University, Tel.2 74 12 41).

-at the German Representative Office, Gaza Branch (al-Rimal) on Sunday, October 15, 1999. (For detailed information call Mrs. Anke Abu Sitteh, Tel. 07-282 55 84).

Results of these interviews will be available by October 30, 2000.

 

The final decision will be made by a German academic committee at the DAAD Head Office in Bonn, in February 2001. Results will be available by the end of March 2001.

The candidates chosen will be registered at Jordanian Universities through the Palestinian Ministry of Higher Education in June 2001.

 

If your application is not successful, you can pick up your application-papers at the place where you submitted it, i.e. your university, the DAAD office in Jerusalem resp. Birzeit or at the German Representative Office in Gaza, by the end of November 2000. Application-papers sent to Germany cannot be returned.

 

Documents required for registration at the Jordanian universities:

  1. Certified copy of birth certificate (in Arabic).
  2. Certified copies of Tawjihe and Bachelor’s-Degree as well as of complete transcript (in Arabic)
  3. C.V. in Arabic.
  4. Photocopy of relevant pages in Jordanian Passport (if candidates do hold such a document) resp. photocopy of Palestinian Passport.
  5. 6 recent passport-photos.

 

All candidates who were selected or were put on the reserve-list should submit these documents to the office of the DAAD-Representative in East Jerusalem or in Birzeit (resp. to the German Representative Office in Gaza) not later than April 15, 2001.

 

The academic year in Jordan will most probably start by the end of September 2001 (Check the website of the University of Jordan, Amman, resp. our website in Jerusalem).

Amount of scholarship:

  • Tuition and fees at the university are paid by DAAD.

  • A monthly stipend of JD 300,00 is given to every student, in addition to an allowance of JD 100,00 per semester for books.
