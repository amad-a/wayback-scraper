For a ground-zero view of the bloody and seemingly endless Israeli­Palestinian conflict, check out this memoir by an English photographer/writer who works at a Palestinian university in the West Bank. Sensitive and mature despite its unapologetically anti-Israel tone, this heartfelt diary personalizes a distant and abstract tragedy, reminding you that Hebron and Ramallah are more than just words in headlines. Extensive links and great photos, but the small text will strain your eyes.
