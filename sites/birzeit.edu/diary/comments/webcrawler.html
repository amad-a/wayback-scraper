Diary of the Israeli-Palestinian Conflict

"Music is one of the ways to keep your shit together in the West Bank..." -- Nigel Parry

A personal diary from "on-the-ground" in Palestine. Our host is the British Nigel Parry, a writer, photographer, and Birzeit University Webmaster in Palestine. Nigel draws upon his experiences with the Birzeit University community and, as such, writes from a Palestinian perspective. His collection of thoughts, events, and photos, however, rise above culture to be sensitive meditations on war, human rights, and the conflict in the West Bank.
