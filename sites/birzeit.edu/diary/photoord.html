Ordering photos
October 1998

Most of the photographs in the diary are available to publications, and for collectors and exhibitions, with the exception of those credited to other photographers. Provision can be made for sending photographs to publications via e-mail or FTP.

An initial portfolio of my images from the Palestinian West Bank can be found here.

The vast majority of the photographs taken in Palestine between 1994 and 1998 are colour. Photographs taken on my return to Palestine, whenever that will be, will be mostly black and white.

Please contact Nigel Parry if you would like more information about the photographs in the Diary.
