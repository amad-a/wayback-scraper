About the author
October 1998
Brief CV

Nigel Parry

  • Present - Webmaster, Middle East International magazine.
  • 1992-present - Freelance writer and photographer for publications that have included Middle East International magazine, The Jerusalem Times, The Cairo Times, Link Magazine, Al-Muhajir (The Immigrant), Living Stones magazine, and Renewal.
  • 1994-1998 - Public Relations Office, Birzeit University, with responsibility for English language publications and the university's website.
  • 1995 (Publication) - "Making Education Illegal, Students from the Gaza Strip: Israeli restrictions and international reactions", a report for the Human Rights Action Project at Birzeit University by Nigel Parry.
  • 1993-1994 - Coordinator, Friends of Birzeit University, London.
  • 1993-1994 - Coordinator, Living Stones, Twickenham.
  • 1992 (Publication) - "Street Children in the Philipines", a report for Jubilee Campaign UK edited by Fr. Shay Cullen, Danny Smith and Nigel Parry.
  • 1992-1993 - Parliamentary Unit, Jubilee Campaign, London.
  • 1991-1992 - Voluntary work with Vietnamese refugees and recovering-heroin addicts, Hong Kong.
  • 1989-1990 - Student Union President, Southlands College Students' Union, London.
  • 1988-1989 - External Affairs Officer, Southlands College Students' Union, London.
  • 1988 - Voluntary Researcher, Oxfam in Scotland, Edinburgh.
  • 1986-1989 - BA (Hons.) English and Psychology (Surrey University).
