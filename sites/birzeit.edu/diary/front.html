A PERSONAL DIARY OF THE ISRAELI-PALESTINIAN CONFLICT



by Nigel Parry
Ramallah, West Bank.

A photodiary of the conflict from "on the ground" in the West Bank from 1994 to 1998, focusing on the experiences of the Birzeit University community, and other developments in the Ramallah area and beyond after the Oslo 2 agreement.
