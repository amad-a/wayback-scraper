10 september 1998 - portrait and testimony
The portrait and testimony section of the website has finally been added. Visit this section for portraits by photographer Joni Lane of Gazan students and read about the lives of these students in their own voices.

27 august 1998 - website launch
The 1998 Gaza Students Campaign for Academic Freedom website has finally been launched. Keep checking the website for regular updates on the campaign in Palestine and internationally. The website will go through some minor changes throughout the week. Hey, we can't always get it right the first time!
