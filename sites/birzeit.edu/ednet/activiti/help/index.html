How You Can Help Palestinian Education

Many of our readers have suggested we provide concrete ideas for ways in which they could support Palestinian education. The Network has therefore compiled a list of ideas to guide you. If you need more information, assistance or avice on any of these activites, please do not hesitate to contact us.

General Activities

  • Lobby for the Release of all Imprisoned Palestinian Schoolchildren
  • Raise Your Community's Awareness about Palestinian Education
  • Contribute to Network Information Provision, Library and Newsletter
  • Support a Development Project
  • Protest Israeli Measures against Palestinian Education
  • Help the Network Find Interested and Active Subscribers
  • Set up a Scholarship Fund for Palestinian Teachers or Students
  • Experience the Educational Situation First-Hand at a Palestinian University

Activities for Schools

  • Organize a Campaign for a Palestinian school
  • Invite a Palestinian Teacher or Student
  • Donate Used Educational Materials

Activities for Teachers

  • Volunteer in a Palestinian School
  • Lobby for Scholarships for Palestinian Students
  • Meet Palestinian Teachers When You Visit the Country
  • Link Your Teachers' Union to the Palestinian Teachers' Union
  • Inform the Network about Educational Conferences
  • Come and Teach Teachers
