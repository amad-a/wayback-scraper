Invite a Palestinian Teacher or Student

Palestinian teachers and students have been very much isolated from the outside world. Your invitation would give them a chance to learn about schools and teaching methods in your country, tell people their about their life and work and they could share what they have learned in a meeting with fellow teachers or students upon their return. The Network will provide a living allowance for students and teachers while they are abroad.
