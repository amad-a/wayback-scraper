Organize a Campaign for a Palestinian School

Palestinian schools need your help and solidarity! Your school and a Palestinian school could undertake a teaching project, a support campaign, twinning, a pen-pal scheme, or an art or student/teacher exchange project. The Educational Network could provide you with information, photos and the names and addresses of children at a Palestinian school. In the case of short-term exchange projects, the Network will provide a living allowance for the Palestinian teacher or students while they are abroad.
