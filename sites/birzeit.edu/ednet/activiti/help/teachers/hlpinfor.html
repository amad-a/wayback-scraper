Inform the Network about Educational Conferences

Under Occupation, teacher training and methodology development has been a neglected area in Palestine. Please inform us in writing of any educational conferences that might be useful for Palestinian teachers. We will then try and send a teacher to attend who will share his/her experiences with other teachers upon return.
