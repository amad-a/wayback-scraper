Raise Your Community's Awareness about Palestinian Education

The Palestinian educational system is beset by many problems and challenges. You could set up a teaching project in your school (with photos and stories of Palestinian children and their school and community), give a lecture or presentation to your teachers union or community or write an article for your local newspaper or teachers magazine. The Network, through its newsletter, photo-file and its specialized reference library can provide the information.
