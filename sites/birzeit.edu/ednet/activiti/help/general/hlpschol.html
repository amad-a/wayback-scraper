Set up a Scholarship Fund for Palestinian Teachers or Students

Many Palestinian students are not able to study for lack of sufficient local opportunities or financial problems. Your local organization/university may be interested to set up a scholarship fund for a Palestinian student to study at a school/university in Palestine or abroad. Alternatively, you might like to help a teacher to follow a teacher training course at a university or college in your country. The Network can help with facilitation and coordination.
