Director:

Dr. Munir Fasheh

Type of Organization:

Non-profit; community building; youth development; resource; networking.

Description:

Tamer Institute for Community Education was established in 1989 in response to the needs and realities of the Palestinian community. Tamer aspires to contribute to building the social-intellectual-spiritual-cultural fabric of the community as the backbone of more visible levels of development. Strategies include the creation of learning environments in various locations; mobilization of available resources; a focus on working with youth and the encouragement of small groups as part of a larger network. Projects are often initiated by the community in cooperation with Tamer. Activities include:

  • the National Reading campaign;
  • Summer Days program for youth;
  • the Publishing Unit -- publications for and by youth, children and teachers;
  • the Palestinian home construction toy;
  • puppet workshop;
  • the Small Continent Project -- youth travel guide to Palestine
  • Palestinian Oral History project.

Administrative Structure:

Ten Board Members, all from different educational institutions in the West Bank; a Director and Management Team, a program team, and two administrative staff (eight full-time employees in total).
