Mission

  • Established in 1989 by the Ramallah Friends' Schools as a networking body concerned with the protection, effectiveness, and development of Palestinian education.
  • Development of Palestinian education into a viable system that meets the needs of the Palestinian people
  • Generates a relevant and effective philosophy of education, teaching methods and course materials
  • Forms international links with educators, teachers' unions, and organizations concerned with Palestinian education in order to build greater international cooperation
  • Provides information about Palestinian education and educational organizations and development
