Organizational and Administrative Structure

Palestinian National Authority -- Ministry of Education & Higher Education

Educational Network, No. 16/17, Winter 1994, p. 6.

Minister: Mr. Yaser 'Ammer

Deputy Minister: Dr. Na'eem Abu'l-Humus

Assistants to the Deputy Minister:

West Bank Affairs: Mr. Mutee' Abu Hijleh

Gaza Strip Affairs: Mr. 'Abd Allah 'Abd al-Man'em

Education Committee and Council of Education

General Secretary: Mr. Jihad Zakarneh

Palestinian National Committee for Education, Culture and Science

Mr. Ghasan Qurshalli

Council for Higher Education

Mr. Gabi Baramki

Legal Advisor: Ms. Amal al-Imam

Financial and Administrative Affairs Department

Director General: Mr. Fa'iq Hamadallah

Supplies (Purchasing, Tenders and Warehouses); Financial Affairs (Accounting, Documentation, Cash, Safe Deposits, Auditing) and Administrative Affairs (Personnel, Records, Payroll, Employment, Transport).

General Education Department

Director General Mr. Walid al-Zagha

Vocational Education (Industrial, Agricultural, Tourism, Nursing, Home Economics, Finances and Administration) and General Education (Kindergartens, Basic, Secondary, Non-formal, Private, Special and UNRWA).

Technical Education and Community Colleges Department

Director General: Mr. Hisham Keheil

Curricula and Exams (Curricula, Examinations and Educational Supervision) and Community Colleges (Colleges Affairs, Accreditation and Student Affairs).

Buildings and Projects Department

Director General: Mr. Fawaz Mujahed

Buildings (Real Estate Acquisition, Engineering and Maintenance) and Projects (Technical Assistance, School Equipment and Projects).

Exams, Measurements and Evaluation Department

Director General: Mr. 'Abd Allah Abu Samhadani

School Tests and Record (School Records, Measurement and Evaluation) and Test and Measurement (General Exams and Certification).

Supervision, Training and Qualifications Department

Director General: Mr. Sa'eed 'Assaf

Teacher Training and Qualifications (Headmaster Training and Administrator Training) and Administrative Staff Training (Educational Supervision, Educational Training and Qualifications and Teacher Educational Leave).

Student Affairs Department

Director General: Mr. Subhi al-Kayed

Student Services (Food and Medical Services and Educational Counseling) and Student Activities (Sports, Social, Sciences, and Cultural Activities, B/G Scouts Division, Voluntary Work and Youth Clubs).

Planning, Research and Development Department

Director General: Mr. Jamil Sarhan

Computer Service (Programming, Data Processing and Maintenance; Research (Research, Archives and Publications) and Planning and Educational Development (Planning, Programming, Budget, Development).

Public Relations and Cultural Affairs Department

Director General: Mr. Khalil Mahshi

Cultural Affairs (UNESCO and International Organizations, Arabic and Islamic Organizations, Scholarships and Cultural Attaches) and Public Relations (Public Relations and Educational Information).
