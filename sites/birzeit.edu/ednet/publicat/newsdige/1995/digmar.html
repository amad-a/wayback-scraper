Educational News Digest

March 1995

Mar 01

  • An-Najah Student Council calls for release of the 300 students held by Israelis for interrogation or administrative detention, most of whom where arrested during finals
  • Bir Zeit issues report Making Education Illegal - Students from the Gaza Strip stating that 350 students could not attend first semester, permits were only granted to some and only if students signed declaration supporting the peace process; report points out that Gazans need three permits which may be cancelled or confiscated at any time

Mar 17

  • Nearly 600 Gazans still waiting for permits to study in West Bank institutes of higher education
  • Japanese government and UNDP fund advanced water analysis lab at Bir Zeit university

Mar 24

  • Al-Najah Architecture College exhibits students' projects and designs
  • Children's book exhibition at Palestinian Children's Art Centre in Hebron under auspices of PNA

Mar 28

  • Minister of Education opens the Central Art Exhibition at Salam Basic School in Qalqilia; and the first Cultural Festival at Al-Bireh Girls School (Q)

Mar 29

  • UNESCO delegation visits MoE in Ramallah
  • Forward plan, covering 3 years, discussed in meeting between MoE and donor countries
  • General Committee for the Defence of Land calls for special school classes to discuss Land Day
  • Parents council formed in Aqraba Secondary and Elementary schools (Q)
  • Student council formed at Amro Ibn Al-As School (N)
  • UNESCO delegation on two-week visit to MoE

Mar 30

  • Parent/teacher council formed at Ibn Al-Aus School, Nablus (Q)
  • Technical and vocational training courses by British Council and Scottish Vocational Training Council lead to new specialisations - Art, TV, radio & film production, hotel management, food technology - for community colleges
  • Two-week training course in health education by MoE and MoH for Gazan primary teachers (N)
