Educational News Digest

September 1994

Sept 1

  • Festive start of new school year with Palestinian flag and national anthem

Sept 2

  • Minister of Education confirms that poor families will be exempt from school fees and children of teaching staff will receive 50% discounts (J)
  • First graduation ceremony of al-Quds University (J)
    • 81 Palestinian students studying at Russian universities miss flight due to slow and strict security checks at Ben Gurion airport (J)
    • Call for increase in teacher salaries, new Palestinian curricula, expansion of vocational training and reform of examinations in article in (J)
    • Seminar in Bir Zeit University of General Union of Palestinian Handicapped addresses problems of the physically handicapped in education (J)

Sept 8

  • Arafat visits Islamic University Gaza and promises it a place on Council for Higher Education (J)

Sept 9

  • Announcement of plans for a law school at al-Najah University, Nablus (J)
  • Higher Fatah Committee affirms that the education sector will be excluded from future strikes and mourning days (J)

Sept 12

  • Bethlehem University starts new academic year with 1900 students and expansion of campus (Q)
  • UNRWA teachers hold sit-in at UNRWA HQ in Gaza against emergency program appointments (Q)
  • Request by students recently released from prison for assistance in completing their education (Q)
    • British Council delegation visits MoE to discuss needs, priorities, and Community Colleges, and expresses interest to support teacher training and technical education (Q)

Sept 13

  • German funding for development of Technical Training Center in Nablus Industrial School (Q)
  • The approximately 2000 teachers dismissed under Occupation request MoE to return them to their jobs (Q)
  • Hind Husseini, educator and founder of Dar al-Tifl Society, dies at age 78 in Jerusalem (J)

Sept 16

  • Jerusalem Municipality approves plan for library for Jewish school on grounds allocated for construction of Palestinian school (J)
  • Call for recognition of rights to education for the disabled (J)
  • Minister accredits Islamic University in Gaza (J)

Sept 24

  • UNDP opens new school in rural area of Jinsafut for 300 girls, and stresses that, due to overcrowding, a 3000 extra classrooms need to be built, and that there is an unequal distribution of schools between cities and villages (Q)

Sept 25

  • Education and Democracy Seminar in Bir Zeit University; discussion of democracy in curricula (Q)

Sept 27

  • Ceremony at Arab Orphans Vocational School to distribute European Union-donated educational equipment (J)
  • Israeli authorities at Erez turn back scholar on his way to receive doctorate in Sudan (J)

Sept 28

  • Israeli forces intrude into Jelazoun Prep Boys School, force out students and staff and close school after alleged student demonstrations and stone throwing (Q)
  • Due to closure of road from Beiteen village to Ramallah by Israeli soldiers Beiteen children cannot reach their schools (Q)

Sept 29

  • Yearly Meeting of Council of Higher Education announces formation of a new Council headed by the Minister, presidents of Palestinian universities and academics (Q)

Sept 30

  • Bir Zeit students refuse to register in protest against 35% raise in University fees (J)
