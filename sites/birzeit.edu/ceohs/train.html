Training Unit
Some of the activities of our Training Unit are as follows:
  • Safe use of pesticides and integrated pest management (IPM) to farmers
  • Train-the-trainer programs involving agronomists on the rational use of pesticides and IPM
  • Scouting for rational pest control
  • Women’s training on health and pesticides as related to their daily lives
  • Training on Good Laboratory and Field Work Practice
  • Training in Laboratory analysis in the various units
