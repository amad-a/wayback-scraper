The Specific Objectives of the Center

  1. monitor changes in water quality from Palestinian aquifers.

  2. perform research related to the effects of pollution on the environment and people.

  3. work towards the reduction in the use of pesticides, with special emphasis on their safe use and integrated pest management.

  4. participate in formulating a comprehensive plan for the environmentally sound policies and legislation.

  5. exchange information and expertise with local and foreign groups concerned with environmental contamination.

  6. disseminate scientific information related to environmental contamination and work to raise the environmental consciousness for workers in various fields as well as general public.

  7. monitor the quality of locally manufactured pharmaceuticals.

  8. perform clinical studies on the bioequivalance and efficacy of drugs.

  9. assist industrial development in areas related to our concerns aiming at helping to achieve improvement in the quality of finished products through various means including training of personnel.
