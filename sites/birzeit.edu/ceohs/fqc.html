Food Quality Control Unit
The Food Quality Control Unit was established in 1997. We have a modern, fully-equipped laboratory which is ISO Guide 25 Certified. In this laboratory we conduct tests including biological, chemical, physical, labeling, packaging, organic, inorganic and radioactive contamination for food and food products. Our services are used by food manufacturers, associations, government and non government institutions.
