Mathematics Department

The Department offers a programs study leading to a B.Sc degree in Mathematics and Mathematical Economics, also it offers minors in Math and Statistics.

In order to be considered for acceptance in the program of Mathematics, the student should have:-

  • An average of 70% or more in Math131 and Math132, and a grade of at least 65% in each of these courses,
  • The department aproval.
