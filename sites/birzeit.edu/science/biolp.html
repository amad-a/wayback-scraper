Biology and Biochemistry Department

The Department offers a program study leading to a B.Sc degree in Biology, also it offers a minor in Biochemistry.

In order to be considered for acceptance in the program, the student should have:-

  • An average of 70% or more in BIOL111, BIOL131 and BIOL241, and a grade of at least 65% in each of these courses,
  • The department aproval.
