Primary Health Care Diploma

Course Admissions:

The following are the general criteria for acceptance into the degree program.
1) All students must have a minimum of a Bachelor's degree ( or an equivalent degree from an accredited 4-year college).
2) All students must have a minimum of three years experience working in the primary health care sector.
In addition to the items above, the student's proficiency in English will be assessed before acceptance. Students with unsufficient proficiency will be required to take a remedial course in English before entry into the program.
All students will be interviewed by a committee before their admission status is determined.
