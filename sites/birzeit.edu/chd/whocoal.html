Who We Are ?

The Palestinian Coalition for Women's Health is a professional platform composed of representatives of the major institutions working in the area of women's health in the country.
Members include practitioners with expertise in women's health, academics and researchers as well as individuals working at the policy making level from the governmental, non-governmental and UN agencies working in the area( UNDP, UNRWA and UNICEF). Currently, the members of the Coalition are:
  • Arab College of Medical Professions "Al-Quds University"
  • Bethlehem University
  • Birzeit University Department of Community and Public Health
  • Department of Women's Health and Development (Ministry of health, Palestinian National Authority)
  • Health Services Council
  • Maqassed Islamic Charitable Hospital
  • Palestine Council for Health
  • Palestinian Family Planning and Protection Association, Jerusalem
  • Patients' Friends Society, Jerusalem
  • UNDP, Jerusalem
  • UNICEF, Jerusalem
  • Union of heatlh Work Committees
  • Union of Palestinian Medical Relief Committees
  • UNRWA**
** UNRWA is a supporting member of the Coalition
