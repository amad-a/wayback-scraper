Goals and Objectives

  • Working to enrich national level policies regarding women's health through the generation of data required for policy formulation, the delineation of policy recommendations, and lobbying with the Palestinian women's movement, the Palestinian authorities, non-governmental and UN organizations for the adoption of these policy recommendations at the national level.
  • Developing innovative and gender sensitive curricula for training and upgrading existing human health resources. This objective includes the production of prototype training materials on women's health that reflect a holistic, life cycle approach to the understanding of women's health.
  • Assisting in the development of plans for the implementation of new programmes for women's health in governmental, non-governmental and UNRWA health services.
  • Monitoring developments in women's health programmes, including the assessment of pilot projects, with the aim of re-formulating and updating policies, and in order to prepare new plans of action.
  • Providing technical and advisory assistance to health and women's groups, whenever needed.
