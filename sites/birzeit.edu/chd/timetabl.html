Primary Health Care Diploma

Timetable and Duration

The diploma will be offered on a part-time basis to accommodate working professionals. It will be initiated with a two-week intensive period after which full-day classes will be taught once a week. At the end of the third semester there will be a one week intensive session designed to synthesize all information into a model useful in clinical practice. In addition, the students will evaluate the course and instructors. The total time will be divided into three semesters (one calendar year) as follows:

Semester 1

  • Medical sciences (1){only physicians}
  • Nursing sciences (1){only nurses}
  • Health sciences (1){health professionals}
  • Classical epidemiology
  • Social epidemiology

Semester 2

  • Medical sciences (2){only physicians}
  • Nursing sciences (2){only nurses}
  • Health sciences (2){health professionals}
  • Advanced epidemiology
  • Principles of primary health care
  • Health Education & Communications

Semester 3

  • Research & information systems
  • Planning & management
