The Resource Centre

This Centre offers general resource services in the area of community and public health to the Department's researchers, to Birzeit university students, as well as to researchers and students from outside the university. The Centre's holdings include books and periodicals, as well as health education materials such as slides, videos and charts. The Centre also houses a thorough collection of local publications about the Palestinian health sector. Moreover, the Centre serves the specific research and training focuses and needs of the Department.
Recently, the Department computerized the resource centre. Utilizing the computer program CDS-ISIS, the Department was able to enter multi-lingual texts--mostly Arabic and English-- into this computer cataloging and retrieval system. The Department has also recently developed the infrastructure needed to operate computer-line literature searches, and, at the moment, Medline, Healthplan, Popline, Social Science Index and Sociofile are available for use by researchers and students. Plans are under way to gain the technical and infrastructural capacity for retrieving original and full texts via Internet for storage in the Resource Centre and for local use.
