Birzeit University - Web Site Update
December 14, 2000:
Birzeit University web site is currently under major restructuring, maintenance and update.
More on this at home.birzeit.edu (must read by those who already have a sub-site at www.birzeit.edu and those who plan to have one)
Departments are encouraged to review content related to their work. Before moving your web page to the new server, please make sure that all content is valid, accurate and in compliance with BZU web policies and guidelines.
The current site (www.birzeit.edu) will host official BZU information, including basic information on all university departments as well as links to all independent departmental sites. The main page at home.birzeit.edu will also link to all active sites.
