SPECIAL REPORT:
NABLUS: THE PRIDE OF THE OLD TOWN

-N-A-B-L-U-S---R-E-P-O-R-T---S-P-O-N-S-O-R-S---

The publcation that this report was based on, was achieved with the contributions of private companies, who wanted to give prominance to the great potential of Nablus' Old Town, a city whose history reaches back two thousand years.

GENERAL PROGETTI and RIWAQ intend to use the report to encourage private entrpreneurs, public administrations and international bodies aware of the need to recover and protect the Old City.
