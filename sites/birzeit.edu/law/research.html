APPLIED RESEARCH

Another task of the Law Center is to offer Palestinian jurists (and also, when appropriate, political scientists, sociologists and educators), a venue where to think systematically and creatively about the problems and options relative to the unification of the West Bank and Gaza Strip legal systems, and to the modernization of Palestinian law in the light of the sociology of law and comparative legal studies.

With the legal compilation and databank as a starting reference point, researchers at the Law Center and the Palestinian Law Commissions can review all the laws in force in the West Bank and Gaza and suggest future legislative amendments to the Palestinian Legislative Council.

Research Papers

To date, the Law Center has completed the following research papers:

  • The Law of Banking in Palestine, by Haytham Al-Zuabi
  • Legislation Pertaining to Planning and Construction in Palestine, by Usama Halabi
  • Legislation Pertaining to Child Welfare in Palestine, by Mervat Rishmawi
  • Legal Background Paper for MAS report on "Towards Sustainable Growth in the West Bank and Gaza" prepared for the World Bank
  • Proposal for the Palestinian Basic Law, co-authored with Al-Haq
