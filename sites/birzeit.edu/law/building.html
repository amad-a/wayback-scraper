Construction of New Law Center Building

GROUND-BREAKING CEREMONY - A reception to mark the start of construction of the new Law Center Building was held on December 5th, 1995. The new 1,200 square metre building, funded by the French and Qatari governments, is set to be completed in the spring of 1997, and will contain a library and documentation service (catalogue, shelves, reading room, computer room), offices for the research, teaching and administrative people, meeting and teaching rooms.

VISIT BY THE QATARI MINISTER OF JUSTICE AND DELEGATION - The Law Center welcomed Dr. Najib Nu'aimi, the Qatari Minister of Justice, and an official delegation from Qatar, in May 1996. The Minister came on behalf of His Highness Sheik Hamad Ben Khalifa Al-Thani, Emir of Qatar who has generously supported the Center by providing funding towards the construction of a second floor for the new Law Center building, a professorship in international law, and international law materials for the library.
