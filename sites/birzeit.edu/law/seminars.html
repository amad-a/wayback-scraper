Birzeit University Law Center

Seminars

Prior to the fall of 1995, we held seminars at the Law Center on the following topics:

- the American legal process (in cooperation with U.S.I.S.);

- the legal and institutional conditions of economic activity (in cooperation with IRIS of the University of Maryland);

- the legal system in the West Bank between 1948 and 1967;

- the Palestinian drafts for the electoral system;

- international humanitarian law (in cooperation with ICRC);

- the system of co-ownership in Palestine.
