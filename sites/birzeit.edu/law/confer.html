Conferences

The Law Center has held several major conferences to date, with the participation of the local and international legal communities.

  • In November of 1994, we held our first conference on Public Administration (in cooperation with the Paris Bar Association).
  • In April of 1995 in Gaza, the Center hosted a two-day conference entitled "Which Legal System for Palestine?" with presenters from Belgium, England, France, Germany, Jordan, West Bank and Gaza Strip. The conference was well attended and was aimed at provoking debate about the future of the law in Palestine as well as getting together the legal communities in the West Bank and Gaza. A transcript of the conference is now available (Publications),
  • In June of 1995, the Center hosted another two-day conference on the Draft Labour Code for Palestine, with the cooperation of the Union of Palestine Labour, which was attended be representatives from Palestinian labour unions, employers, and labour law experts from Jordan, CFDT (France), International Labour Organization (Geneva), Egypt, the West Bank and Gaza strip. A transcript of this conference will be available in the near future.
  • In June of 1996, the Law Center held a two day conference on International Contracts and Investment Laws with the participation of experts from Egypt, Tunis, Jordan, France and Palestine.
