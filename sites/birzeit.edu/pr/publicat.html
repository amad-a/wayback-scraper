Arabic & English Publications


Newsletters in English and Arabic

For many years, the university has published two main newsletters, one in English and one in Arabic. Both have covered many aspects of university life and news, while also providing insights into the wider Palestinian society the university serves. The Arabic newsletter is published every two months. Today, the English newsletter is only published on the Web, in the Birzeit News section. Previous copies are available in the Web newletter archive. The Arabic Web version is due soon.

Other Information

Other publications include the PAS programme prospectus detailing courses available for internationals, Brief Information and other brochures. Copies of all publications are available from the Public Relations Office. Subscriptions to the Arabic newsletter is available on request, donations to cover postage and printing costs are welcomed. Organisations interested in an exchange of publications are invited to contact us. Regular press releases are published in English and Arabic. A press release archive is currently available in English. The Arabic Web archive is due soon.
