The Visitors Section

During the 1996/97 academic year, the Public Relations Office received and briefed around 6,000 visitors. This figure included around 4,000 students from local schools, approximately 200 international students studying or participating in university courses or workcamps, and guests from around the world. A number of the groups required special lectures tailored to their particular interests. Several tours were organised by the Office to places as diverse as Nablus, Hebron, the Golan Heights, and Jerusalem, briefing political leaders, diplomatic representatives, international students, journalists and others. The Office also welcomed many Palestinians returning to visit from the Diaspora, including several PLO ambassadors and others.
