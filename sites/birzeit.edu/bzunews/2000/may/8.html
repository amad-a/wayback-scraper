AL QAMARIAH - BIRZEIT UNIVERSITY'S FIRST ART GALLERY

Birzeit, 8 May , 2000
Birzeit University wishes to announce the opening of its first Fine Arts Gallery Al- Qumariah . The gallery, designed to be a permanent exhibition space, was inaugurated on the 20th of April in the Diana Tamari Sabbagh Center.

Al- Qumariah contains an outstanding group of art works which were donated to the university by renowned Palestinian and Arab artists. These include the works of Sari Khoury, Kamal Boullata, Marwan Qassab Bashi, Samia Halaby, Rashid Qurayshi, Vladimir Tamari, Nasser Soumi, Mona Saoudi, Etil Adnan, Vera Tamari and Mona Bassili Sehnaoui.

Al- Qumariah is situated near the Palestine and Arabic Studies Programme, on the way to the main cafeteria and opens daily from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
