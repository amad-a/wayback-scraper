MESSAGE OF THANKS FROM BIRZEIT UNIVERSITY STUDENT MOVEMENT

Birzeit, 6 March , 2000
The following message was released by the student movement at Birzeit University this morning:

"The Birzeit University student movement sends its deepest thanks to all human rights organizations, media, institutions and individuals who extended their support and solidarity over the last week. This solidarity on the local and international level was critical in winning the release of our colleagues arrested by the Palestinian security forces. Our thanks go to all those who supported human rights and struggled against the violation of our university campus. You helped us guarantee our right to protest, hold sit-ins and participate in changing our society for the better - aims which were achieved through united, joint struggle with the rest of the institutions in our society. "

Student Movement - Birzeit University
