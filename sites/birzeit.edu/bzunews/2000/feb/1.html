NEW ACADEMIC SEMESTER BEGINS AT BIRZEIT UNIVERSITY.

Birzeit, 1 February , 2000
Birzeit University began the new academic semester with 112 new students entering undergraduate programs. Among the new students, 42 students joined the Faculty of Arts, 21 students in the Faculty of Commerce and Economics, 31 students in the Faculty of Science and 18 students in the Faculty of Engineering. Twenty-nine international students entered the Palestine and Arabic Studies Program Arab and there were 60 new students in Masters Degree Programs, bringing the total number of students in the university to 4800 students.

Students at the University for the new semester

Procedures to add and drop courses began and is expected to end by the ninth of this month. The Registration Department emphasized the importance of all students altering their schedules within the allocated time.
