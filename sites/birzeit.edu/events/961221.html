Piano Concert by Salim Abboud

Salim Abboud is a young and talented musician from Nazareth. After distinguishing himself as a young musician he was offered a scholarship to complete his studies at the Royal Academy for Music in London, where he is currently studying.

Mr. Abboud will be performing pieces by Brahms and Chopin.

The Concert will be held at the Friends Boys School in Ramallah on Saturday, the 21st of December 1996. Tickets are 25 NIS for the general public and 10 NIS for students. Tickets are available at the following locations:

  • The National Conservatory of Music
  • Sakkakini Center
  • Zabanneh Stores
  • Kan Batazaman
  • At the entrance of the hall

For further information, please contact the National Conservatory of Music at (02) 995-6229
