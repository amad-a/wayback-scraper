    Bat Shalom is a feminist center for peace and social justice. Our aim is to work toward a democratic and pluralistic society in Israel, where women will be of more influence. By bringing together women peace activists, educators, and community leaders, we strive to raise one another's consciousness and to create together a culture of peace and social justice in Israel. Bat Shalom and its Palestinian counterpart, The Jerusalem Center for Women, were founded together in 1994 and jointly form the Jerusalem Link -- Israeli and Palestinian women working for peace.
