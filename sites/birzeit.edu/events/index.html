Events in Palestine

This section of the Birzeit University website aims to serve as a focus for announcements and reports of cultural and other significant events, and selected news events in Palestine, with a bias towards those with websites. Please also check the events page of the Khalil Sakakini Cultural Centre in Ramallah for details of other events. Organisations are invited to submit coming events here.
