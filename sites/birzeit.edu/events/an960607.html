Rosa Juanco - Marti

"Two Years After" (an exhibition of oil paintings)

Date: Friday 7 to 29 June 1996
Place: Anadial Gallery


The opening of the exhibition takes place on Friday 7 June 1996 at 5.00 pm. The Anadiel Gallery is located by the New Gate of the Old City, Jerusalem, and is daily open from 5.00 pm until 8.00 pm, except Sundays.
