                   The Computer Science department at Birzeit University is one of the main

                  departments in the university.

                    The department offers B.Sc. degree in computer science, the department also
                   provides service courses for students from other departments and faculties to
                   enhance the computer skills of the university graduates.

                    As part of the overall objective of the university, the department tries to bring its
                   activities closer to the local community via making links with the computer industry to
                   keep pace with advances in the technology. Such activities include arranging
                   seminars, training courses and open Discussion mixing people from and outside the
                   university.
                   

                   The department works in collaboration with the computer center to Improve
                   computing services in the university. With 3 labs, and a fourth under construction the
                   department hopes to be on the edge of Technology.
                   

                    We are continuously evaluating teaching plans, improving syllabuses, Bolstering the
                   research base and bringing in more expertise via attracting Skilled staff. The
                   department also has future plans to offer higher degree in the computer science.
