Requirements for the admission

Requirements for the admission to the Major & Major-Minor in Computer Science:

In order to be considered for acceptance in the program, the student should have:-
             

1- A grade of 70 or more in both Comp132 and 234.

2- An average of 70% or more in Math 131, 132.

3- Department approval.
