People's Rights Magazine

People's Rights is a monthly magazine published by LAW. The magazine was initiated in March 1997 and is published in two versions- Arabic and English.

The magazine covers a wide range of topics and is unique in that it is the only magazine published in the Palestinian territories which not only documents the ongoing activities of the israeli Occupation forces in the West Bank and Gaza but also highlights human rights abuses by the Palestinian National Authority.
