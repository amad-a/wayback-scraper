Goals

The goals of the conference initiative are as follows:

  • Address the current distortion of historical fact concerning the last 50 years, with a focus on human rights abuses
  • Create a consciousness and understanding of these issues amongst the world community
  • Develop strategies for addressing those past human rights abuses and providing just restitution for Palestinian victims in the framework of establishing a lasting peace
  • Develop networks and increased communication between people concerned with human rights abuses committed against the Palestinian people.
  • Establish a basis for further negotiation which considers all sectors of Palestinian society in the context of a continuous pattern of human rights violations.
  • Highlight the promises of the UN Declaration of Human Rights and the failure of the Israeli government to fulfill even the minimum standards of this Declaration
