People's Rights Magazine

People's Rights is a monthly magazine published by LAW. The magazine was initiated in March 1997 and is published in two versions- Arabic and English.

The magazine covers a wide range of topics and is unique in that it is the only magazine published in the Palestinian territories which highlights human rights abuses by the Palestinian National Authority.

The magazine also documents the ongoing activities of the Israeli government in areas A, B and C.

The first 6 months of the magazine has seen interviews with Dr Iyad Sarraj, Haidar Abd Ashafi and the newly elected Palestinian Attorney General Faiz Abu Rahma.

We have conducted detailed investigations into the situation in Hebron, practices of monopolies controlled by the PNA and problems confronting the Bethlehem Mental Hospital.
