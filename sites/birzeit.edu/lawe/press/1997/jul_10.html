ISRAELI MILITARY AUTHORITIES TO DEMOLISH THREE HOUSES IN NABLUS

10 July 1997

Three homes in the village of Bani Fadel near Nablus have received house demolition orders. The houses have been standing for more than a year and over 20 people reside in the homes. Yesterday, LAW received an Israeli High Court order temporarily halting the house demolition until the Court holds a hearing concerning the case. As with nearly every case heard in front of the Israeli High Court, it is likely that at the hearing date, the petition will be rejected and the houses demolished.

In another case concerning five house demolition orders in Hebron, the Israeli government agreed to cancel the demolition orders after LAW's attorneys showed that the Hebron homeowners had built with permits (these permits had been issued by the Palestinian Hebron municipality, but the Israeli military authorities tried to invalidate these permits when they unilaterally “re-zoned” the Hebron municipal limits).

LAW calls on the Israeli government to stop its policy of demolishing Palestinian homes. LAW proposes that as a confidence building measure to restart the negotiations, the Israeli government announce a freeze on all house demolitions in the West Bank and Jerusalem.
