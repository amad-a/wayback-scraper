LAND AND WATER ESTABLISHMENT AGREES TO TAKE CONFISCATION CASES TO COURT

In response to requests for help from landowners in Bet Safafa and Bet Hanina, Land and Water Establishment has taken these cases and will pursue them to the Supreme Court, if necessary.
