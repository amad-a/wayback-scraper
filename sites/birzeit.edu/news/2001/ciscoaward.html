Birzeit University Receives Cisco Award

6 October 2001

Birzeit University received a Cisco Networking Academy Program Award on Monday, October 1, in Copenhagen, Denmark. Birzeit University, a Cisco Regional Academy since September 2000, received the Against All Odds award, one of five awards for the 2,300 Cisco Academies in Europe, Africa and the Middle East. See complete story

Marwan Tarazi, Director of the Birzeit University Information Technology Unit, who received the award said: “the success of this award was a result of the team work and effort of everyone that was involved. I believe that this has been one of the most successful collaborative efforts that I have experienced during my work in Palestine. I really hope that we will be able to jointly pull off more of these successes in the future.”

The Cisco Networking Academy Program is a partnership between Cisco Systems, education, business, government, and community organizations around the world. The Networking Academy curriculum centers on teaching students to design, build, and maintain computer networks.

Last August, Birzeit University, sponsored by the Welfare Association, finished the Cisco Networking Training Program for 10 local academies (CCNA Track) in the West Bank and Gaza Strip, and was able to launch e-learning using Cisco's web-based training material, with video conferencing to connect Birzeit University with the students in the Gaza Strip.
