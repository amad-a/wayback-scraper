Selected scenes from the latest bulldozed road between Ramallah and Birzeit
March 10, 2001

After the Israeli Occupation Forces destroyed the Ramallah - Birzeit main (and only) road, travel in both directions became a nightmare.


The trip to Ramallah - from one trench to another. The last passenger is barely visible in the trench.


Carefully crossing over the filled trenches to catch a car to Ramallah


Ramallah - Birzeit travel instructions. Trench 1 is on the Ramallah side. People have to walk from one trench to the other, then take a car. This could be tedious for children and the elderly and almost impossible for physically handicapped people and medical emergencies.


Carry thy luggage and walk from trench 1 to trench 2 and vice versa

All photos by Yasser Darwish, BZU photographer
