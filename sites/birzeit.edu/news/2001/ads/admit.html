Applications for the 2001/2002 Academic Year

Posted: July 7, 2001

The Registrar’s Office at Birzeit University is happy to announce that applications for the 2001/2002 Academic Year are now being accepted at the University for those bearing the following certificates:
1. The General Secondary Certificate (Tawjihi)
2. The equivalent of the above Certificate such as the GCE, the SATII, the IB or the BIGRUT

Students can pick up the application forms at the Al-Maktoum Building on the University Campus for a fee of 70 NIS from Tuesday, June 26 until Tuesday, July 24 from 9.00 a.m. until 3.00 p.m. except Fridays and Sundays.

The application forms should be accompanied with:
1. Two personal Photographs
2. A copy of the Birth Certificate
3. A copy of the Identity Card
4. School Transcripts
5. A copy of the Identification Number used during the Tawjihi Exams
6. A certified copy of the Tawjihi transcripts

In the Gaza Strip, students can pick up the forms for a fee of 70 NIS at:
