Classes Resuming November 8th

November 7, 2001

The Israeli occupation forces pulled out their tanks early this morning from Ramallah, and re-stationed their troops on the periphery of the city. The curfew imposed on the road to Birzeit has been lifted, but there still is a checkpoint on Surda and only crossing by foot is allowed.

Classes at Birzeit University will resume tomorrow, Thursday, November 8, and all staff and faculty are requested to be present at the University today, Wednesday, November 7. The University will make its first compensation program (for the 18 lost days) on Sunday the 11th.


For further information about Birzeit University, you can contact us at webinfo@birzeit.edu or visit the University website at www.birzeit.edu
