Graduate Institute of International Studies - International Conference:
The New Palestine, The New Europe: Emerging Models in Society & Culture
February 24th-25th, 2001

You are cordially invited to attend the international conference this coming Saturday and Sunday, February 24th-25th, 2001, on "The New Palestine, The New Europe: Emerging Models in Society and
Culture".
It will be addressed by a variety of prominent researchers from Palestine, as well as France, Italy, Austria and England. All sessions are to be held in the Law Building, Room 317. Simultaneous interpretation will be provided throughout.
