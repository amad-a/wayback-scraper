Stage was written after I started playing gigs regularly at university. It was a strange experience seeing how a bunch of people that you've never met before react to songs you've written. That's to say nothing about the reaction of the people you know!

One of the few recurring dreams I had went like this: I was going off to war, and got to the battlefield to find people I knew on the perimeter. "Where do you think are you going?" asks one, his face full of fear. "Who do you think you are?" sneers someone else. The remainder just looked away and ignored me. I turned to them and said, "What else are we here for? Who comes to a battle and isn't prepared to fight?", grabbed a weapon and set off.

The dream wasn't specifically about music, but the experience of making the decision to play in a public certainly brought to mind the realities that live inside this dream: Fear. Mockery. Indifference.
