Work Experience
1994-present day Birzeit University Public Relations Officer/Webmaster

Working in the West Bank Palestinian institution Birzeit University, writing English-language newsletter, other brief publications and press releases; developing an e-mail system and other computer systems for the P.R. office; developing a network of international contacts; constructing and maintaining university website, the largest Palestinian website; training students and faculty in authoring for the Web; assisting with human rights publicity and campaigning, special events and conferences at BZU; organising study tour itineraries and logistics; and briefing delegations and guests about BZU with the Public Relations Office team.

1993-1994 Friends of Birzeit University Coordinator

Day-to-day running of this London-based charity set up to ‘support education at Bir Zeit’; writing and desktop publishing newsletter, press releases and other publications; organising and leading trips to the Occupied Palestinian West Bank and Gaza Strip; orientation of international students visiting the University; lobbying and campaigning; database work and fundraising.

1993-1994 Living Stones Coordinator

Day-to-day administration of this British organisation that promotes links between the Christian communities of the Middle East and Christians in Britain; writing and desktop publishing magazine, and fundraising.

1992-1993 Jubilee Campaign Parliamentary Unit

Worked on a variety of projects for this London-based human rights organisation, including computerising the case files of prisoners in detention and producing a 133-page report on the sexual abuse of street children in the Philippines; writing press releases, lobbying, and database work.

1991-1992 Revival Christian Church Co-worker

Worked in a number of capacities for this Hong Kong-based church, including full-time work with Vietnamese boat people in a number of detentioncentres, offering free financial services, purchasing goods for detainees, distributing clothes, and holding services for Christian detainees.

1991 St. Stephen’s Society International helper

Worked for a month in this Christian community based in Hong Kong, helping ex-Triad (Chinese Mafia) heroin addicts in a first-stage rehabilitation house.

1989-1990 Southlands College Students’ Union Student Union President

Served for a sabbatical year in this elected post after graduating in this college of about 700 full-time students, one of four in the Roehampton Institute. The student union provides educational, social, sporting, welfare and financial facilities; represents students on college and Institute committees, and local and national bodies; and provides an official channel of communication between the student body and college authorities.

1988-1989 Southlands College Students’ Union External Affairs Officer

Served for a year during my degree course in this elected post that exists to maintain links with campaigning and charitable bodies that the Student Union is affiliated to, publicising these links, the work of the bodies, and campaigns run by the bodies to the Student Union Executive and Student Union.

1988 OXFAM In Scotland Researcher

Worked full-time voluntarily for two months in the Scottish headquarters of this registered charity that both campaigns for a change in debt, aid, trade, agriculture and arms policies between UK and the rest of the world, and raises money for direct-aid projects in developing countries world-wide. Research was into "Edinburgh’s links with the Third World", uncovering local links with the developing world to be used in campaign materials.
