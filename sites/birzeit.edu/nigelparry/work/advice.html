now that you understand the issues a little better, why not look at the available website design and hosting options for your organisation?
there are very few good palestinian websites - the majority of bad ones are the result of organisations trying to launch a website cheaply.
the three most common reasons websites are bad are: spelling and grammer mistakes, difficulty in finding the information you want, and pages that are slow to load - a bad website will negatively affect your business or the public profile of your organisation.
organisations should begin, as a standard procedure, to budget for websites in the same way as they budget for advertising, brochures and other publications.
for a more detailed look at the state of the web in palestine and challenges for organisations, see the following paper the past and future of information technology in palestine, presented at the united nations in geneva, in april 1997.
