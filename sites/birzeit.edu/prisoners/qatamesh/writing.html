    "forever human"
    - an anthological collection of Ahmad Qatamesh’s writings in prison.
    The following are selected excerpts from the three published books of Ahmad Qatamesh, covering a wide spectrum of philosophical, social, political and theoretical issues and concepts:
  • Conversations with his Israeli interrogators - on occupation and its repercussions on Israeli society, on dreaming and the Jewish question, and on the peace process.
  • On his experiences during torture
  • On oppression
  • On ethics, religion and political Islamic movements
  • On education (upbringing, and difference and tolerence)
  • On sexual love and women

    writing for children:

  • Excerpts from Ahmad Qutamesh's prison letters to his daughter Haneen
  • "The Bewitched Cave", a children's story by Ahmad Qutamesh
