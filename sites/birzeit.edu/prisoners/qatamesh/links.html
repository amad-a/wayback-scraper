    selected articles and reports about administrative detention:

  • B'Tselem's Urgent Action - End Administrative Detention rapid response network. Lots of suggestions on how to take action.
  • Current Birzeit administrative detainees from the Human Rights Action Project, Birzeit University.
  • Jailed without Trial. A Protest Exhibition. Nearly fifty Israeli artists -- Jews and Arabs -- using an exhibit opening on Human Rights Day on Dec 10 to protest against the administrative detention of more than 500 Palestinians held without trial. (10 December 1997).
  • LAW Palestinian Prisoners Page. A website devoted to information about Palestinian prisoners, torture, administrative detention, detailed information about individual prisoners and a solidarity campaign. (October 1997).
  • Prisoners of Peace. A B'Tselem report documenting widespread, political use of administrative detention with no due process (28 May 28 1997).
  • Administative detention: Despair, uncertainty and lack of due process" A report by Amnesty International (30 April 1997).
  • Human rights -- A year of shattered hopes A report by Amnesty International (15 July 1995).
  • Detained Without Trial: Administrative Detention in the Occupied Territories Since the Beginning of the Intifada. A Report by B'Tselem, the Israeli Center for October 1992.
