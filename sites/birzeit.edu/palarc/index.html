The Palestine Archive
An independent archive of all things Palestinian, located on the website of Birzeit University. The responsibility for any of the information in the Palestine Archive is solely that of the organisations and authors. Any organisations or individuals in Palestine, with material on Palestine, are welcome to submit material or a website. For more information on any of these issues, contact: webmaster@birzeit.edu

    ON THIS SERVER

  • A PERSONAL DIARY
    Covering the Israeli-Palestinian Conflict (1989-present), by Nigel Parry. The diary includes photographs and comment, opening up issues such as the closure of the occupied territories and "autonomous areas", as seen from the inside.

  • ARCHITECTURE - An archive of Palestinian Architecture, provided by the Ramallah-based organisation RIWAQ. Includes a "virtual exhibition of Palestinian tiles" and a special report on the Palestinian town of Nablus.

  • SCHOOL EDUCATION - To understand the challenges facing the Palestinian university system, the school system is a good beginning. The material in these pages has been provided by the Ramallah-based Educational Network.
