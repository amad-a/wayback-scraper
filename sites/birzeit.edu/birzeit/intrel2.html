International Relations


Exchange Programmes

The university has academic relations with a number of universities abroad for the purposes of joint research, guest lecturers, and faculty student exchanges. Among these universities are:

  • University of Deakin (Australia)
  • Free University of Brussels (Belgium)
  • Catholic University of Louvain (Belgium)
  • University of Ghent (Belgium)
  • Carleton University (Canada)
  • University of Copenhagen (Denmark)
  • University of Paris VII (France)
  • Montpellier III (France)
  • University of Orsay (France)
  • Aix-En-Provence (France)
  • University of Bremen (Germany)
  • International Centre for Theoretical Physics (Italy)
  • University of Amsterdam (Netherlands)
  • Delft University of Technology (Netherlands)
  • University of Bergen (Norway)
  • University of Oslo (Norway)
  • Umea University (Sweden)
  • University of Durham (United Kingdom)
  • Calvin College (USA)
  • Indiana University (USA)
  • University of Missouri (USA)
  • Villanova University (USA)

In addition, Birzeit University is linked to more than a dozen European Universities through the multilateral Palestinian-European Academic Cooperation in Education (PEACE) programme, and the MedCampus and MedMedia programmes.

Institutions interested in forming links with Birzeit University should contact the Director of External Relations Department at the university address given on our homepage, also available at cquteneh@admin.birzeit.edu
