Listing of Donors
Birzeit University
Yusuf Ahmad Alghanim Library

 

The following is an alpabetical listing of people and institutions who have generously donated to the Birzeit Library in the past.  These names are also recognized on a plaque within the library and on book plates placed in the materials given.  Bizeit would like to thank all of the people who have supported the library throughout the years with their efforts of time, money, and materials.  If you are interested in joining this group of people please contact Mrs. Aida Hadad. Thank you for your support.
