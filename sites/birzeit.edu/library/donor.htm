Donation Information

 

The Birzeit Library has been fortunate to receive many donations from generous people and institutions throughout its history.  These donations have allowed the Library to develop and grow in a manner that would not have been possible without outside support.  As the University continues to expand and add new programs and classes, so must the library expand to help meet the ever increasing demands from the students.  In order to do this effectively, the library is always in need of support.  The Library is moving with the times and adding more electronic resources, but to use these new resources requires computers and other equipment.  In addition, the Library is currently facing space constraints and we can always use more books and journals.  If you can help us in any of these areas please contact us and we will be happy to talk to you.  For more information on previous donors, or a more specific listing of our needs, please see below or contact Mrs. Aida Hadad.
