Library Catalog

This search interface will provide users with access to information on English language books contained in the library's MINISIS database.  Currently no information is available for Arabic materials or on the availability of an item in the library.  You may search for materials by author, title, subject, or a combination of these terms using the Boolean operators. 

The search engine is under construction and currently shows users the twenty oldest books in the catalog.  In addition to searching for books in the main library including Law Institute library, users have the option to search for books located in the libraries of Palestine Economic Policy Research Institute (MAS) located in Ramallah, and Institute of Community Health (One of the Branch Libraries at BZU), or to search for material in all three of the libraries.
