What's New?

This section of the Birzeit University website is maintained by the Birzeit Webmaster, webmaster@birzeit.edu to help regular users of the website keep track of what is new since their last visit. When this section gets a little too big, it becomes part of the What Was New Archive. Current and archived entries are listed with most recent additions at the top of the page. Check Birzeit News regularly for news from the university community not usually listed here! For university events, check the announcements page.

  • 1 July 2000 Eight hundred and ninety-seven students graduated on Wednesday and Thursday from Birzeit University marking 25 years since the first graduation ceremony. Friday saw an Open Day for University Alumni.


  • 24 June 2000 Andre Brink, the famed South African writer and Professor of English at the University of Cape Town, gave a lecture last week at Birzeit University under the title "We are waiting for the miracle". Brink has been twice been shortlisted for the Booker Prize and has been nominated several times for the Nobel Prize for Literature

  • 3 January 1998 - Welcome back and Happy New Year! www.birzeit.edu was under the weather for a couple of days, but recovered to full health this morning. The new, improved Complete Guide to Palestine's Websites is relaunched today, with new categories, new reviews, around 30 new websites and some useful new features including "Palestine's Websites at a Glance" (all on one page), "Key Peace Process Links", and a list of local Palestinian mailing lists from which you can receive information about events in the country. Hope you like the new design, which will get a few minor tweaks in the next two weeks. In the rush to get it out quickly, there are several more websites that still need to be listed, a task which will be completed by mid-January, at which point the results of the 1998 Golden Olive Awards will be announced along with January's site of the month. The good news is that Palnet, the leading Palestinian ISP, and us at Birzeit will be teaming up to offer a new Palestinian website search service. We will continue to provide an index and searchable reviews of Palestinian websites (a Yahoo-type service), while Palnet will offer a searchable index of the text all of Palestine's websites (an AltaVista-type service). We are both excited about this and plan to make the interface of both options available in both resources. In addition, Birzeit will become the clearing house of all local Palestinian URLs, and will pass them on to Palnet for indexing. This makes it very important to alert us of new URLs of sites based in Palestine. The Complete Guide will also now offer links to recommended external websites related to Palestine. Please note that if you are based in Palestine you are guaranteed of a listing. If you are outside Palestine, however, we will only list the best resources. There is enough bad content on the Internet without further wasting people's time by attempting to provide a complete listing. Send us sites we overlooked, let us know your feedback, and consider advertising on the Complete Guide. Every banner advert that runs for a year buys Birzeit a new computer to expand our work on the Web.
