 Centers & Institutes

The dynamic purpose and mission of each of our centers in expanding scientific and social frontiers has been exceptional considering the daily realities of life in this region.

Birzeit University is an integral part of the Palestinian community and sees itself as instrumental in the maintenance and development of the society through the nurturing of one of our most valuable natural resources, human beings. Birzeit continuously aims to ensure that its strong ties with the community are maintained and evolve through a range of activities that aim to assist in the advancement of our students and the community.

The research centers and community-oriented programs at Birzeit University were themselves established in response to specific needs within the community and mainly work with and serve the community. They provide training, workshops, professional skills, post secondary courses and Masters Programs. They facilitate community discussions on pertinent topics in their specified fields and they also conduct research, which is instrumental in the overall development of Palestinian society.
