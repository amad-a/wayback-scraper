MOHAMED MOHAMED ZIARA

Assistant Professor in Civil Engineering

Ph.D : Structural Engineering, 1993, Department of Civil and Offshire Enginnering Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh, U.K. The influence of confining the compression zone in the design of structural concrete beams

M.Sc. : Structure-Geotechnic, 1982, Department of Civil Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, U.S.A. Comparison between elastic and plastic design for multi-story steel frames using reliability approach.

B.Sc. : Civil Engineering, 1980, Department of Civil Engineering, Alexandria University, Egypt.

Previous Professional and Academic Experience

Research Assistant, (1991 - 1993), Department of Civil and Offshore Engineering at Heriot-Watt University, U.K.

Consultant (1980-present): Design and Structural Evaluation and Analysis for many projects.

Director General of the Ministry of Housing in the Palestinian National Authority.

Field of Interest

Utilizing triaxial state of stress in the design of structural concrete elements.

Development of flexure-shear interaction design model for prevention of diagonal failures in beams.

Development of design approach for over-reinforced beams utilizing ductility due to confinement of concrete.

Upgrading and strengthening of existing structures.

Punching shear failure in reinforced concrete slabs.

Development of issues related to the housing sector including housing strategy, and construction industry in Palestine.
