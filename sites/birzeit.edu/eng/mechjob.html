Mechanical Engineering Department
 
 
 

Faculty Position

The department of Mechanical Engineering at Birzeit University invites applications for faculty position starting spring semester 1998.  Applicants should have a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering. Preferred applicants should have their research or teaching experience in the areas of materials, production and computer aided design.

A complete application shall consist of a cover letter, curriculum vitae, list of publications, summary of teaching experience, two reference letters, a copy of graduate and undergraduate transcripts.

Applications will be reviewed beginning Dec. 15, 1998.
