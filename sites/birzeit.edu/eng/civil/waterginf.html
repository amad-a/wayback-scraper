Introduction and Background

The provision of a good quality domestic and industrial water supply and the protection of existent water resources from pollution are matters of great concern in Palestine. The country is increasingly suffering from water shortages and environmental pollution suggesting that groundwater sources may be facing both over-exploitation and deterioration in quality.

One obstacle facing the ability of Palestinian society to address these crucial issues is the limited number of local engineers who are qualified in the fields of water and the environment. Thus, there is an urgent need for developing local skills in the design, planning and implementation of water and environment policies and projects, as well as in the current abilities to upgrade existing facilities.

Birzeit University and the International Institute of Infrastructure, Hydraulic and Environmental Engineering (IHE) are cooperating in a long-term project on Water Sector Capacity Building in Palestine funded by the Netherlands Government. The long-term objective of the cooperation project is to build integrated research and education capacity in the fields of water and environmental engineering.

The project will help meet local needs for trained professional staff while developing academic capacity the fields of water and environment. It will also strengthen existing research capacity and practical know-how in the areas of water and environment at Birzeit University. It is in the framework of this cooperation project, that Birzeit University offers a Masters of Science Program in Water Engineering.

Aims of the Masters Program

The Masters Program aims to train engineers working in governmental, municipal, non-governmental and private organizations, to deal with the complex problems of environmental pollution and degradation. It will provide professional training and qualifications in sanitary engineering and hydrology, with an emphasis on integrating theoretical concepts with engineering practice and the ability to translate them into master planning processes and frameworks. Considerable attention is given to issues of infrastructural sustainability, cost recovery, institutional arrangements and sound operation and maintenance practices.

The Masters Program is aimed at engineers who have successfully completed their B.Sc. degree in civil, mechanical or chemical engineering. The Program is also targeted at professionals with an engineering background who have practical work experience. A part-time Program is offered to enable participants to combine work and study.

Structure and Organization of the Program

The Program offers a total of fifteen taught courses divided over three specializations: water supply, wastewater engineering and groundwater hydrology. Each taught course is equivalent to three credit hours. Courses comprise lectures and workshops and may involve design, computer and laboratory exercises. The Program will also include visits to relevant institutions, organizations and field sites.

To fulfill the requirements, students must successfully complete ten taught courses and a research thesis of six credit hours. The full-time Program lasts one and a half years. Completion of the part-time Program takes between two and three years, depending on individual circumstances.

Admission Requirements

The Program accepts applicants with B.Sc. degrees in civil, chemical and mechanical engineering. Applications are evaluated on an individual basis. When deemed necessary, the applicant may have to complete prerequisite courses, scheduled before or during the Masters Program. Engineers working at municipalities, non-governmental organizations or at other community service institutions are strongly encouraged to join the Program.

Language of Instruction

Instruction and examinations are held in English. It is therefore essential that applicants have a good working knowledge of the English language. Applicants will have to demonstrate acceptable language proficiency before being admitted to the course.

Tuition Fee

The tuition fee is JD 85 per credit hour, but may be subject to change. The tuition fee does not cover the costs of books, technical papers and other educational materials.
