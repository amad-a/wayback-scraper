Current Position:

Assistant Professor of Civil Engineering, Birzeit University
Teaching civil engineering courses in the fields of statistics and probability, building materials, design of concrete and steel structures, and geotechnical engineering.
Conducting analytical and experimental research works for the development of design approaches to strengthening of existing structures and lowering the housing costs. Part of this research is conducted jointly with Heriot-Watt and Maryland Universities in UK and USA, respectively.
