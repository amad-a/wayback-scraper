DEPARTMENT OF CIVIL

The Department of Civil Engineering offers a program of study leading to the Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering.

Students are admitted into the department after meeting the following requirements:

1. Completion of the following faculty requirements with a cumulative average of not less than 70%: Mathematics 141, 142; Physics 111, 112, 131, 132; Architecture 121, 122; and Mechanical Engineering 111, 112.

2. Consent of the department.

Reauirements for the major

1. Completion of the introductory courses: Civil Engineering 233, Electrical Engineering 231, and Mechanical Engineering 232.

2. Completion of the following set of courses with a cumulative average of not less than 70%: Mathematics 241, 246, 332, 436; Mechanical Engineering 333, 335; Electrical Engineering 211, 321; Civil Engineering 226, 231, 233, 330, 331, 332, 333, 334, 335, 339, 401, 431, 432, 433, 434, 435, 436, 437, 438, 439, 520, 530, 533, 535, 537.
