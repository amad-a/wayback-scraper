Faculty of Engineering


 
  The Faculty of Engineering at Birzeit University was established in 1979 with the development of a  five years program leading to a Bachelor's Degree in Engineering in the fields of Civil, Electrical, Mechanical Engineering. A fourth department, The Architecture department joined the Faculty of Engineering in 1990.

The five years program (170 credit hours) is comprehensive and compact. Its effectiveness
was proven through the out standing record of the graduates in their respective professional
fields as well as in the graduate school of highly recognized universities.
