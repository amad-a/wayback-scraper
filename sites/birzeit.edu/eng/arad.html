Birzeit University - Faculty of Engineering

Department of Architectural Engineering


 
 

The Department of Architectural Engineering offers a program of study leading to the Bachelors degree in Architectural Engineering.

Admission Requirements:

Students are admitted into the Department after fulfilling the following requirements:

  1. Completion of the following faculty requirements with a cumulative average of not less than 70% : Mathematics 131,132, physics 131, 132 and Mechanical Engineering 121.
  2. Successfully passing the Audio-visual Capabilities Exam.
  3. Consent of the Department.
