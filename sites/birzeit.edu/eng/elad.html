ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT

The department offers a program which leads to the degree of Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering. Students who Have finished their first may be admitted to the department upon satisfying the following conditions:

CONDITIONS FOR ADMISSION TO THE DEPARTMENT:

in order to be admitted to the Electrical Engineering Department, student must:

1- Pass the following courses with an overall grade point average of 70% or more: ME 121, ME 122, ME 111, ME 112, Math 141, Math 142, Physics 111, Physics 112. Physics 131, Physics 132, ( Unless the faculty council decides otherwise ).

2- The consent of the department, where students are taken in accordance with the needs of the department and vacancies, and will depend basically on the overall grade point average in the above mentioned courses.

REQUIREMENTS FOR SPECIALIZATION:

The bachelor degree is conferred upon satisfactory completion of the all university and faculty requirements as well as the following departmental requirements:

Electrical Engineering Courses:

EE 212, EE 232, EE 234, EE 311, EE 314, EE 316, EE 332, EE 333, EE 334, EE 335, EE 337, EE 338, EE 401, EE 411, EE 412, EE 413, EE 431, EE 432, EE 433, EE 434, EE 435, EE 436, EE 437, EE 438, EE 501, EE 531; EE 551.

Mechanical Engineering Courses:

ME 222, ME 223.

In addition to one of the following two groups:

GROUP A - Light current :

EE 532, EE 535, CE 539 1, and two elective courses a total of six credit hours to. be chosen from: ( IC 531, LC 532, LC 533, LC 535, LC 537. )

GROUP B - HEAVY CURRENT:

EE 533, EE 534, EE 537, and two elective courses of a total of six credit hours to be chosen from. ( HC 536, HC 537, HC 538, HC 539. )

NOTE: NAME OF SPECIALIZATION ( HEAVY OR LIGHT CURRENT ) DOES NOT appear on BACHELOR CERTIFICATE.
