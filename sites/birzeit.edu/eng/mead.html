Mechanical Engineering Department



 
 

Specialization requirements


The Department of Mechanical Engineering offers a program of study leading to the Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering.
 

Admission Requirements

Students are admitted into the department after fulfilling the following requirements:

1. Completion of the following faculty requirements with a cumulative average of not less than 70% : Mathematics 131,132, physics 131, 132, and Mechanical Engineering 121.

2. Consent  of  the  department.
 

Requirements for the Major

Completion of the following sets of course with accumulative  average not less than 70%:

I. Twelve credit hours from Mathematics Department:
 

  • 231 Calculus & Analytic Geometry.
  • 234 Introduction to Linear Algebra.
  • 331 Ordinary Differential Equation.
  • 330 Methods in Numerical Analysis.


II. Three credit hours from Civil Engineering Department
 

  • 233  Mechanics of Material.


III. Fourteen credit hours from  Electrical  Engineering Department
 

  • 211 Basic Electrical Engineering Lab.
  • 231 Introduction to Electrical Engineering.
  • 331 Probability and Engineering Statistics.
  • 410 General Electrical  Machines Laboratory.
  • 430 General Electrical Machines.


IV. Three credit hours from the faculty of Commerce and Economics
 

  • 337  Engineering  Economics.

  • 336   Fluid Mechanics -II-

VI. Thirteen concentration credit hours from the Mechanical Engineering Department
 

  • Completion of ten obligatory credit hours from either Power or Design and  Production concentration courses.
  • Completion of three elective credit hours from the elective courses or from the other concentration courses.

A. Obligatory courses in the power concentration:

B. Obligatory Courses in the Design and Production Concentration:

- Power Courses:
