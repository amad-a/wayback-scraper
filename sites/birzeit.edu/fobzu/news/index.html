Friends of Birzeit University (UK)

Information dissemination

Friends of Birzeit University (FoBZU) regularly produces newsletters based on the Birzeit University Newsletter and Press Releases, as well as original material.

FoBZU sends the newsletter free to over 100 British Members of Parliament, many academic and research centres and institutions, as well as to its members.

You can subscribe to the FoBZU newsletter (ISSN 1353-7083) by contacting FoBZU at the address below:
