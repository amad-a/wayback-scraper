Friends of Birzeit University: Campaigning

Friends of Birzeit University (UK)

Campaigning

FoBZU's campaigning activities have included:

  • Lobbying of Political Representatives - FoBZU helped to organise a lobby of Parliament on the issue of academic freedom when the Palestinian universities were closed by Israeli military order. FoBZU regularly encourages its members to write expressing concern, to British and Israeli government representatives. FoBZU sends a complimentary copy of its newsletter to over 100 British MPs.
  • Work with trade and students unions - In November 1995, a delegation of three educational trades unionists and two members of the British Parliament visited Palestine with FoBZU. They visited Palestinian Ministers, human rights organisations , and academics in Gaza, Ramallah, and Jerusalem.
  • Producing reports - In 1991, FoBZU published a detailed account of the experiences of a December 1990 study tour. The previous year, FoBZU jointly produced a report of another study tour with the World University Service.
  • Public meetings - In June 1993, FoBZU held a meeting with Albert Aghazarian, Director of Public Relations at Birzeit, who outlined the problems faced by the University after the March 1993 sealing of the occupied territories by Israel. A pub lic meeting was held by the FoBZU VIP delegation after their return to UK in November 1995.
