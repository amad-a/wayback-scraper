Friends of Birzeit University: Fundraising

Friends of Birzeit University (UK)

Fundraising

FoBZU is a registered British charity, no. 279026, relying on voluntary donations to fulfil its work to "support education at Birzeit". As well as its information, cultural exchange and campaigning roles, FoBZU has several appeals which need your suppo rt:

  • Adela Every Women's Scholarship Programme - FoBZU is paying for the tuition fees for two female students in exceptionally difficult family circumstances, for the duration of their courses at the University; the result of a bequest by the late Adela Ev ery, a teacher at Birzeit in the 1930s, when it was a school. The bequest is in a deposit account, the interest of which is used to pay the tuition. FoBZU would like to extend the amount in order to increase the reach of the scholarship to cover living ex pences of the two students and eventually, further scholarships.
  • Various library appeals - The Law Centre and main Birzeit Library both need texts and endowments to purchase books. Contact FoBZU for more details:
