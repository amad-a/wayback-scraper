Articles and comments about Birzeit's Website


The Birzeit University Website has been the subject of several newspaper reports. In this section of Birzeit University and the World Wide Web, these articles will be reproduced.

Contents:

  • "The Web is Mightier than the Sword", Martin Jerrett, The Jerusalem Times, 21 June 1996. Not bad, although Martin needs to bone up a little on his Internet jargon.
  • "SURFING THE NET: Get your kicks on the information superhighway", Allison Kaplan Sommer, The Jerusalem Post Magazine, 21 June 1996. We upset Allison's sensibilities a little I think.
  • "Internet: A Good Place to Find Yourself - Palestinians start out strongly on the Internet", David Gordon, Ma'ariv, 14 August 1996. In Hebrew, a first on the Birzeit website! A good translation would be welcomed. Apparently, it's quite a complementary article.
  • "New technology takes Intifada on to the Internet", Robert Fox, Daily Telegraph, 1 October 1996. A little sensationalist but the title made us laugh.

If anyone finds an article not included in this page, in any language, please send it over to us.
