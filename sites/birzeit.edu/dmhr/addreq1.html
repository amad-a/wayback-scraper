Enrollment

 

Admission Requirements

 

To be admitted in The MA Program in Democracy and Human Rights at Birzeit University, a student must hold  a first university degree (BA or equivalent) from an accredited university in a Social Science discipline, or  in the field of Law. An Admission Committee determines the acceptability of applications from other disciplines, as well as  the need, if any, for remedial course work.  Remedial course work does not count towards satisfying graduation requirements.  In cases where the Admissions Committee deems necessary, an interview with candidates for admission may be held.

Students whose native language is Arabic are expected to  pass a competency test in English, or in some other Foreign Language approved by the Program Council, before completion of 15  credit hours of study. Students must also pass a competency test in the area of Information Technology, before completion of 15 credit hours of study.  

 

Graduation Requirements

 

In order to obtain the MA degree in Democracy and Human Rights from Birzeit University, a student must  successfully complete a minimum of 36 credit hours,  in accordance with a plan  of study determined by the Program Council. Students  have an option to write an MA Thesis (Stream A) or  to take additional courses instead (Stream B).   Course work  is organized thus:

  1. Three required courses ( 9 credit hours) from Group I  ( DMHR 631, 632, 633)

  2. A minimum of one course (3 credit hours) from Group II (DMHR 634, 635)

  3. A minimum of one course (3 credit hours) from Group III (DMHR 636, 637)

  4. A minimum of five courses (15 credit hours) from Group IV (DMHR 630, 730-739).  A maximum of two courses (6 credit hours) from Group IV may be replaced by two  courses (6 credit hours)  chosen from other Programs.(Two Graduate courses, or one Graduate course  and a 400-level  course)

  5. Students who choose to write a Thesis (Stream A) must successfully complete DMHR  860, and pass their Thesis Defense.

  6. Students who choose not to write a Thesis must successfully complete DMHR 831 and 832, an pass  two Oral Examinations.
