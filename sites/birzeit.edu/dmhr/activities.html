Workshops

The MA Program in Democracy and Human Rights at Bierzeit University  held  a two-day workshop between  25-26 November 1999.  The workshop was intended to develop mutual understandings on content and methods of teaching Democracy and Human Rights, interaction with the community and Palestinian society at large, ways for keeping abreast with international developments in the theory and practice of  Democracy  and Human Rights.

Many people attended the workshop, including faculty  members from Birzeit and other Palestinian universities, representatives of Palestinian NGO's  and other organizations which work in the areas of Democracy and Human Rights, representatives from the Palestinian Ministry of  Higher Education and the Ministry of Education, and participants from international  organizations that are concerned with Human Rights ( the UN, UNESCO, UNICEF, Human Rights Watch, Red Cross, Red Crescent).

For a summary of  some of the discussions which took place, see the paper (in Arabic) "Curriculum and Methods of Teaching Democracy and Human Rights at Birzeit University" which was prepared by Raja Bahlul, Program Director.
