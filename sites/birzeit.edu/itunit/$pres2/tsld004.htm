The Structure

  • Module 1, will illustrate the MM and WWW development life-cycle and the role the ITNs must play in the overall market scene.

  • Module 2, will present by examples and cases the role of the ITN for the support of the supply part of the WWW and MM market.

  • Module 3, will present by examples and cases the role of the ITN for the support of the demand part of the WWW and MM market.

  • Module 4, is hidden behind all the other modules, since these modules will present by example successful action plans. But in the last day there will be a session to conclude everything discussed, and to assist ITNs to build a concrete action plan which fits better to their market’s local needs.
