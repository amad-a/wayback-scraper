Training Objectives

    To provide the IT nodes with the competence to organise training, build awareness and promote the importance of Software Quality Audit and Certification to SMEs, local industry and other organisations.
    In the long run, this training will assist IT nodes to:
  • generate the potential existing in their local markets
  • stimulate transfer and uptake of know-how
  • establish national networks of excellence
  • become fully integrated with European networks
