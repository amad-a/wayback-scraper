    Program Description

    This 56 hours training program is designed to provide network engineers with experience needed to administer, maintain, troubleshoot windows NT 4.0 networks. The program will be instructed by a certified Microsoft Systems Engineer. In addition, this program will include a heavy practical training component that will be given between modules. Computer labs will be converted into an enterprise environment, and participants will be requested to apply the training material to this environment.

    Practical Training
    In addition to the Microsoft Curriculum, most of the modules of this program will involve an additional practical training component. This will permit the trainees to apply the skills acquired in the training to an advanced working environment. The laboratory will be configured to simulate an organization or multiple organizations with multiple servers and functional descriptions. Trainees will be given different scenarios and cases to configure or complex problems to solve. At the end of each module the trainees will be given a project (preferably from their working environment) to work on.

    Back to top

    Training Requirements

    1. Trainees must have strong background in computer work some experience in networking (priority will be given to participants with stronger experience).
    2. Participants must complete all requirements of a module and pass the evaluation criteria to qualify for the following module
    3. Participants must develop a project from their professional environments based on the skills acquired during the training. The projects will be evaluated.
