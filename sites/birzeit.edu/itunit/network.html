    Advanced Seminar In Internetworking

    The Information Technology Unit is conducting an advanced seminar/workshop in Internetworking . The duration of the seminar is one day. The training class will be an actuall design and implementation solution for a fictitious company called Xellex. The topics discussed will be in the following:

    1- Business objective of the company and how to develop the solution

    2- Cabling and infrastructure, CAT 5, E1, ISDN, Frame Relay

    3- Ethernet, Frame Formats and Sniffer Software

    4- Hubs and Switches, Collision and broadcast domains, Class I and Class II

    Repeaters

    5- Routers Static, RIP I, RIPII, OSPF

    6- Network Operating Systems, Novell/NT/Linux

    7- DMZ and Security
