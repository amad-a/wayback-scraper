P R O G R A M M E

 

 

• Aim of the Training Seminar on Management

The aim of the seminar is to provide experts from IT centers in the various Mediterranean countries with a qualification in regards to organisational and management activities in the field of information and communication technology (basic knowledge in regards to working in cooperation with other organisations, project management, relationship with companies, technology transfer, management of changes).

At the end of the seminar, participants will have learned the methodology to manage the project and twill be capable of setting up a local IT cooperative network; the action plan will be established for 1999

• Duration

35 hours on two parts (3 days + 2 days, one month later)

• The public targeted

Decision-maker (directors, technical or commercial managers, project managers, those responsible for programmes or studies...) working within of Information Technology Centers.

• Number of participants in the seminar

10 or 12

• Profile of the participants

In order to participate actively in the seminar, the participants must have the following background :

- academic training level Bac + 4 minimum,

- a university degree in the field of information technology,

- professional experience (periods of training within a company for an educational institution are not taken into consideration) with 5 years minimum within a national organisation or specialised organisation in information technology,

- a position as a decision-maker within a project (or research programme) consisting of several engineers or technicians (responsible for a team) or several organisations (responsible for administrative or financial management) for at least one year,

- fluent in French or English speaking (according to the seminar).

• Teacher

1 teacher from Bureau van Dijk
