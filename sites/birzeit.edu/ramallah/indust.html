Local Industry

Try a tour of one of the local factories for an inside look at production as well as the obligatory tasting!


Taybeh Beer Company

(Taybeh, tel. 02 998 5868)

The first Palestinian beer and the only micro-brewery in the Middle East. Though still a new comer, this much written about beer is growing more popular everyday, despite heavy Israeli and international competition. Nadim Khoury, owner of Taybeh, attributes the popularity of his beer to its pure ingredients and good quality. The factory welcomes visitors and will gladly give you a tour of the site.
