Palestinian Main Meals

Musakhan chicken cooked on bread with spices, onion and oil.

Ma'lubah layers of rice,meat and vegetables served upside down.

Mujadara rice cooked with lentils and onions.

Khudra Mahshi practically any type of vegetable stuffed with rice and sometimes meat as well.

Waraq Dawali vine leaves stuffed with spiced rice and sometimes meat.

Moloquia this is a green vegetable something like spinach, made into a soup-like stew and served with rice dishes.
