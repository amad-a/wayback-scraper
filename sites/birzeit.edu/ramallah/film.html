Al-Walid Cinema
(Shari' A-Nahda)

Ramallah used to have three cinemas, Al-Walid, Al-Jamil and A-Dunya. A-Dunya closed in 1948 and the remaining two closed during the Intifada due to a number of factors including the problem of curfews, strikes and the economic situation. Al-Walid has recently reopened to screen a mixture of Egyptian, Lebanese, Indian and American film releases. Two films are shown daily at 10am and 3pm and one of these films is repeated at 7.15pm. Tickets are 7NIS and 10NIS. The clientele appear to be exclusively male.

The Popular Arts Centre
(Al-Bireh, tel. 02 995 3891 Sat-Thur 10am-4pm)

This centre runs a regular programme of films (in Arabic, French and with English subtitles), theatre, lectures and classes in music and folk (dubka) dancing. It also organises a number of special events including the Palestine International Festival. Pick up a programme in the local area or call for information.

Birzeit University
(Public Relations Office, tel. 02 998 2059 Mon-Thur, Sat 8am-4pm)


The University is a short and speedy service ride from Ramallah and frequently hosts local and touring international performers. In the recent past these have included a troupe of Spanish Flamenco dancers, a British production of a Midsummer Night's Dream, a Mormon orchestra and a reading by the world renowned Palestinian poet Mahmoud Darwish. For information telephone the Public Relations Office.

Ramallah First Sarriyeh
(Lower Ramallah, tel. 02 995 2091)

The Ramallah First Sarriyeh has a pleasant swimming pool, with a women's day on Tuesdays. Entrance is 15 NIS. To get here walk down the Main Street and keep going for about twenty five minutes, it will be on your right hand side.
