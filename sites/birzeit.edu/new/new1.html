The What Was New Archive

ARCHIVED LINKS NO.1
6 June - 9 July 1996

Compiled by the Birzeit Webmaster, webmaster@birzeit.edu.
  • 9 July 1996 - PRESS RELEASE ADDED. A student has been put in detention for a year, without charge or trial, by the Israeli Military Authorities. This link will get you to the story, released today.

  • 6 July 1996 - SHORT UPDATE. For those interested in the student HTML training mentioned in a couple of entries below, it finished today an hour and a half ago (at 3.00pm)! The webmaster was very pleased to see that 60% of the students could be categorised as "excellent". Work from the students will soon be put on the web and the coming "New & Improved" Birzeit & the World Wide Web section will have a report on the training with links to the sections of the website that the students worked on for their training project. ANOTHER UPDATE. For those following the court case (see 25 & 26 June below), the General Prosecutor only signed the order last Thursday, so the eight days begin from then. Stay tuned, as this is truly a significant case to watch. ETA on the complete "New & Improved Website" is about a fortnight from today, all things being equal.

  • 3 July 1996 - Our phone, e-mail and fax lines have started working again, a couple of weeks after some people stole some cables between the University and Ramallah. Apparently, this not infrequent experience for us is the result of a booming trade in copper wire, which is melted down with gold to make jewellery. Mmmm. Please note that the major website facelift promised in the announcement below from 25 June will be delayed for maybe a couple of weeks after the intended deadline. This is due to the delays caused by the lack of phones and a few other minor technical difficulties. The wait will be worth it, we think, as we will be rectifying a few of the errors (such as in link colours) that have been kindly pointed out in comments to the webmaster. Thanks again. For those interested in the student training mentioned in the website anouncement of 25 June, we are on day three and the courses are going well. This one course, of around 17 students - our first for students at the university - will probably double the number of people who can HTML in Palestine, as unbelievable as this may sound!

  • 3 July 1996 - Many apologies for all of those who have tried to get us in the last day or two and have had a "Node currently unreachable" message. We are now alive. It seems that our webserver, Felafel, keeps getting tired and goes to sleep, powering itself down every few weeks. Having rested, Felafel is now back on-line.

  • 26 June 1996 - Check out the result of today's hearing at the Palestinian High Court, mentioned in yesterday's release (see below).

  • 25 June 1996 - Check out this link to tomorrow's press release, detailing a significant court case to establish a legal precident in Palestine.

  • 25 June 1996 - Please be notified that the Birzeit Website will undergo a major facelift within the next fortnight. All our link colours and backgrounds will be standardised, before the website gets much larger and we lose all hope of this kind of maintainance! A few surprises lie in wait for the discerning browser. As students are finishing exams at the end of June, we are beginning training for them in preparation for the summer WWW volunteer programme. As well as encouraging students to produce their own home pages, they will be cutting their HTML teeth on adding to the Birzeit Archives section of the website and helping our various departments get on line. Stay tuned. In the meantime, if you haven't already done so, check out A Personal Diary in the Palestine Archive section of our website, for "on the ground" reports of some of the major events that have taken place this year that you may have read about in our press releases. Three hyperlinks in one sentence can't be wrong.

  • 23 June 1996 - IMPORTANT NOTE - Please note that the telephone lines between Birzeit and Ramallah are currently down, making telephone calls, faxes and e-mail impossible. Copper wire theives again, we are told! No word yet as to when this service will be resumed. You can contact the university in the meantime by e-mail via the following address: nparry@baraka.org Faxes to the university can be sent via the Birzeit Board of Trustees building on +972-2-995-4383.

  • 23 June 1996 - The Birzeit Press Release Archive section of the Birzeit Archives was activated today, containing almost all of the press releases from the beginning of 1996, pending one last update in the next few days. All press releases will be added to the archive at the same time they are sent out as e-mail on the Internet, making this the most reliable source of Birzeit press releases.

  • 21 June 1996 - The English language Jerusalem Post and Jerusalem Times newspapers featured the Birzeit Website today, both articles with pictures.

  • 16 June 1996 - This "What's New" page was introduced, along with information about events going on in Palestine, currently the Palestine International Festival 1996.

  • 13 June 1996 - We press released the various Palestine-relevant newsgroups and friends on our e-mail mailing lists, announcing our website launch.

  • 11 June 1996 - We began the process of announcing our presence on the Internet, registering with the various search engines.

  • 11 June 1996 - Web counters were installed on the homepage, archives, and organisations with pages. Counter Digits, who provides this service, has us on counters that does not record repeated hits from the same browser.


  • 6 June 1996 - We were connected to the Internet via UNDP in Jerusalem, the Internet service provider to the Palestinian Universities. Our first hit was from a lucky browser in the University of Missouri.
