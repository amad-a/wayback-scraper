Bibliography of Palestinian and Middle East costume and material culture

This is a Palestine Costume Archive working document, subject to regular updating. All care has been taken with titles and publication details, however we would be happy to correct any errors or add new references if contacted.

For those wishing to collect books on this subject, a small recommended reading list can be found in Archive Publications and under each country in Costume from other areas of the Middle East .  A full annotated bibliography is available upon request.  For those interested in contemporary Palestinian embroidery more information can be found in Contemporary post 1948 Palestinian costume and embroidery.
