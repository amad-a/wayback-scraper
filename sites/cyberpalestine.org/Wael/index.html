Hello and welcome to my home page. If you expected a multimedia extravaganza, you are going to be disappointed... If you expected a page with some information about me and some interesting links, read on...

I recently  completed my law degree at the University of Kentucky College of Law in Lexington, Kentucky, USA. I graduated in December 1999 and got my law license in May 2000. 

I was born and spent my early childhood in the village of Shuqba in the West Bank, Palestine, but I have been living in the United States ever since with my parents and six younger brothers. I am married and my wife, Kholoud, is a nursing undergraduate. We have an 2 year-old son named Mohammad.

I graduated from the Math, Science, and Technology Center (MSTC) at Paul Laurence Dunbar High School (also in Lexington) in 1993. I entered UK in the Fall of 1993 and graduated with a B.S. in Biology in May 1997. During college, was involved with a few extra-curricular activities, including the local Muslim Students' Association and the UK Arab Students' Union. Some of my interests/hobbies include: computers, the stock market, historical fiction, and political activism. I don't have any mammalian pets but I do have a decent collection of gouaramis (mostly from my local Wal-Mart). I also enjoy meeting interesting, open-minded people participating in online discussion forums.
