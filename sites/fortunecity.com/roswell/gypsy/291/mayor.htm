Curriculum vitae of the Mayor
 
Birth: He was born in Halhul in 4/9/1929.
Mayor of Halhoul Municipality
Mr. Mohammed Milhim.
 
Birth: He was born in Halhul in 4/9/1929.

Education: He studied in the Arab College in Jerusalem in 1947. Then in Lebanese University from which he obtained a certificate in English literature in 1974.
Experience:
Five years (1950-1955) Head instructor - Aramco in Saudi Arabia. 
Twenty years (1956-1976) High School Teacher of English - Jordan.
Seven years (1984-1991) Member of PLO (Palestinian Liberation Organization) Executive Committee.
Head of the Depts of education of occupied Territories.

 
Presently: 
Member of PLO Central Counsel. 
Consultant of chairman Arafat.  
Mayor of Halhul Municipality.  
Head of the bureau of statistics Damascus.  
Status: Married and has six sons, three daughters and twelve grand children.

Deported by Israel in 2/5/1980. 

Allowed back home in 21/4/1996. 

 
Internationally:
  1. Member of the United Towns of the world.
  2. Member of Arab town Organization.
  3. Represented Palestine:
    1. Non Aligned Summit - 1987.
    2. Arab Summit - 1986.
    3. Islamic Summit - 1988.
    4. UN General Assembly / Security Council between 1984 and 1990.
    5. Inauguration of the Presidents of: Brazil, Peru and Mexico.
