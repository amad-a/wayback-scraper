Israeli violations during the months of June, July, and August

House Demolitions:

30 houses in the West Bank and Jerusalem were demolished, and 147 others received a notice of demolition. In addition, dozens of sheep barracks were destroyed in different areas in the West Bank. The total number of demolished houses since the beginning of this year amounted to 80.

Killings:

  • On 16 June 1998, 49 year-old Abdul Majid Mohammad Abu-Turki, resident of Hebron, was shot by two Israeli settlers.
  • On 10 September 1998, the Israeli Military Forces shot Adel Awadallah and his brother Imad, both residents of El-Bireh.
  • On 17 September 1998, 17 year-old Iyad Qarabsah, resident of Bitunya, was shot by Israeli settler Avashalom Ladani.

Attacks by Settlers:

  • In addition to daily attacks on the vegetable market in Hebron,
  • Settlers in Hebron opened fire at Hebron Police Deputy Wasef Tahboub while he was travelling in his car. The shooting resulted in damage to the car.
  • Settlers in Hebron attacked with stones and bottles the house of Abu-Eisha causing serious head injury to child Bajes Abu-Eisha.
  • Settlers in Hebron set fire to a brass-welding factory owned by Jawad Abu-Eisha.
  • Settlers attacked al-Musrara quarter in East Jerusalem, destroying Palestinian-owned shops and vehicles.
  • Settlers from Kfar Atzion uprooted trees and assaulted the owner, Ali Abu-Swayy.

Trespass and Property Damage:

  • Salem, Nablus, settlers set fire to a five-dunum wheat field. A similar act took place in Kufr Qaddoum, Nablus.
  • Settlers bulldozed one thousand olive trees owned by the residents of Salfit. The settlers were planning to establish a new settlement on the land.
  • Wadi El-Shunnar, Hebron, the Israeli Military Forces confiscated land owned by Wadi El-Shunnar residents. The Israeli Forces bulldozed the land to expand the highway, which leads to Kharsina settlement.
  • Beit-Amin, Qalqilya, the Israeli Forces confiscated land owned by Beit-Amin residents. The Israeli Forces bulldozed the land to expand Shiari Tekfat settlement.
  • Similar actions were taken in Dier Istya, Nablus / Kufr Thulth, Qalqilya / Wadi Fukin, Bethlehem.

Land Confiscation and Settlements:

  • Silwan, East Jerusalem, the Al-Qara'in family received a notice from the Israeli authorities stating that their land is categorized as "Absentee's Property" and therefore, will be confiscated.
  • The Israeli Ministry of Interior gave the green light to the Jerusalem Municipality to build 58 housing units to accommodate Bet Orah's students on Mount of Olives in East Jerusalem.
  • Husan, Bethlehem, the Israeli authorities confiscated around 5000 dunums of land owned by the residents of Husan to build a new settlement.
  • Ras El- Amoud, East Jerusalem, the Israeli authorities approved 156 housing unit permits.
