PALESTINIAN NGO WORKING GROUP FOR THE ENFORCEMENT OF THE FOURTH GENEVA CONVENTION
Al-Haq, together with four other Palestinian NGOs, formed a working group to encourage effective and principled actions by the High Contracting Parties to the Fourth Geneva Convention of 1949 to defend international humanitarian law in general, and enforce to the Fourth Geneva Convention in the territories occupied by Israel in particular. The working group will call on the High Contracting Parties to the Fourth Geneva Convention to comply with their Article 1 responsibility and ensure Israel's respect for its provisions.
The United Nations General Assembly Emergency Session resolutions [ES 10/2 - 10/5] reaffirmed that Israel is bound to apply the Convention de jure in all the territories occupied in 1967, including East Jerusalem.
