THE PALESTINIAN COMMITTEE OF HUMAN RIGHTS ORGANIZATIONS (PCHRO)
A meeting was convened between members of the Palestinian Legislative Council's Human Rights Monitoring Committee and PCHRO. Modalities of joint actions and coordination were discussed to safeguard Palestinian human rights against violations.
The establishment of a sub-committee composed of representatives of PCHRO and the PLC's Human Rights Monitoring Committee was recommended to coordinate work.
Another meeting was held between PCHRO and the Ministry of Agriculture to discuss the Israeli human rights violations in the agricultural sector. The Minister of Agriculture raised the issue of the systematic destruction of the Palestinian Agricultural Sector as a result of the Israeli occupation. Farmers and fishermen are both seriously affected by this campaign to impede Palestinian economic development. It was noted that the most serious violations occur during the summer months when farmers are preparing to harvest their crops. The Ministry provided PCHRO with files containing all the statistics and information gathered by them for the past two years. The parties agreed that a campaign against Israeli army and settler practices towards farmers must be launched to stop the violations and to secure compensation to the injured farmers.
