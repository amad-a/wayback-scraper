OSLO - FIVE YEARS LATER:

WILL ISRAELI VIOLATIONS EVER STOP?

Demolition of Houses

During the last five years, about 547 houses have been demolished in the Occupied Territories under the pretexts of security or the lack of building permits and are therefore illegal structures. Houses are frequently built without permits because the period required for processing planning applications is so long as to force commencement of illegal construction. There is no legal obligation to offer compensation for such demolitions. If we accept local engineers' estimate that the average cost of building a house is at least JD 20,000, then the value of all houses totally demolished since the Oslo Agreement amounts to at least JD 10.94 million.

Uprooting Trees

A form of destruction of Palestinian capital, which has been common for years, is the uprooting of fruit and olive bearing trees. In the last five years, Israeli bulldozers have uprooted approximately 72 500 trees. The practice shows no sign of abating. Uprooting trees is officially justified as either "security" or as "eminent domain" measures for the purposes of settlement expansion and the construction of by-pass roads. On average, income from olive oil constitutes approximately one-third of total agricultural income in Palestine.

Land Confiscation

Land confiscation has increased substantially during the past five years. The Israeli military government confiscated about 192 000 dunums since September 1993. The practice is justified as "eminent domain"; the power to take private property for public use by the state. This justification has no merit in law. The property is confiscated from Palestinian owners and used for establishing and expanding existing settlements, and constructing by-pass roads.
During the past five years, it became clear that Israeli policies against Palestinians have nothing to do with "security", or "law", and has everything to do with punishment, and reprisal. As such, these policies are blatantly illegal. Despite its' illegality, however, appeals against these violations to the Israeli High Court of Justice have not provided an effective remedy.
