News in Brief...
Danish Delegation Visits al-Haq
On 13 July 1998, a Danish delegation of 25 visited al-Haq to express support for education in Palestine. Al-Haq informed the delegation about the current educational situation in Palestine and about the human rights situation in general. The parties agreed on future exchange visits and cooperation through Dan Church Aid Institution.
Salam al-Zaitoun Visits al-Haq
On 25 July 1998, delegates from the Italian human rights organization Salam al-Zaitoun met with al-Haq. The meeting focused on Israeli human rights violations against Palestinians in the occupied territories. In addition, the delegation expressed its concerns regarding the human rights situation under the Palestinian National Authority, and the role the Palestinian human rights organizations play.
The United Nations-High Commissioner For Human Rights
On 28 July 1998, al-Haq presented an affidavit to the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights in Amman, Jordan. The affidavit addressed the Israeli violations against Palestinians, in particular the establishment and expansion of settlements and their effect on Palestinian civilians, the deteriorating health conditions of Palestinian prisoners in Israeli detention centers, and the closure policy, which Israel forces against Jerusalem and the Gaza Strip. In addition, al-Haq commented on the Israeli draft "Intifada Law," which denies compensation to Palestinians injured during the Intifada.
Jericho Central Prison
On 4 August 1998, representatives from al-Haq's Legal Counseling Department visited Jericho Central Prison and met with officials to discuss means of cooperation between the two sides in matters relating to human rights. The parties agreed on modalities to facilitate cooperation and interventions when need arises.
Experts on the Fourth Geneva Convention meet in Gaza
On the 8th and 9th of August 1998, al-Haq participated in the meetings of experts held in Gaza. The meetings focused on ways by which Israel is to be brought into compliance with the Fourth Geneva Convention. The meeting also dealt with General Assembly Emergency Session resolutions [ES 10/2 - 10/5]. The experts came up with various recommendations.
YMCA Invites al-Haq to Lecture on Human Rights
On 10 August 1998, al-Haq gave a lecture on human rights and international law at the YMCA, Ramallah. Al-Haq discussed the Israeli violations against Palestinians and the applicability of international declarations and conventions to the occupied territories.
Al-Haq visits Nevi Tertza Detention Center
On 17 August 1998, al-Haq visited Nevi Tertza detention center in Ramleh and met with Palestinian women detainees. The detainees informed al-Haq about bad detention conditions and discriminatory practices employed by prison officials. The detainees told al-Haq that Israel rarely issues visit permits to their relatives, and when they do, they keep the visitors waiting long hours before admitting them into the detention center.
Al-Haq visits Nitsan Prison
On 17 August 1998, al-Haq visited Nitsan prison and met with eight political prisoners from the Syrian Golan. The visit came after reports that Arab prisoners at Nitsan were being subjected to continuous harassment, i.e. beating and confiscation of personal belongings. The prisoners demanded that all their personal belongings be returned, that their relatives be allowed to visit them, and that they be separated from civil prisoners.
Al-Haq Goes on Air
Al-Haq participated in a special BBC production on the role of human rights organizations in the Arab world. Al-Haq addressed the problem of coordination between the organizations. In addition, al-Haq suggested a list of priorities, which need to be addressed and dealt with by organizations.
In the documentary series 'Prisoners in Israeli Prisons' produced by al-Watan television, al-Haq explained the significant role of non-governmental organizations and other institutions in releasing the prisoners, and ensuring adequate services and healthy living conditions inside prisons.
Meeting with Netherlands Representative Office
On 19 August 1998, al-Haq met with the Dutch Representative in Ramallah. Al-Haq spoke about the progress it has made since the period of re-organization. Information was given to Mr. Rentenaar, which describes al-Haq's plans for the coming period, and the role al-Haq plays in the community. Mr. Rentenaar expressed the support of his country for Palestinians, and spoke about the efforts that are being exerted to explore the ramifications for the Dutch government should it continue to strengthen its ties with the Palestinians.
The Awadallahs, ...Prisoners in their Own House
Following the escape of Imad Awadallah from prison, the Palestinian Preventive Security imposed a house arrest on Imad Awadallah's family. Al-Haq visited the family to investigate their living conditions. Al-Haq reported a severe deterioration in living conditions due to house arrest. Subsequently, al-Haq organized a demonstration with other human rights organizations and PLC members, to protest human rights violations against the Awadallah family.
Al-Haq Reports on Political Arrests in Palestinian National Authority
In a meeting between The Palestinian Human Rights Organizations Council (PHROC) and the PLC's Human Rights Monitoring Committee, al-Haq presented a special report on political arrests in the Palestinian National Authority, and the political, legal, social, economic, and psychological effects it has on prisoners.
