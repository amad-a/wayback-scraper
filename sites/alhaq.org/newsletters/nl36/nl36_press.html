September 1998 | 36th Issue

PRESS RELEASES
  • On 18 July 1998, al-Haq issued a press release condemning the Israeli military raid on Rashaideh village. Al-Haq regarded the operation and its ramifications as an extreme violation of Palestinian residents' rights. In addition, al-Haq viewed the attack as a representation of the Israeli settlement policy, which employs all means to expel residents from their land. Al-Haq demanded the immediate halt of confiscating Palestinian land for military, settlement, or any other purposes, and demanded that Israel abide by international agreements, especially the Fourth Geneva Convention of 1949.
  • On 23 July 1998, al-Haq issued a press release condemning the establishment of an Israeli police station in the Old City of occupied Jerusalem. The act occurred at a time when the status of Jerusalem has yet to be determined through final status negotiation between the Israeli government and the Palestinian Authority. According to the Oslo Agreement, both parties to the Agreement are to refrain from any measures that will alter the status quo of the City. Al-Haq demanded that Israel reverse its decision and refrain from creating facts on the ground in an attempt to pre-determine the final status of the City.
  • On 30 July 1998, al-Haq issued a press release condemning the neglect of Israeli prison officials in ensuring care and medical treatment for Palestinian prisoners. Al-Haq held Israel responsible for the death of Mr. Jamal Khamisi, who suffered from liver cancer and failed to receive adequate medical treatment while he was imprisoned. Al-Haq demanded that Israel comply with the Fourth Geneva Convention of 1949, specifically with Articles 91 and 92, which emphasize the right to proper health and medical care for prisoners. In addition, al-Haq demanded that Israel comply with the Gaza-Jericho Agreement and release all Palestinian prisoners in accordance with the terms of the Agreement.
  • On 13 August 1998, al-Haq issued a press release confirming that the death of Mr. Walid Qawasmeh, who died on the 9th of August 1998, while in the custody of the General Palestinian Intelligence, was due to severe physical torture. Al-Haq viewed the death of Mr. Qawasmeh as a clear violation of international conventions, especially the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights, the Convention against Torture and other Cruel, Inhuman, or Degrading Treatment, or Punishment, and the Body of Principles for the protection of All Persons under Any form of Detention or Imprisonment. Al-Haq demanded that the Palestinian Authority take firm steps to ensure that the Palestinian Security Apparatus respect its commitment to international treaties and denounce torture, all forms of mistreatment, harsh punishment, and inhumane or humiliating acts. In addition al-Haq demanded that steps be taken to ensure the prosecution, with sound and public legal procedures, of all those who are responsible for human rights violations or excesses, whether relating to this particular event or to the work of the Palestinian Security Apparatus in general.
  • On 29 August 1998, al-Haq issued a press release condemning the intense military siege and the curfew imposed on the city of Hebron. Due to the military siege, many residents, either from the City itself or from the surrounding areas, were unable to seek urgent medical assistance from health centers in the City, which led to several deaths. Al-Haq demanded the immediate lift of the military siege and the curfew, and that Israel refrains from its illegal actions against the residents of Hebron. In addition, al-Haq demanded that the High Contracting Parties to the Fourth Geneva Convention undertake their Article 1 responsibilities to ensure respect for the Convention and to ensure that Israel cease its violations against the residents of Hebron.
  • On 1 September 1998, al-Haq and Mandela Institute issued a joint press release condemning the first implementation of a death sentence by the Palestinian National Authority and viewed it as an alarming precedent of violating Palestinian civil liberties. The release emphasized the recommedations of the International Conference convened by Amnesty International in Stockholm in December 1977, to abolish the death penalty. Al-Haq and Mandela called on the Palestinian National Authority to become one of those countries which does not practice the death penalty.
  • On 9 September 1998, al-Haq issued a press release condemning the practice of torture and inhumane acts by the Palestinian Security Apparatus. Mr. Sharif Jabarin was detained by the Palestinian Preventive Security. Throughout his detention, he was subjected to various forms of torture and inhumane treatment as well as insults and degrading remarks. According to the medical report conducted upon his release, severe bruising from torture was evident on his shoulders, face, back, buttocks, and genital area. In addition, his hearing has been partially impaired due to severe beating to his left ear.
  • On 17 September 1998, al-Haq issued a press release condemning the murder of the 'Awadallah brothers and the unlawful burial of their bodies by the Israeli government. Al-Haq condemned the premeditated, extra-judicial execution, which constitutes a clear violation of the Fourth Geneva Convention, specifically Article 32, which prohibits all measures that cause the physical suffering or extermination of protected persons whether by murder, or other measures of brutality, applied by civilian or military agents. In addition al-Haq viewed the decision of the Israeli Authorities concerning the burial of the bodies as a violation of the laws and regulations of international humanitarian instruments. Al-Haq demanded the immediate hand over of the bodies so that they be properly buried according to the wishes of their families.
  • On 22 September 1998, al-Haq issued a press release condemning the murder of Iyad Qarabsah by Avashalom Ladani, a 35 year-old settler. Ladani opened fire at a group of Palestinian teenagers resulting in the death of Iyad Qarabsah and seriously wounding Issa Jabarin. Al-Haq demanded that the Israeli government take on its legal responsibility to ensure the protection of Palestinians against all acts or threats of violence by Israeli settlers.
