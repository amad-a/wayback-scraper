LEGAL SERVICE UNIT
Al-Haq's Legal Service Unit (LSU) continues to provide free consultative services on legal and administrative issues of immediate concern to members of the Palestinian local community. Al-Haq advises clients of the legal resources available to them, and, where appropriate, advises them to consult a lawyer of their choice. Al-Haq itself does not take cases to court, nor does it offer financial aid for that purpose. However, when the issue involves a human rights violation, al-Haq intervenes directly on behalf of the client in an attempt to restore the individual's rights under the law.
Between 1 February 1998 and 10 August 1998, al-Haq's LSU intervened in 154 cases. Following is a breakdown of the type and number of cases.
