Al-Haq Urges International Protection Force for Palestinian Civiliansas Israeli Aggression Escalates
Today at the time of the writing of this press release, the most intensive attacks to date are being carried out in Beit Jala and Beit Sahour. Three civilian homes have been destroyed in Beit Sahour and civilian areas are being shelled by tanks and helicopters in Beit Jala.  Three more Palestinian civilians were shot dead by Israeli Occupying Forces in the West Bank and Gaza Strip and yesterday, October 31 five Palestinians were killed. According to al-Haq fieldworkers this brings the death toll to 147 Palestinians and the number of injured to nearly 5500. The Israeli Army’s excessive use of force has intensified as evidenced by the increasing number of gunshot wounds to the head and chest.

Today, November 1, those shot and killed were:
1. Ahmad Salman Tayeh, 15, from al-Shati Camp Gaza
2. Mohammad Ibrahim Hajjaj, 17, from al-Shati Camp, Gaza
3. Mohammad Mahmoud, from the Palestinian National Police Forces, 27, from Hebron 

Those killed on October 31 were as follows:
1. Shadi Awad Odeh, 22, from Gaza
2. Hazem Musa Abu Daf, 22, from Gaza
3. Mohammad Khazaa Hallass, 23, from Gaza
4. Mahmoud Ahmad Abu Al-Kheir, 20 from Gaza
5. Thaer Ibrahim Abu Zaid, 18, Al-Jalazoun Refugee Camp, near Ramallah

The Israeli Occupying Forces continue to intensify their assault against Palestinian private property as Israeli Forces shelled a Fateh office in a one-story building in al-Bireh on October 30. Nearby buildings were damaged by shrapnel and broken glass. In the Gaza Strip, the offices of Force 17 in Khan Younis were damaged by machine gunfire. Nearby homes and businesses were also damaged during the assault. The Fateh Office in Nablus was also struck by Israeli helicopter gunships and damaged severely.

Today, Israeli Occupying Forces heightened their siege of Occupied East Jerusalem by increasing the presence of the Israeli Occupying Forces, border guards and police. Once more, Israel continues to act in total disregard of international humanitarian law and international human rights principles and daily demands by local and international NGOs continue to be ignored.

Al- Haq views with concern the continued  Israeli aggression against Palestinian civilians, and reiterates the following:
1. The United Nations Security Council should take the necessary measures to pressure the Israeli government  to stop its excessive use of force and aggression against Palestinian civilians and immediately send an international force to protect Palestinian civilians. 
2. The United Nations Security Council should take action and form a tribunal in order to begin the trials of  war criminals.
   3. The reconvening of the adjourned meeting of July 15, 1999 of all High Contracting Parties to the   Fourth Geneva Convention. High Contracting Parties are obliged by Article 1 of the Fourth   Geneva Convention “to respect and ensure respect for the present Convention in all   circumstances.” Furthermore Article 146 states that High Contracting Parties are committed   “to enact any legislation necessary to provide effective penal sanctions for persons committing   or ordering to be committed any of the grave breaches of the Convention” and “… and shall   bring such persons regardless of their nationality before such courts.”
