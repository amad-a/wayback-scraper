Al-Haq Urges the United Nations Security Council to Convene Meeting Immediately in Order to Provide International Protection Forces for the Palestinian Occupied Territories
For the second consecutive month, the Israeli Occupying Forces continued its excessive use of force against Palestinian civilians. To date, al-Haq documented 139 Palestinian deaths, of which 45 are children, since the eruption of clashes in the Palestinian Occupied Territories. During the past four days, 11 Palestinians were killed and more than 500 were injured. Those killed were:

Jabir al- Misihal,23, from al- Shati refugee camp in Gaza  
Bashir Shalwit, 15, from the West Bank city of Qalqilyah
Ahmad Qasim, 28, from Tulkarm
Ghassan ‘Awaysah, 27, from Bietounaya in Ramallah
Shadi al- Shouli, 23, from Asirah al- Shamaleyyeh in Nablus
Fadi Dabayah, 22, Jenin refugee camp, Jinin
Ismat al- Sabir, 35, from Nablus
Samir ‘Liewah, 31, from al-Zaytoun in Gaza
*Bilal Abu Salah, 19, from Ya’bad in Jenin
*Hilal Abu Salah,20, from Ya’bad in Jenin
Husni al- Najjar, 15 from Rafah
*Hilal and Husni Abu Salah are brothers.

During these clashes, Israeli Occupying Forces continued their use of live ammunition, rubber- coated steel bullets, exploding dum- dum bullets, heavy machine gunfire and helicopter gunships. At the same time, the Israeli Occupying Forces have intensified their siege and aggression on the Palestinian Occupied Territories. Ahmad Abdelqader Sbietani, 64, from Hebron, died as a result of being delayed at an Israeli Army checkpoint on his way to Ramallah hospital for urgent medical treatment. Yesterday, Israeli settlers shot and wounded a Palestinian guard, Musa Hirzullah, at the Augusta Victoria Hospital in Jerusalem. Al-Haq considers this a violation of Article 18, 19 and 20 of the Fourth Geneva Conventions of 1949.

Today, Israeli settlers opened fire upon a  broken-down,  Palestinian car on the road between Hebron and Halhoul. Iyyad Ayed Abdiljalil Quqas, 28, from Biet Ummar in Hebron was injured in his left hand as a result of the gunfire.    

Al- Haq views with concern the Israeli aggression against Palestinian civilians, and urges the following:

1. The United Nations Security Council should take the necessary measures to pressure the Israeli Government  to stop its excessive use of force and aggression against Palestinian civilians and send, immediately,  an international force to protect Palestinian civilians. 

2. The United Nations Security Council should take actions and form a tribunal in order to begin the trials of  war criminals.

3. The reconvening of the adjourned meeting of July 15, 1999 of all High Contracting Parties to the Fourth Geneva Convention. High Contracting Parties are obliged by Article 1 of the Fourth Geneva Convention “to respect and ensure respect for the present Convention in all circumstances.” Furthermore Article 146 states that High Contracting Parties are committed “to enact any legislation necessary to provide effective penal sanctions for persons committing or ordering to be committed any of the grave breaches of the Convention” and “… and shall bring such persons regardless of their nationality before such courts.”
