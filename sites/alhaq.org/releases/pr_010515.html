Press Release 

May 15 , 2001
Press Release Index

Four Palestinians Killed Al-Nakba Protests Met by Violence

Demonstrations and rallies were held throughout the Occupied Territories today in commemoration of al-Nakba, which is held every year to remember Palestinian displacement and to demand the right of return for Palestinian Refugees, as well as to protest against the continuing Israeli occupation of the West Bank and the Gaza Strip. In acts of civil disobedience against the restrictions placed upon the Palestinian people by Israeli Forces, groups of protestors throughout the territories marched to checkpoints and roadblocks where they were met with violent responses from Israeli forces. 

In Ramallah protestors marched to a checkpoint at the northern edge of the city where they were met with live and rubber bullets. Al-Haq monitored the protest and by all accounts it was generally peaceful and no threat was posed to the Israeli soldiers when they opened fire. At the time of the writing of this news update two Palestinians had been killed and ten Palestinians and a French journalist had been wounded, one critically. Abdel Jowad Shehadeh (20) from Gaza was killed by a snipers bullet, which entered his neck and exited through his head. He was a member of the Palestinian police force. Burham Shakhir (22) from A-Ram was also killed in Ramallah. 
He was shot in the neck with a live bullet. The journalist was struck in the chest by live ammunition, but was saved by a bulletproof vest. All of those injured today are currently being treated at the Ramallah municipal hospital. 

This morning at Kalandia Refugee camp between Jerusalem and Ramallah peaceful protests turned into clashes when Israeli forces attacked protestors with teargas and clubs. A march by prominent Palestinian leaders, including several Palestinian members of the Israeli Knesset, proceeded from A-Ram near Jerusalem to a military roadblock near to the Kalandia camp. As the protestors approached the checkpoint the Israeli soldiers stationed there opened fire upon the protestors with teargas and charged the crowd wielding clubs. 

Clashes also broke out in Bethlehem and Hebron. Nine Palestinians have been reported wounded by rubber bullets in the Bab al-Zawieh and Al-Shalaleh Road areas of Hebron. Two Israeli soldiers were wounded near al-Fawar Refugee Camp near the village of Dura next to Hebron. The area was immediately designated a closed military zone and travel too and from the village has been prohibited. 

In Gaza another two Palestinians were killed today. Mahmoud Mohammad Jihad Abu Jaser (18) was killed in Beit Hannoun. He was shot in the chest with live ammunition during clashes between stone throwers and Israeli soldiers. At this time clashes are continuing in Erez and al-Mantar in Gaza. 

This morning Abed Hakim al-Manameh (35) from al-Maghazi Refugee camp was also killed when an Israeli tank shelled his car. This case is still being investigated by al-Haq. Al-Manameh was one of Sheikh Ahmed Yassins personal bodyguards. Sheikh Yassin is the spiritual leader of Hamas. Three other men were in the car with al-Manameh when he was killed. His car was in an area under Palestinian control when it was fired upon. Israeli forces briefly entered the area to retrieve the body of al-Manameh and to arrest one of the others in the car. Al-Manamehs body is still in the custody of the Israeli authorities. The Israeli authorities have claimed that the occupants of the car had been involved in the firing of two mortars at Kibbutze Kfar Aza earlier in the morning. Israeli forces also opened fire in the area where al-Manameh was killed using heavy machineguns. At least four Palestinians were reported injured. 

In response to recent events al-Haq now calls upon the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights to send several special rapporteurs to the Occupied Territories. Al-Haq makes a specific request for the visit of Dr. Asma Jahangir, Special Rapporteur of the Commission On Human Rights on extrajudicial, summary or arbitrary executions.
