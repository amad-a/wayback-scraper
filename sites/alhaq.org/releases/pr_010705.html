Al-Haq Appeals for the release of Mohammad Ahmed Abd al-Rahman

Al-Haq calls upon the International Community to demand the release of Mohammad Ahmed Abd al-Rahman from detention in Israeli prison. Al-Rahman (38) is a resident of Bethlehem, and is married with six children. He is currently employed at the Al-Quds Open University of Bethlehem and is working on his doctoral dissertation through Jordan University. He was arrested on April 13, 2001 as he was leaving for Jordan to work on his studies. 

According to information gathered by al-Haq from al-Rahmans lawyer, the Israeli authorities are holding him under the suspicion that he is a member of Islamic Jihad. He has been formally accused of carrying letters to and from Islamic Jihad activists in the West Bank and Jordan. 
Al-Rahman has appeared before a judge on numerous occasions since his arrest. There has been no evidence presented in any of these instances, and no witnesses have been called. Al-Rahman has testified to being interrogated for extended periods while handcuffed to a chair with his hands behind his back during his time in prison. However, he has not offered any confession. Due to the lack of evidence presented against him and his own unwillingness to confess no charges have been brought against him, but in each instance in which he has appeared before a judge he has been remanded back to prison and a new court date is set. His lawyers have been told that he was arrested based on information from intelligence sources, and that he is being held on secret evidence that cannot be aired publicly.

During one of his appearances before the court, his wife, who had come to visit him, was arrested for two hours. The stress of his wifes arrest combined with the difficulties of his own situation caused him to collapse and strike his head on the wall causing moderate injury. For two weeks he was kept in a collaborator cell in an attempt to get him to confess, and he has now been transferred to the Moskabiya Detention Center (Russian Compound) in Jerusalem. 

Al-Rahman has now been imprisoned for almost three months without having formal brought against him. Mr. Al-Rahmans case is but one example of the fate of many Palestinians who have been arbitrarily detained through court proceedings lacking in due process or through administrative detention orders. Israeli laws and regulations legalize arbitrary detention in several ways:

- The laws do not allow challenges to the lawfulness of detention, or for release if a detention is illegal - They allow incommunicado detention, delays in charging a detainee, and delays in bringing him or her before a judge.
- Interrogation by the Israeli intelligence system is systematically carried out without prisoners being afforded the presence of an attorney. It is often carried out over extended periods of time and in many cases prisoners are bound or handcuffed throughout the proceedings. These methods are used in order to extract a confession based on the presumption that the detainee is guilty.
- The 1945 British (Defense) Emergency Regulations which were revoked prior to the end of the British Mandate have been improperly reinstated by the Israeli authorities and permit the lengthy detention of persons, whom the military wishes to confine but whose alleged guilt it is unable to prove in a public court of law, without charge or trial. 

Since al-Haq was founded, evidence has been gathered by its fieldworkers and lawyers concerning thousands of arbitrary and illegal detentions carried out by the Israeli authorities. 
Following the outbreak of the current intifada the Israeli government began to intensify its use of administrative detention and arbitrary arrest.

Al-Rahman has now spent three months arbitrarily detained in prison without evidence ever having been presented against him. Al-Haq believes that al-Rahman is now at risk of being administratively detained. Al-Haq therefore asks the International community, international human rights organizations, and concerned individuals to take action and demand the immediate release of Mr. al-Rahman. We also ask that the International community demand that Israel respect Palestinians right to a fair trial, and request that they exert efforts aimed at ensuring that international humanitarian law and human rights standards are enforced in the Occupied Territories.
