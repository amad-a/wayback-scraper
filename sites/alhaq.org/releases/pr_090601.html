Al-Haq urges on UNESCO and the ICRC to intervene on behalf of Palestinian students

Al-Haq calls for UNESCO and the International Committee of the Red Cross to intervene on behalf of 854 Palestinian students held in Israeli detention centers, to be allowed to take their High school exams Tawjihi. Forty-eight thousand students throughout the Palestinian territories are currently sitting their public exams. Despite the strict closure that has been imposed on the occupied territories by the Israeli authorities, the Palestinian Ministry of Education reports that most students have been able to reach educational facilities in order to take their exams.
However, despite the best efforts of the Education Ministry, they have been unable to ensure that the 854 students held in Israeli detention facilities will be given the opportunity to sit their exams.
The Ministry has applied for permits from the Israeli authorities to allow teachers to conduct the exams in eleven halls designated for detainees. So far, the Ministry has not received a response from the Israeli authorities. The Tawjihi is of particular important, because it is from the results of these exams that determine students to inter to colleges and universities. 

Article 94 of the Fourth Geneva Convention obliges an occupying power to ensure those held as internees be given the opportunity to pursue their education. According to the article ..The education of children and young people shall be ensured; they shall be allowed to attend schools either within the place of internment or outside By so far denying the students under detention the opportunity to sit their exams, Israel is in breach of article 94 of the Convention.

In this light, Al-Haq calls on UNESCO and the International Committee of the Red Cross to intervene on behalf of the students.
