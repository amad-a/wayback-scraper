Internship Program

The al-Haq Internship Program provides an opportunity for individuals to gain practical experience in the field of human rights by working with a human rights organization. In selecting interns, al-Haq chooses those individuals who will most benefit from the experience as well as those who can contribute to the work al-Haq is undertaking in fulfillment of its mandate.

Internships run for a period of three to six months, and are voluntary (unpaid). Interns receive a stipend to cover local travel expenses, but otherwise must arrange their own funding for all costs associated with their internship.

Regarding applications for summer internships, al-Haq receives many such applications throughout the year.  Regretfully, al-Haq is only able to accept a limited number of interns each summer.  Thus, the deadline for receipt of applications for a summer internship at al-Haq is March 15th each year.  Decisions regarding all applications for a summer internship will be made by April 15th of each year.  All other applications for internship during the year will be considered and decided upon as they are received.  

A background in law, international politics, international relations, political science, Middle Eastern studies or a similar field is essential, as is familiarity with the history of the Palestinian-Israeli Conflict. Knowledge of international human rights law is useful. The working languages of al-Haq are Arabic and English. A strong command of written and spoken English is required. Knowledge of Arabic is helpful, but not required. Computer literacy and familiarity with word-processing programs is essential.

Interns are required to comply with al-Haq's policy and rules relating to health and safety, cultural diversity, equal opportunities and security regulations.

Note: The Internship Program does not lead to employment with al-Haq.

If you are interested in the al-Haq Internship Program, please contact Mr. kahder Rantisi krantisi@alhaq.org for further details and an application.
